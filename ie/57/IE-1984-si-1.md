# S.I. No. 1/1984 - Local Government (Planning and Development) (Exemption From Fees) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 1 of 1984.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (EXEMPTION FROM FEES) REGULATIONS, 1984.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963) and [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0021/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1982) and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Exemption From Fees) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1983 may be cited together as the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1984.

###### 3.

3. The Local Government (Planning and Development) (Fees and Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0030.html) ) are hereby amended by:—

( *a* ) the insertion before article 4 of the following article:—

"3A (1) Subject to sub-article (2), where a planning application relates in whole or in part to development which in the opinion of the planning authority—

( *a* ) is designed or intended to be used for social, recreational, educational or religious purposes by inhabitants of a locality generally or by people of a particular group or religious denomination and is not to be used mainly for profit to gain,

( *b* ) is designed or intended to be used as a hostel, work-shop or other accommodation for disabled, poor or homeless persons, and is not to be used mainly for profit or gain, or

( *c* ) is ancillary to development referred to at paragraphs (*a*) or (*b*),

a fee shall not be payable in respect of any such development.

- (2) Sub-article (1) shall have effect only where the relevant planning application is made by or on behalf of a voluntary organisation.";

( *b* ) the insertion in article 4 after "Subject" of "to article 3A and"; and

( *c* ) the deletion of article 15.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Fees and Amendment) Regulations, 1983 and provide that planning fees shall not be payable:

—in respect of applications for planning permission for certain development,

—for submissions or observations to a planning authority as regards a planning application.
