DISTRICT COURT (TAXES CONSOLIDATION ACT 1997) (AMENDMENT) RULES, 2004.

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Taxes Consolidation Act 1997) (Amendment) Rules 2004.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 6th day of October 2004 and shall be read together with all other District Court Rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be amended by the substitution for sub-rules (7), (8) and (9) of rule 2 of Order 38 of the following:

> - “(7) An application by an authorised officer of the Revenue Commissioners under the provisions of section 908A of [the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) as inserted by [section 207](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec207) of the [Finance Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) , and as amended by [section 68](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec68) of the [Finance Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , by [section 132](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec132) of the [Finance Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) and by [section 88](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec88) of the [Finance Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) , shall be by information on oath and in writing, in the Form 38.12, Schedule B. Such application shall be made to a District Court Judge assigned to the District Court District wherein the offence in question is alleged is being, has been or is or was about to be committed, or where the financial institution in respect of which the order is sought is located, and may be heard otherwise than in public.
>
> - (8) The order of the Court granting the application shall be in the Form 38.13, Schedule B, which shall, unless the Court otherwise directs, be served upon the relevant financial institution three clear days before the order is to be obeyed. Service may be effected by delivering a copy of the order to the office of the financial institution wherein the relevant books or records or other documents or documentation are kept”.

###### 4.

4. The Forms numbered 38.12 and 38.13 in Schedule 1 hereto shall be substituted for the Forms of the same number in Schedule B to the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules amend Rule 2, sub-rules (7), (8) and (9) Order 38 of the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and prescribe procedures under section 908A of [the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , as inserted by [section 207](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec207) of the [Finance Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) and as amended by [section 68](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec68) of the [Finance Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , by [section 132](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec132) of the [Finance Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) and by [section 88](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec88) of the [Finance Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) , whereby an authorised officer of the Revenue Commissioners may make application on oath and in writing to the District Court to inspect and take copies of books or records held in financial institutions where he/she suspects that there are reasonable grounds to suspect that an offence in serious prejudice to the proper assessment and collection of tax is being, or has been or is or was about to be committed and where such information is likely to be of substantial value to the investigateion.
