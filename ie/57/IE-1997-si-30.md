# S.I. No. 30/1997 - Salmon (Restriction of Fishing at Sea) (Amendment) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 30 of 1997.

SALMON (RESTRICTION OF FISHING AT SEA) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1997

I, SEÁN BARRETT, Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 223A (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1978), as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1983)), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Fisheries and Forestry (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0082.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Salmon (Restriction of Fishing at Sea) (Amendment) Order, 1997.

- (2) The Salmon (Restriction of Fishing at Sea) Order, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0117.html) ) and this Order may be cited together as the Salmon (Restriction of Fishing at Sea) Orders, 1984 and 1997.

###### 2.

2. The Salmon (Restriction of Fishing at Sea) Order, 1984 is hereby amended by the substitution for Article 2 of the following Article—

> "2. Fishing for salmon is hereby prohibited in that part of the exclusive fishery limits of the State that lies outside the line every point of which is at a distance of 6 nautical miles seaward from the nearest point of the baseline.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of January, 1997.

**SEÁN BARRETT,**

Minister for the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order reduces the sea area, in which salmon fishing is permitted, from twelve to six nautical miles from the baselines.
