# S.I. No. 136/2006 - Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 136 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0136.html)**

**Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006**

I, Martin Cullen. Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (inserted by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec12) of the [Road Traffic Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2002) and as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0044/print.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0044/print.html) (No. 44 of 2004)) of the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1975), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform in relation to declaring offences to be fixed charge offences, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 3 April 2006.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations -

“Act of 1975” means Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975 (No. 14 of 1975);

“Act of 1994” means Road Traffic Act 1994 (No. 7 of 1994);

“vehicle” means mechanically propelled vehicle.

###### 4.

4. The offences under the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 mentioned in Regulation 5 and Schedules 1 to 3 and [section 73](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0073.html#sec73) of the [Finance Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976) are declared to be fixed charge offences.

###### 5.

5. The amount in relation to a fixed charge is -

- (a) €80, in respect of the offence under section 35(5) of the Act of 1994 for a contravention of the provision of the Regulations mentioned in Schedule 1,

- (b) €60, in respect of an offence under section 73 (requiring a licence to be fixed to and exhibited on a vehicle) of the [Finance Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) ,

  and

- (c) €40, in respect of an offence under -

    - (i) a provision of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) mentioned in Part 1,

    - (ii) the provision of the Act of 1994 mentioned in Part 2, and

    - (iii) section 35(5) of the Act of 1994, for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Part 3,

of Schedule 2.

###### 6.

6. The form of notice set out in Part 1 of Schedule 3 at A is prescribed as a form of notice for the purposes of section 3 of the Act of 1975.

###### 7.

7. The form of document set out in Part 2 of Schedule 3 at B is prescribed as the form of document for the purposes of section 3(3)(i) of the Act of 1975.

###### 8.

8. The [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (Section 3) (Offences) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0395.html) ) are revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Regulations (operative from 3 April 2006) declare which offences involving the use of mechanically propelled vehicles are fixed charge offences for the purposes of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , determine the amount of fixed charge for each offence and prescribe the form of notice and document to be used in the enforcement of those fixed charge offences.
