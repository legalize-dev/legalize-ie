# S.I. No. 168/2014 - European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th April*, 2014.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 and, subject to Regulations 3 and 4, are deemed to have come into operation on 24 March 2014.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) Regulations 2014 (S.I. 112 of 2014) are amended—

- (*a*) In Regulation 4, by the substitution for “column (3)” of “column (4)”,

- (*b*) in Schedule 1, by the substitution for “348 of 2003” of “548 of 2003”, and

- (*c*) In Schedule 2, by—

    - (i) the substitution at reference 43 for “100 of 2009” of “100 of 2008”,

    - (ii) the substitution at reference 62 for “57 of 2011” of “57 of 2012”,

    - (iii) the substitution at reference 66 for “Regulation 21” of “Regulation 23”, and

    - (iv) the substitution at reference 67 for “Regulation 21” of “Regulation 22”.

###### 3.

3. Nothing in these Regulations purports to create a criminal offence or to revive criminal proceedings in respect of an event that occurred before the making of these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. For the avoidance of doubt, Regulation 4 of the European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) Regulations 2014 (S.I. 112 of 2014) is considered to have effect, and always to have had effect, as if the amendment effected by Regulation 2(*a*) of these Regulations always applied to the first mentioned provision.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 March 2014.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend typographical errors contained in the European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) Regulations 2014 (S.I. 112 of 2014).
