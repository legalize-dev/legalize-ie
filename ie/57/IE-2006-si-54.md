# S.I. No. 54/2006 - Misuse of Drugs (Exemption) (Amendment) Order, 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 54 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0054.html)**

**MISUSE OF DRUGS (EXEMPTION) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 2006**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (*No. 12 of 1977*), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997, ( *[S.I. No.308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html)* ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Exemption) (Amendment) Order, 2006.

###### 2.

2. The Misuse of Drugs (Exemption) Order , 1988 ( *[S.I. No. 326 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0326.html)* ) is hereby amended as follows:-

- (a) In Part 1 of the Schedule to the aforementioned Order, the following new paragraph is inserted:

3 Any substance, product or preparation including a Fungus of any kind or description, which contains psilocin or an ester of psilocin, where that substance or fungus is growing uncultivated.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

The effect of this Order is to exempt from the provisions of [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (*which makes the possession of controlled drugs an offence*) substances, products or preparations including those fungi of any kind or description, containing psilocin or an ester of psilocin (which are commonly described as “magic mushrooms”) in certain limited circumstances.
