# S.I. No. 421/2015 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th October*, 2015.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) (as amended by the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 446 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0446.html) )) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2—

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by substituting for the definition of “levy period” the following:

      > “ ‘levy period’ means the period commencing on 1 October 2015 and ending on 30 September 2016.”,

    - (ii) by deleting paragraph (2), and

    - (iii) in paragraph (3), by substituting “30 June 2015” for “30 June 2014”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3(1), by substituting “1 October 2015” for “1 October 2014”,

- (*c*) in Regulation 4(1), by substituting “29 February 2016” for “28 February 2015”, and

- (*d*) in the Schedule by deleting the rows at reference numbers 1 to 4.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 September 2015.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
