# S.I. No. 531/2015 - Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th November*, 2015.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 1 of Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 197 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0197.html) ) are amended by substituting in Regulation 2(1) the definition of “Technical Measures Regulation” for the following-

> “Technical Measures Regulation’ means Commission Regulation (EEC) No. 3440/84 of 6 December 1984[^2] (as amended by Commission Regulation (EEC) No 955/87 of 1 April 1987[^3] , Commission Regulation (EEC) No 2122/89 of 14 July 1989[^4] and Commission Regulation No 146/2007 of 15 February 2007[^5] ), and Council Regulation (EC) No 850/98 of 30 March 1998[^6] (as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No 308/1999 of 8 February 1999[^7] , Council Regulation (EC) No 1459/1999 of 24 June 1999[^8] , Council Regulation (EC) No 2723/1999 of 17 December 1999[^9] , Council Regulation (EC) No 1298/2000 of 8 June 2000[^10] , Council Regulation (EC) No 724/2001 of 4 April 2001[^11] , Council Regulation (EC) No 973/2001 of 14 May 2001[^12] , Council Regulation (EC) No 602/2004 of 22 March 2004[^13] , Council Regulation (EC) No 1568/2005 of 20 September 2005[^14] , Council Regulation (EC) No 2166/2005 of 20 December 2005[^15] , Regulation (EU) No. 579/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2011[^16] , Regulation (EU) No 227/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 March 2013[^17] , Council Regulation (EU) No 1385/2013 of 17 December 2013[^18] and Regulation (EU) 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015);”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal.

19 November 2015.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations, amend the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 197 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0197.html) ) which relate to technical conditions for all fishing operations (e.g. permitted nets, species composition of catches, etc.) to include Regulation (EU) 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015 as regards the landing obligation. These regulations make a contravention of any provisions of European legislation implementing technical measures, an offence under the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) .
