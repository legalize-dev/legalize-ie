# S.I. No. 582/2007 - Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 582 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0582.html)

ELECTRICITY REGULATION ACT 1999 (PUBLIC SERVICE OBLIGATIONS) (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th August*, 2007.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec39) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999) and the Communications, Energy and Geological Survey of Ireland (Transfer of Departmental and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0300.html) ) (and as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )), in consideration of general social, economic and environmental factors and following consultation with the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, and having, in accordance with section 39(10) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , given a draft of this Order to the Electricity Supply Board, being the person upon whom the obligations are to be imposed, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Public Service Obligations) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0217.html) ) is amended:

- (*a*) in Article 2(1) by substituting the following for the definition of “levy period”:

  > “ ‘levy period’ means such period as the Commission may from time to time designate following public consultation; ”,

- (*b*) in Article 9(3)(*a*) by substituting “At least 5 months prior to the commencement of” for “No later than 30 April in the year immediately preceding”,

- (*c*) in Article 9(3)(*b*) by substituting “At least 5 months prior to the commencement of” for “No later than 30 April in the year immediately preceding”,

- (*d*) in Article 9(3)(*c*) by substituting “At least 3 months prior to the commencement of a levy period” for “No later than 1 July in the year immediately preceding the levy period concerned”,

- (*e*) in Article 9(3)(*e*)(v) of the Principal Order is amended by substituting “at least 2 months before the commencement of a levy period” for “no later than 1 August in the year immediately preceding the levy period”,

- (*f*) in Article 9(3)(*f*) by substituting “in accordance with paragraph (3)(*e*),” for “in accordance with paragraph (3)(*e*).”,

- (*g*) in Article 9(3) by inserting the following subparagraph after subparagraph (*f*):

  > - “(*g*) The Commission shall notify all suppliers, the Board, the distribution system operator and the transmission system operator of any change to the commencement date of a levy period at least 6 months in advance of that date.”,

- (*h*) in Article 10(1) by substituting “No later than 7 months after the end of each levy period-“ for “Within 120 days of the end of each levy period-”, and

- (*i*) in Article 10(2) by substituting “Within 60 days of receipt of all of the information’ for “Within 60 days of receipt of the information”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 July 2007

**EAMON RYAN**

Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order amends the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Public Service Obligations) Order 2002, ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0217.html) ), so as to align the Public Service Obligation levy period with the new Single Electricity Market timetable.
