# S.I. No. 302/1982 - European Communities (Employment Equality) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 302 of 1982.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (EMPLOYMENT EQUALITY) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive 76/207/EEC of 9 February 1976,* hereby make the following Regulations:

* O.J. No. L39 of 14.2.1976, pp. 40-42

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Employment Equality) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 30th day of September, 1982.

###### 3.

3. Section 11 (2) and paragraphs (*c*) and (*d*) of [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/sec0017.html#sec17) (2) of the [Employment Equality Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1977), are hereby repealed.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of September, 1982.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the [Employment Equality Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , to remove all prohibitions on the training and employment of men as midwives and to terminate the exclusion of employment in single-sex institutions and those hitherto excluded on the grounds of privacy or decency.
