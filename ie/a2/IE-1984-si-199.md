# S.I. No. 199/1984 - Worker Participation (British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 199 of 1984.

WORKER PARTICIPATION (BRITISH AND IRISH STEAM PACKET COMPANY LIMITED) (NOMINATION DAY) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Labour in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The 20th day of November, 1984, is in relation to the election to be held in 1984 pursuant to the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), as regards the British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited, hereby fixed as the nomination day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of July, 1984.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations appoint 20th November, 1984 as the day on which the nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) in the British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited shall be received by the returning officer.
