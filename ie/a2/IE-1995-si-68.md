# S.I. No. 68/1995 - European Communities (Road Passenger Transport) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 68 of 1995.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (ROAD PASSENGER TRANSPORT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, MICHAEL LOWRY, T.D., Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purposes of giving further effect, in so far as road passenger transport operations are concerned, to Council Directive No. 74/562/EEC of 12 November, 1974^(a), Council Directive No. 77/96/EEC of 12 December, 1977^(b), Council Directive No. 89/438/EEC of 21 June, 1989^(c), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Road Passenger Transport) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995.

- (2) The European Communities (Road Passenger Transport) Regulations, 1991 and 1992, and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Road Passenger Transport) Regulations, 1991 to 1995, and shall be construed together as one.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation—

( *a* ) in the case of Regulations 3 and 13 of these Regulations, on the 24th day of March, 1995, in respect of any offence alleged to have been committed on or after that date, and

( *b* ) in the case of any other Regulations, on or after the 24th day of March, in respect of applications for the grant of a road passenger transport operator's licence duly received on or after that date.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the European Communities (Road Passenger Transport) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 59 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0059.html) ).

^(a) O.J. No. L. 308. 19.11.1974, p. 23.

^(b) O.J. No. L. 334, 24.12.1977, p. 37.

^(c) O.J. No. L. 212, 22.7.1989, p. 101.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 4 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (5):

> - "(6) Where in proceedings for an offence under this Regulation it is shown that the mechanically propelled vehicle concerned was being used for the carriage of nine or more persons (other than the driver of that vehicle) who were not in the employment of the owner of that vehicle, then, notwithstanding Regulation 17 (6) of these Regulations, those persons shall, until the contrary is shown, be presumed to have been carried in the vehicle for hire or reward.".

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5 of the Principle Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (2)—

( *a* ) by the substitution in subparagraph (*a*) of "in the form set out in the Second Schedule to these Regulations" for "in the form set out in Part I, II or III, as appropriate, of the Second Schedule to these Regulations", and

( *b* ) by the deletion of subparagraph (*c*).

###### 5.

5. Regulation 6 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (3)—

( *a* ) by the substitution of the following clauses for clauses (i) and (ii) of subparagraph (*a*):

- "(i) a relevant offence tried on indictment which is of such a nature that the Minister is of the opinion that the undertaking concerned does not satisfy, or does not continue to satisfy, as appropriate, the good repute requirement, or

- (ii) the same relevant offence more than once or a number of relevant offences, which are of such a nature that the Minister is of the opinion that the undertaking concerned does not satisfy, or does not continue to satisfy, as appropriate, the good repute requirement,",

  and

( *b* ) by the substitution of the following clauses for clause (iv) of subparagraph (*b*):

- "(iv) where the undertaking concerned is a partnership, the partners, and

- (v) where the provisions of Regulation 8 (1) (*e*) of these Regulations apply, the transport manager or transport managers concerned.".

###### 6.

6. Regulation 7 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (3) by the substitution of "paragraph 4 of the affidavit" for "paragraph 2 of the affidavit".

###### 7.

7. Regulation 8 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (4)—

( *a* ) by the substitution of "the provisions of paragraph (2) (*d*) of Regulation 5 of these Regulations" for "the provisions of paragraph (2) (other than the provisions of subparagraphs (*a*) and (*b*)) of Regulation 5 of these Regulations", and

( *b* ) by the substitution of "an affidavit in the form set out in the Fifth Schedule to these Regulations" for "affidavits in the form set out in the Fourth and Fifth Schedules".

###### 8.

8. Regulation 12 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (3) (*a*) by the substitution of "paragraph 4 of that affidavit" for "paragraph 2 of that affidavit".

###### 9.

9. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the insertion of the following Regulation after Regulation 12:

"*Change in vehicle fleet*

12A. (1) Where a change occurs in the vehicle fleet operated under a road passenger transport operator's licence, by way of increase or decrease in the number of vehicles or by way of substitution in the vehicle fleet, the holder shall notify the Minister of such occurrence within one month of it taking place.

- (2) A person who contravenes paragraph (1) of this regulation shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £1,000.".

###### 10.

10. Regulation 14 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended—

( *a* ) in paragraph (3) by the deletion of "subject to written application and appointment and" and

( *b* ) in paragraph (4) by the deletion of "written application and".

###### 11.

11. Regulation 16 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (2) (*a*) by the substitution of "inspection of the register, a fee of £5 per hour or part thereof and," for "inspection of the register, a fee of £3 per hour or part thereof, and".

###### 12.

12. Regulation 17 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended—

( *a* ) in paragraph (2) by the deletion of ", unless within ten days after the date on which the production was demanded he produces such document in person to a member of the Garda Síochána at a Garda Síochána Station to be named by such person at the time the production is demanded,",

( *b* ) in paragraph (3)—

- (i) by the deletion of "or where a person produces such document at a Garda Síochána Station in accordance with paragraph (2) of this Regulation but refuses to allow the member of the Garda Síochána to whom the document is then produced to read it", and

- (ii) by the substitution of "the member or transport officer may demand of such person" for "the member may demand of such person",

( *c* ) in paragraph (4) by the insertion of "or transport officer" after "as having permitted such member",

and

( *d* ) by the deletion of paragraph (5).

###### 13.

13. The First Schedule to the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (*p*):

> - "(*pp*) any offence under [section 215](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0215.html#sec215) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993);".

###### 14.

14. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the provisions set out in Schedule A to these Regulations for the Second Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 15.

15. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the provisions set out in Schedule B to these Regulations for the Third Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 16.

16. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the revocation of the Fourth Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 17.

17. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the provisions set out in Schedule C to these Regulations for the Fifth Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Instrument is to amend the format of various affidavits which must be submitted with an application for a Road Passenger Transport Operator's Licence. The Instrument also amends the existing Regulations with regard to good repute and enforcement.
