**S.I. No. 253 of 2007**

**Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2007.**

I, John Browne, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4 of the Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959 (No. 14 of 1959) section 25 (inserted by section 3(b) of the Fisheries (Amendment) Act 2000 (No. 34 of 2000)) of the Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1999 (No. 35 of 1999) and section 2 of the Fisheries (Amendment) Act 2000 (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 (S.I. No. 307 of 2002)) and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2006 (S.I. No. 82 of 2006), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2007.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Act of 1999” means the Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1999 (No. 35 of 1999);

“Act of 2000” means the Fisheries (Amendment) Act 2000 (No. 34 of 2000);

“Regulations of 2006” means the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2006 (S.I. No.330 of 2006).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that section 25 (inserted by section 3(b) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 applies to offences under section 24(6) (inserted by section 3(a)(i) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 in respect of a contravention of -

- (a) Regulation 3, 10 (2), 10 (3), 10(4), 10(5), 10(6), 10(7), 11(2), 14, 15, 16 or 20 of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations (No. 2) 2006 (S.I. No. 672 of 2006), or

- (b) Regulation 4, 8 (1), 8 (2), 8 (3), 8 (4), or 9 of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (Brown Tags) Regulations.2007 (S.I. No. 209 of 2007).

###### 4.

4. The form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations is prescribed for the purposes of section 25(2) (inserted by section 3(b) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 and section 2(1) of the Act 2000.

###### 5.

5. The amount standing specified in section 25 (2) (b) (inserted by section 3 (b) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 and section 2 (1) (ii) of the Act of 2000 (varied by Regulation 5 of the Regulations of 2006) is varied, in lieu of that amount, to €130.

###### 6.

6. The Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2006 (S.I. No. 330 of 2006) is revoked.
