# S.I. No. 703/2005 - District Court (Taxes Consolidation Act 1997) (Amendment) Rules 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**SI 703 of 2005**

**District Court (Taxes Consolidation Act 1997) (Amendment) Rules 2005**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Taxes Consolidation Act 1997)(Amendment) Rules 2005.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 7^th day of December 2005 and shall be read together with all other District Court Rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [SI No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be amended by the substitution in sub-rule (7) of rule 2 of Order 38 for the words “has been or is or was about to be committed” of the words “has been or is about to be committed”.

###### 4.

4. The Forms numbered 38.12 and 38.13 in Schedule 1 hereto shall be substituted for the Forms of the same number in Schedule B to the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

**TAXES CONSOLIDATION ACT, 1997**

**section 908A as amended**

**INFORMATION IN SUPPORT OF APPLICATION FOR ORDER TO INSPECT AND TAKE COPIES OF ENTRIES IN BOOKS OR RECORDS**

THE INFORMATION of ................................................

of ..........................................................................

Who says on oath-

I am an authorised officer of the Revenue Commissioners. I make this application with the consent in writing of a Revenue Commissioner.

I believe that there are reasonable grounds for suspecting that an offence (falling within the provisions of the Acts as defined by section 908A of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) as amended by [section 132](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec132) of the [Finance Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ) which would result (or but for its detection would have resulted) in serious prejudice to the proper assessment or collection of tax, is being, has been, or is about to be committed [^star1] (in the said district) (having regard to the amount of a liability in relation to any person which might be, or might have been, evaded but for the detection of the relevant facts) and that there is material in the possession of.............................................a financial institution at............................. [^star1] (in the said district) which is likely to be of substantial value (whether by itself or together with other material) to the investigation of the relevant facts.

The basis for my so believing is as follows:-

And I hereby apply for an order under the provisions of section 908A of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , as inserted by [section 207](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec207) of the [Finance Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) and as amended by [section 68](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec68) of the [Finance Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , by [section 132](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec132) of the [Finance Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) and by [section 88](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec88) of the [Finance Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) that I, the applicant, be authorised to inspect and take copies of any entries in the books, records or other documents of the said .....................a financial institution, and any documentation associated with or relating to an entry in such books, records or other documents at...................................[^star1] (within said district) for the purposes of investigation of the relevant facts.

Signed............................................

Informant
