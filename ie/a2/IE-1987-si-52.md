S.I. No. 52 of 1987.

HARBOUR RATES (ARKLOW HARBOUR) ORDER, 1987.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), and the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Arklow Harbour) Order, 1987.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 16th day of March, 1987.

###### 2.

2. (1) The rates set out in the Schedules to this Order are hereby fixed as the maximum rates to be charged by the Arklow Harbour Commissioners in respect of Arklow Harbour.

- (2) Where rates fixed under this Order are payable at a metric tonne charge, lesser weights shall be chargeable as follows:

    - (*a*) any weight not exceeding 250 kilogrammes shall count as 250 kilogrammes,

    - (*b*) any weight exceeding 250 kilogrammes but not exceeding 500 kilogrammes shall count as 500 kilogrammes,

    - (*c*) any weight exceeding 500 kilogrammes but not exceeding 750 kilogrammes shall count as 750 kilogrammes,

    - (*d*) any weight exceeding 750 kilogrammes but not exceeding 1 tonne shall count as 1 tonne.

###### 3.

3. The Harbour Rates (Arklow Harbour) Order, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0184.html) ) is hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order authorises the Arklow Harbour Commissioners to charge revised goods, tonnage and other charges at Arklow Harbour.
