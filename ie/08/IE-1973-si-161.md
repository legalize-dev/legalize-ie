S.I. No. 161 of 1973.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (MAINTENANCE) REGULATIONS, 1973.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and subsection (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1973.

###### 3.

3. The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 169 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0169.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations—

"the Act" means the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947);

"outgoings" means the rent, ground rent, rates and purchase annuity payable in respect of premises and mortgage repayments relating to the purchase of premises;

"receiving in-patient services" means receiving in-patient services made available under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) ( [S.I. No. 1 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0001.html) ):

"person" means a person to whom section 44 of the Act applies by virtue of subsection (1) of that section, and

"the payment" means the payment to be made under subsection (3) of section 44 of the Act to or in respect of a person or his dependants.

###### 5.

5. (1) The payment shall be a weekly payment not exceeding—

- (*a*) the sum specified in Part One of the Schedule hereto which is appropriate to the person, and

- (*b*) such of the increases specified in Part Two of the said Schedule as are appropriate.

- (2) Where a married woman whose husband is unable to make proper provision for the care of their family is undergoing treatment for an infectious disease to the satisfaction of a medical officer of the health board in whose functional area she resides and a helper is employed with the approval of the health board to take care of such family, the payment shall not exceed two pounds and seventy-five new pence per week.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to revoke the existing Regulations governing the payment of maintenance allowances to persons being treated for certain infectious diseases—the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 169 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0169.html) )—and to re-make them with amendments providing, from 1st July, 1973, for increases in the maximum allowances payable.

The infectious diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are tuberculosis, acute anterior poliomyelitis, typhoid, diphtheria, paratyphoid A, paratyphoid B, typhus fever, dysentery, salmonella infection, scarlet fever and streptococcal sore throat.
