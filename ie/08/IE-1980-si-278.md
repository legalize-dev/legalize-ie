S.I. No. 278 of 1980.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF LEITRIM) REGULATIONS, 1980.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Leitrim) Regulations, 1980.

2 Commencement

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 15th day of September, 1980.

3 Interpretation

###### 3.

**3.** In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires "the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"road" means a public road;

"special speed limit" and "built-up area speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road.

4 Built-up area speed limit

###### 4.

**4.** Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

5 Speed Special limit

###### 5.

**5.** Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Second Schedule to these Regulations.

6 Exceptions

###### 6.

**6.** There shall be excepted from the special speed limit prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:—

( *a* ) ambulances,

( *b* ) fire brigade vehicles, and

( *c* ) vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochána in the performance of their duties as such members.

7 Revocations

###### 7.

**7.** The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Leitrim) Regulations, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0009.html) ) and the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Leitrim) (Amendment) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 15 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0015.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Leitrim) Regulations, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0009.html) ) and the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Leitrim) (Amendment) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 15 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0015.html) ) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in this area.

The Regulations declare the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule to be subject to the built-up area speed limit of 30 mph prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

The Regulations also prescribe a speed limit of 40 miles per hour in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Second Schedule for all mechanically propelled vehicles.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and Garda Siochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are excepted from all speed limits by the Regulations.
