# S.I. No. 402/2002 - Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, on the request of the Minister for Transport and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment (No. 2) Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions Order 2002

###### 2.

2. The powers and duties of the Minister for Transport conferred on him —

a. by or under the provisions of the enactments specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1,

b. by or under the enactments specified in part 2 of Schedule 1, and

c. under the Regulations specified in Schedule 2,

to this Order are delegated to James McDaid, Minister of State at the Department of Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order delegates to Jim McDaid, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, the powers and duties of the Minister for Transport as conferred on the Minister by or under the enactments specified in the First Schedule to this Order and the Statutory Instruments and provisions of Statutory Instruments specified in the Second Schedule to this Order.
