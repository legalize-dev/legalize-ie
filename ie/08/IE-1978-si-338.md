# S.I. No. 338/1978 - Health (Hospital Bodies) Regulations, 1972, (Amendment) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 338 of 1978.

HEALTH (HOSPITAL BODIES) REGULATIONS, 1972, (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1978.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 ), after compliance with the provisions of subsection (1) (*a*) of that section, hereby makes the following Regulations as respects which, pursuant to the said section 41, a draft has been approved by a resolution passed by each House of the Oireachtas:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Hospital Bodies) Regulations, 1972 (Amendment) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 11th day of December, 1978.

###### 3.

3. The Health (Hospital Bodies) Regulations, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0164.html) ) are hereby amended as follows:—

( *a* ) for sub-article (2) of article 4 there shall be substituted the following sub-article—

> - "(2) There shall be twenty-seven members of Comhairle na nOspideal, including not less than fourteen persons who are registered medical practitioners engaged in a consultant capacity in the provision of hospital services ".,

and

( *b* ) in sub-article (3) of article 4 the words "During the period ending on 31st December, 1975" shall be deleted.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this

11th day ofDecember, 1978.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to increase the membership of Comhairle na nOspideal to 27 persons, all of whom shall be selected for appointment by the Minister for Health.
