# S.I. No. 259/1976 - Social Welfare (Variation of Rates of Payments) Regulations, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 259 of 1976.

SOCIAL WELFARE (VARIATION OF RATES OF PAYMENTS) REGULATIONS, 1976.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1952) and [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0001/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1975), hereby makes the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Variation of Rates of Payments) Regulations, 1976.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1935" means the [Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1935);

"the Act of 1952" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) ;

"the Act of 1966" means the [Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1966);

"the Act of 1969" means the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1969);

"the Act of 1970" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1970);

"the Act of 1973" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1973);

"the Act of 1974" means the [Social Welfare (No. 2) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1974);

"the Act of 1976" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1976);

"old age (non-contributory) pension" means a pension payable under the Old Age Pensions Acts, 1908 to 1976;

"widow's (non-contributory) pension" or "orphan's (non-contributory) pension" means, as the context requires, a pension payable under the Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Acts, 1935 to 1976;

"occupational injuries benefits" means benefits payable under the Act of 1966.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

[Section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0001/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) enables the Minister for Social Welfare, by regulations made with the consent of the Minister for Finance, to vary (but not so as to reduce) all or any of the rates of benefit, allowance, or pension payable under the Old Age Pensions Acts, 1908 to 1976, the Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Acts, 1935 to 1976, and the Social Welfare Acts, 1952 to 1976.

These Regulations provide for increases in the existing statutory rates of old age contributory and non-contributory pensions, widows' and orphans' contributory and non-contributory pensions, deserted wife's benefit, invalidity and retirement pensions, occupational injuries death benefit pensions and social assistance allowances for deserted wives, unmarried mothers, prisoners' wives and elderly single women, with effect from 30th September, 1976 or 1st October, 1976 as appropriate.
