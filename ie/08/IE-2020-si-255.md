# S.I. No. 255/2020 - Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Prescription of Bodies and Persons) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th July,* 2020.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/sec0018.html#sec18) (4) (as amended by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (1) of the [Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2003)) of the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Prescription of Bodies and Persons) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 August 2020.

###### 2.

2. The [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (Prescription of Bodies and Persons) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0395.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the following for paragraph (c) of Regulation 2:

> - “(*c*) Caroline Gill;”

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

20 July, 2020.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for the nomination of a person for appointment as director of The Investor Compensation Company DAC.
