> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

1. ###### 3.

**3. Prescribed period(s) of employment under section 28(2)**

For the purpose of section 28(2), a prescribed period of employment is a period of not less than 12 months, which should be consecutive, of which at least 3 months must be spent in Medicine in general and 3 months in Surgery in general. As part of this 12 month period, a person may also be employed for not less than 2 months and not more than 3 months in the following specialties:

Emergency Medicine

Obstetrics and Gynaecology

Paediatrics

Psychiatry
