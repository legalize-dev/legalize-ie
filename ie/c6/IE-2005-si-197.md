**Wireless Telegraphy Act 1926 (section 3) (Exemption of Receive Only Apparatus for Wireless Telegraphy) Order 2005**

The Commission for Communications Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by subsection 6 (inserted by section 11(c) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) 5 of 1972)) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) 45 of 1926), transferred to the Commission for Communications Regulation by section 9(1) of the [Communications Regulation Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) 20 of 2002) hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (section 3 (Exemption of Receive Only Apparatus for Wireless Telegraphy) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. In this Order-

“Act of 1926” means the [Wireless Telegraphy Act 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) 45 of 1926);

“Act of 1988” means the [Broadcasting and Wireless Telegraphy Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) 19 of 1988);

“apparatus for wireless telegraphy” has the meaning assigned to it by the Act of 1988;

“harmful interference” means interference which endangers the functioning of a radio navigation service or of other safety services or which otherwise seriously degrades obstructs or repeatedly interrupts a radio communications service operating in accordance with the applicable European Community or national regulations;

“Regulations” means the European Communities (Radio Equipment and Telecommunications Terminal Equipment) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 240 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0240.html) ).

“transmission” means any intentional emission by wireless telegraphy;

“wireless telegraphy” has the meaning assigned to it by the Act of 1988;

###### 3.

3. Subject to the conditions contained in Articles 4 and 5 of this Order, the following classes of apparatus for wireless telegraphy are hereby declared to be classes of apparatus for wireless telegraphy to which section 3 of the Act of 1926 does not apply: apparatus for wireless telegraphy only capable of reception, which is not licensed under the Television Licences Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 165 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0165.html) ) and is inherently incapable of transmission.

###### 4.

4. The classes of apparatus for wireless telegraphy described in Article 3 of this Order, shall fulfil all requirements of the Regulations and shall not cause any harmful interference.

###### 5.

5. Apparatus for wireless telegraphy of the class described in Article 3 of this Order shall not be used to improperly divulge the purport of any message communication, or signal sent or proposed to be sent by wireless telegraphy.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This Order provides for the exemption of certain wireless telegraphy apparatus from the requirement to be licensed under the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) .
