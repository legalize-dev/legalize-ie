# S.I. No. 499/2016 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th September*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) are amended-

- (*a*) in Regulation 2—

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “levy period”:

      > “ ‘levy period’ means the period commencing on 1 October 2016 and ending on 30 September 2017.”, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (3), by the substitution of “30 June 2016” for “30 June 2015”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3(1), by the substitution of “1 October 2016” for “1 October 2015”, and

- (*c*) in Regulation 4(1), by the substitution of “28 February 2017” for “29 February 2016”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 September 2016.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
