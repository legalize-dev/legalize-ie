###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 June 2015.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe the forms to be used for the purposes of certain provisions of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) .
