# S.I. No. 246/1996 - Consumer Credit Act, 1995 (Section 60) (No. 2) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 246 of 1996.

CONSUMER CREDIT ACT, 1995 (SECTION 60) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 60) (No. 2) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. Paragraph 2 of the Fifth Schedule to the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), is hereby amended -

( *a* ) by the insertion in subparagraph (*a*)(ii) after "the total amount" of "which he has paid", and

( *b* ) by the substitution for subparagraph (*b*) of the following subparagraph

> - "(b) purchase the goods by paying the difference between the amount already paid under this agreement and either the hire-purchase price reduced in arrordance with [section 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0052.html#sec52) or [53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , or such lesser amount if specified in this agreement.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 8th day of August, 1996.

**RICHARD BRUTON**

Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the notice to be included in hire purchase agreements set out in the Fifth Schedule to the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
