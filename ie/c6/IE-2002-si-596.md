# S.I. No. 596/2002 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby make the following regulations with respect to which, pursuant to section 2(5) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies Regulations 2002.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the 1^st day of January, 2003.

###### 2.

2. The prescribed amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979) shall be -

- (a) as regards milk, one cent per gallon, and

- (b) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, €5.08 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 224 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0224.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

**These Regulations which come into effect on the 1^st of January 2003 prescribe new rates of disease levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.**
