# S.I. No. 129/1997 - Jury Districts Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 129 of 1997.

JURY DISTRICTS ORDER, 1997

I, NORA OWEN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976) hereby Order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 25th day of March, 1997.

###### 3.

3. Part II of the Schedule to the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ) is hereby amended by the deletion of the reference to the jury district. County of Leitrim (Jury District No. I (Carrickon-Shannon) and Jury District No. 2 (Manorhamilton)).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of March, 1997.

**NORA OWEN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Statutory Instrument which comes into operation on 25 March, 1997 amends the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ). For the purposes of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) the County of Leitrim is now one Jury district.
