# S.I. No. 474/2009 - Dublin Transport Authority Act 2008 (Dissolution of Dublin Transportation office) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th December*, 2009.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, at the request of the National Transport Authority and in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 102](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec102) (1) of the [Dublin Transport Authority Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2008), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin Transport Authority Act 2008 (Dissolution of Dublin Transportation Office) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed as the day for the dissolution of the Dublin Transportation Office is 1 December 2009.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 December 2009.

**NOEL DEMPSEY,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order appoints 1 December 2009 as the day for the dissolution of the Dublin Transportation Office.
