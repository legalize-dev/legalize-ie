# S.I. No. 158/1974 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 35, Fiftieth Period) (Sparking Plugs) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 158 of 1974.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 35, FIFTIETH PERIOD) (SPARKING PLUGS) ORDER, 1974.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 35, Fiftieth Period) (Sparking Plugs) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints the quantity of Sparking Plugs which may be imported during the period 1st July, 1974 to 30th June, 1975 at 1,250,000 articles.
