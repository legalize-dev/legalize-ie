# S.I. No. 385/2005 - Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Bodies and Individuals) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 385 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0385.html)**

**Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Bodies and Individuals) (Amendment) Regulations 2005**

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 18(4) of the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998) (as amended by the [Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2003)), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Bodies and Individuals) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. The [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Bodies and Individuals) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 570 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0570.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the following for paragraphs (c) and (d) of Regulation 2:

> - “(c) Ms Gina Quin, Dublin Chamber of Commerce, 7 Clare Street, Dublin 2;
>
> - (d) Mr Mark Redmond, Irish Taxation Institute, 19 Sandymount Avenue Dublin 4;”,

and the said Regulation 2, as so amended, is set out in the Table to this Regulation.

TABLE

2. For the purposes of section 18(4) of the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998 (as amended by the [Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2003)), the following bodies and individuals are prescribed as bodies and individuals that appear to the Minister to represent the interests of clients of investment firms:

- (a) The Consumers Association of Ireland;

- (b) The Office of the Director of Consumer Affairs;

- (c) Ms Gina Quin, Dublin Chamber of Commerce, 7 Clare Street, Dublin 2;

- (d) Mr Mark Redmond, Irish Taxation Institute, 19 Sandymount Avenue Dublin 4;

- (e) Ms Inge Clissmann, P.O. Box 4460, Law Library Building 158/9 Church Street, Dublin 7.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for the nomination of two persons for appointment as directors of the Investor Compensation Company Ltd.
