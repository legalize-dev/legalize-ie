# S.I. No. 498/2017 - Forestry (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st November*, 2017.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec6) , [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec10) , [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec17) , [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec22) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec30) of the [Forestry Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2014) and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive No 2011/92/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 December 2011[^1] as amended by Directive 2014/52/EU of the Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014[^2] , insofar as it applies to development consent in respect of certain proposed forestry related activities, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Forestry (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Forestry Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0191.html) ) are amended by substituting Regulation 13(3) for the following:

- (3) A person shall—

  > - “(*a*) in respect of development referred to in paragraphs (2)(*a*) or (2)(*b*), submit-
  >
  >     - (i) an EIS with his or her application, and
  >
  >     - (ii) details of registration of the application requiring environmental impact assessment with the Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, or
  >
  > - (*b*) in respect of development referred to in paragraph (2)(*c*) or (2)(*d*), on request from the Minister, submit-
  >
  >     - (i) an EIS to accompany his or her application, and
  >
  >     - (ii) details of registration of the application requiring environmental impact assessment with the Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.”.

Given under my Official Seal,

9 November 2017.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.
