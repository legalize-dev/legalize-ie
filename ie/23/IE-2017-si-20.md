# S.I. No. 20/2017 - Rent Pressure Zone (Local Electoral Area of Naas) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st January*, 2017.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (5) of section 24A (inserted by the [Planning and Development (Housing) and Residential Tenancies Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) (No. 17 of 2016)) of the [Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2004), and consequent on having duly received from the Director of the Residential Tenancies Board, pursuant to that subsection, a rent zone report confirming that the criteria in subsection (4) of that section are satisfied in respect of the area to which the following Order relates, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Rent Pressure Zone (Local Electoral Area of Naas) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 27 January 2017.

###### 3.

3. The area consisting of the Local Electoral Area of Naas (as provided for by the County of Kildare Local Electoral Areas and Municipal Districts Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 52 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0052.html) )) is prescribed as a rent pressure zone for a period of 3 years commencing on the date of coming into operation of this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 January 2017.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the designation of the Local Electoral Area of Naas as a rent pressure zone under section 24A of the [Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (as inserted by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec36) of the [Planning and Development (Housing) and Residential Tenancies Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) ), for a period of 3 years from the date of coming into operation of this Order.
