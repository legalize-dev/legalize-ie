# S.I. No. 116/1976 - District Court (Fees) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 116 of 1976.

DISTRICT COURT (FEES) ORDER, 1976.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the District Court (Fees) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. Article 6 of the District Court (Fees) Order, 1970 ( [S.I. No. 236 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0236.html) ), is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the addition to paragraph (1) of the following sub-paragraph:

> " ( *f* ) a person authorised by a local authority to engage in the relevant prosecution or proceedings.",

and

( *b* ) the addition to paragraph (2) of the following sub-paragraph:

> " ( *e* ) in a prosecution or proceedings instituted by a local authority under the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1975).".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of May, 1976.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 28th day of May, 1976.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the exemption of local authorities from the payment of fees in respect of summonses issued by them in pursuance of the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
