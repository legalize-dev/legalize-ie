# S.I. No. 469/2016 - Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction (Mussel Seed) (Opening of Fisheries) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th September*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec15) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction (Mussel Seed) (Opening of Fisheries) Regulations 2016.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 7 September 2016 and shall cease to have effect on 11 December 2016.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “mussel seed” means mussels (mytilus edulis) whether alive or dead and includes any part of a mussel and the shell, or any part of the shell, of a mussel and which are not intended for direct human consumption.

###### 3.

3. (1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person may fish for mussel seed in the exclusive fishery limits of the State where that person holds an authorisation to fish for mussel seed granted under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec13) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) or a licence granted by the relevant competent authority of Northern Ireland.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to—

    - (*a*) areas subject to Fisheries Natura Declarations made under Regulation 9 of European Union (Birds and Natural Habitats) (Sea-fisheries) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0290.html) ) referring to mussel seed other than areas subject to an aquaculture licence issued under the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997), and

    - (*b*) the Moville Area (within the meaning of the [Foyle Fisheries Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1952)) and the Louth Area (within the meaning of [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec31) of the [British-Irish Agreement Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 1999)) or any part thereof, if, and to the extent that mussel seed fishing is subject to Regulations under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0005/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Foyle Fisheries Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 September 2016.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations are to provide for the opening of the mussel seed fishery from 7 September 2016.
