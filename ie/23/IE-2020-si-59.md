# S.I. No. 59/2020 - European Communities (Marketing of Vegetable Propagating and Plant Material, other than Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd March,* 2020.

I, Michael Creed, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2019/990 of 17 June 2019[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Marketing of Vegetable Propagating and Plant Material, other than Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Vegetable Seeds) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 152 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0152.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “Directive”, the following –

> “‘Directive’ means Council Directive No. 2008/72/EC of 15^th July 2008[^2] as amended by Commission Implementing Decision 2013/166/EU of 2 April 2013[^3] , Commission Implementing Directive 2013/45/EU of 7^th August 2013[^4] and Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2019/990 of 17 June 2019.”;

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 February, 2020.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations give effect to the provisions of Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2019/990 as they apply to Council Directive 2008/72/EC on the marketing of vegetable propagating and planting material, other than seed by updating the listing of scientific name of the species contained in Annex I and Annex II.
