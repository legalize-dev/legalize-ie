**Fisheries (Commercial Fishing Licences) (Alteration of Duties and Fees) Order 2006.**

I, John Browne, Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 68(4)(b) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1976) (“Act of 1976”)), 159(5)(a) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1994)), 160(5)(a) (as so inserted) and 279(2) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) of the Act of 1976) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )) and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2006 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0082.html) ), hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to sections 68(7) and 279(4) (inserted by section 3 of the Act of 1976) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Fisheries (Commercial Fishing Licences) (Alteration of Duties and Fees) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. This Order has effect as respects licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after 1 January 2007.

###### 3.

3. In this Order -

“Act of 1994” means Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994 (No. 23 of 1994);

“Principal Act” means Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959 (No. 14 of 1959);

“Order of 2005” means Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 793 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0793.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The amount of the ordinary licence duty in respect of an ordinary fishing licence authorising the use of a fishing engine of a kind specified in column (2) of the Schedule at any reference number is altered to the amount specified in column (3) of the Schedule at that reference number.

###### 5.

5. The licence duty in respect of an oyster fishing licence is altered to €94.

###### 6.

6. The amount standing specified in section 159(1)(b)(ii) (inserted by section 20 of the Act of 1994) of the Principal Act (altered by Regulation 5 of the Order of 2005) is altered, in lieu of that amount, to €122.

###### 7.

7. The amount standing specified in section 160(1)(b) (inserted by section 20 of the Act of 1994) of the Principal Act (altered by Regulation 6 of the Order of 2005) is altered, in lieu of that amount, to €122.

###### 8.

8. The Order of 2005 is revoked with effect from 1 January 2007.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order prescribes the licence fees to be payable from 1 January 2007 in respect of salmon commercial fishing licences including a salmon conservation levy equivalent to 50% of the licence fee. The proceeds of this levy will be invested in wild salmon management initiatives designed to rehabilitate wild salmon stocks and habitats. The Order also prescribes the licence fees to be payable in respect of eel and oyster commercial fishing licences and salmon, eel and molluscan shellfish dealers' licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after 1 January 2007.
