# S.I. No. 330/2014 - Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th July*, 2014.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 95 (as amended by [section 78](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec78) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2012 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 181 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0181.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) by inserting after Regulation 19 the following:

  > *“Car Club Vehicle Parking Bay*
  >
  > 19A. Traffic sign number RRM 034 shall—
  >
  > - (*a*) indicate a car club vehicle parking bay, and
  >
  > - (*b*) consist of the words ‘Car Club Only’, written on the roadway in white in letters at least 500mm in height.
  >
  >   *Electrically Powered Vehicle Recharging Bay*
  >
  >   19B.Traffic sign number RRM 035 shall—
  >
  > - (*a*) indicate an electrically powered vehicle recharging bay, and
  >
  > - (*b*) consist of the words ‘Electric Vehicle Charging Only’, written on the roadway in white in letters at least 500mm in height.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 July 2014.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for signage for parking spaces reserved for (*a*) car club vehicles and (*b*) electric vehicles which are being recharged.
