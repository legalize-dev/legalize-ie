# S.I. No. 55/2016 - Electoral Act 1992 (Special Difficulty) (Assent to Nomination of Candidate at Dáil Election) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th February*, 2016.

I, ALAN KELLY, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 164](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0164.html#sec164) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1992) (as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) )) where it appears to me there is a special difficulty with regard to the assents required by section 46(5)(*a*) (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec1) of the [Electoral Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2007)) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) to the nomination of certain candidates at the election to be held as a consequence of the Proclamation issued on 3 February 2016 dissolving Dáil Éireann because the register of Dáil electors under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) which came into force on 15 February 2015 ceases to be in force on 15 February 2016 and a new register of Dáil electors comes into force on that day, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electoral Act 1992 (Special Difficulty) (Assent to Nomination of Candidate at Dáil Election) Order 2016.

###### 2.

2. At the Dáil election to be held as a consequence of the Proclamation issued on 3 February 2016 dissolving Dáil Éireann section 46(6)(*e*) (inserted by the [Electoral Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2007)) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1992) is modified so that it does not apply to assents under subsection (5)(*a*) of that section in respect of candidates at that Dáil election.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 February 2016.

**ALAN KELLY,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order dis-applies [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0046.html#sec46) (6)(*e*) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) for the February 2016 general election, for the avoidance of any doubt about assents to candidate nominations given in accordance with section 46(6)(*a*), in view of the fact that the current register of electors ceases to be in force on 15 February 2016, after the latest time for receipt of nominations.
