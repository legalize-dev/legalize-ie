# S.I. No. 606/2006 - National Economic and Social Development Office Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No 606 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0606.html)

**National Economic and Social Development Office Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006**

I, Bertie Ahern, Taoiseach, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec3) of the [National Economic and Social Development Office Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2006), hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to section 4 of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Economic and Social Development Office Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (Establishment Day) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Economic and Social Development Office Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2006) is 1 January 2007.
