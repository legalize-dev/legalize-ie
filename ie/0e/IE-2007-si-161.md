# S.I. No. 161/2007 - Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998 (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 161 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0161.html)

AIR NAVIGATION AND TRANSPORT (AMENDMENT) ACT 1998 (AGGREGATE BORROWINGS LIMIT VARIATION) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th April*, 2007.

I, MARTIN CULLEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 13(6) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998) as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) ), with the consent of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with the Dublin Airport Authority, hereby order as follows—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The amount specified in paragraph (*a*) of section 13(5) (inserted by paragraph 6 of the Schedule to the [State Airports Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2004)) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998) is varied by substituting “€1,800,000,000” for “€700 million”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 April 2007

**MARTIN CULLEN**

Minister for Transport.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance.

20 April 2007

**BRIAN COWEN**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order provides for an increase, from €700 million to €1,800 million of the aggregate amount of moneys borrowed at any one time by the Dublin Airport Authority as specified in paragraph (*a*) of section 13(5) (inserted by paragraph 6 of the Schedule to the [State Airports Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2004)) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998).
