# S.I. No. 243/2015 - Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th June*, 2015.

I, PAUDIE COFFEY, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 6 and 18 of the [Building Control Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990) and the Environment, Community and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 524 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0524.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the Building Control Regulations 1997 to 2015 and shall be construed together as one.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 July 2015.

*Interpretation generally*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations:

“Principal Regulations” means the Building Control Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 496 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0496.html) ) as amended by the Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 10 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0010.html) ), the Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 85 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0085.html) ), the Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0351.html) ), the Building Control (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0009.html) ) and the Building Control (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 105 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0105.html) ).

*Amendment of the Fifth Schedule to the Principal Regulations*

###### 4.

4. The Fifth Schedule to the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) By substituting “€800 per building, or €500 per building where the application is made prior to commencement and coincides with an application for a fire safety certificate, where relevant” for “€800 per building” where it occurs in column 2 of Part A,

- (*b*) In clause 2(*e*) of Part B by substituting “€500” for “€800”.

GIVEN under my hand,

10 June 2015.

PAUDIE COFFEY,

Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The above Regulations provide for a revision in certain cases of the fee specified in Part A of the Fifth Schedule to the Principal Regulations in respect of an application for a Disability Access Certificate or a Revised Disability Access Certificate.

While the standard fee for an application for a Disability Access Certificate or a Revised Disability Access Certificate shall continue to be €800, a reduced fee of €500 shall apply where the application is made prior to the commencement of works and coincides with an application for a fire safety certificate, where relevant.

Clause 2(*e*) of Part B of the Fifth Schedule to the Principal Regulations has also been revised to clarify that where a building control authority makes a refund in respect of an application for a Disability Access Certificate or a Revised Disability Access Certificate the refund shall not reduce the balance of the fee paid in respect of such application to less than €500.
