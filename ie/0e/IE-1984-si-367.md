# S.I. No. 367/1984 - European Communities (Motor Vehicles (Registration of Importers) Act, 1968 (Repeal)) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 367 of 1984.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (MOTOR VEHICLES (REGISTRATION OF IMPORTERS) ACT, 1968 (REPEAL)) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Industry, Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities ( [Motor Vehicles (Registration of Importers) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (Repeal)) Regulations, 1984.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The [Motor Vehicles (Registration of Importers) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1968), is hereby repealed.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 20th day of December, 1984.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Industry, Trade, Commerce and Tourism.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations repeal the [Motor Vehicles (Registration of Importers) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) .
