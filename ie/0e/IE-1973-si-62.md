S.I. No. 62 of 1973.

DUBLIN TRAFFIC TEMPORARY RULES, 1973.

I, PATRICK MALONE, Commissioner of the Gárda Síochána, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 89](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0089.html#sec89) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968)), and with the consent of the Minister for Local Government, hereby make the following temporary rules in respect of the area comprising the county borough of Dublin, the borough of Dun Laoghaire, the county of Dublin and the town of Balbriggan.

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Dublin Traffic Temporary Rules, 1973.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 20th day of March, 1973.

###### 3.

3. These rules shall apply to the area comprising the county borough of Dublin, the borough of Dun Laoghaire, the county of Dublin and the town of Balbriggan.

###### 4.

4. A driver shall comply with a prohibition, restriction or requirement indicated by means of a traffic sign.

GIVEN under my hand this 28th day of February, 1973.

PATRICK MALONE,

Commissioner of the Gárda Síocána.

The Minister for Local Government hereby consents to the making of the foregoing temporary rules.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government, this 13th day of March, 1973.

**ROBERT MOLLOY,**

Aire Rialtais Áitiúil.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

1. Rule 4 provides that a driver shall comply with a prohibition, restriction or requirement indicated by means of a traffic sign.
