S.I. No. 291 of 1977.

GREAT NORTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY (IRELAND) PENSION FUND FOR WAGES STAFF (AMENDMENT) SCHEME (CONFIRMATION) ORDER, 1977.

I, PADRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Transport and Power, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (6) of the [Transport Act, 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), as applied by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1958), and adapted by the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), hereby, after consultation with the Minister for the Public Service, order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the scheme" means the scheme amending the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff prepared by the Board and submitted to the Minister under [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , (No. 20 of 1958), and set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The scheme is hereby confirmed and shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1977.

SCHEDULE.

GREAT NORTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY (IRELAND)

PENSION FUND FOR WAGES STAFF AMENDING SCHEME.

1. *Definitions:*

In this Amending Scheme "the existing Scheme" means the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff as amended by

( *a* ) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund (Amendment) Scheme for Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order, 1962, ( [S.I. No. 53 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0053.html) );

( *b* ) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1965, ( [S.I. No. 50 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0050.html) );

( *c* ) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1967, ( [S.I. No. 9 of 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/si/0009.html) );

( *d* ) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1969, ( [S.I. No. 60 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0060.html) );

( *e* ) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund (Amendment) Scheme for Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order, 1971, ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0081.html) ), and

( *f* ) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund (Amendment) Scheme for Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order, 1974, ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0251.html) ).

The term "operative date" means the 1st day of July 1977.

2. *Continuance of the existing Scheme:*

- (1) The benefits provided by the existing Scheme shall continue to be paid or be payable under and in accordance with the terms thereof and without alteration to every pensioner whose right to pension accrued prior to the operative date and to the personal representative of any deceased pensioner if the deceased's right to pension accrued prior to the operative date.

- (2) Nothing in this Amending Scheme shall affect the rights of any of the persons mentioned in the previous sub-article, nor shall this Amending Scheme affect any subsisting right or liability accrued to or incurred by any person under the existing Scheme prior to the operative date.

- (3) Deferred and special pensions, now payable or at any time to become payable shall not be affected by this Amending Scheme.

3. *Amendment of the existing Scheme:*

- (1) Subject to the provisions of Article 2 of this Amending Scheme, the existing Scheme shall be amended so as to conform to the provisions hereinafter contained, and every provision of the existing Scheme which is inconsistent with the provisions hereinafter contained shall cease to have effect.

- (2) The increased contributions and benefits provided for by this Amending Scheme are, where applicable, in substitution for the corresponding contributions and benefits provided for by the existing Scheme.
