# S.I. No. 100/2019 - Planning and Development Act 2000, Section 181(2)(a) Order No. 2, 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil”* 22*nd of March,* 2019.

I Paschal Donohoe, Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 181](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec181) (2)(a) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (as amended), and, being satisfied that the carrying out of the development on my behalf by the Office of Public Works as described in the Schedule to this Order is required by reason of an emergency, being the exceptional circumstances arising as a result of the impending withdrawal and/or the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union on 29 March 2019, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Planning and Development Act 2000, Section 181(2)(a) Order No. 2, 2019.

###### 2.

2. The provisions of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) , and the provisions of Part 9 of the Planning and Development Regulations, 2001 shall not apply to the development being carried out on my behalf by the Office of Public Works as described in the Schedule to this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 March, 2019.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

## **Schedule**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Site: | Interim Government Control Centre |
| Address: | FORMER ROSSLARE IMPORT CENTRE, N25, ROSSLARE HARBOUR, CO. WEXFORD, Y35 P797. |
| Description: | The development envisaged by this order is the refurbishment of existing buildings, the construction of ancillary custom, agriculture and health inspection structures, staff welfare structures, associated truck and car parking, including ancillary site works, signage and all other necessary works, all within the existing boundary of lands of the Former Rosslare Import Centre, for the provision of facilities that are required by reason of the impending withdrawal/and or withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union on 29 March 2019 in order to provide for the required infrastructure for customs, sanitary and phytosanitary and health checks and controls. |
