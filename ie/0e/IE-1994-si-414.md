# S.I. No. 414/1994 - Local Government Act, 1994 (Section 53 (1)) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 414 of 1994.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT ACT, 1994 (SECTION 53 (1)) ORDER, 1994.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0053.html#sec53) (1) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , (Section 53 (1)) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The 9th day of January, 1995 shall be the day appointed for the purposes of [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0053.html#sec53) (1) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 9th day of December, 1994.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order fixes the day on which the Local Government Reorganisation Commission is formally established under the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) .
