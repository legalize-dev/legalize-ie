# S.I. No. 60/1989 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 60 of 1989.

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT, 1966 (FIRST SCHEDULE) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1989.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0097.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1989.

- (2) The [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1986, and this Order may be cited together as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1989.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"the Act" means the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966);

"the First Schedule" means the First Schedule to the Act, as amended by the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1986.

###### 3.

3. For all the purposes of the Act, the First Schedule is hereby amended by the insertion in Part III thereof at the end of Class A of the following:

> "26. Bovine spongiform encephalopathy."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of March, 1989.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and

Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order extends the definition of "disease" in the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) to include Bovine Spongiform Encephalopathy.
