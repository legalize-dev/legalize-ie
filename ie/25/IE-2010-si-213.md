# S.I. No. 213/2010 - National Solidarity Bond and State Savings Account

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st May*, 2010.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, hereby designate the National Solidarity Bond, as offered by the National Treasury Management Agency, and the State Savings Account, as offered by the National Treasury Management Agency, to be “specified deposits” as defined in section 256 Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 for the purposes of Part 8, Chapter 4 of that Act.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 May 2010.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.
