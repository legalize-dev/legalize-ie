# S.I. No. 60/2025 - European Union (Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th March,* 2025.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2022/993 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2022[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0242.html) ), as amended by the European Union (Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 454 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0454.html) ), are amended –

- (a) in Regulation 2(1), by substituting for the definition of “Directive” the following:

  > “‘Directive’ means Directive (EU) 2022/993 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2022[^1] ;
  >
  > ‘Directive of 2008’ means Directive 2008/106/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 November 2008[^2] (as amended by Directive 2012/35/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012[^3] , and Directive (EU) 2019/1159 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^4] );”,

- (b) in Regulation 2, by substituting paragraph (3) with the following:

  > - “(3) In these Regulations a reference –
  >
  >     - (a) to an Article is a reference to an Article of the Directive of 2008, which shall be construed as an Article of the Directive, in accordance with the correlation table in Annex V to the Directive,
  >
  >     - (b) to an Annex is a reference to an Annex to the Directive of 2008, which shall be construed as an Annex to the Directive, in accordance with the correlation table in Annex V to the Directive,
  >
  >     - (c) to a Chapter is a reference to a Chapter of Annex I, and
  >
  >     - (d) to Part A is a reference to Part A of the code.”,

- (c) by deleting Regulation 31, and

- (d) in Schedule 3 –

    - (i) in the third entry, relating to Drogheda, in column (3) by substituting for:

      > “In winter:
      >
      > No partially smooth waters”

the following:

> “In winter:
>
> In favourable weather only, within a radius of 1.3 nautical miles from the eastern tip of the north breakwater and terminating at the northern and southern foreshores respectively for vessels engaged in dredging operations only”, and

- (ii) in the seventh entry, relating to Howth Sound, by substituting for the matter in column (3), the following:

  > “Within a line from the North end of Eastern Breakwater to Thulla and south of a line drawn westward from Steer Rock”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 March, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the European Union (Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0242.html) ), as amended, in relation to:

- (a) the references to Directive 2008/106/EC, as amended, which has been codified and repealed by Directive (EU) 2022/993,

- (b) the transitional provisions, which are no longer relevant, and

- (c) Schedule 3, as inserted by the European Union (Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 454 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0454.html) ), regarding the descriptions of the areas of partially smooth waters in Drogheda and Howth Sound, respectively.
