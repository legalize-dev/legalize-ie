# S.I. No. 189/1999 - Firearms (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1998, Continuance Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0032/sec0004.html#sec4) (5) of the [Firearms (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Firearms (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1998, Continuance Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The [Firearms (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1998), is hereby continued in operation for a period of 12 months from the 14th day of July, 1999.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government this 22nd day of June, 1999. |
|  | BERTIE AHERN, |
|  | Taoiseach |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the continuing in operation of the provisions of the [Firearms (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) , for a period of 12 months from 14 July, 1999.
