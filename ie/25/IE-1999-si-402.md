# S.I. No. 402/1999 - Health (Out-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) as substituted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Health (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1987) as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Out-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2000.

###### 3.

3. Article 4 of the Health (Our-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 37 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0037.html) ) (as amended by Article 3 of Health (Out-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 509 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0509.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of £25 for £20 in sub-article (2).

The Minister for Finance consents to these regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Health (Out-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1994 and 1997 by raising the charge from £20 to £25 in respect of attendance at accident and emergency or casualty departments where the person concerned has not been referred by a medical practitioner. The charge shall not apply where such an attendance results in hospital admission. The usual exemptions, which include medical card holders, and hardship provision shall continue to apply. The increase in the charge comes into effect on 1st January, 2000.
