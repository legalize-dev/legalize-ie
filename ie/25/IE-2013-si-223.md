# S.I. No. 223/2013 - Prisons Act 1970 (Section 7) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd July*, 2013.

I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick’s Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Prisons Act 1970 (Section 7) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. The period of 6 months beginning on 28 June 2013 is specified for the purposes of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 June 2013.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
