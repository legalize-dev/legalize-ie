# S.I. No. 477/1998 - Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991 (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) (Revocation) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 477 of 1998.

CHILD ABDUCTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF CUSTODY ORDERS ACT, 1991 (CONTRACTING STATES)(HAGUE CONVENTION)(REVOCATION) ORDER, 1998

I, DAVID ANDREWS, T.D., Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1991), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) (Revocation) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 238 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0238.html) ) is hereby revoked.

4. This Order shall come into operation on the 10th day of December, 1998.

GIVEN under my Official Seal at Dublin this 4 day of December, 1998.

David Andrews, T.D.,

Minister for Foreign Affairs

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to revoke, in accordance with [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 238 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0238.html) ).
