# S.I. No. 320/1975 - Imposition of Duties (No. 222) (Customs Duties and Form of Customs Tariff) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 320 of 1975.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (NO. 222) (CUSTOMS DUTIES AND FORM OF CUSTOMS TARIFF) ORDER, 1975.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 222) (Customs Duties and Form of Customs Tariff) Order, 1975.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The Imposition of Duties (No. 200) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0220.html) ), as amended, is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution of "Nil" for the rates specified in the eighth and ninth columns, respectively, of the Schedule at each tariff code number mentioned in Schedule I to this Order,

( *b* ) the substitution in the eighth and ninth columns of the Schedule at tariff code number 7320-023 of "2.6%" for "5.3%" and "2.1%" for "4.2%", respectively,

( *c* ) the substitution in the eighth and ninth columns of the Schedule at tariff code numbers 7338-230 and 7338-250 of "2.6%" for "5.1%" and "1.9%" for "3.8%" respectively,

( *d* ) the substitution in the eighth and ninth columns of the Schedule at tariff code numbers 7615-415 and 7615-455 of "4%" for "8%" and "3%" for "6%", respectively.

( *e* ) the substitution in the fourth column of the Schedule at each tariff code number mentioned in Schedule II to this Order of "(a)" for "(d)", "(e)" or "(h)", as the case may be,

and

( *f* ) the deletion in the fourth column of the Schedule at each tariff code number other than a tariff code number mentioned in Schedule II to this Order of "(d)", "(e)" and "(h)" wherever they occur.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the rates of Customs duty to be applied to certain goods of United Kingdom and Northern Ireland origin. This Order also provides for the continuation of the provision, where required, for the reduction or remission of certain duties on motor vehicles, motor vehicle accessories and parts, including tyres.
