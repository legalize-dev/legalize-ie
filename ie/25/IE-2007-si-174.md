# S.I. No. 174/2007 - Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 174 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0174.html)

CONSTITUENCY COMMISSION (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st May*, 2007.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on him by section 5(1) of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. A Constituency Commission is hereby established to perform the function assigned to it by Part II of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 April 2007

**DICK ROCHE.**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order establishes a Constituency Commission to report on Dáil and European Parliament constituencies.
