S.I. No. 32 of 1984.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (SUPERANNUATION) REGULATIONS, 1984.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980), with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service, hereby makes the following regulations:

1 Short Title.

###### 1.

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations, 1984.

2 Definitions.

###### 2.

**2.** In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1925" means the [Local Government Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1925);

"the Act of 1945" means the [Mental Treatment Act, 1945](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1945/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1945);

"the Act of 1948" means the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1948);

"the Act of 1956" means the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956).

3 Amendment of section 10 of the Act of 1956.

###### 3.

**3.** Subsection (1) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) is hereby amended:

( *a* ) by the addition to paragraph (*a*) of the words "nor, in the case of a member of the Reserve Defence Force, being a period commencing on or after the 1st day of January, 1981, in respect of which he is granted special leave without pay for attending training with that Force," and

( *b* ) by the addition to paragraph (*d*) of the words "other than in a case where he is immediately appointed to or employed in a position service in which is capable of being reckoned under this Act, the date of such appointment or employment not being earlier than the 1st day of January 1968.".

- (III) was a person to or in respect of whom any payment was made under the Defence Forces (Pensions) Scheme, 1937, as amended, or the Army Pensions Acts 1923 to 1980, unless such payment is refunded or reduced as the Minister for the Environment may consider appropriate;
