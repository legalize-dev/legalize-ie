# S.I. No. 380/1977 - Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), hereby, with the approval of the Government, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Notwithstanding the provisions of the Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) Regulations, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 64 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0064.html) ), as amended by the Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0118.html) ), the members of the Representative Bodies provided for by Regulation 3 of those Regulations, as so amended, who hold office on the commencement of these Regulations shall continue to hold office until the members of the associations established pursuant to section 13 of the Garda Síochána Act, 1924 (No. 25 of 1924) (as amended by the Garda Síochána Act, 1977 (No. 24 of 1977)), take office as such members.

###### 4.

4. The Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0187.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of December, 1977.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 15th day ofDecember, 1977.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide that the members of the Garda Síochána representative bodies shall continue to hold office until members of the associations established in accordance with the Garda Síochána Act, 1924, as amended by the Garda Síochána Act, 1977, take office.
