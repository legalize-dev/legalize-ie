# S.I. No. 427/2015 - Microchipping of Dogs (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th October*, 2015.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec36) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Microchipping of Dogs (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The Microchipping of Dogs Regulations 2015 are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 4 (2), by the deletion of “, by appointment, to a facility operated by a veterinary practitioner,”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 8(1) (*b*) by the deletion of “with a copy of the accepted form of identification and utility bill referred to in that paragraph”,

- (*c*) by the substitution for Regulation 10 (6) of—

  > - “(6) Paragraph (4) does not apply to–
  >
  >     - (*a*) a person employed by a charitable organisation (within the meaning of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec2) of the [Charities Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) ) engaged in providing shelter for animals, or
  >
  >     - (*b*) a person employed by a hunt club within the meaning of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec2) of the [Dog Breeding Establishments Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) , or
  >
  >     - (*c*) an officer, servant or agent of a local authority acting in his or her official capacity.”, and

- (*d*) in the Schedule, by the substitution for the first sentence of paragraph 1.1 of “A dog identification database operator must be a full member of Europetnet or until 30th June 2016 an associate member.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 October 2015.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the Microchipping Of Dogs Regulations 2015.
