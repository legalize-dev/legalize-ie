# S.I. No. 13/1974 - Courts (Guardianship of Infants) (Fees) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 13 of 1974.

COURTS (GUARDIANSHIP OF INFANTS) (FEES) ORDER, 1974.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Courts (Guardianship of Infants) (Fees) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 31st day of January, 1974.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. No fee shall be payable in the Circuit Court Offices, the Central Office or the Office of the Registrar of the Supreme Court on any application, order, judgment or report or on the filing of any notice of motion, affidavit or other document necessary in connection with proceedings under the [Guardianship of Infants Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of January, 1974.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

22nd day of January, 1974.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, which will come into operation on 31st January, 1974, exempts proceedings in any Court under the [Guardianship of Infants Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) from court fees.
