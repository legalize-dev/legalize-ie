# S.I. No. 91/2002 - Mental Health Act, 2001 (Establishment Day) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Micheal Martin, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec31) of the [Mental Health Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2001), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Mental Health Act, 2001 (Establishment Day) Order 2002.

###### 2.

2. The 5th day of April, 2002, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of [Part 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) of the [Mental Health Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2001).
