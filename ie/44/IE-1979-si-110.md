# S.I. No. 110/1979 - Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 110 of 1979.

HEALTH SERVICES (LIMITED ELIGIBILITY) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 ), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations, as respects which, pursuant to the said section, a draft has been approved by a resolution passed by each House of the Oireachtas:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. For subsections (1) and (2) of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) [as substituted by the Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 276 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0276.html) ) and by the Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0141.html) )] there shall be substituted the following subsection—

> - "(1) A person who is without full eligibility shall have limited eligibility for services under this Part."

###### 3.

3. The Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1971, and the Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1976 are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. These Regulations shall come into operation on 6th day of April, 1979.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 4th day of April, 1979.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.Dated the 4th day of April, 1979.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations enable health benefits within the full range of limited eligibility services to be made available to persons without full eligibility.
