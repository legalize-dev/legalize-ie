# S.I. No. 573/2011 - Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) Regulations, 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th November*, 2011.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec4) and [147](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec147) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) Regulations, 2011.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “the Act” means the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. (1) The prescribed notification referred to in section 147(2) of the Act shall be in the form specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) The period within which a prescribed notification shall be given by a manager shall be the period beginning on the commencement of the seventh year and ending 6 months prior to the end of the seventh year of the tenure period of that manager.

- (3) Subject to the Local Government (Officers Age Limit) Order, 1957 ( [S.I. No. 140 of 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/si/0140.html) ), the period by which the tenure period of a manager who gives the prescribed notification referred to in section 147(2) of the Act shall be extended shall be 3 years.

- (4) Section 147(5) of the Act shall not apply to the following positions

    - (*a*) the position of Dublin City Manager;

    - (*b*) the position of Cork County Manager;

    - (*c*) the position of Cork City Manager;

    - (*d*) the position of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown County Manager;

    - (*e*) the position of Fingal County Manager;

    - (*f*) the position of South Dublin County Manager;

    - (*g*) the position of Meath County Manager, and;

    - (*h*) the position of Kildare County Manager.

- (5) Article 21 or 78, as appropriate, of the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0455.html) ) shall not apply to a manager whose tenure period is extended by section 147 of the Act and who ceases to hold the position before the expiration of his or her tenure period as so extended.

###### 4.

4. The Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) Regulations, 2003 ( [S.I. No. 47 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0047.html) ) are hereby revoked.
