# S.I. No. 414/1993 - Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993 (Vesting Day) Order, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 414 of 1993.

IRISH AVIATION AUTHORITY ACT, 1993 (VESTING DAY) ORDER, 1993.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Vesting Day) Order, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January, 1994, is hereby appointed to be the vesting day for the purposes of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of December, 1993.

**BRIAN COWEN,**

Minister for Transport, Energy

& Communications.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order fixes the 1st January 1994 as vesting day for the Irish Aviation Authority, which was set up under the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993).
