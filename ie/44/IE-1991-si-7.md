# S.I. No. 7/1991 - Labour Relations Commission (Establishment) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 7 of 1991.

LABOUR RELATIONS COMMISSION (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1991.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/sec0024.html#sec24) (2) of the [Industrial Relations Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Labour Relations Commission (Establishment) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The 21st day of January, 1991, is hereby appointed as the day on which the Labour Relations Commission shall stand established.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of January, 1991.

**BERTIE AHERN, T.D.,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints the 21st day of January, 1991, as the day on which the Labour Relations Commission is established.
