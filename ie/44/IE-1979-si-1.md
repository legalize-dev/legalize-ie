# S.I. No. 1/1979 - Worker Participation (Cómhlucht Siúicre Éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Cómhlucht Siúicre Éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The 14th day of March, 1979, is in relation to Comhlucht Siúicre Éireann Teoranta hereby appointed to be the nomination day for the purposes of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977).

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 2nd day of January, 1979.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations appoint 14th March, 1979, as the day on which nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to Cómhlucht Siúicre Éireann Teoranta shall be received by the returning officer.
