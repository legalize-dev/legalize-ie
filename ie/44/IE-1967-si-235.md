# S.I. No. 235/1967 - Foot and Mouth Disease (Importation of Animals) Order, 1967

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 235 of 1967.

FOOT AND MOUTH DISEASE (IMPORTATION OF ANIMALS) ORDER, 1967.

I, NEIL T. BLANEY, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Foot and Mouth Disease (Importation of Animals) Order, 1967.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into force on the 15th day of November, 1967.

###### 3.

3. The import from Great Britain, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man or the Continent of Europe of any animal is hereby prohibited unless such animal is imported under and in accordance with a licence granted in that behalf by the Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries and such licence is, at the time of importation, delivered to the proper officer of Customs and Excise.

###### 4.

4. Nothing in this Order shall be construed as affecting the operation of the Orders set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 5.

5. For the purpose of extending the application of [sections 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) , [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0030.html#sec30) , [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0042.html#sec42) , [46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0046.html#sec46) and [51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), and all other sections (including sections relating to offences and legal proceedings) containing provisions relative to or consequent on those sections, the First Schedule to that Act is hereby amended by the insertion at the end of Part 1 thereof of the following:

> "10. Any four-footed beast not hereinbefore mentioned.".

SCHEDULE

Importation of Horses, Asses and Mules (Ireland) Order of 1907, No. II.

Importation of Dogs and Cats Order of 1929 ( [S.R. & O. No. 4 of 1929](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/si/0004.html) ).

Foreign Animals Order of 1931 ( [S.R. & O. No. 9 of 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/si/0009.html) ).

Rodents and Insectivora (Restriction on Importation) Order, 1954 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/si/0114.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of November, 1967.

**NEIL T. BLANEY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order—

- (1) prohibits the importation, except under licence, of animals from Great Britain, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man and the Continent of Europe;

- (2) extends the definition of "animals" in the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) to include all four-footed animals.
