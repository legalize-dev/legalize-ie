S.I. No. 30 of 1995.

POUNDS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, NORA OWEN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8 (as amended by the [Animals Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1985 )) of the [Pounds (Provision and Maintenance) Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1935), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 13th day of February, 1995.

###### 3.

3. The Pounds Regulations, 1985 and 1990, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Pounds Regulations, 1985 to 1995.

###### 4.

4. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Pounds Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0306.html) ), as amended by the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 4 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0004.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution for the Second Schedule of the following Schedule:
