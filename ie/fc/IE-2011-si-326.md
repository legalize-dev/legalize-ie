# S.I. No. 326/2011 - Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st July*, 2011.

I, PAT RABBITTE, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec69) and [78](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec78) (3)(*a*)(v) of the [Inland Fisheries Act, 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) at the request of Inland Fisheries Ireland and being satisfied that it has had regard to the requirements of Regulation 31 of the European Communities (Natural Habitats) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 94 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0094.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. The Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (No. 2) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 665 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0665.html) ) are amended by substituting the Schedule set out in these Regulations for Schedule 2.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (No. 2) Regulations, 2010 ( [S.I. No. 665 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0665.html) ) to provide for the quota of fish that can be harvested in Castlemaine Harbour, Kerry fishery district.

## Footnotes

[^1]: If draft netting in the common estuary is allowed, the quotas for the Caragh, Laune and Maine have to be fixed for the common estuary and this quota is lower than a combination of the three individual river quotas because it is a mixed stock fishery and the lower amount is necessary to ensure meeting the 75% target.

[^2]: If draft netting in the common estuary is allowed the quotas for the Erriff and Bundorragha have to be fixed for the common estuary and this quota is lower than a combination of the two individual river quotas because it is a mixed stock fishery and the lower amount is necessary to ensure meeting the 75% target.

[^3]: If draft netting in the common estuary is allowed the quotas for the Owenduff and Owenmore have to be fixed for the common estuary and this quota is lower than a combination of the two individual river quotas because it is a mixed stock fishery and the lower amount is necessary to ensure meeting the 75% target.
