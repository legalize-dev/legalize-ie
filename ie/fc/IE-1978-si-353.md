# S.I. No. 353/1978 - Harbour Rates (Cappa Pier At Kilrush) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 353 of 1978.

HARBOUR RATES (CAPPA PIER AT KILRUSH) ORDER, 1978.

I, PÁDRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Tourism and Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Transport and Power (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0305.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Cappa Pier at Kilrush) Order, 1978.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 25th day of December, 1978.

###### 2.

2. (1) The rates set out in the Schedules to this Order are hereby fixed as the maximum rates to be charged by the Kilrush Urban District Council in respect of Cappa Pier at Kilrush.

- (2) Where rates fixed under this Order are payable at a metric tonne charge, lesser weights shall be chargeable as follows:—

( *a* ) any weight not exceeding 250 kilogrammes shall count as 250 kilogrammes,

( *b* ) any weight exceeding 250 kilogrammes but not exceeding 500 kilogrammes shall count as 500 kilogrammes,

( *c* ) any weight exceeding 500 kilogrammes but not exceeding 750 kilogrammes shall count as 750 kilogrammes,

( *d* ) any weight exceeding 750 kilogrammes but not exceeding 1 tonne, shall count as 1 tonne.

###### 3.

3. Every statutory or other provision fixing, restricting or otherwise regulating the rates or the maximum or minimum rates chargeable by the Kilrush Urban District Council on subjects of charge set out in the Schedules to this Order, or the basis on which, or method by which, such rates are computed, is hereby revoked.

**PÁDRAIG FAULKNER,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order authorises the Kilrush Urban District Council to charge revised maximum goods, tonnage and other rates at Cappa Pier, Kilrush.
