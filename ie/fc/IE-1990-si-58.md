# S.I. No. 58/1990 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 58 of 1990.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY BOROUGH OF DUBLIN AND COUNTY OF DUBLIN) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 206 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0206.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed together and may be cited collectively as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1989 to 1990.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 11th day of March, 1990.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 206 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0206.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Reference 5 (*c*) of the Second Schedule to the Principal Regulations is hereby amended

( *a* ) by the addition thereto after paragraph (xxx) of the following paragraph—

> - "(xxxi) The Western Parkway Motorway from a point 80 metres south of its junction with the Navan Road (National Road N3)", and

( *b* ) by the substitution for "thence in a northwesterly direction along the southern edge of the Navan Road (National Road N3) to a point 192 metres from its junction with Beech Park Avenue" of "thence in a northwesterly direction along the southern edge of the Navan Road (National Road N3) to a point 80 metres from its junction with Beech Park Avenue".

###### 5.

5. Reference 6 (*n*) of the Third Schedule to the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the addition of the following:

- "(ix) The Palmerstown By-Pass between a point 1,195 metres east of its junction with the Fonthill-Newlands Road and a point 668 metres west of its junction with Kennelsfort Road Upper.

- (x) The Western Parkway Motorway from a point 97 metres north of the centre line of the northernmost bridge on the Palmerstown By-Pass, above the Western Parkway Motorway, to a point 955 metres north of the said point.

- (xi) The Northbound On-Ramp linking the Palmerstown By-Pass to the Western Parkway Motorway.

- (xii) The Northbound Off-Ramp linking the Western Parkway Motorway to the Palmerstown By-Pass.

- (xiii) The Southbound On-Ramp linking the Palmerstown By-Pass to the Western Parkway Motorway.

- (xiv) The Southbound Off-Ramp linking the Western Parkway Motorway to the Palmerstown By-Pass".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 6th day of March, 1990.

**PÁDRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 206 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0206.html) ).

The Regulations prescribe 40 mph and 30 mph speed limits on certain sections of the Western Parkway Motorway and of adjoining roads.
