# S.I. No. 538/2010 - Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th November*, 2010.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1992), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. The Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 75 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0075.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 November 2010.

**JOHN GORMLEY,**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations restore the requirements set out in the Electoral Regulations 2007 in relation to the specified documents which a voter may be required to produce at a polling station and the photographic identification documents required to be produced by a person assenting to the nomination of a non-party candidate (by signing and completing a statutory declaration) in order to reflect requirements appropriate to a Dáil election.
