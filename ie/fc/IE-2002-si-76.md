# S.I. No. 76/2002 - Irish Aviation Authority (Rules of the Air) (Amendment) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Irish Aviation Authority, in pursuance of [sections 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0058.html#sec58) , [59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0059.html#sec59) and [60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993) as amended by the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998), hereby orders as follows -

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Irish Aviation Authority (Rules of the Air (Amendment) Order, 2002;

- (2) In this Order “the Principal Order” means the Irish Aviation Authority (Rules of the Air) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No 568 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0568.html) );

- (3) The Principal Order and this Order may be cited together as the Irish Aviation Authority (Rules of the Air) Orders 2001 and 2002;

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the date of its publication in the Iris Oifigiuil or on the first day of April, 2002, whichever date occurs first.

###### 2.

2. The Principal Order is amended as follows:-

In Rule 34 (General Rules for VFR Flight) -

Replace sub-paragraph (c) of ***Note to paragraph (1) of the Rule with the following sub-paragraph to that Note:-

> - “(c) A helicopter may be permitted to operate in less than 1500m flight visibility, where a lower flight visibility is prescribed by the Authority, if manoeuvred at a speed that will give adequate opportunity to observe other traffic or any obstacles in time to avoid collision.”
