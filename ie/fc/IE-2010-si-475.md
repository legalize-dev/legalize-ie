# S.I. No. 475/2010 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Notification and Control of Animal Diseases) (Amendment) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th October*, 2010.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0012.html#sec12) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food) (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister Order) 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) ), hereby order as follows:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The effect of this Order is to consolidate the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Notification and Control of Animal Diseases) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0101.html) ).
