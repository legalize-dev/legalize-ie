# S.I. No. 338/1987 - Imposition of Duties (No. 287) (Excise Duty on Table Waters) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 338 of 1987.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 287) (EXCISE DUTY ON TABLE WATERS) ORDER, 1987.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), and [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0006/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Finance Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1975), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 287) (Excise Duty on Table Waters) Order, 1987.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding the provisions of [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986), no rebate of excise duty shall be allowed under paragraph (*a*) of subsection (3) of [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0014/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Finance Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1980) (inserted by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0037.html#sec37) of the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1981)), in respect of table waters on which duty was paid on or after the 1st day of January, 1988.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 18th day of December, 1987.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the termination of the rebate of excise duty on table waters with effect from 1st January, 1988.
