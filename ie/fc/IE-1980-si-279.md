# S.I. No. 279/1980 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Cavan) (Amendment) Regulations, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 279 of 1980.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF CAVAN) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1980.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Cavan) (Amendment) Regulations, 1980.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Cavan) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 283 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0283.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Cavan) Regulations, 1979 and 1980.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 15th day of September, 1980.

###### 3.

3. The Second Schedule to the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Cavan) Regulations, 1979 is hereby amended by the addition of the following reference—

"19. *The following roads at Dowra*:—

( *a* ) The Drumkeeran-Ballyconnel road between a point 165 metres south-west of its junction with the Blacklion Road and the boundary between the counties of Cavan and Leitrim at the Shannon Bridge.

( *b* ) Main Street and its continuation as the Blacklion Road for a distance of 200 metres from the junction of Main Street with the Drumkeeran-Ballyconnell road."

GIVEN under the Official Seal, this 5th day of September, 1980.

**SYLVESTER BARRETT,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Cavan) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 283 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0283.html) ) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in the County of Cavan.
