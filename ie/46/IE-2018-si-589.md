# S.I. No. 589/2018 - Derelict Sites Act 1990 (Urban Areas) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th January*, 2019.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Derelict Sites Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites Act 1990 (Urban Areas) Regulations 2018.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations 1990 to 2016 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations 1990 to 2018.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 1990” means the [Derelict Sites Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990).

###### 3.

3. (1) Each of the areas situated in the administrative county of Galway specified in Part 1 of the Schedule is prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the Act of 1990.

- (2) Each of the areas situated in the administrative county of Kerry specified in Part 2 of the Schedule is prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the Act of 1990.

- (3) Each of the areas situated in the administrative county of Kildare specified in Part 3 of the Schedule is prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the Act of 1990.

- (4) Each of the areas situated in the administrative county of Kilkenny specified in Part 4 of the Schedule is prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the Act of 1990.

- (5) Each of the areas situated in the administrative county of Louth specified in Part 5 of the Schedule is prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the Act of 1990.

- (6) Each of the areas situated in the administrative county of Roscommon specified in Part 6 of the Schedule is prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the Act of 1990.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe areas in the counties of Galway, Kerry, Kildare, Kilkenny, Louth and Roscommon indicated to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
