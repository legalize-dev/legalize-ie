# S.I. No. 517/2005 - Companies (Fees) (No. 3) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 517 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0517.html)**

**Companies (Fees) (No. 3) Order 2005**

I, Michael Ahern, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 395 (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) and 396 of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 808 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0808.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) (No. 3) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. (1) Article 3 of this Order comes into operation on the date of the making of this Order.

- (2) Articles 4 and 5 of this Order come into operation on 1 December 2005.

###### 3.

3. The Companies (Fees) (No.2) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 365 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0365.html) ) is revoked.

###### 4.

4. In this Order “Eighth Schedule” means the Eighth Schedule (substituted by the Companies (Fees) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 477 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0477.html) ), and amended by the Companies (Fees) (No.2) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 569 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0569.html) ), the Companies (Fees) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 557 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0557.html) ), the Companies (Fees) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0187.html) ) and the Companies (Fees) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 179 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0179.html) )) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

###### 5.

5. The Eighth Schedule is altered by substituting the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order for that Schedule.

## PART II *Limitations on operation of Part I*

Where the registrar directs, pursuant to section 23(2), that a company shall change its name, any fee that would, but for this Part, be required to be paid under Part I of this Schedule in respect of the registration of that change shall not be charged, levied or paid”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the introduction from 1 December 2005 of a new table of fees to be paid to the Registrar of Companies. The table makes separate provision for fees payable in respect of paper transactions and fees payable in respect of electronic transactions.

The Order also revokes the Companies (Fees) (No. 2) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 365 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0365.html) which will not come into operation.
