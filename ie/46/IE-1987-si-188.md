# S.I. No. 188/1987 - Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 188 of 1987.

SOCIAL WELFARE (PAY-RELATED BENEFIT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981) and by section 76 (1) of that Act as amended by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0002/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1987) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:—

"the Principal Act" means the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on:

( *a* ) the 20th day of July, 1987 in the case of pay-related benefit to which a person is entitled by virtue of section 72 (1) (*a*) of the Principal Act,

( *b* ) the 16th day of July, 1987 in the case of pay-related benefit to which a person is entitled by virtue of section 72 (1) (*b*) of the Principal Act.

The Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0141.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution for article 10 of the following article,

> "10. The weekly rate of pay-related benefit or the appropriate amount of such weekly rate payable in respect of a period less than a week shall be rounded up to the nearest 10p where it is a multiple of 5p but not also a multiple of 10p and shall be rounded to the nearest 10p where it is not a multiple of 5p or 10p".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 15th day of July, 1987.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide under powers given in the [Social Welfare Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) that the weekly rate of pay-related benefit and payments of pay-related benefit in respect of periods less than one week shall be rounded up or down to the nearest unit of 10p.
