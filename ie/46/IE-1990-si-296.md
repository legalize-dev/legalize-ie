S.I. No. 296 of 1990.

SHANNON NAVIGATION ACT, 1990 (FORMS) REGULATIONS, 1990.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraphs 1 (2) and 4 of the Schedule to the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (Forms) Regulations, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in Part I of the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby prescribed as the form of a notice under clause (*b*) of paragraph 1 (1) of the Schedule to the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in Part II of the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby prescribed as the form of a notice under clause (*c*) of the said paragraph 1(1).

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Part III of the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby prescribed as the form of a vesting order under paragraph 3 of the Schedule to the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) .

## *Part III* VESTING ORDER

[Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html)

____________________________

Compulsory Acquisition of Land

____________________________

WHEREAS the Commissioners of Public Works in Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990), propose to acquire compulsorily the land described in the Schedule to this Order, being land which is required for the purposes of their functions in relation to the Shannon navigation:

AND WHEREAS the provisions of paragraph 1 of the Schedule to the said Act have been complied with in relation to the acquisition of the said land:

* AND WHEREAS no objection to the acquisition of the said land has been submitted to the Commissioners in accordance with subparagraph (1) of paragraph 2 of the said Schedule:

* AND WHEREAS an objection to the acquisition of the said land was submitted to the said Commissioners in accordance with subparagraph (1) of paragraph 2 of the said schedule but was subsequently withdrawn in accordance with subparagraph (2) of the said paragraph 2:

* AND WHEREAS an objection to the acquisition of the said land was submitted to the said Commissioners in accordance with subparagraph (1) of paragraph 2 of the said schedule and the Minister for Finance has given his consent in accordance with subparagraphs (3) and (5) of the said paragraph 2 to the said acquisition:

NOW the Commissioners of Public Works in Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 2 and 5 of and the Schedule to the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

1. This Order may be cited as the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. .....) Vesting Order, 1990.

2. The land specified in the Schedule to this Order is hereby vested on the.... day of .............., 19 ........., in the Commissioners of Public Works in Ireland in fee simple free from encumbrances and all estates, rights, easements, titles and interests of any kind over or in respect of the land.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the form of notices to be issued where the Commissioners of Public Works propose to acquire land compulsorily for the purposes of their functions in relation to the Shannon Navigation in accordance with the Schedule to the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990). The regulations also prescribe the form of Vesting Order to be used when lands have been compulsorily acquired.
