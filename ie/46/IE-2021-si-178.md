# S.I. No. 178/2021 - European Communities (Reception Conditions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th April,* 2021.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2013/33/EU of 26 June 2013[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Reception Conditions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the European Communities (Reception Conditions) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 230 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0230.html) ) is amended –

- (*a*) in the definition of “applicant”, by the deletion of “, subject to paragraph (2),”,

- (*b*) in the definition of “recipient”, by the deletion of “, subject to paragraphs (2) and (3),”, and

- (*c*) by the deletion of paragraphs (2) and (3).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 April, 2021.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Reception Conditions) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 230 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0230.html) ) to give further effect in Irish law to Directive 2013/33/EU of 26 June 2013.

The Regulations delete paragraphs (2) and (3) from Regulation 2 of [S.I. No. 230 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0230.html) , which excluded from access to the labour market applicants for international protection the subject of notification of a transfer decision under Regulation 5(2) or an appeal under Regulation 16(2) of the European Union (Dublin System) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0062.html) ).
