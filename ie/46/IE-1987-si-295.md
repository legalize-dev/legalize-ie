# S.I. No. 295/1987 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 51, Twenty-Fourth Period) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1987

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 295 of 1987.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA No. 51, TWENTY-FOURTH PERIOD) (MISCELLANEOUS TEXTILES) ORDER, 1987.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 6 (as amended by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Control of Imports (Amendment) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1937)) of the [Control of Imports Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1934), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 51 Twenty-Fourth Period) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1977), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This instrument renews for the year 1988 an existing instrument which will expire on 31 December, 1987. It appoints a nominal quota of £100 as the value of the miscellaneous textiles (wearing apparel and other made-up textile articles) originating in certain countries which may be imported during the period 1 January, 1988 to 31 December, 1988. This nominal quota has been appointed to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister for Industry and Commerce is prepared to license, during the quota period, not less than 15 tonnes of the goods concerned, consonant with the restrictions in operation for the different countries concerned.
