S.I. No. 171 of 1972.

SECONDARY TEACHERS' SUPERANNUATION (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) SCHEME, 1972.

I, PÁDRAIG Ó FACHTNA, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) , [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/sec0003.html#sec3) and [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Teachers' Superannuation Act, 1928](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1928), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Scheme :

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Secondary Teachers' Superannuation (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1972, and the collective citation " the Secondary Teachers' Superannuation Scheme, 1929 to 1972 " shall include this Scheme.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into force immediately upon its being confirmed by each House of the Oireachtas.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. In this Scheme " the Principal Scheme " means the Secondary Teachers' Superannuation Scheme, 1929 ( [S. R. & O. No. 19 of 1929](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/si/0019.html) ), as amended.

###### 5.

5. Each paragraph of the Principal Scheme and each rule of the Schedule to that Scheme mentioned in column (1) of the Schedule to this Scheme is hereby amended in the manner stated in column (2) of that Schedule opposite the mention of that paragraph or rule in column (1).

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This amending scheme provides for the admission of Comprehensive School teachers to membership of the Secondary Teachers' Superannuation Scheme.
