# S.I. No. 512/2006 - Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 512 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0512.html)**

**Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec7) of the [Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (Establishment Day) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The 4^th day of November, 2006 is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2006).
