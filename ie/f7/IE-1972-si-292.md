# S.I. No. 292/1972 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 50, Sixteenth Period) (Superphosphates) (No. 2) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 292 of 1972.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 50, SIXTEENTH PERIOD) (SUPERPHOSPHATES) (NO. 2) ORDER, 1972.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 50, Sixteenth Period) (Superphosphates) (No. 2) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

SCHEDULE.

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quota Order for which quota period and quota are appointed | Quota Period | Quota | General description of subject matter of quota |
| (1) | (2) | (3) | (4) |
| Control of Imports (Quota No. 50) Order, 1959 (S.R. & O., No. 129 of 1959), as amended. | The period beginning on the 1st day of January, 1973, and ending on the 31st day of December, 1973. | Six thousand five hundred and seventy (6,570) tons. | Superphosphates. |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 28th day

of November, 1972.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints the quantity of superphosphates which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1973, to 31st December, 1973, from countries other than the United Kingdom at 6,570 tons.
