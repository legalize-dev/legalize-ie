# S.I. No. 434/2010 - Road Traffic Act 1994 (Sections 18 and 19) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th September*, 2010.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Sections 18 and 19) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 11 October 2010.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 1994” means [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in Schedule 1 is prescribed as the form for the purposes of section 18 of the Act of 1994.

###### 4.

4. The form set out in—

- (a) Part 1 of Schedule 2, in the case of a certificate determining the concentration of alcohol in a specimen, or

- (b) Part 2 of Schedule 2, in the case of a certificate determining the presence of a drug or drugs in a specimen,

is the prescribed form of certificate for the purposes of section 19 of the Act of 1994.

###### 5.

5. The Road Traffic Act 1994 (Part III) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0351.html) ) (other than Regulation 5) and the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Part III) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 173 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0173.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations prescribe the form to be completed by a designated doctor under [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) and the certificate, both in English and Irish, to be issued by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety under [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
