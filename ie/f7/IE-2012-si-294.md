# S.I. No. 294/2012 - Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2011 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd August*, 2012.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5 and section 103 (as amended by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec14) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Administrative Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ) and the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )) and after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality in relation to declaring offences to be fixed charge offences, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2011 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 3 August 2012.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

“Principal Act” means [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961);

“vehicle” means mechanically propelled vehicle.

###### 4.

4. The offences under the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2011 mentioned in Schedules 1 and 2 and in Part 1 of Schedule 3 are declared to be fixed charge offences for the purposes of section 103 of the Principal Act.

###### 5.

5. The amount in relation to a fixed charge is—

- (*a*) €200,in respect of an offence under section 12 of the Principal Act mentioned in Schedule 1,

- (*b*) €80,in respect of an offence under section 11 of the Principal Act, for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Schedule 2, and

- (*c*) €60,in respect of—

    - (i) an offence under section 11 of the Principal Act for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Part 1 of Schedule 3, or

    - (ii) an offence mentioned in Part 2 of Schedule 3.

###### 6.

6. Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0135.html) ) is revoked in so far as it relates to the Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 359 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0359.html) ).

Regulations 4 and 5(*a*)

## **Schedule 1** **€200 fixed charge**

An offence under section 12 of [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (as amended by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) consisting of using on a public road a vehicle,

- (*a*) whose weight unladen exceeds maximum permitted weight,

- (*c*) any part of which transmits to ground greater weight than maximum permitted weight,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations declare offences involving the driving or use of mechanically propelled vehicles to be fixed charge offences for the purposes of [section 103](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0103.html#sec103) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , as amended, with effect from 3rd August 2012. They also prescribe the amount of the fixed charge for each offence.
