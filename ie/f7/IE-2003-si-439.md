# S.I. No. 439/2003 - Fishery Harbour Centres (Rates and Charges) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Dermot Ahern, Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0018/sec0004.html#sec4) (2) of the [Fishery Harbour Centres Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1968), as amended by the [Fishery Harbour Centres Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1980), and the Marine and Natural Resources (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0300.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Fishery Harbour Centres (Rates and Charges) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 1 October 2003.

###### 3.

3. In this Order-

“fish” has the same meaning as in the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), as amended by the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1978);

Gross Registered Tonnage (GRT) and Gross Tonnage (GT) are regarded as equivalent for the purpose of levying rates or charges;

“length”, in relation to a vessel, means the overall length of the vessel;

“the Minister” means the Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 4.

4. (1) Each rate or charge specified in column (3) of Schedule 1 and 2 to this Order at a particular charge number is hereby fixed as the rate or charge for the use of the facility or service described in column (2) of the respective Schedule at that charge number in the following Fishery Harbour Centres:

- (a) Castletownbere Fishery Harbour Centre;

- (b) Dunmore East Fishery Harbour Centre;

- (c) Howth Fishery Harbour Centre;

- (d) Killybegs Fishery Harbour Centre;

- (e) Rossaveel Fishery Harbour Centre.

- (2) The rates or charges in Schedule 2 of this Order are hereby fixed as the maximum rates or charges chargeable in respect of the use of the facility or service described.

- (3) The rates or charges specified in the Schedules to this Order in respect of a year, month, week or day apply to the full period or part thereof as the case may be.

###### 5.

5. (1) A rate or charge fixed by this Order shall be paid to the Minister by the person using the facility or service concerned.

- (2) The amount of any rate or charge payable under this Order may be recovered by the Minister either as a simple contract debt in a court of competent jurisdiction or by distress and sale of any vessel or goods in respect of which the rate or charge is payable.

- (3) In the event that any rate or charge is in arrears by ninety days or more the Minister may withdraw all services from that vessel or any other vessel owned by the same person and refuse entry to any of the Fishery Harbour Centres to which this Order applies until such time as the debt is discharged.

###### 6.

6. (1) Subject to paragraph (2) the rates and charges fixed by this Order shall be in lieu of any tolls, rates or charges which, immediately before the commencement of this Order, were in force in respect of any Fishery Harbour Centre to which this Order relates.

- (2) Without prejudice to the due collection of rates and charges due thereunder (including annual charges straddling the years 2003 and 2004), the Fishery Harbour Centres (Rates and Charges) Order 1990 ( [S.I. No. 239 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0239.html) ) is hereby revoked.
