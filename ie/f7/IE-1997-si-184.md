# S.I. No. 184/1997 - National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 184 of 1997.

NATIONAL MILK AGENCY (ELECTION DAY) REGULATIONS, 1997

I, IVAN YATES, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph 5 (2) of the Schedule to the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , as amended by section 4 (b) of the Milk (Regulation of Supply ^Amendment) Act, 1996 (No. 41 of 1996) hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day), Regulations, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The 27th day of June, 1997 is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the National Milk Agency.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of April, 1997.

**IVAN YATES,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry.
