# S.I. No. 273/2005 - Central Bank Act 1942 (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 273 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0273.html)**

**CENTRAL BANK ACT 1942 (SECTIONS 33J and 33K) REGULATIONS 2005**

**[S.I. No. 273 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0273.html)**

**Central Bank Act 1942 (Section 33J and 33K) Regulations 2005**

I, Liam O'Reilly, Chief Executive of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Sections 33J and 33K of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (as inserted by the Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act, 2003), with the agreement of the members of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority, and with the approval of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. Part B of the Schedule to the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0447.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution therefor of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 5(b) of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K Regulations 2004 shall take effect and be applied as if the due date was the earlier of the date set out in a levy notice and 16 weeks from the making of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2005.

> **EXPLANATORY NOTE**
>
> *(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*
>
> These Regulations, made by the Chief Executive of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority in accordance with Sections 33J and 33K of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (as amended) amend the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0447.html) ) which set out the requirements of regulated financial service providers to pay a levy contribution to the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority. The 2004 Regulations also provide for the collection and recovery of the levies as well as providing for certain obligations as to self-assessment and record keeping by regulated financial service providers. These Regulations will be published from time to time by the Authority on its website and in other ways that the Chief Executive thinks appropriate.
