# S.I. No. 223/1989 - Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees), Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 223 of 1989.

MEDICAL PREPARATIONS (AMENDMENT OF FEES), REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) as amended by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0039.html#sec39) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. (1) The Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 225 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0225.html) ) as amended by the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 10 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0010.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution in article 8 of "£965" for "£900".

- (2) The Medical Preparations (Wholesale Licences) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 333 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0333.html) ) as amended by the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1988 are hereby amended by the substitution in article 8(2) of "£480" for "£450" and of "£160" for "£150".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 5th day of September, 1989.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 5th day of September, 1989.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for increases in the fees charged for licences granted in respect of the manufacture and sale by wholesale of medical preparations.
