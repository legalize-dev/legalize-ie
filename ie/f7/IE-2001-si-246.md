# S.I. No. 246/2001 - Education Act, 1998 (National Council For Curriculum and Assessment) (Establishment Day) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Michael Woods, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Education Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No 51 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Education Act, 1998 (National Council for Curriculum and Assessment) (Establishment Day) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The 12^th day of July 2001, is hereby appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of Part VII of the [Education Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1998).
