# S.I. No. 403/1983 - Local Government (Planning and Development) (Postal and Telecommunications) (Exempted Development) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 403 of 1983.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (POSTAL AND TELECOMMUNICATIONS) (EXEMPTED DEVELOPMENT) REGULATIONS, 1983.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0004.html#sec4) and [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963) hereby makes the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Postal and Telecommunications) (Exempted Development) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The collective citation "the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1983" shall include these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1984.

###### 4.

4. The Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0065.html) ) are hereby amended by the insertion after Class 25 in columns 1 and 2 of Part I of the Third Schedule of the following matter—

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 so as to provide that certain development carried out by the Post Office and by The Irish Telecommunications Board shall be exempted development for the purposes of the Local Government (Planning and Development) Acts, 1963 to 1983.
