# S.I. No. 844/2004 - Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, on the request of the Minister for Health and Children and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order, 2004.

###### 2.

2. The powers and duties of the Minister for Health and Children conferred on that Minister of the Government by or under the enactments specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby delegated to Seán Power, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order delegates to Seán Power T.D., Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children the Statutory powers and duties of the Minister for Health and Children mentioned in the Order.
