S.I. No. 38 of 1985.

DISTRICT COURT (FEES) ORDER, 1985.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the District Court (Fees) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The District Court (Fees) Order, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0021.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for the Schedule thereto of the following Schedule.

SCHEDULE

PART I — General

PART II — Civil Proceedings

PART III — Enforcement of Court Orders Proceedings

PART IV — Licensing Matters

PART VII — Proceedings under the [Housing (Private Rented Dwellings) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/index.html)

PART VIII — Fees payable in Appeals under the [Betting Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) .

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order amends the District Court (Fees) Order, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0021.html) ) by the substitution of a new Schedule for the Schedule thereto and thereby increases, as from 1st March, 1985, the fees to be charged in District Court Offices.
