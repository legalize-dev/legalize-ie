# S.I. No. 303/1997 - Trade (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 303 of 1997.

TRADE (TRANSFER OF DEPARTMENTAL ADMINISTRATION AND MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1997

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Trade (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 11th day of July, 1997.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "functions" includes powers and duties.

###### 3.

3. (1) The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 4 of this Order are hereby transferred to the Department of Enterprise and Employment.

- (2) References to the Department of Tourism and Trade contained in any Act or any instrument made thereunder and relating to any administration and business transferred by paragraph (1) of this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Department of Enterprise and Employment.

###### 4.

4. (1) There are hereby transferred to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment the functions vested in the Minister for Tourism and Trade by or under any of the Acts and regulations mentioned in the Schedule to this Order:

- (2) References to the Minister for Tourism and Trade contained in any Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any functions transferred by this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

###### 5.

5. Where, immediately before the commencement of this Order, any legal proceedings are pending to which the Minister for Tourism and Trade is a party and the proceedings have reference to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment, the name of the Minister for Enterprise and Employment shall, in so far as the proceedings relate to any function transferred by this Order, be substituted in the proceedings for that of the Minister for Tourism and Trade, or added in the proceedings, as may be appropriate, and the proceedings shall not abate by reason of such substitution.

###### 6.

6. Anything commenced before the commencement of this Order by or under the authority of the Minister for Tourism and Trade may, in so far as it relates to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment, be carried on or completed on or after such commencement by the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

###### 7.

7. Every document (including any certificate or licence) granted or made in the exercise of a function transferred by this Order shall, if and in so far as it was operative immediately before the commencement of this order, have effect on an after such commencement as if it had been granted or made by the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

###### 8.

8. References to the Minister for Tourism and Trade contained in the memorandum and articles of association of any company and relating to any function transferred by this Order shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

###### 9.

9. (1) All property, rights and liabilities held, enjoyed or incurred before the commencement of this Order by the Minister for Tourism and Trade in connection with the functions transferred by this Order shall be and are hereby transferred to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment and, accordingly, without any further conveyance, transfer or assignment

( *a* ) the said property, real and personal, shall, on such commencement, vest in the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

( *b* ) the said rights shall, as on and from such commencement, be enjoyed by the Minister for Enterprise and Employment, and

( *c* ) the said liabilities shall, as on and from such commencement, be the liabilities of the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

- (2) All moneys, stocks, shares and securities transferred to the Minister for Enterprise and Employment by this Article which, on such commencement, are standing in the name of the Minister for Tourism and Trade shall on the request of the Minister for Enterprise and Employment be transferred into the name of the Minister for Enterprise and Employment.
