# S.I. No. 588/2017 - Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1997 (Prescribed Organisations For Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th December*, 2017.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0023.html#sec23) (3) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1997 (Prescribed Organisations for the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are prescribed for the purposes of [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0023.html#sec23) (3) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997).

###### 3.

3. The Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1997 (Prescribed Organisations for the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 621 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0621.html) ) is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations prescribe certain organisations for the purpose of enabling them to nominate persons from whom the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine may appoint 6 ordinary members of the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board under [Section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) .
