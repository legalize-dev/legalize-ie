# S.I. No. 435/1986 - Tour Operators (Licensing) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 435 of 1986.

TOUR OPERATORS (LICENSING) (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0007.html#sec7) and [25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Transport (Tour Operators and Travel Agents) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1982), and the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Tour Operators (licensing) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) The collective citation "the Tour Operators (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 to 1986" shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 19th day of December, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The Tour Operators (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 100 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0100.html) ), as amended by the Tour Operators (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 215 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0215.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution, in paragraph 6 of the General Conditions in the Second Schedule, of "regulations made under the said Act" for "the Travellers' Protection Fund Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 139 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0139.html) )".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 18th day of December, 1986.

**JIM MITCHELL,**

Minister for Communications.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for a technical amendment to the General Conditions of tour operators' licences.
