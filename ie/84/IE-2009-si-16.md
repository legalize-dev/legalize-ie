# S.I. No. 16/2009 - Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice Act) (Transfer of Property) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 16 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0016.html)

AGRICULTURE (RESEARCH, TRAINING AND ADVICE ACT) (TRANSFER OF PROPERTY) ORDER 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th January*, 2009.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 21(5)(*a*) of the [Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice) Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1988) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister)) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice Act) (Transfer of Property) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. This Order is deemed to have come into operation in place of the Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice Act) (Transfer of Property) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 214 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0214.html) ).

###### 3.

3. All property contained in Land Registry Folio 22836 of the Register in County Cork described at entry No. 4 of Part II of the Folio being Plan No. 5. Is transferred to Teagasc without further transfer.

###### 4.

4. The Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice Act) (Transfer of Property) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 214 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0214.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 January 2009

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This statutory Instrument gives effect to the Minister for Agriculture Fisheries & Food decision to transfer lands situated at Darrary County Cork and Registered on Folio 22836 County Cork to Teagasc-The Agriculture Food and Development Authority.
