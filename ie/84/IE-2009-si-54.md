# S.I. No. 54/2009 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Early Childcare Supplement) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 54 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0054.html)

SOCIAL WELFARE (CONSOLIDATED CLAIMS, PAYMENTS AND CONTROL) (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) (EARLY CHILDCARE SUPPLEMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th February*, 2009.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 4 (as amended by section 17 and Schedule 3 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2006)), 223B (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec21) of the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2008)) and 242 (as amended by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec29) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby makes the following regulations:

*Citation and construction.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Early Childcare Supplement) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2009.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 9 February 2009.

*Definitions.*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “the Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ).

*Early childcare supplement — Commencement of awards.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by substituting the following article for article 194:

> “194. Early childcare supplement shall commence to accrue in the case of a claim made by a person who becomes a qualified person by and on the death of another person who has been entitled to early childcare supplement at the date of death, on the 1st day of the month in which the claim is made.”

*Early childcare supplement — time of payment.*

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended in article 196(1) by substituting for paragraph (*f*) the following:

> - “(*f*) in the case of early childcare supplement, in respect of each month specified in column (1) of the table to this sub-article on the day specified in column (2) of that table.
>
>   **TABLE**
>
>   |  |  |
>   | --- | --- |
>   | Month(1) | Day(2) |
>   | January | 2 nd Monday in February |
>   | February | 2 nd Monday in March |
>   | March | 2 nd Monday in April |
>   | April | 2 nd Monday in May |
>   | May | 2 nd Monday in June |
>   | June | 2 nd Monday in July |
>   | July | 2 nd Monday in August |
>   | August | 2 nd Monday in September |
>   | September | 2 nd Monday in October |
>   | October | 2 nd Monday in November |
>   | November | 2 nd Monday in December |
>   | December | 2 nd Monday in January |
>
>   ”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations set out the dates on which Early Childcare Supplement will be payable for 2009 and subsequent years.
