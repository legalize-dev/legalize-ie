# S.I. No. 88/1979 - Harbour Rates (Dublin Harbour) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 88 of 1979.

HARBOUR RATES (DUBLIN HARBOUR) ORDER, 1979.

I, PÁDRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Tourism and Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Transport and Power (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0305.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Dublin Harbour) Order, 1979.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The Harbour Rates (Dublin Harbour) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0115.html) ), shall have effect as if the rates specified in Part IV of the Schedule thereto were increased by ten per cent.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of March, 1979.

**PÁDRAIG FAULKNER,**

Minister for Tourism and Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order increases the rates for warehousing services provided for port users at the port of Dublin.
