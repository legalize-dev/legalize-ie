# S.I. No. 656/2016 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th January*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5(1) and 42 (substituted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec10) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) and as amended by section 12(2) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2007)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2016.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2017.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Regulations of 2006” means the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ).

“Regulations of 2013” means the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 420 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0420.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Regulations of 2006 are amended in Schedule 1—

- (i) by substituting for form D302, the form set out in Schedule 1, and

- (ii) by substituting for forms D201, D401, D501 (substituted by Regulation 3 (*o*) of the Regulations of 2013), the forms set out in Schedule 2.

## SCHEDULE 1

D302 —-- Certificate of competency

## SCHEDULE 2

D. 201 — Application for learner permit

D. 401 — Application for driving licence

D. 501 — Medical Report

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

23 December 2016.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Licensing of Drivers regulations by updating forms for a certificate of competency, application for a learner permit, application for a driving licence, and for a medical report.
