# S.I. No. 299/1982 - District Court (Fees) (Private Rented Dwellings) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 299 of 1982.

DISTRICT COURT (FEES) (PRIVATE RENTED DWELLINGS) ORDER, 1982.

I, SEÁN DOHERTY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the District Court (Fees) (Private Rented Dwellings) Order, 1982.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 22nd day of September, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Schedule to the District Court (Fees) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0045.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution of the following for the entry in Part VII:

"Proceedings under the [Housing (Private Rented Dwellings) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) .

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item | Fee | Document to be stamped |
| 1. An application under the above Act (including an appeal under section 20 (5)) | £4.00 | The application or the notice of appeal |
| 2. Order determining an application referred to at 1. | £4.00 | The order. |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of September, 1982.

**SEÁN DOHERTY,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

14th day September, 1982.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, which comes into operation on 22nd September, 1982 prescribes the fees to be charged in the District Court in respect of applications and orders made under the [Housing (Private Rented Dwellings) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) .
