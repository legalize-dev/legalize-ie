# S.I. No. 359/1980 - Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations, 1980

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 359 of 1980.

BROADCASTING (RECEIVING LICENCES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1980.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), as amended by the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1956) and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations, 1980.

- (2) The Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) Regulations, 1961 to 1978, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) Regulations, 1961 to 1980.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of December, 1980.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Principal Regulations" means the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) Regulations, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0279.html) );

"the Regulations of 1973" means the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 274 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0274.html) );

"the Regulations of 1978" means the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 319 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0319.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 7(1) of the Principal Regulations (inserted by Regulation 4(*b*) of the Regulations of 1973), as amended by Regulation 4 of the Regulations of 1978, is hereby amended by the substitution of "forty five pounds" for "thirty eight pounds" and the substitution of "twenty seven pounds" for "twenty three pounds".

###### 5.

5. Regulation 14 of the Principal Regulations (inserted by Regulation 4(*d*) of the Regulations of 1973), as amended by Regulation 5 of the Regulations of 1978, is hereby amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2):

- "(2) The rebate in respect of a surrendered licence mentioned in paragraph (1) of this Regulation shall be calculated at the rate of—

    - (i) in the case of a licence for which a fee of twenty three pounds was paid, one twelfth of twenty three pounds,

    - (ii) in the case of a licence for which a fee of twenty seven pounds was paid, one twelfth of twenty seven pounds,

for each complete month or part (if any) of a month for which the licence would otherwise have continued in force from the date of the surrender thereof".

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations increase the television (monochrome only) licence fee from £23 to £27 and increase the television (colour and monochrome) licence fee from £38 to £45 and also provide for a revised rate of rebate where a television (monochrome only) licence is surrendered in certain circumstances.
