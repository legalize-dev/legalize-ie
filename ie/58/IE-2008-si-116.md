# S.I. No. 116/2008 - European Communities (Civil Aviation Security) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 116 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0116.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (CIVIL AVIATION SECURITY) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd May*, 2008.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 300/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008 [^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Civil Aviation Security) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 29 April 2008.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Civil Aviation Security) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 226 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0226.html) ) are amended:

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1), by substituting for the definitions of “cargo”, “Council Regulation” and “known consignor” the following:

  > “Council Regulation” means Regulation (EC) No. 300/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008[^1];”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 6, by substituting for paragraphs (3) and (4) the following:

  > - “(3) An entity required under the national civil aviation security programme to apply aviation security standards shall establish, implement and maintain a security programme.
  >
  > - (4) A security programme referred to in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) shall comply with the terms of the national civil aviation security programme.
  >
  > - (5) An airport security programme, an air carrier security programme and an entity security programme referred to in this Regulation shall be submitted to the Minister.”,

- (*c*) in Regulation 8(1), by substituting “Article 19” for “Article 9”,

- (*d*) in Regulation 10, by substituting “€5,000” for “€2,000 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 3 months, or to both”, and

- (*e*) by substituting for Regulation 17 the following:

  > “17. A person guilty of an offence under Regulation 11, 12, 13, 14 or 15 is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding €5,000.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 April 2008

**NOEL DEMPSEY.**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Civil Aviation Security) Regulations 2003 and give effect to Regulation (EC) 300/2008.
