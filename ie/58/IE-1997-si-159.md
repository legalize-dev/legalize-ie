# S.I. No. 159/1997 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 159 of 1997.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) ORDER, 1997

I, NIAMH BHREATHNACH, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979) (as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) )), with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The institution specified in the Schedule to this Order is hereby designated as being an institution to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) , applies.

SCHEDULE

Tipperary Rural and Business Development Institute.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of April, 1997.

**NIAMH BHREATHNACH,**

Minister for Education.

The Minister for Finance hereby concurs in the making of this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 16th day of April, 1997.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order designates the Tipperary Rural and Business Development Institute as an institution to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) applies.
