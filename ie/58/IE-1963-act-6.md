# RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (CONFIRMATION OF ORDER) ACT, 1963

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO CONFIRM THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (HAND KNITTING YARNS) ORDER, 1962, MADE BY THE MINISTER FOR INDUSTRY AND COMMERCE UNDER SECTION 9 OF THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES ACT, 1953. [12*th March*, 1963.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Confirmation of Order. 1962, S.I. No. 197, 1953, No. 14.

**1.**—The Restrictive Trade Practices (Hand Knitting Yarns) Order, 1962, made by the Minister for Industry and Commerce under section 9 of the Restrictive Trade Practices Act, 1953, is hereby confirmed.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Restrictive Trade Practices (Confirmation of Order) Act, 1963.
