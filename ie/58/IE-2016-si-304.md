# S.I. No. 304/2016 - European Communities (Import of Personal Consignments of Products of Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th June*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Regulation (EC) No 206/2009 of 5 March 2009[^1] , hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Import of Personal Consignments of Products of Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Import of Personal Consignments of Products of Animal Origin) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 374 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0374.html) ) are amended by the insertion, after Regulation 4, of-

> “5.(1) Where an authorised officer, in exercise of the powers conferred on him or her by [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec38) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) , seizes and detains an animal product, the officer may destroy or dispose of the animal product or cause it to be destroyed or disposed of.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 June 2016.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Regulation gives effect to Commission Regulation (EC) No. 206/2009, in relation to destruction or disposal of animal products.
