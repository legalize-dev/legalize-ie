# S.I. No. 407/2000 - Copyright and Related Rights (Recording For Purposes of Time-Shifting) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec101) (3) and [section 250](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec250) (3) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Copyright and Related Rights (Recording for Purposes of Time-Shifting) Order, 2000 and shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “the Act” means the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).

###### 3.

3. The descriptions of establishments set out in the Schedule to this Order are specified for the purposes of section 101 and section 250 of the Act.

## SCHEDULE

*(Regulation 3)*

Multiple occupancy residential units supplied for the accommodation-

- (a) of students, by educational establishments within the meaning of the Act;

- (b) of patients or inmates, by establishments providing health, nursing, recuperative or hospice care; or

- (c) of inmates at prisons or places of restraint which are under the administration, management or control of the Irish Prisons Service.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

This Order sets out the types of establishments that are prescribed for the purposes of [sections 101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec101) and [250](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec250) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).
