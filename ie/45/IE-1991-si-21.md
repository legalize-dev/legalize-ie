# S.I. No. 21/1991 - Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1988 (Aer Lingus Maintenance and Engineering Services Ltd.) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 21 of 1991.

WORKER PARTICIPATION (STATE ENTERPRISES) ACT, 1988 (AER LINGUS MAINTENANCE AND ENGINEERING SERVICES LTD.) ORDER, 1991.

WHEREAS Aer Lingus Maintenance and Engineering Services Ltd. is a subsidiary of Aer Lingus plc to which [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0013/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1988), applies:

AND WHEREAS Aer Lingus plc and representatives of employees of that company have jointly made a request in writing to the Minister for Tourism and Transport for the making of this Order, and that company has sent a copy of the request to me, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour:

NOW, I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by the said section 9 and after consultation with the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Tourism and Transport, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (Aer Lingus Maintenance and Engineering Services Ltd.) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. Every employee of Aer Lingus Maintenance and Engineering Services Ltd., shall, for the purposes of the Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Acts, 1977 and 1988, be regarded as an employee of Aer Lingus Maintenance and Engineering Services Ltd.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of February, 1991.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to allow employees of Aer Lingus Maintenance and Engineering Services Ltd. to participate in the election of employees representatives to the Board of Aer Lingus plc.
