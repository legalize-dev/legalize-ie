**[S.I. No. 813 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0813.html)**

**DERELICT SITES (URBAN AREAS) REGULATIONS, 2005**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4 and 21 of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

Citation

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2005.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2003 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2005.

Prescription of urban areas

###### 2.

2. The areas in the administrative counties of Cork Kilkenny, Limerick, Meath, Roscommon, Sligo South Dublin, South Tipperary, Waterford and Westmeath as indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

Revocation

###### 3.

3. The Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2004 ( [S.I. No. 659 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0659.html) ) are hereby revoked.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These regulations prescribe areas in the Counties of Cork, Kilkenny, Limerick, Meath Roscommon, Sligo, South Dublin, South Tipperary, Waterford and Westmeath to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , and revoke the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2004 ( [S.I. No. 659 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0659.html) ).
