# S.I. No. 95/1993 - Data Protection Act, 1988 (Section 5 (1) (D)) (Specification) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN, Minister for Justice, being of opinion that the functions described in column (1) of the Schedule to these Regulations, being functions conferred by or under the enactments specified in column (2) of that Schedule, are designed to protect members of the public against the financial loss referred to in subsection (1) (*d*) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1988), hereby, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsections (1) (*d*) and (2) of that section, and after consultation with the other Ministers of the Government who, having regard to their functions, ought, in my opinion, to be consulted, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Section 5 (1) (*d*) (Specification) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 7th day of April, 1993.

###### 3.

3. The functions described in column (1) of the Schedule to these Regulations are, in so far as they are conferred by or under the enactments specified in column (2) thereof and are designed to protect members of the public against the financial loss referred to in section 5 (1) (*d*) of the [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1988), hereby specified for the purposes of the said section 5 (1) (*d*).

###### 4.

4. The [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Section 5 (1) (*d*)) (Specification) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0084.html) ), are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations update the [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Section 5 (1) (*d*)) (Specification) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0084.html) ). They restrict access to personal data kept by persons or bodies with statutory functions designed to prevent financial loss to members of the public through (*a*) dishonesty, incompetence or malpractice in the provision of financial services or the management of companies or (*b*) the conduct of persons who have been adjudicated bankrupt. The restriction applies only where access to the data would be likely to prejudice the proper performance of those functions.
