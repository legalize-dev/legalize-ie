# S.I. No. 154/1982 - Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930 (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 154 of 1982.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE (FRESH MEAT) ACT, 1930 (EXPORTER'S LICENCES) (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 51 of the Schedule (inserted by the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1978)) to the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1930), and the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following regulations with respect to which, pursuant to [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) (9) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (inserted by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ), a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 31st day of May, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of paragraph (*a*) of Rule 1 of the Schedule (inserted by the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , the sum of £1.75 is hereby prescribed as the appropriate sum for a beef exporter's licence.

###### 5.

5. For the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of the said Rule 1, the sum of £0.50 is hereby prescribed as the appropriate sum for a pork exporter's licence.

###### 6.

6. For the purposes of paragraph (*c*) of the said Rule 1, the sum of £0.30 is hereby prescribed as the appropriate sum for a mutton exporter's licence.

###### 7.

7. For the purposes of paragraph (*d*) of the said Rule 1, the sum of £1.75 is hereby prescribed as the appropriate sum for a horse-flesh exporter's licence.

###### 8.

8. The [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 393 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0393.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of May, 1982.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Agriculture.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations increase as from 31st May, 1982 fees payable by fresh meat exporters for veterinary examination of animals in accordance with the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .
