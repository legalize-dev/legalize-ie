# S.I. No. 237/1978 - Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977 (Appointed Day) (Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977 (Appointed Day) (Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta) Order, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The 23rd day of August, 1978, is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), in relation to Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 23rd day of August, 1978.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order prescribes the appointed day for determining election years for the purposes of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta.
