# S.I. No. 31/1986 - Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 31 of 1986.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1986.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows—

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1986.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1985, and this order may be cited as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1986, and shall be construed together as one order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 31st day of January, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) )) to the Aliens Order, 1946 ( [S. R. & O. No. 395 of 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/si/0395.html) ), is hereby amended by the insertion after "India" of "Israel" and by the insertion after "Zambia" of "Zimbabwe".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of January, 1986.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order removes the obligation on citizens of Israel and Zimbabwe to have a valid Irish Visa for admission to the State, with effect from 31st January, 1986.
