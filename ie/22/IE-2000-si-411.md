# S.I. No. 411/2000 - Copyright and Related Rights (Material Open To Public Inspection) (International Organisations) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec74) (7), [240](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec240) (7) and [334](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec334) (7) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Copyright and Related Rights (Material Open to Public Inspection)(International Organisations) Order, 2000, and shall come into operation on the 1^st day of January 2001.

###### 2.

2. Subsections (1) to (5) of [sections 74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec74) , [240](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec240) and [334](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec334) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) , shall apply to any material contained in registers made open to public inspection by the-

- (a) European Patent Office;

- (b) World Intellectual Property Organization; or

- (c) Office for Harmonization in the Internal Market (Trade Marks and Designs).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Hand, this 12th day of December, 2000. |
|  | ***Tom Kitt*** |
|  | Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

This order provides that subsections (1) to (5) of [sections 74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec74) , [240](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec240) and [334](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec334) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) will apply to material made open to public inspection by the European Patent Office, the World Intellectual Property Organization and the Office for Harmonization in the Internal Market (Trade Marks and Designs).
