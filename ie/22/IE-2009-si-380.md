# S.I. No. 380/2009 - European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 380 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0380.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (HYGIENE OF FOODSTUFFS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th September*, 2009.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec2) of the [European Communities Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2007)) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Regulation (EC) No. 852/2004 [^1] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004 on the hygiene of foodstuffs, as affected by the Corrigendum to Regulation (EC) No. 852/2004, [^2] and for the purpose of giving effect to Regulation (EC)No. 1019/2008 [^3] of 17 October 2008 amending Annex II to Regulation (EC)No. 852/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the hygiene of foodstuffs, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) Regulations 2006 and 2009 and shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0369.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1), in the definition of “EC Regulation”, by substituting—

  > “Regulation (EC) No. 852/20041 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004 on the hygiene of foodstuffs, as affected by the Corrigendum to Regulation (EC) No. 852/20042, as amended by Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1019/20082A”

for

> “Regulation (EC) No. 852/20041 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004 on the hygiene of foodstuffs, as affected by the Corrigendum to Regulation (EC) No. 852/20042”,

And by the addition of the following footnote to the definition of “EC Regulation”—

> “2A OJ L 277, 18.10.2008, p. 7”

- (*b*) in Regulation 18, by substituting for subsection (4) the following—

  > “A person who is guilty of an offence under these Regulations is liable:
  >
  > - (*a*) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding €5,000 or at the discretion of the Court to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or both, or
  >
  > - (*b*) on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding €500,000, or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years, or both.”

- (*c*) by substituting for Regulation 19 the following—

  > “Notwithstanding section 57 of the Act of 1998, a summary offence under these Regulations may be prosecuted by:
  >
  > - (*a*) the Authority, or
  >
  > - (*b*) an official agency.”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 September 2009.

**MARY HARNEY,**

Minister for Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1019/2008 of 17 October 2008 amending Annex II to Regulation (EC) No. 852/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the hygiene of foodstuffs and give further effect to Regulation (EC) No. 852/2004 by providing for indictable offences and penalties.

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0369.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.
