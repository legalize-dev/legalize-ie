# S.I. No. 151/1982 - Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 151 of 1982.

HEALTH SERVICES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and by [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

- (2) In these Regulations the "Regulations of 1979" means the Health Services Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 109 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0109.html) ).

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Regulations of 1979 (as amended by article 2 of the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 173 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0173.html) )) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£9,500" for "£8,500" in sub-article (2) (*a*).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1982.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 21st day of May, 1982.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations raise the income limit, above which there is a restriction on the extent to which services are available under [sections 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0052.html#sec52) (1), [56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) (2), [62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0062.html#sec62) and [63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , from £8,500 to £9,500, with effect from the 1st June, 1982.
