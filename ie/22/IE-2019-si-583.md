# S.I. No. 583/2019 - Misuse of Drugs (Prescription and Control of Supply of Cannabis for Medical Use) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th November*, 2019.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0004.html#sec4) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0005.html#sec5) (as amended by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1984) and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec3) of the [Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2016)) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Prescription and Control of Supply of Cannabis for Medical Use) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Misuse of Drugs (Prescription and Control of Supply of Cannabis for Medical Use) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 262 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0262.html) ) are amended by substituting for Schedule 1 the following:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Misuse of Drugs (Prescription and Control of Supply of Cannabis for Medical Use) Regulations 2019 to replace Schedule 1 in order to list certain cannabis products or preparations for medical use as “specified controlled drugs”.

These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse Of Drugs (Prescription And Control Of Supply Of Cannabis For Medical Use) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.
