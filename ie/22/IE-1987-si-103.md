# S.I. No. 103/1987 - Seanad Electoral (Charges of Returning Officers in University Constituencies) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 103 of 1987.

SEANAD ELECTORAL (CHARGES OF RETURNING OFFICERS IN UNIVERSITY CONSTITUENCIES) ORDER, 1987.

I, RAY MAC SHARRY, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0030/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Seanad Electoral (University Members) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1937), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Seanad Electoral Charges of Returning Officers in University Constituencies Order, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The scale of maximum charges for returning officers in university constituencies prescribed by the Seanad Electoral (Charges of Returning Officers in University Constituencies) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 23 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0023.html) ), shall not apply to any election to Seanad Éireann in a university constituency as regards which, pursuant to [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0030/sec0023.html#sec23) (1) of the [Seanad Electoral (University Members) Act 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1937), a declaration is made by the returning officer after the commencement of this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of April, 1987.

**RAY MAC SHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order revokes the Seanad Electoral (Charges of Returning Officers in University Constituencies) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 23 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0023.html) ) which set out a scale of maximum charges for returning officers in university constituencies.
