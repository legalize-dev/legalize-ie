# S.I. No. 119/1985 - Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 119 of 1985.

TRUSTEE SAVINGS BANKS REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0033/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Finance Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , (No. 33 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 21st day of May, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest allowed on moneys standing to the credit of every separate investment account of a trustee savings bank in the name of the Minister for Finance in the Post Office Savings Bank shall, in lieu of the rate specified in the Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 314 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0314.html) ) be 12.90 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 314 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0314.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of April, 1985.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations decrease to 12.90 per cent per annum as from 21st May, 1985 the rate of interest payable by the Minister for Finance on moneys deposited with him by the trustee savings banks in every separate special investment account.
