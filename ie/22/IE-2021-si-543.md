# S.I. No. 543/2021 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) (No. 2) Order 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th October,* 2021.

I, FRANK FEIGHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) ), the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) ) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 599 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0599.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) (No. 2) Order 2021.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998);

“Order of 2021” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2021 ( [S.I. No. 152 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0152.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*center note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

This Order amends the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) .

This Order may be cited as the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) (No. 2) Order 2021.

For ease of reference, a list of all food legislation and official agencies, as contained in the First and Second Schedules to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , as well as a list of Regulations made under the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) and deemed to be food legislation for the purposes of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , and Regulations made under [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0054.html#sec54) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , as substituted by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec25) of the [Irish Medicines Board (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , can be found on the website www.fsai.ie (https://www.fsai.ie/legislation/fsai_act_related.html).
