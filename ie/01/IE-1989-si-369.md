# S.I. No. 369/1989 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) (No. 2) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 369 of 1989.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) (No. 2) ORDER, 1989.

I, MARY O'ROURKE, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979) (as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0031.html) )), with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance, and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards, hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) (No. 2) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The institutions specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as being institutions to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), applies.

Schedule

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| (i) St. Peter's College, Wexford. | (ii) Abbey School of Theatre, National Theatre Society Limited, Abbey Street, Dublin 1. |
| (iii) Milltown Institute of Theology and Philosophy, Milltown Park, Dublin 6. |  |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of November, 1989.

**MARY O'ROURKE,**

Minister for Education.
