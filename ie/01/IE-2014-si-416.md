# S.I. No. 416/2014 - European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance Groups and Financial Conglomerates) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th September*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Articles 1 and 2 of Directive 2011/89/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 November 2011[^1] and Articles 38, 63(1) and 72 of Directive 2013/36/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance Groups and Financial Conglomerates)(Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. (1) The European Communities (Insurance and Reinsurance Groups Supplementary Supervision) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0366.html) ) are amended as set out in Schedule 1.

- (2) The European Communities (Financial Conglomerates) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 727 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0727.html) ) are amended as set out in Schedule 2.

- (3) The European Communities (Capital Requirements) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0158.html) ) are amended as set out in Schedule 3.

- (4) The [Central Bank Act 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1971) is amended as set out in Schedule 4.

- (5) The [Building Societies Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1989) is amended as set out in Schedule 5.
