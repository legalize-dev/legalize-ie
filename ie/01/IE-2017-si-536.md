# S.I. No. 536/2017 - Adoption Act 2010 (Section 85) (Fees) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st December*, 2017.

I, KATHERINE ZAPPONE, Minister for Children and Youth Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 150](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec150) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) and the Office of the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 218 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0218.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Section 85) (Fees) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 4 December 2017.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “Principal Act” means the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010).

###### 4.

4. (1) The amount of €20 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under section 85(2)(*b*) and (3)(*b*) of the Principal Act for each certified copy or certified extract of an entry in the Adopted Children Register given by An tArd-Chláratheoir pursuant to either of those provisions.

- (2) The amount of €4 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under section 85(2)(*b*) and (3)(*b*) of the Principal Act for each copy of an entry in the Adopted Children Register given by an tArd-Chláraitheoir pursuant to either of those provisions.

###### 5.

5. (1) The amount prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under section 85(2)(*a*) of the Principal Act for each search of the index to the Adopted Children Register carried out by an tArd-Chláraitheoir pursuant to that provision is—

- (a) where the period of time between the search dates specified for the search is 5 years or less, €2, and

- (b) where the period of time between the search dates specified for the search is greater than 5 years and the time taken to conduct the search does not exceed 7 consecutive hours, €20.

- (2) The amount prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under section 85(3)(*a*) of the Principal Act for each search of the Adoption Children Register and the index to that Register carried out by an tArd-Chláraitheoir pursuant to that provision is—

    - (a) where the period of time between the search dates specified for the search is 5 years or less, €2, and

    - (b) where the period of time between the search dates specified for the search is greater than 5 years and the time taken to conduct the search does not exceed 7 consecutive hours, €20.

###### 6.

6. The Adoption Act 2010 (Section 85) (Fees) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 518 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0518.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 November 2017.

**KATHERINE ZAPPONE,**

Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations set the fees required to be paid for—

- • obtaining copies or extracts of entries in the Adopted Children Register, and

- • searching the index of the Adopted Children Register.

These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Section 85) (Fees) Regulations 2017 and come into operation on 4 December 2017.
