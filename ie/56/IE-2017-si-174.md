# S.I. No. 174/2017 - Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th May*, 2017.

I, CATHERINE BYRNE, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2016 ( [S.I. No. 511 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0511.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Designation) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 4 May 2017.

###### 3.

3. In this Order “Principal Act” means the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977).

###### 4.

4. The drugs specified in Schedule 1 are designated as drugs to which subsection (1) of section 13 of the Principal Act applies.

###### 5.

5. The manufacture, production, preparation, sale, supply, distribution and possession of the drugs specified in Schedule 1 is unlawful except for the purposes specified in Schedule 2.

###### 6.

6. It is unlawful for any person who is either a practitioner or a pharmacist to have in his possession the drugs specified in Schedule 1, or to do in relation to any such drugs any of the things mentioned in subsection (2) of section 5 (as amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec3) of the [Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2016)) of the Principal Act except under a licence or other authority issued by the Minister.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Sections 4 (2) and 5 (2) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) as amended, require provision to be made in Regulations to allow the use for medical purposes of the drugs specified in the Schedule to the Act.

That obligation is removed, however, in the case of any such drug which is designated by order under section 13 of the Act as a drug to which that section is to apply. This Order designates for that purpose the drugs specified in the Schedule to the Order since it would not be in the public interest that they should be so available.
