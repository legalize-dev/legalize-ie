# S.I. No. 359/1984 - Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Reduced Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 359 of 1984.

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS AND MARRIAGES (REDUCED FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1984

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Vital Statistics and Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1952), by [section 114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0114.html#sec114) (2) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1955), by [section 150](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0007/sec0150.html#sec150) (2) of the [Mines and Quarries Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1965), by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1977) and by [section 303](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0303.html#sec303) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the Minister for Industry, Trade, Commerce and Tourism, the Minister for Education, the Minister for Social Welfare and the Minister for Labour hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Reduced Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 7th day of January, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Reduced Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 148 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0148.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Reduced Fees) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 46 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0046.html) ) as amended by the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Reduced Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 are hereby amended by the substitution in Articles 3(*a*) and 5 of "sixty pence" for "fifty pence".

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to fix at 60p the fee payable with effect from 7th January, 1985 for birth, death and marriage certificates issued at reduced rates for the purposes of certain enactments.
