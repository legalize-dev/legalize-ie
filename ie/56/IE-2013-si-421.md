# S.I. No. 421/2013 - Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th November*, 2013.

I, PAT RABBITTE, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec39) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999)(as adapted by the Communications, Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0706.html) )), following consultation with the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government and having, in accordance with section 39(10) of that Act, given a draft of the following order to the persons upon which the obligations concerned are to be imposed, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, “Order of 2012” means the Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2012 ( [S.I. No. 438 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0438.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0217.html) ) is amended—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order modifies and updates elements of Statutory Instrument 217 of 2002, the principal order, dealing with the public services obligation on electricity consumers. This Order specifically updates the principal order in respect of renewable energy projects in the Alternative Energy Requirement (AER) and Renewable Energy Feed in Tariff (REFIT) schemes. The Order also extends the end dates for the REFIT schemes.
