# S.I. No. 340/1988 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 340 of 1988.

DIPLOMATIC AND CONSULAR FEES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939), as adapted by the External Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0158.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

- (2) The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1982 to 1984, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1982 to 1988.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 344 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0344.html) ), as amended by the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0242.html) ), are hereby amended:

( *a* ) in Regulation 7—

- (i) by the substitution of "processing" for "granting" in both places where it occurs, and

- (ii) by the substitution of "for citizens" for "to citizens".

( *b* ) in the Second Schedule by the substitution of the following for Parts I and II:

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for changes in the fees payable in regard to travel documents and documents of identity and for the amendment of Regulation 7 in the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 1982 ( [S.I. No. 344 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0344.html) ) and for the amendment of the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0242.html) ).
