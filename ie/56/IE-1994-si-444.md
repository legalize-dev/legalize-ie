# S.I. No. 444/1994 - Licensing of Sea Fishing Boats (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 444 of 1994.

LICENSING OF SEA FISHING BOATS (EXEMPTION) (REVOCATION) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, HUGH COVENEY, Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) (*b*) of Section 222B (inserted by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1983)) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adopted by Tourism, Fisheries and Forestry (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0082.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing of Sea Fishing Boats (Exemption) (Revocation) Regulations, 1994.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1995.

###### 2.

2. The Licensing of Sea-Fishing Boats (Exemption) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0245.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of December, 1994.

**HUGH COVENEY,**

Minister for the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to revoke the licensing of Sea Fishing Boats (Exemption) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0245.html) ) whereby vessels of 65 feet and under were exempted from the provisions of Section 222B of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) which requires that sea fishing boats shall not fish except under a sea fishing boat licence granted in respect of the boat by the Minister.
