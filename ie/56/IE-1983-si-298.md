# S.I. No. 298/1983 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 298 of 1983.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF KILDARE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1983.

In exercise of the powers vested in the Minister for the Environment by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment (Delegation of Functions) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 272 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0272.html) ), I, Ruairí Quinn, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The First Schedule to the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0277.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition of the following matter at reference 3:—

> - "(n) The Naas By-Pass.".

Dated this 18th day of October, 1983.

RUAIRÍ QUINN,

Minister of State at the Department of the Environment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) Regulations, 1983 by declaring that the Naas By-Pass is not a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
