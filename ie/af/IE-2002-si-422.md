# S.I. No. 422/2002 - National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000 (Approval) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec1) (5) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec8) (1) of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000 (Approval) Order, 2002.

###### 2.

2. The period for participants to apply for the grant of a certificate of approval under section 8 of the Act is extended until 31 December 2003.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the period for participants to apply for approval under the National Beef Assurance Scheme.
