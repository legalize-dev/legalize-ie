> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 206 of 1989.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY BOROUGH OF DUBLIN AND COUNTY OF DUBLIN) REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the power conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1989.

2 Commencement

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 28th day of August, 1989.

3 Interpretation

###### 3.

**3.** In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires "the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"built-up area speed limit" and "special speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road;

"National Road" means a road declared to be a National Road in the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Declaration of National Roads) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0164.html) ) and any reference to the number of such road shall be a reference to the number of such road as shown in column 1 of the First Schedule or column 1 of the Second Schedule to the aforementioned Order;

"perimeter road" means a road on and along the roadway of which there runs the boundary of a county borough, an urban district or a town and shall be considered as being within such boundary for the purposes of these Regulations; "road" means a public road.

4 Built-up area speed limit

###### 4.

**4.** (1) Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

5 Special speed limits

###### 5.

**5.** (1) Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) Fifty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Fourth Schedule to these Regulations.

6 Exceptions

###### 6.

**6.** There shall be excepted from the special speed limits prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:—

- (*a*) ambulances,

- (*b*) fire brigade vehicles, and

- (*c*) vehicles used by members of the Garda Siochana in the performance of their duties as such members.

7 Revocations

###### 7.

**7.** The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0358.html) ), the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 328 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0328.html) ), the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 169 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0169.html) ), the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0330.html) ) and the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0217.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations revoke and replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations 1977 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0358.html) ) (as amended) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in Dublin City and County and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area speed limit prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

The Regulations declare that the public roads mentioned in the Second Schedule which would otherwise be outside the scope of the built-up area speed limit of 30 mph shall be subject to that limit.

The Regulations prescribe a speed limit of 40 mph in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Third Schedule and a speed limit of 50 mph in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Fourth Schedule.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and Garda Siochana vehicles while being used in the course of duty are exempted from all the speed limits prescribed by the Regulations.
