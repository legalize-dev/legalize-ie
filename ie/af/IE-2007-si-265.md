# S.I. No. 265/2007 - Prisons Act 1970 (Section 7) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Prisons Act 1970 (Section 7) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The period of 24 months beginning on 28 June 2007 is specified for the purposes of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970).
