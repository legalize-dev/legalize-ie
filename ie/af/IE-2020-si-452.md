# S.I. No. 452/2020 - Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd October,* 2020.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 1 November 2020.

###### 2.

2. The name of the Department of State, the present name of which is, in the Irish language, an Roinn Dlí agus Cirt agus Comhionannais and, in the English language, the Department of Justice and Equality, is altered, in the Irish language, to that of an Roinn Dlí agus Cirt and, in the English language, to that of the Department of Justice.

###### 3.

3. The title of the Minister having charge of the Department of State, whose present title is, in the Irish language, an tAire Dlí agus Cirt agus Comhionannais and, in the English language, the Minister for Justice and Equality, is altered, in the Irish language, to that of an tAire Dlí agus Cirt and, in the English language, to that of the Minister for Justice.

###### 4.

4. In any enactment or any instrument made under an enactment -

- (*a*) references to the Department of Justice and Equality shall be construed as references to the Department of Justice;

- (*b*) references to the Minister for Justice and Equality shall be construed as references to the Minister for Justice.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

20 October, 2020.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Taoiseach.
