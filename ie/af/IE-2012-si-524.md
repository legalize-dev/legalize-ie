# S.I. No. 524/2012 - Totalisator (Horse Racing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st December*, 2012.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Totalisator Act 1929](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1929), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as Totalisator (Horse Racing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 18 December 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Totalisator (Horse Racing) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 72 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0072.html) ), are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 3(1), in the definition of “multiple pool” by substituting for paragraph (*a*) the following:

  > - “(*a*) in which 2 or more races are involved,”,

  and

- (*b*) in Regulation 9(1) and (2), by substituting “70 percent” for “75 percent”, where it occurs.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 December 2012.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
