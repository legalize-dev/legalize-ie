# S.I. No. 453/2016 - Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010 (Competent Authority and State Competent Authority) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd August*, 2016.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec3) (1), [60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec60) (3) and [62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec62) (2) of the [Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2010) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), being satisfied as to the matters referred to in sections 60(3) and 62(2) of that Act and having consulted with the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010 (Competent Authority and State Competent Authority) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 September 2016.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations, “Act of 2010” means the [Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2010).

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of section 60(1) of the Act of 2010, the Property Services Regulatory Authority is prescribed as the competent authority for property service providers.

###### 5.

5. For the purposes of section 62(1)(*c*) of the Act of 2010, the Property Services Regulatory Authority, prescribed under Regulation 4 as the competent authority for property service providers, is prescribed as a State competent authority.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 August 2016.

**FRANCES FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
