# S.I. No. 214/1999 - Electricity Regulation Act, 1999 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MARY O'ROURKE, T.D., Minister for Public Enterprise, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec8) (1) of the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electricity Regulation Act, 1999 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The 14th day of July, 1999, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of July, 1999. |
|  | MARY O'ROURKE, T.D., |
|  | Minister for Public Enterprise. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order appoints 14 July, 1999, to be the establishment day for the purposes of [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec8) of the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) . On that day there shall stand established the Commission for Electricity Regulation, to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) .
