> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[SI No.149 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0149.html)**

**District Court (Order 24) Rules 2006**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec24) of the [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

1 These rules may be cited as the District Court (Order 24) Rules 2006.

2 These rules shall come into operation on the 1^st day of May 2006 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

3 Order 24 of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the words “fourteen days” for the words “ten days” where they appear in rule 11 thereof.

Given this 20^th day of February 2006

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This does not form part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)**

**These Rules amend Order 24 rule 11 to provide fourteen days for the transmission of documents to the County Registrar when an accused has been sent forward for trial.**
