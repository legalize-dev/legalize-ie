# S.I. No. 321/1986 - Farm Tax (Adjusted Acreage) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 321 of 1986.

FARM TAX (ADJUSTED ACREAGE) REGULATIONS, 1986.

WHEREAS the compilation of the classification lists under the [Farm Tax Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1985), has not been completed:

NOW, I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (3) of the said [Farm Tax Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) , hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Farm Tax (Adjusted Acreage) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. An area of 150 adjusted acres is hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 3 and subsection (3) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Farm Tax Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1985), for the year 1986.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of September, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations provide that only holdings of 150 adjusted acres and over will be classified by the Farm Tax Office for the purposes of assessment for Farm Tax in 1986.
