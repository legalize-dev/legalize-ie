# S.I. No. 585/2021 - Health Act 1947 (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (Extension of various periods of operation) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th November,* 2021.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and 31A (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec10) of the [Health (Preservation and Protection and other Emergency Measures in the Public Interest) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2020)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and -

- (a) having regard to the immediate, exceptional and manifest risk posed to human life and public health by the spread of Covid-19 and to the matters specified in subsection (2) of section 31A, and

- (b) having consulted with the Minister for Finance, the Minister for Foreign Affairs, the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, the Minister for Justice, the Minister for Transport, the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment and the Minister for Tourism, Culture, Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media,

hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1947 (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (Extension of various periods of operation) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 10th day of November 2021.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 1 of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A –Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 2) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0217.html) ) is amended, in paragraph (2), by the substitution of “9th day of January 2022” for “9th day of November 2021”.

No. 244 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0244.html) ) Regulations 2020 is amended by the substitution of “9th day of February 2022” for “9th day of November 2021”.

No. 296 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0296.html) ) is amended by the substitution of “9th day of February 2022” for “9th day of November 2021”.

No. 569 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0569.html) ) is amended, in paragraph (2), by the substitution of “9th day of February 2022” for “9th day of November 2021”.

###### 6.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

**Health Act 1947 (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (Extension of various periods of operation) Regulations 2021**

These regulations extend S.I 217 of 2021 (Section 31A-Temporary restrictions) to the 9^th of January, 2022. They also extend the face coverings’ Regulations S.I 244 of 2020, S.I 296 of 2020, S.I 571 of 2020, and S.I. 569 of 2020 to the 9^th February, 2022.
