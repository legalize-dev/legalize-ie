# S.I. No. 238/1989 - Cork District Milk Board (Election Day, 1989) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 238 of 1989.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1989) ORDER, 1989.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936) (as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0097.html) )), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Election Day, 1989) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 27th day of November, 1989 is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Cork District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 3rd day of October, 1989.

- (Signed) MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,

Minister for Agriculture and Food

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1989 on which ordinary members of the Cork District Milk Board shall be elected.
