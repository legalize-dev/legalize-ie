# S.I. No. 298/2014 - National Oversight and Audit Commission (Number of Members) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th July*, 2014.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in the exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 126I(7) (as inserted by [section 61](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec61) of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014)) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001), hereby order as follows:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Oversight and Audit Commission (Number of Members) Order 2014.

*Number of Members.*

###### 2.

2. The number of members of the National Oversight and Audit Commission (established by the National Oversight and Audit Commission (Establishment) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 297 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0297.html) )), shall be increased to 12 members for the period of 2 years from the date of this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government,

1 July 2014.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This order increases the number of members of the National Oversight and Audit Commission (established by the National Oversight and Audit Commission (Establishment) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 297 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0297.html) )) to 12 members for a period of two years from 1 July 2014.
