# S.I. No. 28/1975 - Dublin Parking Temporary Rules, 1975

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 28 of 1975.

DUBLIN PARKING TEMPORARY RULES, 1975.

I, PATRICK MALONE, Commissioner of the Garda Síochána, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), and with the consent of the Minister for Local Government, hereby make the following temporary rules in respect of the area comprising the county borough of Dublin, the borough of Dun Laoghaire, the county of Dublin and the town of Balbriggan:

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Dublin Parking Temporary Rules, 1975.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 28th day of February, 1975.

###### 3.

3. No person shall park a vehicle (other than a street service vehicle) at a place which is an appointed stand for street service vehicles.

###### 4.

4. Where on a public road parking is permitted or restricted at the edge of the roadway by means of a traffic sign, a vehicle may be parked only if it is parked within eighteen inches of the edge.

###### 5.

5. (1) Where a roadway marking authorised by Article 12 (1) (i) (inserted by the Regulations of 1969) of the Regulations of 1962 is not accompanied by an information plate, the prohibition or restriction on the parking of vehicles imposed by the roadway marking shall apply during business hours.

- (2) In this rule—

"business hours" means the period from 8.00 a.m. to 6.30 p.m. on a weekday which is not a Saturday or a holiday;

"holiday" means any day that is a public holiday for the purposes of the [Holidays (Employees) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1973);

"information plate" means a plate of the type authorised by and described in Part IX (inserted by the Regulations of 1969) of the First Schedule to the Regulations of 1962;

"the Regulations of 1962" means the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 171 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0171.html) );

"the Regulations of 1969" means the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0217.html) ).

GIVEN under my hand this 3rd day of January, 1975.

PATRICK MALONE,

Commissioner of the Garda Síochána.

The Minister for Local Government hereby consents to the making of the foregoing temporary rules.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government

this 4th day of February, 1975.

**JAMES TULLY,**

Minister for Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

1. Rule 3 provides that a vehicle (other than a small street service vehicle) shall not be parked at a place which is an appointed stand.

2. Rule 4 provides that a vehicle parked on a roadway shall not be parked more than eighteen inches from the edge of such roadway.

3. Rule 5 provides that where a roadway marking (single yellow line) is not accompanied by an information plate the prohibition on parking shall apply during business hours.
