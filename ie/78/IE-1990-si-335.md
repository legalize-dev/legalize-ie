# S.I. No. 335/1990 - Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 335 of 1990.

HEALTH (IN-PATIENT CHARGES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 of the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0116.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£12.50" for "£10" and by the substitution of "£125" for "£100" in sub-article (2).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1991.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health

this 21st day of December, 1990.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

this 21st day of December, 1990.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 by raising the daily charge for in-patient services from £10 to £12.50 and by raising the maximum amount payable in any period of twelve consecutive months from £100 to £125.
