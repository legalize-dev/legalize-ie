# S.I. No. 211/1990 - European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 211 of 1990.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (NON-LIFE INSURANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

I, DESMOND O' MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive No. 73 /239/EEC of 24 July 1973*, hereby make the following Regulations.

*O.J. No. L228, 16.8.1973, pp.3-19

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990, and shall come into operation on the 2nd day of August, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) Regulations, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0115.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution for subarticles (*a*) and (*b*) of Article 34 (inserted by the European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 297 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0297.html) )) of the following:

"( *a* ) in the case where the application is made by an undertaking mentioned in Article 9 (*a*) of these Regulations, a fee of £2,000, and

( *b* ) in the case where the application is made by an undertaking mentioned in subarticles (*b*) or (*c*) of Article 9, or in Article 25 of these Regulations, a fee of £1,000.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of August, 1990.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) Regulations, 1976 by increasing the fees payable by insurance undertakings applying for an authorisation and for an extension to transact non-life insurance business in the State.
