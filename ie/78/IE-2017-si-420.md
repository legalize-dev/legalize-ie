# S.I. No. 420/2017 - Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd October*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 101B (inserted by [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec32) of the [Vehicle Clamping Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2015)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )) after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality and the National Transport Authority, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) Regulations 2017.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 October 2017.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 1961” means the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961).

###### 3.

3. The prescribed form of notice for the purpose of section 101B(7)(*b*) of the Act of 1961 is set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The prescribed charge for the purpose of section 101B(7)(*a*) of the Act of 1961 is €80.

###### 5.

5. Payment of the prescribed charge shall be made to a clamping officer as specified in the notice.

###### 6.

6. Payment of the prescribed charge shall be made by payment card or cash.

###### 7.

7. The following are revoked:

- (*a*) Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 247 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0247.html) );

- (*b*) Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 406 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0406.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations are made under section 5 and section 101B (as inserted by [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec32) of the [Vehicle Clamping Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) ) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) and their purpose is to prescribe the amount of the charge to be paid for the removal of an immobilisation device, the form of the notice affixed to a clamped vehicle and the manner in which and the person to whom such charge shall be paid when a vehicle is clamped on a public road.
