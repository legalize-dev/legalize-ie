# S.I. No. 458/2016 - Companies Act 2014 (Section 897) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th August*, 2016.

I, MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 897](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec897) (1) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) , and following consultation with the Registrar of Companies, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Section 897) Order 2016.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 1 June 2017.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act of 2014” means the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014);

“Regulations of 2015” means the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0147.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The sole means to be used to deliver the following documents to the Registrar shall be those provided for under the Electronic Commerce Act (No. 27 of 2000) effected in a manner which complies with any requirements of the Registrar of the kind referred to in sections 12(2)(*b*) and 13(2)(*a*) of that Act:

- (*a*) Form B1 which is prescribed under Regulation 25 of the Regulations of 2015 for the purposes of section 343(4) of the Act of 2014,

- (*b*) Form B2 which is prescribed under Regulation 7 of the Regulations of 2015 for the purposes of section 50(3) of the Act of 2014,

- (*c*) Form B10 which is prescribed under Regulation 18 of the Regulations of 2015 for the purposes of section 149(8) of the Act of 2014

- (*d*) Form B73 which is prescribed under Regulation 26 of the Regulation of 2015 for the purposes of section 346(2)(*b*) of the Act of 2014.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 August 2016.

**MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this Order is to provide that certain Forms, prescribed under the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0147.html) ), may be delivered to the Registrar of Companies only by the means provided for under the [Electronic Commerce Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) , effected in a manner which complies with any requirements of the Registrar of the kind referred to in sections 12 (2) (*b*) and 13 (2) (*a*) of that Act.
