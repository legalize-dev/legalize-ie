# S.I. No. 207/1990 - Wildlife (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 207 of 1990.

WILDLIFE (TRANSFER OF DEPARTMENTAL ADMINISTRATION AND MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1990.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Wildlife (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1990.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 2nd day of August, 1990.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "functions" includes powers and duties.

###### 3.

3. The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 4 of this Order are hereby transferred to the Department of Finance.

###### 4.

4. There are hereby transferred to the Minister for Finance the functions vested in the Minister for the Marine by the European Communities ( [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 254 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0254.html) ).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 2nd day of August, 1990.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order transfers to the Minister for Finance with effect from the date specified certain statutory functions under the European Communities ( [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 254 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0254.html) ).
