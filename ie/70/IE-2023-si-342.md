# S.I. No. 342/2023 - Maternity Protection (Protection of Mothers Who Are Breastfeeding) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th July,* 2023.

I, RODERIC O’GORMAN, Minister for Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 15B (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec9) of the [Maternity Protection (Amendment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2004)) of the [Maternity Protection Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1994) and the Disability, Equality, Human Rights, Integration and Reception (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 436 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0436.html) ) (as adapted by the Children and Youth Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0437.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Maternity Protection (Protection of Mothers who are Breastfeeding) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 3rd day of July 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Maternity Protection (Protection of Mothers who are Breastfeeding) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 654 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0654.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “employee who is breastfeeding”:

> “ ‘employee who is breastfeeding’ has the same meaning as it has in section 2(1) (as amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec34) (a) of the [Work Life Balance and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2023)) of the Principal Act;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 June, 2023.

**RODERIC O’GORMAN,**

Minister for Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth.
