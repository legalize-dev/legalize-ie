# S.I. No. 58/2008 - Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 58 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0058.html)

REFERENDUM COMMISSION (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th March*, 2008.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsections (1) and (8) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1998), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. A Referendum Commission is hereby established to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , as amended by the [Referendum Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0053/print.html) , in relation to the proposal for an amendment of the Constitution contained in the Twenty-Eighth Amendment of the Constitution Bill 2008.

###### 3.

3. The 6th day of March 2008 is hereby appointed to be the day for the purposes of section 2(5) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the chairperson of the Referendum Commission established by Article 2 of this order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 March 2008

**JOHN GORMLEY**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order establishes a Referendum Commission for the purposes of the Referendum Acts 1998 and 2001 in connection with the Twenty-Eighth Amendment of the Constitution Bill 2008 and appoints the 6th March 2008 as the day on which the position of chairperson of the Commission takes effect.
