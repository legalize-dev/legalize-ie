# S.I. No. 370/1994 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) (Waterford) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 370 of 1994.

URBAN RENEWAL ACT, 1986 (DESIGNATED AREAS) (WATERFORD) ORDER, 1994.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) (Waterford) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. Each of the areas enclosed by red lines and shaded pink on the map attached hereto is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. Each of the streets or part of those streets described in the Schedule to this Order is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

SCHEDULE

The following streets or parts of streets in the county borough of Waterford which are not part of any area declared to be a designated area under article 2 of this Order.

- (i) Great Georges Street,

- (ii) Coal Quay/Custom House Quay/Parade (from its junction with Barronstrand Street to its junction with Greyfriar's),

- (iii) Broad Street from its junction with Blackfriars Street to its junction with Michael Street,

- (iv) Michael Street.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, this

30th day of November, 1994.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 30th

day of November, 1994.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the designation of areas in Waterford under the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

**WATERFORD**
