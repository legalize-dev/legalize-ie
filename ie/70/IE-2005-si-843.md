**MARINE (DELEGATION OF MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) (No. 5) ORDER 2005**

**MARINE (DELEGATION OF MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) (No. 5) ORDER 2005**

The Government, on the request of the Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions (No. 5) Order 2005.

- (2) This order comes into operation on 1 January 2006.

###### 2.

2. The powers and duties of the Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources conferred on him-

- (a) by or under the Acts specified in Part 1,

- (b) by or under the provisions of the Acts specified in column (3) opposite mention of the Act concerned in column (2), of Part 2 and

- (c) under the Regulations specified in Part 3,

of Schedule 1 to this Order, are delegated to Pat the Cope Gallagher, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 3.

3. The Orders listed in Schedule 2 to this Order are revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The Government made this Order, operative on and from 1 January 2006, to replace the 6 Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Orders already made by the Government in favour of Pat the Cope Gallagher TD Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources (those Orders are listed in Schedule 2 to this Order and revoked by the making of this Order) by deleting the functions transferred by Government Order to the Minister for Transport with effect on and from 1 January 2006, and to delegate to Mr. Gallagher further functions, under 4 additional European Communities Regulations listed in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to this Order (viz. S.I. No. 170 and 253 of 1996, No. 191 of 1997 and No. 26 of 1999).
