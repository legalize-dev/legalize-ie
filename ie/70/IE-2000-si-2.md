# S.I. No. 2/2000 - National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) (No. 2) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The functions of the Minister for Finance under subsections (2), (4) and (6) of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec1) of the [Temporary Holding Fund for Superannuation Liabilities Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 1999), are hereby delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government this 22nd day of December, 1999. |
|  | BERTIE AHERN, |
|  | Taoiseach. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

As provided for in [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) and subsections 2, 4 and 6 of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec1) of the [Temporary Holding Fund for Superannuation Liabilities Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) this Order delegates to the National Treasury Management Agency certain statutory functions of the Minister for Finance with regard to the Temporary Holding Fund for Superannuation Liabilities.
