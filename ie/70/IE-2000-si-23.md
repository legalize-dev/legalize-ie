# S.I. No. 23/2000 - The Fire Services Council (Establishment) Order, 1983 (Amendment) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) (8) of the [Local Government Services (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) as applied by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/sec0016.html#sec16) (5) of the [Fire Services Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. Article 4(2) of the Fire Services Council (Establishment) Order, 1983 is hereby amended by the substitution of “five years” for “two years”.

###### 2.

2. This Order may be cited as the Fire Services Council (Establishment) Order, 1983 (Amendment) Order, 2000.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order extends the maximum term of office of members of the Fire Services Council from two years to five years.
