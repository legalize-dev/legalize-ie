# S.I. No. 620/2007 - Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 620 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0620.html)

INDUSTRIAL DESIGNS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th September*, 2007.

I, MICHAEL AHERN, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec85) of the [Industrial Designs Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2001) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 4) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 562 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0562.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2007.

*Amendment of Regulation 8 of Industrial Designs Regulations 2002.*

###### 2.

2. Regulation 8(1) of the Industrial Designs Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0280.html) ) is amended by substituting “in the European Union” for “in the State”.

GIVEN under my hand,

5 September 2007

MICHAEL AHERN,

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The Regulations make a slight change to the Industrial Design Regulations 2002 by allowing a person, involved in any proceeding before the Controller of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks or a registered proprietor of a design, to have an address for service in the European Union as opposed to in the State.
