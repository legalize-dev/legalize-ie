**[S.I. No. 226 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0226.html)**

**NATIONAL CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS ACT 1997 (AMENDMENT OF FIRST SCHEDULE) ORDER 2005**

I, John O'Donoghue T.D., Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 16(3) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) and the Arts and Culture (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0302.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Sport and Recreation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 309 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0309.html) ), with the consent of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with the Commissioners of Public Works in Ireland, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2005.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 3^rd May 2005.

###### 2.

2. The First Schedule to the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997 is amended by -

- (a) the addition, in column (2) opposite the mention of Board of the National Library of Ireland, of the following premises:

  > “The premises known as the Heraldic Museum Kildare Street, Dublin.
  >
  > The premises known as the National Photographic Archive, Meeting House Square, Temple Bar Dublin.
  >
  > The premises formerly known as the National College of Art and Design building, Leinster Lane Dublin.
  >
  > The premises at No. 4 Kildare Street, Dublin.
  >
  > The premises at No. 5 Kildare Street, Dublin.
  >
  > The premises known as the ‘Racquet Court’ Leinster Lane, Dublin.
  >
  > The premises known as ‘Ryans Garage’, Leinster Lane, Dublin.
  >
  > Those parts of the premises known as OPW Drainage Section, Cootehill Road, Monaghan, Co. Monaghan, occupied by the National Library of Ireland.
  >
  > Those parts of the premises known as Unit 39D Park West Industrial Estate, Clondalkin, Dublin occupied by the National Library of Ireland.
  >
  > Those parts of the premises known as Trinity College Sports Ground, Santry Book Repository Santry Avenue, Dublin, occupied by the National Library of Ireland.”,

  and

- (b) the addition, in column (2) opposite the mention of Board of the National Museum of Ireland, of the following premises:

  > “The premises known as the Museum of Decorative Arts and History, Collins Barracks, Benburb Street Dublin.
  >
  > The premises known as the Museum of Country Life, Turlough Park, Castlebar, Co. Mayo.
  >
  > The premises known as Folklife and Antiquities Division Storage, St. Conleth’s, Daingean, Co. Offaly.
  >
  > The premises known as Natural History Division Storage, Beggars Bush, Ballsbridge, Dublin 4.
  >
  > Those parts of the premises known as Art and Industry Division Storage, Jamestown Road Inchicore, Dublin, occupied by the National Museum of Ireland.
  >
  > Those parts of the premises known as Irish Antiquities Division Storage and Workshop Conservation Facility, Lanesboro, Co. Roscommon occupied by the National Museum of Ireland.”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This order appoints the day of the 3^rd of May 2005 as the day on which amendments to the *First Schedule* to the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997) which set out the authorised repositories of Bord Leabharlann Náisiúnta na hÉireann and Bord Ard-Mhúsaem na hÉireann will come into effect.
