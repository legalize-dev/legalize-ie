# Appropriation Act 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE TO THE PROPER SUPPLY SERVICES AND PURPOSES SUMS GRANTED BY THE CENTRAL FUND (PERMANENT PROVISIONS) ACT 1965, TO MAKE PROVISION IN RELATION TO DEFERRED SURRENDER TO THE CENTRAL FUND OF CERTAIN UNDISCHARGED APPROPRIATIONS BY REFERENCE TO THE CAPITAL SUPPLY SERVICES AND PURPOSES AS PROVIDED FOR BY SECTION 91 OF THE FINANCE ACT 2004 AND TO MAKE PROVISION IN RELATION TO THE FINANCIAL RESOLUTIONS PASSED BY DÁIL ÉIREANN ON 9 DECEMBER 2009.

[20*th December*, 2009]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1. Appropriation of sums voted for supply services, totalling €47,868,895,000.

**1**.— (1) The sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act 1965 out of the Central Fund towards making good the supply granted, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of forty seven thousand, eight hundred and sixty-eight million, eight hundred and ninety-five thousand euro for the service of the year ending on 31 December 2009 are appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in *Schedule 1*.

- (2) In addition to the grants out of the Central Fund referred to in *subsection (1)*, there may be applied out of any money directed under section 2 of the Public Accounts and Charges Act 1891 to be applied as appropriations-in-aid of the grants for the services and purposes specified in *Schedule 1*, the sums respectively set out in the last column of that Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the sum of five thousand, nine hundred and seventy-eight million, onehundred and twelve thousand euro.

###### 2. Deferred surrender relating to capital supply services, totalling €125,717,000.

**2**.— For the purposes of section 91 of the Finance Act 2004, in respect of certain undischarged appropriations by reference to the capital supply services and purposes included in a vote referred to in *Schedule 1*, there is set out in *Schedule 2* the sums deferred into the year ending 31 December 2010, and the related votes and titles in respect of each of those sums, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of one hundred and twenty-five million, seven hundred and seventeen thousand euro.

###### 3. Financial resolutions passed by Dáil Éireann on 9 December 2009.

**3**.— (1) The legislation required to give effect to the financial resolutions passed by Dáil Éireann on 9 December 2009 and referred to in *subsection (2)* may, subject to the time period specified in section 4 of the Provisional Collection of Taxes Act 1927, be enacted in the year 2010.

- (2) The Financial Resolutions referred to in *subsection (1)* are as follows:

    - (*a*) Financial Resolution No. 1 (which relates to excise duty and to a reduction in the rates of alcohol products tax);

    - (*b*) Financial Resolution No. 2 (which relates to excise duty and a carbon charge to be included in the rates of mineral oil tax on petrol and auto-diesel);

    - (*c*) Financial Resolution No. 3 (which relates to value-added-tax and to a reduction in the standard rate);

    - (*d*) Financial Resolution No. 4 (which relates to value-added-tax and to the introduction of a margin-scheme for second-hand vehicles).

###### 4. Short title.

**4**.— This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act 2009.
