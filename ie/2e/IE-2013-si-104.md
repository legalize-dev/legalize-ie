# S.I. No. 104/2013 - Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012 (Conferral of Functions) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd April*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec37) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012 (Conferral of Functions) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 27th March 2013.

###### 3.

3. The functions of the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport under [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec5) (1), [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec9) (1) and (2), [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec11) (other than subsection (2)), [12 to 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec12) , [16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec16) (2), [17 to 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec17) , [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec24) and [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec33) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012) are conferred on the Road Safety Authority.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 March 2013.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the transfer of certain functions conferred on the Minister to Transport, Tourism, and Sport under the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012) to the Road Safety Authority relating to the:

- (*a*) arrangements for the testing of commercial vehicles and the issuance of certificates of roadworthiness;

- (*b*) authorisation of commercial vehicle roadworthiness test operators and testers;

- (*c*) suspension and revocation of authorisations and handling of appeals where an authorisation is suspended or revoked; and

- (*d*) appointment of authorised officers, CVR inspectors and deciding officers.
