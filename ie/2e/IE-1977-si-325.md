# S.I. No. 325/1977 - European Communities (Marketing Standards For Eggs) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 325 of 1977.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (MARKETING STANDARDS FOR EGGS) REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Article 10 of Council Regulation (EEC) No. 2772/75*, hereby make the following Regulations :

*OJ No. L282/56 of 1/11/1975.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Marketing Standards for Eggs) Regulations, 1977, and shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1978.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 10 of the European Communities (Marketing Standards for Eggs) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0128.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 26th day of October, 1977.

**JAMES GIBBONS,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The regulations revoke Regulation 10 of the European Communities (Marketing Standards for Eggs) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0128.html) ) which allowed, on a temporary basis, the weight grading of eggs in four Imperial weight categories. The effect of this revocation is to apply fully the seven metric weight grade system as provided for in Article 10 of Regulation (EEC) No. 2772/75 of the Council of 29 October, 1975 on marketing standards for eggs.
