# S.I. No. 627/2020 - Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th December,* 2020.

I, ROBERT TROY, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec12) (1), [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec22) (2), [343](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec343) (4)(a), [430](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec430) (3), [436](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec436) (1) and [441](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec441) (2)(b) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014) (as adapted by the Business, Enterprise and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 519 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0519.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 580 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0580.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 16 December 2020.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations,

“Regulations of 2015” means the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (Forms) Regulations 2015 (S.I. 147 of 2015).

###### 3.

3. The form (Form A1) set out in Part 1 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 4.

4. The form (Form B1) set out in Part 2 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 23 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 5.

5. The form (Form E9) set out in Part 3 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 35 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 6.

6. The form (Form E8) set out in Part 4 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 36 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 7.

7. The [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (Forms) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 95 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0095.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe amended forms for the purposes of sections 22(2), 343(4)(a), 430(3), 436(1) and 441(2)(b) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) . The Regulations also revoke the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (Forms) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 95 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0095.html) ).
