# S.I. No. 340/1997 - Registration of Title Act, 1964 (Central Office) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 340 of 1997.

REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT, 1964 (CENTRAL OFFICE) ORDER, 1997

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (5) (inserted by the [Registration of Title (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1997)) of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Registration of Title Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1964), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Registration of Title Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Central Office) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 18th day of August, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The County of Waterford is hereby designated as an area within which one or more than one constituent office of the central office shall be located.

John O'Donoghue

GIVEN under my Official Seal

this 5th day of August, 1997.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates County Waterford as an area within which one or more than one constituent office of the Central Office of the Land Registry shall be located.
