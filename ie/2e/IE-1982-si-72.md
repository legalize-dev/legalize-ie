# S.I. No. 72/1982 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Carlow) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 72 of 1982.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF CARLOW) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Carlow) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Carlow) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0121.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed together and may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Carlow) Regulations, 1978 to 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 5th day of April, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The Third Schedule to the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Carlow) Regulations, 1978, is hereby amended by the addition after reference 2 of the following:

"3. *The following road at Mortarstown:—*

The Carlow-Kilkenny road between a point 1,000 metres south-west of the boundary of the urban district of Carlow and a point 1,625 metres south-west of the said boundary."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 26th day of March, 1982.

**RAPHAEL P. BURKE,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Carlow) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0121.html) ) by prescribing a speed limit of 40 miles per hour on a section of the Carlow-Kilkenny road at Mortarstown.
