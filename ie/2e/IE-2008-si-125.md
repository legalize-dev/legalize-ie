# S.I. No. 125/2008 - Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) (No. 1) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 125 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0125.html)

CONSUMER CREDIT ACT 1995 (SECTION 2) (No. 1) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th May*, 2008.

I, MARY ODEA, Consumer Director of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority pursuant to Section 33S of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) and by Section 2(1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 2) (No. 1) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 30 April 2008.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2 of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 2) (No. 2) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0369.html) ) is hereby amended by the deletion of the following:

###### 4.

4. The words “Arnotts Limited” are hereby deleted from the Table to Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 432 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0432.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The following Statutory Instruments are hereby repealed:

Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0113.html) );

Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) (No. 2) Regulations 2002( [S.I. No. 339 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0339.html) ); and

MARY ODEA

Consumer Director of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to revoke the prescription of certain entities prescribed pursuant to paragraph (*e*) of the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
