# S.I. No. 4/1981 - Worker Participation (Aer Lingus Teoranta/Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM NOLAN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby make the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Aer Lingus Teoranta/Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The 19th day of March, 1981, is in relation to Aer Lingus Teoranta and Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta hereby appointed to be the nomination day for the purposes of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977).

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 5th day of January, 1981.

**TOM NOLAN,**

Minister for Labour.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations appoint 19th March, 1981, as the day on which nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to Aer Lingus Teoranta/Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta shall be received by the returning officer.
