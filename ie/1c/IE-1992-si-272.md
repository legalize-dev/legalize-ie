# S.I. No. 272/1992 - Local Government (Water Pollution) (Transfer of Appeals) (Revocation) Order, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 272 of 1992.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (WATER POLLUTION) (TRANSFER OF APPEALS) (REVOCATION) ORDER, 1992.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) (Amendment) Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1990), which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 136 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0136.html) ), I, MARY HARNEY, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Local Government (Water Pollution) (Transfer of Appeals) (Revocation) Order, 1992.

###### 2.

2. This order shall come into operation on the 1st day of November, 1992.

###### 3.

3. The [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , (Transfer of Appeals) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 96 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0096.html) ) is hereby revoked.

DATED this 25th day of September, 1992.

MARY HARNEY,

Minister of State at the Department of

the Environment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Order revokes, with effect from 1st November 1992, the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Transfer of Appeals) Order, 1978 pursuant to [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0016.html#sec16) (2) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) (Amendment) Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .
