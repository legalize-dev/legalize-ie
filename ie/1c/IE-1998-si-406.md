S.I. No. 406 of 1998.

BRAY URBAN DISTRICT LOCAL ELECTORAL AREAS ORDER, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Bray Urban District Local Electoral Areas Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Urban District" means the Urban District of Bray as altered for the purposes of local elections by the Local Government (Boundaries) (Town Elections) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0114.html) ) and references to the urban boundary shall be construed accordingly.

###### 3.

3. (1) The Urban District of Bray shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area set forth in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of the council of the Urban District of Bray to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set forth in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 4.

4. (1) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along any road, street, lane, bridge or river shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road, street, lane, bridge or river.

- (2) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which any road, street, lane, bridge or river intersects or joins any other road, street, lane, bridge or river shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of one would be intersected or joined by an imaginary line drawn along the centre of the other.

- (3) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which any road, street, lane, bridge or river intersects or joins a boundary shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road, street, lane, bridge or river would intersect or join such boundary.

###### 5.

5. The Bray Urban District Local Electoral Areas Order, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0115.html) ) is hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of the Urban District of Bray into local electoral areas and to fix the number of members to be elected for each electoral area.
