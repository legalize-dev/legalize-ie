# S.I. No. 22/1989 - Ringaskiddy Free Port (Control and Management) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 22 of 1989.

RINGASKIDDY FREE PORT (CONTROL AND MANAGEMENT) ORDER, 1989.

I, BRENDAN DALY, Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Free Ports Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1986), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Industry and Commerce, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Ringaskiddy Free Port (Control and Management) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. Patrick J. Keenan of "Glenkeen", Clogheen, Blarney Road, in the County of Cork, is hereby designated for the purposes of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Free Ports Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1986), in relation to the free port established by the Ringaskiddy Free Port (Establishment) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0113.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of February, 1989.

**BRENDAN DALY,**

Minister for the Marine.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,this 1st day

of February, 1989.

**D. B. QUIGLEY.**

A person authorised under [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0015.html#sec15) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , to authenticate the Seal of the Minister for Finance.

The Minister for Industry and Commerce hereby consents to the making of this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Industry and Commerce,

this 1st day of February, 1989.

**S. DORGAN.**

A person authorised under [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0015.html#sec15) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , to authenticate the Seal of the Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints Patrick J. Keenan to control and manage the Ringaskiddy Free Port.
