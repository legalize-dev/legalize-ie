# S.I. No. 275/2008 - Pensions Act 1990 (Register of Administrators) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 275 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0275.html)

PENSIONS ACT 1990 (REGISTER OF ADMINISTRATORS) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd July*, 2008.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 5(1) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Register of Administrators) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 November 2008.

###### 2.

2. For the purposes of subsection (2) of section 64(C) (inserted by section 27(i) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2008)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), the information set out in the Schedule is prescribed as being required for inclusion in each entry in the Register referred to in subsection (1) of the said section 64(C) in respect of a registered administrator.

*Article 2.*

SCHEDULE INFORMATION RELATING TO A REGISTERED ADMINISTRATOR

1. The name and address of the registered administrator concerned and, if different, the address in the State for service of notices on such administrator.

2. The registered number assigned by the Board in respect of the registration concerned.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social and Family Affairs,

17 July 2008

**MARY HANAFIN**

Minister for Social and Family Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order sets out the information required to be included in the Register of Administrators established by the Board pursuant to Section 64C(1) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .
