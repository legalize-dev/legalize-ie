# S.I. No. 461/2001 - Prisons Act, 1972 (Section 3) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Prisons Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1972), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), hereby specify Wheatfield Place of Detention, situated at Cloverhill Road, Clondalkin, Dublin as a place to be used as a prison with effect from the 10th day of October, 2001, which place shall be known as Wheatfield Prison.
