# S.I. No. 65/2013 - Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th February*, 2013.

I, ENDA KENNY, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1099/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 September 2010[^1] , hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. This Order applies to an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under Section B, C, D, E, G, H, J, K and M of NACE Rev. 2, as set out in Annex IV to Commission Regulation (EC) No. 973/2007 of 20 August 2007[^2] , and which is engaged in economic activities at any time during a year in respect of which a survey is undertaken.

###### 3.

3. An undertaking to which this Order applies is required to provide the Central Statistics Office with information under the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , the general nature of which is specified in the Schedule, in relation to its economic activities in the year preceding the year in which a survey is undertaken.

###### 4.

4. The information referred to in Article 3 shall be provided annually commencing in 2013.

**SCHEDULE**

Article 3

- (*a*) Particulars of all types of energy used by the undertaking by quantity and value;

- (*b*) Particulars of all types of energy used by the undertaking purchased from a third party by quantity and value;

- (*c*) Particulars of the heating system used by the undertaking by quantity;

- (*d*) Particulars of the air conditioning system used by the undertaking by quantity;

- (*e*) Particulars of time taken to complete the questionnaire or form.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 February 2013.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.
