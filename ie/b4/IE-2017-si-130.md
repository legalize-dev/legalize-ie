# S.I. No. 130/2017 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th April*, 2017.

I, CHARLES FLANAGAN, Minister for Foreign Affairs and Trade, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (amended by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) of the [Passports Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2008)) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Provision of Services) Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1993) (as adapted by the Foreign Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0246.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 30 March 2017.

###### 3.

3. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 46 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0046.html) ) (amended by the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 119 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0119.html) )) are amended by the substitution of the text set out in the Schedule for Part 1 of Schedule 2 to those Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these regulations is to amend the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2013 to provide for a change in the fee structure for passports.
