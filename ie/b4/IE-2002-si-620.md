# S.I. No. 620/2002 - Shanganagh Castle (Place of Detention) (Closure) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0051/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Prisons Act 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1933), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Shanganagh Castle (Place of Detention (Closure) Order, 2002

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) shall apply to this order.

###### 3.

3. It is directed that Shanganagh Castle situated in the administrative county of Dun Laoghaire-Rathdown, being a place provided under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Prisons Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), shall be closed on and from 20 December, 2002.
