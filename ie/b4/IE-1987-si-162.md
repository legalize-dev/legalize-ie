S.I. No. 162 of 1987.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF SLIGO) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Sligo) Regulations, 1987.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 6th day of July, 1987.

3 Interpretation.

###### 3.

**3.** In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires—

"the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"built-up area speed limit" and "special speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road;

"National Road" means a road declared to be a National Road in the [Local Government (Roads and motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Declaration of National Roads) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0164.html) ) and any reference to the number of such road shall be a reference to the number of such road as shown in column I of the First Schedule or column 1 of the Second Schedule to the aforementioned Order;

"road" means a public road.

4 Built-up area speed limit.

###### 4.

**4.** (1) Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

5 Special speed limit.

###### 5.

**5.** Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles in the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

6 Exceptions.

###### 6.

**6.** There shall be excepted from the special speed limit prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:—

- (*a*) ambulances,

- (*b*) fire brigade vehicles, and

- (*c*) vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochána in the performance of their duties as such members.

7 Revocation.

###### 7.

**7.** The Road Traffic (County of Sligo) (Speed Limits) Regulations 1979 ( [S.I. No. 284 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0284.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Sligo) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 284 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0284.html) ) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in the area.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area speed limit prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , and declare the public roads mentioned in the Second Schedule to be subject to that speed limit.

The regulations also prescribe a speed limit of 40 miles per hour in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Third Schedule.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and vehicles used by the Garda Síochána in the course of duty are exempted from all speed limits by the Regulations.
