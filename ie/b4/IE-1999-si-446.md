# S.I. No. 446/1999 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of its powers under the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 10th day of April, 1999.

###### 3.

3. Rates of interest on deposits within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , are hereby prescribed as follows:

- (*a*) where any such deposit is less than £5,000, the rate of interest shall be 0.25 per cent per annum; and

- (*b*) where any such deposit is £5,000 or more, the rate of interest shall be 0.50 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 2) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 162 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0162.html) ) are hereby revoked.
