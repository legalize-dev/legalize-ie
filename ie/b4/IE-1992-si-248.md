# S.I. No. 248/1992 - Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 248 of 1992.

TAXIMETER (FEES ON VERIFICATION AND STAMPING BY SPECIAL INSPECTORS) REGULATIONS, 1992.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 83](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0083.html#sec83) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1992.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 14th day of September, 1992.

###### 2.

2. (1) The fee to be paid in respect of the verification and stamping of a taximeter by a special inspector shall be £50.00.

- (2) Every such fee shall be paid by the person who submits the taximeter for such verification and stamping.

###### 3.

3. The Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 436 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0436.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of September, 1992.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of September, 1992.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order was made to set out revised fees for the verification and stamping of taximeters.
