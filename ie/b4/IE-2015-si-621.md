# S.I. No. 621/2015 - European Communities (Machinery) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January*, 2016.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving partial effect to Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013[^1] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Machinery) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2011 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Machinery) Regulations 2008 to 2015.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2016.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Machinery) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 407 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0407.html) );

“Regulations of 2011” means the European Communities (Machinery) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 310 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0310.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 3 of the Principal Regulations (as amended by Regulation 3(*a*)(i) of the Regulations of 2011) is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph (1) by substituting for the definition of “Directive” the following:

  > “‘Directive’ means Directive 2006/42/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 May 2006[^2] , as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 596/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^3] , Directive 2009/127/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009[^4] and Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 20131;”,

- (*b*) in paragraph (3) by substituting “Subject to paragraph (4) these Regulations” for “These Regulations”, and

- (*c*) by inserting after paragraph (3), the following paragraph—

  > - “(4) Tractors covered by type approval legislation under Directive 2003/37/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 May 2003[^5] put into service prior to 1 January 2018 shall be subject to these Regulations for risks not covered by that Directive”.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 4 of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting “putting it into service” for “putting it into use”.

###### 6.

6. Schedule 12 of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the first indent of paragraph (*e*) the following paragraph:

> “— agricultural and forestry tractors, with the exclusion of machinery mounted on those vehicles,”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 December 2015.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations transpose in Irish law an amendment to Directive 2006/42/EC on machinery that was given effect by Article 77 of Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013 on the approval and market surveillance of agricultural and forestry vehicles.

These Regulations also amend the European Communities (Machinery) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 407 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0407.html) ) that gave effect to Directive 2006/42/EC and which were subsequently amended by the European Communities (Machinery) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 310 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0310.html) ) that gave effect to Directive 2009/127/EC with regard to machinery for pesticide application.

In particular, the provisions to give effect to Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013 will come into effect from 1 January 2016. From that date Directive 2006/42/EC will no longer apply to new type approved tractors since such vehicles will be completely excluded from that Directive in accordance with Article 77 of Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013.

New tractors covered by existing type approval legislation (Directive 2003/37/EC) may continue to be registered until 31 December 2017 and such vehicles are subject to the requirements of Directive 2006/42/EC.
