# CONNAUGHT RANGERS (PENSIONS) ACT, 1964

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO AMEND THE CONNAUGHT RANGERS (PENSIONS) ACTS, 1936 TO 1962. [15*th July*, 1964.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Increase of service pensions and disability pensions.

**1.**—(1) A pension to which this section applies shall be increased by five per cent.

- (2) This section applies to—

    - (*a*) a service pension payable under the Connaught Rangers (Pensions) Acts, 1936 to 1962, and

    - (*b*) a disability pension so payable.

- (3) An increase under this section shall have effect from (and inclusive of) the 1st day of November, 1963.

###### 2 Short title and collective citation.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Connaught Rangers (Pensions) Act, 1964.

- (2) The Connaught Rangers (Pensions) Acts, 1936 to 1962, and this Act may be cited together as the Connaught Rangers (Pensions) Acts, 1936 to 1964.
