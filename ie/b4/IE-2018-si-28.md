# S.I. No. 28/2018 - Garda Síochána Act 2005 (Retirement) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th February*, 2018.

I, CHARLES FLANAGAN, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (*h*) (as amended by section 43(*m*) of the [Criminal Justice Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2007)) of section 122(1) (as amended by section 56(*a*) of the [Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html) (No. 49 of 2015)) of the Garda Síochána Act 2005 (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), after consulting with the Garda Commissioner and the Policing Authority and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána Act 2005 (Retirement) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. Subject to [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec4) of the [Public Service Superannuation (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2004) and notwithstanding the Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1934 ( [S. R. & O., No. 146 of 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/si/0146.html) ) and the Garda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations 1951 ( [S.I. No. 335 of 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/si/0335.html) ), a person appointed to the rank of Commissioner after the commencement of these Regulations shall retire—

- (*a*) on completion of 5 years’ service in that rank, or

- (*b*) on attaining the age of 60 years,

whichever is the earlier.

###### 3.

3. The Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1990 ( [S.I. No. 318 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0318.html) ) are revoked.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

6 February 2018.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,
