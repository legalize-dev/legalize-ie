# S.I. No. 572/2022 - European Union (Temporary Increase Of Official Controls And Emergency Measures On Imports Of Food And Feed Of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th November,* 2022.

I, Charlie Mc Conalogue, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/913 of 30 May 2022 , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0009.html) ) are amended—

- (a) in Regulation 2, by substituting for paragraph (2) (as inserted by the European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 237 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0237.html) ) the following–

  > - “(2) These Regulations do not apply to–
  >
  >     - (a) consignments of products, unless their net weight exceeds 5 kg of fresh products or 2 kg of other products which are:
  >
  >         - (i) consignments which form part of passengers’ personal luggage and are intended for personal consumption or use;
  >
  >         - (ii) non-commercial consignments sent to natural persons, which are not intended to be placed on the market,
  >
  >     - (b) consignments of products, unless their net weight exceeds 50 kg of fresh products or 10 kg of other products which are:
  >
  >         - (i) consignments sent as trade samples, laboratory samples or as display items for exhibitions, which are not intended to be placed on the market;
  >
  >         - (ii) consignments intended for scientific purposes, and
  >
  >     - (c) food and feed referred to in points (a) and (b) of Article 1(1) of Regulation 2019/1793 on board means of transport operating internationally, which are not unloaded and are intended for consumption by the crew and passengers.”, and

- (b) in Regulation 3(1), by substituting for the definition of “Regulation 2019/1793” the following –

  > “‘Regulation 2019/1793’ means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 of 22 October 2019 as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/625 of 6 May 2020, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1540 of 22 October 2020, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/608 of 14 April 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1900 of 27 October 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/2246 of 15 December 2021 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/913 of 30 May 2022;”.

###### 3.

3. The European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No.241 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0241.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 November, 2022.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations update existing Regulations with regard to additional levels of controls on the import of certain food of non-animal origin from specific third countries with regard to pesticide residues and on the import of certain feed of non-animal origin from specific third countries
