# S.I. No. 309/1995 - National Milk Agency (Revocation of Election Day) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 309 of 1995.

NATIONAL MILK AGENCY (REVOCATION OF ELECTION DAY) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, IVAN YATES, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Revocation of Election Day) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. The National Milk Agency (Election Day) Order, 1994 (S.I. No. 460) of 1994, is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 20th day of November, 1995.

**IVAN YATES,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry.
