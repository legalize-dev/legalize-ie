S.I. No. 29 of 1997.

PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS (FORMS) REGULATIONS, 1997.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0028/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Presidential Elections Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1993) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Presidential Elections (Forms) Regulations, 1997.

###### 2.

2. Each of the forms set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form for the purpose for which such form is expressed to be applicable.
