# S.I. No. 125/1990 - Local Government (Transfer of Service) (Amendment) Scheme, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 125 of 1990.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (TRANSFER OF SERVICE) (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1990.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0002.html#sec2) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Scheme:

1 Short Title.

###### 1.

**1.** ( *a* ) This Scheme may be cited as the Local Government (Transfer of Service) (Amendment) Scheme, 1990.

( *b* ) This Scheme, the Local Government (Transfer of Service) Scheme, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0298.html) ) and the Local Government (Transfer of Service) (Amendment) Scheme, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 243 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0243.html) ) shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Local Government (Transfer of Service) Scheme, 1984 to 1990.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of January, 1990.

3 Amendments.

###### 3.

**3.** The Local Government (Transfer of Service) Scheme, 1984 is hereby amended by the insertion of ", in respect of the period beginning on the date of application of this Scheme and ending on the 31st day of December, 1989," after the word "payment" in articles 8 (*a*) (i) and 9 (3) (*b*) (i).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 23rd day of May, 1990.

**PÁDRAIG FLYNN**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the Local Government (Transfer of Service) (Amendment) Scheme, 1990.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 23rd day of May, 1990.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Scheme amends the Local Government (Transfer of Service) Scheme, 1984 to provide for revised financial arrangements for the superannuation of persons employed by vocational education committees.
