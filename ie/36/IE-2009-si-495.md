# S.I. No. 495/2009 - Regional Fisheries Boards (Postponement of Elections) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 495 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0495.html) .

REGIONAL FISHERIES BOARDS (POSTPONEMENT OF ELECTIONS) ORDER 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th December*, 2009.

I, CONOR LENIHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 15(1) of the [Fisheries Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980) and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications, Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0706.html) )) and the Energy and Natural Resources (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2009 ( [S.I. No. 199 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0199.html) ), hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to subsection (3) of that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Regional Fisheries Boards (Postponement of Elections) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. The year 2010 is fixed as the year in which the elections of members of regional boards (which, but for this Article, would fall to be held in the year 2009) shall be held.

GIVEN under my hand,

10 December 2009.

CONOR LENIHAN,

Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the postponement of elections for members of the Regional Fisheries Boards from 2009 to 2010.
