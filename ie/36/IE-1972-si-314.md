# S.I. No. 314/1972 - European Communities (Shipbuilding) Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 314 of 1972.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (SHIPBUILDING) REGULATIONS, 1972.

I, PATRICK J. LALOR, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Shipbuilding) Regulations, 1972.

###### 2.

2. These regulations shall come into operation on the First day of January, 1973.

###### 3.

3. (1) The references in [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0020/sec0037.html#sec37) (4) of the [Finance Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1932), to goods shall be construed as references to goods which are shown to the satisfaction of the Revenue Commissioners to be goods that have originated or are in free circulation in a member State of the European Communities.

- (2) [Section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0020/sec0037.html#sec37) (5) of the [Finance Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , is hereby repealed.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of December, 1972.

**PATRICK J. LALOR,**

Minister for Industry and

Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations align with the Common Customs Tariff of the European Communities Irish customs regulations exempting from duty materials for use in shipbuilding.
