# S.I. No. 423/1979 - Higher Education Authority Act, 1971 (Designation of Institution of Higher Education) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 423 of 1979.

HIGHER EDUCATION AUTHORITY ACT, 1971 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION) REGULATIONS, 1979

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1971), and after consultation with An tÚdarás Um Ard-Oideachas, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Designation of Institution of Higher Education) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The institution specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby designated as an institution of higher education for the purpose of the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1971).

SCHEDULE.

Thomond College of Education.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this Twenty-First day of December, 1979.

**JOHN P. WILSON,**

Minister for Education.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations provide that the institution mentioned in the schedule of these regulations will be an institution of higher education within the meaning of the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , Section 1.
