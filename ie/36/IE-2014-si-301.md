# S.I. No. 301/2014 - Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th July*, 2014.

I, FERGUS O’DOWD, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec69) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) and the Energy and Natural Resources (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 631 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0631.html) ) and after consultation with Inland Fisheries Ireland, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 573 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0573.html) ) are amended in Regulation 25(*k*) by substituting for paragraph (*k*) the following:

> - “(*k*) Regulation 20(2), (3), (4) and (8)”.

GIVEN under my hand,

2 July 2014.

FERGUS O’DOWD,

Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order amends the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 573 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0573.html) ) in Regulation 25(*k*) by substituting for paragraph (*k*) the following:

> - “(*k*) Regulation 20(2), (3), (4) and (8)”.
