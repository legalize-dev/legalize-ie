S.I. No. 271 of 1991.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY BOROUGH OF DUBLIN AND COUNTY OF DUBLIN) (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1991.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act (as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968)) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1991.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations 1989 to 1991 and these Regulations shall be construed together and may be cited collectively as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations 1989 to 1991.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 24th day of October, 1991.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations 1989 ( [S.I. No. 206 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0206.html) ).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations further amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations 1989 by detailing the speed limits to be applied on the Shankill-Bray By-Pass and roads adjacent to it. The Regulations also amend the 1989 Regulations to take account of the realignment of the Navan Road and the opening of the Blanchardstown By-Pass.

Ambulances, Fire Brigade vehicles and Garda Síochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are exempt from the speed limits.
