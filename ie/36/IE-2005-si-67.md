STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 67 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0067.html) .**

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (HYGIENE OF FOODSTUFFS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2005.

[S.I. No. 67 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0067.html) .

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (HYGIENE OF FOODSTUFFS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2005.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ( [No. 27 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ), and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2004/4/EC[^1] of 15 January 2004 amending Directive 96/3/EC granting a derogation from certain provisions of Council Directive 93/43/EEC on the hygiene of foodstuffs as regards the transport of bulk liquid oils and fats by sea, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 165 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0165.html) ) are amended by:

- (1) in Regulation 3(1) by inserting the definition “‘Directive 2004/4/EC’ means Commission Directive 2004/4/EC^7 of 15 January 2004 amending Directive 96/3/EC granting a derogation from certain provisions of Council Directive 93/43/EEC on the hygiene of foodstuffs as regards the transport of bulk liquid oils and fats by sea.”,

and by the addition of the following footnote thereto:

> “^7 OJ L 15, 22.1.2004, p.25.”

- (2) in Regulation 3(1) by inserting in the definition of “the Directives“ after “Directive 96/3/EC” “as amended by Directive 2004/4/EC,”, and

- (3) by substituting for Regulation 4(4) the following:

  > “the proprietor of a food business may derogate from the rules of hygiene as set down in the Second Schedule provided that he or she does so in accordance with Directive 96/3/EC as amended by Directive 2004/4/EC or with Directive 98/28/EC.”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 165 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0165.html) ) and may be cited as the European Communities (Hygiene of Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

These Regulations implement Commission Directive 2004/4/EC which amend the Annex to Commission Directive 96/3/EC granting a derogation from certain provisions of Council Directive 93/43/EEC on the hygiene of foodstuffs as regards the transport of bulk liquid oils and fats by sea.

DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND CHILDREN.
