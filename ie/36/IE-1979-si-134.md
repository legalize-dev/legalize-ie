# S.I. No. 134/1979 - Health Services (No. 2) Regulations, 1979

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 134 of 1979.

HEALTH SERVICES (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and by [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 ) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (No. 2) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"voluntary contributions" and "voluntary contributor" have the meanings assigned to them in the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1952);

"contribution year" means a contribution year prescribed under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) ;

"employment contribution" includes an employment contribution as referred to in [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) and a contribution paid outside the State which is treated as an employment contribution for the purpose of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) , relating to disability benefit and maternity benefit.

###### 3.

3. Article 3 of the Health Services Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 109 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0109.html) ) shall not apply to any person who,

( *a* ) was a voluntary contributor on the 5th day of April, 1979 or on the 31st day of March, 1974 and who in the contribution year before the 5th day of April, 1979, had twenty-four contributions, being voluntary contributions or employment contributions paid, payable or credited or in respect of whom not less than seventy-two such contributions had been paid, payable or credited in the three contribution years before the 5th day of April, 1979, or

( *b* ) is a dependant of a person referred to in paragraph (*a*).

###### 4.

4. These Regulations shall cease to have effect on and from the 6th day of April, 1984.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 11th day of April, 1979.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the retention of their entitlement to services under [Sections 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0052.html#sec52) (1), [56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) (2), [62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0062.html#sec62) and [63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) by persons who had such entitlement on the 5 April, 1979 by virtue of their being voluntarily insured under the Social Welfare Acts on 31 March, 1974 or 5 April, 1979.

These Regulations will cease to have effect on 6 April, 1984 but the Minister may, by regulations, provide for the extension of their provisions.
