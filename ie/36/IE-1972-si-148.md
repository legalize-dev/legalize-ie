# S.I. No. 148/1972 - Home-Grown Wheat (National Percentage For Cereal Year 1971-72) (Variation) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 148 of 1972.

HOME-GROWN WHEAT (NATIONAL PERCENTAGE FOR CEREAL YEAR 1971-72) (VARIATION) ORDER, 1972.

I, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in excercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0026/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1935), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), and by the Wheat Milling (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0217.html) ), hereby order as follows :—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Home-Grown Wheat (National Percentage for Cereal Year 1971-72) (Variation) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. Article 3 of the Home-Grown Wheat (National Percentage for Cereal Year 1971-72) Order, 1970 ( [S.I. No. 293 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0293.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution of " fifty-nine per cent " for " seventy-five per cent ".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 12th day of June, 1972.

**JAMES GIBBONS,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Order requires licensed millers to mill 59 per cent. (instead of 75 per cent. as originally prescribed) of their milling quotas in the form of home-grown wheat during the cereal year commencing on 1st September 1971.
