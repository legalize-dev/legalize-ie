# S.I. No. 67/1982 - Telegraph (Foreign Written Telegram) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 67 of 1982.

TELEGRAPH (FOREIGN WRITTEN TELEGRAM) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telegraph (Foreign Written Telegram) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

- (2) The Telegraph (Foreign Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 and 1981 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telegraph (Foreign Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 to 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Telegraph (Foreign Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 198 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0198.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution of the following for the entries in the Schedule thereto (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Telegraph (Foreign Written Telegram) (Amendment (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I.
