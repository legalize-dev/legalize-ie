S.I. No. 387 of 1998.

COUNTY OF FINGAL LOCAL ELECTORAL AREAS ORDER, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the County of Fingal Local Electoral Areas Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. (1) The County of Fingal shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area set forth in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of the council of the County of Fingal to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set forth in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division or townland shall be construed as referring to such electoral division or townland existing at the date of this Order.

###### 4.

4. (1) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along any road or laneway shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road or laneway.

- (2) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which any road or laneway intersects or join any other road, laneway or boundary shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of a road or laneway or, in the case of a boundary along the boundary, would be intersected or joined by an imaginary line drawn along the centre of the other, or in the case of a boundary, along the boundary.

- (3) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along a road which consists of a dual carriageway shall be construed as an imaginary line drawn along the central median of such dual carriageway.

###### 5.

5. Those parts of the Dublin Electoral Counties Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 133 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0133.html) ), not previously revoked, are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of County Fingal into county electoral areas and to fix the number of members to be elected for each electoral area.
