# S.I. No. 337/1991 - Jury Districts Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 337 of 1991.

JURY DISTRICTS ORDER, 1991.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976) hereby revoke and Order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 16th day of December, 1991.

###### 3.

3. Part II of the Schedule to the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ) is hereby amended by the deletion of the reference to the jury district, County of Louth (Jury District No. 1 (Dundalk) and Jury District No. 2 (Drogheda)).

###### 4.

4. I hereby revoke Jury Districts Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 59 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0059.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 16th day of December, 1991.

**RAY BURKE**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Statutory Instrument which comes into operation on 16th December, 1991 amends Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ) and revokes Jury Districts Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 59 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0059.html) ). For the purposes of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) the County of Louth is now one jury district.
