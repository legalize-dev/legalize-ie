# S.I. No. 180/2003 - Infectious Diseases (Amendment No. 2) Regulations, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and by section 31 of the said Act, as amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953), hereby makes the following regulations:-

Citation

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Amendment No. 2) Regulations, 2003.

Interpretation

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations the expression “Principal Regulations” means the Infectious Diseases Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ).

Amendment

###### 3.

3. (1) Article 8 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the insertion before “Smallpox” of “Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS)”.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal representation.)

These Regulations amend the Infectious Diseases Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) to include Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS) in the exceptions listed in Article 8.
