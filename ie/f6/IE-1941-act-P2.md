# PIER AND HARBOUR PROVISIONAL ORDER CONFIRMATION ACT, 1941

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO CONFIRM THE WATERFORD HARBOUR ORDER, 1941. [10*th December*, 1941.]

WHEREAS the Waterford Harbour Order, 1941, set out in the Schedule to this Act has been duly made by the Minister for Industry and Commerce under the General Pier and Harbour Act, 1861, and the General Pier and Harbour Act, 1861, Amendment Act, 1862, as adapted by or under subsequent enactments:

AND WHEREAS under the provisions of the said Acts as so adapted the said Order is of no validity or force whatever unless and until confirmed by the Oireachtas:

BE IT THEREFORE ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Confirmation of Order in Schedule.

**1.**—The Order set out in the Schedule to this Act is hereby confirmed and all the provisions thereof shall have full validity and force.

###### 2 Short Title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Pier and Harbour Provisional Order Confirmation Act, 1941.

## SCHEDULE. Waterford Harbour Order, 1941.

ORDER FOR AUTHORISING THE PAYMENT OF PENSIONS, ALLOWANCES AND GRATUITIES TO OFFICERS AND SERVANTS OF THE COMMISSIONERS FOR IMPROVING THE PORT AND HARBOUR OF WATERFORD, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
