# S.I. No. 187/2003 - Companies (Fees) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Michael Ahern, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0157.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. In this Order the “Eighth Schedule” means the Eighth Schedule (substituted by the Companies (Fees) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 477 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0477.html) ), and amended by the Companies (Fees) (No.2) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 569 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0569.html) ) and the Companies (Fees) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 557 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0557.html) ), to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order amends the Companies (Fees) Order 2001 (S.I. 477 of 2001), as amended.

The Order provides for disapplication of filing fees where certain specified documents are filed electronically with the Registrar of Companies.
