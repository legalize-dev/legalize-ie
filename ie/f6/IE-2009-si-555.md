# S.I. No. 555/2009 - Competition Act 2002 (Section 34(11)) (Commission For Taxi Regulation) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January*, 2010.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec34) (11) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002), after consultation with the Minister for Transport, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Competition Act 2002 (Section 34(11)) (Commission for Taxi Regulation) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 1 to the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002) is amended, in columns (1) and (2), by inserting after the matters lastly mentioned in them “Commission for Taxi Regulation” and “Minister for Transport”, respectively.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 December 2009

**MARY COUGHLAN,**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Schedule 1 of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) specifies the statutory bodies who are required to enter into co-operation agreements with the CompetitionAuthority as provided for in Section 34 of the Act. This Order adds theCommission for Taxi Regulation to those bodies and requires the entering into of a co-operation agreement between the Commission for Taxi Regulation and the Competition Authority.
