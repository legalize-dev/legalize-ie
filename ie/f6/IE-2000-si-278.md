# S.I. No. 278/2000 - Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Marketing, Sale and Distribution of Fuels) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0010.html#sec10) , [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0014.html#sec14) and [53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Air Pollution Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1987), which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 428 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0428.html) ), I, Dan Wallace, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby make the following Regulations:—

Citation

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Marketing, Sale and Distribution of Fuels) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000.

No. 118 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0118.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be collectively cited as the [Air Pollution Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Marketing, Sale and Distribution of Fuels) Regulations, 1998 and 2000.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Marketing, Sale and Distribution of Fuels) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0118.html) ).

The Regulations provide for the introduction of the ban on the sale of bituminous coal to five additional restricted areas in Celbridge, Galway, Leixlip, Naas and Waterford and an additional location in the restricted area of South Dublin.

The Regulations come into force on 1 October 2000.
