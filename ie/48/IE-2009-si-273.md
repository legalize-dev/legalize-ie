COURT-MARTIAL RULES 2009

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Court-Martial Rules 2009.

- (2) The Court-Martial Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 205 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0205.html) ) and these Rules may be cited together as the Court-Martial Rules 2008 to 2009.

- (3) These Rules shall come into operation on 23 July 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Court-Martial Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 205 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0205.html) ) are hereby amended—

- (1) by deleting Rule 37 and substituting the following therefor:

  > *“Arraignment of accused*
  >
  > 37. (1) Where an accused is brought before the military judge for his or her trial the accused shall be required to indicate whether a board is required to try some or all of the charges.
  >
  > - (2) Where the accused indicates that a board is not required for some or all of the charges the accused shall be arraigned forthwith before the military judge on those charges.
  >
  > - (3) Where the accused indicates that a board is required to try some or all of the charges the accused shall be arraigned on the charges to be tried before the board and he or she shall be required to plead separately to each charge. Thereafter the procedures in Part 13 shall be followed.”

- (2) by the substitution in each place it occurs in Form 2 of “A.F. 43(*a*)” with “*A.F. 43(*a*)/*A.F. 108”.
