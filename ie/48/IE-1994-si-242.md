# S.I. No. 242/1994 - Comptroller and Auditor General (Section 21) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 242 of 1994.

COMPTROLLER AND AUDITOR GENERAL (SECTION 21) ORDER, 1994.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1) of [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Comptroller and Auditor General (Section 21) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. In this order, "the Act" means the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993).

###### 3.

3. The Second Schedule to the Act is hereby amended by the addition of the Central Bank of Ireland to the list of persons excluded from application of sections 8 and 9 of the Act.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of July, 1994.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.
