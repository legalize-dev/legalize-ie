I, PATRICK POWER, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) , [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0003.html#sec3) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1932), and by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1938), as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service, make the following Scheme:

1 Short title, collective citation and construction.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1982.

- (2) The Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1981, and this Scheme shall be construed together and may be cited together as the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1982.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall come into force on the day after the date on which it is made.

3 Application of [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .

###### 3.

**3.** The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

4 Definitions.

###### 4.

**4.** In this Scheme—

"the Principal Scheme" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) Scheme, 1937 (S. R. & O., No. 249 of 1937);

"the Amending Scheme of 1947" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1947 (S. R. & O., No. 81 of 1947);

"the Amending Scheme of 1949" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1949 ( [S.I. No. 209 of 1949](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1949/en/si/0209.html) );

"the Amending Scheme of 1953" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1953 ( [S.I. No. 259 of 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/si/0259.html) );

"the Amending Scheme of 1960" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1960 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/si/0135.html) );

"the No. 3 Amending Scheme of 1972" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0306.html) );

"the Amending Scheme of 1978" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0246.html) );

"the No. 3 Amending Scheme of 1978" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 379 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0379.html) ).

5 Increase of retired pay of officers retired before 27th day of June, 1979.

###### 5.

**5.** The retired pay payable under Article 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 9A, 10 or 10A of the Principal Scheme (including retired pay increased under Article 13 (1) (*a*) of the Principal Scheme) to an officer who retired or was retired before the 27th day of June, 1979, shall, with effect from the 1st day of July, 1979, be the amount which would be payable to him if the date of his retirement had been the 27th day of June, 1979, and his appointment, rank, service in that rank and total service were the same as those on the date of his actual retirement.

6 Further increase of retired pay of officers retired before 2nd day of March, 1980.

###### 6.

**6.** The retired pay payable under Article 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 9A, 10 or 10A of the Principal Scheme (including retired pay increased under Article 13 (1) (*a*) of the Principal Scheme) to an officer who retired or was retired before the 2nd day of March, 1980, shall, with effect from the 1st day of July, 1980, be the amount which would be payable to him if the date of his retirement had been the 2nd day of March, 1980, and his appointment, rank, service in that rank and total service were the same as those on the date of his actual retirement.

7 Increase of retired pay under Article 19 of the Amending Scheme of 1960.

###### 7.

**7.** The retired pay payable under Article 19 of the Amending Scheme of 1960 to an officer shall, with effect from the 1st day of July, 1979, be the amount which would be payable to him if the date of his retirement had been the 27th day of June, 1979, and his rank, service in that rank and total service were the same as those on the date of his actual retirement.

9 Increase of pensions of certain deceased officers' widows.

###### 9.

**9.** (1) Article 20 (1) of the Principal Scheme shall, with effect from the 1st day of July, 1979, have effect as if for the Table thereto (inserted by Article 7 of the No. 3 Amending Scheme of 1978) there were substituted the following Table:—

TABLE

> "

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Rank | Amount of Pension |
| Second-Lieutenant or Lieutenant | £747.84 a year or 25 per cent of the deceased officer's pay, whichever is greater. |
| Captain | £916.80 a year or 30 per cent of the deceased officer's retired pay, whichever is greater. |
| Commandant | £1,081.08 a year or 31.5 per cent of the deceased officer's retired pay, whichever is greater. |
| Major or Lieutenant-Colonel | £1,226.16 a year or 28 per cent of the deceased officer's retired pay, whichever is greater. |
| Colonel | £1,464.72 a year of 27.5 per cent of the deceased officer's retired pay, whichever is greater. |
| Major-General or higher rank | £1,627.20 a year or 25.5 per cent of the deceased officer's retired pay, whichever is greater. |

> "

- (2) For purposes of Article 20 (1) of the Principal Scheme, as amended by this Article, a deceased officer's retired pay means—

( *a* ) in case the officer died while in receipt of retired pay, the retired pay which would be payable to him if the date of his retirement had been the 2nd day of July, 1979, and his appointment, rank, service in that rank and total service were the same as those on the date of his actual retirement,

( *b* ) in case the officer died before qualifying for retired pay, his accrued retired pay which will be calculated by crediting him with the amount of pensionable service including service in his rank which he would have had if he had served to the retiring age prescribed for his rank, or the age of 65 in the case of a person of higher rank than Major- General, and which will be based on the appropriate rate of retired pay in force on the 1st day of July, 1979, for an officer of his rank and service.

- (3) Notwithstanding anything contained in Article 20 (1) of the Principal Scheme, where an officer—

( *a* ) died while serving prior to the 23rd day of July, 1968, with five years pensionable service or more, or

( *b* ) retired prior to the 23rd day of July, 1968, and dies while in receipt of retired pay,

there may be paid to the widow of such deceased officer—

- (i) the appropriate amount in the Table to the said Article 20 (1) (inserted by this Article), or

- (ii) an amount equal to five-sixths of the pension which would be payable to her if her deceased husband had been a member of the Contributory Scheme administered by the Minister for the widows and children of officers of the Permanent Defence Force and the date of his death or retirement had been the 2nd day of July, 1979,

whichever is the greater.

- (4) This Article shall be deemed to have come into effect on the 1st day of July, 1979.

24 Retired pay and gratuities of certain officers.

###### 24.

**24.** (1) Where an officer who was appointed Assistant Chief of Staff—

( *a* ) was granted special leave without pay and allowances to take up an appointment as Force Commander of the United Nations Force in Cyprus,

( *b* ) did not qualify for any superannuation benefits other than a refund of his own contributions from the United Nations, and

( *c* ) retires or is retired from the Permanent Defence Force in the rank of Major-General

the yearly rate of his retired pay shall be the rate appropriate to the rank of Colonel, increased by 20 per cent.

- (2) The gratuity payable to an officer to whom subarticle (1) of this Article applies shall be the rate appropriate to the rank held by him at the date of his retirement.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Statutory Instrument provides for the Budgetary increases effective from 1st July, 1979, and 1st July, 1980, in the pensions of Army personnel (including members of the Nursing and Chaplaincy Services) payable under the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1981. It also amends in other respects the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes 1937 to 1981.
