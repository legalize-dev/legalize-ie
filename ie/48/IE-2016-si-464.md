# S.I. No. 464/2016 - Aquaculture (Licence Application) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th September*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0010.html#sec10) , [68](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0068.html#sec68) and [70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Aquaculture (Licence Application)(Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Aquaculture (Licence Application) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 236 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0236.html) ) are amended by substituting for Regulation 5(1)(i) (substituted by Regulation 2 of the Aquaculture (Licence Application) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0280.html) )) the following-

> - “(i) a seawater salmonid breeding installation (other than for trial or research purposes where the output would not exceed 50 tonnes), or”

Given under my Official Seal,

25 August 2016.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the Aquaculture (Licence Application) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 236 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0236.html) ) by specifying the Environmental Impact Statement requirements for applications solely for salmonid aquaculture research and development.
