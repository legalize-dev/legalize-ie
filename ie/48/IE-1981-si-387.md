# S.I. No. 387/1981 - Public Service (Designation of Bodies) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, LIAM KAVANAGH, Minister for the Public Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1973), and after consideration of the advice of the Public Service Advisory Council, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service (Designation of Bodies) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. Each body referred to or specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby designated for the purpose of the definition of “the public service” in [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1973).

## SCHEDULE

Every body that is a local authority for the purpose of the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) .

Eastern Health Board

Mid Western Health Board

Midland Health Board

North Eastern Health Board

North Western Health Board

Southern Health Board

South Eastern Health Board

Western Health Board

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these regulations is to bring local authorities and regional health boards within the scope of interest of the Public Service Advisory Council.
