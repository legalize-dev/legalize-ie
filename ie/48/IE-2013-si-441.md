# S.I. No. 441/2013 - Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th November*, 2013.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 9 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 811/2004 of 21 April 2004[^1] , Article 25 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 1342/2008 of 18 December 2008[^2] , Council Regulation (EC) No. 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009[^3] (other than Article 92) and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011[^4] (other than Title VII) hereby make the following regulations:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations have the purpose of correcting a typographical error in the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 354 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0354.html) ) in relation to the ICES areas for the landing of Cod and Pelagic Species.
