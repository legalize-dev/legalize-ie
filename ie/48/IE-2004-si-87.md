# S.I. No. 87/2004 - Electoral Act 1997 (Section 3) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), hereby makes the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electoral Act 1997 (Section 3) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. The aggregate of election expenses which may be incurred by or on behalf of a candidate in a European Parliament election constituency in connection with his or her candidature at the European election shall not exceed €230,000.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government this 3rd day of March 2004. |
|  | MARTIN CULLEN, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the setting of a limit of €230,000 for the aggregate election expenses for a candidate in any European Parliament constituency.
