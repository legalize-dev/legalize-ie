# S.I. No. 721/2024 - Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th December,* 2024.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/sec0003.html#sec3) (4) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1992), and the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0418.html) ), and the Public Expenditure and Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0019.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These regulations come into operation on 18 December 2024.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 11A of Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 84 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0084.html) ) as amended by the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 196 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0196.html) ) is amended-

- (a) in subparagraph (1) by substituting “to the member a sum not exceeding €25,000, save for in exceptional circumstances as may be determined at the discretion of the Oireachtas Commission after consideration of a report of a Crime Prevention Officer (CPO)” for “on a once-off basis to the member a sum not exceeding €5,000, or 50% of their total costs, whichever is the lesser,”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 December, 2024.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform.
