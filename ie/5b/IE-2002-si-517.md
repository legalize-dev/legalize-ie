# S.I. No. 517/2002 - Housing (New House Grant Etc.) Regulations, 1990 (Amendment) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966), as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1992), and by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0028/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Housing Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1988), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (New House Grant etc.) Regulations, 1990 (Amendment) Regulations, 2002.

###### 2.

2. The following article is hereby substituted for article 4 of the Housing (New House Grant etc.) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 34 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0034.html) ).

> “4. (1) This part shall apply to grants by the Minister under section 4 of the Act of 1979, as amended by the Act of 1988, for the provision of new houses.
>
> - (2) A grant under this part shall not be paid other than in cases where
>
>     - (a) an application for payment of a grant is
>
>         - (i) approved prior to 14 November 2002; or
>
>         - (ii) received by the Minister for the Environment and Local Government on or before 14 November 2002, or
>
>     - (b) (i) where a contract to purchase or a contract to build is entered into on or before 14 November 2002; or
>
>         - (ii) in the case of a “self build” house, where the foundations are poured on or before 14 November 2002; and
>
>           an application for payment of a grant under (b above is received by the Minister for the Environment and Local Government on or before 4 December 2002.
>
>     - (c) In all cases covered by (a) and (b) above a new house, the subject of a grant application must be completed and occupied and an application for payment of a grant received by the Minister for the Environment and Local Government on or before 13 November 2003.”

*Explanatory Note*

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These regulations provide for the payment of new house grants in certain circumstances where the conditions of the scheme are satisfied, following the abolition of the new house grant scheme.
