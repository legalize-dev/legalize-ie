# S.I. No. 648/2005 - European Communities (European Enforcement Order) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENT**

**( [S.I. No. 648 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0648.html) )**

**EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (EUROPEAN ENFORCEMENT ORDER) REGULATIONS 2005**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004[^1] creating a European Enforcement Order for uncontested claims make the following Regulations:

**Citation and commencement.**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (European Enforcement Order) Regulations 2005.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 21 October 2005.

**Interpretation.**

###### 2.

2. (1) A word or expression that is used in these Regulations and is also used in Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 April 2004 creating a European Enforcement Order for uncontested claims shall have in these Regulations the same meaning as it has in that Regulation unless the contrary intention appears.

- (2) A reference in these Regulations-

    - (a) to any enactment shall, unless the context otherwise requires be construed as a reference to that enactment as amended or extended by any other enactment,

    - (b) a reference to a Regulation is a reference to a Regulation of these Regulations unless it is indicated that a reference to some other provision is intended,

    - (c) a reference to a paragraph, subparagraph or clause is a reference to a paragraph, subparagraph or clause of the provision in which the reference occurs unless it is indicated that a reference to some other provision is intended.

**Amendment of section 84 of Courts of Justice Act 1924.**

###### 3.

3. [Section 84](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0084.html#sec84) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1924) is amended by substituting “Subject to Article 10(4) of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 April 2004 creating a European Enforcement Order for uncontested claims, an” for “An”.

**Amendment of Courts of Justice Act 1936.**

###### 4.

4. The [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936) is amended by inserting the following after section 38:

> **“Sections 37 and 38 not to prejudice Article 10(4) of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004.**
>
> **38A**. Neither section 37 nor 38 of this Act shall operate to prejudice the generality of Article 10(4) of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 April 2004 creating a European Enforcement Order for uncontested claims.”.

**Amendment of section 25 of Courts and Court Officers Act 1995.**

###### 5.

5. Section 25(5) of the [Courts and Courts Officers Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No.31 of 1995) is amended by substituting “Subject to Article 10(4) of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 April 2004 creating a European Enforcement Order for uncontested claims, all” for “All”.

**Amendment of section 34 of Courts and Court Officers Act 1995.**

###### 6.

6. Section 34(2) of the [Courts and Court Officers Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) is amended by substituting “Subject to Article 10(4) of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 April 2004 creating a European Enforcement Order for uncontested claims, all” for “All”.

**Enforcement of judgment, court settlement or authentic instrument on uncontested claim which has been certified as European Enforcement Order.**

###### 7.

7. Where a judgment, court settlement or authentic instrument on an uncontested claim has been certified as a European Enforcement Order in a Member State of origin, that judgment, court settlement or authentic instrument, as the case may be-

- (a) shall be of the same force and effect as a judgment of the High Court, and

- (b) may be enforced by the High Court, and proceedings taken on it, as if it were a judgment of that Court.

**Applications under Articles 6, 10, 21 and 23 of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004.**

###### 8.

8. (1) An application referred to in Article 6.1 of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004 shall be made to the court which delivered the judgment on the uncontested claim.

- (2) An application referred to in Article 6.2 or 10 of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004 shall be made to the court which delivered the judgment certified as a European Enforcement Order.

- (3) An application referred to in Article 6.3 of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004 shall be made to the court which delivered the decision following a challenge to a judgment certified as a European Enforcement Order.

- (4) The High Court shall have sole jurisdiction in relation to any application referred to in Article 21 or 23 of Regulation (EC) No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This Regulation sets out the effect on domestic legislation of Council Regulation (EC No. 805/2004 of 21 April 2004 creating a European Enforcement Order for Uncontested Claims and makes the necessary provisions for the good administration of the Regulation.
