# S.I. No. 241/1972 - Maternity Cash Grants Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 241 of 1972.

MATERNITY CASH GRANTS REGULATIONS, 1972.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947) and [Section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0064.html#sec64) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Maternity Cash Grants Regulations, 1972.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Minister" means the Minister for Health.

###### 3.

3. The prescribed conditions for the purpose of subsection (2) of [Section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0064.html#sec64) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , shall be that the woman shall have made application to the health board in a form approved by the Minister for this purpose within a period of three months of the confinement or such longer period as the health board may determine.

###### 4.

4. The Maternity Cash Grants Regulations, 1953 ( [S.I. No. 410 of 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/si/0410.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 27th

day of September, 1972.

**ERSKINE CHILDERS,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the conditions relating to the payment of a maternity cash grant under [section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0064.html#sec64) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .
