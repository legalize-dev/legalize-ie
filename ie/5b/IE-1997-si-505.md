# S.I. No. 505/1997 - Regional Fisheries Boards (Postponement of Elections) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 505 of 1997.

REGIONAL FISHERIES BOARDS (POSTPONEMENT OF ELECTIONS) ORDER, 1997

I, MICHAEL WOODS, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0301.html) )) hereby order as follows:.

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Regional Fisheries Boards (Postponement of Elections) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The year 1998 is hereby fixed as the year in which the elections of members of regional boards (which, but for this Article, would fall to be held in the year 1997) shall be held.

GIVEN under my Official seal, this 15th day ofDecember, 1997.

**MICHAEL WOODS.**

Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order provides for the postponement of elections for members of the Regional Fisheries Boards from 1997 to 1998.
