# S.I. No. 192/2004 - European Communities (Protection of Animals At Time of Slaughter) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 93/119/EC of 22 December 1993[^1] , hereby make the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Protection of Animals at Time of Slaughter) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Protection of Animals at Time of Slaughter) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0114.html) ) are amended, in point II.9 of the First Schedule, by substituting “12” for “24”.

###### 3.

3. [Section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0045/sec0012.html#sec12) (1)(*c*) of the [Slaughter of Animals Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1935) shall be applied as though “twenty-four hours” has been substituted by “twelve hours”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of April, 2004. |
|  | JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to change from 24 hours to 12 hours the length of period after which animals kept in a slaughter-house are required to be provided with suitable food.
