# S.I. No. 538/2006 - European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 538 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0538.html)**

**European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 2006**

I, MARTIN CULLEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving further effect to Article 7(1) of Council Directive No. 91/439/EEC of 29 July 1991[^1] , as amended by Council Directive No. 2000/56 of 14 September 2000[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 21st October 2006.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 4 of the European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No 52 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0052.html) ) is amended by substituting for paragraph 4 the following:

> - “(4) The theoretical test shall be divided into 4 tests as follows:
>
>     - (a) a test, in relation to vehicles of categories B, and W,
>
>     - (b) a test, in relation to vehicles of categories A, and M,
>
>     - (c) a test, in relation to vehicles of category C.
>
>     - (d) a test, in relation to vehicles of category D.”

Explanatory Note

These regulations revise the categories of driving theoretical test to provide for a separate motorcycle and moped test.
