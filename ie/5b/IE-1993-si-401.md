# S.I. No. 401/1993 - Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993 Establishment Day Order, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 401 of 1993.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (DUBLIN) ACT, 1993 ESTABLISHMENT DAY ORDER, 1993.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1993) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) , Establishment Day Order, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January, 1994 shall be the day appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, this 22nd day of December, 1993.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to fix 1 January, 1994 as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) ; on which day, *inter alia*, new administrative counties and county councils will be established for Fingal, South Dublin and Dun Laoghaire-Rathdown.
