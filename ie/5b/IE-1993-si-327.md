# S.I. No. 327/1993 - County of Kerry Traffic and Parking (Amendment) Bye-Laws, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 327 of 1993.

COUNTY OF KERRY TRAFFIC AND PARKING (AMENDMENT) BYE-LAWS, 1993.

I, PATRICK J. CULLIGAN, Commissioner of the Garda Síochána, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 89 and 90 (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968)) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 269 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0269.html) ), with the consent of the Minister for the Environment and after consultation with the council of the county of Kerry and the councils of the urban districts of Killarney, Listowel and Tralee hereby make the following bye-laws in respect of the area comprising the administrative county of Kerry, including the urban districts of Killarney, Listowel and Tralee:

###### 1.

1. These Bye-laws may be cited as the County of Kerry Traffic and Parking (Amendment) Bye-laws, 1993.

###### 2.

2. Bye-law 26 of the County of Kerry Traffic and Parking Bye-laws, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0184.html) ) is hereby amended by the insertion after "20p" of the following:

> "except that in respect of the area comprising the Urban district of Killarney the said fee shall be 30p"

and the said bye-law as so amended is set out in the Table to this bye-law.

TABLE

26. The fee for a parking disc shall be 20p except that in respect of the area comprising the Urban district of Killarney the said fee shall be 30p.

GIVEN under my hand, this 13th day of September, 1993.

PATRICK J. CULLIGAN,

Commissioner of the Garda Síochána.

The Minister for the Environment consents to the making of the foregoing Bye-laws.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 21st day of October, 1993.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Bye-laws amend Bye-law 26 of the County of Kerry Traffic and Parking Bye-laws 1986 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0184.html) ) to provide for an increase in the fee for a parking disc from 20p to 30p in the Urban district of Killarney. The fee for the rest of the county will remain at 20p.
