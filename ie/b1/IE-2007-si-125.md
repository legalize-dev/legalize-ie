# S.I. No. 125/2007 - National Treatment Purchase Fund Board (Establishment) Order, 2004 (Amendment) Order, 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 125 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0125.html)

NATIONAL TREATMENT PURCHASE FUND BOARD (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 2004 (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th March*, 2007.

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by Sections 3 to 6 of the [Health (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , (No. 27 of 1961), (as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treatment Purchase Fund Board (Establishment) Order, 2004 (Amendment) Order, 2007.

###### 2.

2. The National Treatment Purchase Fund Board (Establishment) Order 2004, [S.I.No. 179 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0179.html) , shall be amended by the substitution for sub-articles (1) and (2) of Article 7 of the following:

> “7. (1) The Chairperson shall receive such remuneration for acting as Chairperson of the Board as may from time to time be determined by the Minister, with the consent of the Minister for Finance.
>
> - (2) Each member of the Board, other than the Chairperson, shall receive such remuneration for acting as a member of the Board as may from time to time be determined by the Minister, with the consent of the Minister for Finance.
>
> - (3) Each member of the Board may be paid travelling and subsistence allowances in accordance with such scales as may from time to time be approved by the Minister, with the consent of the Minister for Finance".
>
>   GIVEN under my Official Seal
>
>   20 March 2007

**MARY HARNEY, TD**

Minister for Health and Children

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to amend the National Treatment Purchase Fund Board (Establishment) Order, 2004, S. I. No. 179 of 2004, to provide for the payment of remuneration to the members of the National Treatment Purchase Fund Board.
