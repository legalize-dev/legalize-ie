S.I. No. 71 of 1983.

FISHERIES ACT, 1980 (SECTION 26) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, PADDY O'TOOLE, Minister for Fisheries and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0195.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Section 26) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 14th day of March, 1983.

###### 3.

3. Every instrument which is an instrument of appointment referred to in [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980), shall be prepared and printed in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the instrument of appointment by the Central Fisheries Board or a Regional Fisheries Board of an officer or other person to be an authorised person for the purposes of Part XVIII of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
