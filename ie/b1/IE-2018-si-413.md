# S.I. No. 413/2018 - European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th October*, 2018.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1776 of 6 October 2016[^1] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1814 of 13 October 2016[^2] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/324 of 24 February 2017[^3] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/335 of 27 February 2017[^4] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/839 of 17 May 2017[^5] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/871 of 22 May 2017[^6] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2016 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Union (Food Additives) Regulations 2015 to 2018.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Food Additives) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0330.html) );

“Regulations of 2016” means the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 484 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0484.html) ).

###### 3.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1776 of 6 October 2016, Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1814 of 13 October 2016, Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/324 of 24 February 2017, Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/335 of 27 February 2017, Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/839 of 17 May 2017 and Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/871 of 22 May 2017, which amend Regulation (EC) No. 1333/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 231/2012 of 9 March 2012.

These Regulations amend the European Union (Food Additives) Regulations 2015 for that purpose.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 and they come into effect on the date they are signed.
