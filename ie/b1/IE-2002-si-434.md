# S.I. No. 434/2002 - European Communities (Second General System For The Recognition of Professional Education and Training) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, **[illegible]** Minister for Education and Science in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive No. **[illegible]** of 25 February 2000. hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Second General System for the Recognition of Professional Education and Training (Amendment) Regulations 2002.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 20^th August 2002.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2(1) of the European Communities (Second General System for the Recognition of Professional Education and Training) Regulations. 1996 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0135.html) ). is amended by substituting for the definition of “the directive” the following:

> “ ‘the Directive’ means Council Directive No. 92/51/EEC[^2] of 18 June 1992 as amended by Commission Directive No. 94/38/EC of 26 July 1994[^3] , Commission Directive No. 95/43/EC[^4] of 20 July 1995, Commission Directive No. 1997/38 of 20 June 1997[^5] and Commission Directive No. 2000/5/EC[^1] of 25 February 2000;”

**Expalantory Note**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

The purpose of these regulations is to give legal effect to Commission Directive No. 2000/5/EC[^6] of 26 February, 2000 which provides for the amendment of Annexes C and D to Directive 92/51/EEC. Annex C to Directive 92/51/EEC is a list of courses which have a special structure as outlined in Article 1(a)(ii) of that Directive. Annex to Directive 92/51/EEC is a list of courses which have a special structure as outlined in Article 3(b) of that Directive. The amendments relate to courses in Austria and the United Kingdom.
