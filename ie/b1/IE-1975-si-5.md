# S.I. No. 5/1975 - Imposition of Duties (No. 216) (Excise Duties) (Vehicles) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 5 of 1975.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 216) (EXCISE DUTIES) (VEHICLES) ORDER, 1975.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), hereby make the following order :

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 216) (Excise Duties) (Vehicles) Order, 1975.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1975.

###### 2.

2. The Imposition of Duties (No. 170) (Excise Duties) (Vehicles) Order, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 68 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0068.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph 3 :

" 3. The duty of excise imposed by section 1 of the Act at the rates specified in paragraph 3 (*b*) of Part 1 of the Schedule to the Act shall not be charged or levied in respect of vehicles which are—

- (i) not used for the carriage of persons ( other than children, or children and teachers, being carried to or from school or to or from school-related physical education activities) for reward, and

- (ii) either licensed under Article 60 of the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) Regulations, 1963 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0191.html) ), as amended, or owned or operated by a statutory transport undertaking."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 10th day ofJanuary, 1975.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Order is to secure that school buses, which are taxed at the private car rate, may be used, without paying road tax at a higher level, for the carriage of children, or children and teachers to or from school or to or from school-related physical education activities. The Order comes into operation on 1st February, 1975.
