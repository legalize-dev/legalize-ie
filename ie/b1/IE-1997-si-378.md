> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 378 of 1997.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 6) SCHEME, 1997 DUBLIN

**Bord Telecom Éireann plc**, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 6) Scheme, 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1997" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Article 4 (*a*) and Article 4 (*b*) shall be deemed to come into operation on 1 June 1997.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by;

( *a* ) the substitution of the following Part I of the First Schedule for Part I of the First Schedule thereto:
