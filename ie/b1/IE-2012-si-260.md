# S.I. No. 260/2012 - Land Purchase Acts Rules 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th July*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0038/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Land Act 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1933) as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0024/print.html#sec9) of the [Land Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 2005) as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) ) and after consultation with the Judge of the High Court exercising the jurisdiction of the Judicial Commissioner and with the President of the Law Society of Ireland, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Land Purchase Acts Rules 2012.

###### 2.

2. (1) A person seeking a service listed in the Schedule shall submit an application to the Minister.

- (2) An application for a service shall be accompanied by the fee mentioned opposite that service in the Schedule.

- (3) The Minister may refuse to deal with any application where that application is not accompanied by the appropriate fee.

- (4) A fee may be paid—

    - (*a*) in cash,

    - (*b*) by means of cheque, banker’s draft, money order or postal order payable to the Minister, or

    - (*c*) by another means if the Minister has given prior approval for an alternative means of payment.

###### 3.

3. The Land Purchase Acts Rules 1984 ( [S.I. No. 253 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0253.html) ) are revoked.

**Schedule**

Search fee for each document or map €10

Copy fee for each document or map €10

Given under my Official Seal,

19 July 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY TD,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules provide for revised fees for searching for and copying documents and maps of the former Irish Land Commission. The documents and maps are stored in the Records Branch of the Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine.
