# S.I. No. 332/1982 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 2, Fifty-Eighth Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 332 of 1982.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 2, FIFTY-EIGHTH PERIOD) (CYCLE TYRES) ORDER, 1982.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 2, Fifty-Eighth Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints a nominal quota of 200 articles as the quantity of cycle tyres and inner tubes which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1983 to 31st December,1983. This nominal quota has been appointed to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister is prepared to license, during the quota period, all necessary importations consonant with the restrictions in operation for different countries.
