# S.I. No. 458/2001 - Local Government Act, 2001 (Commencement) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec7) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act, 2001 (Commencement) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. On the 9^th day of October, 2001, subsections (1), (6) and (7) of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec1) and [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec2) , [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec3) , [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec4) , [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec6) , [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec7) , [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec8) and [247](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec247) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) shall come into operation.

###### 3.

3. On the 9^th day of October, 2001 section 5(1) of, and Part 1 of Schedule 3 to, the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) shall come into operation to the extent specified in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. On the 1^st day of January, 2002, Part 23 of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) shall come into operation.

>**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order brings into operation on the 9^th day of October 2001 certain general provisions in Part 1 of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) ; also section 247 which provides for certain technical amendments to the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) : as well as the repeal of certain enactments specified in the Schedule to the Order. The order also brings into operation on 1^st day of January, 2002, Part 23 of the Act, which relates to transfer of shareholding in Temple Bar Properties Limited to Dublin Corporation.
