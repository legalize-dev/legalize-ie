# S.I. No. 371/2009 - National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 371 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0371.html)

NATIONAL MILK AGENCY (ELECTION DAY) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th September*, 2009.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 20 of, and paragraph 5(2) of the Schedule to, the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations, 2009.

###### 2.

2. The 27th day of October 2009 is prescribed as an election day for producer members of the National Milk Agency.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 August 2009

**BRENDAN SMITH**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to fix 27th October 2009 as an election day for the National Milk Agency.
