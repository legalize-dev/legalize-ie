# S.I. No. 366/1991 - Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 366 of 1991.

HEALTH (IN-PATIENT CHARGES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1991.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on her by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 of the Health (In-Patients Charges) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0116.html) ) (as amended by article 2 of the Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 335 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0335.html) )) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£15" for "£12.50" and by the substitution of "£150" for "£125" in sub article (2).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st Day of January, 1992.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this

30th day of December, 1991.

**MARY O'ROURKE,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this

30th day of December, 1991.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 by raising the daily charge for in-Patient services form £12.50 to £15.00 and by raising the maximum amount payable in any period of twelve consecutive months from £125 to £150.
