# S.I. No. 221/2000 - Local Government (Tenure of Office) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) and [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1991), hereby specifies and orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Tenure of Office) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding article 4 of the Local Government (Tenure of Office) Order, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0128.html) ), as respects a person who is appointed to an office of manager on or after the date of the making of this Order, 7 years shall be the specified period for the purposes of section 47(1)(*a*) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) and 65 years shall be the specified age for the purposes of [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0047.html#sec47) (1)(*b*) of that Act.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation thereof.)*

This Order specifies a period and age for the purposes of [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0047.html#sec47) (1) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) .
