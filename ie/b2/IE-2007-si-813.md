# S.I. No. 813/2007 - National Safety Council Dissolution Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 813 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0813.html)

NATIONAL SAFETY COUNCIL DISSOLUTION ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st December*, 2007.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5 of Local Government Services (Corporate Bodies) Act 1971 (No. 6 of 1971) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Safety Council Dissolution Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The following are revoked:

- (*a*) the National Safety Council (Establishment) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 327 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0327.html) ), and

- (*b*) the National Safety Council (Establishment) Order 1987 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1999 ( [S.I. No. 360 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0360.html) ),

and, accordingly, the National Safety Council is dissolved.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 October 2007

**NOEL DEMPSEY.**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order revokes the National Safety Council (Establishment) Order 1987 and the National Safety Council (Establishment) Order 1987 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1999
