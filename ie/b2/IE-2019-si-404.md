# S.I. No. 404/2019 - National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) (Amendment) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd August,* 2019.

The Government, at the request of the Minister for Health (being a Minister of the Government in whom functions in relation to a State authority are vested) and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec9) (5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) (Amendment) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. The National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 63 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0063.html) ) is amended, in Schedule 2, by the insertion of “Children’s Health Ireland”.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

25 July, 2019.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of the Delegation of Functions Order is to provide that Children’s Health Ireland may be added to the Clinical Indemnity Scheme. This scheme provides cover in respect of personal injury claims for clinical incidents occurring in public hospitals and HSE facilities.
