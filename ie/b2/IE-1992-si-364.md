# S.I. No. 364/1992 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 364 of 1992.

DERELICT SITES (URBAN AREAS) REGULATIONS, 1992.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 1992.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 and 1991 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 1992.

2 Prescription of urban areas.

###### 2.

**2.** The areas in the administrative counties of Clare, Dublin, Tipperary (North Riding) and Westmeath indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
