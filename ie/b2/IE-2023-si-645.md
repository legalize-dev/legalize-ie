# S.I. No. 645/2023 - Planning And Development, Maritime And Valuation (Amendment) Act 2022 (Commencement Of Certain Provisions) (No. 2) Order 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th December,* 2023.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec1) (4) of the [Planning and Development, Maritime and Valuation (Amendment) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2022), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development, Maritime and Valuation (Amendment) Act 2022 (Commencement of Certain Provisions) (No. 2) Order 2023.

###### 2.

2. The 16th day of December 2023 is appointed as the day on which the following provisions of the [Planning and Development, Maritime and Valuation (Amendment) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) shall come into operation:

- (a) sections 10 to 21,

- (b) sections 23 to 40, and

- (c) subsections (8) to (12) of section 41.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 December, 2023.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.
