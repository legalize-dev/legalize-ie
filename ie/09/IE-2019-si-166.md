# S.I. No. 166/2019 - Finance (Local Property Tax) Act 2012 (Section 13(3)) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd April,* 2019.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0052/print.html#sec13) (3) of the [Finance (Local Property Tax) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0052/print.html) (No.52 of 2012), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Finance (Local Property Tax) Act 2012 (Section 13(3)) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. The valuation date referred to in subsection (2) of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0052/print.html#sec13) of the [Finance (Local Property Tax) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0052/print.html) (No.52 of 2012) is altered by the substitution of the following paragraphs for paragraphs (a) and (b) of that subsection:

- (a) 1 May 2013 for the years 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020, and

- (b) For each consecutive 3-year period after the year 2020, 1 November in the year preceding the first year of the particular 3 year period”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 April 2019

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order defers the revaluation date for the local property tax from 1 November 2019 to 1 November 2020.
