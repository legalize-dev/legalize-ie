# S.I. No. 304/1985 - Imposition of Duties (No. 280) (Excise Duties) (Vehicles) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 304 of 1985.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 280) (EXCISE DUTIES)(VEHICLES) ORDER, 1985.

The Government, in excise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), and [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0006/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Finance Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1975), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 280) (Excise Duties) (Vehicles) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The [Finance Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1985), is hereby amended by the insertion in section 33 (2)(h), after

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| "exceeding 2,387 cubic centimeters but not exceeding 2,512 cubic centimetres ... ... | £320.00 |
| of |  |
| "exceeding 2,512 cubic centimetres but not exceeding 2,637 cubic centimetres ... ... | £357.00". |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 12th day of September, 1985.

**GARRET FITZGERALD,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order rectifies a textual defect in [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0010/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Finance Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , the section which substituted scales of road tax rates based on engine cubic capacity (c.c.) for those based on road tax horse-power, from 1 October 1985. In that section the rate for private motor cars of twenty-one horse-power under the former system was omitted from the new scale of rates, an omission made good by this order.
