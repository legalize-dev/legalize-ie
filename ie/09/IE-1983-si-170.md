# S.I. No. 170/1983 - Export Promotion (Provision of Services) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 170 of 1983.

EXPORT PROMOTION (PROVISION OF SERVICES) ORDER, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0020/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Export Promotion Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1959) (inserted by the [Export Promotion (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1983)), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Export Promotion (Provision of Services) Order, 1983, and shall come into operation on the 23rd day of June, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The following service activities are hereby specified for the purpose of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0020/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Export Promotion Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1959) (inserted by the [Export Promotion (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1983) ):—

( *a* ) agricultural development and processing services;

( *b* ) construction-related services;

( *c* ) medical services;

( *d* ) training services covering all activities;

( *e* ) technical and general consulting services, including commercial laboratory services and research and development services;

( *f* ) international financial services;

( *g* ) computer software and data processing services;

( *h* ) public administration services;

( *i* ) media recording and publishing services.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day ofJune, 1983.

**F. CLUSKEY,**

Minister for Trade, Commerce and

Tourism.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order specifies, in Paragraph 2, those service activities which Córas Tráchtála may promote, assist and develop for export purposes.
