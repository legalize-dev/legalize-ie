# S.I. No. 245/1982 - Coras Iompair Eireann Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (Amendment) Scheme, 1982 (Confirmation) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 245 of 1982.

CORAS IOMPAIR EIREANN SUPERANNUATION SCHEME, 1951 (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1982 (CONFIRMATION) ORDER, 1982.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Transport Act, 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), as adapted by the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0011.html) ), hereby, after consultation with the Minister for the Public Service, order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Coras Iompair Eireann Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (Amendment) Scheme, 1982 (Confirmation) Order, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The scheme set out in the Schedule to this Order, being a scheme amending the CIE Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (set out in the Schedule to the Coras Iompair Eireann Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (Confirmation) Order, 1951 ( [S.I. No. 353 of 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/si/0353.html) )) as amended, which was prepared and submitted to the Minister for Transport by Coras Iompair Eireann pursuant to [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Transport Act, 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), is hereby confirmed and shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1982.

SCHEDULE

Coras Iompair Eireann, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [transport Act, 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), hereby submits to the Minister the following scheme:—

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Coras Iompair Eireann Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (Amendment) Scheme, 1982.

2. Rule 21 (7) (*a*) of the Coras Iompair Eireann Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (inserted by Paragraph 3 of the scheme contained in the Schedule to the C.I.E. Superannuation Scheme, 1951 (Amendment) Scheme, 1981 (Confirmation) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 126 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0126.html) ) shall, during the period beginning on the 1st day of March, 1982, and ending on the 28th day of February, 1985, be construed and have effect as if the reference therein to the 1st day of March, 1982, were a reference to the 1st day of March, 1985.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of July, 1982.

**JOHN P. WILSON.**

Minister for Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of the scheme is to provide for the reduction, under certain conditions, of the retiring ages prescribed in the existing scheme, to allow for voluntary early retirement on redundancy.
