# S.I. No. 602/2021 - Sea-fisheries (Revocation) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd November,* 2021.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011[^1] , Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^2] and Commission Regulation (EC) No 129/2003 of 24 January 2003[^3] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Revocation) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. The following statutory instruments are revoked:

- (*a*) the Passive Fishing Gear and Beam Trawls Marking and Identification (No.2) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 341 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0341.html) );

- (*b*) the Sea Fisheries (Tuna and Certain Other Species Fishing) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 353 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0353.html) );

- (*c*) the Sea Fisheries (Tuna and Certain Other Species Fishing) Order, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 322 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0322.html) );

- (*d*) the Sea Fisheries (Regulation of Nets) Order, 1993, ( [S.I. No. 264 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0264.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 November, 2021.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations revoke certain Statutory Instruments relating to sea fisheries which are no longer in force/redundant.
