# S.I. No. 262/1983 - Jury Districts Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 262 of 1983.

JURY DISTRICTS ORDER, 1983.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me be [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order, 1983.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 5th day of September, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The county of Cavan shall be and is hereby divided into the jury districts specified in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE.

JURY DISTRICT NO. 1 (CAVAN)

*Consisting of the District Electoral Divisions of*:—

Ardue, Arvagh, Ballintemple, Ballyconnell, Ballyhaise, Ballymachugh, Ballymagauran, Bawnboy, Bellananagh, Belturbet (Urban), Benbrack, Bilberry, Brucehall, Butlersbridge, Carrafin, Carn, Castlesaunderson, Cavan Rural, Cavan Urban, Clonervy, Corr, Crossdoney, Crosskeys, Cuttragh, Denn, Derrin, Derrylahan, Dorrynananta, Diamond, Doogarry, Dowra, Drumcarban, Drumcarn, Drumlumman, Dunmakeever, Drung, Esky, Graddum, Grilly, Kill, Killeshandra, Killinagh, Killykeen, Kilbride, Kilcogy, Kilconny, Kilgolagh, Kilnaleck, Kinawley, Lissanover, Loughdawan, Milltown, Moynehall, Pedara Vohers, Rakenny, Redhill, Scrabby, Springfield, Stradone, Swanlinbar, Teebane, Templeport, Tircahan, Tuam.

JURY DISTRICT NO. 2 (BAILIEBOROUGH)

*Consisting of the District Electoral Divisions of*:—

Ashfield, Bailieborough, Ballyjamesduff, Carnagarve, Canningstown, Castlerahan, Cootehill Rural, Cootehill Urban, Corroneary, Crossbane, Drumanespic, Enniskeen, Killenkere, Kingscourt, Knappagh, Larah North, Larah South, Lisagoan, Lurgan, Mullagh, Munterconnaught, Shercock, Skeagh, Taghart, Termon, Tullyvin West, Tullyvin East, Virginia, Waterloo.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of September, 1983.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order divides the County of Cavan into two jury districts with effect from the 5th day of September, 1983.
