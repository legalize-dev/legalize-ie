# S.I. No. 375/2023 - European Union (Protection of Persons Who Report Breaches of Union Law) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st July,* 2023.

I, PASCHAL DONOHUE, Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2019/1937 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2019[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Protection of Persons Who Report Breaches of Union Law) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 22 July 2023.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations –

“Act of 2022” means the [Protected Disclosures (Amendment) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2022);

“Principal Act” means the [Protected Disclosures Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2014).

###### 4.

4. Section 5 (amended by section 6 of the Act of 2022) of the Principal Act is amended, in subsection (1), by the insertion of “7B,” after “7,”.

###### 5.

5. Section 5A (inserted by section 7 of the Act of 2022) of the Principal Act is amended, in subsection (2), by the insertion of “7B,” after “7,”.

###### 6.

6. Section 6A (inserted by section 9 of the Act of 2022) of the Principal Act is amended, in subsection (1)(g)(iii), by the insertion of “in the manner specified in section 7B” after “European Union”.

###### 7.

7. The Principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following section after section 7A (inserted by section 11 of the Act of 2022):

> “**Disclosure to relevant institutions, bodies, offices or agencies of European Union**
>
> **7B.** A disclosure is made in the manner specified in this section if the worker –
>
> - (a) makes the disclosure to a relevant institution, body, office or agency of the European Union, and
>
> - (b) has reasonable grounds to believe that the information on breaches reported was true at the time of reporting and that such information fell within the scope of the Directive.”.

###### 8.

8. Section 10 (amended by section 13 of the Act of 2022) of the Principal Act is amended, in paragraph (c) –

- (a) in subparagraph (i), by the substitution of “as the case may be,” for “as the case may be, or”,

- (b) by the insertion of the following subparagraph after subparagraph (i):

  > - “(ia) has previously made a disclosure of substantially the same information in the manner specified in section 7B, or”,

  and

- (c) in subparagraph (ii)(II), by the insertion of “, 7B” after “7”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 July, 2023.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform.
