# S.I. No. 74/1987 - Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935 (Part II) (No. 12) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 74 of 1987.

PIGS AND BACON ACT, 1935 (PART II) (No. 12) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0008.html#sec8) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1978)) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), and the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations, with respect to which, pursuant to subsection 7 of the said section 28, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 12) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 11) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 247 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0247.html) ), are hereby amended by the insertion after Regulation 3 of the following Regulation:

> "3A Notwithstanding the provisions of Regulation 3 of these Regulations, for the purposes of [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , the prescribed sum shall, for the period beginning on the 1st day of July, 1986, and ending on the 30th day of June, 1987, be 50p."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of March, 1987.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations reduce as and from 1 July, 1986 the fees payable by bacon curers for veterinary examination of pigs in accordance with the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
