# S.I. No. 577/2024 - Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022 (Section 11) Order 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th October,* 2024.

WHEREAS the Government considers it necessary to by order increase -

- (a) the amount referred to in [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec10) (3) of the [Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) , and

- (b) the amount referred to in each of paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec10) (4) of the [Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ;

AND WHEREAS it is more than 12 months after the coming into operation of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec11) of the [Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ;

AND WHEREAS no order has heretofore been made under subsection (1)(a) or subsection (1)(b) of the said section 11;

AND WHEREAS the Government has -

- (a) in relation to Article 3(a) of the following order, had regard to the matters referred to in subsection (6) of the said section 11,

- (b) in relation to Article 3(b) of the following order, had regard to the matters referred to in subsections (6)(b) and (7)(a) of the said section 11,

- (c) in relation to Article 3(c) of the following order, had regard to the matters referred to in subsections (6)(b) and (7)(b) of the said section 11,

- (d) in relation to Article 3(d) of the following order, had regard to the matters referred to in subsections (6)(b) and (7)(c) of the said [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec11) ;

AND WHEREAS the Minister, for the purposes of preparing a draft of the following order, commissioned such research as he considered appropriate under subsection (8) of the said [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec11) ;

AND WHEREAS pursuant to subsection (9) of the said [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec11) , a draft of the following order has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving the draft has been passed by each such House of the Oireachtas;

NOW the Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec11) (1) of the [Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022 (Section 11) Order 2024.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Act of 2022” means Remediation of Dwellings Damaged by the Use of Defective Concrete Blocks Act 2022.

###### 3.

3. The amount referred to -

- (a) in section 10(3) of the Act of 2022 shall stand increased to €462,000,

- (b) in section 10(4)(a) of the Act of 2022 shall stand increased to €16,500,

- (c) in section 10(4)(b) of the Act of 2022 shall stand increased to €5,500, and

- (d) in section 10(4)(c) of the Act of 2022 shall stand increased to €5,500.

###### 4.

4. This Order shall come into operation on the 23rd day of October 2024.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

22 October, 2024.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Taoiseach.
