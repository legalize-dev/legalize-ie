# S.I. No. 114/1987 - Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1987

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 114 of 1987.

HEALTH SERVICES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) (3) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) as substituted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Health (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1987) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

" the Act" means the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. The following diseases and disabilities are hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 56 (3) of the Act: mental handicap, mental illness, phenylketonuria, cystic fibrosis, spina bifida, hydrocephalus, haemophilia and cerebral palsy.

###### 4.

4. Article 7 of the health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0277.html) ) is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 14th day of April, 1987.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 14th day of April, 1987.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the diseases and disabilities in respect of which out-patient services are to be provided without charge for children under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .
