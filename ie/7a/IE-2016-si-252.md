S.I. No. 252 of 2016

DISTRICT COURT (CRIMINAL JUSTICE (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT 1997) RULES 2016

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which shall come into operation on the 16th day of June 2016, may be cited as the District Court (Criminal Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1997) Rules 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. (1) The forms numbered 34.37 and 34.38 in Schedule 1 shall be substituted for the forms bearing the like numbers respectively in Schedule B to the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

I AM SATISFIED THAT there are reasonable grounds for suspecting that—

evidence of or relating to the commission of an arrestable offence (within the meaning of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0014/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Criminal Law Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , as amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec8) of the [Criminal Justice Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) ), to wit, the offence of ......, contrary to..........., is to be found in a place (within the meaning of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0004/sec0010.html#sec10) (6) of the [Criminal Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) ), namely............... in the court (area and) district aforesaid.

THIS IS TO AUTHORISE.........................., of..........., a member of the Garda Síochána, accompanied by such other members of the Garda Síochána or persons or both as the said member thinks necessary,

TO ENTER, at any time or times within one week of the date of issue of this warrant, on production if so requested of this warrant, and if necessary by the use of reasonable force, the place namely.............. in the said court (area and) district as aforesaid,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Regulation and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These rules amend the District Court Rules by substituting forms 34.37 and 34.38 in Schedule B to the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).
