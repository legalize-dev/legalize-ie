# S.I. No. 193/2018 - Water Services Act 2017 (Part 5) (Establishment Day) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th June*, 2018.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec24) of the [Water Services Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2017), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Water Services Act 2017 (Part 5) (Establishment Day) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. The 11th day of June 2018 is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of Part 5 of the [Water Services Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2017).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 June 2018.

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order establishes, on 11 June 2018, An Fóram Uisce or, in the English Language the Water Forum to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Water Services Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) .
