# S.I. No. 67/1988 - National Social Service Board (No. 2) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 67 of 1988.

NATIONAL SOCIAL SERVICE BOARD (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (7) of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [National Social Service Board Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1984), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Social Service Board (No. 2) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. Article 7 of the National Social Service Board Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0019.html) ), is hereby repealed.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 13th day of April, 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to repeal a provision varying the number of members of the National Social Service Board, thereby bringing back into operation the original provision in the [National Social Service Board Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) .
