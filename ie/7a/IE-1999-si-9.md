# S.I. No. 9/1999 - Western Development Commission Act, 1998 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0042/sec0006.html#sec6) , of the [Western Development Commission Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) (No. 42 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Western Development Commission Act, 1998 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of February, 1999, is hereby appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Western Development Commission Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) (No. 42 of 1998).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal this 27th day of January, 1999. |
|  | JOE WALSH, |
|  | Minister for Agriculture and Food. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order appoints 1 February, 1999 as the day on which the Western Development Commission is to be established.
