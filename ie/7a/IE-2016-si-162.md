# S.I. No. 162/2016 - Pet Passport (No. 2) Regulations 2014 Amendment Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th April*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec36) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013) and, for the purposes of giving further effect to Article 10 of Council Directive 92/65/EEC of 13 July 1992[^1] (as amended by Directive 2013/31/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013) and Regulation (EU) No. 576/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pet Passport (No. 2) Regulations 2014 Amendment Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Pet Passport (No. 2) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 602 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0602.html) ) are amended

- (1) by the insertion after Regulation 9 of—

  > “ 9A.(1) A person shall not import or attempt to import a cat, dog or ferret from another member state unless the cat, dog or ferret—
  >
  > - (*a*) satisfies the conditions set out in Article 6 of the Pet Passport Regulation,
  >
  > - (*b*) has undergone a clinical examination by a veterinary practitioner carried out within 48 hours of dispatch, and
  >
  > - (*c*) is accompanied by a duly completed health certificate which corresponds to the specimen in Part 1 of Annex E to the Council Directive.”, and

- (2) by the insertion in Regulation 11, after “9” of “, 9A”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 April 2016.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the Pet Passport (No. 2) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 602 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0602.html) ) to provide for the creation of an offence for the commercial movement into the State from other Member States, of cats, dogs and ferrets that are not compliant with the EU Regulations.
