# S.I. No. 270/1986 - Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 270 of 1986.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANISATION (SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS) (DUBLIN) (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1986.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1985), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0128.html) ), as amended by the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0418.html) ) and by the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 70 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0070.html) ), is hereby amended by:—

( *a* ) the deletion in paragraph (*a*) of "until the 1st day of August, 1986", and

( *b* ) the deletion in paragraph (*f*) of ",until the 31st day of December, 1986".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

28th day of July, 1986.

**JOHN BOLAND,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the continuation in effect for the time being of certain provisions of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) and of the School Attendance Acts, 1926 to 1967, in the added areas and in the excluded areas as defined in [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
