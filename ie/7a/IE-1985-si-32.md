# S.I. No. 32/1985 - State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984 (Section 2 (3)) (Cesser) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 32 of 1985.

STATE FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, 1984 (SECTION 2 (3)) (CESSER) ORDER, 1985.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance, having ceased to be satisfied that, owing to special circumstances of a temporary nature, it is not possible, as regards certain securities, being securities to which, immediately before the commencement of the Order following, Orders under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/sec0002.html#sec2) (3) of the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1984), related, to effect the redemption of, or to pay interest or dividends as regards, those securities, hereby, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 (1) (*b*) of the said Act, make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 2 (3)) (Cesser) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The 14th day of February, 1985, is hereby appointed as the day on which subsection (3) of Section 2 of the State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act (No. 23 of 1984), shall cease to be in force.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of February, 1985.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order specifies the date from which the Minister's power to make further postponement orders under [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/sec0002.html#sec2) (3) of the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) is cancelled.
