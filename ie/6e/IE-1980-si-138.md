# S.I. No. 138/1980 - Conditions of Employment (Manufacture of Workmates, Jobbers and Drill Attachments) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 138 of 1980.

CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT (MANUFACTURE OF WORKMATES, JOBBERS AND DRILL ATTACHMENTS) (EXCLUSION) REGULATIONS, 1980.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these Regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following regulations;

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Manufacture of Workmates, Jobbers and Drill Attachments) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1980.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely, the manufacture of workmates, jobbers and drill attachments as carried on at the premises of Black and Decker (Ireland) Limited, Melitta Road, Kildare, in the county of Kildare, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purpose of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provisions shall have effect in respect of the work to which these Regulations relate:

( *a* ) no woman shall be employed to do any such work at any time between the hour of 11 p.m. and the hour of 7 a.m. the following morning;

( *b* ) no woman shall be employed on any day until the expiration of eleven hours from the time at which she ceased to do industrial work on the previous day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 8th day of May, 1980

**GENE FITZGERALD**

Minister for Labour

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) prohibits the Employment of adult female workers between the hours of 10 p. m. on any day and 8 a. m. the following morning. The effect of these regulations is to permit the employment of adult females on 8hour shifts between the hours of 7 a. m. and 11 p.m. on the manufacture and assembly of workmates, jobbers and drill attachments as carried on at the premises of Black and Decker (Ireland) Limited, Melitta Road, Kildare.
