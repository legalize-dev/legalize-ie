# S.I. No. 76/1992 - Competition Act, 1991 (Notice of Licences and Certificates) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 76 of 1992.

COMPETITION ACT, 1991 (NOTICE OF LICENCES AND CERTIFICATES) REGULATIONS, 1992.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1991), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. ( *a* ) These Regulations may be cited as the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Notice of Licences and Certificates) Regulations, 1992.

( *b* ) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 13th day of April, 1992.

###### 2.

2. Notice of the grant of a licence or the issue of a certificate under section 4 shall be given to every body to which it relates by being sent by registered post to that body at the address indicated in the relevant notification under section 7 as the address of that body.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of April, 1992.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the appropriate manner in which the Competition Authority shall give notice of its decisions.
