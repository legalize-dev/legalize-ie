# S.I. No. 748/2021 - Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975 (Fixed Charge Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th January,* 2022.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1975), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975 (Fixed Charge Offences) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of February 2022.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 136 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0136.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (a):

> - “(aa) €80, in respect of the offence under section 35 (5) of the Act of 1994 for a contravention of the provision of the Regulations mentioned in Schedule 1A,”.

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended by the insertion of the following Schedule after Schedule 1:

> **“***Regulation 5(aa)*

## **Schedule 1A** **€80 fixed charge**

Offence under Section 35(5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html)

Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 (S.I . No. 182 of 1997)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Article** | **Description of Offence** |
| 36(2)(i) | Parking a vehicle on a footway, a grass margin or a median strip |
| 36(2)(m) | Parking a vehicle in a cycle track where traffic sign number RUS 009, RUS 058 or RUS 059 is provided |
| 39 | Parking a vehicle in a bus lane or on a bus only street”. |

6. The Table in Part 3 of Schedule 2 to the Principal Regulations is amended -

- (*a*) in the column entitled “Article”, by the insertion of “other than paragraphs (2)(i) and (m)” after “36 (as amended by Article 7 of [S.I. No. 274 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0274.html) )”,

- (*b*) in the column entitled “Article”, by the deletion of “39”, and

- (*c*) in the column entitled “Description of Offence”, by the deletion of “Contravention of prohibition on parking a vehicle in a bus lane/bus-only street”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 December, 2021.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations increase the fixed charge for parking on a footway, a cycle track, or a bus lane.
