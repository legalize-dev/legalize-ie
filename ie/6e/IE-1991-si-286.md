# S.I. No. 286/1991 - Derelict Sites (Commencement of Derelict Sites Levy) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 286 of 1991.

DERELICT SITES (COMMENCEMENT OF DERELICT SITES LEVY) REGULATIONS, 1991.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0023.html#sec23) (1) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Commencement of Derelict Sites Levy) Regulations, 1991.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** The year 1992 is hereby prescribed as the year in which the derelict sites levy shall begin.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

20th day of November, 1991.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe 1992 as the year in which the derelict sites levy under the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) shall come into operation.
