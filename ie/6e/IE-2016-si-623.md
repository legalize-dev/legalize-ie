# S.I. No. 623/2016 - Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th December*, 2016.

I, MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec12) and [737](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec737) (1)(*b*) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2016

###### 2.

2. The form (Form H1) set out in the Schedule to these Regulations is substituted for the form at part 44 of the Schedule to the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (Forms) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0147.html) ).

## SCHEDULE

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 December 2016.

**MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe an amended form for the purposes of [section 737](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec737) (1)(*b*) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) .
