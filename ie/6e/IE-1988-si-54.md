# S.I. No. 54/1988 - Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982 (Section 13 (2) ) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 54 of 1988.

COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1982 (SECTION 13 (2) ) ORDER, 1988.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Industry and Commerce in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) (2) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Section 13 (2) ) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that the provisions of [section 376](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0376.html#sec376) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (prohibiting partnerships with more than 20 members) shall not apply to the formation of a partnership to which the Limited Partnerships Act, 1907, relates (subject, by virtue of this Article, to [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) (5) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) ) and which is formed for the purpose of carrying on or promoting the business of thoroughbred horse breeding.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of March, 1988.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 376](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0376.html#sec376) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) prohibits the formation of partnerships with more than 20 members. This Order provides that section 376 shall not apply to partnerships set up for the purpose of carrying on or promoting the business of thoroughbred horse breeding.
