# S.I. No. 818/2004 - Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, PAT THE COPE GALLAGHER, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 68(4)(b) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1976), 159(5)(a) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1994)), 160(5)(a) (as so inserted) and 279(2) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )) and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 703 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0703.html) ), hereby make the following Order with respect to which, pursuant to section 68(7) and section 279(4) (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall have effect as respects licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after 1 January 2005.

###### 3.

3. The amount of the ordinary licence duty in respect of an ordinary fishing licence authorising the use of a fishing engine of a kind specified in column (2) of the Schedule to this Order at any reference number is altered to the amount specified in column (3) of the said schedule at that reference number.

###### 4.

4. The licence duty in respect of an oyster fishing licence is altered to €88.

###### 5.

5. Section 159(1)(b)(ii) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1994)) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) and as amended by the Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 703 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0703.html) ) is amended by substituting “€115,” for “€110,”.

###### 6.

6. Section 160(1)(b) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) and as amended by the Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 703 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0703.html) ) is amended by substituting “€115,” for “€110,”.

###### 7.

7. The Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2003 (S.I. No. 703) is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order prescribes the licence fees to be payable in respect of salmon, eel and oyster commercial fishing licences and salmon, eel and molluscan shellfish dealers’ licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after 1 January 2005.
