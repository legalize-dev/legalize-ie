# S.I. No. 94/2011 - Companies (Forms) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th March*, 2011.

I, BILLY KELLEHER, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minster) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0185.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 63 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0063.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 1 April 2011.

###### 3.

3. The form (Form B1) as set out in the Schedule is prescribed as the form for the purpose of [section 125](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0125.html#sec125) (inserted by [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec59) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

###### 4.

4. Article 4 of and Schedule 1 to the Companies (Forms) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 133 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0133.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my hand,

1 March 2011.

BILLY KELLEHER,

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Innovation.
