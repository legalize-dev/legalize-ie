# S.I. No. 77/1983 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kerry) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 77 of 1983.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF KERRY) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1983.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kerry) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kerry) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 145 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0145.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kerry) Regulations, 1977 to 1983.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 28th day of March, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The First Schedule to the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kerry) Regulations, 1977, is hereby amended by the substitution of the following matter for the matter at reference 3 (*e*)—

> "3 (*e*) The Abbeydorney Road between the urban district boundary and a point 120 metres south-west of its junction with the Bullock Hill Road".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

14th day of March, 1983.

**DICK SPRING,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kerry) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 145 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0145.html) ) by extending the speed limit of 30 miles per hour on the Abbeydorney Road at Tralee.
