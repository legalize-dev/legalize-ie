# S.I. No. 615/2003 - Circuit Court Rules (No. 4) (Restoration of Companies To The Register Pursuant To Section 12 of the Companies Act, 1982 As Inserted by Section 46 of the Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999), 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

We, the Circuit Court Rules Committee, constituted pursuant to the provisions of [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , and [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0020/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , by virtue of the powers conferred on us by [section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0066.html#sec66) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , and [section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , (as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ) and [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , and of all other powers enabling us in this behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice Equality and Law Reform make the annexed Rules of Court.

S.I. No. 615 of 2003

**CIRCUIT COURT RULES (No. 4) (RESTORATION OF COMPANIES TO THE REGISTER PURSUANT TO SECTION 12 OF THE COMPANIES ACT, 1982 AS INSERTED BY SECTION 46 OF THE COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) ACT 1999), 2003**

###### 1.

1. These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (No. 4) (Restoration of Companies to the Register Pursuant to Section 12 of the Companies Act, 1982 as inserted by [Section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec46) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) ), 2003 shall come into operation on the 11th day of December, 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Order referred to in these rules shall be added to and construed together with those Orders contained in the Circuit Court Rules, 2001, as amended.

ORDER 53

1. Order 53 rule 2 of the Circuit Court Rules, 2001 shall be amended by the addition of the following provisions to that rule:

> “All applications pursuant to this Order shall be made in accordance with the form annexed hereto. The Affidavit grounding such applications shall contain the following information:
>
> - (a) the status of the Plaintiff in relation to the company sought to be restored;
>
> - (b) that all required notices have been given together with proof of service thereof;
>
> - (c) details of the debt due to the applicant creditor and the manner in which such debt has arisen, that such debt was due at the time the company concerned was struck off;
>
> - (d) an averment that it is intended to pursue that said debt as against the company concerned;
>
> - (e) that the Revenue Commissioners consent to the restoration and, if applicable, the terms upon which such consent has been given and exhibiting documentation to support such consent and the terms thereof;
>
> - (f) any other relevant information.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Rules amend Order 53 Rule 2 of the Circuit Court Rules, 2001 by the addition of provisions and a form in respect of applications for restoration of Companies to the register.
