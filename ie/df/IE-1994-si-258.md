# S.I. No. 258/1994 - Transit of Animals (Amendment) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 258 of 1994.

TRANSIT OF ANIMALS (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1994.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) , [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) and [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0011.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Transit of Animals (Amendment) Order, 1994, and shall come into operation on the 29th day of July, 1994.

- (2) The Principal Order and this Order may be cited together as the Transit of Animals (General) Orders, 1973 and 1994.

- (3) The Transit of Animals Orders, 1927 to 1973 and this Order may be cited together as the Transit of Animals Orders, 1927 to 1994.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Principal Order" means the Transit of Animals (General) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 292 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0292.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Order is hereby amended by the deletion from the Schedule thereto of "Transit of Greyhounds Order, 1954 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/si/0121.html) ).".

###### 4.

4. The Transit of Greyhounds Order, 1954 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/si/0121.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of July, 1994.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food

and Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This instrument extends the provisions of the Transit of Animals (General) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 292 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0292.html) ) to the transport of greyhounds and revokes the Transit of Greyhounds Order, 1954 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/si/0121.html) .).
