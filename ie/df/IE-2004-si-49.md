# S.I. No. 49/2004 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) and [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec21) of the [Pensions (Amendement) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2002)) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), and having consulted with the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations—

Citation.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2004.

Revaluation Percentage.

###### 2.

2. The revaluation percentage in respect of the 2003 revaluation year shall be 3.5 per cent.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec21) of the [Pensions (Amendment) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) ) provides that the Minister for Social and Family Affairs, after consultation with the Minister for Finance, shall, in respect of each revaluation year, make Regulations specifying the percentage which shall determine the amount by which the preserved benefit is to be increased in that year.

This Regulation provides for a revaluation percentage of 3.5 per cent for 2003.
