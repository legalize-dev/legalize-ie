**[SI No.545 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0545.html)**

**District Court (Public Order) Rules 2006**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec24) of the [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

1 These rules may be cited as the District Court (Public Order) Rules 2006.

2 These rules shall come into operation on the 20^th day of November 2006 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

3 The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) are hereby amended:

- (i) by the insertion in Order 23, immediately after rule 12 thereof, of the following:

  > *“13. Where the Court makes an order under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec3) of the [Criminal Justice (Public Order) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) , the order shall be in the Form 23.2, Schedule B. The Clerk shall send a copy of such order by ordinary prepaid post to the person who has been convicted at his or her last known or most usual place of abode, and to any other person the Court directs be given a copy of such order.”*

- (ii) by the insertion immediately after Order 96A thereof, of the following:

  > *“Order 96B*
  >
  > *Closure Orders under the [Criminal Justice (Public Order) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0016/print.html)*
  >
  > *1 In this Order:*
  >
  > *“the Act” means the [Criminal Justice (Public Order) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2003);*
  >
  > *“closure order” has the meaning given to it by the Act*.
  >
  > *2 An application pursuant to section 4 of the Act for a closure order may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district in which:*
  >
  > - *(i) in the case of a food stall or food vehicle, the stall or vehicle is used for the sale of food or is kept when not being so used, or*
  >
  > - *(ii) in the case of any other catering premises, the catering premises are situated*.
  >
  >   *3 Notice of an application for a closure order shall be in the Form 96B.1, Schedule C and there shall be attached to such notice a copy of the notice in writing which was served in accordance with section 4(2) of the Act. A copy of the notice of application shall be served upon the licensee or manager of the catering premises concerned not later than seven days before the date fixed for the hearing of the application. The original notice of application shall be lodged with the Clerk not later than two days before the date fixed for the hearing of the application. The Court may adjourn the hearing of such application and direct service of notice of the application upon such persons as the Court may direct. A closure order shall be in the Form 96B.2, Schedule C*.
  >
  >   *4 An application pursuant to section 7 of the Act for an order extending a closure order may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district in which the closure order was made. Save where the Court otherwise directs or permits, such an application shall be preceded by the issue of a notice of application in the Form 96B.3, Schedule C. A copy of the notice of application shall be served upon the licensee or manager of the catering premises concerned not later than four days before the date fixed for the hearing of the application, and the original notice of application shall be lodged with the Clerk not later than two days before the date fixed for the hearing of the application. Where the Court makes an order extending the closure order, including where the Court makes any variation in the terms of the closure order, the period of such extension and the terms of any such variation may be indorsed upon the closure order and re-signed by the Judge.”*

4 The Form numbered 23.2 in the Schedule hereof shall be added to the Forms in Schedule B of the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ). The Forms numbered 96B.1, 96B.2 and 96B.3 in the Schedule hereof shall be added to the Forms in Schedule C of the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).
