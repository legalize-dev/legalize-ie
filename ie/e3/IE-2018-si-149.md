# S.I. No. 149/2018 - European Union (Financial Conglomerates) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th May*, 2018.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Directive 2011/89/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 November 2011[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Financial Conglomerates) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Financial Conglomerates) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 727 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0727.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 3(1) (amended by Regulation 2(2) of, and Schedule 2 to, the European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance Groups and Financial Conglomerates) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 416 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0416.html) )), in paragraph (*a*) of the definition of “group” by substituting “participation, and includes any subgroup thereof, or” for “participation, or”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 4 (amended by Regulation 2(2) of, and Schedule 2 to, the European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance Groups and Financial Conglomerates) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 416 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0416.html) ))—

    - (i) in paragraph (6), by substituting for subparagraph (*b*) the following:

      > - “(*b*) that all or any of Regulations 9 to 11 should not be applied to the group,”, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (7) by substituting for subparagraph (*b*) the following:

      > - “(*b*) not to apply the provisions of Regulations 9 to 11,”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 May 2018.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This statutory instrument amends the European Union (Financial Conglomerates) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 727 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0727.html) ). The amendments include a technical amendment to definitions and an amendment to address a referencing issue in Regulation 4.
