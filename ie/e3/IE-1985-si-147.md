# S.I. No. 147/1985 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 147 of 1985.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) ORDER, 1985.

I, GEMMA HUSSEY, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), with the concurrence of the Minister for the Public Service and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards and with the Minister for Finance (being a Minister of the Government whom the Minister for Education considers to be concerned), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order. 1985.

###### 2.

2. The institution specified in the Schedule of this Order is hereby designated as being an institution to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) applies.

SCHEDULE

St. Patrick's College, Carlow.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this twentieth day of May, 1985.

**GEMMA HUSSEY,**

Minister for Education.
