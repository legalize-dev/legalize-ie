# S.I. No. 182/1995 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 4) Scheme, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 182 of 1995.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 4) SCHEME, 1995.

Bord Telecom Éireann, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 4) Scheme, 1995.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 and 1995" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Articles 4 (*g*), 4 (*k*), 4 (*l*), 4 (*q*) and 4 (*s*) shall come into operation on 12 July 1995.

- (3) Articles 4 (*a*), 4 (*b*), 4 (*c*), 4 (*d*), 4 (*e*), 4 (*f*), 4 (*h*), 4 (*i*), 4 (*j*), 4 (*p*), 4 (*r*), 4 (*t*) and 4 (*u*) shall be deemed to have come into effect on 1 June 1995.

- (4) Articles 4 (*m*), 4 (*n*) and 4 (*o*) shall be deemed to have come into effect on 25 June 1995.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by:

( *i* ) the addition of the following Part VIII thereto:

"PART VIII

MAINTENANCE SERVICES

112. The Articles in this Part of this Scheme apply to maintenance services only but are in addition to the Articles contained in Part II of this Scheme.

113. Maintenance services performed by Bord Telecom Éireann under this Scheme shall, at the sole discretion of Bord Telecom Éireann, be carried out at the subscriber's premises or at a premises of Bord Telecom Éireann or elsewhere.

114. In undertaking maintenance services under this Scheme, Bord Telecom Éireann does not assure uninterrupted operation of telecommunications lines, fixtures and accessories or subscriber equipment.

115. The maintenance services provided under this Scheme do not include service (or increased service time) necessitated by or attributable to

( *a* ) work external to telecommunications lines or fixtures and accessories;

( *b* ) rectifying any failure or malfunction of the telecommunications lines or fixtures and accessories caused directly or indirectly by:

- (i) accident, neglect, misuse or alterations to the telecommunications lines or fixtures and accessories; or

- (ii) any subscriber equipment or the operation thereof otherwise than in accordance with instructions; or

- (iii) adverse environmental conditions including lightning, fire, floods, storms or other catastrophes; or

- (iv) electrical current fluctuations; or

- (v) deliberate damage or damage resulting from reckless, negligent or careless conduct (including omission) or resulting other than by fair wear and tear or resulting from war, civil strife, commotion;

( *c* ) use of telecommunications lines or fixtures and accessories or any subscriber equipment other than for the use for which they were intended or otherwise than in accordance with instructions given from time to time by Bord Telecom Éireann;

( *d* ) the undertaking of work on telecommunications lines or fixtures and accessories (including moving, relocation, conversion or adaptation) by persons other than employees or agents of Bord Telecom Éireann, unless approved by Bord Telecom Éireann;

( *e* ) work on subscriber equipment;

( f ) riot or civil commotion, natural disaster (including flooding, fire, wind, lightning) or act of a third party whether acting (including by omission) maliciously, negligently, carelessly or imprudently.

116. (1) Bord Telecom Éireann shall, between the hours of 9.00 a.m. and 5.00 p.m. on days other than Sundays and Public Holidays, within four working hours of the notification to a designated repair centre of Bord Telecom Éireann by Rapid Plan Maintenance subscriber of a fault in a telephone exchange line or telephone set rented from Bord Telecom Éireann and to which the Rapid Plan Maintenance Service extends, respond to such request either by causing an employee or agent of Bord Telecom Éireann to visit the appropriate premises of the Rapid Plan Maintenance subscriber, or at the discretion of Bord Telecom Éireann, by causing the telephone exchange line or telephone set to be tested at a location other than such premises.

- (2) Bord Telecom Éireann shall thereafter use reasonable endeavours to locate and repair such fault as soon as possible.

117. A Rapid Plan Maintenance subscriber shall pay on demand to Bord Telecom Éireann, in respect of Rapid Plan Maintenance Service, the charges specified in Part 9 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

118. (1) Where loss is caused to a Rapid Plan Maintenance subscriber solely by failure on the part of Bord Telecom Éireann to discharge its obligations pursuant to Article 116 (1) of this Scheme, Bord Telecom Éireann shall provide an appropriate credit on the account of such subscriber, provided however that in no circumstances shall the credit afforded in respect of any such failure exceed the amount specified in Part 9 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (2) The provision of such credit shall discharge the entire liability of Bord Telecom Éireann in respect of such loss and failure.

119. (1) Bord Telecom Éireann shall, within four hours of notification to a designated repair centre of Bord Telecom Éireann by a Master Plan Maintenance subscriber of a fault in a telephone exchange line or telephone set rented from Bord Telecom Éireann and to which the Master Plan Maintenance Service extends, respond to such a request either by causing an employee or agent of Bord Telecom Éireann to visit the appropriate premises of the Master Plan Maintenance subscriber or, at the discretion of Bord Telecom Éireann, by causing the telephone exchange line or telephone set to be tested at a location other than such premises.

- (2) Bord Telecom Éireann shall thereafter use reasonable endeavours to locate and to repair such fault as soon as possible.

120. A Master Plan Maintenance subscriber shall pay on demand to Bord Telecom Éireann in respect of Master Plan Maintenance Service the charges specified in Part 9 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

121. (1) Where loss is caused to a Master Plan Maintenance subscriber solely by failure on the part of Bord Telecom Éireann to discharge its obligations pursuant to Article 119 (1) of this Scheme, Bord Telecom Éireann shall provide an appropriate credit on the account of such subscriber, provided however that in no circumstances shall the credit afforded in respect of any such failure exceed the amount specified in Part 9 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (2) The provision of such credit shall discharge the entire liability of Bord Telecom Éireann in respect of such loss and failure.

## PART IX FRAME RELAY SERVICE

122. The Articles in this Part of the Scheme apply to Frame Relay Service only but are in addition to the Articles contained in Part II and Part IV of this Scheme.
