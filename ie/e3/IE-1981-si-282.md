# S.I. No. 282/1981 - Irish Film Board (Establishment Day) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN KELLY, Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0036/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Irish Film Board Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1980), hereby make the following Order:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Irish Film Board (Establishment Day) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The 14th day of August, 1981, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Irish Film Board Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1980).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of August, 1981. |
|  | JOHN KELLY, |
|  | Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this Order is to establish the date on which [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0036/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Irish Film Board Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) , comes into force. This provides for the establishment of the Irish Film Board, on 14th August, 1981.
