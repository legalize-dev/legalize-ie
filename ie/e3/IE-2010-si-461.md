# S.I. No. 461/2010 - Road Traffic (Specialised Vehicle Permits) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st October*, 2010.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0011.html#sec11) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Specialised Vehicle Permits) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations amend the Road Traffic (Specialised Vehicle Permits) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0147.html) ) by adding further routes to the schedule of designated roads.
