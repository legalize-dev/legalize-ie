# S.I. No. 514/2008 - Housing Act 1966 (Compulsory Acquisition of Land for Motorways and Service Areas, Etc) (form of Notice) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 514 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0514.html)

HOUSING ACT 1966 (COMPULSORY ACQUISITION OF LAND FOR MOTORWAYS AND SERVICE AREAS, ETC) (FORM OF NOTICE) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th December*, 2008.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 5(1) and 78(1) of the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) (as adapted by section 10 (inserted by [section 86](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0086.html#sec86) of the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) ) of the [Local Government Act 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1960) and the Environment and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0233.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Compulsory Acquisition of Land for Motorways and Service Areas, etc) (Form of Notice) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule is the form of notice prescribed for the purposes of section 78(1) of the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) in respect of the compulsory acquisition of land under section 52(1) of the [Roads Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. Regulation 8 of, and Form 12 of the Schedule to, the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Acquisition of Land) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 454 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0454.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order prescribes the form to be used to indicate approval, or approval with modifications, by An Bord Pleanála to the National Roads Authority or the relevant road authority to use its powers under the [Roads Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , as amended, to compulsorily acquire land for the purposes of providing a motorway/busway/protected road /service area.

The Order also revokes Regulation 8 and Form 12 of the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Acquisition of Land) Regulations 2000 (S.I. 454 of 2000).
