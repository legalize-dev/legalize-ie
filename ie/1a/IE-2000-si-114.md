# S.I. No. 114/2000 - Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers vested in him by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) (5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order (S.I. 308 of 1997), hereby makes the following regulations:-

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 3rd day of May 2000.

###### 3.

**3.** The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0157.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

**4.** In these Regulations -

“outgoings” means the rent, ground rent, rates and purchase annuity payable in respect of premises and mortgage repayments relating to the purchase of premises;

“receiving in-patient services” means receiving in-patient services made available under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970); and

“person” means a person to whom [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) applies by virtue of sub-section (1) of that Section.

###### 5.

**5.** The payment to be made under sub-section (3) of [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) to or in respect of a person or his/her dependants shall be a weekly payment not exceeding:-

- (a) the sum specified in Part One of the Schedule hereto which is appropriate to the person, and

- (b) such of the increases specified in Part Two of the said Schedule as are appropriate.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation. The effect of these Regulations is to provide as from 3 May 2000, for increases in the maximum rates of maintenance allowances payable to persons being treated for certain infectious diseases.

The infectious diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are acute anterior poliomyelitis, diphtheria, dysentery, salmonellosis, tuberculosis, typhoid and paratyphoid fevers, typhus and viral haemorrhagic diseases (including lassa fever and marburg disease).
