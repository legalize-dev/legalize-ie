# S.I. No. 177/2025 - Policing, Security and Community Safety Act 2024 (Retirement) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th May,* 2025.

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me -

- (a) by [section 254](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec254) of the [Policing, Security and Community Safety Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2024), having consulted with the Garda Commissioner and the Authority, and

- (b) in respect of *Regulation 3*, by section 261(3) of that Act,

and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Policing, Security and Community Safety Act 2024 (Retirement) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 17 April 2025.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding anything contained in -

- (a) the Gárda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1934 (S. R. & O. No. 146 of 1934), as continued in force by section 128 of the Garda Síochána Act 2005 and [section 261](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec261) of the [Policing, Security and Community Safety Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2024), or

- (b) the Gárda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations 1951 ( [S.I. No. 335 of 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/si/0335.html) ), as so continued in force,

the member of An Garda Síochána who, immediately before the coming into operation of these regulations, holds the rank of Garda Commissioner shall retire on 1 September 2025.

###### 3.

3. The Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0397.html) ) are revoked.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

15 April, 2025.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Taoiseach.

**JIM O’CALLAGHAN,**

Minister for Justice.
