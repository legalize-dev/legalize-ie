# S.I. No. 165/1990 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 165 of 1990.

PRISONS ACT, 1970 (SECTION 7) ORDER, 1990.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970 ), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1990.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Order of 1989" means the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 85 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0085.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The period of 12 months beginning on the 28th day of June, 1990, is hereby specified for the purposes of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970 ).

###### 4.

4. The Order of 1989 is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of June, 1990.

**RAPHAEL P. BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order continues in operation [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) for a further period of twelve months.
