# S.I. No. 78/1995 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) (Amendment) Act, 1994 (Section 2) Order, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 78 of 1995.

OIREACHTAS (ALLOWANCES TO MEMBERS) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1994 (SECTION 2) ORDER, 1995.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0021/sec0002.html#sec2) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0021/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1994), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Amendment) Act (Section 2) Order, 1995.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Sections 2 and 3) Order, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0302.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Part I thereof of the following Part:

> "

**JOHN BRUTON,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the payment of allowances to the Chairpersons of certain Oireachtas Committees.
