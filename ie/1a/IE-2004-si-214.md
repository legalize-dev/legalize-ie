# S.I. No. 214/2004 - Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice) Act (Transfer of Property) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food in exercise of the powers conferred on me in [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0018/sec0021.html#sec21) (5)(a) of the [Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice) Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1988) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Agriculture (Research, Training and Advice) Act (Transfer of Property)Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. All property contained in Land Registry Folio 22836 of the Register in County Cork other than lands described at Entry No. I of Part II of the Folio being Plan No. 6 on the Folio Map is transferred to Teagasc without further transfer.
