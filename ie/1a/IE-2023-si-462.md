# S.I. No. 462/2023 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd September,* 2023.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of the powers of the Minister for Finance under [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), delegated to it by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 October 2023.

###### 3.

3. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0014.html) ) are revoked with effect from the date specified in Regulation 2.

###### 4.

4. The rate of interest on a deposit, within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1948), is hereby prescribed as 0.75 per cent per annum.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency,

21 September, 2023.

**FRANK O’CONNOR,**

Chief Executive.

**DAVE MCEVOY,**

Director.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe a new rate of interest for deposits in the Post Office Savings Bank.
