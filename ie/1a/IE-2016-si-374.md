# S.I. No. 374/2016 - Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th July*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order 2016.

###### 2.

2. A Constituency Commission is hereby established to perform the function assigned to it by Part II of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 July 2016.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order establishes a Constituency Commission to report on Dáil and European Parliament constituencies following on from Census 2016.
