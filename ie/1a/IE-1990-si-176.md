# S.I. No. 176/1990 - Payment of Interest on Overpaid Tax Regulations, 1990

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 176 of 1990.

PAYMENT OF INTEREST ON OVERPAID TAX REGULATIONS, 1990.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 429](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0006/sec0429.html#sec429) of the [Income Tax Act, 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1967), as amended by [section 114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0114.html#sec114) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986), [section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976), as so amended, [section 107](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/sec0107.html#sec107) of the [Finance Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1983), as so amended and [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0008/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Capital Acquisitions Tax Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1976), as amended by [section 109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0109.html#sec109) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Payment of Interest on Overpaid Tax Regulations, 1990.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1990.

###### 3.

3. The rate of 0.6 per cent is hereby prescribed for the purposes of:

( *a* ) paragraph (*a*) of the proviso to subsection (4) of [section 429](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0006/sec0429.html#sec429) of the [Income Tax Act, 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1967), as amended by [section 114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0114.html#sec114) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986),

( *b* ) subsection (4) of [section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976), as amended by the said section 114,

( *c* ) subsection (2) of [section 107](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/sec0107.html#sec107) of the [Finance Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1983), as amended by the said section 114, and

( *d* ) subsection (1) of section 46 of the Capital Acquisition Tax Act, 1976 (No. 8 of 1976), as amended by [section 109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0109.html#sec109) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of July, 1990.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to reduce from 1% to 0.6% per month or part of a month, the rate of interest payable on overpayments of the following taxes: income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, residential property tax and capital acquisitions tax. The new rate applies from 1 August, 1990.
