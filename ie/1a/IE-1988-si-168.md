# S.I. No. 168/1988 - Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978 (Section 5 (3)) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 168 of 1988.

INDUSTRIAL AND PROVIDENT SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1978 (SECTION 5 (3)) REGULATIONS, 1988

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1978), and, after consultation with the Central Bank, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (section 5 (3)) Regulations, 1988, and they shall come into operation on the 15th day of July, 1988.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1978" means the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1978);

"the Court" means the High Court;

" the Registrar" means the Registrar of Friendly Societies;

"society" means a society registered under the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1978, and to which, by virtue of [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) and [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , section 10 to 18 of that Act apply.

###### 3.

3. (1) This Regulation applies to a society in respect of which the Court has by order—

( *a* ) appointed a liquidator or liquidators pursuant to the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1978, and the Companies Acts 1963 to 1986, or

( *b* ) confirmed and extended the period of operation of a direction given to the society by the Registrar under section 16 of the Act of 1978:

Provided that the said order is in force on the date of commencement of these Regulations.

- (2) The period referred to in section 5 (2) of the Act of 1978 (as amended by Regulation 4 (2) of the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 5 (3) Regulations, 1983), is hereby extended by three years for a society to which this Regulation applies.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 4th day of May 1988.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for an extension in the period of time during which certain classes of Industrial and Provident Societies may continue to accept and hold deposits.
