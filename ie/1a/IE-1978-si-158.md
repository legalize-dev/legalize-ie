# S.I. No. 158/1978 - Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 158 of 1978.

TAXIMETER (FEES ON VERIFICATION AND STAMPING BY SPECIAL INSPECTORS) REGULATIONS, 1978.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 83](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0083.html#sec83) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), and the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0306.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. (1) The fee to be paid in respect of the verification and stamping of a taximeter by a special inspector shall be fifty new pence.

- (2) Every such fee shall be paid by the person who submits the taximeter for such verification and stamping.

###### 3.

3. The Weights and Measures (Verification and Stamping Fees) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 137 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0137.html) ), shall not apply in respect of the verification and stamping of taximeters by special inspectors.

###### 4.

4. The Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1938 (S. R. & O., No. 284 of 1938), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of May, 1978.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 9th dayof June, 1978.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order was made to set out revised fees for the verification and stamping of taximeters.
