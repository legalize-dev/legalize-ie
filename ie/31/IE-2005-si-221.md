# S.I. No. 221/2005 - Criminal Evidence Act 1992 (Section 13) (Commencement) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 221 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0221.html)**

**CRIMINAL EVIDENCE ACT 1992 (SECTION 13) (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER 2005**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 1(3) of the [Criminal Evidence Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) 12 of 1992) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Criminal Evidence Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (Section 13 (Commencement) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The 2nd day of May 2005 is fixed as the day on which [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Criminal Evidence Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) shall come into operation for the Circuit Court sitting in the Cork Circuit.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 27th April, 2005. |
| L.S. | Michael McDowell Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Regulation brings into operation a provision of the Criminal Evidence Act, 1992 which allows a witness to give evidence through a live television link in cases involving physical or sexual abuse before the Cork Circuit Criminal Court.
