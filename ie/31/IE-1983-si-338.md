# S.I. No. 338/1983 - Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978 (Section 5 (3)) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 338 of 1983.

INDUSTRIAL AND PROVIDENT SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1978 (SECTION 5 (3)) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1978), and the Industry, Commerce and Tourism (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0290.html) ), after consultation with the Central Bank of Ireland, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 5 (3)) Regulations, 1983, and shall come into operation on the 14th day of November, 1983.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1978" means the [Industrial and Provident Societies (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ;

"the Court" means the High Court;

"society" means a society registered under the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1978, and to which, by virtue of sections 4 and 9 of the Act of 1978, sections 10 to 18 of that Act apply.

###### 3.

3. (1) This Regulation applies to a society the membership of which consists wholly of credit unions.

- (2) The period referred to in section 5 (2) of the Act of 1978 is hereby extended by eight years for a society to which this Regulation applies.

###### 4.

4. (1) This Regulation applies to a society in respect of which the Court has by order—

- (*a*) appointed a liquidator or liquidators pursuant to the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1978, and the Companies Acts 1963 to 1983, or

- (*b*) confirmed and extended the period of operation of a direction under section 16 of the Act of 1978.

- (2) The period referred to in section 5 (2) of the Act of 1978 is hereby extended by three years for a society to which this Regulation applies.

###### 5.

5. (1) This Regulation applies to a society which was registered before the commencement of the [Central Bank Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1971).

- (2) The period referred to in section 5 (2) of the Act of 1978 is hereby extended by three years for a society to which this Regulation applies.

###### 6.

6. (1) This Regulation applies to a society other than a society to which Regulations 3 to 5 apply.

- (2) The period referred to in section 5 (2) of the Act of 1978 is hereby extended by one year for a society to which this Regulation applies.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 11th day of November, 1983.

**FRANK CLUSKEY,**

Minister for Trade, Commerce andTourism.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for an extension in the period of time during which certain classes of Industrial and Provident Societies may continue to accept and hold deposits.
