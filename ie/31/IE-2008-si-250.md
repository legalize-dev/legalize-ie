# S.I. No. 250/2008 - Stamp Duty (Designation of Clearing Houses) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 250 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0250.html)

STAMP DUTY (DESIGNATION OF CLEARING HOUSES) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th July*, 2008.

The Revenue Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 75A(6)(*a*) (inserted by [section 109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec109) of the [Finance Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) (No. 11 of 2007)) of the [Stamp Duties Consolidation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 1999), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Stamp Duty (Designation of Clearing Houses) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 7 July 2008.

###### 2.

2. European Central Counterparty Limited is designated as a recognised clearing house for the purposes of section 75A (inserted by [section 109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec109) of the [Finance Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) (No. 11 of 2007)) of the [Stamp Duties Consolidation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 1999).

GIVEN under my hand,

2 July 2008

MICHAEL O’GRADY,

Revenue Commissioner.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Section 75A (inserted by [section 109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec109) of the [Finance Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) ) of the [Stamp Duties Consolidation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) provides for an exemption from stamp duty on a transfer of securities to and from a recognised clearing house, commonly known as a “CCP”, in specified circumstances.

The purpose of these Regulations is the designation by the Revenue Commissioners, as on and from 7 July 2008, of European Central Counterparty Limited as a recognised clearing house for the purposes of section 75A of the [Stamp Duties Consolidation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) .
