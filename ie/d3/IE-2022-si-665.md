# S.I. No. 665/2022 - Value - Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010 (Section 46(5)) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th December,* 2022.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec46) (5) of the [Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2010), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010 (section 46(5)) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. Subclause (ii) of paragraph 11(4)(a) of Schedule 2 to the [Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2010) is amended by the substitution of “30 June 2022” for “31 December 2021”.

###### 3.

3. This Order shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 January 2022.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 December, 2022.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

This Order extends the application of the zero rate of VAT to certain goods used in the delivery of Covid-19 related health care services, from 31 December 2021 to 30 June 2022. The zero rate of VAT will continue to apply to the supply of personal protection equipment, thermometers, hand sanitiser, oxygen, medical ventilators and specialist respiratory equipment including respirators for intensive and sub-intensive care and other oxygen therapy apparatus including oxygen tents, where such goods are supplied to the HSE, hospitals, nursing homes, care homes and GP practices, for use in the delivery of Covid-19 related health care services to their patients.
