JURY DISTRICTS ORDER, 2004

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Juries Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 22nd December, 2004.

###### 3.

3. The jury district for the county of Cork (including the city of Cork) is limited to the parts of that county specified in Schedule 1 hereto.

###### 4.

4. The county of Cavan is divided into the 2 jury districts specified in part 1 of Schedule 2 hereto.

###### 5.

5. The county of Donegal is divided into the 2 jury districts specified in part 2 of Schedule 2 hereto.

###### 6.

6. The county of Galway is divided into the 2 jury districts specified in part 3 of Schedule 2 hereto.

###### 7.

7. The county of Kerry is divided into the 2 jury districts specified in part 4 of Schedule 2 hereto.

###### 8.

8. The county of Kildare is divided into the 2 jury districts specified in part 5 of Schedule 2 hereto.

###### 9.

9. The county of Monaghan is divided into the 2 jury districts specified in part 6 of Schedule 2 hereto.

###### 10.

10. The Jury Districts Order 1976 (No. 57 of 1976) and the Jury Districts Order 1983 (No. 262 of 1983) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note does not form part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order revokes the Jury Districts Order of 1976 ( [S.I. No 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ) and the Jury Districts Order 1983 ( [S.I. No. 262 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0262.html) ). The Jury Districts in the Counties of Offaly, Roscommon and Wicklow are abolished and each of these counties becomes a Jury District.
