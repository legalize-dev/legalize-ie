> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 95 of 1990.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (MULTI-STOREY BUILDINGS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers vested in him by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/sec0011.html#sec11) (1) of the [Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 17 of 1988) and article 2 of the [Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Determination of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 244 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0244.html) ) hereby makes the following Regulations.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990.

- (2) These Regulations and the Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Regulations, 1988 may be cited together as the Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Regulations, 1988 and 1990.

###### 2.

2. The Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 286 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0286.html) ) is hereby amended as follows:

( *a* ) by the insertion in article 3 (3) (*b*) of the words "or by the 1st day of November, 1990 whichever shall later occur" after the word "notice" where it secondly occurs,

( *b* ) by the substitution in article 5 of the words "by the 1st day of November, 1990" for the words "within 6 months of the commencement of the Act",

( *c* ) by the substitution for forms 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 of the forms respectively numbered 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Regulations, 1988 and prescribe new forms of certificates of appraisal of multi-storey buildings and the time within which they must be submitted by their owners.
