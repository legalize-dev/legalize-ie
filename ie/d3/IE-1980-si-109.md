# S.I. No. 109/1980 - Harbour Authorities (Allowances To Members) Rules, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 109 of 1980.

HARBOUR AUTHORITIES (ALLOWANCES TO MEMBERS) RULES, 1980.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) (7) of the [Harbours Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1947), and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Title of Minister and Name of Department) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0011.html) ), hereby make the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Harbour Authorities (Allowances to Members) Rules, 1980.

###### 2.

2. The allowance to be paid under paragraph (*a*) or paragraph (*b*) of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) (7) of the [Harbours Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1947), to a member of a harbour authority in respect of absence from his official residence by reason of attendance at a meeting or meetings to which either such paragraph relates shall not exceed—

( *a* ) £5.80 in respect of a continuous period of less than seven hours and not less than three hours, and

( *b* ) £8.00 in respect of a continuous period of not less than seven hours.

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule hereto if the form to be used in respect of applications under these rules.

###### 4.

4. The Harbour Authorities (Allowances to Members) Rules, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 144 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0144.html) ), are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these rules is to provide for increases in the allowances which a Harbour Authority may pay to its members in respect of attendance at meetings of the authority.
