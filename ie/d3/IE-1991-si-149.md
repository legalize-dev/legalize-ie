# S.I. No. 149/1991 - Derelict Sites (Appeal Fees) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 149 of 1991.

DERELICT SITES (APPEAL FEES) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), as applied by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0022.html#sec22) (10) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Appeal Fees) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The fee to be charged in respect of an appeal to the Valuation Tribunal under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0022.html#sec22) (4) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , against a determination by a local authority of a market value mentioned in column (1) of the following Table shall be the amount specified in column (2) opposite the mention of that market value.

TABLE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Market value of urban land as determined by local authority. | Appeal fee. |
| (1) | (2) |
|  | £ |
| Less than £50,000 | 50 |
| £50,000 to less than £100,000 | 100 |
| £100,000 or over | 150 |

GIVEN under the Official Seal, this 7th day of June, 1991.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) requires local authorities to determine the market value of derelict sites on urban land for the purposes of a derelict sites levy payable by the owners of such land. The section also allows appeals to the Valuation Tribunal against local authorities' determinations. This Regulation sets out the fees payable in respect of appeals made to the Valuation Tribunal against local authorities' determinations.
