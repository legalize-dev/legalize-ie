# S.I. No. 217/1991 - Video Recordings Act, 1989 (Register of Prohibited Video Works) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 217 of 1991.

VIDEO RECORDINGS ACT, 1989 (REGISTER OF PROHIBITED VIDEO WORKS) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Register of Prohibited Video Works) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The Register of Prohibited Video Works shall be in the form specified in the Schedule to these Regulations or in a form to the like effect.

SCHEDULE

*Register of Prohibited Video Works.*

Title of Video Work

Running Time

Prohibition Order No.

Date of publication of Order in "Iris Oifigiúil".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 31st day of July, 1991.

**RAY BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the form of the Register of Prohibited Video Works to be established by the Official Censor under [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
