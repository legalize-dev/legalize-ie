# S.I. No. 302/2025 - Central Bank Act 1942 (Service of Notices and Other Documents) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th July,* 2025.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 61A of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942), and after consulting the Central Bank of Ireland, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Central Bank Act 1942 (Service of Notices and Other Documents) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 15 July 2025.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 3 of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Service of Notices and Other Documents) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0300.html) ) is amended by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (da):

> - “(db) Part 2 of the [Finance (Provision of Access to Cash Infrastructure) Act 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 2025);”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 July, 2025.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
