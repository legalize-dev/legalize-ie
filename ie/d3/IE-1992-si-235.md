S.I. No. 235 of 1992.

DUBLIN METER PARKING PLACES RULES, 1992.

Rules for the administration of meter parking places in the County Borough of Dublin, made by the Right Honourable, the Lord Mayor, Aldermen and Burgesses of Dublin, the appropriate local authority in exercise of powers conferred by Section 90 (3) (*b*) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961)—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules, 1992.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Rules shall come into force on 1st September, 1992.

###### 3.

3. In these Rules:—

"the Principal Rules" means the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1985 ( [S.I. No. 27 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0027.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Rule 7 of the Principal Rules, as amended by the Dublin Meter Parking Places (No. 2) Rules 1985 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0366.html) ), and the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules ( [S.I. No. 232 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0232.html) ) and the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1989 ( [S.I. No. 198 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0198.html) ) is hereby further amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (B):—

- (B) The parking fee payable under paragraph (A) of this Rule shall in the "prime area" be:—

- (i) one twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding fifteen minutes, or

- (ii) two twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding thirty minutes, or

- (iii) three twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding forty-five minutes, or

- (iv) four twenty-pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour, or

- (v) two fifty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour fifteen minutes, or

- (vi) two fifty pence coins or five twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour, fifteen minutes, or

- (vii) two fifty pence coins and one twenty pence coin for a period not exceeding one hour and thirty minutes, or

- (viii) two fifty pence coins and two twenty pence coins for a period not exceeding one hour and forty-five minutes, or

- (ix) two fifty pence coins and two twenty pence coins or seven twenty pence coins for a period not exceeding one hour and forty-five minutes, or

- (x) two fifty pence coins and three twenty pence coins for a period not exceeding two hours.

###### 6.

- (C) The parking fee payable under paragraph (A) of this Rule shall in the "residual area" be:—

- (i) one twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding twenty minutes, or

- (ii) two twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding forty minutes, or

- (iii) one fifty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding fifty minutes, or

- (iv) three twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour, or

- (v) one fifty pence coin and one twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and ten minutes, or

- (vi) four twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and twenty minutes, or

- (vii) one fifty pence coin and two twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and thirty minutes, or

- (viii) five twenty pence coins or two fifty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and forty minutes, or

- (ix) three twenty pence coins and one fifty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and fifty minutes, or

- (x) six twenty pence coins or two fifty pence coins and one twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding two hours.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Rules amend Rule 4 of the Principal Rules whereby the "prime area" has been extended by the inclusion of certain streets which had been heretofore in the "residual area".

These Rules also amend Rule 7 of the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1987 ( [S.I. No. 232 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0232.html) ) and the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1989 ( [S.I. No. 198 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0198.html) ) to provide for increases in the rates of parking fees payable in the "prime area" from 60p per hour to 80p per hour and for an increase in the rate of parking fees payable in the "residual area" from 40p per hour to 60p per hour.
