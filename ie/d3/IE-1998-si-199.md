# S.I. No. 199/1998 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 199 of 1998.

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT, 1966 (FIRST SCHEDULE) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1998

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Forestry (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0302.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1998.

- (2) The [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1995, and this Order may be cited together as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1998.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"the Act" means the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966);

"the first Schedule" means the First Schedule to the Act, as amended by the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1995.

###### 3.

3. For the purposes of the Act, the First Schedule is hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the insertion at the end of Part 1 of the following:

  > "10 Bees"

- (*b*) the insertion in part III thereof at the end of Class B of the following:

  > "44 Varroasis".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 12th day of June, 1998.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Order adds bees to Part I and varroasis to Part III class B of the First Schedule to [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966).
