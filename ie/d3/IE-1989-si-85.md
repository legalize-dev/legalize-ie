# S.I. No. 85/1989 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 85 of 1989.

PRISONS ACT, 1970 (SECTION 7) ORDER, 1989

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Order of 1988" means the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 75 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0075.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0118.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (2) (inserted by the Order of 1988) of Article 3 of the following paragraph:

> - "(2) The section shall be in operation for the period of five years commencing on the 26th day of April, 1985, and shall cease to be in operation upon the expiration of that period."

###### 4.

4. The Order of 1988 is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 20th day of April, 1989.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order continues in operation [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) for a further period of twelve months.
