HOUSING REGULATIONS, 1980 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations make a number of amendments to the Housing Regulations, 1980:

— the amounts of loans a housing authority may advance for the building or acquisition of a house and the income limits applicable to such loans are increased with effect from 5th June, 1986;

— the amount of an unsecured improvement loan is increased with effect from 1st May, 1986 and the income limit in respect of improvement loans generally is increased with effect from 5th June, 1986;

— special category status which gives entitlement to house purchase loans at a higher rate is extended to non-commissioned members of the Permanent Defence Forces who surrender their houses to the Minister for Defence and to persons who purchased new houses provided by local authorities;

— provision is made for the increase in the interest rate on loans for the building or acquisition of a house to enable local authorities to recover from borrowers the cost of mortgage protection insurance;

— the subsidy for sites provided by local authorities for private housing on or after 15th June, 1984, is increased;

— article 62 (*c*) of the 1980 Regulations which prescribed a maximum floor area limit of110 square metres for houses qualifying for site subsidy is revoked; and

— article 65 of the 1980 Regulations is revoked to allow payment of housing subsidy where a local authority acquires a private house for letting to a tenant.
