# S.I. No. 68/2019 - Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil”*1*st of March,* 2019.

I, Eoghan Murphy, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsections (1) and (8) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. A Referendum Commission is established to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , as amended by the [Referendum Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0053/print.html) , in relation to the proposal for an amendment of the Constitution contained in the Thirty-fifth Amendment of the Constitution (Divorce) Bill 2016.

###### 3.

3. The 26^th day of February 2019 is appointed to be the day for the purposes of section 2(5) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the chairperson of the Referendum Commission.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 February, 2019.

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This order establishes a Referendum Commission for the purposes of the Referendum Acts 1998 and 2001 in connection with the Thirty-fifth Amendment of the Constitution (Divorce) Bill 2016 and appoints 26 February, 2019 as the day on which the position of chairperson of the Commission takes effect.
