# S.I. No. 113/2004 - Video Recordings Act 1989 (Fees) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHAEL McDOWELL, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) , [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0010.html#sec10) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Video Recordings Act 1989 (Fees) Regulations 2004.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1st June, 2004.

###### 2.

2. References in these Regulations to a section are references to a section of the [Video Recordings Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. The following fees are prescribed for the purposes of section 3(4):

- (1) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (4) of this Regulation, where a video recording containing the video work in respect of which the application for a supply certificate is being made is to be released for letting on hire or for sale to the ultimate consumer—

    - (*a*) €10 per minute for the first hour of the video work concerned,

    - (*b*) €8 per minute for the second hour, and

    - (*c*) €6 per minute thereafter;

- (2) where the video work is a preview of a larger video work, €50;

- (3) where the video work is an advertisement, €50;

- (4) where the video work is a video game which is not exempted work, €2,500.

###### 4.

4. A fee of €20 is prescribed for the purposes of section 10(3)(*b*).

###### 5.

5. (1) A fee of €1,000 is prescribed for the purposes of an appeal to the Appeal Board under section 10(6).

- (2) In accordance with section 10(6), a fee so charged shall be refunded to the appellant concerned if the Appeal Board revokes a prohibition order or directs that the video work concerned be given a higher classification.

- (3) The reference in the preceding paragraph to section 10(6) is to that provision as inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0029/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Censorship of Films (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1992).

###### 6.

6. A fee of €7,000 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 18(1).

###### 7.

7. A fee of €200 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 18(2).

###### 8.

8. The Video Recordings Act, 1989 (Fees) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 137 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0137.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the fees to be charged in relation to applications for supply certificates under Section 3, appeals under Section 10 and for licences issued under [Section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
