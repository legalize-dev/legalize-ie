# S.I. No. 203/1978 - State Guarantees Act, 1954 (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 203 of 1978.

STATE GUARANTEES ACT, 1954 (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE) ORDER, 1978.

The Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1978.

- (2) The collective citation "the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Orders, 1957 to 1978" shall include this order.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954) is hereby amended by adding "Avoca Mines Ltd" to the bodies specified in the second column and inserting in the third column "£2,300,000' in respect of that body.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 11th dayof July, 1978.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to enable borrowing by Avoca Mines Ltd. up to a maximum of £2,300,000 to be guaranteed by the Minister for Finance.
