# S.I. No. 298/1974 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 298 of 1974.

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 1974.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for the Public Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations :

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1974.

- (2) The collective citation "the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1974", shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The organisations set out in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

SCHEDULE.

An Bord Bainne Co-Operative Limited

Ceimicí Teoranta

Cork Savings Bank

Dublin Savings Bank

Ostlanna Iompair Éireann Teoranta

Voluntary Health Insurance Board

Voluntary hospitals and nursing organisations to which the Voluntary Hospitals Superannuation Scheme, 1969, applies.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 25th day of September, 1974.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for the Public Service.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and "approved organisations" and between one "approved organisation" and another. These Regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as "approved organisations".
