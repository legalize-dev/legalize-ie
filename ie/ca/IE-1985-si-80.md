# S.I. No. 80/1985 - Telex (Amendment) Scheme 1985

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 80 of 1985.

TELEX (AMENDMENT) SCHEME 1985.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Telex (Amendment) Scheme, 1985.

- (2) The Telex Schemes, 1980 to 1984, and this Scheme may be cited together as the Telex Schemes 1980 to 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on the 1st day of April 1985.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Minister for Communications pursuant to [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) (2) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) has concurred in the increase of charges effected by this Scheme.

###### 5.

5. The Telex Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 197 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0197.html) ) are hereby amended by:

- (*a*) the deletion in Regulations 5 (1) of the words from "to an installation" to "the Channel Islands" and for the words "in the Schedule" there shall be substituted "in Parts I and II of the Schedule":

- (*b*) the addition of the following paragraph to Regulation 9:

  > - "(5) The subscriber shall pay on demand to Bord Telecom Éireann for the use of the installation the subscription specified in Part V of the Schedule to these Regulations as appropriate to the type of installation."

- (*c*) the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule thereto (inserted by paragraph 4 (*b*) of the Telex (Amendment) Scheme 1984 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0082.html) )).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The main purpose of this Scheme is to provide for an increase of 15 per cent in the charges for domestic and cross channel calls and to include revised rental charges involving a reduction in the previous maximum and an increase in the previous minimum rate to arrive at a single nationwide charge for each type of installation.
