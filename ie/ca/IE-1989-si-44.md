# S.I. No. 44/1989 - Minerals Development (Amendment) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 44 of 1989.

MINERALS DEVELOPMENT (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1989.

I, MICHAEL SMITH, T.D., Minister for Energy, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0031/sec0080.html#sec80) of the [Minerals Development Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1940), and the Energy (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0009.html) ) (as adapted by the Industry and Energy (Alteration of name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 385 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0385.html) )), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Minerals Development (Amendment) Regulations, 1989, and shall come into operation on the 10th day of April, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The Minerals Development Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 340 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0340.html) ), are hereby amended:—

( *a* ) by the substitution in Regulation 3 (1) (*b*) for "£10.00" of "£50.00", and

( *b* ) by the substitution in Form No. 1 in the First Schedule for "£10.00" of "£50.00".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of March, 1989.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for Energy.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 14th day

of March, 1989.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Regulation, which comes into operation on the 10th day of April, 1989 increases the application fee for a prospecting licence to £50.00.
