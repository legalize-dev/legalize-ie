# S.I. No. 348/1985 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) (No. 2) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 348 of 1985.

PRISONS ACT, 1970 (SECTION 7) (NO. 2) ORDER, 1985.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee of Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) (No. 2) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 24th day of October, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0118.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Article 3 (2) of the following:

> - "(2) This section shall be in operation for the period of twelve months commencing on the 26th day of April, 1985, and shall cease to be in operation upon the expiration of that period".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of October, 1985.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order continues in operation [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) for a further period of six months.
