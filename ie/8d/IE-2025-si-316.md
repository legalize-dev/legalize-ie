# S.I. No. 316/2025 - European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th July,* 2025.

I, JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2024/1262 of 13 March 2024[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

No. 174 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0174.html) ), the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0324.html) ), and the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. 2. These Regulations come into operation on 4 December 2026.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 543 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0543.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 3(1) (as last amended by Regulation 3 of the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0324.html) )) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “Directive” the following definition:

> “‘Directive’ means Directive 2010/63/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 September 2010[^2] as amended by Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2024/1262 of 13 March 2024[^1] ;”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) Regulations 2012 to give further effect to Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2024/1262 of 13 March 2024 amending Directive 2010/63/EU of the European Parliament and the Council of 22 September 2010 as regards the requirements for establishments and for the care and accommodation of animals, and as regards the method of killing animals.

Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2024/1262 amends Annex III of Directive 2010/63/EU (which lays down requirements for establishments and for the care and accommodation of animals used for scientific purposes) as well as Annex IV thereof (which lays down appropriate species-specific killing methods for animals used for scientific purposes).

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Protection of Animals used for Scientific Purposes) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 and come into operation on 4 December 2026.
