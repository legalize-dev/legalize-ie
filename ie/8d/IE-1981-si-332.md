# S.I. No. 332/1981 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

Citation.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) Regulations, 1981.

Commencement.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 28th day of September, 1981.

Interpretation.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires “the Act” means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

“road” means a public road;

“perimeter road” means a road on and along the roadway of which there runs the boundary of a county or other borough, an urban district or a town;

“special speed limit” and “built-up area speed limit” have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

“distance” means distance as measured along the centre line of a road.

Built-up area speed limit.

###### 4.

4. (1) Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby delared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

Special speed limit.

###### 5.

5. Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

Exceptions.

###### 6.

6. There shall be excepted from the special speed limit prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:—

- (*a*) ambulances,

- (*b*) fire brigade vehicles, and

- (*c*) vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochána in the performance of their duties as such members.

Revocation.

###### 7.

7. The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 399 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0399.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 399 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0399.html) ) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in this area.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area speed limit prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) and declare the public roads mentioned in the Second Schedule to be subject to that speed limit.

The Regulations also prescribe a speed limit of 40 miles per hour in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Third Schedule.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and Garda Síochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are expected from all speed limits by the Regulations.
