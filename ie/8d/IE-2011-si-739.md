# S.I. No. 739/2011 - European Communities (Marketing of Seeds) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th January*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2010/60/EU of 30 August 2010[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Marketing of Seeds) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Marketing of Seeds) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 431 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0431.html) ) are amended by substituting, in Regulation 2, the definition of “Commission Directive” (as amended by the European Communities (Marketing of Seeds) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 531 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0531.html) ) for the following—

> “ ‘Commission Directive’ means Commission Directive 2008/62/EC of 20 June 2008[^2] , Commission Directive 2009/145/EC of 26 November 2009[^3] and Commission Directive 2010/60/EU of 30 August 2010;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 December 2011.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for derogations in relation to the marketing of preservation mixtures from the requirements of the Council Directive 66/401/EEC on the marketing of fodder plant seed and give effect to Commission Directive 2010/60/EU. Preservation mixtures of fodder plant seed are intended for recreation of the flora of natural and semi-natural habitats. These Regulations specify requirements for the production and marketing of such mixtures.
