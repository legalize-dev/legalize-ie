S.I. No. 37 of 1983.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (WATER POLLUTION) ACT, 1977 (TRANSFER OF APPEALS) ORDER, 1978, (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1983.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1977), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , (Transfer of Appeals) Order, 1978 (Amendment) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 11th day of April, 1983.

###### 3.

3. Article 5 (4) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Transfer of Appeals) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 96 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0096.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "to 1982" for "and 1976".

###### 4.

4. The Schedule to the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Transfer of Appeals) Order, 1978 is hereby amended as follows:—

( *a* ) by the deletion of the references therein to section 6 and to [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) (1) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , and

( *b* ) by the addition to the said Schedule of the following matter:—

**EXPLANATORY NOTES.**

1. This Order provides for certain procedural matters related to appeals under the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) to An Bord Pleanála.

2. The Order also authorises the Minister for the Environment with the consent of the Minister for Finance to make regulations providing for the payment to An Bord Pleanála of prescribed fees in relation to appeals to the Board.
