# S.I. No. 81/2003 - European Communities (Fresh Meat and Poultry) (Checks on General Hygiene) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Decision 2001/471/EC of 8 June 2001[^1] hereby make the following regulations —

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Fresh Meat and Poultry) (Checks on General Hygiene) Regulations 2002.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 3 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0003.html) ) are amended —

- (a) by inserting, after Regulation 3 —

  > “3A(1) A person shall not use an establishment for the production, rewrapping, cutting or storage of fresh poultrymeat unless he or she has in place a procedure that complies with Article 1 of Commission Decision 2001/471/EC of 8 June 2001 and conducts regular checks on the general hygiene conditions in the establishment in accordance with that Article.
  >
  > - (2) This Regulation shall not apply to an establishment to which Regulation 3(4) of these Regulations applies until 8 June 2003.”

- (b) by substituting “€3,000” for £1,500” in Regulation 19.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Fresh Meat) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 434 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0434.html) ) are amended —

- (a) by inserting, after Regulation 3 —

  > “3A(1) Notwithstanding Regulation 12, a person shall not use an establishment for the production wrapping, rewrapping, cutting, storage packaging or repackaging of fresh meat unless he or she has in place a procedure that complies with Article 1 of Commission Decision 2001/471/EC of 8 June 2001 and conducts regular checks on the general hygiene conditions in the establishment in accordance with that Article.
  >
  > - (2) This Regulation shall not apply to —
  >
  >     - (i) an abattoir to which the [Abattoirs Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1988 applies,
  >
  >     - (ii) a cutting plant in respect of which a derogation has been granted under Regulation 6(5), or
  >
  >     - (iii) a cold store in respect of which a derogation has been granted under Regulation 6(6),
  >
  >       until 8 June 2003.”

- (b) by inserting, after “microbiological checks” in Article 12 (1)(a) “carried out in accordance with Article 2 of Commission Decision 2001/471/EC of 8 June 2001.”

- (c) by substituting “€3,000” for £1,000” in Regulation 20.
