# S.I. No. 151/2021 - Pet Travel (Cats, dogs and ferrets) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th April,* 2021.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec36) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EU) No 576/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 June 2013[^1] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 577/2013 of 28 June 2013[^2] (as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1219/2014 of 13 November 2014[^3] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/561 of 11 April 2016[^4] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1293 of 29 July 2019[^5] ) and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/772 of 21 November 2017[^6] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pet Travel (Cats, dogs and ferrets) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. The Pet Travel (Cats, dogs and ferrets) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0141.html) ) are amended-

- (*a*) in Regulation 7 (2) by substituting “Finland, Malta or Norway” for “Finland, Malta or the United Kingdom”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 8(2) by inserting “Dublin Port,” after “Dublin Airport,”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 March, 2021.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

This Statutory Instrument amends the Pet Travel (Cats, dogs and ferrets) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0141.html) ) in view of the departure of the UK from the European Union.
