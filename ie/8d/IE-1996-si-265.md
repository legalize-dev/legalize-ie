# S.I. No. 265/1996 - National Milk Agency (Fees) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 265 of 1996.

NATIONAL MILK AGENCY (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1996

I, Ivan Yates, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0014.html#sec14) (4) and [20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Fees) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994).

###### 3.

3. The following fees are hereby prescribed for the purposes of Section 14 of the Act:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For inspecting a register | £1.00 |
| For obtaining a certified copy under Section 14(2) of the Act | £2.00 |
| For obtaining a certificate under Section 14(3) of the Act | £2.00 |

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 3rd day of September, 1996

______________________

Minister for Agriculture, Food

and Forestry
