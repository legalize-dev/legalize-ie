# S.I. No. 643/2018 - Inland and Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th January*, 2019.

I, SEÁN CANNEY, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Climate Action and Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec70) and [80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec80) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) (as adapted by the Communications, Energy and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2016 ( [S.I. No. 421 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0421.html) )) and the Communications, Climate Action and Environment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2018 ( [S.I. No. 472 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0472.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2019.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “Act of 2010” means the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010).

###### 4.

4. A contravention of Regulation 3, 5, 11(5), 12, 14(2), 15(4), 16, 17, 18, 19(1), 20(2), (3), (4) or (8), 21(1) or 22 of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 585 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0585.html) ) is declared to be a contravention to which section 70 of the Act of 2010 applies.

###### 5.

5. The form set out in Schedule 1 is prescribed for the purposes of sections 70(2) and 80(1) of the Act of 2010.

###### 6.

6. (1) The Bye-laws specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2 are prescribed for the purpose of section 80(1)(*b*) of the Act of 2010.

- (2) The Bye-laws specified in Part 2 of Schedule 2 are prescribed for the purpose of section 80(1)(*c*) of the Act of 2010.

###### 7.

7. The prescribed amount of a fixed charge in a fixed charge notice under sections 70 and 80 of the Act of 2010 is €150.

###### 8.

8. The Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 363 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0363.html) ) are revoked.
