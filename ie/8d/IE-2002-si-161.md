# S.I. No. 161/2002 - Life Assurance (Provision of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Noel Treacy, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 43B, 43D and 43E (inserted by the [Insurance Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0042/print.html) (No. 42 of 2000)) of the [Insurance Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1989) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 265 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0265.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Life Assurance (Provision of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2002.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 July 2002.

###### 3.

3. The Life Assurance (Provision of Information) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 15 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0015.html) ) are amended by substituting “6%” for “8%” in paragraph 2 of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to those Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of these regulations is to amend the maximum illustration rate specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2, of the Life Assurance (Provision of Information) Regulations 2002.
