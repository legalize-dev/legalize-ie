**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**S. I. No. 199 of 2005**

**District Court (Sex Offenders) (Amendment) Rules, 2005**

**S.I. 199 of 2005**

**District Court (Sex Offenders) (Amendment) Rules, 2005.**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

1 These rules may be cited as the District Court (Sex Offenders) (Amendment) Rules, 2005.

2 These rules shall come into operation on the 6^th day of April 2005 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the being in force.

3 Order 38 of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition immediately after rules 4 thereof, of the following:

> ***“Sex Offenders Act, 2001***
>
> *5 (1) A certificate for the purposes of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec14) of the [Sex Offenders Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) shall be in the Form 38.14, Schedule B and shall be signed by a District Court clerk.*
>
> - *(2) An order imposing post-release supervision pursuant to [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec29) of the [Sex Offenders Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) may be in the Form 38.15 Schedule B and copy of such Order shall be transmitted to the Governor of the Prison or person in charge of the other place of detention to which the accused is committed”.*

4. The Forms Numbered 38.14 and 38.15 in Schedule 1 hereof shall be added to Schedule B of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

5 The District Court (Sex Offenders) Rules, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 206 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0206.html) ) are hereby repealed.
