# S.I. No. 264/1997 - Local Government (Value For Money) Unit (Establishment) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 264 of 1997.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (VALUE FOR MONEY) UNIT (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1997

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/sec0014.html#sec14) (2) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1997), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Value for Money) Unit (Establishment) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The Local Government (Value for Money) Unit shall be established on the 1st day of July, 1997.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 19th day of June, 1997.

Brendan Howlin

_____________________

Minister for the Environment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order establishes the Local Government (Value for Money) Unit under [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) .
