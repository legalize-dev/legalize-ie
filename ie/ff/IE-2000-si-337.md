# S.I. No. 337/2000 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) , 5A and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

Citation and construction.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations, the Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 419 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0419.html) ) and the Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 568 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0568.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) Regulations, 1993 to 2000.

Revocation.

###### 2.

2. The Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999 ( [No. 298 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0298.html) ) are hereby revoked.

Modification of Part IV in the case of certain schemes.

###### 3.

3. Article 7 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Funding Standard) Regulations, 1993 is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (2) of the following sub-article:

> - “(2)
>     - (*a*) In the case of an external scheme section 43(1) of the Act is hereby modified by the substitution for paragraphs (*a*) and (*b*) of the following:
>
>     - (*a*) in the case of a relevant scheme which commenced before the commencement of this Part and to which paragraph (*c*) does not apply, not later than 4 years after such commencement or such later date not being later than 1 April 2000 as the Board may determine on application in the case of any particular external scheme to be reasonable and not contrary to the interests of the members of the scheme,
>
>     - (*b*) (i) in the case of a relevant scheme which commenced between 1 January 1991 and 30 June 1991 and to which paragraph (*c*) does not apply, not later than 31 December 1994 or such later date not being later than 1 April 2000 as the Board may determine on application in the case of any particular external scheme to be reasonable and not contrary to the interests of the members of the scheme,
>
>         - (ii) in the case of a relevant scheme which commenced on or after 1 July 1991 and to which paragraph (*c*) does not apply, not later than 3½ years after the commencement of the scheme or such later date not being later than 1 April 2000 as the Board may determine on application in the case of any particular external scheme to be reasonable and not contrary to the interests of the members of the scheme, and
>
>     - (*c*) in the case of a relevant scheme being an external scheme which applied for and was granted an extension to 1 January 1998, 1 January 1999 or 1 January 2000 of the latest effective date of the first actuarial funding certificate, not later than 1 April 2000”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Funding Standard Regulations so as to extend from 1 January 2000 to 1 April 2000, the latest effective date of first actuarial funding certificates in respect of external schemes, with members in this State. This extension has the corresponding effect of extending the latest date for submission of first actuarial funding certificates having an effective date of 1 April 2000 to 1 January 2001.
