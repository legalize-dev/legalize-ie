# S.I. No. 367/1974 - Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1974 (Revocation) (No. 2) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 367 of 1974.

AGRICULTURAL WAGES (MINIMUM RATES) ORDER, 1974 (REVOCATION) (No. 2) ORDER, 1974.

The Agricultural Wages Board, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0053/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Agricultural Wages Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0053/index.html) (No. 53 of 1936), and of every and any other power them in this behalf enabling and after due compliance with subsection (7) of the said Section 17, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1974 (Revocation) (No. 2) Order, 1974.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 3rd day of February, 1975.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 107 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0107.html) ) is hereby revoked.

SEALED with the Common Seal of the Agricultural Wages

Board this 20th day of December, 1974.

The Seal of the Board was affixed in the

presence of—

SEÁN LEE,

Chairman of the Board.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

The effect of this Order is to revoke the Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 107 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0107.html) ).
