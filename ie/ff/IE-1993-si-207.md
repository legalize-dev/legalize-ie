# S.I. No. 207/1993 - Shannon Navigation Act, 1990 (Ballinamore and Ballyconnell Navigation) (Delegation of Functions) Order, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 207 of 1993.

SHANNON NAVIGATION ACT, 1990 (BALLINAMORE AND BALLYCONNELL NAVIGATION) (DELEGATION OF FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1993.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 5 (9) *(a)* of the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (Ballinamore and Ballyconnell Navigation) (Delegation of Functions) Order, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The functions of the Commissioners under the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1990) (other than sections 2 *(f)* and 3 thereof and the Schedule thereto) in relation to the restoration, repair, improvement, extension and development of the Ballinamore and Ballyconnell navigation are hereby delegated to the Electricity Supply Board for the period of two years beginning on the 22nd day of August, 1993.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of July, 1993.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order delegates to the Electricity Supply Board for a period of a further 2 years from 22nd August, 1993 certain functions of the Commissioners of Public Works under the [Shannon Navigation Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) for the purpose of enabling the Electricity Supply Board to undertake the restoration of the Ballinamore and Ballyconnell Navigation.
