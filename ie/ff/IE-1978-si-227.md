# S.I. No. 227/1978 - Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977 (Appointed Day) (British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 227 of 1978.

WORKER PARTICIPATION (STATE ENTERPRISES) ACT, 1977 (APPOINTED DAY) (BRITISH AND IRISH STEAM PACKET COMPANY LIMITED) ORDER, 1978.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Appointed Day) (British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited) Order, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The 15th day of August, 1978, is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), in relation to the British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Labour this 15th dayof August, 1978.

**B. MAC MAGHNUIS,**

A person authorised by the Minister for Labour

to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order prescribes the appointed day for determining election years for the purposes of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to the British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited.
