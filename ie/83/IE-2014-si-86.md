# S.I. No. 86/2014 - Maritime Jurisdiction (Boundaries of Exclusive Economic Zone) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st February*, 2014.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 87](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec87) (3) of the [Sea Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Maritime Jurisdiction (Boundaries of Exclusive Economic Zone) Order 2014.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 31 March 2014.

###### 4.

4. As provided by [Section 87](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec87) (2) of the [Sea Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) , the outer limit of the exclusive economic zone between reference number 84 set out in Article 2 of this Order and reference number 115 set out in Article 3 of this Order is the line every point of which lies at a distance of 200 nautical miles from the nearest point of the baseline.

###### 5.

5. The Maritime Jurisdiction (Exclusive Fishery Limits) Order 1976 ( [S.I. No. 320 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0320.html) ) is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this Order is to designate the boundaries of the exclusive economic zone of the State.
