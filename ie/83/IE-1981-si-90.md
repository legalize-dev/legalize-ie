S.I. No. 90 of 1981.

CIRCUIT COURT (FEES) ORDER, 1981.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 16th day of March, 1981.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. (1) The fees set out in the second column of the Schedule to this Order shall be charged in the Circuit Court Offices in respect of the items set out in the first column.

- (2) The Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 51 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0051.html) ), is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. The fee in relation to each item shall be taken by means of a stamp affixed to or impressed on the document indicated in the third column of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 6.

6. Where the fee is denoted by means of an adhesive stamp, the stamp shall be cancelled by the officer in the Circuit Court Office who issues or receives the document.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order increases as from 16th March, 1981, the fees at present authorised to be charged in Circuit Court Offices. The present fees are prescribed in the Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1980 which is revoked by this Order.

The Order also provides that the fees in all cases may be taken by either adhesive stamps or impressed stamps.

It should be noted that, by reason of [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0009/sec0042.html#sec42) of the [Workmen's Compensation Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , no court fees are payable by any party in any proceedings by or against a workman under the Workmen's Compensation Acts prior to the order of the Circuit Court granting or refusing compensation.

It should also be noted that, under [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0042/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Rent Restrictions Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) , no court fees are payable by the tenant in any proceedings (including any appeal or case stated) under Part III of the Act.
