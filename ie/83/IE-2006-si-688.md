# S.I. No. 688/2006 - National Sports Campus Development Authority Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No. 688 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0688.html)

NATIONAL SPORTS CAMPUS DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY ACT 2006 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER 2006

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0019/print.html#sec5) of the [National Sports Campus Development Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Sports Campus Development Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (Establishment Day) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January 2007 is appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Sports Campus Development Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2006).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 19 December 2006. |
|  |  |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be legal interpretation.)*

This Order appoints the 1^st day of January 2007 as the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Sports Campus Development Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) .

The purpose of the National Sports Campus Development Authority Act, 2006 is to provide for the establishment of the National Sports Campus Development Authority (NSCDA) on a statutory basis. NSCDA will succeed the present limited company Campus and Stadium Ireland Development Company Ltd (CSID) in function and responsibility and will continue the role of overseeing the planning and development of a sports campus at Abbotstown.
