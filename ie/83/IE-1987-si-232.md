# S.I. No. 232/1987 - Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 232 of 1987.

DUBLIN METER PARKING PLACES RULES, 1987.

Rules for the administration of meter parking places in the County Borough of Dublin, made by the Right Honourable, The Lord Mayor, Aldermen and Burgesses of Dublin, the appropriate local authority in exercise of powers conferred by Section 90 (3) (*b*) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961)—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1987.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Rules shall come into force on 1st October, 1987.

3 ..

###### 3.

**3.** In these Rules—

"the Principal Rules" means the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1985 ( [S.I. No. 27 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0027.html) ).

4 ..

###### 4.

**4.** Rule 7 of the Principal Rules, as amended by the Dublin Meter Parking Places (No. 2) Rules 1985 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0366.html) ), is hereby further amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (b)—

( *b* ) The Parking fee payable under paragraph (*a*) of this Rule shall in the "prime area" be:—

- (i) One twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding twenty minutes, or

- (ii) Two twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding forty minutes, or

- (iii) One Fifty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding fifty minutes, or

- (iv) Three twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour, or

- (v) One fifty pence coin and one twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and ten minutes, or

- (vi) Four twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and twenty minutes, or

- (vii) One fifty pence coin and two twenty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and thirty minutes, or

- (viii) Five twenty pence coins or two fifty pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and forty minutes or

- (ix) Three twenty pence coins and one fifty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and fifty minutes, or

- (x) Six twenty pence coins or two fifty pence coins and one twenty pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding two hours.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 26th day of August, 1987.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Rules amend Rule 7 of the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1985 (S.I. 27 of 1985) to provide for an increase in the rate of parking fees payable in the "prime area" from 30p per hour to 60p per hour.
