# S.I. No. 536/2018 - Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th December*, 2018.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (4) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) and Article 4 of the Córas Iompair Éireann (Additional Powers) Order 1988 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0381.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “amending Scheme” means the Scheme amending the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order 1945 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 1945](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1945/en/si/0242.html) )), prepared by Córas Iompair Éireann and submitted to the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport under section 44(5) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950) and set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The amending Scheme is confirmed and is deemed to have come into operation on 17 January 2018.

## SCHEDULE Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme 2018

The Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff in paragraph (*a*) and (*b*) of Article 9(3) (inserted by [S.I. No. 288 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0288.html) ) is amended by substituting “17 January 2022” for “17 January 2018” (inserted by [S.I. No. 476 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0476.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 December 2018.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this Order is to extend voluntary severance terms applicable to members of the Regular Wages Grade Pension Scheme to 17 January 2022.
