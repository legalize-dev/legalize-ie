# S.I. No. 308/2012 - Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999 (Section 32) Order 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th August*, 2012.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec32) (7) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999 (Section 32) Order 2012.

###### 2.

2. [Section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec32) (3)(*a*) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999) is amended—

- (*a*) in subparagraph (ii) (amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0041/print.html#sec9) (1)(*b*)(i) of the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0041/print.html) (No. 41 of 2006)), by substituting “€8.8 million” for “€7.3 million”, and

- (*b*) in subparagraph (iii) (amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0041/print.html#sec9) (1)(*b*)(ii) of the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0041/print.html) ), by substituting “€4.4 million” for “€3.65 million”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 August 2012.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order amends the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999) (as amended by the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0041/print.html) (No. 41 of 2006)) by increasing the balance sheet total and turnover thresholds for the purpose of availing of audit exemption to €4.4 million and €8.8 million respectively.
