S.I. No. 201 of 1973.

CREDIT UNION REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, JUSTIN KEATING, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0019/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Credit Union Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1966), and after consultation with the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Union Regulations, 1973, and shall come into operation on the 21st day of August, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The [Credit Union Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1966), is hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution in section 7 (4) for "the 31st day of December " of " the 30th day of September ";

- (*b*) the substitution in section 12 (3) for " ten days " of " twenty-one days";

- (*c*) the substitution in section 17 (4) for " fourteen days " of " twenty-one days ";

- (*d*) the substitution in section 19 (1) for " January or February " of " October, November, December or January of the following year ";

- (*e*) the insertion in section 19 (2) after " seven days " of " and not earlier than twenty-one days";

- (*f*) the deletion in section 22 (1) of " outstanding at the end of the preceding financial year "; and

- (*g*) the substitution in section 22 (1) for " shares which have been allotted during that financial year " of " shares which have been held during a portion of that financial year ".

###### 3.

3. (1) The accounts and balance sheets required to be made out by a credit union under [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0019/sec0007.html#sec7) (5) of the [Credit Union Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , shall show the following information, that is to say—

- (*a*) the number and amount of loans overdue for payment including the net unpaid principal balances after deduction of shares and deposits applicable,

- (*b*) the limits on secured and unsecured loans, and

- (*c*) the amount invested by the credit union and the security or securities in which it is invested.

- (2) The information required under paragraph (1) of this Regulation to be shown in the accounts and balance sheets of a credit union shall be shown in the form specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations amend a number of time limits prescribed in the [Credit Union Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , and provide for the inclusion of additional information on loans and investments in the accounts and balance sheets required to be made out by a credit union.
