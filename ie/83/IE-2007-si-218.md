# S.I. No. 218/2007 - British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002 (Expiry) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 218 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0218.html)

BRITISH-IRISH AGREEMENT (AMENDMENT) ACT 2002 (EXPIRY) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th May*, 2007.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 7(4) of the [British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2002), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (Expiry) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The 10th day of May 2007 is hereby fixed as the day on which the [British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2002) shall expire.

GIVEN under my Official Seal

9 May 2007

**BERTIE AHERN, T.D.**

Taoiseach

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order appoints the day on which the [British-Irish Agreement (Amendment) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2002) shall expire.
