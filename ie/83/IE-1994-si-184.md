S.I. No. 184 of 1994.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF WESTMEATH) REGULATIONS 1994.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Westmeath) Regulations, 1994.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 18th day of July, 1994.

3 Interpretation.

###### 3.

**3.** In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires—

"the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"road" means a public road;

"National Road" means a road declared to be a National Road in the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Declaration of National Roads Order 1977) ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0164.html) ) and any reference to the number of such road as shown in column 1 of the First Schedule or column 1 of the Second Schedule to the aforementioned Order.

"perimeter road" means a road on and along the roadway of which there runs the boundary of a borough or urban district or a town and shall be considered as being within such boundary for the purpose of these Regulations;

"special speed limit" and "built up area speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road.

4 Built-up area speed limit.

###### 4.

**4.** (1) Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

5 Special speed limit.

###### 5.

**5.** Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

6 Exceptions.

###### 6.

**6.** There shall be excepted from the special speed limit prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:—

( *a* ) ambulances,

( *b* ) fire brigade vehicles, and

( *c* ) vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochana, in the performance of their duties as such members.

7 ..

###### 7.

**7.** The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wesmeath) Regulations, 1981 (S.I. No. 365 of 1981) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) County of Westmeath Regulations, 1981 (S.I. No. 365 of 1981), and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in County Westmeath.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area speed limit prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) and declare the roads mentioned in the Second Schedule, to be subject to that limit.

The Regulations also prescribed a speed limit of 40 mph in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Third Schedule.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and Garda Síochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are exempt from the speed limits prescribed by the Regulations.
