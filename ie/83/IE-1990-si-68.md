# S.I. No. 68/1990 - Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 68 of 1990.

COURTS-MARTIAL (LEGAL AID) REGULATIONS, 1990.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1986, ( [S.I. No. 425 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0425.html) ), as amended by the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 25 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0025.html) ), are hereby amended with effect from the 1st day of January, 1990—

( *a* ) by the substitution in Regulation 12 (2) (a) of "£104.26" for "£101.72" and of "£46.38" for "£45.24", and

( *b* ) by the substitution in Regulation 14 of "£27.03" for "£26.37".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of March, 1990.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Defence.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 21st day of March, 1990.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.
