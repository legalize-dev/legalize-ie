# S.I. No. 354/2026 - Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th July,* 2026.

I, SEÁN CANNEY, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 41(1) (as amended by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec11) (c) of the [Road Traffic and Roads Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2023)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )) and the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 144 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0144.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Justice, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 14 August 2026.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 5(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 460 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0460.html) ) is amended –

- (a) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (a):

  > - “(a) in the case of a vehicle with a design gross weight not exceeding 3,500 kg –
  >
  >     - (i) €250 in respect of its detention, removal and storage for one day or a part of a day, and
  >
  >     - (ii) €70 in respect of each further day or part of a day that it is stored;”,

  and

- (b) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (b):

  > - “(b) in the case of a vehicle with a design gross weight exceeding 3,500 kg –
  >
  >     - (i) €500 in respect of its detention, removal and storage for one day or a part of a day, and
  >
  >     - (ii) €100 in respect of each further day or part of a day that it is stored.”.

GIVEN under my hand,

21 July, 2026.

SEÁN CANNEY

Minister of State at the Department of Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 460 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0460.html) ) by increasing the charges payable to the Garda Commissioner by the owner of a vehicle which has been detained, removed and stored in accordance with the Regulations. The increased charges come into operation on 14 August 2026.
