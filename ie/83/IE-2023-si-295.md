# S.I. No. 295/2023 - Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th June,* 2023.

I, DARA CALLEARY, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec12) (1), [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec22) (2), [149](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec149) (8), [152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec152) (3) and [343](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec343) (4) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014) (as adapted by the Business, Enterprise and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 519 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0519.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 15 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0015.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 11 June 2023.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, Regulations of 2015 means the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (Forms) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0147.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The form (Form A1) set out in Part 1 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 4.

4. The form (Form B10) set out in Part 2 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 16 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 5.

5. The form (Form B10a) set out in Part 3 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 17 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 6.

6. The form (Form B69) set out in Part 4 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 19 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 7.

7. The form (Form B1) set out in Part 5 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 23 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe amended forms for the purposes of sections 22(2), 149(8), 149(9), 152(3) and 343(4) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) .
