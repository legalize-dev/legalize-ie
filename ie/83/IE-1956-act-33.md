# RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (CONFIRMATION OF ORDER) (NO. 3) ACT, 1956

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO CONFIRM THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (MOTOR CARS) ORDER, 1956, MADE BY THE MINISTER FOR INDUSTRY AND COMMERCE UNDER SECTION 9 OF THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES ACT, 1953. [8*th August,* 1956.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Confirmation of Order.

**1.**—The Restrictive Trade Practices (Motor Cars) Order, 1956 (S.I. No. 86 of 1956), made by the Minister for Industry and Commerce under section 9 of the Restrictive Trade Practices Act, 1953 (No. 14 of 1953), is hereby confirmed.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Restrictive Trade Practices (Confirmation of Order) (No. 3) Act, 1956.
