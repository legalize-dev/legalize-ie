# S.I. No. 194/1998 - Local Government (Planning and Development) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 194 of 1998.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1998.

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963) and by Section 4 of that Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) The collective citation, "the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1994 to 1998" shall include these Regulations.

2 Commencement

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 10th day of June, 1998.

3 Amendment of 1994 Regulations

###### 3.

**3.** Subarticle (4) (*b*) of article 11 of the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1994 is hereby amended by the insertion after subparagraph (vi) of the following subparagraph:

> - "(vii) as a supermarket, the retail floor space of which exceeds 3,000 square metres."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment and Local Government, this 9th day of June, 1998.

**NOEL DEMPSEY,**

Minister for the Environment and Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 86 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0086.html) ) to provide that a change of use of any premises to a supermarket the retail floor space of which exceeds 3,000 square metres shall not be exempted development.
