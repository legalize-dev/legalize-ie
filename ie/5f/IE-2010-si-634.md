# S.I. No. 634/2010 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st December*, 2010.

I, ÉAMON Ó CUÍV, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 25 (amended by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec37) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) (as adapted by the Social and Family Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 186 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0186.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2011.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 (amended by article 2 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 465 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0465.html) )) of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 610 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0610.html) ) is amended—

- (*a*) in sub-article (2)(*a*), by substituting “€8.00” for “€8.80”,

- (*b*) in sub-article (2)(*b*), by substituting “€4,000” for “€4,395”, and

- (*c*) in sub-article (2)(*c*), by substituting “€4.00” for “€4.40”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

21 December 2010.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 December 2010.

**ÉAMON Ó CUÍV,**

Minister for Social Protection.
