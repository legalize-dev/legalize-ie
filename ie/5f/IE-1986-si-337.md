# S.I. No. 337/1986 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 337 of 1986.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) (No. 4) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 21st day of October, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on a deposit within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1948), is hereby prescribed as 8.50 per cent. per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 3) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0251.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 20th day of October, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe as 8.50 per cent. per annum the rate of interest payable on deposits in the Post Office Savings Banks as from 21st October, 1986.
