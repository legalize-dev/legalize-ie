# S.I. No. 61/2005 - European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENT

**[S.I. No. 61 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0061.html)**

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (ADDITIVES, COLOURS AND SWEETENERS IN FOODSTUFFS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2005

[S.I. No. 61 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0061.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (ADDITIVES, COLOURS AND SWEETENERS IN FOODSTUFFS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2005

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ( [No. 27 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ), and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2003/115/EC[^1] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 December 2003 amending Directive 94/35/EC^2 on sweeteners for use in foodstuffs, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2001 and 2002 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 to 2005.

- (3) Subject to Regulation 4(2), these Regulations shall come into operation on 29 July 2005.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Minister” means the Minister for Health and Children;

“Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0437.html) );

“Regulations of 2001” means the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 342 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0342.html) ), and

“Regulations of 2002” means the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs (Amendment) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 344 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0344.html) ) and the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment No. 3) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 380 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0380.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1), in the definition of “the Directives” by substituting

  > “European Parliament and Council Directive 94/35/EC of 30 June 1994 (as amended by Directive 96/83/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 December 1996 and Directive 2003/115/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 December 2003)”

for

> “European Parliament and Council Directive 94/35/EC of 30 June 1994 (as amended by Directive 96/83/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 December 1996)”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 2(1), by substituting for the definition of “Directive 94/35/EC” the following definition:

  > “‘Directive 94/35/EC’ means European Parliament and Council Directive 94/35/EC of 30 June 1994 on sweeteners for use in foodstuffs (as amended by Directive 96/83/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 December 1996, and Directive 2003/115/EC^10C of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 December 2003)”,

and by the addition of the following footnote thereto:

> “^10C OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p.65”;

- (*c*) in Regulations 10, 11, 12 and 14, by deleting the words (as supplemented by the Annex to Directive 96/83/EC)” in each place where they occur and

- (*d*) by substituting for Regulation 15(1)(*b*) the following:

- (*b*) “the labelling of a table-top sweetener, containing polyols, or aspartame, or salt of aspartame and acesulfame, or any of these bears the following warnings, as appropriate—

    - (i) in the case of polyols, “excessive consumption may induce laxative effects”,

    - (ii) in the case of aspartame, “contains a source of phenylalanine”, and

    - (iii) in the case of salt of aspartame and acesulfame “contains a source of phenylalanine.”

###### 4.

4. (1) Products placed on the market before 29 July 2005, which do not comply with Directive 2003/115/EC may be marketed until 29 January 2006 at the latest and such provisions of these Regulations as give effect to Directive 2003/115/EC shall not apply to such stocks.

- (2) Products complying with Directive 2003/115/EC may be placed on the market from the date these Regulations are signed by the Minister.

- (3) In this Regulation, “Directive 2003/115/EC” means Directive 2003/115/EC[^1-fn_1] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 December 2003 amending Directive 94/35/EC on sweeteners for use in foodstuffs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Directive 2003/115/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 December 2003 amending European Parliament and Council Directive 94/35/EC of 30 June 1994 on sweeteners for use in foodstuffs (as previously amended by Directive 96/83/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 December 1996).

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0437.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No.61 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0061.html) ).
