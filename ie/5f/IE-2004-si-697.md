# S.I. No. 697/2004 - Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 697 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0697.html) .**

BUILDING REGULATIONS ADVISORY BODY ORDER, 2002 (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2004.

[S.I. No. 697 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0697.html) .

BUILDING REGULATIONS ADVISORY BODY ORDER, 2002 (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2004.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Building Control Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order, 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0002.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of “Seán Balfe” for “John Purcell”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government this 2nd day of November 2004. |
|  | DICK ROCHE, Minister for Environment, Heritage and Local Government. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the appointment of a member to the Building Regulations Advisory Body (BRAB).
