# S.I. No. 381/1996 - Merger Or Take-Over (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Richard Bruton, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1A) (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0019/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Competition (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1996)) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1978), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. ( *a* ) These Regulations may be cited as the Merger or Take-over (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1996.

( *b* ) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January 1997.

###### 2.

2. The fee to accompany a notification, for the purposes of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1978), of a merger or take-over shall be £4,000.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of December, 1996

**RICHARD BRUTON**

Minister for Enterprise and Employment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe the amount of the fee to accompany notifications of mergers, take-overs or acquisitions in accordance with subsection (1A) (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0019/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Competition (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) ) under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) .
