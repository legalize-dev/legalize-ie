# S.I. No. 12/2019 - Planning And Development Act 2000 (Exempted Development) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th January,* 2019

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4A) (inserted by paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec17) of the [Environment (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2011)) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec4) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0358.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Development Act 2000 (Exempted Development) Regulations 2019.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 25th January 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ) are amended by the insertion of the following article:

> “8H. (1) Peat extraction within the meaning of the Act of 1992 shall be exempted development.
>
> - (2) Development necessary to enable compliance with a condition attached to a licence or revised licence under Part IV of the Act of 1992 to carry on peat extraction referred to in paragraph (1) shall be exempted development.
>
> - (3) In this article ‘Act of 1992’ means the [Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992).”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

25 January 2019

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

Article 2 of these Regulations amend the Planning and Development Regulations 2001, as amended, by inserting a new Article 8H to provide that peat extraction within the meaning of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), and any development necessary to comply with a condition attached to a licence or revised licence under Part IV of that Act to carry on such peat extraction, is exempt from the requirement to obtain planning permission.
