# S.I. No. 210/1991 - Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 210 of 1991.

COURTS-MARTIAL (LEGAL AID) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, BRENDAN DALY, Minister for the Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1990" means the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 68 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0068.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 425 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0425.html) ), are hereby amended with effect from the 1st day of January, 1991—

( *a* ) by the substitution in Regulation 12 (2) (*a*) of "£108.44" for "£104.26" (inserted by the Regulations of 1990) and of "£48.24" for "£46.38" (inserted by the Regulations of 1990);

( *b* ) by the substitution in Regulation 14 of "£28.11" for "£27.03" (inserted by the Regulations of 1990), and

( *c* ) by the substitution in Regulation 14 (*a*) of "£0.60" for "£0.50".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of July, 1991.

**BRENDAN DALY,**

Minister for Defence.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

30th day of July, 1991.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.
