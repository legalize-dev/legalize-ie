# S.I. No. 151/1996 - Coroners Act, 1962 (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 151 of 1996.

CORONERS ACT, 1962 (FEES AND EXPENSES) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, Nora Owen, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) (1), [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0029.html#sec29) and [57](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0057.html#sec57) of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1962), and the Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 269 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0269.html) ), and after, in relation to the fees and expenses to be prescribed under section 57 of that Act, consultation with the Minister for the Environment, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1962).

###### 3.

3. The fee to be paid to a coroner who holds an inquest by virtue of section 20 of the Act or to a coroner, other than the coroner who would ordinarily hold the inquest, who holds an inquest by virtue of section 24 of the Act shall be £23. 95.

###### 4.

4. The fee chargeable by a coroner or a county registrar for furnishing a copy of any document to an applicant under section 29 of the Act shall be —

- (*a*) in the case of a coroner, £4. 00 for each document furnished under subsection (3) of the said section 29,

- (*b*) in the case of a county registrar, £4.00 for each document furnished under subsection (4) of the said section 29.

###### 5.

5. The fees and expenses to be prescribed under section 57 of the Act in respect of post-mortem and special examinations, the attendance of witnesses at inquests and the removal or custody of a body shall be those specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 6.

6. The [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0019.html) ), are hereby revoked.

###### 7.

7. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1995.

**EXPENSES**

5. EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe various fees and expenses for the purposes of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , viz., the fee to be paid to a coroner who holds an inquest outside his own district: the fee chargeable by the coroner or a county registrar for furnishing copies of inquest documents and the fees and expenses payable in respect of post-mortem and special examinations, the attendance of witnesses at inquests and the removal or custody of a body.

These Regulations replace the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0019.html) ).
