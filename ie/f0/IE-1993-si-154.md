# S.I. No. 154/1993 - Social Welfare (Adult Dependant) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 154 of 1993.

SOCIAL WELFARE (ADULT DEPENDANT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981 ) (as amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0014/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (No. 2) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1985)) and section 3 of the said Act of 1981, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Short title and collective citation.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Adult Dependant) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Adult Dependant) Regulations, 1986 and 1990, shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Adult Dependant) Regulations, 1986 to 1993.

2 Earnings disregard.

###### 2.

**2.** Article 4 of the Social Welfare (Adult Dependant) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0369.html) ) (as amended by article 4 of the Social Welfare (Adult Dependant) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 137 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0137.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£60" for "£55" in each place in which it appears.

3 Application.

###### 3.

**3.** These Regulations shall come into operation—

( *a* ) on the 2nd day of June, 1993 for the purposes of unemployment assistance,

( *b* ) on the 3rd day of June, 1993 for the purposes of unemployment benefit,

( *c* ) on the 7th day of June, 1993 for the purposes of disability benefit, injury benefit and disablement benefit,

( *d* ) on the 10th day of June, 1993 for the purposes of invalidity pension, retirement pension and pre-retirement allowance,

( *e* ) on the 11th day of June, 1993 for the purposes of old age (contributory) pension, old age pension and blind pension.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 9th day of June, 1993.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 10th day of June, 1993.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations provide for an increase from £55 to £60 in the income limit which is used in determining whether a person is to be regarded as an adult dependant for social welfare payment purposes. The increased limit takes effect, in general, from the beginning of June, 1993.
