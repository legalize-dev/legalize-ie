# S.I. No. 350/2009 - Registration of Deeds Rules 2009

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 350 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0350.html)

REGISTRATION OF DEEDS RULES 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th September*, 2009.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Registration of Deeds Rules, 2009.

- (2) These Rules and the Registration of Deeds Rules 2008 may be cited together as the Registration of Deeds Rules 2008 to 2009.

- (3) These Rules and the Land Registration Rules 2008 shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on the 1st day of November, 2009.

###### 3.

3. An application for cancellation of a Property Adjustment Order pursuant to [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0026/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Family Law Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) or [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0033/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Family Law (Divorce) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) may be made in Form 13, 14 or 15 in the Schedule of Forms hereto.

###### 4.

4. Registration may be effected by entry opposite the entry of the property adjustment order, to the effect that the order has been complied with.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules made under [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec48) of the [Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) , come into effect on 1st November, 2009 and provide for the registration of an entry of compliance with a property adjustment order, pursuant to [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0026/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Family Law Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) or [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0033/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Family Law (Divorce) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) as amended by sections 74 and 75 of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) .
