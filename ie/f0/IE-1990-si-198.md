# S.I. No. 198/1990 - Harbour Authorities (Allowances To Members) Rules, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 198 of 1990.

HARBOUR AUTHORITIES (ALLOWANCES TO MEMBERS) RULES, 1990.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) (7) of the [Harbours Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1947), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ), hereby make the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Harbour Authorities (Allowances to Members) Rules, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The allowance to be paid under paragraph (*a*) or paragraph (*b*) of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) (7) of the [Harbours Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , to a member of a harbour authority in respect of absence from his official residence by reason of attendance at a meeting or meetings to which either such paragraph relates shall with effect as on and from the 1st day of January, 1990, not exceed—

( *a* ) £13.50 in respect of a continuous period of less than seven hours and not less than three hours, and

( *b* ) £18.00 in respect of a continuous period of not less than seven hours.

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule hereto is the form to be used in respect of applications under these Rules.

###### 4.

4. The Harbour Authorities (Allowances to Members) Rules, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 7 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0007.html) ), are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these rules is to provide for increases in the allowances which a harbour authority may pay to its members in respect of attendance at meetings of the authority.
