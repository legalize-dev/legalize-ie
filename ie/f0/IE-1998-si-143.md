# S.I. No. 143/1998 - Irish Medicines Board (Competent Authority) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 143 of 1998.

IRISH MEDICINES BOARD (COMPETENT AUTHORITY) ORDER, 1998.

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on him under subsection (4) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0029/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Irish Medicines Board Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1995), hereby orders that the Irish Medicines Board be appointed as the competent authority for the purposes of Council Directive No. 65/65/EEC of 26 January, 1965, (Official Journal No. 22 of 9 February 1965), as amended, and for the purposes of any regulations under the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and the [Irish Medicines Board Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , giving effect to that Directive, as amended.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health and Children this 6th day of May, 1998.

**BRIAN COWEN,**

Minister for Health and Children

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this order is to appoint the Irish Medicines Board as the competent authority for the purposes of Council Directive No. 65/65/EEC of 26 January 1965, as amended.
