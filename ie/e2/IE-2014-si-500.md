# S.I. No. 500/2014 - European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) (No. 4) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th November*, 2014.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 885/2014 of 13 August 2014[^1] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1021/2014 of 26 September 2014[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) (No.4) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 426 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0426.html) ) are amended by substituting—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1) for the definition of “Commission Regulation 669/2009” the following—

  > “ ‘Commission Regulation 669/2009’ means Commission Regulation (EC) No. 669/2009 of 24 July 2009[^3] as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 212/2010 of 12 March 2010[^4] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 323/2014 of 28 March 2014[^5] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 718/2014 of 27 June 2014[^6] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1021/2014 of 26 September 2014 and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 885/2014 of 13 August 2014;”, and

- (*b*) Regulation 12(2)(*o*) for the following—

  > - “(*o*) provides misleading information in relation to a proposed re-dispatch of a consignment in accordance with Article 21 of EC Regulation 882/2004;
  >
  > - (*p*) contravenes Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 885/2014 of 13 August 2014.”.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No.343 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0343.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 October 2014.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations revise controls on the import of certain food and feed of non-animal origin from specific third countries with regard to pesticide residues.
