# S.I. No. 243/2011 - Planning and Development Act 2000 (Strategic Development Zone) (Amendment) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th May*, 2011.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by subsection (6) of [section 166](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec166) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development Act 2000 (Strategic Development Zone) (Amendment) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 of the Planning and Development Act 2000 (Strategic Development Zone) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 678 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0678.html) ) is amended by inserting “residential development,” after “childcare services,”.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

24 May 2011.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.
