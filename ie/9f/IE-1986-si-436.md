# S.I. No. 436/1986 - Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 436 of 1986.

TAXIMETER (FEES ON VERIFICATION AND STAMPING BY SPECIAL INSPECTORS) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 83](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0083.html#sec83) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 5th day of January, 1987.

###### 2.

2. (1) The fee to be paid in respect of the verification and stamping of a taximeter by a special inspector shall he £2.00.

- (2) Every such fee shall be paid by the person who submits the taximeter for such verification and stamping.

###### 3.

3. The Taximeter (Fees on Verification and Stamping by Special Inspectors) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0158.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 10th day of December, 1986.

**MICHAEL NOONAN.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of December, 1986.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order was made to set out revised fees for the verification and stamping of taximeters.
