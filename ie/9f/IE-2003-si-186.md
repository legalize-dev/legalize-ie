# S.I. No. 186/2003 - Civil Defence Act 2002 (Commencement and Establishment Day) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHAEL SMITH, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec1) (3) and [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec6) of the [Civil Defence Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2002), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Civil Defence Act 2002 (Commencement and Establishment Day) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. The 7th day of May 2003 is appointed as—

- (a) the day on which so much of the [Civil Defence Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2002) as is not in operation immediately before the making of this Order comes into operation, and

- (b) the establishment day for the purposes of that Act.
