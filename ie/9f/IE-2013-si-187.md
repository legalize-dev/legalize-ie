# S.I. No. 187/2013 - Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th June*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 95 (as amended by [section 78](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec78) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and collective citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2013.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2012 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2013.

*Alternative design for traffic sign number RUS 013*

###### 2.

2. The Second Schedule (as amended by Regulation 27 of the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 331 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0331.html) ) to the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 181 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0181.html) ) is amended by inserting an alternative design for traffic sign number RUS 013 (no turn left) as set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE **Alternative Prohibitory Traffic Sign**

Regulation 2

**Traffic sign number RUS 013**

**No Turn Left**

Detail of alternative design

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 June 2013.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This does not form part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe an alternative design that may be used for traffic sign number RUS 013 (no turn left) and the Second Schedule to the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 181 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0181.html) ) is amended accordingly.
