# S.I. No. 806/2005 - National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000 (Approval) Order, 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 806 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0806.html)**

**NATIONAL BEEF ASSURANCE SCHEME ACT, 2000 (APPROVAL) ORDER, 2005**

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 1(5) and 8(1) of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (Approval) Order, 2005.

###### 2.

2. The period for participants to apply for the grant of a certificate of approval under section 8 of the Act is extended until 31 December 2006.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the period for participants to apply for approval under the National Beef Assurance Scheme.
