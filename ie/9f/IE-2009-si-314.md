# S.I. No. 314/2009 - Sea-fisheries (Prosecution of offences) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 314 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0314.html)

SEA-FISHERIES (PROSECUTION OF OFFENCES) ORDER 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st July*, 2009.

The Government, on the request of the Attorney General and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 39(1) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Sea- Fisheries (Prosecution of Offences) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed for the purposes of section 39(1) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) is 1 August 2009, being the day from which the Director of Public Prosecutions is the prosecutor, in lieu of the Attorney General, of sea-fisheries offences as specified in that subsection.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

30 June 2009.

**BRIAN COWEN,**

Taoiseach.
