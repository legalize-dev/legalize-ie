> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**S.I. No. 63 of 2000.**

**Companies (Fees) Order, 2000**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), and section 12C of [the Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982) (inserted by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec46) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999)), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 18th. day of April, 2000.

###### 3.

3. The Eighth Schedule to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) (inserted by the Companies (Fees) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0358.html) )), is hereby altered:

- (a) by the substitution for any reference to the “Companies Acts 1963 to 1990” of “Companies Acts 1963 to 1999”, and

- (b) by the addition to the Table of Fees set out in Part I of the matter set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. There is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 12C(2) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982), (inserted by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec46) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999)), a fee of £250.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The purpose of this Order is to set the level of fees to be paid in connection with certain provisions of the Companies Acts, 1963 to 1999.
