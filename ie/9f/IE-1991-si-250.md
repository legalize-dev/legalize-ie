# S.I. No. 250/1991 - Competition (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 250 of 1991.

COMPETITION (NOTIFICATION FEE) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1991), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. ( *a* ) These Regulations may be cited as the Competition (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1991.

( *b* ) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1991.

###### 2.

2. Every agreement, decision and concerted practice to be notified to the Authority under [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1991), shall be accompanied by a fee of £100.00.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of September, 1991.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the appropriate fee to be sent with any agreement, decision and concerted practice which is notified under the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
