S.I. No. 33 of 1993.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1993 DUBLIN.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) Scheme, 1993.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "the Telecommunications Schemes, 1992 and 1993" includes this Scheme.

- (2) This Scheme shall be deemed to have come into operation on 23 March 1992.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Minister for Communications, pursuant to [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) (2) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), has concurred in the increase of charges effected by this Scheme.

###### 5.

5. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1992, ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0019.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Part 2, Part 3 and Part 5 for Part 2, Part 3 and Part 5 of the First Schedule thereto:
