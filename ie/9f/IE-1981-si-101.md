# S.I. No. 101/1981 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 101 of 1981.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1981.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into effect on the 1st day of February, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall be—

( *a* ) as regards milk, nil per gallon,

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, nil per animal.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 18th day of March, 1981.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations reduce to nil the amounts payable on milk and bovine animals under the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) with effect from 1st February, 1981.
