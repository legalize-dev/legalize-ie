# S.I. No. 36/1994 - Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980, (Section 13 (4)) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 36 of 1994.

LANDLORD AND TENANT (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1980, (SECTION 13 (4)) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0002/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1989)) of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1980), hereby make the following Regulations with respect to which, pursuant to that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , (Section 13 (4)) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The period of 10 years from the passing of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1989), is hereby specified for the purposes of subsection (4) (inserted by section 1 of the said Act) of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1980).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of February, 1994.

**MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations extend the period of operation of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) (4) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .
