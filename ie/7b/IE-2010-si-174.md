# S.I. No. 174/2010 - Mussel Seed (Opening of Fisheries) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th May*, 2010.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec15) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Mussel Seed (Opening of Fisheries) Regulations 2010 and come into operation on 4 May 2010.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “mussel seed” means mussels (*mytilus edulis*) whether alive or dead and includes any part of a mussel and the shell, or any part of the shell, of a mussel and which are not intended for direct human consumption.

###### 3.

3. (1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person may fish for mussel seed in the exclusive fishery limits of the State where that person holds an authorisation to fish for mussel seed granted under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec13) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) or a licence granted by the relevant competent authority of Northern Ireland.

- (2) Paragraph (1) shall not apply to—

    - (*a*) the areas listed in the Schedule to the European Communities (Control on Mussel Seed Fishing) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0347.html) ) as amended by the European Communities (Control on Mussel Fishing) (Amendment) 2009 ( [S.I. No. 108 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0108.html) ) and the European Communities (Control on Mussel Fishing) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Revocation) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 379 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0379.html) ), or

    - (*b*) the Moville Area (within the meaning of the [Foyle Fisheries Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1952)) and the Louth Area (within the meaning of section 31 of the British-Irish Agreement Acts 1999) or any part thereof, if, and to the extent that mussel seed fishing is subject to Regulations under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0005/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Foyle Fisheries Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 April 2010.

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations are to provide for the opening of the mussel seed fishery from 4 May 2010.
