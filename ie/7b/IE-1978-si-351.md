# S.I. No. 351/1978 - Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 351 of 1978.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1978.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1978.

- (2) The Aliens Order 1946 and 1975 and this Order may be cited together as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1978, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 15th day of December, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The Fourth Schedule to the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975, is hereby amended by the insertion after "Wexford Garda Station" of "the Training Unit, Glengarriff Parade, Dublin.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 15th day of December, 1978.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order adds the Training Unit, Glengarriff Parade, Dublin, to the list of places in which an alien to whom leave to land has been refused may be detained.
