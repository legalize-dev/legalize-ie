# S.I. No. 733/2005 - Animal Remedies Act 1993 (Section 2) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Statutory Instruments

**[S.I. No. 733 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0733.html)**

**Animal Remedies Act 1993 (Section 2) Order 2005**

**S.I. No. of 2005**

**Animal Remedies Act 1993 (Section 2) Order 2005.**

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Animal Remedies Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2003) and the Agriculture, Food and Forestry (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0294.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Forestry (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby order as follows-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Animal Remedies Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 2) Order 2005 and comes into operation on 17 November 2005.

###### 2.

2. The [Animal Remedies Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , the Control of Animal Remedies and Their Residues Regulations 1998 and the Animal Remedies Regulations 2005 apply to an animal, being-

- (a) a domestic animal,

- (b) a wild animal in captivity, and

- (c) any other wild animal,

to which that Act does not, but for the making of this Order, apply by virtue of section 2(1) thereof.

**Explanatory Note.**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order clarifies the species of animal to which the [Animal Remedies Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) and Regulations made thereunder apply.
