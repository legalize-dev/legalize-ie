# S.I. No. 28/1985 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in Urban District of Kilrush) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 28 of 1985.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN URBAN DISTRICT OF KILRUSH) ORDER, 1985.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for Health by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947(S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), which said powers are delegated to me by the Health (Delegation of Functions) Order, 1984, I, JOHN F. DONNELLAN, Minister of State at the Department of Health, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in Urban District of Kilrush) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of April, 1985 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the Urban District of Kilrush unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

Dated this 11th day of February, 1985.

JOHN DONNELLAN,

Minister of State at the Department of

Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of April, 1985 the sale of milk in the Urban District of Kilrush unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
