ROAD TRAFFIC (NATIONAL CAR TEST) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2005.

I, IVOR CALLELY, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ( [No. 24 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 840 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0840.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 March 2005.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 9 of the Road Traffic (National Car Test Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 405 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0405.html) ) is amended—

- (*a*) by substituting for paragraph (1) the following:

  > - “(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the fee to be paid for a test certificate shall be—
  >
  >     - (*a*) €40.50, in the case of a test certificate issued following a test of a vehicle and
  >
  >     - (*b*) €22.73, in the case of a test certificate issued following a re-test of a vehicle.”,

  and

- (*b*) in paragraph (4), by substituting “€74.38” for “€73.97”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my hand, 10th February 2005. IVOR CALLELY, Minister of State at the Department of Transport. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations revise, with effect from 1 March 2005 the fee for a NCT test, a retest and a taxi suitability test to €40.50, €22.73 and €74.38 respectively. The fees specified are exclusive of Value Added Tax.
