# S.I. No. 134/2020 - Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Licensing) (Revocation) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th April,* 2020.

The Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec8) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Licensing) (Revocation) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. The Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Licensing) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0397.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport,

20 April, 2020.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

The purpose of these regulations is to revoke the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Licensing) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0397.html) ).
