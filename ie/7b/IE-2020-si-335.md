# S.I. No. 335/2020 - Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020 (Remote Meetings and Remote Hearings of State Body – Adoption Authority of Ireland) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th September,* 2020.

The Minister for Children and Youth Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec29) (3) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec31) (3) of the [Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2020), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020 (Remote Meetings and Remote Hearings of State Body – Adoption Authority of Ireland) Order 2020.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “the Adoption Authority of Ireland” means the body established by [section 94](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec94) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. The Adoption Authority of Ireland is a designated State body for the purposes of [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec29) of the [Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) .

###### 4.

4. The Adoption Authority of Ireland is a designated body for the purposes of [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec31) of the [Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs,

4 September, 2020.

**RODERIC O‘GORMAN,**

Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Instrument designates the Adoption Authority of Ireland as a state body for the purposes of holding remote meetings under [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec29) of the [Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) .

This Instrument also designates the Adoption Authority of Ireland as a body for the purposes of holding remote hearings under [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec31) of the [Civil Law and Criminal Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) .
