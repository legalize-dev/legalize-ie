# S.I. No. 219/1999 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Partick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The period of 24 months beginning on the 28th day of June, 1999, is hereby specified for the purpose of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of June, 1999. |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE, |
|  | Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order continues in operation [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) for a further period of 24 months.
