# S.I. No. 341/2013 - Ombudsman Act 1980 (Section 4(10)) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th September*, 2013.

I, BRENDAN HOWLIN, Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (10) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0026/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Ombudsman Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1980) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec6) of the [Ombudsman (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2012)), and having complied with paragraphs (*a*), (*c*) and (*d*) of that subsection, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Ombudsman Act 1980 (Section 4(10)) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Part II” means Part II of the First Schedule (inserted by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec16) of the [Ombudsman (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2012)) to the Principal Act;

“Principal Act” means the [Ombudsman Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1980).

###### 3.

3. This Order is without prejudice to the provisions of the Principal Act by virtue of which the Department of Justice and Equality is a reviewable agency in respect of any action taken by or on behalf of that Department of State that does not fall within paragraph (*f*)(ii) of Part II.

###### 4.

4. It is declared that the Department of Justice and Equality is a reviewable agency in respect of any action taken by or on behalf of it, being action that—

- (*a*) consists of the administration of prisons or other places for the custody of persons committed to custody by the Courts, and

- (*b*) relates to a person under the age of 18 years who is committed to custody in such a prison or other place mentioned in subparagraph (*a*),

and, accordingly, there are excluded from paragraph (*f*)(ii) of Part II the elements of that Department of State that fall within both subparagraphs (*a*) and (*b*).

The Minister for Justice and Equality consents to the making of the above Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 September 2013.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 September 2013.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.
