# S.I. No. 380/1995 - The Solicitors Acts, 1954 To 1994 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 380 of 1995.

THE SOLICITORS ACTS, 1954 TO 1994 (APPRENTICES' FEES) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Law Society of Ireland in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0005.html#sec5) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Solicitors Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) , and with the concurrence of the President of the High Court, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. On and after the date on which these Regulations shall come into operation, the fees specified in the schedule hereto shall be paid to the Law Society of Ireland in respect of the courses and examinations laid down under the Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0009.html) ), the Solicitors Acts, 1954 and 1960 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 359 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0359.html) ), the Solicitors Acts, 1954 and 1960 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 360 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0360.html) ), the Solicitors Acts, 1954 and 1960 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 320 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0320.html) ) and the Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 102 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0102.html) ).

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) shall apply for the purposes of the interpretation of these Regulations as it applies for the purposes of the interpretation of an Act of the Oireachtas except insofar as it may be inconsistent with the Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 or with these Regulations.

The Solicitors Acts, 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 250 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0250.html) ) shall be revoked as from the date of operation of these Regulations.

4. These Regulations may be cited as the Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations 1995 and they shall come into operation on the 1st day of January 1996.
