# S.I. No. 234/2026 - Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999 (Variation of Monetary Amounts) Order 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th June,* 2026.

I, JAMES BROWNE, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 19C(1) (as amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0036/print.html#sec34) of the [Electoral (Amendment) (Political Funding) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) (No. 36 of 2012)) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 1999) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 408 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0408.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999 (Variation of Monetary Amounts) Order 2026.

###### 2.

2. In the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 1999) the monetary amount specified in -

- (a) section 19A, in the definition of “third party”, is varied to €125,

- (b) section 19B, is varied to -

    - (i) in subparagraph (i) of paragraph (a) of subsection (1), an amount of €1,250,

    - (ii) in subparagraph (ii) of paragraph (a) of subsection (1), an amount of €250,

    - (iii) in subparagraph (I) of paragraph (b) of subsection (1), an amount of €1,250,

    - (iv) in subparagraph (II) of paragraph (b) of subsection (1), an amount of €3,100,

    - (v) in subparagraph (III) of paragraph (b) of subsection (1), an amount of €250, and

    - (vi) in subsection (3), an amount of -

        - (I) €1,250 for €1,000, and

        - (II) €3,100 for €2,500,

- (c) section 19BB, is varied to –

    - (i) in paragraph (a) of subsection (1), an amount of €250, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (b) of subsection (1), an amount of €250,

- (d) section 19D(1), is varied to an amount of €125,

- (e) section 19E(1), is varied to an amount of €750, and

- (f) section 19F(1), is varied to an amount of €125.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 May, 2026.

**JAMES BROWNE,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This order varies, in line with changes in the Consumer Price Index, the permissible limits and donation thresholds provided for in Part IVA of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) which apply at local elections and to local authority members.
