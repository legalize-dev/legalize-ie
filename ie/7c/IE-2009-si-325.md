# S.I. No. 325/2009 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary offices (Amendment) Act 1992 (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st August*, 2009.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(4) (as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1992), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992 (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992 (Allowances) (No. 2) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0397.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations (as amended by Regulation 2 of the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 100 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0100.html) )) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 3—

    - (i) in paragraph (*a*), by substituting “€5,000” for “ £4,000”, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (*b*), by substituting:

        - (I) in subparagraph (i), “€2,500” for “ £2,000”,

        - (II) in subparagraph (ii), “€5,000” for “ £4,000”, and

        - (III) in subparagraph (iii), “€8,000” for “ £6,400”,

- (*b*) in paragraph (*b*) of Regulation 4, by substituting—

    - (i) in subparagraph (i), “€13,000” for “ £10, 650”,

    - (ii) in subparagraph (ii), “€12,000” for “ £9,770”, and

    - (iii) in subparagraph (iii), “€11,000” for “ £8,800”,

- (*c*) in Regulation 5, by substituting “€3,750” for “ £3,000”, and

- (*d*) in Regulation 6, by substituting “€8,200” for “ £6,660”.

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by substituting for Regulation 6A (inserted by Regulation 4 of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 387 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0387.html) )) and Regulation 6B (inserted by the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992 (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 464 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0464.html) )) the following:

> “6A The rate of the allowance payable to the holder of the office of Attorney General while not a member of the Oireachtas is, with effect from 5 August 2009, the sum of €12,000 per year.
>
> 6B The rate of allowance payable under section 3 of the Act of 1992 to a member of Seanad Éireann who is also the holder of the office of Leader of the House of Seanad Éireann is, with effect from 5 August 2009, the sum of €8,200 per annum.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 August 2009

**BRIAN LENIHAN**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations reduce the amounts paid in relation to the constituency travel and miscellaneous expense allowances payable to Members of the Houses of the Oireachtas and Parliamentary Office holders.
