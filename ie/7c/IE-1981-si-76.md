# S.I. No. 76/1981 - Harbour Rates (Harbours in County Donegal) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 76 of 1981.

HARBOUR RATES (HARBOURS IN COUNTY DONEGAL) ORDER, 1981.

I, Albert Reynolds, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Harbours (Regulation of Rates) Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1934), the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0011.html) ), and after due compliance with section 5 of that Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Harbours in County Donegal) Order, 1981.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 14th day of March, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The rates set out in the First Schedule to this Order are hereby fixed as the maximum rates to be charged by the council of the county of Donegal in respect of the harbours, piers, quays and landing places specified in the Second Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. Every statutory or other provision fixing, restricting or otherwise regulating the rates or the maximum or minimum rates chargeable by the council of the county of Donegal on subjects of charge set out in the First Schedule to this Order, or the basis on which or the method by which such rates are computed, is hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order fixes the rates which the Donegal County Council may charge at certain harbours, piers and landing places in County Donegal.
