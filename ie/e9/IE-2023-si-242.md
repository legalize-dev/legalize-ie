# S.I. No. 242/2023 - Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th May,* 2023.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) (9), (10) and (11) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), and for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 10 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/392 of 4 March 2021[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 415 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0415.html) ) are amended -

- (a) in Regulation 2(1), by inserting the following definitions:

  > “‘Commission Implementing Regulation’ means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/392 of 4 March 2021[^1] ;
  >
  > ‘real-world data’ shall be construed in accordance with Article 2 (c) of the Commission Implementing Regulation;
  >
  > ‘VIN’ means the unique alphanumeric code assigned to a vehicle by the manufacturer in order to ensure proper identification of the vehicle;”,

- (b) by inserting after Regulation 7 the following:

  > “*Collecting of real-world data and VINS*
  >
  > 7A When carrying out a test on a vehicle to which Article 10 of the Commission Implementing Regulation applies, a tester shall collect real-world data and VINS in accordance with that Article.”, and

- (c) in Part 2 of Schedule 3 (inserted by the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 (S. I. No. 4 of 2023)), by inserting after the matter at “7.11 Odometer” the following:

  > “
  >
  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | **8.2.1.2 Gaseous emissions** | (a) Advisory Pass applies where an error code is detected that is not a reason for failure. |
  > | **8.2.2.2 Opacity** | (a) Advisory Pass applies where an error code is detected that is not a reason for failure. |
  >
  > ”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 May, 2023.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations -

- • provide for the collection of real-world data from new passenger cars at the National Car Test that are first registered from 1 January 2021 and that are equipped with on-board fuel and/or energy consumption monitoring devices required by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/392, and

- • amend Part of 2 of Schedule 3 to S.I. 415 of 2017 to provide for the classification of certain emission diagnostic trouble codes as ‘pass advisory’.
