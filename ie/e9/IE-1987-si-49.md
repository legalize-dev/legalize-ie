# S.I. No. 49/1987 - Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 49 of 1987.

MEDICAL PREPARATIONS (AMENDMENT OF FEES) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) as amended by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0039.html#sec39) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. (1) The Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 225 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0225.html) ) as amended by the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0049.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution in article 8 of "£880" for "£650".

- (2) The Medical Preparations (Wholesale Licences) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 333 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0333.html) ) as amended by the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 159 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0159.html) ) and by the Medical Preparations (amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1986 are hereby amended by the substitution in article 8 (2) of "£400" for "£300" and of "£135" for "£100".

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of March, 1987.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 26th day of February, 1987.

**JOHN BOLAND,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 26th day

of February, 1987.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the increases in the fees charged for licences granted in respect of the manufacture and sale by wholesale of medical preparations.
