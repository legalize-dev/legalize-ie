# S.I. No. 453/2000 - Roads Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0007.html#sec7) , [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0047.html#sec47) , [48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0048.html#sec48) and [51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Roads Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Roads Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations and the Roads Regulations, 1994 shall be construed as one and may be collectively cited as the Roads Regulations, 1994 to 2000.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations, “the Act” means the [Roads Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993).

- (2) Except where otherwise specified any word which has a meaning assigned to it by the Act shall have the same meaning for the purposes of these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2001.

###### 4.

4. (1) The prescribed forms for the purposes of sections 47, 48 and 51 of the Act are set out in the Schedule as follows:

- (*a*) Form No. 1 shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of section 47 of the Act.

- (*b*) Form No. 2A shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of section 48 of the Act.

- (*c*) Form No. 2B shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of section 51 of the Act.

- (*d*) Form Nos. 3 and 4 shall be the prescribed forms for the purposes of section 48 of the Act.

- (*e*) Form No. 5 shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of section 51 of the Act.

- (2) The area of a prescribed notice required to be published in a newspaper for the purposes of section 48(a) and subsection(3)(a) of section 51 of the Act shall exceed 800 square centimetres.

3. Part 9 and Schedule D of the Roads Regulations, 1994 (S.I. 119 of 1994) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation of it.)*

These Regulations prescribe the forms to be used by local authorities in connection with the preparation and submission to An Bord Pleanála of schemes for the provision of motorways, busways and protected roads, and to notify the public and persons affected by such schemes and the forms to be used in relation to the environmental impact assessment of roads projects and revoke Part 9 and Schedule D of the Roads Regulations, 1993 in their entirely. The forms are prescribed to take account of the transfer of the Minister for the Environment and Local Government's functions under [sections 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0049.html#sec49) , [50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0050.html#sec50) and [51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Roads Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , to An Bord Pleanála in accordance with [section 215](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec215) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) .
