# EXCHANGE CONTROL (CONTINUANCE) ACT, 1970

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO CONTINUE IN OPERATION THE EXCHANGE CONTROL ACT, 1954. [26*th December*, 1970.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Continuance of Exchange Control Act, 1954.

**1.**—The following is hereby substituted for section 2 (2) of the Exchange Control Act, 1954, as amended by section 1 of the Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1966:

> - “(2) This Act shall continue in operation until the 31st day of December, 1974, and shall then expire”.

###### 2 Short title and collective citation.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1970.

- (2) The Exchange Control Acts, 1954 to 1966, and this Act may be cited together as the Exchange Control Acts, 1954 to 1970.
