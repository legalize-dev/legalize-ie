# S.I. No. 210/2021 - Planning and Development (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th May,* 2021.

WHEREAS I, DARRAGH O‘BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, am of the opinion that development to which the following regulations apply would not offend against principles of proper planning and sustainable development by reason of the nature and limited effect of development belonging to that class on its surroundings;

NOW I, DARRAGH O‘BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 254](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec254) and [section 262](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec262) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000), as amended, (as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 408 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0408.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

**Citation and construction**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Development (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall be included in the collective citation the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 to 2021.

**Appliances, apparatus and structures suitable for being licensed**

###### 2.

2. The Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ) are amended in article 201 by inserting after paragraph (b) the following paragraph:

> - “(ba) Awnings, coverings, canopies, parasols, shades, windbreakers, heaters or other similar structure for the purpose of facilitating outdoor dining where tables and chairs are being provided under paragraph (b),”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 April, 2021.

**DARRAGH O‘BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.
