# S.I. No. 349/2008 - Companies (forms) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12 *September*, 2008.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (3) and [226](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0226.html#sec226) of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990) as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister Order 1997 ( [S.I. No 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 16 September 2008.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “Act of 1990” means the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990).

###### 4.

4. The form (Form H5) set out in the Schedule is prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 226 of the Act of 1990.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 3 of the Companies (Forms) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 162 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0162.html) ) in so far as it relates to prescribing Form H5 as the form to be used for the purposes of the provisions of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) and the Schedule to the said Regulations in so far as it sets out the said Form H5 are revoked.

*Regulation 4.*

## SCHEDULE

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 August 2008

**MARY COUGHLAN.**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend and update Form H5 which is to be completed by a company that is purchasing its own shares and/or shares in a holding company.
