# S.I. No. 204/1972 - Carriage of Wheat Order, 1972

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 204 of 1972.

CARRIAGE OF WHEAT ORDER, 1972.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Transport and Power, in exercise of the power conferred on me by subsection (3) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0021/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Transport (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1955), as amended by subsection (1) of [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0024/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) (Amendment) Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1958), and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Carriage of Wheat Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. During the period commencing on the 22nd day of August, 1972, and ending on the 31st day of January, 1973, [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Road Transport Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1933) (which restricts the carriage of merchandise by unlicensed persons) shall not apply to the carriage of wheat by a person authorised under the Wheat Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 190 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0190.html) ), to act as the agent of the licensee of a mill licensed under [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0007/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1933), where the wheat was purchased by the agent for the licensee or for An Bord Grain and is carried in a vehicle owned by the agent.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of August, 1972.

**BRIAN LENIHAN**

Minister for Transport and Power.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Instrument is that persons authorised to act as agents of licensed mills during the period 22nd August, 1972 to 31st January, 1973 may use their own vehicles for the transport of wheat purchased by them for licensed mills during that period, although not licensed under the [Road Transport Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , for the purpose, and may also carry in their own vehicles wheat purchased for An Bórd Gráin.
