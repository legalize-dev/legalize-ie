# S.I. No. 456/2020 - Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998 (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th October,* 2020.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/sec0013.html#sec13) (6) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform and the Minister for Finance and after consultation with daa, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998 (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) Order 2020.

###### 2.

2. The amount specified in paragraph (a) of section 13(5) (inserted by paragraph 6 of the Schedule to the [State Airports Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2004)) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998) is varied by substituting “€2,200,000,000” for “€1,800,000,000”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Order provides for an increase, from €1.8bn to €2.2bn of the aggregate amount of moneys borrowed at any one time by the daa as specified in paragraph (a) of section 13(5) (inserted by paragraph 6 of the Schedule to the [State Airports Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2004)) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998).
