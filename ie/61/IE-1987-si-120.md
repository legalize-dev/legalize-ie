# S.I. No. 120/1987 - Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0025/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Garda Síochána Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1924), as amended by the Garda Síochána Act, 1977 (No. 24 of 1977), and [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), hereby, with the approval of the Government, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The First Schedule to the Garda Síochána (Associations) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0135.html) ), is hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution in Article 3 (3) for "January" of "October", and

- (*b*) the substitution in Article 7 (4) for "in the month of April" of "before the 31st day of May".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of April, 1987.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

The Government hereby approve the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 23rd day of April, 1987.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Garda Síochána (Associations) Regulations, 1978.
