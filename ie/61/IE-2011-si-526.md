# S.I. No. 526/2011 - Courts Service Act 1998 (Section 29(2)) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th October*, 2011.

I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/sec0029.html#sec29) (2) of the [Courts Service Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1998) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Courts Service Act 1998 (Section 29(2)) Order 2011.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 1 November 2011.

###### 2.

2. [Schedule 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) to the [Courts Service Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1998) is amended by the insertion in columns 1, 2 and 3, respectively, of that Schedule after the seventh entry therein of:

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “ | No. 27 of 1965 | [Succession Act 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) | Section 131 | ”. |

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 October 2011.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This does not form part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order amends Schedule 2 to the [Courts Service Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1998) in order to transfer the responsibility for the appointment of temporary District Probate Registrars, from the Minister for Justice and Equality to the Courts Service.
