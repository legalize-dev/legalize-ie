# S.I. No. 139/2014 - Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st March*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 92 (as amended by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/sec0017.html#sec17) of [Finance (No. 2) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1992)) of the [Finance Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1989), after consultation with the Minister for Health (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )) and the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government (as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) )) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2015.

###### 3.

3. The Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 353 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0353.html) ) are amended by revoking Regulation 16.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 March 2014.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order ends the excise relief on fuel, provided as part of the Disabled Drivers and Passengers Scheme, from 1 January 2015 by revoking Regulation 16 of the Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) Regulations 1994 from that date.
