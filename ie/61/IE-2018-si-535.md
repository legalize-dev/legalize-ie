# S.I. No. 535/2018 - Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th December*, 2018.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (4) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) and Article 4 of the Córas Iompair Éireann (Additional Powers) Order 1988 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0381.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “amending Scheme” means the Scheme amending the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Confirmation) Order 1951 ( [S.I. No. 353 of 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/si/0353.html) )), prepared by Córas Iompair Éireann and submitted to the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport under section 44(5) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950) and set out in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The amending Scheme is confirmed and is deemed to have come into operation on 17 January 2018.

## SCHEDULE Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Amendment) Scheme 2018

Rule 21 (inserted by [S.I. No. 421 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0421.html) ) of the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Confirmation) Order 1951 in paragraph (*a*) of Clause 11 (re-numbered by [S.I. No. 390 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0390.html) ) is amended by substituting “17 January 2022” for “17 January 2018” (inserted by [S.I. No. 475 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0475.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 December 2018.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this Order is to extend voluntary severance terms applicable to members of the CIÉ Superannuation Scheme 1951 to 17 January 2022.
