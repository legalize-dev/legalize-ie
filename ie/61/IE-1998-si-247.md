S.I. No. 247 of 1998.

ROAD TRAFFIC (IMMOBILISATION OF VEHICLES) REGULATIONS, 1998

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and by section 101B of that Act (inserted by the [Dublin Transport Authority (Dissolution) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1987) (as amended by [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0049.html#sec49) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994))), which powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 427 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0427.html) ), I, Robert Molloy, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, having consulted the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations :

"the Principal Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"traffic warden" means -

- (a) a person referred to in sub-section (1) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1975), or

- (b) an authorised person under section 103 of the Principal Act, as amended by [section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0064.html#sec64) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968).

###### 3.

3. The following are hereby prescribed as authorised persons for the purposes of section 101B of the Principal Act: -

- (a) a member of the Garda Síochána;

- (b) a person appointed in writing by a local authority for the said purposes;

- (c) a traffic warden.

###### 4.

4. The prescribed form of the notice to be affixed to a vehicle to which an immobilisation device has been fitted shall be as set out in the Schedule to these Regulations, or shall be substantially to the like effect.

###### 5.

5. (1) The prescribed charge for the purpose of section 101B(5) of the Principal Act shall be sixty five pounds.

- (2) The charge referred to in sub-article (1) of this article shall be paid as may be indicated in the notice affixed to the vehicle in accordance with section 101B(3) of the Principal Act, to the Member-in-Charge of the appropriate Garda Station or to the appropriate road authority as the case may be.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations give effect to section 101B of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (as inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Dublin Transport Authority (Dissolution) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) ) which provides for the immobilisation of vehicles which are found to be parked on a public road in contravention of a prohibition or restriction under section 35 or [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0036.html#sec36) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
