# S.I. No. 233/1993 - Social Welfare (Deserted Wife's Benefit) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 233 of 1993.

SOCIAL WELFARE (DESERTED WIFE'S BENEFIT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for Social Welfare in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , (No. 1 of 1981 ) and by section 101 of that Act (as amended by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1992)), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Short title and collective citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Deserted Wife's Benefit) Regulations, 1993.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Deserted Wife's Benefit) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 237 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0237.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Social Welfare (Deserted Wife's Benefit) Regulations, 1992 and 1993.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 29th day of July, 1993.

3 Increased rates.

###### 3.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Social Welfare Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) provided for increases in the maximum social insurance payments arising from the 1993 Budget, with effect from the end of July, 1993.

These Regulations provide for increases in the reduced rates of Deserted Wife's Benefit from 29th July, 1993.
