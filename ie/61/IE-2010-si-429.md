# S.I. No. 429/2010 - Companies (Forms) Order 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order prescribes a Companies Registration Office form that is to used by applicants under section 256G of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) . This section was inserted into the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) Act by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (*j*) of the [Companies (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) .

Applicants under this section would be seeking to migrate the registered office of a certain type of collective investment fund company from Ireland to a prescribed jurisdiction without firstly having to wind up the Irish investment company.

The form being prescribed by this Order is to be used to notify the Companies Registration Office of the company’s registration in the other jurisdiction.
