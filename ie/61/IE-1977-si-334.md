# S.I. No. 334/1977 - Employment Equality Act, 1977 (Section 28) Regulations, 1977

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 334 of 1977.

EMPLOYMENT EQUALITY ACT, 1977 (SECTION 28) REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Employment Equality Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1977), hereby make the following Regulations :

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Employment Equality Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Section 28) Regulations, 1977, and shall come into operation on the 2nd day of November, 1977.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Employment Equality Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1977).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations is hereby prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 28 of the Act by a person (hereinafter called "the employee") proposing to refer under section 19 of the Act a dispute to the Labour Court and proposing to ascertain from the other person concerned the reason for the act believed by the employee to have constituted discrimination under the Act.

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations is hereby prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 28 of the Act by a person when stating the reason for an act believed by another person to have constituted discrimination under the Act.

###### 5.

5. A person stating reasons under section 28(1) of the Act shall do so not later than 21 days from the date of service of the request for the reasons.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the forms to be used for the purposes of [Section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Employment Equality Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) by a person who proposes to refer a dispute under Section 19 of the Act to the Labour Court and who wishes to ascertain the reason for an act believed to be discriminatory.

Article 5 of the Regulations prescribes the time limit for the completion and return of the forms.
