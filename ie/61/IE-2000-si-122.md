# S.I. No. 122/2000 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 5) (Increase in Rates) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

No. 25 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0025.html) ) and amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec24) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 43 (as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec24) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 84 (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec21) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 89 (as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) ), 102 (as amended by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1996)), 111 and 245A (as amended by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec20) of the [Social Welfare Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) ) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

**Citation and construction.**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 5) (Increase in Rates) Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations, 1994 to 2000 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations, 1994 to 2000.

**Commencement.**

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation -

- (a) in so far as it relates to unemployment assistance and farm assist on 26 April, 2000,

- (b) in so far as it relates to unemployment benefit, on 27 April, 2000,

- (c) in so far as it relates to disability benefit, maternity benefit, adoptive benefit, health and safety benefit and injury benefit on 1 May, 2000,

- (d) in so far as it relates to disability allowance, on 3 May, 2000,

- (e) in so far as it relates to retirement pension, invalidity pension, pre-retirement allowance and a relevant payment by virtue of [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) (1) (a) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , on 4 May, 2000, and

- (f) in so far as it relates to old age (contributory) pension, widow's and widower's (contributory) pension and unemployability supplement on 5 May, 2000.

**Definitions.**

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations -

“the Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. no. 417 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0417.html) );

“the Regulations of 1999” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Increase in Rates) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0164.html) ).

“the Regulations of 2000” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (Increase for Qualified Adult) Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0081.html) ).

**Interpretation.**

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations, save where the context otherwise requires, a reference to a Schedule is to a Schedule to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Social Welfare Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) provided for increases in the maximum rates of social insurance payments arising from the 2000 Budget, with effect from May 2000.

These Regulations provide for increases in the reduced rates of Disability Benefit, Unemployment Benefit, Health and Safety Benefit, Old Age (Contributory) Pension (OACP), Retirement Pension, Widow's and Widower's (Contributory) Pension, Deserted Wife's Benefit, and also provides for increases in the rates of tapered increases in respect of Qualified Adults. All of these increases are effective from May 2000.

The Regulations also amend the yearly average bands for receipt of reduced rate OACP and Retirement Pension where the contribution conditions are partially satisfied. The highest payment of reduced rate OACP will now apply to claimants with a yearly average of 20 to 47 and is set at 98% of the full-rate pension.

The new bands for the yearly average are:

**20 to 47**

**15 to 19**

**10 to 14**

The reduced rate of Retirement Pension will now apply to claimants with a yearly average of **24 to 47.**

The Regulations also provide for increases in the minimum and maximum weekly rate of Maternity Benefit and Adoptive Benefit with effect from May 2000.
