# S.I. No. 266/1977 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 51) (Miscellaneous Textiles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 266 of 1977.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 51) (MISCELLANEOUS TEXTILES) (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ORDER, 1977.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0012/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Control of Imports Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1934) hereby make the following Order :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 51) (Miscellaneous Textiles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. In this Order "the Order of 1964" means the Control of Imports (Quota No. 51) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 297 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0297.html) ).

###### 4.

4. (1) The prohibition effected by the Order of 1964 in respect of the importation of sacks and bags of a kind used for the packing of goods, of wool, cotton and man-made fibres which are—

( *a* ) classified at tariff heading number 6203-ex-561, 6203-623, 6203-631, 6203-637 and 6203-ex-649 in the Schedule to the Imposition of Duties (No. 200) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0220.html) ), and

( *b* ) are shown to the satisfaction of the Revenue Commissioners to have been manufactured in Hong Kong, India, Japan, Macao, Malaysia, Pakistan or Yugoslavia,

shall cease to have effect.

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1977.

###### 5.

5. (1) The prohibition effected by the Order of 1964 in respect of the importation of goods which are—

( *a* ) specified at a tariff heading number in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order or at a tariff code number specified in column (2) of that Schedule,

( *b* ) of a description specified in column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention in column (1) or column (2), as the case may be, of a tariff heading number or a tariff code number and

( *c* ) shown to the satisfaction of the Revenue Commissioners to have been manufactured in Romania,

shall cease to have effect.

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 5th day of April, 1977 and shall cease to have effect on the 31st day of December, 1977.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to remove from the scope of the Control of Imports (Quota No. 51) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1964:

( *a* ) certain textile yarns, apparel and make-up textiles originating in Romania with effect from 5 April, 1977 and until 31 December, 1977,

( *b* ) sacks and bags of wool, cotton and man-made fibres of a kind used for the packing of goods originating in Hong Kong, India, Japan, Macao, Malaysia, Pakistan and Yugoslavia with effect from 1 April, 1977.
