# S.I. No. 319/2001 - Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 To 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers) (Amendment) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 and section 29A (inserted by the [Diseases of Animals (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2001)) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 to 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers) (Amendment) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The collective citation Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 to 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers) Order, 2001, shall include this Order.

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into force on the 12 day of July 2001.

###### 4.

4. The time period specified in Regulation 6(9) of the Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 to 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 79 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0079.html) ) is extended to 31 October 2001.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the time period specified in Regulation 6(9) of the Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 to 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 79 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0079.html) )
