# S.I. No. 272/1984 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 272 of 1984.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1984.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 5th day of November, 1984, and shall expire on the 31st day of December, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall be—

( *a* ) as regards milk, 0.6 new pence per gallon,

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, £3.80 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 400 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0400.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of November, 1984.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which come into effect from 5 November, 1984 provide for the payment of an increased rate of levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
