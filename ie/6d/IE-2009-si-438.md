# S.I. No. 438/2009 - Prison (Disciplinary Code For officers) (Amendment) Rules 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 438 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0438.html)

PRISON (DISCIPLINARY CODE FOR OFFICERS) (AMENDMENT) RULES 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th November*, 2009.

I, DERMOT AHERN, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 35 of the [Prisons Act, 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2007) and with the approval of Government, hereby make thefollowing Rules.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) (Amendment) Rules, 2009.

- (2) These Rules shall come into operation on the 1st day of November 2009.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires-

“Rules of 1996” means the Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) Rules, 1996, Statutory Instrument No. 289 of 1996.

###### 3.

3. Rule 4(1) of the Rules of 1996 is amended by the substitution of the following for the definition of “the Minister”:

> “ ‘the Minister’ means the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform or a person designated by him or her to perform the duties assigned to the Minister under these Rules”.

###### 4.

4. Rule 11 of the of the Rules of 1996 is amended by the substitution of the following for that Rule

> “A decision to dismiss an officer from the Prison Service shall be made in accordance with the provisions of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0046/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Civil Service Regulation Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0046/index.html) (No. 46 of 1956).”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 October 2009.

**DERMOT AHERN,**

Minister for Justice Equality and Law Reform.
