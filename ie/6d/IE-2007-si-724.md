# S.I. No. 724/2007 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Learner Drivers) (No. 2) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 724 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0724.html)

ROAD TRAFFIC (LICENSING OF LEARNER DRIVERS) (No. 2) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd November*, 2007.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0042.html#sec42) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Learner Drivers) (No. 2) Regulations 2007.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 30 October 2007 and cease to have effect on 30 June 2008.

###### 2.

2. The amendment effected by Regulation 2(*b*)(i) and (iii) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Learner Drivers) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 719 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0719.html) ) to Regulation 17(6) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) does not apply, until 30 June 2008, to a person who holds or held a provisional licence for a category of vehicle in force at any time within the 5 year period before 30 October 2007 in relation to that category and, accordingly, subparagraphs (*a*) and (*c*) of paragraph (6) of that Regulation as they stand before their amendment apply to such a holder.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 October 2007

**NOEL DEMPSEY.**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal Interpretation.)*

These regulations provide that the amendments effected by Regulation 2(*b*)(i) and (iii) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Learner Drivers) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 719 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0719.html) ) to Regulation 17(6) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) do not apply, until 30 June 2008, to a person who holds or held a provisional licence for a category of vehicle in force at any time within the 5 year period before 30 October 2007, where the person has a learner permit (provisional licence) in that category.
