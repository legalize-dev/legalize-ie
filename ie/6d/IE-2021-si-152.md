# S.I. No. 152/2021 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th April,* 2021.

I, FRANK FEIGHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) ), the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) ) and the Communications, Climate Action and Environment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 373 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0373.html) )) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 599 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0599.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine and the Minister for Environment, Climate and Communications, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2021.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998);

“Order of 2020” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 173 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0173.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Part II (as amended by Article 3 of the Order of 2020) of the First Schedule to the Act is amended—

- (a) by deleting the following statutory instruments:

  > “European Communities (Importation of Animals and Animal Products from Third Countries) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 255 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0255.html) )”;
  >
  > “European Communities (Veterinary Checks on Products Imported from Third Countries) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 292 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0292.html) )”;
  >
  > “European Communities (Veterinary Checks on Fish and Fishery Products Imported from Third Countries) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 548 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0548.html) )”; and
  >
  > “European Communities (Import of Personal Consignments of Products of Animal Origin) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 374 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0374.html) ).”, and

- (b) by inserting after “Radiological Protection Act 1991 (Ionising Radiation) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0030.html) ).” the following statutory instruments:

  > “Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ).
  >
  > European Union (Persistent Organic Pollutants) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0146.html) ).
  >
  > European Union (Imports of Animals and Animal Products from Third Countries) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 656 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0656.html) ).”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)*.*

This Order amends the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) .

This Order may be cited as the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2021.

For ease of reference, a list of all food legislation and official agencies, as contained in the First and Second Schedules to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , as well as a list of Regulations made under the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) and deemed to be food legislation for the purposes of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , and Regulations made under the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec25) of the [Irish Medicines Board (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , can be found on the website www.fsai.ie (https://www.fsai.ie/legislation/fsai_act_related.html).
