# S.I. No. 441/2016 - Greyhound Industry (Control Committee and Control Appeal Committee) (Amendment) Regulations, 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th August*, 2016.

Bord na gCon in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Greyhound Industry Act 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958), as amended by [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0024/print.html#sec9) of the [Horse and Greyhound Racing (Betting Charges and Levies) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 1999), and also by sub-section (3) of Section 5 of the said 1958 Act, hereby makes the following regulations, that is to say—

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Greyhound Industry (Control Committee and Control Appeal Committee) (Amendment) Regulations, 2016

###### 2.

2. The Greyhound Industry (Control Committee and Control Appeal Committee) Regulations, 2007 to 2015 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Greyhound Industry (Control Committee and Control Appeal Committee) Regulations, 2007 to 2016.

###### 3.

3. Article 6(1) of the Greyhound Industry (Control Committee and Control Appeal Committee) Regulations, 2007 is hereby amended by the addition of the following paragraph after paragraph (vi):

> - “(vii) To determine appeals against a refusal to grant or renew, or a revocation of, a Kennelhand Authorisation by the Board under Article 5 (13) of the racing regulations.”

###### 4.

4. These regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of September, 2016.

GIVEN under the Seal of Bord na gCon,

14 July 2016.

**PHILIP MEANEY,**

Chairman of the Board.

**GERALDINE LARKIN,**

Chief Executive of the Board.

An officer authorised by the Board to authenticate the Seal of the Board.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of these regulations is to amend the Greyhound Industry (Control Committee and Control Appeal Committee) Regulations, 2007 so as enable the Control Committee and the Control Appeal Committee determine appeals by an applicant for, or holder of, a Kennelhand Authorisation against a refusal by the Board to grant or renew, or a revocation by the Board of, such authorisation.
