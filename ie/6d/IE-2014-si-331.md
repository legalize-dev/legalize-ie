# S.I. No. 331/2014 - Garda Síochána (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st August*, 2014.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 121](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec121) and [122](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec122) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No.138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), after consultation with the Commissioner of the Garda Síochána and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Garda Síochána (Promotion) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 485 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0485.html) ).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations come into operation on the 23rd day of July 2014.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 12 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution of the following for paragraph (4):

> “(4)(*a*) Subject to subparagraphs (*b*) and (*c*), the third member of the board shall be appointed by the Commissioner and shall be—
>
> - (i) a chief superintendent, if the promotion is to the rank of sergeant,
>
> - (ii) an assistant commissioner, if the promotion is to the rank of inspector, and
>
> - (iii) the Commissioner or a deputy commissioner, if the promotion is to the rank of superintendent, chief superintendent or assistant commissioner.
>
> - (*b*) Where, in relation to the selection of members for promotion to the rank of superintendent, chief superintendent or assistant commissioner, a competition is commenced after the coming into operation of the Garda Síochána (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 331 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0331.html) ), subparagraph (*a*)(iii) shall not apply and, instead, for the purpose of any such competition, the third member of the board to be appointed by the Commissioner shall be—
>
>     - (i) the Commissioner or a deputy commissioner, if the promotion is to the rank of assistant commissioner, and
>
>     - (ii) the Commissioner, a deputy commissioner or an assistant commissioner, if the promotion is to the rank of superintendent or chief superintendent.
>
> - (*c*) Subparagraph (*b*) shall not apply to a competition for the selection of members for promotion to the rank of superintendent, chief superintendent or assistant commissioner that is commenced after 1 January 2015.
>
> - (*d*) For the purposes of this paragraph, a competition is deemed to commence on the latest date on which candidates may enter the competition.”.

The Government hereby approve the making of the foregoing Regulations.
