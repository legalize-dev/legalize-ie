S.I. No. 86 of 1979.

SOCIAL WELFARE (CONTRIBUTIONS) (TRANSITIONAL) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by virtue of [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1952 ) and by section 52 of that Act (as substituted by the [Social Welfare (No. 2) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1976)) and by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Social Welfare (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1978), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Contributions) (Transitional) Regulations, 1979.

2 Interpretation.

###### 2.

**2.** In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1952" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) ;

"the Acts" means the Social Welfare Acts, 1952 to 1978;

"the Fourth Schedule" means the Fourth Schedule to the Act of 1952;

"the Regulations of 1953" means the Social Welfare (Contributions) Regulations, 1953 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/si/0005.html) );

"the Regulations of 1954" means the Social Welfare (Treatment Benefit) Regulations, 1954 ( [S.I. No. 156 of 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/si/0156.html) );

"the Regulations of 1967" means the Social Welfare (Collection of Contributions) Regulations, 1967 ( [S.I. No. 90 of 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/si/0090.html) );

"the Regulations of 1970" means the Social Welfare (Contributions) (Amendment) Regulations, 1970 ( [S.I. No. 222 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0222.html) );

"local office" has the meaning assigned to it in article 2 of the Regulations of 1967;

"reckonable earnings" has the meaning assigned to it in section 2 (1) of the Act of 1952 (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Social Welfare (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ).

3 Contribution week immediately preceding the 6th day of April 1979.

###### 3.

**3.** For the purposes of the Acts the period of four days which begins on the 2nd day of April, 1979 and ends on the 5th day of April, 1979 shall be a contribution week.

4 Return of insurance cards and emergency cards.

###### 4.

**4.** (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of articles 7(6) and 10(2) (*c*) of the Regulations of 1967, an employer who, immediately before the 6th day of April, 1979, is responsible for the custody of an insurance card or an emergency card by virtue of the provisions of those Regulations shall, within six days after that day or such longer period as the Minister may in a particular case allow, return such card to a local office by hand or by registered post with a statement in duplicate setting out the name and the insurance number of the employed contributor to whom the card refers.

- (2) Notwithstanding the provisions of article 20(*e*) (ii) of the Regulations of 1967, a voluntary contributor shall, within six days after the 6th day of April, 1979 or such longer period as the Minister may in a particular case allow, surrender his insurance card to a local office or in such other manner as the Minister may direct.

5 Earnings received on or after the 6th day of April, 1979 in respect of employment before that day.

###### 5.

**5.** (1) Subject to sub-article (2) of this article, where a payment of reckonable earnings is made on or after the 6th day of April, 1979 in respect of any period of employment before that day an employment contribution in accordance with the provisions of the Acts effective before that day shall not be payable in respect of any part of the period of employment covered by that payment of reckonable earnings.

- (2) Sub-article (1) of this article shall not affect the liability for employment contributions under the provisions of the Acts effective before the 6th day of April, 1979 in respect of any period of employment where

( *a* ) the payment of reckonable earnings referred to in sub-article (1) of this article is additional to a payment of earnings made before that day, and

( *b* ) such contributions were required to be paid before that day in respect of that period of employment.

6 Contributions treated as paid under the Acts.

###### 6.

**6.** Where but for the provisions of sub-article (1) of article 5 of these regulations an employment contribution would have been payable, an employment contribution at the appropriate rate shall be treated as having been paid by or on behalf of the employed contributor for each contribution week within the period of employment for which the payment of reckonable earnings was made but not more than one such contribution shall be treated as having been paid in respect of any one contribution week.

7 Breach of regulations.

###### 7.

**7.** A person who contravenes or fails to comply with the provisions of article 4 of these regulations shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable at the discretion of the court on summary conviction of such offence to a fine not exceeding £500 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

8 Modification of contribution and benefit years for men for disability, unemployment and treatment benefits, maternity and death grants.

###### 8.

**8.** (1) In the application of the contribution condition contained in paragraph 1(*b*) of the Fourth Schedule to a claim for disability benefit or unemployment benefit by a male insured person, the last complete contribution year referred to in that condition shall be taken to be—

( *a* ) where the day for which benefit is claimed occurs during the period commencing on the 6th day of April, 1979 and ending on the 3rd day of June, 1979, the period commencing on the 3rd day of January, 1977 and ending on the 1st day of January, 1978,

( *b* ) where the day for which benefit is claimed occurs during the period commencing on the 4th day of June, 1979 and ending on the 1st day of June, 1980, the period commencing on the 2nd day of January, 1978 and ending on the 31st day of December, 1978, and

( *c* ) where the day for which benefit is claimed occurs during the period commencing on the 2nd day of June, 1980 and ending on the 4th day of January, 1981, the period commencing on the 2nd day of January, 1978 and ending on the 5th day of April, 1979.

- (2) In the application of the contribution condition contained in paragraph 3 (*a*) (ii) of the Fourth Schedule to a claim for maternity grant in respect of the insurance of a male insured person,

( *a* ) the last complete contribution year referred to in that condition shall be taken to be—

- (ii) where the relevant time occurs during the period commencing on the 4th day of June, 1979 and ending on the 1st day of June, 1980, the period commencing on the 2nd day of January, 1978 and ending on the 31st day of December, 1978, and

- (iii) where the relevant time occurs during the period commencing on the 2nd day of June, 1980 and ending on the 4th day of January, 1981, the period commencing on the 2nd day of January, 1978 and ending on the 5th day of April, 1979, and

( *b* ) a subsequent complete contribution year referred to in that condition shall be taken to be—

- (i) where the relevant time occurs during the period commencing on the 6th day of April, 1979 and ending on the 3rd day of June, 1979, the period commencing on the 2nd day of January, 1978 and ending on the 31st day of December, 1978,

- (ii) where the relevant time occurs during the period commencing on the 7th day of January, 1980 and ending on the 5th day of April, 1980, the period commencing on the 2nd day of January, 1978 and ending on the 5th day of April, 1979, and

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Social Welfare (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) provides for a system of fully pay-related social insurance and occupational injuries insurance contributions. This system will come into effect from 6th April, 1979. These Regulations contain provisions necessary to secure the continuity of the new contribution system with the existing flat-rate contribution arrangements.

The Regulations provide that the four-day period immediately preceding 6th April, 1979, shall be treated as a contribution week and that social insurance and emergency cards issued in relation to the flat-rate contribution system shall be returned to a local office within a specified period after that date. The Regulations also remove in certain circumstances the obligation to pay flat-rate contributions for periods before the 6th April, 1979 in respect of which reckonable earnings are paid, and the new contribution levied, on or after that date, and for the treatment of such periods as periods for which contributions have been paid.

The Regulations also provide for special contribution years to be used in the determination of the contribution conditions for benefit and other purposes during a transitional period.
