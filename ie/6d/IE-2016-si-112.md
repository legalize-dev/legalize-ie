# S.I. No. 112/2016 - Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st March*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Council Regulation (EU) 2016/72 of 22 January 2016[^1] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations—

“Quota Regulation” means Council Regulation (EU) 2016/72 of 22 January 2016.

- (2) A word or expression that is used in the Quota Regulation and is also used in these Regulations, has, unless the context otherwise requires, the same meaning in these Regulations as it has in the Quota Regulation.

###### 3.

3. The master of—

- (*a*) an Irish sea-fishing boat wherever it may be, or

- (*b*) a sea-fishing boat in the exclusive fishing limits of the State

shall comply with Articles 5(2), 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 16 (1), 24, 25, 29, 30, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37(2), 38, 40, 41, 42, 45, 46 and 47 of the Quota Regulation.

###### 4.

4. (1) The master of a sea-fishing boat in the exclusive fishing limits of the State, other than the master of an Irish sea-fishing boat, shall comply with Article 10(1), (2), (3) and (4) of the Quota Regulation in relation to sea bass.

- (2) Any person engaged in recreational fisheries that is fishing for sea bass shall comply with Article 10(5) and (6) of the Quota Regulation.

- (3) These Regulations are in addition to and not in substitution for the Bass (Conservation of Stocks) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 230 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0230.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) No. 2 Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 478 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0478.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 February 2016.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations have the purpose of giving effect to the provisions of Council Regulation

2016/72 of 22 January 2016 fixing for 2016 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks and groups of fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters, and amending Regulation (EU) 2015/104.
