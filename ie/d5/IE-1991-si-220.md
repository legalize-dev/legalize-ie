# S.I. No. 220/1991 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 220 of 1991.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) ORDER, 1991.

I, MARY O'ROURKE, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979) (as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) )), with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The institutions specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as being institutions to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), applies.

SCHEDULE

LSB College Ltd.

Accountancy and Business College (Ireland) Ltd.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of July, 1991.

**MARY O'ROURKE,**

Minister for Education.

The Minister for Finance hereby concurs in the making of the foregoing order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

2nd day of August, 1991.

**PHILIP FURLONG,**

Assistant Secretary, Department of

Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order includes the LSB College Ltd. and the Accountancy and Business College (Ireland) Ltd. as institutions designated under the National Council for Educational Awards.
