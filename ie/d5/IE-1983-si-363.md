# S.I. No. 363/1983 - Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978 (Part III) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 363 of 1983.

ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1978 (PART III) REGULATIONS, 1983.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1978) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Part III) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Part III) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0193.html) ) shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Part III) Regulations 1978 and 1983.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1984.

###### 4.

4. The [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Part III) Regulations 1978 are hereby amended by the substitution for the Second Schedule thereto of the following:—

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Part III) Regulations, 1978 so as to revise the certificates issued under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , in relation to the manner of authentication of the seal of the Bureau.
