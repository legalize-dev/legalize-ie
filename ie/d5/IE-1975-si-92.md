# S.I. No. 92/1975 - Stock Transfer Act, 1963 (Amendment of Forms) Regulations, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 92 of 1975.

STOCK TRANSFER ACT, 1963 (AMENDMENT OF FORMS) REGULATIONS, 1975.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1963), hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (Amendment of Forms) Regulations, 1975.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1975.

###### 3.

3. The First Schedule to the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1963), is hereby amended by the substitution in the stock transfer form specified therein for "Full name of Undertaking" and "Full description of Security" of "Name of Undertaking" and "Description of Security", respectively.

###### 4.

4. The Second Schedule to the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , is hereby amended by the substitution in the brokers transfer form specified therein for "Full name of Undertaking" and "Full description of Security" of "Name of Undertaking" and "Description of Security", respectively.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of April, 1975.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the stock transfer form and brokers transfer form set out in the First and Second Schedules to the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) in order to allow standard abbreviations of names of undertakings and of descriptions of securities to be used in the forms.
