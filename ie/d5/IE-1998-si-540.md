# S.I. No. 540/1998 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998 (Establishment Day) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 540 of 1998.

FOOD SAFETY AUTHORITY OF IRELAND ACT, 1998 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER, 1998

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), hereby orders as follows.

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Establishment Day) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January, 1999, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health and Children

this 23rd day of December, 1998.

Brian Cowen, T.D.

Minister for Health and Children

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to provide that the establishment day shall be the 1st day of January, 1999.
