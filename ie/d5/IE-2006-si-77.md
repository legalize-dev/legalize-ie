# S.I. No. 77/2006 - Education (Former Residents of Certain Institutions for Children) Finance Board (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No. 77 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0077.html)

ORDER

entitled

Education (Former Residents of Certain Institutions for Children) Finance Board (Establishment Day) Order 2006

I, Mary Hanafin T.D., Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec23) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) (No. 17 of 2005), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Education (Former Residents of Certain Institutions for Children) Finance Board (Establishment Day) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The 17^th day of February 2006 is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of Part 3 of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) (No. 17 of 2005).
