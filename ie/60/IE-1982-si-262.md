S.I. No. 262 of 1982.

HARBOUR RATES (GALWAY HARBOUR) ORDER, 1982.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0011.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Galway Harbour) Order, 1982.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 6th day of August, 1982.

###### 2.

2. (1) The rates set out in the Schedules to this Order are hereby fixed as the maximum rates to be charged by the Galway Harbour Commissioners in respect of Galway Harbour.

- (2) Where rates fixed under this Order are payable at a metric tonne charge, lesser weights shall be chargeable as follows:

( *a* ) any weight not exceeding 250 kilogrammes shall count as 250 kilogrammes,

( *b* ) any weight exceeding 250 kilogrammes but not exceeding 500 kilogrammes shall count as 500 kilogrammes,

( *c* ) any weight exceeding 500 kilogrammes but not exceeding 750 kilogrammes shall count as 750 kilogrammes,

( *d* ) any weight exceeding 750 kilogrammes but not exceeding 1 tonne shall count as 1 tonne.

###### 3.

3. The Harbour Rates (Galway Harbour) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0182.html) ), is hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to authorise Galway Harbour Commissioners to charge increased maximum goods, tonnage and other rates at Galway Harbour.
