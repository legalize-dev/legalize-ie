# S.I. No. 508/2008 - County of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown Local Electoral Areas Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th December*, 2008.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the County of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown Local Electoral Areas Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. (1) The County of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area described in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown County Council to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set out in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division shall be construed as referring to such electoral division as existing at the date of this Order.

###### 4.

4. (1) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along any road or drive shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road or drive.

- (2) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which any road or drive intersects or joins any other road or drive shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of one would be intersected or joined by an imaginary line drawn along the centre of the other.

- (3) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which a line intersects or joins a boundary shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the line would intersect or join such boundary.

- (4) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along a road which consists of a dual carriageway shall be construed as an imaginary line drawn along the central median of such dual carriageway.

###### 5.

5. The County of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown Local Electoral Areas Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 341 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0341.html) ) is hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of the County of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown into local electoral areas and to fix the number of members of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown County Council to be elected for each such area.
