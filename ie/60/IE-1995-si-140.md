# S.I. No. 140/1995 - Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 1995

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 140 of 1995.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and sub-section (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 1955.

###### 2.

2. These regulations shall come into operation on the 5th day of June, 1995.

###### 3.

3. The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 236 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0236.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations—

"outgoings" means the rent, ground rent, rates and purchase annuity payable in respect of premises and mortgage repayments relating to the purchase of premises;

"receiving in-patient services" means receiving in-patient services made available under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 ); and

"person" means a person to whom [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) applies by virtue of sub-section (1) of that section.

###### 5.

5. The payment to be made under sub-section (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) to or in respect of a person or his/her dependants shall be a weekly payment not exceeding:—

( *a* ) the sum specified in Part One of the Schedule hereto which is appropriate to the person, and

( *b* ) such of the increases specified in Part Two of the said Schedule are as appropriate.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to provide as from 5th June, 1995, for increases in the maximum rates of maintenance allowances payable to persons being treated for certain infectious diseases.

The infectious diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are acute anterior poliomyelitis, diptheria, dysentery, salmonellosis, tuberculosis, typhoid and paratyphoid fevers, typhus and viral haemorrhagic disease (including lassa fever and marburg disease).
