EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 13) (Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 332 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0332.html) ) specified the 8th of July 2021 as the date when applications for Covid-19 pandemic unemployment payment should cease.

The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 16) (Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment) Regulations 2021 (S.I. No. 546 of 201) provided for the phased ending of the payment of Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment, with 25 March 2022 specified as the final date after which no further payments of the Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment would be made, regardless of individual circumstances.

The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 18) (Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 699 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0699.html) ) provided for an exception to the two foregoing Regulations in circumstances where, directly as a result of the Government’s decision of 3rd December 2021 to restrict the operating hours of certain hospitality and entertainment venues, a person’s income from employment ceased, or in the case of the self-employed, ceased or was substantially reduced. A person so affected could apply for payment of Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment in respect of any period after 7th December 2021.

These Regulations amend the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to provide for the phased ending of Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payments for those who became entitled to the payment as a result of the Government’s decision of 3rd December 2021 to restrict the operating hours of certain hospitality and entertainment venues. The Regulations specify the 22nd of January as the date when applications for the payment cease. Payments to this group will cease on 25th March 2022 with those eligible for Jobseeker’s benefit transitioning to that payment, while 31st May 2022 is specified as the final date after which no further payments of the Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment will be made, regardless of individual circumstances.

The Regulations also provide for a one month’s deferral in the reduction of rates and transition of Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payments for those who are still in payment.
