# S.I. No. 151/2017 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th April*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec17) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 13 April 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic Act 2010 (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 540 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0540.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) by inserting after Regulation 5 the following:

  > “6.The form set out in Part 3 of Schedule 2 is the prescribed form of certificate for the purposes of section 17 of the Act of 2010 to certify the concentration of a drug or drugs.”

  and

- (*b*) in Schedule 2, after Part 2, by inserting the text set out in the Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The Regulations prescribe the certificate, both in English and Irish, to be issued by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety under [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec17) of the [Road Traffic Act, 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) in respect of a concentration of a drug or drugs in a specimen.
