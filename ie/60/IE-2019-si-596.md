# S.I. No. 596/2019 - Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd December,* 2019.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 33 of Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ) are amended by substituting in Regulation 2(1) the definition of “Council Regulation” for the following-

> “‘Council Regulation’ means Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009[^2] as amended by Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013[^3] , Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013[^4] , Council Regulation (EU) No 1385/2013 of 17 December 2013[^5] , Regulation (EU) No 508/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^6] , Regulation (EU) 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015[^7] , Regulation (EU) 2019/473 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019[^8] and Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 November, 2019.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide for requirements in relation to on-board processing, catch handling and discharge restrictions on pelagic vessels and restrictions on the use of automatic grading equipment as inserted by Article 33 of Council Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 into Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009.
