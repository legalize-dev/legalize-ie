# S.I. No. 186/1962 - Customs-Free Airport (Extension of Laws) Regulations, 1962

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 186 of 1962.

CUSTOMS-FREE AIRPORT (EXTENSION OF LAWS) REGULATIONS, 1962.

I, ERSKINE H. CHILDERS, Minister for Transport and Power, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0005/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Customs-free Airport Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1947), and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Justice, hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Customs-free Airport (Extension of Laws) Regulations, 1962.

###### 2.

2. [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Firearms Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1925), shall extend to the Customs-free airport.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of November, 1962.

**ERSKINE H. CHILDERS,**

Minister for Transport and Power.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of these regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 6th day of November, 1962.

**SÉAMAS Ó RIAIN,**

Minister for Finance.

The Minister for Justice hereby consents to the making of these regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Justice, this 6th day of November, 1962.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Justice.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order extends to the Customs-free Airport the provisions of [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Firearms Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (Number 17 of 1925) regarding restrictions on the import of firearms, prohibited weapons and ammunition.
