# S.I. No. 283/2001 - European Communities (Units of Measurement) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive 89/617/EEC of 27 November 1989, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Units of Measurement) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations-

“Principal Regulations” mean the European Communities (Units of Measurement) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 255 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0255.html) );

“Regulations of 1998” mean the European Communities (Units of Measurement) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 510 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0510.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 4(2) of the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution for paragraph (e) (inserted by the Regulations of 1998) of the following paragraphs:

> - “(e) for the specific field of application set out in Schedule 6 to these Regulations, the units of measurement listed therein may be used until 31 December 2002 and shall not be used thereafter, and
>
> - (f) for the specific field of application set out in Schedule 7 of these Regulations, the units of measurement listed therein may be used until 31 December 2005 and shall not be used thereafter.”.

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by the substitution for Schedule 6 (inserted by the Regulations of 1998) of the following:

> ### “Schedule 6
>
> Legal Units of Measurement referred to in Regulation 4(2)(e)
>
> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Field of Application | Units | Units | Units |
> |  | Name | Symbol | Approximate Value |
> | Road traffic signs for speed and speed measurement | mile | mile | 1 mile = 1609 m |
> | Road traffic signs for speed and speed measurement | yard | yd. | 1 yd. = 0.9144 m |
> | Road traffic signs for speed and speed measurement | foot | ft. | 1 ft. = 0.3048 m |
> | Road traffic signs for speed and speed measurement | inch | in. | 1 in. = 2.54 × 10^-2 m |

## Schedule 7 Legal Units of Measurement referred to in Regulation 4(2)(f)

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field of Application | Units | Units | Units |
|  | Name | Symbol | Approximate Value |
| Road traffic signs for distance | mile | mile | 1 mile = 1609 m |
|  | yard | yd. | 1 yd. = 0.9144 m |
|  | foot | ft. | 1 ft. = 0.3048 m |
|  | inch | in. | 1 in. = 2.54 × 10^-2 m |

Until the dates referred to in Regulations 4(2)(e) and (f) of these Regulations, the units listed in Schedules 6 and 7 may be combined with each other and with those in Schedule 1 of the Principal Regulations to form compound units.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Regulations provide for a further extension of the time period during which conversion of road distance and speed measurement signs to units of the metric system may be carried out.
