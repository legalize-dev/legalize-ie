# S.I. No. 325/1988 - Electricity (Supply) (Amendment) Act, 1988 (Section 9) (Commencement) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 325 of 1988.

ELECTRICITY (SUPPLY) (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1988 (SECTION 9) (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 1988.

I, MICHAEL SMITH, Minister for Energy, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Electricity (Supply) (Amendment) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1988), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electricity (Supply) (Amendment) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (Section 9) (Commencement) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January, 1989, is hereby appointed for the purposes of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Electricity (Supply) (Amendment) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1988).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of December, 1988.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for Energy.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order specifies the day appointed for the purposes of [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Electricity (Supply) (Amendment) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) .
