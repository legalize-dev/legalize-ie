# S.I. No. 440/2009 - Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000 (Notice of Seizure) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th November*, 2009.

I, CONOR LENIHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec7) , [133](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec133) (9), [257](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec257) (9) and [338](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec338) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2009 ( [S.I. No. 250 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0250.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the >Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000 (Notice of Seizure) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule is the form prescribed for the purposes of subsections (8) and (9) of [section 133](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec133) and subsections (8) and (9) of [section 257](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec257) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).

(No. 28 of 2000))

[*name and address of the person claiming to be the owner of the copyright in the work concerned or name and address of the person claiming to be the owner of the rights in the recording of a performance concerned*]* in accordance with section 133/section 257* of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) hereby give notice to the owner/occupier/person in charge [^star1]

[*name and address of the place from where the copies, recordings, articles or protection-defeating devices are seized*] of the seizure of the following copies/recordings/articles/protection-defeating devices [^star1]

[*Separate sheet/s may be attached*]

[*the District Court Area shall be that in which the copies, recordings, articles or protection-defeating devices are to be seized*]

###### 3.

3. The owner of the copies, articles or protection-defeating devices seized pursuant to [section 133](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec133) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) , has the right to apply to the District Court for their return pursuant to [section 133](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec133) (10) of that Act on the grounds that they are not (*a*) infringing copies of a work, (*b*) articles that have been or are to be used to make infringing copies, or (*c*) protection-defeating devices.

[*Delete paragraph 3 if seizure is pursuant to section 257*]

###### 4.

4. The owner of the recordings, articles or protection-defeating devices seized pursuant to [section 257](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec257) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) , has the right to apply to the District Court for their return pursuant to [section 257](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec257) (10) of that Act on the grounds that they are not (*a*) illicit recordings of a performance, (*b*) articles that have been or are to be used to make illicit recordings, or (*c*) protection-defeating devices.

[*Delete paragraph 4 if seizure is pursuant to section 133*]

Signature:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*[This Note is not a part of the Instrument, and does not purport to be a legal interpretation]*

These Regulations prescribe the form of the notice, to be given at the time of seizure, to the owner, occupier or person in charge of the place where infringing copies, illicit recordings, protection-defeating devices or articles associated with making infringing copies or illicit recordings, as the case may be, are seized under Section 133 or [Section 257](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec257) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) .
