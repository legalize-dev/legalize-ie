# S.I. No. 2/2001 - Data Protection (Registration) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE MEADE, Data Protection Commissioner, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/sec0016.html#sec16) (1) (e) of the [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1988), and with the consent of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Data Protection (Registration Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 10th day of January, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations-

“internet access provider” means a person whose business consists wholly or partly in the connection of persons to the internet, and who holds personal data relating to such persons;

“telecommunications service provider” means a person who holds a licence granted under [section 111](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0111.html#sec111) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), as amended by the European Communities (Telecommunications Licences Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 96 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0096.html) ), and who holds personal data relating to a person to whom a network, service or system is provided under such licence.

###### 3.

3. The following persons are hereby prescribed for the purposes of [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/sec0016.html#sec16) (1)(e of the [Data Protection Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1988), that is to say:

- (a) data controllers who are telecommunications service providers, and

- (b) data controllers who are internet access providers.

The Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform hereby consents to the making of these Regulations.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the registration, under [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Data Protection Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , of persons who provide telecommunications services and internet services, and who keep personal data relating to users of those services.
