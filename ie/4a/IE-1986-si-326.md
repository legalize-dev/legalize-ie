# S.I. No. 326/1986 - Cork District Milk Board (Election Day, 1986) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 326 of 1986.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1986) ORDER, 1986.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Election Day, 1986) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 28th day of November, 1986, is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Cork District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 29th day of September, 1986.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1986 on which ordinary members of the Cork District Milk Board shall be elected.
