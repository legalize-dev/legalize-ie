# S.I. No. 111/1988 - Payment of Wages (Mode of Payment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 111 of 1988.

PAYMENT OF WAGES (MODE OF PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0040/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Payment of Wages Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1979), after consultation with the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the payment of Wages (Mode of Payment) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1988.

###### 3.

3. The instrument or modes of payment set out in the Table to this Regulation are hereby specified for the time being for the purpose of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0040/sec0003.html#sec3) (3) of the [payment of Wages Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) :

TABLE.

Any Warrant or Paying Order or such other such document issued by An Post or drawn on An Post.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of May, 1988.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the introduction by An Post of additional modes of payment of wages to those specified in the [Payment of Wages Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1979).
