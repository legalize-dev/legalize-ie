# S.I. No. 55/1988 - Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 55 of 1988.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1988.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1988.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1987 and this Order may be cited together as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1988, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The Principal Order is hereby amended by the insertion of the following Article after Article 5 as inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975:

> "5A. An alien coming from a place outside the State other than Great Britain or Northern Ireland who is a citizen of Iran shall not enter a port in the State for the purposes of transit unless he is the holder of a valid Irish transit visa".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of March, 1988.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order requires a citizen of Iran to have a valid Irish transit visa for the purposes of transitting a port in the State with effect form 1st April, 1988.
