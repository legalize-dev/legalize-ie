# S.I. No. 156/1998 - European Communities (Telecommunications Infrastructure) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 156 of 1998.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (TELECOMMUNICATIONS INFRASTRUCTURE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, MARY O'ROURKE, Minister for Public Enterprise, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 96/19/EC of 13 March, 1996^1 (amending Commission Directive 90/388/EEC of 28 June, 1990^2 with regard to the implementation of full competition in telecommunications markets) hereby make the following Regulations:

^1 O.J. No. L.74 of 22 March, 1996.

^2 O.J. No. L.192 of 24 July, 1990.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Telecommunications Infrastructure) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Telecommunications Infrastructure) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 338 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0338.html) ), are hereby amended by the insertion after Regulation 9 of those Regulations of the following Regulations:

"Repeal

10. Section 9 of the Telegraph Act, 1868 (31 & 32 Vic. c.110) is hereby amended by the repeal of subsections (8) and (9) of that section.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of May, 1998.

**MARY O'ROURKE,**

Minister for Public Enterprise.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to amend the European Communities (Telecommunications Infrastructure) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 338 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0338.html) ), by the repeal of subsection 8 and 9 of section 9 of the Telegraph Act, 1868, to give further effect to Council Directive 96/19/EC of 13 March, 1996 (amending Commission Directive 90/388/EEC of 28 June, 1990 with regard to the implementation of full competition in telecommunications markets).
