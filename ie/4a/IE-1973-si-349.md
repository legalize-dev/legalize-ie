# S.I. No. 349/1973 - Health Act, 1970 (Adaptation) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 349 of 1973.

HEALTH ACT, 1970 (ADAPTATION) REGULATIONS, 1973.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0085.html#sec85) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 ) hereby makes the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act, 1970 (Adaptation) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The references in sub-paragraphs (vii) and (viii) of paragraph (*a*) of subsection (2) of [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0027/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Nurses Act, 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1950) to councils of counties and to councils of county boroughs respectively shall be construed as references to health boards established under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 21st day of December, 1973.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations adapt the references to representatives of county councils and County Borough Councils to mean representatives of health boards, for the purpose of making appointments to An Bord Altranais.
