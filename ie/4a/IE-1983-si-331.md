# S.I. No. 331/1983 - Road Transport Act, 1978 (Section 5) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 331 of 1983.

ROAD TRANSPORT ACT, 1978 (SECTION 5) ORDER, 1983.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Transport Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1978), and the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0011.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Road Transport Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (Section 5) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The following class of vehicle, namely, vehicles being operated by a carrier established in the Kingdom of the Netherlands and engaged in transport operations referred to in Article 3 of the agreement made on the 30th day of May, 1983, between the Government and the Government of the Kingdom of the Netherlands on the international carriage of goods by road (being an international agreement relating to international transport by road), is hereby exempted from the provisions of [sections 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/sec0009.html#sec9) and [34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Road Transport Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (as amended and extended) and [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0023/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Road Transport Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (as amended and extended).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of November, 1983.

**JIM MITCHELL,**

Minister for Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is that a merchandise licence, a vehicle plate, a licence under [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0023/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Road Transport Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , or a restricted road freight licence under the Road Transport Acts are not required in respect of vehicles operated by carriers established in the Netherlands and engaged in transport operations referred to in Article 3 of the Road Transport Agreement between the Government and the Government of the Kingdom of the Netherlands dated 30 May, 1983.
