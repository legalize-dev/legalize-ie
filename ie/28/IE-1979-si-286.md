# S.I. No. 286/1979 - Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979 (Section 4) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 286 of 1979.

MILK (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1979 (SECTION 4) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) and [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 4) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979).

###### 3.

3. The 28th day of each month shall be the day by which the return and the fee referred to in Section 4 (1) (*c*) of the Act shall be forwarded to the Minister.

###### 4.

4. The form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations shall be the form in which the return referred to in Section 4 (1) (*c*) of the Act shall be forwarded to the Minister.

###### 5.

5. The fee referred to Section4 (1) (*c*) of the Act shall be forwarded to the Minister in the form of a cheque, money order or banker's draft.
