# S.I. No. 464/2000 - Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (Amendment) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, FRANK FAHEY, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 24 (as amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec3) (a) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2000)) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999) and section 90(3)(a)(v) (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (Amendment) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Act of 2000” means [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2000);

“Principal Regulations” means Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0256.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended by the substitution for Regulation 2 of the following Regulation:

> “2. These Regulations, other than Regulation 8, shall come into operation on 20 December 2000 and Regulation 8 shall come into operation on 1 January 2001.”

###### 4.

4. It is hereby declared that section 90(3) (inserted by [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980), and as amended by section 4 of the Act of 2000) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), applies to the offences under section 24(6) (inserted by section 3(a)(i) of the Act of 2000) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999), in respect of a failure to comply with Regulation 4 or a contravention of Regulation 7 of the Principal Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations provide for the commencement on 20 December 2000 of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations ( [S.I. No. 256 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0256.html) ) other than regulation 8 of those regulations. These regulations also provide for the application of the consequential disqualification order provisions of section 90(3) (inserted by [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) to certain contraventions of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2000.
