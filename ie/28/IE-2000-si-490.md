# S.I. No. 490/2000 - Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 12 (as amended by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1998) (“the Act of 1998”)), 25 (as amended by section 13 of the Act of 1998) and 26 of the [Plant Varieties (Propriertary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980) (“the Act of 1980”) and section 15 of the Act of 1998 (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Finance and, after consulation with the Controller of Plant Breeders' Rights and representatives of such interest as appear to me to be concerned, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The maximum period to be specified in a grant of plant breeders' rights as being that in which the plant breeders' rights are to be exercisable, within the meaning of [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0015.html#sec15) (5) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1998), in respect of a plant variety which is of a species specified in column (1) of the Schedule to these Regulations shall be the period of years specified in column (2) of that Schedule opposite the mention of that species.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations which implement certain amendments and extensions to the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) prescribe:

- (1) The crop species to which the grant of rights applies.

- (2) Duration of rights.

- (3) Fees to be charged.

- (4) Allow the holder of an Irish grant of plant breeders' rights to keep in place his national plant breeders' rights (but without exercising such rights) and thereby retain the option of reverting to the exercise of national plant breeders' rights if the Community plant breeders' rights are surrendered, by payment of a special reduced renewal fee.

- (5) Repeal of previous regulations.
