# S.I. No. 78/2011 - National Treasury Management Agency (State Authority) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th February*, 2011.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), and with the consent of the Minister for Health and Children, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authority) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. The Office of the Ombudsman for Children is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000).

The Minister for Health and Children consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 February 2011.

**MARY COUGHLAN,**

Minister for Health and Children.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 February 2011.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.
