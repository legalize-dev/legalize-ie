# S.I. No. 328/1984 - Casual Trading Act, 1980 (Licences Fees) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 328 of 1984.

CASUAL TRADING ACT, 1980 (LICENCES FEES) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Industry, Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0043/sec0004.html#sec4) (10) of the [Casual Trading Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1980), and the Trade, Commerce and Tourism (Alternation of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 384 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0384.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Casual Trading Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (Licences Fees) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 7th day of January, 1985.

###### 3.

3. [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0043/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Casual Trading Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1980), is hereby amended—

( *a* ) by the substitution of "£175" for "£100" in subsection (1), and

( *b* ) by the substitution of "£20" for "£5" in subsection (8).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of December, 1984.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Industry, Trade,

Commerce and Tourism.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to increase the fees for Casual Trading licences.
