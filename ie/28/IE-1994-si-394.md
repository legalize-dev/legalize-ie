# S.I. No. 394/1994 - An Bord Bia Act, 1994 (Establishment Day) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 394 of 1994.

AN BORD BIA ACT, 1994 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER, 1994.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/sec0005.html#sec5) of [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1994) hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Establishment Day) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The first day of December, 1994, is hereby appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1994).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of November, 1994.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and

Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes 1st December 1994 as the establishment day for the purposes of [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) . These purposes include the establishment of An Bord Bia and the dissolution of Coras Beostoic agus Feola.
