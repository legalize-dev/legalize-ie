# FISHERY HARBOUR CENTRES ACT, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO AMEND AND EXTEND THE FISHERY HARBOUR CENTRES ACT, 1968. [2*nd July*, 1980]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Definition.

**1.**—In this Act “the Principal Act” means the Fishery Harbour Centres Act, 1968.

###### 2 Amendment of section 4 of Principal Act.

**2.**—Section 4 of the Principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution in subsection (6) (*a*) of “£500” for “£100”.

###### 3 Amendment of Schedule to Principal Act.

**3.**—The Schedule to the Principal Act is hereby amended by the substitution of “Rossaveel” for “Galway”.

###### 4 Short title and collective citation.

**4.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Fishery Harbour Centres Act, 1980.

- (2) The Principal Act and this Act may be cited together as the Fishery Harbour Centres Acts, 1968 and 1980.
