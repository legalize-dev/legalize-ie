# S.I. No. 349/2019 - Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Representatives of Financial Services Industry) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th July,* 2019.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5(3) and 18(3) (as amended by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec35) (1) of the [Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2003)) of the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Representatives of Financial Services Industry) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The following bodies which appear to the Minister to represent the financial services industry are prescribed for the purposes of section 18(3) of the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998):

- (*a*) the Association of Insurance & Financial Brokers;

- (*b*) the Irish Stock Exchange plc;

- (*c*) the Banking and Payments Federation Ireland;

- (*d*) the Irish Association of Investment Managers;

- (*e*) Chartered Accountants Ireland Limited.

###### 3.

3. The Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Representatives of Financial Services Industry) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 394 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0394.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal of the Minister for Finance

12 July, 2019.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister For Finance.
