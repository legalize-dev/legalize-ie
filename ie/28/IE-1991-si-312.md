S.I. No. 312 of 1991.

SOCIAL WELFARE ACT, 1991 (SECTION 26) ORDER, 1991.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0007/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1991), hereby orders as follows:—

PART I. GENERAL.

1 Short title.

###### 1.

**1.** This Order may be cited as the [Social Welfare Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Section 26) Order, 1991.

2 Interpretation.

###### 2.

**2.** In this Order—

"the Act of 1981" means the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981);

"the Act of 1991" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .

3 Commencement.

###### 3.

**3.** This Order shall come into operation—

( *a* ) on the 28th day of November, 1991 as respects retirement pension, and

( *b* ) on the 29th day of November, 1991 as respects old age (contributory) pension.

PART II. MODIFICATION OF PART IV OF THE ACT OF 1991.

4 Modification of Part IV of the Act of 1991.

###### 4.

**4.** The provisions of the enactments mentioned in column (1) of the Schedule hereto are hereby modified so that the said provisions shall read as set out in column (2) of the said Schedule.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

Part IV of the [Social Welfare Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) provides for an entitlement to old age (contributory) pension/retirement pension to persons who have gaps in their insurance records as a result of having been insured for periods in public service type employment.

This Order provides for a number of minor modifications to Part IV of the Act.
