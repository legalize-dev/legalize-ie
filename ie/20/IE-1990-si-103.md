# S.I. No. 103/1990 - Safety, Health and Welfare At Work Act, 1989 (Repeals) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 103 of 1990.

SAFETY, HEALTH AND WELFARE AT WORK ACT, 1989 (REPEALS) ORDER, 1990

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) (2) and [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1989), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Repeals) Order, 1990.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1990.

###### 2.

2. [Section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0010/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Dangerous Substances Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1972), is hereby repealed.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 24th day of April, 1990.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order repeals [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0010/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Dangerous Substances Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) from the 1st day of May, 1990. That provision is replaced by [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/sec0056.html#sec56) of the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
