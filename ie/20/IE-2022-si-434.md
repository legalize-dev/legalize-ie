# S.I. No. 434/2022 - Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences Held by Relevant Persons) (Ukraine) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th September,* 2022.

I, Eamon Ryan, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23B of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), (inserted by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0019/print.html#sec46) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No.19 of 2022)) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) and the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alternation of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences Held by Relevant Persons) (Ukraine) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. In this Order ‘Regulations of 2006’ means the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that a licence or permit permitting a relevant person to drive a mechanically propelled vehicle, not being a licence or permit issued to a relevant person to enable that person to learn or provisionally to drive such a vehicle, issued by the competent authority of Ukraine in a category which corresponds to a category specified in the Table to Regulation 6 of the 2006 Regulations is recognised for the purpose of use in the State in respect of the category.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 September, 2022.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Order provides for the recognition of Ukrainian driving licences held by persons with a permission to reside under [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0066/print.html#sec60) (6) of the [International Protection Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0066/print.html) for use in Ireland while that permission is valid.
