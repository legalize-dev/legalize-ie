# S.I. No. 570/2009 - National Treasury Management Agency (State Authority) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 570 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0570.html)

NATIONAL TREASURY MANAGEMENT AGENCY (STATE AUTHORITY) ORDER 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January*, 2010.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in section 7(1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), and with the consent of the Minister for Health and Children, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authority) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. Subject to Article 3, the Health Service Executive is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in section 7(1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000).

###### 3.

3. This Order is without prejudice to the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 46 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0046.html) ).

The Minister for Health and Children consents to the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 December 2009

**MARY HARNEY,**

Minister for Health and Children.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 December 2009

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.
