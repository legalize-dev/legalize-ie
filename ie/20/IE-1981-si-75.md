# S.I. No. 75/1981 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in Urban District of Ballinasloe) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 75 of 1981.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN URBAN DISTRICT OF BALLINASLOE) ORDER, 1981.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in Urban District of Ballinasloe) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of May, 1981 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the Urban District of Ballinasloe unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 5th day of March, 1981.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of May, 1981 the sale of milk in the Urban District of Ballinasloe unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
