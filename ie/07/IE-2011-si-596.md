EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Air Pollution from Ships) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 313 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0313.html) ) on the prevention of air pollution from ships.

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for an amendment to paragraph 2.3 of the form of Supplement to the International Air Pollution Prevention Certificate.
