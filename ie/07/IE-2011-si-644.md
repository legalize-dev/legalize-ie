# S.I. No. 644/2011 - Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th December*, 2011.

I, BRENDAN HOWLIN T.D., Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Ethics in Public Office Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1995) and after consultation with such other Ministers of the Government as I consider appropriate, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into effect on 1 January 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 698 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0698.html) ), as amended by the Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies)(Amendment)(No. 2) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 596 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0596.html) ), are amended by substituting for the Schedule the following:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations are made under the [Ethics in Public Office Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1995) to apply the provisions of the Act to persons occupying specified positions of employment in the Civil Service. The Regulations prescribe certain positions in the Civil Service as “designated positions of employment” as defined in Section 18 of the Act. Persons occupying positions prescribed by these regulations are required, where an interest (as defined in the Second Schedule to the Act) exists, which could materially influence the person in his or her official duties, to furnish a statement of those interests to the relevant authority for the position (as defined in Section 18 of the Act). If no such interest or interests exists a “nil” statement is requested.

The Secretary General of a Department (Head of Office for a Civil Service Office) is the relevant authority to whom statements of interests must be furnished by every established civil servant occupying a designated position of employment in a grade below Secretary General level in that Department or Office. Secretaries General must furnish their statements of interests to the Secretary General, Department of Public Expenditure & Reform. The Secretary General, Department of Public Expenditure & Reform must furnish his or her statements of interests to the Secretary General, Department of Finance.
