# S.I. No. 320/1979 - Social Welfare (Variation of Rates of Payments) Regulations, 1979

S.I. No. 320 of 1979.

SOCIAL WELFARE (VARIATION OF RATES OF PAYMENTS) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1952 ) and [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0001/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1975 ), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Variation of Rates of Payments) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1933" means the [Unemployment Assistance Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0046/index.html) (No. 46 of 1933);

"the Act of 1935" means the [Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1935);

" the Act of 1952" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) ;

"the Act of 1979" means the [Social Welfare Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1979);

"old age (non-contributory) pension" means a pension payable under the Old Age Pensions Acts, 1908 to 1979;

"unemployment assistance" means assistance payable under the Unemployment Assistance Acts, 1933 to 1979;

"widow's (non-contributory) pension" or "orphan's (non-contributory) pension" means, as the context requires, a pension payable under the Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Acts, 1935 to 1979;

"deserted wife's allowance" means an allowance payable under section 22 of the Act of 1970;

"social assistance allowance for unmarried mothers" means an allowance payable under section 8 of the Act of 1973;

"prisoner's wife's allowance" means an allowance payable under section 9 of the Act of 1974;

"single woman's allowance" means an allowance payable under section 8 of the Act of 1974;

"occupational injuries benefits" means benefits payable under the Act of 1966.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The provisions of these Regulations are intended to give effect to the undertaking contained in the National Understanding for Economic and Social Development to increase social welfare payments during the months October 1979 to March 1980.

The Regulations accordingly provide for increases in the existing statutory rates of old age contributory and non-contributory pensions, disability benefit, maternity allowance, unemployment benefit and assistance, widows' and orphans' contributory and non-contributory pensions, deserted wife's benefit, invalidity and retirement pensions, occupational injuries benefits and social assistance allowances for deserted wives, unmarried mothers, prisoners' wives and elderly single women, the new rates being payable from appropriate dates in September and October 1979.
