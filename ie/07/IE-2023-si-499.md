*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th October,* 2023.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) , [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0035.html#sec35) (as amended by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec44) of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2006)) and [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0037.html#sec37) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) (as adapted by the Employment Affairs and Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0447.html) )), and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform (as adapted by the Public Expenditure and Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0019.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

**Citation**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Preservation of Benefits) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

**Amendment to regulations**

###### 2.

2. The Occupational Pension Schemes (Preservation of Benefits) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0279.html) ) are amended by –

- (*a*) in Regulation 2 by -

    - (i) in the definition of “affiliated employer”, by the substitution “of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec7) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014)” for “of [section 155](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0155.html#sec155) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , (No. 33 of 1963)”;

    - (ii) the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “authorised trade union”:

      > “ “authorised trade union” means a trade union which holds a negotiation licence under Part II of the [Trade Union Act 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1941);”;

    - (iii) in the definition of “confinement”, by the substitution of “ [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec51) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005)” for “ [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) ”;

    - (iv) the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “notify the member”:

      > “ “notify the member” means delivering a notice to the person to whom it relates which shall be addressed to the person by name and shall be given, sent or served to the person in one of the following ways:
      >
      > - (i) by delivering it to the person;
      >
      > - (ii) by leaving it at the address at which the person ordinarily resides or, in a case in which an address for service has been furnished, to that address;
      >
      > - (iii) by sending it by post in a pre-paid registered letter or by any other form of recorded delivery to the address at which the person ordinarily resides or, in a case in which an address for service has been furnished, to that address;
      >
      > - (iv) by means of any electronic method;”;

- (*b*) in Regulation 3(1)(a), by the substitution of “€20,000” for “€10,000”;

- (*c*) in Regulation 6(b), by the substitution of the following subparagraph for subparagraph (iii) -

  > - “(iii) the Maternity Protection Acts 1994 and 2004 (No. 34 of 1994 and No. 28 of 2004) or section 81A of the Act requires that such period is a period of membership of the scheme.”, and

- (*d*) by the substitution of the following for the Schedule:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the Occupational Pension Schemes (Preservation of Benefits) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0279.html) ), as amended, in order to –

- •amend various definitions set out in Article 2;

- •amend Article 3(1)(a) to provide that for the purposes of [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0035.html#sec35) of the Pensions Act 1990, the trustees of an occupational pension scheme may effect a transfer payment from the scheme, without the consent of the member concerned, where the total amount of the transfer payment from the scheme in respect of that member is less than €20,000;

- •make a technical amendment to Article 6(b)(iii) to ensure that the legislation referenced therein refers to extant legislation, and

- •revise the list of schemes exempt from the preservation requirements set down in Part III of the Pensions Act 1990.

That Act provides for the preservation of pension benefits for members of occupational pension schemes who leave the relevant employment before normal pensionable age and who have completed a minimum service of at least two years. This preserved benefit is a proportion of the benefits to which the member would have been entitled if he or she had remained in the relevant employment until normal pensionable age.

This list amends the Schedule to the Occupational Pension Schemes (Preservation of Benefits) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0279.html) ), as amended by Article 2 of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Preservation of Benefits) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 70 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0070.html) ), which specifies the occupational pension schemes exempt from the preservation requirements of the Pensions Act.
