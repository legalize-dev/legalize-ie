# S.I. No. 74/1986 - Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 74 of 1986.

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY (EXPERIMENTER'S LICENCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) The Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) Regulations, 1937 to 1983, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) Regulations, 1937 to 1986.

3.These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 11 of the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) Regulations, 1937, (S.R. & O., No. 330 of 1937) (as amended by Regulation 3 of the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 85 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0085.html) )) is hereby amended by the substitution in paragraph (1) of "eight pounds" and "six pounds and fifty new pence" for "seven pounds and fifty new pence", and "six pounds", respectively and the said paragraph as so amended is set out in the Table to this Regulation.

TABLE

A fee of eight pounds shall be payable on the issue of an experimenter's licence and a fee of six pounds and fifty new pence shall be payable on every renewal thereof.

JIM MITCHELL,

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of March, 1986.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 25th

day of March, 1986.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for increases in the licence fees for the experimenter's licence.
