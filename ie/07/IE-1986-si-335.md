# S.I. No. 335/1986 - Conditions of Employment (Manufacture of Wood Briquettes) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 335 of 1986.

CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT (MANUFACTURE OF WOOD BRIQUETTES) (EXCLUSION) REGULATIONS, 1986

I, RUAIRI QUINN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these Regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Manufacture of Wood Briquettes) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely, the manufacture of wood briquettes from wood waste, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purposes of [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provisions shall have effect in respect of the work to which these Regulations relate:

- (*a*) no shift shall be longer than twelve hours in duration;

- (*b*) no worker shall work on two consecutive shifts;

- (*c*) no worker shall work on any shift unless at least twelve hours have elapsed since he last worked a shift;

- (*d*) no worker shall work for so many hours in any week that the average number of hours per week worked in any six consecutive weeks would exceed forty two;

- (*e*) each worker shall be allowed in each shift at least:—

    - (i) one ten-minute break to be taken after the first two hours of work;

    - (ii) one thirty-minute break to be taken after the first five hours of work; and

    - (iii) one ten-minute break to be taken during the last three hours of work;

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of October, 1986.

**RUAIRI QUINN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) provides that no shift shall be longer than 9 hours duration. The purpose of these Regulations is to permit the employment of adult workers on 12 hour shifts on the manufacture of wood briquettes.
