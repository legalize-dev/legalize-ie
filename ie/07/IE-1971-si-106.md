S.I. No. 106 of 1971.

HEALTH ACT, 1970 (ADAPTATION) REGULATIONS, 1971.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0085.html#sec85) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Adaptation) Regulations, 1971.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1971.

###### 3.

3. A reference in any statute, order or regulation to a chief medical officer shall be construed as a reference to a medical officer of a health board performing duties analagous to those performed before 1 April, 1971 by a chief medical officer of a health authority.

###### 4.

4. A reference in any statute, order or regulation to a district medical officer shall be construed as a reference to a medical officer of a health board performing duties analagous to those performed before 1 April, 1971 by a district medical officer of a health authority.

###### 5.

5. A reference in any statute, order or regulation to a medical officer of health shall be construed as a reference to a medical officer of a health board performing duties analagous to those performed before 1 April, 1971 by a medical officer of health.

###### 6.

6. Each of the enactments and regulations mentioned in the first column of the Schedule to this Order shall be adapted in the manner and to the extent specified in the second column of the said Schedule.
