# S.I. No. 6/2014 - Civil Liability Act 1961 (Section 49) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th January*, 2014.

WHEREAS I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, pursuant to [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/sec0049.html#sec49) (1A) of the [Civil Liability Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1961), am satisfied that the monetary amount for the time being standing specified in paragraph (*b*) of [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/sec0049.html#sec49) (1) of the [Civil Liability Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) should, having regard to the changes in value of money generally in the State since the monetary amount was so specified, be varied;

AND WHEREAS I consider that the amount specified in Article 2 is appropriate for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of the said section 49(1);

NOW I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/sec0049.html#sec49) (1A) of the [Civil Liability Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1961) (adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Civil Liability Act 1961 (Section 49) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. The amount of €35,000 is specified for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/sec0049.html#sec49) (1) of the [Civil Liability Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1961).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 January 2014.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order increases to €35,000 the maximum amount of damages that can be awarded to the dependants of a deceased person for mental distress in fatal personal injuries actions. This sum is to be shared among the dependants.
