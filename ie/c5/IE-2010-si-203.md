# S.I. No. 203/2010 - National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Delegation of NAMA Banking Transactions) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th May*, 2010.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. —This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Delegation of NAMA Banking Transactions) Order 2010.

*Delegation of functions.*

###### 2.

2. —The functions of the Minister for Finance under section 54(7E) of the [Finance Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (inserted by the [National Asset Management Agency Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2009)) are delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

11 May 2010.

**BRIAN COWEN,**

Taoiseach.
