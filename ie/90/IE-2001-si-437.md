# S.I. No. 437/2001 - European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) Regulations, 1994 (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) Regulations, 1994 (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 October, 2001.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 275 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0275.html) ), are amended by the deletion of Regulation 12.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal. 26^th September, 2001. |
|  | MARY HARNEY |
|  | Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide that the European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) Regulations, 1994 are amended by the deletion of Regulations 12 with effect from 1^st. October, 2001.
