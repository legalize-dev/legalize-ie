[S.I. No. 668 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0668.html) .

NATIONAL STUD (AMENDMENT) ACT 2000 (VESTING) ORDER 2004.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the power conferred on me by section 2 (1) of [the National Stud (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0040/print.html) (No. 40 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as [the National Stud (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0040/print.html) (Vesting) Order 2004 and shall come into operation on 1 October 2004.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Company” means the Irish National Stud Company Limited.

###### 3.

3. The Minister vests all the property described in the Schedule to this Order in the Company subject to all rights, burdens and encumbrances.

###### 4.

4. The Company shall maintain that portion of the lands known as the Japanese Gardens as a show place for visitors and shall keep the same open to visitors at such times and on such terms and conditions as the Company consider appropriate.

|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of September, 2004. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order vests the land contained in the Schedule in the Irish National Stud Company Ltd. with effect from 1 October 2004.
