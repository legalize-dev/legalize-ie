# S.I. No. 302/2026 - Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd July,* 2026.

I, JIM O’CALLAGHAN, Minister for Justice, Home Affairs and Migration in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 6A(4) of the [Enforcement of Court Orders Act 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1940) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0242.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 July 2026.

###### 2.

2. The Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0301.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations for Schedule 2.

## SCHEDULE

> ### “SCHEDULE 2

## *Part 1* SOLICITOR’S FEE

*Proceedings in District Court*

Fee in respect of proceedings:

€258.53 per case

## *Part 2* SOLICITOR’S FEE

*Appeal against order for imprisonment of debtor*

Fee in respect of an appeal against an order for imprisonment of a debtor made under paragraph (c) or (d) of subsection (7) of section 6 of the Act of 1940:

€258.53 per case

## *Part 3* SOLICITOR’S FEE

*Hearing by High Court of case stated*

Fee in respect of the hearing by the High Court of a case stated:

€1,710.16 per case

## *Part 4* COUNSEL’S FEE

*Hearing by High Court of case stated*

Fee in respect of the hearing by the High Court of a case stated:

€1,710.16 per case”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 June, 2026.

**JIM O’CALLAGHAN,**

Minister for Justice, Home Affairs and Migration.
