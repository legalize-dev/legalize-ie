# S.I. No. 440/2022 - National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014 (Section 29(2)) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th September,* 2022.

I, MICHAEL MCGRATH, Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec29) (2) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2014), following consultation with the Minister for Further and Higher Education, Research, Innovation and Science, and being of the opinion that, having regard to the dissolution of the Dublin Institute of Technology pursuant to the [Technological Universities Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (Section 36) (Appointed Day) Order 2018 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0437.html) ), the reference to Dublin Institute of Technology in Schedule 3 to the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) ought cease to have effect, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014 (Section 29(2)) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that the reference to Dublin Institute of Technology in Schedule 3 to the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2014) shall cease to have effect.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 August, 2022.

**MICHAEL MCGRATH,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.
