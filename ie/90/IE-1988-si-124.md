# S.I. No. 124/1988 - Finance Act 1987 (Designation of Urban Renewal Areas) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 124 of 1988.

FINANCE ACT 1987 (DESIGNATION OF URBAN RENEWAL AREAS) ORDER, 1988.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers vested in me by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0010/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Finance Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1987), after consultation with the Minister for the Environment, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Finance Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Designation of Urban Renewal Areas) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The definition of "designated area" contained in [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986), shall include each of the areas described in Part II of the Schedule to the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0092.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The definition of "qualifying period" contained in [sections 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0042.html#sec42) , [44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0044.html#sec44) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , shall, in relation to each of the designated areas specified in the said Part II, be construed as a reference to the period commencing on the 9th day of July, 1987 and ending on the 31st day of May, 1991.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of June, 1988.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order declares certain urban areas to be designated areas for the purposes of the incentive tax reliefs for urban renewal provided for in the [Finance Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , comprising of capital allowances for commercial buildings, owner-occupier allowance and double rent allowance. The Order also prescribes, in relation to such areas, the period for the availability of these incentives.
