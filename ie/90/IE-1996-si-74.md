# S.I. No. 74/1996 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 74 of 1996.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, IVAN YATES, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), as amended by the [Bovine Diseases Levies (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1996) and as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0011.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1996.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall—

( *a* ) as regards milk, be 0.5 pence per gallon, and

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, be £2.50 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0115.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of March, 1996.

**IVAN YATES,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which came into effect on the 1st of April, 1996 prescribe new rates of disease levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
