# S.I. No. 237/1989 - Safety, Health and Welfare At Work Act, 1989 (Repeals) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 237 of 1989.

SAFETY, HEALTH AND WELFARE AT WORK ACT, 1989 (REPEALS) ORDER, 1989.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) (2) and [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1989), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Repeals) Order, 1989.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of November, 1989.

###### 2.

2. [Section 77](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0077.html#sec77) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1955), and [section 101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0007/sec0101.html#sec101) of the [Mines and Quarries Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1965), are hereby repealed.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 2nd day of October, 1989.

**BERTIE AHERN, T.D.,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order repeals [section 77](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0077.html#sec77) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) and [section 101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0007/sec0101.html#sec101) of the [Mines and Quarries Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) from the 1st day of November, 1989. Those provisions are replaced by [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/sec0056.html#sec56) of the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
