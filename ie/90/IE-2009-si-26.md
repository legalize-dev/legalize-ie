# S.I. No. 26/2009 - Commission To Inquire Into Child Abuse Act 2000 (Section 5) (Specified Period) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th February*, 2009.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec5) (5)(*b*) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000) and after consultation with the Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000 (Section 5) (Specified Period) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. The specified period for the purposes of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec5) (5)(*b*) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000) is hereby extended to the 31st day of May, 2009.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

30 January 2009

**BRIAN COWEN.**

Taoiseach.
