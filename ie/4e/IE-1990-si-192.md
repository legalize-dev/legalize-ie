S.I. No. 192 of 1990.

DERELICT SITES REGULATIONS, 1990.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 *Citation*

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990.

2 *Interpretation*

###### 2.

**2.** In these Regulations:—

"the Act" means the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) ;

"the register" means the derelict sites register established under section 8 of the Act;

"the Minister" means the Minister for the Environment.

3 *Derelict sites register*

###### 3.

**3.** (1) A local authority shall enter on to the register, in addition to the particulars specified in section 8 (1) (*a*) to (*e*) of the Act,:—

( *a* ) particulars of any development objective for the purpose of reserving the land for roads or parking places, or for any of the purposes of reserving or preserving land indicated in Part IV of the Third Schedule to the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963), which exists in relation to land particulars of which are entered on to the register, and

( *b* ) particulars of any bond entered into by an owner of urban land under section 25 of the Act.

- (2) The register shall incorporate a map for enabling a person to trace any land entered on to the register.

4 *Form of notice under section 15 (1) (b) of the Act*

###### 4.

**4.** Form No. 1 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations, or a form substantially to the like effect, shall be the form of notice of intention to acquire a derelict site compulsorily to be served by a local authority under section 15 (1) (*b*) of the Act.

5 *Application for consent of Minister to compulsory acquisition of derelict site*

###### 5.

**5.** An application by a local authority under section 16 of the Act for the consent of the Minister to the compulsory acquisition of any derelict site shall be accompanied by, in addition to the documents and particulars specified in subsection (4) of that section:—

( *a* ) a copy of the newspaper or newspapers in which notice of the intention of the local authority to acquire the derelict site compulsorily was published in accordance with section 15 (1) (a) of the Act, and

( *b* ) a copy of the notice of intention to acquire the derelict site compulsorily served by the local authority in accordance with section 15(1) (*b*) of the Act.

6 *Form of vesting order*

###### 6.

**6.** Form No. 2 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations, or a form substantially to the like effect, shall be the form of a vesting order to be made by a local authority in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 17(1) of the Act.

7 *Revocation*

###### 7.

**7.** The [Derelict Sites Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) Regulations, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 105 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0105.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe procedural matters for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), and revoke the [Derelict Sites Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) Regulations, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 105 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0105.html) ).
