# S.I. No. 827/2005 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 827 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0827.html)**

**Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2005.**

I, Sean Power, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture and Food, the Minister for Environment, Heritage and Local Government and the Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. (a) Part II of the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), is amended by the addition of:

European Communities (General Authorisations for Exports of Agricultural Products) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 266 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0266.html) ),

European Communities (Official Control of Foodstuffs) (Approved Examiners) Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 465 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0465.html) ),

European Communities (Certification of Animals and Animal Products Regulations 1999 ( [S.I. No. 380 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0380.html) ),

European Communities (Veterinary Checks on Fish and Fishery Products Imported from Third Countries) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 548 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0548.html) )

Genetically Modified Organisms (Transboundary Movement) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0054.html) ) in respect of genetically modified organisms intended for direct use as food or for processing,

European Communities (Control on imports of animal products for personal consumption) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 267 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0267.html) ),

European Communities (Registration of Importers of Animal Products Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 269 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0269.html) ),

European Communities (Marketing Standards for Olive Oil) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0397.html) ),

European Communities (Removal of Bovine Vertebral Column Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0528.html) ),

District Court (Food Safety) Rules 2004 ( [S.I. No. 700 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0700.html) ),

European Communities (Trade in the Production, Processing, Distribution and Introduction of Products of Animal Origin for Human Consumption Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 820 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0820.html) ),

European Communities (Introduction of Products of Animal Origin from Third Countries for Human Consumption) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 893 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0893.html) ),

European Communities (Marketing Standards for Eggs) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 172 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0172.html) ).

- (b) Part II of the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), is amended by the deletion of:

European Communities (Marketing Standards for Eggs) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 254 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0254.html) ),

European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 243 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0243.html) ),
