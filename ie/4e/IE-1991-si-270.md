# S.I. No. 270/1991 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wicklow) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 270 of 1991.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF WICKLOW) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1991.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wicklow) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wicklow) Regulations 1987 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0141.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed together and may be cited collectively as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wickow) Regulations 1987 to 1991.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 24th day of October, 1991.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wicklow) Regulations 1987 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0141.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Reference 2 of the First Schedule to the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the addition thereto after paragraph (*e*) of the following paragraph:

> - "(*f*) The Shankill-Bray By-Pass".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 23rd day of October, 1991.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Wicklow) Regulations 1987 to take account of the fact that a section of the Shankill-Bray By-Pass crosses the Bray UDC boundary.

Ambulances, Fire Brigade vehicles and Garda Síochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are exempt from the speed limits.
