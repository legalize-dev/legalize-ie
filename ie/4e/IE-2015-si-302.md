# S.I. No. 302/2015 - Valuation Act 2001 (Appeal to Tribunal) (Fees) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th July*, 2015.

I, BRENDAN HOWLIN, Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) (2) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001) and the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0418.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation Act 2001 (Appeal to Tribunal) (Fees) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 20 July 2015.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2001” means [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001).

###### 3.

3. There shall be charged by the Tribunal in respect of an appeal under section 34 (as amended by section 18 of the Valuation Act 2015) of the Act of 2001-

- (*a*) where the property concerned is on an existing valuation list, the fee set out in column (3) opposite the value of the property referred to in column (2) at a reference number in column (1), of Part 1 of the Schedule, or

- (*b*) where the property concerned is on a valuation list, the fee set out in column (3) opposite the value of the property referred to in column (2) at a reference number in column (1), of Part 2 of the Schedule.

###### 4.

4. The following are revoked:

- (*a*) the Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 312 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0312.html) );

- (*b*) the Valuation Tribunal (Revaluation Appeals) (Fees) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 18 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0018.html) ).

## **SCHEDULE**

*Regulation 3*

*Fees for appeals to Tribunal*

## Part 1

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference Number (1) | Value of property on an existing valuation list (2) | Fee (3) |
| 1 | Not exceeding €50 | €95 |
| 2 | Exceeding €50 but not exceeding €150 | €125 |
| 3 | Exceeding €150 but not exceeding €650 | €300 |
| 4 | Exceeding €650 | €500 |

## Part 2

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference Number (1) | Value of property on a valuation list (2) | Fee (3) |
| 1 | Not exceeding €20,000 | €95 |
| 2 | Exceeding €20,000 but not exceeding €50,000 | €125 |
| 3 | Exceeding €50,000 but not exceeding €250,000 | €300 |
| 4 | Exceeding €250,000 | €500 |

GIVEN under my Official Seal,
