# S.I. No. 369/1999 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers vested in him by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) (sub-section 5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order (S.I. 308 of 1997), hereby makes the following regulations:—

Citation.

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1999.

Rate of Payment.

###### 2.

2. Where a person is or becomes entitled to payment under sub-section (3) of [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0157.html) ), any amount so payable in respect of the week ending 3 December 1999 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20.00 whichever is the greater.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week ending 3 December, 1999 the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20.00, whichever is the greater.
