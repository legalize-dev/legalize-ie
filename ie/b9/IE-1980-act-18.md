# EXPORT PROMOTION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO AMEND AND EXTEND THE EXPORT PROMOTION ACTS, 1959 TO 1977. [1*st July*, 1980]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Increase of non-repayable grants to Córas Tráchtála.

**1.**—The aggregate amount of the sums which the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, may pay to Córas Tráchtála for the purpose of enabling it to exercise or perform its functions (excluding any sum paid to it under section 4 of the Export Promotion (Amendment) Act, 1963) shall not exceed £90,000,000 and, accordingly, section 16 (1) of the Export Promotion Act, 1959, shall be construed as if that sum were substituted for the sum mentioned therein.

###### 2 Short title and collective citation.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Export Promotion (Amendment) Act, 1980.

- (2) The Export Promotion Acts, 1959 to 1977, and this Act may be cited together as the Export Promotion Acts, 1959 to 1980.
