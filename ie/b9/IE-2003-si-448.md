# S.I. No. 448/2003 - Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, on request of the Minister for Transport and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. In this Order -

“Act of 1961” means [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961);

“Act of 1968” means [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1968);

“Act of 2002” means [Road Traffic Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2002).

###### 3.

3. The powers and duties of the Minister for Transport conferred on him -

- (a) by or under the provisions of the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2002 specified in Schedule 1 to this Order, and

- (b) under the Regulations specified in Schedule 2 to this Order,

are delegated to James McDaid, Minister of State at the Department of Transport.

## **SCHEDULE 1** **Provisions of Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2002 delegated**

Section 5 (insofar as it relates to a provision of the Act of 1961 delegated under this Order) of the Act of 1961,

Sections 11, 12, 13, 15 and 16 (as amended by section 6 of the Act of 1968) of the Act of 1961,

Section 61 (inserted by section 55 of the Act of 1968) of the Act of 1961,

Section 65 (as amended by section 6 of the Act of 1968) of the Act of 1961,

Sections 66, 68, 75, 78, 79, 80 and 121 of the Act of 1961,

Sections 8 to 14 and 18 (as amended by section 19 of the Act of 2002) of the Act of 1968,

Section 14 of the Act of 2002.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order delegates to James McDaid, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, the powers and duties of the Minister for Transport as conferred on the Minister by or under the enactments specified in the First Schedule to this Order and the Statutory Instruments and provisions of Statutory Instruments specified in the Second Schedule to this Order.
