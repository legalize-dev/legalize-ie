# S.I. No. 76/2025 - Air Pollution Act 1987 (Solid Fuels) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th March,* 2025.

I, DARRAGH O BRIEN, Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1987) and the Climate Action and Environment (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2016 ( [S. I. No. 393 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0393.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications, Climate Action and Environment) (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S. I. No. 373 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0373.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

Air Pollution Act 1987 (Solid Fuels) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

Citation and commencement

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Pollution Act 1987 (Solid Fuels) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 31 March 2025.

###### 2.

2. The [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Solid Fuels) Regulations 2022, Regulation 5(4) is amended -

By substituting for Regulation 5(4)(b) the following:

- (b) subject to a market assessment, with effect from 1 September 2030, have sulphur content less than 1% by weight on a dry ash-free basis.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 March, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The original regulation proposed that coal products and manufactured solid fuels, including manufactured part biomass products, must have sulphur content less than 1% by weight on a dry ash-free basis from the 1^st of September 2025, subject to a market assessment.

Following the assessment, it was determined that it was not appropriate to reduce the sulphur level at this point and instead it should be re-assessed before September 2030. as such this provision is being amended in this regulation.
