# S.I. No. 551/2015 - European Communities (Interoperability of the Rail System) Regulations 2011 (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th December*, 2015.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2008/57/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 June 2008[^1] , as amended by Commission Directive 2014/106/EU of 5 December 2014[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Interoperability of the Rail System) Regulations 2011 (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Interoperability of the Rail System) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 419 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0419.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) (as amended by Regulation 2 of the European Communities (Interoperability of the Rail System) Regulations 2011 (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 523 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0523.html) )), by substituting for the definition of “Railway Interoperability Directive” the following:

> “ ‘Railway Interoperability Directive’ means Directive 2008/57/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 June 20081 as amended by—
>
> - (*a*) Commission Directive 2009/131/EC of 16 October 2009[^3] ,
>
> - (*b*) Commission Directive 2011/18/EU of 1 March 2011[^4] ,
>
> - (*c*) Commission Directive 2013/9/EU of 11 March 2013[^5] ,
>
> - (*d*) Commission Directive 2014/38/EU of 10 March 2014[^6] , and
>
> - (*e*) Commission Directive 2014/106/EU of 5 December 2014 [^2];”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 December 2015.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these amendment Regulations is to transpose Commission Directive 2014/106/EU of 5 December 2014 into Irish law. This Directive amends Directive 2008/57/EC (the principal Directive on interoperability of the rail system within the EU) by replacing two of its annexes — Annex V (EC Declaration of Verification of Subsystems) and Annex VI (EC Verification Procedures for Subsystems). These Regulations amend the principal national regulations on interoperability of the rail system i.e. the European Communities (Interoperability of the Rail System) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 419 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0419.html) ) to provide for the updating of these annexes.
