# S.I. No. 228/1990 - Aliens (Amendment) (No. 4) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 228 of 1990.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) (No. 4) ORDER, 1990.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) (No. 4) Order, 1990.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 17th day of September, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Aliens Order, 1946 ( [S. R. & O. No. 395 of 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/si/0395.html) ), is hereby amended by the insertion in the Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) )) of "Czechoslovakia" and "Hungary".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of September, 1990.

**RAY BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides that citizens of Czechoslovakia and Hungary will not require a valid Irish visa for admission to the State.

The Order comes into effect on the 17th September, 1990.
