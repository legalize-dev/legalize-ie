# S.I. No. 247/1985 - Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935 (Part II) (No. 11) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 247 of 1985.

PIGS AND BACON ACT, 1935 (PART II) (NO. 11) REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0008.html#sec8) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1978)) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), and the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following regulations, with respect to which, pursuant to subsection (7) of the said section 28, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 11) Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. (1) For the purposes of [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), the prescribed sum shall, for the period beginning on the 1st day of April, 1985, and ending on the commencement of these Regulations, be 75p.

- (2) For the purposes of [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), the prescribed sum shall, from the commencement of these Regulations, be 75p.

###### 4.

4. The [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 10) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 410 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0410.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of July, 1985.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations reduce as and from 1 April 1985 the fees payable by bacon curers for veterinary examination of pigs in accordance with the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
