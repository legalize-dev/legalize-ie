# S.I. No. 634/2007 - Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal (Amendment) Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 634 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0634.html)

HEPATITIS C COMPENSATION TRIBUNAL (AMENDMENT) ACT 2006 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th September*, 2007.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 7(11) of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1997) (as inserted by Section 3 of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal (Amendment) Act, 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2006)) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal (Amendment) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) , (Establishment Day) Order, 2007

###### 2.

2. The 12th day of September, 2007, is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of section 7(11) of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1997) (as inserted by Section 3 of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal (Amendment) Act, 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2006)).

GIVEN under my official seal,

12 September 2007

**MARY HARNEY.**

Minister for Health and Children

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order appoints the 12th of September as the establishment day for the relevant insurance scheme, referred to in section 7(11) of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1997) (as inserted by Section 3 of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal (Amendment) Act, 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2006)).
