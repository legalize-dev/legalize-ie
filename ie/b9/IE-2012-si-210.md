# S.I. No. 210/2012 - Ombudsman for Children Act 2002 (Section 11(2)(a)) Order 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd June*, 2012.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Children and Youth Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec11) (2)(*a*) of the [Ombudsman for Children Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2002) and the Office of the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 218 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0218.html) ) and with the consent of the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Ombudsman for Children Act 2002 (Section 11(2)(*a*)) Order 2012.

###### 2.

2. Subsection (1)(*e*)(iii) of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec11) of the [Ombudsman for Children Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) shall cease to have effect on and after 1 July 2012.

The Minister for Justice and Equality consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 June 2012.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 June 2012.

**FRANCES FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of this Order is that subsection (1)(*e*)(iii) of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec11) of the [Ombudsman for Children Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) shall cease to have effect on and after 1 July, 2012.
