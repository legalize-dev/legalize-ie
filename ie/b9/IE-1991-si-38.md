# S.I. No. 38/1991 - Video Recordings Act, 1989 (Fees) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 38 of 1991.

VIDEO RECORDINGS ACT, 1989 (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. For the purposes of subsection (1) of [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989) — a fee of £4,000 is hereby prescribed for a wholesale licence.

###### 3.

3. For the purposes of subsection (2) of the said section 18, a fee of £100 is hereby prescribed for a retail licence.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of February, 1991.

**RAY BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 27th

day of February, 1991.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the fees to be charged for licences issued under [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
