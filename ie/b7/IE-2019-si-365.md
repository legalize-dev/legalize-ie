# S.I. No. 365/2019 - Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd July,* 2019.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009[^1] (as amended by Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013[^2] , Council Regulation (EU) No 1385/2013 of 17 December 2013[^3] , Regulation (EU) No 508/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^4] and Regulation (EU) No 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015[^5] ), Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011[^6] (other than Title VII) (as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1962 of 28 October 2015[^7] ) and Regulation (EU) 2019/472 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019[^8] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ) is amended:

- (*a*) In Regulation 2(1) by deleting the definition of “Hake Regulation”,

- (*b*) By substituting the following for Regulation 2(2): “A word or expression that is used in the Commission Regulation or the Council Regulation and is also used in these Regulations, has, unless the context otherwise requires, the same meaning in these Regulations as it has in the Commission Regulation or the Council Regulation, as the case may be.”, and

- (*c*) substituting the following for the Schedule (as inserted by the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 446 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0446.html) )):

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide for the removal of designated ports for landings of Hake greater than 2 tonnes given the repeal of Council Regulation (EC) No 811/2004 of 21 April 2004 which established measures for the recovery of the Northern hake stock.
