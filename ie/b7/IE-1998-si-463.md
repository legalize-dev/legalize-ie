S.I. No. 463 of 1998.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 10) SCHEME, 1998.

Bord Telecom Eireann plc, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 90 of the Postal Telecommunications Services Act, 1983 (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme.

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited ad the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 10) Scheme, 1998.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation *"the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1998"* includes this Scheme.

- (2) Article 4(a), Article 4 (b), Article 4(c) and Article 4(d) shall come into operation on 1st January 1999.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by:

Per period or part of a period of time specified in the Table of this Part of this Schedule appropriate to the call.

| Description of Call | Daytime Rate (Seconds) | Evening Rate (Seconds) |
| Calls to telephone exchange line numbers prefixed by an 1891 code | 450 | 900 |

Daytime Rate:

The daytime rate applies to calls made at or after 8 a.m. and before 6 p.m. on normal working days from Monday to Friday (inclusive).

Evening Rate:

The evening rate applies to calls made as follows:

- (i) at or after 6 p.m and before 8 a.m. from Monday to Friday (inclusive);

- (ii) all day on Saturday, Sunday and Public Holidays; and

- (iii) on such other times as Bord Telecom Éireann plc may from time to time announce by advertisement in the national morning daily newspapers.
