# S.I. No. 349/2012 - Pleasure Craft (Personal Flotation Devices and Operation) (Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th September*, 2012.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 20 (inserted by [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec47) (1) of the [Maritime Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) (No. 11 of 2005)) of the [Merchant Shipping Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1992) and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pleasure Craft (Personal Flotation Devices and Operation) (Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Pleasure Craft (Personal Flotation Devices and Operation) (Safety) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 921 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0921.html) ) are amended, in Regulation 2, by inserting after the definition of “open craft” the following:

> “ ‘personal watercraft’ means a craft of less than 4 metres in length which uses an internal combustion engine having a water jet pump as its primary source of propulsion, and which is designed to be operated by a person or persons sitting, standing or kneeling on, rather than within the confines of, a hull;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 September 2012.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Pleasure Craft (Personal Flotation Devices and Operation) (Safety) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 921 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0921.html) ) by inserting a definition of “personal watercraft” into the 2005 regulations.
