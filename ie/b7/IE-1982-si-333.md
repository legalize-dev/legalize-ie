# S.I. No. 333/1982 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 51, Nineteenth Period) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 333 of 1982.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 51, NINETEENTH PERIOD) (MISCELLANEOUS TEXTILES) ORDER, 1982.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 51, Nineteenth Period) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quanity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints a nominal Quota of £IR100 as the value of miscellaneous textiles (wearing apparel and other made-up textile articles) originating in the countries listed below, which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1983 to 31st December, 1983. This nominal Quota has been appointed to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister will in fact be prepared to license during the quota period goods not less than 15 tonnes.

The countries to which the Instrument applies are as follows:—
