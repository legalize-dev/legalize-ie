# S.I. No. 92/2001 - The Women's Health Council (Establishment) (Amendment) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 3 to 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Health (Corporate Bodies) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (as amended) as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Women's Health Council (Establishment)(Amendment Order 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Women's Health Council (Establishment) Order, 1997 ( [SI No. 278 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0278.html) ) is hereby amended by the insertion of the following Article:

> “28. The [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) and the schemes and regulations made thereunder shall apply to the Council from the 24th June, 1997.”
