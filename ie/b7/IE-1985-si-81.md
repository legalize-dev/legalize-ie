S.I. No. 81 of 1985.

TELEPHONE (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1985.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Telephone (Amendment) Scheme, 1985.

- (2) The Telephone Schemes, 1980 to 1984, and this Scheme may be cited together as the Telephone Schemes 1980 to 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. In this Scheme "the Regulations of 1983" means the Telephone (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 73 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0073.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Minister for Communications pursuant to [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) (2) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) has concurred in the increase of charges effected by this Scheme.

###### 6.

6. The Telephone Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0195.html) ), are hereby amended by:

- (*a*) The substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 20 (2):

  > "In case a reverse charge call is made to a telephone in the State there shall be charged to and payable by the subscriber who is the subscriber in respect of the called telephone the charge specified in paragraph 4 of Part 1 of the Fifth Schedule to these Regulations which is appropriate to the call (save any personal charge in case the call is made from a coinbox exchange line)."

- (*b*) The substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 21(2):

  > "The subscriber shall pay, on demand, to Bord Telecom Éireann, for the installation, removal and rental of equipment specified in the Sixth Schedule to these Regulations, the charges specified in the said Schedule opposite the mention of the equipment."

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The main purpose of this Scheme is to provide for an increase in telephone call, rental and connection charges of approximately 7½%.
