# S.I. No. 74/1973 - Veterinary Surgeons (Registration Fees and Annual Fees) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 74 of 1973.

VETERINARY SURGEONS (REGISTRATION FEES AND ANNUAL FEES) ORDER, 1973.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [sections 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/sec0027.html#sec27) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1931), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons (Registration Fees and Annual Fees) Order, 1973.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. Ten pounds is hereby appointed as the amount of the fee payable under [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. Ten pounds is hereby appointed as the amount of the fee payable under [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 23rd day ofMarch, 1973.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to fix at ten pounds the registration and annual fees payable to the Veterinary Council of Ireland by veterinary surgeons.
