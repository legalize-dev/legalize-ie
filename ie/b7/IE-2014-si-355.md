# S.I. No. 355/2014 - Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) (No. 1) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st August*, 2014.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Central Bank of Ireland by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , the Central Bank of Ireland, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) (No. 1) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. Section 2(1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) is hereby amended by the deletion from the definition of “credit institution” of the following:

Friends First Finance Limited.

Nua Homeloans Limited.

###### 3.

3. The Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) (No. 1) Regulations 1999 ( [S.I No. 15 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0015.html) ) and the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 2) (No. 1) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 100 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0100.html) ) are hereby repealed.

Signed for and on behalf of the CENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND

25 July 2014.

CYRIL ROUX,

Deputy Governor.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to revoke the prescription of a certain entity prescribed pursuant to paragraph (*e*) of the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
