# S.I. No. 21/1997 - Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 21 of 1997.

VALUATION TRIBUNAL (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1997.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The fee to be charged in respect of an appeal to the Valuation Tribunal under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) (5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , of a class mentioned in column (1) of the following Table shall be the amount specified in column (2) opposite the mention of that class.

###### 3.

3. The Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations, 1988 (S.I . No. 182 of 1988), are hereby revoked.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Class of valuation of property as determined by the Commissioner of Valuation (1) | Appeal Fee (2) |
|  | £ |
| Less than £25 | 75 |
| £25 to £99 | 100 |
| £100 to £499 | 200 |
| £500 or over | 300 |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of January, 1997.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.
