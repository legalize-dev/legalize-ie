# S.I. No. 242/2012 - European Communities (Bluetongue) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th July*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2012/5/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 March 2012,[^1] and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 456/2012 of 30 May 2012[^2] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Bluetongue) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Bluetongue) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 133 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0133.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2—

    - (i) by the substitution, for the definition of ‘Bluetongue Directive’, of—

      > “ ‘Bluetongue Directive’ means Council Directive 2000/75/EC of 20 November 2000 as amended by Directive 2012/5/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 March 2012;”

      and

    - (ii) by the substitution, for the definition of ‘Bluetongue Regulation’, of—

      > “ ‘Bluetongue Regulation’ means “Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1266/2007 of 26 October 2007 as amended by Commission Regulation (EC) No. 289/2008 of 31 March 2008; Commission Regulation (EC) No. 384/2008 of 29 April 2008; Commission Regulation (EC) No. 394/2008 of 30 April 2008 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 456/2012 of 30 May 2012;”,
      >
      > - (*b*) in Regulation 8, by the deletion of paragraph (3), and
      >
      > - (*c*) in Regulation 10, by the substitution, for paragraph (2), of—
      >
      > - “(2) A person shall not, in a surveillance zone, vaccinate against bluetongue using live attenuated vaccines.”.
      >
      >   GIVEN under my Official Seal,
      >
      >   21 June 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give effect to Directive 2012/5/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 March 2012 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 456/2012 of 30 May 2012.
