# S.I. No. 610/2016 - European Union (Customs Code) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th December*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and commencement*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Customs Code) Regulations 2016.

*Amendment of Customs Act 2015*

###### 2.

2. The [Customs Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2015) is amended—

- (*a*) in section 2(1)—

    - (i) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “Customs Code”:

      > “ ‘Customs Code’ means the Union Customs Code established by the Customs Code Regulation, as last supplemented by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/341 of 17 December 2015[^2] and as last implemented by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2447 of 24 November 2015[^3] ;”, and

    - (ii) by the insertion of the following definition:

      > “ ‘Customs Code Regulation’ means Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013[^4] ;”,

- (*b*) in section 40(1)(*a*), by the substitution of “Article 135, Article 233(1), (2) and (3) or Article 242 of the Customs Code Regulation” for “Article 38, 96 or 101 of Council Regulation (EEC) No. 2913/92 of 12 October 1992[^5] ”,

- (*c*) in section 44, in the definition of “decision”, by the substitution of “Article 33 of the Customs Code Regulation” for “Article 12 of the Customs Code”,

- (*d*) in section 45(4)(*b*)—

    - (i) in subparagraph (ii), by the substitution of “Article 22(3) of the Customs Code Regulation” for “Article 6(2) of the Customs Code”, and

    - (ii) by the insertion of the following subparagraph after subparagraph (v):

      > - “(vi) where an application for a decision under Article 22 of the Customs Code Regulation has been made and no decision has been taken within the time period for the taking of a decision under Article 22(3) of the Customs Code Regulation, the expiry of that period,”, and

- (*e*) by the deletion of section 51.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 December 2016.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give effect to Regulation (EU) No 952/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 October 2013 insofar as a number of cross references to the former Community Customs Code are substituted by corresponding cross references to the new Union Customs Code and by the deletion of [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec51) of the [Customs Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) in relation to the calculation and payment of interest.
