# S.I. No. 768/2005 - Civil Registration (Fees And Allowances) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 768 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0768.html)**

**CIVIL REGISTRATION (FEES AND ALLOWANCES) REGULATIONS 2005.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 9 of the [Vital Statistics and Births, Deaths and Marriages Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1952) and sections 3 and 67 of the [Civil Registration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2004), (as amended by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0019/print.html#sec1) of the [Civil Registration (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2005)), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Registration (Fees and Allowances) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into effect on 5^th December 2005.

###### 3.

3. The fee fixed in section 3 (b) of the [Registration of Marriages Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) is altered to €0.86.

###### 4.

4. (a) The allowance fixed by section 2 (1) (a) of the [Registration of Marriages Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) is altered to €43.17 in respect of each year.

- (b) The allowance fixed by section 2 (1) (b) of the [Registration of Marriages Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) is altered to an amount bearing to €43.17 the same proportion as the said part of such year bears to one year.

###### 5.

5. The amount of the fee for each entry in a register of births, a register of stillbirths or a register of deaths as provided for under Section 67A(1)(a) of the [Civil Registration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) is €0.86. This regulation applies to registers maintained under [Section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec13) of the [Civil Registration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) .

###### 6.

6. (a) The amount of the allowance payable to a Registrar in respect of registration of births, stillbirths and deaths under Section 67A(1)(b) of the [Civil Registration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) is €172.68 in respect of each year, and

- (b) In the case of a part of a year during which the Registrar held office an amount bearing to €172.68 the same ratio as the said part of such year bears to one year.

###### 7.

7. Fees and allowances paid under section 67A of the [Civil Registration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (as amended) will be paid quarterly, pro-rata as appropriate on submission of an account in the form set out in the Schedule and certified by the Superintendent Registrar.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note does not form part of the Instrument nor does it purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

The effect of this order is to set fees and allowances payable to Registrars of Births, Stillbirths, Deaths and Marriages.
