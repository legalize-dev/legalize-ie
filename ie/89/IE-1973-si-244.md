# S.I. No. 244/1973 - Conditions of Employment (Medical Products Assembly) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 244 of 1973.

CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT (MEDICAL PRODUCTS ASSEMBLY) (EXCLUSION) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, MICHAEL O'LEARY, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Medical Products Assembly) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely, the assembly of medical products as operated at the premises of Travenol Laboratories S.A. in the county of Mayo, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purpose of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provisions shall have effect in relation to the work to which these Regulations relate:

( *a* ) no woman shall be employed to do any such work at any time between the hour of 12.00 midnight on any day and the hour of 8 a.m. on the following day;

( *b* ) no woman shall be so employed on any day until after the expiration of eleven hours from the time at which she ceased to do such work on the previous day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of August, 1973.

**MICHAEL O'LEARY,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) provides that female adult workers may not work later than 10 p.m on any day. The purpose of these Regulations is to allow the employment of female adult workers between 8 a.m. and 12 midnight on the assembly of medical products at the premises of Travenol Laboratories S.A. in Co. Mayo.
