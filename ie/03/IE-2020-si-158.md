# S.I. No. 158/2020 - European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th May,* 2020.

The Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009[^1] as amended by Directive (EU) 2019/2177 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2019[^2] , hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 30 June 2020.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Insurance and Reinsurance) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 485 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0485.html) ) are amended -

- (*a*) in Regulation 3, by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “the Directive”:

  > “ ‘the Directive’ means Directive 2009/138/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009[^1] on the taking up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II) as amended by Directive 2011/89/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 November 2011[^3] , Directive 2012/23/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 September 2012[^4] , Council Directive 2013/23/EU of 13 May 2013[^5] , Directive 2013/58/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013[^6] , Directive 2014/51/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014[^7] , Directive 2016/2341/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 December 2016[^8] , Regulation (EU) 2017/2402 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 December 2017[^9] , Directive 2018/843/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018[^10] and Directive (EU) 2019/2177 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2019[^2] ;”,

  and

- (*b*) in Regulation 88(8), by the substitution of “85 basis points” for “100 basis points”.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

1 May, 2020.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
