# S.I. No. 12/1982 - Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 12 of 1982.

TRUSTEE SAVINGS BANKS REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0033/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Finance Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1982.

- (2) The Trustee Savings Banks Regulations, 1968 to 1980, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Trustee Savings Banks Regulations 1968 to 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 21st day of January, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest allowed on moneys standing to the credit of every separate investment account of a trustee savings bank in the name of the Minister for Finance in the Post Office Savings Bank shall, in lieu of the rate specified in the Trustee Savings Banks (No. 2) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 132 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0132.html) ), be 16.15 per cent.

###### 4.

4. The Trustee Savings Banks (No. 2) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 132 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0132.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of January, 1982.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations increase to 16.15 per cent per annum as from 21st January, 1982 the rate of interest payable by the Minister for Finance on moneys deposited with him by the trustee savings banks in every separate special investment account.
