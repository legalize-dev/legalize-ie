# S.I. No. 288/2002 - Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) (Amendment) Scheme, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0002.html#sec2) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Scheme:

Short Title.

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) (Amendment) Scheme, 2002.

- (2) This Scheme and the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0455.html) ) shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1998 to 2002.

Commencement.

###### 2.

2. (1) Article 4 shall be deemed to have come into force on the 29^th day of September, 1998 as respects the provisions of subparagraph (i) of the definition contained therein.

- (2) Article 4 shall be deemed to have come into force on the 30^th day of January, 2001 as respects the provisions of subparagraph (ii) of the definition contained therein.

Interpretation.

###### 3.

3. In this Scheme:-

“the Principal Scheme” means the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0455.html) ).

Amendment of article 3 of the Principal Scheme.

###### 4.

4. Article 3 of the Principal Scheme is hereby amended -

by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “local authority” contained in sub-article (1) thereof:

‘ “local authority” shall have the meaning assigned to it in [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) and, in addition, shall include a body within the meaning of section 2(9) of that Act, subject to the provisos that —

- (i) with effect from the 29^th day of September, 1998, it shall not include a vocational education committee as respects a person referred to in article 3(2) of the Vocational Education Superannuation Schemes (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 362 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0362.html) ), and

- (ii) with effect from the 30^th day of January, 2001, it shall not include a body referred to in article 3(2) of the Education Sector Superannuation Scheme (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0014.html) );’.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Scheme amends the definition of “local authority” contained in the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1998.
