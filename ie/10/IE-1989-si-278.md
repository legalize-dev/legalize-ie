# S.I. No. 278/1989 - Combat Poverty Agency (Membership) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 278 of 1989.

COMBAT POVERTY AGENCY (MEMBERSHIP) REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0014/sec0007.html#sec7) (8) of the [Combat Poverty Agency Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1986), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Combat Poverty Agency (Membership) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The maximum number of members of the Combat Poverty Agency specified in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0014/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Combat Poverty Agency Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) is hereby varied by the substitution of "twelve" for "ten".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this

27th day of October, 1989.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for an increase in the membership of the Board of the Combat Poverty Agency from ten to twelve persons.
