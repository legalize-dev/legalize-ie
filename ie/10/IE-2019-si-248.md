# S.I. No. 248/2019 - European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th June,* 2019.

I, Michael Creed, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1660 of 7 November 2018[^1] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/35 of 9 January 2019[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 426 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0426.html) ) are amended by substituting in Regulation 2(1) for the definition of “Commission Regulation 669/2009” the following –

> “ ‘Commission Regulation 669/2009’ means Commission Regulation (EC) No 669/2009 of 24 July 2009[^3] (as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No 212/2010 of 12 March 2010[^4] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 323/2014 of 28 March 2014[^5] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 718/2014 of 27 June 2014[^6] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/1024 of 23 June 2016[^7] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2298 of 12 December 2017[^8] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/941 of 2 July 2018[^9] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1660 of 7 November 2018 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/35 of 9 January 2019), Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/943 of 18 June 2015[^10] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/186 of 2 February 2017[^11] ;”.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0330.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 May 2019.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations update controls on the import of certain food and feed of non-animal origin from specific third countries with regard to pesticide residues.
