# S.I. No. 53/2020 - Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th February,* 2020.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) , [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0029.html#sec29) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2007” means the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 559 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0559.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) are amended -

- (*a*) in Regulation 8 (amended by Regulation 4(3) of the Regulations of 2007) by the insertion before “diphtheria” of “Covid-19,”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 14(1)(b) (amended by Regulation 4(6) of the Regulations of 2007) by the insertion before “diphtheria” of “Covid-19,”,

- (*c*) in Regulation 15(2)(a) (amended by Regulation 4(7) of the Regulations of 2007) by the insertion before “diphtheria” of “Covid-19,”, and

- (*d*) by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule (amended by Regulation 2(b) of the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 567 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0567.html) )) to those Regulations:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Regulation and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations (Regulation 3(d)) amend the Schedule to the Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) to include Covid-19 amongst the list of diseases designated as an ‘infectious disease’ for the purposes of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , in accordance with Regulation 6 of those Regulations – all diseases that are so designated are required to be notified (by a medical practitioner who becomes aware or suspects an instance of such disease) to a medical officer of health and are subsequently relayed to the HSE’s Health Protection Surveillance Centre in accordance with Regulation 14 of the 1981 Regulations (as amended).

These Regulations (Regulation 3(a)) also amend Regulation 8 of the Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 to provide for the inclusion of Covid-19 amongst the list of infectious diseases that a person (who is a probable source of infection with the disease) may (as a necessary safeguard against the spread of the disease) be detained and isolated for, in accordance with the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) .

These Regulations (Regulations 3(b) and (c) also provide for the preliminary notification (i.e. immediate notification by telephone) of Covid-19.
