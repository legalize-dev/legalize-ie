# S.I. No. 15/1951 - Health Act, 1947 (Adaptation) Order, 1951

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 15 of 1951.

HEALTH ACT, 1947 (ADAPTATION) ORDER, 1951.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0109.html#sec109) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and of every other power in that behalf enabling him, hereby orders as follows :—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Adaptation) Order, 1951, and shall come into operation on 1st day of February, 1951.

###### 2.

2. The [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935) shall be adapted as follows :—

- (1) In paragraph (*c*) of Section 53 thereof :

    - (*a*) the word " sanitary " shall be deleted where it first occurs ;

    - (*b*) after the word " authority " and before the word " whom " where such words occur in sequence, the words " or of a health authority to " shall be inserted ;

    - (*c*) the words " assigned the duty of acting as " shall be substituted for the words " appointed to be a," where they occur.

- (2) In sub-section (1) of section 54 thereof the words " an officer of a sanitary authority or a health authority " shall be substituted for the words " a sanitary officer ", where they occur.

###### 3.

3. For the definition of the expression " authorised sanitary officer " in sub-section (4) of [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0044/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Sale of Food and Drugs (Milk) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0044/index.html) (No. 44 of 1936), there shall be substituted the following definition :

> " the expression ' authorised sanitary officer ' when used in relation to a sanitary district means a person to whom the duty of acting as milk sampling officer under the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935) has been assigned by the sanitary authority for such district ".

###### 4.

4. The Public Health (Saorstát Éireann) (Preservatives, etc. in Food) Regulations, 1928 (S. R. & O., 1928, No. 54) shall be adapted as follows :—

- (1) In Article 3 thereof the words " health authority " shall be substituted for the words " Sanitary Authority " and the words " functional area " shall be substituted for the word " district " wherever they occur.

- (2) In Article 6 thereof the words " health authority " shall be substituted for the words " Sanitary Authority " wherever they occur.

- (3) In Article 7 thereof the words " functional area " shall be substituted for the word " district " wherever it occurs.

- (4) The following paragraph shall be substituted for paragraph (1) of Article 9 thereof :—

  > - "(1) A health authority and any officer of a health authority authorised by them in writing shall also have power to enforce and execute this Part of these Regulations ".

- (5) In Articles 10 and 14 thereof the words " health authority" shall be substituted for the words " Sanitary or Port Sanitary Authority".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this twentieth day of January One Thousand Nine Hundred and Fifty-one.

**NOËL C. BROWNE,**

Minister for Health.
