# S.I. No. 105/1972 - School Attendance Act, 1926 (Extension of Application) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 105 of 1972.

SCHOOL ATTENDANCE ACT, 1926 (EXTENSION OF APPLICATION) ORDER, 1972.

I, PÁDRAIG Ó FACHTNA, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/sec0024.html#sec24) (1) of the [School Attendance Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1926), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [School Attendance Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (Extension of Application) Order, 1972.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The provisions of the [School Attendance Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1926), shall extend to children who have attained the age of fourteen years and have not attained the age of fifteen years.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 12th day of April, 1972.

**PÁDRAIG Ó FACHTNA,**

Minister for Education.
