# S.I. No. 139/1998 - Freedom of Information Act, 1997 (Section 47(3)) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 139 of 1998.

FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT, 1997 (SECTION 47(3)) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, CHARLIE McCREEVY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0003.html#sec3) and [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0047.html#sec47) (3) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (Section 47 (3)) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The amount of£16.50 per hour is hereby prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*a*) of [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0047.html#sec47) (3) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997).

###### 3.

3. For the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of the said section 47(3), there is hereby prescribed—

- (*a*) the amount of 3 pence per sheet in relation to a photocopy,

- (*b*) the amount of 40 pence in relation to a 3 ½" computer diskette, and

- (c) the amount of£8 in relation to a CD-ROM (that is to say, a compact disk read-only memory).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of May, 1998.

CHARLIE McCREEVY,

Minister for Finance.
