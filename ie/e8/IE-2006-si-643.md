# S.I. No. 643/2006 - Child Care (Pre-School Services) (No 2) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**Statutory Instrument**

**S. I. No. 643 of 2006**

**Child Care (Pre-School Services) (No2) (Amendment) Regulations 2006**

**[S.I. No. 643 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0643.html)**

**CHILD CARE (PRE-SCHOOL SERVICES) (No 2) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2006**

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children, in the exercise of the powers conferred on me by Sections 50 and 68 of the [Child Care Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) , (No. 17 of 1991) as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ) and the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0842.html) ) and after consultation with the Minister for Education and Science and the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government hereby make the following Regulations:-

**CITATION**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Child Care (Pre-School Services) (No 2) (Amendment) Regulations 2006.

**COMMENCEMENT**

###### 2.

2. The following paragraph is substituted for Paragraph 2(2) of the Child Care (Pre-School Services) (No 2) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No 604 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0604.html) ).

> - “(2) Regulation 3(2) comes into operation on 19 December 2006.”

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my hand 18 December 2006 |
|  | ____________________________ Brian Lenihan Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Regulation amends the Child Care (Pre-School Services) (No 2) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 604 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0604.html) ) to revoke the Child Care (Pre-School Services) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 505 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0505.html) ) with effect from 19 December 2006.
