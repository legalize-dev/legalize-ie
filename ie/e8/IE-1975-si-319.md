# S.I. No. 319/1975 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 2) (Cycle Tyres) (Amendment) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 319 of 1975.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 2) (CYCLE TYRES) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1975.

The Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0012/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Control of Imports Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1934), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 2) (Cycle Tyres) (Amendment) Order, 1975.

###### 2.

2. The Control of Imports (Quota No. 2) (Fifty-first Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 372 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0372.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "the 31st day of December, 1976" for "the 31st day of December, 1975" in the Schedule, and the Schedule as so amended is set out in the Table to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 16th day of December, 1975.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This instrument appoints 200 articles as the quantity of cycle tyres and inner tubes which may be imported from certain countries during the period from 1st January, 1976 to 31st December, 1976.
