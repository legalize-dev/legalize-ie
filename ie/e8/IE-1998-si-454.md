# S.I. No. 454/1998 - Parental Leave (Notice of Force Majeure Leave) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, T.D., Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0030/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Parental Leave Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1998), and having, in accordance with subsection (2) of that section, consulted persons whom I consider to be representative of employers generally and employees generally, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Parental Leave (Notice of *Force Majeure* Leave) Regulations, 1998, and shall come into operation on the 3rd day of December, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The notice to be given by an employee to his or her employer under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0030/sec0013.html#sec13) (3) of the [Parental Leave Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) , shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations (the "scheduled form") or in a form to the like effect containing the information and declaration referred to in the scheduled form.

SCHEDULE

[Parental Leave Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0030/index.html)

Notice to Employer of *Force Majeure* Leave

This form, or a form to the like effect containing the information and declaration referred to in this form, must be completed by an employee who takes *force majeure* leave as soon as reasonably practicable after the leave is taken.

An employee is entitled to *force majeure* leave where for urgent family reasons, owing to an injury to or the illness of a person referred to in section 13(2) of the Act, the employee's immediate presence is indispensable at the place where the person is.

The persons referred to in section 13(2) of the Act are: child, adopted child or a person in relation to whom the employee is in *loco parentis;* spouse or person with whom the employee is living as husband or wife; brother or sister; parent or grandparent.

*Force majeure* leave must not exceed 3 working days in any period of 12 consecutive months or 5 working days in any period of 36 consecutive months.

In the event of any dispute or difference between an employer and employee in relation to *force majeure* leave the issue may be referred by either party to a rights commissioner.
