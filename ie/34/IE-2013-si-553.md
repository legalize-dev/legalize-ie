# S.I. No. 553/2013 - Excise Duty on Cigarettes (Quantitative Restrictions) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd January*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec104) (4) (amended by [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0041/print.html#sec47) (*d*) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0041/print.html) (No. 41 of 2013)) of the [Finance Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2001), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Excise Duty on Cigarettes (Quantitative Restrictions) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 1 January 2014.

###### 3.

3. In this Order—

“cigarettes” has the same meaning as it has in [section 71](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec71) of the [Finance Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2005);

“specified State” means—

- (*a*) the Republic of Bulgaria,

- (*b*) the Republic of Croatia,

- (*c*) Hungary,

- (*d*) the Republic of Latvia,

- (*e*) the Republic of Lithuania, or

- (*f*) Romania;

“subsection (2)(*a*)” means subsection (2)(*a*) of [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec104) of the [Finance Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2001).

###### 4.

4. Where—

- (*a*) cigarettes are released for consumption in a specified State, and

- (*b*) those cigarettes—

    - (i) are acquired by a private individual in such a State for his or her own use and not for commercial purposes, and

    - (ii) are transported into the State before 1 January 2018 by that private individual and accompanied by him or her during such transportation,

then subsection (2)(*a*) shall not apply to such cigarettes if they exceed 300 in number.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 December 2013.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order excludes cigarettes exceeding 300 in number and transported for personal use from certain Member States from the relief from excise duty, as provided for in [Section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec104) (2)(*a*) of the [Finance Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) .
