# S.I. No. 8/1969 - County Management (Tipperary North Riding and Tipperary South Riding) Order, 1969

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 8 of 1969.

COUNTY MANAGEMENT (TIPPERARY NORTH RIDING AND TIPPERARY SOUTH RIDING) ORDER, 1969.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [City and County Management (Amendment) Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , with the consent of the Minister for Health and the Minister for Social Welfare, hereby orders as follows :—

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the County Management (Tipperary North Riding and Tipperary South Riding) Order, 1969.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the first day of April, 1969.

###### 2.

2. The application in relation to the Counties of Tipperary North Riding and Tipperary South Riding of subsection (2) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , is hereby terminated.

###### 3.

3. [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , shall be amended by the deletion of subsection (3).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government this 28th day of January, 1969.

**CAOIMHGHÍN Ó BEOLÁIN,**

Aire Rialtais Áitiúil.

The Minister for Health hereby consents to the above Order.

Dated this 28th day of January, 1969.

**SEÁN Ó FLANNAGAIN,**

The Minister for Social Welfare hereby consents to the above Order.

Dated this 28th day of January, 1969.

**SEOSAMH Ó BRAONÁIN,**

Aire Leasa Shóisialaigh.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, terminates, as from 1st April, 1969, the application of subsection (2) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , to the Counties of Tipperary North Riding and Tipperary South Riding. Under that subsection, the same person was to be county manager for each of those counties. The effect of this Order is to require each of those counties to have its own individual county manager.
