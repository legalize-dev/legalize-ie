# S.I. No. 317/1975 - Money Order Amendment (No. 21) Regulations, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 317 of 1975.

MONEY ORDER AMENDMENT (NO. 21) REGULATIONS, 1975.

I, CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23 of the Post Office Act, 1908, and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Post Office (Amendment) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1969), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Money Order Amendment (No. 21) Regulations, 1975.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 18 (1) of the Money Order Regulations, 1903 is hereby amended by the substitution of the following tables for Tables A and B (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Money Order Amendment (No. 20) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 283 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0283.html) ) which set out the poundage payable in respect of money orders:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| "A | Poundage |
| In respect of inland money orders: |  |
| for an amount not exceeding £50.00 | 30p |
| for an amount exceeding £50 but not exceeding £100.00 | 40p |
| B | Poundage |
| In respect of foreign money orders: |  |
| for an amount not exceeding £10.00 | 30p |
| for an amount exceeding £10.00 but not exceeding £20.00 | 45p |
| for an amount exceeding £20.00 but not exceeding £30.00 | 60p |
| for an amount exceeding £30.00 but not exceeding £40.00 | 80p |
| for an amount exceeding £40.00 but not exceeding £50.00 | £1.00" |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Warrant applies to the Money Order service and comes into operation on the 1st January, 1976. It authorises increases in charges on inland and foreign money orders.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 18th day of December, 1975.

**CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the provisions of the foregoing Warrant.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 18th day of December, 1975.

**RICHIE RYAN.**
