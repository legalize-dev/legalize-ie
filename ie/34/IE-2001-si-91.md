# S.I. No. 91/2001 - The Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No. 4) (Amendment) Order 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), and Article 41 of the Foot and Mouth Disease Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0324.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No.4) (Amendment) Order, 2001

###### 2.

2. The Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No.4) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 63 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0063.html) ) is amended by the substitution for the definition of “area” of the following definition —

> “‘area’ means that part of County Louth (other than the area to which the Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No.2) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0062.html) ) applies) that lies northwards of an imaginary line drawn from the mouth of the Fane River to Clermont Bridge and from there onwards to the cross-roads at Carnroe and from there to the cross-roads at Balrobin and from there to the Post Office at Hackballs Cross and from there continued westwards along the whole of the road known as the N 53 to the land frontier of the State;”.

|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal 22 March, 2001 |
|  | Joe Walsh |

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The effect of this Order is to extend the control area provided for in ‘The Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No.4) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 63 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0063.html) )’ to a wider area of Co. Louth.
