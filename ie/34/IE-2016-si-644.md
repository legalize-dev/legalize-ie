# S.I. No. 644/2016 - European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd January*, 2017.

I, MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 48 of the European Communities (Safety of Toys) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0014.html) ), is amended by substituting for paragraph (9) the following paragraph—

> - “(9) A person who contravenes Regulation 35 commits an offence”.

GIVEN under my official seal,

21 December 2016.

**MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations rectify a mistake in a cross-reference in Regulation 48(9) of S.I. 14 of 2011 which transposed Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009 relating to toy safety. Regulation 48(9) had mistakenly referred to Regulation 36 instead of Regulation 35.
