# S.I. No. 388/2018 - Competition Act 2002 (Section 27) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th October*, 2018.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec27) (1) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002), having regard to, and only to, such economic data as I consider to be relevant to the purpose, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Competition Act 2002 (Section 27) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 1 January 2019.

###### 3.

3. Section 18(1)(*a*) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002) is amended—

- (*a*) in subparagraph (i), by the substitution of “€60,000,000” for “€50,000,000”, and

- (*b*) in subparagraph (ii), by the substitution of “€10,000,000” for “€3,000,000”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 October 2018.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Ministerial Order amends [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec18) (1) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (as amended) by altering the monetary amounts standing relating to the obligation to notify certain mergers and acquisitions.
