# S.I. No. 312/1997 - Seanad (University Members) General Election (Special Difficulty) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 312 of 1997.

SEANAD (UNIVERSITY MEMBERS) GENERAL ELECTION (SPECIAL DIFFICULTY) ORDER, 1997.

Whereas it appears to the Minister for the Environment that, by reason of certain interference with the postal services occasioning non-delivery of ballot papers to certain electors, there is a special difficulty in relation to the holding of the Seanad (University Members) General Election, the poll at which shall close on the 6th day of August, 1997, at 11.00 a.m. the Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 164](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0164.html#sec164) (1) of the [Electoral Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Seanad (University Members) General Election (Special Difficulty) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the election" means the Seanad (University Members) General Election, the poll at which shall close on the 6th day of August, 1997 at 11.00 a.m.

###### 3.

3. Notwithstanding [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0030/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Seanad Electoral (University Members) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) and paragraph 2 of the Seanad (University Members) General Election Order, 1997, the returning officer shall, in the case of electors to whom ballot papers were duly issued but not delivered by An Post for the election, send by post on the 15th day of July, 1997 to each such elector a ballot paper together with a form of declaration of identity.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 14th day of July, 1997.

**NOEL DEMPSEY,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the issue of a further set of voting documents in the case of certain electors where the original documents were not delivered.
