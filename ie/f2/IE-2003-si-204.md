# S.I. No. 204/2003 - National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003 (Establishment Day) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec6) of the [National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2003) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003 (Establishment Day) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. The 28^th day of May 2003 is appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2003).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | Given under my Official Seal, this 21^st day of May, 2003. |
|  |  |
|  | John O'Donoghue, |
|  | Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order appoints the 28^th of May to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2003).

The purpose of the [National Tourism Development Authority Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) is to establish a statutory body, the National Tourism Development Authority, to promote tourism and the development of tourism facilities and services in Ireland.
