# S.I. No. 388/1999 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) (Telephone and Postal Facilities) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, CHARLIE McCREEVY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Sections 2 (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/sec0011.html#sec11) (1) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1992), and amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1996) and 3 (as amended by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998)) of [the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1962), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Telephone and Postal Facilities) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2000.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 5 of the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Telephone and Postal Facilities) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 99 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0099.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution:

- (*a*) in paragraph (d)(i)(II) of “1,750” for “1,500”,

- (*b*) in paragraph (d)(ii)(II) of “1,750” for “1,500”, and

- (*c*) in paragraph (d)(iii) of “1,750” for “1,500”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend existing regulations which provide for the provision of telephone and postal facilities to Members of the Oireachtas. From the 1st day of January 2000, a monthly allocation of 1,750 ordinary prepaid envelopes will be available to Members of the Oireachtas.
