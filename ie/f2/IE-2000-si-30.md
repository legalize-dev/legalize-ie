# S.I. No. 30/2000 - Regional Fisheries Boards (Date For Holding of First Elections After Commencement of Section 7 of Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Frank Fahey, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html#sec7) (2) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999), Section 7 of that Act having commenced on 20 December, 1999 and no election of members to any of the regional fisheries boards having been held since that commencement, order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Regional Fisheries Boards (Date for Holding of First Elections after Commencement of Section 7 of Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The 20th day of March, 2000 is by this Order fixed as the day for the holding of the first elections of members to each of the regional fisheries boards after the commencement of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html#sec7) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order fixes the 20th day of March, 2000 as the day for the holding of the first elections of members to each of the regional fisheries boards after the commencement of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html#sec7) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) .
