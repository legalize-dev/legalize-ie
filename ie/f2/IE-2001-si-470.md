# S.I. No. 470/2001 - The Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No. 6) (Revocation) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) and Article 41 of the Foot and Mouth Disease Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0324.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby order as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No. 6) (Revocation) Order, 2001 and shall come into operation on 19^th October, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Foot and Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No. 6) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0195.html) ) is revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order removes (from 19^th October, 2001) certain statutory controls on that area of Co. Louth north of the N 53 in place due to the danger presented by Foot-and-Mouth disease
