# S.I. No. 523/2008 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 523 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0523.html)

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th December*, 2008.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powersconferred on me by Section 4(1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) (No. 2) Regulations 2008.

- (2) The collective citation “the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 2008” shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE

County and City Enterprise Boards as listed in the schedule to the [Industrial Development Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0028/index.html)

Children Acts Advisory Board

Crisis Pregnancy Agency

Health Service Executive

Health Insurance Authority

Health Information and Quality Authority

Health and Social Care Professionals Council

Ombudsman for Children

Office of Tobacco Control

Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland *(new superannuation scheme for employees recruited on or after 22 May 2007)*

Stewarts Hospital *(new superannuation scheme for employees recruited on or after 1 January 2007).*

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 December 2008

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and “approved organisations”, and between one “approved organisation” and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as “approved organisations”.
