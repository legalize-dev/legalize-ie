# S.I. No. 193/1978 - Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978 (Part III) Regulations, 1978

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 193 of 1978.

ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1978 (PART III) REGULATIONS, 1978.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Part III) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The form to be completed under [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , by a designated registered medical practitioner shall be in the form set out in the First Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The Certificate to be issued by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , shall be in the form set out in the Second Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Regulations set out in the Third Schedule to these Regulations are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe for the purposes of Part III of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , the forms to be completed by the medical practitioner and by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety in relation to specimens of blood or urine, and revoke Regulations made under Part V of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .
