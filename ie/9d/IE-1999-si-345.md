# S.I. No. 345/1999 - Investor Compensation Act, 1998 (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Individuals) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/sec0018.html#sec18) (4) of the [Investor Compensation Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998), and with the agreement of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Investor Compensation Act, 1998 (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Individuals) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2(b) of the Investor Compensation Act, 1998 (Section 18(4)) (Prescription of Individuals) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 350 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0350.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for “Ms Rady Redmond” of “Mr. Tony McGrath”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for the substitution for “Ms Rady Redmond” of “Mr. Tony McGrath”.
