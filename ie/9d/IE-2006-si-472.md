> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**S. I. No. 472 of 2006**

National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations 2006

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day Regulations, 2006.

###### 2.

2. The 31^st day of October 2006 is prescribed as an election day for producer members of the National Milk Agency.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, |
|  | 26 July 2006 ***Mary Coughlan*** Minister for Agriculture and Food |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

**The purpose of these Regulations is to fix 31^st October 2006 as an election day for producer representatives to the Board of the National Milk Agency.**
