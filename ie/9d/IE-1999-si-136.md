# S.I. No. 136/1999 - Local Elections (Forms) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the power conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec3) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 1999) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Elections (Forms) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. Each form in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form for the purpose for which such form is expressed to be applicable.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the forms to be used for furnishing statements of election expenses and donation at local elections in accordance with [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec13) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) .

## Footnotes

[^star1]: To be completed by Commissioner for Oaths/Notary Public/Peace Commissioner/Solicitor or a person authorised by law to take and receive statutory declarations.

[^star1-fn_1]: Election expenses include expenditure by candidate and by other persons with his/her authorisation.
