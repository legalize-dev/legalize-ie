# S.I. No. 205/2004 - Marketing of Non-Eec Fertilisers Regulations (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I. Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0008/sec0002.html#sec2) , [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0008/sec0004.html#sec4) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Fertilisers, Feedingstuffs and Mineral Mixtures Act 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1955), after consultation with the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, hereby make the following regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Marketing of Non-EEC Fertilisers Regulations (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Marketing of Non-EEC Fertilisers Regulations 1978 ( [S.I. No. 248 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0248.html) ) are amended

- (a) by inserting, in Regulation 2(1) after the definition of “ground limestone”-

  > “’inhibitor’ means a substance that may be incorporated in a fertiliser which effects the efficacy of the fertiliser;”

- (b) by inserting after Regulation 4-

  > “4A A person shall not sell a fertilizer named in column (1) of Part II to the First Schedule to these Regulations (inserted by the Marketing of Non-EEC Fertilisers (Amendment) Regulations 2004 (S.I. No. of 2004)) that contains an inhibitor, other than an inhibitor named opposite the mention of the fertiliser in column (2) of the said Part II in accordance with the conditions set out in columns (3), (4) and (5) of that Part.”, and

- (c) by inserting, in the Schedule, the matter in the Schedule to these Regulations.
