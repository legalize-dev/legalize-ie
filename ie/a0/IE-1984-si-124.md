# S.I. No. 124/1984 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in Urban Districts of Birr and Carlow) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 124 of 1984.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN URBAN DISTRICTS OF BIRR AND CARLOW) ORDER, 1984.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for Health by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), which said powers are delegated to me by the Health (Delegation of Functions) Order, 1984, I, JOHN DONNELLAN, Minister of State at the Department of Health, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in Urban Districts of Birr and Carlow) Order, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of July, 1984 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the Sanitary Districts specified in the Schedule hereto unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

SCHEDULE.

*Sanitary Districts.*

Urban District of Birr,

Urban District of Carlow.

Dated this 15th day of May, 1984.

JOHN DONNELLAN,

Minister of State at the

Department of Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of July, 1984 the sale of milk in the Sanitary Districts specified in the Order unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
