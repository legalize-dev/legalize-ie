# S.I. No. 209/1972 - Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947 (Transfer of Powers) Order, 1956 (Amendment) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 209 of 1972.

AGRICULTURAL AND FISHERY PRODUCTS (REGULATION OF EXPORT) ACT, 1947 (TRANSFER OF POWERS) ORDER, 1956 (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1972.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1947), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Transfer of Powers) Order, 1956 (Amendment) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Transfer of Powers) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 52 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0052.html) ), is hereby amended by the insertion after " Inedible oils and fats " of " Lambskins and pieces of lambskins ".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 2nd day of August, 1972.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to transfer to the Minister for Industry and Commerce power to make Orders prohibiting the export, except under licence, of lambskins and pieces of lambskins in addition to the commodities specified in the schedule to the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Transfer of Powers) Order, 1956.
