# S.I. No. 462/2015 - Water Services (No. 2) Act 2013 (Other Licences, Authorisations and Permits) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th October*, 2015.

I, ALAN KELLY, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0050/print.html#sec18) (1) of the [Water Services (No. 2) Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0050/print.html) (No. 50 of 2013), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Water Services (No. 2) Act 2013 (Other Licences, Authorisations and Permits) Order 2015.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

> “the Act” is the “ [Water Services (No. 2) Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0050/print.html) (No. 50 of 2013)”.

###### 3.

3. The 30th of October 2015 is appointed for the purposes of section 18 of the Act.

###### 4.

4. From the day referred to in article 3, any authorisation or approval issued to a water services authority under [sections 175](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec175) , 177AE, [179](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec179) and [226](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec226) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) , and which relate to a function transferred to Irish Water by section 7 of the Act, shall continue in force for the unexpired period of the authorisation as if issued to Irish Water.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 October 2015.

**ALAN KELLY,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Statutory Instrument gives effect to the decision of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government to designate that planning authorisations issued under the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) to a water services authority shall continue in force as if granted to Irish Water for the unexpired period of the authorisation.
