S.I. No. 179 of 1994.

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY ACT, 1992 (NOISE) REGULATIONS, 1994.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0006.html#sec6) , [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0011.html#sec11) and [108](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0108.html#sec108) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 127 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0127.html) ), I, JOHN BROWNE, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Noise) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1994.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Act" means the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. An offence in relation to a notice, or to a notice as amended, served by a local authority under section 107 of the Act may be prosecuted summarily by that local authority.

###### 5.

5. A notice for the purpose of sub-section 108 (3) of the Act shall be in the form prescribed in the Schedule hereto and shall contain the particulars specified therein.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

These Regulations prescribe the form of notice to be served under [section 108](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0108.html#sec108) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , by a local authority or a person who intends to make a complaint to the District Court, concerning a person who is alleged to have made, caused or been responsible for noise giving reasonable cause for annoyance.

The Regulations also provide that failure to comply with the requirements of a notice or a notice as amended, issued by a local authority under section 107 of the Act, may be prosecuted summarily by the local authority.
