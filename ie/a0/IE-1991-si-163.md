# S.I. No. 163/1991 - Companies (Fees) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 163 of 1991.

COMPANIES (FEES) ORDER, 1991.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1991.

###### 3.

3. The Eighth Schedule to the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) is hereby altered by the insertion in Part I (as amended by the Companies (Fees) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 237 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0237.html) )) of the following:

> "

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For examining an application for the restoration of, and restoring, a company to the register under section 311A (inserted by [section 246](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0246.html#sec246) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990)) of this Act. | £500 |

> "

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of June, 1991.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to prescribe the fees to be paid in connection with section 311A (inserted by [section 246](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0246.html#sec246) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) ) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) .
