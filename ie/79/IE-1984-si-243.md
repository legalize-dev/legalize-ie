# S.I. No. 243/1984 - State Guarantees Act, 1954 (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 243 of 1984.

STATE GUARANTEES ACT, 1954 (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE) ORDER, 1984.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954). hereby make the following order with respect of which, pursuant to that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1984.

- (2) The [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule Orders, 1954 to 1981, and this Order may be cited together as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1957 to 1984.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), is hereby amended by the addition to the bodies specified in the second column of that Schedule of "Alliance and Dublin Consumers' Gas Company" and by the insertion in the third column of that Schedule of £10,000,000 in respect of that company.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 26th day of June, 1984.

**FRANK MURRAY,**

(Assistant Secretary to the Government, an officer duly

authorised by the Taoiseach, undersection 8 (2) of

the Constitution (Consequential Provision Act,1937,

to authenticate the seal of the Government.)
