**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not a legal part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order designates certain statutory health bodies as State authorities under [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) of the *[National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html)* . This designation enables the delegation to the State Claims Agency of responsibility for the management of clinical negligence claims and risks incurred by the health bodies concerned.
