# S.I. No. 104/1999 - Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926 (Section 3) (Exemption of (i) Arcanet Suitcase Terminals) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, ETAIN DOYLE, Director of Telecommunications Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (6) (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972)) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), and [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0034/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Telecommunications (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1996), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926 (Section 3) (for the exemption of ARCANET Suitcase terminals) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. In this Order—

“CEPT” means the European Postal and Telecommunications Conference;

“ERC” means European Radiocommunications Committee of CEPT;

“ERO” means European Radiocommunications Office of CEPT;

“ETSI” means the European Telecommunications Standards Institute;

“ITU” means the International Telecommunications Union.

“satellite earth station” means an apparatus for wireless telegraphy used for the purpose of carrying on radiocommunications by means of satellites or other space-based systems;

“TBR 27” means Technical Basis for Regulation 27 of December 1997 produced by the Satellite and Systems (SES) Technical Committee of ETSI.

###### 4.

4. The following class of apparatus for wireless telegraphy is hereby declared to be a class of apparatus for wireless telegraphy to which [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), is not to apply, namely, the free circulation and use of ARCANET Suitcase terminals which:

- (*a*) operate on the frequency bands 11.45-11.70 GHz (space-to-earth), and 12.5-12.75 GHz (space-to-earth) and 14.0-14.25 GHz (earth-to-space) under the control of the satellite system providing digital and voice and data communications.

- (*b*) that ARCANET suitcase terminals comply with ETS 300 255 — or TBR 027 where applicable.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the exemption of Arcanet Suitcase terminals from the requirement to be licensed under the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) .
