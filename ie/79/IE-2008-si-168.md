# S.I. No. 168/2008 - Waste Management (Landfill Levy) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 168 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0168.html)

WASTE MANAGEMENT (LANDFILL LEVY) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th June*, 2008.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 7 and 73 of the Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008 hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Waste Management (Landfill Levy) Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. In this order, “Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008” mean the [Waste Management Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1996) as amended by the [Waste Management (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) (No. 36 of 2001), Part 3 of the [Protection of the Environment Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2003), the Waste Management (Electrical and Electronic Equipment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0290.html) ), the Waste Management (Environmental Levy) (Plastic Bag) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0062.html) ) and the Waste Management (Registration of Brokers and Dealers) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0113.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008 shall include this Order and shall be construed together as one Act.

###### 4.

4. Section 73(3) of the Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008 is amended by — the substitution of “€20” for “€19”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 June 2008

**JOHN GORMLEY,**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order amends section 73(3) of the Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008 and provides that the amount of the landfill levy shall not exceed €20.
