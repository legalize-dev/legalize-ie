# S.I. No. 204/2026 - Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th May,* 2026.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) and [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and collective citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2024 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2026.

*Amendment of S.I. No. 182 of 1997*

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) ) are amended in Article 33:

- (a) by the insertion of the following after sub-article (2):-

  > - “(2A) A prohibition imposed by paragraph (b) of sub-article (1) shall not apply to the driver of a bus where traffic sign numbers RVMS 104 (bus priority facility sign) and RRM 042 (bus priority facility line (Continuous Yellow)) are provided and traffic sign number RVMS 104 is displayed.”

- (b) by the insertion of the following after sub-article (4):-

  > - “(5) In this article, “bus” has the meaning assigned to it in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec2) of the [Public Transport Regulation Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2009).
  >
  >   GIVEN under my Official Seal,
  >
  >   13 May, 2026.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Statutory Instrument amends the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to create a targeted exemption for buses from an existing traffic prohibition, where a clearly signed bus priority facility is in place.
