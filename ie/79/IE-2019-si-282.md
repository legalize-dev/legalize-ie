# S.I. No. 282/2019 - Misuse Of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th June,* 2019.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 4, 5 (as amended by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1984), and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec3) of the [Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2016)), [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0018.html#sec18) and [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977), and [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2017 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2017 to 2019 and shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 173 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0173.html) )

“Regulations of 2017” means the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 532 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0532.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Paragraph 1(a) of Schedule 1 (as amended by Regulation 3 of the Regulations of 2017) to the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) by substituting “Cannabinol derivatives, not being dronabinol or its stereoisomers” for “Cannabinol derivatives”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for “Cannabis (not being a preparation specified in paragraph 5 of Schedule 4 Part 1)” the following:

  > “Cannabis (not being a preparation specified in paragraph 5 of Part 1 of Schedule 4 or a preparation or product specified in paragraph 10 of Schedule 2)”.

###### 4.

4. Schedule 2 to the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph 1, by inserting “Dronabinol” after “Dipipanone”, and

- (*b*) by inserting after paragraph 9 the following paragraph:

  > “10. A preparation or product specified in Schedule 1 to the Misuse of Drugs (Prescription and Control of Supply of Cannabis for Medical Use) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 262 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0262.html) ) and permitted for supply pursuant to those Regulations.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 June 2019.

**SIMON HARRIS**

Minister for Health

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2017—

- (a) to insert into Schedule 2 preparations containing dronabinol, and

- (b) to insert into Schedule 2 certain cannabis products or preparations for medical use, which are specified in Schedule 1 to the Misuse of Drugs (Prescription and Control of Supply of Cannabis for Medical Use) Regulations 2019.

These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2019.
