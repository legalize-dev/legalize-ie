# S.I. No. 511/1998 - European Communities (Second General System For The Recognition Of Professional Education And Training) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive No. 97/38/EC[^1] of 20 June, 1997, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Second General System for the Recognition of Professional Education and Training) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations should come into operation on the 14th day of October, 1998.

###### 2.

2. Regulations 2(1) of the European Communities (Second General System for the Recognition of Professional Education and Training) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0135.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for the definition of “the Directive” of the following definition:

“the Directive” means Council Directive No. 92/51/EEC[^2] of 18 June, 1992 (as amended by Commission Directive No. 94/38/EC[^3] of 26 July, 1994, Commission Directive No. 95/43/EC[^4] of 20 July, 1995, and Commission Directive No. 97/38/EC[^1] of 20 June, 1997);

|  |  |

|  | MICHEÁL MARTIN. Minister for Education and Science. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these regulations is to give legal effect to Council Directive 97/38/EC which provides for the amendment of Annex C to Council Directive 92/51/EC which is essentially a list of courses which have a special structure as outlined in Article I (*a*) (ii) of that Directive. Directive 92/51/EC provides for the mutual recognition of sub-degree level qualifications such as diplomas and certificates across the Community. Council Directive 97/38/EC deletes three UK training courses from those listed in Annex C.
