# S.I. No. 330/2016 - Freedom of Information Act 2014 (Amendment of Schedule 3) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st July*, 2016.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec3) of the [Freedom of Information Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2014), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Freedom of Information Act 2014 (Amendment of Schedule 3) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. Part 1 of Schedule 3 to the [Freedom of Information Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2014) is amended by inserting

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “ | No. 28 of 1998 | [National Standards Authority of Ireland Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) | Paragraph 5 of the Second Schedule. | ” |

after the following:

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “ | No. 11 of 1996 | [Harbours Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) | Section 33. | ” |

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 June 2016.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the addition of a reference to the [National Standards Authority of Ireland Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) to Schedule 3 of the [Freedom of Information Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) . This means that the Freedom of Information Act will override the non-disclosure provisions in paragraph 5 of the Second Schedule of the [National Standards Authority of Ireland Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) .
