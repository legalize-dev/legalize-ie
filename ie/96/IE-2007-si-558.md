# S.I. No. 558/2007 - Litter Pollution (Increased Notice Payment) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 558 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0558.html)

LITTER POLLUTION (INCREASED NOTICE PAYMENT) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st July*, 2007.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 14(4) of the [Waste Management (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) (No. 36 of 2001) (as adapted by the Environment and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0233.html) )) hereby orders as follows—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Litter Pollution (Increased Notice Payment) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, “the [Litter Pollution Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) ” means the [Litter Pollution Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1997) as amended by the [Waste Management (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) (No. 36 of 2001) and Part 4 of the [Protection of the Environment Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2003).

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into operation on the 30th September 2007.

###### 4.

4. Section 28 (1) (*b*) of the [Litter Pollution Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) is amended by—

the substitution of “€150” for “€125”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 July 2007

**JOHN GORMLEY**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order amends section 28(1)(*b*) of the [Litter Pollution Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) as amended by Section 14(3) of the [Waste Management (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) and provides that the amount of the on-the-spot litter fine shall be €150.
