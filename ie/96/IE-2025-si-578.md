# S.I. No. 578/2025 - Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) (Amendment) Order 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th December,* 2025.

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Regulation (EC) No. 1099/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 2008[^1] , as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 844/2010 of 20 September 2010[^2] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 147/2013 of 13 February 2013[^3] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 431/2014 of 24 April 2014[^4] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2010 of 9 November 2017[^5] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2146 of 26 November 2019[^6] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/132 of 28 January 2022[^7] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/264 of 17 January 2024[^8] , in so far as it relates to Annex B thereto, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) (Amendment) Order 2025.

###### 2.

2. The Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) Order 2024 ( [S.I. No. 15 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0015.html) ) is amended —

- (a) in Article 2(1), in the definition of “Regulation”, by the substitution of “, Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/132 of 28 January 2022[^7] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/264 of 17 January 2024[^8] ” for “, and Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/132 of 28 January 2022[^7] ”,

- (b) by the substitution of the following Article for Article 3:

  > “3. This Order applies to a person who, or an undertaking that, is classified for statistical purposes under any section of NACE Rev. 2.1 set out in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^9] , as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 295/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008[^10] , Regulation (EU) No. 70/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 January 2012[^11] , Regulation (EU) 2019/1243 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^12] and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/137 of 10 October 2022[^13] .”, and

- (c) in Article 4, by the substitution of “from 2025 to 2027” for “from 2023 to 2027”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 December, 2025.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Taoiseach.
