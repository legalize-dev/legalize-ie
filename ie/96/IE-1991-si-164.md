# S.I. No. 164/1991 - Companies (Fees) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 164 of 1991.

COMPANIES (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) and [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1991.

###### 3.

3. For the purposes of subsections (3) of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990), a fee of 15p per page or part of a page is hereby prescribed for furnishing a copy of an inspector's report under paragraph (*b*) of that subsection, subject to a minimum fee of £5 and a maximum of £20 per copy.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of June, 1991.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe the fees to be paid in connection with [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) .
