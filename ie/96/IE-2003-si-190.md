# S.I. No. 190/2003 - Merchant Shipping (Load Lines) (Exemption) (Amendment) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, DERMOT AHERN, MINISTER FOR Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in the exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0017/sec0019.html#sec19) (2) of the [Merchant Shipping (Load Lines) Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1968) and the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. NO. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) ) and having regard to the International Convention on Load Lines signed in London on 5 April 1966, as amended by the Protocol of 1988, hereby make the following order:-

###### 1.

**1. Citation**

This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Load Lines) (Exemption (Amendment) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 3 of the Merchant Shipping (Load Lines) (Exemption) Order 2002 is amended in paragraph (1) by substituting for subparagraph (f) the following paragraph

> - “(f) vessels in respect of which passenger boat licenses specifying the limits beyond which the vessel shall not ply are in force and which operate solely within those limits”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(THIS NOTE IS NOT PART OF THE INSTRUMENT AND DOES NOT PURPORT TO BE A LEGAL INTERPRETATION)**

This Order amends the Merchant Shipping (Load Lines) (Exemption) Order exempting passenger boats from the provisions of the Load Lines Act.
