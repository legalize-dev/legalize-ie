# S.I. No. 312/2002 - Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) (2) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations 2002.

###### 2.

2. The fee to be charged in respect of an appeal to the Valuation Tribunal under [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec34) (1) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001) of a category mentioned in column (1) of the Table to this Regulation shall be the amount specified in column (2) of that Table opposite the mention of that category.

Table

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Category of valuation of property as determined by the Commissioner of Valuation | Appeal Fee |
| (1) | (2) |
| Not exceeding €50 | €95 |
| Exceeding €50 but not exceeding €150 | €125 |
| Exceeding €150 but not exceeding €650 | €300 |
| Exceeding €650 | €500 |

###### 3.

3. The Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0021.html) ), as amended by the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001), are revoked.
