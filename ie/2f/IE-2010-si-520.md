# S.I. No. 520/2010 - Adoption Act 2010 (Pre-Placement Consultation Procedure) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th November*, 2010.

I, BARRY ANDREWS, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 150](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec150) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) and the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 508 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0508.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Pre-Placement Consultation Procedure) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. These regulations come into operation on 3 November 2010.

###### 3.

3. Each of the forms set out in the Schedule shall be used in the case and manner indicated in the form.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.}*

These regulations set out the forms prescribed by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec17) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) which are to be used in circumstances where an adoption placement is deferred for the purposes of allowing the father or guardian of a child make an application to court under section 6A or section 11(4) of the [Guardianship of Infants Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (as inserted by the [Status of Children Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) ).

## Footnotes

[^1]: Inserted by Section 12 of the Status of Children Act 1987 (No. 26 of 1987).

[^2]: Substituted by Section 13 of that Act.
