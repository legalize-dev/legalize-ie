# S.I. No. 70/2015 - Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd March*, 2015.

I, JOE MCHUGH, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1962), the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (adapted by the Communications, Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0706.html) )), and the Energy and Natural Resources (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 585 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0585.html) ) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2015.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 28 February 2015.

###### 2.

2. The Control of Fishing for Salmon Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 132 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0132.html) ) is amended—

- (*a*) in Article 5—

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of “Subject to paragraph (2A), the maximum number” for “The maximum number”, and

    - (ii) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (2):

      > - “(2A) The maximum number of fishing licences for any kind of fishing engine for the taking of salmon issued in respect of a fishery district in any year shall not exceed the average number of such fishing licences issued for that fishery district in the previous 3 fishing seasons when such licences were issued for that fishery district where the average number is the total number of such licences issued in those 3 fishing seasons divided by 3.”,

- (*b*) in Article 11, by the substitution of “23 March 2015” for “28 March 2014”,

- (*c*) by the substitution of the following Schedule for Schedule 1:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order amends the Control of Fishing for Salmon Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 132 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0132.html) ), authorising the issue of commercial fishing licences by Inland Fisheries Ireland, and prescribes the maximum number of commercial licences which may be issued.
