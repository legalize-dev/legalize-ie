# S.I. No. 336/2016 - European Union (Consumer Information, Cancellation and Other Rights) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st July*, 2016.

I, MARY MITCHELL O‘CONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2011/83/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2011[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Consumer Information, Cancellation and Other Rights) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 10 of the European Union (Consumer Information, Cancellation and Other Rights) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 484 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0484.html) ) is amended, in paragraph (7), by the substitution of the following subparagraph for subparagraph (*a*):

> - “(*a*) shall provide the information specified in paragraphs (*a*), (*b*), (*f*) to (*h*), (*l*) and (*s*) of Schedule 2 on that means of communication in accordance with paragraphs (1) and (2), and”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 June 2016.

**MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend regulation 10(7)(*a*) of the European Union (Consumer Information, Cancellation and Other Rights) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 484 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0484.html) ) which give effect to Directive 2011/83/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council on Consumer Rights.

The purpose of the amendment is to ensure that regulation 10(7)(*a*) of the Regulations gives full effect to Article 8(4) of the Directive.
