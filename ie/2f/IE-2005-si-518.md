STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No. 518 OF 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0518.html)

ORDER

entitled

Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002 (Additional Institutions) Order 2004

I, Mary Hanafin, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) of the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2002), and having caused a draft to be laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft having been passed by each such House, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Additional Institutions) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2002) is amended by the insertion of the institutions specified in the Schedule to this Order.

Explanatory Memorandum

(*This does not form part of the Statutory Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

Section 4(1) of the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) allows the Minister for Education and Science to make an order to provide for the insertion in the *Schedule* of the Act any industrial school, reformatory school, orphanage, children's home, special school which was established for the purpose of providing education services to children with a physical or intellectual disability or a hospital providing medical or psychiatric services to people with a physical or mental disability or mental illness in which children were placed and resident and in respect of which a public body had a regulatory or inspection function.

An order under this section requires a resolution of each House of the Oireachtas to approve of the draft of the order. Dáil Éireann passed a resolution approving the draft order on 19^th October 2004. Seanad Éireann passed a resolution on 20^th October 2004.
