# S.I. No. 147/1987 - Companies (Forms) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 147 of 1987.

COMPANIES (FORMS) ORDER, 1987.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Industry and Commerce in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) and [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/sec0059.html#sec59) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Order, 1987.

- (2) The Companies (Forms) Orders, 1964 to 1983, and this Order may be cited together as the Companies (Forms) Orders, 1964 to 1987.

###### 2.

2. (1) The forms set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, or forms to the like effect, are hereby prescribed as the forms to be used for the purposes of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982), and [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983), with such variations as circumstances require.

- (2) The forms set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order, or forms to the like effect, are hereby prescribed as the forms to be used for the purposes of [section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), with such variations as circumstances require.

- (3) The forms set out in Part III of the Schedule to this Order, or forms to the like effect, are hereby prescribed as the forms to be used for the purposes of section 52 of the said [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , with such variations as circumstances require.

###### 3.

3. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0045.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Form No. 10 in the Schedule thereto of Form B4 set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0256.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Form No. 9b in Part I of the Schedule thereto of Form AI set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 5.

5. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0289.html) ), is hereby amended:

- (*a*) by the substitution for Form No. 41a in Part II of the Schedule thereto of Form AI set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, and

- (*b*) by the substitution for Forms Nos. 84 and 85 in Part II of the Schedule thereto of Form D6 set out in Part III of the Schedule to this Order.
