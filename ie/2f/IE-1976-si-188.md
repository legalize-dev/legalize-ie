S.I. No. 188 of 1976.

ROAD TRAFFIC ACT, 1961 (SECTION 103) (OFFENCES) REGULATIONS, 1976.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [103](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0103.html#sec103) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) as amended by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) and [64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0064.html#sec64) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) and [section 74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0074.html#sec74) of the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations, 1976.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

" the Act " means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. The following offences are hereby declared to be offences to which section 103 of the Act applies:—

- (1) the offence of contravening a bye-law or rule made under section 84 of the Act as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) which prohibits street service vehicles from standing for hire at a place in the area in respect of which the bye-law or rule is made, other than an appointed stand:

- (2) the offence of contravening a bye-law made under section 86 of the Act (as amended by [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0058.html#sec58) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ) which—

( *a* ) prohibits the stopping or parking of an omnibus at a stopping place or stand where roadway markings are provided without bringing the omnibus wholly within the area enclosed by such roadway markings, or

( *b* ) prohibits the stopping or parking of a mechanically propelled pehicle other than an omnibus either wholly or partly within the area enclosed by roadway markings provided at a stopping place or stand;

- (3) the offence of contravening a bye-law or rule made under section 89 or section 90 of the Act (as amended by [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ) which—

( *a* ) prohibits the parking of a mechanically propelled vehicle or a mechanically propelled vehicle other than a mechanically propelled vehicle of a specified class, in a place, at or for a time or in a manner which is prohibited in such bye-law or rule, or

( *b* ) prohibits the parking of a mechanically propelled vehicle in a parking place in which parking is subject to the payment of fees without paying the specified fee or without paying the said fee in the manner and at the time specified, or

( *c* ) prohibits the parking of a mechanically propelled vehicle in a parking place in which parking is subject to the exhibition of a document without exhibiting the document in accordance with such bye-law or rule.

- (4) an offence under [section 73](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0073.html#sec73) of the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. The form in the first Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of subsection (2) and subsection (3) of section 103 of the Act.

###### 5.

5. The prescribed amounts for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of subsection (2) and of paragraph (*b*) of subsection (3) of section 103 of the Act for offences declared by the Minister to be offences to which that section applies shall be—

( *a* ) three pounds in respect of the offence of stopping or parking during a clearway period on a part of a roadway which is a clearway in contravention of a bye-law or rule under section 89 of section 90 of the Act (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ), and

( *b* ) five pounds in respect of an offence under [section 73](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0073.html#sec73) of the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) ,

and

( *c* ) two pounds in respect of all other offences.

###### 6.

6. The Regulations specified in the Second Schedule to these Regulations are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which replace previous Regulations made under the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , declare the offences under that Act and under the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976) to which section 103 of the Act applies.

The Regulations prescribe the form of notice which may be affixed to a vehicle or given to a person by a member of the Garda Síochána or Authorised Person who alleges that such an offence has been committed. The amount which a person who is liable to be prosecuted may pay within 21 days of the date of such notice as an alternative to the institution of a prosecution, is £5 for the offence of non-display of a tax disc, £3 for parking or stopping on a clearway, and £2 for other offences.
