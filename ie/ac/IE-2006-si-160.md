# S.I. No. 160/2006 - Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926 (Section 3) (Exemption of Short Range Devices) (Amendment) Order, 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 160 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0160.html)**

**Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926 (Section 3) (Exemption of Short Range Devices) (Amendment) Order, 2006**

*The Commission for Communications Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by subsection 6 (inserted by section 11(c) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972)) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), transferred to the Commission for Communications Regulation by section 9(1) of the [Communications Regulation Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2002) hereby makes the following Order:*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (Section 3) (Exemption of Short Range Devices) (Amendment) Order, 2006.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2005), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. In this Order -

“Principal Order” means the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (Section 3) (Exemption of Short Range Devices) Order, 2002, ( [S.I. No.405 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0405.html) );

“CEPT” means the European Conference of Postal and Telecommunications Administrations;

###### 4.

4. In the Principal Order the definition of “Document 02/71” is replaced by the following definition:

“Document No. 02/71” means the document entitled “Permitted Short Range Devices in Ireland” published on the 30^th day of July 2002 and any amendment, revision or replacement thereof.

###### 5.

5. The schedule to the Principal Order is deleted.

###### 6.

6. Paragraph 4 of the Principal Order is amended by deleting the words “described in paragraph 1 and 2 of the Schedule to this Order”

and substituting the following:

> “described in Document 02/71”

GIVEN under my hand, this 10^th day of April, 2006

_________________________

Isolde Goggin

Chairperson, Commission for Communications Regulation

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the exemption from licensing of certain short range devices which are described in Document ComReg 02/71, as revised. ComReg 02/71 is available on the ComReg website.
