# S.I. No. 861/2007 - Animal Remedies (Poisons Act 1961) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 861 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0861.html)

ANIMAL REMEDIES (POISONS ACT 1961) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th January*, 2008.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8(5) of the [Animal Remedies Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1993) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )), after consultation with the Animal Remedies Consultative Committee, Comhairle na Nimheanna and the Minister for Health and Children, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Remedies (Poisons Act 1961) Regulations 2007 and come into operation on 1 January 2008.

###### 2.

2. The provisions required by Regulations made under section 14 (as amended by the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) ) and [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0012/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Poisons Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) do not apply to animal remedies.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 December 2007

**MARY COUGHLAN,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide that the provisions of Regulations made under section 14 and [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0012/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Poisons Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) do not apply to animal remedies.
