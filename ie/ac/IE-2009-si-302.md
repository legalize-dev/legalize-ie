# S.I. No. 302/2009 - Companies Act 1963 (Alteration of Eighth Schedule) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 302 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0302.html)

COMPANIES ACT 1963 (ALTERATION OF EIGHTH SCHEDULE) ORDER 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th August*, 2009.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 395 (amended by section 63(1) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (Alteration of Eighth Schedule) Order 2009.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 1st September, 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Eighth Schedule (inserted by the Companies (Fees) (No. 3) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 517 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0517.html) )) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) is altered by substituting the Part set out in the Schedule to this Order for Part 1.

###### 3.

3. The Companies (Fees) (No. 4) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 737 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0737.html) ) and Companies (Fees) Order 2006 ( [S.I. No. 502 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0502.html) ) are revoked.

## SCHEDULE

> ### “PART 1
>
> ‘electronic form’ means in relation to the doing of any act specified in the first column to the table set out in this Part the doing of that act by means of—
>
> - (*a*) the electronic form filing system in use by the registrar of companies, or
>
> - (*b*) the Companies Registration Office disk electronic filing system where the act is also effected in paper form;
>
>   ‘paper form’ means, in relation a matter specified in the first column to the table set out in this Part—
>
> - (*a*) the filing of documents or an application in paper form, or
>
> - (*b*) the inspection of documents or an application submitted in paper form, or
>
> - (*c*) the submission of documents in paper form;
>
>   ‘Societas Europaea’ or European Public Limited Liability Company shall be construed in accordance with the European Communities (European Public Limited Liability Company) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0021.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order alters the Eight Schedule to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) by substituting Part 1 of the Schedule.

The Order revokes the Companies (Fees) (No 4) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 737 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0737.html) and the Companies (Fees) Order 2006 (S. I. No. 502 of 2006).
