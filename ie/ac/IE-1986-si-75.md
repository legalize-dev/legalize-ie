# S.I. No. 75/1986 - Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 75 of 1986.

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY (BUSINESS RADIO LICENCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) The Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) Regulations, 1949 to 1985, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) Regulations, 1949 to 1986.

- (3) The Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Regulations of 1949" means the Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) Regulations, 1949 ( [S.I. No. 320 of 1949](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1949/en/si/0320.html) );

"the Regulations of 1956" means the Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) Regulations, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0002.html) );

"the Regulations of 1957" means the Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) Regulations, 1957 ( [S.I. No. 181 of 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/si/0181.html) );

"the Regulations of 1985" means the Wireless Telegraphy (Business Radio Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0084.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 9 (1) of the Regulations of 1949 (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Regulations of 1956 and amended by Regulation 4 of the Regulations of 1985) is hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution in subparagraph (*a*) of "fourteen pounds and fifty new pence" for "thirteen pounds and fifty new pence" in both places where the words occur;

- (*b*) the substitution in subparagraph (*b*) of "fourteen pounds and fifty new pence", "fourteen pounds and fifty new pence", and "forty-eight pounds and fifty new pence" for "thirteen pounds and fifty new pence," "thirteen pounds and fifty new pence" and "forty-six pounds", respectively;

- (*c*) the substitution in subparagraph (*c*) of "sixty-five pounds and fifty new pence" and "fourteen pounds and fifty new pence" for "sixty-two pounds" and "thirteen pounds and fifty new pence", respectively;

- (*d*) the substitution in subparagraph (*d*) of "sixty-five pounds and fifty new pence", "fourteen pounds and fifty new pence" and "forty-eight pounds and fifty new pence" for "sixty-two pounds", "thirteen pounds and fifty new pence" and "forty-six pounds", respectively;

and the said paragraph (1), as so amended, is set out in the Table to this Regulation.

TABLE

- (1) A fee shall be payable on the issue and on each renewal of a business radio licence and such fee shall be calculated on the following basis:

    - (*a*) system comprising base and mobile stations only or system comprising mobile stations only: a fixed sum of fourteen pounds and fifty new pence plus fourteen pounds and fifty new pence for each station,

    - (*b*) system comprising base, repeater and mobile stations or mobile and repeater stations: a fixed sum of fourteen pounds and fifty new pence plus fourteen pounds and fifty new pence for each base station and each mobile station plus forty-eight pounds and fifty new pence in respect of each repeater station,

    - (*c*) system comprising two fixed stations only, that is to say, a fixed point to point system: a fixed sum of sixty-five pounds and fifty new pence plus fourteen pounds and fifty new pence for each station,

    - (*d*) system comprising two fixed stations with repeater stations, that is to say, a fixed point-to-point system with repeater stations: a fixed sum of sixty-five pounds and fifty new pence plus fourteen pounds and fifty new pence for each fixed station plus forty-eight pounds and fifty new pence in respect of each repeater station.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 7 of the Regulations of 1957 (as amended by Regulation 5 of the Regulations of 1985) is hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution in paragraph (*a*) of "eight pounds and fifty new pence" for "eight pounds" in both places where the words occur;

- (*b*) the substitution in paragraph (*b*) of "eight pounds and fifty new pence" for "eight pounds" in both places where it occurs and of "thirty-two pounds and fifty new pence" for "thirty-one pounds";

- (*c*) the substitution in paragraph (*c*) of "forty-one pounds" and "eight pounds and fifty new pence" for "thirty-nine pounds" and "eight pounds", respectively; and

- (*d*) the substitution in paragraph (*d*) of "forty-one pounds", "eight pounds and fifty new pence" and ` `thirty-two pounds and fifty new pence" for "thirty-nine pounds", "eight pounds" and "thirty-one pounds", respectively;

and the said Regulation 7, as so amended, is set out in the Table to this Regulation.

TABLE

7. A fee shall be payable on the issue and renewal of a licence and such fee shall be calculated on the following basis:

- (*a*) system comprising base and mobile stations only or system comprising mobile stations only: a fixed sum of eight pounds and fifty new pence plus eight pounds and fifty new pence for each station,

- (*b*) system comprising base, repeater and mobile stations or mobile and repeater stations: a fixed sum of eight pounds and fifty new pence plus eight pounds and fifty new pence for each base station and each mobile station plus thirty-two pounds and fifty new pence in respect of each repeater station,

- (*c*) system comprising two fixed stations only, that is to say, a fixed point to point system: a fixed sum of forty-one pounds plus eight pounds and fifty new pence for each station,

- (*d*) system comprising two fixed stations with repeater stations, that is to say, a fixed point-to-point system with repeater stations: a fixed sum of forty-one pounds plus eight pounds and fifty new pence for each fixed station plus thirty-two pounds and fifty new pence in respect of each repeater station.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of March, 1986.

**JIM MITCHELL**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 25th

day of March, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for revised fees for the Business Radio and temporary Business Radio Licences.
