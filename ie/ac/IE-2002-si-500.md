# S.I. No. 500/2002 - Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2001/9/EC[^1] of 12 February 2001, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2002.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2002 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2001 and 2002.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Principal Regulations” means the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (No. 3) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 550 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0550.html) );

“Regulations of 2002” means the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 55 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0055.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Article 2(1) of the Principal Regulations is amended by deleting the definition of “Minister”.

###### 4.

4. Article 11 of the Principal Regulations is amended by inserting after sub-article (1A (inserted by Article 4 of the Regulations of 2002) the following:

> - “(1B) The measurement of the carbon monoxide (CO) content, at low engine idling speed in petrol driven vehicles equipped with on-board diagnostic systems in accordance with Directive 98/69/EC[^2] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 1998, may be determined through the appropriate reading of the on-board diagnostic device and simultaneous checking of the proper functioning of the on-board diagnostic systems.”.

**Explanatory Note**

(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

These Regulations transpose Commission Directive 2001/9/EC regarding the use of on-board diagnostic systems to determine certain emissions in the conduct of roadworthiness testing of certain vehicles.
