# S.I. No. 155/2008 - Commission to Inquire Into Child Abuse Act 2000 (Section 5) (Specified Period) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 155 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0155.html)

COMMISSION TO INQUIRE INTO CHILD ABUSE ACT 2000 (SECTION 5) (SPECIFIED PERIOD) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th May*, 2008.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 5(5)(*b*) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000) and after consultation with the Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (Section 5) (Specified Period) Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. The specified period for the purposes of section 5(5)(*b*) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000) is hereby extended to the 31st day of January, 2009.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

22 May 2008

**BRIAN COWEN.**

Taoiseach.
