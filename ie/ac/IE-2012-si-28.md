# S.I. No. 28/2012 - Oireachtas (Allowances) (Certain Ministers of State) Order 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd February*, 2012.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 3A (inserted by [section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec40) of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Ministerial Provisions) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2001) (“Act of 2001”)) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0008.html#sec8) (as amended by section 43 of the Act of 2001) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Certain Ministers of State) Order 2012.

###### 2.

2. There is payable to—

- (*a*) Paul Kehoe, Minister of State at the Department of the Taoiseach and at the Department of Defence, for any period beginning on or after 9 March 2011, and

- (*b*) Jan O’Sullivan, Minister of State at the Department of Environment, Community and Local Government, for any period beginning on or after 20 December 2011,

as the holders of those offices, respectively, who regularly attend meetings of the Government, an annual allowance each at the rate of €17,205.

###### 3.

3. Article 5 of the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Members and Holders of Parliamentary and Certain Ministerial Offices) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0347.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

31 January 2012.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseasch.
