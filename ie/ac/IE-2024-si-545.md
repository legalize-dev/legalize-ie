# S.I. No. 545/2024 - Electoral Act 1997 (Variation of Monetary Amounts) Order 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd October,* 2024.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(1) (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec7) (a) of the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2011)) of the Electoral Act 1997 (No. 25 of 1997) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 408 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0408.html) )), having regard to change in the consumer price index, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electoral Act 1997 (Variation of Monetary Amounts) Order 2024.

###### 2.

2. In the Electoral Act 1997 (No. 25 of 1997) the monetary amount specified in—

- (a) section 21(1)(b)(i), is varied to an amount of €11,200, and

- (b) section 32(1)(a), is varied to—

    - (i) in subparagraph (i), an amount of €38,900,

    - (ii) in subparagraph (ii), an amount of €48,600, and

    - (iii) in subparagraph (iii), an amount of €58,350.

###### 3.

3. The Electoral Act 1997 (Limitation and Reimbursement of Election Expenses at Dáil Election) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0113.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 October, 2024.

**DARRAH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This order varies, in line with changes in the Consumer Price Index, the maximum amounts of expenditure which may be incurred by or on behalf of a candidate in a Dáil election to €38,900 in a 3-seat constituency; €48,600 in a 4-seat constituency; and €58,350 in a 5-seat constituency. It also provides, in line with the changes in the Consumer Price Index, that the amount of election expenses which may be reimbursed to a candidate at a Dáil election is the actual expenses or €11,200, whichever is the less.
