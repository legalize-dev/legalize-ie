S.I. No. 73 of 1983.

TELEPHONE (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telephone (Amendment) Regulations, 1983.

- (2) The Telephone Regulations, 1980 to 1982, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telephone Regulations, 1980 to 1983.

###### 2.

2. (1) These Regulations in so far as they specify charges for local and trunk calls and charges for special operator services shall come into operation on the 21st day of March, 1983.

- (2) These Regulations in so far as they provide for matters other than those mentioned in paragraph (1) of this Regulation shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) , (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1981" means the Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0294.html) ).

###### 5.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is:—

to provide for the following increases in charges:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Local Calls charge to non-coinbox subscriber | from 8.5p to 9.6p |
| Trunk Calls calls from non-coinbox line | (i) from 8.5p to 9.6p for each specified period of time on subscriber dialled trunk calls; (ii) increase in charges for trunk calls via Operator — internal calls exceeding 80 kilometres from 85p to 90p (day) — Cross Channel calls from 165p to 180p (day) and 110p to 120p (night). |
| Personal Call Fee | from 45p to 50p |
| Advice of Duration and Charge | from 25p to 30p |
| Alarm Clock Calls | from 45p to 50p |
| Reverse Charge Calls | from 45p to 50p |
