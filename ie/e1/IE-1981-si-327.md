# S.I. No. 327/1981 - Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907 (Application of Section 82 To County Health District of Sligo) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JAMES MITCHELL, Minister for Justice, on the application of the council of the county of Sligo and in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1907/en/act/pub/0053/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1907/en/act/pub/0053/index.html) , hereby, the requirements of subsection (2) of that section having been duly complied with, order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907 (Application of section 82 to County Health District of Sligo) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. [Section 82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1907/en/act/pub/0053/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1907/en/act/pub/0053/index.html) is hereby declared to be in force in the County Health District of Sligo.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 12th day of September, 1981. |
|  | JIM MITCHELL, |
|  | Minister for Justice. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Instrument applies [section 82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1907/en/act/pub/0053/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1907/en/act/pub/0053/index.html) , to the County Health District of Sligo, thereby enabling the County Council to make bye-laws under the section for the prevention of danger, obstruction or annoyance to persons using the seashore.
