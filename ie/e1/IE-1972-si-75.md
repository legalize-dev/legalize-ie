# S.I. No. 75/1972 - House To House Collections Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 75 of 1972.

HOUSE TO HOUSE COLLECTIONS ORDER, 1972.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0013/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Street and House to House Collections Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the House to House Collections Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Street and House to House Collections Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , shall apply in relation to collections in the course of house to house visits generally as on and from the 1st day of May, 1972.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of March, 1972.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order applies the [Street and House to House Collections Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , to house to house collections with effect as and from 1st May, 1972.
