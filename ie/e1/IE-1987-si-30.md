S.I. No. 30 of 1987.

CONTROL OF DOGS REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0007.html#sec7) , [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0011.html#sec11) , [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0028.html#sec28) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Control of Dogs Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1986), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Dogs Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Control of Dogs Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. (1) Form No. 1 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of dog licence under the Act.

- (2) Form No. 2 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of general dog licence under the Act.

- (3) Form No. 3 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of declaration for the purpose of section 11 (5) (*b*) of the Act.

- (4) Form No. 4 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purpose of section 28 (1) of the Act.

###### 4.

4. The prescribed amount for the purpose of section 28 (1) of the Act shall be £25.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the form of a dog licence and a general dog licence under the [Control of Dogs Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) . They also prescribe the form of:

- (*a*) the declaration to be made when claiming a dog which has been seized and detained, and

- (*b*) "fine-on-the-spot" notices.

The regulations also fix £25 as the amount to be paid by a person who is alleged to have committed an offence under section 27 of the Act as an alternative to the institution of a prosecution.
