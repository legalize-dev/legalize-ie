# S.I. No. 275/1988 - Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979 (Section 3) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 275 of 1988.

MILK (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1979 (SECTION 3) REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979) (as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0097.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. This Regulations may be cited as the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into force on the 1st day of November, 1988.

###### 3.

3. The rate of fee specified in [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979) is hereby altered by the substitution of "0.1875 new pence" (inserted by the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 343 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0343.html) )).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of October, 1988.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations increase with effect from 1 November 1988, the rate of free payable under [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
