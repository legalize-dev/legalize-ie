# S.I. No. 63/1983 - Local Appointments Commissioners (Collection of Fees) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 63 of 1983.

REGULATIONS ENTITLED LOCAL APPOINTMENTS COMMISSIONERS (COLLECTION OF FEES) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 of the Public Offices Fees Act, 1879, as adapted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1922/en/act/pub/0002/sec0011.html#sec11) (1) of the [Adaptation of Enactments Act, 1922](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1922/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1922), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Appointments Commissioners (Collection of Fees) Regulations, 1983.

- (2) These Regulations shall have effect from the date of coming into operation of the Local Appointments Commissioners (Collection of Fees) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The fees chargeable by the Local Appointments Commissioners under [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0039/sec0010.html#sec10) (1) of the [Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1926), as amended by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0009/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Local Government Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1955), and to be taken in the office of the Local Appointments Commissioners shall be paid in legal tender notes or coins or by means of a banker's draft, money order, postal order or cheque.

###### 4.

4. The Local Appointments Commissioners (Collection of Fees) Regulations, 1927 (S.R. and O., No. 11 of 1927), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 2nd day of March, 1983.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This regulation sets out the method of payment of fees chargeable to candidates for competitions held by the Local Appointments Commissioners.
