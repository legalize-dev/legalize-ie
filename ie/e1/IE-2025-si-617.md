# S.I. No. 617/2025 - Statistics (Labour Costs Survey) (Amendment) Order 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th December,* 2025.

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993) and, for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Regulation (EC) No. 530/1999 of 9 March 1999[^1] , as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 1882/2003 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 September 2003[^2] , Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^3] and Regulation (EC) No. 596/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^4] , Regulation (EC) No. 450/2003 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 February 2003[^5] , as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^6] and Regulation (EC) No. 596/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^7] and Regulation (EC) No. 453/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 April 2008[^8] , hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Labour Costs Survey) (Amendment) Order 2025.

###### 2.

2. The Statistics (Labour Costs Survey) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0113.html) ) is amended -

- (a) in Article 2(1) —

    - (i) in the definition of “Regulation (EC) No. 1726/1999 of 27 July 1999”, by the substitution of “, Commission Regulation (EC) No. 973/2007 of 20 August 2007[^9] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/447 of 7 March 2025[^14] ” for “and Commission Regulation (EC) No. 973/2007 of 20 August 2007[^9] ”, and

    - (ii) in the definition of “Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of 20 December 2006”, by the substitution of “, Regulation (EU) 2019/1243 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^12] and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/137 of 10 October 2022[^13] ” for “and Regulation (EU) 2019/1243 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^12] ”,

- (b) in Article 3 —

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of “section A, U, or V, of NACE Rev. 2.1” for “section A, T, or U, of NACE Rev. 2”, and

    - (ii) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2):

      > - “(2) A requirement on undertakings to which this Article applies to provide, once only, for the purposes of the survey information, the general nature of which is specified in paragraphs 1 to 3 of the Schedule relating to the period commencing on 1 October 2025 and ending on 31 December 2025, is hereby prescribed.”,

      and

- (c) in Article 4 —

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of “section A, U, or V, of NACE Rev. 2.1” for “section A, T, or U, of NACE Rev. 2”, and

    - (ii) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2):

      > - “(2) A requirement on undertakings to which this Article applies to provide, once only, for the purposes of the survey information, the general nature of which is specified in paragraphs 1 and 3 of the Schedule relating to the period commencing on 1 October 2025 and ending on 31 December 2025, is hereby prescribed.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 December, 2025.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Taoiseach.
