S.I. No. 75 of 1997.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1997.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) Scheme, 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "*the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1997*" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Article 4 shall be deemed to come into operation on 1 February 1997.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Second Schedule for the Second Schedule thereof;

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

The purpose of this Scheme is to provide for certain modifications to the Telecommunications Scheme, 1994, to give effect to the rebalancing of leased line charges.
