# S.I. No. 247/2023 - Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences New Zealand and Taiwan) (Amendment) Order 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th May,* 2023.

I, Eamon Ryan, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23A (inserted by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec8) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences — New Zealand and Taiwan) (Amendment) Order 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences — New Zealand and Taiwan) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 402 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0402.html) ) is amended –

- (a) by the insertion of the following Article after Article 1:

  > “1A. In this Order ‘Act of 1961’ means the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961).”,

  and

- (b) by the substitution of the following Article for Article 3:

  > “3. This Order does not apply to –
  >
  > - (a) a licence or permit issued by the competent authority of New Zealand in exchange for a licence or permit issued by a state other than –
  >
  >     - (i) the State,
  >
  >     - (ii) another Member State,
  >
  >     - (iii) the Republic of China (Taiwan), or
  >
  >     - (iv) any other state in respect of which an order under section 23A of the Act of 1961 applies,
  >
  >       and
  >
  > - (b) a licence or permit issued by the competent authority of the Republic of China (Taiwan) in exchange for a licence or permit issued by a state other than –
  >
  >     - (i) the State,
  >
  >     - (ii) another Member State,
  >
  >     - (iii) New Zealand, or
  >
  >     - (iv) any other state in respect of which an order under section 23A of the Act of 1961 applies.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 May, 2023.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Order amends the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences - New Zealand and Taiwan) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 402 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0402.html) ) to allow for the recognition of Australian driving licences for exchange, following the introduction of the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licence) (Australia) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0158.html) ).
