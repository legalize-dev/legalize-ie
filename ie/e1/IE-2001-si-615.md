# S.I. No. 615/2001 - Social Welfare (Liable Relative) (Amendment) (Euro) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4 (as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec9) of the [Social Welfare Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2001)) and 286 (1A) (inserted by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/sec0029.html#sec29) (3) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1997)) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

**Citation and construction.**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Liable Relative) (Amendment) (Euro) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Liable Relative) Regulations, 1999 to 2000 shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Social Welfare (Liable Relative) Regulations, 1999 to 2001.

**Commencement.**

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 January, 2002.

**Amendments consequential on the introduction of the euro.**

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Liable Relative) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0138.html) ) are amended by -

- (a) the substitution in article 5(1) for “£1” of “EUR 1” and for “50p” of “50 cent” in each place where those amounts occur, and

- (b) the substitution in article 5(2) for “£2” of “EUR 2.50”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the conversion of certain monetary amounts, contained in the Liable Relative Regulations, 1999, to convenient Euro amounts. This is in line with the conversion to the new Euro currency with effect from January 2002.
