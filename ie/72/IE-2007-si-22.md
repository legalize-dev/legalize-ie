# S.I. No. 22/2007 - European Communities (European Public Limited-Liability Company) (forms) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), as amended, for the purpose of giving full effect to Council Regulation (EC) No. 2157/2001 of 8 October 2001 [^1] on the Statute for a European company (SE) and the European Communities (European Public Limited-Liability Company) Regulations 2007 hereby make the following regulations:

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in “Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th January*, 2007.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (European Public Limited-Liability Company) (Forms) Regulations 2007.

- (2) The Companies Acts and these Regulations shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into effect on 22nd of January 2007.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

“Act of 1963” means the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963);

“Act of 1982” means the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982);

“Act of 1983” means the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983);

“Act of 1990” means the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No.33 of 1990);

“Act of 1999” means the [Stamp Duties Consolidation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 1999);

“Act of 2001” means the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001);

“Council Regulation” means Council Regulation 2157/2001/EC of 8 October 2001[^1] on the Statute for a European Company;

“European Regulations” mean the European Communities (European Public Limited-Liability Company) Regulations 2007;

“No. 2 Act of 1999” means the [Companies (Amendment)(No. 2) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999);

“Regulations of 2002” means the Companies Act 1990 (Form and Content of Documents delivered to Registrar) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. 39 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0039.html) ).

###### 4.

4. A reference in these Regulations to a numbered Article is a reference to the Article so numbered in the Council Regulation.

###### 5.

5. A word or expression used in these Regulations, which is also used in the European Regulations, has the same meaning as it has in those Regulations.

###### 6.

6. The form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations (SE 1), or a form to like effect, is prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of Articles 2(1) and 25(2) of the Council Regulation, Regulations 4(1)(*a*) and 28 of the European Regulations, section 3 and 3A (inserted by section 101 of the Act of 2001) of the Act of 1982, section 5 of the Act of 1983, sections 42 and 43, section 44 (other than subsection (2)) and section 45 (other than subsections (3)(b) and (5)) of the No. 2 Act of 1999, section 117 of the Act of 1999 and the Regulations of 2002.
