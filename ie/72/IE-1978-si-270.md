# S.I. No. 270/1978 - Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 (Amendment) Regulations, 1978

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 270 of 1978.

HOUSING AUTHORITIES (BORROWING AND MANAGEMENT) REGULATIONS, 1974 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1978.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) , [44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0044.html#sec44) and [90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 (Amendment) Regulations, 1978.

- (2) The Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 276 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0276.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed as one and may be cited as the Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 to 1978.

###### 2.

2. The Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 are hereby amended by the substitution for article 5 of the following article—

"5. For the purposes of section 44 of the Act, the amount of any money borrowed by a housing authority for the provision of building sites, shall be deemed to be the amount of the loan borrowed by the authority for the provision of the building sites, or

( *a* ) where the site is provided prior to the 1st day of August, 1978 an amount equal to the number of sites provided multiplied by £900, or

( *b* ) where the site is provided on or after the 1st day of August, 1978 an amount equal to the number of sites provided multiplied by £1,500

whichever is the lesser."

###### 3.

3. Article 9 and Part I of the Schedule to the Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. ( *a* ) The transfer order for the purposes of section 90 of the Act as modified by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0007/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1978) shall be in the form set out in Part I of the Schedule hereto or in a form substantially to the like effect.

( *b* ) The transfer order for the purposes of section 90 of the Act as extended by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1978) shall be in the form set out in Part II of the Schedule hereto or in a form substantially to the like effect.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations repeal Article 9 and Part I of the Schedule to the Housing Authorities (Borrowing and Management) Regulations, 1974 which prescribe the form of transfer order to be used to effect the sale of local authority dwellings under [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .

A revised form of transfer order providing for the sale of local authority houses in fee simple in accordance with [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0007/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) is prescribed in these Regulations. Provision is also made in the transfer order for the sale of local authority flats by way of lease. In addition, a second form of transfer order is prescribed which will enable owners of houses provided by local authorities to acquire the fee simple in their dwellings in accordance with [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) .

These Regulations also provide for an increase in the cost limit of building sites on which contributions to loan charges may be made by the Minister for the Environment under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .
