# S.I. No. 408/2000 - Copyright and Related Rights (Works of Folklore) (Designated Bodies) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 92](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec92) (4) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Copyright and Related Rights (Works of Folklore) (Designated Bodies) Order, 2000 and shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “the Act” means the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).

###### 3.

3. Any archive under the administration, management or control of a body which is not established or conducted for profit and which maintains an archive of fixations of works of folklore, is designated as a body for the purposes of section 92 of the Act.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

This Order specifies the bodies that are designated for the purposes of [section 92](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec92) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).
