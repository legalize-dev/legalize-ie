S.I. No. 270 of 1974.

BROADCASTING (RECEIVING LICENCES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1974.

I, CONOR CRUISE-O'BRIEN, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), as amended by the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1956), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations, 1974.

- (2) The Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) Regulations, 1961 to 1973, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) Regulations, 1961 to 1974.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of October, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. In the Regulations—

"the Principal Regulations" means the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) Regulations, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0279.html) );

"the Regulations of 1973" means the Broadcasting (Receiving Licences) (Amendment) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 274 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0274.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 7 (1) of the Principal Regulations, inserted by Regulation 4 (*b*) of the Regulations of 1973 is hereby amended by the substitution of "twenty pounds" for "fifteen pounds" and the substitution of "twelve pounds" for "nine pounds".

###### 5.

5. Regulation 14 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2) (inserted by Regulation 4 (*d*) of the Regulations of 1973):—

- "(2) The rebate in respect of a surrendered licence mentioned in paragraph (1) of this Regulation shall be calculated at the rate of—

    - (i) in the case of a licence for which a fee of nine pounds was paid, seventy-five new pence,

    - (ii) in the case of a licence for which a fee of twelve pounds was paid, one pound,

for each complete month or part (if any) of a month for which the licence would otherwise have continued in force from the date of the surrender thereof".

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations increase the television (monochrome only) licence fee from £9 to £12 and increase the television (colour and monochrome) licence fee from £15 to £20 and also provide for a revised rate of rebate where a television (monochrome only) licence is surrendered in certain circumstances.
