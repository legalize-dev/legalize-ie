# S.I. No. 202/2012 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Professional Guidance) (Amendment) Regulations, 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th June*, 2012.

I, JOAN BURTON, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 7A (as amended by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec22) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2012)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Professional Guidance) (Amendment) Regulations, 2012.

- (2) The Occupational Pension Schemes (Professional Guidance) Regulations, 2005 ( [S.I. No. 603 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0603.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Guidance) Regulations, 2005 and 2012.

*Amendments.*

###### 2.

2. The Occupational Pension Schemes (Professional Guidance) Regulations, 2005 ( [S.I. No. 603 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0603.html) ) are amended by substituting the following for Article 2—

> *“Guidance*
>
> 2. The guidance specified for the purposes of section 7A of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) is any guidance issued by—
>
> - (*a*) the Society of Actuaries in Ireland in relation to the calculation of transfer values in accordance with [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ,
>
> - (*b*) the Society of Actuaries in Ireland or the Board in relation to the preparation of actuarial funding certificates and funding standard reserve certificates in accordance with [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0042.html#sec42) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ,
>
> - (*c*) the Board in relation to determining whether or not resources or investments must be included for the purposes of any calculation for the purposes of Part IV of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) in accordance with [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ,
>
> - (*d*) the Society of Actuaries in Ireland or the Board in relation to the certification of funding proposals, or the Board in relation to—
>
>     - (i) the requirements with which a funding proposal shall comply with,
>
>     - (ii) the terms on and the circumstances in which trustees are required to notify the Board of a failure to comply with a term of a funding proposal,
>
>     - (iii) the terms on and circumstances in which a date later than the effective date of the next actuarial funding certificate or next funding standard reserve certificate may be specified by the Board,
>
>     - (iv) the terms on and circumstances in which the trustees are not required to submit a funding proposal, and
>
>     - (v) the terms on and circumstances in which the Board may declare that a funding proposal is no longer a valid funding proposal where there has been a failure to comply with a term of the funding proposal or the trustees of the scheme so request,
>
>       in accordance with [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0049.html#sec49) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , and
>
> - (*e*) the Board in relation to the form by which trustees may apply to the Board for a direction, and the requirements to be met by the trustees in relation to any such application, in accordance with [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 June 2012.

**JOAN BURTON,**

Minister for Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations extend the provisions in the Occupational Pension Schemes (Professional Guidance) Regulations, 2005 to include guidance made by the Pensions Board in accordance with [Section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0042.html#sec42) , [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0047.html#sec47) , [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0049.html#sec49) and [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .
