# S.I. No. 116/2021 - Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th March,* 2021.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage in exercise of powers conferred on me by sections [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec3) , [20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) and [32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec32) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2009), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 19^th April 2021.

*Interpretation*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations –

“the Principal Regulations” means the Social Housing Assessment Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0084.html) )

*Amendment of Regulation 19 (Income Limits) of the Principal Regulations.*

###### 4.

4. Regulation 19(2)(b) is amended by deleting the following:

> “, subject to a maximum increase of 10% under this subparagraph”

*Amendment of Regulation 20 (Income Limits) of the Principal Regulations.*

###### 5.

5. Regulation 20(2)(b) is amended by deleting the following:

> “, subject to a maximum increase of 10% under this subparagraph”

*Amendment of Regulation 21 (Income Limits) of the Principal Regulations.*

###### 6.

6. Regulation 21(2)(b) is amended by deleting the following:

> “, subject to a maximum increase of 10% under this subparagraph”

###### 7.

7. The Schedule to the Principal Regulations is substituted by the following Schedule:

## SCHEDULE

*(Regulation 4)*

APPLICATION FORM FOR SOCIAL HOUSING SUPPORT

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 March, 2021.

**DARRAGH O BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations amend the Social Housing Assessment Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0084.html) ) to-

- • Remove the cap on percentage increases in relation to the income eligibility threshold in respect of each household member aged less than 18 years;

- • Provide for a revised Schedule containing an updated social housing support application form.

The Regulations come into force on 19^th April 2021.
