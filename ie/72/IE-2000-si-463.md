# S.I. No. 463/2000 - Fisheries Acts, 1959 To 2000 (Payment in Lieu of Prosecution) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, FRANK FAHEY, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), section 25 (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec3) (b) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2000)) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999), and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec2) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Fisheries Acts, 1959 to 2000 (Payment in Lieu of Prosecution) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“the Act of 1999” means the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999);

“the Act of 2000” means the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2000).

###### 3.

3. The following bye-laws are hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 2(1)(*b*) of the Act of 2000—

- (*a*) the Tagging of Nets and Numbering of Boats for Salmon or Trout Fishing Bye-law No. 583, 1976,

- (*b*) the Prohibition on the Use of Live Fish as Bait in Fresh Water Bye-law No. 592, 1977,

- (*c*) the Control of Fishing for Coarse Fish in Fresh Water Bye-law No. 595, 1977,

  and

- (*d*) the Conservation of Pike Bye-law No. 667, 1990.

###### 4.

4. It is hereby declared that section 25 (inserted by section 3(*b*) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 applies to the offences under section 24(6) (inserted by section 3(*a*)(i) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 in respect of a contravention of Regulation 4, 7(1), 7(2), 7(3), 7(4), 7(5), 8(6), 10, 11 or 16 of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0256.html) ).

###### 5.

5. It is hereby prescribed that a notice served on a person under section 25(2) (inserted by section 3(*b*) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 or section 2(1) of the Act of 2000 shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations provide for a system of on-the-spot fines in the area of inland fisheries to be administered by the regional fisheries boards. The form of the notice of an on-the-spot fine is set out in the Schedule to the regulations.
