# S.I. No. 131/1979 - Medical Practitioners Act, 1978 (Establishment Day) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 131 of 1979.

MEDICAL PRACTITIONERS ACT, 1978 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER, 1979.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Medical Practitioners Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1978), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Medical Practitioners Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (Establishment Day) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The 26th day of April, 1979 shall be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Medical Practitioners Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 10th day of April, 1979.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to provide that the Medical Council will be established on 26th April, 1979 and assume its functions under the [Medical Practitioners Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) in regard to the registration and control of persons engaged in the practice of medicine and that the Medical Registration Council shall be dissolved on the same date.
