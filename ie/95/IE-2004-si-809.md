# S.I. No. 809/2004 - Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, on the request of the Minister, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment and in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. The powers and duties of the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment conferred on him —

- (*a*) by or under the enactments specified in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order, and

- (*b*) under the instruments specified in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order,

are delegated to Tony Killeen, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order delegates to Tony Killeen, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, the Statutory powers and duties of the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment mentioned in the order.
