# S.I. No. 485/1997 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 8) Scheme. 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Bord Telecom Éireann plc. in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme.

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 8) Scheme, 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation *"the Telecommunications Schemes*, 1994 to 1997" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Articles 4 and 5 shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 October 1997.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition of the following Article 89(A) after Article 89 thereof:

> "89(A)(1) Bord Telecom Éireann pie may provide a facility (the "Facility") to Subscribers whereby Subscribers may receive a discount on the prevailing annual rental charges for leased lines used within Ireland as specified in Part I of the Second Schedule (the "Annual Rental Charges") on committing to the payment of one of the levels of annual rental (each a "Committed Rental Level") specified in Part 3, Section 1 of the Second Schedule hereto for a period of 2,3 or 4 years (each a "Rental Period"). Where a Subscriber wishes to avail of the Facility and chooses a Committed Rental Level and a Rental Period, Bord Telecom Éireann pie shall apply the corresponding percentage discount specified in Part 3, Section II of the Second Schedule to the Annual Rental Charges. Bord Telecom Éireann plc may, at its absolute discretion withdraw the Facility from all or any Subscribers."

- (2) It shall be a condition of the application of the Facility that:

( *a* ) the Subscriber shall pay the Committed Rental Level chosen by it for the Rental Period chosen by it;

( *b* ) where the sum payable by the Subscriber in respect of the use of leased lines within Ireland in any three month period during the Rental Period chosen by it is less than one quarter of the Committed Rental Level chosen by it then the Subscriber shall pay to Bord Telecom Éireann pie one quarter of the corresponding sum as set out in Part 3, Section III of the Second Schedule hereto;

( *c* ) in the event of a Subscriber failing to reach the Committed Rental Level chosen by it in the Rental Period chosen by it due to a price change by Bord Telecom Éireann pie then the Subscriber shall be entitled to choose a revised Committed Rental Level and shall be entitled to the appropriate discount based on the revised Committed Rental Level without penalty for the remainder of the Rental Period chosen by it;

( *d* ) in the event the Subscriber wishes to cease to avail of the Facility prior to the expiry of the Rental Period chosen by it the Subscriber shall be liable to pay to Bord Telecom Éireann pie a sum equivalent to 5% of the corresponding minimum rental level calculated in accordance with Part 3, Section III of the Second Schedule hereto for each remaining year of Rental Period chosen by it;

( *e* ) the Subscriber may increase the Committed Rental Level chosen by it annually on the anniversary of the initial implementation of the Facility on the Subscriber's account. In the event of the Committed Rental Level being so increased, the Subscriber shall be entitled to the appropriate increased discount for the unexpired portion of the Rental Period chosen by it;

( *f* ) in the event of the Annual Rental Charges actually paid by the Subscriber in a Rental Period chosen by the Subscriber exceeding the Committed Rental chosen by the Subscriber the discount shall only be calculated on the Committed Rental Level chosen by the Subscriber.

###### 5.

5. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Second Schedule for the Second Schedule thereto.
