# S.I. No. 168/2009 - Criminal Justice Act 1984 (Electronic Recording of Interviews) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 168 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0168.html)

CRIMINAL JUSTICE ACT 1984 (ELECTRONIC RECORDING OF INTERVIEWS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th May*, 2009.

I, DERMOT AHERN, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0022/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Criminal Justice Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1984) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Criminal Justice Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Electronic Recording of Interviews) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the [Criminal Justice Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Electronic Recording of Interviews) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 74 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0074.html) ) is amended by the insertion of the following after the definition of “the Act of 1996”:

> “ ‘the Act of 2007’ means the [Criminal Justice Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2007);”.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 3 of the [Criminal Justice Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Electronic Recording of Interviews) Regulations 1997 is amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2):

> - “(2) These Regulations shall apply to interviews, including the taking and reading back of statements, that take place in a station with persons who have been detained under—
>
>     - (*a*) section 30 or 30A (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0039/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Offences against the State (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1998)) of the Act of 1939,
>
>     - (*b*) section 4 of the Act of 1984,
>
>     - (*c*) section 2 of the Act of 1996,
>
>     - (*d*) section 2 of the Act of 1996 as modified by section 4(3) of that Act,
>
>     - (*e*) section 50 of the Act of 2007, or
>
>     - (*f*) section 50 of the Act of 2007 as modified by section 51(3) of that Act.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 April 2009

**DERMOT AHERN,**

Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the [Criminal Justice Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Electronic Recording of Interviews) Regulations 1997.
