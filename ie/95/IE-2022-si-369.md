# S.I. No. 369/2022 - European Union (Crowdfunding) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th July,* 2022.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 of the European Parliament and Council of 7 October 2020[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Crowdfunding) (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

*Definition*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Crowdfunding) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 702 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0702.html) ).

*Amendment of Regulation 5 of Principal Regulations*

###### 3.

3. (1) Regulation 5 of the Principal Regulations is amended –

- (a) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2):

  > - “(2) For the purposes of these Regulations and the Crowdfunding Regulation and subject to paragraph (3), responsibility for the information given in a key investment information sheet, attaches to the project owner.”, and

- (b) in paragraphs (3) and (4), by the substitution of “project owner” for “responsible persons” in each place it occurs.

*Amendment of Regulation 6 of Principal Regulations*

###### 4.

4. Regulation 6 of the Principal Regulations is amended –

- (a) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of “project owner” for “persons specified in paragraph (2)”, and

- (b) by the deletion of paragraph (2).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 July, 2022.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
