S.I. No. 233 of 1982.

LITTER REGULATIONS, 1982.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/sec0014.html#sec14) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1982), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Litter Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. (1) Form No. 1 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 5 of the Act.

- (2) Form No. 2 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 8 of the Act.

- (3) Form No. 3 set out in the schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (6) of section 8 of the Act.

- (4) Form No. 4 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of section 11 of the Act.

- (5) Form No. 5 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 12 of the Act.

- (6) Form No. 6 set out in the schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (5) of section 12 of the Act.

- (7) Form No. 7 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (1) (*a*) of section 13 of the Act.

- (8) Form No. 8 set out in the schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purposes of subsection (1) (*b*) of section 13 of the Act.

- (9) Form No. 9 set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of declaration for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 13 of the Act.

###### 4.

4. Moneys accruing to a local authority under the Act shall be disposed of by being lodged—

( *a* ) in case the local authority is the corporation of a county or other borough, or the council of an urban district, to the credit of the municipal fund of the county borough, borough or urban district, or

( *b* ) in case the local authority is the council of a county, to the credit of the county fund of the county.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The provisions of these Regulations relate to procedural matters such as the "fine-on-the-spot" notice for litter offences; notice relating to the removal of abandoned vehicles; notices requiring action to be taken in relation to flyposting, graffiti, slogan-writing and, in particular circumstances, vehicles and certain articles; the disposal of moneys accruing to local authorities and other miscellaneous matters under the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) .
