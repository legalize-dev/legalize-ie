# POSTAL AND TELECOMMUNICATIONS SERVICES (AMENDMENT) ACT 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

An Act to provide for the making available to An Post of additional monies for the purposes of maintaining a network of post offices; and for that purpose to amend the Postal and Telecommunications Services Act 1983.

[20*th July*, 2026]

###### 1. **Amendment of section 29A of Postal and Telecommunications Services Act 1983**

**1.** Section 29A(1) of the Postal and Telecommunications Services Act 1983 is amended by the substitution of “€105,000,000” for “€30,000,000”.

###### 2. **Short title**

**2.** This Act may be cited as the Postal and Telecommunications Services (Amendment) Act 2026.
