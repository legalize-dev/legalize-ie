# S.I. No. 322/1981 - Worker Participation (Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, LIAM KAVANAGH, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Nítrigin éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The 17th day of November, 1981, is in relation to the election to be held in 1981 pursuant to the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977) as regards Nítrigin éireann Teoranta, hereby fixed as the nomination day.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal this 14th day of September, 1981. |
|  | LIAM KAVANAGH, |
|  | Minister for Labour. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations appoint 17th November, 1981 as the day on which the nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in Nítrigin éireann Teoranta shall be received by the returning officer.
