S.I. No. 181 of 1984.

POUNDS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Pounds (Provision and Maintenance) Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1935), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1983, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1984.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Pounds Regulations, 1938 (S.R. & O., No. 149 of 1938), shall have effect, in relation to chattels and animals impounded on or after the 23rd day of July, 1984, as if the following Schedule were substituted for the Second Schedule (as inserted by the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0114.html) )):
