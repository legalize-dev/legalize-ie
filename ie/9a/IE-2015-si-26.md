# S.I. No. 26/2015 - Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th January*, 2015.

I, ALAN KELLY, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsections (1) and (8) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2015.

###### 2.

2. A Referendum Commission is established to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , as amended by the [Referendum Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0053/print.html) , in relation to the proposal for an amendment of the Constitution contained in the Thirty-fourth Amendment of the Constitution (Marriage Equality) Bill 2015.

###### 3.

3. The 27th day of January 2015 is appointed to be the day for the purposes of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) (5) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the chairperson of the Referendum Commission.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 January 2015.

**ALAN KELLY,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order establishes a Referendum Commission for the purposes of the Referendum Acts 1998 and 2001 in connection with the Thirty-fourth Amendment of the Constitution (Marriage Equality) Bill 2015 and appoints 27 January 2015 as the day on which the position of chairperson of the Commission takes effect.
