# S.I. No. 289/2014 - European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th June*, 2014.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec4) of the [European Union Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2009)) of the November 2012[^26] and Commission Implementing Decision 2014/150/EU of 18 March 2014[^27] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Cereal Seed) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0217.html) ) are amended-

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1) by substituting the definition of “Directive” for the following-

  > “‘Directive’ means Council Directive 66/402/EEC of 14 June 1966 as amended by Council Directive 69/60/EEC of 18 February 1969, Council Directive 71/162/EEC of 30 March 1971, Council Directive 72/274/EEC of 20 July 1972, Council Directive 72/418/EEC of 6 December 1972, Council Directive 73/438/EEC of 11 December 1973, Council Directive 75/444/EEC of 26 June 1975, Council Directive 78/55/EEC of 19 December 1977, Council Directive 78/692/EEC of 25 July 1978, Commission Directive 79/641/EEC of 27 June 1979, Council Directive 79/692/EEC of 24 July 1979, Council Directive 86/155/EEC of 22 April 1986, Commission Directive 86/320/EEC of 20 June 1986, Commission Directive 87/120/EEC of 14 January 1987, Council Directive 88/380/EEC of 13 June 1988, Council Directive 90/654/EEC of 4 December 1990, Council Directive 96/72/EC of 18 November 1996, Council Directive 98/95/EC of 14 December 1998, Council Directive 98/96/EC of 14 December 1998, Commission Directive 1999/54/EC of 26 May 1999, Council Directive 2001/64/EC of 31 August 2001, Council Directive 2003/61/EC of 18 June 2003, Council Directive 2004/117/EC of 22 December 2004, Commission Directive 2009/74/EC of 26 June 2009, Commission Implementing Directive 2012/1/EU of 6 January 2012, Commission Implementing Directive 2012/37/EU of 22 November 2012 and Commission Implementing Decision 2014/150/EU of 18 March 2014;”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 21 by deleting paragraphs (9) and (10).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 June 2014.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations update the definition of Directive in the European Union (Cereal Seed) Regulations 2013 and remove the arrest and detention provisions in those Regulations.
