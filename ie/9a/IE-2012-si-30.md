# S.I. No. 30/2012 - Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd February*, 2012.

I, SEÁN SHERLOCK, Minister of State at the Department of Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 81](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0081.html#sec81) , [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0082.html#sec82) and [86](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0086.html#sec86) of the [Trade Marks Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1996), [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0029.html#sec29) , [37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0037.html#sec37) (6), [99](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0099.html#sec99) and [114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0114.html#sec114) of the [Patents Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992) and the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 4) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 241 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0241.html) ) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) )), and in relation to the said section 82 and the said section 99, with the sanction of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2012.

- (2) These Rules come into operation on 1 February 2012.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules “Principal Rules” means the Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) Rules 2001 ( [S.I. No. 482 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0482.html) ).
