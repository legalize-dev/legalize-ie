# S.I. No. 432/2020 - European Union (Prevention and Combating of Human Trafficking) (National Rapporteur) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th October,* 2020.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2011/36/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 April 2011[^1] on preventing and combating trafficking in human beings and protecting its victims, and replacing Council Framework Decision 2002/629/JHA, hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the European Union (Prevention and Combating of Human Trafficking) (National Rapporteur) Regulations 2020.

*Interpretation*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2014” means the [Irish Human Rights and Equality Commission Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2014).

*Amendment of section 10 of Act of 2014*

###### 3.

3. Section 10 of the Act of 2014 is amended in subsection (2) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (i):

> - “(ia) to be, and to carry out the tasks of, the national rapporteur referred to in Article 19 of Directive 2011/36/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 April 2011[^2] on preventing and combating trafficking in human beings and protecting its victims, and replacing Council Framework Decision 2002/629/JHA;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 October, 2020.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
