# S.I. No. 318/1979 - Oil Pollution of the Sea (Amendment) Act, 1977 (Application of Section 2) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 318 of 1979.

OIL POLLUTION OF THE SEA (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1977 (APPLICATION OF SECTION 2) ORDER, 1979.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Oil Pollution of the Sea (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Oil Pollution of the Sea (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (Application of Section 2) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of December, 1979.

###### 3.

3. [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Oil Pollution of the Sea (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1977), shall apply to ships which are registered in—

( *a* ) a country, the government of which has been declared by order made under [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0025/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Oil Pollution of the Sea Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1956), to have accepted the International Convention relating to Intervention on the High Seas in Cases of Oil Pollution Casualties, 1969, or any subsequent convention and has not been subsequently declared to have denounced any such convention, or

( *b* ) a territory to which any such convention has been declared to extend and to which it has not been subsequently declared to have ceased to extend,

when such ships are outside the territorial seas of the State.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 18th day of

September, 1979.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order applies [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Oil Pollution of the Sea (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) to ships registered in a country, which has accepted the International Convention Relating to Intervention on the High Seas in Cases of Oil Pollution Casualties, 1969 when such ships are outside the territorial seas of the State.
