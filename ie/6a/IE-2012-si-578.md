# S.I. No. 578/2012 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) (No. 2) Regulations, 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th January*, 2013.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas)(No. 2) Regulations, 2012.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2011 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2012.

*Prescription of Urban Areas*

###### 2.

2. The areas in the administrative counties of Louth and Westmeath as indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

SCHEDULE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Column 1****Administrative County** | **Column 2****Urban Area** |
| LOUTH COUNTY COUNCIL | The Townland of Loughanmore in the Electoral Division of Jenkinstown.The Townland of Ballydonnell in the Electoral Division of Termonfeckin. The Townland of Tinure in the Electoral Division of Monasterboice and Mullary. |
| WESTMEATH COUNTY COUNCIL | The Townland of Creggan Upper in the Electoral Division of Tubritt. |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government,

20 December 2012.

**PHIL HOGAN TD,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These regulations prescribe areas in counties Louth and Westmeath, as indicated, to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
