# S.I. No. 492/2002 - Road Traffic Act 1961 (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Séamus Brennan, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5 and section 103 (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec11) of the [Road Traffic Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2002)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), [section 74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/sec0074.html#sec74) of the [Finance Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1961 (Section 103 (Offences) Regulations 2002.

- (2) The Regulations of 1999 and these Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1961 (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations 1999 and 2002 and shall be construed as one.

- (3) These Regulations come into operation on the 31^st day of October 2002.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Principal Act” means the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

“Regulations of 1999” means the Road Traffic Act 1961 (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations 1999 ( [S.I. No. 12 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0012.html) ).

###### 3.

3. An offence under section 47 of the Principal Act (as amended by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994)) is declared to be a fixed charge offence.

###### 4.

4. € 80 is prescribed for the purposes of subsections (7)(b) and (8)(b) of section 103 (as inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec11) of the [Road Traffic Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2002)) of the Principal Act.

###### 5.

5. (1) The form of document set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect is prescribed for the purposes of subsection (4)(i) of the said section 103.

- (2) The form of notice set out in Part 2 of the said Schedule or a form substantially to the like effect is prescribed for the purposes of subsection (6) of the said section 103 where the notice is served pursuant to subsection (2)(a) or (5) of the said section 103.

- (3) The form of notice set out in Part 3 of the said Schedule or a form substantially to the like effect is prescribed for the purposes of the said subsection (6) where the notice is served pursuant to subsection (2)(b) of the said section 103.

###### 6.

6. Articles 3(9), 4 (in so far as it relates to Form 2 in the First Schedule to the Regulations of 1999) and 5(d) of the Regulations of 1999 and the said Form 2 are revoked.

**A**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

These regulations declare that the fixed charge system will be applied to the offence of exceeding a speed limit.

The regulations also prescribe the form of the notices and documents to be used where a member of the Garda Síochána alleges that a speeding offence has been committed, the amounts which a person, who is liable to be prosecuted, may pay as an alternative to the institution of a prosecution, details of the penalty points that will be endorsed on the driver's entry in the licence record following payment of a fixed charge and the steps that must be taken by a registered owner to identify the driver where he or she was not using the vehicle at the time of the commission of the alleged offence.
