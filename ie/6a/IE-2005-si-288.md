# S.I. No. 288/2005 - Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) (No. 3) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**Statutory Instruments**

The Houses of the Oireachtas Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 10(4) (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec1) of the [Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2001) and transferred by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec4) (3)(a) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2003)) of the [Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices Act 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) and section 2(1)(c (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1996) and transferred by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec4) (3)(b) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2003)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1962), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) (No.3) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Secretarial Facilities) (No.2) Regulations 2005 shall be amended by substituting “the date of dissolution of the 29^th Dáil” for “30 June 2005”.
