# S.I. No. 304/1982 - Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 304 of 1982.

REGISTRY OF DEEDS (FEES) ORDER, 1982.

I, SEÁN DOHERTY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 35 of the Registry of Deeds (Ireland) Act, 1832, as adapted by the Registry of Deeds (Ireland) Act, 1832 (Adaptation) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 281 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0281.html) ), and section 9 of the Land Transfer (Ireland) Act, 1848, as adapted by the Land Transfer (Ireland) Act, 1848 (Adaptation) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0280.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order,

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1983.

###### 4.

4. In lieu of the fees specified in the Schedule to the Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1981 (S.I. No. 363 of 1981.), the fees specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order shall be charged in the Office of the Registrar of Deeds in respect of the items specified in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of September, 1982.

**SEÁN DOHERTY,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, which comes into operation on the 1st February, 1983, provides for a revised scale of fees to be charged in the Registry of Deeds. This Order replaces the Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1981.
