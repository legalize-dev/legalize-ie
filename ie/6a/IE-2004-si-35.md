# S.I. No. 35/2004 - Courts Service Act 1998 (Second Schedule) (Amendment) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHAEL McDOWELL, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/sec0029.html#sec29) (2) of the [Courts Service Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Courts Service Act 1998 (Second Schedule) (Amendment) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Second Schedule to the [Courts Service Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) is amended by the insertion in the appropriate chronological place of:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No. 48 of 1936 | Courts of Justice Act 1936 | In section 65, subsection (1) the words ‘and the persons by whom and the occasions on which such fees are to be paid and the officers by whom and the manner in which such fees are to be collected’. |
|  |  |  |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal this 23rd day of January, 2004. |
|  | MICHAEL McDOWELL, T.D., Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order transfers responsibility for the collection of Court Fees to the Courts Service by way of amendment to the Second Schedule of the [Courts Service Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) with effect from the 23rd January 2004.
