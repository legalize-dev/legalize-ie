# S.I. No. 337/1983 - Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 337 of 1983.

BEAUMONT HOSPITAL BOARD (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1977 (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1983.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) and [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Health (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1961) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. Sub-article (1) of Article 5 of the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 255 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0255.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "sixteen" for "fourteen".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 9th

day of November, 1983.

**BARRY DESMOND,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to enable the Minister for Health to appoint two additional members to the Beaumont Hospital Board.
