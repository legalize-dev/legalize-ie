# S.I. No. 171/1989 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 171 of 1989.

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 1989.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of the 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1989.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1988, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1989.

###### 2.

2. The organisation specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby designated as an approved organisation for the purposes of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE

Foir Teoranta

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of June, 1989.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and "approved organisations" and between one "approved organisation" and another. These regulations designate the organisation set out in the schedule as an "approved organisation".
