# S.I. No. 31/1982 - Milk and Dairies (Fees For Bacteriological Examination) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 31 of 1982.

MILK AND DAIRIES (FEES FOR BACTERIOLOGICAL EXAMINATION) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by [sections 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) and [50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935) as adapted by the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. & O. 1947 No. 58), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Milk and Dairies (Fees for Bacteriological Examination) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) shall apply to these Regulations as it applies to the interpretation of an Act of the Oireachtas.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1982.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations

"The Principal Regulations" means the Milk and Dairies (Fees for Bacteriological Examination) Regulations, 1936 (S.R. and O. 1937 No. 16);

"the Regulations of 1962" means the Milk and Dairies (Fees for Bacteriological Examination) (Amendment) Regulations, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0233.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Article 3 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the substitution of "two pounds" for "fifteen shillings" (substituted therein by the Regulations of 1962).

###### 6.

6. The Table of Fees in article 4 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution in paragraph (1) of "three pounds" for "one pound" (substituted therein by the Regulations of 1962) after "For each cultural examination";

( *b* ) the substitution in paragraph (2) of "two pounds" for "fifteen shillings" (substituted therein by the Regulations of 1962) after "For each serological examination";

( *c* ) the substitution in paragraph (3) after "for each biological examination" of—

"( *a* ) if two animals are used—ten pounds;

( *b* ) if one animal only is used—six pounds";

for the following (substituted therein by the Regulations of 1962)

"( *a* ) if two animals are used—three pounds;

( *b* ) if one animal only is used—two pounds";

( *d* ) the substitution in paragraph (4) of "two pounds" for "ten shillings" (substituted therein by the Regulations of 1962) after "For each microscopic examination".

###### 7.

7. The Milk and Dairies (Fees for Bacteriological Examination) (Amendment) Regulations, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0233.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 26th day of February, 1982.

**EILEEN DESMOND,**

Minister for Health.

I approve of the fees in the foregoing Regulations

Dated this 26th day of February, 1982.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations provide for increases in the fees payable to bacteriological examiners for the examination and testing of milk samples under the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
