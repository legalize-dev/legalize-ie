# S.I. No. 486/2023 - Local Government Act 1991 (Regional Assemblies) (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th October,* 2023.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) and [43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Local Government Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1991) (as amended by [section 62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec62) of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014)) and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform hereby order as follows:-

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the (Regional Assemblies) (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2023.

*Interpretation*

###### 2.

2. *“Principal Order”* means the [Local Government Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Assemblies) (Establishment) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 573 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0573.html) ).

*Amendment of Principal Order*

###### 3.

3. The Principal Order is amended by inserting after Article 51 the following:

> “*Tenure of Director*
>
> 51A A person who holds the position of director of a regional assembly may seek to have his or her tenure period extended where written notification is given by the director to the Cathaoirleach of the regional assembly concerned.
>
> 51B A director seeking to have his or her tenure period extended under article 51A shall provide written notification of this a minimum of 3 months in advance of the expiration date of his or her contract.
>
> 51C A written notification provided by a director to the Cathaoirleach of a regional assembly prior to this amendment coming into operation shall be deemed as valid for the purpose of Article 51B.
>
> 51D On receipt of notification the Cathaoirleach of the regional assembly concerned shall—
>
> - (*a*) inform the members of the regional assembly of it at the next meeting of that regional assembly, and
>
> - (*b*) transmit a copy of it to the Minister and to the chief executive of the Public Appointments Service.
>
>   51E A regional assembly may, with the approval of the Minister, extend the contract of employment of a director subject to such terms and conditions as may be specified by the Minister with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform.
>
>   51F In no case shall the tenure period of a director’s contract be extended so as to exceed 10 years’ service in total.
>
>   51G In cases where notification is not provided under Articles 51A to 51D then a director’s employment shall cease with effect from the expiration date of his or her contract of employment.”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Order amends the [Local Government Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Assemblies) (Establishment) Order 2014.

This Order provides clarification on the provision for extension of a director’s contract of employment by a regional assembly and sets a maximum period of tenure of 10 years.
