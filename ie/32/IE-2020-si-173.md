# S.I. No. 173/2020 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th May,* 2020.

I, CATHERINE BYRNE, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 4) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 339 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0339.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine and the Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2020.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998);

“Order of 2018” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2018 ( [S.I. No. 568 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0568.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Part II (as amended by Article 3 of the Order of 2018) of the First Schedule to the Act is amended—

- (*a*) by deleting the following statutory instruments:

  > “Radiological Protection Act, 1991 (Ionising Radiation) Order, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0125.html) )”;
  >
  > “European Communities (Food and Feed Hygiene) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 432 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0432.html) ).”, and

- (*b*) by inserting after “European Union (Organic Farming) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 683 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0683.html) ).” the following statutory instruments:

  > “European Communities (Sustainable Use of Pesticides) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 155 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0155.html) ).
  >
  > Radiological Protection Act 1991 (Ionising Radiation) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0030.html) ).”.
