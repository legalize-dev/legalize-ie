# S.I. No. 131/1992 - Companies Act, 1990 (Insider Dealing) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 131 of 1992.

COMPANIES ACT, 1990 (INSIDER DEALING) REGULATIONS, 1992.

WHEREAS difficulties have arisen in relation to the operation of [section 108](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0108.html#sec108) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990);

AND WHEREAS it appears to me to be necessary for removing those difficulties and for facilitating the operation of the said section 108 to make the following Regulations;

NOW, I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 121](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0121.html#sec121) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (Insider Dealing) Regulations, 1992.

###### 2.

2. For the removal of doubt, it is hereby declared that [section 108](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0108.html#sec108) of the [companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990), does not apply to dealing outside the State in securities.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of June, 1992.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations clarify that [section 108](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0108.html#sec108) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990), does not apply to dealing outside the State in securities.
