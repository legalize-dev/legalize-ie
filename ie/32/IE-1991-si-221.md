# S.I. No. 221/1991 - Video Recordings Act, 1989 (Fees) (No. 2) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 221 of 1991.

VIDEO RECORDINGS ACT, 1989 (FEES) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0010.html#sec10) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) (No. 2) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989).

###### 3.

3. A fee of £5 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (3) (*b*) of section 10 of the Act.

###### 4.

4. A fee of £50 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (6) of the said section 10.

###### 5.

5. A fee of £4,000 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 18 of the Act.

###### 6.

6. A fee of £100 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (2) of the said section 18.

###### 7.

7. The [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 38 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0038.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 31st day of July, 1991.

**RAY BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

1st day of August, 1991.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the fees to be charged in relation to appeals under section 10 against prohibition orders and for licences issued under [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
