# S.I. No. 280/1939 - Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1939 (Suspendable Provisions Under Section 3) (Suspending) Order, 1939

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS. 1939. No. 280.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE (CEREALS) ACT, 1939 (SUSPENDABLE PROVISIONS UNDER SECTION 3) (SUSPENDING) ORDER, 1939.

WHEREAS it is enacted by sub-section (1) of [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1939), that at any time while the suspendable provisions are, whether by virtue of an order made under the next following sub-section or otherwise, in force, the Minister for Agriculture may by order (in that section referred to as a suspending order) declare that the suspendable provisions shall cease to be in force as on and from such date as the said Minister thinks fit and specifies in such order :

AND WHEREAS it is enacted by sub-section (5) of the said Section 3 that in that section the expression "the suspendable provisions" means sub-sections (2) and (3) of [Section 62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0007/sec0062.html#sec62) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1933), and Part IV of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1936) :

AND WHEREAS the said suspendable provisions are now in force :—

NOW, I, SÉAMAS O RIAIN, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sub-section (1) of [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1939), and of every and any other power me in this behalf enabling, do hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Suspendable Provisions under Section 3) (Suspending) Order, 1939.

###### 2.

2. Sub-sections (2) and (3) of [Section 62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0007/sec0062.html#sec62) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1933), and Part IV of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1936), shall cease to be in force as on and from the 1st day of September, 1939.

Given under my Seal of Office this 14th day of August, 1939.

- (Signed) SÉAMAS O RIAIN,

Minister for Agriculture.
