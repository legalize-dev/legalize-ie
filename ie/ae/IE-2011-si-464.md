S.I. No. 464 of 2011

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (AMENDMENT TO PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS) REGULATIONS 2011

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th September*, 2011.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 85/337/EEC of 27 June 1985 (as amended by Council Directive 97/11/EC of 3 March 1997 and Directive 2003/35/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 May 2003), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and commencement*

1. (*a*) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Amendment to Planning And Development Regulations) Regulations 2011.

- (*b*) These Regulations shall come into force on the date of signature.

*Interpretation*

2. In these Regulations—

“the Regulations” means the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ) as amended.

*Amendment of Article 8 of the Regulations*

3. Article 8 of the Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in 8B by the substitution of “drainage and/or reclamation of wetlands” for “drainage of wetlands”, and

- (*b*) in 8C by the substitution of “Land reclamation works (other than reclamation of wetlands)” for “Land reclamation works”.

*Amendment of Class 11 of Schedule 2, Part 3 of the Regulations*

4. Column 1 of Class 11 of Schedule 2 Part 3 of the Regulations is substituted by the following:

> “Development consisting of the carrying out of drainage and/or reclamation of wetlands”.

*Amendment of Schedule 5, Part 2 of the Regulations*

5. No. 1(*c*) of Schedule 5 Part 2 of the Regulations is substituted by the following:

> “Development consisting of the carrying out of drainage and/or reclamation of wetlands where more than 2 hectares of wetlands would be affected.”

Given under my Official Seal

12 September 2011.

**PHIL HOGAN, T.D.,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The primary purpose of these Regulations is to amend the provisions of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ), as amended, to include the reclamation of wetlands in articles 8B and 8C, Class 11 of Schedule 2 of Part 3, and Schedule 5 Part 2.
