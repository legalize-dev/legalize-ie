# S.I. No. 35/2018 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th February*, 2018.

I, REGINA DOHERTY T.D., Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) and [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec21) of the [Pensions (Amendment) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2002)) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), and having consulted with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2018.

*Rate of Revaluation of Preserved Benefit*

###### 2.

2. In accordance with Section 33(5) of the Pensions Act, there shall be a 0.4% revaluation of preserved pension benefits for 2017.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 February 2018.

**REGINA DOHERTY TD,**

Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for changes in the percentage by which the amount of a preserved benefit is to be increased or decreased in a specified year.

There shall be a 0.4% revaluation of preserved benefits under [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , for 2017.
