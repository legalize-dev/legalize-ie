# S.I. No. 60/1999 - Jury Districts Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 8th day of March, 1999.

###### 3.

3. Part II of the Schedule to the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ) is hereby amended by the deletion of the reference to the jury district, County of Mayo (Jury District No. 1 (Castlebar) and Jury District No. 2 (Ballina)).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 3rd day of March, 1999. |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE |
|  | Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Statutory Instrument which comes into operation on 8th March, 1999 amends the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ). For the purposes of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) the County of Mayo is now one Jury district.
