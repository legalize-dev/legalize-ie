# S.I. No. 82/1991 - Dublin Harbour Works (South Side) (Amendment) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 82 of 1991.

DUBLIN HARBOUR WORKS (SOUTH SIDE) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1991.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Tánaiste and Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 134](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0134.html#sec134) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946) and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ), on the application of the Dublin Port and Docks Board and after compliance with section 136 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin Harbour Works (South Side) (Amendment) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The Dublin Harbour Works Order (South Side), 1980 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0351.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution in Article 4 of "twenty years" for "ten years".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 3rd day of April, 1991.

**JOHN P. WILSON,**

Tánaiste and Minister for the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order authorises the Dublin Port and Docks Board to extend the time limit on the Dublin Harbour Works Order, (South Side), Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0351.html) ), for a further 10 years.
