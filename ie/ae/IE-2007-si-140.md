# S.I. No. 140/2007 - Oireachtas (Allowances to Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees and Sub-Committees) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**S.I. No.140 of 2007**

**ORDER**

entitled

**Oireachtas (Allowances to Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees and Sub-Committees) Order 2007**

**Made by the Government**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 4 and 8 of the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998 (No. 5 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees and Sub-Committees) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of an Oireachtas Committee specified in column (2) of Part 1 of the Schedule at any reference number, an allowance in respect of the member's duties as holder of that position, at the rate per year specified in column (3) of that Part opposite that reference number, in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 June 2006 during which the member held that position.

###### 3.

3. There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of an Oireachtas sub-committee specified in column (2) of Part 2 of the Schedule at any reference number, an allowance in respect of the member's duties as holder of that position, at the rate per year specified in column (3) of that Part opposite that reference number, in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 May 2006 during which the member held that position.

###### 4.

4. The Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998 (Allowances and Allocations) Order 2003 (S.I. No. 59 of 2003) is amended -

- (a) in Article 5(2), by inserting “and ending with 31 May 2006” after “7 November 2002”, and

- (b) in Article 6(2), by inserting “and ending with 30 April 2006” after “7 November 2002”.
