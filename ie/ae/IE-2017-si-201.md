S.I. No. 201 of 2017

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (PERSONAL INJURIES ASSESSMENT BOARD ACT 2003) 2017

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Personal Injuries Assessment Board Act 2003) 2017, shall come into operation on the 31st day of May 2017.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2017.

- (3) The Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2017 as amended by these Rules may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Circuit Court Rules are amended by the substitution therein for rule 5A of Order 5 of the following rule:

> “5A. In the case of proceedings the bringing of which requires to be authorised in accordance with [sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec14) , [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec17) , [32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec32) , [36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec36) or [49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec49) , or rules under [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec46) (3) of the [Personal Injuries Assessment Board Act, 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html) , the indorsement of claim shall contain a statement
>
> - (*a*) confirming whether or not the proceedings have been authorised by the Personal Injuries Assessment Board,
>
> - (*b*) specifying the section of the [Personal Injuries Assessment Board Act, 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html) or the rule made under section 46(3) of that Act in accordance with which any such authorisation has been issued, and
>
> - (*c*) citing the date of issue of the authorisation and any reference or record number relating to any such authorisation.”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules amend the Circuit Court Rules by the substitution for rule 5A of Order 5, of a new rule including a requirement, in the case of proceedings which require to be authorised in accordance with [sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec14) , [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec17) , [32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec32) , [36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec36) and [49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec49) , or rules under [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html#sec46) (3) of the [Personal Injuries Assessment Board Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0046/print.html) , that the indorsement of claim in relevant proceedings shall contain a statement confirming whether or not the proceedings have been authorised by the Personal Injuries Assessment Board.
