# S.I. No. 7/1984 - Harbour Authorities (Allowances To Members) Rules, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 7 of 1984.

HARBOUR AUTHORITIES (ALLOWANCES TO MEMBERS) RULES, 1984.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) (7) of the [Harbours Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1947), and the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), hereby make the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Harbour Authorities (Allowances to Members) Rules, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The allowance to be paid under paragraph (*a*) or paragraph (*b*) of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) (7) of the [Harbours Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , to a member of a harbour authority in respect of absence from his official residence by reason of attendance at a meeting or meetings to which either such paragraph relates shall with effect as on and from the 1st day of September, 1983 not exceed—

( *a* ) £10.45 in respect of a continuous period of less than seven hours and not less than three hours, and

( *b* ) £14.45 in respect of a continuous period of not less than seven hours.

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule hereto is the form to be used in respect of applications under these Rules.

###### 4.

4. The Harbour Authorities (Allowances to Members) Rules, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 356 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0356.html) ), are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these rules is to provide for increases in the allowances which a harbour authority may pay to its members in respect of attendance at meetings of the authority.
