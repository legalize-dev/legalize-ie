# S.I. No. 602/2020 - Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th December,* 2020.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (d) of [section 122](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec122) (1) (as amended by section 56(a) of the [Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html) (No. 49 of 2015)) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )) and section 59(2) of the Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015, after consulting with the Garda Commissioner and the Authority and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. The Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 470 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0470.html) ) are amended in Regulation 4(b)(i)(I) by the substitution of “an EEA State, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland or the Swiss Confederation,” for “an EEA State or the Swiss Confederation,”.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 December, 2020.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 December, 2020.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice.
