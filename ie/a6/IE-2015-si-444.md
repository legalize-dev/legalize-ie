# S.I. No. 444/2015 - Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th October*, 2015.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 95 (as amended by [section 78](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec78) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2014 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2015.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 181 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0181.html) ) are amended by substituting for articles 19A and 19B (both inserted by the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0330.html) )) the following:

> *“Car club vehicle parking bay*
>
> 19A. Traffic sign number RRM 034 shall—
>
> - (*a*) indicate a car club vehicle parking bay, and
>
> - (*b*) consist of the words ‘Car Club Only’, written on the roadway in white in letters at least 350mm in height.
>
>   *Electrically powered vehicle recharging bay*
>
>   19B. Traffic sign number RRM 035 shall—
>
> - (*a*) indicate an electrically powered vehicle recharging bay, and
>
> - (*b*) consist of the words ‘Electric Vehicle Charging Only’ or ‘EV charging Only’, written on the roadway in white in letters at least 350mm in height.”.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0330.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 October 2015.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide a change in the size of lettering on parking spaces reserved for car club cars and for the recharging of electric vehicles.
