# S.I. No. 134/2016 - Immigration Act 1999 (Deportation) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th March*, 2016.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec7) of the [Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 1999) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 1999 (Deportation) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 10 March 2016.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2005” means the [Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (Deportation) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 55 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0055.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 5 of the Regulations of 2005 is amended by the substitution of “section 5(3)” for “section 5(1)”.

###### 4.

4. The Regulations of 2005 are amended by the substitution of the following for Regulation 7:

> “7. A person who is arrested under subsection (1) or (2) of section 5 of the Act may, for the purposes for his or her detention, in a prescribed place, under that section—
>
> - (*a*) be taken by an immigration officer or a member of the Garda Síochána to a prescribed place, and
>
> - (*b*) be detained, until further notice, in the prescribed place under warrant of the immigration officer or member of the Garda Síochána who arrested him or her.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 March 2016.

**FRANCES FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
