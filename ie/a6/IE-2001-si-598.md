# S.I. No. 598/2001 - Circuit Court (Fees) (No. 3) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Circuit Court (Fees)(No. 3) Order, 2001.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st January, 2002.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

This Order provides for an amendment to the Circuit Court (Fees))(No. 2) Order, 2001 - [S.I. No. 486 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0486.html) as follows:-

With effect from 1 January, 2002 the amount specified in column (2) of item 10 of Part One of the Schedule to the Circuit Court (Fees) (No. 2) Order, 2001 shall be substituted by the amount of €6.00.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This Order amends the Circuit Court (Fees) (No. 2) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 486 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0486.html) ).

The Order provides for amendment to the fees to be charged in Circuit Court Offices with effect from 1st January, 2002.
