# S.I. No. 81/2011 - Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st March*, 2011.

I, CONOR LENIHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec70) and [80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec80) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010), and the Energy and Natural Resources (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 2011 ( [S.I. No. 58 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0058.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2010” means the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that section 70 of the Act of 2010 applies in respect of a contravention of Regulation 3, 4, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22(2), 22(3), 22(4), 22(5), 22(6), 22(7) or 22(8) of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme (No. 2) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 665 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0665.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Schedule 1 is prescribed for the purposes of sections 70(2) and 80(1) of the Act of 2010.

###### 5.

5. The bye-laws specified in Schedule 2 are prescribed for the purposes of paragraphs (*b*) and (*c*) of section 80(1) of the Act of 2010.

###### 6.

6. The prescribed amount of a fixed charge in respect of a fixed charge notice under sections 70 and 80 of the Act of 2010 is €150.

###### 7.

7. The Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0324.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations provide for a system of on-the-spot fines to be administered by Inland Fisheries Ireland, as applies to fisheries legislation. The form of notice of an on-the-spot fine is set out in the Schedule to the Regulations.
