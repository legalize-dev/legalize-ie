# S.I. No. 209/2006 - District Court (Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005) Rules 2006

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[SI No.209 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0209.html)**

**District Court (Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005) Rules 2006**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec24) of the [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005) Rules 2006.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 22^nd day of May 2006 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be amended by the insertion immediately following rule 4 of Order 100 of the following rule:

> *“5. (1) In this rule-*
>
> *the “Act of 2005” means the [Safety Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2005)*.
>
> - *(2) Where an appeal has been brought in the manner prescribed under -*
>
>     - *(a) section 66(7) of the Act of 2005 against an improvement notice,*
>
>     - *(b) section 67(7) of the Act of 2005 against a prohibition notice, or*
>
>     - *(c) section 72(3) of the Act of 2005 against an information notice,*
>
>       *an order of the Court made on such application shall be in the Form 100.3, Schedule D and the applicant shall cause the said order to be served upon the respondent and upon any other person directly affected by the said order*.
>
> - *(3) Where an application has been brought in the manner prescribed under section 67(6) of the Act of 2005 for the suspension of a prohibition notice, an order of the Court made on such application directing that the operation of a prohibition notice be suspended shall be in the Form 100.4, Schedule D and the applicant shall cause the said order to be served upon the respondent and upon any other person directly affected by the said order.”*

###### 4.

4. The Forms in the Schedule hereto shall be added to the Forms in Schedule D to the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ), immediately following Form 100.2.
