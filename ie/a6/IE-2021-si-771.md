# S.I. No. 771/2021 - Aquaculture Licensing Appeals (Fees) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th January,* 2022.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0060.html#sec60) and [63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (1)(b) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )) , and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Aquaculture Licensing Appeals (Fees) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the 8 December 2021

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations –

“Act” means the Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997 (No. 23 of 1997);

“appeal” means an appeal under section 40 of the Act;

“Board” means the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board established under section 22 of the Act.

###### 3.

3. (1) A person making an appeal shall pay a fee to the Board in the amount specified in column 2 of the Schedule opposite the mention in column 1 of the Schedule of the class or description of appeal concerned.

- (2) A person making an appeal shall not be entitled to a refund of any fee paid to the Board in respect of that appeal where the appeal is withdrawn or the provisions of section 54 of the Act apply.

###### 4.

4. (1) A person making a request to the Board for an oral hearing shall pay a fee of €75 to the Board.

- (2) The fee referred to in paragraph (1) shall be payable in addition to any fee payable by virtue of Regulation 3.

- (3) A person referred to in paragraph (1) may, by written notice, withdraw such a request before the Board has decided whether or not to hold an oral hearing of the appeal.

- (4) Withdrawal of a request for an oral hearing shall not give rise to an entitlement to a refund of any fee paid to the Board in relation to such request.

###### 5.

5. (1) The Aquaculture Licensing Appeals (Fees) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 449 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0449.html) ) are revoked.

- (2) Any reference made in an instrument to the Regulations revoked by paragraph (1) shall, from the commencement of these Regulations, be construed as a reference to these Regulations.

- (3) Where, immediately before the commencement of these Regulations, any fee was payable under the Regulations revoked by paragraph (1), such fee remains payable as if those Regulations had not been revoked.

## SCHEDULE Regulation 3

Fees payable to the Board

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Column 1** **Class or description of Appeal** | **Column 2** **Amount of Fee** |
| 1. An appeal by an applicant for a licence against a decision by the Minister in respect of that application. | €380 |
| 2. An appeal by the holder of a licence against the revocation or amendment of that licence by the Minister. | €380 |
| 3. An appeal by a person other than a person referred to elsewhere in this Schedule. | €150 |

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations prescribe the fees payable to the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board in relation to appeals to the Board under [Section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0040.html#sec40) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23) including oral hearings of such appeals, against Ministerial decisions on aquaculture licence applications or to revoke or amend aquaculture licences.
