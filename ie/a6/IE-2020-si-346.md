# S.I. No. 346/2020 - European Arrest Warrant (Application to Third Countries) (Iceland and Norway) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th September,* 2020.

WHEREAS, by the Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Iceland and the Kingdom of Norway on the surrender procedure between the Member States of the European Union and Iceland and Norway, done at Vienna on 28 June 2006[^1] (referred to subsequently in these recitals as “the Agreement”), the European Union entered into an agreement with third countries (being Iceland and Norway) for the surrender of persons wanted for prosecution or punishment;

AND WHEREAS the Agreement, pursuant to Article 38(4) thereof, entered into force on 1 November 2019;

NOW I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Foreign Affairs and Trade, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2(2) of the [European Arrest Warrant (Application to Third Countries and Amendment) and Extradition (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2012), after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the European Arrest Warrant (Application to Third Countries) (Iceland and Norway) Order 2020.

###### 2.

2. The provisions of the [European Arrest Warrant Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2003) that would, apart from [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec2) of the [European Arrest Warrant (Application to Third Countries and Amendment) and Extradition (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2012) and this Order, apply only in relation to Member States shall apply in relation to Iceland and Norway.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 September, 2020.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Foreign Affairs and Trade.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Order applies the provisions of the [European Arrest Warrant Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2003) to Iceland and Norway, in light of the entry into force of the Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Iceland and the Kingdom of Norway on the surrender procedure between the Member States of the European Union and Iceland and Norway.
