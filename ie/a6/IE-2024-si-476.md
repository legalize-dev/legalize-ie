# S.I. No. 476/2024 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th September,* 2024.

I, JACK CHAMBERS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) (amended by the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 477 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0477.html) )) are amended –

- (a) in Regulation 2 –

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “levy period”:

      > “ ‘levy period’ means the period commencing on 1 October 2024 and ending on 30 September 2025.”, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (3), by the substitution of “30 June 2024” for “30 June 2023”,

- (b) in Regulation 3(1), by the substitution of “1 October 2024” for “1 October 2023”,

- (c) in Regulation 4(1), by the substitution of “28 February 2025” for “29 February 2024”, and

- (d) in Column (3) of the Schedule, opposite Reference Number 5, by the substitution of “0 per cent” for “0.012007” percent”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 September, 2024.

**JACK CHAMBERS,**

Minister for Finance.
