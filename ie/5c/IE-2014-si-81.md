# S.I. No. 81/2014 - Harbours Act 1996 (Extension of the pilotage district of the Port of Waterford Company) Order 2006 (Revocation) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th February*, 2014.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) (4) of the [Harbours Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1996)) and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Harbours Act 1996 (Extension of the pilotage district of the Port of Waterford Company) Order 2006 (Revocation) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Harbours Act 1996 (Extension of the pilotage district of the Port of Waterford Company) Order 2006 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0193.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 February 2014.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.
