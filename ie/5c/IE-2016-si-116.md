# S.I. No. 116/2016 - Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th March*, 2016.

I, ENDA KENNY, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993) and, in relation to paragraph 1 of the Schedule to the following Order, for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 1099/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 2008[^1] , as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 431/2014 of 24 April 2014[^2] , in so far as it relates to Annex B thereto, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) Order 2016.

###### 2.

2. (1) In this Order—

“Regulation” means Regulation (EC) No. 1099/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 October 20081, as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 431/2014 of 24 April 2014[^2];

“survey” means the Business Energy Use Survey that is conducted by the Central Statistics Office for the purposes of obtaining information specified in the Schedule, including for the purposes of compliance with the Regulation, in so far as it relates to Annex B thereto.

- (2) A word or expression that is used in this Order and also used in the Regulation has the same meaning in this Order as in the Regulation, other than where the context otherwise requires.

###### 3.

3. This Order applies to an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under any section of NACE Rev. 2 set out in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^3] .

###### 4.

4. A requirement on undertakings to which this Order applies—

- (*a*) subject to subparagraph (*b*), to provide, for the purposes of the survey, information the general nature of which is specified in the Schedule, in relation to each year in the period from 2015 to 2019, and

- (*b*) to provide the information referred to in subparagraph (*a*)—

    - (i) annually in the period from 2017 to 2020 in relation to the preceding year, and

    - (ii) in the case of information relating to 2015, not later than 1 month after a direction under [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993) is given, pursuant to this requirement, to an undertaking to which this Order applies,

is prescribed for the purposes of [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .

###### 5.

5. The Statistics (Business Energy Use Survey) Order 2013 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0065.html) ) is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

*Article 4.*

1. Information regarding the following, as it relates to the business activity of the undertaking concerned, and specifying the year to which the information relates:

- (*a*) quantity of energy product used;

- (*b*) quantity of energy product purchased.

2. Information regarding the following, as it relates to the business activity of the undertaking concerned, and specifying the year to which the information relates:

- (*a*) value of energy product used;

- (*b*) value of energy product purchased;

- (*c*) amount of self-generated electricity sold on the electricity grid by quantity;

- (*d*) amount of self-generated electricity sold on the electricity grid by value;

- (*e*) the air conditioning system;

- (*f*) the heating system.

3. Information on the time taken by the undertaking concerned to complete the questionnaire, form or other record prepared by the Director General for the collection of the information referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 February 2016.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.
