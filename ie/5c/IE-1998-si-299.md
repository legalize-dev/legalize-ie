S.I. No. 299 of 1998.

CORK COUNTY BOROUGH LOCAL ELECTORAL AREAS ORDER, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork County Borough Local Electoral Areas Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. (1) The County Borough of Cork shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area set forth in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of the council of the County Borough of Cork to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set forth in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

- (4) The number of the said members to be elected for each such local electoral area who are to be aldermen shall be the number set forth in the fourth column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division shall be construed as a reference to such electoral division as described in the County Borough of Cork (Wards) Regulations, 1970 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0246.html) ) and therein referred to as wards.

###### 4.

4. The Cork County Borough Electoral Areas Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 111 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0111.html) ) is hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of Cork County Borough into local electoral areas, to fix the number of members to be elected for each electoral area and the number of aldermen to be elected for each electoral area.
