# S.I. No. 250/1988 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 250 of 1988.

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1988.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1987, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1988.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE

Schools, service in which is pensionable in accordance with the Contributory Pension Scheme for Clerical Staff in National and Secondary Schools.

The organisation to which belong persons who—

( *a* ) are secretarial assistants to Members of the Oireachtas (other than such Members who are Ministers of the Government or Ministers of State) and

( *b* ) are eligible for membership of the superannuation scheme for such assistants.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of September, 1988.

RAY MacSHARRY,

Minister for Finance.
