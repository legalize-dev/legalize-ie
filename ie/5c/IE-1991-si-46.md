# S.I. No. 46/1991 - Savings Certificates (Eleventh Issue Extension) Rules, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 46 of 1991.

SAVINGS CERTIFICATES (ELEVENTH ISSUE EXTENSION) RULES, 1991.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/sec0030.html#sec30) (2) of the [Finance Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1940), and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following Rules:

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Savings Certificates (Eleventh Issue Extension) Rules, 1991.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules—

"the eleventh issue" means the issue of savings certificates introduced on the 7th day of March 1986;

"relevant date" means, in relation to a savings certificate, the fifth anniversary of the date marked thereon as the date of issue thereof;

"unit certificate" means a savings certificate of the eleventh issue the purchase price of which was ten pounds.

###### 3.

3. A savings certificate of the eleventh issue as respects which the relevant date occurs while these Rules are in force may be held for a period not exceeding five years from the relevant date.

###### 4.

4. The amount repayable at any time in respect of a savings certificate of the eleventh issue which is held under these Rules shall be—

###### 5.

5. Subject to the provisions of these Rules, the Savings Certificates Rules, 1926, shall apply, with the necessary modifications, in relation to a savings certificate which is held under these Rules.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency,

this 6th day of March, 1991.

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This instrument prescribes the rules governing the extension of the Eleventh Issue of Savings Certificates.
