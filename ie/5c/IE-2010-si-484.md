# S.I. No. 484/2010 - Companies Act 1963 (Ninth Schedule) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th October*, 2010.

I, BILLY KELLEHER, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 250(2) of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0185.html) )), and the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No.334 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0334.html) ), and considering it necessary to do so in the interests of the orderly and proper regulation of the business of unregistered companies, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 1963 (Ninth Schedule) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 5th day of October 2010.

###### 2.

2. The Ninth Schedule (amended by section 250 of, and the Schedule to, the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990)) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 37 of 1963) is amended under the heading PRINCIPAL ACT:

- (*a*) in the column relating to Subject matter—

    - (i) after “Prospectuses and allotments” by the addition of:

      > “Share premium account”; and

    - (ii) after “Share premium account” (inserted by subparagraph (i)), by the addition of:

      > “Reduction of Share Capital”; and

- (*b*) in the column relating to Provisions applied—

    - (i) opposite “Share premium account” (inserted by subparagraph (i) of paragraph (*a*)), by the addition of “Section 62”; and

    - (ii) opposite “Reduction of Share Capital” (inserted by subparagraph (ii) of paragraph (*a*)), by the addition of “Sections 72 to 77”.

GIVEN under my hand,

5 October 2010.

BILLY KELLEHER,

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the Ninth Schedule of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) in order to apply [Sections 62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0062.html#sec62) and [72 to 77](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , which deal with the reduction of share capital, to unregistered companies.
