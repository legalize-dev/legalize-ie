**Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities Regulations 2005**

The Houses of the Oireachtas Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(1)(c) (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 39 of 1996 and transferred by section 4(3)(b) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 28 of 2003)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 32 of 1962), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding the revocation of the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 688 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0688.html) ) and notwithstanding Regulation 4 of those Regulations, the secretarial facilities fund which was payable pursuant to Regulation 3 of those Regulations may be paid after 31 January 2005 to Members of Dáil or Seanad Éireann who, after that date and before 1 July 2005, submitted or submit completed claims in accordance with the Schedule to those Regulations.
