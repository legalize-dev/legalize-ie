# S.I. No. 223/1987 - Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 223 of 1987.

PATENTS, DESIGNS AND TRADE MARKS (INTERNATIONAL ARRANGEMENTS) ORDER, 1987.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) (5) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927) (as adapted by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Executive Powers (Consequential Provisions) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1937)), [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0070.html#sec70) (5) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1963), and [Section 93](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/sec0093.html#sec93) of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1964), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) Order, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. It is hereby declared that the provisions of [Section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927) (as amended by [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0013/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) (Amendment) Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1957)), and [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1963), apply in the case of the foreign states specified in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. It is hereby declared that each country specified in the Schedule to this Order is a convention country for the purposes of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1964).

###### 5.

5. (1) The Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 22 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0022.html) ), shall cease to have effect.

- (2) The Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 316 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0316.html) ), is hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order discharges Ireland's obligations under the International Convention for the protection of Industrial Property in respect of Patent, Design and Trade Mark applications made in the Convention Countries listed in the Schedule.
