# S.I. No. 443/1997 - Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997 (Establishment Day) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 443 of 1997.

ORDER entitled HEPATITIS C COMPENSATION TRIBUNAL ACT, 1997 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER, 1997

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1997) (as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) )), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (Establishment Day) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of November, 1997, is hereby appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1997).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health and Children,

this 30th day of October, 1997.

Brian Cowen______________

Minister for Health and Children
