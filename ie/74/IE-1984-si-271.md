# S.I. No. 271/1984 - Extradition Act, 1965 (Part II) (No. 19) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 271 of 1984.

EXTRADITION ACT, 1965 (PART II) (No. 19) ORDER, 1984.

WHEREAS, the Government are satisfied that reciprocal facilities will be afforded by the Commonwealth of Australia to the State for the surrender by each country to the other of persons wanted for prosecution or punishment;

NOW, the Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 8 (1) of the Extradition Act, 1965 (No. 17 of 1965), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Extradition Act, 1965 (Part II) (No. 19) Order, 1984.

- (2) The collective citation "the Extradition Act, 1965 (Part II) Orders, 1966 to 1984" shall include this Order.

- (3) This Order shall come into operation on the 26th day of October, 1984.

###### 2.

2. Part II of the Extradition Act, 1965 (No. 17 of 1965), shall apply in relation to the Commonwealth of Australia.

###### 3.

3. Extradition may be granted notwithstanding the fact that a person claimed is a citizen of Ireland.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 26th day of October,

1984.

**DERMOT NALLY,**

(Secretary to the Government, an Officer duly authorised by theTaoiseach,under Section 8(2) of the Constitution (Consequential Provisions)Act, 1937, to authenticate the seal of the Government.)
