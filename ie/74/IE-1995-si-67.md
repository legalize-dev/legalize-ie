# S.I. No. 67/1995 - European Communities (Merchandise Road Transport) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 67 of 1995.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (MERCHANDISE ROAD TRANSPORT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, MICHAEL LOWRY, T.D., Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purposes of giving further effect, in so far as road haulage operations are concerned, to Council Directive No. 74/561/EEC of 12 November, 1974^(a), Council Directive No. 77/796/EEC of 12 December, 1977^(b), Council Directive No. 84/647/EEC of 19 December, 1984^(c), Council Directive No. 89/438/EEC of 21 June, 1989^(d), and Council Directive No. 90/398/EEC of 24 July, 1990^(e), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Merchandise Road Transport) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995.

- (2) The European Communities (Merchandise Road Transport) Regulations, 1991 and 1992, and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Merchandise Road Transport) Regulations, 1991 to 1995, and shall be construed together as one.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation—

( *a* ) in the case of Regulation 8 of these Regulations, on the 24th day of March, 1995, in respect of any offence alleged to have been committed on or after that date, and

( *b* ) in the case of any other Regulations, on or after the 24th day of March, 1995, in respect of applications for the grant of a carrier's licence duly received on or after that date.

^(a) O.J. No. L. 308, 19.11.1974, p. 18.

^(b) O.J. No. L. 334, 24.12.1977, p. 37.

^(c) O.J. No. L. 335, 22.12.1984, p. 72.

^(d) O.J. No. L. 212, 22.7.1989, p. 101.

^(e) O.J. No. L. 202, 31.07.1990, p. 46.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the European Communities (Merchandise Road Transport) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 60 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0060.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 5 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (2):

( *a* ) by the substitution in subparagraph (*a*) of "in the form set out in the Second Schedule to these Regulations" for "in the form set out in Part I, II or III, as appropriate, of the Second Schedule to these Regulations", and

( *b* ) by the deletion of subparagraph (*c*).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 7 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (4)—

( *a* ) by the substitution of the following clauses (i) and (ii) of subparagraph (*a*):

- "(i) a relevant offence tried on indictment which is of such a nature that the Minister is of the opinion that the undertaking concerned does not satisfy or does not continue to satisfy, as appropriate, the good repute requirement, or

- (ii) the same relevant offence more than once or a number of relevant offences, which are of such a nature that the Minister is of the opinion that the undertaking concerned does not satisfy, or does not continue to satisfy, as appropriate, the good repute requirement,",

  and

( *b* ) by the substitution of the following clauses for clause (iv) of subparagraph (*b*):

- "(iv) where the undertaking concerned is a partnership, the partners, and

- (v) where the provisions of Regulation 9 (1) (*c*) of these Regulations apply, the transport manager or transport managers concerned.".

###### 5.

5. Regulation 8 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (3) by the substitution of "paragraph 4 of the affidavit" for "paragraph 2 of the affidavit".

###### 6.

6. Regulation 9 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (3)—

( *a* ) by the substitution of "the provisions of paragraph (2) (*d*) of Regulation 5 of these Regulations" for "the provisions of paragraph (2) (other than the provisions of subparagraphs (*a*) and (*b*)) of Regulation 5 of these Regulations", and

( *b* ) by the substitution of "an affidavit in the form set out in the Fifth Schedule to these Regulations" for "affidavits in the forms set out in the Fourth and Fifth Schedules".

###### 7.

7. Regulation 13 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended in paragraph (3) (*a*) by the substitution of "paragraph 4 of the affidavit" for "paragraph 2 of the affidavit".

###### 8.

8. The First Schedule to the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (*w*):

> - "(*ww*) any offence under [section 215](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0215.html#sec215) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993);".

###### 9.

9. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the provisions set out in Schedule A to these Regulations for the Second Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 10.

10. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the provisions set out in Schedule B to these Regulations for the Third Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 11.

11. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the revocation of the Fourth Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 12.

12. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the provisions set out in Schedule C to these Regulations for the Fifth Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Instrument is to amend the format of various affidavits which must be submitted with an application for a Road Freight Carrier's Licence. The Instrument also amends the existing Regulations with regard to good repute.
