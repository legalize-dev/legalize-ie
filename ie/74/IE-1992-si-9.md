# S.I. No. 9/1992 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 9 of 1992.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) REGULATIONS, 1992.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of the powers of the Minister for Finance under [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), delegated to it by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1992.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1992.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on a deposit within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1948), is hereby prescribed as 6.0 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 3) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 258 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0258.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency, this 23rd day of January, 1992.

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**

Chief Executive.
