# S.I. No. 142/1990 - Aliens (Amendment) (No. 3) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 142 of 1990.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) ORDER, 1990.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) (No. 3) Order, 1990.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1990, and this Order may be cited as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1990, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 12th day of June, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) )) to the Aliens Order, 1946 (S. R. & O., No. 395 of 1946), is hereby amended by the insertion of "German Democratic Republic" after "Gambia, The".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 11th day of June, 1990.

**RAY BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides that a citizen of the German Democratic Republic will not require a valid Irish visa for admission to the State.

The Order comes into effect on the 12th June, 1990.
