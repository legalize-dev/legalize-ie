# S.I. No. 36/1973 - Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972. (Form of Notice For The Purpose of Section 2) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 36 of 1973.

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY ACT, 1972. (FORM OF NOTICE FOR THE PURPOSE OF SECTION 2) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (Form of Notice for the Purpose of Section 2) Regulations, 1973.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The notice to be given to the Minister pursuant to [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972), by a television dealer shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.
