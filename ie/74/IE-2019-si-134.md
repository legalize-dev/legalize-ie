# S.I. No. 134/2019 - Planning And Development Act 2000 (Part IIB) (Establishment Day) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th April,* 2019.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 31L (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) of the [Planning and Development (Amendment) Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2018)) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development Act 2000 (Part IIB) (Establishment Day) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. The 3rd day of April 2019 is appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of Part IIB (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) of the [Planning and Development (Amendment) Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2018)) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 April 2019.

**EOGHAN MURPHY**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order sets April 3^rd 2019 as the establishment day of the Office of the Planning Regulator.
