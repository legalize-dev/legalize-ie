# S.I. No. 433/2018 - Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th October*, 2018.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0042.html#sec42) (2) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 5 November 2018.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 681 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0681.html) ) are amended—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Regulations relating to Initial Basic Training to be taken by learner motorcyclists, including the content of courses, the format of certificates issued on completion of courses, and related matters.
