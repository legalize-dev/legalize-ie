S.I. No. 78 of 1997.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) REGULATIONS, 1997.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963) and by section 4 of that Act as amended by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1976), hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1997.

No. 86 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0086.html) ), the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 69 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0069.html) ), the Local Government (Planning and Development) (No. 2) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 75 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0075.html) ) and the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 100 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0100.html) ) may be cited together as the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1994 to 1997.

2 Commencement

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 13th day of February, 1997.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Second Schedule to the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 86 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0086.html) ) which prescribes development which is exempted development for the purposes of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) . The new Regulations provide that, subject to certain limitations, the addition of antennae for mobile telephony to an existing radio mast is exempted development. The replacement of masts is also exempted development provided, inter alia, that the replacement does not exceed the height of the original mast and complies with the other prescribed requirements.
