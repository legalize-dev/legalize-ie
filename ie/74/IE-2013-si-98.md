# S.I. No. 98/2013 - An Bord Bia Act 1994 (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th March*, 2013.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/sec0037.html#sec37) (3) of [An Bord Bia Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1994), (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following Order, with respect to which, pursuant to section 37(4) of the said Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving the draft has been passed by each House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the An Bord Bia Act 1994 (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order 2013 and comes into operation on 1st May 2013.

###### 2.

2. The rate of levy set out in section 37 (2)(*c*) of [An Bord Bia Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (as amended by the An Bord Bia Act 1994 (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order 1996 ( [S.I. No. 90 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0090.html) ) and the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001)) is amended by substituting “35c” for “25c”.

###### 3.

3. The An Bord Bia Act 1994 (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order 1996 ( [S.I. No. 90 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0090.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 March 2013.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The purpose of this Order is to change the Levy collected on Slaughtered or Exported Pigs under the [Bord Bia Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) from 25c to 35c.
