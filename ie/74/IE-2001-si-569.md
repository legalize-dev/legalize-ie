# S.I. No. 569/2001 - Companies (Fees) (No. 2) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Noel Treacy, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade & Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 395 (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade & Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 265 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0265.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) (No. 2) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. Part I of the Eighth Schedule (inserted by the Companies (Fees) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 477 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0477.html) ) to the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963)) is hereby amended by the substitution in the second column for “€3.50” of “€2.50” opposite the entry in the first column that reads “For the provision of an uncertified copy of any matter entered in a register aforesaid”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The purpose of this Order is to set the level of fees to be paid in connection with certain provisions of the Companies Acts, 1963 to 2001, with effect from 1st January, 2002.
