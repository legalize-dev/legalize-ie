# S.I. No. 252/1980 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 252 of 1980.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) ORDER, 1980.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), with the concurrence of the Minister for the Public Service and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards and with the Minister for Finance (being a Minister of the Government whom the Minister for Education considers to be concerned), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1980.

###### 2.

2. Each of the colleges, schools or other educational institutions specified in the Schedule to this Order is hereby designated as being an institution to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) applies.

SCHEDULE.

College of Technology, Bolton Street, Dublin.

College of Technology, Kevin Street, Dublin.

College of Commerce, Rathmines, Dublin.

Dublin College of Catering, Cathal Brugha Street, Dublin.

College of Marketing and Design, 18/19 Parnell Square, Dublin.

Crawford Municipal School of Art, Emmet Place, Cork.

Dún Láoghaire School of Art and Design, Dún Laoghaire.

Limerick Technical College, Upper Cecil Street, Limerick.

Institute of Public Administration, Ballsbridge, Dublin.

Shannon College of Hotel Management, Shannon Airport.

All Hallows College, Drumcondra, Dublin.

Holy Cross College, Clonliffe, Dublin.

St. Patrick's College, Thurles.

Holy Ghost College, Kimmage Manor, Dublin.

College of Industrial Relations, Sandford Road, Dublin.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 1st day of August, 1980.

**JOHN P. WILSON,**

Minister for Education.
