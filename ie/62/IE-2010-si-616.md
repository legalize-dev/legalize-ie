# S.I. No. 616/2010 - European Communities (Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th December*, 2010.

I, BATT O’KEEFFE, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 2008 on the inland transport of dangerous goods[^1] , as amended by Commission Decision 2010/187/EU of 25 March 2010[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. [Section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0043/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1998) is amended by—

- (*a*) substituting the following definition for the definition of “the Annexes”:

  > “ ‘the Annexes’ means Annexes A and B to the ADR referred to in Annex I to Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 20081, as amended by Commission Decision 2010/187/EU of 25 March 20102;
  >
  > and
  >
  > - (*b*) substituting the following definition for the definition of “the Directives”:
  >
  >   “ ‘the Directives’ means Council Directive 95/50/EC of 6 October 1995[^3] and Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 20081, as amended by Commission Decision 2010/187/EU of 25 March 20102;
  >
  >   GIVEN under my Official Seal,
  >
  >   21 December 2010.

**BATT O’KEEFFE,**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the [Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) in order to allow for the transposition of Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 2008 on the inland transport of dangerous goods as amended by Commission Decision 2010/187/EU of 25 March 2010 by means of Regulations made under that Act.
