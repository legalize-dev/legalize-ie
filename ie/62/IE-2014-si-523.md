# S.I. No. 523/2014 - European Communities (Interoperability of the Rail System) Regulations 2011 (Amendment) Regulations 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to give effect to Commission Directive 2014/38/EU of 10 March 2014 which, as far as noise pollution is concerned, amends Annex III to Directive 2008/57/EC on the interoperability of the rail system within the EU. This new Directive amends Point 1.4.4 of Annex III to specify that the design and operation of the rail system must not lead to an inadmissible level of noise generated by it in areas close to railway infrastructure and in the driver’s cab.
