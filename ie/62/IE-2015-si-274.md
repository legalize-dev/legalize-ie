# S.I. No. 274/2015 - European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd July*, 2015.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2014/84/EU of 30 June 2014[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 July 2015.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Safety of Toys) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0014.html) ) are amended, in Regulation 4(1), by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “Toy Safety Directive”:

> “ ‘Toy Safety Directive’ means Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^2] , amended by—
>
> - (*a*) Commission Directive 2012/7/EU of 2 March 2012[^3] ,
>
> - (*b*) Commission Regulation (EU) No. 681/2013 of 17 July 2013[^4] , and
>
> - (*c*) Commission Directive 2014/84/EU of 30 June 2014.[^1]”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 June 2015.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Directive 2014/84/EU of 30 June 2014, which amends (for the purpose of adaptation to technical progress) Appendix A of Annex II to Directive 2009/48/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009 relating to toy safety, by adapting the list of uses for which nickel is permitted in toys and toy components.
