# S.I. No. 577/2012 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th January*, 2013.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2012.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2011 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2012.

*Prescription of Urban Areas.*

###### 2.

2. The areas in the administrative counties of Fingal, Kilkenny, Mayo, Offaly and South Tipperary as indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These regulations prescribe areas in counties Fingal, Kilkenny, Mayo, Offaly and South Tipperary as indicated, to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
