# S.I. No. 829/2007 - Road Traffic (Weight Laden of 5 Axle Articulated Vehicles) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 829 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0829.html)

ROAD TRAFFIC (WEIGHT LADEN OF 5 AXLE ARTICULATED VEHICLES) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th January*, 2008.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 96/53/EC of 25 July 1996 [^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Weight Laden of 5 Axle Articulated Vehicles) Regulations 2007.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 14(2) of the Road Traffic (Construction and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0005.html) ) is amended by substituting “1 January 2009” for “1 January 2008”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 December 2007

**NOEL DEMPSEY**

Minister for Transport

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend Regulation 14(2) of the Road Traffic (Construction and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0005.html) ) to provide that, until 1 January, 2009 the weight laden of a combination of a 2 axle tractor unit and a 3 axle semi-trailer may exceed 40 tonnes but shall not exceed 42 tonnes.
