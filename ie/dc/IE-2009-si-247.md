# S.I. No. 247/2009 - European Communities (Repeal of Section 26(4) of the National Oil Reserves Agency Act 2007) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 247 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0247.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (REPEAL OF SECTION 26(4) OF THE NATIONAL OIL RESERVES AGENCY ACT 2007) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th July*, 2009.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purposes of giving effect to Council Directive 2006/67/EC of 24 July 2006 [^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Repeal of Section 26(4) of the [National Oil Reserves Agency Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) ) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. Section 26(4) of the [National Oil Reserves Agency Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2007) is repealed.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 June 2009

**EAMON RYAN**

Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal Interpretation)*

These Regulations provide for the repeal of Section 26(4) of the [National Oil Reserves Agency Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) to make the Act compatible with Article 3(1) of Council Directive 2006/67/EC of 24 July 2006 imposing an obligation on Member States to maintain minimum stocks of crude oil and/or petroleum products.
