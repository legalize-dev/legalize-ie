# S.I. No. 667/2022 - Immigration Act 2004 (Registration Certificate Fee) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th December,* 2022.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0019/print.html#sec19) (as amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0019/print.html#sec34) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2022)) and [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec20) of the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2004) (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 2004 (Registration Certificate Fee) (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 4 of the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (Registration Certificate Fee) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0444.html) ) is amended-

- (a) in paragraph (k), by the substitution of “in force;” for “in force.”, and

- (b) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (k):

  > - “(l) persons to whom the Minister has granted a residence permission in accordance with the International Protection Regularisation Scheme, which permission is still valid.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 December, 2022.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Justice.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This is not part of the Statutory Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (Registration Certificate Fee) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0444.html) ) to provide that persons who hold a valid residence permission granted in accordance with the International Protection Regularisation Scheme are exempt from paying the fee for obtaining a registration certificate.
