# S.I. No. 275/2014 - Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th June*, 2014.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0010.html#sec10) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0012.html#sec12) , [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0014.html#sec14) , [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0015.html#sec15) and [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Sea Pollution Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1991) and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 October 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Supplement to the International Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate in Schedule 2 to the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 788 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0788.html) ) is amended—

- (*a*) in Form A, by substituting the following for paragraph 3.2.1—

  > “3.2.1 Incinerator for oil residues (sludge) □”, and

- (*b*) in Form B, by substituting the following for paragraph 3.2.1—

  > “3.2.1 Incinerator for oil residues (sludge) □”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 June 2014.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 788 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0788.html) ).

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for an amendment to the form of the Supplement to the International Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate, which is set out in Schedule 2 to the above-mentioned Regulations.
