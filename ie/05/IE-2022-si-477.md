# S.I. No. 477/2022 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th September,* 2022.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) (amended by the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 481 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0481.html) )) are amended -

- (a) in Regulation 2 -

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “levy period”:

      > “ ‘levy period’ means the period commencing on 1 October 2022 and ending on 30 September 2023.”, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (3), by the substitution of “30 June 2022” for “30 June 2021”,

- (b) in Regulation 3(1), by the substitution of “1 October 2022” for “1 October 2021”,

- (c) in Regulation 4(1), by the substitution of “28 February 2023” for “28

February 2022”, and

- (d) in Column (3) of the Schedule, opposite Reference Number 5, by the substitution of “0.024725 per cent” for “0.024914 per cent”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 September, 2022.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
