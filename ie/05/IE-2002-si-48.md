# S.I. No. 48/2002 - Firearm Certificates For Non-Residents Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec2) (3) of the [Firearms (Firearm Certificates for Non-Residents) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2000) and having consulted with the Commissioner of the Garda Síochána, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Firearm Certificates for Non-Residents Order 2002.

###### 2.

2. The period specified in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec2) (2)(a)(i) of the [Firearms (Firearm Certificates for Non-Residents) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2000) is reduced so as to end on 28 February 2002.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This Order provides for the transfer to the Garda Síochána from the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform with effect from the 1 March, 2002 of the power to grant firearm certificates to non-residents under the provisions of the [Firearms (Firearm Certificates for Non-Residents) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) .
