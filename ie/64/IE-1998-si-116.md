# S.I. No. 116/1998 - Freedom of Information Act, 1997 -Delegation Under Section 4

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 116 of 1998.

FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT, 1997 — DELEGATION UNDER SECTION 4.

I, MICHAEL WOODS, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources and head of the Department of the Marine and Natural Resources within the meaning of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) , hereby delegate under section 4 of that Act:

to officers in the grades specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1, the functions specified in Part 2 of Schedule 1

and

to officers in the grades specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2, the functions specified in Part 2 of Schedule 2.

For the purposes of this delegation and the avoidance of doubt officers who are in receipt of a higher duties allowance for performing the duties of a grade specified in Schedule 1 or Schedule 2 of this Order are deemed to be officers in that grade.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of April, 1998.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

SCHEDULE 1

## PART 1

- (i) each officer not lower than the grade of Higher Executive Officer;

- (ii) each officer in grades in respect of which the maximum salary is not less than that of Higher Executive Officer.

## PART 2

All functions other than those under Section 14.

SCHEDULE 2

## PART 1

- (i) each officer not lower than the grade of Assistant Principal officer;

- (ii) each officer in grades in respect of which the maximum salary is not less than that of Assistant Principal officer.

## PART 2

All functions.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The order delegates to officers serving in specified grades in the Department of the Marine and Natural Resources decision making functions (as contained in [Section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ) and internal review functions (as contained in Section 14 of the Act).
