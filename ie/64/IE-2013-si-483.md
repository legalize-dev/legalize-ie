# S.I. No. 483/2013 - Irish Aviation Authority Act 1993 (Amendment of Schedule) (Annexe 19 to Chicago Convention) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th December*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0058.html#sec58) (2) of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )) and after consultation with the Irish Aviation Authority in accordance with section 5(4) of that Act hereby make the following order, a draft of which has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas in accordance with section 5(7) of that Act and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House: hereby order the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Irish Aviation Authority Act 1993 (Amendment of Schedule) (Annexe 19 to Chicago Convention) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule (as amended by the Irish Aviation Authority Act 1993 (Amendment of Schedule) Order 1995 ( [S.I. No. 171 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0171.html) )) to the [Irish Aviation Authority Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993) is amended by inserting the following entry:

> “Annexe 19 — Safety Management”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 November 2013.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.
