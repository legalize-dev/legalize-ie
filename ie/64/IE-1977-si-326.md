# S.I. No. 326/1977 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 326 of 1977.

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT, 1966 (FIRST SCHEDULE) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1977.

I, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , (No. 6 of 1966) as adapted by the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1977.

- (2) The [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1975, and this Order may be cited together as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1977.

###### 2.

2. For the purpose of extending the application of [sections 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0012.html#sec12) , [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) , [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0014.html#sec14) , [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0015.html#sec15) , [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0017.html#sec17) , [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0018.html#sec18) , [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0027.html#sec27) , [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0042.html#sec42) , [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0045.html#sec45) , [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0047.html#sec47) and [58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0058.html#sec58) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), the First Schedule to that Act (as amended by the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1975) is hereby amended by the insertion in Part III thereof at the end of Class A of the following :

> "22. Bovine Leukosis".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of October, 1977.

**JAMES GIBBONS,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order extends the definition of "disease" in the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) to include Bovine Leukosis.
