# S.I. No. 95/2002 - The Postal Order (Inland) Amendment (No. 26) Scheme 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

An Post, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Section 23 of the Post Office Act, 1908 and [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , hereby makes the following Scheme:-

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Postal Order (Inland) Amendment (No. 26 Scheme 2002.

###### 2.

2. Unless otherwise specified, reference to regulations followed by a number refers to the relevant regulation of this scheme.

***Commencement***

###### 3.

3. These regulations shall come into operation on the 1^st day of January 2002 and on and from that date the regulations mentioned in Schedule One hereto shall be and the same are hereby repealed, provided that the repeal of the said regulations shall not:

- (1) Revise anything not in force or existing at the time at which the repeal takes effect.

- (2) Affect anything done or suffered before these regulations came into operation.
