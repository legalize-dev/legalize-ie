# S.I. No. 332/1986 - State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984 (Section 2 (2)) (Cesser) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 332 of 1986.

STATE FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, 1984 (SECTION 2 (2)) (CESSER) ORDER, 1986.

I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Finance, having ceased to be satisfied that, owing to special circumstances of a temporary nature, it is not possible to operate the Exchequer Account in accordance with the provisions of the Exchequer and Audit Departments Act, 1866, or to operate certain State accounts or to carry out certain other financial transactions on behalf of the State, hereby, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 (1) (*b*) of the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1984), order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 2 (2)) (Cesser) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The 17th day of October, 1986, is hereby appointed as the day on which subsection (2) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1984), shall cease to be in force.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 17th day of October, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order specifies the date on which [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) of the [State Financial Transactions (Special Provisions) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , ceases to be in force.
