# S.I. No. 98/2010 - National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh (Closed) Pension Scheme 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th March*, 2010.

National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh, with the approval of An tÚdarás um Ard-Oideachas with the consent of the Minister for Education and Science and the Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 25(7) of the [Universities Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1997), hereby make the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh (Closed) Pension Scheme 2010.

###### 2.

2. In this Scheme—

“An tÚdarás um Ard-Oideachas” means the body established by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Higher Education Authority Act 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1971);

“resolution” means a resolution made by the governing authority of National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh;

“statute” means the superannuation provisions of a statute made by the governing authority of National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh under the Irish Universities Act 1908 (8 Edw. 7. c. 38.); and

“National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh” means the National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh established by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0024/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Universities Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1997).

###### 3.

3. The instruments or provisions contained in the Schedule comprise the National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh (Closed) Pension Scheme 2010.

###### 4.

4. This Scheme is closed to new members appointed to National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh on or after 1 January 2005.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Scheme is made under Section 25(7) of the [Universities Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) . Section 25(7) requires each university to determine the terms and conditions of any superannuation scheme for its employees. The Fifth Schedule to the 1997 Act places an obligation on each university to submit their pension scheme to the Higher Education Authority for its approval which is given with the consent of the Ministers for Education and Science, and Finance. This instrument refers to all the provisions which comprise National University of Ireland, Galway Ollscoil na hÉireann, Gaillimh’s closed pension scheme and formally records that approval and consent.
