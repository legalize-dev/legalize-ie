WASTE MANAGEMENT (RESTRICTION OF CERTAIN HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES IN ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT)(AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th September*, 2008.

I, JOHN GORMLEY, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 7, 15, 27, 28, 29, 53H, and 62 of the Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008 and section 53 of the Environmental Protection Agency Acts 1992 and 2003, and following consultation with the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Management (Restriction of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations, any reference to an article that is not otherwise identified is a reference to an article of these Regulations.

- (2) In these Regulations, any reference to a sub-article that is not otherwise identified is a reference to the sub-article of the provision in which the reference occurs.

- (3) In these Regulations, save where the context otherwise requires—

“the Waste Management Acts 1996 to 2008” mean the [Waste Management Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1996) as amended by the [Waste Management (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) (No. 36 of 2001), Part 3 of the [Protection of the Environment Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2003), Part 2 of the Waste Management (Electrical and Electronic Equipment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0290.html) ), the Waste Management (Environment Levy) (Plastic Bag) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0062.html) ), the Waste Management (Registration of Brokers and Dealers) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No 168 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0168.html) );

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the Waste Management (Restriction of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 341 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0341.html) ). They clarify that producers must have access in the State to any records that certify that electrical and electronic equipment which he or she has placed on the market is in compliance with the Regulations.
