# S.I. No. 715/2024 - Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th December,* 2024.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Regulation (EEC) No. 3440/84 of 6 December 1984[^1] (as amended by Regulation (EEC) No 955/87 of 1 April 1987[^2] , Commission Regulation (EEC) No 2122/89 of 14 July 1989[^3] and Commission Regulation No 146/2007 of 15 February 2007[^4] ), and Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^5] , and effect to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/491 of 30 November 2023[^6] and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/492 of 30 November 2023[^7] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0520.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by the insertion after “19 July 2022” in the definition of “Council Regulation” (as amended by the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 13 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0013.html) )) of the following: “, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/491 of 30 November 2023 and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/492 of 30 November 2023”.

###### 3.

3. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 13 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0013.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 December, 2024.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This instrument amends the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0520.html) ) to give effect to (a) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/491 of 30 November 2023 as regards as regards specific technical measures for red seabream (*Pagellus bogaraveo*) in ICES subareas 6 to 8 and (b) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/492 of 30 November 2023 as regards the prolongation of technical measures for certain demersal and pelagic fisheries in the Celtic Sea, the Irish Sea and West of Scotland.
