# S.I. No. 628/2021 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November,* 2021.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS T.D., Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) and [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0060.html#sec60) (as amended by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec37) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) (as adapted by the Employment Affairs and Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0447.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

**Citation and Construction**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Occupational Pension Schemes (Registration) Regulations 1991 shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Registration) Regulations 1991 to 2021.

**Amendments**

###### 3.

3. The Occupational Pension Schemes (Registration) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 325 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0325.html) ) are amended –

- (*a*) in Regulation 2, by the deletion of the following definitions –

    - (i) “qualifying Irish scheme”, and

    - (ii) “qualifying UK scheme”;

- (*b*) in Regulation 3, in sub-article (3), by the substitution of “1 month” for “12 months”;

- (*c*) by the deletion of Regulation 4, and

- (*d*) by the deletion of Regulation 5.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

25 November, 2021.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS T.D.,**

Minister for Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend [S.I. No. 325 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0325.html) to delete certain provisions which no longer apply arising from the revocation of the Occupational Pension Schemes (External Schemes)(United Kingdom) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 469 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0469.html) ) and the Occupational Pension Schemes (Schemes with External Members) (United Kingdom) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 470 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0470.html) ) and to amend the period specified in Regulation 3(3) in which the trustees of a scheme are required to notify the Pensions Authority of changes to the information that has been provided to it for the purposes of registration under [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .
