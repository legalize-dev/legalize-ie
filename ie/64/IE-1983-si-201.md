# S.I. No. 201/1983 - Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983 (Application of Part II) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 201 of 1983.

COURTS-MARTIAL APPEALS ACT, 1983 (APPLICATION OF PART II) ORDER, 1983

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1983), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Application of Part II) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The 15th day of July, 1983, is hereby fixed as the day referred to in [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1983), and accordingly the provisions of Part II of that Act have effect by virtue of that section in relation to convictions by courts-martial of which the findings are promulgated on or after that day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of July, 1983.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides that the provisions of Part II of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) shall have effect in relation to convictions by courts-martial of which the findings are promulgated on or after the 15th day of July, 1983.
