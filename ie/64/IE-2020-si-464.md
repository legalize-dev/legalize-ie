# S.I. No. 464/2020 - Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th October,* 2020.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0059.html#sec59) (amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec3) of the [Health (Amendment) Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2017)) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), being of the opinion that a further variation of the amounts referred to in subsections (1A) and (1B)(a) of the said [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0059.html#sec59) is desirable, having formed such opinion in accordance with subsection (4)(b) of the said [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0059.html#sec59) , and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 November 2020.

###### 3.

3. The amount referred to in section 59(1A) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No.1 of 1970) is varied to €1.50.

###### 4.

4. The amount referred to in section 59(1B)(a) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) is varied to €15.00.

###### 5.

5. The Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 553 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0553.html) ) are revoked.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 October, 2020.

**MICHAEL MCGRATH,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 October, 2020.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide for a reduction in the prescription charge from €2.00 to €1.50 per item, and for a reduction in the monthly cap from €20.00 to €15.00, per person or family.

These Regulations revoke the Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 553 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0553.html) ).
