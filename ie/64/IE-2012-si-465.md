# S.I. No. 465/2012 - Housing (Sale of Houses to Long-Standing Tenants) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November*, 2012.

I, JAN O’SULLIVAN, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) and [90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0090.html#sec90) (7) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966), as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) ), which powers are delegated to me by the Environment, Community and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2012 ( [S.I. No. 148 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0148.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Housing (Sale of Houses to Long-Standing Tenants) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Housing (Sale of Houses to Long-Standing Tenants) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0082.html) ) are amended in Regulation 5 by substituting “30 June 2013” for “31 December 2012”.

GIVEN under my hand,

27 November 2012.

JAN O’SULLIVAN,

Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations extend from 31 December 2012 to 30 June 2013 the date by which sales must be completed under the 2011 tenant purchase scheme for long-standing tenants.
