# S.I. No. 15/1999 - Consumer Credit Act, 1995 (Section 2) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Consumer Credit Act, 1995 (section 2) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The definition of “credit institution” contained in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), shall include Friends First Finance Limited and Fiat Auto Financial Services Ireland.

Provided that the APR charged by such persons in respect of any credit granted to a consumer is less than 23 per cent.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations further supplement the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
