# S.I. No. 216/1986 - Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 216 of 1986.

TRAVEL AGENTS (LICENSING) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0007.html#sec7) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0012.html#sec12) and [25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Transport (Tour Operators and Travel Agents) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1982), and the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) The Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 and 1985, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 to 1986.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 20th day of June, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0101.html) ), as amended by the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 166 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0166.html) ), are hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution in Regulation 9 (*a*) for "£125" of "£137.50", and

( *b* ) the substitution in Regulation 9 (*b*) for "£12.50" of "£13.75".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of June, 1986.

**JIM MITCHELL,**

Minister for Communications.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

this 13th day of June, 1986.

**SEAN MAC LIONNSIGH,**

A person authorised to authenticate the Seal of the said Minister.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for an increase in the fees payable in respect of applications for travel agents' licences and for the amendment of such licences.
