# S.I. No. 348/2003 - Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) and [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:**

###### 1.

**1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment Regulations, 2003.**

###### 2.

**2. Section 4(1) of the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0116.html) ), is hereby amended by the insertion of the following new paragraph “(i persons who pursuant to section 2 of the Heath (Amendment) Act, 1996, in the opinion of the chief executive officer of a health board, have contracted hepatitis C directly or indirectly from the use of Human Immunoglobulin-Anti-D or the receipt within the State of another blood product or a blood transfusion”.**
