# S.I. No. 122/1979 - Companies (Amendment) Act, 1977 (Designation of Stock Exchange Nominee) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 122 of 1979.

COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1977 (DESIGNATION OF STOCK EXCHANGE NOMINEE) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/sec0001.html#sec1) (1) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1977), hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (Designation of Stock Exchange Nominee) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. SEPON Limited is hereby designated as a nominee of a recognised stock exchange for the purposes of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1977).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 4th day of April, 1979.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations designate Sepon Limited as a nominee of a recognised stock exchange for the purposes of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) .
