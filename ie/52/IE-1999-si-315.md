# S.I. No. 315/1999 - Adoption Rules, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

An Bord Uchtala, with the consent of the Minister for Health and Children and in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) (*a*) and (*b*) of the [Adoption Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1952) (as substituted by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0010/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Adoption Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) ), hereby makes the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Adoption Rules, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The Adoption Rules, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 304 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0304.html) ), the Adoption Rules, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 170 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0170.html) ), the Adoption Rules, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 223 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0223.html) ), and these rules shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Adoption Rules 1988 to 1999.

###### 3.

3. Each of the forms set out in the Schedule shall be used in the case and manner indicated by the form.

###### 4.

4. These rules shall come into operation on the 8th day of October, 1999.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules prescribe the forms to be used for the purpose of the [Adoption Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) . They are to be read in conjunction with the Adoption Rules, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 304 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0304.html) ), the Adoption Rules, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 170 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0170.html) ), the Adoption Rules, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 223 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0223.html) ).
