# S.I. No. 553/2026 - Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th November,* 2026.

I, JACK CHAMBERS, Minister for Public Expenditure, Infrastructure, Public Service Reform and Digitalisation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/sec0003.html#sec3) (4) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1992), and the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0418.html) ), the Public Expenditure and Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0019.html) ) and the Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 243 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0243.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Regulations of 2010” means Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0084.html) );

###### 3.

3. Regulation 4 (9) of the Oireachtas (Allowances and Facilities) Regulations ( [S.I. No.84 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0084.html) )

is amended-

- (a) in subparagraph (b), by deleting after “Committee,” the word “or”.

- (b) in subparagraph (c), by inserting after “member.” the word “or” on a line below.

- (c) by inserting after subparagraph (c) the following subparagraph:

- (d) absence due to maternity leave or additional maternity leave within the meaning of the [Maternity Protection, Employment Equality and Preservation of Certain Records Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) which has been certified by a medical practitioner, or absence within the meaning of the [Adoptive Leave Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) which has been confirmed by a certification of placement of adoption, or absence within the meaning of the [Paternity Leave and Benefit Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) which has been confirmed with appropriate certificate as preventing the member from carrying out their duties as a member.”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 November, 2026.

**JACK CHAMBERS,**

Minister for Public Expenditure, Infrastructure, Public Service Reform and Digitalisation.
