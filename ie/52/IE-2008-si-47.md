# S.I. No. 47/2008 - Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 47 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0047.html)

GAS (AMENDMENT) ACT 1987 (SECTION 2) (DISTRIBUTION) (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th February*, 2008.

The Commission for Energy Regulation, having received and considered a request made to it by Bord Gáis Éireann and, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Gas (Amendment) Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , hereby orders as follows:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Gas (Amendment) Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2008.

*Interpretation.*

###### 2.

2. In this Order, “Principal Order” means Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 196 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0196.html) ) as amended by Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0528.html) ).

*Commencement*

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into operation on 26 February 2008.

*Amendment of Principal Order.*

###### 4.

4. (1) The Principal Order is is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule to that Order:

Article 4 (1)

## SCHEDULE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Area of Application** | **Functions applicable** |
| The administrative county of Galway. | All functions contained in this Order. |
| The administrative county of Mayo. | All functions contained in this Order. |
| The administrative county of Roscommon. | All functions contained in this Order. |
| The administrative county of Offaly. | All functions contained in this Order. |
| The administrative county of Westmeath. | All functions contained in this Order. |

SEALED with the common seal of the Commission for Energy Regulation on the 26 February 2008.

MICHAEL G. TUTTY,

Member of Commission.

DENIS CAGNEY,

Member of Staff of Commission.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order amends the Schedule of the Principal Order so as to bring the entirety of counties Roscommon, Westmeath and Offaly within BGÉ’s remit.
