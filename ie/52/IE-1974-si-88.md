# S.I. No. 88/1974 - Cork District Milk Board (Election Day, 1974) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 88 of 1974.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1974) ORDER, 1974.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Election Day, 1974) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 30th day of May, 1974, is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Cork District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of April, 1974.

- (Signed) MARK CLINTON,

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1974 on which ordinary members of the Cork District Milk Board shall be elected.
