PRISONERS (TEMPORARY RELEASE) RULES 2004

I, MICHAEL McDOWELL, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec1) of the [Criminal Justice (Temporary Release of Prisoners) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) ( [No. 34 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) )) of [the Criminal Justice Act 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1960) hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Prisoners (Temporary Release) Rules 2004.

- (2) These Rules shall come into operation on 12 November, 2004.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules “direction” means a direction given under section 2 (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec1) of the [Criminal Justice (Temporary Release of Prisoners) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2003)) of [the Criminal Justice Act 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1960).

###### 3.

3. In addition to any conditions specified in a direction, the release of a person pursuant to a direction shall be subject to the following conditions:

- (*a*) that the person shall keep the peace and be of good behaviour during the period of his or her release,

- (*b*) that the person shall be of sober habits during that period, and

- (*c*) that the person shall return to the prison from which he was released on or before the expiration of the period for which he was released.

###### 4.

4. Where a direction has been given in relation to a person—

- (*a*) a notice in the form set out in the Schedule to these Rules, or in a form of like effect, shall be completed and signed by the governor of the prison concerned or such other person as is for the time being performing the functions of the governor in relation to that prison,

- (*b*) the person to whom the direction applies shall sign or place his mark on the notice in the presence of a witness (other than the governor of the prison or person for the time being performing the functions of the governor in relation to that prison) who shall also sign the notice, and

- (*c*) the person to whom the direction applies shall be given a copy of the notice.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules provide for the temporary release of persons from prisons, from St. Patrick’s Institution, and from places provided under [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) .
