# S.I. No. 186/1981 - Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 186 of 1981.

HOUSING REGULATIONS, 1980 (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) REGULATIONS, 1981.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:

"the Regulations of 1980" means the Housing Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 296 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0296.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Sub-article (5) of article 14 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby amended by the substitution in paragraphs (*b*) and (*c*) of "30th day of June, 1981" for "31st day of March, 1981".

###### 4.

4. Article 23 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby amended by the insertion after sub-article (4) of the following sub-article:—

> - "(5) A grant shall not be paid in accordance with this article in respect of a house for which a grant has previously been paid under this article or [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0027/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing (Loans and Grants) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1962) or section 23 of the Act of 1966".

###### 5.

5. Sub-article (2) of article 24 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Housing Regulations, 1980 in relation to house improvement grants:—

- (i) to extend the final date for the completion of work and the receipt of applications by the Minister for payment of grants from 31st March, 1981 to 30th June, 1981.

- (ii) to remove the bar on the payment of grants for the accommodation of the handicapped and mentally ill in respect of a house for which an essential repairs grant has been previously paid.
