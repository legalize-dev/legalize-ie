# S.I. No. 197/1994 - Comptroller and Auditor General (Section 7) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 197 of 1994.

COMPTROLLER AND AUDITOR GENERAL (SECTION 7) ORDER, 1994.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsections (5) and (6) of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993), and after consultation with the Minister for Education, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Comptroller and Auditor General (Section 7) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, "section 7" means [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993).

###### 3.

3. The 1st day of April in the year following the financial year to which the accounts concerned relate is hereby specified for the purposes of subsection (5) of section 7.

###### 4.

4. The 30th day of September in the year following the financial year to which the accounts concerned relate or, if on that date Dáil Éireann stands dissolved, the date that is one week after—

( *a* ) the date of the reassembly of Dáil Éireann after the first-mentioned date, or

( *b* ) the date of the reassembly of Seanad Éireann after the first-mentioned date,

whichever is the later, is hereby specified for the purposes of subsection (6) of section 7.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of June, 1994.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, made by the Minister for Finance under Section 7 (5) and (6) of the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , specifies the dates for submission of the annual accounts of the Vocational Education Committees (i) to the Comptroller and Auditor General and (ii) to each House of the Oireachtas.
