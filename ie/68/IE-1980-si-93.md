# S.I. No. 93/1980 - Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1980

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 93 of 1980.

HEALTH SERVICES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1980.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and by [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1980.

- (2) In these Regulations the "Regulations of 1979" means the Health Services Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 109 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0109.html) ).

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Regulations of 1979 is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-article (3) of "last completed contribution year before the 1st day of June proceeding the claim for services" for "contribution year immediately preceding the contribution year in which a claim for services is made".

###### 3.

3. Article 3 of the Regulations of 1979 is hereby further amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution of "£7,000" for "£5,500" in sub-article (2) (*a*), and

( *b* ) the substitution of "117" for "92" in sub-article (4).

###### 4.

4. (1) These Regulations, save article 3, shall come into operation on the 6th day of April, 1980.

- (2) Article 3 of these Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1980.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 31st day of March, 1980.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations raise the income limit, above which there is a restriction on the extent to which services are available under [section 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0052.html#sec52) (1), [56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) (2), [62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0062.html#sec62) and [63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , from £5,500 to £7,000. The Regulations provide that the new income limit will take effect from 1st June, 1980 and will relate to income for the year which ended on 5th April, 1980.
