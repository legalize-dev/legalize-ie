# S.I. No. 113/2007 - Electoral Act 1997 (Limitation and Reimbursement of Election Expenses at Dáil Election) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in “Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th March*, 2007.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Electoral Act 1997 (Limitation and Reimbursement of Election Expenses at Dáil Election) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The amount of election expenses which may be reimbursed to a candidate under [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , as provided for in subsection (1)(*b*)(i) of that section, shall be the actual expenses incurred by the candidate or €8,700, whichever is the less.

###### 3.

3. The aggregate of election expenses which may be incurred by or on behalf of a candidate in connection with his or her candidature at a Dáil election, as provided for in [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0032.html#sec32) (1)(*a*)(i), (ii) and (iii) of the [Electoral Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , shall not exceed €30,150, €37,650 and €45,200 respectively.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 March 2007

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order varies, in line with changes in the Consumer Price Index, the maximum amounts of expenditure which may be incurred by or on behalf of a candidate in a Dáil election to €30,150 in a 3-seat constituency; €37,650 in a 4-seat constituency; and €45,200 in a 5-seat constituency. It also provides, in line with the changes in the Consumer Price Index, that the amount of election expenses which may be reimbursed to a candidate at a Dáil election is the actual expenses or €8,700, whichever is the less.
