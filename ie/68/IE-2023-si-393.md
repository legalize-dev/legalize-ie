# S.I. No. 393/2023 - European Union (Temporary Increase of official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non - Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th August,* 2023.

I, CHARLIE MC CONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1110 of 6 June 2023[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0009.html) ) are amended in Regulation 3(1), by substituting for the definition of “Regulation 2019/1793” the following –

> “‘Regulation 2019/1793’ means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 of 22 October 2019 as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/625 of 6 May 2020, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1540 of 22 October 2020, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/608 of 14 April 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1900 of 27 October 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/2246 of 15 December 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/913 of 30 May 2022, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/174 of 26 January 2023 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1110 of 6 June 2023;”.

###### 3.

3. The European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No.307 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0307.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 July, 2023.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)
