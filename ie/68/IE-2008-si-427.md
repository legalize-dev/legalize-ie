# S.I. No. 427/2008 - City of Dublin Local Electoral Areas Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th November*, 2008.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the City of Dublin Local Electoral Areas Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. (1) The City of Dublin shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area described in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of Dublin City Council to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set out in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division shall be construed as a reference to such area as described in the County Borough of Dublin (Wards) Regulations 1986 ( [S.I. No. 12 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0012.html) ), as amended by the County Borough of Dublin (Wards) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 109 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0109.html) ) and the County Borough of Dublin (Wards) (Amendment) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 43 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0043.html) ), and therein referred to as a ward.

###### 4.

4. The County Borough of Dublin Local Electoral Areas Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 386 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0386.html) ) is hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of Dublin City into local electoral areas and to fix the number of members of Dublin City Council to be elected for each such area.
