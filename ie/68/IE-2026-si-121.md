# S.I. No. 121/2026 - European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd April,* 2026.

I, Martin Heydon, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026[^1] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/459 of 24 February 2026[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0009.html) ) are amended in Regulation 3(1), by substituting for the definition of “Regulation 2019/1793” the following –

> “‘Regulation 2019/1793’ means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 of 22 October 2019 as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/625 of 6 May 2020, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1540 of 22 October 2020, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/608 of 14 April 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1900 of 27 October 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/2246 of 15 December 2021, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/913 of 30 May 2022, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/174 of 26 January 2023, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1110 of 6 June 2023, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/286 of 16 January 2024, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1662 of 11 June 2024 Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3153 of 18 December 2024, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1441 of 18 July 2025, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/459 of 24 February 2026;”.

###### 3.

3. The European Union (Temporary Increase of Official Controls and Emergency Measures on Imports of Food and Feed of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 ( [S.I. No. 8 of 2026](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2026/en/si/0008.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 March, 2026.

**MARTIN HEYDON,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations update existing Regulations with regard to additional levels of controls on the import of certain food of non-animal origin from specific third countries with regard to pesticide residues and on the import of certain feed of non-animal origin from specific third countries.
