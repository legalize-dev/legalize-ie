# S.I. No. 185/1980 - Worker Participation (Córas Iompair Éireann) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Córas Iompair Éireann) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1980.

###### 2.

2. The 2nd day of September, 1980, is in relation to Córas Iompair Éireann hereby appointed to be the nomination day for the purposes of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977).

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 18th day of June, 1980.

**GENE FITZGERALD.**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations appoint 2nd September, 1980, as the day on which nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to Córas Iompair Éireann shall be received by the returning officer.
