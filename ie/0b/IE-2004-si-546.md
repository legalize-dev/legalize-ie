# S.I. No. 546/2004 - International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1987 (Section 7 (2)) International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Consolidation) Regulations 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No 546 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0546.html)

INTERNATIONAL CARRIAGE OF PERISHABLE FOODSTUFFS ACT 1987 (Section 7 (2)) INTERNATIONAL CARRIAGE OF PERISHABLE FOODSTUFFS (CONSOLIDATION) REGULATIONS 1993

**Statutory Instrument 546 of 2004**

**International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1987 (Section 7 (2)) International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Consolidation) Regulations 1993**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food hereby appoint the National Standards Authority of Ireland (NSAI) as a “certifying authority” pursuant to and for the purposes of Section 7 (2) of [the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) and for the purposes of the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Consolidation) Regulations 1993 as such a “certifying authority”.

Dated this 29 day of June 2004

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Signed |  |
| ________________ Mr Joe Walsh Minister for Agriculture and Food |  |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order confers the function of “certifying authority” in Ireland for the purposes of the carriage of perishable foodstuffs on the NSAI (National Standards Authority of Ireland).

Published by the Stationery Office Dublin
