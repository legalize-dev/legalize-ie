# S.I. No. 412/1986 - Imposition of Duties (No. 283) (Value-Added Tax) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 412 of 1986.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 283) (VALUE-ADDED TAX) ORDER, 1986.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/sec0038.html#sec38) (2) of the [Value-Added Tax Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1972), hereby order as follows.

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 283) (Value-Added Tax) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1987.

###### 3.

3. [Section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/sec0001.html#sec1) (1) of the [Value-Added Tax Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) is hereby amended by the substitution in the definition of "livestock" of ", pigs and deer" for "and pigs".

###### 4.

4. This Order and the Value-Added Tax Acts, 1972 to 1986, shall be construed together.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this day of 11th

December, 1986.

**GARRET FITZGERALD.**

The Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to reduce the rate of VAT on the supply and importation of live deer from 25 per cent to 2.4 per cent.
