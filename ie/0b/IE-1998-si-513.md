EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations implement Commission Directive 97/69/EC of 5 December, 1997, the 23rd Adaptation to Technical Progress of Council Directive 67/548/EEC on the classification, packaging and labelling of dangerous substances. They amend the European Communities (Classification, Packaging, Labelling and Notification of Dangerous Substances) Regulations of 1994, [S.I. No. 77 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0077.html) .

The European Communities (Classification, Packaging, Labelling and Notification of Dangerous Substances) Regulations of 1994 apply to all substances which are intended to be placed on the market either on their own or in a preparation with exceptions for certain categories of substances such as medicinal, cosmetic, pesticide, waste. etc., products which are covered by other Directives. The Regulations require suppliers to put warning labels on containers for dangerous substances placed on the market and ensure that the containers are properly designed, constructed and secured to prevent spillage or seepage during normal use, and also supply safety data sheets.

Under the Amending Regulations, glass, slag and rock mineral wools are classified as category 3 carcinogens unless they fulfil one or more of a number of conditions which are laid down in Directive 97/69/EC, and are also classified as irritant. Refractory Ceramic Fibres and Special Purpose Fibres are classified as category 2 carcinogens and as irritant. Warning labels must indicate these hazards.
