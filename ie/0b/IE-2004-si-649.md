**[S.I. No. 649 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0649.html) .**

RESIDENTIAL TENANCIES ACT 2004 (SECTION 202) REGULATIONS 2004.

[S.I. No. 649 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0649.html) .

RESIDENTIAL TENANCIES ACT 2004 (SECTION 202) REGULATIONS 2004.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 8 and 202 of [the Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) 27 of 2004), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as [the Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (Section 202) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. Section 62(1)(*g*) of [the Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) does not apply to a notice of termination the date of service of which is earlier than the day on which Part 6 of the Act comes into operation.

|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, this 7th day of October 2004. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide that the statement referred to in section 62(1)(*g*) of [the Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) is not required to be included in a notice of termination under that Act that is served before Part 6 of the Act comes into operation.
