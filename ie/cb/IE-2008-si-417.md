# S.I. No. 417/2008 - European Communities (Safeguard Decisions) (Avian Influenza) (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 417 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0417.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (SAFEGUARD DECISIONS) (AVIAN INFLUENZA) (MISCELLANEOUS REVOCATIONS) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th October*, 2008.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , (No. 27 of 1972) hereby make the following regulations:—

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Safeguard Decisions) (Avian Influenza) (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. The following are revoked:—

- (*a*) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports from Russia) Regulations, 2005, ( [S.I. No. 669 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0669.html) ),

- (*b*) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on imports of Avian Products from Ukraine) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 908 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0908.html) ),

- (*c*) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on imports from Russia) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0081.html) ),

- (*d*) Regulation 2(1) of European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Amendment of Regulations) Regulations (No. 2) 2006 ( [S.I. No. 356 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0356.html) ),

- (*e*) Regulation 2(2) of European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Amendment of Regulations) Regulations (No. 2) 2006 ( [S.I. No. 356 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0356.html) ),

- (*f*) Regulation 2(5) of European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Amendment of Regulations) Regulations (No. 2) 2006 ( [S.I. No. 356 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0356.html) ),

- (*g*) Regulation 2(6) of European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Amendment of Regulations) Regulations (No. 2) 2006 ( [S.I. No. 356 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0356.html) ),

- (*h*) Regulation 2(2) of European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Amendment of Regulations) (No. 3) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 555 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0555.html) ),

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 October 2008

**BRENDAN SMITH.**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order removes certain restrictions imposed as result of EU Commission Safeguard Decisions, which have lapsed.
