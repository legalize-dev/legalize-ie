# S.I. No. 368/1998 - Europol Act, 1997 (Designation of National Unit) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 368 of 1998.

EUROPOL ACT, 1997 (DESIGNATION OF NATIONAL UNIT) ORDER, 1998

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0038/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Europol Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1997), following consultation with the Commissioner of the Garda Síochána, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Europol Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (Designation of National Unit) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Europol National Unit within the Garda Síochána is hereby designated as the national unit for the purposes of the [Europol Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1997), and the Convention.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of October, 1998.

**JOHN O'DONOGHUE**

Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates the Europol National Unit within the Garda Síochána as the national unit for the purposes of the [Europol Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) and the Europol Convention.
