# S.I. No. 327/1985 - Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1985) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 327 of 1985.

DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1985) ORDER, 1985.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1985) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The Interpretation Act , 1937 (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 29th day of November 1985, is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Dublin District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of September 1985.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1985 on which ordinary members of the Dublin District Milk Board shall be elected.
