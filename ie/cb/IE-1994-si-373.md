# S.I. No. 373/1994 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) (Ballinasloe) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) (Ballinasloe) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The area described in Part II of the Schedule to this Order is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. Each of the areas enclosed by red lines and shaded pink on the map attached hereto is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

SCHEDULE

## Part I

In this Schedule—

a reference to a line drawn along any road, street, or wall is a reference to a line drawn along the centre of that road, street or wall.

a reference to the point where any street or road is intersected by the projection of a wall or boundary is a reference to the point where a line drawn along the centre of that road or street would be intersected by a line drawn along the centre of the projection of such wall or boundary.

## Part II Canal Basin Area

That part of the urban district of Ballinasloe bounded by a line commencing at the point (hereafter in this description referred to as the first-mentioned point) where Slí Na Habhann (also known as the Inner Relief Road) joins Hymany Street, then continuing in a north-westerly direction along Hymany Street to the point where it is intersected by the south-westerly projection of the south eastern boundary wall of Bannerton Motors, then continuing in a north-easterly direction along that projection and boundary and the north-easterly projection thereof to the point where it is intersected by the south-easterly projection of the south western boundary of number 13 St. Michaels Place, then continuing in a south-easterly direction along the last-mentioned projection to the point where it intersects Slí Na Habhann, then continuing in a south-westerly direction along Slí Na Habhann to the first-mentioned point.

This Order provides for the designation of areas in Ballinasloe under the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .
