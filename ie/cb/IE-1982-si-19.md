# S.I. No. 19/1982 - Plant Breeders' Rights (Form of Certificate) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 19 of 1982.

PLANT BREEDERS' RIGHTS (FORM OF CERTIFICATE) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980) hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Breeders' Rights (Form of Certificate) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The certificate referred to in [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980), shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of January, 1982.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations prescribe the form of certificate of grant of Plant Breeders' Rights.
