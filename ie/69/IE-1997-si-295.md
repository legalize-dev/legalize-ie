# S.I. No. 295/1997 - Irish Aviation Authority (Fees) Order, 1997

S.I. No. 295 of 1997.

IRISH AVIATION AUTHORITY (FEES) ORDER, 1997

The Irish Aviation Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 5, 58, 59, 60, 60(1)(*j*)(i) and 75 of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Irish Aviation Authority (Fees) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of September, 1997.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Authority" means the Irish Aviation Authority and references to the charging and levying of fees shall be construed as references to the charging and levying of fees by the Authority.

###### 3.

3. The Air Navigation (Fees) Order, 1995 (S.I. 74 of 1995) and the Air Navigation (Fees) (Amendment) Order, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0049.html) ) are hereby revoked and Schedule 2 to the Air Navigation (Air Operator Certificates) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 325 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0325.html) ) is hereby revoked and replaced by Schedule II to this Order.

###### 4.

4. ( *a* ) Fees shall be charged and levied by the Authority in accordance with the provisions of the Schedules to this Order numbered 1 to 29.

( *b* ) The Authority, through such of its officers as may be so authorised in that behalf, may remit or reduce, in any case or class in relation to which it is satisfied that it is reasonable to do so, any fee prescribed by this Order.

###### 5.

5. The Authority may give directions for carrying out the purposes of this Order in respect of any matter or thing provided for by this Order.

SCHEDULE 1

*Fees for the Registration of an Aircraft*

( *a* ) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a certificate of registration of an aircraft fees in accordance with the following scale:—

where the maximum total authorised weight of the aircraft—

- (i) does not exceed 2,730 kgs., a fee of £25,

- (ii) exceeds 2,730 kgs. but does not exceed 5,700 kgs., a fee of £85,

- (iii) exceeds 5,700 kgs. but does not exceed 13,600 kgs., a fee of £110,

- (iv) exceeds 13,600 kgs. but does not exceed 45,500 kgs., a fee of £180,

- (v) exceeds 45,500 kgs., a fee of £275.

( *b* ) There shall be charged and levied for the reservation of an out of sequence certificate of registration of an aircraft, for each period of six months or less, a fee of £110.

( *c* ) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of an out of sequence certificate of registration of an aircraft, in addition to any other fee payable under (*a*) above, a fee of £275.

SCHEDULE 2

*Fees for a Certificate of Type Approval for an Aircraft, an Aircraft Engine or any other Aviation Product*

There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a Certificate of Type approval for an aircraft, an aircraft engine or other aviation product a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purpose of such issue.

SCHEDULE 3

*Fees for Certificates of Airworthiness*

- (1) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a certificate of airworthiness, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such issue, subject to the following minimum fees for each year to which the certificate relates:—

( *a* ) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £100;

( *b* ) in the case of other aircraft of which the maximum total authorised weight:—

- (i) does not exceed 2,000 kgs., for each 50 kgs. or part thereof, a fee of £10,

- (ii) exceeds 2,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 2,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (i) above, a fee of £60,

- (iv) exceeds 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (ii) above, a fee of £55.

- (2) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a certificate of airworthiness, in the case of an aircraft intended for export, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such issue, subject to the following minimum fees:—

( *a* ) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £100,

( *b* ) in the case of other aircraft of which the maximum total authorised weight:—

- (i) does not exceed 2,000 kgs., for each 50 kgs. or part thereof, a fee of £10,

- (ii) exceeds 2,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 2,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (i) above, a fee of £60,

- (iii) exceeds 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (ii) above, a fee of £55.

- (3) There shall be charged and levied for the validation of a Certificate of Airworthiness issued by another State, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such validation, subject to the following minimum fees for each year for which the validation relates:—

( *a* ) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £100;

( *b* ) in the case of other aircraft of which the maximum total authorised weight:—

- (i) does not exceed 2,000 kgs., for each 50 kgs. or part thereof, a fee of £10,

- (ii) exceeds 2,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 2,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (i) above, a fee of £60,

- (iii) exceeds 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs., or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (ii) above, a fee of £55.

- (4) There shall be charged and levied for the renewal of a Certificate of Airworthiness and for the renewal of a validation of a Certificate of Airworthiness issued by another State, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such renewal or such renewal of a validation, subject to the following minimum fees for each year for which the renewal or the renewal of the validation relates:—

( *a* ) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £100,

( *b* ) in the case of other aircraft of which the maximum total authorised weight:—

- (i) does not exceed 2,000 kgs., for each 50 kgs., or part thereof, a fee of £10,

- (ii) exceeds 2,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof above 2,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (i) above, a fee of £60,

- (iii) exceeds 100,000 kgs. for each 500 kgs., or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs. in addition to the maximum fee payable under (ii) above, a fee of £55.

- (5) Where an aircraft is based in another state under a lease agreement acceptable to the Authority and operated by an operator who is not the holder of an air operator's certificate issued by the Authority, the fees applicable at (1), (3) and (4) above shall be generally applicable to that aircraft but there shall be charged and levied a minimum fee of £5,000 per aircraft for each year to which an issue or renewal action relates.

*Fees for Inspection for the Purposes of Approval of an Organisation and Related Matters (Overhaul/Maintenance)*

- (1) There shall be charged and levied by the Authority for the inspection of an organisation for the purposes of the grant or renewal of its approval as an organisation competent to overhaul and to maintain aircraft and aircraft components, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required for the purposes of the inspection, subject to the following minimum fees in respect of each year to which the grant or renewal relates:

( *a* ) where the grant or renewal of approval of an organisation is in respect of base maintenance of aircraft of maximum takeoff weight not exceeding 2,725 kgs. or the line maintenance only of aircraft of any weight, a fee of £630;

( *b* ) where the grant or renewal of approval of an organisation is in respect of base maintenance of rotorcraft of maximum takeoff weight exceeding 2,725 kgs., a fee of £790;

( *c* ) where the grant or renewal of approval to an organisation is in respect of base maintenance of aeroplanes of maximum takeoff weight exceeding 2,725 kgs. but not exceeding 30,000 kgs., a fee of £950;

( *d* ) where the grant or renewal of approval to an organisation is in respect of base maintenance of aeroplanes of maximum takeoff weight exceeding 30,000 kgs., a fee of £1,300;

( *e* ) where the grant or renewal of approval to an organisation is in respect of the overhaul, reconditioning or major maintenance of aircraft engines or their component parts (excluding accessories):

if Piston Engines, a fee of £630,

if Gas Turbine Engines, a fee of £1,300;

( *f* ) where the grant or renewal of approval to an organisation is in respect of the overhaul, reconditioning or major maintenance of aircraft components other than engines or their component parts, a fee of £630.

- (2) There shall be charged and levied by the Authority for the conduct of a full or a partial technical audit of an organisation, referred to in (1) above, at any interim period (i.e. other than at the time of grant or renewal of approval), a fee of £210 per diem.

- (3) There shall be charged and levied by the Authority for the conduct of an initial review of the Company manual of an organisation, referred to in (1) above, applying for an approval, a fee of £130.

- (vi) Class B — Heliports: Heliports, without a Control Zone or Instrument Approach, which are licensed for Public use (VFR-DAY).

- (vii) Class C — Heliports: Heliports which are licensed for Private and/or Aerial Work use only (including use for student pilot training).

SCHEDULE 20

*Fees for flight Tests*

1. *General*

There shall be charged and levied for flight tests conducted to assess the skill of applicants for the grant, renewal or reissue of various flight crew licences and associated ratings or, where required, for the validation of licences issued by the relevant authorities of other states fees as set out hereunder.

( *a* ) For the purposes of this Schedule, the term "Professional Pilot's Licence" includes a Commercial Pilot's Licence (C.P.L.), or an Airline Transport Pilot's Licence (A.T.P.L.).

2. *Classification of Flight Tests*

For the purposes of this Schedule, flight tests are classified as follows:—

( *a* ) *Flight Tests — Group A:*

The flight tests in this group are for the grant, or (if required) for the renewal, reissue or validation of a Private Pilot's Licence (P.P.L.), a multi-engine rating, an aircraft class or type rating (aircraft under 5,700 kgs. Maximum Total Authorised Weight) and for the renewal of an Instrument Rating (endorsed on any licence) or the renewal of an Instrument Rating validation (endorsed on any licence).

( *b* ) *Flight Tests — Group B:*

The flight tests in this group are for the grant, or (if required) for the renewal, reissue or validation of a Professional Pilot's Licence (single or multi-engine aircraft under 5,700 kgs. Maximum Total Authorised Weight) and for the grant of a single or multi-engine Instrument Rating or instrument rating validation.

( *c* ) *Flight Tests — Group C:*

The flight tests in this group are for the renewal of a Flight Instructor Rating Class I or Class 2.

3. *Fees Payable*

The amounts listed below are the fees for a flight test. No reductions are allowed for group applications:

( *a* ) Flight Tests — Group A: £190 per candidate.

- (*b*) Flight Test — Group B: £265 per candidate.

- (*c*) Flight Test — Group C: £125 per applicant.

Repeat tests will be charged at 50% of the standard rate.

4. *Fee Reduction for Combined Flight Tests*

Where a candidate intends to take a combined flight test, e.g. a C.P.L. and Instrument rating, the fees for the additional ratings will be 25% of the fee otherwise applicable. The total fee will be rounded up to the nearest whole pound.

5. *Supplementary Provisions for Flight Test Fees*

( *a* ) *Failure to attend for flight test*

Where a flight test candidate does not attend for the flight test and such absence has not been excused by prior arrangement with the flight test examiner the candidate's fees shall be forfeited. However, 50% of the fee will be offset against the fee otherwise payable should the candidate apply for another flight test.

( *b* ) *Withdrawal from flight test*

Where a flight test candidate withdraws his flight test application before any costs have been incurred by the flight test examiner, he may be paid a refund of his flight test fee less an administration fee of £20. Where a flight test candidate withdraws his flight test application after costs have been incurred by the flight test examiner, the rules applicable to failure to attend as set out in (*a*) above shall apply.

( *c* ) *Failure to provide aircraft for flight test*

It is the responsibility of the applicant(s) to provide a serviceable, suitable and acceptable aircraft for the flight test(s). Where a flight test examiner attends at a location for a flight test(s) and such test(s) cannot be carried out because of the unavailability of a serviceable, suitable and acceptable aircraft, the sum of £180 shall be forfeited by the candidate.

( *d* ) *Flight tests for heavy aircraft*

Fees for flight tests for Aircraft Type ratings or instrument ratings on aircraft which exceed a Maximum Total Authorised Weight of 5,700 kgs. requiring a flight test examiner supplied by the Authority, shall be charged on a cost recovery basis, subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee specified at paragraph 3 above.

( *e* ) *Flight tests for Flight Engineer's Licence or Flight Navigator's Licence*

Fees for a flight test, when required, for the grant, renewal, reissue or validation of a Flight Engineer's Licence or Flight Navigator's Licence shall be charged on a cost recovery basis, subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee specified at paragraph 3 above for "Flight Test — Group B".

( *f* ) *Skill tests conducted in an aircraft simulator*

Fees for skill tests conducted in an aircraft simulator shall be charged on a cost recovery basis subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee for the licence or rating sought as specified in this Schedule.

( *g* ) *Delegated flight tests*

Flight tests delegated to an external flight test examiner may be charged at the rates applicable to the particular flight test as set out in this Schedule.

###### 6.

6. *Flight Tests giving rise to extraordinary costs*

Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions of this Schedule, where any flight tests are conducted which, due to the particular circumstances, give rise to extraordinary costs, such costs shall be recovered through increased fees which shall be notified to and agreed with the flight test candidate(s) in advance.

SCHEDULE 23

*Fees for Aeronautical Examinations*

There shall be charged and levied in respect of Aeronautical Examinations, listed in the Appendix to this Schedule and conducted for the purpose of the grant of flight crew licences, or, if required, for the validation of licences issued by the Aeronautical Authorities of other states, the fees specified in the said Appendix.

( *a* ) *Late application fee supplement*

Examination applications, accompanied by the appropriate fees, should be received at least 21 days before the examination starting date. Where an examination application is received less than 21 days but at least 7 days before the examination starting date, the fees for such application, if accepted, shall be increased by 20%. Where an examination application is received less than 7 days before the examination starting date, the fees for such application, if accepted, shall be increased by 50%. Where a change is made to an examination application less than 21 days before the examination starting date, the appropriate late application fee supplement as set out above shall be charged for each subject changed.

( *b* ) *Withdrawal from and non-attendance at examinations*

- (i) Any withdrawal of an examination application should be made in writing and if made orally must be confirmed in writing within 5 days;

- (ii) Where an examination application is withdrawn at least 21 days before the examination starting date there shall not be a cancellation fee;

- (iii) Where an examination application is withdrawn less than 21 days and more than 7 days before the examination starting date 20% of the fees shall be forfeited;

- (iv) Where an examination application is withdrawn 7 days or less before the examination starting date 50% of the fees shall be forfeited;

- (v) Where an applicant fails to attend an examination, the fees in respect of that examination shall be forfeited. Where such failure to attend is in respect of subject(s) the fees in respect of that subject(s) shall be forfeited;

- (vi) Where a portion of the fee has been forfeited under (iii) or (iv) above the balance will be retained by the Authority and will be set against the fees for future examination applications, unless the applicant requests a refund in writing.

( *c* ) *Additional fees*

Where it is expedient to hold examinations at times or locations other than those normally appointed, or where the holding of such examinations has been specially requested, the fees may be increased to meet any additional costs. Such increased fees shall be determined by the Authority and notified in advance.

( *d* ) *Miscellaneous*

Where an examination is required for the grant, renewal, reissue or validation of a Flight Engineer's Licence, a Flight Navigator's Licence, a Flight Radiotelephony Operator's Licence (General or Restricted), a Flight Radiotelegraphy Operator's Licence (Temporary or Full) or of any other licence or rating not otherwise provided for, the fees shall be determined by the Authority and notified in advance.

    - (ii) For permission to operate a tethered or free manned balloon for private purposes, a fee of £25 for any single operation, or a fee of £38 for a number of operations at a single event, or a fee of £175 for an unlimited number of operations performed within a period of one year in accordance with conditions specified by the Authority.

( *b* ) (i) For permission to operate a tethered unmanned balloon or balloons for aerial work or private purposes, a fee of £25 for a single operation, or a fee of £38 for a number of operations at a single event, or a fee of £175 for an unlimited number of operations performed within a period of one year in accordance with conditions specified by the Authority.

( *c* ) Where any investigations and work for any permissions for which fees are prescribed by this Schedule give rise to greater costs than can be recovered from the said fees, such costs shall be recovered from the applicant.

No. 165 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0165.html) ), which is being flown for payment required to be made, or promised to the operator of the aircraft, being a payment for the flight or in respect of the purpose for which the flight is undertaken;

( *c* ) For each acceptance of a variation in terms of an aerial work operator's Operations Manual and Maintenance Manual so as to include a new form of aerial work, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required, subject to a minimum fee of £65.

( *d* ) For each acceptance of a variation in the terms of an aerial work operator's Operations Manual and Maintenance Manual so as to include a new type of aircraft, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required, subject to a minimum fee of £65. However no fee shall be payable for the inclusion of a new type of aircraft if, in the opinion of the Authority, such inclusion does not entail any further investigations and work.

( *e* ) For the grant of an exemption(s) from any of the requirements for aerial work aircraft or for aerial work operators, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required, subject to a minimum fee of £65.

- (3) The fees prescribed at Paragraph 2 (*a*) above shall be reduced by 50% where that aerial work operator also proposes to engage in public transport under an Air Operator's Certificate and has paid the appropriate fees specified in Schedule 10 of this Order and where the aerial work operation is included in the Operations Manual and the Maintenance Manual provided for the purpose of the Air Operator's Certificate.
