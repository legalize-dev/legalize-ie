# S.I. No. 346/1999 - Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 35 of the Registry of Deeds (Ireland) Act, 1832 as adapted by the Registry of Deeds (Ireland) Act, 1832, (Adaptation) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 281 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0281.html) ), and section 9 of the Land Transfer (Ireland) Act 1848 as adapted by the Land Transfer (Ireland) Act 1848 (Adaptation) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 180 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0180.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. (1) [The Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (2) In this Order—

“memorial”, other than in respect of item number 2 of the Schedule to this Order, includes an affidavit to register a judgement as a mortgage;

“name” does not include any variation either of the forename or the surname.

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 2000.

###### 4.

4. In lieu of the fees specified in the Schedule to the Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 360 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0360.html) ), the fees specified in column number 2 of the Schedule to this Order shall be charged in the Office of the Registrar of Deeds in respect of the items specified in column number 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order, which comes into operation on the 1st day of February, 2000, provides for a revised scale of fees to be charged in the Registry of Deeds. This Order replaces the Registry of Deeds (Fees) Order, 1991.
