# S.I. No. 580/2002 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Tim O'Malley, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), after consultation with the Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, the Minister For Agriculture and Food, and the Minister for Enterprise Trade and Employment hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order, 2002.

###### 2.

2. Part I of the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No.29 of 1998), is amended by

The addition of “ [Irish Whiskey Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1980)”;

The addition of “ [Animal Remedies Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1993)” (other than Sections 4 and 5).

###### 3.

3. Part II of the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No.29 of 1998), is amended by

The deletion of “Health (Arsenic and Lead in Food), 1972 ( [S.I. No. 44 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0044.html) )”;

The deletion of “European Communities (Suspension of Imports of Pistachios and Certain Products Derived from Pistachios Originating in or Consigned from Iran Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 432 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0432.html) )”;

The substitution for “European Communities (Labelling, Presentation and Advertising of Foodstuffs) Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0092.html) )” of “European Communities (Labelling, Presentation and Advertising of Foodstuffs Regulations, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 483 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0483.html) )”;

The addition of “European Communities (Importation of Animals and Animal Products from Third Countries) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 255 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0255.html) )”;

The addition of “European Communities (Trade in Animals and Animal Products Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No.289 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0289.html) ) (other than Regulation 5)”;

The addition of “European Communities (Zoonoses) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No.2 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0002.html) )”;

The addition of “European Communities (Trade in Certain Animal Products Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 102 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0102.html) ) (Other than Regulation 4, 6, 8-9, 12-13 15-19)”;

The addition of “Control of Animal Remedies and their Residues Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 507 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0507.html) ) (other than Regulations 3-5, 10-14, 20-21, 24)”;

The addition of “European Communities (Veterinary Checks on Products Imported from Third Countries) Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 292 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0292.html) )”;

The addition of “European Communities (Specified Risk Material) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 332 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0332.html) )”;

The addition of “European Communities (Labelling of Beef and Beef Products Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No.435 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0435.html) )”;

The addition of “European Communities (Suspending the Placing on the Market the Importation and the Use in Manufacture of Jelly Confectionary Containing the Food Additive E425 Konjac) Regulations, [S.I. No. 442 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0442.html) ”;

###### 4.

4. Part III of the First Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), is amended by

The deletion of “Commission Regulation (EC) No. 194/97[^1] of 31 January 1997 setting maximum levels for certain contaminants in foodstuffs”;

The addition of “Council Regulation (EC) No. 1139/98[^2] of 26 May 1998 concerning the compulsory indication of the labelling of certain foodstuffs produced from genetically modified organisms of particulars other than those provided for in Directive 79/112/EEC, as amended”;

The addition of “Commission Regulation (EC) No. 50/2000[^3] of 10 January 2000 on the labelling of foodstuffs and food ingredients containing additives and flavourings that have been genetically modified or have been produced from genetically modified organisms”;

The addition of “Council Regulation (EC) No. 999/2001 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^4] of 22 May 2001 laying down rules for the prevention, control and eradication of certain transmissible spongiform encephalopathies (other than Articles 5, 7, 11-15, 17-21, 24-25) as amended”;

The addition of “Commission Regulation (EC) No. 178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^5] of 28 January 2002 laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety”.

###### 5.

5. The Second Schedule to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998), is amended by the inclusion of “The Marine Institute”.

**Explanatory Note**

(This is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The effect of this Order is to amend the First and Second Schedules to the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act. Since the Schedules were last amended in June 2000 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0184.html) ) new food legislation has been passed and existing legislation has been amended or revoked. In effect this Order updates the list of food legislation contained in the First Schedule. It also updates the Second Schedule to include the Marine Institute as an official agency.

For ease of reading, a list of all food legislation is included below:
