# S.I. No. 311/2025 - European Union (Anti-Money Laundering: Beneficial Ownership of Trusts) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th July,* 2025.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Article 31 of Directive (EU) 2015/849 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015[^1] , as amended by Article 74(2) of Directive (EU) 2024/1640 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31 May 2024[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Anti-Money Laundering: Beneficial Ownership of Trusts) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 10 July 2025.

###### 3.

3. The European Union (Anti-Money Laundering: Beneficial Ownership of Trusts) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 194 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0194.html) ) are amended –

- (a) in Regulation 27, by the deletion of paragraph (6), and

- (b) in Regulation 29 –

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by the substitution of “Regulation 27(1) or (3)” for “Regulation 27(1), (3) or (6)” in each place where it occurs, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (3), by the substitution of “Regulation 27(1) or (3)” for “Regulation 27(1), (3) or (6)”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 July, 2025.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
