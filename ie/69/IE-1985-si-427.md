# S.I. No. 427/1985 - Coras Beostoic agus Feola Act, 1979. (Levy on Slaughtered Or Exported Livestock) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 427 of 1985.

CORAS BEOSTOIC AGUS FEOLA ACT, 1979. (LEVY ON SLAUGHTERED OR EXPORTED LIVESTOCK) ORDER, 1985.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/sec0028.html#sec28) (3) of the [Coras Beostoic agus Feola Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1979), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Coras Beostoic agus Feola Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The amount of levy payable under [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Coras Beostoic agus Feola Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1979), shall, in lieu of the sum (as varied by the [Coras Beostoic agus Feola Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 231 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0231.html) )) specified in subsection (2) *(a)*, be £1 per animal and, in lieu of the sum (as so varied) specified in subsection (2) *(b)*, be 10p per animal.

###### 4.

4. The [Coras Beostoic agus Feola Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1982, is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of December, 1985.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to increase the levy payable to CBF on cattle and sheep slaughtered or exported live.
