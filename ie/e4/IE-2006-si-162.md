# S.I. No. 162/2006 - District Court (Temporary Closure Orders) Rules 2006

**[SI No. 162 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0162.html)**

**District Court (Temporary Closure Orders) Rules 2006**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec24) of the [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform make the following rules of court:-

1 These rules may be cited as the District Court (Temporary Closure Orders) Rules 2006.

2 These rules shall come into operation on the 9^th day of May 2006 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

3 Order 82 of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) is hereby amended by the insertion immediately after rule 2 thereof, of the following:

> *“3 Where a temporary closure order is made pursuant to section 36A(2) of the [Intoxicating Liquor Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , the order shall be in the Form 82.3, Schedule C.”*

4 The Form numbered 82.3 in Schedule 1 hereof shall be added to the Forms in Schedule C of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

Schedule C

O. 82, r. 3

No. 82.3

INTOXICATING LIQUOR ACT 1988

Section 36A as inserted by section 13, Intoxicating Liquor Act 2000

WHEREAS.................................(hereinafter referred to as the “licensee”), the holder of a licence for the sale of intoxicating liquor by retail in respect of the premises............................................., situate at ................................... in the court area and district aforesaid, has been convicted of an offence under ✻section 31(1) ✻section 31(2) ✻section 35(1) ✻section 36(1) of the above-mentioned Act of 1988, this being the said licensee's.................such offence.

✻HAVING HAD from Garda...................., a member of the Garda Síochána involved in the investigation of the offence, a report on the circumstances in which the said offence was committed and such other information as the Court considers of assistance to it in dealing with the case

THE COURT HEREBY ORDERS pursuant to section 36A(2) of the said Act of 1988 that ✻the said premises/✻that part of the said premises comprising......................................be closed for a period of.......................days, commencing on the thirtieth day after the date of this Order (provided that, if appeal if made from this Order, the said period shall not commence until the thirtieth day after the conviction or period of closure is affirmed).
