# S.I. No. 339/1979 - Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) (No. 2) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 339 of 1979.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (MAINTENANCE) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and subsection (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) (No. 2) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 78 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0078.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Act" means the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947);

"outgoings" means the rent, ground rent, rates and purchase annuity payable in respect of premises and mortgage repayments relating to the purchase of premises;

"receiving in-patient services" means receiving in-patient services made available under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 );

"person" means a person to whom section 44 of the Act applies by virtue of subsection (1) of that section, and

"the payment" means the payment to be made under subsection (3) of section 44 of the Act to or in respect of a person or his dependants.

###### 4.

4. The payment shall, with effect from the 7th day of October, 1979, be a weekly payment not exceeding:—

( *a* ) the sum specified in Part One of the Schedule hereto which is appropriate to the person, and

( *b* ) such of the increases specified in Part Two of the said Schedule as are appropriate.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to amend the existing Regulations governing the payment of maintenance allowances to persons being treated for certain infectious diseases—the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 78 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0078.html) )—to provide as from 7th October, 1979, for increases in the maximum rates of allowance payable.

The Infectious diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are acute anterior poliomyelitis, diphtheria, dysentery, lassa fever, paratyphoid A, paratyphoid B, rabies, salmonella infection, scarlet fever, streptococcal sore throat, tuberculosis, and typhus fever.
