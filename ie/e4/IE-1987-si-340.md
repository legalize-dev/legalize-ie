# S.I. No. 340/1987 - Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 340 of 1987.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1987.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1987.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1986, and this Order may be cited together as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1987, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) )) to the Aliens Order, 1946 (S.R. & O. 395 of 1946), is hereby amended by the deletion of "Bangladesh", "Ghana", "India", "Nigeria" and "Pakistan".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 18th day of December, 1987.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order requires a citizen of Bangladesh, Ghana, India, Nigeria and Pakistan to have a valid Irish Visa for admission to the State with effect from 1st January, 1988.
