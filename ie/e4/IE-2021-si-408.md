# S.I. No. 408/2021 - European Union (Counterfeiting of Euro) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd August,* 2021.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Regulation (EC) No. 1338/2001 of 28 June 2001[^1] laying down measures necessary for the protection of the euro against counterfeiting, as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No. 44/2009 of 18 December 2008[^2] , Regulation (EU) No. 1210/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 December 2010[^3] concerning authentication of euro coins and handling of euro coins unfit for circulation and Decision ECB/2010/14 of the European Central Bank of 16 September 2010[^4] on the authenticity and fitness checking and recirculation of euro banknotes, as amended by Decision ECB/ 2012/19 of 7 September 2012[^5] and by Decision ECB/2019/2195 of 5 December 2019[^6] ; hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Counterfeiting of Euro) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 3^rd day of August 2021.

###### 2.

2. The [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942) is amended -

- (*a*) in section 2(2A) –

    - (i) in paragraph (ba) (inserted by Regulation 5 of the European Union (Sustainability-related Disclosures in the Financial Services Sector) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0146.html) )), by the substitution of “2019;” for “2019.”, and

    - (ii) by the insertion of the following paragraphs after paragraph (ba):

      > - “(bb) Council Regulation (EC) No. 1338/2001 of 28 June 2001[^1] laying down measures necessary for the protection of the euro against counterfeiting, as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No. 44/2009 of 18 December 2008[^2] ;
      >
      > - (bc) Regulation (EU) No. 1210/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 December 2010[^3] concerning authentication of euro coins and handling of euro coins unfit for circulation;
      >
      > - (bd) Decision ECB/2010/14 of the European Central Bank of 16 September 2010[^4] on the authenticity and fitness checking and recirculation of euro banknotes, as amended by Decision ECB/2012/19 of 7 September 2012[^5] and by Decision ECB/2019/2195 of 5 December 2019[^6] .”,

      and

- (*b*) in Part 1 of Schedule 2 by the insertion of the following:

  > “49 No. 16 of 2021 Counterfeiting Act 2021 Part 3”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 July, 2021.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
