# S.I. No. 224/1998 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 224 of 1998.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Forestry (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0302.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall—

- (*a*) as regards milk, be 0.4 penny per gallon, and

- (*b*) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, be£2 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 74 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0074.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of July, 1998.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which came into effect on the 1st of July, 1998 prescribe new rates of disease levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
