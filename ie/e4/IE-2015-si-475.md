# S.I. No. 475/2015 - Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) (No. 2) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd November*, 2015.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (4) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950) and Article 4 of the Córas Iompair Éireann (Additional Powers) Order 1988 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0381.html) ), the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )) after consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) (No. 2) Order 2015.

###### 2.

2. The superannuation scheme set out in the Schedule is confirmed and is deemed to have come into operation on 17 January 2014.

## Schedule Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation

Scheme 1951 (Amendment) Scheme 2015

Rule 21 (inserted by [S.I. No. 421 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0421.html) ) of the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme 1951 in paragraph (*a*) of Clause (II) (re-numbered by [S.I. No. 390 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0390.html) ) is amended by substituting “17 January 2018” for “the 17th day of January 2014” (inserted by [S.I. No. 50 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0050.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 October 2015.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The purpose of this Order is to extend voluntary severance terms applicable to members of the CIÉ Superannuation Scheme 1951 to 17 January 2018.
