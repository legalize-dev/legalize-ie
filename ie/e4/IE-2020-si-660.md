# S.I. No. 660/2020 - European Union (Food and Feed Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January,* 2021.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , for the purpose of giving further effect to Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 January 2002[^1] , Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004[^2] , Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004[^3] , Regulation (EC) No 183/2005 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 January 2005[^4] , Commission Regulation (EU) No 210/2013 of 11 March 2013[^5] , Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 March 2017[^6] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1013 of 16 April 2019[^7] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/466 of 30 March 2020[^8] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1087 of 23 July 2020[^9] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1341 of 28 September 2020[^10] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1158 of 5 August 2020[^11] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1191 of 11 August 2020[^12] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1478 of 14 October 2020[^13] , , Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2020/1550 of 23 October 2020[^14] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1560 of 26 October 2020[^15] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1572 of 28 October 2020[^16] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1641 of 5 November 2020[^17] , hereby make the following regulations:

  > - “(6) An application for approval in respect of-

- (*d*) by substituting for Regulation 10 the following:

  > “Notice of import of food and feed
  >
  > 10. (1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), a person shall, in accordance with Article 56(3)(a) of Regulation No 2017/625, no later than 1 working day before the expected arrival, notify the Minister in writing, by means of Part I of the common health entry document, of his or her intention to move into the State food or feed to which these Regulations apply and that are referred to in Article 47(1) of Regulation No 2017/625.
  >
  > - (2) A person referred to in paragraph (1) may, where logistical constraints prevent compliance with the time limit set out in paragraph (1), avail of the derogation provided for in Article 1(2) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1013 of 16 April 2019, but in such a case the period of prior notification shall be at least 24 hours.
  >
  > - (3) Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (1), where the consignment to be moved into the state contains animal feed, the person referred to in paragraph (1) shall provide the Minister with notice of the nature and quantity of animal feed including its GM and Organic Status.”, and

- (*e*) in Regulation 32(1), by substituting for subparagraph (j) the following:

  > - “(j) except as provided for in Regulation 10, being an operator, fails to provide prior notification in contravention of Article 56(4),”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 December, 2020.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations update the definitions of the Official Controls Regulation providing an alternative for competent authority and it also amends the notification period for imports of food and feed.
