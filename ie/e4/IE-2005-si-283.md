# S.I. No. 283/2005 - Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road (Fees and Section 18 Payments) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 283 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0283.html)**

**Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road (Fees and Section 18 Payments (Amendment) Regulations 2005**

I, Tony Killeen, Minster of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 18 of the [Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) 43 of 1998) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 809 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0809.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road (Fees and Section 18 Payments) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road (Fees and Section 18 Payments) Regulations 2005 (S. I. [No. 25 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) ) is amended in the definition of “competent authority” by substituting “Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998 (Appointment of Competent Authorities) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 24 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0024.html) )” for “Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road Act 1998 (Appointment of Competent Authorities) Order 2004 (S. I. No. ___ of 2005)”.
