# S.I. No. 185/1981 - Housing Regulations, 1980, (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Regulations of 1980" means the Housing Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 296 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0296.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Article 14 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby amended by the substitution therein for sub-article (3) of the following sub-article—

- "(3) ( *a* ) Save in respect of grants payable in accordance with paragraphs (*d*) (i) of article 16 (1), (*a*) of article 16 (3), (*c*) and (*d*) of article 19 (3) and paragraph (*a*) (i) of article 20 (1), the amount of a grant shall not exceed the maximum grant specified or two thirds of the approved cost of the works whichever is the less.

( *b* ) In respect of grants payable in accordance with paragraphs (*d*) (i) of article 16 (1), (*c*) and (*d*) of article 19 (3) and paragraph (*a*) (i) of article 20 (1) the amount of a grant shall not exceed the maximum grant specified or 80 per cent of the approved cost of the works whichever is the less."

###### 4.

4. Article 16 of the Regulations of 1980, is hereby amended by the substitution therein for paragraph (*d*) of sub-article (1) of the following paragraph—

"( *d* ) (i) £600, for the provision and installation of a private water supply in a house, located in the Region described in the *Fifth Schedule* as part of a group water supply scheme, where there is no existing water supply in the house or where the existing water supply is, in the opinion of the Minister, seriously deficient and where not less than 35% of the members of the group scheme are farmers or inhabitants of villages who are dependent principally on agriculture,

- (ii) in any other case, £300, for the provision and installation of a private water supply in a house as part of a group water supply scheme, where there is no existing water supply in the house or where the existing water supply is in the opinion of the Minister, seriously deficient."

###### 5.

5. Article 16 of the Regulations of 1980, is hereby further amended by the addition thereto of the following sub-article—

> - "(7) The provisions of *paragraph* *(d*) *(i)* of *sub-article* *(1)* shall not apply as respects works commenced before the 15th day of April, 1981 and after the 31st day of December, 1983".

###### 6.

6. Sub-article (3) of article 19 of the Regulations of 1980, is hereby amended by the addition thereto of the following paragraphs—

"( *c* ) £750, where the house is located on an island specified in Part 1 of the *Second Schedule* and where the island is located in the Region described in the *Fifth Schedule* and where not less than 35% of the members of the group scheme are farmers or inhabitants of villages who are dependent principally on agriculture.

( *d* ) £840, where the house is located on an island specified in Part 11 of the *Second Schedule* and where the island is located in the Region described in the *Fifth Schedule* and where not less than 35% of the members of the group scheme are farmers or inhabitants of villages who are dependent principally on agriculture."

###### 7.

7. Article 19 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby further amended by the addition thereto of the following sub-article—

> - "(5) the provisions of *paragraphs* *(c) and* *(d)* *of sub-article* (3) shall not apply as respects works commenced before the 15th day of April, 1981, and after the 31st day of December, 1983."

###### 8.

8. Article 20 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby amended by the substitution therein for paragraph (*a*) of sub-article (1) of the following paragraph—

"( *a* ) (i) £600, for the provision and installation of a private water supply in a house, located in the Region described in the *Fifth Schedule,* as part of a group water supply scheme, where there is no existing water supply in the house or where the existing water supply is, in the opinion of the Minister, seriously deficient and where not less than 35% of the members of the group scheme are farmers or inhabitants of villages who are dependent principally on agriculture.

- (ii) in any other case £300 for the provision and installation of a private water supply in a house as part of a group water supply scheme where there is no existing water supply in the house or where the existing supply is, in the opinion of the Minister, seriously deficient."

###### 9.

9. Article 20 of the Regulations of 1980 is hereby further amended by the addition thereto of the following sub-article—

> - "(4) The provisions of *paragraph (a)* *(i)* *of sub-article (1)* shall not apply as respects works commenced before the 15th day of April, 1981 and after the 31st day of December, 1983."

###### 10.

10. The Regulations of 1980, are hereby amended by the addition thereto of the following schedule—

"FIFTH SCHEDULE

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Housing Regulations, 1980, to provide for increases in the maximum grants payable by the Minister for the Environment, for the installation of water supplies in dwelling houses located in the Western Region as part of a group water supply scheme, where work commences on or after the 15th day of April, 1981 and before the 31st day of December, 1983.

The increased grants are being financed with assistance from the European Agricultural Guidance and Guarantee Fund (F.E.O.G.A.) under Regulation E.E.C. No. 1820/80 of 24th June, 1980, (O.J. L 180/1 of 14th July, 1980) for the stimulation of agricultural development in the less-favoured farming areas of the Western Region which is set out in the Fifth Schedule of the Regulations and which accords with the definitions in Directive No. 75/272/E.E.C., of 28th April, 1975 (O.J. No. L 128 of 19/5/75). Decision No. 77/5/E.E.C., of 13th December, 1976 (O.J. No. L 3 of 5/1/77) and Decision No. 81/47/E.E.C., of 12th January, 1981 (O.J. No. L 50 of 25/2/81).
