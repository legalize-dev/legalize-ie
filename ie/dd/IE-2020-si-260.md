> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th July,* 2020.

I, Eamon Ryan, Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0010.html#sec10) , [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0014.html#sec14) and [53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1987)(as amended), hereby make the following Regulations:—

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Air Pollution Act (Marketing, Sale, Distribution and Burning of Specified Fuels) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 September 2020.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the Air Pollution Act (Marketing, Sale, Distribution and Burning of Specified Fuels) Regulations, 2012 ( [S.I. No. 326 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0326.html) ) (as amended).

These Regulations provide for the introduction of the ban on the marketing, sale, distribution and burning of specified fuels in specified areas of Ashbourne, Ballina, Castlebar, Cavan, Cobh, Enniscorthy, Killarney, Longford, Mallow, Midleton, Tramore and Tullamore with effect from 1 September 2020.

These Regulations also extend the Specified Area of Cork, to include certain other designated townlands in the Electoral Division of Carrigtwohill, with effect from 1 September 2020.
