# S.I. No. 385/2006 - Road Traffic Act 2006 (Mobile Phones-Prescribed Numbers) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 385 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0385.html)**

**ROAD TRAFFIC ACT 2006 (MOBILE PHONES-PRESCRIBED NUMBERS) REGULATIONS 2006**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(7)(a) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) and section 5(1) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Mobile Phones - Prescribed Numbers Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. The numbers prescribed for the purposes of section 3(7)(a) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) are 999 and 112.

**Explanatory Note**

***(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)***

[Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec3) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) provides that it is a defence for a person charged with the offence of driving while using a mobile phone to show that he/she was using the phone to call the Gardaí, an ambulance a fire or other emergency service on numbers prescribed for such a service. This Regulation prescribes 999 and 112 as the emergency numbers.
