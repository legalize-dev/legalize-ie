# S.I. No. 210/2015 - National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd June*, 2015.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), and with the consent of the Taoiseach, the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, the Minister for Education and Skills, the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, and the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2015.

###### 2.

2. The bodies specified in the Schedule are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000).

SCHEDULE

*Article 2*

Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board

An Bord Iascaigh Mhara

The commission established by the Commission of Investigation (Ronan MacLochlainn) Order 2014 ( [S.I No. 346 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0346.html) )

The commission established by the Commission of Investigation (Certain Matters relative to the Cavan/Monaghan Division of the Garda Síochána) Order 2015 ( [S.I. No. 38 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0038.html) )

The commission established by the Commission of Investigation (Certain Matters relative to An Garda Síochána and other persons) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 192 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0192.html) )

Charities Regulatory Authority

Marine Institute

Pyrite Resolution Board

Qualifications and Quality Assurance Authority of Ireland

Sea Fisheries Protection Authority

The Taoiseach consents to the making of the foregoing Order.
