CRIMINAL JUSTICE (LEGAL AID) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2005.

I, MICHAEL McDOWELL, Minister for Justice Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 10(1)(*b*) of the [Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1962) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

- (2) The Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Regulations 1965 to 2003 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Regulations 1965 to 2005.

- (3) These Regulations are deemed to have come into operation on 1 June 2005.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations “prison” means a place of custody administered by the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform other than a Garda Síochána station.

- (2) In these Regulations a reference to a Schedule is a reference to a Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. (1) The scales of payment of fees payable to a solicitor assigned, pursuant to a legal aid certificate, to a person charged before the District Court or to an appellant in the Circuit Court shall be as follows:

- (*a*) from 1 June 2005 to 30 November 2005, those specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1,

- (*b*) from 1 December 2005 to 31 May 2006, those specified in Part 1 of Schedule 3, and

- (*c*) from 1 June 2006, those specified in Part 1 of Schedule 5.

- (2) The rate of payment of fee payable to a solicitor assigned, pursuant to a legal aid certificate, to a person before the Circuit Court or Special Criminal Court in relation to an application to be admitted to bail that is contested by the prosecution shall be as follows:

    - (*a*) from 1 June 2005 to 30 November 2005, that specified in Part 2 of Schedule 1,

    - (*b*) from 1 December 2005 to 31 May 2006, that specified in Part 2 of Schedule 3, and

    - (*c*) from 1 June 2006, that specified in Part 2 of Schedule 5.

- (3) The rate of payment of fee payable to a solicitor assigned, pursuant to a legal aid certificate, to a person in respect of any visit to the person in prison that is essential to the preparation and conduct of the person's defence appeal or case stated, as the case may be, shall be as follows:

    - (*a*) from 1 June 2005 to 30 November 2005, that specified in Part 3 of Schedule 1,

    - (*b*) from 1 December 2005 to 31 May 2006, that specified in Part 3 of Schedule 3, and

    - (*c*) from 1 June 2006, that specified in Part 3 of Schedule 5.

###### 4.

4. (1) The rate of payment of fee payable to counsel assigned, pursuant to a legal aid certificate, to a person before the Circuit Court or Special Criminal Court in relation to an application to be admitted to bail that is contested by the prosecution shall be as follows:

- (*a*) from 1 June 2005 to 30 November 2005, that specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2,

- (*b*) from 1 December 2005 to 31 May 2006, that specified in Part 1 of Schedule 4, and

- (*c*) from 1 June 2006, that specified in Part 1 of Schedule 6.

- (2) The rate of payment of fee payable to counsel assigned, pursuant to a legal aid certificate, to a person in respect of any visit to the person in prison that is essential to the preparation and conduct of the person's defence appeal or case stated, as the case may be, shall be as follows:

    - (*a*) from 1 June 2005 to 30 November 2005, that specified in Part 2 of Schedule 2,

    - (*b*) from 1 December 2005 to 31 May 2006, that specified in Part 2 of Schedule 4, and

    - (*c*) from 1 June 2006, that specified in Part 2 of Schedule 6.

###### 5.

5. The Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 713 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0713.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for an increase in the fees payable under the Criminal Legal Aid Scheme to solicitors for attendance in the District Court and for appeals to the Circuit Court, and for an increase to solicitors and counsel in respect of essential visits to prisons and other custodial centres (other than Garda Stations) and for certain bail applications, as follows;

1.5% with effect from 1st June 2005

1.5% with effect from 1st December 2005

2.5% with effect from 1st June 2006.
