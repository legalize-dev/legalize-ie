# S.I. No. 150/1998 - Housing (Improvement Grants) (Thatched Roofs) Regulations, 1990 (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 150 of 1998.

HOUSING (IMPROVEMENT GRANTS) (THATCHED ROOFS) REGULATIONS, 1990 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966), as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1992), and by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), which powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 427 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0427.html) ), I, ROBERT MOLLOY, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Improvement Grants) (Thatched Roofs) Regulations, 1990 (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1990" means the Housing (Improvement Grants) (Thatched Roofs) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0300.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Article 4 of the Regulations of 1990 is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (*b*) of the following:

- "(*b*) Where work commenced—

    - (i) before 21st day of February, 1996,£2,000, or, in the case of a house on an island specified in the Schedule,£3,000, or

    - (ii) on or after 21st day of February, 1996,£2,400, or, in the case of a house on an island specified in the Schedule,£3,600."

###### 4.

4. Article 7 of the Regulations of 1990 is hereby amended by the insertion of "appropriate" before "amount specified in article 4 (*b*)".

Dated this 22nd day of April, 1998.

ROBERT MOLLOY,

Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance. Dated this 1st day of May, 1998.

CHARLIE McCREEVY,

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for increases in the grants for the renewal or repair of thatched roofs of houses.
