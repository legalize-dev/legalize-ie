# S.I. No. 104/2012 - Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th April*, 2012.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 567 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0567.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended as follows:

By substituting for Regulation 10(1)—

> “Subject to paragraph (2) and (3A), following a test, the issuing authority shall issue a test certificate for the vehicle tested, where the test report relating to the vehicle shows that all the items specified in Schedule 3 applicable at the time of testing and tested in respect of the vehicle are satisfactory.”

At Regulation 10 by adding the following after subsection (3)—

> - “(3A) Where the issuing authority exercises its discretion to carry out the test or a re-test notwithstanding Regulation 5(*f*), the issuing authority may refuse to issue a test certificate for the vehicle tested until it receives the required identification from the person who presented the vehicle to be tested.”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 April 2012.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide that the National Car Testing Service may refuse to issue a test certificate if required identification is not presented, until such required identification is presented.

For these purposes, these Regulations amend the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 567 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0567.html) ).
