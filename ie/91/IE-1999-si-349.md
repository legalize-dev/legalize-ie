# S.I. No. 349/1999 - Courts Service Act, 1998 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Courts Service Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Courts Service Act, 1998 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The 9th day of November, 1999, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Courts Service Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1998).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 9th day of November, 1999. |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE, |
|  | Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides that the 9th November, 1999 is the Establishment Day for the purposes of the [Courts Service Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) .
