S.I. No. 196 of 1984.

CORONERS ACT, 1962 (FEES AND EXPENSES) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) (1), [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0029.html#sec29) and [57](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0057.html#sec57) of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1962), and the Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 269 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0269.html) ), and after, in relation to the fees and expenses to be prescribed under section 57 of that Act, consultation with the Minister for the Environment, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1962).

###### 3.

3. The fee to be paid to a coroner who holds an inquest by virtue of section 20 of the Act or to a coroner, other than the coroner who would ordinarily hold the inquest, who holds an inquest by virtue of section 24 of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) with effect from the 1st day of December, 1981, £12.88,

- (*b*) with effect from the 1st day of March, 1982, £13.65,

- (*c*) with effect from the 1st day of October, 1982, £14.33,

- (*d*) with effect from the 1st day of September, 1983, £15.01, and

- (*e*) with effect from the 1st day of February, 1984, £15.50.

###### 4.

4. The fee chargeable by a coroner or a county registrar for furnishing a copy of any document to an applicant under section 29 of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) in the case of a coroner, £2.65 for each document subject to a maximum charge of £15.98, in respect of all documents furnished under subsection (3) of the said section relating to one inquest,

- (*b*) in the case of a county registrar, in respect of all documents supplied by him under subsection (4) of the said section, for each folio of 72 words, £0.50.

###### 5.

5. The fees and expenses to be prescribed under section 57 of the Act in respect of post-mortem and special examinations, the attendance of witnesses at inquests and the removal or custody of a body shall be those specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 6.

6. The [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 (S.I. No. 416 of 1981), are hereby revoked.

EXPENSES

5. The expenses payable to a registered medical practitioner (other than a person appointed under section 33 (2) of the Act) for his attendance at an inquest in his professional capacity or to perform a post-mortem examination (except where there is an inquest and it is held on the same day as the examination) shall be an amount equal to a subsistence allowance at the highest rate applying to a person holding a position in the Civil Service of the State, and first class rail fare where travel by rail is suitable, or the appropriate mileage rate applying to a person holding a position in the Civil Service of the State.

6. The expenses payable to a person who conducts a post-mortem examination arranged under section 33 (2) of the Act for his attendance for the examination (and also for his attendance at the inquest if there is one and it is held on a day other than that on which the examination was conducted) shall be an amount equal to subsistence and travelling expenses at the highest rate applying to a person holding a position in the Civil Service of the State.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe various fees and expenses for the purposes of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , viz., the fee to be paid to a coroner who holds an inquest outside his own district; the fee chargeable by the coroner or a county registrar for furnishing copies of inquest documents and the fees and expenses payable in respect of post-mortem and special examinations, the attendance of witnesses at inquests and the removal or custody of a body.

These regulations replace the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 (S.I. No. 416 of 1981).
