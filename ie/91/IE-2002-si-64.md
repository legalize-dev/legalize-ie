# S.I. No. 64/2002 - Valuation (Revisions and New Valuations) (Fees) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Valuation Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988) hereby making the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation (Revisions and New Valuations (Fees) Regulations 2002.

###### 2.

2. The fee to be charged in respect of an application by an owner or occupier of any property or by a rating authority under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Valuation Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988) shall be €250, in respect of each rateable hereditament separately valued as such by the Commissioner of Valuation. All such applications shall be accompanied by an initial payment of €250 and the balance, if any, of the fee shall be paid by the applicant within 45 days of receipt of notification of the determination of the Commissioner of Valuation as to the number of separate rateable hereditaments in respect of the said application.

###### 3.

3. The Valuation (Revisions and New Valuations) (Fees) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0418.html) ), as amended by the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001), are hereby revoked.
