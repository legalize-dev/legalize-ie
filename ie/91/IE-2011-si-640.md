# S.I. No. 640/2011 - European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th December*, 2011.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No.799/2011 of 9 August 2011[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin for Pesticide Residues) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 426 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0426.html) ) are amended by substituting in Regulation 2(1) the definition of “Commission Regulation 669/2009” for the following—

> “ ‘Commission Regulation 669/2009’ means Commission Regulation (EC) No. 669/2009 of 24 July 2009 as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 212/2010 of 12 March 2010, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 433/2011 of 4 May 2011 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 799/2011 of 9 August 2011;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 December 2011.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations revise controls on the import of certain food of non-animal origin from specific third countries with regard to pesticide residues.
