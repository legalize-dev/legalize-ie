# S.I. No. 295/1981 - Telex (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telex (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) The collective citation “the Telex Regulations, 1980 and 1981,” shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. (*a*) These Regulations, in so far as they specify the charges payable for the installation, connection for use or provision of a Telex Exchange Line, shall come into operation on 1st day of September, 1981.

- (*b*) These Regulations in so far as they provide for matters other than those mentioned in paragraph (*a*) of this Regulation, shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1981.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is:

- (*a*) to provide for an increase in the connection charge for Telex service from £150 to £170.

- (*b*) to provide for an increase in other Telex charges of 5% approximately.
