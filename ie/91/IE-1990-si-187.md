# S.I. No. 187/1990 - Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 187 of 1990.

SOCIAL WELFARE (RENT ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Housing (Private Rented Dwellings) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1982) and by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1982 to 1989, shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1982 to 1990.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 19th day of July, 1990.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1981" means the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) ;

"the Principal Regulations" means the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0220.html) );

"the Regulations of 1989" means the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0187.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Article 5 of the Principal Regulations (as amended by article 4 of the Regulations of 1989) is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution in sub-article (2) of—

- (i) "£107" for "£102",

- (ii) "£61" for "£59", and

- (iii) "£5.00" for "£4.60" in each case in which the latter sum appears;

( *b* ) the substitution in paragraph (*a*) of sub-article (3) of—

- (i) "£107" for "£102", and

- (ii) "£117" for "£112" in each case in which the latter sum appears;

( *c* ) the substitution in paragraph (*b*) of sub-article (3) of—

- (i) "£61" for "£59", and

- (ii) "£66" for "£64" in each case in which the latter sum appears.

###### 5.

5. Article 6 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the insertion after sub-paragraph (v) (inserted by article 7 of the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 171 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0171.html) )) of sub-article (1) (*a*) of the following sub-paragraph:—

> - "(vi) any sums received by way of payments by a health board in respect of a child who is boarded out.",

( *b* ) the substitution in sub-article (5) (inserted by article 4 of the Regulations of 1989) of "£11" for "£10".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare,

this 18th day of July, 1990.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 18th day of July, 1990.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the rules for the calculation of the amount of rent allowance so as to take account of the general increases in social welfare payments from July, 1990. The Regulations also exempt payments made by a health board in respect of a child who has been boarded out from being assessed as means for the purposes of the rent allowance.
