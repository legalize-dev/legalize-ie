# S.I. No. 95/1986 - Money Order Amendment (No. 29) Scheme, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 95 of 1986.

MONEY ORDER AMENDMENT (No. 29) SCHEME, 1986.

AN POST, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Communications, hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Money Order Amendment (No. 29) Scheme, 1986.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on the 3rd day of March, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. In this Scheme—

"the Principal Regulations" means the Money Order Regulations, 1903;

"the Regulations of 1982" means the Money Order Amendment (No. 27) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 61 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0061.html) );

"the Regulations of 1985" means the Money Order Amendment (No. 28) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0138.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Regulation 18 (1) of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the substitution of the following tables for Tables A, B and C (inserted by Regulations 5 of the Regulations of 1985) which set out the poundage payable in respect of money orders:

###### 6.

6. Regulation 25 of the Principal Regulations, as amended by Regulation 6 of the Regulations of 1985 is hereby amended by the substitution of "a supplementary fee of thirty pence" for "a supplementary fee of twenty-five pence".

###### 7.

7. Regulation 26 of the Principal Regulations (inserted by Regulation 7 of the Regulations of 1982 and as amended by Regulation 7 of the Regulations of 1985) is hereby amended by the substitution of "thirty pence" for "twenty-five pence".

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Scheme applies to the Money Order service and comes into operation on the 3rd March, 1986. It authorises increases in charges on inland and foreign money orders, telegraph money orders and in other miscellaneous charges.
