# S.I. No. 614/2003 - District Court (Maintenance) Rules, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , and by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/sec0021.html#sec21) (1) of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Maintenance) Rules, 2003.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 11th day of December, 2003 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. The Forms numbered 54.5, 54.6, 54.7 and 54.8 respectively in Schedule 1 hereof shall be substituted for the Forms numbered 54.5, 54.6, 54.7 and 54.8 respectively in Schedule C of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).
