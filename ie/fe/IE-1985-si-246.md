# S.I. No. 246/1985 - Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930 (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 246 of 1985.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE (FRESH MEAT) ACT, 1930 (EXPORTER'S LICENCES) (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 51 of and the Schedule (inserted by the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1978)) to the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1930), and the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following regulations with respect to which, pursuant to [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) (9) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (inserted by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0006.html#sec6) (l) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ), a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. (1) In this Regulation "Rule 1" means Rule 1 of the Schedule (Inserted by the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .

- (2) For the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of Rule 1, the sum of £0.75 is hereby prescribed, for the period beginning on the 1st day of April, 1985, and ending on the commencement of these Regulations, as the appropriate sum for a pork exporter's licence.

- (3) For the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of Rule 1, the sum of £0.75 is hereby prescribed, for the period beginning on the commencement of these Regulations, as the appropriate sum for a pork exporter's licence.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5 of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0412.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of July, 1985.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations reduce as and from 1 April 1985 fees payable by fresh meat exporters for veterinary examination of pigs in accordance with the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .
