# S.I. No. 192/2022 - Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences) (Ukraine) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th April,* 2022.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23A of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences) (Ukraine) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. In this Order –

“Category B” has the same meaning as it has in Regulation 6 of the Regulations of 2006;

“Regulations of 2006” means the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that a licence or permit permitting a person, to whom [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0066/print.html#sec60) of the [International Protection Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0066/print.html) (No. 66 of 2015) applies, to drive a mechanically propelled vehicle, not being a licence or permit issued to the person to enable that person to learn or provisionally to drive such a vehicle, issued by the competent authority of Ukraine in a category which corresponds to category B in the Table to Regulation 6 of the Regulations of 2006, is recognised for the purpose of exchange of that licence or permit for a driving licence.

###### 4.

4. Notwithstanding Regulation 10(2) of the Regulations of 2006, a driving licence granted in exchange for a licence or permit specified in Article 3 shall be so granted for a period of 12 months beginning on the day on which the licence is granted.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 41(8) of the Regulations of 2006 does not apply to the exchange of a licence or permit specified in Article 3.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 April, 2022.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Order provides for a driving licence issued by the licensing authority of Ukraine to be exchanged for an Irish driving licence to drive a car (category B only) for a period of 12 months.
