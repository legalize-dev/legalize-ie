# S.I. No. 209/1980 - Dublin District Milk Board (Minimum Prices For Milk) Order, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 209 of 1980.

DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD (MINIMUM PRICES FOR MILK) ORDER, 1980.

I, RAY MacSHARRY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1980, and shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1980.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"registered producers" means persons registered in the register of producers kept by the Dublin District Milk Board;

"registered retailers" means persons registered in the register of retailers kept by the Dublin District Milk Board;

"registered wholesalers" means persons registered in the register of wholesalers kept by the Dublin District Milk Board.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Dublin District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0097.html) ), is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. The minimum prices to be paid to registered producers for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and retailers shall be at the rates shown in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE

Minimum Prices

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Sold in May, June or July | Sold in April or August | Sold in January, February, March, September, October, November or December |
| Price per gallon (whether supplied at the registered premises of the producer, or at a collection place agreed on with the purchaser,orotherwise supplied). | 53.0p | 56.2p | 70.0p |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30 day of June, 1980.

- (Signed) RAY MacSHARRY,

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the minimum prices to be paid to registered producers, as from 1st July, 1980 for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and registered retailers in the Dublin District.
