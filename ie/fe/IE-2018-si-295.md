# S.I. No. 295/2018 - European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations, 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th July*, 2018.

I, Heather Humphreys, Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^1] as amended by Council Directive 2017/738/EU of 27 March 2017[^2] , Commission Directive 2017/774/EU[^3] of 3 May 2017 and Commission Directive 2017/898/EU[^4] of 24 May 2017 hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and Commencement*

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations, 2018.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation from—

    - (*a*) 28October 2018 as regards Council Directive 2017/738/EU relating to lead,

    - (*b*) 4November 2018 as regards Commission Directive 2017/774/EU relating to phenol,

    - (*c*) 26November 2018 as regards Commission Directive 2017/898/EU relating to bisphenol A.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Safety of Toys) Regulations, 2011 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0014.html) ), are hereby amended in Regulation 4(1), by substituting for the definition of “Toy Safety Directive”:

“Toy Safety Directive” means Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^1] as amended by—

- (*a*) Commission Directive 2012/7/EU of 2 March 2012[^5] ,

- (*b*) Commission Regulation (EU) No 681/2013 of 17 July 2013[^6] ,

- (*c*) Commission Directive 2014/79/EU of 20 June 2014[^7] ,

- (*d*) Commission Directive 2014/81/EU of 23 June 2014[^8] ,

- (*e*) Commission Directive 2014/84/EU of 30 June 2014[^9] ,

- (*f*) Commission Directive 2015/2115/EU of 23 November 2015[^10] ,

- (*g*) Commission Directive 2015/2116/EU of 23 November 2015[^11] ,

- (*h*) Commission Directive 2015/2117/EU of 23 November 2015[^12] ,

- (*i*) Council Directive 2017/738/EU of 27 March 2017[^2],

- (*j*) Commission Directive 2017/774/EU of 3 may 2017[^3],

- (*k*) Commission Directive 2017/898/EU of 24 May 20174”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 July 2018.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Council Directive 2017/738/EU of 27 March 2017, Commission Directive 2017/774/EU of 3 May 2017 and Commission Directive 2017/898/EU of 24 May 2017. Council Directive 2017/738/EU amends, for the purpose of adaptation to technical progress, point 13 of the table in Part III of Annex II of Directive 2009/48/EC dealing with the permitted use of lead in toys. Commission Directive 2017/774/EU and Commission Directive 2017/898/EU each amend, for the purpose of adaptation to technical progress, Appendix C to Annex II of Directive 2009/48/EC dealing with the permitted use of phenol and bisphenol A in toys.
