LOCAL ELECTIONS (FORMS) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd April*, 2009.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec3) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) , as amended, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Elections (Forms) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. Each form in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form for the purpose for which such form is expressed to be applicable.

###### 3.

3. The Local Elections (Forms) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 689 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0689.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations prescribe the forms to be used by candidates, national agents, designated persons and third parties for furnishing statements of donations and election expenses at local elections in accordance with [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec13) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) . They repeal [S.I. No. 689 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0689.html) , which prescribed the forms used in respect of the 2004 local elections.
