# S.I. No. 185/2013 - Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th June*, 2013.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsections (1) and (8) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1998), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. A Referendum Commission is hereby established to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , as amended by the [Referendum Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0053/print.html) , in relation to the proposal for an amendment of the Constitution contained in the Thirty-second Amendment of the Constitution (Abolition of Seanad Éireann) Bill 2013.

###### 3.

3. The 6th day of June 2013 is hereby appointed to be the day for the purposes of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) (5) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the chairperson of the Referendum Commission established by Article 2 of this order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 June 2013.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order establishes a Referendum Commission for the purposes of the Referendum Acts 1998 and 2001 in connection with the Thirty-second Amendment of the Constitution (Abolition of Seanad Éireann) Bill 2013 and appoints the 6th June 2013 as the day on which the position of chairperson of the Commission takes effect.
