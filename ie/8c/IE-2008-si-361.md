# S.I. No. 361/2008 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 361 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0361.html)

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th September*, 2008.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 4(1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 2008.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 2007 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 2008.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

## SCHEDULE

Personal Injuries Assessment Board

Sea Fisheries Protection Authority

National Consumer Agency

Teaching Council of Ireland

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 September 2008

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and “approved organisations”, and between one “approved organisation” and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as “approved organisations”.
