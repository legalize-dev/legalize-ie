# S.I. No. 581/2011 - Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th November*, 2011.

I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 240 (as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec9) of the [Defence (Amendment) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) (No. 17 of 2011)) of the [Defence Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1954) and of all other powers enabling me in this behalf, hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) 2011.

- (2) The Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) 2008 ( [S.I. No. 204 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0204.html) ) and these Rules may be cited together as the Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) 2008 and 2011.

###### 2.
