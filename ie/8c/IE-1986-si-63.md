# S.I. No. 63/1986 - State Guarantees Act, 1954 (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 63 of 1986.

STATE GUARANTEES ACT, 1954 (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE) ORDER, 1985.

The Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1985.

- (2) The [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule Orders, 1957 to 1983, and this Order may be cited together as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Orders, 1957 to 1985.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , is hereby amended by the addition of "Dublin Port and Docks Board" to the bodies specified in the second column and the insertion in the third column of "£7,000,000" in respect of that body.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government this 19th day of

December, 1985.

**GARRET FITZGERALD,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order enables the guaranteeing by the State, pursuant to the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , of borrowings by Dublin Port and Docks Board up to a maximum of £7,000,000.
