# S.I. No. 58/1973 - Higher Education Authority Act, 1971 (Designation of Institution of Higher Education) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 58 of 1973.

HIGHER EDUCATION AUTHORITY ACT, 1971 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, PÁDRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1971), and after consultation with an tÚdarás um Ard-Oideachas, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Designation of Institution of Higher Education) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. Each of the institutions specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby designated as an institution of higher education for the purposes of the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1971).

SCHEDULE

The College of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland.

The Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this twelfth day of March, 1973.

**PÁDRAIG FAULKNER,**

Minister for Education.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations provide that the institutions mentioned in the schedule of these regulations will be institutions of higher education within the meaning of the [Higher Education Authority Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , Section 1.
