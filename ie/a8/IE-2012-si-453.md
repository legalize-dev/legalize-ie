# S.I. No. 453/2012 - Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th November*, 2012.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

- (*a*) in Regulation 4(2), by substituting the following-

  > - “(2) The Marine Survey Office is the competent authority in the State for the purposes of—

  >     - (*a*) Articles 39(2), 39(3), 40 and 41(2) of the Council Regulation, and

  >     - (*b*) Articles 7(4), 62(6) and 63 of the Commission Regulation.”

- (*b*) in Regulation 4(3), by substituting the following-

  > - “(3) A surveyor of ships may carry out the functions of a sea fisheries protection officer under [sections 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec17) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec18) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) for the purpose of Articles 39(2) and (3) and 41(2) of the Council Regulation and Article 62(6) and 63 of the Commission Regulation.”,

- (*c*) by substituting Regulation 7 for the following—

  > *“Obligations for a buyer*
  >
  > 7. (1) A buyer shall comply with the requirements of the Council Regulation and Commission Regulation which relate to buyers.

  > - (2) Paragraph (1) shall apply to a buyer with an annual financial turnover in first sales of fisheries products of less than €200,000 in accordance with Article 62(2) of the Council Regulation.

  > - (3) The SFPA may, by notice, exempt a buyer from the obligations in paragraph (2).

  > - (4) A notice issued under paragraph (3) may be issued or published in a manner the SFPA considers appropriate.”, and

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal Interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend [S.I. No. 320 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0320.html) which implement Council Regulation (EC) No. 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 which relate to fisheries control systems and rules for the recording of fish catches and are made under the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) . The regulations amend the articles of the regulations for which the Marine Survey Office is the competent authority within the state and also the list of ports within the State designated for the landing of pelagic species and stocks subject to a multi-annual plan.
