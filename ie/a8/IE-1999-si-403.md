# S.I. No. 403/1999 - Patents and Trade Marks (Fees) Rules, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 99](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0099.html#sec99) and [114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0114.html#sec114) of the [Patents Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992), [sections 81](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0081.html#sec81) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Trade Marks, Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1996), (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 (S.I. 330 of 1997)), and with the sanction of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Patents and Trade Marks (Fees) Rules, 1999.

- (2) These Rules shall come into operation on the 20th day of December, 1999.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Rules, unless the context otherwise requires—

“Act of 1992” means the [Patents Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992);

“Act of 1996” means the [Trade Marks Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1996);

“certification mark” has the meaning assigned to it by section 55 of the Act of 1996;

“collective mark” has the meaning assigned to it by section 54 of the Act of 1996;

“designating” means—

- (i) in relation to an application for a patent, designating (in pursuance of the European Patent Convention or the Treaty, as the case may be) the state or states in which protection is sought for an invention,

- (ii) in relation to a patent, designating (in pursuance of the European Patent Convention) the state or states in which the patent has effect;

“European patent” means a patent granted under the European Patent Convention;

“European Patent Convention” means the Convention on the Grant of European Patents signed at Munich on the 5th day of October, 1973;

“international application for a patent” means an application made under the Treaty;

“patent” means an exclusive right conferred pursuant to Part II or Part III of the Act of 1992;

“patent application” means an application under Part II or Part III of the Act of 1992 for a patent;

“proprietor”, in relation to a patent, means the person to whom the patent was granted or the person whose title is subsequently registered under section 85 of the Act of 1992;

“published”—

- (*a*) in relation to any document which is required to be published under the Act of 1992, means made available to the public and includes the making available for inspection of the document as of right, at the Office and such other place (if any) as may be for the time being specified for the purpose by the Minister, by the public, whether on payment of a fee or not, and

- (*b*) in relation to any other document, matter, record or information which the Controller is authorised to publish, means made available to the public and includes the making available of the document, matter, record or information in any form at the Office and such other place (if any) as may be for the time being specified for the purpose by the Minister, whether on payment of a fee or not;

“right”, in relation to any patent or application, includes an interest in the patent or application and, without prejudice to the foregoing, any reference in these Rules to a right in a patent includes a reference to a share in the patent;

“1992 Rules” means the Patents Rules, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 179 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0179.html) );

“1996 Rules” means the Trade Mark Rules, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 199 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0199.html) );

“Schedule” means a Schedule to these Rules;

“trade mark” has the meaning assigned to it by section 6 of the Act of 1996;

“the Treaty” means the treaty known as the Patent Co-operation Treaty signed at Washington on the 19th day of June, 1970.

- (2) In Schedule I, and II, unless otherwise indicated—

    - (*a*) a reference to a section or Part is a reference to a section or Part of the Act specified in the heading of that Schedule,

    - (*b*) a reference to a Rule is a reference to a Rule of the Rules specified in the heading of that Schedule.

- (3) In these Rules, a reference to any statute or rules shall be construed as a reference to that statute or those rules as amended, adapted or extended by or under any subsequent statute or rules including these Rules.

###### 3.

3. (1) There is hereby prescribed in respect of the Act of 1992 and the 1992 Rules the fee specified in column (3) or (4) of Schedule 1 in respect of each matter mentioned in column (2) opposite the reference number concerned.

- (2) There is hereby prescribed in respect of the Act of 1996 and the 1996 Rules the fee specified in column (3) or (4) of Schedule 2 in respect of each matter mentioned in column (2) opposite the reference number concerned.

- (3) Where a person has paid the prescribed fee in respect of the matter at reference number 60 of Schedule 1 that person shall not be required to pay the prescribed fee in relation to the matter at reference number 37 of Schedule 2.

- (4) Where a person has paid the prescribed fee in respect of the matter at reference number 37 of Schedule 2 that person shall not be required to pay the prescribed fee in relation to the matter at reference number 60 of Schedule 1.

###### 4.

4. Fees prescribed by these Rules shall be paid in the currency of the State as provided for by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Central Bank Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1989), as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1998).

###### 5.

5. Schedules I and III to, and so much of Rule 3 as relates to those Schedules of, the Patents, Trade Marks, Copyright and Designs (Fees) Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 433 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0433.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules, made under [sections 99](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0099.html#sec99) and [114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0114.html#sec114) of the [Patents Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , and [sections 81](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0081.html#sec81) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , abolish and restructure certain fee items. They also provide a new fee for the dial-in connection for access to patent and trade mark information and for photocopying documents relating to patents.
