# S.I. No. 158/1991 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 158 of 1991.

PRISONS ACT, 1970 (SECTION 7) ORDER, 1991.

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The period of 2 years beginning on the 28th day of June, 1991, is hereby specified for the purposes of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of June, 1991.

**RAY BURKE,**

Minister for Justice.
