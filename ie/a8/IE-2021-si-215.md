# S.I. No. 215/2021 - Road Traffic (Driving Instructor Licensing) (Extension of Period of Validity of Licence) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th May,* 2021.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Driving Instructor Licensing) (Extension of Period of Validity of Licence) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations –

“ADI card” shall be construed in accordance with Regulation 15(3)(b) of the Regulations of 2009 insofar as it relates to a driving instructor’s licence but does not relate to a trainee driving instructor’s licence;

“driving instructor’s licence” has the same meaning as it has in the Regulations of 2009;

“Regulations of 2009” means the Road Traffic (Driving Instructor Licensing) (No. 2) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 203 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0203.html) );

“trainee driving instructor’s licence” has the same meaning as it has in the Regulations of 2009.

###### 3.

3. Notwithstanding Regulations 27(1) and 28(3) of the Regulations of 2009, a driving instructor’s licence (other than a trainee driving instructor’s licence) granted during the period beginning on 1 September 2018 and ending on 3 January 2021 shall expire at the end of the period of 3 years from the date of grant of the licence.

###### 4.

4. A holder of a driving instructor’s licence (other than a trainee driving instructor’s licence) referred to in Regulation 3 shall, notwithstanding the period referred to in Regulation 32(1) of the Regulations of 2009, pass a check test as provided for in that Regulation 32 within the period of 3 years from the date of grant of the licence.

###### 5.

5. Notwithstanding Schedules 5 and 6 of the Regulations of 2009, the date of expiry of a driving instructor’s licence (other than a trainee driving instructor’s licence) referred to in Regulation 3 and of an ADI card issued to the holder of such a licence shall be 1 year from the date of expiry specified on the licence and on the ADI card to be the date of its expiry.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 May, 2021.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Driver Instructor Licensing) (No. 2) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 203 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0203.html) ) to provide that a driving instructor’s licence issued during the period beginning on 1 September 2018 and ending on 03 January 2021 is extended by 1 year.
