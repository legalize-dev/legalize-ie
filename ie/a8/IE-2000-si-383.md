EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order sets out the amendments to the Schedule to the [Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) as provided by Section 3 of the Act. The amendments consist of additions to, deletions from and substitutions to the Schedule. They are necessary to take account of changes, which have come about since the Act came into operation on 2 January 1998. The Schedule, as amended, is set out in the Schedule to this Order. The amended Schedule will be effective from 2 January 2001.
