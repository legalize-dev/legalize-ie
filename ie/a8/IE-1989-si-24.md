# S.I. No. 24/1989 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in Urban District of Buncrana) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 24 of 1989.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN URBAN DISTRICT OF BUNCRANA) ORDER, 1989.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for Health by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), which said powers are delegated to me by the Health (Delegation of Functions) Order, 1988, I, TERRY LEYDEN, Minister of State at the Department of Health, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in Urban District of Buncrana) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of April, 1989 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the Urban District of Buncrana unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

Dated this 6th day of February, 1989.

TERRY LEYDEN,

Minister of State at the Department

of Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of April, 1989 the sale of milk in the Urban District of Buncrana unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
