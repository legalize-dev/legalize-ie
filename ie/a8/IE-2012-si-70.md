# S.I. No. 70/2012 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th March*, 2012.

I, JOAN BURTON T.D., Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5(2A) (as amended by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec44) of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2006)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 4(1)(a) and Article 4(3) of Directive 2010/78/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010[^1] amending Directive 2003/41/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 June 2003[^2] on the activities and supervision of institutions for occupational retirement provisions hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012.

- (2) These Regulations shall be included in the collective citation of the Occupational Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2006 to 2012.

*Amendment of Occupational Pension Schemes Disclosure Regulations*

###### 2.

2. The Occupational Pension Schemes (Disclosure of Information) Regulations 2006 (S.I. 301 of 2006) are amended by inserting the following after article 21—

> **“***Disclosure of Information to EIOPA*
>
> 22. The Board may disclose information to the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority established by Regulation (EU) No. 1094/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^3] for the purpose of enabling or assisting the performance by the Board or the State of its obligations under the Directive 2010/78/EU1.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 March 2012.

**JOAN BURTON,**

Minister for Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations enable the Pensions Board disclose information to the European Supervisory Authority, (European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA)) established by Regulation (EU) No. 1094/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council and as required by Directive 2010/78/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council.
