# S.I. No. 25/1989 - Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 25 of 1989.

COURTS-MARTIAL (LEGAL AID) REGULATIONS, 1989

I, MICHAEL J. NOONAN, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 425 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0425.html) ), as amended by the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0125.html) ), are hereby amended with effect from the 1st day of January, 1989

( *a* ) by the substitution in Regulation 12 (2) (*a*) of "£101.72" for "£99.24" and of "£45.24" for "£44.14", and

( *b* ) by the substitution in Regulation 14 of "£26.37" for "£25.73".

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 7th day of February, 1989.

**MICHAEL J. NOONAN**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 7th day of February,

1989.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS.**
