# S.I. No. 40/2008 - European Communities (Food Additives Other Than Colours and Sweeteners) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 40 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0040.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (FOOD ADDITIVES OTHER THAN COLOURS AND SWEETENERS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th February*, 2008.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to specific provisions relating to food additives other than colours and sweeteners of Directive 2006/52/EC [^1] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 July 2006, amending Directive 95/2/EC [^2] on food additives other than colours and sweeteners and Directive 94/35/EC [^3] on sweeteners for use in foodstuffs, hereby make the following regulations—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Food Additives other than Colours and Sweeteners) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2005 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Food Additives other than Colours and Sweeteners) Regulations 2004 to 2008.

- (3) Subject to Regulation 4(3), these Regulations shall come into operation on 15 August 2008.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Minister” means the Minister for Health and Children;

“Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Food Additives other than Colours and Sweeteners) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 58 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0058.html) ), and

“Regulations of 2005” means the European Communities (Food Additives other than Colours and Sweeteners) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0369.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1) by inserting the following words in the definition of “Annexes” after “22 December 2003”:

  > “and Directive 2006/52/EC 5b of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 July 2006”

and by adding the following footnote thereto:

> “ 5b OJ L 204, 26.7.2006, p. 10.”;

- (*b*) in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “carriers” the following definition:

  > “‘carriers’ (including carrier solvents) means substances used to dissolve, dilute, disperse or otherwise physically modify a food additive or flavouring without altering its function (and without exerting any technological effect themselves) in order to facilitate its handling, application or use;”;

- (*c*) in Regulation 2(1) by inserting the following words in the definition of “Directive” after “22 December 2003”:

  > “and Directive 2006/52/EC 5b of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 July 2006”;

- (*d*) in Regulation 5(2) by substituting the words “infant formulae, follow-on formulae and weaning foods” with the words “infant formulae, follow-on formulae, processed cereal-based foods and baby foods”.

###### 4.

4. (1) Products placed on the market or labelled before 15 August 2008 which do not comply with the provisions of Directive 2006/52/EC 1 relating to food additives other than colours and sweeteners may be marketed until stocks are exhausted.

- (2) Such provisions of these Regulations as give effect to Directive 2006/52/EC 1 shall not apply to the stocks referred to in paragraph (1).

- (3) Products complying with Directive 2006/52/EC 1 may be used and placed on the market from the date these Regulations are signed by the Minister.

- (4) In this Regulation, “Directive 2006/52/EC” means Directive 2006/52/EC [^1] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 July 2006 amending Directive 95/2/EC [^2] on food additives other than colours and sweeteners and Directive 94/35/EC [^3] on sweeteners for use in foodstuffs.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 February 2008

**MARY HARNEY**

Minister for Health and Children

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Food Additives other than Colours and Sweeteners) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 58 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0058.html) ). These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Food Additives other than Colours and Sweeteners) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

These Regulations give effect to Directive 2006/52/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 July 2006 insofar as it amends Directive 95/2/EC on food additives other than colours and sweeteners.
