# S.I. No. 236/2018 - Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th July*, 2018.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0010.html#sec10) , [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0014.html#sec14) and [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Sea Pollution Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1991) and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation 21 days after the date on which they are made.

###### 2.

2. The Supplement to the International Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate in Schedule 2 to the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 788 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0788.html) ) is amended in Form B—

- (*a*) by the deletion of paragraphs 1.11.8 and 1.11.9,

- (*b*) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph 5.1:

  > “5.1 In accordance with the requirements of regulation 18, the ship is qualified as a segregated ballast tanker in compliance with regulation 18.9 ....................................... ”,

- (*c*) by the deletion of paragraphs 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.1.3, 5.1.4, 5.1.5 and 5.1.6,

- (*d*) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph 5.2:

  > “5.2 Segregated ballast tanks (SBT) in compliance with regulation 18 are distributed as follows:
  >
  > |  |  |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  > |  | Tank | Volume (m^3) | Tank | Volume (m^3) |
  > |  |  |  |  |  |
  > |  |  |  | Total volume ................................. m^3 | Total volume ................................. m^3 |
  >
  > ”,

- (*e*) by the deletion of paragraphs 5.2.1, 5.2.2 and 5.2.3,

- (*f*) by the deletion of paragraphs 5.3, 5.3.1, 5.3.2, 5.3.3, 5.3.4 and 5.3.5,

- (*g*) by renumbering paragraphs 5.4, 5.4.1, 5.4.2, 5.4.3 and 5.4.4 as paragraphs 5.3, 5.3.1, 5.3.2, 5.3.3 and 5.3.4 as a result of the deletion, under paragraph (*f*), of paragraphs 5.3, 5.3.1, 5.3.2, 5.3.3, 5.3.4 and 5.3.5,

- (*h*) by the deletion of paragraph 5.5, 5.5.1 and 5.5.2, and

- (*i*) by renumbering all subsequent paragraphs in section 5 as a result of the deletion, under paragraph (*h*), of paragraphs 5.5, 5.5.1 and 5.5.2.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 June 2018.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Oil Pollution) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 788 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0788.html) ).

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for an amendment to the form of the Supplement to the International Oil Pollution Prevention Certificate, which is set out in Schedule 2 to the above-mentioned Regulations.
