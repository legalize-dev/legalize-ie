# S.I. No. 143/2004 - Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulation 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Bord na gCon in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sub-section (3) of [Section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0040.html#sec40) of the [Greyhound Industry Act 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958) hereby makes the following regulation, that is to say:

###### 1.

1. The percentage deduction on all totalisator pools operated by the board shall be 20%.

###### 2.

2. This regulation may be cited as the Greyhound Race Track (Percentage Deduction) Regulation 2004.

###### 3.

3. This regulation shall come into operation on the 12th day of April 2004.
