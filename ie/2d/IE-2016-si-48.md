# S.I. No. 48/2016 - Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th February*, 2016.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 41(1) (as last amended by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec60) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of the Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 6 of the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 460 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0460.html) ) is amended by inserting after paragraph (*a*) the following:

> - “(aa) in the case of a vehicle referred to in section 41(1)(*a*) of the Act of 1994, that the driver was, at the time when the vehicle was detained, the holder of a driving licence or learner permit licensing him or her to drive the vehicle,”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 February 2016.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations amend the regulations relating to Garda seizure of vehicles under [Section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) to allow waiving of charges for removal, detention and storage of a vehicle where the vehicle was seized on the basis that the driver did not have a valid driving licence or learner permit and it was subsequently demonstrated that they did have such a licence or permit.
