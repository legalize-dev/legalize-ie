# S.I. No. 4/2017 - Building Regulations (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th January*, 2017.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (as amended by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec4) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec7) of the [Building Control Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2007)) and [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Building Control Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990) (as adapted by the Environment, Community and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2016 ( [S.I. No. 394 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0394.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Building Regulations (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

- (2) These Regulations and the Building Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 497 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0497.html) ) may be cited together as the Building Regulations 1997 to 2017 and shall be construed together as one.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on the day after their making is notified in Iris Oifigiúil.

*Purpose of the Regulations*

###### 3.

3. The purpose for which these Regulations are made include, in particular, the giving of further effect to Directive 2010/31/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2011[^1] .

*Amendment of Article 5(4) of the Building Regulations 1997*

###### 4.

4. Article 5(4) of the Building Regulations 1997 is amended by inserting after the definition of “the Minister” the following:—

> “ ‘nearly zero-energy building’ means a building that has a very high energy performance, as determined in accordance with Annex I to Directive 2010/31/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 May 2010 on the energy performance of buildings (recast) (O.J. No. L 153, 18.6.2010, page 13). The nearly zero or very low amount of energy required should be covered to a very significant extent by energy from renewable sources, including energy from renewable sources produced on-site or nearby;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 January 2017.

**SIMON COVNEY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the Building Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 497 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0497.html) ) to include a definition of nearly zero energy buildings in accordance with the provisions of Article 2(2) of Directive 2010/31/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 May 2010 on the energy performance of buildings (recast).

Article 9(1) of Directive 2010/31/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 May 2010 on the energy performance of buildings (recast) requires that all new buildings:—

- (*a*) shall be nearly zero energy buildings by 31 December 2020;

- (*b*) occupied and owned by public authorities shall be nearly zero energy buildings after 31 December 2018.
