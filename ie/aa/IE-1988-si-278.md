# S.I. No. 278/1988 - Health (Superannuation Provisions) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 278 of 1988.

HEALTH (SUPERANNUATION PROVISIONS) ORDER, 1988.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), as amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Health (Superannuation Provisions) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The General Medical Services (Payments) Board (Establishment) Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0184.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition after article 19 thereof of the following article:—

> "20. Schemes and Regulations made under the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) shall apply to the Board as if it were a local authority".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 3rd day of November, 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for the Environment hereby consents to the making of the above Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 3rd day of November, 1988.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order amends the Establishment Order of the General Medical Services (Payments) Board in order to allow the revised superannuation arrangements for local authority staff made under the powers contained in the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) to be applied to the Board.
