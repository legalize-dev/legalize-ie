# S.I. No. 231/2010 - Waste Water Discharge (Authorisation) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 231 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0231.html)

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th May*, 2010.

I, JOHN GORMLEY, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (as inserted by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec2) of the [European Communities Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) ), consider it necessary for the purpose of giving full effect to Directive 2006/11/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 February 2006 on pollution caused by certain dangerous substances discharged into the aquatic environment of the Community [^1] and Directive 2000/60/EC of the European Council of 23 October 2000 establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy [^2] , hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Water Discharge (Authorisation) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

*Definitions*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“the 2007 Regulations” means the Waste Water Discharge (Authorisation) Regulations 2007.

*Amendment of Regulation 5 of the 2007 Regulations*

###### 3.

3. Regulation 5 (2) of the 2007 Regulations is amended by substituting 2011 for 2010.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 May 2010.

**JOHN GORMLEY,**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide that a water services authority must obtain certification from the EPA for waste water discharges from a waste water works serving a population equivalent of less than 500 with effect from 22 June 2011.
