# S.I. No. 434/1999 - Patents and Trade Marks (Fees) (No. 2) Rules, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 81](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0081.html#sec81) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1996), (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) )), and with the sanction of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Rules:

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Patents and Trade Marks (Fees) (No. 2) Rules, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The Patents and Trade Marks (Fees) Rules, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 403 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0403.html) ) are hereby amended—

- (*a*) by the substitution of “253.95” for “259.95” in column (4) at reference number 23 of Schedule 1,

- (*b*) by the substitution of “76.18” for “56.18” in column (4) at reference numbers 1 and 2 of Schedule 2 at each place where it occurs, and

- (*c*) by the substitution of “63.49” for “53.49” in column (4) at reference number 4 of Schedule 2.

The fees prescribed by these Rules are sanctioned by the Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules rectify errors in euro amounts shown in Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 of the Patents and Trade Marks (Fees) Rules, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 403 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0403.html) ).
