# RATES ON AGRICULTURAL LAND (RELIEF) ACT, 1950

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO EXTEND THE RATES ON AGRICULTURAL LAND (RELIEF) ACT, 1946. [26*th July*, 1950.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Application of the Rates on Agricultural Land (Relief) Act, 1946, to 1950-51.

**1.**—The Rates on Agricultural Land (Relief) Act, 1946 (No. 36 of 1946), shall apply to the local financial year ending on the 31st day of March, 1951.

###### 2 Short title and collective citation.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Rates on Agricultural Land (Relief) Act, 1950.

- (2) The Rates on Agricultural Land (Relief) Acts, 1939 to 1948, and this Act may be cited together as the Rates on Agricultural Land (Relief) Acts, 1939 to 1950.
