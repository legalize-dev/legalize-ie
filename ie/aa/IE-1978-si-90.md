# S.I. No. 90/1978 - Social Welfare (Single Woman's Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 90 of 1978.

SOCIAL WELFARE (SINGLE WOMAN'S ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1978.

The Minister for Social Welfare in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1952) and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0014/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (No. 2) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1974) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Single Woman's Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Single Woman's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Single Woman's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 and 1978.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1st day of April, 1978.

###### 4.

4. Article 5 of the Social Welfare (Single Woman's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 209 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0209.html) ) is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 29th

day of March 1978.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Regulation is to remove the condition for receipt of a Single Woman's Allowance which requires an applicant to have resided in the State for a period of at least two years at any time before her claim.
