# S.I. No. 356/2026 - European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th July,* 2026.

I, PETER BURKE, Minister for Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^1] as amended by Commission Directive (EU) 2026/192 of 28 January 2026[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations give effect to Commission Directive (EU) 2026/192 of 28 January 2026, which amends Appendix A of Annex II to Directive 2009/48/EC by adding cobalt (CMR 1B) to the list of substances permitted for use in toys under specified conditions.
