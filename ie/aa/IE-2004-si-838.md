# S.I. No. 838/2004 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations with respect to which, pursuant to section 2(5) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 2004.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2005.

###### 2.

2. The prescribed amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979) shall be—

- (*a*) as regards milk, 0.11 cent per litre and

- (*b*) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, €2.54 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 2003 ( [S.I. No. 714 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0714.html) ) are revoked.

|  | MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Agriculture and Food. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations, which come into effect on 1st January 2005 prescribe new rates of disease levies on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
