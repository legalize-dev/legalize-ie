# S.I. No. 138/2014 - National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th March*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), and with the consent of the Taoiseach, the Minister for Arts, Heritage and the Gaeltacht, the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs, the Minister for Defence, the Minister for Education and Skills, the Minister for Environment, Community and Local Government, the Minister for Health, the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, the Minister for Justice and Equality, the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, the Minister for Social Protection and the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. The bodies specified in the Schedule are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*j*) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000).
