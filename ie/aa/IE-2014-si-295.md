# S.I. No. 295/2014 - Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Members of Investor Compensation Company) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st July*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1998), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Investor Compensation Act 1998 (Members of Investor Compensation Company) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The following shall be members of the Investor Compensation Company Limited:

- (*a*) the Central Bank of Ireland;

- (*b*) the Irish Stock Exchange p.l.c.;

- (*c*) the Irish Association of Investment Managers.

###### 3.

3. The [Investor Compensation Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (Appointment of Members of Investor Compensation Company) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 352 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0352.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 May 2014.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Following the restructuring of the Irish Stock Exchange, an amendment is required in order to reflect the change in its corporate structure and name. This amendment will allow the Irish Stock Exchange p.l.c. (CRO Registration Number 539157) replace the Irish Stock Exchange Limited (CRO Registration Number 233947) as one of the bodies or persons that shall be members of the Investor Compensation Company.
