# S.I. No. 477/2001 - Companies (Fees) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Noel Treacy, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)), and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 265 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0265.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. (1) Articles 3 and 4 of this Order shall come into operation on 26 October 2001.

- (2) Articles 5, 6 and 7 of this Order shall come into operation on 1 January 2002.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order amends the Companies (Fees) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0358.html) ), as amended, and the Companies (Fees) Order, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 63 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0063.html) ).

The Order provides for -

- • a progressively increasing late filing fee where an annual return is not received by the registrar on time;

- • no charge in respect of the registration of certain documents with the CRO where, in addition to submitting a hard copy, the material is also supplied electronically to the CRO; and

- • Euro alternatives to the existing £ denominated fees as and from 1 January 2002.
