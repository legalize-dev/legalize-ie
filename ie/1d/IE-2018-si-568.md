# S.I. No. 568/2018 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st January*, 2019.

I, CATHERINE BYRNE, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 4) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 339 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0339.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998);

“Order of 2017” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 107 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0107.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Part II (as amended by Article 3 of the Order of 2017) of the First Schedule to the Act is amended—

- (*a*) by deleting the following statutory instrument:

  > “European Communities (Organic Farming) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 112 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0112.html) ) other than that which relates to Articles 8 to 18 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 834/2007 of 28 June 2007 on organic production and labelling of organic products and repealing Regulation (EEC) No. 2092/91.”, and

- (*b*) by inserting after “European Union (Plastics and other materials) (Contact with food) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0049.html) ).” the following statutory instrument:

  > “European Union (Organic Farming) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 683 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0683.html) ).”.
