S.I. No. 292 of 1983.

TRADE UNION (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 13 of the Trade Union Act, 1871, as adapted by section 2 of the Registry of Friendy Societies Act, 1936 (No. 51 of 1936), and [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1975), and the Industry, Commerce and Tourism (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0290.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance and with the consent of the Minister for Labour, insofar as these Regulations relate to fees chargeable under the said [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trade Union (Fees) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 17th day of October, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) The fees specified in Part I of the Schedule to these Regulations shall be payable in respect of matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1975, other than matters to be transacted under the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1975).

- (2) The fees specified in Part II of the Schedule shall be payable in respect of matters to be transacted under the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) .

- (3) No fees shall be payable in respect of the services specified in Part II of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Trade Union (Fee) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 86 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0086.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1975.
