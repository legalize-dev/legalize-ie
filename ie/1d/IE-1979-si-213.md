# S.I. No. 213/1979 - Control of Exports (Southern Rhodesia) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 213 of 1979.

CONTROL OF EXPORTS (SOUTHERN RHODESIA) ORDER, 1979.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1947), [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Control of Exports (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1956) (which Act is continued in force by the [Control of Exports (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Continuance) Act, 1971 (No. 5 of 1971)), the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Transfer of Powers) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 52 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0052.html) ) (as amended by the [Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Transfer of Powers) Order, 1956 (Amendment) Order, 1963 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0021.html) )), and the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0306.html) ), and after consultation with the Minister for Foreign Affairs, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Control of Exports (Southern Rhodesia) Order, 1979.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 25 day of June, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The exportation to Southern Rhodesia of any goods to which this Order applies is hereby prohibited save under and in accordance with a licence.

###### 3.

3. This Order applies to goods of a kind mentioned in the first column of the Schedule to the Imposition of Duties (No. 200) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0220.html) ), at any tariff code number, other than products to which the Agricultural and Fishery Products (Regulation of Export to Southern Rhodesia) Order, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 105 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0105.html) ), applies.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of June, 1979.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry, Commerce and

Energy.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to enable the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy to control the export to Southern Rhodesia of the goods specified in the Order.
