# S.I. No. 540/2023 - Statutory Instrument to Increase Fixed Payment Notices Under the Control of Dogs Act 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th November,* 2023.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Rural and Community Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0027.html#sec27) , [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0028.html#sec28) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Control of Dogs Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1986), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Dogs (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. (1) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1^st December 2023.

- (2) In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the Control of Dogs Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 442 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0442.html) ).

###### 3.

3. A breach of Article 5(2) of the Principal Regulations shall be an offence to which section 28(1) applies.

###### 4.

4. The Control of Dogs Regulations 1998 (S.I. No. 442/1990) are amended by the substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 10:

> “10. The prescribed amounts for the purpose of section 28(1) of the Act are as follows:
>
> - (a) €150, in respect of an offence referred to in section 27(1)(a),
>
> - (b) €300, in respect of an offence of failing to comply with section 9(1), referred to in section 27(1)(b),
>
> - (c) €250, in respect of an offence of failing to comply with section 10, referred to in section 27(1)(b),
>
> - (d) €150, in respect of an offence of failing to comply with section 13, referred to in section 27(1)(b),
>
> - (e) €150, in respect of an offence referred to in section 27(1)(c),
>
> - (f) €300, in respect of an offence being a breach of Article 5, referred to in section 27(1)(f) and
>
> - (g) €200, in respect of an offence being a breach of Article 6(1) or (2), referred to in section 27(1)(f).”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 November, 2023.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Rural and Community Development.
