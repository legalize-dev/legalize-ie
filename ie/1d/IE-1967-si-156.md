S.I. No. 156 of 1967.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 164) (CUSTOMS DUTIES AND FORM OF CUSTOMS TARIFF) ORDER, 1967

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 164) (Customs Duties and Form of Customs Tariff) Order, 1967.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1967.

###### 3.

3. (1) In this Order "the Principal Order" means the Imposition of Duties (No. 159) (Customs Duties and Form of Customs Tariff) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 132 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0132.html) ).

- (2) References in this Order to an Act or Order shall be construed as references to that Act or Order as amended by any subsequent Act or Order.

###### 4.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effects of the Order are :—

- (i) to make certain reductions in customs duties on goods of United Kingdom origin in accordance with the provisions of the Free Trade Area Agreement;

- (ii) to maintain in operation the special tariff concessions for certain goods of Northern Ireland origin;

- (iii) to exempt from import duty cameras which are designed solely for medical or surgical purposes; and

- (iv) to make minor editorial changes in the form of customs tariff.

The Order comes into operation on 1st July, 1967.
