# S.I. No. 92/1994 - Garda Síochána (Associations) (Superintendents and Chief Superintendents) (Amendment) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN, T.D., Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 13 of the Garda Síochána Act, 1924 (No. 25 of 1924), as amended by the Garda Síochána Act, 1977 (No. 24 of 1977), and [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), hereby with the approval of the Government, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Superintendents and Chief Superintendents) (Amendment) Regulations, 1994.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Superintendents and Chief Superintendents) Regulations, 1987 and 1994, and shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations the "Principal Regulations" means the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Superintendents and Chief Superintendents) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 200 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0200.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 3 of the First Schedule to the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (3) thereof:

- "(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2) of this Regulation.

( *a* ) Garda Headquarters shall be deemed to be a division and it shall be deemed to be a division in the Leinster Area and not in the Dublin Metropolitan Area;

( *b* ) Dublin Metropolitan Area Headquarters shall be deemed to be a division;

( *c* ) The Garda Training Centre at Templemore in the County of Tipperary shall be deemed to be a division."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of April, 1994.

**MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN,**

Minister for Justice.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 19th day of April, 1994.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend, in a minor respect the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Superintendents and Chief Superintendents) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.
