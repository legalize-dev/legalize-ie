S.I. No. 85 of 2016

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (LOCAL ELECTIONS (PETITIONS AND DISQUALIFICATIONS) ACT 1974) 2016

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which shall come into operation on the 25th day of February 2016, may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Local Elections (Petitions and Disqualifications) Act 1974) 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2016.

- (3) The Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2016 as amended by these Rules may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. Order 58 of the Circuit Court Rules is amended:

by the substitution for rules 8 to 11 inclusive of the following rules:

> “8.Every appeal to the Court of Appeal under section 7(3) of the Act shall be by notice of appeal.
>
> 9. (1) Every notice of appeal shall contain:
>
> - (*a*) the terms and provisions of the order of the Court appealed against, and
>
> - (*b*) the grounds of appeal.
>
> - (2) Every notice of appeal shall be in accordance with Form 38A of the Schedule of Forms, or such modification as may be necessary.
>
>   10. The Rules of the Superior Courts relating to service and entry of an appeal to the Court of Appeal shall apply to service and entry of every appeal under section 7(3) of the Act.
>
>   11. The appellant must lodge a copy of the notice of appeal with the County Registrar within 14 days of the date of the order appealed against.”

###### 3.

3. The Form numbered 38A in Schedule 1 shall be substituted for the forms bearing the numbers 38A and 38B respectively in the Schedule of Forms annexed to the Circuit Court Rules.
