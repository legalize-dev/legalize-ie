# S.I. No. 100/1973 - Stock Transfer (Recognition of Stock Exchanges) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 100 of 1973.

STOCK TRANSFER (RECOGNITION OF STOCK EXCHANGES) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1963), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Stock Transfer (Recognition of Stock Exchanges) Regulations, 1973, and shall come into operation on the 26th day of March, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The Stock Exchange is hereby declared to be a recognised stock exchange for the purposes of the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. The Stock Transfer (Recognition of Stock Exchanges) Regulations, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0251.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of March, 1973.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations declare The Stock Exchange to be a recognised stock exchange for the purposes of the [Stock Transfer Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , and revoke the Stock Transfer (Recognition of Stock Exchanges) Regulations, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0251.html) ).
