# S.I. No. 492/2023 - Court-Martial Rules 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Court-Martial Rules 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Court-Martial Rules 2008 to 2011 and these Rules may be cited together as the Court-Martial Rules 2008 to 2023 and shall be construed together as one.

###### 3.

3. These Rules shall come into operation on the 2nd day of October 2023.

###### 4.

4. In these Rules “Principal Rules” means the Court-Martial Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 205 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0205.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Principal Rules are amended by substituting the following for Rule 82—

> **“***Record of proceedings– Trial by court-martial*
>
> 82. (1) The Court-Martial Administrator shall record or cause to be recorded the proceedings of a court-martial.
>
> - (2) The record referred to in sub-rule (1) shall include the following:
>
>     - (a) the original or a certified copy of the direction of the Director to convene a general court-martial or limited court-martial or to refer the charge for trial by the summary court-martial;
>
>     - (b) the original or a certified copy of an order convening the court and of any further order in that regard;
>
>     - (c) the original or a certified copy of any summons to the accused to appear before the summary court-martial;
>
>     - (d) the original or a certified copy of any summary or abstract of evidence;
>
>     - (e) the original or a certified copy of the charge sheet;
>
>     - (f) a list of witnesses for the prosecution and defence;
>
>     - (g) a list of exhibits;
>
>     - (h) any order of the court;
>
>     - (i) any order or sentence of the military judge;
>
>     - (j) any record, pursuant to sub-rule (3), held by the Court-Martial Administrator.
>
> - (3)
>     - (a) The proceedings shall be recorded using any means suitable for the making or production of a record within the meaning of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0033.html#sec33) (3) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , as substituted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0004/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Criminal Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) .
>
>     - (b) No person other than the Court-Martial Administrator or a person authorised by him or her shall make any record of proceedings of a court-martial otherwise than by written or shorthand notes.
>
> - (4) The Court-Martial Administrator shall submit the record of the proceedings of the trial to the military judge for his or her signature, where necessary.”.

###### 6.

6. Rule 83 and Rule 84 of the Principal Rules are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Rules amend the provisions set out in Rule 82 of the Court-Martial Rules 2008 in relation to the record of proceedings of a court-martial and provide for the revocation of Rule 83 and Rule 84.
