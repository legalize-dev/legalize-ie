# S.I. No. 210/2022 - Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd May,* 2022.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0004.html#sec4) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0005.html#sec5) (amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec3) of the [Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2016)), [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0018.html#sec18) and [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977) and [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1984), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 173 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0173.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) by substituting for Schedule 1 to those Regulations the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations, and

- (*b*) by substituting for Schedule 4 to those Regulations the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following are revoked:

- (*a*) the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 532 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0532.html) ); and

- (*b*) the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0121.html) ).

- (a)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2017 by adding certain additional substances to Schedules 1 and 4 of those Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations 2022.
