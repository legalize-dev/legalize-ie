# S.I. No. 189/2003 - Companies (Forms) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Michael Ahern, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0157.html) ) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Act of 1963” means the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963 and “Act of 2001” means the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001).

###### 3.

3. The Form B1 as set out in the First Schedule to this Order is prescribed as a form that may be used up to and including 31^st December 2003 for the purposes of section 125 (as amended by section 59 of the Act of 2001) of the Act of 1963.

###### 4.

4. The Form B1 (version 2) as set out in the Second Schedule to this Order is prescribed as a form that may be used up to and including 31^st December 2003 for the purposes of section 125 (as amended by section 59 of the Act of 2001 of the Act of 1963, and is further prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 125 (as amended by section 59 of the Act of 2001) of the Act of 1963 with effect from 1^st January 2004.

###### 5.

5. The Companies (Forms)(No.3) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0114.html) ) is revoked.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this Order is to amend the form prescribed for the purposes of [section 125](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0125.html#sec125) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , by introducing a new Form B1. This Form updates the form to be completed when furnishing an annual return to the Registrar of Companies. The Order further provides that the Form B1 previously in use (set out in the First Schedule) may continue to be used to deliver an annual return to the Registrar of Companies until 31^st December 2003, and that with effect from 1^st January 2004, only the version of the Form B1 set out in the Second Schedule of this Order may be completed when furnishing an annual return to the Registrar of Companies.
