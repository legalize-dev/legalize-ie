# S.I. No. 127/1978 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in South Cork County Health District) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 127 of 1978.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN SOUTH CORK COUNTY HEALTH DISTRICT) ORDER, 1978.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O., 1947, No. 58), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in South Cork County Health District) Order, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of August, 1978 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the South Cork County Health District unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this twenty-seventh

day of April, One Thousand Nine Hundred and Seventy-eight.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of August, 1978 the sale of milk in the South Cork County Health District unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
