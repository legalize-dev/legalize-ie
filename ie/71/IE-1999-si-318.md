# S.I. No. 318/1999 - Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 1998 (Amendment) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Building Control Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 1998 (Amendment) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 348 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0348.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of “Paul Kelly” for “Derek Maynard”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment and Local Government this 13th day of October 1999. |
|  | NOEL DEMPSEY, |
|  | Minister for the Environment and Local Government. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order changes the membership of the Building Regulations Advisory Body by the substitution of Mr. Paul Kelly for Mr. Derek Maynard.
