# S.I. No. 190/1999 - Local Government (Major Offices) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Part II of the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1941) hereby declares and orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Major Offices) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings hereby assigned to them—

“the Act” means the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1941);

“office” means any office within the meaning of Part II of the Act in relation to which the Minister for the Environment and Local Government is the appropriate Minister.

###### 3.

3. The Local Government (Offices) Order, 1952 ( [S.I. No. 170 of 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/si/0170.html) ) and every declaration or order made under Section 9 of the Act declaring an office to be a major office are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. (1) Subject to sub-article (2) hereof, every office in respect of which the maximum remuneration exceeds the maximum remuneration for the Office of Clerical Officer shall be a major office.

- (2) Sub-article (1) shall not apply to an office for so long but only for so long as the holder thereof on the date hereof is a member of a local authority consequent upon the local elections held on the 11th day of June, 1999.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order declares that every local authority ofice in respect of which the maximum remuneration exceeds the maximum remuneration for the office of Clerical Officer shall be a major office. All existing Major Offices orders are revoked whilst exemptions are provided for certain serving officers.
