# S.I. No. 450/2012 - National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations, 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th November*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 20 of, and paragraph 5(2) of the Schedule to, the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations, 2012.

###### 2.

2. 19 December 2012 is prescribed as an election day for producer members of the National Milk Agency.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 November 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to fix the 19th December 2012 as an election day for the National Milk Agency.
