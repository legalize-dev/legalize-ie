# S.I. No. 325/2011 - European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th July*, 2011.

I, JAMES REILLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Regulation (EU) No. 433/2011 of 4 May 2011[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 July 2011.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0391.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “EC Regulation 669/2009” the following—

> “ ‘EC Regulation 669/2009’ means Commission Regulation (EC) No. 669/2009 of 24 July 20095 implementing Regulation (EC) No. 882/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the increased level of official controls on imports of certain feed and food of non-animal origin and amending Decision 2006/504/EC, as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 433/2011 of 4 May 20116;”,

and by substituting for footnote 6 thereto the following:

> “6 OJ No. L. 115, 05.05.2011, p.5.”.

###### 4.

4. The European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 131 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0131.html) ) are revoked.

Signed,

30 June 2011.

JAMES REILLY,

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No. 433/2011 of 4 May 2011 amending Annex 1 to Regulation (EC) No. 669/2009 implementing Regulation (EC) No. 882/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the increased level of official controls on imports of certain feed and food of non-animal origin.

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0391.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations revoke the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 131 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0131.html) ).

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2011.
