# S.I. No. 99/2017 - Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st March*, 2017.

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on the said Society by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec11) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Health, hereby makes the following rules: —

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2017.

- (2) The Principal Rules, the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2015 ( [S.I. No. 238 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0238.html) ), the Rules of 2016 and these Rules may be cited together as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 to 2017 and shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules—

“Principal Rules” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) );

“Rules of 2016” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2016 ( [S.I. No. 311 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0311.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Rule 3(1) of the Principal Rules is amended—

- (*a*) by substituting for the definition of “Act” the following:

  > “‘Act’ means the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007), as amended by the European Communities (Recognition of Professional Qualifications relating to the Profession of Pharmacist) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 489 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0489.html) ), the [Civil Partnership and Certain Rights and Obligations of Cohabitants Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 2010), the [Health (Pricing and Supply of Medical Goods) Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2013), the [Health Identifiers Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2014), the European Union (Amendment of the Pharmacy Act 2007) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 86 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0086.html) ) and the Professional Qualifications Regulations;”,

- (*b*) in the definition of “adaptation period”, by substituting “Regulation 3 of the Professional Qualifications Regulations” for “section 16(8) of the Act”,

- (*c*) in the definition of “aptitude test”, by substituting “Regulation 3 of the Professional Qualifications Regulations” for “section 24A(11) of the Act”, and

- (*d*) by inserting after the definition of “Professional Registration Examination” the following definition:

  > “‘Professional Qualifications Regulations’ means the European Union (Recognition of Professional Qualifications) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 8 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0008.html) );”.

###### 4.

4. The Schedule (as amended by Rule 3 of the Rules of 2016) to the Principal Rules is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph 39, by substituting “Part 5 of the Professional Qualifications Regulations” for “section 16(5) of the Act”,

- (*b*) in paragraph 41—

    - (i) by substituting “Regulation 13 of the Professional Qualifications Regulations” for “section 24A(3) of the Act”, and

    - (ii) by substituting “Regulation 15 of those Regulations” for “section 24A(5)(*b*) of the Act”, and

- (*c*) by inserting after paragraph 43 the following paragraphs:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Rules amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) ) to (*a*) provide for fees for the recognition of professional qualifications and (*b*) changes the fees for recognition in the context of the EPC system.

These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2017.
