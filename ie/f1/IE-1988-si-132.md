# S.I. No. 132/1988 - Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 132 of 1988.

TRAVEL AGENTS (LICENSING) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Tourism and Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0007.html#sec7) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0012.html#sec12) and [25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Transport (Tour Operators and Travel Agents) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1982), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

- (2) The Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 to 1987, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 to 1988.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1988.

###### 3.

3. The Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0101.html) ), as amended by the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 176 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0176.html) ), are hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution in Regulation 9 (*a*) for "£160" of "£175",

- (*b*) the substitution in Regulation 9 (*b*) for "£16.00" of "£17.50", and

- (*c*) the insertion of additional Regulations 9 (*d*), 9 (*e*), 9 (*f*) and 9 (*g*) as follows:

- "(*d*) In the case of an applicant for a travel agent's licence who has not held a tour operator's or a travel agent's licence within the two years immediately prior to application, an additional fee of £175 shall be payable.

- (*e*) Where an application for a travel agent's licence is received less than two months but more than one month prior to the date on which the licence is required to commence, an additional fee of £87.50 shall be payable.

- (*f*) Where an application for a travel agent's licence is received less than one month prior to the date on which the licence is required to commence, an additional fee of £175 shall be payable.

- (*g*) The Minister may, at his discretion, waive the additional fees payable under paragraphs (*e*) and (*f*) where he is satisfied that the delay in submission of an application was due solely to circumstances outside the control of the applicant, his agents or servants."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of June, 1988.

**JOHN P. WILSON,**

Minister for Tourism andTransport.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 24th day of June, 1988.

**P. MOLLOY.**

A person authorised to authenticate the Seal of the said Minister.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for an increase in the fees payable in respect of an application for a travel agent's licence and the amendment of such a licence. They also provide for the introduction of special fees for first time applicants and a system of late application fees.
