# S.I. No. 476/2001 - Credit Union Act, 1997 (Alteration of Financial Limit) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Noel Treacy, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 182](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/sec0182.html#sec182) (1)(i) of the [Credit Union Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1997), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 265 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0265.html) ), and following consultation with the Registrar and the Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Union Act, 1997 (Alteration of Financial Limit) Regulations, 2001.

a) [Section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/sec0027.html#sec27) (1)(b) of the [Credit Union Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1997), is amended as respects the period from the commencement of these Regulations to 31 December 2001, by the substitution of “£50” for “£1,000”.

b) The said section 27(1)(b) (as amended by the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001)), is amended, with effect from 1 January 2002, by the substitution of “€63” for “€1,250”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations substitutes “£50” for “£1,000” in [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/sec0027.html#sec27) (1)(b) of [the Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) from commencement until 31^st December 2001 and “€63” after that date
