# S.I. No. 749/2004 - Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 749 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0749.html) .**

GARDA SÍOCHÁNA (ADMISSIONS AND APPOINTMENTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2004.

[S.I. No. 749 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0749.html) .

GARDA SÍOCHÁNA (ADMISSIONS AND APPOINTMENTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2004.

I, MICHAEL McDOWELL, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), as amended by section 1 of the Garda Síochána Act 1958 (No. 14 of 1958), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 23rd November 2004.

###### 2.

2. [The Interpretation Act 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 5 of the Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) Regulation 1988 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0164.html) ) is amended:

- (*a*) in paragraph (1)(*c*) by substituting “35, years of age” for “26, years of age” and

- (*b*) by deleting paragraphs (4), (5), (6) and (7).

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations increase the maximum entry age for admission as a trainee Garda from 26 years to 35 years.
