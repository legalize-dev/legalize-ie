# S.I. No. 84/1972 - Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1971 (Revocation) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 84 of 1972.

AGRICULTURAL WAGES (MINIMUM RATES) ORDER, 1971 (REVOCATION) ORDER, 1972.

The Agricultural Wages Board, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0053/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Agricultural Wages Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0053/index.html) (No. 53 of 1936), and of every and any other power them in this behalf enabling, and after due compliance with subsection (7) of the said Section 17, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1971 (Revocation) Order, 1972.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 149 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0149.html) ) is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Common Seal of the Agricultural Wages Board this 24th day of March, 1972.

The Seal of the Board was affixed in the presence of—

SEÁN LEE,

Chairman of the Board.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

The effect of this Order is to revoke the Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 149 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0149.html) ).
