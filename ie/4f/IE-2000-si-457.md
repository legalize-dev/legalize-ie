# S.I. No. 457/2000 - Planning and Development (No. 2) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 73](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec73) , [75](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec75) and [262](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec262) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Development (No.2) Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations and the Planning and Development Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. no. 350 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0350.html) ) shall be construed as one and may be collectively cited as the Planning and Development Regulations, 2000.

Commencement.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January, 2001.

Interpretation.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations:—

“the Act of 2000” means the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) .

Compulsory acquisition of protected structure.

###### 4.

4. The prescribed documents and particulars for the purposes of section 73(4)(*d*) of the Act of 2000 shall be—

- (*a*) a copy of the relevant notice published in accordance with section 72(1)(*a*) of that Act, and

- (*b*) a copy of the relevant notice served in accordance with section 72(1)(*b*) of that Act.

Vesting order.

###### 5.

5. The prescribed form of vesting order for the purposes of section 75 of the Act of 2000 shall be the form set out in the Schedule to these regulations or a form substantially to the like effect.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe certain documents which must be submitted with an application for the consent of the Board to the compulsory acquisition of a protected structure under the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) , and to set out the form of a vesting order for the acquisition of a protected structure.

## Footnotes

[^1]: The map should be sealed and marked by a heading containing a reference to the order, e.g. “Map referred to in vesting order made under section 74 of the Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1999, on the day of ............., ............, by .................. (name of planning authority)”.

[^2]: Insert full description of planning authority.

[^3]: Delete recitals which do not apply.

[^4]: Section 75(2) of the Planning and Development Act, 2000 provides that the vesting date shall be a specified date not earlier than 3 weeks after the making of the vesting order.

[^5]: The description and location of the protected structure should be set out, with an appropriate reference to the manner in which the structure is shown on the map and its unique identifying number in the relevant record of protected structures.

[^6]: The description of the persons in whose presence the seal is affixed should be stated, e.g. “the Lord Mayor”, “Mayor”, “Chairman”, “Nominated Member”, “City Manager and Town Clerk”, “Manager”, etc.
