# S.I. No. 377/2026 - European Union (Recognised Research Ethics Committees) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st July,* 2026.

I, JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 2013/59/EURATOM of 5 December 2013[^1] , as affected by Corrigendum of 17 March 2012[^2] and Corrigendum of 11 June 2019[^3] , insofar as it relates to medical exposures, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Recognised Research Ethics Committees) Regulations 2026.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Basic Safety Standards for Protection Against Dangers Arising from Medical Exposure to Ionising Radiation) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0256.html) ) are amended —

- (a) in Regulation 37(4), by substituting “Supervisory Body” for “Supervisory Authority”,

- (b) in Regulation 38, by inserting “or recognised under Regulation 40” after “Regulation 37”,

- (c) by substituting for Regulation 40 (Inserted by Regulation 5(b) of [S.I. No. 29 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0029.html) ) the following:

  > “40. Notwithstanding Regulation 37, the research ethics committees specified in Schedule 2 are recognised as research ethics committees for the purposes of these Regulations.”,

  and

- (d) by inserting after Schedule 1 the following:

  > “Regulation 40

## Schedule 2 Recognised Research Ethics Committees

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 July, 2026.

**JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations list the Recognised Research Ethics Committees for the purposes of S.I. 256/2018.
