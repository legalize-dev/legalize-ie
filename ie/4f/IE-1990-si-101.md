# S.I. No. 101/1990 - Trade Union Act, 1941 (Exclusion From Section 6) (No. 1) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 101 of 1990.

TRADE UNION ACT, 1941 (EXCLUSION FROM SECTION 6) (No. 1) ORDER, 1990.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (6) of [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Trade Union Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1941), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Trade Union Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Exclusion from Section 6) (No. 1) Order, 1990.

###### 2.

2. [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Trade Union Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1941), shall not apply in respect of the body of persons specified in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE

Irish Hospital Consultants Association.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 25th day of April, 1990.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to allow the Irish Hospital Consultants Association to carry on negotiations for the fixing of wages or other conditions of employment without holding a negotiation licence.
