# S.I. No. 389/1999 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) (Travelling Facilities and Overnight Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, CHARLIE McCREEVY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 1 (inserted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1996), as amended by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998) (“the Act of 1998”)) and 3 (as amended by section 17 of the Act of 1998) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1962), and the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) ), and section 5 (as amended by section 18 of the Act of 1998) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1964), and the Order aforesaid, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Travelling Facilities and Overnight Allowance) (Amendment), Regulations, 1999.

- (2) These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The Oireachtas (Allowance to Members) (Travelling Facilities and Overnight Allowance) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0101.html) ), is hereby amended:

- (*a*) in Regulation 10, by the substitution for paragraph (2) of the following paragraph:

  > - “(2) The period referred to in paragraph (1) is the period of 365 days from the day on which the allowance referred to in that paragraph becomes payable to a member.”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 11(b), after “1994” by the insertion of “( [S.I. No. 343 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0343.html) ).”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend existing regulations which provide for the payment of overnight and travel expense allowances to Members of the Oireachtas. A time limit of 365 days has been set for the submission of claims by Members of the Oireachtas for the payment of overnight and travel expenses.
