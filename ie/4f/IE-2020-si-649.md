# S.I. No. 649/2020 - Statistics (Quarterly Accounts Survey) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st January,* 2021.

I, JACK CHAMBERS, Minister of State at the Department of the Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993), and the Statistics (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 263 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0263.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Quarterly Accounts Survey) Order 2020.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 2 January 2021.

###### 2.

2. In this Order -

“quarter” means a period of 3 months ending on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September or 31 December in any year;

“survey” means the Quarterly Accounts Survey that is conducted by the Central Statistics Office for the purposes of obtaining the information referred to in Article 4.

###### 3.

3. Article 4 applies to an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under any of the following sections of NACE Rev. 2 set out in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^1] , as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 295/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008[^2] , Regulation (EU) No. 70/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 January 2012[^3] and Regulation (EU) 2019/1243 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^4] -

- (*a*) B to E,

- (*b*) G,

- (*c*) H to N,

- (*d*) R, or

- (*e*) S.

###### 4.

4. A requirement on undertakings referred to in Article 3,

- (*a*) subject to subparagraph (b), to provide, for the purpose of the survey, information the general nature of which is specified in the Schedule in relation to each quarter in the period from 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2025, and

- (*b*) to provide the information in relation to each quarter in the period referred to in paragraph (a), once only,

is hereby prescribed.

###### 5.

5. The Statistics (Quarterly Accounts Surveys) Order 2016 ( [S.I. No. 501 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0501.html) ) is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

*Article 4*

1. Information regarding the following, as it relates to the undertaking concerned and specifying the quarterly period to which the information relates:

- (*a*) the number of persons engaged in a particular week;

- (*b*) particulars in relation to the stocks and work in progress at the start and the end of the quarterly period, including the total value for accounting purposes of such stocks and work in progress;

- (*c*) particulars in relation to capital assets in the quarterly period, including changes in such capital assets, separately identifying -

    - (i) the total additions to capital assets, and

    - (ii) the total receipt on sales of capital assets.

2. Information on the time taken by the undertaking concerned to complete the questionnaire, form or other record prepared by the Director General for the collection of the information referred to in paragraph 1.

GIVEN under my hand,

17 December 2020

JACK CHAMBERS,

Minister of State at the Department of the Taoiseach.
