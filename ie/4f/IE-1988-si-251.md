S.I. No. 251 of 1988.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF ROSCOMMON) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) , [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) and [46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) amended as regards the said section 46 by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Roscommon) Regulations, 1988.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 10^th day of October, 1988.

3 Interpretation.

###### 3.

**3.** In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires "the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"built-up area speed limit" and "special speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road;

"National Road" means a road declared to be a National Road in the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Declaration of National Roads) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0164.html) ) and any reference to the number of such road shall be a reference to the number of such road as shown in column 1 of the First Schedule or column 1 of the Second Schedule to the aforementioned Order;

"perimeter road" means a road on and along the roadway of which there runs the boundary of a town;

"road" means a public road;

4 Built-up area speed limit.

###### 4.

**4.** (1)Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

5 Special speed limit.

###### 5.

**5.** Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

6 Exceptions.

###### 6.

**6.** There shall be excepted from the special speed limit prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:

( *a* ) ambulances,

( *b* ) fire brigade vehicles, and

( *c* ) vehicles used by members of the Garda Siochana in the performance of their duties as such members.

7 Revocation.

###### 7.

**7.** The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Roscommon) Regulations, 1980 (S.I. 355 of 1980) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Roscommon) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 355 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0355.html) ) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in County Roscommon.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area speed limit of 30 mph prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

The Regulations declare that the roads mentioned in the Second Schedule which would otherwise be outside the scope of the built-up area speed limit of 30 mph shall be subject to that limit. The Regulations prescribe a speed limit of 40 mph in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Third Schedule.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and Garda Síochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are excepted from all speed limits by the Regulations.
