SOCIAL WELFARE (CONSOLIDATED CLAIMS, PAYMENTS AND CONTROL) (AMENDMENT) (CARER’S INCOME DISREGARD) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th March*, 2008.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4 (as amended by section 17 and Schedule 3 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2006)) and 100 (as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec12) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005) and Rules 1(5) and 4(3) of Part 5 (as inserted by section 25 and Schedule 5 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ) of Schedule 3 to the said Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (Carer’s Income Disregard) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2008.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 3 April 2008.

*Definitions.*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “the Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ).

*Income disregards — carer’s benefit and carer’s allowance.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in article 57 (amended by article 4 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (Carer’s Income Disregard and Family Income Supplement) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 148 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0148.html) )) by substituting “€332.50” for “€320” in each place where it occurs, and

- (*b*) in article 144 (amended by article 4 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (Carer’s Income Disregard and Family Income Supplement) Regulations 2007) by substituting—

    - (i) “ €332.50” for “€320”, and

    - (ii) “€665” for “€640”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Social Welfare legislation currently provides that a person in receipt of Carers Benefit may engage in employment or self employment outside the home, subject to a maximum of fifteen hours per week and an income ceiling of €320. Article 4(*a*) of these regulations increases the income ceiling to €332.50 per week, effective from 3 April, 2008.

Article 4(*b*) of these regulations further provides for an increase in the income disregarded for the purposes of Carers Allowance. In the case of a single person, the increase is €12.50 per week, bringing the total amount of the disregard to €332.50. In the case of a married person, the disregard is increased by €25 per week, to a new total of €665. These changes take effect from 3 April 2008.
