# S.I. No. 163/1988 - Finance Act, 1981 (Certificates of Reasonable Cost) Regulations, 1981 (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 163 of 1988.

FINANCE ACT, 1981 (CERTIFICATES OF REASONABLE COST) REGULATIONS, 1981 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979) (as applied by [sections 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0023.html#sec23) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1981)), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Certificates of Reasonable Cost) Regulations, 1981 (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The following article shall be substituted for article 5 of the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Certificates of Reasonable Cost) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0300.html) ) (as substituted by the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Certificates of Reasonable Cost) Regulations (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 39 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0039.html) )):

"5. The fee accompanying an application for a certificate shall be as follows:

( *a* ) in case the application relates to one house only, £50

( *b* ) in case the application relates to more than one house, £50 plus £20 in respect of each house exceeding one.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 8th day of July, 1988.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations further amend article 5 of the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Certificates of Reasonable Cost) Regulations, 1981. They provide for an increase in the fee accompanying an application for a Certificate of Reasonable Cost for the purposes of [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0023.html#sec23) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) .
