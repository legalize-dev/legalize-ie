# S.I. No. 435/1981 - Housing Act, 1969 (Continuance) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0016/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Housing Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1969) hereby makes the following Order, the draft of which has been approved by a resolution passed by each House of the Oireachtas:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Housing Act, 1969 (Continuance) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The [Housing Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (other than section 11 and subsections (1) and (2) of section 13) shall continue in force until 31st December, 1984.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 22nd day of December, 1981. |
|  | PETER BARRY, |
|  | Minister for the Environment. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This notice is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Section 11 and subsections (1) and (2) of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0016/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Housing Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) are in force permanently. The remaining provisions would expire on the 31st day of December, 1981 unless continued by Order of the Minister for the Environment. The effect of this Order is to continue the [Housing Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) in force for three years ending on the 31st day of December, 1984.
