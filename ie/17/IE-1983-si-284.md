# S.I. No. 284/1983 - Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1983, (Commencement) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 284 of 1983.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) ACT, 1983, (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 1983.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/sec0026.html#sec26) (3) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1983), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (Commencement) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"the Act of 1983" means the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) ;

"the Act of 1976" means the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1976).

###### 3.

3. On the 7th day of October, 1983—

- (*a*) sections 1, 2, 5 (except subsections (11), (12), (13), (14) and (15)), 7 (except subsections (8), (9), (10), (11) and (12)), 9, 24 and 26 of the Act of 1983 shall come into operation; and

- (*b*) section 25 of the Act of 1983 shall come into operation for the purpose of effecting the repeal of articles 1, 25, 26, 27 and 28 of the Schedule to the Act of 1976.

###### 4.

4. On the 25th day of October, 1983—

- (*a*) sections 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 and 22 of the Act of 1983 shall come into operation; and

- (*b*) section 25 of the Act of 1983 shall come into operation for the purpose of effecting the repeal of sections 16, 18 (1) and 18 (2) of the Act of 1976.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 6th day of October, 1983.

**DICK SPRING,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order brings into operation on 7th October and on 25th October, 1983, certain provisions of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , and brings into operation on those days the repeal of certain provisions of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) .
