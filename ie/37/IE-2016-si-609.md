# S.I. No. 609/2016 - Statistics (Trading Day and Product Profile Survey) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th December*, 2016.

I, ENDA KENNY, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Trading Day and Product Profile Survey) Order 2016.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act of 1993” means the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993);

“day of the week” means a Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday or Sunday;

“direction” means a direction under section 26 of the Act of 1993 made pursuant to the requirement prescribed under Article 4;

“Regulation” means Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006;[^1]

“relevant aggregate figure” means, in relation to a day of the week, the aggregate of the turnover achieved on that day of the week in each week of 2016;

“relevant period” means such period occurring in 2016 as the Director General may specify in the notice of a direction;

“survey” means the Trading Day and Product Profile Survey that is conducted by the Central Statistics Office for the purpose of obtaining the information specified in the Schedule.

###### 3.

3. Article 4 applies to an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under division 45 or 47 or class 56.30 of NACE Rev. 2 set out in Annex I to the Regulation.

###### 4.

4. A requirement on undertakings referred to in Article 3 to provide, once only and for the purposes of the survey, information the general nature of which is specified in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 25 of the Act of 1993.

## SCHEDULE

Article 4.

Information in relation to 2016 as it relates to the undertaking concerned regarding the following:

- (*a*) total annual turnover;

- (*b*) either:

    - (i) in relation to each day of the week, the percentage of the figure referred to in paragraph (*a*) that the relevant aggregate figure represents, or

    - (ii) in relation to such week, occurring in the relevant period, as the undertaking may nominate—

        - (I) the turnover achieved in that week, and

        - (II) in relation to each day of that week, the percentage of the figure referred to in clause (I) that the turnover achieved on that day represents;

- (*c*) the percentage of the figure referred to in paragraph (*a*) that the turnover, achieved in 2016, in respect of each category of product sold by the undertaking represents.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 December 2016.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.
