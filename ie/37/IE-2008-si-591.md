# S.I. No. 591/2008 - European Communities (Energy Performance of Buildings) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th January*, 2009.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) as amended by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec2) of the [European Communities Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2007) and for the purpose of giving further effect to certain provisions of Directive 2002/91/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2002 on the energy performance of buildings [^1] , hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and commencement*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Energy Performance of Buildings) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008.

- (2) The collective citation “the European Communities (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations 2006 — 2008” shall include these Regulations.

- (3) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2009.

*Interpretation*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “the Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 666 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0666.html) ).

*Amendment of Regulation 2*

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended in Regulation 2(1)—

- (i) by substituting the following for the existing definition of “accredited BER training provider”:

  > “ “accredited BER training provider” means—
  >
  > - (1) Sustainable Energy Ireland;
  >
  > - (2) an accredited BER training provider providing a validated BER training course leading to an award recognised through the National Qualifications Authority of Ireland;
  >
  > - (3) an accredited BER training provider providing a BER training course designated in writing by the issuing authority; or
  >
  > - (4) an accredited BER training provider providing a BER training course which is recognised pursuant to the requirements of the Recognition of Professions Qualifications (Directive 2005/36/EC) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 139 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0139.html) ).”.

- (ii) by inserting the following new definition after the definition of “building control authority”:

  > “ “display energy certificate” means a certificate in a prescribed form which is created on the basis of an assessment of a large building occupied by a public body, displayed in a prominent place clearly visible to the public, and in accordance with directions issued by the issuing authority;”.

*Substitution of Regulation 8*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by substituting the following for Regulation 8:

> *“Public Service Buildings*
>
> 8. (1) A public body shall, in relation to a large building occupied by it on or after 1 January 2009, secure and display a valid display energy certificate.
>
> - (2) A display energy certificate shall be updated and renewed each year.
>
> - (3) A display energy certificate shall be in the form prescribed in the Fifth Schedule to these Regulations.
>
> - (4) The issuing authority may issue a Code of Practice from time to time in relation to display energy certificates.
>
> - (5) For the avoidance of doubt, the requirement for a display energy certificate is additional to the requirement to secure a BER certificate pursuant to Regulation 7.”.

*Amendment of Regulation 20*

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended in Regulation 20(4)(*d*) by substituting “Data Protection Acts 1988 and 2003” for “Data Protection Act 1987”.

*Amendment of Schedules*

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended by deleting each occurrence of the word “new” from the titles of the First Schedule and the Third Schedule.

*New Schedule*

###### 7.

7. The Principal Regulations are amended by inserting the following Schedule after the Fourth Schedule, as inserted by the European Communities (Energy Performance of Buildings) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 229 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0229.html) ):

*Article 8*

FIFTH SCHEDULE

Form of Display Energy Certificate for Public Buildings

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government,

23 December 2008

**JOHN GORMLEY.**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations 2006 — 2008 ( [S.I. No. 666 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0666.html) and [S.I. No. 229 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0229.html) ). They provide for a display energy certificate for large buildings occupied by public bodies on or after 1 January 2009. Existing buildings offered for sale or rent on or after 1 January 2009 must have a BER certificate in the form prescribed in schedules 1 or 3 of the Principal Regulations as appropriate.
