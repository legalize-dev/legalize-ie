S.I. No. 64 of 1982.

TELEPHONE (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1982.

- (2) The Telephone Regulations, 1980 and 1981 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telephone Regulations, 1980 to 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st Day of April, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1981" means the Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0294.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Telephone Regulations, 1980, are hereby amended by:

*Regulation 10*

Charges for Subscriber Dialled Trunk Calls

In respect of each period or part of a period of time specified in the Table to this Schedule which is appropriate to the call and during which the call continues—

to provide for the following increases in charges:

| subscriber |  |

|  | (ii) increase in charges for trunk calls via Operator- internal calls under 56 kilometres from 25p to 30p (day)-Cross-Channel calls from 135p to 165p (day) and 90p to 110p (night). |
| Personal Call Fee .. .. .. | from 37p to 45p |
| Advice of Duration and Charge .. .. .. .. .. .. .. | from 21p to 25p |
| Alarm Clock Calls .. .. .. | from 37p to 45p |
| Reverse Charge Calls .. .. | from 37p to 45p |
| Connection Charges: |  |
| Exchange Line .. .. .. | from £160 to £180 |
| Other Connection Charges | 15% approximately |
