# S.I. No. 562/2001 - Standards in Public Office Act, 2001 (Sections 15 and 27) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec15) and [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec27) of the [Standards in Public Office Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2001), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Standards in Public Office Act, 2001 (Sections 15 and 27) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The [Standards in Public Office Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2001), is amended -

- (a) in section 14(2)(e), by the substitution for “1999” of “2001” and “paragraph (b)” for “subsection (2)”,

- (b) in section 20(2), by the deletion of “(a)”.

###### 3.

3. The [Ethics in Public Office Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1995), is hereby amended in section 5(2) by the substitution of “Commission” for “Clerk”.
