# S.I. No. 174/1979 - Customs-Free Airport (Extension of Laws) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 174 of 1979.

CUSTOMS-FREE AIRPORT (EXTENSION OF LAWS) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, PÁDRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Tourism and Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0005/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Customs-free Airport Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1947) (as amended by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0029/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Customs-free Airport (Amendment) Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1958)) the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ) and the Transport and Power (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0305.html) ), with the consent of the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Health, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Customs-free Airport (Extension of Laws) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 (1) (*c*) of the Misuse of Drugs Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0032.html) ) shall extend to the Customs-free Airport.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of May, 1979.

**PÁDRAIG FAULKNER,**

Minister for Tourism and Transport.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 16th day of May, 1979.

**M. NEVILLE,**

A person authorised to authenticate the seal of the said Minister.

The Minister for Health hereby consents to the making of the foregoing regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 16th day of May, 1979.

**J. O'ROURKE,**

A person authorised to authenticate the seal of the said Minister.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to extend to the Customs-free Airport at Shannon Article (4) (1) (*c*) of the Misuse of Drugs Regulations, 1979.
