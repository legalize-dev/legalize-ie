# S.I. No. 10/1999 - Organisation of Working Time (Public Holiday) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister for Labour, Trade and Consumer Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph 1(g) of the Second Schedule to the [Organisation of Working Time Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1997), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Organisation of Working Time (Public Holiday) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The 31st day of December, 1999, is hereby prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 1 of the Second Schedule to the [Organisation of Working Time Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1997).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my hand, this 27th day of January, 1999. |
|  | TOM KITT, T.D., |
|  | Minister for Labour, Trade and Consumer Affairs |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations appoint the 31st December, 1999 to be a special public holiday in celebration of the Millennium.
