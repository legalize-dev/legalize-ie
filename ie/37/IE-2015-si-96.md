# S.I. No. 96/2015 - Health Insurance Act 1994 (Minimum Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th March*, 2015.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec4) of the [Health Insurance (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2012)) and section 10 (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec4) of the [Voluntary Health Insurance (Amendment) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2008)) of the [Health Insurance Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1994), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Insurance Act 1994 (Minimum Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, "Principal Regulations" means the [Health Insurance Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Minimum Benefit) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 83 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0083.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Schedule A to the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph 1, by the substitution of the following sub-paragraph for sub-paragraph (1):

> “
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 1 | Prescribed health services which are in-patient services provided by a publicly-funded hospital where the insured person avails of services provided under [section 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0052.html#sec52) (1) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . | The public hospital daily in-patient charges made under regulations pursuant to [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . |
>
> ”.

###### 4.

4. Schedule A to the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph 1, by the substitution of the following sub-paragraph for sub-paragraph (2):

> “
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 2 | Prescribed health services which are in-patient services provided by a publicly funded hospital where the insured person avails of services provided under [section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0055.html#sec55) (1)(a)(i) or (ii) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . | The amount of the charge payable under Section 55 of, and specified in column 4 of the Fourth Schedule to, the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . |
>
> ”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 March 2015.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These regulations clarify the prescribed minimum payment relating to prescribed health services provided by a publicly funded hospital under section 52 (1) and [Section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0055.html#sec55) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .
