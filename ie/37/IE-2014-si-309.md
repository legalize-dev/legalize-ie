# S.I. No. 309/2014 - European Union (Cross-Border Mergers) (Interconnection of Business Registers) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th July*, 2014.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive No. 2012/17/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2012[^1] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cross-Border Mergers) (Interconnection of Business Registers) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Cross-Border Mergers) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0157.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1) by—

    - (i) the substitution for the definition of “Directive” of the following:

      > “ ‘Directive’ means Directive No. 2005/56/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 October 2005[^2] on cross-border mergers of limited liability companies as amended by Directive No. 2009/109/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 September 2009[^3] and Directive No. 2012/17/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2012[^4] ;”, and

    - (ii) the insertion of the following:

      > “ ‘Directive No. 2009/101/EC’ means Directive No. 2009/101/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 September 2009[^5] as amended by Directive No. 2012/17/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2012[^6] ;
      >
      > ‘interconnection system’ means the system of interconnection of central, commercial and companies registers established in accordance with Article 4a(2) of Directive No. 2009/101/EC;”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 18 by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (1):

  > - “(1A) On and from the date of the establishment of the interconnection system, the Registrar shall give the notice referred to in subparagraph (*a*) of paragraph (1) through the interconnection system.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 July 2014.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give effect to the amendments of Directive No. 2005/56/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 October 2005, on cross-border mergers of limited liability companies, by Directive 2012/17/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2012 on the interconnection of central, commercial and companies registers.
