# S.I. No. 53/1989 - International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act, 1987 (Authorised Person) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 53 of 1989.

INTERNATIONAL CARRIAGE OF PERISHABLE FOODSTUFFS ACT, 1987 (AUTHORISED PERSON) ORDER, 1989.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Tourism and Transport, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/sec0006.html#sec6) (4) of the [International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1987), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (Authorised Person) Order, 1989, and shall come into operation on the 22nd day of March, 1989.

###### 2.

2. Eolas — The Irish Science and Technology Agency is hereby authorised to act on behalf of the Minister for Tourism and Transport for the purposes of subsections (1) to (3) of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1987).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of March, 1989.

**JOHN P. WILSON,**

Minister for Tourism and

Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, made by the Minister for Tourism and Transport, appoints Eolas — The Irish Science and Technology Agency to type-approve transport equipment pursuant to the [International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1987).
