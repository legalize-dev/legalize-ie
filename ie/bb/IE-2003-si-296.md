# S.I. No. 296/2003 - Inland Post Amendment (No. 67) Scheme 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN POST, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Section 70 of the Postal and Telecommunications Act 1983 (No. 24 of 1983) as amended by regulation 8 (4) of the European Communities (Postal Services) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 616 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0616.html) ) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Inland Post Amendment (No. 67) Scheme 2002.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall be operative with effect from the 2^nd day of April 2002.

###### 3.

3. In this Scheme, “the Principal Warrant” means the Inland Post Warrant 1939 ( [S.I. No. 202 of 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/si/0202.html) ) as amended;

“the Warrant of 1953” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 5) Warrant 1953 ( [S.I. No. 248 of 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/si/0248.html) );

“the Warrant of 1974” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 19) Warrant 1974 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0280.html) );

“the Warrant of 1981” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 25) Warrant 1981 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0116.html) );

“Scheme No. 34” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 34) Scheme 1985 ( [S.I. No. 445 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0445.html) );

“Scheme No. 36” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 36) Scheme 1986 ( [S.I. No. 362 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0362.html) );

“Scheme No. 47” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 47) Scheme 1991 ( [S.I. No. 95 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0095.html) );

“Scheme No. 57” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 57) Scheme 1998 ( [S.I. No. 129 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0129.html) );

“Scheme No. 63” means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 63) Scheme 2002 ( [S.I. No. 632 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0632.html) ).

###### 4.

4. All rates and charges payable upon letters and postal packets in this Scheme pertain to postal packets outside the “reserved area” as defined by the European Communities (Postal Services) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 310 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0310.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Words defined in the Principal Warrant shall have the same meaning in this Scheme.

###### 6.

6. The First Schedule to the Principal Warrant is hereby amended by the addition to the entries therein contained (as inserted by Regulation 6 of Scheme No. 63) of the entries in the First Schedule to this Scheme.

###### 7.

7. The Fourth Schedule to the Principal Warrant is hereby amended by the substitution for the entries therein contained (as inserted by Regulation 18 of Scheme No. 57) of the entries in the Second Schedule to this Scheme.

###### 8.

8. The First Schedule to Scheme No. 36 is hereby amended by the addition to the entries therein contained (as inserted by Regulation 13 of Scheme No. 47 and Regulation 21 of Scheme No. 57) of the entries in the Third Schedule to this Scheme. In the event that there is a conflict between a rate payable in accordance with the entries inserted in Scheme No. 36 by Regulation 13 of Scheme No. 47 and Regulation 21 of Scheme No. 57 and a rate payable in accordance with those inserted by this Regulation, this Regulation and the entries contained in the Third Schedule to this Scheme shall prevail.

###### 9.

9. Regulation 5 of the Warrant of 1953 (inserted by Regulation 12 of the Warrant of 1974, as amended by Regulation 11 of the Warrant of 1981) is hereby amended by the deletion of the words “and, in addition to postage, a fee of one new penny shall also be charged”.

###### 10.

10. Regulation 6 of Scheme No. 34 is hereby amended by the deletion of the words “and, in addition, a fee of one penny per item shall also be charged.”

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

This note is not party of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Scheme applies to the Inland Post service and comes into operation on the 2^nd day of April 2002. It changes the rates of postage for items and services outside of the “reserved area “ as defined by the European Communities (Postal Services Regulations 2000 (S.I. 310 of 2000).
