# S.I. No. 590/2014 - Control on Animal Vaccines (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd January*, 2015.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec36) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), for the purpose, inter alia, of giving further effect to Article 71 of Directive 2001/82/EC of the European Parliament and the Council of 6 November 2001[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Control on Animal Vaccines (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Control on Animal Vaccines Regulations 2014 are amended in Part 1 of the Second Schedule after “Bluetongue” by inserting “Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (BVD) Type 2”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 December 2014.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for control on a certain vaccine for animal health reasons.
