# S.I. No. 148/1977 - Cork District Milk Board (Election Day 1977) Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 148 of 1977.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY 1977) ORDER, 1977.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Election Day 1977) Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 25th day of July 1977, is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Cork District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of May, 1977.

**MARK CLINTON,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1977 on which ordinary members of the Cork District Milk Board shall be elected.
