# S.I. No. 442/2018 - National Oversight and Audit Commission (Number of Members) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th October*, 2018.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning, and Local Government, in the exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 126I(7) (as inserted by [section 61](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec61) of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014)) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001), hereby order as follows:-

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Oversight and Audit Commission (Number of Members) Order 2018.

*Number of Members.*

###### 2.

2. The number of members of the National Oversight and Audit Commission (established by the National Oversight and Audit Commission (Establishment) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 297 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0297.html) )), and was increased to 11 members for the period of 2 years from the date of SI 536 of 2016 18th Oct 2016 shall be extended for a further 2 years in accordance with the provisions of the Act from the date of this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government,

22 October 2018.

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This order increases the number of members of the National Oversight and Audit Commission (established by the National Oversight and Audit Commission (Establishment) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 297 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0297.html) )) to 11 members for a period of two years from 19 October 2016.
