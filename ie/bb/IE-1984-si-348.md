S.I. No. 348 of 1984.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (EXEMPTED DEVELOPMENT AND AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1984.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0004.html#sec4) and [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963) and by section 25 of that Act as amended by the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1976), and by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , hereby makes the following Regulations:—

PART IPRELIMINARY AND GENERAL

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local government (Planning and Development) (Exempted Development and Amendment) Regulations, 1984.

- (2) The collective citation "the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1984" shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation as follows:—

( *a* ) Parts I and II shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1985,

( *b* ) Parts III and IV shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1985.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"habitable house" means a building or part of a building which—

( *a* ) is used as a dwelling,

( *b* ) is not in use but when last used was used, disregarding any unauthorised use, as a dwelling, or

( *c* ) was provided for use as a dwelling but has not been occupied;

"the Minister" means the Minister for the Environment;

"the 1977 Regulations" means the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0065.html) );

"the 1983 Regulations" means the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Fees and Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0030.html) ).

PART IVMISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS

###### 8.

8. The 1977 Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution for article 13A (inserted by article 25 of the 1983 Regulations) of the following article:

> "13A. Notwithstanding any other provision of these Regulations, an outline application may not be made in respect of the retention on land of any structure or the continuance of any use of any structure or other land.".

###### 9.

9. The 1977 Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution for article 19A (inserted by article 26 of the 1983 Regulations) of the following article:

"19A. (1) A planning application shall state whether the application is for a permission, an outline permission or an approval and shall, in addition to the matters prescribed in articles 17, 18 and 19, be accompanied by the following particulars—

( *a* ) a statement of the area of the land to which the application relates, and

( *b* ) where the application relates to a building or buildings—

- (i) a statement of the number of dwellings (if any) to be provided, and

- (ii) except in the case of an outline application for a single dwellinghouse or a number of such houses, a statement of the gross floor space of the building or buildings.

- (2) In this article, "gross floor space" means the area ascertained by the internal measurement of the floor space on each floor of a building or buildings (including internal walls and partitions), disregarding any floor space provided for the parking of vehicles by persons occupying or using the building or buildings where such floor space is incidental to development to which the application primarily relates.".

###### 10.

10. The 1977 Regulations are hereby amended by the insertion in article 25 of the following paragraph after paragraph (*e*):

> - "(*f*) where it appears to the planning authority that the development might give rise to appreciable discharges of polluting matter to waters or be likely to cause serious water pollution or the danger of such pollution — to the appropriate Regional Fisheries Board.".

###### 11.

11. The 1977 Regulations are hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the deletion of the word "and" from paragraph (*g*) of article 65, and

( *b* ) the addition to article 65 of the following paragraphs—

- "(i) An Post — The Post Office,

- (j) Bord Telecom Éireann — The Irish Telecommunications Board.".

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 in relation to certain classes of development which are exempted from the requirement to obtain planning permission and in relation to certain requirements to be observed in the making of planning applications. Provision is also made for the giving of notice of certain planning applications to Regional Fisheries Boards and for the declaration of certain bodies as public authorities for the purposes of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) .
