S.I. No. 74 of 1995.

AIR NAVIGATION (FEES) ORDER, 1995.

The Irish Aviation Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 5, 58, 59, 60, 60 (1) (*j*) (i) and 75 of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , (No. 29 of 1993) hereby orders as follows

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation (Fees) Order, 1995.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 18th day of April, 1995.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Authority" means the Irish Aviation Authority and references to the charging and levying of fees shall be construed as references to the charging and levying of fees by the Authority.

###### 3.

3. The Air Navigation (Fees) Order, 1990, (S.I.321 of 1990) is hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. The Authority hereby directs that

( *a* ) fees shall be charged and levied by the Authority in accordance with the provisions of the Schedules to this Order numbered 1 to 29

( *b* ) conditions under which fees, payments, expenses or other costs shall be refunded, reduced or waived in whole or part, or shall be forfeit in whole or part to the Authority shall be as prescribed in the Schedules to this Order.

SCHEDULE 1

*Fees for the Registration of an Aircraft*

( *a* ) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a certificate of registration of an aircraft fees in accordance with the following scale:—

where the maximum total authorised weight of the aircraft—

- (i) does not exceed 2,730 kgs., a fee of £25

- (ii) exceeds 2,730 kgs. but does not exceed 5,700 kgs., a fee of £80

- (iii) exceeds 5,700 kgs. but does not exceed 13,600 kgs., a fee of £105

- (iv) exceeds 13,600 kgs. but does not exceed 45,500 kgs., a fee of £175

- (iii) exceeds 30,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 30,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (*ii*) above, a fee of £55

- (iv) exceeds 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (*iii*) above, a fee of £50.

- (2) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a certificate of airworthiness, in the case of an aircraft intended for export, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such issue, subject to the following minimum fees:—

( *a* ) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £100

( *b* ) in the case of other aircraft of which the maximum total authorised weight:—

- (i) does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £250

- (ii) exceeds 2,000 kgs., for every 500 kgs or part thereof, a fee of £75.

- (3) There shall be charged and levied for the validation of a certificate of airworthiness issued by another State, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such validation, subject to the following minimum fees for each year for which the validation relates:—

( *a* ) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £100

- (i) does not exceed 2,000 kgs., for each 50 kgs. or part thereof, a fee of £10

- (iv) exceeds 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs., or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (*iii*) above, a fee of £50.

- (iii) exceeds 30,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs., or part thereof, above 30,000 kgs. in addition to the maximum fee payable under (*ii*) above, a fee of £55

- (iv) exceeds 100,000 kgs. for each 500 kgs., or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs. in addition to the maximum fee payable under (*iii*) above, a fee of £50.

*Fees for Flight Tests*

1. *General*

There shall be charged and levied for flight tests conducted to assess the skill of applicants for the grant, renewal or re-issue of various flight crew licences and associated ratings or, where required, for the validation of licences issued by the relevant authorities of other States fees as set out hereunder.

( *a* ) For the purposes of this Schedule, the term "Professional Pilot's Licence" includes a Commercial Pilot's Licence (C.P.L.), a Senior Commercial

Pilot's Licence (S.C.P.L.) or an Airline Transport

2. *Classification of Flight Tests*

For the purposes of this Schedule, flight tests are classified as follows:—

( *a* ) *Flight Tests — - Group A:*

The flight tests in this group are for the grant, or (if required) for the renewal, re-issue or validation of a Private Pilot's Licence (P.P.L.), a Multi-engine rating, an Aircraft Type rating (aircraft under 5,700 kgs. maximum total authorised weight) and for the grant or renewal of an Instrument rating (endorsed on any licence) or the grant or renewal of an Instrument rating validation (endorsed on any licence).

( *b* ) *Flight Tests — Group B:*

The flight tests in this group are for the grant, or (if required) for the renewal, re-issue or validation of a Professional Pilot's Licence (single or multi-engine aircraft under 5,700 kgs. maximum total authorised weight) and for Flight Instructor ratings Class I and II.

4. *Reduction of Fees for Group Flight Test Applications*

( *a* ) There is a sliding scale for group flight test applications (hereinafter referred to as "group applications") made by a Flight Training Organisation (F.T.O.), a Flying Club or other body recognised for this purpose by the Authority. Where a group application is made, it must be guaranteed that the flight test candidates will be presented consecutively (in any order) at an agreed single location during the period for which the flight test examiner is in attendance.

( *b* ) A group application may be made on behalf of candidates requiring a combination of flight tests from Flight Test Groups A and B as defined in paragraph 2 above. Where such an application is made the fee for an individual candidate will be determined as if all the applications were for that individual's Flight Test Group. By way of example, should such an application be made on behalf of four candidates, two requiring P.P.L. flight tests (i.e. Flight Test— Group A) and two requiring C.P.L. flight tests (i.e. Flight Test— Group B), the fees would be as follows:—

For each P.P.L. candidate: £184 (the fee as if all four candidates were from Flight Test— Group A).

For each C.P.L. candidate: £264 (the fee as if all four candidates were from Flight Test— Group B).

( *c* ) A substitution may be made within any group application, without alteration of the basis upon which an individual applicant's fees are determined, subject to the payment of an administration fee of £20 on written notification to the Authority of the substitution. It shall be the responsibility of the person who made the group application to arrange payment of the flight test fee between the substitute candidate and the withdrawing candidate and to settle any adjustment in the total fee payable resulting from the substitution.

( *d* ) Where a series of flight tests is being conducted on foot of a group application, an interruption in testing as a result of unsuitable weather conditions shall not be regarded as interrupting the consecutive nature of the flight tests.

###### 5.

5. *Fee Reduction for Combined Flight Tests*

Where a candidate intends to take a combined flight test, e.g. a C.P.L. and Instrument rating, the fees for the additional ratings will be 25% of the fee otherwise applicable. The total fee will be rounded up to the nearest whole pound. This arrangement will also apply to individual candidates within a group application. By way of example, should such an application be made on behalf of a candidate within a group application for five candidates, the fee would be as follows:—

£237 (for C.P.L. flight test)

£44.50 (25% of £178 for Instrument rating flight test)

£282 Total (rounded) fee.

###### 6.

6. *Supplementary Provisions for Flight Test Fees*

( *a* ) *Failure to attend for flight test*

Where a flight test candidate does not attend for the flight test and such absence has not been excused by prior arrangement with the flight test examiner the candidate's fees shall be forfeited. However, 50% of the fee will be offset against the fee otherwise payable should the candidate apply for another flight test. Where the candidate was within a group application his absence will be without prejudice to the other candidates.

( *b* ) *Withdrawal from flight test*

Where a flight test candidate withdraws his flight test application before any costs have been incurred by the flight test examiner, he may be paid a refund of his flight test fee less an administration fee of £20. Where a flight test candidate withdraws his flight test application after costs have been incurred by the flight test examiner, the rules applicable to failure to attend as set out in (*a*) above shall apply. Where the candidate was within a group application, the withdrawal must be through the person who made the application and any increase of the group's flight test fees as a result of there being a smaller group available shall be settled at that time.

( *c* ) *Failure to provide aircraft for flight test*

It is the responsibility of the applicant(s) to provide a serviceable, suitable and acceptable aircraft for the flight test(s). Where a flight test examiner attends at a location for a flight test(s) and such test(s) cannot be carried out because of the unavailability of a serviceable, suitable and acceptable aircraft, the sum of £178 shall be forfeited by the candidate. In the case of a group application the total sum forfeited shall also be £178.

( *d* ) *Flight tests for heavy aircraft*

Fees for flight tests for Aircraft Type ratings or Instrument ratings on aircraft which exceed a maximum total authorised weight of 5,700 kgs. requiring a flight test examiner supplied by the Authority, shall be charged on a cost recovery basis, subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee specified at paragraph 3 above.

( *e* ) *Flight tests for Flight Engineer's Licence or Flight Navigator's Licence*

Fees for a flight test, when required, for the grant, renewal, re-issue or validation of a Flight Engineer's Licence or Flight Navigator's Licence shall be charged on a cost recovery basis, subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee specified at paragraph 3 above for "Flight Test— Group B".

( *f* ) *Skill tests conducted in an aircraft simulator*

Fees for skill tests conducted in an aircraft simulator shall be charged on a cost recovery basis subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee for the licence or rating sought as specified in this Schedule.

( *g* ) *Delegated flight tests*

Flight tests delegated to an external flight test examiner may be charged at the rates applicable to the particular flight test as set out in this Schedule.

###### 7.

7. *Flight Tests giving rise to extraordinary costs*

Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions of this Schedule, where any flight tests are conducted which, due to the particular circumstances, give rise to extraordinary costs, such costs shall be recovered through increased fees which shall be notified to and agreed with the flight test candidate(s) in advance.

SCHEDULE 23

*Fees for Aeronautical Examinations*

There shall be charged and levied in respect of Aeronautical Examinations, listed in the Appendix to this Schedule and conducted for the purpose of the grant of flight crew licences, or, if required, for the validation of licences issued by the Aeronautical Authorities of other States, the fees specified in the said Appendix.

( *a* ) *Late application fee supplements*

Examination applications, accompanied by the appropriate fees, should be received at least 21 days before the examination starting date. Where an examination application is received less than 21 days but at least 7 days before the examination starting date, the fees for such application, if accepted, shall be increased by 20%. Where an examination application is received less than 7 days before the examination starting date, the fees for such application, if accepted, shall be increased by 50%. Where a change is made to an examination application less than 21 days before the examination starting date, the appropriate late application fee supplement as set out above shall be charged for each subject changed.

( *b* ) *Withdrawal from and non-attendance at examinations*

- (i) Any withdrawal of an examination application should be made in writing and if made orally must be confirmed in writing within 5 days.

- (ii) Where an examination application is withdrawn at least 21 days before the examination starting date there shall not be a cancellation fee.

- (iii) Where an examination application is withdrawn less than 21 days and more than 7 days before the examination starting date 20% of the fees shall be forfeited.

- (iv) Where an examination application is withdrawn 7 days or less before the examination starting date 50% of the fees shall be forfeited.

- (v) Where an applicant fails to attend at an examination, the fees in respect of that examination shall be forfeited. Where such failure to attend is in respect of a subject(s) the fees in respect of that subject (s) shall be forfeited.

- (vi) Where a portion of the fee has been forfeited under (iii) or (iv) above the balance will be renamed by the Authority and will be set against the fees for future examination applications, unless the applicant requests a refund in writing.

( *c* ) *Additional fees*

Where it is expedient to hold examinations at times or locations other than those normally appointed, or where the holding of such examinations has been specially requested, the fees may be increased to meet any additional costs. Such increased fees shall be determined by the Authority and notified in advance.

( *d* ) *Miscellaneous*

Where an examination is required for the grant, renewal, re-issue or validation of a Flight Engineer's Licence, a Flight Navigator's Licence, a Flight Radiotelephony Operator's Licence (General or Restricted), a Flight Radiotelegraphy Operator's Licence (Temporary or Full) or of any other licence or rating not otherwise provided for, the fees shall be determined by the Authority and notified in advance.
