# S.I. No. 509/1997 - Health (Outpatient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1997

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 509 of 1997.

HEALTH (OUTPATIENT CHARGES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1997

The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) as substituted by section I of the [Health (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1987) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Out-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1997.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1998.

###### 3.

3. Article 4 of the Health (Out-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 37 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0037.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of £20 for £12 in sub-article (2).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide that instead of the £12 charge for outpatient services introduced in March 1994 a charge of £20 shall be introduced from 1 January 1998 in respect of attendance at accident and emergency or casualty departments where the person concerned has not been referred by a medical practitioner. The charge shall not apply where such an attendance results in hospital admission. The usual exemptions and hardship provision shall continue to apply.
