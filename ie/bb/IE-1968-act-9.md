# CONTROL OF EXPORTS (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACT, 1956 (CONTINUANCE) ACT, 1968

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO EXTEND THE PERIOD DURING WHICH THE CONTROL OF EXPORTS (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACT, 1956, CONTINUES IN FORCE. [2*nd April*, 1968.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Continuance of the Control of Exports (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1956.

**1.**—(1) The Control of Exports (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1956, shall continue in force until the 31st day of March, 1971, and shall then expire.

- (2) The Control of Exports (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1956 (Continuance) Act, 1965, is hereby repealed.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Control of Exports (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1956 (Continuance) Act, 1968.
