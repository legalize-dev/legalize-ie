# S.I. No. 272/2000 - National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) and paragraph 5(2) of the Schedule to that Act, as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0041/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1996) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day), Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The 27th day of October, 2000 is hereby prescribed as an election day for producer members of the National Milk Agency.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of August, 2000. |
|  | JOE WALSH, |
|  | Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to fix 27th October 2000 as an election day for the National Milk Agency.
