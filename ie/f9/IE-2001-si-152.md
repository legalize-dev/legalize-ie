# S.I. No. 152/2001 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I. Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 and 30, and in relation to Article 5 of this Order, [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0005.html#sec5) , [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0027.html#sec27) (a) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (a), of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Revocations) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Restriction on Movement) (No.3) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No 62 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0062.html) ), the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Restriction on Movement (No. 3) (Amendment) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 90 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0090.html) ) and the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Import Restrictions) (No. 2) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 83 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0083.html) ) are revoked.

###### 3.

3. Each of the following Orders shall be construed as not including a reference to France, that is to say. -

- (a) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Restriction of Import of Vehicles. Machinery and Other Equipment) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 51 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0051.html) ), and

- (b) the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Restriction of Import of Horses and Greyhounds) (No. 2) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 85 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0085.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Foot-and-Mouth (Restriction on Movement (No. 4) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No 63 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0063.html) ) is amended by the deletion in the definition of “area” (inserted by the Foot-and-Mouth (Restriction on Movement) (No. 4) (Amendment) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No 91 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0091.html) )), of “(other than the area to which the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Restriction on Movement) (No. 3) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No 62 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0062.html) ) applies)”.

###### 5.

5. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Restriction on Movement of Certain Animals) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 121 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0121.html) ) is amended, in Article 3(1), by the deletion of “unless the animal is moved directly”.

###### 6.

6. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Import Restrictions) (No. 3) Order, 2001 (S.I. 108 of 2001) is amended, in Article 2(1), by -

- (a) the substitution for the definition of “restricted areas” of the following definition -

“‘restricted areas”’ means the Kingdom of the Netherlands and

- (b) the insertion of the following definition -

  > “’Netherlands” means the Kingdom of the Netherlands other than the restricted areas.”.

###### 7.

7. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Import Restrictions) (No. 4) Order, 2001 (S.I. 118 of 2001) is amended, in Article 2(1), by the substitution for the definition of “restricted areas” of the following definition —

“’restricted areas” means Great Britain and the Districts of Mourne and Newry in Northern Ireland.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The effect of this Order is:

- (a) to remove restrictions on certain imports from France imposed due to the foot-and-mouth disease situation,

- (b) to remove the “protection zone” around Proleek, Co. Louth while retaining the wider “surveillance area”,

- (c) to amend rules relating to movement of certain animals within the State, and

- (d) to amend rules relating to imports from the United Kingdom and the Netherlands.
