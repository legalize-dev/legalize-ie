# S.I. No. 368/2003 - National Milk Agency (Election Day) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) and paragraph 5(2) of the Schedule to that Act (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0041/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply)(Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1996)) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day Regulations, 2003.

###### 2.

2. The 31^st October 2003 is prescribed as an election day for producer members of the National Milk Agency.

|  |
| --- |
| GIVEN under my Official Seal, 14 August 2003 |
| *Joe Walsh* Minister for Agriculture and Food |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

**The purpose of these Regulations is to fix 31^st October 2003 as an election day for the National Milk Agency.**
