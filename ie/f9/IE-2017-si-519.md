# S.I. No. 519/2017 - Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th November*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec5) , [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec9) , [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec28) and [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec38) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012) and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2014/45/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 April 2014[^1] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) (Revocation) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 413 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0413.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

23 November 2017.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism &amp; Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these regulations is to revoke [S.I. No. 413 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0413.html) which needs to be revoked for technical reasons.
