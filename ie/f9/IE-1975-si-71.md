# S.I. No. 71/1975 - Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts (Amendment) Regulations, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 71 of 1975.

CENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND (FORM OF STATEMENT OF ACCOUNTS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1975.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0032/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Currency Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1927), as adapted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942), and amended by the Exchequer and Local Financial Years Act (Adaptation of Enactments and Statutory Instruments) (No. 2) Order, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 293 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0293.html) ), and after consultation with the Board of Directors of the Central Bank of Ireland, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations, 1975.

- (2) The Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1961 to 1973, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1961 to 1975.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0187.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution of "31st December" for "31st March", in each place where it occurs, in the Schedule.

###### 4.

4. The statement of accounts prepared by the Central Bank of Ireland under [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0032/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Currency Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1927), as adapted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942), in respect of the period from the 1st day of April to the 31st day of December, 1974, shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations save that, whenever in any statement of accounts a nil entry would require to be made under any heading in that form, the Bank may, in lieu of making the nil entry, omit that heading.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations amend the form in which the annual accounts of the Central Bank are prepared to take account of the change in the Bank's financial year, which now coincides with the calendar year. They also make provision for the form of accounts in respect of the transitional period from 1 April 1974 to 31 December 1974.
