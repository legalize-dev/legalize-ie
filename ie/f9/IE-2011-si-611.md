# S.I. No. 611/2011 - Adoption Act 2010 (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd December*, 2011.

I, JOAN BURTON, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 89](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec89) (as amended by regulation 3 and Schedule 1 of the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0418.html) )) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010), following consultation with the Minister for Health and Children, and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation from the date of signature.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Abridged Certificate” means, as respects an entry in the Adopted Children Register, a certificate of the items set out in the Schedule to these Regulations endorsed in the manner set out in the said Schedule.

###### 3.

3. An tArd-Chlaráitheoir shall issue an abridged certificate to any applicant on payment of a fee of €10.

###### 4.

4. In order to allow an tArd-Chlaráitheoir to issue an abridged certificate, the application shall contain as many as possible of the following particulars:

- (*a*) the forename,

- (*b*) the date and country of birth,

- (*c*) the name, address and occupation of the adopter or adopters,

- (*d*) the date of the adoption order.

###### 5.

5. The fact that any of the information specified in Regulation 4 of these Regulations is not furnished shall not be a valid reason for an tArd-Chlaráitheoir to refuse to issue an abridged certificate if he is satisfied that the appropriate entry can be identified.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to allow for the issue of an abridged certificate, containing specified particulars, from a register of adoptions maintained by an tArd-Chlaráitheoir and to allow for a fee to be charged for the issue of such abridged certificates.
