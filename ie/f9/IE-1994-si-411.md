# S.I. No. 411/1994 - Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Abolition of the Dublin District Milk Board) Order 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 411 of 1994.

MILK (REGULATION OF SUPPLY) (ABOLITION OF THE DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD) ORDER 1994.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me under [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0006.html#sec6) (3) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) , [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) and every and any other power me in this behalf enabling do hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. The Dublin District Milk Board shall stand dissolved on the 30th day of December 1994.

###### 2.

2. As from the establishment of the Interim Board the assets, liabilities, choses in action, contracts and commitments of the Dublin District Milk Board shall stand transferred to the Interim Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 5th day of December, 1994.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food

and Forestry.
