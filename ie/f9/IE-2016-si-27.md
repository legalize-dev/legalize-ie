# S.I. No. 27/2016 - Control on Animal Vaccines (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th January*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec36) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), for the purpose of inter alia, giving further effect to Article 71 of Directive 2001/82/EC of the European Parliament and the Council of 6 November 2001[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Control on Animal Vaccines (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Control on Animal Vaccines Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0193.html) ) (as amended by the Control on Animal Vaccines (Amendment) Regulations 2014) ( [S.I. No. 590 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0590.html) )) are amended in Part 1 of the Second Schedule by deleting the following—

> “Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (BVD) Type 2”

###### 3.

3. The Control on Animal Vaccines (Amendment) Regulations 2014 are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 January 2016.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This regulation provides for the removal of a certain disease from Part 1 of the Second Schedule of the Control on Animal Vaccines Regulations 2014.
