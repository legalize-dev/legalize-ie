S.I. No. 395 of 1977.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (ROADS AND MOTORWAYS) ACT, 1974 (PRESCRIBED FORMS) REGULATIONS, 1977.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0004.html#sec4) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Act" means the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. ( *a* ) Form No. 1 in the Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 4 of the Act.

( *b* ) Form No. 2 in the Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of subsection (1) (*a*) of section 5 of the Act.

( *c* ) Form No. 3 in the Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of subsection (1) (*b*) of section 5 of the Act.

( *d* ) Form No. 4 in the Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of subsection (3) of section 8 of the Act.

The ................ (hereinafter referred to as "the road authority") in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) have made a Scheme entitled as above for the purpose of providing a motorway from ................ to ........................ ^1

The Minister may not, however, approve the Scheme until he has caused to be held a public local inquiry into all matters relating to the Scheme and until he has considered the report of the person conducting such inquiry and has considered any objections to the Scheme which have been lodged and which have not been withdrawn.

If the Scheme is approved, the road authority will thereupon be authorised under [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) and [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) :—

( *a* ) to acquire compulsorily the lands described in the First Schedule hereto,

( *b* ) to extinguish the public right(s) of way described in the Second Schedule hereto, by order made after the acquisition of the lands over which there is the said public right(s) of way,

( *c* ) to close, stop up, remove, alter or divert any existing means of access to the motorway from the lands described in Part I of the Third Schedule hereto.

( *d* ) by notice under [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , to suspend, revoke or modify planning permissions in respect of the lands referred to in Part II of the Third Schedule hereto.

In any case where the closing, stopping up, removing, altering or diverting of any means of access results in the owner of any land being deprived of the only means of access from that land to any public road the road authority shall not close, stop up, remove, alter or divert such means of access until an alternative means of access to a public road has been provided.

Where, by reason of the construction of the motorway a person is deprived of reasonable access from one part of his property to another, the Minister is empowered to direct that a suitable alternative means of access shall be provided.

If land to which the Scheme, as approved by the Minister, relates, is acquired by the road authority, compensation will be assessed in accordance with Part II of the Fourth Schedule to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , and the provisions of the Acquisition of Land (Assessment of Compensation) Act, 1919, as amended by the [Acquisition of Land (Reference Committee) Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , the [Property Values (Arbitrations and Appeals) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) , [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , subject to the modifications contained in the Third Schedule to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .

In the case of land consisting of a house unfit for human habitation and not capable of being rendered so fit at reasonable expense, compensation will be assessed in accordance with Part I of the Fourth Schedule to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , and the provisions of the Acquisition of Land (Assessment of Compensation) Act, 1919, as amended and modified as aforesaid, as the value of the land at the time the relevant notice to treat is served as a site cleared of buildings and available for development in accordance with the requirements of building bye-laws in force in the area under the Local Government (Sanitary Services) Acts, 1878 to 1964, or building regulations in force in the area under the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , less such sum as may be determined by the arbitrator to be the cost of clearing and levelling the land.

If the Minister is of the opinion that a house described in the Scheme as being unfit for human habitation and not capable of being rendered so fit, ought not to have been so described, he may modify the Scheme in so far as it relates to the house, so as to authorise the road authority to acquire the house and to pay compensation for it assessed in accordance with Part II of the Fourth Schedule to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , and the provisions of the Acquisition of Land (Assessment of Compensation) Act, 1919, as amended and modified as aforesaid.

The land/part of the land in which you have an interest consists of a house or houses which is/are, in the opinion of the road authority, unfit for human habitation and not capable of being rendered fit for human habitation at reasonable expense.

Whenever by reason of the construction of a motorway, access from any land to a public road has been closed, stopped up, removed, altered or diverted by a road authority pursuant to [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (3) of the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) and any person has suffered damage in consequence thereof by the depreciation of any interest in the land to which he is entitled or by being disturbed in his enjoyment of the land, he shall be entitled to recover from the road authority compensation in respect of that damage but in assessing compensation regard shall be had to any new means of access provided by the road authority.

A claim for compensation under [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) shall, in default of agreement, be determined by arbitration under the Acquisition of Land (Assessment of Compensation) Act, 1919, in the like manner in all respects as if such claim arose in relation to the compulsory acquisition of land.

Any question of disputed compensation shall be referred to and be determined by a property arbitrator appointed under the [Property Values (Arbitrations and Appeals) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) . A claimant for compensation may, at any time after the expiration of fourteen days from the date on which the relevant notice to treat is served, send to the Secretary, the Reference Committee, Four Courts, Dublin, an application in writing for the nomination of a property arbitrator for the purpose of determining the compensation to be paid. The application should be made in accordance with the Property Values (Arbitrations and Appeals) Rules, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0091.html) ).
