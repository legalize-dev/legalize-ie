# S.I. No. 423/2011 - Diseases of Animals (Inspection Fees) (Revocation) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th August*, 2011.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Inspection Fees) (Revocation) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals (Inspection Fees) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0030.html) ) and the Diseases of Animals (Inspection Fees) Order 2004 (S.I. 439 of 2004) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 August 2011.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order revokes two Orders regarding the collection of inspection fees made under the Diseases of Animals Act.
