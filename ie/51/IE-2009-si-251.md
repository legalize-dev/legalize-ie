CIRCUIT COURT RULES (MISCELLANEOUS) 2009

###### 1.

1. These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Miscellaneous) 2009, shall come into operation on the 28th day of July 2009.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2009.

###### 3.

3. The Circuit Court Rules are amended:

- (i) by the substitution in the “Interpretation of Terms” provisions for the definition of “The Court” of the following:

  > “6. “The Court” means the Circuit Court as established by the [Courts (Establishment and Constitution) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .”;

- (ii) by the substitution for rule 1 of Order 46 of the following:

  > “1. Proceedings for the care of infants’ estates referred to at reference number 24 in the Third Schedule to the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) or proceedings referred to at reference number 25 or reference number 26 in the Third Schedule to that Act shall be commenced by issuing a Civil Bill in accordance with Form 2B of the Schedule of Forms, entitled in the matter of the Act under which the proceeding is taken and of the trust or settlement, as the case may be, which said Civil Bill shall be entitled “Equity Civil Bill”.”;

- (iii) by the substitution for rule 20 of Order 66 of the following:

  > “20. Costs in proceedings heard by the Court by virtue of a consent referred to in section 22(1)(*b*) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) shall be taxed according to the appropriate High Court scale for the time being in force and the Rules applicable thereto, less one-fifth, together with actual and necessary outlay, witnesses’ expenses where allowed by the Court, and such fees for Counsel as, having regard to the nature of the proceedings and the issues involved, the County Registrar may allow.”;

- (iv) by the deletion from rule 27 of Order 66 of paragraph (13); and

- (v) by the deletion from Order 67 of rule 8.

###### 4.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This does not form part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

Circuit Court Rules (Miscellaneous) 2009 amend the Interpretation of Terms section of Rules to refer to the Circuit Court as established by the [Courts (Establishment and Constitution) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) as well as amending a number of Orders and ancillary text by removing references to the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) and, amend Form 39 - ORDER TRANSFERRING A TRIAL TO THE DUBLIN CIRCUIT to refer to [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0031/sec0032.html#sec32) of the [Courts and Court Officers Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) in accordance with Order 63 rule 1.
