# S.I. No. 615/2022 - Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th December,* 2022.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec20) (4)(a) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2009) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 (S. I. No. 408 of 2020)), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Social Housing Assessment Regulations 2011 (S. I. No. 84 of 2011) (as amended by the Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) Regulations (S. I. No. 136 of 2011)) are amended—

- (a) in Regulation 19(1), by substituting “€40,000” for “€35,000”,

- (b) in Regulation 20(1), by substituting “€35,000” for “€30,000”, and

- (c) in Regulation 21(1), by substituting “€30,000” for “€25,000”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 December, 2022.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations amend Regulations 19(1), 20(1) and 21(1) of the Social Housing Assessment Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 84 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0084.html) ) to increase by €5,000 each the 3 bands of maximum income limits for eligibility for social housing support across the State.
