# S.I. No. 213/1995 - Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 213 of 1995.

TRAVEL AGENTS (LICENSING) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, MICHAEL LOWRY, Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Transport (Tour Operators and Travel Agents) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1982), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Transport and Communications (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 17 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0017.html) ) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1995.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 10 of the Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1993, is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution in paragraph (*a*) of "£243" for "£221",

( *b* ) the substitution in paragraph (*b*) of "£24" for "£22", and

( *c* ) the substitution in paragraph (*c*) of "£243" for "£221",

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 1st day of August, 1995.

**PHILIP FURLONG,**

A person authorised to authenticate the Seal of the Minister for Finance.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, this 1st day of August, 1995.

**JOHN LOUGHREY,**

A person authorised to authenticate the Seal of the Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to revise the level of fees payable in respect of applications for a travel agents licence and in respect of amendment of such licences.
