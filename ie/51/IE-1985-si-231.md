# S.I. No. 231/1985 - Cork District Milk Board (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 231 of 1985.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (LEVY) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), and by section 41 (as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), and [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1952)) of that Act, do hereby order and make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Board" means the Cork District Milk Board;

"the section" means [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), and [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1952).

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) For the purposes of the levy payable to the Board under subsection (1) (*b*) of the section.

( *a* ) the prescribed sum shall, in respect of the month of July, 1985 and each subsequent month, be 0.862 new pence;

( *b* ) regulation 4(1) (*a*) of the Cork District Milk Board (levy) (Amendment) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 106 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0106.html) ), shall not apply in relation to any such month.

- (2) For the purposes of the levy payable to the Board under subsection (2) (*b*) of the section:

( *a* ) the prescribed sum shall, in respect of the month of July, 1985 and each subsequent month, be 0.862 new pence;

( *b* ) regulation 4(2) (*a*) of the Regulations mentioned in paragraph (1) (b) shall not apply in relation to any such month.

###### 5.

5. The Cork District Milk Board (levy) (Amendment) Regulations 1973 ( [S.I. No. 106 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0106.html) ) are hereby revoked with effect from the 1st day of July, 1985.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 10th day of July, 1985.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the amount of the milk levy payable by registered retailers and registered wholesalers and by certain registered producers as from 1 July 1985.
