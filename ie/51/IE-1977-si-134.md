# S.I. No. 134/1977 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk) Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 134 of 1977.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK) ORDER, 1977.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk) Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of July, 1977 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the Sanitary Districts specified in the Schedule hereto unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

SCHEDULE.

SANITARY DISTRICTS.

Urban District of Arklow

Urban District of Athy

Urban District of Bray

County Health District of Cavan

Urban District of Ceanannus Mór

Urban District of Dundalk

Urban District of Midleton

Urban District of Naas

Urban District of Navan

Urban District of Trim

County Borough of Waterford

County Health District of Waterford

Urban District of Wicklow

County Health District of Wicklow

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 9th day of May, 1977.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of July, 1977 the sale of milk in the Sanitary districts listed in the order unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) . The special designations are set out in the Milk and Dairies (Special Designations) Regulations 1938 to 1955 (S.R. and O. 1938 No. 148).
