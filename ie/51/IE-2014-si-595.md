# S.I. No. 595/2014 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 8) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd January*, 2015.

I, JOAN BURTON, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), by section 4 (amended by [section 96](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec96) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2011)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , and by sections 2(2)(*c*), 232 and 297 of, and Reference 19 in Table 2 to Schedule 3 to, the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 8) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2014.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2014 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2014.

*Definition*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Regulations of 2014” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 1) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 155 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0155.html) ).

*Calculation of means*

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in article 8(3) (amended by article 2 of the Regulations of 2014), by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (*b*):

  > - “(ba) any *ex gratia* payments—
  >
  >     - (i) approved by the Lourdes Hospital Redress Board under the terms of the Lourdes Hospital Redress Scheme 2007,
  >
  >     - (ii) made under the terms of the Lourdes Hospital Payment Scheme established by the Minister for Health, or
  >
  >     - (*iii*) made under the terms of the Surgical Symphysiotomy *ex gratia* Scheme established by the Minister for Health and referred to as the Symphysiotomy Payment Scheme,”,

- (*b*) in article 155 (amended by article 2 of the Regulations of 2014), by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (*b*):

  > - “(ba) any *ex gratia* payments—
  >
  >     - (i) approved by the Lourdes Hospital Redress Board under the terms of the Lourdes Hospital Redress Scheme 2007,
  >
  >     - (ii) made under the terms of the Lourdes Hospital Payment Scheme established by the Minister for Health, or
  >
  >     - (iii) made under the terms of the Surgical Symphysiotomy *ex gratia* Scheme established by the Minister for Health and referred to as the Symphysiotomy Payment Scheme,”,

  and

- (*c*) in article 174—

    - (i) by designating paragraph (ff) (inserted by article 2 of the Regulations of 2014) as paragraph (fa), and

    - (ii) by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (fa):

      > - “(fb) any *ex gratia* payments—
      >
      >     - (i) approved by the Lourdes Hospital Redress Board under the terms of the Lourdes Hospital Redress Scheme 2007,
      >
      >     - (ii) made under the terms of the Lourdes Hospital Payment Scheme established by the Minister for Health, or
      >
      >     - (iii) made under the terms of the Surgical Symphysiotomy *ex gratia* Scheme established by the Minister for Health and referred to as the Symphysiotomy Payment Scheme, or”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the foregoing Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide that any income received by way of *ex gratia* payments from—

— the Lourdes Hospital Redress Scheme 2007,

— the Lourdes Hospital Payment Scheme, and

— the Surgical Symphysiotomy *ex gratia* Scheme,

will be disregarded in the assessment of means for the purposes of weekly social assistance payments, increases for qualified adults and Family Income Supplement.
