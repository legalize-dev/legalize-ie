S.I. No. 65 of 1994.

ARMY PENSIONS (INCREASE) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, DAVID ANDREWS, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0021/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Army Pensions Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1980), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

1 Short title and collective citation.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1994.

- (2) The Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 to 1993, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 to 1994.

2 Definitions and construction.

###### 2.

**2.** (1) In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1927" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1927);

"the Act of 1932" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1932);

"the Act of 1937" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1937);

"the Act of 1941" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1941);

"the Act of 1943" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1943](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1943/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1943);

"the Act of 1953" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1953);

"the Act of 1959" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1959);

"the No. 2 Act of 1960" means the [Army Pensions (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1960);

"the Act of 1962" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1962);

"the Act of 1971" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1971);

"the Acts" means the Army Pensions Acts, 1923 to 1973;

"the Increase Regulations of 1969" means the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0191.html) );

"the No. 2 Increase Regulations of 1993" means the Army Pensions (Increase) (No. 2) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 421 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0421.html) ).

- (2) These Regulations shall be construed as one with the Acts and the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 to 1993.

- (3) References in these Regulations to any enactment shall be construed as references to that enactment as amended by any subsequent enactment or regulations.

4 Amendment of paragraph 4 of Increase Regulations of 1969.

###### 4.

**4.** (1) A wound or disability pension to which paragraph 4 of the Increase Regulations of 1969 applies, and which is payable to an officer discharged from the forces before—

- (*a*) the 2nd day of January, 1991, shall, with effect from the 1st day of January, 1991, be increased to the amount which would be payable to such officer under the Acts if he had been discharged from the forces on the 2nd day of January, 1991, and his rank and service in that rank on that date were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge;

- (*b*) the 2nd day of January, 1992, shall, with effect from the 1st day of January, 1992, be increased to the amount which would be payable to such officer under the Acts if he had been discharged from the forces on the 2nd day of January, 1992, and his rank and service in that rank on that date were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge;

- (*c*) the 2nd day of December, 1992, shall, with effect from the 1st day of December, 1992, be increased to the amount which would be payable to such officer under the Acts if he had been discharged from the forces on the 2nd day of December, 1992, and his rank and service in that rank on that date were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge;

- (*d*) the 2nd day of January, 1993, shall, with effect from the 1st day of January, 1993, be increased to the amount which would be payable to such officer under the Acts if he had been discharged from the forces on the 2nd day of January, 1993, and his rank and service in that rank on that date were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge.
