# S.I. No. 198/1997 - Air Navigation and Transport (Application of Regulations To State Aircraft) (Government) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 198 of 1997.

AIR NAVIGATION AND TRANSPORT (APPLICATION OF REGULATIONS TO STATE AIRCRAFT) (GOVERNMENT) ORDER, 1997.

The Government

( *a* ) in pursuance of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0040/sec0003.html#sec3) (2) of the [Air Navigation and Transport Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1936), hereby directs that—

- (i) [Section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0040/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Air Navigation and Transport Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (as amended by the [Air Navigation and Transport Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1988)), and

- (ii) any regulations made under that section, to the extent that those regulations so provide,

shall apply to State Aircraft, and

( *b* ) in pursuance of section 6 (*b*) of that Act, authorises the Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications to make regulations for carrying out the purposes of this Order.

This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation and Transport (Application of Regulations to State Aircraft) (Government) Order, 1997.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 6th day of May, 1997.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Taoiseach.
