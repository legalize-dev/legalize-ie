# S.I. No. 24/2000 - Irish Aviation Authority (Fees) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Irish Aviation Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) , [58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0058.html#sec58) , [59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0059.html#sec59) , [60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0060.html#sec60) , [60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0060.html#sec60) (1)(j)(i) and [75](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0075.html#sec75) of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , (No. 29 of 1993) as amended by the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998) hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Irish Aviation Authority (Fees) Order, 2000.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of March 2000 or on the date of its publication in the Iris Oifigiuil, whichever date occurs later.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “the Authority” means the Irish Aviation Authority and references to the charging and levying of fees shall be construed as references to the charging and levying of fees by the Authority.

###### 3.

3. The Air Navigation (Fees) Order, 1998, ( [S.I. No. 486 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0486.html) ) is hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. (1) Fees shall be charged and levied by the Authority in accordance with the provisions of the Schedules to this Order numbered 1 to 29;

- (2) The Authority, through such of its officers as may be so authorised in that behalf, may remit or reduce, in any case or class of cases in relation to which it is satisfied that it is reasonable to do so, any fee prescribed by this Order.

###### 5.

5. The Authority may give directions for carrying out the purposes of this Order in respect of any matter or thing provided for by this Order. The fee scales in this Order are specified in IR£ but payment of fees due in Euro will be acceptable at the fixed conversion rate established in January 1999.

**SCHEDULE 1 - FEES FOR THE REGISTRATION OF AN AIRCRAFT**

- (1) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a certificate of registration of an aircraft fees in accordance with the following scale:-

where the maximum total authorised weight (MTWA) of the aircraft:-

- (a) does not exceed 2,730 kgs., a fee of £30 (€38.09);

- (b) exceeds 2,730 kgs. but does not exceed 5,000 kgs., a fee of £90 (€114.28);

- (c) exceeds 5,000 kgs. but does not exceed 13,600 kgs., a fee of £125 (€158.72);

- (d) exceeds 13,600 kgs. but does not exceed 45,500 kgs., a fee of £210 (€266.65);

- (e) exceeds 45,500 kgs., a fee of £315 (€399.97).

- (2) There shall be charged and levied for the reservation of an out of sequence certificate of registration of an aircraft, for each period of six months or less, a fee of £125 (€158.72).

- (3) There shall be charged and levied for the issue of an out of sequence certificate of registration of an aircraft, in addition to any other fee payable under (1) above, a fee of £315. (€399.97).

**SCHEDULE 2 - FEES FOR A CERTIFICATE OF TYPE APPROVAL FOR AN AIRCRAFT, AN AIRCRAFT ENGINE OR ANY OTHER AVIATION PRODUCT**

There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a Certificate of Type approval for an aircraft, an aircraft engine or other aviation product a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purpose of such issue.

**SCHEDULE 3 - FEES FOR CERTIFICATES OF AIRWORTHINESS**

- (1) There shall be charged and levied for the issue or renewal of a certificate of airworthiness, or the issue of an export certificate of airworthiness, or the issue or renewal of a validation for a certificate of airworthiness issued by another state, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such issue, or renewal subject to the following minimum fees for each year or part thereof to which the certificate relates:-

    - (a) in the case of a glider or balloon of which the maximum total authorised weight does not exceed 2,000 kgs., a fee of £115 (€146.02);

    - (b) in the case of other aircraft of which the maximum total authorised weight:-

        - (i) does not exceed 5,000 kgs., for each 50 kgs. or part thereof, a fee of £11 (€13.97),

        - (ii) exceeds 5,000 kgs. but does not exceed 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 5,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (i) above, a fee of £75 (€95.23),

        - (iii) exceeds 100,000 kgs., for each 500 kgs. or part thereof, above 100,000 kgs., in addition to the maximum fee payable under (ii) above, a fee of £60 (€76.18).

- (2) Where an aircraft is based in another state under a lease agreement acceptable to the Authority and operated by an operator who is not the holder of an air operator's certificate issued by the Authority, the fees applicable at (i), (ii) and (iii)of paragraph (I) of this Schedule shall be generally applicable to that aircraft but there shall be charged and levied a minimum fee of £5,750 (€7300.99) per aircraft for each year to which an issue or renewal action relates.

**SCHEDULE 4 - FEES FOR A FLIGHT PERMIT**

There shall be charged and levied for the grant or renewal of a flight permit, a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purpose of such grant or renewal, subject to a minimum fee of £185 (€234.90).

**SCHEDULE 5 - FEES FOR APPROVAL OF MODIFICATION TO AN AIRCRAFT**

There shall be charged and levied for approval of a modification to an aircraft less than 2700 kgs. a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such an approval, subject to a minimum fee of £265 (€336.48).

There shall be charged and levied for approval of a modification to an aircraft greater than 2700 kgs. a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such an approval, subject to a minimum fee of £265 (€336.48), where such modification is fully supported at the time of application by a state of manufacturer approval or where the design is carried out by an IAA accepted design organisation.

There shall be charged and levied for approval of a modification to an aircraft greater than 2700 kgs. a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purposes of such an approval, subject to a minimum fee of £950 (€1206.25), where such modification is not fully supported by a state of manufacturer approval or where the design is not carried out by an IAA accepted design organisation.

**SCHEDULE 6 - FEES FOR THE ISSUE OR VALIDATION OF A NOISE CERTIFICATE**

There shall be charged and levied for the issue of a noise certificate or for the validation of a noise certificate issued by the competent authority of another state a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required by the Authority for the purpose of such issue or validation, subject to a minimum fee of £30 (€38.09). This charge is incorporated in the registration and airworthiness fees for aircraft of MTWA greater than 5000 kg.

**SCHEDULE 7 - FEES FOR THE ALLOCATION AND ISSUE OF MODE ‘S’ TRANSPONDER CODES**

There shall be charged and levied for the allocation and issue of a Mode ‘S’ transponder code a fee of £30 (€38.09). This charge is incorporated in the registration and airworthiness fees for aircraft of MTWA greater than 5000 kg.

**SCHEDULE 9 - FEES FOR THE APPROVAL OF AN ORGANISATION (DESIGN AND/OR MANUFACTURE)**

There shall be charged and levied by the Authority for the approval of an organisation as an organisation competent to design and/or to manufacture aircraft parts or aviation materials a fee of an amount equivalent to the cost of such investigations and work as may be required for the purposes of the grant or renewal of such an approval subject to a minimum fee of £2,300 (€2920.40) per approval.

**SCHEDULE 10 - FEES FOR INSPECTION FOR THE PURPOSES OF APPROVAL OF A PREMISES (STORAGE)**

    - (i) Class A1 - Aerodromes: Aerodromes, having a Control Zone and/or Instrument Approach and having at least one runway of length 1800m or more, which are licensed for Public use.

**SCHEDULE 20 - FEES FOR FLIGHT TESTS**

**1. GENERAL**

There shall be charged and levied for flight tests conducted to assess the skill of applicants for the grant, renewal or re-issue of various flight crew licences and associated ratings or, where required, for the validation of licences issued by the relevant authorities of other states fees as set out hereunder:

- (a) For the purposes of this Schedule, the term “Professional Pilot's Licence” includes a Commercial Pilot's Licence (C.P.L), or an Airline Transport Pilot's Licence (A.T.P.L).

**2. CLASSIFICATION OF FLIGHT TESTS**

For the purposes of this Schedule, flight tests are classified as follows:-

- (a) Flight Tests - Group A:

The flight tests in this group are for the grant or, where required, the renewal, re-issue or validation of a Private Pilot's Licence (P.P.L.), a multi-engine rating or an aircraft class or type rating in respect of an aircraft of under 5,700 Kgs. Maximum Total Authorised Weight and for the renewal of an Instrument Rating endorsed on any licence.

- (b) Flight Tests - Group B:

The flight tests in this group are for the grant, or, where required, for the renewal, re-issue or validation of a Professional Pilot's Licence and for the grant of a single or multi-engine Instrument Rating or the grant of a Flight Instructor Rating Class 1 or Class 2 in respect of single or multi-engine aircraft of under 5,700 kgs. Maximum Total Authorised Weight.

- (c) Flight Tests - Group C:

The flight tests in this group are for the renewal of a Flight Instructor Rating or the renewal of an Examiner authorisation.

- (d) Flight Tests - Group D:

The flight tests in this group are for the grant, or, where required, for the renewal, re-issue or validation of a Professional Pilot's Licence and for the grant of a single or multi-engine Type Rating or Instrument Rating, or for the grant of an Instructor Rating or the grant of an Examiner authorisation in respect of a single or multi-engine aircraft of over 5,700 kgs. Maximum Total Authorised Weight.

**3. FEES PAYABLE**

The amounts listed below are the fees for a flight test.

- (a) Flight Test - Group A:

£210 (€266.65) per candidate

- (b) Flight Test - Group B:

£288 (€365.69) per candidate

- (c) Flight test - Group C:

£135 (€171.42) per candidate

- (d) Flight test Group D:

£375 (€476.16) per candidate

Repeat tests will be charged at 50% of the standard rate.

**4. FEE REDUCTION FOR COMBINED FLIGHT TESTS**

Where a candidate intends to take a combined flight test, e.g. a C.P.L. and Instrument rating, the fees for the additional ratings will be 25% of the fee otherwise applicable. The total fee will be rounded up to the nearest whole pound.

**5. SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS FOR FLIGHT TEST FEES**

- (a) Failure to attend for flight test

Where a flight test candidate does not attend for the flight test and such absence has not been excused by prior arrangement with the flight test examiner the candidate's fees shall be forfeited. However, 50% of the fee will be offset against the fee otherwise payable should the candidate apply for another flight test.

- (b) Withdrawal from flight test

Where a flight test candidate withdraws his flight test application before any costs have been incurred by the flight test examiner, he may be paid a refund of his flight test fee less an administration fee of £20 (€25.40). Where a flight test candidate withdraws his flight test application after costs have been incurred by the flight test examiner, the rules applicable to failure to attend as set out in (a) above shall apply.

- (c) Failure to provide aircraft for flight test

It is the responsibility of the applicant(s) to provide a serviceable, suitable and acceptable aircraft for the flight test(s). Where a flight test examiner attends at a location for a flight test(s) and such test(s) cannot be carried out because of the unavailability of a serviceable, suitable and acceptable aircraft, the sum of £180 (€228.56) shall be forfeited by the candidate.

- (d) Flight tests for heavy aircraft

Fees for flight tests for Aircraft Type ratings or instrument ratings on aircraft which exceed a Maximum Total Authorised Weight of 5,700 kgs. requiring a flight test examiner supplied by the Authority, shall be charged on a cost recovery basis, subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee specified at paragraph 3 above.

- (e) Flight tests for Flight Engineer's Licence or Flight Navigator's Licence

Fees for a flight test, when required, for the grant, renewal, re-issue or validation of a Flight Engineer's Licence or Flight Navigator's Licence shall be charged on a cost recovery basis, subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee specified at paragraph 3 above for “Flight Test - Group B”.

- (f) Skill tests conducted in an aircraft simulator

Fees for skill tests conducted in an aircraft simulator shall be charged on a cost recovery basis subject to a minimum of the appropriate flight test fee for the licence or rating sought as specified in this Schedule.

- (g) Delegated flight tests

Flight tests delegated to an external flight test examiner may be charged at the rates applicable to the particular flight test as set out in this Schedule.

###### 6.

**6. FLIGHT TESTS GIVING RISE TO EXTRAORDINARY COSTS**

Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions of this Schedule, where any flight tests are conducted which, due to the particular circumstances, give rise to extraordinary costs, such costs shall be recovered through increased fees which shall be notified to and agreed with the flight test candidate(s) in advance.

**SCHEDULE 23 - FEES FOR AERONAUTICAL EXAMINATIONS**

There shall be charged and levied in respect of Aeronautical Examinations, listed in the Appendix to this Schedule and conducted for the purpose of the grant of flight crew licences, or, if required, for the validation of licences issued by the Aeronautical Authorities of other states, the fees specified in the said Appendix.

- (a) Late application fee supplements

Examination applications, accompanied by the appropriate fees, should be received at least 21 days before the examination starting date. Where an examination application is received less than 21 days but at least 7 days before the examination starting date, the fees for such application, if accepted, shall be increased by 20%. Where an examination application is received less than 7 days before the examination starting date, the fees for such application, if accepted, shall be increased by 50%. Where a change is made to an examination application less than 21 days before the examination starting date, the appropriate late application fee supplement as set out above shall be charged for each subject changed.

- (b) Withdrawal from and non-attendance at examinations

    - (i) Any withdrawal of an examination application should be made in writing and if made orally must be confirmed in writing within 5 days;

    - (ii) Where an examination application is withdrawn at least 21 days before the examination starting date there shall not be a cancellation fee;

    - (iii) Where an examination application is withdrawn less than 21 days and more than 7 days before the examination starting date 20% of the fees shall be forfeited.;

    - (iv) Where an examination application is withdrawn 7 days or less before the examination starting date 50% of the fees shall be forfeited;

    - (v) Where an applicant fails to attend at an examination, the fees in respect of that examination shall be forfeited. Where such failure to attend is in respect of a subject(s) the fees in respect of that subject(s) shall be forfeited.;

    - (vi) Where a portion of the fee has been forfeited under (iii) or (iv) above the balance will be retained by the Authority and will be set against the fees for future examination applications, unless the applicant requests a refund in writing.

- (c) Additional fees

Where it is expedient to hold examinations at times or locations other than those normally appointed, or where the holding of such examinations has been specially requested, the fees may be increased to meet any additional costs. Such increased fees shall be determined by the Authority and notified in advance.

- (d) Miscellaneous

Where an examination is required for the grant, renewal, re-issue or validation of a Flight Engineer's Licence, a Flight Navigator's Licence, a Flight Radiotelephony Operator's Licence (General or Restricted), a Flight Radiotelegraphy Operator's Licence (Temporary or Full) or of any other licence or rating not otherwise provided for, the fees shall be determined by the Authority and notified in advance.
