# S.I. No. 513/2016 - District Court (Issue of civil proceedings) Rules 2016

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th October*, 2016.

S.I. No. 513 of 2016

DISTRICT COURT (ISSUE OF CIVIL PROCEEDINGS) RULES 2016

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which shall come into operation on the 8th day of November 2016, may be cited as the District Court (Issue of civil proceedings) Rules 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) are amended:

- (i) by the substitution for sub-rule (5) of rule 4 of Order 40 of the following sub-rule:

  > - “(5) Except in a case to which section 7(6)(*a*)(ii) of the [Courts Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) applies, a claim notice which has been issued by a Clerk is deemed to have been issued on the date on which it was filed with the Clerk for issue.”, and

- (ii) by the substitution for sub-rule (3) of rule 3 of Order 40A of the following sub-rule:

  > - “(3) Except in a case to which section 7(6)(*a*)(ii) of the [Courts Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) applies, a personal injuries summons which has been issued by a Clerk is deemed to have been issued on the date on which it was filed with the Clerk for issue.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules amend Order 40 rule 5(4) and Order 40A rule 3(3) of the District Court Rules to provide for [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (6)(a)(ii) of the [Courts Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) .
