# S.I. No. 485/2002 - European Communities (Labelling of Beef and Beef Products) (Amendment) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Articles 11 to 24 of the European Parliament and Council Regulation (EC) No. 1760/2000 of 17 July 2000[^1] and Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1825/2000 of 25 August 2000[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Labelling of Beef and Beef Products)(Amendment) Regulations, 2002.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Labelling of Beef and Beef Products) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 435 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0435.html) ) are amended by —

- (a) substituting for Regulation 15 -

  > “15. An offence under these Regulations may be prosecuted by —
  >
  > - (a) the Minister,
  >
  > - (b) the local authority in whose functional area the offence is committed, or
  >
  > - (c) the health board in whose functional area the offence is committed.”.

- (b) substituting “€ 2,500” for “£1,500” in Regulation 13.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | **GIVEN** under my Official Seal, this 16^th day of October 2002. |
|  |  |
|  | Joe Walsh Minister for Agriculture and Food |

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Regulations provide for the prosecution of offences under the European Communities (Labelling of Beef and Beef Products) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 435 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0435.html) ) by the Minister, local authority or health board and increase the maximum fine on conviction to € 2,500.
