# S.I. No. 551/2016 - Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction (Mussel Seed) (Opening of Fisheries) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th November*, 2016.

The Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec15) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction (Mussel Seed) (Opening of Fisheries) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction (Mussel Seed) (Opening of Fisheries) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 469 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0469.html) ) are amended in Regulation 1(2) by substituting “11 December 2016” for “5 November 2016”.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine,

4 November 2016.

**PASCHAL HAYES,**

A Person Authorised Under [Section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Ministers and Secretaries Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) to Authenticate the Seal of the Minister for Agriculture, Food and Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The effect of these Regulations is to close the fishing season by prohibiting fishing for mussel seed.
