# S.I. No. 130/2019 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments And Control) (Amendment) (No. 7) (Assessment Of Means) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil”* of 5*th April,* 2019.

I, REGINA DOHERTY, Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) (amended by [section 96](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec96) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2011)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), and by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec2) (2)(c), [232](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec232) and [297](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec297) of, and Reference 19 in Table 2 to Schedule 3 to, the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

***Citation and construction***

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 7) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2019.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2019 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2019.

***Definition***

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Regulations of 2018” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 8) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 654 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0654.html) ).

###### 3.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide that any payments made by the Minister for Education and Skills as part of the School Transport Scheme for Children with Special Educational Needs in the form of the Special Transport Grant will be disregarded in the assessment of means for the purposes of weekly social assistance payments, increases for qualified adults and the Working Family Payment.
