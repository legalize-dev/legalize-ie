# S.I. No. 434/2001 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) (Special Secretarial Allowance) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section (2B) (inserted by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1962), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Special Secretarial Allowance) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In these regulations “the 1962 Act” means the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1962),

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of December, 2000.

###### 4.

4. The amount of the special secretarial allowance payable under Section (2A) of the 1962 Act shall be increased as follows:

- (a) in the case of a member of Dáil Éireann from £5,000 per year to £7,000 per year and

- (b) in the case of a member of Seanad Éireann from £3,750 per year to £5,250 per year

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for an increase in the Special Secretarial Assistance Allowance payable to Members of the Oireachtas as a contribution towards the engagement of additional secretarial assistance by Members. From the 1st day of December 2000, an annual allowance of £7,000 shall be payable to a TD and £5,250 shall be payable to a Senator.
