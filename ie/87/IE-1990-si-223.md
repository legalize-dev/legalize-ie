# S.I. No. 223/1990 - General Medical Services (Payments) Board (Establishment) Order, 1972 (Amendment) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 223 of 1990.

GENERAL MEDICAL SERVICES (PAYMENTS) BOARD (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1972 (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1990.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), hereby orders as follows:—

- (1) This Order may be cited as the General Medical Services (Payments) Board (Establishment) Order, 1972 (Amendment) Order, 1990.

- (2) The General Medical Services (Payments) Board (Establishment) Order 1972 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0184.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution in articles 3 and 6 of "section 59" for "section 59 (1)" and by the addition of the words "or arising from the provision of such services" after the word "services" in (*a*) of article 3.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 29th day of August, 1990.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order extends the area of responsibility of the General Medical Services (Payments) Board.
