# S.I. No. 370/2017 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Impairment Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th August*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 11(4) (amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec12) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Impairment Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 8 August 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic Act 2010 (Impairment Testing) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 534 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0534.html) ) are amended:

  > “In these Regulations, ‘subsections (1) and (2) of section 11’ means subsections (1) and (2) of section 11 (amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec12) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of2016)) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of2010).”;

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These regulations amend the form for recording results of non-technological cognitive impairment tests which may be carried out on drivers to test for impairment in accordance with [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec11) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010), as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec12) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No.21 of 2016). Changes to the form are technical and take account of amendments to [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec11) .
