# S.I. No. 460/2016 - Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd September*, 2016.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) and [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994), and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2014 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. Article 5 (amended by article 5 of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 274 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0274.html) )) of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) ) is amended in sub-article (3) by inserting after paragraph (*f*) the following paragraph:

> - “(fa) a vehicle being used in connection with the operation of electronic or other apparatus referred to in subsection (1) of [section 81](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec81) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) under an agreement referred to in subsection (7) of that section and which is at a location determined, for the purposes of that section, by a member of the Garda Síochána not below the rank of Superintendent;”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 August 2016.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) ) to provide an exemption to the prohibition on the entry of a vehicle to a road or an area or the prohibition on the stopping or parking of a vehicle imposed by the 1997 Regulations to speed camera vehicles.
