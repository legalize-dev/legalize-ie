> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 590 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0590.html)

ROAD TRAFFIC (NATIONAL CAR TEST) (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th January*, 2009.

I, NOEL AHERN, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, inexercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (Road Traffic) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 320 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0320.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 February 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 405 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0405.html) ) are amended by the substitution of Regulation 9 with the following:

> “9. (1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the fee to be paid for a test certificate shall be—
>
> - (*a*) €41.15, in the case of a test certificate issued following a test of a vehicle, and
>
> - (*b*) €23.045, in the case of a test certificate issued following a re-test of a vehicle.
>
> - (2) A test certificate issued following re-test of a vehicle that does not require the use of test equipment shall not be subject to a fee.
>
> - (3) Subject to paragraph (6), in any case where an appointment for a test or re-test has been confirmed to the issuing authority and the vehicle is not presented for the test or re-test at the appointed time and place, the issuing authority may require payment of a fee of—
>
>     - (*a*) €59.26, for a test certificate subsequently issued following a test of the vehicle, and
>
>     - (*b*) €32.51, for a test certificate subsequently issued following are-test of the vehicle.
>
> - (4) The fee for a certificate of suitability shall be €74.90.
>
> - (5) Subject to paragraph (6), in any case where an appointment for an inspection in connection with a certificate of suitability for a vehicle has been confirmed to the issuing authority and the vehicle is not presented for the inspection at the appointed time and place, the issuing authority may require payment of a fee of €106.585 in respect of a subsequently issued certificate of suitability for the vehicle.
>
> - (6) Paragraphs (3) and (5) do not apply in any case where the owner or the owner’s agent gives notice at least 5 working days before the day of the appointment in person, in writing, by telephone by facsimile or by e-mail to the issuing authority, of intention not to present the vehicle for a test, are-test, or an inspection in connection with a certificate of suitability, as the case may be, at the appointed time and place.
>
> - (7) The fees prescribed in this Regulation are exclusive of value added tax.”.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0062.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my hand,

23 December 2008

NOEL AHERN,

Minister of State at the Department of Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide for an increase in the scale of NCT fees.
