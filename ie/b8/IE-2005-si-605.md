# S.I. No. 605/2005 - Registration of Title Act 1964 (Compulsory Registration of Ownership) (Longford, Roscommon and Westmeath) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 605 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0605.html)**

**Registration of Title Act 1964 (Compulsory Registration of Ownership) (Longford, Roscommon and Westmeath) Order 2005**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Registration of Title Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1964), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Registration of Title Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Compulsory Registration of Ownership) (Longford, Roscommon and Westmeath) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. [Section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Registration of Title Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1964) shall apply to the counties of Longford, Roscommon and Westmeath on and after the 1^st day of April, 2006.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 23^rd September 2005. [image omitted] Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order extends compulsory registration of ownership of land to the counties of Longford, Roscommon and Westmeath. The Order will come into effect on 1 April 2006.
