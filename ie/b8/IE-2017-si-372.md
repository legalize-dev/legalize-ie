# S.I. No. 372/2017 - Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences) (British Columbia) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th August*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23A (inserted by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec8) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alternation of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences) (British Columbia) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “vehicle with automatic transmission” means a vehicle in which no clutch pedal (or lever operated manually for category A1) is present.

###### 3.

3. It is declared that a licence or permit permitting a person to drive a mechanically propelled vehicle, not being a licence or permit issued to a person to enable that person to learn or provisionally to drive such a vehicle, issued by the competent authority of the Canadian province of British Columbia in a category which corresponds to category AM, A1 or B in the Table to Regulation 6 of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) (amended by Regulation 2(*a*) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0242.html) )) is recognised for the purpose of exchange of that licence or permit for a driving licence.

###### 4.

4. (1) A driving licence being granted in exchange for a licence or permit referred to in Article 3 shall only permit the person concerned to drive a vehicle with automatic transmission in the relevant category.

- (2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), where the competent authority referred to in Article 3 confirms that the person exchanging a driving licence or permit completed a test in a vehicle with manual transmission which demonstrates the necessary ability to drive the vehicle to the satisfaction of the licensing authority (within the meaning of section 21 (amended by [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec50) (*a*) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961)) the licence granted on exchange shall also permit the person to drive a vehicle with manual transmission in the category concerned.

- (3) Paragraph (2) shall not apply where, subsequent to the test referred to in that paragraph, the competent authority issued the person with a licence which only permits that person to drive a vehicle with automatic transmission in the category concerned.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 August 2017.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order gives effect to an Agreement between Ireland and the Canadian Province of British Columbia on the mutual exchange of driving licences.
