1 Short title, collective citation and construction.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1983.

- (2) The collective citation, the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1983, shall include this Scheme.

- (3) The Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1983, shall be construed together as one Scheme.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall come into force on the day after the date on which it is made.

3 Application of [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .

###### 3.

**3.** The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

4 Definitions.

###### 4.

**4.** In this Scheme—

"the Principal Scheme" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) Scheme, 1937 (S. R. & O., No. 249 of 1937);

"the Amending Scheme of 1947" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1947 (S. R. & O. , No. 81 of 1947);

###### 5.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This statutory instrument provides for increases in the amount of retirement benefits payable to officers of the Permanent Defence Force retiring on or after 2nd October, 1982 and in the pensions and gratuities payable to soldiers discharged or transferred to the Reserve Defence Force on or after 1st October, 1982.

DEFENCE FORCES (PENSIONS) (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) SCHEME, 1983.

**EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM**

The Defence Forces (Pensions) Acts, 1932 to 1975 empower the Minister for Defence with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service, to make pension schemes in relation to former members of the Permanent Defence Force (including the Army Nursing Service and the Chaplaincy Service) [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0002/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) provides that such a scheme shall not require a resolution of either House of the Oireachtas in order to come into force but shall come into force as provided by that Scheme.

The present Scheme provides for increases in the retirement benefits of officers of the Permanent Defence Force who retired on or after 2nd October, 1982 and non-commissioned officers and privates who were discharged or transferred to the Reserve Defence Force on or after 1st October, 1982. The increases are related to the increases in Army pay operative from 1st January, 1983, under the third phase of the agreement on Pay in the Public Service postponed from 1 October, 1982 to 1 January, 1983. An explanation of the various Articles is set out briefly below:—

Articles 1 to 4 are merely definitory.

Article 5 provides for increases of retired pay for certain officers who retired on or after the 2nd October, 1982.

Article 6 provides for increases in the retirement gratuities to officers to whom the preceding Article applies.

Article 7 provides for increases in the pensions payable to long-service soldiers discharged on or after the 1st October, 1982.

Article 8 provides for increases in the gratuities payable to soldiers discharged or transferred to the Reserve Defence Force on or after the 1st October, 1982.
