# S.I. No. 2/1993 - Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Fees) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 2 of 1993.

SOLICITORS ACTS 1954 AND 1960 (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1993.

By virtue and in pursuance of Sections 4, 5, 79 and 82 of the Solicitor's Acts 1954 and 1960 the Incorporated Law Society of Ireland hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. From and after the date of these Regulations the fees specified in the Schedule hereto shall be paid by the applicant in respect of the matters therein mentioned.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 6th day of January 1993.

###### 3.

3. The Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Fees) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0002.html) ) are revoked as from the date of operation of these Regulations as regards applications for Practising Certificates for the practice year 1993/94 or any later practice year but shall continue to apply to applications for Practising Certificates for the practice year 1992/93 or any earlier practice year.

###### 4.

4. These Regulations may be cited as the Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Fees) Regulations, 1993.

SCHEDULE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| (i) On application for a Practising Certificate for the practice year 1993/94 by a solicitor who practises or carries on his business in the State | £450.00 |
| (ii) On application by a solicitor who has been admitted less than three years | £314.00 |
| (iii) On application under [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0037/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Solicitors (Amendment) Act 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) for a copy of an entry on File A or File B | £1.00 |

Dated this 4th day of January, 1993. Signed on behalf of The Incorporated

Law Society of Ireland.

RAYMOND T. MONAHAN,

President of The Incorporated

Law Society of Ireland.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

The fee payable by a solicitor on taking out a Practising Certificate is raised from £431 to £450 in the case of a solicitor admitted more than three years, and from £301 to £314 in the case of a solicitor admitted less than three years. The fee payable for a copy of an entry on File A or File B is unchanged.
