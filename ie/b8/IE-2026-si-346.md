# S.I. No. 346/2026 - European Union (Energy Efficiency Obligations Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st July,* 2026.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Climate, Energy and the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2023/1791 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 September 2023[^1] and for the purposes of giving further effect to Directive 2024/1275 of the European Parliament and of the Council of the 24 April 2024[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as European Union (Energy Efficiency Obligations Scheme) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the Energy Efficiency Obligations Scheme Regulations 2025 to 2026 and shall be construed together as one.

*Interpretation*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Energy Efficiency Obligations Scheme) Regulations 2025 ( [S.I. No. 683 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0683.html) ).

*Amendment of Regulation 12 of the Principal Regulations*

###### 3.

3. Regulation 12 of the Principal Regulations is amended-

- (a) by substituting the following for paragraph (2)(b):

  > - “(b) (i) result in the achievement of a post-works BER of B or better for the dwelling concerned, or
  >
  >     - (ii) meet the B pathway requirement,”

- (b) by substituting the following for paragraph (3)(a):

  > - “(a) (i) result in the achievement of a post-works BER of B or better for the dwelling concerned, or
  >
  >     - (ii) meet the B pathway requirement,”

- (c) by substituting the following for (4)(b)(i):

  > - “(b) (i) the qualifying action implemented by the obligated party results in the achievement of a post-works BER of B or better”

- (d) in paragraph (6) –

    - (i) by substituting the following definitions for the definitions of “BER” and “dwelling”:

      > “ ‘BER’ means a building energy rating within the meaning of the European Union (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 243 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0243.html) ) as amended by [S.I. No. 168 of 2026](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2026/en/si/0168.html) ;
      >
      > ‘dwelling’ has the meaning given to it in Regulation 3 of the European Union (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations 2012 ( [SI. No. 243 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0243.html) ) as amended by [S.I. No. 168 of 2026](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2026/en/si/0168.html) ;”, and,

    - (ii) by deleting the definitions of “B2 or better” and “B2 pathway requirement” and inserting the following definitions:

      > “ ‘B or better’ means the indicators A0, A, or B as set out in a BER certificate (or, in the case of a BER certificate issued before 24 May 2026, when S.I. 168 of 2026 came into effect, B3, B2, B1, A3, A2, or A1), which are equivalent to a primary energy performance of 150 kWh/m2/yr or less;
      >
      > ‘B pathway requirement’ means that following the implementation of an energy efficiency improvement measure in a dwelling-
      >
      > - (a) that dwelling has moved closer to achieving a post-works BER of B or better, and
      >
      > - (b) the advisory report relating to the relevant BER certificate is discussed with, and provided to, the owner of occupant of the dwelling concerned;”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 July, 2026.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for the Climate, Energy and the Environment.
