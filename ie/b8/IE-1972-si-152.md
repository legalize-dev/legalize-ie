# S.I. No. 152/1972 - Imposition of Duties (No. 198) (Customs Duties and Form of Customs Tariff) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 152 of 1972.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 198) (CUSTOMS DUTIES AND FORM OF CUSTOMS TARIFF) ORDER, 1972.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 198) (Customs Duties and Form of Customs Tariff) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1972.

###### 3.

3. (1) References in this Order to an Act or Order shall be construed as references to that Act or Order as amended by any subsequent Act or Order.

- (2) References in the Schedule to this Order to a tariff heading number shall be construed as references to that tariff heading number as amended by this Order.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effects of the Order are :—

- (i) to make certain reductions in customs duties on goods of United Kingdom origin in accordance with the provisions of the Free Trade Area Agreement ;

- (ii) to maintain in operation the special tariff concessions for certain goods of Northern Ireland origin ; and

- (iii) to make minor editorial changes in the form of Customs Tariff.

The Order comes into operation on the 1st July, 1972.
