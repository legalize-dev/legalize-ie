# S.I. No. 727/2021 - Registration of Deeds Rules 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st December,* 2021.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Registration of Deeds Rules 2021.

- (2) These Rules and the Registration of Deeds Rules 2008 to 2013 may be cited together as the Registration of Deeds Rules 2008 to 2021.

- (3) These Rules and the Registration of Deeds Rules 2008 to 2013 shall be construed together as one.

- (4) In these Rules, “the Rules of 2008” means Registration of Deeds Rules 2008.

- (5) In these Rules, “the Rules of 2009” means Registration of Deeds Rules 2009.

- (6) In these Rules, “the Rules of 2013” means Registration of Deeds Rules 2013.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on the 16^th day of December 2021.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Rules, which come into effect on the 16^th of December 2021, amend the Registration of Deeds Rules 2008 and the Schedule of Forms in those Rules to provide for the registration of notices and statements under the [Civil Partnership and Certain Rights and Obligations of Cohabitants Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) .

The Registration of Deeds Rules 2009 and the Schedule of Forms in those Rules are amended to include provision for the registration of the cancellation of property adjustment orders made under the [Civil Partnership and Certain Rights and Obligations of Cohabitants Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) .

The Registration of Deeds Rules 2013, relating to the registration of judgment mortgages pursuant to [Section 116](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec116) of the [Land and Conveyancing Law Reform Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) , are amended to include judgments of the Court of Appeal
