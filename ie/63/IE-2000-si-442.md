S.I. No. 442 of 2000.

IRISH HORSERACING INDUSTRY ACT, 1994.

RACECOURSES (COURSE-BETTING REPRESENTATIVE PERMITS) REGULATIONS, 2000.

The Irish Horseracing Authority in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by sub-section (21)(*b*) of [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Irish Horseracing Industry Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and Commencement.

###### 1.

1. (*a*) These Regulations may be cited as the Racecourses (Course-Betting Representative Permits) Regulations, 2000;

- (*b*) These Regulations shall take effect from the 1st day of December, 2000.

Definitions

###### 2.

2. (*a*) “the Act” means the [Irish Horseracing Industry Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) ;

- (*b*) Section 2 of the Act shall also apply to the interpretation of these Regulations;

- (*c*) Nothing in these Regulations shall prejudice the powers and entitlement of the Authority under the Act.

Amendment to definition of “employee or agent” in Section 48(21)(*a*) of the Act.

###### 3.

3. The definition of “employee or agent” in Section 48(21)(*a*)(ii) of the Act, as amended, is hereby amended by deleting the text contained in Section 48(21)(*a*)(ii) and inserting in its place the following:

> “a person who has been nominated as his employee or agent by a bookmaker.”
