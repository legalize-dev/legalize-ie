# S.I. No. 595/2001 - Bankruptcy Act, 1988 (Alteration of Monetary Limits) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 143](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0027/sec0143.html#sec143) of the [Bankruptcy Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1988) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Bankruptcy Act, 1988 (Alteration of Monetary Limits) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. With effect from 1 January, 2002, in the case of each provision of the [Bankruptcy Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1988) specified in *column (2)* of the Schedule to this Order, for the monetary amount specified in *column (3)* of that Schedule at that reference number there shall be substituted the monetary amount specified in *column (4)* of that Schedule at that reference number.

###### 3.

3. This Order shall not affect any case in which a person was adjudicated bankrupt or granted protection before it came into force.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this order is to make provision for the substitution, with effect from 1 January 2002, of convenient amounts expressed in Euro for amounts expressed in Irish Pounds in provisions of the [Bankruptcy Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) .
