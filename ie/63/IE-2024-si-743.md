# S.I. No. 743/2024 - Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd January,* 2025.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) of section 6A (inserted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec2) of the [Enforcement of Court Orders (Amendment) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2009)) of the [Enforcement of Court Orders Act 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1940) (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2009” means the Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0301.html) ).

###### 3.

3. (1) Subject to Regulation 4, the Regulations of 2009 are amended by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations for Schedule 2 (amended by Regulation 3 of the Enforcement of Court Orders (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 (No. 363 of 2011)).

- (2) This Regulation shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 January 2024.

###### 4.

4. (1) The Regulations of 2009 are amended by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations for Schedule 2 (amended by Regulation 4).

- (2) This Regulation shall come into operation on 1 January 2025.

## **SCHEDULE 1**

> ### “SCHEDULE 2

## *Part 1* SOLICITOR’S FEE

*Proceedings in District Court*

Fee in respect of proceedings:

€221.65 per case

## *Part 2* SOLICITOR’S FEE

*Appeal against order for imprisonment of debtor*

Fee in respect of an appeal against an order for imprisonment of a debtor made under paragraph (c) or (d) of subsection (7) of section 6 (inserted by section 2 of the Act of 2009) of the Act of 1940:

€221.65 per case

## *Part 3* SOLICITOR’S FEE

*Hearing by High Court of case stated*

Fee in respect of the hearing by the High Court of a case stated:

€1,466.19 per case

## *Part 4* COUNSEL’S FEE

*Hearing by High Court of case stated*

Fee in respect of the hearing by the High Court of a case stated:

€1,466.19 per case”.
