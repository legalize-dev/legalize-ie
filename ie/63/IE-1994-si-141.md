S.I. No. 141 of 1994.

ROAD TRANSPORT ACTS (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/sec0006.html#sec6) , [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/sec0007.html#sec7) and [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Road Transport Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1933), [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Road Transport Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1986), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Transport and Communications (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 17 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0017.html) )), with the sanction of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Transport Acts (Fees) Regulations, 1994.

- (2) The Road Transport Acts Regulations, 1933 to 1988, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Transport Acts Regulations, 1933 to 1994.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The fee payable to the Minister on application for the grant of a carrier's licence shall be that prescribed in the Schedule hereto.

###### 4.

4. The fee payable for inspection of the Register shall be a fee of £5 per hour or part thereof.

###### 5.

5. The fee payable for a copy of an entry in the Register shall be a fee of £5.

###### 6.

6. Every fee under these Regulations shall be payable either in the form of cash or in the form of a cheque, money order, bank draft or postal order payable to the Department of Transport, Energy and Communications and shall be lodged with the application.

###### 7.

7. Every fee collected under these Regulations shall be paid into the Exchequer.

###### 8.

8. The [Road Transport Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , Regulations, 1955 ( [S.I. No. 69 of 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/si/0069.html) ), and the Road Transport Acts (Fees) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0193.html) ), are hereby revoked.

SCHEDULE.

FEES.

National and International Road Freight Carrier's Licences.

1. ( *a* ) On an application for the grant of a national road freight carrier's licence under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/sec0003.html#sec3) (6) of the [Road Transport Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , a fee of £55 together with, in respect of each vehicle to be specified in the licence, a fee of £75.

( *b* ) On an application during the period of validity of a national road freight carrier's licence, in respect of an additional vehicle to be specified in the licence, a fee, in respect of each period of 6 months or less of the unexpired period of the licence, of £13.

2. ( *a* ) On an application for the grant of an international road freight carrier's licence under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/sec0003.html#sec3) (6) of the [Road Transport Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , a fee of £55 together with, in respect of each vehicle to be specified in the licence, a fee of £180.

( *b* ) On an application, during the period of validity of an international road freight carrier's licence, in respect of an additional vehicle to be specified in the licence, a fee, in respect of each period of 6 months or less of the unexpired period of the licence, of £18.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Instrument is to prescribe fees on application for the grant of national and international road freight carriers' licences under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/sec0003.html#sec3) (6) of the [Road Transport Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) . It also eliminates stamp duty fees for vehicle plates.
