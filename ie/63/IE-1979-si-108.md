# S.I. No. 108/1979 - Health Contributions (Rate of Contribution and Yearly Reckonable Income) (Confirmation and Variation) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 108 of 1979.

HEALTH CONTRIBUTIONS (RATE OF CONTRIBUTION AND YEARLY RECKONABLE INCOME) (CONFIRMATION AND VARIATION) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1979), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Contributions (Rate of Contribution and Yearly Reckonable Income) (Confirmation and Variation) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The rate specified in sections 5 (1) (*a*), 6 (1) and 7 of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , is hereby confirmed.

###### 3.

3. The sum of £5,000 specified in [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) (2), [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , is hereby varied by the substitution therefor of a sum of £5,500.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 4th day of April, 1979.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance hereby sanctions the above Regulations.

Dated this 4th day of April, 1979.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , provides that the contribution rate and the ceiling amount specified in sections 5 to 8 of the Act shall either be confirmed or varied by regulations made by the Minister for Health before the commencement of sections 5 to 8 of the Act. These regulations confirm the rate of contribution of one per cent which is specified in sections 5, 6 and 7 of the Act and substitute the sum of £5,500 for the sum of £5,000 which is specified in sections 5, 6 and 8 of the Act.
