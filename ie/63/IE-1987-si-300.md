# S.I. No. 300/1987 - Health (Charges For In-Patient Services) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 300 of 1987.

HEALTH (CHARGES FOR IN-PATIENT SERVICES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the first day of January, 1988.

###### 3.

3. Paragraph (*b*) of sub-article (1) of article 3 of the Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) Regulations, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 180 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0180.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of `thirty days' for 'ninety days' in both places where the latter occurs.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 4th day of December 1987.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

Dated this 4th day of December 1987.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) Regulations, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 180 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0180.html) ) to enable charges towards the cost of hospital in-patient services provided under [Section 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) to be made after thirty day in the case of patients without dependents who have not full eligibility for health services.
