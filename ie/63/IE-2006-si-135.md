# S.I. No. 135/2006 - Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 135 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0135.html)**

**Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5 and section 103 (as amended by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0044/print.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0044/print.html) (No. 44 of 2004)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform in relation to declaring offences to be fixed charge offences, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 3 April 2006.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations -

“Act of 1968” means Road Traffic Act 1968 (No. 25 of 1968);

“Act of 1994” means Road Traffic Act 1994 (No. 7 of 1994);

“Act of 2004” means Road Traffic Act 2004 (No. 44 of 2004)

“light rail vehicle” has the meaning assigned to it by section 2(1) of the [Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (No. 55 of 2001);

“Principal Act” means Road Traffic Act 1961 (No. 24 of 1961);

“vehicle” means mechanically propelled vehicle.

###### 4.

4. The offences under the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 mentioned in Regulation 5 and Schedules 1 to 4 are declared to be fixed charge offences in so far as they relate to mechanically propelled vehicles.

###### 5.

5. The amount in relation to a fixed charge is -

- (a) €80, in respect of an offence under -

    - (i) a provision of the Principal Act mentioned in Part 1, and

    - (ii) section 35(5) of the Act of 1994, for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Part 2,

of Schedule 1,

- (b) €60, in respect of an offence under -

    - (i) section 11 of the Principal Act, for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Part 1 of Schedule 2,

    - (ii) section 35(5) of the Act of 1994, for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Part 2 of Schedule 2, and

    - (iii) section 73 (requiring a licence to be fixed to and exhibited on a vehicle) of the [Finance Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) ,

      and

- (c) €40, in respect of an offence under -

    - (i) a provision of the Principal Act mentioned in Part 1, and

    - (ii) section 35(5) of the Act of 1994, for a contravention of a provision of the Regulations mentioned in Part 2,

of Schedule 3.

###### 6.

6. The form of notice set out in Schedule 4 at A is prescribed as the form of notice for the purposes of section 103 of the Principal Act.

###### 7.

7. The form of document set out in Schedule 4 at B is prescribed as the form of document for the purposes of section 103(4)(i) of the Principal Act.

###### 8.

8. The following are revoked:

- (a) the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations 1999 ( [S.I. No. 12 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0012.html) ),

- (b) the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 492 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0492.html) ), and

- (c) the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 103) (Offences) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 322 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0322.html) ).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Regulations (operative from 3 April 2006) declare which offences involving the driving or use of mechanically propelled vehicles are fixed charge offences for the purposes of [section 103](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0103.html#sec103) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , determine the amount of fixed charge for each offence and prescribe the form of notice and document to be used in the enforcement of these fixed charge offences and, where applicable, the penalty point system, in relation to those offences.
