**District Court (Registration of Clubs) Rules, 2005**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and by section 21(1) of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following Rules of Court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Registration of Clubs) Rules 2005.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 6th day of April 2005 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. Order 83 of the District Court Rules, 1997 (S. I. No. 93 of 1997) is hereby amended by the deletion of rule 9 thereof from the said Order.
