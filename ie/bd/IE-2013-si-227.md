# S.I. No. 227/2013 - Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 (Cessation of section 472A in respect of certain claims) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd July*, 2013.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (7) (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec7) (2)(*b*) of the [Finance Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2013)) of section 472A of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 (Cessation of section 472A in respect of certain claims) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of July 2013 is appointed as the day on which section 472A of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997) shall cease to have effect for the purposes of subsection (7) (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec7) (2)(*b*) of the [Finance Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2013)) of that section.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 June 2013.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Statutory Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order closes the Revenue Job Assist Scheme, as provided for under section 472A of the TCA 1997, in respect of employments commencing on or after 1 July 2013.
