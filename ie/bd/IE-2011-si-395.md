# S.I. No. 395/2011 - National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Revocation of Delegation of Banking System Functions) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th August*, 2011.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Revocation of Delegation of Banking System Functions) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 5 August 2011.

###### 3.

3. The [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of Banking System Functions) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0115.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

26 July 2011.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.
