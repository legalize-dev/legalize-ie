# S.I. No. 394/2000 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers vested in him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) (sub-section 5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations—

Citation.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2000.

Rate of Payment.

###### 2.

2. Where a person is or becomes entitled to payment under sub-section (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0114.html) ), any amount so payable in respect of the week ending 8 December, 2000 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to 100 per cent of that amount of £20 whichever is the greater.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week ending 8th December, 2000 the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) by a sum equal to one hundred per cent of that amount or £20, whichever is the greater.
