# S.I. No. 227/2014 - Prison (Amendment) Rules 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd June*, 2014.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec35) of the [Prisons Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Prison (Amendment) Rules 2014.

- (2) The Principal Rules and these Rules shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Prison Rules 2007 to 2014.

- (3) These Rules shall come into operation on 29 May 2014.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules “Principal Rules” means the Prison Rules 2007 ( [S.I. No. 252 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0252.html) ) (as amended by the Prison Rules (Amendment) 2013 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0011.html) )).

###### 3.

3. Rule 59 of the Principal Rules is amended by substituting the following for paragraph (1):

> - “(1) A prisoner who has been sentenced to—
>
>     - (*a*) a term of imprisonment, or
>
>     - (*b*) terms of imprisonment to be served consecutively,
>
>       shall be eligible, by good conduct, to earn a remission of sentence not exceeding one quarter of such term or terms, as the case may be.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 May 2014.

**FRANCES FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
