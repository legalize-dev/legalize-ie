# S.I. No. 81/1995 - Savings Certificates (Seventh Issue Extension) Rules, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 81 of 1995.

SAVINGS CERTIFICATES (SEVENTH ISSUE EXTENSION) RULES, 1995.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/sec0030.html#sec30) (2) of the [Finance Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1940), and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following Rules:

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Savings Certificates (Seventh Issue Extension) Rules, 1995.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules—

"the seventh issue" means the issue of Savings Certificates introduced on the 14th day of March, 1966;

"the relevant date" means, in relation to a Savings Certificate, the twenty-eighth anniversary of the date marked thereon as the date of issue thereof; and

"unit Certificate" means a Savings Certificate of the seventh issue of purchase price of which was one pound.

###### 3.

3. A Savings Certificate of the seventh issue may be held for a period not exceeding five years and nine months from the relevant date.

###### 4.

4. The amount repayable at any time in respect of a Savings Certificate of the seventh issue which is held under these Rules shall be—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| ( a ) in the case of a unit Certificate | £ |
| (i) if it is held for less than six months from the relevant date | 7.26 |
| (ii) if it is held for six months or more and less than one year from such date | 7.42 |
| (iii) if it is held for one year or more and less than one and one half years from such date | 7.59 |
| (iv) if it is held for one and one half years or more and less than two years from such date | 7.76 |
| (v) if it is held for two years or more and less than two and one half years from such date | 7.95 |
| (vi) if it is held for two and one half years or more and less than three years from such date | 8.15 |
| (vii) if it is held for three years or more and less than three and one half years from such date | 8.37 |
| (viii) if it is held for three and one half years or more and less than four years from such date | 8.61 |
| (ix) if it is held for four years or more and less than four and one half years from such date | 8.88 |
| (x) if it is held for four and one half years or more and less than five years from such date | 9.18 |
| (xi) if it is held for five years or more and less than five and one half years from such date | 9.51 |
| (xii) if it is held for five and one half years or more and less than five years and nine months from such date | 9.87 |
| (xiii) if it is held for five years and nine months from such date | 10.16 |

( *b* ) in any other case, the appropriate corresponding amount.

###### 5.

5. Subject to the provisions of these Rules, the Savings Certificates Rules, 1926, shall apply, with the necessary modifications, in relation to a Savings Certificate which is held under these Rules.

GIVEN under the Seal of the NATIONAL TREASURY MANAGEMENT AGENCY, this 4th day of April, 1995.

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**
