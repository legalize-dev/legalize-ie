# S.I. No. 54/2025 - Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th March,* 2025.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0041.html#sec41) (1) (as amended by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec11) (c) of the [Road Traffic and Roads Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2023)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Justice, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the 27th day of March 2025.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 6 of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Detention of Vehicles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 460 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0460.html) ) is amended –

- (a) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (aa) (inserted by regulation 3 of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0537.html) ) –

  > - “(aa) in the case of a person to whom subparagraph (i), (ii), (iii) or (v) (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec11) (c)(i)(III) of the [Road Traffic and Roads Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2023)) of section 41(1)(a) of the Act of 1994 applies, he or she was, at the time when the vehicle was detained, the holder of a driving licence or learner permit licensing him or her to drive the vehicle,”,

  and

- (b) by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (e):

  > - “(f) in the case of a vehicle referred to in section 41(1)(f) (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec11) (c)(ii) of the [Road Traffic and Roads Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) ) of the Act of 1994, that at the time it was detained, a test certificate in accordance with Directive 2014/45/EU of the European Parliament and of the Counsel of 3 April 2014[^1] on periodic roadworthiness tests for motor vehicles and their trailers and repealing Directive 2009/40/EC was in force in respect of the vehicle.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 February, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Transport.
