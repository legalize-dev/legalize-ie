EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be legal interpretation).*

These Regulations were made to give effect to Regulation (EU) No 2006/2004 of the European Parliament and the Council of 27 October 2004 as amended by Directive 2009/136/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 and Regulation (EU) No. 954/2011 of the European Parliament and the Council of 14 September 2011 on cooperation between national authorities responsible for the enforcement of consumer protections laws.

Several of the legislative acts listed in the Annex to Regulation (EC) No. 2006/2004 have been repealed and new legislation has been adopted in regard to the Regulation.

In addition, these Regulations further provide that specified agencies of the State are designated as competent authorities for the purpose of implementing the new legislative acts listed in the Annex to Regulation (EU) No. 954/2011.
