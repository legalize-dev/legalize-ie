# S.I. No. 55/2005 - Immigration Act 1999 (Deportation) Regulations 2005

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 55 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0055.html) .**

IMMIGRATION ACT 1999 (DEPORTATION) REGULATIONS 2005.

[S.I. No. 55 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0055.html) .

IMMIGRATION ACT 1999 (DEPORTATION) REGULATIONS 2005.

I, MICHAEL McDOWELL, Minister for Justice Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec7) of the [Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ( [No. 22 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1.—These Regulations may be cited as the [Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (Deportation) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2.—In these Regulations “the Act” means the [Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) 22 of 1999) as amended by the [Illegal Immigrants (Trafficking) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) 29 of 2000) and the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) 1 of 2004).

###### 3.

3.— [The Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (Deportation) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 103 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0103.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4.—The form set out in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 3(7 of the Act.

###### 5.

5.—Each place listed in the Second Schedule to these Regulations and every Garda Síochána station is a place prescribed for the purposes of section 5(1) of the Act.

###### 6.

6.—Immigration officers and members of the Garda Síochána are hereby authorised to deport a person from the State under the Act.

###### 7.

7.—Where an immigration officer or member of the Garda Síochána arrests a person pursuant to section 5(1) of the Act and where he or she proposes to detain the person in a prescribed place, he or she shall, in writing, inform the Member in Charge, in the case of a Garda Síochána station or the Governor, in any other case, of the arrest and direct that the person be detained until further notice.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order was made for the purpose of updating the list of prescribed places of detention for the purpose of section 5(1 of the [Immigration Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (as amended) by the removal of the Central Mental Hospital, Dundrum, from that list.
