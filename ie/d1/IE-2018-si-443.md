# S.I. No. 443/2018 - Appointment of Special Advisers (Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th October*, 2018.

The Government, on the request of the Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0027/sec0011.html#sec11) (1) of the [Public Service Management Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1997), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Appointment of Special Advisers (Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. Mr Patrick Cluskey, particulars of whose address have been deposited at the offices of the Department of Communications, Climate Action and Environment, is appointed to be a Special Adviser to the Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment with effect from 16th October 2018.

###### 3.

3. Ms Sarah O’Neill, particulars of whose address have been deposited at the offices of the Department of Communications, Climate Action and Environment, is appointed to be a Special Adviser to the Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment with effect from 16th October 2018.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

23 October 2018.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.
