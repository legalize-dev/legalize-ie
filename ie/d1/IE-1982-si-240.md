# S.I. No. 240/1982 - Whale Fisheries Act, 1937 (Extension To Mammals of the Order Cetacea) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 240 of 1982.

WHALE FISHERIES ACT, 1937 (EXTENSION TO MAMMALS OF THE ORDER CETACEA) ORDER, 1982.

I, BRENDAN DALY, Minister for Fisheries and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 (2) of the [Whale Fisheries Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1937), and the Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0195.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Whale Fisheries Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (Extension to Mammals of the order Cetacea) Order, 1982.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The provisions of the [Whale Fisheries Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1937), shall apply to all mammals of the order Cetacea.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of July, 1982.

**BRENDAN DALY,**

Minister for Fisheries and Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to extend the provisions of the [Whale Fisheries Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) to cover all whale species including dolphins and porpoises.
