# S.I. No. 642/2010 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st December*, 2010.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2010.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2009 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2010.

*Prescription of Urban Areas.*

###### 2.

2. The areas in the administrative counties of Cavan, Carlow, Kilkenny, Laois, Louth, Mayo, Meath, and Roscommon indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
