S.I. No. 106 of 1981.

TELEPHONE (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1981.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telephone (Amendment) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) The Telephone Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0195.html) ), and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telephone Regulations, 1980 and 1981.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1981.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Telephone Regulations, 1980, are hereby amended by:

( *a* ) the substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 45:

> "45. The Minister may from time to time increase or reduce the subscription or charges. In the event of an increase he shall give to the subscriber 28 days notice before the increase takes effect. When any increase or reduction takes effect, the agreeemnt shall be construed as if the increased or reduced subscription or charges were provided for in the agreement.";

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is:

( *a* ) to reduce the period of notice of increased charges to be given to subscribers from 1 calendar month to 28 days.

( *b* ) to provide for the following increases in charges:

Local Calls

charge to non-coinbox subscriber from 5.85p to 6.75p.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Trunk Calls calls from non-coinbox line | (i) from 5.85p to 6.75p for each specified period of time on subscriber dialled trunk calls; |
|  | (ii) 15% on internal and Cross-Channel calls obtained through operator. |
| Personal Call Fee ... ... ... | from 30p to 35p |
| Advice of Duration and Charge ... | from 17p to 20p |
| Alarm Clock Calls ... ... ... | from 30p to 35p |
| Reverse Charge Calls ... ... | from 30p to 35p |
| Re-Connection Fee ... ... | from £5.00 to £10.00 |
| Connection Charges: |  |
| Exchange Line ... ... | from £100 to £140 |
| Other Connection Charges ... | pro-rata with introduction of a minimum charge of £10. |
