S.I. No. 286 of 2012

DISTRICT COURT (DOMESTIC VIOLENCE) RULES 2012

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the District Court (Domestic Violence) Rules 2012, shall come into operation on the 15th day of August 2012.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2012.

###### 2.

2. The District Court Rules 1997 are amended by the substitutions for the Forms 59.1 and 59.3 in Schedule C of the forms bearing the like numbers respectively in the Schedule hereto.

***WARNING**

IT IS AN OFFENCE under [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0027/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Family Home Protection Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , as applied by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0001/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Domestic Violence Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , for a spouse to DISPOSE OF OR REMOVE ANY OF THE HOUSEHOLD CHATTELS pending the determination of the application herein, and if a safety order is made, while that order is in force (unless the other spouse consents or the Court permits it).

A spouse who contravenes that provision shall be liable on conviction to a Class D fine, being a fine not exceeding €1,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This does not form part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules amend the District Court Rules by the substitution of forms 59.1 and 59.3 to expand the categories of person entitled to seek a safety or barring order, as prescribed in the [Domestic Violence Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , as amended.
