# S.I. No. 216/1973 - Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930 (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 216 of 1973.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE (FRESH MEAT) ACT, 1930 (EXPORTER'S LICENCES) (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 51 and Rule 2 (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1954)), of the Schedule to the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1930), and the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby make the following regulations, with respect to which pursuant to section 4 (2) of the said [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1969.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of the Schedule to the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1930) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1954)) the appropriate sum for—

( *a* ) a beef exporter's licence shall be 5 new pence;

( *b* ) a pork exporter's licence shall be 1.25 new pence; and

( *c* ) a mutton exporter's licence shall be 0.625 new pence.

###### 5.

5. The [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) Fees Regulations, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 36 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0036.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of July, 1973.

**MARK CLINTON**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations adjust as from 1st January 1969 the fees payable by fresh-meat exporters for veterinary examination of animals in accordance with the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .
