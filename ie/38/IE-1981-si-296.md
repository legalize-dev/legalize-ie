# S.I. No. 296/1981 - Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) The collective citation “Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 and 1981,” shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October 1981.

###### 3.

3. [The Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 196 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0196.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule thereto (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I.
