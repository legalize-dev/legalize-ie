# S.I. No. 8/1974 - Garda Síochána (Retirement (No. 2) Regulations, 1958 (Revocation) Regulations, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), hereby, with the approval of the Government, make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations, 1958 (Revocation) Regulations, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The Garda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations, 1958 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/si/0177.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of January, 1974.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

Minister for Justice.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 15th day of

January, 1974.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations revoke the Garda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations, 1958 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/si/0177.html) ) which prescribed that a woman member of the Garda Síochána shall retire on marriage.
