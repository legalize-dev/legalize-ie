# S.I. No. 315/1976 - Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927 (Section 152) (Declaration) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 315 of 1976.

INDUSTRIAL AND COMMERCIAL PROPERTY (PROTECTION) ACT, 1927 (SECTION 152) (DECLARATION) ORDER, 1976.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) (5) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927), as amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1963), and [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1964), and adapted by section 22 of the [Executive Powers (Consequential Provisions) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1937), and to fulfil an arrangement with the Government of India concerning the protection of designs, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Section 152) (Declaration) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. It is hereby declared that the provisions of [section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927), are applicable to India.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 20th day of

December, 1976.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Order declares that the provisions of [Section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , apply to India.
