# S.I. No. 261/2020 - Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) (Revocation) Rules 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th July,* 2020.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec35) of the [Prisons Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2007) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) ), and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) (Revocation) Rules 2020.

- (2) These Rules come into operation on 1 October 2020.

###### 2.

2. Subject to Rule 3, the following are revoked:

- (*a*) the Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) Rules 1996 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0289.html) );

- (*b*) the Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) (Amendment) Rules 2009 ( [S.I. No. 438 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0438.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Where, before the date on which these Rules come into operation, an allegation against an officer of a breach of discipline has been made under Rule 7 of the Prison (Disciplinary Code for Officers) Rules 1996 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0289.html) ) (“revoked Rules”) and, by that date, the allegation has not been dealt with, the allegation shall continue to be dealt with under the revoked Rules.

The Government approve of the making of the foregoing Rules

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

21 July, 2020.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 July, 2020.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
