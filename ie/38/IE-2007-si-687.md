# S.I. No. 687/2007 - Data Protection (Processing of Genetic Data) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 687 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0687.html)

DATA PROTECTION (PROCESSING OF GENETIC DATA) REGULATIONS 2007.

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th October*, 2007.

I, BILLY HAWKES, Data Protection Commissioner, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 12A (inserted by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec13) of the [Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2003)) of the [Data Protection Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1988), and with the consent of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Data Protection (Processing of Genetic Data) Regulations 2007.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 8 October 2007.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “genetic data” has the meaning assigned to it by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec41) of the [Disability Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2005).

###### 3.

3. The processing of genetic data in relation to the employment of a person is prescribed for the purposes of section 12A (inserted by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec13) of the [Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2003)) of the [Data Protection Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1988).

GIVEN under my hand,

3 October 2007

BILLY HAWKES.

Data Protection Commissioner.

The Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 October 2007

**BRIAN LENIHAN.**

Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the designation, under section 12A of the Data Protection Acts 1988 and 2003 for the purposes of section 42(2)(*a*) of the [Disability Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) , of the processing of genetic data in relation to the employment of a person as processing that can only take place with the prior approval of the Data Protection Commissioner.
