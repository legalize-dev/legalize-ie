# S.I. No. 4/2023 - Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th January,* 2023.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) ) for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2021/1717 of 9 July 2021[^1] amending Directive 2014/45/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 April 2014[^2] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 415 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0415.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for Schedule 3:
