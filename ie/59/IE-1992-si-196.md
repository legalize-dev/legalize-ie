# S.I. No. 196/1992 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) (Amendment) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 196 of 1992.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF KILDARE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1992.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act (as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (i) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) (Amendment) Regulations, 1992.

- (ii) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0049.html) ) and these Regulations shall be construed together and may be cited collectively as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) Regulations 1992.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 30th day of July, 1992.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0049.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by

( *a* ) the deletion of article 5 and the substitution therefor of the following:

"5. (1) 40 miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) 70 miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Fourth Schedule to these Regulations."

( *b* ) the addition after the Third Schedule of the following:

"FOURTH SCHEDULE.

Roads in respect of which a special speed limit of 70 miles per hour is prescribed.

1. The following road in and adjacent to the Urban District of Naas:

The Naas By-Pass."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 16th day of July, 1992.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kildare) Regulations, 1992 by prescribing a 70 miles per hour speed limit as being the maximum permissible speed on the Naas By-Pass.

Ambulances, Fire Brigades and Garda Síochána vehicles, while being used in the course of duty, are exempt from the speed limits.
