# S.I. No. 110/1998 - Capital Gains Tax (Multipliers) (1998-99) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 110 of 1998.

CAPITAL GAINS TAX (MULTIPLIERS) (1998-99) REGULATIONS, 1998.

The Revenue Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 556](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0556.html#sec556) (6) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Capital Gains Tax (Multipliers) (1998-99) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. In relation to the disposal of an asset made in the year 1998-99, the multiplier for the purposes of [section 556](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0556.html#sec556) (2) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), shall be the figure mentioned in column (2) of the Table to this Regulation opposite the mention in column (1) of the said Table of the year of assessment in which the deductable expenditure was incurred.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

These Regulations specify the multipliers by reference to which sums (such as the base cost of an asset and enhancement expenditure incurred on it) which are allowable as a deduction from the consideration for the disposal of an asset in the year of assessment 1998-99 are to be increased under [section 556](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0556.html#sec556) (2) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , for the purpose of computing the chargeable gain accruing to a person on such a disposal. The multipliers are derived from the consumer price index numbers compiled by the Central Statistics Office expressed on the basis that the consumer price index at mid-November, 1968, is 100.
