# S.I. No. 303/2023 - European Union (Open Internet Access) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th June,* 2023.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Articles 1 to 6 of Regulation (EU) 2015/2120 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2015[^1] as amended by Regulation (EU) 2018/1971 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018[^2] and Directive (EU) 2018/1972 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018[^3] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Open Internet Access) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 9 June 2023.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Open Internet Access) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 343 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0343.html) ) are amended by -

- (a) the substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 8:

  > “8. An undertaking shall comply with -
  >
  > - (a) its obligations under Articles 3, 4 and 5 of the EU Regulation, and
  >
  > - (b) a direction under Regulation 4(1) within the period specified in the direction.”,

- (b) the substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 11:

  > “11. (1) Where the Regulator issues a direction or notification under these Regulations, such direction or notification shall -
  >
  > - (a) be in writing,
  >
  > - (b) state the reasons on which it is based,
  >
  > - (c) be addressed to the undertaking concerned, and
  >
  > - (d) be sent or given as soon as practicable.
  >
  > - (2) Section 60(1) of the Act of 2002 shall apply to a direction issued by the Regulator under these Regulations as if it were a notice referred to in that section.”,

  and

- (c) the deletion of Regulation 9(4).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 June, 2023.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications.
