# S.I. No. 12/1988 - Therapeutic Substances (Fees) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 12 of 1988.

THERAPEUTIC SUBSTANCES (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0025/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Therapeutic Substances Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1932), hereby makes the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Therapeutic Substances (Fees) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The Therapeutic Substances (Fees) Regulations, 1954 are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. The fees set out in the attached Schedule to these regulations shall be the fees chargeable for licences under the [Therapeutic Substances Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. These regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1988.

SCHEDULE

- (1) For a licence to manufacture

    - (*a*) in respect of licence £300

    - (*b*) in respect of each therapeutic substance specified £60

- (2) For a licence to import

    - (*a*) in respect of licence £100

    - (*b*) in respect of each therapeutic substance specified £20

- (3) For a research licence

    - (*a*) in respect of licence £20

    - (*b*) in respect of each therapeutic substance specified £10

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

Health this 27th day of January, 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

Finance this 27th day of January, 1988.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for increases in the fees charged for licences granted under the [Therapeutic Substances Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1932).
