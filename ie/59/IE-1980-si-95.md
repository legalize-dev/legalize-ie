# S.I. No. 95/1980 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 95 of 1980.

DIPLOMATIC AND CONSULAR FEES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1980.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931), and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939), hereby, with consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1980.

###### 2.

2. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 and 1976, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 to 1980.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the second day of June, 1980.

###### 4.

4. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 are hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the Substitution for Regulation 7 of the following Regulation:

"7. The Minister may waive any fee chargeable under Article 5 in any of the following circumstances:

( *a* ) where the service for which the fee is chargeable is rendered for, and at the request of, any official or public authority,

( *b* ) where the service for which the fee is chargeable is rendered for a citizen, subject or national of any other country the Government of which waives the fee or does not charge a fee for the same or a similar service when rendered for an Irish citizen,

( *c* ) where the service for which the fee is chargeable is rendered for official purposes or for reasons of public policy.",

and

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to increase as indicated in the Regulations fees chargeable for services rendered under the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931) and the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939).
