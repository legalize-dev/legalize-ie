IRISH HORSERACING INDUSTRY ACT, 1994.

AUTHORISED BOOKMAKERS (COURSE BETS TURNOVER CHARGE) REGULATIONS, 2004.

Horse Racing Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by Section 54A of the [Irish Horseracing Industry Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (as inserted by the [Horse And Greyhound Racing (Betting Charges And Levies) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. *Citation And Commencement.*

- (*a*) These Regulations may be cited as the Authorised Bookmakers (Course Bets Turnover Charge) Regulations, 2004;

- (*b*) These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of March 2004.

###### 2.

2. *Definitions.*

- (*a*) In the Regulations, “the Act” means the [Irish Horseracing Industry Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) ;

- (*b*) Section 2 of the Act shall also apply to the interpretation of these Regulations;

- (*c*) Nothing in these Regulations shall prejudice the powers and rights of Horse Racing Ireland under the Act.

###### 3.

3. *Payment Of Turnover Charge By Bookmakers On Authorised Course Bets.*

The existing rate of turnover charge of 0.3 per cent standing specified in section 54A(2)(*a*) of the Act (as inserted by the [Horse And Greyhound Racing (Betting Charges And Levies) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) ) shall be amended to 0.5 per cent.

###### 4.

4. *Amendments to Regulations.*

These Regulations may be amended from time to time by Horse Racing Ireland.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

It is proposed, following consultations between the Irish National Bookmakers Association and Horse Racing Ireland, that the existing charge of 0.3% payable by oncourse bookmakers in respect of all bets placed with an authorised bookmaker, other than in a betting shop and placed solely on a horse race taking place at the authorised racecourse at which the bet is placed, as provided for by section 54A(2)(*a*) of the Act (as inserted by the [Horse And Greyhound Racing (Betting Charges And Levies) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) ) be increased to 0.5%
