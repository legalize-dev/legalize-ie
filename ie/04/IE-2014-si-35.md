# S.I. No. 35/2014 - Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th February*, 2014.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0003.html#sec3) , [46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0046.html#sec46) and [111](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0111.html#sec111) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1992), hereby make the following Regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Electoral Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 156 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0156.html) ) are hereby amended by:—

- (*a*) the substitution of the following regulation for regulation 4:

  > “4.The prescribed photographic identification for the purposes of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0046.html#sec46) (6)(*b*) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) shall be:
  >
  > - (i) a passport;
  >
  > - (ii) a driving licence;
  >
  > - (iii) an employee identity card containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (iv) a student identity card issued by an educational institution and containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (v) a travel document containing name and photograph;
  >
  > - (vi) a Public Services Card;
  >
  > - (vii) a Temporary Residence Certificate;
  >
  > - (viii) a Garda National Immigration Bureau Card.”, and

- (*b*) the substitution of the following regulation for regulation 8:

  > “8. A specified document for the purposes of [section 111](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0111.html#sec111) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) shall be:
  >
  > - (i) a passport;
  >
  > - (ii) a driving licence;
  >
  > - (iii) an employee identity card containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (iv) a student identity card issued by an educational institution and containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (v) a travel document containing name and photograph;
  >
  > - (vi) a Bank or Savings or Credit Union book containing address in constituency or local electoral area;
  >
  > - (vii) a Public Services Card;
  >
  > - (viii) a Temporary Residence Certificate;
  >
  > - (ix) a Garda National Immigration Bureau Card;
  >
  > - (x) a cheque book;
  >
  > - (xi) a cheque card;
  >
  > - (xii) a credit card;
  >
  > - (xiii) a birth certificate;
  >
  > - (xiv) a marriage certificate.
  >
  >   Items (x) to (xiv) shall be accompanied by a further document which establishes the address of the holder in the constituency or local electoral area.”.

###### 3.

3. The Electoral (Amendment) (No.2) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 260 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0260.html) ) and the Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 538 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0538.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal

29 January 2014.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations amend the list of photographic identifications that may be produced when a statutory declaration is being made by a person, who lives in the relevant constituency or local electoral area, assenting to the nomination of a candidate at an election to include a Public Services Card, a Garda National Immigration Bureau Card and a Temporary Residence Certificate and to remove a bank card with a photograph. The Regulations amend the list of specified documents which a voter may be required to produce at a polling station, to include a Public Services Card, a Garda National Immigration Bureau Card and a Temporary Residence Certificate.
