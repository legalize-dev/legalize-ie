# S.I. No. 362/2012 - European Communities (Food and Feed Hygiene) (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th September*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Regulation (EU) No 225/2012 of 15 March 2012[^1] and Commission Regulation (EC) No 669/2009 of 24 July 2009[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Food and Feed Hygiene) (Amendment) (No 2) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Food and Feed Hygiene) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 432 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0432.html) ) are amended by—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1), substituting for the definition of “Regulation 183/2005” (as amended by the European Communities (Food and Feed Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0164.html) )) for the following—

  > “‘Regulation 183/2005’ means Regulation (EC) No 183/2005 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 January 2005 as amended by Regulation (EC) No 219/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2009 and Commission Regulation (EU) No 225/2012 of 15 March 2012;”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 27A(1) (inserted by the European Communities (Food and Feed Hygiene) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 312 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0312.html) )) for the following—

  > “27A.(1) The designated points of entry for the purposes of Article 5 of Commission Regulation (EC) No 669/2009 of 24 July 2009 are—
  >
  > - (*a*) Dublin Port in respect of animal feed, or
  >
  > - (*b*) Dublin Airport or Dublin Port in respect of fruit and vegetable products.”.

###### 3.

3. The following are revoked—

- (*a*) European Communities (Feedingstuffs) (Methods of Sampling and Analysis) Regulations 1999 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0289.html) ),

- (*b*) European Communities (Feedingstuffs) (Methods of Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 288 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0288.html) ),

- (*c*) European Communities (Feedingstuffs) (Methods of Sampling and Analysis) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0185.html) ).

Given under my Official Seal,

19 September 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations update the principal Food and Feed Hygiene Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No 432 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0432.html) ) in relation to:

- (i) the approval and requirements for establishments placing on the market, for feed use, products derived from vegetable oils and blended fats;

  and

- (ii) officially nominating import Designated Points of Entry.
