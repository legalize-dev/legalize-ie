# S.I. No. 535/2006 - Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 535 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0535.html)**

**Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2006**

The Houses of the Oireachtas Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(1)(c) (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1996 and transferred by section 4(3)(b) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2003)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1962), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2005 shall be amended by substituting “31 October 2005” for “1 July 2005” and by deleting the words “or submit”.

GIVEN under my hand,

19 October, 2006

Dr. Rory O'Hanlon,

Chairperson of the Houses of the Oireachtas Commission.
