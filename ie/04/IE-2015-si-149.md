# S.I. No. 149/2015 - Student Support Act 2011 (Prescribed Persons) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th April*, 2015.

I, JAN O’SULLIVAN, Minister for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) (2) of the [Student Support Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2011) and following consultation with the Data Protection Commissioner, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Student Support Act 2011 (Prescribed Persons) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The following persons stand prescribed for the purposes of [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) (1) of the [Student Support Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2011):

- (*a*) the Central Applications Office (Universities and Other Education Institutions);

- (*b*) the Child and Family Agency;

- (*c*) Oifig an Ard-Chláraitheora.

###### 3.

3. The Student Support (Amendment of Second Schedule) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0157.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 April 2015.

**JAN O’SULLIVAN,**

Minister for Education and Skills.
