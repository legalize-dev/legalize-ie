# S.I. No. 467/2012 - Child Care Act 1991 (Section 29(7)) Regulations

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November*, 2012.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Children and Youth Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/sec0029.html#sec29) (7) of the [Child Care Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1991), being satisfied as to the matters referred to in subsection (7) and having consulted with the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Child Care Act 1991 (Section 29(7)) Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The following classes of persons are specified in accordance with the provisions of [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/sec0029.html#sec29) (7) of the [Child Care Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1991), for the purposes of subsection (5):

- (*a*) persons engaged in child care law research, and

- (*b*) nominated by a body specified in the Schedule to these Regulations, and

- (*c*) approved by the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 November 2012.

**FRANCES FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations specify the class of persons entitled to apply to attend hearings of proceedings under Part III, IV or VI of the [Child Care Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) , in respect of child care and protection cases conducted in the District Court, to prepare a report on cases for the purpose of assisting in the better operation of the [Child Care Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) . The person who will prepare the report is required to be engaged in child care law research, be nominated by a specified body and be approved by the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.
