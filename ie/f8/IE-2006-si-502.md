# S.I. No. 502/2006 - Companies (Fees) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 502 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0502.html)**

**Companies (Fees) Order 2006**

I, Michael Ahern, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 395 (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) and 396 of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 808 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0808.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Eighth Schedule” means the Eighth Schedule (substituted by the Companies (Fees) (No. 3) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 517 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0517.html) ), and amended by the Companies (Fees) (No. 4) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 737 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0737.html) )) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

###### 3.

3. Part I of the Eighth Schedule is altered by adding the following to the Table of Fees therein:

> “
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | For the registration of any document nominating to the registrar a new annual return date for a company pursuant to section 127(9) (inserted by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec60) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ) | €15 | Nil |
>
> .”.
