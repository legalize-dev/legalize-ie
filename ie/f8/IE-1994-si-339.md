S.I. No. 339 of 1994.

NATIONAL MONUMENTS (PRESCRIPTION OF FORM) REGULATIONS, 1994

I, MICHAEL D. HIGGINS, T.D., Minister for Arts, Culture and the Gaeltacht, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) (5) of the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1994), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Monuments (Prescription of Form) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The form to be furnished under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) (3) of the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) to a person making a report of the possession or control of an archaeological object shall be in the form set out in the Schedule hereto.
