S.I. No. 332 of 1993.

PLANT VARIETIES (PROPRIETARY RIGHTS) (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1993.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0012.html#sec12) and [26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , (No. 24 of 1980), as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0011.html) ), and after compliance with subsection (2) of the said section 26, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980).

###### 3.

3. (1) Each of the genera and species mentioned in column (2) of the Schedule to these Regulations at any reference number is hereby specified for the purposes of section 4 of the Act.

- (2) The maximum period for which plant breeders' rights are exercisable in respect of a plant variety which is of a genus or species mentioned in column (2) of the said Schedule at any reference number shall be the period of 20 years mentioned in column (3) of that Schedule.

- (3) The period of 4 years is hereby prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 2 (*d*) (ii) of the First Schedule to the Act in respect of a plant variety of a genus or species mentioned in the said Schedule.

###### 4.

4. Each of the genera and species mentioned in column (2) of the said Schedule at any reference number is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 12 (5) (*b*) (i) of the Act.
