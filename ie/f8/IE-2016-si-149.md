S.I. No. 149 of 2016

DISTRICT COURT (AMENDMENT) RULES 2016

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the District Court (Amendment) Rules 2016, shall come into operation on the 3rd day of May 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) are amended:

- (i) by the substitution of the heading to rule 6 of Order 19 of the following “Remands under s.5 1997 Act”;

- (ii) the substitution for rule 4 of Order 26 of the following rule;

  > “Warrants of committal in Dublin Metropolitan District
  >
  > 4. Warrants of committal may, in the Dublin Metropolitan District, be addressed to the Governor of the Prison into whose custody the person is being committed.”

- (iii) by the substitution of the form No.19.1 set out in Schedule 1 for the form bearing the like number in the Schedule B annexed to the District Court Rules 1997; and

- (iv) by the substitution of the form No. 28A.15 set out in Schedule 1 for the form bearing the like number in the Schedule B annexed to the District Court Rules 1997; and

- (v) by the substitution of the form No. 66.4 set out in Schedule 1 for the form bearing the like number in the Schedule C annexed to the District Court Rules 1997.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules amend Order 19, rule 6 and Order 26, rule 4 of the Rules of the District Court and substitute Form No. 19.1 and Form No. 28A.15, and Form No. 66.4 in Schedule B and Schedule C, respectively,annexed to District Court Rules 1997.
