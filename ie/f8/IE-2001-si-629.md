# S.I. No. 629/2001 - Merchant Shipping (Training and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Frank Fahey, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0046/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Merchant Shipping Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0046/index.html) (No. 46 of 1947), and [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0037/sec0003.html#sec3) (2) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0037/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Merchant Shipping (Certification of Seamen) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1979), as adapted by [the Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983) and the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0301.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Training and Certification) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Merchant Shipping (Training and Certification) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 553 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0553.html) ), are amended by the insertion after Regulation 7 of the following:

Exemptions

7A The Minister if, (s)he considers that a ship's size and the conditions of its voyage are such as to render the application of the full requirements of regulation II/3 and section A-II/3 of the STCW Code unreasonable or impracticable, may to that extent exempt the master and the officer in charge of a navigational watch on such a ship or class of ships from some of the requirements, bearing in mind the safety of all ships which may be operating in the same waters.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These Regulations allow for exemptions to the full requirements of regulation II/3 and section A-II/3 of the STCW Code for certain personnel working in near coastal waters.
