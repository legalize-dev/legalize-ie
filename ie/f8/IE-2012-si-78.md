# S.I. No. 78/2012 - Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th March*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec12) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Regulation (EU) No 161/2012 of 23 February 2012[^1] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 481 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0481.html) ) are amended by the substitution, for Regulation 2(1) of—

> “2.(1) In these Regulations “Technical Measures Regulation” means those provisions of Annex III to Council Regulation (EC) No. 43/2009 of 16 January 2009[^2] as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No 753/2009 of 27 July 2009[^3] and Council Regulation (EC) No. 1288/2009 of 27 November 2009[^4] (which is amended by Regulation (EU) No. 579/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2011[^5] ) and Commission Regulation (EU) No 161/2012 of 23 February 2012.”

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall remain in force until the 25 August 2012.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 March 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does note purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations have the purpose of giving effect to the provisions of Commission Regulation (EU) No. 161/2012 of 23 February 2012 on emergency measures for the protection of haddock stocks in waters to the west of Scotland.
