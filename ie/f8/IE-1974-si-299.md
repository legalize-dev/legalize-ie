# S.I. No. 299/1974 - The Pigs and Bacon Acts, 1935 To 1961 (Pigs and Bacon Commission) Bacon Pig Production Levy (No. 1) Order 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 299 of 1974.

THE PIGS AND BACON ACTS, 1935 to 1961 (PIGS AND BACON COMMISSION) BACON PIG PRODUCTION LEVY (No. 1) ORDER 1974.

WHEREAS sub-section (1) of [Section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0035/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Pigs and Bacon (Amendment) Act 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1939) provides that every person who holds or has held a licence shall, for every levy period during which or any part of which he holds or has held such licence, pay to the Commission in respect of such licence a levy calculated at the rate, which is the appropriate rate in respect of such levy period, for every carcase used for the production of bacon during such half year at the premises to which such licence relates.

AND WHEREAS sub-section (3) of the said Section provides that the following provisions shall apply and have effect in relation to the rate of the levy under this Section, that is to say:—

( *a* ) the Commission shall not later than the expiration of the levy period commencing on the transfer date and may thereafter whenever and so often as they think fit make an Order fixing the rate of such levy;

( *b* ) the first Order under this sub-section shall be deemed to have come into force on the transfer date and every subsequent Order under this sub-section shall come into force on the first day of the levy period commencing next after the making of such Order;

( *c* ) every Order made under this sub-section shall remain in force until a subsequent Order under this sub-section is made and comes into force;

( *d* ) references in sub-section (1) of this Section to the appropriate rate of levy in respect of a particular levy period shall be construed as references to the rate of levy fixed by an Order in this sub-section which was in force during such levy period:

AND WHEREAS sub-section (4) of the said Section 34 provides that for the purpose of that Section each of the following periods shall be a levy period, that is to say:—

( *a* ) the period commencing on the transfer date and ending on the 31st day of March 1940;

( *b* ) any period of three months commencing after the transfer date, on any 1st day of April, 1st day of July, 1st day of October, or 1st day of January:

AND WHEREAS Section (3) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1940) provides that sub-section (1) of Section 34 of the Act of 1939 shall have effect as if there were substituted for the words "Half year" the words "levy period" and further provides that the said Section 3 shall be deemed to have come into operation on and shall have effect as on and from the 1st day of January 1940.

AND WHEREAS [Section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Pigs and Bacon (Amendment) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1961) provides that an Order under this [Section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0035/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Pigs and Bacon (Amendment) Act 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1939) shall not be made by the Commission without the consent of the Minister.

AND WHEREAS the Commission considers it desirable to make an Order fixing the rate of levy which Order shall come into force on the first day of the levy period commencing next after the making of this Order.

NOW THEREFORE THE PIGS AND BACON COMMISSION WITH THE CONSENT OF THE MINISTER FOR AGRICULTURE AND FISHERIES HEREBY ORDERS AS FOLLOWS:—

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Pigs and Bacon Acts, 1935 to 1961 (Pigs and Bacon Commission) Bacon Pig Production Levy (No. 1) Order 1974.

- (2) Unless a contrary intention appears every word and every expression used in this Order shall have the same meaning as it has in the Pigs and Bacon Acts, 1935 to 1961.

- (3) The rate hereinafter specified shall be the appropriate rate for the purpose of Section 34 of the Act of 1939, while this Order continues in force.

- (4) The rate of levy for the purposes of this Order is hereby fixed at £0.60 (sixty pence) for every carcase used for the production of bacon.

- (5) This Order shall come into force on the 1st day of October, 1974.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order fixes the rate of levy to be paid by each Licensed Curer in respect of carcases converted to bacon at £0.60 (sixty pence) as from the 1st October, 1974.
