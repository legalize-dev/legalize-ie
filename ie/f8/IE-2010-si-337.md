# S.I. No. 337/2010 - Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act 1993 (Section 21(1)) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th July*, 2010.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/sec0021.html#sec21) (1) of the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act 1993 (Section 21(1)) Order 2010.

###### 2.

2. The [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) is amended—

- (*a*) in the First Schedule, by inserting “Dublin Docklands Development Authority” after “Defence Forces Canteen Board”, and

- (*b*) in the Second Schedule, by deleting “Custom House Docks Development Authority”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 July 2010.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.
