# S.I. No. 502/2003 - Housing (New House Grant Etc.) Regulations, 1990 (Amendment) Regulations, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No.21 of 1966), as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1992), and by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0028/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Housing Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1988), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (New House Grant etc.) Regulations, 1990 (Amendment) Regulations, 2003.

###### 2.

2. The following sub article is hereby substituted for article 4 (2) (c) of the Housing (New House Grant etc.) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 34 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0034.html) ).

> “(2)(c) In all cases covered by (a) and (b) above a new house the subject of a grant application, must be completed and occupied and an application for payment of a grant received by the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government on or before 5.30pm, 2 April 2004”.

###### 3.

3. The following sub article (d) is hereby added to article 4 (2) of the Housing (New House Grant etc.) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 34 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0034.html) ).

> “(2)(d) The Department will seek appropriate evidence of occupation on or before 2 April 2004, from inhabitants.”.

*Explanatory Note*

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These regulations provide for the extension of the deadline for the completion and occupation of houses where a new house grant application was received on or before 4 December 2002.
