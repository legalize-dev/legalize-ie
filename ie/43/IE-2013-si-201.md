# S.I. No. 201/2013 - European Union (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) (Amendment) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st June*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purposes of giving further effect to Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 2008[^1] , as amended by Commission Directive 2012/45/EU of 3 December 2012[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) (Amendment) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 651 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0651.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1)—

    - (i) by deleting the definition of “Contracting Party”, and

    - (ii) by substituting for the definition of “Directive” the following:

      > “ ‘Directive’ means Directive 2008/68/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 September 2008[^1] (as amended by Commission Directive 2012/45/EU of 3 December 2012[^2]);”.

      and

- (*b*) in Regulation 27 by substituting for paragraph (12) the following:

  > - “(12) A person who fails to comply with a prohibition notice is guilty of an offence and is liable—
  >
  >     - (*a*) on summary conviction, to a class A fine, or
  >
  >     - (*b*) on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding €500,000.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 June 2013.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of these Regulations is amend the European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 651 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0651.html) ) in line with the amending Commission Directive 2012/45/EU of 3 December 2012 adapting for the second time the Annexes to Directive 2008/68/EC on the inland transport of dangerous goods to scientific and technical progress.
