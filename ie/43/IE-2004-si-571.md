# S.I. No. 571/2004 - Animal Remedies Consultative Committee (Prescribed Organisations) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 571 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0571.html) .**

ANIMAL REMEDIES CONSULTATIVE COMMITTEE (PRESCRIBED ORGANISATIONS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2004.

[S.I. No. 571 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0571.html) .

ANIMAL REMEDIES CONSULTATIVE COMMITTEE (PRESCRIBED ORGANISATIONS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2004.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(2) of [the Animal Remedies Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1993), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Remedies Consultative Committee (Prescribed Organisations) (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Animal Remedies Consultative Committee (Prescribed Organisations) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 175 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0175.html) ) are amended as follows:

- (*a*) in Regulation 3, by—

    - (i) substituting for paragraph (*b*) the following:

      > - “(*b*) Animal and Plant Health Association.”,

      and

    - (ii) substituting for paragraph (*g*) the following:

      > - “(*g*) Veterinary Ireland.”,

      and

    - (iii) deleting paragraph (*h*),

      and

- (*b*) in Regulation 5, by substituting for paragraph (*c*) the following:

  > - “(*c*) An Bord Bia.”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 23rd September, 2004. |
|  | JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations update the list of organisations from which nominations of candidates for appointment to the Animal Remedies Consultative Committee are invited.
