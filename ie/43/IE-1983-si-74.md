S.I. No. 74 of 1983.

TELEX (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telex (Amendment) Regulations, 1983.

- (2) The Telex Regulations, 1980 to 1982, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telex Regulations, 1980 to 1983.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Telex Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 197 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0197.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule thereto (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Telex (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I.
