# S.I. No. 169/1981 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 46 of the said Act as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1977 and 1978 and these Regulations shall be construed together and may be cited collectively as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1977 to 1981.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 25th day of May, 1981.

###### 3.

3. The Third Schedule to the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0358.html) ) is hereby amended as follows:—

- (i) by the addition of the following matter at reference 4 (*a*)—

  > "The New Belgard Road.",

- (ii) by the substitution for the matter at reference 4 (*f*) of the following—

  > "( *f* ) Belgard Road and its continuation as the Belgard Road Extension other than the portion of the said road between its northernmost and southernmost junctions with the New Belgard Road.",

- (iii) by the deletion of the matter at reference 4 (*m*).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

11th day of May, 1981.

**RAPHAEL P. BURKE.**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Dublin and County of Dublin) Regulations, 1977 and 1978 and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in County Dublin.
