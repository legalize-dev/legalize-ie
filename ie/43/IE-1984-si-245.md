# S.I. No. 245/1984 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 245 of 1984.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) ORDER, 1984.

I, GEMMA HUSSEY, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), with the concurrence of the Minister for the Public Service and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards and with the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Labout (being Ministers of the Government whom the Minister for Education considers to be concerned), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The institution specified in the Schedule of this Order is hereby designated as being an institution to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) applies.

SCHEDULE

Irish Management Institute.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 8th day of October, 1984.

**GEMMA HUSSEY,**

Minister for Education.
