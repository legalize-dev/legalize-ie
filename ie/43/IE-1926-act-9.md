# DEFENCE FORCES (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACT, 1926

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO CONTINUE THE DEFENCE FORCES (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACTS, 1923 TO 1925. [11*th March*, 1926.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS OF SAORSTÁT EIRE ANN AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Continuance of the Defence Forces (Temporary Provisions) Acts, 1923 to 1925.

**1.**—The Defence Forces (Temporary Provisions) Acts, 1923 to 1925 shall continue in force until the 31st day of March, 1927, and shall then expire.

###### 2 Short title and construction.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Defence Forces (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1926.

- (2) This Act shall be construed as one with the Defence Forces (Temporary Provisions) Acts, 1923 to 1925, and those Acts and this Act may be cited together as the Defence Forces (Temporary Provisions) Acts, 1923 to 1926.
