# S.I. No. 315/2025 - European Union (Novel Foods) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th July,* 2025.

I, JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2470 of 20 December 2017[^1] , as amended by the Commission implementing acts specified in Part A of Schedule 1 to these Regulations, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Novel Foods) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2025.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the European Union (Novel Foods) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 159 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0159.html) ), the Regulations of 2025 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Union (Novel Foods) Regulations 2022 to 2025.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations –

“Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Novel Foods) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 253 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0253.html) );

“Regulations of 2025” means the European Union (Novel Foods) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 ( [S.I. No. 40 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0040.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations give further effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2470 of 20 December 2017 which establishes the Union List of Novel Foods as amended by the Commission Implementing Regulations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Novel Foods) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2025.

These Regulations amend the European Union (Novel Foods) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 253 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0253.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations and can be cited together with all previous amending instruments as the European Union (Novel Foods) Regulations 2022 to 2025.
