# S.I. No. 566/2019 - Statistics (Producer Prices Survey) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th November*, 2019.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993), and for the purpose of giving full effect to Council Regulation (EC) No. 1165/98 of 19 May 1998[^1] as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 1882/2003 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 September 2003[^2] , Regulation (EC) No. 1158/2005 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 July 2005[^3] , Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1503/2006 of 28 September 2006[^4] , Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^5] , Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1178/2008 of 28 November 2008[^6] , Commission Regulation (EC) No. 329/2009 of 22 April 2009[^7] , Regulation (EC) No 596/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^8] and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 461/2012 of 31 May 2012[^9] , in so far as it relates to Annexes A, B and D thereto, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Producer Prices Survey) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. (1) In this Order -

“Act of 1993” means the Statistics Act 1993 (No. 21 of 1993);

“quarter” means a period of 3 months ending on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September or 31 December in any year;

“Regulation” means Council Regulation (EC) No. 1165/98 of 19 May 1998[^1] as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 1882/2003 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 September 2003[^2] , Regulation (EC) No. 1158/2005 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 July 2005[^3] , Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1503/2006 of 28 September 2006[^4] , Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006, Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1178/2008 of 28 November 2008[^6] , Commission Regulation (EC) No. 329/2009 of 22 April 2009[^7-fn_14] , Regulation (EC) No 596/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^8] and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 461/2012 of 31 May 2012[^9] ;

“Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006” means Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^5] as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 295/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008[^10] , Regulation (EU) No. 70/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 January 2012[^11] and Regulation (EU) 2019/1243 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^12] ;

“survey” means the Producer Prices Survey that is conducted by the Central Statistics Office for the purposes of obtaining information set out in the Schedule for the purposes of compliance with the Regulation, in so far as it relates to Annexes A, B and D thereto.

- (2) A word or expression that is used in this Order and also used in the Regulation has the same meaning in this Order as in the Regulation, except where the context otherwise requires.

###### 3.

3. A requirement on an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under section B (other than class 7.21 or division 9), C (other than division 18 or 33, class 24.46 or group 25.4, 30.1, 30.3 or 30.4) or D of NACE Rev. 2 set out in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 to provide for the purposes of the survey the information set out in paragraphs 1(a), (c) and 2 of the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 25 of the Act of 1993.

###### 4.

4. A requirement on an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under-

- (*a*) section B (other than class 7.21), C (other than class 24.46 or group 25.4, 30.1, 30.3 or 30.4) or D or division 36, or

- (*b*) section G (other than division 47), H, I, J, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R or S,

of NACE Rev. 2 set out in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 to provide for the purposes of the survey the information set out in paragraphs 1(b), (c) and 2 of the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 25 of the Act of 1993.

###### 5.

5. A requirement on an undertaking that is classified for statistical purposes under section F of NACE Rev. 2 set out in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No. 1893/2006 to provide for the purposes of the survey the information set out in paragraphs 1(d) and 2 of the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 25 of the Act of 1993.

###### 6.

6. The undertakings in relation to whom a requirement to provide information is prescribed in Article 3 or 4(a) shall provide the relevant information in relation to the 15th day of each month (in this Article referred to as the “monthly reference day”) in the period from November 2019 to October 2024, monthly in relation to the immediately preceding monthly reference day.

###### 7.

7. The undertakings in relation to whom a requirement to provide information is prescribed in Article 4(b) or 5 shall provide the relevant information in relation to the 15th day of the second month in each quarter (in this Article referred to as the “quarterly reference day”) in the period from November 2019 to October 2024, quarterly in relation to the immediately preceding quarterly reference day.

## SCHEDULE

*Articles 3, 4 and 5*

1. Information regarding the following, as it relates to the undertaking concerned, and specifying the monthly reference day or quarterly reference day, as the case may be, to which the information relates:

- (a) the purchase price of goods or services purchased in or outside the State by the undertaking;

- (b) the sale price of goods or services sold in or outside the State by the undertaking;

- (c) the following particulars in respect of the goods and services referred to in subparagraph (a) or (b), as the case may be:

    - (i) a description of the goods or services;

    - (ii) the terms of purchase or sale, as the case may be, of the goods or services;

    - (iii) the country of purchase or sale, as the case may be, of the goods or services;

    - (iv) the discount or surcharges applied to the purchase or sale, as the case may be, of the goods or services;

    - (v) the currency of purchase or sale, as the case may be, of the goods or services;

- (d) the sale price of materials sold in or outside the State by the undertaking.

2. Information regarding the time taken by the undertaking concerned to complete the form, questionnaire or other record prepared by the Director General for the collection of the information referred to in paragraph 1.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 November, 2019.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.
