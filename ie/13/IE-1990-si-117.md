# S.I. No. 117/1990 - Harbour Rates (Youghal Harbour) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 117 of 1990.

HARBOUR RATES (YOUGHAL HARBOUR) ORDER, 1990.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Youghal Harbour) Order, 1990.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1990.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Youghal Harbour Order, 1878, shall have effect as if, for Parts I and II of the Schedule thereto (inserted by the Harbour Rates (Youghal Harbour) Order, 1943 ( [S. R. & O. No. 346 of 1943](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1943/en/si/0346.html) )), as amended by the Harbour Rates (Youghal Harbour) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0395.html) ), there were substituted the following—

ACTUAL RATES

I. Rates on Vessels.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | £ p |
| 1. On every ship, vessel or steamer ( other than a yacht or other pleasure boat) entering the harbour to discharge or load cargo, on each occasion of entering the harbour, per net registered ton | 0.40 |
| 2. On every ship, vessel or steamer ( other than a yacht or other pleasure boat) entering the harbour for the purpose of seeking refuge, per net registered ton | 0.40 |
| 3. On every ship, vessel or steamer ( other than a yacht or other pleasure boat) entering the harbour unladen and using the docks or quays within the harbour for the purpose of repairs or cleaning, per net registered ton | 0.40 |
| 4. On every yacht or other pleasure boat entering or using the harbour, per day | 4.00 |
| 5. On every yacht or other pleasure boat entering the harbour, payable in advance, on the 1st day of June in each year as a composition in full for one year of all tonnage rates mentioned in this Part of this Schedule if the gross weight of the yacht or other pleasure boat— |  |
| (a) is less than one ton | 10.00 |
| (b) is one ton or more but less than five tons | 20.00 |
| (c) is five tons or more but less than twenty tons | 30.00 |
| (d) is more than twenty tons | 40.00 |

II. Rates on goods, whether landed on, or shipped from, the quays, or loaded from, or unloaded into, lighters or barges.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Animal foodstuffs, per tonne | 0.40 |
| 2. Coal, per tonne | 0.40 |
| 3. Fertiliser, per tonne | 0.40 |
| 4. Grain, per tonne | 0.40 |
| 5. Goods not mentioned elsewhere in this Schedule, per tonne. | 0.40 |
| 6. The Harbour Rates (Youghal Harbour) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0395.html) ), is hereby revoked. |  |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of May, 1990.

**JOHN P. WILSON,**

Minister for the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order authorises Youghal Urban District Council to charge increased actual goods and tonnage rates at Youghal Harbour.
