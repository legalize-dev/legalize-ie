[S.I. No. 198 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0198.html)

SOCIAL WELFARE (REDUCTION OF PAYMENTS TO HEALTH PROFESSIONALS) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th May*, 2009.

I, MARY HANAFIN, Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec10) of the [Financial Emergency Measures in the Public Interest Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2009), having complied with subsections (4) and (5) of that section, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Reduction of Payments to Health Professionals) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall apply to payments in respect of services rendered by a registered dentist, dispensing optician, ophthalmologist, optometrist or audiologist, to or on behalf of the Minister for Social and Family Affairs to persons who claim treatment benefit under [section 138](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec138) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005).

###### 3.

3. The payments that shall be made in respect of the services referred to in Regulation 2 from the date of the making of these Regulations shall be—

- (*a*) in the case of registered dentists, those specified in Table 1 below,

- (*b*) in the case of dispensing opticians, ophthalmologists and optometrists, those specified in Table 2 below, and

- (*c*) in the case of medical appliances supplied by audiologists or medically prescribed contact lenses supplied by dispensing opticians, optometrists or ophthalmologists, those specified in Table 3 below.

###### 4.

4. Nothing in these Regulations affects the payments to be made by claimants, within the meaning of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) in respect of the services referred to in Regulation 2.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Financial Emergency Measures in the Public Interest Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) requires the Minister to fix the rates of payment to be made to certain healthprofessionals for services that they provide for the Department.Following a consultative process these regulations set out the revised fees payable by the Department to those health professionals in respect of services as indicated in the Tables.These regulations do not affect the payments that claimants may also be required to make in respect of certain services.
