# S.I. No. 765/2007 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 765 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0765.html)

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT 1966 (CONTROL ON ANIMAL AND POULTRY VACCINES) (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th November*, 2007.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 and 13 of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )) for the purpose, *inter alia*, of giving further effect to Article 71 of Directive 2001/82/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 November 2001 [^1] on the Community code relating to veterinary medicinal products, hereby order as follows—

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) is amended in the First Schedule by the deletion of “Bluetongue” and “Equine encephalomyelitis”.

###### 3.

3. The [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) is amended, in Part 1 of the Second Schedule, by the insertion before “Brucellosis in ruminating animals and swine” of “Bluetongue” and before “Equine viral arteritis” of “Equine encephalomyelitis”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 November 2007

**MARY COUGHLAN.**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the removal of certain diseases from the First Schedule of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines)Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) and the insertion of certain diseases in Part 1 of the Second Schedule of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) )
