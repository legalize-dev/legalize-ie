# S.I. No. 406/1996 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 406 of 1996.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) SCHEME, 1996.

Bord Telecom Éireann, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme.

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1996.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation *"the Telecommunications Schemes*, *1994 to 1996"* includes this Scheme.

- (2) Articles 4(a) and 4(e) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 20 May 1996.

- (3) Article 4(b) shall be deemed to have come into operation on the following dates:

    - (i) Section 3(a) of Part 3 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 12 June 1996;

    - (ii) Sections 3(c) and 3(d) of Part 3 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 July 1996;

    - (iii) Sections 3(e) and 3(f) of Part 3 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 4 July 1996.

- (4) Article 4(c) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 September 1996.

- (5) Article 4(d) shall be deemed to have come into operation on the following dates:

    - (i) Section 3(a) of Section IV of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 12 June 1996;

    - (ii) Sections 3(c) and 3(d) of Section IV of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 July 1996;

    - (iii) Sections 3(e) and 3(f) of Section IV of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 4 July 1996.

- (6) Article 4(f) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 July 1996.

- (7) Article 4(g) shall be deemed to have come into operation on the following dates:

    - (i) Section 3(a) of Section VIII of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 12 June 1996;

    - (ii) Section 3(c) and 3(d) of Section VIII of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 July 1996;

    - (iii) Sections 3(e) and 3(f) of Section VIII of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall be deemed to have come into operation on 4 July 1996.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.
