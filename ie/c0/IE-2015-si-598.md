# S.I. No. 598/2015 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 9) (Change in Rates) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January*, 2016.

I, JOAN BURTON, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 (amended by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec16) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2010)), 4 (amended by [section 96](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec96) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2011)), [109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec109) (9), [109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec109) (10), [109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec109) (15) and 178A of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), and by sections 2(2), 109(8) (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0043/print.html#sec4) of the [Social Welfare Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0043/print.html) (No. 43 of 2012)), 109(14) (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0043/print.html#sec4) of the [Social Welfare Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0043/print.html) ), [125](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec125) (3) and [297](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec297) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 9) (Change in Rates) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2015 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2015.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation–

- (*a*) in so far as they relate to invalidity pension and a payment referred to in paragraph (*a*) of the definition of “relevant payment” in section 178(1) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , on 7 January 2016, and

- (*b*) in so far as they relate to disablement pension, State pension (contributory), widow’s (contributory) pension, widower’s (contributory) pension and surviving civil partner’s (contributory) pension, on 8 January 2016.

*Amendment of Schedules to Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007*

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) are amended–

- (*a*) by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations for Schedule 1,

- (*b*) by the substitution of the Schedules set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations for Schedules 2 and 2A,

- (*c*) by the substitution of the Schedules set out in Schedule 3 to these Regulations for Schedules 3 and 3A,

- (*d*) by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 4 to these Regulations for Schedule 4,

- (*e*) by the substitution of the Schedules set out in Schedule 5 to these Regulations for Schedules 8, 8A and 8B,

- (*f*) by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 6 to these Regulations for Schedule 9, and

- (*g*) by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 7 to these Regulations for Schedule 11.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0047/print.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0047/print.html) provides for increases in the maximum rates of certain social insurance payments arising from Budget 2016.

These Regulations provide for increases to the reduced personal rates of State Pension (Contributory), Widow’s (Contributory) Pension, Widower’s (Contributory) Pension and Surviving Civil Partner’s (Contributory) Pension, and Deserted Wife’s Benefit for those who have attained the age of 66.

The Regulations also provide for changes in the rates of tapered increases for the qualified adults of recipients of State Pension (Contributory), with a proportionally higher increase in the case of qualified adults over 66. In the case of recipients of Incapacity Supplement, the Regulations provide for changes in the tapered increases for qualified adults who have attained the age of 66 years.Finally, in the case of Invalidity Pension recipients, the Regulations provide for increases in the tapered rates for a qualified adult who attained 66 years of age before 2 January 2014.

These changes are effective from 7 January 2016 in the case of Deserted Wife’s Benefit and Invalidity Pension.The changes are effective from 8 January 2016 in the case of State Pension (Contributory), Widow’s (Contributory) Pension, Widower’s (Contributory) Pension, Surviving Civil Partner’s (Contributory) Pension and Incapacity Supplement.
