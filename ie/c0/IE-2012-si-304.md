# S.I. No. 304/2012 - European Union (Accounts) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th August*, 2012.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 78/660/EEC of 25 July 1978[^1] , as amended by Directive 2006/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2006[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Accounts) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. [Section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/sec0008.html#sec8) (2) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1986) is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph (*a*) (amended by Regulation 4(*a*) of the European Communities (Accounts) Regulations 1993 ( [S.I. No. 396 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0396.html) )), by substituting “€4.4 million” for “ £1,500,000”, and

- (*b*) in paragraph (*b*) (amended by Regulation 4(*b*) of the European Communities (Accounts) Regulations 1993), by substituting “€8.8 million” for “ £3,000,000”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 August 2012.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give further effect to Directive 2006/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2006, amending Council Directive 78/660/EEC, by increasing the balance sheet total and turnover thresholds set out at [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/sec0008.html#sec8) (2) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) for the purpose of availing of certain accounting exemptions for small companies to €4.4 million and €8.8 million respectively.
