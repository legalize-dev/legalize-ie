# S.I. No. 414/2013 - Finance Act 1993 (Section 60) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th November*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 58 and 60 (as amended by [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec56) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012)) of the [Finance Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1993), subsection (4) of section 22 (as amended by [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec50) (*b*)(ii) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) ) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the Roads and Road Vehicles (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 815 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0815.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Finance Act 1993 (Section 60) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. The persons or categories of person and the purpose or purposes for the access specified in the Schedule are prescribed for the purposes of section 60(3) of the [Finance Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1993) and subsection (4) of section 22 (inserted by [section 57](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec57) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961).

###### 3.

3. The Finance Act 1993 (Section 60) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 382 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0382.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations prescribe the persons or categories of person who may have access to records established under [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Finance Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) and the purpose or purposes for such access.
