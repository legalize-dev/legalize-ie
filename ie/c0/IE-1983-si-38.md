# S.I. No. 38/1983 - Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 38 of 1983.

HOUSING REGULATIONS, 1980 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1983.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The following article shall be substituted for article 82 of the Housing Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 296 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0296.html) ) as substituted by article 12 of the Housing Regulations 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0177.html) ):—

"82. The fee accompanying an application for a certificate or a certificate of exemption received on or after the 1st day of March, 1983 shall be as follows—

( *a* ) in case the application for a certificate relates to one house only, £40,

( *b* ) in case the application for a certificate relates to more than one house, £40 plus £14 in respect of each house exceeding one, or

( *c* ) in case the application relates to a certificate of exemption, £5."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 15th day of February, 1983.

**DICK SPRING,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend article 82 of the Housing Regulations, 1980. They provide for an increase in the fee accompanying an application for Certificate of Reasonable Value for the purposes of [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) .
