# S.I. No. 96/1989 - Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Acts, 1977 and 1988 (Appointed Day) (Aer Rianta) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 96 of 1989.

WORKER PARTICIPATION (STATE ENTERPRISES) ACTS, 1977 AND 1988 (APPOINTED DAY) (AER RIANTA) ORDER, 1989.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Acts, 1977 and 1988 (Appointed Day) (Aer Rianta) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of May, 1989 is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of section 6 [as amended by section 10 of the Worker Participation (State Enterprises] Act, 1988 (No. 13 of 1988)] of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), in relation to Aer Rianta cuideachta phoibli theoranta.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of May, 1989.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the appointed day for determining election years for Aer Rianta cuideachta phoiblí theoranta under the Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Acts, 1977 and 1988.
