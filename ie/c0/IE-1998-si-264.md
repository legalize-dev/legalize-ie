# S.I. No. 264/1998 - Industrial Relations Act, 1990 (Definition of "Worker") Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 264 of 1998.

INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS ACT, 1990 (DEFINITION OF "WORKER") ORDER, 1998

The Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) (5) of the [Industrial Relations Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Industrial Relations Act, 1990 (Definition of "Worker") Order, 1998.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1998.

###### 2.

2. [Section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Industrial Relations Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1990), is hereby amended by the deletion in subsection (1) of paragraph (d).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government this 22nd day of July, 1998.

**BERTIE AHERN**

Taoiseach

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to amend the definition of "worker" in [Section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) (1) of the [Industrial Relations Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1990) in order to give officers of local authorities (including health boards) access to the Labour Relations Commission, Labour Court and Rights Commissioners.
