# S.I. No. 629/2021 - Trust RACs (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November,* 2021.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS T.D., Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) and [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0060.html#sec60) (as amended by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec37) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) (as adapted by the Employment Affairs and Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0447.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

**Citation and Construction**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trust RACs (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Trust RACs (Registration) Regulations 2007 shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Trust RACs (Registration) Regulations 2007 to 2021.

**Amendments**

###### 3.

3. The Trust RACs (Registration) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0187.html) ) are amended in Regulation 3 by –

- (*a*) in sub-article (2), by the substitution of “60(2A)” for “60(2)”, and

- (*b*) in sub-article (3), by the substitution of “1 month” for “12 months”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

25 November, 2021.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS T.D.,**

Minister for Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend [S.I. No. 187 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0187.html) by amending the section of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) referred to in Regulation 3(2) and by amending the period specified in Regulation 3(3) in which the trustees of a trust RAC are required to notify the Pensions Authority of changes to the information that has been provided to it for the purposes of registration under [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .
