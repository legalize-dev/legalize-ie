# S.I. No. 85/1978 - Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933 (Fees) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 85 of 1978.

PERPETUAL FUNDS (REGISTRATION) ACT, 1933 (FEES) ORDER, 1978.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1933), and the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0306.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) Order, 1978.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 14th day of April, 1978.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The fees to be paid to the Registrar of Friendly Societies pursuant to [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) (2) of the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1933), shall be the fees specified in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 5.

5. The Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act (Fees Regulations) Order, 1935 (S. R. & O., No. 247 of 1935), is hereby revoked.

SCHEDULE.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ref. No. | Service | Fee |
|  |  | £ |
| 1. | Registration of a perpetual fund | £10.00 |
| 2. | Registration of complete alteration of Rules | £5.00 |
| 3. | Registration of partial alteration of Rules | £2.50 |
| 4. | Rectification of the Register on the application of the trustees of a registered perpetual fund | £5.00 |
| 5. | Cancellation of the registration of a registered perpetual fund | £5.00 |
| 6. | Issue of a certificate by the Registrar | £5.00 |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of March, 1978.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA.**

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 8th day

of March, 1978.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted under the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
