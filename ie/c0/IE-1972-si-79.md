# S.I. No. 79/1972 - Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices For Milk) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 79 of 1972.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (MINIMUM PRICES FOR MILK) ORDER, 1972.

1, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1972, and shall come into operation on the 22nd day of March, 1972.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"registered producers" means persons registered in the register of producers kept by the Cork District Milk Board;

"registered retailers" means persons registered in the register of retailers kept by the Cork District Milk Board;

"registered wholesalers" means persons registered in the register of wholesalers kept by the Cork District Milk Board.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) (No. 2) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 155 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0155.html) ), is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. The minimum prices to be paid to registered producers for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and retailers shall be at the rates shown in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE.

*Minimum Prices.*

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Price per gallon (whether supplied at the registered premises of the producer, or at a collection place agreed on with the purchaser, or otherwise supplied .. .. | 15·5p | 19·5p | 20·5p |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of March, 1972.

- (Signed) JAMES GIBBONS,

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the minimum prices to be paid to registered producers, as from 22nd March, 1972, for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and registered retailers in the Cork District.
