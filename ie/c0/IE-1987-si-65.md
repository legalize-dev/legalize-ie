# S.I. No. 65/1987 - Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Enterprise Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 65 of 1987.

SOCIAL WELFARE (PAY-RELATED BENEFIT) (ENTERPRISE ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981) and by section 76 of that Act as amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0005/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) , (No. 5 of 1984), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Enterprise Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Enterprise Allowance) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 214 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0214.html) ) may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Enterprise Allowance) Regulations, 1984 to 1987.

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Enterprise Allowance) Regulations, 1984 are hereby amended by the substitution for article 6 of the following article:—

" 6. Where a person has received payment of pay- related benefit in the form of a lump sum under these Regulations, he shall notwithstanding the provisions of [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) —

- (*a*) not be entitled to a further payment of pay-related benefit in respect of a period of interruption of employment in respect of such number of days for which he is entitled to unemployment benefit by virtue of subsections (4) (*c*) and (4A) of [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , and which are treated as part of one period of interruption of employment, as is equal to the number of days in respect of which payment of pay-related benefit by way of lump sum has already been made.

- (*b*) not be entitled to a further payment of pay-related benefit in respect of any day of incapacity for work which forms part of a period of interruption of employment in respect of which the person is entitled to disability benefit or maternity allowance payable under section 24 (2) (*b*) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , or would be so entitled but for the fact that injury benefit is payable under Chapter 5 of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , in respect of any period which coincides with any period in respect of which payment of pay-related benefit by way of lump sum has already made.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 27th day of February, 1987.

**GEMMA HUSSEY,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Social Welfare (Pay-Related Benefit) (Enterprise Allowance), Regulations 1984 ( [S.I. No. 214 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0214.html) ). They are designed to ensure that a person who has received pay-related benefit in the form of a lump sum payment under the terms of the Enterprise Allowance Scheme administered by the Department of Labour shall not be entitled to receive a further payment of that benefit for an equivalent period where the person makes a claim to unemployment benefit within thirteen weeks following the cessation of his participation in that scheme.
