# S.I. No. 359/2022 - National Archives Act 1986 (Section 1(2)(d)) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th July,* 2022.

I, CATHERINE MARTIN, Minister for Tourism, Culture, Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media, after consultation with the Director and the Council, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/sec0001.html#sec1) (2)(d) of the [National Archives Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1986) and the Arts and Culture (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1993 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0021.html) ) (as adapted by the Culture, Heritage and the Gaeltacht (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 403 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0403.html) )), hereby make the following Order with respect to which, pursuant to Section 1(2)(e) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Archives Act 1986 (Section 1(2)(d)) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. The [Schedule to the National Archives Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1986) is amended by the addition of “An Bord Pleanála”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 July, 2022.

**CATHERINE MARTIN,**

Minister for Tourism, Culture,

Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media.
