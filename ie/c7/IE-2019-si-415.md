# S.I. No. 415/2019 - Health Act 1970 (Section 45A(7)) (Classes of Payments) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th August,* 2019.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 45A (7) (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec4) of the [Health Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2008)) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1970 (Section 45A(7)) (Classes of Payments) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The following classes of payments are prescribed for the purposes of section 45A(7) (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec4) of the [Health Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2008)) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970):

- (*a*) an ex-gratia payment or payments made to a person under the Scheme (within the meaning of section 1 (amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec2) of the [Redress for Women Resident in Certain Institutions (Amendment) Act 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2019)) of the [Redress for Women Resident in Certain Institutions Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2015));

- (*b*) any subsequent income from the investment of the monies arising from an ex-gratia payment or payments referred to in paragraph (a).

###### 3.

3. The Health Act 1970 (Section 45A(7)) (Classes of Payments) Regulations 2015 (No. 277 of 2015) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 August, 2019.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Order provides that any sums received by way of payments made to a person under the Scheme (within the meaning of section 1 (amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec2) of the [Redress for Women Resident in Certain Institutions (Amendment) Act 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2019)) of the [Redress for Women Resident in Certain Institutions Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2015)) will be disregarded in the calculation of gross income for eligibility for health services under section 45A and section 45(5A) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .
