# S.I. No. 615/2011 - Central Bank Reform Act 2010 (Sections 20 and 22) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd December*, 2011.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Central Bank of Ireland (“the Bank”) by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec20) (1) and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec22) (2) of the [Central Bank Reform Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , the Bank hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Central Bank Reform Act 2010 (Sections 20 and 22) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the [Central Bank Reform Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Sections 20 and 22) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0437.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended by inserting after Regulation 1:

> “1A. These Regulations apply to and in relation to regulated financial service providers other than credit unions.”

###### 4.

4. Regulation 3 of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting “the controlled functions” for “the functions” in each place where the phrase occurs.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 3(1) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting “11B” for “11”.

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended by inserting after Regulation 11:

> “11A. A function shall not be taken to be a pre-approval controlled function with respect to a regulated financial service provider where that function is carried on by another person (the “other person”) and the following conditions are satisfied:
>
> - (*a*) there is in place a written agreement between the regulated financial service provider and the other person for the carrying on of that function by that other person on behalf of the regulated financial service provider; and
>
> - (*b*) the other person is a financial service provider whose business is subject to regulation by either:
>
>     - (i) the Bank; or
>
>     - (ii) an authority that performs functions in an EEA country that are comparable to the functions performed by the Bank; or
>
>     - (iii) an authority that performs functions in a non EEA country that are comparable to the functions performed by the Bank.
>
>       11B. A function is and shall remain prescribed as a controlled function notwithstanding that such function, or part thereof, is comprised within a function, or part thereof, prescribed as a pre-approval controlled function.”

###### 7.

7. Schedule 2 to the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution therefor of the Schedule to these Regulations.
