# S.I. No. 127/2022 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th March,* 2022.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection (as adapted by the Employment Affairs and Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0447.html) )), in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) and [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) (as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec23) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2012)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), and having consulted with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations 2022.

*Rate of Revaluation of Preserved Benefit*

###### 2.

2. In accordance with Section 33 of the Pensions Act there shall be a 2.4% revaluation of preserved benefits for 2021.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 March, 2022.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations provide for changes in the percentage by which the amount of a preserved benefit is to be increased or decreased in a specified year.

There shall be a 2.4% revaluation of preserved benefits under [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , for 2021.
