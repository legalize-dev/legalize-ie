# S.I. No. 314/1981 - Dublin District Milk Board (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, ALAN DUKES, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), and section 41 (as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), and [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1952)) of that Act, as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“the Board” means the Dublin District Milk Board;

“the section” means [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), and [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1952).

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) For the purposes of the levy payable to the Board under subsection (1) (*b*) of the section—

- (*a*) the prescribed sum shall, in respect of the month of October 1981, and each subsequent month be 0.862 new pence;

- (*b*) regulation 4(1) (*a*) of the Dublin District Milk Board (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 16 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0016.html) ), shall not apply in relation to any such month.

- (2) For the purposes of the levy payable to the Board under subsection 2 (*b*) of the section—

    - (a) the prescribed sum shall, in respect of the month of October 1981, and each subsequent month be 0.862 new pence;

    - (b) regulation 4 (2) (*a*) of the Regulations mentioned in paragraph (1) (*b*) shall not apply in relation to any such month.

###### 5.

5. The Dublin District Milk Board (Levy) (Amendment) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 107 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0107.html) ), are hereby revoked as on and from the 1st day of October 1981.
