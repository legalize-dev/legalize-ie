# S.I. No. 476/2012 - Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th December*, 2012.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 5 and 12 of the Roads Act 1920 and [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0024/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Finance (Excise Duties) (Vehicles) Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1952), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

- (2) These Regulations and the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 1992 to 2008 shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 1992 to 2012.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation on 6 December 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 385 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0385.html) ) are hereby amended:—

- (1) by the substitution of the amount “€119” for “€99” in sub-article (2) of Article 15 and sub-article (1) of Article 26.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

28 November 2012.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of the Regulations is to provide a technical change increasing the threshold from €99 to €119, above which a person can get a refund of motor tax and obtain a half-year or quarterly tax disc.
