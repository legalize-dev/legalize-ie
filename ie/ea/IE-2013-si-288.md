# S.I. No. 288/2013 - Employment Equality Acts 1998 to 2011 (section 12) (Church of Ireland College of Education) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th July*, 2013.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Education and Skills, following an application to me in that behalf by the Church of Ireland College of Education and with the consent of the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0021/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Employment Equality Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1998), make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to section 3(2) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Employment Equality Acts 1998 to 2011 (section 12) (Church of Ireland College of Education) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. The Church of Ireland College of Education, Rathmines, in the city of Dublin, may reserve 32 places in its vocational education courses leading to the degree of Bachelor of Education of the University of Dublin and commencing in the academic years 2013/2014, 2014/2015, 2015/2016, 2016/2017 and 2017/2018, being such number of places as seem reasonably necessary to the Minister for Education and Skills to meet the purposes set out in [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0021/sec0012.html#sec12) (4) of the [Employment Equality Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1998).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 July 2013.

**RUAIRÍ; QUINN,**

Minister for Education and Skills.

I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby consent to the making of the foregoing Order.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
