# S.I. No. 173/1999 - Irish Sports Council Act, 1999 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JAMES McDAID, Minister for Tourism, Sport and Recreation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec4) of the [Irish Sports Council Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 1999), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Irish Sports Council Act, 1999 (Establishment Day) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of July, 1999, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Irish Sports Council Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 1999).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of June, 1999. |
|  | JAMES McDAID, |
|  | Minister for Tourism, Sport and Recreation |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The Order appoints the 1st day of July, 1999, as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Irish Sports Council Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) .

The purpose of the [Irish Sports Council Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) , is to establish a statutory body, the Irish Sports Council, to promote and encourage the development of sport in Ireland.
