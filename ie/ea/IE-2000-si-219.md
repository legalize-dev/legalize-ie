# S.I. No. 219/2000 - Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

THE MINISTER for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1991) and section 47A of that Act, as inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec1) of the [Local Government Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2000), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“the Act” means the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) , as amended by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec1) of the [Local Government Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. (1) The prescribed notification referred to in section 47A(2) of the Act shall be in the form specified in the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2)
    - *(a)* Subject to paragraph *(b)*, the period within which a prescribed notification shall be given by a manager shall be the period beginning on the commencement of the fifth year and ending on the expiration of the fifth year of the tenure period of that manager.

    - (*b*) Subject to section 47A(5) of the Act, where, on the date of the making of these regulations, less than two years of a manager's tenure period remains to be served, the period within which a prescribed notification shall be given by such manager shall be the period beginning on the date of the making of these regulations and ending 6 months following that date.

- (3) Subject to section 47A(6)*(a)* of the Act, the period by which the tenure period of a manager who gives the prescribed notification referred to in section 47A(2) of the Act shall be extended shall be 3 years.

- (4) Section 47A(4) of the Act shall not apply to the following offices:

    - (*a*) the office of Dublin City Manager and Town Clerk;

    - (*b*) the office of Cork County Manager;

    - (*c*) the office of Cork City Manager and Town Clerk;

    - (*d*) the office of Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown County Manager;

    - (*e*) the office of Fingal County Manager; and

    - (*f*) the office of South Dublin County Manager.

- (5) Article 21 or 78, as appropriate, of the Local Government (Superannuation) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0455.html) ) shall not apply to a manager whose tenure period is extended by section 47A of the Act and who ceases to hold office before the expiration of his or her tenure period as so extended.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations make provision in relation to the extension of tenure of office of certain managers of local authorities.
