> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 31 of 1991.

PLANT VARIETIES (PROPRIETARY RIGHTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1991

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0012.html#sec12) and [26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0097.html) ), and after compliance with subsection (2) of the said section 26, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980).

###### 3.

3. (1) Each of the species specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby specified for the purposes of section 4 of the Act.

- (2) The period of 20 years is hereby prescribed as the maximum period for the purposes of subsection (12) (*b*) of the said section 4 in respect of a plant variety which is of a species specified in the said Schedule.

- (3) The period of 4 years is hereby prescribed in respect of a plant variety of a species specified in the said Schedule for the purposes of paragraph 2 (*d*) (ii) of the First Schedule to the Act.

###### 4.

4. Each of the species specified in the said Schedule is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 12 (5) (*b*) (i) of the Act.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 21st day of February, 1991.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations extend the list of plant species for which plant breeders' rights may be granted to include the species Malus Mill, Rosa L., Fragaria L. and Betula L.
