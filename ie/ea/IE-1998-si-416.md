# S.I. No. 416/1998 - Criminal Justice Act, 1951 (Section 23) (Delegation of Powers) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 416 of 1998.

CRIMINAL JUSTICE ACT, 1951 (SECTION 23) (DELEGATION OF POWERS) ORDER, 1998

The Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 23A (inserted by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0004/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Criminal Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1997)) of the [Criminal Justice Act, 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1951), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Criminal Justice Act, 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (Section 23) (Delegation of Powers) Order, 1998.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 20th day of October, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The powers conferred on the Government by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0002/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Criminal Justice Act, 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1951), are hereby delegated to the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 20th day of October, 1998.

**BERTIE AHERN, TAOISEACH**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order delegates the powers of the Government, except in capital cases, to commute or remit, in whole or in part, any punishment imposed by a Court exercising criminal jurisdiction and to remit, in whole or in part, any forfeiture or disqualification imposed by such a Court and restore or revive, in whole or in part, the subject of the forfeiture.
