# S.I. No. 260/2025 - Planning and Development Act 2000 (Section 181(2)(a)) (No. 3) Order 2024 (Revocation) Order 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th June,* 2025.

I, JIM O’CALLAGHAN, Minister for Justice, Home Affairs and Migration, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 181](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec181) (2)(b) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) and the Integration and Reception (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 159 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0159.html) ) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0242.html) ), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development Act 2000 (Section 181(2)(a)) (No. 3) Order 2024 (Revocation) Order 2025.

###### 2.

2. The [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (Section 181(2)(a)) (No.3) Order 2024 ( [S.I. No. 481 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0481.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 June, 2025.

**JIM O’CALLAGHAN,**

Minister for Justice, Home Affairs and Migration.
