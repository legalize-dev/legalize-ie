# S.I. No. 657/2016 - European Union (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th January*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive No. 2006/126/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^1] as amended by Commission Directive No. 2015/653/EU of 24 April 2015[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2016.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2017.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“No. 3 Regulations of 2013” means the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 420 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0420.html) );

“Regulations of 2013” means the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 6 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0006.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 27(3), by substituting for subparagraph (*b*) the following:

  > - “(*b*) For the purposes of subparagraph (*a*) a driving licence shall, in addition to the categories set out in column (2) of the Table above—
  >
  >     - (i) in respect of category B, granted prior to 19 January 2013, be regarded from 19 January 2013 as also being a driving licence in respect of category A with the code 79.03, other than where the driving licence was for category B with the code 73,
  >
  >     - (ii) in respect of category A1, granted prior to 19 January 2013, be regarded from 19 January 2013 as a driving licence in respect of category A1 with the code 79.05,
  >
  >     - (iii) in respect of category BE, granted prior to 19 January 2013, be regarded from 19 January 2013 as a driving licence in respect of category BE with the code 79.06,
  >
  >     - (iv) in respect of category A, granted for the first time on or after 19 January 2013, other than a licence in respect of category A granted in accordance with clause (i), and where the holder is aged less than 24 years, and has not held a licence in respect of category A2 for a minimum period of 2 years, be regarded as a driving licence in respect of category A with the code 80, and
  >
  >     - (v) in respect of category A, granted for the first time on or after 19 January 2013, other than a licence in respect of category A granted in accordance with clause (i), and where the holder is aged less than 21 years, be regarded as a driving licence in respect of category A with the code 81.”,

  and
