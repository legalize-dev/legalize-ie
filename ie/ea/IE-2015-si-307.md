# S.I. No. 307/2015 - Valuation Act 2001 (Case Stated for High Court) (Fee) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st July*, 2015.

I, BRENDAN HOWLIN, Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) (2) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001) and the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0418.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Valuation Act 2001 (Case Stated for High Court) (Fee) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 20 July 2015.

###### 2.

2. The fee payable by a party requiring the Tribunal to state and sign a case for the opinion of the High Court under [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec39) (2) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) shall be €380.

###### 3.

3. The Valuation (Case Stated for High Court) (Fee) Regulation 1988 ( [S.I. No. 331 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0331.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 July 2015.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.
