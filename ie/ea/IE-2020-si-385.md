# S.I. No. 385/2020 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd October,* 2020.

I, Eamon Ryan, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec17) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 October 2020.

###### 2.

2. The [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 540 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0540.html) ) are amended –

- (*a*) by the substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 5:

  > “5. The form set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2 is the prescribed form of certificate for the purposes of section 17 of the Act of 2010 to certify-
  >
  > - (*a*) the presence of a drug or drugs, and
  >
  > - (*b*) the concentration of a drug or drugs.”,

- (*b*) by the deletion of Regulation 6 (inserted by Regulation 2(a) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 151 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0151.html) )),

  and

- (*c*) in Schedule 2, by the substitution for Part 2 and Part 3 (inserted by Regulation 2(b) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Sections 15 and 17) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 of the text set out in the Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide for a new drug testing form, allowing the presence and the concentration of drugs to be recorded together on a single document.
