# S.I. No. 323/1979 - Social Welfare (Variation of Weekly Amounts of Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 323 of 1979.

SOCIAL WELFARE (VARIATION OF WEEKLY AMOUNTS OF SUPPLEMENTARY WELFARE ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS, 1979.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) (3) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1975) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Variation of Weekly Amounts of Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The weekly amounts of supplementary welfare allowance are hereby varied with effect on and from the 1st day of October, 1979 by the substitution for the amounts set out in the Table to subsection (1) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (as amended by the [Social Welfare Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1979)) of the amounts set out in the Table to this article.

| Classes of persons to whom the amounts of supplementary welfare allowance are applicable | Weekly amount of supplementary welfare allowance |

| Person without a dependant | £13.70 |

| Person with one child dependant | £18.30 |
| Person with two or more child dependants | £22.70 |
|  | plus, where there are more than two child dependants, £3.40 for each child dependant in excess of two. |
