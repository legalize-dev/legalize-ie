# S.I. No. 365/2010 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th July*, 2010.

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Provision of Services) Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1993), as amended by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) of the [Passports Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2008), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 September 2010.

2.The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0117.html) ) (as amended by the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0021.html) )) are amended in Part I of the Second Schedule by substituting “or within five days” for “or on the day” in column (2) at Reference Numbers 8 and 9.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

22 July 2010.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 July 2010.

**MICHEÁL MARTIN,**

Minister for Foreign Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2001, as amended, to provide for changes in the fee structure for passports.
