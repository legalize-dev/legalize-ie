# S.I. No. 20/2008 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 20 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0020.html)

OCCUPATIONAL PENSION SCHEMES (REVALUATION) REGULATIONS, 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th February*, 2008.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 5 and 33 (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec21) of the [Pensions (Amendment) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2002)) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), and having consulted with the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2008.

*Revaluation percentage.*

###### 2.

2. The revaluation percentage in respect of the 2007 revaluation year shall be 4 per cent.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social and Family Affairs,

5 February 2008

**MARTIN CULLEN.**

Minister for Social and Family Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (as amended by [Section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec21) of the [Pensions (Amendment) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) ), provides that the Minister for Social and Family Affairs, after consultation with the Minister for Finance, shall, in respect of each revaluation year, make Regulations specifying the percentage which shall determine the amount by which a preserved pension benefit is to be increased by in that year.

This Regulation provides for a revaluation percentage of 4% for 2007.
