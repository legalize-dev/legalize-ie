# S.I. No. 322/1998 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 6) Scheme, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Bord Telecom Eireann plc, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme.

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 6) Scheme, 1998.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation *"the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1998"* includes this Scheme.

- (2) Articles 4(a) and 4(b) shall come into operation on 1 October 1998.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by:

- (a) the substitution of the following Article 89(A) for Article 89(A) thereof:

  > "89(A)(1) Bord Telecom Eireann plc may provide a facility (the "Facility") to Subscribers whereby Subscribers may receive a discount on the prevailing annual rental charges for leased lines as specified in Part 1 of the Second Schedule (the "Annual Rental Charges") on committing to the payment of one of the levels of annual rental (each a "Committed Rental Level") specified in Part 3, Section 1 of the Second Schedule hereto for a period of 2, 3 or 4 years (each a "Rental Period"). Where a Subscriber wishes to avail of the Facility and chooses a Committed Rental Level and a Rental Period, Bord Telecom Eireann plc shall apply the corresponding percentage discount specified in Part 3, Section II of the Second Schedule to the Annual Rental Charges. Bord Telecom Eireann plc may, at its absolute discretion withdraw the Facility from all or any Subscribers."

- (2) It shall be a condition of the application of the Facility that:

    - (a) the Subscriber shall pay the Committed Rental Level chosen by it for the Rental Period chosen by it;

    - (b) where the sum payable by the Subscriber in respect of the use of leased lines in any 12 month period during the Rental Period chosen by it, is less than the minimum threshold of the Committed Rental Level chosen by it, then the Subscriber shall pay to Bord Telecom Eireann plc the corresponding Minimum Annual Rental as set out in Part 3, Section III of the Second Schedule hereto. For the purposes of this Scheme a 12 month period shall be calculated from the commencement of the Rental Period and after the first year from the anniversary of the commencement of the Rental Period;

    - (c) in the event of a Subscriber failing to reach the Committed Rental Level chosen by it, in the Rental Period chosen by it, due to a price reduction by Bord Telecom Eireann plc then the Subscriber shall be entitled to negotiate a revised Committed Rental Level with Bord Telecom Eireann plc and shall be entitled to the appropriate discount based on the revised Committed Rental Level so negotiated without penalty for the remainder of the unexpired term of the Rental Period chosen by it;

    - (d) in the event that the Subscriber ceases to avail of the Facility prior to the expiry of the Rental Period chosen by it, the Subscriber shall be liable to pay to Bord Telecom Eireann plc a sum equivalent to 5% of the corresponding Minimum Rental Level calculated in accordance with Part 3, Section III of the Second Schedule hereto for the unexpired term of the Rental Period chosen by it;

    - (e) the Subscriber may increase but not decrease the Committed Rental Level chosen by it annually on the anniversary of the initial implementation of the Facility on the Subscriber's account. In the event of the Committed Rental Level being so increased, the Subscriber shall be entitled to the appropriate increased discount for the unexpired term of the Rental Period chosen by it;

    - (f) in the event of the Annual Rental Charges actually paid by the Subscriber in a Rental Period chosen by the Subscriber exceeding the upper threshold of the Committed Rental Level chosen by the Subscriber the discount shall only be calculated on the Committed Rental Level chosen by the Subscriber.
