> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 15 of 1988.

EXCHANGE CONTROL REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), as continued by the [Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1986), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Exchange Control Regulations, 1988.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 4th day of February 1988.

- (3) The Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 to 1987, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 to 1988.

###### 2.

2. Anglo Irish Bank Corporation, public limited company, 2 Lower Merrion Street, Dublin 2, is hereby appointed to be an authorised dealer.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 28th day of January 1988.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the lists of authorised dealers for the purposes of the Exchange Control Acts 1954-1986 by appointing the Anglo Irish Bank Corporation, public limited company of 2 Lower Merrion Street, Dublin 2, to be an authorised dealer.
