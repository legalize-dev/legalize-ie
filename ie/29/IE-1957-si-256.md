# S.I. No. 256/1957 - State Guarantees Act, 1954 (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1957

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 256 of 1957.

STATE GUARANTEES ACT, 1954 (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE) ORDER, 1957.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1957.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), is hereby amended by adding " Dundalk Engineering Works Limited " to the bodies specified in the second column and inserting in the third column " £500,000 " in respect of that body.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 10th day of December, 1957.

**ÉAMON DE VALERA,**

Taoiseach.

*Explanatory Note.*

The Order enables the Minister for Finance to guarantee borrowing by the Dundalk Engineering Works Limited—up to a maximum of £500,000—under the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) .
