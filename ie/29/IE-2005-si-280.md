**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 280 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0280.html)**

**Tallaght Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1980 (Amendment) Order, 2005**

TALLAGHT HOSPITAL BOARD (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1980 (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 2005.

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 3, 4, 5 and 6 of the [Health (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) 27 of 1961), as amended by [Section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0032/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Health (Amendment) (No. 3) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) ( [No. 32 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) ) and adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ) hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Tallaght Hospital Board (Establishment) Order 1980 (Amendment) Order, 2005.

###### 2.

2. In this Order:-

“the Establishment Order” means the Tallaght Hospital Board (Establishment Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No 38 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0038.html) );

“the Order of 1982” means the Tallaght Hospital Board (Establishment Order, 1980 (Amendment) Order 1982 ( [S.I. No 313 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0313.html) );

“the Order of 1984” means Tallaght Hospital Board (Establishment) Order 1980 (Amendment) Order 1984 ( [S.I. No 162 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0162.html) );

###### 3.

3. The Establishment Order is hereby amended:

- (a) by the deletion in Article 4 of paragraph (b) and paragraph (c)

- (b) by the substitution for Article 5 (as amended by Article 2 (a) of the Order of 1982 and as amended by Article 2 (a) of the Order of 1984) of the following Article:-

  > “The Board shall consist of eight members who shall be appointed by the Minister.”

- (c) by the substitution for Article 6 (as amended by article 2 (b) of the Order of 1984), of the following Article:-

  > “The term of office of a member of the Board shall unless he or she sooner dies, resigns or ceases to be a member under Article 9 of this Order, terminate on the 31^st December 2005.”

- (d) by the substitution for Article 7 (as amended by article 2 (c) of the Order of 1984), of the following Article:-

  > “7. In the event of a casual vacancy arising through the death, resignation or cessation of membership under article 9 of this Order, of a member of the Board the vacancy shall be filled by appointment by the Minister for the unexpired period of the term of office of the said member.”

- (e) by the substitution for Article 9 (as amended by article 2 (d) of the Order of 1984), of the following Article:-

  > “A member shall cease to be a member of the Board on his being requested by the Minister to resign.”

- (f) by the substitution for Article 10 of the following Article :-

  > “The Minister shall appoint one of the members of the Board to be Chairman of the Board”

- (g) by the substitution for Article 11 (as amended by Article 2 (b) of the Order of 1982 and as amended by Article 2 (e) of the Order of 1984) of the following Article:-

  > “The quorum of the Board shall be four.”

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

The effect of this Order is to restructure the Tallaght Hospital Board providing for a Board of eight members appointed directly by the Minister, one of whom shall be appointed to be the Chairman of the Board.
