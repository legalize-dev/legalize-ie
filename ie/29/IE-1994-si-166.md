# S.I. No. 166/1994 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 2) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 166 of 1994.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) (NO.2) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of its powers under the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of the Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 2) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1994.

###### 3.

3. Rates of interest on deposits within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , are hereby prescribed as follows:

( *a* ) where any such deposit is less than £1,000, the rate of interest shall be 0.50 per cent per annum;

( *b* ) where any such deposit is £1,000 or more and less than £3,000, the rate of interest shall be 0.75 per cent per annum;

( *c* ) where any such deposit is £3,000 or more and less than £5,000, the rate of interest shall be 1.25 per cent per annum; and

( *d* ) where any such deposit is £5,000 or more, the rate of interest shall be 2.00 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 6 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0006.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency

this 31st day of May, 1994.

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**

Chief Executive.
