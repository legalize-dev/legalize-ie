# S.I. No. 765/2005 - Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees and Allowances) Regulations 1987 (Revocation) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 765 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0765.html)**

**Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees and Allowances) Regulations 1987 (Revocation) Regulations 2005**

The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6 and 9 of the [Vital Statistics and Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , section 5 (3) of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) and by [section 303](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0303.html#sec303) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the Minister for Social and Family Affairs and the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees and Allowances) Regulations 1987 (Revocation) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees and Allowances Regulations 1987 ( [S.I. No. 278 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0278.html) ) are revoked with effect from 5^th December 2005.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

These Regulations provide for the revocation of the existing regulations in relation to fees for the issue of certificates of registration of births, stillbirths deaths and marriages.
