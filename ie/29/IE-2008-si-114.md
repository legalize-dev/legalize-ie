# S.I. No. 114/2008 - National Development Finance Agency Act 2002 (Prescription of Public Authority) (Grangegorman Development Agency) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 114 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0114.html)

NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCE AGENCY ACT 2002 (PRESCRIPTION OF PUBLIC AUTHORITY) (GRANGEGORMAN DEVELOPMENT AGENCY) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th April*, 2008.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 22(1) (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec9) of the [National Development Finance Agency (Amendment) Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2007)) of the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002), hereby make the following order, with respect to which, pursuant to section 22(2) (as so inserted) of the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and no resolution disapproving of the draft has been passed by either such House within the next subsequent 21 days on which each House sat after the draft was laid before it:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (Prescription of Public Authority) (Grangegorman Development Agency) Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. The Grangegorman Development Agency is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 17 of the Schedule (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec11) of the [National Development Finance Agency (Amendment) Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2007)) to the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 April 2008

**BRIAN COWEN**

Minister for Finance.
