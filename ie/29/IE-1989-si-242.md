# S.I. No. 242/1989 - Local Government Officers (Widows and Orphans Ex-Gratia Pension) (Amendment) Scheme, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 242 of 1989.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT OFFICERS (WIDOWS AND ORPHANS EX-GRATIA PENSION) (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1989.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0002.html#sec2) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Scheme:

1 Short Title.

###### 1.

**1.** This Scheme may be cited as the Local Government Officers (Widows and Orphans Ex-Gratia Pension) (Amendment) Scheme, 1989.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of October, 1969.

Amendments of article 6.

The Local Government Officers (Widows and Orphans Ex-Gratia Pension) Scheme, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 320 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0320.html) ), as amended by the Local Government Officers (Widows and Orphans Ex-Gratia Pension) (Amendment) Scheme, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 313 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0313.html) ) is hereby amended by the insertion of the following sub-article after article 6 (5):

> - "(6) Notwithstanding the provisions of sub-article (5), where the deceased's pension was reduced by virtue of articles 12(1) (b) and 15 of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 316 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0316.html) ) or sections 23(1) (b) and 26 of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1948), the Minister in his absolute discretion may, on application from the relevant local authority, direct that the widow's pension shall be calculated by reference to the deceased's pension as if it had not been so reduced.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 4th day of October, 1989.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the Local Government Officers (Widows and Orphans Ex-Gratia Pension) (Amendment) Scheme, 1989.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 4th day of October, 1989.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Scheme amends the superannuation provisions contained in the Local Government Officers (Widows and orphans Ex-Gratia Pension) Scheme, 1984 to give effect to changes in the benefits payable to the widows of certain male officers of local authorities.
