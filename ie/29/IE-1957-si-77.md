# S.I. No. 77/1957 - Emergency Imposition of Duties (No. 397) (Unprinted Paper Duty) Order, 1957

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 77 of 1957.

EMERGENCY IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 397) (UNPRINTED PAPER DUTY) ORDER, 1957.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0016/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Emergency Imposition of Duties Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1932), as adapted in consequence of the enactment of the Constitution, hereby order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Emergency Imposition of Duties (No. 397) (Unprinted Paper Duty) Order, 1957.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. As respects articles imported on or after the 17th day of April, 1957—

- (*a*) [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0031/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Finance Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1936), shall have effect as if the matter contained at reference number 21 in the Fourth Schedule were deleted, and

- (*b*) [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0018/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Finance Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1939), shall have effect as if the matter contained at reference number 5 in the Third Schedule were deleted.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 16th day of April, 1957.

**EAMON DE VALERA,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order removes the exemption in respect of paper for newspapers, periodicals and paper-covered novels from the Customs duty of 5 per cent. *ad valorem* on unprinted paper.
