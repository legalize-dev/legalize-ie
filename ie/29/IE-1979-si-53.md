S.I. No. 53 of 1979.

DUBLIN APPOINTED STANDS (STREET SERVICE VEHICLES) BYE-LAWS, 1979.

I, PATRICK McLAUGHLIN, Commissioner of the Garda Siochána, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 84](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0084.html#sec84) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961)), and after consultation with the Right Honourable the Lord Mayor, Aldermen and Burgesses of Dublin, the council of the county of Dublin and the corporation of Dun Laoghaire hereby make the following bye-laws in respect of the area comprising the county borough of Dublin, the county of Dublin and the borough of Dun Laoghaire.

1 Short title.

###### 1.

**1.** These bye-laws may be cited as the Dublin Appointed Stands (Street Service Vehicles) Bye-Laws, 1979.

2 Interpretation.

###### 2.

**2.** In these bye-laws—

"building line" means in relation to a side of a street on which there is no straight line of buildings, the edge of that side of the street;

"street" means a public road;

reference to the head of an appointed stand shall be construed as referring to that end of the appointed stand from which the limits of the appointed stand are reckoned in column (3) of the appropriate Schedule to these bye-laws;

reference to the rear of an appointed stand shall be construed as referring to that end of the appointed stand to which the limits of the appointed stand are reckoned in column (3) of the appropriate Schedule to these bye-laws;

reference to the order of the vehicles on an appointed stand shall be construed as referring to the order of the vehicles reckoned from the head of the appointed stand towards the rear of the appointed stand.

3 Appointment of appointed stands.

###### 3.

**3.** (1) In each street named in column (2) of the First Schedule to these bye-laws, each part of the street described in column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention in column (2) of the street shall be an appointed stand available, subject to these bye-laws, for use without limitation as to time or occasion of user.

- (2) In each street named in column (2) of the Second Schedule to these bye-laws, each part of the street described in column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention in column (2) of the street shall be an appointed stand available, subject to these bye-laws, for use on the occasions only and at the times only specified in column (6) of that Schedule opposite the mention in column (2) of the street.

4 Main and subsidiary appointed stands.

###### 4.

**4.** For the purpose of these bye-laws—

( *a* ) each of the appointed stands mentioned in column (2) of the Third Schedule to these bye-laws shall be known as a main appointed stand;

( *b* ) each of the appointed stands mentioned in column (3) of the said Third Schedule shall be known as a subsidiary appointed stand.

5 Position of vehicles on appointed stands.

###### 5.

**5.** (1) For the purpose of these bye-laws, a street service vehicle shall be deemed not to be standing for hire on an appointed stand unless the whole of the vehicle is within the limits of the appointed stand described in column (3) of the First or Second Schedule to these bye-laws.

- (2) Every street service vehicle standing for hire on an appointed stand shall be placed—

( *a* ) in the position specified in column (5) of the First or Second Schedule to these bye-laws opposite the mention of the appointed stand in column (3) of that Schedule and

( *b* ) in case there is no street service vehicle already on the appointed stand, at the head of the appointed stand and, in any other case, next in order to the street service vehicle already on the appointed stand and as near as practicable to the street service vehicle immediately in front,

and a street service vehicle shall be deemed for the purpose of these bye-laws not to be standing for hire on the appointed stand unless it complies with this paragraph.

- (3) Where the First or Second Schedule to these bye-laws requires a street service vehicle standing for hire on an appointed stand to be placed in single line by the kerb and parallel to the kerb or with the rear of the vehicle to the kerb, a vehicle shall not be placed more than eighteen inches from the kerb.

6 Maximum number of street service vehicles at an appointed stand.

###### 6.

**6.** Whenever the number of street service vehicles standing for hire on an appointed stand is equal to the number mentioned in Column (4) of the First or Second Schedule to these bye-laws opposite the mention of the appointed stand in Column (3) of that Schedule, the driver of a street service vehicle not already so standing for hire shall not place that street service vehicle on that appointed stand.

7 Vacancies at appointed stands.

###### 7.

**7.** (1) Whenever a vacancy occurs at an appointed stand by reason of the departure from the stand of a street service vehicle, the street service vehicle nearest to the space so vacated shall be moved forward by its driver into that space and each driver of any other street service vehicle then standing on that appointed stand shall in turn move forward his vehicle into the space which is nearest to him and in the direction of the head of the appointed stand.

- (2) Whenever a vacancy occurs on a main appointed stand by reason of the departure from the stand of a street service vehicle, the driver of the street service vehicle at the head of the subsidiary appointed stand first referred to in Column (3) of the Third Schedule to these bye-laws opposite the mention in Column (2) of that Schedule of the main appointed stand (or, where two subsidiary appointed stands are so referred to, the driver of the street service vehicle at the head of the first subsidiary appointed stand so referred to), shall place that street service vehicle on the main appointed stand in the space created by the vacancy.

- (3) Whenever a vacancy occurs on a subsidiary appointed stand by reason of the departure from the stand of a street service vehicle, the driver of the street service vehicle at the head of a subsidiary appointed stand referred to secondly in Column (3) of the Third Schedule to these bye-laws opposite the mention in Column (2) of that Schedule of the main appointed stand shall place that street service vehicle on the subsidiary appointed stand first referred to in Column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention in Column (2) of that Schedule of the main appointed stand.

8 General provisions relating to appointed stands.

###### 8.

**8.** The following provisions shall apply to street service vehicles standing for hire on an appointed stand—

( *a* ) a street service vehicle shall not be so placed on an appointed stand that it rests wholly or partly on a footway, or that portion of the vehicle projects over the footway so as to interfere with the free movement of pedestrians on the footway;

( *b* ) a person shall not wash, overhaul or execute repairs to such a street service vehicle, except such repairs as may be necessary to enable the vehicle to be removed from the appointed stand;

( *c* ) neither the driver of such a street service vehicle nor any other person shall make any unnecessary noise by means of or in relation to the vehicle or the engine or any equipment, fittings or instruments fitted to or carried on the vehicle or any loudspeaker in or on or in any way connected with the vehicle;

( *d* ) the driver of such a street service vehicle shall comply with all directions given go him by a member of the Garda Siochána in relation to the placing of the street service vehicle on the appointed stand or the bringing of the street service vehicle into or the removal of the street service vehicle from the appointed stand.

9 General restrictions on street service vehicles standing for hire.

###### 9.

**9.** A driver of a street service vehicle shall not stand the street service vehicle for hire otherwise than on an appointed stand and in accordance with these bye-laws.

10 Revocation.

###### 10.

**10.** The Dublin Appointed Stands (Street Service Vehicles) Bye-Laws, 1963 ( [S.I. No. 50 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0050.html) ), are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These bye-laws:

1. Provide stands for street service vehicles as indicated in the Schedules to these bye-laws, and

2. Prohibit the driver of a street service vehicle from standing the vehicle for hire elsewhere than on an appointed stand.
