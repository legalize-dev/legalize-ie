# S.I. No. 817/2005 - Ringaskiddy Free Port (Control and Management) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 817 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0817.html)**

**Ringaskiddy Free Port (Control and Management) Order 2005**

I, Pat the Cope Gallagher, Minister of State at the Department for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Free Ports Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1986) and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ) (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )), and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions (No. 2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 703 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0703.html) ), with the consent of the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Ringaskiddy Free Port (Control and Management Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. Port of Cork Company, a company incorporated on 28 February 1997, is designated for the purposes of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Free Ports Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1986) in relation to the free port established by the Ringaskiddy Free Port (Establishment) Order 1988 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0113.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Ringaskiddy Free Port (Control and Management) Order 1989 ( [S.I. No. 22 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0022.html) ) is revoked.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order provides for the designation of the Port of Cork Company to control and manage the Ringaskiddy Free Port.
