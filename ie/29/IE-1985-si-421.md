# S.I. No. 421/1985 - The Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 421 of 1985.

THE SOLICITORS ACTS 1954 AND 1960 (APPRENTICES' FEES) REGULATIONS, 1985.

The Incorporated Law Society of Ireland in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0005.html#sec5) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Solicitors Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) and with the concurrence of the President of the High Court hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. On and after the date on which these Regulations shall come into operation, the fees specified in the Schedule hereto shall be paid to the Incorporated Law Society of Ireland by the apprentice or applicant in respect of the courses and examinations laid down in the Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations 1975 ( [S.I. No. 66 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0066.html) ) hereinafter referred to as "the New Regulations".

###### 2.

2. These Regulations may be cited as the Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations 1985; they shall be read with the Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (apprentices' Fees) Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 203 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0203.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Solicitors Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations 1984 ( [S.I. No. 48 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0048.html) ) shall be revoked as from the date of the operation of these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1985.

SCHEDULE

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. | On each application to attend the course of lectures and associated studies in the subjects specified in appendix I of the New Regulations known as the Professional Course | £1,820.00 |
| II. | On each application to attend the course of lectures and associated studies in the subjects specified in appendix II of the New Regulations known as the Advanced Course | £645.00 |
| III. | On each application (whether for the first or any sub sequent time) to attend the Final Examination — First Part ... ... ... ... ... ... ... | £60.00 |
| IV. | For each day or part of a day re-attending the Professional Course or the Advanced Course (including retaking any individual part of the Final Examination — Second Partor the Final Examination — Third Part) ... ... | £15.00 |

Dated this 28th day of January 1985.

Signed on behalf of the Incorporated Law Society of Ireland.

ANTHONY E. COLLINS,

President of the Incorporated Law Society of Ireland.

In pursuance of the provisions of [Section 82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Solicitors Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) as amended by [Section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0037/sec0025.html#sec25) (I) of the [Solicitors (Amendment) Act 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) , I concur in the making of the above Regulations.

Signed

LIAM HAMILTON,

President of the High Court.
