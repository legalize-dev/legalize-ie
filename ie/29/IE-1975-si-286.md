# S.I. No. 286/1975 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 52, Twelfth Period) (Miscellaneous Textile Piece Goods) Order, 1975

S.I. No. 286 of 1975.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 52, TWELFTH PERIOD) (MISCELLANEOUS TEXTILE PIECE GOODS) ORDER, 1975.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 52, Twelfth Period) (Miscellaneous Textile Piece Goods) Order, 1975.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule, and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints a nominal quota of 1,000 square yards as the quantity of textile piece goods (other than woven woollen) originating in the countries listed below, which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1976 to 31st December, 1976. This nominal Quota has been appointed instead of the usual quota of 12,000,000 square yards to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister for Industry and Commerce will license during the quota period not less than 12,000,000 square yards as hitherto.
