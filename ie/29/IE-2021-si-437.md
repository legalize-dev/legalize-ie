# S.I. No. 437/2021 - Valuation Act 2001 (Application to Commissioner) (Amendment of Fees) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th August,* 2021.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in accordance with the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) (2) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No.13 of 2001), and the Valuation Office and Valuation Tribunal (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 575 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0575.html) ), as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No 408 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0408.html) ), hereby make the following amendment to the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Application to Commissioner) (Fees) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 303 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0303.html) );

###### 1.

1. This Regulation may be cited as the Valuation Act 2001 (Application to Commissioner) (Amendment of Fees) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 September, 2021.

###### 3.

3. The [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Application to Commissioner) (Fees) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 303 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0303.html) ) is amended by deleting Regulation 4(a).

###### 4.

4. The Schedule to the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Application to Commissioner) (Fees) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 303 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0303.html) ) is amended by deleting for the matter set out at reference No. 1 the following:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | Section 27(2) | €250 |

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

25 August, 2021.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.
