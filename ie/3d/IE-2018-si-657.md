# S.I. No. 657/2018 - Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in “Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th February,* 2019*. A supplementary notice was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*^st March*, 2019.

I, Charles Flanagan, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec18) , [122](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec122) (1)(m) (as amended by [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html#sec56) (a) of the [Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html) (No. 49 of 2015)) and [128](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec128) (2) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )) and [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html#sec59) (2) of the [Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html) , after consulting with the Garda Commissioner and the Policing Authority and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. The Garda Síochána (Associations) Regulations 1978 to 2011, the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1987 ( [S.I. No. 120 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0120.html) ), the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1990 ( [S.I. No. 111 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0111.html) ), the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 342 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0342.html) ), the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0366.html) ), the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 222 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0222.html) ), the Garda Síochána (Associations (Amendment) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 451 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0451.html) ), the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0706.html) ) and these Regulations may be cited together as the Garda Síochána (Associations) Regulations 1978 to 2018.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 19 of the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 63 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0063.html) ) is amended by deleting paragraph (1).

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

10 December 2018

**LEO VARADKAR**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

**CHARLES FLANAGAN**
