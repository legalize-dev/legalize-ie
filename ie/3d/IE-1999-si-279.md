# S.I. No. 279/1999 - National Social Service Board (Membership) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (7) of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [National Social Service Board Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1984), hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Social Service Board (Membership) Regulations, 1998.

Number of members.

###### 2.

2. The maximum number of members of the Board, in addition to the chairman and vice-chairman, specified in [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [National Social Service Board Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (as varied by the National Social Service Board (Membership) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 221 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0221.html) ) is hereby varied by the substitution of “eighteen” for “fifteen”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for an increase in the number of members of the National Social Service Board, in addition to the chairman and vice-chairman, from fifteen to eighteen.
