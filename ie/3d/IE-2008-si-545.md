[S.I. No. 545 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0545.html)

OIREACHTAS (ALLOWANCES TO MEMBERS) AND MINISTERIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY OFFICES (AMENDMENT) ACT 1992 (ALLOWANCES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th December*, 2008.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN TD, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3(4) (as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998)) and 5A (inserted by section 20 of that Act) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1992), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations are deemed to have come into operation on 24 May 2007.
