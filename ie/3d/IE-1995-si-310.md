S.I. No. 310 of 1995.

COMPANIES (STOCK EXCHANGE) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, PAT RABBITTE, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) as amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (2) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990), and the Enterprise and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 42 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0042.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Stock Exchange) Regulations, 1995.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 9th day of December 1995.

###### 2.

2. In these regulations "the Irish Stock Exchange" has the meaning given to it by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Stock Exchange Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1995).

###### 3.

3. The Irish Stock Exchange is hereby prescribed as a recognised stock exchange for the purposes of the following provisions of the Companies Acts—

- (*a*) sections 44, 45, 361 and 362 of, and paragraph 28 of the Sixth Schedule to, the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

- (*b*) [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1977).

- (*c*) sections 65, 66, 78, 90 to 96, 107, 112, 115, 116, 117, 118, 120, 212, 229 and 230 of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990).

###### 4.

4. The Companies (Stock Exchange) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 337 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0337.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of November, 1995.

**PAT RABBITTE,**

Minister of State at the Department of

Enterprise and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the Irish Stock Exchange for the purposes of the provisions listed in the Regulations and revoke the Companies (Stock Exchange) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 337 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0337.html) ).
