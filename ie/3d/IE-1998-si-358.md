# S.I. No. 358/1998 - Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 358 of 1998.

ROAD TRAFFIC (REMOVAL, STORAGE AND DISPOSAL OF VEHICLES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 97 of the said Act, as substituted by [section 63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations and the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations 1983 may be collectively cited as the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations, 1983 to 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991 are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. Sub-article (1) of article 7 of the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0091.html) ) is hereby amended -

- (a) by the substitution of "£100" for " 7 pound;75" in paragraph (a) and

- (b) by the substitution of "£30" for "£25" in paragraph (b).

###### 4.

4. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 29th day of September, 1998.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment and Local Government this 25th day of September, 1998.

Noel Dempsey

Minister for the Environment and Local Government

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations, 1983 by increasing from£75 to£100 the charge for the release of a vehicle payable to a road authority or the Garda Commissioner by the owner of a vehicle which has been towed away in accordance with the Regulations and by increasing from£25 to£30 per day the storage charge payable for, the period during which such vehicles have been stored.
