# S.I. No. 234/1972 - Exchange Control (No. 2) Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 234 of 1972.

EXCHANGE CONTROL (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1972.

I, SEOIRSE O COLLA, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), as continued in force by the [Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1970), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Exchange Control (No. 2) Regulations, 1972.

- (2) The collective citation " the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 to 1972 " shall be deemed to include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The Algemene Bank Nederland (Ireland), Ltd., of 35-38 St. Stephen's Green, Dublin, 2, is hereby appointed to be an authorised dealer.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of September, 1972.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minster for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959, by appointing the Algemene Bank Nederland (Ireland), Ltd., of 35-38 St. Stephen's Green, Dublin 2, to be an authorised dealer for the purposes of the Exchange Control Acts.
