# S.I. No. 172/2000 - Vocational Education (Grants For Annual Schemes of Committees) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHAEL WOODS, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1930), hereby, with the approval of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Vocational Education (Grants for Annual Schemes of Committees) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:—

“the Act” means [the Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1930);

“the Minister” means the Minister for Education and Science;

“committee” means a vocational education committee;

“the Dublin City Vocational Education Area” means the vocational education area which is the county borough of Dublin;

“the Dun Laoghaire Vocational Education Area” means the vocational education area which up to 31st December, 1993 was the borough of Dun Laoghaire.

###### 3.

3. [The Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. Where in the financial year commencing on the 1st day of January, 1999, the annual local contribution paid by a rating authority to a committee exceeds the minimum annual liablilty of such rating authority in respect of such committee by any sum (in these Regulations referred to as “the contribution excess”), the Minister may, in that financial year, make to such committee under section 53 of the Act a grant (to be called and known as an additional grant) of—

- (*a*) if such committee is the committee for a borough Vocational Education Area (other than the Dublin City Vocational Educational Area), an amount equal to four times the contributions excess, or

- (*b*) if such committee is the committee for the Dublin City Vocational Education Area, or for the Dun Laoghaire Vocational Education Area, an amount equal to twice the contribution excess, or

- (*c*) if such committee is the committee for a county vocational education area, an amount equal to the contribution excess.

###### 6.

6. For the purpose of any calculations made in accordance with these Regulations—

- (*a*) special statutory provisions for the different assessment of particular kinds of property or particular parts of the area of a rating authority shall be disregarded;

- (*b*) any fraction of a pound equal to or less than the sum of 50 pence may be disregarded and every other such fraction may be treated as a pound; and

- (*c*) “the minimum annual liability” has the meaning assigned to it by section 43 (2) of the Act, as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0033/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Vocational Education (Amendment) Act, 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1950).

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations govern the payment of additional and supplemental and special grants to Vocational Education Committees in the financial year commencing on the 1st January 1999.
