# S.I. No. 312/1985 - Trustee Savings Banks (No. 3) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 312 of 1985.

TRUSTEE SAVINGS BANKS (NO. 3) REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0033/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Finance Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trustee Savings Banks (No. 3) Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 21st day of October, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest allowed on moneys standing to the credit of every separate investment account of a trustee savings bank in the name of the Minister for Finance in the Post Office Savings Bank shall, in lieu of the rate specified in the Trustee Savings Banks (No. 2) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0251.html) ), be 10.65 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Trustee Savings Banks (No. 2) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0251.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of September, 1985.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations decrease to 10.65 per cent per annum as from 21st October, 1985 the rate of interest payable by the Minister for Finance on moneys deposited with him by the trustee savings banks in every separate special investment account.
