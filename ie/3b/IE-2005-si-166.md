> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 166 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0166.html) .**

OIREACHTAS (MINISTERIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY OFFICES) (SECRETARIAL FACILITIES) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS 2005.

[S.I. No. 166 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0166.html) .

OIREACHTAS (MINISTERIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY OFFICES) (SECRETARIAL FACILITIES) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS 2005.

The Houses of the Oireachtas Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 10(4) (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec1) of the [Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) ( [No. 30 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) ) and transferred by section 4(3)(*a*) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 28 of 2003)) of the [Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices Act 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) and section 2(1)(*c* (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ( [No. 39 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ) and transferred by section 4(3)(*b*) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 28 of 2003)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 32 of 1962), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) (No. 2) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding Regulation 5 of the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) (No. 6) Regulations 2004, those Regulations shall continue in force until 30 June 2005.
