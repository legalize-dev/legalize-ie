# S.I. No. 307/1995 - Social Welfare (School Meals (National Schools)) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 307 of 1995.

SOCIAL WELFARE (SCHOOL MEALS (NATIONAL SCHOOLS)) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Social Welfare in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/sec0039.html#sec39) of the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (School Meals (National Schools)) Regulations, 1995.

2 School meals — national schools.

###### 2.

**2.** Chapter 1 of Part IV of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. of 1981) shall continue to have effect in the borough area as defined in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 13th

day of November, 1995.

**PROINSIAS DE ROSSA,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the continuation of the Urban School Meals Scheme in the Dun Laoghaire Borough area, which now forms part of the Dun Laoghaire Rathdown County Council.
