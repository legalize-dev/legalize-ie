# S.I. No. 534/2001 - Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and by section 82 of that Act as amended by [section 57](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0057.html#sec57) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) which powers were delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 427 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0427.html) ), I, Robert Molloy, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) The collective citation the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) Regulations, 1963 to 2001 shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. Articles 5(a), 6 and 7 of these regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2002.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations -

“the Principal Regulations” means the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) Regulations, 1963 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0191.html) );

“the Regulations of 1977” means the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 111 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0111.html) );

“the Regulations of 1995” means the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 136 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0136.html) );

“the Regulations of 2000” means the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 367 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0367.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Regulations of 2000 are hereby amended —

- (a) by the replacement in article 15(d) of the words “1 January, 2002” with the words “1 September, 2002”, and

- (b) by the substitution of the following sub-article for article 15(e) -

  > - “(e) be in good order, repair and condition and subject to the terms of the [Metrology Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1996) have affixed the mark(s) signifying conformity with that Act.”.

###### 5.

5. The Regulations of 1995 are hereby amended —

- (a) by the substitution in article 32(6)(a) of “€60” for “ £50”, and

- (b) by the replacement in article 33(6), as inserted by article 19 of the Regulations of 2000, of the words “1 January 2002” by the words “1 September 2002”.

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended —

- (a) by the substitution for sub-articles (1) and (2) of article 41, as amended by article 13 of the Regulations of 1977, of the following sub-articles:-

  > - “(1) A fee of €12 shall be payable in respect of the first application by a person for a licence to drive small public service vehicles.
  >
  > - (2) A fee of €3 shall be payable in respect of the grant of a licence to drive small public service vehicles.”, and

- (b) by the substitution in article 41(4), as amended by article 14 of the Regulations of 1977, of “€2.50” for “ £2”.

###### 7.

7. The Fourth Schedule to the Regulations of 1995, as substituted by the Regulations of 2000 and amended by article 4 of the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 38 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0038.html) ), is substituted by the following -

**FOURTH SCHEDULE**

**FEES**

1. A fee of €125 shall be payable in respect of —

- (a) the grant of a wheelchair accessible taxi licence,

- (b) the renewal of a wheelchair accessible taxi licence,

- (c) the renewal of a hackney licence,

- (d) the renewal of a limousine licence,

- (e) the continuation in force of a taxi licence or a wheelchair accessible taxi licence in respect of another person,

- (f) the renewal of a taxi licence.

2. A fee of €250 shall be payable in respect of —

- (a) the grant of a hackney licence,

- (b) the grant of a limousine licence.

3. A fee of €6,300 shall be payable in respect of the grant of a taxi licence.”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).**

These regulations provide for amendments to existing regulatory provisions in relation to taximeters and for the introduction of Euro licence fees from 1 January 2002.
