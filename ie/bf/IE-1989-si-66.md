# S.I. No. 66/1989 - European Communities (Fresh Meat) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 66 of 1989.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (FRESH MEAT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1989.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purposes of giving effect to Council Directive 88/288/EEC of 3 May, 1988^(1) hereby make the following Regulations:

^(1)O.J. No. L124, 18.5. 1988, p. 28.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Fresh Meat) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Fresh Meat) Regulations, 1987 and 1989, and shall be construed together as one.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 4th day of April, 1989.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the European Communities (Fresh Meat) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 284 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0284.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The definition of "the Council Directive" in Regulation 2 (1) of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the substitution for "and Council Directive 86/587 of 18 November, 1986^(5)" of "Council Directive 86/587 of 18 November, 1986^(5) and Council Directive 88/288/EEC of 3 May, 1988".

###### 4.

4. Paragraph (i) (inserted by Regulation 4 (4) ( *a* ) (ii) of the Principal Regulations of subsection (3) of [Section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1930) is hereby amended by the substitution for "and Council Directive 86/587 of 18 November, 1986" of "Council Directive 86/587 of 18 November, 1986, and Council Directive 88/288/EEC of 3 May, 1988".

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 4th day of April, 1989.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and

Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Fresh Meat) Regulations, 1987 so as to bring into force amendments made to Council Directive 64/433/EEC of 26 June, 1964 through Council Directive 88/288/EEC of 3 May, 1988, which provides for rules for intra-Community trade in sliced bovine livers and additional requirements in relation to the storage of frozen meat.
