# S.I. No. 156/1973 - Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices For Milk) (No. 2) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 156 of 1973.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (MINIMUM PRICES FOR MILK) (No. 2) ORDER, 1973.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) (No. 2) Order, 1973, and shall come into operation on the 18th day of June, 1973.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"registered producers" means persons registered in the register of producers kept by the Cork District Milk Board;

"registered retailers" means persons registered in the register of retailers kept by the Cork District Milk Board;

"registered wholesalers" means persons registered in the register of wholesalers kept by the Cork District Milk Board.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 86 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0086.html) ), is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. The minimum prices to be paid to registered producers for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and retailers shall be at the rates shown in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE.

Minimum Prices.

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Sold in May, June or July | Sold in April, August or September | Sold in January, February, March, October, November or December |
| Price per gallon (whether supplied at the registered premises of the producer, or at a collection place agreed on with the purchaser, or otherwise supplied) ... ... ... | 22p | 25p | 26p |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of June, 1973.

**MARK CLINTON,**

Minister for Agriculture and

Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the minimum prices to be paid to registered producers, as from 18th June 1973, for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and registered retailers in the Cork District.
