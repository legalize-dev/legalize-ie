# S.I. No. 611/2023 - Garda SÍochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th December,* 2023.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (d) of [section 122](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec122) (1) (as amended by [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html#sec56) (a) of the [Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html) (No. 49 of 2015)) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )) and [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html#sec59) (2) of the [Garda Síochána (Policing Authority and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0049/print.html) (as so adapted), after consulting with the Commissioner of the Garda Síochána and the Policing Authority and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 470 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0470.html) ) are amended in paragraph (a) of Regulation 4 by the substitution of “50” for “35”.

The Government hereby approve of the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Taoiseach,

5 December, 2023.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 December, 2023.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice.
