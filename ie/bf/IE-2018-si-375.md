# S.I. No. 375/2018 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 6) (Prescribed Time) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th September*, 2018.

I, REGINA DOHERTY, Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 4 (adapted by the Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0366.html) )) and 241 (amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec8) of the [Social Welfare Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2017)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 6) (Prescribed Time) Regulations 2018.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2018 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2018.

*Prescribed time*

###### 2.

2. Article 182(*d*) of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) is amended by the substitution of “6 weeks” for “7 days”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 September 2018.

**REGINA DOHERTY,**

Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 241](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec241) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) sets out the time and manner in which claims for various social welfare payments are to be made. [Section 241](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec241) (2) provides that where a person fails to make a claim for a social welfare payment within a prescribed time, then he or she will be disqualified for receiving payment for a period before the claim was made. Different periods are specified depending on the nature of the particular social welfare payment scheme involved.

Reflecting changes in the administration of the scheme and employer practices, these Regulations provide for the extension of the prescribed time for claiming Illness Benefit from 7 days to 6 weeks.
