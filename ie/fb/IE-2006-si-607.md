**FISHERY HARBOUR CENTRES (FIXED PAYMENT NOTICE) BYE-LAWS 2006**

**FISHERY HARBOUR CENTRES (FIXED PAYMENT NOTICE) BYE-LAWS 2006**

I, John Browne, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4(10) (inserted by [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec54) of the [Maritime Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) (No. 11 of 2005)) of the [Fishery Harbour Centres Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1968) and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2006 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0082.html) ), hereby make the following Bye-laws:

###### 1.

1. These Bye-laws may be cited as the Fishery Harbour Centres (Fixed Payment Notice) Bye-laws 2006.

###### 2.

2. In these Bye-laws “Act” means Fishery Harbour Centres Act 1968 (No. 18 of 1968).

###### 3.

3. The form specified in the Schedule is the form of the notice to be served on a person in relation to an alleged offence under section 4(6)(a) of the Act.

###### 4.

4. The amount specified to accompany a notice under section 4(10) of the Act is €150.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Bye-laws prescribe the form of Fixed Payment Notice and a Fixed Payment of €150 (“on-the-spot-fine”) in lieu of prosecution, if appropriate, in relation to an alleged offence involving contravention of any bye-law or order made under [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0018/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fishery Harbour Centres Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1968).
