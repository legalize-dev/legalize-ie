# S.I. No. 662/2006 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 662 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0662.html)**

**DERELICT SITES (URBAN AREAS) REGULATIONS, 2006.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4 and 21 of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations: —

Citation.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2006.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 1993 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2006.

Prescription of urban areas

###### 2.

2. The areas in the administrative counties of Clare, Dublin, Kerry, Kilkenny, and Leitrim indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

These regulations prescribe areas in the counties of Clare, Dublin, Kerry, Kilkenny and Leitrim to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
