# S.I. No. 623/2002 - Competition Act 2002 (Notification Fee) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec18) (8) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Competition Act 2002 (Notification Fee Regulations 2002.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2003.

###### 3.

3. A fee of €8000 is prescribed for the purposes of [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec18) (8) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002).

|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, |

|  | MARY HARNEY Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Regulations prescribe the amount of the fee to accompany notifications of mergers or acquisitions in accordance with [Section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec18) of the [Competition Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) .
