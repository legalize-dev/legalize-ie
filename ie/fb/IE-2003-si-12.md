# S.I. No. 12/2003 - Capital Gains Tax (Multipliers) (2003) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Revenue Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 556](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0556.html#sec556) (6) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Capital Gains Tax (Multipliers (2003) Regulations 2003.

###### 2.

2. In relation to the disposal of an asset made in the year of assessment 2003, the multiplier for the purposes of [section 556](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0556.html#sec556) (2) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997) shall be the figure mentioned in column (2) of the Table to this Regulation opposite the mention in column (1) of that Table of the year of assessment in which the deductible expenditure was incurred.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations specify the multipliers by reference to which sums (such as the base cost of an asset and enhancement expenditure incurred on it) which are allowable as a deduction from the consideration for the disposal of an asset in the year of assessment 2003 are to be increased under [section 556](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec0556.html#sec556) (2) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) for the purpose of computing the chargeable gain accruing to a person on such a disposal. The multipliers are derived from the consumer price index numbers compiled by the Central Statistics Office expressed on the basis that the consumer price index at mid-November, 1968, is 100.
