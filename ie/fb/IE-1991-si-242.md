# S.I. No. 242/1991 - Local Elections Act, 1965 (Harbour Authorities) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 242 of 1991.

LOCAL ELECTIONS ACT, 1965 (HARBOUR AUTHORITIES) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Tánaiste and Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0019/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Local Elections Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1965), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Local Elections Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Harbour Authorities) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The 18th day of September in an election year is hereby substituted for the 7th day of July referred to in subsection (3) of [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946) for the purpose of an order providing for the appointment by an assisting authority of one or more of the local authority members of a harbour authority.

###### 3.

3. The period beginning on the 7th day of July and ending on 24th day of September in an election year is hereby substituted for the period mentioned in subsection (1) and subsections (3) to (9) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of September, 1991.

**JOHN P. WILSON.**

Tánaiste and Minister for the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to substitute different dates for the dates mentioned in Sections 8 (3), 10 (1) and 10 (3) to 10 (9) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) .
