# S.I. No. 197/1984 - Worker Participation (Nítrigin Éireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, RUAIRI QUINN, Minister for Labour in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Nítrigin Eireann Teoranta) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The 13th day of November, 1984, is in relation to the election to be held in 1984 pursuant to the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), as regards Nítrigin Éireann Teoranta, hereby fixed as the nomination day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of July, 1984.

**RUAIRI QUINN**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations appoint 13th November, 1984 as the day on which the nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) in Nítrigin Éireann Teoranta shall be received by the returning officer.
