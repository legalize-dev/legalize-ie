# S.I. No. 193/1983 - Land Bond Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 193 of 1983.

LAND BOND ORDER, 1983.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0011/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Land Bond Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1934), [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0041/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Land Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1936), and [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0018/sec0004.html#sec4) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0018/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Land Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1953), hereby order as follows:—

1 Short title and citation

###### 1.

**1.** (1) This Order may be cited as the Land Bond Order, 1983.

- (2) The Land Bond Orders, 1934 to 1982, and this Order may be cited together as the Land Bond Orders, 1934 to 1983.

2 Interpretation

###### 2.

**2.** The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

3 Creation of series of land bonds

###### 3.

**3.** A series of land bonds is hereby created of the total amount of one and one half million pounds to be known as eighteen and one quarter per cent. land bonds.

4 Denominations of the land bonds

###### 4.

**4.** The series of land bonds created by this Order shall be issued in denominations of one pound and in no other denominations.

5 Consolidation

###### 5.

**5.** The series of land bonds created by this Order is hereby consolidated with the series of land bonds created by the Land Bond Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 18 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0018.html) ), to the intent that the two series shall form one consolidated series of land bonds created under the [Land Bond Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1934), amounting to the sum of eight million five hundred thousand pounds and Articles 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 of the said Land Bond Order, 1982, shall apply to the consolidated series accordingly.

6 Restriction in relation to certain amounts

###### 6.

**6.** The said consolidated series of land bonds shall be for the purpose of making advances and paying purchase moneys the amounts of which are agreed or fixed or deemed to be fixed in the period commencing on the 1st day of January, 1982, and ending on the 31st day of December, 1982, except in relation to advances and purchase moneys required to be made or paid by means of an issue of land bonds bearing the same rate of interest as land bonds previously issued.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 5th day of July, 1983.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The main purpose of this Order is to create a series of bonds bearing interest at 18¼% payable in respect of lands acquired by the Land Commission, where the purchase prices are agreed or fixed or deemed to be fixed between 1 January, 1982 and 31 December, 1982.

The Order also prescribes the redemption arrangements, specifying a sinking fund rate of ¼% and an annuity rate of 18½% to provide therefor.
