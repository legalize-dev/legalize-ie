# S.I. No. 465/2003 - Diseases of Animals (Protection of Animals During Transport) (Amendment) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0029.html#sec29) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Disease of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) ) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 91/628/EEC[^1] of 19 November 1991, as amended by Council Decision No. 92/438/EEC[^2] of 13 July 1992. Commission Decision No. 94/96/EC[^3] of 3 February 1994, Council Directive No. 95/29/EC[^4] of 29 June 1995, Council Directive No. 97/12/EC[^5] of 17 March 1997 in so far as it relates to the transport of bovine animals and swine, and Council Regulation 1255/97/EC[^6] of 25 June, 1997 as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No. 1040/2003[^7] of 11 June 2003 and Commission Decision 2003/483/EC[^8] of 30 June 2003, hereby order as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Protection of Animals During Transport) (Amendment) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals (Protection of Animals During Transport) Order 1995 ( [S.I. No. 98 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0098.html) ) as amended by the Diseases of Animals (Protection of Animals During Transport) (Amendment) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 362 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0362.html) ) and the Diseases of Animals (Protection of Animals During Transport) (Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 215 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0215.html) ) is amended —

- (a) in Article 10, by substituting —

    - (i) for paragraph (i) the following:

      > - “(i) submits to an inspector the completed route plan referred to in paragraph (h), not less than 24 hours prior to the proposed first time of departure of the animals indicated in that route plan, on which must be indicated the number or numbers of the health certificates if any and the authorisation number allocated to him or her in accordance with Article 12 and which shall then be stamped and signed by an inspector where the inspector is satisfied that such route plan complies with the provisions of this Order;”

    - (ii) for paragraph (j) (ii)(II) the following:

      > - “(II) return the completed Part B of the route plan to the Minister within 10 days of the date indicated on Part A of the route plan as the proposed date of arrival of the animals at their place of destination;”

    - (iii) for paragraph (o) the following:

      > - “(o) notifies the Minister in writing of any deaths of or serious injuries to animals being transported during the journey and the reasons for such deaths or serious injuries, within a maximum of 10 days following the date indicated on Part A as the proposed date of arrival of the animals at their place of destination,;”

- (b) in Article 12, by substituting for paragraph (8) the following:

  > - “(8) The Minister may, if he is satisfied that the relevant provisions of this Order are not being complied with by a transporter, refuse to authorise the transporter and shall notify the transporter in writing of the refusal and the reason for such refusal. Any infringement of this order, within the jurisdiction of the State or not, may result in the suspension or withdrawal of an authorisation granted by the Minister under this Article”

- (c) by substituting for Article 19 the following:—

  > “19. Animals to which this Order applies may, before being loaded into the means of transport by which they are exported from the State, be required to be inspected by an inspector. If the inspector is satisfied that such animals are fit for transport he may issue a certificate to that effect.”

- (d) by substituting for the Second Schedule the Schedule to this Order.
