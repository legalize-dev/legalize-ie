S.I. No. 253 of 1992.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (RECOGNITION OF QUALIFICATIONS IN VETERINARY MEDICINE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1992

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to the amendment and extension of Council Directive No. 78/1026/EEC of 18 December, 1978^1 and Council Directive No. 78/1027/EEC of the said 18 December^2 by Council Directive No. 89/594/EEC of 30th October, 1989,^3 and Council Directive 90/658/EEC of 4 December 1990^4 hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Recognition of Qualifications in Veterinary Medicine) (Amendment) Regulations, 1992.

- (2) The European Communities (Recognition of Qualifications in Veterinary Medicines) Regulations, 1980 to 1987, and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Recognition of Qualifications in Veterinary Medicine) Regulations, 1980 to 1992.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Principal Regulations" means the European Communities (Recognition of Qualifications in Veterinary Medicine) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0391.html) ).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations implement Council Directives 89/594/EEC and 90/658/EEC by amending existing legislation in relation to rights of establishment of veterinary surgeons in the European Community, the effect of which is:

( *a* ) to recognise:

- (i) qualifications for which training commenced before December, 1980.

^1O.J. No. L.341, 23/11/1989, p. 19.

^2O.J. No. L.353, 17/12/1990, p.73.

- (ii) the special position of veterinarians whose training commenced in Italy before 31 December, 1984.

- (iii) qualifications certified by Member States to be equal to the recognised qualifications, and

- (iv) the qualifications issued in the former German Democratic Republic, and

( *b* ) to update the descriptions of the qualifications for Greece and Spain.
