# S.I. No. 541/2011 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Section 13) (Prescribed Form and Manner of Statements) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st November*, 2011.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec13) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Section 13) (Prescribed Form and Manner of Statements) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 28th October, 2011.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2010” means [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed as the form of the statements to be automatically produced for the purposes of section 13(2) of the Act of 2010 by an apparatus referred to in section 12(1)(*a*) of that Act.

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of completing the statements referred to in section 13(2) of the Act of 2010 in the prescribed manner the member of the Garda Síochána supplying the statements shall—

- (*a*) before the person provides a specimen of his or her breath in accordance with section 12(1)(*a*) of the Act of 2010, input into the apparatus referred to in that section—

    - (i) the name, address, date of birth and gender of the person providing the specimens,

    - (ii) the provision that it is alleged the person has contravened, namely, section 4(4) or 5(4) of the Act of 2010, and

    - (iii) his or her name and number,

      and

- (*b*) following the automatic production of the statements referred to in section 13(2) of the Act of 2010, sign the statements.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the form and manner of the statements, both in Irish and English, to be produced by an apparatus for determining the concentration of alcohol in the breath pursuant to [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec13) (2) of the [Road Traffic Act, 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) and prescribe the manner in which the statements are to be duly completed by a member of the Garda Síochána.
