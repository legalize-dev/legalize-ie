> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 136 of 1977.

EXCHANGE CONTROL REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), as continued by the [Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1974), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Exchange Control Regulations, 1977:

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 16th day of May, 1977.

- (3) The Exchange Control Regulations 1959 to 1975, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 to 1977.

###### 2.

2. Lombard and Ulster Banking Ireland Ltd of 54/57 Lower Mount Street, Dublin 2 is hereby appointed to be an authorised dealer.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 9th day of May, 1977.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 by appointing Lombard and Ulster Banking Ireland Ltd of 54/57 Lower Mount Street to be an authorised dealer for the purposes of the Exchange Controls Acts.
