# S.I. No. 189/1992 - Social Welfare (Unemployment Benefit) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 189 of 1992.

SOCIAL WELFARE (UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFIT) REGULATIONS, 1992.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981) and section 35 of that Act (as amended by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1992)), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Unemployment Benefit) Regulations, 1992.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 20th day of July, 1992.

3 Disqualification for Unemployment Benefit.

###### 3.

**3.** The amount prescribed for the purposes of subsection (3) *(e)* of [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0035.html#sec35) (as inserted by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) ) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) shall be £12,000.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 10th day of July, 1992.

CHARLIE McCREEVY,

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) provides that a person under age 55 who receives moneys in respect of redundancy in excess of a prescribed amount may be disqualified for receiving unemployment benefit for a period of up to nine weeks. These regulations prescribe an amount of £12,000 for the purposes of this disqualification.
