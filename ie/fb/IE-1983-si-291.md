# S.I. No. 291/1983 - Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations, 1983

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 291 of 1983.

INDUSTRIAL AND PROVIDENT SOCIETIES (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 73 of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act, 1893, as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Registry of Friendly Societies Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1936), and the Industry, Commerce and Tourism (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1981, ( [S.I. No. 290 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0290.html) ) hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 17th day of October, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) The fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1978, shall be the fees that are specified in Part I of the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) No fee shall be payable for the services specified in Part II of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Industrial and Provident Societies (Fees) Regulations, 1978, ( [S.I. No. 84 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0084.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts, 1893 to 1978.
