# S.I. No. 564/2016 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) (No. 2) Regulations, 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th November*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for the Department of Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government as adapted by the Environment, Community and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2016, [S.I. No 394 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0394.html) , in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 4 and 21 of the [Derelict Sites Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No.14 of 1990), hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas)(No. 2) Regulations, 2016.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2016 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2016.

*Prescription of Urban Areas*

###### 2.

2. The areas in the administrative counties of Cork, Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown, Kilkenny, Louth, Meath, Tipperary indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule are hereby prescribed to be urban areas for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
