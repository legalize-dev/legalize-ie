# S.I. No. 89/1996 - Aliens (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 89 of 1996.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) ORDER, 1996

I, Nora Owen, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1996.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1996, and this Order may be cited together as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1996, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 4th day of April, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) ) to the Aliens Order 1946 (S.R. and O. No. 395 of 1946), is hereby amended by the deletion of: "Fiji", "Guyana", "Mauritius", "Tanzania" and "Zambia".

GIVEN UNDER my official Seal, this the 3rd day of April, 1996.

_____________________

Nora Owen, T.D..

**MINISTER FOR JUSTICE**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order was made for the purpose of imposing entry visa requirements for nationals of Fiji, Guyana, Mauritius, Tanzania, and Zambia.
