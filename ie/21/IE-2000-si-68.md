# S.I. No. 68/2000 - Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act, 1999 (Establishment Day) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec3) of the [Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No 13 of 1999) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act, 1999, (Establishment Day) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of March 2000 is hereby appointed as the Establishment Day for the purposes of the [Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 1999).
