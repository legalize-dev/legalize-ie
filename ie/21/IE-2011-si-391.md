# S.I. No. 391/2011 - Radiological Protection Act 1991 (Licensing Application and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th July*, 2011.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0009/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Radiological Protection Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1991) (as amended by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec2) of the [Radiological Protection (Amendment) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2002)) and the Meteorological Services, Radiological Protection and Nuclear Safety (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 303 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0303.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Radiological Protection Act 1991 (Licensing Application and Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the [Radiological Protection Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Licensing Application and Fees) Regulations, 2007 ( [S.I. No. 654 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0654.html) ) is hereby amended by substituting for the definition of “ionising radiation” the following definition:

> “ ‘ionising radiation’ means the transfer of energy in the form of particles or electromagnetic waves of a wavelength of 100 nanometers or less, or a frequency of 3 x 10^15 Hertz or more, capable of producing ions directly or indirectly; ”

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government,

22 July 2011.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.
