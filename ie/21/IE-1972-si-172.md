# S.I. No. 172/1972 - Redundancy Contributions (Variation of Rates) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 172 of 1972.

REDUNDANCY CONTRIBUTIONS (VARIATION OF RATES) ORDER, 1972.

I, JOSEPH BRENNAN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0021/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Redundancy Payments Act, 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (21 of 1967), hereby make the following order :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Redundancy Contributions (Variation of Rates) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 2nd day of October, 1972.

###### 3.

3. In this Order " the Order of 1971 " means the Redundancy Contributions (Variation of Rates) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 36 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0036.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The [Redundancy Payments Act, 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1967) shall have effect as if in section 28, which relates to weekly contributions to the Redundancy Fund—

- (*a*) " five new pence " were substituted for " three new pence " (inserted by the Order of 1971) where that first occurs in subsection (1),

- (*b*) " four new pence " were substituted for " three new pence " (inserted by the Order of 1971) where that secondly occurs in subsection (1), and

- (*c*) " three new pence " and " two new pence " were substituted for " two new pence " (inserted by the Order of 1971) and " one new penny " (inserted by the Order of 1971) respectively, in subsection (2).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 10th day of July, 1972.

**JOSEPH BRENNAN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the increase of the rates of weekly contributions to the Redundancy Fund as from 2nd October, 1972.
