# S.I. No. 182/1988 - Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulation, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 182 of 1988.

VALUATION TRIBUNAL (FEES) REGULATION, 1988.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), hereby make the following Regulation:

###### 1.

1. This Regulation may be cited as the Valuation Tribunal (Fees) Regulation, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The fee to be charged in respect of an appeal to the Valuation Tribunal under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) (5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) of a class mentioned in column (1) of the following Table shall be the amount specified in column (2) opposite the mention of that class.

TABLE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Valuation of property as determined by Commissioner of Valuation | Appeal Fee |
| (1) | (2) |
|  | £ |
| less than £100 | 50 |
| £100 to £499 | 100 |
| £500 or over | 150 |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of July 1988.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.
