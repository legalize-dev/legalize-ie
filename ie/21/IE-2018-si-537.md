# S.I. No. 537/2018 - Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th December*, 2018.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec4) 1(1) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec4) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2018)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 22 December 2018.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 6 of the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 460 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0460.html) ) is amended by substituting for paragraph (aa) (inserted by Regulation 2 of the Road Traffic Act 1994 (Detention of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 48 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0048.html) )) the following:

> - “(aa) in the case of a person to whom subparagraph (i), (ii) or (iii) of section 41(1)(*a*) of the Act of 1994 applies, he or she was, at the time when the vehicle was detained, the holder of a driving licence or learner permit licensing him or her to drive the vehicle,
>
> - (ab) in the case of a person to whom subparagraph (iv) (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0018/print.html#sec4) (*c*) of the [Road Traffic (Amendment) Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0018/print.html) (No. 18 of 2018)) of section 41(1)(*a*) of the Act of 1994 applies, he or she was, at the time when the vehicle was detained-
>
>     - (i) the holder of a driving licence, or
>
>     - (ii) accompanied by and under the supervision of a qualified person within the meaning of Regulation 17(6)(*a*) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ).”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 December 2018.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic Detention of Vehicles Regulations to allow for a waiver of fees in a case where a vehicle is incorrectly detained for being driven by a learner driver unaccompanied by a qualified driver.
