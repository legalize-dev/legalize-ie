# S.I. No. 167/2000 - Comhairle Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec3) of the [Comhairle Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2000), orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Comhairle Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The 12th day of June 2000 is appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Comhairle Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2000).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, this 2nd day of June, 2000. |
|  | DERMOT AHERN, |
|  | Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides that the 12th day of June, 2000 shall be the day of establishment of the new body Comhairle which will replace the National Social Service Board and certain functions of the National Rehabilitation Board.
