# S.I. No. 316/2001 - The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports From The Netherlands) (Revocation) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby order as follows -

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the Netherlands) (Revocation) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the Netherlands) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 210 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0210.html) ) and the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the Netherlands) (Amendment) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0233.html) ) are revoked.

###### 3.

3. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Movement of Persons) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 160 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0160.html) ) is amended —

- (a) in Article 3, by the substitution for “and the Netherlands are” of “is”,

- (b) in Article 4(1), by the deletion of “either” and “or the Netherlands,”,

- (c) in Article 4(5), by the deletion of “or the Netherlands”, and

- (d) in Article 5(1), by the deletion of “or the Netherlands”.

###### 4.

4. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Movement of Horses) (Amendment) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 216 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0216.html) ) is amended by the deletion, in Article 2, of “or the Netherlands”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order revokes restrictions on certain imports from the Netherlands imposed due to Foot and Mouth disease in that country.
