# S.I. No. 132/1984 - Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907 (Application of Section 82 To County Health District of Meath) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 132 of 1984.

PUBLIC HEALTH ACTS AMENDMENT ACT, 1907 (APPLICATION OF SECTION 82 TO COUNTY HEALTH DISTRICT OF MEATH) ORDER, 1984.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, on the application of the council of the county of Meath and in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907, hereby, the requirements of subsection (2) of that section having been duly complied with, order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907 (Application of section 82 to County Health District of Meath) Order, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. Section 82 of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907 is hereby declared to be in force in the County Health District of Meath.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of May, 1984.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument applies section 82 of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act, 1907, to the County Health District of Meath, thereby enabling the County Council to make bye-laws under the section for the prevention of danger, obstruction or annoyance to persons using the seashore.
