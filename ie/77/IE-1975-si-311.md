S.I. No. 311 of 1975.

TELEX (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1975.

I, CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telex (Amendment) Regulations, 1975.

- (2) The Telex Regulations 1955 to 1974 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telex Regulations, 1955 to 1975.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Telex Regulations, 1955 ( [S.I. No. 229 of 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/si/0229.html) ), shall be amended by the substitution of the following for the Schedule thereto (inserted in those Regulations by the Telex (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 355 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0355.html) ):

"SCHEDULE

## PART I Charges for calls other than Subscriber Keyed Calls

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Description of Call | Charge | Charge |
| 1. A call between two installations connected with the same telex exchange | 4.2p for each three minutes or part of three minutes. | 4.2p for each three minutes or part of three minutes. |

|  | Charge | Charge |
|  | p | p |
| 2. Any other call to an installation in Ireland, the distance between the telex exchanges connected for the purpose of the call being in excess of 80 Kilometres | 12.6 | 4.2 |

## PART II Charges for Subscriber Keyed Calls

The charge for a subscriber keyed call shall be the sum of 4.2p in respect of each period or part of a period of time specified in the Table to this part of this Schedule which is appropriate to the call and during which the call continues.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Description of Call | Period |
| 1. A call between two installations connected with the same telex exchange | 3 Minutes |
| 2. Any other call to an installation in Ireland, the distance between the telex exchanges connected for the purpose of the call being in excess of 80 Kilometres | 60 Seconds |
| 3. A call to an installation in Great Britain, the Isle of Man or the Channel Islands | 30 Seconds |

> "

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 18th day of December, 1975.

**CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.
