# S.I. No. 220/2005 - Bord Ard-Mhúsaem na Héireann (Establishment Day) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 220 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0220.html)**

**BORD ARD-MHÚSAEM NA HÉIREANN (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER 2005**

**[S.I. No. 220 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0220.html)**

**BORD ARD-MHÚSAEM NA HÉIREANN (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER 2005**

I, John O'Donoghue T.D., Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) ( [No. 11 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) ) and the Arts and Culture (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0302.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Sport and Recreation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 309 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0309.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Bord Ard-Mhúsaem na hÉireann (Establishment Day Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The 3^rd day of May 2005 is appointed as the Museum establishment day for the purposes of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 27^th April 2005 |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This order appoints the day of the 3^rd of May 2005 as the Museum establishment day on which the provisions of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997) which will give effect to the establishment of Bord Ard-Mhúsaem na hÉireann to perform the functions conferred on it by or under this Act will come into effect.
