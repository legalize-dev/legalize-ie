# S.I. No. 135/1993 - Mergers, Take-Overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978 (Section 2) Order, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 135 of 1993.

MERGERS, TAKE-OVERS AND MONOPOLIES (CONTROL) ACT, 1978 (SECTION 2) ORDER, 1993.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/sec0002.html#sec2) (4) of the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1978), as adapted by the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0019.html) ), make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (Section 2) Order, 1993.

###### 2.

2. [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) is hereby amended by the substitution, in subsection (1), of "£10,000,000" and "£20,000,000" for the amounts inserted by the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (Section 2) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 230 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0230.html) ), being "£5,000,000" and "£10,000,000" respectively.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of May, 1993.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Enterprise and

Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this order is to increase the financial thresholds specified in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Mergers, Take-overs and Monopolies (Control) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (as amended). Section 2 of the Act indicates the size of enterprises in financial terms to which the Act applies.

While the order is effective from the date of its signature, it is nevertheless required under section 2 (6) of the Act to be confirmed by a resolution of each House of the Oireachtas within 21 sitting days in each case.
