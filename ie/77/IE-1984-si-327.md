S.I. No. 327 of 1984.

DISTRICT COURT [CRIMINAL JUSTICE (COMMUNITY SERVICE) ACT, 1983] RULES, 1984.

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ), and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, make the following Rules of Court—

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the District Court [ [Criminal Justice (Community Service) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ] Rules, 1984.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on the 10th day of December, 1984, and shall be read together with all other District Court Rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. In these Rules—

"the Act" means the [Criminal Justice (Community Service) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1983);

"Form" means a form set out in the Schedule of Forms herein or such modification thereof as may be suitable;

"the Principal rules" means the District Court Rules, 1948 S.R. & O., 1947 (No. 431).

###### 4.

4. A community service order made by the Court under section 3 of the Act shall be in the Form 1.

###### 5.

5. A complaint under section 7 (4) of the Act shall be made by summons in the form provided in the Principal Rules, and an application under section 9, 10 (1) or 11 (1) of the Act. when made by a relevant officer, shall be by summons in the Form 2 in the Schedule of Forms herein.

###### 6.

6. A summons issued under these Rules shall be served on the offender by a member of An Garda Síochána in accordance with the provisions of the Principal Rules relating to the service of summonses.

###### 7.

7. An application under section 9, 10 (1) or 11 (1) of the Act, when made by the offender, shall be by notice in the Form 3 in the Schedule of Forms herein. A copy of the notice shall be given, or sent by ordinary post, to the relevant officer concerned at least seven days before the date fixed for the hearing.

###### 8.

8. The original of every summons and every notice of application served shall be lodged with the District Court Clerk at least four days before the date fixed for the hearing.

###### 9.

9. An order under section 10 of he Act shall be in the Form 4.

###### 10.

10. (i) Where the Court makes a community service order and the justice so making is not the justice assigned to the district of residence the District Court Clerk shall forthwith send a certified copy of the order by ordinary post to the latter justice.

- (ii) Where the Court makes, or amends under section 10 (1) of the Act, a community service order, the District Court Clerk shall forthwith send two certified copies of the order or the order as so amended by ordinary post to the relevant officer specified therein.
