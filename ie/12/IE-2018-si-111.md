# S.I. No. 111/2018 - National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014 (Specified Sector) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th April*, 2018.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec22) (2) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2014), following consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform and the Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment, and believing it to be desirable to do so in the interests of supporting economic activity and employment, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014 (Specified Sector) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “postal services” has the same meaning as in [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec6) of the [Communications Regulation (Postal Services) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2011).

###### 3.

3. The postal services sector is specified as a sector within section 22(1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2014).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 March 2018.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
