# S.I. No. 353/1988 - Wildlife (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 353 of 1988.

WILDLIFE (TRANSFER OF DEPARTMENTAL ADMINISTRATION AND MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1988.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Wildlife (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1988.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 17th day of December, 1988.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "functions" includes powers and duties.

###### 3.

3. (1) The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 4 of this Order are hereby transferred to the Department of Finance.

- (2) References to the Department of Energy contained in any Act or any instrument made under an Act and relating to any administration and business transferred by paragraph (1) of this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Department of Finance.

###### 4.

4. (1) There are hereby transferred to the Minister for Finance the functions vested in the Minister for Energy by or under section 55 and subsections (1) to (4) of [section 63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1976), for the purposes of that Act.

- (2) References to the Minister for Energy contained in cy Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any functions transferred by this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Finance.

###### 5.

5. Where, immediately before the commencement of this Order, any legal proceedings are pending to which the Minister for Energy is a party and the proceedings have reference to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Finance, the name of the Minister for Finance shall, in so far as the proceedings relate to any function transferred by this Order, be substituted in the proceedings for that of the Minister for Energy, or added in the proceedings, as may be appropriate, and the proceedings shall not abate by reason of such substitution.

###### 6.

6. Anything commenced before the commencement of this Order by or under the authority of the Minister for Energy may, so far as it relates to functions transferred by this Order, to the Minister for Finance, be carried on or completed on or after such commencement by the Minister for Finance.

###### 7.

7. Every document (including any certificate or licence) granted or made in the exercise of a function transferred by this Order shall, if and in so far as it was operative immediately before the commencement of this Order, have effect on and after such commencement as if it has been granted or made by the Minister for Finance.

###### 8.

8. References to the Minister for Energy contained in the memorandum and articles of association of any company and relating to any function transferred by this Order shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Finance.

###### 9.

9. (1) All property, rights and liabilities held, enjoyed or incurred before the commencement of this Order by the Minister for Energy in connection with the functions transferred by this Order shall be and are hereby transferred to the Minister for Finance, accordingly, without any further conveyance, transfer or assignment—

- (*a*) the said property, real and personal, shall, on such commencement, vest in the Minister for Finance,

- (*b*) the said rights shall, as on and from such commencement, be enjoyed by the Minister for Finance,

- (*c*) the said liabilities shall, as on and from such commencement, be the liabilities of the Minister for Finance.

- (2) All moneys, stocks, shares and securities transferred to the Minister for Finance by this Article which, on such commencement, are standing in the name of the Minister for Energy shall on the request of the Minister for Finance be transferred into his name.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 16th day of December, 1988.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order transfers to the Minister for Finance with effect from the date specified certain statutory functions under the [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) .
