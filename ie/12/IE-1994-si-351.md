> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 351 of 1994.

ROAD TRAFFIC ACT, 1994 (PART III) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) , [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) , [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) and [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Part III) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 2nd day of December, 1994.

###### 3.

3. The form to be completed under [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) by a designated doctor shall be in the form set out in the First Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The certificate to be issued by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety under [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) shall be in the form set out in the Second Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. (1) The amount prescribed for the purpose of subsection (1) of [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) shall be £75.

- (2) Payments under subsection (1) of [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) shall be paid into or disposed of for the benefit of the Exchequer in such manner as the Minister for Finance may direct.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe:

( *a* ) the form to be completed by a designated doctor under [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) ;

( *b* ) the certificate to be issued by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety under [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) ; and

*c* ) the maximum contribution which may be required under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) and the manner of disposal of such payments.
