# S.I. No. 123/2020 - Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) (Extension of Period of Validity of Certificate) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th April,* 2020.

The Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) (Extension of Period of Validity of Certificate) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Regulations of 2011” means the Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 681 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0681.html) );

“IBT certificate” has the same meaning that it has in the Regulations of 2011.

###### 3.

3. Notwithstanding Regulation 9 of, and Schedule 2 (inserted by Regulation 2(i) of the Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 433 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0433.html) )) to, the Regulations of 2011, an IBT certificate issued during the period beginning on 1 March 2018 and ending on 30 June 2018 is valid for a period of 2 years and 4 months from the date of issue.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport,

9 April, 2020.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Courses of Instruction) (Motorcycles) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 681 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0681.html) ) to provide that a certificate of satisfactory completion of initial basic training for motorcyclists, an IBT certificate, issued during the period beginning on 1 March 2018 and ending on 30 June 2018 is valid for a period of 2 years and 4 months from the date of issue.
