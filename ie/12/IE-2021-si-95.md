# S.I. No. 95/2021 - Health Act 1947 (Section 31A - Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 10) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th March,* 2021.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and 31A (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec10) of the [Health (Preservation and Protection and other Emergency Measures in the Public Interest) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2020)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and –

- (*a*) having regard to the immediate, exceptional and manifest risk posed to human life and public health by the spread of Covid-19 and to the matters specified in subsection (2) of section 31A, and

- (*b*) having consulted with the Minister for Transport, the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, the Minister for Finance, the Minister for Justice, the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, the Minister for Foreign Affairs and the Minister for Tourism, Culture, Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media,

hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1947 (Section 31A - Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 10) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 1 of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A - Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 10) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 701 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0701.html) ) is amended, in paragraph (2), by the substitution of “5th day of April 2021” for “5th day of March 2021”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 March, 2021.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A - Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 10) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 701 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0701.html) ) to provide that the Regulations shall remain in operation until 05 April 2021.
