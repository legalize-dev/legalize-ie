# S.I. No. 103/1989 - State Guarantees Act, 1954 (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 103 of 1989.

STATE GUARANTEES ACT, 1954 (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE) ORDER, 1989.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Amendment of Schedule Order, 1989).

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [State Guarantees Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1954), is hereby amended by the deletion in the second column of "Irish Potato Marketing Co. Ltd".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 9th day of May, 1989.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Taoiseach.
