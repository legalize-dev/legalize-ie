# S.I. No. 606/2017 - Adoption Act 2010 (Pre-Placement Consultation Procedure) (Forms) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January*, 2018.

I, KATHERINE ZAPPONE, Minister for Children and Youth Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec17) (3) and [150](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec150) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) and the Office of the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 218 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0218.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Pre-Placement Consultation Procedure) (Forms) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 20 December 2017.

###### 3.

3. Each of the forms set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec17) (3)(*b*)(i) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) and shall be used in the case and manner indicated in the form.

###### 4.

4. The Adoption Act 2010 (Pre-Placement Consultation Procedure) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0520.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 December 2017.

**KATHERINE ZAPPONE,**

Minister for Children and Youth Affairs.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations prescribe the forms for the purposes of [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec17) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) which are to be used in circumstances where an adoption placement is deferred for the purposes of allowing the relevant non-guardian of a child make an application to court under the [Guardianship of Infants Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964).

These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Pre-Placement Consultation Procedure) (Forms) Regulations 2017 and come into operation on 20 December 2017.
