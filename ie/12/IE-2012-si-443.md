# S.I. No. 443/2012 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th November*, 2012.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (Number 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) is amended-

- (*a*) in Column (3), at Reference Number 1, by the substitution of “€1,000,000” for “€1,000,000,000”,

- (*b*) in Column (3), at Reference Number 2, by the substitution of “€1,000,000” for “€1,000,000,000”, and

- (*c*) in Column (3), at Reference Number 3, by the substitution of “€1,000,000” for “€1,000,000,000”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 October 2012.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
