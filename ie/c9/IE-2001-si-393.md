# S.I. No. 393/2001 - National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on my by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec5) (2) of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act. 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000), hereby order as follows

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The First Schedule of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000) is hereby amended by the insertion in paragraph 1. (b) after “live animals” of

> “and persons to whom the Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 to 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers and Dealers Premises) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 79 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0079.html) ) applies who deal in bovine animals”

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the application of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000) to persons to whom the Diseases of Animals Acts, 1966 to 2001 (Approval and Registration of Dealers and Dealers Premises) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 79 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0079.html) ) applies who deal in bovine animals.
