# S.I. No. 177/1997 - Metrology Act, 1996 (Commencement and Establishment Day) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 177 of 1997.

METROLOGY ACT, 1996 (COMMENCEMENT AND ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER, 1997

I, PAT RABBITTE, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/sec0001.html#sec1) (2) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Metrology Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1996), and the Enterprise and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 350 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0350.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Metrology Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (Commencement and Establishment Day) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The 12th day of May, 1997, is hereby appointed as the day—

( *a* ) on which the [Metrology Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1996), shall come into operation, and

( *b* ) to be the establishment day for the purpose of Part II of that Act.

GIVEN under my hand, this 30th day of April, 1997.

PAT RABBITTE,

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Order provides that the [Metrology Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , will come into force and that the Legal Metrology Service be established on 12th May, 1997.
