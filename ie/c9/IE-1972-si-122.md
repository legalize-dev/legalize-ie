# S.I. No. 122/1972 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 31, Fifty-Third Period) (Electric Filament Lamps) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 122 of 1972.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA No. 31, FIFTY-THIRD PERIOD) (ELECTRIC FILAMENT LAMPS) ORDER, 1972.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 31, Fifty-third Period) (Electric Filament Lamps) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

SCHEDULE.

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quota Order for which quota period and quota are appointed | Quota Period | Quota | General description of subject matter of quota |
| (1) | (2) | (3) | (4) |
| Control of Imports (Quota No. 31) Order, 1936 (S. R. & O., No. 238 of 1936), as as amended. | The period beginning on the 1st day of July, 1972, and ending on the 31st day of December, 1972. | 500,000 (Five hundred thousand) articles. | Certain electric filament lamps. |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 16th day of May, 1972.

**ERSKINE CHILDERS,**

Tánaiste.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints the quantity of electric filament lamps which may be imported during the period 1st July, 1972 to 31st December, 1972 at 500,000 articles.
