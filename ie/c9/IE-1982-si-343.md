# S.I. No. 343/1982 - Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979 (Section 3) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 343 of 1982.

MILK (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1979 (SECTION 3) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the first day of December, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The rate of fee specified in [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) (i) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979) is hereby altered by the substitution of "O. 1875 new pence" for "three twentieths of one new penny" (inserted by the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 29 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0029.html) .)).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of November, 1982.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations increase with effect from 1 December 1982, the rate of fee payable under [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
