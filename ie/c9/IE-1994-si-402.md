# S.I. No. 402/1994 - Investment Limited Partnerships Act, 1994 (Authorisation Fee) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 402 of 1994.

INVESTMENT LIMITED PARTNERSHIPS ACT, 1994 (AUTHORISATION FEE) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0024/sec0008.html#sec8) (4) of the [Investment Limited Partnerships Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1994), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Investment Limited Partnerships Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Authorisation Fee) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. An amount of £1,000 is hereby prescribed for the purposes of [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0024/sec0008.html#sec8) (4) of the [Investment Limited Partnerships Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Enterprise and

Employment, this 30th day of November 1994.

**JOHN CORCORAN,**

A person authorised under [Section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0015.html#sec15) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister.
