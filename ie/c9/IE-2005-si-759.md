# S.I. No. 759/2005 - Valuation Act 2001 (Global Valuation) (Apportionment) (Waterways Ireland) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 759 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0759.html)**

**ORDER**

entitled

**Valuation Act 2001 (Global Valuation) (Apportionment) (Waterways Ireland) Order 2005**

Made by the Minister for Finance.

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 53(8) and 58 of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001), after consultation with the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Global Valuation (Apportionment) (Waterways Ireland) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. In this Order -

“areas” means administrative areas within the meaning of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001);

“authorities” means rating authorities within the meaning of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001);

“valuation” has the meaning assigned by Article 3(1).

###### 3.

3. (1) The Commissioner of Valuation shall apportion the global valuation of Waterways Ireland, determined pursuant to the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Global Valuation (Waterways Ireland) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No.707 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0707.html) ) (in this Order referred to as “the valuation”), between authorities, pursuant to section 53(8) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2001), in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3) of this Article.

- (2) The valuation shall be apportioned between the authorities in whose areas property comprised in the valuation is situate in the proportion, expressed as a percentage that the population resident in the area of each such authority, as determined by the census of population taken under the Statistics (Census of Population) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 491 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0491.html) ), bears to the total population of those authorities, as so determined.

- (3)
    - (a) In respect of the period for which an existing valuation list remains in force in relation to the area of an authority to which paragraph (2) of this Article relates, the valuation apportioned to such authority under that paragraph shall be multiplied by a factor of 0.002664 to establish the valuation to be entered in the central valuation list pursuant to [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec58) of the [Valuation Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) . The apportioned and adjusted valuation for each rating authority shall be rounded to the nearest whole euro.

    - (b) Where the apportioned and adjusted valuation established pursuant to subparagraph (a) of this paragraph results in the amount concerned being an amount in euro and 50 cent, that amount shall be rounded upwards to the nearest whole euro.
