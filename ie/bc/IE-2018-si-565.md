S.I. No. 565 of 2018

DISTRICT COURT (BAIL) RULES 2018

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which shall come into operation on the 9th day of January 2019, may be cited as the District Court (Bail) Rules 2018.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2018.

###### 2.

2. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) are amended by the insertion, immediately following rule 2 of Order 20, of the following rule:

> *“Arrest without warrant: section 6(10) of the [Bail Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0016/index.html)*

###### 3.

3. —A person who is arrested without a warrant in accordance with [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0016/sec0006.html#sec6) (10) of the [Bail Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) shall, as soon as practicable, be brought before the Court that made the order directing that the recognisance be entered into.”

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Rules amend Order 20 of the District Court Rules to facilitate the operation of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0016/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Bail Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , as amended by [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec6) of the [Criminal Justice Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) .
