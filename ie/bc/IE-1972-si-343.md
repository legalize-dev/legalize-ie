# S.I. No. 343/1972 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 343 of 1972.

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT, 1966 (FIRST SCHEDULE) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1972.

I, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1972.

- (2) The [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 and 1971, and this Order may be cited together as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1972.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Act" means the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966).

###### 3.

3. For all the purposes of the Act, the First Schedule to the Act (as amended by the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 and 1971), is hereby amended by the insertion in Part III thereof at the end of Class A of the following :

> "12. Swine Vesicular Disease."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of December, 1972.

**JAMES GIBBONS,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order extends the definition of " disease " in the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) to include Swine Vesicular Disease.
