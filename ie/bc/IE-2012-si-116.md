# S.I. No. 116/2012 - Planning and Development (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th April*, 2012.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 231](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec231) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) , as amended, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Development (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

- (2) These Regulations and the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 to 2011 shall be construed as one and may be collectively cited as the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 to 2012.

*Interpretation.*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, except where the context otherwise requires, “the Regulations” means the Planning and Development Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ), as amended.

*Commencement.*

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the date of signature.

*Amendment of Article 186 of the Regulations.*

###### 4.

4. Article 186(1) of the Regulations is amended by the substitution of “10 weeks” for “16 weeks” wherever it occurs.

*Amendment of Article 191 of the Regulations.*

###### 5.

5. Article 191(1) of the Regulations is amended by the insertion of “and the local authority shall not make a decision on the application until the applicant has supplied the requested information” after “any such request”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 April 2012.

**PHIL HOGAN T.D.,**

Minister for the Environment Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Planning and Development Regulations, 2001 to reduce the period prior to the event that an application for a licence for an outdoor event must be made from 16 weeks to 10 weeks.
