# S.I. No. 632/2021 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Cross-Border) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November,* 2021.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) (as amended by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec44) of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2006)), [section 148](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (as amended by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec37) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)), [section 149](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (as amended by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec37) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)) and [section 151](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (inserted by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec37) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2005)) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990) (as adapted by the Employment Affairs and Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. **Citation and Construction**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Cross-Border) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. The Occupational Pension Schemes (Cross-Border) Regulations 2006 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Cross-Border) Regulations 2006 to 2021 and shall be construed together as one.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend [S.I. No. 292 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0292.html) in order to give further effect to Directive (EU) 2016/2341 and to take account of obligations imposed on schemes that operate on a cross-border basis by the European Union (Occupational Pension Schemes) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0128.html) ) by -

- prescribing the relevant statutory requirements for the purposes of sections 151B of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ;

- specifying additional compliance requirements in respect of statements to be signed by trustees for the purposes of satisfying the Pensions Authority that they comply with section 149(4)(a) of the Act;

- inserting a new Regulation 5A to specify the information to be provided to the Pensions Authority by the trustees of a scheme, who are deemed, under section 151A(13)(a), authorised under section 149, for the purposes of satisfying the Pensions Authority that they comply with the conditions of authorisation under section 149(4)(a);

- amending the sections of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) that are prescribed for the purposes of section 149(3) of the Act;

- inserting a new Regulation 9A to require trustees of a receiving scheme referred to in section 151A(13) of the Act, where approval under section 151 has been deemed to have been granted under section 151(13)(b), to notify the Pensions Authority of the information set out in Schedule 2;

- making textual and technical amendments to the existing Schedule;

- inserting a new Schedule 2 to specify the information to be provided to the Pensions Authority by schemes referred to in section 151A(13), where approval under section 151 has been deemed to have been granted under section 151A(13)(b), and

- making a number of other technical amendments.
