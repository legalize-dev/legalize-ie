# S.I. No. 251/1996 - Aliens (Amendment) (No. 4) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 251 of 1996.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 4) ORDER, 1996

I, Nora Owen, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) (No. 4) Order,

1996.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1996, and this Order may be cited together as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1996, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) , (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 19 day of July 1996.

###### 2.

2. The principal order is hereby amended by the insertion into Article 5A (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 53 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0053.html) )) of "Iraq" after "Iran".

GIVEN under my Official Seal

this 19 day of July, 1996.

_________________

Nora Owen, T.D.,

**MINISTER FOR JUSTICE**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This order was made for the purpose of imposing transit visa requirements on citizens of Iraq.
