# S.I. No. 780/2021 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 19) (Change in Rates) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st February,* 2022.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec2) (2), [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec3) , [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) (amended by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec5) of the [Social Welfare (Covid-19) (Amendment) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2020)), [41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec41) , [53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec53) , [64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec64) , 68O, [109](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec109) , [125](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec125) , 178A and [297](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec297) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

***Citation and construction***

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 19) (Change in Rates) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2021 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2021.

***Definitions***

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ).

***Commencement***

###### 3.

3. These Regulations come into operation–

- (*a*) in so far as they relate to Covid-19 pandemic unemployment payment, on 24 December 2021,

- (*b*) in so far as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance, pre-retirement allowance and farm assist, on 29 December 2021,

- (*c*) in so far as they relate to jobseeker’s benefit and jobseeker’s benefit (self-employed) on 30 December 2021,

- (*d*) in so far as they relate to illness benefit, health and safety benefit and injury benefit on 3 January 2022,

- (*e*) in so far as they relate to disability allowance, on 5 January 2022,

- (*f*) in so far as they relate to invalidity pension and a payment referred to in paragraph (a) of the definition of “relevant payment” in section 178(1) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) on 6 January 2022, and

- (*g*) in so far as they relate to State pension (contributory), widow’s (contributory) pension, widower’s (contributory) pension and surviving civil partner’s (contributory) pension, on 7 January 2022.

***Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment***

###### 4.

4. (1) Article 52J (amended by article 3 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 14) (Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 448 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0448.html) )) of the Principal Regulations is amended:

- (*a*) in sub-article (3) by the substitution of the following paragraphs for paragraphs (a) and (b):

  > - “(a) €250.00, from 3 September 2021,
  >
  > - (b) €203.00, from 5 November 2021, and
  >
  > - (c) €208.00, from 24 December 2021.”, and

- (*b*) in sub-article (4) by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (c):

  > - “(c) €208.00, from 28 January 2022.”.

- (2) Article 52MB (inserted by article 2(f) of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 18) (Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment) Regulations 2021) of the Principal Regulations is amended in sub-article (1) by the substitution “€208” for “€203”.

***Amendment of Schedules to Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007***

###### 5.

- (*a*) by the substitution of the Schedule set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations for Schedule 1,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The [Social Welfare Act 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/act/pub/0044/print.html) provides for increases in the maximum rates of certain social insurance payments arising from Budget 2022. These rates will apply where the contribution conditions and relevant weekly earnings or weekly income conditions are fully satisfied.

Where contribution conditions are partially satisfied, the Social Welfare Consolidation Act provides for Regulations to specify the reduced personal rates and for tapered increases for a qualified adult (where such a qualified adult has a weekly income).

These Regulations provide for increases to the reduced personal rates of Illness Benefit, Jobseeker’s Benefit, Jobseeker’s Benefit (Self-Employed), Health and Safety Benefit, State Pension (Contributory), Widow’s (Contributory) Pension, Widower’s (Contributory) Pension, and Surviving Civil Partner’s (Contributory) Pension. The reduced rates applicable for qualified adults in such cases are also increased.

These Regulations also provide for changes to the rates of tapered increases for qualified adults (whose income is between €100.01 and €310.00), of recipients of Illness Benefit, Jobseeker’s Benefit, Jobseeker’s Benefit (Self-Employed), Injury Benefit, Jobseeker’s Allowance, Pre-Retirement Allowance, Disability Allowance, Farm Assist and Invalidity Pension.

Finally these Regulations provide for changes to the tapered increases for qualified adults (whose income is between €100.01 and €310.00), of recipients who are on reduced rates of State Pension (Contributory), Illness Benefit, Jobseeker’s Benefit and Jobseeker’s Benefit (Self-Employed).

These changes will take effect from 29 December 2021 to 7 January 2022 to coincide with the increases to the maximum rates for the schemes in question provided for in the [Social Welfare Act 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/act/pub/0044/print.html) .

In addition, these Regulations provide for an increase in the lower rate of Covid-19 Pandemic Unemployment Payment from €203 to €208 in January 2022.
