# S.I. No. 7/2016 - Sea-Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation of Hake) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th January*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Regulation (EC) No 494/2002 of 19 March 2002[^1] as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1867 of 19 October 2015[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation of Hake) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Sea Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation of Hake) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No 179 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0179.html) ) are amended by substituting in Regulation 2(1) for the definition of “Commission Regulation” the following-

> “‘Commission Regulation’ means Commission Regulation (EC) No 494/2002 of 19 March 2002 as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1867 of 19 October 2015;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 January 2016.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend The Sea Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation of Hake) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No 179 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0179.html) ) which provide for technical control measures on Hake, as amended, to adopt the measures in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1897 of 21 October 2015 concerning unintended catches subject to the landing obligation requirement of the Common Fisheries Policy.
