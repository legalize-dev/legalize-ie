EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013 to give effect to a number of Commission Regulations. These Regulations have the effect of—

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Benzophenone-3 as a UV filter (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/238 of 10 February 2017),

- accounting for amendments allowing the Commission, at the request of a Member State or on its own initiative, to adopt the necessary measures to determine whether or not a specific product or group of products falls within the definition ‘cosmetic product’ (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/745 of 5 April 2017),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Methylisothiazolinone (M.I.) as a preservative in rinse-off products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1224 of 6 July 2017),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the prohibitions in relation to HICC, Atranol and chloroatranol in cosmetic products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1410 of 2 August 2017),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to non-nano Zinc Oxide as a colorant (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1413 of 3 August 2017),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to peanut oil and hydrolysed wheat proteins in cosmetic products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/2228 of 4 December 2017),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Methylene Bis-Benzotriazolyl Tetramethylbutylphenol (MBBT) and MBBT (nano) as UV filters (Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/885 of 20 June 2018),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Tagetes minuta and Tagetes patula extracts and oils and prohibitions in relation to Tagetes erecta (Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/978 of 9 July 2018),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to O-phenylphenol (OPP), sodium o-phenylphenate, potassium o-phenylphenate and MEA o-phenylphenate as preservatives (Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1847 of 26 November 2018),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Phenylene Bis-Diphenyltriazine as a UV filter (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/680 of 30 April 2019),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the prohibitions in relation to 2-Chloro-p-Phenylenediamine and its sulfate and dihydrochloride salts when used as a substance in hair dye products, including eyebrow dye products, and eyelash dye products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/681 of 30 April 2019),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Climbazole as an anti-dandruff agent and as a preservative (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/698 of 30 April 2019),

- accounting for amendments to uniformly implement the prohibition of CMR substances as substances prohibited in cosmetic products and pertaining to restrictions in relation to the use of Trimethylbenzoyl diphenylphosphine oxide, Furfural and Polyaminopropyl biguanide (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/831 of 22 May 2019),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to the use of three forms of titanium dioxide (nano), coated with either silica and cetyl phosphate, alumina and manganese dioxide, or alumina and triethoxycaprylylsilane as UV filters (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1857 of 6 November 2019),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Hydroxyethoxyphenyl Butanone (HEPB) as a preservative (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1858 of 6 November 2019),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Salicylic acid, including use as a preservative and non-preservative use, and to uniformly implement the prohibition of CMR substances as substances prohibited in cosmetic products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1966 of 27 November 2019),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to the use of 2-hydroxyethyl methacrylate (HEMA) and 11,14-Dioxa-2,9-diazaheptadec-16-enoic Acid, 4,4,6,16-tetramethyl-10,15-dioxo, 2-[(2-methyl-1-oxo-2-propenyl)oxy]ethyl ester (Di-HEMA Trimethylhexyl Dicarbamate or Di-HEMA TMHDC) in nail products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/1682 of 12 November 2020),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the prohibition in relation to the use of 1,2,4-Trihydroxybenzene, 2-[(4-Amino-2-nitrophenyl)-amino]-benzoic acid and 4-Amino-3-hydroxytoluene from cosmetic products and restrictions in relation to the use of specific substances in hair dye products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/1683 of 12 November 2020),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to use of Methoxypropylamino Cyclohexenylidene Ethoxyethylcyanoacetate as a UV filter in cosmetic products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2020/1684 of 12 November 2020),

- accounting for amendments to the prohibition and restriction of CMR substances and restrictions in relation to the use of titanium dioxide in face products, hair aerosol spray products, as a UV filter and as a colourant, the use of salicylic acid in body lotion, eye shadow, mascara, eyeliner, lipstick and roll-on deodorant, and removing a duplicate entry for Nickel bis(tetrafluoroborate) (Commission Regulation 2021/850 of 26 May 2021),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the prohibition of the use of deoxyarbutin in cosmetic products and restriction on the use of Dihydroxyacetone in non-oxidative hair dye products and self-tanning products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2021/1099 of 5 July 2021),

- accounting for amendments to uniformly implement the prohibition of CMR substances within the internal market (Commission Regulation (EU) 2021/1902 of 29 October 2021),

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the use of Methyl-N-methylanthranilate in cosmetic products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/135 of 31 January 2022, and

- accounting for amendments pertaining to restrictions on the use of Benzophenone-3 and Octocrylene when used as UV filters in cosmetic products (Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/1176 of 7 July 2022), and

- accounting for amendments pertaining to the use of certain substances that release formaldehyde in finished cosmetics products by lowering the threshold regarding the requirement to indicate the presence of formaldehyde on the label of such products and to change the specific label warning to “releases formaldehyde” (Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/1181 of 8 July 2022).

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.
