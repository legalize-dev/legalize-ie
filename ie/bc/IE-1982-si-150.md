# S.I. No. 150/1982 - District Court (Fees) (No. 2) Order, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 150 of 1982.

DISTRICT COURT (FEES) (No. 2) ORDER, 1982.

I, SEÁN DOHERTY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the District Court (Fees) (No. 2) Order, 1982.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 27th day of May, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. Part II of the Schedule to the District Court (Fees) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0045.html) ), is hereby amended by the insertion after item 7 of the following:

> "

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8. On the issue of a notice of application for compensation under the [Malicious Injuries Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) | £2.00 | The original notice of application. |
| 9. On the issue of a decree for compensation or order refusing compensation under the [Malicious Injuries Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) | £2.00 | The original decree or order refusing compensation. |
| 10. In addition to the fee at item 9 above, on every such decree in respect of an award of a sum of money in excess of £250, for every full £50, in excess of £250 | £0.50 | The original decree. |
| (but the additional fee chargeable in respect of this item shall not in any case exceed £10.00) |  |  |

> "

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 20th day of May, 1982.

**SEÁN DOHERTY,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 20th day of May, 1982.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Rules prescribe fees to be charged in the District Court in respect of applications made under the [Malicious Injuries Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) .
