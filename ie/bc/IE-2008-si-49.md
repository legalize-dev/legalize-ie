ROADS (SCHEMES) (FORMS) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th March*, 2008.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 7 and sections 47, 48 and 51 (as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec9) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2007)) of the [Roads Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Roads (Schemes) (Forms) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Act” means the [Roads Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993).

###### 3.

3. The form (Form No. 1) contained in Schedule 1 is prescribed for the purposes of section 47(2) of the Act.

###### 4.

4. The form of the notice (Form No. 2) contained in Schedule 2 is prescribed for the purposes of section 48(*a*) of the Act.

###### 5.

5. The form of the notice (Form No. 3) contained in Schedule 3 is prescribed for the purposes of section 48(*b*) of the Act.

###### 6.

6. The form of the notice (Form No. 4) contained in Schedule 4 is prescribed for the purposes of section 51(3)(*a*) of the Act.

###### 7.

7. The form of the notice (Form No. 5) contained in Schedule 5 is prescribed for the purposes of section 51(3)(*b*) of the Act.

###### 8.

8. The form of the notice (Form No. 6) contained in Schedule 6 is prescribed for the purposes of section 51(3)(*c*) of the Act.

###### 9.

9. The Roads Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 453 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0453.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations, which give effect to the provisions of the Roads Acts 1993 & 2007 (sections 47 & 9 respectively), set out the forms to be used when the National Roads Authority and local authorities are preparing and submitting plans for the provision of a motorway/busway/protected road/service area scheme to An Bord Pleanala.

These Regulations also prescribe the forms to be used to notify the public and persons affected by such schemes and the forms to be used in relation to the environmental impact assessment of road projects.

This Regulation revokes S.I. 453/2000 (Roads Regulations 2000).
