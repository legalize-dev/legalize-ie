# S.I. No. 249/2025 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th June,* 2025.

I, JENNIFER MURNANE O’CONNOR, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) ) and the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 66 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0066.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2025.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, “Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998).

###### 3.

3. Part II of the First Schedule (as amended by Article 4 of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2024 ( [S.I. No. 284 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0284.html) )) to the Act is amended-

- (a) by deleting the following statutory instruments:

  > “European Communities (Control of salmonella in breeding flocks of domestic fowl) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0706.html) ).”,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Order amends the First and Second Schedules to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) .

This Order may be cited as the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2025.

For ease of reference, a list of all food legislation and official agencies, as contained in the First and Second Schedules to the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , as well as a list of Regulations made under the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) and deemed to be food legislation for the purposes of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) , and Regulations made under the Health Act 1947 as amended by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec25) of the [Irish Medicines Board (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , can be found on the website www.fsai.ie (https://www.fsai.ie/enforcement-and-legislation/enforcement/fsai-act-and-related-legislation)
