# S.I. No. 220/1978 - Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978 (Fees) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 220 of 1978.

LANDLORD AND TENANT (GROUND RENTS) (No. 2) ACT, 1978 (FEES) ORDER, 1978

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1978), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Fees) Order, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The fees to be taken in the Land Registry for the purposes of Part III of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (other than the fees specified in section 23 (1)) shall be as follows:

( *a* ) for the issue of a vesting certificate on an application under section 20 of the Act— £10;

( *b* ) (i) for an arbitration under section 21 of the Act— £20;

- (ii) for the issue of a vesting certificate in that case— £10;

( *c* ) for the inspection of a register kept for the purposes of the Act—10p;

( *d* ) for a copy of an entry signed by the Registrar of Titles—50p.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 27th day of July, 1978.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

I, GEORGE COLLEY, Minister for Finance, consent to the making of this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 27th day of July, 1978.

**GEORGE COLLEY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the fees to be taken in the Land Registry for the purposes of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1978), other than the fees specified in section 23 (1) of that Act.
