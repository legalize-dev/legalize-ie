# S.I. No. 6/2019 - County Of Louth Local Electoral Areas And Municipal Districts (Amendment) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd January,* 2019.

I, JOHN PAUL PHELAN, Minister of State at the Department of Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec23) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) and having regard to section 28(1)(*d*) of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014) and the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 473 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0473.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the County of Louth Local Electoral Areas and Municipal Districts (Amendment) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. Article 5(c) of the County of Louth Local Electoral Areas and Municipal Districts Order 2018 (S.I. 626 of 2018) is amended by substituting “Municipal” for “Borough”.

GIVEN under my Hand,

17 January 2019

JOHN PAUL PHELAN

Minister of State at the Department of Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to amend the title of the Dundalk municipal district in accordance with the naming convention as provided for in part 3 of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) .
