# S.I. No. 10/1972 - Conditions of Employment (Aluminium Foundry Work) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOSEPH BRENNAN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Aluminium Foundry Work) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely, moulding preparation, casting, sawing, sanding, machining and heat treatment as carried on in the aluminium foundry of Upright (Ireland) Limited, Pottery Road, Kill o' the Grange, Dún Laoghaire, in the county of Dublin, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purpose of [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provisions shall have effect in relation to the hours of work worked by workers employed to do the industrial work specified in these Regulations :

- (*a*) no shift shall be longer than 10½ hours in duration;

- (*b*) no such worker shall work on two consecutive shifts;

- (*c*) no such worker shall work on any shift unless at least eight hours have elapsed since he last worked on a shift;

- (*d*) no such worker shall work for so many hours in any week that the average number of hours per week worked by him in any three consecutive weeks would exceed forty-eight;

- (*e*) no such worker shall be permitted to work on such shift work in such a manner as to contravene any conditions including conditions with regard to the time and duration of rest periods contained in the licence under section 37 of the said Act authorising such shift work.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of January, 1972.

**JOSEPH BRENNAN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these regulations is to permit Upright (Ireland) Limited to employ adult male workers on 10½ hour shifts at their premises at Pottery Road, Kill o' the Grange, Dún Laoghaire, Co. Dublin.
