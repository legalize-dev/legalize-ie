# S.I. No. 210/2006 - European Communities (Electronic Communications Networks and Services) (Framework) Regulations 2003 (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 210 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0210.html)**

**European Communities (Electronic Communications Networks and Services) (Framework) Regulations 2003 (Amendment) Regulations 2006**

I, Noel Dempsey, Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive No. 2002/21/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 March 2002,[^1] hereby make the following Regulations:

**Citation and commencement**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Electronic Communications Networks and Services) (Framework) (Amendment) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 2 May 2006.

**Principal Regulations**

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Electronic Communications Networks and Services) (Framework) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0307.html) ).

**Amendment of Regulation 2 of Principal Regulations (Interpretation)**

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2 of the Principal Regulations is amended by inserting in paragraph (1) the following definition after the definition of “Data Protection Directive”:

> “‘decision’ includes designation, determination, specification, requirement direction, notification, and notice, and any other act of an equivalent nature;”.

**Amendment of Regulation 3 of Principal Regulations (Application for appeal against decision of Regulator)**

###### 4.

4. Regulation 3 of the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (a) by substituting the following paragraphs for paragraph (1):

  > - “(1) Except as provided by paragraph (1A), this Regulation applies to a decision made or given by the Regulator under—
  >
  >     - (a) these Regulations, or
  >
  >     - (b) the Authorisation Regulations, or
  >
  >     - (c) the Access Regulations, or
  >
  >     - (d) the Universal Service Regulations, or
  >
  >     - (e) the European Communities (Electronic Communications Networks and Services) (Data Protection and Privacy) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 535 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0535.html) ), or
  >
  >     - (f) Regulation (EC) No. 2887/2000 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 December 2000 on unbundled access to the local loop.[^6]
  >
  > - (1A) This Regulation does not apply to a notification or notice given under any of the following provisions:
  >
  >     - (a) Regulation 35(1) of these Regulations;
  >
  >     - (b) Regulation 18(1) of the Access Regulations;
  >
  >     - (c) Regulation 16(2) of the Authorisation Regulations;
  >
  >     - (d) Regulation 32(2) of the Universal Service Regulations;
  >
  >     - (e) Regulation 20(1) of the European Communities (Electronic Communications Networks and Services (Data Protection and Privacy) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 535 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0535.html) );
  >
  >     - (f) section 44 of the Act of 2002.”;

- (b) by substituting the following paragraph for paragraph (3):

  > - “(3) A user or undertaking aggrieved by a decision to which this Regulation applies may appeal against the decision by lodging an appeal notification with the Minister and the Regulator.”;

- (c) in paragraph (4), by substituting the following subparagraph for subparagraph (b):

  > - “(b) state the name and address of the appellant and of the person (if any acting on behalf of the user or undertaking,”;

- (d) in paragraph (4), by substituting the following subparagraph for subparagraph (e):

  > - “(e) be made within 28 days after the decision was notified to the user or undertaking.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)**

The amending regulations widen the scope of the decisions made by the Commission for Communications Regulation (ComReg) that can be appealed to the Electronic Communications Appeals Panel (ECAP) by a user or undertaking.
