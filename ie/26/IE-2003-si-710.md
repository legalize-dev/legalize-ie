# S.I. No. 710/2003 - Risk Equalisation (Amendment) Scheme, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Micheál Martin, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/sec0003.html#sec3) and [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Health Insurance Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1994) as amended and substituted by [sections 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec9) , [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec10) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec13) of the [Health Insurance (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) (No. 17 of 2001) and [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec5) of the [Health Insurance (Amendment) Act, 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) (No. 11 of 2003), hereby make and prescribe the following Scheme-

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Risk Equalisation (Amendment) Scheme, 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Risk Equalisation Scheme, 2003 is hereby amended -

in Article 3,

- (i) by the substitution for the definition of “hospital stay” of-

  > “**‘hospital stay’** means an in-patient stay or a day-patient day or any attendance at a hospital during the provision of services described in paragraph 1(a) of the First Schedule while the insured person was maintained in accommodation other than private hospital accommodation;”, and

- (ii) by the substitution for the definition of “period” of-

  > “**‘period’**, as it relates to returns or calculations, means two consecutive quarters ending on the 30^th day of June and on the 31^st day of December in any given year;”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This scheme amends the definitions of “hospital stay” and “period” in Article 3 of the Risk Equalisation Scheme, 2003.
