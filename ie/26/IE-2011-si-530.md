# S.I. No. 530/2011 - Oireachtas (Allowances) (Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th October*, 2011.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0004.html#sec4) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of an Oireachtas Committee specified in the Schedule at any reference number, an annual allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, at the rate specified in column (3) of the Schedule opposite that reference number, in respect of any period beginning on or after 8 June 2011 during which the member held that position.

###### 3.

3. Article 6 of and Schedule 3 to the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Members and Holders of Parliamentary and Certain Ministerial Offices) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0347.html) ) are revoked.
