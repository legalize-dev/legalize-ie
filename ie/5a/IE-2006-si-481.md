# S.I. No. 481/2006 - Horse Racing On-Course Betting Office (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**REGULATIONS**

**S.I. Number 481 of 2006**

entitled

**Horse Racing On-Course Betting Office (Amendment) Regulations 2006**

Horse Racing Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Irish Horseracing Industry Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1994) and the [Horse and Greyhound Racing Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2001) hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Horse Racing On-Course Betting Office (Amendment) Regulations 2006

- (2) These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1^st day of March 2006

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“the Principal Regulations” means the Horseracing (On-Course Betting Office Regulations, 1995;

“the 1998 Amendment Regulations” means the Horseracing (On-Course Betting Office) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998;

Regulation 2 of the Principal Regulations shall apply also to the interpretation of these Regulations;

Nothing in these Regulations shall prejudice the powers and entitlement of Horse Racing Ireland under the Horseracing Industry Act 1994 and the [Horse and Greyhound Racing Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. Revocation of certain provisions of the Principal Regulations

Paragraphs 4 (a) (i) and 4 (b) of the Principal Regulations are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. Revocation of the 1998 Amendment Regulations

The 1998 Amendment Regulations are hereby revoked.

Explanatory Memorandum

These Regulations remove the restrictions previously applicable in relation to the number of on-course betting offices and the range and scope of services which such betting offices could operate.
