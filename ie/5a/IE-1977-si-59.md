# S.I. No. 59/1977 - Jury Districts Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 59 of 1977.

JURY DISTRICTS ORDER, 1977.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 15th day of April, 1977.

###### 3.

3. Part II of the Schedule to the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for—

"Jury District No. 2 (Drogheda).

Consisting of the Borough of Drogheda and the following District Electoral Divisions in the County of Louth Collon, Drumcar, Dunleer, Clogher, Dysart, Monasterboice, Mullary, Termonfeckin, St. Peter's".

of

"Jury District No. 2 (Drogheda).

Consisting of the Borough of Drogheda, the following District Electoral Divisions in the County of Louth Collon, Drumcar, Dunleer, Clogher, Dysart, Monasterboice, Mullary and Termonfeckin and the parts of the District Electoral Division of St. Peter's in the County of Louth which are not comprised in the Borough of Drogheda."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of February, 1977.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order amends Schedule II to the Jury Districts Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0057.html) ) in so far as the Jury District No. 2 (Drogheda) is concerned.
