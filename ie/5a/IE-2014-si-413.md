# S.I. No. 413/2014 - Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences) (Ontario) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th September*, 2014.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23A (inserted by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec8) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ), as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Recognition of Foreign Driving Licences) (Ontario) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. (*a*) It is declared that a licence or permit permitting a person to drive a mechanically propelled vehicle, not being a licence or permit issued to a person to enable that person to learn or provisionally to drive such a vehicle, issued by the competent authority of the Canadian province of Ontario, shall be recognised for the purpose of exchange of that licence or permit for a driving licence, in respect of any one or more of the categories in respect of which the licence or permit is held that corresponds to a category mentioned in the Table to regulation 6 of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ), as amended, subject to Decision 2014/20/EU of the European Commission on Equivalences Between Categories of Driving Licences[^1] .

- (*b*) A driving licence being granted in exchange for such a licence shall only give entitlement to drive an automatic drive vehicle in the category concerned.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 September 2014.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the driver Regulations in order to provide for recognition of mutual exchange of driving licences between Ireland and Ontario.
