S.I. No. 309 of 1997.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 4) SCHEME, 1997

BORD TELECOM plc, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 4) Scheme, 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1997" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Article 4 shall be deemed to come into operation on 2 July 1977.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Second Schedule for the Second Schedule thereof.

^(11) This refers to the time duration (in hours) within which 80% of leased lines of the same type are repaired.
