# SHANNON FREE AIRPORT DEVELOPMENT COMPANY LIMITED (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO AMEND AND EXTEND THE SHANNON FREE AIRPORT DEVELOPMENT COMPANY LIMITED ACTS, 1959 TO 1986. [7*th June*, 1989]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Interpretation.

**1.**—(1) In this Act—

“the Act of 1970” means the Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited (Amendment) Act, 1970;

“the Act of 1983” means the Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited (Amendment) Act, 1983;

“the Authority” means the Industrial Development Authority;

“the Company” means the Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited.

- (2) The Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited Acts, 1959 to 1986, and this Act shall be construed as one.

###### 2 Amendment of section 2 of Act of 1970.

**2.**—(1) Section 2 of the Act of 1970 is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (1) (as amended by the Act of 1983) of the following subsection:

> - “(1) The Minister for Industry and Commerce, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, may, out of moneys provided by the Oireachtas, make grants to the Company to enable it to meet the running expenses of the Company in respect of its functions in relation to industrial development in the counties of Limerick, Clare (excluding the airport) and Tipperary (North Riding), and in the Birr Urban District, the District Electoral Divisions of Banagher, Birr Rural, Broughal, Cloghan, Derrinboy, Derryad, Dromoyle, Drumcullen, Eglish, Ferbane, Gallen, Huntston, Kilcolman, Kilcormac, Killyon, Kinnitty, Knockbarron, Lea, Letter, Lusmagh, Mounterin, Seirkieran, Shannonbridge and Shannonharbour in the former Rural District of Birr No. 1 and the District Electoral Divisions of Aghancon, Ballincor, Barna, Cangort, Cullenwaine, Dunkerrin, Ettagh, Gorteen, Mountheaton, Roscomroe, Shinrone, Templeharry and Tulla in the former Rural District of Roscrea No. 2, all in the county of Offaly, and in the District Electoral Divisions of Abbeydorney, Arabela, Ardagh, Ardfert, Astee, Ballyseedy, Ballincloher, Ballyconry, Ballyduff, Ballyegan in the former Rural District of Listowel, Ballyegan in the former Rural District of Tralee, Ballyheige, Ballyhorgan, Ballynahaglish, Ballynorig, Banna, Beal, Blennerville, Brosna, Carrig, Castleisland, Causeway, Clogherbrien, Cloontubbrid, Cordal, Crinny, Doon, Drommartin, Duagh, Ennismore, Gneeves, Gullane, Gunsborough, Kerryhead, Kilfeighny, Kilfelin, Kilflyn, Killahan, Killehenny, Killury, Kilmeany, Kilmurry, Kilshenane, Kiltomy, Knocknagashel, Lackabaun, Leitrim, Lislaughtin, Listowel Rural, Listowel Urban, Lisselton, Lixnaw, Mount Eagle, Moynsha, Newtownsandes, Nohaval, O'Brennan, Ratass, Rathea, Shronowen, Tarbert, Tarmon, Tralee Rural, Tralee Urban, Trienearagh, Tubrid and Urlee, all in the county of Kerry, and in the county borough of Limerick.”.

###### 3 Amendment of section 4 of Act of 1970.

**3.**—Section 4 of the Act of 1970 is hereby amended by the substitution for subsection (1) (as amended by the Act of 1983) of the following subsection:

> - “(1) Notwithstanding [section 3](#3-amendment-of-section-4-of-act-of-1970) of this Act, the Authority may delegate to the Company some or all of such powers as the Authority may have to provide financial assistance to or in respect of an industrial undertaking in the counties of Limerick, Clare (excluding the airport) and Tipperary (North Riding), and in the Birr Urban District, the District Electoral Divisions of Banagher, Birr Rural, Broughal, Cloghan, Derrinboy, Derryad, Dromoyle, Drumcullen, Eglish, Ferbane, Gallen, Huntston, Kilcolman, Kilcormac, Killyon, Kinnitty, Knockbarron, Lea, Letter, Lusmagh, Mounterin, Seirkieran, Shannonbridge and Shannonharbour in the former Rural District of Birr No. 1 and the District Electoral Divisions of Aghancon, Ballincor, Barna, Cangort, Cullenwaine, Dunkerrin, Ettagh, Gorteen, Mountheaton, Roscomroe, Shinrone, Templeharry and Tulla in the former Rural District of Roscrea No. 2, all in the county of Offaly, and in the District Electoral Divisions of Abbeydorney, Arabela, Ardagh, Ardfert, Astee, Ballyseedy, Ballincloher, Ballyconry, Ballyduff, Ballyegan in the former Rural District of Listowel, Ballyegan in the former Rural District of Listowel, Ballyegan in the former Rural District of Tralee, Ballyheige, Ballyhorgan, Ballynahaglish, Ballynorig, Banna, Beal, Blennerville, Brosna, Carrig, Castleisland, Causeway, Clogherbrien, Cloontubbrid, Cordal, Crinny, Doon, Drommartin, Duagh, Ennismore, Gneeves, Gullane, Gunsborough, Kerryhead, Kilfeighny, Kilfelin, Kilflyn, Killahan, Killehenny, Killury, Kilmeany, Kilmurry, Kilshenane, Kiltomy, Knocknagashel, Lackabaun, Leitrim, Lislaughtin, Listowel Rural, Listowel Urban, Lisselton, Lixnaw, Mount Eagle, Moynsha, Newtownsandes, Nohaval, O'Brennan, Ratass, Rathea, Shronowen, Tarbert, Tarmon, Tralee Rural, Tralee Urban, Trienearagh, Tubrid and Urlee, all in the county of Kerry, and in the county borough of Limerick.”.

###### 4 Short title and collective citation.

**4.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited (Amendment) Act, 1989.

- (2) The Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited Acts, 1959 to 1986, and this Act may be cited together as the Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited Acts, 1959 to 1989.
