S.I. No. 223 of 1995.

MINERALS DEVELOPMENT (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, MICHAEL LOWRY, Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0031/sec0080.html#sec80) of the [Minerals Development Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1940), and the Energy (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 12 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0012.html) ) (as adapted by the Energy (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 16 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0016.html) ) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Minerals Development (Amendment) Regulations, 1995, and shall come into operation on the 1st day of September, 1995.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Minerals Development Regulations, 1979 and 1994, may be cited together as the Minerals Development Regulations, 1979 to 1995, and shall be construed together as one.

###### 3.

3. An application under [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0031/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Minerals Development Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1940), for a prospecting licence in respect of gypsum on or under the townlands specified in the First Schedule to these Regulations shall be made, not later than 5 p.m. on the 31st day of October, 1995, to the Minister for Transport, Energy and Communications in accordance with the Form set out in the Second Schedule to these Regulations and shall not be made in accordance with Form No. 1 set out in the First Schedule to the Minerals Development Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 340 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0340.html) ) (as amended by the Minerals Development (Amendment) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 319 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0319.html) )).
