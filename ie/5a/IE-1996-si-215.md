# S.I. No. 215/1996 - Local Government Act, 1991 (Commencement) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 215 of 1996.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT ACT, 1991 (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 1996.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0001.html#sec1) (10) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1991), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Commencement) Order, 1996.

###### 2.

2. Part V of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) , as amended by the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), shall come into operation on the 16th day of September, 1996.

###### 3.

3. Section 4(1) of and the Schedule to the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) shall come into operation to the extent specified in the Schedule to this Order on the 16th day of September, 1996.

###### 4.

4. [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0004.html#sec4) (2) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) shall, insofar as the same has not been brought into operation, come into operation on the 16th day of September, 1996.
