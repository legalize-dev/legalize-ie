# S.I. No. 89/2001 - Nursing Homes (Subvention) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for Health and Children by [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0023/sec0007.html#sec7) (2) of the [Health (Nursing Homes) Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1990) (as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) )), which said powers are delegated to me by the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0032.html) ) I, Dr. Tom Moffatt T.D., Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children hereby make the following Regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Nursing Homes (Subvention) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 2001.

###### 3.

3. The Nursing Homes (Subvention) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 227 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0227.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution for the Fourth Schedule of the following:-

FOURTH SCHEDULE

MAXIMUM RATES OF SUBVENTION (PER WEEK)

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level of Dependency (of the person seeking a subvention) | Maximum Rates of Subvention |  |
|  | (Per Week) | €Euro |
| As assessed under the First Schedule Dependency Assessment Procedure | As per level of dependency |  |
| Medium Dependency | £90 | €114.30 |
| High Dependency | £120 | €152.40 |
| Maximum Dependency | £150 | €190.50 |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

These Regulations amend the Nursing Homes (Subvention) Regulations, 1993, ( [S.I. No. 227 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0227.html) ) by increasing the rates of subvention payable from £70 to £90 in respect of medium dependency, from £95 to £120 in respect of high dependency and from £120 to £150 in respect of maximum dependency.
