# S.I. No. 224/1997 - Dublin Institute of Technology Act, 1992 (Assignment of Function) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 224 of 1997.

DUBLIN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ACT, 1992 (ASSIGNMENT OF FUNCTION) ORDER, 1997.

I, NIAMH BHREATHNACH, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5 (2) (a) of the Dublin Institute of Technology Act, 1992 (No. 15 of 1992), and with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as Dublin Institute of Technology Act, 1992 (Assignment of Function) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. With effect from the 1st day of September, 1998, there is hereby assigned to the Dublin Institute of Technology the function of conferring degrees, postgraduate degrees and honorary awards.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of May, 1997.

**NIAMH BHREATHNACH,**

Minister for Education.

The Minister for Finance hereby concurs with the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 27th day of May, 1997.

**RUAIR QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.
