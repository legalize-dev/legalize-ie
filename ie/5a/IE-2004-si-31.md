# S.I. No. 31/2004 - Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) (Revocation) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec47) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) (Revocation) Regulations 2004.

2 The Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 481 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0481.html) ) are revoked.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of January, 2004. |
|  | MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Health and Children. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) (Revocation) Regulations 2004.

The effect of these Regulations is to revoke the Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) Regulations 2003.
