# S.I. No. 418/1985 - Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 418 of 1985.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANISATION (SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS) (DUBLIN) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1985.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1985) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provision) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0128.html) ) is hereby amended by:—

- (*a*) the substitution in paragraph ( *f*) of "31st day of March, 1986" for "1st day of January, 1986", and

- (*b*) the substitution in paragraph (*g*) of "31st day of March, 1986" for "1st day of January, 1986".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

13th day of December, 1985.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the continuation in effect until the 31st day of March, 1986 of certain provisions of several enactments in the added areas and in the excluded areas as defined in [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
