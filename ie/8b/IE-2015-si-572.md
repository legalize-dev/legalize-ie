# S.I. No. 572/2015 - European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th December*, 2015.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2014/79/EU of 20 June 2014[^1] and Commission Directive 2014/81/EU of 23 June 2014[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 21 December 2015.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Safety of Toys) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0014.html) ) are amended, in Regulation 4(1), by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “Toy Safety Directive”:

> “ ‘Toy Safety Directive’ means Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^3] , amended by—
>
> - (*a*) Commission Directive 2012/7/EU of 2 March 2012[^4] ,
>
> - (*b*) Commission Regulation (EU) No 681/2013 of 17 July 2013[^5] ,
>
> - (*c*) Commission Directive 2014/79/EU of 20 June 20141,
>
> - (*d*) Commission Directive 2014/81/EU of 23 June 20142,
>
> - (*e*) Commission Directive 2014/84/EU of 30 June 2014[^6] .”.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 274 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0274.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 December 2015.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Directive 2014/79/EU of 20 June 2014 and Commission Directive 2014/81/EU of 23 June 2014, which amend (for the purpose of adaptation to technical progress) Appendix C of Annex II to Directive 2009/48/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009 relating to toy safety, by adapting the limit value of chemicals TCEP, TCPP, TDCP and Bisphenol A permitted in toys and toy components.
