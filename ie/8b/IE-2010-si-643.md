# S.I. No. 643/2010 - Taxes (Publication of Names of Tax Defaulters) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st December*, 2010.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4A) (inserted by [section 143](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec143) of the [Finance Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2005)) of [section 1086](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec1086.html#sec1086) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), hereby order, in accordance with paragraph (*c*) of that subsection, as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Taxes (Publication of Names of Tax Defaulters) Order 2010.

###### 2.

2. For the purposes of [section 1086](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/sec1086.html#sec1086) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), the amount of €33,000 is specified as the amount in lieu of the amount of €30,000 referred to in subsection (4)(*c*) of that section.

###### 3.

3. The amount specified in paragraph 2–—

- (*a*) takes effect from 1 January 2010, and

- (*b*) does not apply to any case in which the specified liability referred to in paragraphs (*c*) and (*d*) of section 1086(2) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) includes tax, the liability in respect of which arose before, or which relates to periods which commenced before, 1 January 2010.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 December 2010.

**BRIAN LEHIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.
