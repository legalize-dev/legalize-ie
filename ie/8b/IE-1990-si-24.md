# S.I. No. 24/1990 - Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 24 of 1990.

ROAD TRAFFIC (REMOVAL, STORAGE AND DISPOSAL OF VEHICLES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and section 97 of the said Act as substituted by [section 63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990.

###### 2.

2. Sub-Article (1) of Article 7 of the Road Traffic (Removal, Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0091.html) ) is hereby amended.

( *a* ) by the substitution of "£50" for "£20" in paragraph (*a*) and

( *b* ) by the substitution of "£15" for "£2" in paragraph (*b*).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into force on the 30th day of January 1990.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 26th day of January, 1990.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Removal Storage and Disposal of Vehicles) Regulations, 1983 by increasing from £20 to £50 the charge for the release of a vehicle payable to a road authority or the Garda Commissioner by the owner of a vehicle which has been towed away in accordance with the Regulations and by increasing from £2 to £15 per day the storage charge payable for the period during which such vehicles have been stored.
