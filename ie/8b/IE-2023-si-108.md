# S.I. No. 108/2023 - European Union (Passenger Ships) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th March,* 2023.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2009/45/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 May 2009[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Passenger Ships) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 2 to the European Union (Passenger Ships) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 676 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0676.html) ) is amended in the Sea Area entry entitled West Coast of Ireland, by substituting for the second row (in relation to “Ballaghaline Point to Trawkeera Point”) the following two rows:

> “
>
> |  |  |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Hag’s Head to Ballaghaline Point | X | X | ✓ | X | Doolin to Cliffs of Moher |
> | Ballaghaline Point to Trawkeera Point | X | X | ✓* | X | * Doolin to Inisheer from 1 March to 31 October |
>
> ”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 March, 2023.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the legal instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend Schedule 2 to the European Union (Passenger Ships) Regulations 2019, which give effect to Directive 2009/45/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on safety rules and standards for passenger ships, by updating the Sea Areas for the West Coast of Ireland.
