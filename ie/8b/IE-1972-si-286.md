# S.I. No. 286/1972 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 52, Ninth Period) (Miscellaneous Textile Piece Goods) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 286 of 1972.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA No. 52, NINTH PERIOD) (MISCELLANEOUS TEXTILE PIECE GOODS) ORDER, 1972.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows :—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 52, Ninth Period) (Miscellaneous Textile Piece Goods) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints 12,000,000 square yards as the quantity of textile piece goods (other than of wool), originating in the countries listed below, which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1973, to 31st December, 1973. The Countries to which the Instrument applies are as follows :—

Japan

Czechoslovakia

Roumania

India

Malaysia

Macao

People's Republic of China

Hungary

Hong-Kong

China (Nationalist)

Bulgaria

Poland

East Germany

Pakistan

Yugoslavia

**U.S.S.R.**
