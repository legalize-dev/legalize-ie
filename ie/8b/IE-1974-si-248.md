# S.I. No. 248/1974 - Aliens Orders, 1974 (Revocation) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 248 of 1974.

ALIENS ORDERS, 1974 (REVOCATION) ORDER, 1974.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (7) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Aliens Orders, 1974 (Revocation) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Orders listed in the Schedule to this Order are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 8th day of August, 1974.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

Minister for Justice.
