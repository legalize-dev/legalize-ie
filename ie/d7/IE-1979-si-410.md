# S.I. No. 410/1979 - Marketing of Non-Eec Fertilisers Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 410 of 1979.

MARKETING OF NON-EEC FERTILISERS REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, RAY MacSHARRY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3, 8 and 11 of the Fertilisers, Feeding Stuffs and Mineral Mixtures Act, 1955 (No. 8 of 1955), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Marketing of Non-EEC Fertilisers Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the Marketing of Non-EEC Fertilisers Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 248 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0248.html) ), is hereby amended by

( *a* ) the substitution for the definition of

"the Commission Directive" of the following definition:

> " 'the Commission Directive' means Commission Directive No. 77/535/EEC of 22 June, 1977^1 as amended by Commission Directive No. 79/138/EEC of 14 December, 1978^2;"

^1OJ No. L 213/1, 22.8.1977.

^2OJ No. L 39/3, 14.2.1979.

and

( *b* ) the substitution for the definition of

"the Sampling and Analysis Regulations" of the following definition:

> " 'the Sampling and Analysis Regulations' means the European Communities (Sampling and Analysis of Fertilisers) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 12 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0012.html) ), as amended by the European Communities (Sampling and Analysis of Fertilisers) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 409 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0409.html) );".

###### 3.
