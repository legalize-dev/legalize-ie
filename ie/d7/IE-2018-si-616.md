# S.I. No. 616/2018 - County of Fingal Local Electoral Areas Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th January*, 2019.

I, JOHN PAUL PHELAN, Minister of State at the Department of Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec23) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) and having regard to [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec28) (1)(*d*) of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014) and the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 473 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0473.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the County of Fingal Local Electoral Areas Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. (1) Fingal shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area described in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of Fingal County Council to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set out in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division shall be construed as referring to such electoral division as existing at the date of this Order.

###### 4.

4. (1) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along a motorway or dual carriageway shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre median of such motorway or dual carriageway.

- (2) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along a road or railway line shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road or railway line.

- (3) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which a line intersects or joins a boundary or junction shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the line would intersect or join such boundary or junction.

###### 5.

5. The County of Fingal Local Electoral Areas Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 47 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0047.html) ) is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of the County of Fingal into local electoral areas and to fix the number of members of Fingal County Council to be elected for each such area.
