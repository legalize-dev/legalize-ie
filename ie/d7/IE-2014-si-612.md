# S.I. No. 612/2014 - Health Insurance Act 1994 (Minimum Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th January*, 2015.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec4) of the [Health Insurance (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2012)) and section 10 (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec4) of the [Voluntary Health Insurance (Amendment) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2008)) of the [Health Insurance Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1994), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Insurance Act 1994 (Minimum Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January 2015.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the [Health Insurance Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Minimum Benefit) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 83 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0083.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Schedule A to the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph 1, by the substitution of the following sub-paragraph for sub-paragraph (2):

> “
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 2 | Prescribed health services which are in-patient services provided by a publicly-funded hospital. | The amount of the charge payable under [Section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0055.html#sec55) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . |
>
> ”.

###### 4.

4. Schedule A to the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph 1, by the substitution of the following sub-paragraph for sub-paragraph (4):

> “
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 4 | Prescribed health services which are day-patient services provided by a publicly-funded hospital. | The amount of the charge payable under [Section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0055.html#sec55) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . |
>
> ”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

23 December 2014.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations clarify that the amount of the charge payable under [Section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0055.html#sec55) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) is payable in respect of services provided in a publicly funded hospital.
