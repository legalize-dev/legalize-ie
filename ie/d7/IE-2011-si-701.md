# S.I. No. 701/2011 - Dog Breeding Establishments (Prescribed Fees and Forms) Regulations, 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd January*, 2012.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec3) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec7) of the [Dog Breeding Establishments Act, 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2010) (as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Dog Breeding Establishments (Prescribed Fees and Forms) Regulations, 2011.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 2nd day of January, 2012.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “the Act” means the [Dog Breeding Establishment Act, 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2010).

###### 4.

4. The prescribed amount for the purpose of section 7(1)(*b*) of the Act is €60.

###### 5.

5. The form set out in the Schedule is the prescribed form for the purposes of the fixed payment notice under section 7(1) of the Act.
