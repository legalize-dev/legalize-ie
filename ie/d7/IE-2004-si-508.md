# S.I. No. 508/2004 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 508 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0508.html) .**

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT 1966 (CONTROL ON ANIMAL AND POULTRY VACCINES) (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2004.

[S.I. No. 508 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0508.html) .

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT 1966 (CONTROL ON ANIMAL AND POULTRY VACCINES) (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2004.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 and 13 of [the Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), for the purpose, inter alia, of giving further effect to Article 71 of Directive 2001/82/EC of the European Parliament and the Council of 6 November 2001[^1] , hereby order as follows—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as [the Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. [The Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) is amended in Part 1 of the Second Schedule by—

- (*a*) the substitution for “Fowl pest in any of its forms, including Newcastle Disease and fowl plague” of “Avian influenza”, and

- (*b*) the deletion of “Infectious bursal disease (Gumboro disease)” and “Rabies”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 5th day of August, 2004. |
|  | JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the removal of certain diseases from Part 1 of the Second Schedule of [the Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ).
