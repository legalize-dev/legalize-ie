# S.I. No. 722/2007 - Taxi Regulation Act 2003 (Fixed Charges offences) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 722 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0722.html)

TAXI REGULATION ACT 2003 (FIXED CHARGES OFFENCES) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th October*, 2007.

The Commission for Taxi Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec46) of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2003) hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Fixed Charges Offences) Regulations 2007.

###### 2.

2. An offence under Part 3 of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2003) consisting of a contravention of a provision of the Regulations referred to in Schedule 1 is declared to be a fixed charge offence.

###### 3.

3. The amount of €250 is prescribed for the fixed charges offences referred to in Schedule 1 for the purposes of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec46) of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) .

###### 4.

4. The form of notice set out in Schedule 2 is prescribed as the form of notice for the purposes of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec46) of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) .

###### 5.

5. The [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Fixed Charges Offences) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 494 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0494.html) ) is revoked.

**EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations declare contraventions of certain Regulations to be fixed charges offences. These are:

- •Operation of more than one small public service vehicle licence in respect of a single vehicle;

- •Removal or attempt to remove tamper-proof disc;

- •Failure to display tamper-proof disc;

- •Failure to display the relevant in-vehicle information in a small public service vehicle;

- •Removal or attempt to remove required in-vehicle information;

- •Unreasonable refusal to carry a passenger for a journey of not more than 30 kilometres;

- •Failure to have taximeter properly calibrated in line with the current maximum fare;

- •Operating taximeter while taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi standing for hire or plying for hire;

- •Failure to operate the taximeter while the taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi is on hire

- •Failure to comply with requirements in relation to the fitting and operation of the taximeter;

- •Charging a booking fee when plying for hire or standing for hire or, without having been pre-booked by a consumer, at an airport, transport station or other location;

- •Standing at an appointed stand while vehicle is not available for hire;

- •Standing for hire in a taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi otherwise than at an appointed stand;

- •Plying for hire or standing for hire in a hackney or limousine;

- •Refusal to carry a guide dog or other assistance dog;

- •Failure to provide a required receipt in respect of a taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi;

- •Failure to provide a required receipt in respect of a hackney or limousine;

- •Operating as a taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi without having demonstrated satisfactory knowledge of the local area;

- •Failure to comply with roof sign requirements for taxis and wheelchair accessible taxis, including failure to display current licence number within 28 days of the date of issue of a new licence number;

- •Failure to display required driver identification;

- •Displaying forged driver identification on a SPSV;

- •Displaying on a hackney or limousine a sign or advertisement other than one prescribed by the Commission;

All fixed charges offences declared by these Regulations attract a fixed charge of €250. The Regulations prescribe the form of notice for the purposes of enforcement of the fixed charge offences.
