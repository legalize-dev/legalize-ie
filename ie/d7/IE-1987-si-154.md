# S.I. No. 154/1987 - Trustee Savings Banks (Interest on Deposits in Special Account) (No. 2) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 154 of 1987.

TRUSTEE SAVINGS BANKS (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS IN SPECIAL ACCOUNT) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1987.

I, RAY MAC SHARRY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (2) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trustee Savings Banks (Interest on Deposits in Special Account) (No. 2) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 22nd day of June, 1987.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on moneys paid by the trustees of every trustee savings bank into a special account referred to in [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/sec0031.html#sec31) (3) of the [Finance Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1940), is hereby prescribed as 7.97 per cent. per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Trustee Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits in Special Account) (No. 1) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 37 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0037.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of June, 1987.

**RAY MAC SHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the rate of interest payable on moneys paid by the trustees of every trustee savings bank into the special account in the name of the Minister for Finance as 7.97 per cent. per annum as from 22nd June, 1987.
