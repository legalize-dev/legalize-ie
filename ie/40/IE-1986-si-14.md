# S.I. No. 14/1986 - Dublin City Management (Reserved Functions) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 14 of 1986.

DUBLIN CITY MANAGEMENT (RESERVED FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1986.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (2) of [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0027/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1930), hereby directs and orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin City Management (Reserved Functions) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. As on and from the 27th day of January, 1986—

( *a* ) the power of Dublin County Borough Corporation to make charges for services provided in respect of domestic premises by virtue of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) (No. 2) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1983), and

( *b* ) the power of Dublin County Borough Corporation to make charges for water supplied for domestic purposes by virtue of section 65A of the Public Health (Ireland) Act, 1878, inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0026/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Local Government (Sanitary Services) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1962), as amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) (No. 2) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1983),

shall be exercisable and performable directly by the Dublin City Council.

###### 3.

3. In this order "domestic premises" means a premises used wholly or mainly as a dwelling.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 21st day of January 1986.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order transfers the power to make charges for services provided in respect of domestic premises situated in the Dublin County Borough Corporation area from the Manager to the elected members.
