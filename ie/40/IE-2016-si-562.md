# S.I. No. 562/2016 - Companies Act 2014 (Commencement) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th November*, 2016.

I, MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec1) (2) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No 38 of 2014), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Commencement) Order 2016.

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Companies Act 2014 (Commencement) Order 2015 ( [S.I. No. 169 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0169.html) ) is amended by the deletion of paragraph (*a*) from Article 3.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 November 2016.

**MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.
