# S.I. No. 342/1991 - Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, RAY BURKE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 13 (as amended by the [Garda Síochána Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1977), of the [Garda Síochána Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1924), and [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), hereby with the approval of the Government make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1983" means the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 151 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0151.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Second Schedule to the Garda Síochána (Associations) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0135.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution—

( *a* ) in paragraph 3 (8) (amended by the Regulations of 1983) for "biennially" of "triennially",

( *b* ) in paragraph 5 (1) (inserted by the Regulations of 1983) for "biennially" of "triennially", and

( *c* ) in paragraph 6 (1) (amended by the Regulations of 1983) for "biennially" of "triennially".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 12th day of December, 1991.

**RAY BURKE**

Minister for Justice.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 12th day

of December, 1991.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY**

Taoiseach.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Garda Síochána (Associations) Regulations, 1978 and the Garda Síochána (Associations) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983.
