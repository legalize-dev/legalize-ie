# S.I. No. 294/1981 - Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) The collective citation “the Telephone Regulations, 1980 and 1981,” shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. (1) These Regulations in so far as they specify connection charges for items of telephone equipment, subsidiary apparatus, private wires and tie lines shall come into operation on the 1st day of September, 1981.

- (2) These Regulations in so far as they provide for matters other than those mentioned in paragraph (1), of this Regulation shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1981.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) , (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations “the Regulations of 1981” means the Telephone (Amendment) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 106 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0106.html) ).
