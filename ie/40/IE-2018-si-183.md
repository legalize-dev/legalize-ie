# S.I. No. 183/2018 - European Union (End of Life Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st June*, 2018.

I, DENIS NAUGHTEN, Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2000/53/EC[^1] of the European Union and of the Council of 18 September 2000, as amended, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (End-of-Life Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (End-of-Life Vehicles) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No 281 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0281.html) ) are amended by substituting the following definition:

> “ ‘the Directive’ means the European Parliament and Council Directive 2000/53/EC of 18 September, 2000 on end-of-life vehicles as amended by Commission Decision 2002/525/EC[^2] of 27 June 2002 and Commission Decision 2005/438/EC[^3] of 10 June 2005 and Council Decision 2005/673/EC[^4] of 20 September 2005 and Council Directive 2008/33/EC[^5] of 11 March 2008 and Commission Decision 2008/689/EC[^6] of 1 August 2008 and Council Directive 2008/112/EC[^7] of 16 December 2008 and Commission Decision 2010/115/EU[^8] of 23 February 2010 and Commission Directive 2011/37/EU[^9] of 30 March 2011 and Commission Directive 2013/28/EU[^10] of 17 May 2013, Commission Directive 2016/774/EU of 18 May 2016[^11] and Commission Directive (EU) 2017/2096 of 15 November 2017[^12] ;”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 May 2018.

**DENIS NAUGHTEN,**

Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the European Union (End-of-Life Vehicles) Regulations 2014 in order to give effect to Commission Directive 2017/2096/EU of 15 November 2017 amending Annex II Commission Directive 2000/53 of the European Parliament and of the Council on end-of-life vehicles. The effect of these Regulations is to restrict the use of lead in the manufacturing of materials and components of vehicles where lead-free alternatives are available.
