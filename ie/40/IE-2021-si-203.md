# S.I. No. 203/2021 - European Union (Markets in Financial Instruments) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th April,* 2021.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^1] as amended by Directive (EU) 2020/1504 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 October 2020[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Markets in Financial Instruments) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 10 November 2021.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 4 of the European Union (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 375 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0375.html) ) is amended in paragraph (1) –

- (*a*) by the substitution of the following subparagraph for subparagraph (t):

  > - “(t) branches of third country firms established in the State, save as otherwise provided for in Regulation 48;”, and

- (*b*) by the insertion of the following subparagraph after subparagraph (t):

  > - “(u) crowdfunding service providers as defined in point (e) of Article 2(1) of Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 of the European Parliament and of the Council.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 April, 2021.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations give effect to Directive (EU) 2020/1504 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 October 2020 amending Directive 2014/65/EU on markets in financial instruments.

Directive (EU) 2020/1504 provides that the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive (Directive 2014/65/EU or MiFID II) does not apply to crowdfunding service providers as defined in the Crowdfunding Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2020/1503).
