# S.I. No. 2/2002 - Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Building Control Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002.

###### 2.

2. (1) A body to be known as the Building Regulations Advisory Body, hereinafter referred to as “the Advisory Body”, is hereby appointed.

- (2) The Advisory Body shall, for the period commencing on the 9^th day of January 2002 and expiring on the 31^st day of December, 2006, consist of the persons named in the Schedule to this Order.

3 The Chairperson of the Advisory Body for the period commencing on the 9^th day of January, 2002 and expiring on the 31^st day of December, 2006, shall be Professor Owen Lewis.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order appoints the Building Regulations Advisory Body under [Section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Building Control Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) for a 5 year period.
