# S.I. No. 422/1985 - Waterford City Management (Reserved Functions) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 422 of 1985.

WATERFORD CITY MANAGEMENT (RESERVED FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1985.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (2) of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0025/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Waterford City Management Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (no. 25 of 1939), hereby directs and orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Waterford City Management (Reserved Functions) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into effect on the 1st day of January, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The power of Waterford County Borough Corporation to make charges for services provided in respect of domestic premises by virtue of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) (no. 2) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1983) shall be exercisable and performable by the Waterford City Council.

###### 4.

4. The power of Waterford County Borough Corporation to make charges for water supplied for domestic purposes by virtue of section 65A of the Public Health (Ireland) Act, 1878 (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0026/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Local Government (Sanitary Services) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (no. 26 of 1962)), as amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) (no. 2) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (no. 21 of 1983) shall be exercisable and performable directly by the Waterford City Council.

###### 5.

5. In this order "domestic premises" means a premises used wholly or mainly as a dwelling.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

17th day of December 1985.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister of the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order transfers the power to make charges for the services provided in respect of domestic premises situated in the Waterford County Borough Corporation area from the Manager to the elected members.
