S.I. No. 232 of 1997.

CRIMINAL JUSTICE (LEGAL AID) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1997

I, NORA OWEN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 10 (1) (*b*) of the [Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1962) and in so far as the following Regulations relate to rates or scales of payment of fees with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) (Amendment) Regulations, 1997.

- (2) The Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1965 to 1996, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1965 to 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations-

"the Act" means the [Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) ;

"the No. 2 Regulations of 1993" means the Criminal Justice (Legal Aid) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0057.html) ).

- (2) In these Regulations a reference to a Schedule is a reference to a Schedule to these Regulations unless it is indicated that reference to some other Regulations is intended.

###### 3.

3. The fees, payable under the Act, of a solicitor assigned in relation to a case in pursuance of a certificate for free legal aid for cases in the District Court and appeals to the Circuit Court, for bail applications to the Circuit Court or Special Criminal Court which are contested by the prosecution and for essential visits to prisons and other custodial centres (other than Garda Stations), shall be -

( *a* ) with effect from the 1st day of January, 1994, those set out in the First Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the First Schedule to the No. 2 Regulations of 1993),

( *b* ) with effect from the 1st day of June, 1994, those set out in the Third Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the First Schedule),

( *c* ) with effect from the 1st day of June, 1995, those set out in the Fifth Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Third Schedule),

( *d* ) with effect from the 1st day of June, 1996, those set out in the Seventh Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Fifth Schedule),

( *e* ) with effect from the 1st day of October, 1996, those set out in the Ninth Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Seventh Schedule).

###### 4.

4. The fees, payable under the Act, of counsel assigned in relation to a case in pursuance of a certificate for free legal aid for bail applications to the Circuit Court or the Special Criminal Court which are contested by the prosecution and for essential visits to prisons and other custodial centres (other than Garda Stations), shall be—

( *a* ) with effect from the 1st day of January, 1994, those set out in the Second Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Second Schedule to the No. 2 Regulations of 1993),

( *b* ) with effect from the 1st day of June, 1994, those set out in the Fourth Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Second Schedule),

( *c* ) with effect from the 1st day of June, 1995, those set out in the Sixth Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Fourth Schedule),

( *d* ) with effect from the 1st day of June, 1996, those set out in the Eight Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Sixth Schedule),

( *e* ) with effect from the 1st day of October, 1996, those set out in the Tenth Schedule (in lieu of those set out in the Eighth Schedule).

###### 5.

5. Regulations 4 and 5 of, and the First and Second Schedules to, the No. 2 Regulations of 1993 are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for an increase in the fees payable under the criminal legal aid scheme to solicitors for attendance in the District Court and for appeals to the Circuit Court, and for an increase to solicitors and counsel in respect of visits to prisons and other custodial centres (other than Garda Stations) and for certain bail applications, as follows:

3.75% with effect from 1st January, 1994,

2% with effect from 1st June, 1994,

2% with effect from 1st June, 1995,

1.5% with effect from 1st June, 1996 and

1.5% with effect from 1st October, 1996.
