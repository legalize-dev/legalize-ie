# S.I. No. 13/2011 - National Minimum Wage Act 2000 (Section 11) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st January*, 2011.

I, BATT O’KEEFFE, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1) of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec11) (inserted by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec13) of the [Financial Emergency Measures in the Public Interest Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2010)) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0185.html) )), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the National Minimum Wage Act 2000 (Section 11) Order 2011.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 1 February 2011.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that the national minimum hourly rate of pay for the purposes of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) shall be €7.65.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 January 2011.

**BATT O’KEEFFE,**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order sets the national minimum hourly rate of pay from 1 February 2011 at €7.65.
