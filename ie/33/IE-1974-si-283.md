# S.I. No. 283/1974 - Money Order Amendment (No. 20) Regulations, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 283 of 1974.

MONEY ORDER AMENDMENT (No. 20) REGULATIONS, 1974.

I, CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23 of the Post Office Act, 1908, and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Post Office (Amendment) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1969), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Money Order Amendment (No. 20) Regulations, 1974.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1974.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 18 (1) of the Money Order Regulations, 1903, is hereby amended by the substitution of the following tables for Tables A, B, and C (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Money Order Amendment (No. 19) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 35 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0035.html) )) which set out the poundage and fees payable in respect of money orders:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| "A | Poundage |
| In respect of inland money orders: |  |
| for an amount not exceeding £50.00 | 20p |
| for an amount exceeding £50.00 but not exceeding £100.00 | 25p |
| B | Poundage |
| In respect of foreign money orders: |  |
| for an amount not exceeding £10.00 | 20p |
| for an amount exceeding £10.00 but not exceeding £20.00 | 35p |
| for an amount exceeding £20.00 but not exceeding £30.00 | 50p |
| for an amount exceeding £30.00 but not exceeding £40.00 | 65p |
| for an amount exceeding £40.00 but not exceeding £50.00 | 80p |
| C | Poundage |
| In respect of supplementary services: |  |
| for correcting or altering the name of the remitter or payee | 5p |
| for stopping payment | 10p |
| for the issue of a duplicate money order (including stopping payment of the original order) | 20p |
| for the renewal of a void money order | 20p |
| for an advice or certificate of payment of an inland money order | 5p |
| for an advice or certificate of payment of a foreign money order | 5p". |

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 20th day of September, 1974.

**CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the provisions of the foregoing Warrant.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 20th day of

September, 1974.

**RICHIE RYAN.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Warrant applies to the Money Order service and comes into operation on the 1st October, 1974. It authorises increases on charges on inland and foreign money orders and on other miscellaneous services.
