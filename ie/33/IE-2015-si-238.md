# S.I. No. 238/2015 - Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th June*, 2015.

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on the said Society by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec11) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 532 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0532.html) ), with the consent of the Minister of State at the Department of Health, hereby makes the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2015.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed as one with the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) ) and may be cited together with those Rules as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 and 2015.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on 24 June 2015.

###### 3.

3. The Schedule to the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 is amended by inserting after paragraph 41 the following paragraphs:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Rules amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) ) to provide for fees payable to the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland in respect of the ISS supply list.

The ISS supply list was established, and is maintained, by the Society pursuant to Regulation 19A (as inserted by Regulation 8 of the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 87 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0087.html) )) of the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 540 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0540.html) ) and section 7(1)(*f*) (as inserted by the European Union (Amendment of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) ) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 86 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0086.html) )) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007).

These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2015.
