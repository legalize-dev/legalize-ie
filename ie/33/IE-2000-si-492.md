# S.I. No. 492/2000 - Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Form of Certificate of Plant Breeders' Rights) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0015.html#sec15) (2) and (7) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1998) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Form of Certificate of Plant Breeders' Rights) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby prescribed that the certificate referred to in [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0015.html#sec15) (2)(*a*) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1998), shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The Plant Breeders' Rights (Form of Certificate) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0019.html) ), are revoked.

## SCHEDULE Office of the Controller of Plant Breeders' Rights

CERTIFICATE OF PLANT BREEDERS' RIGHTS

WHEREAS the Controller of Plant Breeders' Rights has granted plant breeders' rights to _________________________, of _______________________, on _________, in respect of the variety of plant genus or species specified in the Schedule hereto.

NOW, the said Controller hereby issues under [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0015.html#sec15) (2) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1998), this certificate in respect of that variety, which may be exercisable during the period of ____________ years from the date of issue of this certificate.
