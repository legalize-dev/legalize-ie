# S.I. No. 13/1977 - Factories Act 1955 (Hoists) (Exemption) Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 13 of 1977.

FACTORIES ACT 1955 (HOISTS) (EXEMPTION) ORDER, 1977.

I, MICHAEL O'LEARY, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the power conferred on me by subsection (13) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1955), and it having been shown to my satisfaction that it would be unreasonable in the special circumstances of the case to enforce certain requirements of the said section 33 in respect of a certain class or description of hoists, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Hoists) (Exemption) Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The requirements of subsections (3), (4) and (7) (*c*) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1955), shall not apply as respects the class or description of hoists specified in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE

Hoists and lifts, used as working platforms, not of a movable type, which do not pass through any floor, and which are used in the butchering of animals.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of January, 1977.

**MICHAEL O'LEARY,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides that the provisions of subsections (3), (4) and (7) (*c*) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , which require hoistways to be protected by an adequate enclosure fitted with gates, shall not apply to hoistways of certain types used in the butchering of animals.
