# S.I. No. 333/2021 - Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th July,* 2021.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1962) and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications, Climate Action and Environment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 373 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0373.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2021.

###### 2.

2. The Control of Fishing for Salmon Order ( [S.I. no 102 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0102.html) ) is amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for Schedule 1:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Order amends the Control of Fishing for Salmon Order 2021 ( [S.I. No. 102 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0102.html) ) by substitution of a new Schedule for Schedule 1. The effect of this amendment is to increase the number of District Draft Net Licences available in the Letterkenny District from 2 to 3.
