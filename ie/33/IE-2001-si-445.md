# S.I. No. 445/2001 - National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. There is delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency the functions of the Minister for Finance under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec2) of the [Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2001), a reference to which was inserted into the First Schedule to the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec3) of the [Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) .
