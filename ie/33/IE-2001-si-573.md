# S.I. No. 573/2001 - Local Government (Water Pollution) (Fees) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/sec0009.html#sec9) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1977), by sections 6, 7, 17 and 19 of that Act as amended respectively by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0004.html#sec4) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) , [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0013.html#sec13) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) (Amendment) Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1990), and by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) as substituted by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Local Government (Water Pollution) (Amendment) Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 428 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0428.html) ), I, Dan Wallace, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby make the following Regulations:**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as Local Government (Water Pollution)(Fees) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations, the Local Government (Water Pollution) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 108 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0108.html) ), the Local Government (Water Pollution)(Nutrient Management Planning and Consultation) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 257 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0257.html) ), and the Local Government (Water Pollution) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 271 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0271.html) ) as amended by the Local Government (Water Pollution) (Amendment) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0184.html) ) and the Local Government (Water Pollution)(Amendment) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 42 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0042.html) ), shall be construed together as one and may be collectively cited as the Local Government (Water Pollution) Regulations, 1978 to 2001.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2002.

###### 3.

3. The Local Government (Water Pollution) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 271 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0271.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution of —

- (a) “€380” for “£300” in articles 7(2) and 8(2)

- (b) “€126” for “£100” in article 14(2)

- (c) “€63” for “£50” in articles 14(3) and 16(3)

- (d) “€38” for “£30” in article 18(4), and

- (e) “€6” for “£5” in article 38.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note does not form part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for conversion to convenient euro (€) amounts of fees payable under the Local Government (Water Pollution) Acts. The new fees will take effect on 1 January 2002 and have in all cases been rounded down on conversion in favour of the consumer.
