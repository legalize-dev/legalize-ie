# S.I. No. 352/2015 - Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th August*, 2015.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Central Bank of Ireland (the “Bank”) by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , the Bank hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) is hereby amended by the deletion from the list of such persons prescribed pursuant to paragraph (*e*) of the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , the following:

CIT Group Finance (Ireland)

###### 3.

3. The Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2)(No. 2) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I No. 414 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0414.html) ) is hereby revoked.

Signed for and on behalf of theCENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND,

On this 31 day of July 2015.

CYRIL ROUX,

Deputy Governor.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to revoke the prescription of a certain entity prescribed pursuant to paragraph (*e*) of the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
