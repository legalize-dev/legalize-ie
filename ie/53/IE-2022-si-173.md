# S.I. No. 173/2022 - Fisheries (Commercial Fishing Licences) (Alteration of Duties and Fees) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th April,* 2022.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 68](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0068.html#sec68) (4)(b), [159](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0159.html#sec159) (5)(a), [160](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0160.html#sec160) (5)(a) and [279](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0279.html#sec279) (2) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications, Climate Action and Environment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 373 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0373.html) )), hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to sections [68](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0068.html#sec68) (7), [159](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0159.html#sec159) (5)(b), [160](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0160.html#sec160) (5)(b) and [279](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0279.html#sec279) (4) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Fisheries (Commercial Fishing Licences) (Alteration of Duties and Fees) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on the 1 day of March 2022 and has effect as respects licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after that day.

###### 3.

3. In this Order -

“Act of 1994” means the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1994);

“Principal Act” means the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959).

###### 4.

4. The ordinary licence duty in respect of an ordinary fishing licence authorising the use of a fishing engine of a kind specified in column (2) of the Schedule at any reference number is altered to the sum specified in column (3) of the Schedule at that reference number.

###### 5.

5. The licence duty in respect of an oyster fishing licence is altered to €94.

###### 6.

6. The sum standing specified in section 159(1)(b)(ii) of the Principal Act is altered to €128.

###### 7.

7. The sum standing specified in section 160(1)(b) of the Principal Act is altered to €128.

###### 8.

8. The Fisheries (Commercial Fishing Licences) (Alteration of Duties and Fees) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 40 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0040.html) ) is revoked.
