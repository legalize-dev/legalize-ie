# S.I. No. 16/2001 - National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act, 2000 (Central Treasury Services) (Delegation of Functions) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, CHARLIE McCREEVY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Sections 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec21) (2) and [23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec23) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (Central Treasury Services) (Delegation of Functions) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The functions of the Minister under [Section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec20) (1) and [Section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec21) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000) are delegated to the Agency.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 31 January 2001. |
|  | Charlie McCreevy |
|  | Minister for Finance |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order delegates to the National Treasury Management Agency the functions of the Minister for Finance in relation to the provision of central treasury services to designated bodies and the advance of moneys to the Post Office Savings Bank Fund for these purposes.
