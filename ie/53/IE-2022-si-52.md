# S.I. No. 52/2022 - European Union (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th February,* 2022.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive No. 2006/126/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the 21^st day of February 2022.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 10 of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) is amended by the deletion of paragraph (2) (inserted by the European Union (Licensing of Drivers) (Validity of Licences) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 176 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0176.html) ) and amended by the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0242.html) )).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 February, 2022.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations delete paragraph (2) of Regulation 10 of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ). An amended Regulation 10(2) is inserted by the Road Traffic (Licensing of Driver) (Amendment) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 53 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0053.html) ).
