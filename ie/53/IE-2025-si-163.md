# S.I. No. 163/2025 - European Union (Road Transport) (Working Conditions and Road Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th May,* 2025.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 561/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 March 2006[^1] , as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1258 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 April 2024[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Road Transport) (Working Conditions and Road Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Regulations of 2023” means European Union (Road Transport) (Working Conditions and Road Safety) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 567 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0567.html) );

“Principal Regulations” means European Union (Road Transport) (Working Conditions and Road Safety) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 229 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0229.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “First Council Regulation” (inserted by Regulation 3(a)(i) of the Regulations of 2023) the following:

> “ ‘First Council Regulation’ means Council Regulation 561/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 March 2006[^1] , as amended by Regulation (EC) No. 1073/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009[^3] , the Second Council Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2020/1054 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 July 2020[^4] and Regulation (EU) 2024/1258 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 April 2024[^2] ;”.

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (a) in Regulation 41(1)(a)**,** by substituting “16(2), (3), (4)(a) or (4)(b)” for “16(2) or (3)” (inserted by Regulation 24(a) of the Regulations of 2023), and

- (b) in Regulation 44(1)(b), by inserting “16(4)” after “12,”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 May, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the European Union (Road Transport) (Working Conditions and Road Safety) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 229 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0229.html) ) in line with the amendments made to Regulation (EC) 561/2006 by Regulation (EU) 2024/1258 of 24 April 2024 in regards to minimum requirements on minimum breaks and daily and weekly rest periods in the occasional passenger transport sector.
