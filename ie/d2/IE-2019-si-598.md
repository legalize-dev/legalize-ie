# S.I. No. 598/2019 - Planning and Development (Housing) and Residential Tenancies Act 2016 (Section 4) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th December,* 2019.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (b) of subsection (2) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0017/print.html#sec4) of the [Planning and Development (Housing) and Residential Tenancies Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0017/print.html) (No. 17 of 2016) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 358 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0358.html) )) and having complied with paragraph (a) of that subsection, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development (Housing) and Residential Tenancies Act 2016 (Section 4) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. The specified period is extended to include the period commencing on 1 January 2020 and ending on 31 December 2021.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 December, 2019.

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.
