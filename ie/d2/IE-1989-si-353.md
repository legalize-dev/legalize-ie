# S.I. No. 353/1989 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 353 of 1989.

ROAD TRAFFIC (LICENSING OF DRIVERS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1989

The Minister for the Environment. in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) , [23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0023.html#sec23) (2), [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0031.html#sec31) , [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0033.html#sec33) , [34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0034.html#sec34) , [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0035.html#sec35) , [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0038.html#sec38) (7) and [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0042.html#sec42) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1990.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 285 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0285.html) ) are hereby amended by:—

( *a* ) the substitution of the following sub-article for sub-article (3) of article 14:—

> - "(3) A driving licence may be granted for a period of one year where the application for the licence is required under article 42 of these Regulations to be accompanied by a medical report and the medical report indicates that the applicant appears to require medical review within a period which is less than three years.";

( *b* ) the insertion of the following article after article 19:—

> "19A. Notwithstanding sub-article (2) of article 19 of these Regulations, a provisional licence may be granted for a period of one year where the application for the licence is required under article 42 of these Regulations to be accompanied by a medical report and the medical report indicates that the applicant appears to require medical review within a period which is less than two years.";

( *c* ) the addition to article 23 of the following sub-article:—

> - "(3) Sub-article (2) of this article shall not apply to a provisional licence being granted to a person who held a provisional licence in relation to class C under the revoked Regulations prior to the 12th day of August, 1985.";

( *d* ) the substitution of the following sub-article for sub-article (1) of article 27:——

- "(1) The fee to be paid to the issuing authority on application for a certificate of competency shall be—

( *a* ) £50 where the application relates to vehicles in category EC1, EC ED1 or ED,

( *b* ) £40 where the application relates to vehicles in category C1, C, D1 or D, and

( *c* ) £25 in any other case.";

( *e* ) the substitution of the following sub-article for sub-article (2) of article 61:—

- "(2) References in these Regulations to a category of vehicles shall, unless the context otherwise requires, be construed as including references to the equivalent class of vehicles in accordance with this article save, however, that—

( *a* ) a certificate of fitness in relation to class C under the revoked Regulations shall not be regarded as a certificate of fitness in relation to category C1 or EC1, and

( *b* ) a provisional licence granted under the revoked Regulations in relation to class C, D or F shall not be regarded as provisional licence in relation to category EB, EC1, EC, ED1 or ED." and

- (*f*) the deletion of sub-article (6) of article 61.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

21st day of December, 1989.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the issue of provisional licences under the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) for the period of one year (instead of two years) in certain cases where for medical reasons a longer term licence cannot be granted. They also provide for increased fees for driving tests and for certain other technical amendments to the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations, 1989.
