**[SI No.161 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0161.html)**

**District Court (Equal Status Act 2000) Rules 2006**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec24) of the [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

1 These rules may be cited as the District Court (Equal Status Act 2000) Rules 2006.

2 These rules shall come into operation on the 8^th day of May 2006 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

3 Order 83 of the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be amended:

- (a) By the addition to rule 1 of the following definitions:

  > *“ “the Act of 2000”* means the [Equal Status Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2000);
  >
  > *“Determination Order”* means an Order made under sub-section (6) or sub-section (15) of section 8 of the Act of 2000;”

- (b) By the addition immediately following rule 11 of the following:

  > *“12. An application pursuant to section 8(3) or section 8(15) of the Act of 2000 for a Determination Order may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the club is situated. Notice of an application for a Determination Order shall be in the Form 83.9, Schedule C a copy of such notice shall be served upon the club (or in the case of an application under section 8(15), upon the original applicant) in the manner prescribed by section 8(5) of the Act of 2000 not later than seven days before the date fixed for the hearing of the application. The original notice shall be lodged with the Clerk not later than two days before the date fixed for the hearing of the application. The Court may adjourn the hearing of such application and direct service of notice of the application upon such persons as the Court may direct. A Determination Order shall be in the Form 83.10, Schedule C. The Clerk shall transmit a copy of any Determination Order to the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.”*

4 The Forms numbered 83.9 and 83.10 in Schedule 1 hereof shall be added to the Forms in Schedule C of the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This does not form part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)**

**These Rules amend Order 83 to provide a form of application and order for determination as to whether a club is/continues to be a discriminating club under sections 8(3) or 8(15) of the above Act.**
