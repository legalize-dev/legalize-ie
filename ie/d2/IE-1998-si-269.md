# S.I. No. 269/1998 - Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997 (Section 33) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 269 of 1998.

FISHERIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1997 (SECTION 33) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, MICHAEL WOODS, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on my by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) and [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0033.html#sec33) (1) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997) (as adapted by the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0301.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 33) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 14th day of July, 1998.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby prescribed that the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations shall be the form of a declaration to be given to the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board under [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997).

SCHEDULE

Regulation 2

DECLARATION TO THE AQUACULTURE LICENCES APPEALS BOARD OF INTERESTS FOR PURPOSES OF [SECTION 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0033.html#sec33) of [FISHERIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html)

I,_____________________, hereby give to the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board the following declaration of interests in compliance with the requirements of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) :

- (*a*) Particulars of any estate or interest which I have in any land, or in any process, development or operation, associated with aquaculture or fish processing or the manufacture, sale or distribution of products used in aquaculture or fish processing;

- (*b*) Particulars of any business or dealing in or developing land, or any process, development or operation associated with aquaculture or fish processing or the manufacture, sale or distribution of products used in aquaculture or fish processing, in which I am engaged or employed and any such business carried on by a company or other body of which I am, or any nominee of mine is, a member; and

- (*c*) Particulars of any profession, business or occupation in which I am engaged, whether on my own behalf or otherwise, and which relates to dealing in or developing land or to any process, development or operation associated with aquaculture or fish processing or the manufacture, sale or distribution of products used in aquaculture or fish processing.

I hereby declare that the foregoing is a true and complete declaration of every interest of mine to which [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , applies.

Signature: _________________________

Date: _____________________________

*Note:* Some of the required particulars may be shown on a separate page or on separate pages, duly signed and dated, if there is insufficient space on this Form for all the particulars required to be declared.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of July, 1998.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe the Form of Declaration of Interests, required by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23), to be completed by the Chairperson and by all other members and Secretary of the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board and by any consultant or adviser who may be engaged by the Board.
