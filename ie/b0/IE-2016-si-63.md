# S.I. No. 63/2016 - Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th February*, 2016.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (4) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950) and Article 4 of the Córas Iompair Éireann (Additional Powers) Order 1988 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0381.html) ) and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )) after consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2016.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “amending Scheme” means the Scheme amending the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order 1945 (S.R. & O. No. 242 of 1945)), prepared by Córas Iompair Éireann and submitted to the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (5) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950), and set out in the Schedule.

###### 3.

3. The amending Scheme is confirmed and comes into operation on 1 April 2016.

## SCHEDULE CÓRAS IOMPAIR ÉIREANN PENSION SCHEME FOR REGULAR WAGES STAFF (AMENDMENT) SCHEME 2016

*Interpretation*

1. (1) In this amending Scheme—

“existing Scheme” means the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Superannuation Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order 1945 (S.R. & O. No. 242 of 1945) as last amended by the 2015 amending scheme;

“1957 amending scheme” means the amending scheme confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Amending Superannuation Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order 1957 ( [S.I. No. 226 of 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/si/0226.html) );

“1992 amending scheme” means the amending scheme confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme (Confirmation) (No. 2) Order 1992 ( [S.I. No. 420 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0420.html) );

“1996 amending scheme” means the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme For Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 1996 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0115.html) );

“2007 amending scheme” means the amending scheme confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 671 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0671.html) );

“2015 amending scheme” means the amending scheme confirmed by the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order 2015 ( [S.I. No. 90 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0090.html) );

“operative date” means 1 April 2016 in respect of Articles 2 and 3 of this amending Scheme.

- (2) In this amending Scheme, unless otherwise expressly stated, the words and expressions used shall have the meanings assigned to them by the existing Scheme.

*Continuance of existing Scheme*

2. (1) Subject as hereinafter specifically provided the benefits provided by the existing Scheme shall continue to be paid or be payable under and in accordance with the terms thereof and without alteration to every existing pensioner whose pension commenced prior to the operative date and to the personal representative of any deceased pensioner or member if the deceased’s pension commenced or death occurred prior to the operative date.

- (2) Subject as hereinafter specifically provided nothing in this amending Scheme shall affect the rights of any of the persons mentioned in the preceding sub-article, nor shall this amending Scheme affect any subsisting right or liability accrued to or in respect of, or incurred under the existing Scheme by, any person who retired from or left the service of the Board or died prior to the operative date.

*Amendment of existing Scheme*

3. Subject to Article 2 of this amending Scheme, the existing Scheme shall be amended with effect on and from the operative date so as to conform to the provisions hereinafter contained and every provision of the existing Scheme which is inconsistent with the provisions hereinafter contained shall cease to have effect.

4. The 1957 amending scheme is amended-

- (*a*) by substituting for Article 4(2) the following:

  > - “(2) Save where otherwise expressly provided, no person of fifty one years of age or over can become a member of this Scheme.”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for Article 6 (amended by Article 6 of the 1992 amending scheme) the following:

  > “6. Normal Age of Retirement:
  >
  > For the purposes of this Scheme, the normal age of retirement shall be 66 years.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this Order is to amend the definition of Scheme Membership, increase the maximum entry age to age 51, increase the normal age of retirement for members of the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff to the age of 66 years and to increase the lump sum gratuity payable on retirement at age 66.
