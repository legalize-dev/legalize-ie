**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 494 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0494.html)**

**Taxi Regulation Act 2003 (Fixed Charges Offences) Regulations 2006**

The Commission for Taxi Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec46) of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2003), with the consent of the Minister for Transport, hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Fixed Charges Offences) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 25 September 2006.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Amendment (No. 2) Regulations of 2006” means the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Small Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2 Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 493 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0493.html) ).

###### 3.

3. An offence under Part 3 of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2003 consisting of a contravention of a provision of the Regulations referred to in Schedule 1 is declared to be a fixed charge offence.

###### 4.

4. The amount of €250 is prescribed for the fixed charges offences referred to in Schedule 1 for the purposes of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec46) of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) .

###### 5.

5. The form of notice set out in Schedule 2 is prescribed as the form of notice for the purposes of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec46) of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) .

**Explanatory Memorandum**

**(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

These Regulations declare contraventions of certain Regulations to be fixed charges offences. These are contraventions of:

▪ Failure to comply with roof sign requirements for taxis and wheelchair accessible taxis, including failure to display current licence number within 28 days of the date of issue of a new licence number;

▪ Failure to display tamper-proof disc;

▪ Failure to provide a receipt;

▪ Operation of more than one small public service vehicle licence in respect of a single vehicle;

▪ Removal or attempt to remove tamper-proof disc;

▪ Failure to display the relevant in-vehicle information in a small public service vehicle;

▪ Removal or attempt to remove the in-vehicle information;

▪ Refusal to carry a passenger for a journey of not more than 30 kilometres;

▪ Failure to have taximeter properly calibrated in line with the current maximum fare;

▪ Causing the taximeter in a taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi to operate while the vehicle is standing for hire or plying for hire;

▪ Failing to operate the taximeter while the taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi is on hire

▪ Refusal to carry a guide dog or other assistance dog;

▪ Standing at an appointed stand when the vehicle is not available for hire;

▪ Operating as a taxi or wheelchair accessible taxi without having demonstrated satisfactory knowledge of the local area;

▪ Failure to display driver identification.

All fixed charges offences declared by these Regulations attract a fixed charge of €250. The Regulations prescribe the form of notice for the purposes of enforcement of the fixed charge offences.
