ROAD TRAFFIC ACTS 1961 TO 2007 (FIXED CHARGE OFFENCES) (PRESCRIBED NOTICE AND DOCUMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th April*, 2009.

I, NOEL AHERN, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 103 (as amended by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec14) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )) and by the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (Road Traffic) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 320 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0320.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2007 (Fixed Charge Offences) (Prescribed Notice and Document) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 4th day of May, 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2005 (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 135 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0135.html) ) are amended by substituting for Schedule 4 the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.
