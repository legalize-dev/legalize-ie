S.I. No. 139 of 1967.

PENSIONS (INCREASE) REGULATIONS, 1967.

I, CHARLES J. HAUGHEY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1964), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1967.

###### 2.

2. For the purposes of these regulations and subsequent regulations under [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1964) the following paragraphs are hereby added to Part I of the Schedule to that Act—

- "(*t*) A pension payable under the Military Service Pensions Acts, 1924 to 1964.

- (*u*) A service pension payable under the Connaught Rangers (Pensions) Acts, 1936 to 1964.

- (*v*) A disability pension payable under the Connaught Rangers (Pensions) Acts, 1936 to 1964.

- (*w*) A pension payable under the MacSwiney (Pension) Acts, 1950 to 1964."

###### 3.

3. (1) A pension to which this paragraph applies shall be increased by five per cent. with effect from the 1st day of October, 1964.

- (2) A pension as so increased under subparagraph (1) shall be further increased by nine per cent. with effect from the 1st day of August, 1965.

- (3) This paragraph applies to the persons mentioned in paragraphs (*t*), (*u*), (*v*) and (*w*) (inserted by paragraph 1 of these regulations) of the Schedule to the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 24th day of May, 1967.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the addition to the Schedule to the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , of the pensions mentioned in Paragraph 1 of these Regulations and also for an increase of 5% as from 1st October, 1964, in these pensions and a further increase of 9% as from 1st August, 1965, in the pensions as so increased.
