# S.I. No. 193/2004 - Mineral Oil Tax (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Revenue Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec104) of the [Finance Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 1999) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Decision No. 2003/900/EC of 17 December 2003[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Mineral Oil Tax (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 34 of the Mineral Oil Tax Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 442 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0442.html) ), as amended by Regulation 3 of the Mineral Oil Tax (Amendment) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 399 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0399.html) ), is amended by substituting “not less than 3 kilogrammes and not more than 4.5 kilogrammes” for “not less than 3 kilogrammes” in both places where it occurs.

GIVEN under my hand,

29 April 2004.

JOSEPHINE FEEHILY,

Revenue Commissioner.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Mineral Oil Tax Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 442 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0442.html) ), as amended by the Mineral Oil Tax (Amendment) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 399 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0399.html) ), by specifying the maximum proportion of marker that may be added to gas oil or kerosene where such oil is intended for use other than as a propellant.

These changes are to comply with EU Law.
