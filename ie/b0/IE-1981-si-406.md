# S.I. No. 406/1981 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 52, Eighteenth Period) (Miscellaneous Textile Piece Goods) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 52, Eighteenth Period) (Miscellaneous Textile Piece Goods) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Instrument appoints a nominal quota of 1,000 square yards as the quantity of textile piece goods originating in the countries listed below, which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1982 to 31st December, 1982. This nominal Quota has been appointed to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister is in fact prepared to license during the quota period not less than 300 tonnes of textile piece goods.

The countries to which the Instrument applies are as follows:—
