# S.I. No. 331/1986 - Farm Tax (Fees) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 331 of 1986.

FARM TAX (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, JOHN BRUTON, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Farm Tax Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1985), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Farm Tax (Fees) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The fee payable to the Farm Tax Commissioner in respect of appeals under [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Farm Tax Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) , and applications under section 7 of that Act shall be £70.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 10th day of October, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations provide that a fee of £70 shall be payable to the Farm Tax Commissioner in respect of appeals and applications for revision on foot of assessments made under the [Farm Tax Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) .
