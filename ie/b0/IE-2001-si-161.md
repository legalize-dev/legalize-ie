# S.I. No. 161/2001 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0005.html#sec5) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), for the purpose of preventing the spread of foot-and-mouth disease, hereby order as follows-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Artificial Insemination) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 144 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0144.html) ) is amended by the substitution for “19 April, 2001” of “23 April, 2001” in each place where it occurs.

|  |  |

|  | **GIVEN** under my Official Seal 18 April, 2001 |
|  | Joe Walsh |
|  | Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development |

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to prohibit artificial insemination until 23 April 2001 and from that date, permit artificial insemination only under licence.
