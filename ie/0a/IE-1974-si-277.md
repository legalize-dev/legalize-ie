# S.I. No. 277/1974 - Postal Order (Inland) Amendment (No. 11) Regulations, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 277 of 1974.

POSTAL ORDER (INLAND) AMENDMENT (No. 11) REGULATIONS, 1974.

I, CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 23 and 24 of the Post Office Act, 1908, [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Post Office (Amendment) Act, 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1951), and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Post Office (Amendment) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1969), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Postal Order (Inland) Amendment (No. 11) Regulations, 1974.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1974.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Postal Order (Inland) Regulations, 1905, are hereby amended by the substitution of the following for Regulation 5 (inserted by the Postal Order (Inland) Amendment (No. 10) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 34 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0034.html) )):

"5. The poundage payable on postal orders shall be as follows :

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For an order for any multiple of £0.05 between £0.05 and £0.95, both inclusive | 3p. |
| For an order for any multiple of £1.00 between £1.00 and £5.00, both inclusive, and for £1.25 and £1.50 | 4p. |
| For an order for any multiple of £1.00 between £6.00 and £10.00, both inclusive | 5p." |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 20th day of September, 1974.

**CONOR CRUISE O'BRIEN.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the provisions of the foregoing Warrant.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 20th day of

September, 1974.

**RICHIE RYAN.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument, which comes into operation on the 1st October, 1974, provides for amending the poundage on postal orders.
