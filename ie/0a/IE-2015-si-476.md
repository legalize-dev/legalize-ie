# S.I. No. 476/2015 - Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) (No. 2) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd November*, 2015.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/sec0044.html#sec44) (4) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950) and Article 4 of the Córas Iompair Éireann (Additional Powers) Order 1988 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0381.html) ), the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )) after consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) (No. 2) Order 2015.

###### 2.

2. The superannuation scheme set out in the Schedule is confirmed and is deemed to have come into operation on 17 January 2014.

Schedule

Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for

Regular Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme 2015

The Córas Iompair Éireann Pension Scheme for Regular Wages Staff in paragraphs (*a*) and (*b*) of Article 9(3) (inserted by [S.I. No. 288 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0288.html) ) is amended by substituting “17 January 2018” for “the 17 January 2014” (inserted by [S.I. No. 49 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0049.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 October 2015.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this Order is to extend the voluntary severance terms applicable to members of the Regular Wages Grade Pension Scheme to 17 January 2018.
