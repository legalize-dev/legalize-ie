# S.I. No. 675/2025 - Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th January,* 2025.

I, TIMMY DOOLEY, Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Agriculture, Food and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 368 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0368.html) ) for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Regulation (EEC) No. 3440/84 of 6 December 1984[^1] (as amended by Regulation (EEC) No 955/87 of 1 April 1987[^2] , Commission Regulation (EEC) No 2122/89 of 14 July 1989[^3] and Commission Regulation No 146/2007 of 15 February 2007[^4] ) and Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^5] and effect to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/3204 of 11 October 2024[^6] , Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/283 of 28 November 2024[^7] , Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1726 of 27 June 2025[^8] , Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2192 of 6 August 2025[^9] and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2193 of 8 August 2025[^10] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0520.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by the insertion after “30 November 2023” in the definition of “Council Regulation” (as amended by the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 715 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0715.html) )) of the following: “, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/3204 of 11 October 2024, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/283 of 28 November 2024, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1726 of 27 June 2025, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2192 of 6 August 2025 and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2193 of 8 August 2025”.

###### 3.

3. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 715 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0715.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my hand,

19 December 2025

TIMMY DOOLEY,

Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This instrument amends the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0520.html) ) to give effect to (a) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/3204 of 11 October 2024 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards short-necked clam and red seabream and (b) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/283 of 28 November 2024 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards technical measures for certain demersal and pelagic fisheries in the Celtic Sea, the Irish Sea and the West of Scotland, (c) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1726 of 27 June 2025 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 as regards the correction of the territorial scope of provisions concerning short-necked clam and red seabream, (d) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2192 of 6 August 2025 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards specific technical measures for red seabream (Pagellus bogaraveo)in ICES subareas 6 to 8 and (e) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2193 of 8 August 2025 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the prolongation of technical measures for certain demersal and pelagic fisheries in the Celtic Sea, the Irish Sea and the West of Scotland.
