# S.I. No. 867/2007 - Greyhound Race Track (totalisator) (Operating) Amendment Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 867 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0867.html)

GREYHOUND RACE TRACK (TOTALISATOR) (OPERATING) AMENDMENT REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st February*, 2008.

Bord na gCon in exercise of the powers conferred on it by subsection (3) of [Section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0040.html#sec40) of the [Greyhound Industry Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958) hereby makes the following regulation, that is to say—

- (1) The minimum unit on Win and Place Pools ONLY shall be €2. (minimum unit on all other pools shall remain at €1.)

- (2) The minimum totalisator dividend on ALL POOLS shall be 1 euro.

- (3) The regulation shall come into operation on 13th day of November 2007.

GIVEN under the seal of Bord na gCon,

13 November 2007

**RICHARD O'SULLIVAN.**

**CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD**

**ADRIAN NEILAN.**

**CHIEF EXECUTIVE OF THE BOARD**

An officer authorised by the Board to authenticate the seal of the Board.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal* *Interpretation).*

The effect of this regulation is to amend the Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Operating) Regulations 1971 to allow for the minimum unit on all win and place pools to be €2.
