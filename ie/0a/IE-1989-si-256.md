# S.I. No. 256/1989 - Eircell (Amendment) Scheme, 1989

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 256 of 1989.

EIRCELL (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1989.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Section 90, [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Eircell (Amendment) Scheme, 1989.

- (2) The collective citation "the Eircell Schemes 1985 to 1989" includes this Scheme.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on 1 October, 1989.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. In this Scheme "the Scheme of 1988 means the Eircell (Amendment) Scheme, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 257 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0257.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Eircell Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 414 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0414.html) ), is hereby amended by:

- (*a*) the substitution of the following Schedule for the first Schedule there-to (inserted by paragraph 5 of the Scheme of 1988):

  > *"*

*Paragraph 11* (*a*)

FIRST SCHEDULE

*Eircell Call Charges*

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |

*Full Rate:* The Full Rate applies to calls made at or after 8 a. m. and before 6 p.m. on normal working days from Monday to Friday (inclusive).

*Reduced Rate:* The Reduced Rate applies to calls made as follows:

- (*a*) at or after 6 p.m. and before 8 a.m. from Monday to Friday (inclusive);

- (*b*) all day on Saturday, Sunday and Public Holidays; and

- (*c*) such other times as Bord Telecom Éireann may from time to time announce by advertisement in the national morning daily newspapers.

The main purpose of this Scheme is to provide for a decrease of about 28% in the Eircell rental charge.
