# S.I. No. 616/2021 - Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th November,* 2021.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec5) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012), (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020) ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) ) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2014/45/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 3 April 2014[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. The Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0347.html) ) are amended –

- (*a*) in Regulation 3 –

    - (i) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “Agricultural Vehicle Type-approval Framework Regulation”:

      > “ ‘Agricultural Vehicle Type-approval Framework Regulation’ means Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013[^2] , as amended by –
      >
      > - (*a*) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No. 1322/2014 of 19 September 2014[^3] ,
      >
      > - (*b*) Regulation (EU) 2016/1628 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 September 2016[^4] ,
      >
      > - (*c*) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1788 of 14 July 2016[^5] ,
      >
      > - (*d*) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/830 of 9 March 2018[^6] , and
      >
      > - (*e*) Regulation (EU) 2019/519 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 March 2019[^7] ;”,

    - (ii) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “category”:

      > “ ‘category’ means –
      >
      > - (*a*) in relation to a vehicle other than a tractor, a category of vehicle within the meaning of Article 4 of the Type-approval Framework Regulation and references to particular categories of vehicle are to those categories of vehicle specified in that Article, or
      >
      > - (*b*) in relation to a tractor, a category T vehicle within the meaning of Article 4 of the Agricultural Vehicle Type-approval Framework Regulation;”,

    - (iii) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “motor caravan”:

      > “ ‘motor caravan’ has the meaning assigned to it in Annex I of the Type-approval Framework Regulation;”,

    - (iv) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “special purpose vehicle”:

      > “ ‘special purpose vehicle’ means a special purpose vehicle within the meaning of Annex I to the Type-approval Framework Regulation and references to special purpose vehicles in a particular category are to special purpose vehicles in that category within the meaning of that Annex;”,

    - (v) by the insertion of the following definition:

      > “ ‘Type-approval Framework Regulation’ means Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018[^8] on the approval and market surveillance of motor vehicles and their trailers, and of systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles, amending Regulations (EC) No 715/2007 and (EC) No 595/2009 and repealing Directive 2007/46/EC, as amended by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1445 of 23 June 2021[^9] ;”, and

    - (vi) by the deletion of the definition of “Type-approval Framework Directive”,

      and

- (*b*) by the substitution of the following regulation for Regulation 9B:

  > “*Test due dates – converted vehicles*
  >
  > 9B. (1) Subject to paragraph (3), the first test due date for a converted vehicle after it has undergone a post-registration conversion shall be the date of conversion and each subsequent test due date shall be calculated by reference to that date in accordance with Regulation 6 as modified by Regulations 6A and 6B for the class of vehicle concerned.
  >
  > - (2) Where, on the occurrence of a post-registration conversion, there is in force in respect of a CVR vehicle a certificate of roadworthiness or a test certificate within the meaning of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), that certificate shall be deemed to expire on the date of conversion.
  >
  > - (3) Where the date of conversion of a CVR vehicle occurred during the period beginning on 28 March 2020 and ending on 30 June 2020 –
  >
  >     - (*a*) the first test due date for the vehicle shall be the date that is 3 months from the date of conversion, and
  >
  >     - (*b*) each subsequent test due date for the vehicle shall be determined in accordance with Regulation 6 as modified by Regulations 6A and 6B.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 November, 2021.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

The purpose of these regulations is to amend the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0347.html) ) to provide that:

i. a 3 month extension is given to test due dates for converted CVR vehicles with a first test due date falling during the period beginning on 28 March 2020 and ending on 30 June 2020, or to converted CVR vehicles with a certificate of roadworthiness in force on 28 March 2020.

ii. the definitions in the regulations are in line with both the Agricultural Vehicle Type-approval Framework Regulation and the Type-approval Framework Regulations.
