# S.I. No. 198/1998 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Bord Telecom Éireann plc, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme.

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1998.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation *"the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1998"* includes this Scheme.

- (2) Articles 4(a), (h), (i) and (j) shall come into operation on 1 July 1998.

- (3) Articles 4(b), 4(d) and 4(e) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1 January 1998.

- (4) Article 4(c) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 8 April 1998.

- (5) Article 4(f) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 20 October 1997.

- (6) Article 4(g) shall come into operation on 15 June 1998.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by:

- (a) the addition of the following Article 19(G) after Article 19(F) thereof:

"19(G)(1) Bord Telecom Eireann plc may provide a facility (the Facility) to telephone subscribers generally or to such groups of telephone subscribers as it shall determine, whereby a telephone subscriber connected to the fixed telephone network may receive a discount on Eligible Calls on a single telephone account. The discount shall be calculated as set out in Part 11 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme. Bord Telecom Eireann plc may, at its absolute discretion, withdraw the Facility from all or any telephone subscribers.

- (2) For the purposes of this Scheme "Eligible Calls" are subscriber direct dialled calls (excluding Chargecard calls, calls to the Eirpac Service, calls to other packet-switched networks, calls made to Premium Rate Telephone Service, calls to Directory Enquiry Service, calls to call management services, calls made by use of the National Virtual Private Network facility, calls made by use of the International Virtual Private Network facility, calls made to Inmarsat and Skyphone mobile telephony systems, operator assisted calls, Freefone 1800 calls received by the subscriber, 1350 calls received by the subscriber and 1890 calls received by the subscriber).

- (3) It shall be a condition of the application of the Facility that:

    - (i) an account to which the Facility applies shall be discharged in full within 14 days of the date of issue of the bill or by means of a direct debit mandate approved by Bord Telecom Eireann plc;

    - (ii) Eligible Calls shall not be eligible for any further or other discounts (including any discounts under Articles 18, 19(A), 19(B), 19(C), 19(D), 19(E) and 19 (F) of this Scheme);

- (4) The name of the Facility shall be

    - (a) TalkSaver when availed of by residential subscribers and

    - (b) Business Savers when availed of by business subscribers.

    - (b) the substitution of the following paragraph 19(A) of Article 63 for paragraph 19(A) of Article 63 thereof:

"63(19)(A)(i) Bord Telecom Eireann plc may provide a facility (in this Scheme referred to as "SwitchLink Call Termination Plan 1 Facility") to Service Providers whereby Service Providers may avail of the SwitchLink Call Termination Plan 1 Facility for the purpose of procuring that Bord Telecom Eireann plc delivers Eligible SwitchLink Termination Calls from telephone exchanges owned or operated by Bord Telecom Eireann plc either to subscribers of Bord Telecom Eireann plc within the State or for onward transmission. There shall be charged to the Service Provider and paid by him in respect of the provision to him of the SwitchLink Termination Plan 1 Facility the Subscription Charge and the Call Charges both of which are specified in Part 3(a) of Section XI of Part 6 of the First Schedule to the Scheme.

- (ii) For the purposes of this Scheme:

"Call Charges" shall mean the charges payable by the Service Provider in respect of Eligible SwitchLink Termination Calls.

"Eligible SwitchLink Termination Calls" shall mean direct dialled calls made on Inter City Routes and between exchanges in the State and from exchanges in the State to Northern Ireland and from exchanges in the State to exchanges outside the State (excluding Chargecard calls, Eirpac calls, calls to other Packet Switching Networks, unrestricted 64Kb calls made by use of the Integrated Services Digital Network (ISDN) facility, calls made to Premium Rate Telephone Services, Directory Enquiry calls, calls to Call Management Services, calls made by use of the National Virtual Private Network Facility, calls made by use of the International Virtual Private Network Facility, calls made to Inmarsat and Skyphone mobile telephony systems, calls made to 1850, 1890, 1891, 1511 and 1801 services and Freefone 1800, 1850 and 1890 calls received by the Service Provider).

"InterCity Routes" shall mean calls made between exchanges on routes specified in Part 3(b) of Section XI of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

"Subscription Charge" shall mean the monthly charge (if any) payable by the Service Provider to avail of the Facility.

It shall be a condition of the application of the SwitchLink Call Termination Plan 1 Facility that:

- (i) the Service Provider pays the monthly Subscription Charge set out in Part 3(a) of Section XI of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (ii) accounts to which the SwitchLink Call Termination Plan 1 Facility applies must be discharged in full within 14 days of the date of issue of the bill or by means of a direct debit mandate approved by Bord Telecom Eireann plc.

- (iii) Eligible SwitchLink Termination Calls shall not be eligible for any further or other discounts including any discounts under Article 18, 19, 19(A), 19(B), 19(C), 19(D), 19(E) and 19(F) of this Scheme."
