# S.I. No. 924/2005 - Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002 (Additional Institutions) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MARY HANAFIN, Minister for Education and Science in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) of the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2002), and having caused a draft to be laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft having been passed by each such House, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002 (Additional Institutions) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2002) is amended by the insertion of the institutions specified in the Schedule to this Order.

## SCHEDULE

Mary Immaculate School for the Deaf, Beechpark, Co. Dublin.

“The Boys Home” Grand Canal Street, Dublin 2.

“Racefield”, Upper Mountown Road, Dún Laoghaire.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec4) (1) of the [Residential Institutions Redress Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) allows the Minister for Education and Science to make an order to provide for the insertion in the *Schedule* of the Act any industrial school, reformatory school, orphanage children's home, special school which was established for the purpose of providing education services to children with a physical or intellectual disability or a hospital providing medical or psychiatric services to people with a physical or mental disability or mental illness in which children were placed and resident and in respect of which a public body had a regulatory or inspection function.

An order under this section requires a resolution of each House of the Oireachtas to approve of the draft of the order. Dáil Éireann and Seanad Éireann each passed a resolution approving the draft order on 30th June 2005.
