# S.I. No. 244/2017 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th June*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 34 (as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) and 41 of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 June 2017.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

“Act of 1968” means [Road Traffic Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968);

“Act of 1994” means [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994);

“Act of 2004” means [Road Traffic Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0044/print.html) (No. 44 of 2004);

“Act of 2006” means [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006);

“Act of 2010” means [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010);

“No. 2 Act of 2011” means [Road Traffic (No. 2) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2011);

“Act of 2016” means [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016);

“Principal Act” means [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961).

###### 4.

4. Each of the offences mentioned in Schedules 1 to 4 is declared to be a fixed charge offence.

###### 5.

5. The amount of a fixed charge is—

- (*a*) €200 in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 1,

- (*b*) €80 in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 2,

- (*c*) €60 in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 3,

  and

- (*d*) €40 in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 4.

## **Schedule 1**

Regulation 5(a)

**€200 fixed charge**

An offence under Section 12 of the Principal Act (as amended by section 18 of the Act of 2006) consisting of using on a public road a vehicle—

- (*a*) whose weight unladen exceeds maximum permitted weight,

- (*b*) whose weight laden exceeds maximum permitted weight, or

- (*c*) any part of which transmits to ground greater weight than maximum permitted weight,

specified in the Road Traffic (Construction and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0005.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations (operative from 1 June 2017) declare which offences involving the driving or use of mechanically propelled vehicles and pedal cycles are fixed charge offences for the purposes of [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec36) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) and determine the amount of fixed charge for each offence.
