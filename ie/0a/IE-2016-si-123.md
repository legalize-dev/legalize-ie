S.I. No. 123 of 2016

DISTRICT COURT (SOLICITORS’ COSTS) RULES 2016

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the District Court (Solicitors’ costs) Rules 2016, shall come into operation on the 31st day of March 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. The District Court Rules are amended by the substitution of the table in Schedule 1 for the table bearing the like number in the Schedule of Costs annexed to the District Court Rules 1997.
