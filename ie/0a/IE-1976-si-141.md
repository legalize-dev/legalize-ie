# S.I. No. 141/1976 - Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 141 of 1976.

HEALTH SERVICES (LIMITED ELIGIBILITY) REGULATIONS, 1976.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations, as respects which, pursuant to the said section, a draft has been approved by a resolution passed by each House of the Oireachtas:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1976.

###### 2.

2. For paragraph (*b*) of subsection (1) of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (as substituted therein by article 2 of the Health Services (Limited Eligibility) Regulations, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 276 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0276.html) )) there shall be substituted the following paragraph:—

> "( *b* ) adult persons whose yearly incomes are less than £3,000".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this

1st day of July, 1976.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

Dated this 1st day of July, 1976.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations extend the income limit for limited eligibility for health services from £1,600 to £3,000 a year.
