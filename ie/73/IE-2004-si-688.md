[S.I. No. 688 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0688.html) .

OIREACHTAS (MINISTERIAL AND PARLIAMENTARY OFFICES) (SECRETARIAL FACILITIES) (No. 4) REGULATIONS 2004.

The Houses of the Oireachtas Commission, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by section 2(1)(*c*) of [the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and transferred by section 4(3)(*b*) of [the Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices) (Secretarial Facilities) (No. 4) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:

“the Commission” means the Houses of the Oireachtas Commission established under [the Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ;

“the 2003 Regulations” means the Oireachtas (Ministerial and Parliamentary) Offices (Secretarial Facilities) Regulations 2003.

###### 3.

3. Notwithstanding Regulation 3 of the 2003 Regulations, additional secretarial facilities shall be provided by the Commission to members of Dáil Éireann or Seanad Éireann by way of a secretarial facilities fund up to a maximum of €4,000 per member of Dáil Éireann and €1,700 per member of Seanad Éireann, subject to Regulations 4 and 5.

###### 4.

4. Payment from the secretarial facilities fund shall be on the basis of expenditure vouched in accordance with the form set out in the Schedule: provided that no payment in respect of such expenditure shall be made after 31 January 2005.

###### 5.

5. The secretarial facilities fund may be used to pay for secretarial services other than equipment, under one or more contracts for services.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to make temporary arrangements for additional secretarial services to facilitate the members of the Houses of the Oireachtas in the exercise of their parliamentary duties.
