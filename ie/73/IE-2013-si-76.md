# S.I. No. 76/2013 - Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th March*, 2013.

I, FERGUS O’DOWD, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec70) and [80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec80) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) and the Energy and Natural Resources (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 631 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0631.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2010” means the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that section 70 of the Act of 2010 applies in respect of a contravention of Regulations 3, 4, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22(2), 22(3), 22(4), 22(5), 22(6), 22(7) or 22(8) of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 556 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0556.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Schedule 1 is prescribed for the purposes of sections 70(2) and 80(1) of the Act of 2010.

###### 5.

5. The Bye-laws specified in Schedule 2 are prescribed for the purposes of paragraphs (*b*) and (*c*) of section 80(1) of the Act of 2010.

###### 6.

6. The prescribed amount of a fixed charge to be specified in a fixed charge notice under sections 70 and 80 of the Act of 2010 is €150.

###### 7.

7. The Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) (No. 2) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0706.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations provide for a system of on-the-spot fines to be administered by Inland Fisheries Ireland as applies to fisheries legislation. The form of notice of an on-the-spot fine is set out in the Schedule to the Regulations.
