# S.I. No. 340/2001 - Human Rights Commission Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec3) of the [Human Rights Commission Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Human Rights Commission Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2001

###### 2.

2. The 25th day of July, 2001, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Human Rights Commission Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2000).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of July, 2001. |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE |
|  | Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This Order prescribes that the 25th day of July, 2001 is the establishment day of the Human Rights Commission for the purposes of the [Human Rights Commission Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2000).
