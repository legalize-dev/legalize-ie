# S.I. No. 331/2011 - National Minimum Wage Act 2000 (Section 11) (No. 2) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th July*, 2011.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1) of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec11) (inserted by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec22) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2011)) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade And Innovation (Alteration Of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) )), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the National Minimum Wage Act 2000 (Section 11) (No. 2) Order 2011.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 1 July 2011.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that the national minimum hourly rate of pay for the purposes of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) shall be €8.65.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 June 2011.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order sets the national minimum hourly rate of pay from 1 July 2011 at €8.65.
