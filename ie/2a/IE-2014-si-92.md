# S.I. No. 92/2014 - European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st February*, 2014.

I, JAMES REILLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2013/46/EU of 28 August 2013[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2009, the Regulations of 2013 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) Regulations 2007 to 2014.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 852 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0852.html) );

“Regulations of 2009” means European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 209 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0209.html) );

“Regulations of 2013” means European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 384 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0384.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) (as amended by Regulation 3 of the Regulations of 2013) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “Directive 2006/141/EC” the following:

> “‘Directive 2006/141/EC’ means Commission Directive 2006/141/EC of 22 December 2006[^2] , as amended by Commission Regulation (EC) No. 1243/2008 of 12 December 2008[^3] , Commission Directive 2013/26/EU of 8 February 2013[^4] and Commission Directive 2013/46/EU of 28 August 2013[^1];”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 3 of the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph (3), by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”, and

- (*b*) in paragraph (5), by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 4(5) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”.

###### 6.

6. Regulation 5 of the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph (3), by substituting “Subject to paragraph 3A, follow-on formulae” for “Follow-on formulae”, and

- (*b*) by inserting after paragraph (3) the following paragraph:

  > - “(3A) In the case of follow-on formulae manufactured from protein hydrolysates defined in point 2.2 of Schedule 2 with a protein content between the minimum and 0.56g/100 kJ (2.25g/100 kcal):
  >
  >     - (*a*) the suitability of the follow-on formula for the particular nutritional use by infants shall be demonstrated through appropriate studies, performed following generally accepted expert guidance on the design and conduct of such studies, and
  >
  >     - (*b*) shall be in accordance with the appropriate specifications set out in Schedule 6”.

###### 7.

7. Schedule 1 to the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in point 2.1—

    - (i) in the title, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”, and

    - (ii) in footnote 1, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”,

- (*b*) in the title to point 2.3, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”,

- (*c*) in the title to point 10.1, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”, and

- (*d*) in the title to point 10.2, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”.

###### 8.

8. Schedule 2 to the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in the title to point 2.1, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”,

- (*b*) in point 2.2—

    - (i) by substituting for the maximum and minimum values the following:

      > “Minimum(1) 0.45 g/100 kJ (1.8 g/100 kcal)
      >
      > Maximum 0.8 g/100 kJ (3.5 g/100 kcal)”, and

    - (ii) by inserting after the maximum and minimum values the following footnote:

      > - “(1) Follow-on formulae manufactured from protein hydrolysates with a protein content between the minimum and 0.56 g/100 kJ (2.25 g/100 kcal) shall be in accordance with Regulation 5(3A).”

- (*c*) in the title to point 2.3, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”,

- (*d*) in the title to point 8.1, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cows’ milk proteins”, and

- (*e*) in the title to point 8.2, by substituting “cows’ milk or goats’ milk proteins” for “cow’s milk proteins”.

###### 9.

9. Point 3 of Schedule 3 (as amended by Regulation 3 (*d*) of the Regulations of 2009) to the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for footnote 1 the following:

> - “(1) L-arginine and its hydrochloride shall only be used in the manufacture of infant formulae referred to in Regulation 4(6) and follow-on formulae referred to in Regulation 5(3A)”.

###### 10.

10. The title to Schedule 6 to the Principal Regulations is amended by inserting “INFANT FORMUALE AND FOLLOW-ON FORMULAE” for “INFANT FORMULAE”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations transpose into Irish law Commission Directive 2013/46/EU of 28 August 2013 amending Directive 2006/141/EC with regard to the protein requirements for infant formulae and follow-on formulae.

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 852 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0852.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-on Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.
