# S.I. No. 184/1978 - Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices For Milk) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 184 of 1978.

CORK DISTRICT MILK BOARD (MINIMUM PRICES FOR MILK) ORDER, 1978.

I, JAMES GIBBONS, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1978, and shall come into operation on the sixth day of July, 1978.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"registered producers" means persons registered in the register of producers kept by the Cork District Milk Board;

"registered retailers" means persons registered in the register of retailers kept by the Cork District Milk Board;

"registered wholesalers" means persons registered in the register of wholesalers kept by the Cork District Milk Board.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Cork District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) (No. 2) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0142.html) ), is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. The minimum prices to be paid to registered producers for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and retailers shall be at the rates shown in the Schedule to this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of July, 1978.

**JAMES GIBBONS,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the minimum prices to be paid to registered producers, as from 6 July 1978 for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and registered retailers in the Cork District.
