# S.I. No. 378/1994 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) (Dungarvan) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 378 of 1994.

URBAN RENEWAL ACT, 1986 (DESIGNATED AREAS) (DUNGARVAN) ORDER, 1994.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) (Dungarvan) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The area enclosed by red lines and shaded pink on the map attached hereto is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 30th day of November, 1994.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

30th day of November, 1994.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the designation of an area in Dungarvan under the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

**DUNGARVAN**
