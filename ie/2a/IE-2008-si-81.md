# S.I. No. 81/2008 - Registration of Title Act 1964 (Compulsory Registration of Ownership) (Clare, Kilkenny, Louth, Sligo, Wexford and Wicklow) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 81 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0081.html)

REGISTRATION OF TITLE ACT 1964 (COMPULSORY REGISTRATION OF OWNERSHIP) (CLARE, KILKENNY, LOUTH, SLIGO, WEXFORD AND WICKLOW) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st April*, 2008.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 24 (as amended by [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec53) of the [Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2006)) of the [Registration of Title Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1964) and after consultation with the Property Registration Authority, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Registration of Title Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Compulsory Registration of Ownership) (Clare, Kilkenny, Louth, Sligo, Wexford and Wicklow) Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. Section 24 (as amended by [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html#sec53) of the [Registration of Deeds and Title Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2006)) of the [Registration of Title Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1964) shall apply to the counties of Clare, Kilkenny, Louth, Sligo, Wexford and Wicklow after (but not on) 30 September 2008.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 March 2008

**BRIAN LENIHAN**

Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order extends compulsory registration of ownership of land to the counties of Clare, Kilkenny, Louth, Sligo, Wexford and Wicklow. The Order will come into effect on 1 October 2008.
