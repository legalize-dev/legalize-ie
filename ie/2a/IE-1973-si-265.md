# S.I. No. 265/1973 - Exchange Control (No. 2) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 265 of 1973.

EXCHANGE CONTROL (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), as continued by the [Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1970), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Exchange Control (No. 2) Regulations, 1973.

- (2) The collective citation " the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 to 1973 ", shall be deemed to include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The Banque Nationale de Paris (Ireland) Limited of 111 Grafton Street, Dublin 2, is hereby appointed to be an authorised dealer.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of September, 1973.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959, by appointing the Banque Nationale de Paris (Ireland), Ltd., of 111 Grafton Street, Dublin 2, to be an authorised dealer for the purposes of the Exchange Control Acts.
