# S.I. No. 652/2007 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) (No. 3) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 652 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0652.html)

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) (No. 3) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th September*, 2007.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 4 (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) (No. 3) Regulations 2007.

- (2) The collective citation “the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations 1964 to 2007” shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

## SCHEDULE

Schools, service in which is pensionable in accordance

with the Superannuation (Education) Scheme

Further Education & Training Awards Council

Mental Health Commission

National Treatment Purchase Fund Board

Ordnance Survey Ireland

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 September 2007

**BRIAN COWEN,**

Tánaiste and Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and “approved organisations”, and between one “approved organisation” and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as “approved organisations”.
