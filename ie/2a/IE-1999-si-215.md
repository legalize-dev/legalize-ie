# S.I. No. 215/1999 - European Communities (Knackery) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Knackery) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 21st day of July 1999.

- (3) The European Communities (Knackery) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 396 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0396.html) ) and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Knackery) Regulations, 1996 and 1999.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 (1) of the European Communities (Knackery) Regulations, 1996 is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (b) in the definition of “knackery” of the following paragraph:

> - “(*b*) for the cutting up of dead animals, for sale as food for packs of hounds, or”

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of July, 1999. |
|  | JOE WALSH, |
|  | Minister for Agriculture and Food |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Knackery) Regulations, 1996, by restricting the sale of meat from knackeries to feed for packs of hounds.
