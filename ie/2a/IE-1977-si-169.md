# S.I. No. 169/1977 - Social Welfare (Variation of Rates of Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 169 of 1977.

SOCIAL WELFARE (VARIATION OF RATES OF SUPPLEMENTARY WELFARE ALLOWANCE) REGULATIONS, 1977.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) (3) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1975) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Variation of Rates of Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The rates of supplementary welfare allowance are hereby varied by the substitution for the rates set out in the Table to subsection (1) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) of the rates set out in the following Table:—

TABLE.

WEEKLY AMOUNTS OF SUPPLEMENTARY WELFARE ALLOWANCE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Classes of persons to whom the amounts of supplementary welfare allowance are applicable | Weekly amount of supplementary welfare allowance |
| (1) | (2) |
| Person without a dependant | £9·80 |
| Person with an adult dependant | £17·05 |
| Person with an adult dependant and one child dependant | £20·20 |
| Person with an adult dependant and two or more child dependants | £23·35 |
|  | plus, where there are more than two child dependants, £2·40 for each child dependant in excess of two. |
| Person with one child dependant | £13·10 |
| Person with two or more child dependants | £16·25 |
|  | plus, where there are more than two child dependants, £2·40 for each child dependant in excess of two. |

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1977.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 8th day of June, 1977.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby sanctions the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 8th day of June, 1977.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) (3) of the [Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , provides that the Minister for Social Welfare may, with the sanction of the Minister for Finance, by regulations vary (but not so as to reduce) the weekly amounts of supplementary welfare allowance payable under that Act.

These Regulations provide for increased statutory rates of supplementary welfare allowance. The new rates correspond with the maximum rates of unemployment assistance at present applicable to persons resident in rural areas.
