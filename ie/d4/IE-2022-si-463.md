# S.I. No. 463/2022 - Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd September,* 2022.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006), for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Regulation (EU) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009[^1] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011[^2] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/30 of 14 January 2020[^3] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ) are amended—

- (a) In Regulation 2(1), by inserting after “28 October 2015” in the definition of “Commission Regulation” the following: “and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/30 of 14 January 2020”,

- (b) In Regulation 4(1)(b), by inserting “81,” after “80(1) and (2),”,

- (c) In Regulation 4(4)(a), by substituting “Council Regulation” for “Control Regulation”, and

- (d) In Regulation 7, by substituting “Council Regulation” for “Control Regulation” in each place it occurs.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 September, 2022.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ) in certain respects.
