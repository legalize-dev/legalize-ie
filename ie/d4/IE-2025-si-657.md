# S.I. No. 657/2025 - European Union (Motor Vehicles –Type Approval) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd January,* 2026.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018[^1] (as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 of 24 April 2024[^2] of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation (EU) 2024/1610 of 14 May 2024[^3] of the European Parliament and of the Council), Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013[^4] and Regulation (EU) No. 168/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 January 2013[^5] , hereby make the following regulations:

***Citation***

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Motor Vehicles –Type Approval) Regulations 2025.

***Interpretation***

###### 2.

2. (1) In these Regulations —

“European Type-Approval Regulations” means the following EU type-approval Regulations insofar as they contain requirements applicable to the following vehicle categories:

- (a) for vehicles of categories M, N, and O, Regulation (EU) 2018/858;

- (b) for vehicles of categories T, R, and S, Regulation (EU) No 167/2013;

- (c) for vehicles of category L, Regulation (EU) No. 168/2013;

“Regulation (EU) 167/2013” means Regulation (EU) No. 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013[^4] , supplemented by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/985 of 12 February 2018[^5] ;

“Regulation (EU) 168/2013” means Regulation (EU) No. 168/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 January 2013[^6] , as amended by Regulation (EU) 2020/1694 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 November 2020[^7] ;

“Regulation (EU) 2018/858” means Regulation (EU) 2018/858 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018[^1] , as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 of 24 April 2024[^2] of the European Parliament and of the Council and Regulation (EU) 2024/1610 of 14 May 2024[^3] of the European Parliament and of the Council;

“type-approval” includes type approval in the State or another Member State;

“type-approved vehicle” means any motor vehicle with type-approval (including in respect of vehicles produced in small series) as classified in the following, namely–

- (a) for M, N, O category vehicles, Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858,

- (b) for T, R, S category vehicles, Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 167/2013, or

- (c) for L category vehicles, Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 168/2013.

- (2) A word or expression that is used in these Regulations and is also used in Regulation (EU) 2018/858, Regulation (EU) 167/2013 or Regulation (EU) 168/2013, has, unless the context otherwise requires, the same meaning in these Regulations as it has in the Regulation, or Regulations, concerned.

***Type-approval of vehicles***

###### 3.

3. (1) The provisions of—

- (a) the Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 1963 ( [S.I. No. 190 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0190.html) ),

- (b) the Road Traffic (Lighting of Vehicles) Regulations 1963 ( [S.I. No. 189 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0189.html) ),

- (c) the Road Traffic (Control of Supply of Vehicles) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 35 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0035.html) ),

- (d) the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 385 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0385.html) ), and

- (e) the Road Traffic (Construction and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0005.html) ),

insofar as they relate to the use or supply of a type-approved vehicle, with respect to the design, construction, fitting, mounting or performance standards of a component of such a vehicle, or of such vehicle as a whole, shall not apply where -

- (i) the type-approved vehicle, systems, components or separate technical units have a valid type-approval certificate and the system, component or separate technical unit bears the required type-approval mark in accordance with the European Type-Approval Regulations relating to such vehicle, systems, components or separate technical units or vehicle, or

- (ii) the type-approved vehicle, systems, components or separate technical units fulfils the performance standards of the European Type-Approval Regulations relating to such vehicle component or vehicle, and, in the case of a vehicle component, the vehicle component is mounted and fitted in accordance with the requirements of the European Type-Approval Regulations relating to such component.

- (2) Where a vehicle, system, component or separate technical unit no longer complies with the original type-approval, the provisions of—

    - (a) the Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 1963 ( [S.I. No. 190 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0190.html) ),

    - (b) the Road Traffic (Lighting of Vehicles) Regulations 1963 ( [S.I. No. 189 of 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/si/0189.html) ), or

    - (c) the Road Traffic (Construction and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0005.html) ),

as applicable, shall apply in relation to the design, construction, fitting, mounting or performance standards of such vehicle, system, component, or separate technical unit.

***Revocation***

###### 4.

4. The European Communities (Road Traffic) (Vehicle Type Approval) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 34 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0034.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

19 December, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide that a European Type-Approval Regulations compliant vehicle is deemed to meet the vehicle standard requirements in the State.
