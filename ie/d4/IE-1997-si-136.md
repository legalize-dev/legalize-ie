# S.I. No. 136/1997 - Dublin Docklands Development Authority Act, 1997 (Establishment Day) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 136 of 1997.

DUBLIN DOCKLANDS DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY ACT, 1997 (ESTABLISHMENT DAY) ORDER, 1997

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Dublin Docklands Development Authority Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1997), hereby orders that the 1st day of May, 1997 shall be the day appointed for the purposes of the said section 14(1).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 27th day of March, 1997.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints 1 May, 1997 as the day on which the Dublin Docklands Development Authority is to be established.
