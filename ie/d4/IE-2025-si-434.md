# S.I. No. 434/2025 - Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Load Lines) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th September,* 2025.

I, SEÁN CANNEY, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0017/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Merchant Shipping (Load Lines) Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1968), and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 144 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0144.html) ), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure, Infrastructure, Public Service Reform and Digitalisation, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Fees) (Load Lines) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 2 to the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 595 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0595.html) ) is amended by substituting for Part 1 the following:

> ### **“Part 1**
>
> **Load line surveys and inspections – Prescribed fees**
>
> 1. The fees prescribed as payable in respect of each survey or inspection mentioned in Chapters A, B and C of this Part shall be determined by the amount of work involved, charged at an hourly rate of €72.
>
> 2. Notwithstanding paragraph 1, the fees prescribed as payable in respect of each survey or inspection mentioned in Chapters A, B and C shall not exceed the maximum amounts prescribed in those Chapters.

###### 3.

3. References in Chapters B or C to a standard fee are references to the fee set out in Chapter A appropriate to the tonnage of the ship concerned.

7. In respect of a survey of a sister ship for stability requirements the fee prescribed as payable shall be 20 per cent of the appropriate fee specified in the Table at paragraph 6 for the first ship of the same type.

###### 10.

10. A fee of €172 is prescribed as payable in respect of the issue, in substitution for an existing load line certificate, or load line exemption certificate, of a corresponding certificate showing amended or extended plying limits (including such survey or inspection as may be necessary).

###### 11.

11. A fee of €86 is prescribed as payable in respect of the endorsement of a load line exemption certificate.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 595 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0595.html) ), to highlight the maximum fees payable for load line surveys and certificates.
