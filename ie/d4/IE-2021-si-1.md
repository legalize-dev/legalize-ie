# S.I. No. 1/2021 - Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th January,* 2021.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 32 (as amended by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec16) of the [Irish Medicines Board (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2006)) of the [Irish Medicines Board Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1995), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2001/83/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 November 2001[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) Regulations 2007 (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0002.html) ), the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) Regulations 2007 (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 286 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0286.html) ), the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 274 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0274.html) ), the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) (Amendment) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0164.html) ), the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0217.html) ) and these Regulations may be cited together as the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 to 2021.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 538 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0538.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 6 of the Principal Regulations is amended by inserting after paragraph (c) the following paragraph:

> - “(d) the supply of a medicinal product specified in column 1 of the Twelfth Schedule to the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 540 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0540.html) ) (inserted by Regulation 6 of the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) (Amendment) (No. 7) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 698 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0698.html) )) to a person referred to in Regulation 4F of the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) Regulations 2003 (inserted by Regulation 3 of the Medicinal Products (Prescription and Control of Supply) (Amendment) (No. 7) Regulations 2020) for the purpose of supply and administration in accordance with that Regulation.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 January, 2021.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the Medicinal Products (Control of Wholesale Distribution) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 538 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0538.html) ).

The purpose of these Regulations is to exempt the supply of Covid-19 vaccinations from the prohibition on the sale by wholesale of medicinal products by registered pharmacists in nominated public and private hospital pharmacy departments, who do not hold a wholesaler’s authorisation.
