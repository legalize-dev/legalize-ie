# S.I. No. 682/2011 - Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act 1980 (Ratification of UPOV) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th December*, 2011.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 (as amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41. of 1998)) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act 1980 (Ratification of UPOV) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. Any country that is a member of the International Convention for the Protection of New Varieties of Plants shall be a Contracting Party for the purposes of the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Acts 1980 and 1998.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 December 2011.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the ratification by Ireland of the 1991 revision of the UPOV Agreement on Plant Variety Rights.
