# S.I. No. 176/1986 - Litter Act, 1982 (Section 5 Payment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 176 of 1986.

LITTER ACT, 1982 (SECTION 5 PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/sec0005.html#sec5) (4) of the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1982) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 5 Payment) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of July, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The amount standing specified in subsection (1)(b) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) shall be £25.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 1st day of June, 1986.

**JOHN BOLAND,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations vary from £5 to £25 the amount which a person who is liable to be prosecuted for an offence under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Litter Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) , may pay to the local authority within 21 days of the date of the giving of a notice pursuant to section 5 of that Act.
