# S.I. No. 397/2023 - Garda SÍochÁna (Retirement) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th August,* 2023.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 122(1)(h) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )), after consulting with the Garda Commissioner and the Authority and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 2 September 2023.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding anything contained in -

- (a) the Gárda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1934 (S. R. & O. No. 146 of 1934), as continued in force by section 128 of the Garda Síochána Act 2005,

- (b) the Gárda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations 1951 ( [S.I. No. 335 of 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/si/0335.html) ), as so continued in force, or

- (c) the Garda Síochána Act 2005 (Retirement) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 28 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0028.html) ),

the member of the Garda Síochána who, immediately before the coming into operation of these regulations, holds the rank of Garda Commissioner shall retire on 1 June 2025.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

25 July, 2023.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Justice,

25 July, 2023.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice.
