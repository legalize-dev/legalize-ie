# S.I. No. 239/1998 - European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 239 of 1998.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (RETIREMENT OF FARMERS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive No. 72/160/EEC of 17 April 1972(^1), hereby make the following Regulations:

^(1) O.J. No. L96, 23.4.72. p.9.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1998.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1997" means the European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 283 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0283.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Farmers' Retirement Scheme, 1974, set out in the Schedule to the European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0116.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution—

- (a) in Article 7, for "£3,727" (inserted by the Regulations of 1997) of "£3,791" and for "£2,485" (inserted by the Regulations of 1997) of "£2,528", and

- (b) in Article 8, for "£2,485" (inserted by the Regulations of 1997) of "£2,528".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of July, 1998.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.
