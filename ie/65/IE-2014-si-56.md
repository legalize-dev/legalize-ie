# S.I. No. 56/2014 - City and County of Limerick Local Electoral Areas and Municipal Districts Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th February*, 2014.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec4) and [23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec23) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) and having regard to [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec28) (1)(*d*) of the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the City and County of Limerick Local Electoral Areas and Municipal Districts Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. (1) The City and County of Limerick shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area described in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of Limerick City and County Council to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set out in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division shall be construed as referring to such electoral division as existing at the date of this Order. Every reference to a former rural district shall be construed as a reference to that district as constituted immediately before the 1st day of October 1925.

###### 4.

4. The City and County of Limerick shall have the following municipal districts for the purposes of section 22A (inserted by the [Local Government Reform Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2014)) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001):

- (*a*) the Metropolitan District of Limerick consisting of the Local Electoral Areas of Limerick City East, Limerick City North and Limerick City West;

- (*b*) the Municipal District of Adare-Rathkeale consisting of the Local Electoral Area of Adare-Rathkeale;

- (*c*) the Municipal District of Cappamore-Kilmallock consisting of the Local Electoral Area of Cappamore-Kilmallock;

- (*d*) the Municipal District of Newcastle West consisting of the Local Electoral Area of Newcastle West.

###### 5.

5. The City of Limerick Local Electoral Areas Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 430 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0430.html) ) and the County of Limerick Local Electoral Areas Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0437.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to divide Limerick City and County into local electoral areas, to fix the number of members of Limerick City and County Council to be elected for each such area and to determine the municipal districts for Limerick City and County.
