S.I. No. 266 of 1973.

WATERFORD SUBURBS TRAFFIC TEMPORARY RULES, 1973.

I, PATRICK MALONE, Commissioner of the Garda Síochana, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 89](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0089.html#sec89) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968), and with the consent of the Minister for Local Government, hereby make the following temporary rules in respect of the area specified in paragraph 3 of these rules:

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Waterford Suburbs Traffic Temporary Rules, 1973.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 21st day of September, 1973.

###### 3.

3. These rules shall apply to the area comprising the county borough of Waterford and the townlands of Newrath, Ballyrobin, Rockshire and Mountsion in the county of Kilkenny.

###### 4.

4. A driver shall comply with any prohibition, restriction or requirement indicated by means of a traffic sign.

GIVEN under my hand this 30th day of August, 1973.

PATRICK MALONE,

Commissioner of the Garda Síochána.

The Minister for Local Government hereby consents to the making of the foregoing temporary rules.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government this 14th day of September, 1973.

**JAMES TULLY,**

Minister for Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

1. These rules provide for the making of streets in the county borough of Waterford and the townlands of Newrath, Ballyrobin, Rockshire and Mountsion in the county of Kilkenny one-way thoroughfares.

2. Rule 4 provides that a driver shall comply with any prohibition, restriction or requirement indicated by means of a traffic sign.
