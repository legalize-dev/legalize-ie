# S.I. No. 669/2006 - National Cultural Institutions Act 1997 (Delegation of Functions) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 669 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0669.html)**

**NATIONAL CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS ACT 1997 (DELEGATION OF FUNCTIONS) ORDER 2006**

I, John O'Donoghue, T.D., Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 51(1) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1997) and the Arts and Culture (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0302.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Sport and Recreation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 309 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0309.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Delegation of Functions) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The functions conferred on the Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism by [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1997) in relation to archaeological objects (being a class of museum heritage object) are delegated to the Board of the National Museum of Ireland.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This order delegates to the Board of the National Museum of Ireland the functions set out in [Section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1997) regarding the issue of export licences in relation to archaeological objects (being a class of museum heritage object).
