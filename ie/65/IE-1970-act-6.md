# GAMING AND LOTTERIES ACT, 1970

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO REPEAL SECTION 10 OF THE GAMING AND LOTTERIES ACT, 1956. [26*th May*, 1970.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Repeal of section 10 of Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956.

**1.**—Section 10 of the Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956, is hereby repealed.

###### 2 Short title and collective citation.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1970.

- (2) The Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956, and this Act may be cited together as the Gaming and Lotteries Acts, 1956 and 1970.
