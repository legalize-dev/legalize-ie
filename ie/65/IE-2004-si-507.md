[S.I. No. 507 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0507.html) .

AGRICULTURE APPEALS ACT 2001 (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE) REGULATIONS 2004.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5(2) of [the Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2001) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as [the Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule (as amended by [the Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 558 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0558.html) )) to [the Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) 29 of 2001) is amended by adding after the mention of “Installation Aid Scheme (IAS)” the following scheme:

‘Landslide Damage Relief Scheme for the Pullathomas Area of County Mayo’

and the said Schedule, as so amended, is set out in the Table to this Regulation.
