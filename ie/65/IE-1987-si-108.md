# S.I. No. 108/1987 - Dublin Metropolitan Street Commission Act, 1986 (Termination of Transfer of Functions) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 108 of 1987.

DUBLIN METROPOLITAN STREET COMMISSION ACT, 1986 (TERMINATION OF TRANSFER OF FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1987.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Dublin Metropolitan Streets Commission Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1986), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Dublin Metropolitan Streets Commission Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Termination of Transfer of Functions) Order, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The transfer of functions to the Dublin Metropolitan Streets Commission under [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0007.html#sec7) , [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0008.html#sec8) and [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Dublin Metropolitan Streets Commission Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) , is hereby terminated.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

the Environment this 9th day of April, 1987.

**PÁDRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order terminates the transfer of functions under [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0007.html#sec7) , [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0008.html#sec8) and [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Dublin Metropolitan Streets Commission Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) which would have come into effect by virtue of the [Dublin Metropolitan Streets Commission Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Commencement) (No. 2) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 67 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0067.html) ).
