S.I. No. 230 of 1980.

ROAD FUND (WINDING UP) REGULATIONS, 1980.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0021/sec0050.html#sec50) (4) of the [Finance Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1978) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Fund (Winding Up) Regulations, 1980.

###### 2.

2. Each Act, Order and Regulation mentioned in columns (1) and (2) of the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby adapted or modified in the manner stated in column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention of that Act, Order or Regulation in columns (1) and (2).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0021/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [Finance Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1978), provided for the winding up of the Road Fund. The purpose of these Regulations is to adapt or modify the provisions of certain Acts, Orders and Regulations insofar as they refer or relate to the Road Fund so as to bring them into conformity with the provisions of [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0021/sec0050.html#sec50) of the [Finance Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .
