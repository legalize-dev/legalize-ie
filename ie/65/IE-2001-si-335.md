# S.I. No. 335/2001 - Planning and Development Act, 2000 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 270](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec270) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development Act, 2000 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The following provisions of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) shall come into operation -

- (a) section 2 insofar as it relates to the sections referred to in paragraphs (b) and (c) of this article,

- (b) Chapter I of Part VI, and

- (c) section 264 and the Sixth Schedule insofar as they relate to the repeals effected by article 3 of this Order.

###### 3.

3. [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1976) and sections 2, 3 (as inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0009/sec0001.html#sec1) (*a*) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1998)), 5, 7, 8 (as amended by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0009/sec0001.html#sec1) (*b*) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) ) and [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act,1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1983) are repealed.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This Order brings Chapter I of Part VI of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) into force which relates to the composition and membership of An Bord Pleanála. The provisions in the Local Government (Planning and Development) Acts, 1963 to 1999 which this Chapter replaces are repealed.
