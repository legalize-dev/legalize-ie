# S.I. No. 163/2016 - Bathing Water Quality (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th April*, 2016.

I, ALAN KELLY, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2000/60/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2000[^1] and Directive 2006/7/EC of 15 February 2006[^2] and Commission Implementing Decision 2011/321/EU of 27 May 2011[^3] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Bathing Water Quality (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

*Interpretation*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Bathing Water Quality Regulations 2008 ( [S.I No. 79 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0079.html) ).

*Amendment of Principal Regulations*

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 18(1), by substituting for subparagraph (*a*) the following:

  > - “(*a*) the current bathing water classification and any prohibition or advice against bathing referred to in this Regulation by means of a clear and simple sign or symbol, such sign or symbol to be used—
  >
  >     - (i) in the case of any prohibition or advice against bathing, as set out in Schedule 7 to these Regulations;
  >
  >     - (ii) in the case of current bathing water classification, as set out in Schedule 8 to these Regulations.”

  and

- (*b*) by inserting the following Schedules after Schedule 6:

  > ### “SCHEDULE 7
  >
  > For informing on bathing prohibition or advice against bathing:

## SCHEDULE 8

For informing on bathing water classification:

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 April 2016.

**ALAN KELLY,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations implement Commission Implementing Decision 2011/321/EU establishing, pursuant to Directive 2006/7, symbols for providing information to the public on bathing water classification and any bathing prohibition or advice against bathing.

The Regulation gives further effect to Directive 2006/7/EC of 15 February 2006, on the quality of bathing waters and amends Statutory Instrument No. 79 of 2008.
