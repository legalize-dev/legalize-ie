# S.I. No. 227/2024 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st May,* 2024.

I, Eamon Ryan, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (inserted by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) and [41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec41) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )) and after consultation with the Minister for Justice with respect to offences declared to be fixed charge offences under the said section 34(2)(a) (as adapted by the Justice and Equality (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2024.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Act of 1968” means Road Traffic Act 1968 (No. 25 of 1968);

“Act of 1994” means Road Traffic Act 1994 (No. 7 of 1994);

“Act of 2006” means Road Traffic Act 2006 (No. 23 of 2006);

“Act of 2010” means Road Traffic Act 2010 (No. 25 of 2010);

“electric scooter” has the meaning assigned to it in Regulation 2 of the Road Traffic (Electric Scooters) Regulations 2024 (S. I No. 199 of 2024);

“No. 2 Act of 2011” means Road Traffic (No. 2) Act 2011 (No. 28 of 2011);

“Principal Act” means Road Traffic Act 1961 (No. 24 of 1961);

“Regulations of 1997” means Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) );

“Regulations of 2012” means Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012) ( [S.I. No. 332 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0332.html) );

“Regulations of 2024” means the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 222 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0222.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The following are revoked:

- (a) the Road Traffic (Fixed Charge Offences — Cyclists) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 331 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0331.html) );

- (b) the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 526 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0526.html) ).

###### 7.

7. Each of the offences under the Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2024 mentioned in Schedules 1 to 8 (other than in Part 2 of Schedule 4 or Part 2 of Schedule 6) is declared to be a fixed charge offence.

###### 8.

8. The prescribed amount of a fixed charge —

- (a) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 1, is €200,

- (b) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 2, is €160,

- (c) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 3, is €150,

- (d) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 4, is €120,

- (e) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 5, is €80,

- (f) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 6, is €60,

- (g) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 7, is €50, and

- (h) in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 8, is €40.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations specify which offences involving the driving or use of mechanically propelled vehicles, pedal cycles and electric scooters are fixed charge offences for the purposes of [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec34) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) and prescribe the amount of the fixed charge for each offence.
