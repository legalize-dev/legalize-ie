# S.I. No. 68/1993 - Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1993 (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 68 of 1993.

MEDICAL PREPARATIONS (LICENSING OF MANUFACTURE) REGULATIONS, 1993 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and section 65 of that Act as amended by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0039.html#sec39) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953) and [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0036.html#sec36) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1993 (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. Schedule II of the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 40 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0040.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition of the following paragraph:

> - "(*c*) (but for the revocation of the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 225 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0225.html) ) and the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) (Amendment) Regulations, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0302.html) ) effected by Article 2 of the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1993), was on or immediately before the coming into force of these Regulations, engaged in, or was entitled to be engaged in, the activities of a qualified person pursuant to the provisions of the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1974 and the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) (Amendment) Regulations, 1975."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 11th day of March, 1993.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations continue recognition as qualified persons of those persons covered by Article 24 of E.C. Council Directive 75/319 of 20 May 1975 (O.J. No. L147 p.13).
