# S.I. No. 95/1991 - Inland Post Amendment (No. 47) Scheme, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 95 of 1991.

INLAND POST AMENDMENT (No. 47) SCHEME, 1991

AN POST, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Tourism, Transport and Communications, hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Inland Post Amendment (No. 47) Scheme, 1991.

- (2) This Scheme shall come into operation on the 2nd day of April, 1991.

###### 2.

2. In this Scheme—

"the Principal Warrant" means the Inland Post Warrant, 1939 ( [S.I. No. 202 of 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/si/0202.html) ).

"Scheme No. 36" means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 36) Scheme, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 362 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0362.html) ).

"Scheme No. 44" means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 44) Scheme, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 363 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0363.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 3 (16) of the Principal Warrant is hereby amended by the substitution for that Regulation of the following:

> "The expression inland when used in relation to any postal packet or any description thereof means posted within Ireland and addressed to some place in Ireland and Northern Ireland."

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5 of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 5) Warrant, 1953 (inserted by Regulation 12 of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 19) Warrant, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0280.html) ), as amended by Regulation 4 of Scheme No. 44, is hereby amended by the substitution for "forty pounds" of "one hundred pounds".

###### 5.

5. Regulation 3 (2) (ii) of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 30) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 396 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0396.html) ), as amended by Regulation 5 of Scheme No. 44, is hereby amended by the substitution for "twenty five pounds" or "£25" of "fifty pounds" or "£50".

###### 6.

6. (*a*) Regulation 6 (*b*) of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 33) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0444.html) ), as amended by Regulation 7 (*a*) of Scheme No. 44, is hereby amended by the substitution for "6p" of "10p".

- (*b*) Regulation 8 (*a*) of Inland Post Amendment (No. 33) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0444.html) ), as amended by Regulation 7 (*b*) of Scheme No. 44, is hereby amended by the substitution for "forty pounds" or "£40" of "one hundred pounds" or "£100".

###### 7.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme applies to the Inland Post Service and comes into operation on the 2nd day of April, 1991. It authorises increases in the letter, postcard, printed packet, newspaper, sample packet and parcel post rates of postage and in the fees charged for the express delivery, registration, customs clearance, cash-on-delivery, Business Reply, Freepost, Publicity Post, Postaim, Same Day Service, Parcel Sure-Service, Parcel Forward and other miscellaneous services.
