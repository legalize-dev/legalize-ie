# S.I. No. 250/2017 - Companies (Accounting) Act 2017 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th June*, 2017.

I, MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec1) (2) of the [Companies (Accounting) Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2017), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Accounting) Act 2017 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Companies (Accounting) Act 2017 (Commencement) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0246.html) ) is amended by the substitution of the following Article for Article 3:

> “3.Subject to Article 4, the 9th day of June 2017 is appointed as the day on which the Act of 2017, other than section 80, shall come into operation.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 June 2017.

**MARY MITCHELL O’CONNOR,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.
