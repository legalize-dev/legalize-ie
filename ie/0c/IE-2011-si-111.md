# S.I. No. 111/2011 - Primary School Teachers Pension (Amendment) Scheme 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th March*, 2011.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) (as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Teachers Superannuation (Amendment) Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1990)) of the [Teachers Superannuation Act 1928](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1928) (as adapted by the Education and Science (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0184.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following scheme:

###### 1.

1. This scheme may be cited as the Primary School Teachers Pension (Amendment) Scheme 2011.

###### 2.

2. This scheme comes into operation on 8 March 2011.

###### 3.

3. The Primary School Teachers Pension Scheme 2009 ( [S.I. No. 434 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0434.html) ) is amended by substituting for Article 13 the following:

> “A member shall not be entitled to reckon—
>
> - (*a*) the same period of service more than once unless, in the case of a gratuity, he or she has repaid a gratuity having an entitlement to do so,
>
> - (*b*) the same period of service for the purposes of this Part and also for the purposes of an approved pension scheme, or
>
> - (*c*) service in any week which brings total service in respect of that week to more than full time service for the purposes of this Part.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 March 2011.

**MARY COUGHLAN,**

Minister for Education and Skills.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing scheme.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

8 March 2011.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Scheme is made under section 2 of the Teachers’ Superannuation Act 1928 (as amended by the Teachers’ Superannuation (Amendment) Act 1990). The purpose of the scheme is to remove an exemption from a prohibition on dual accrual of pension entitlements for Oireachtas members for service given on or after 9 March, 2011, the commencement of the next Oireachtas. (31st Dail and the 24th Seanad). This gives effect to the Government decision of 1 February, 2011, Uimhir Thagartha: S180/20/10/1217
