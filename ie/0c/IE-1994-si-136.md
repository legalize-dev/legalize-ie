# S.I. No. 136/1994 - Video Recordings Act, 1989 (Registers) Regulations, 1994

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 136 of 1994.

VIDEO RECORDINGS ACT, 1989 (REGISTERS) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0014.html#sec14) , [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Registers) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The Register of Certificated Video Works shall be a register containing an entry in the form specified in Part I of the Schedule to these Regulations or in a form to the like effect in relation to each video work in respect of which a supply certificate is in force for the time being.

###### 3.

3. The Register of Prohibited Video Works shall be a register containing an entry in the form specified in Part II of the Schedule to these Regulations or in a form to the like effect in relation to each video work in respect of which a prohibition order is in force for the time being.

###### 4.

4. The [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Register of Prohibited Video Works) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0217.html) ) are hereby revoked.
