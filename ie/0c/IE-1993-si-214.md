# S.I. No. 214/1993 - Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 214 of 1993.

HOUSING ACT, 1966 (ACQUISITION OF LAND) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of and the Third Schedule to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1960) [as substituted by [section 86](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0086.html#sec86) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) as further amended by section 6, and the Second Schedule to the [Roads Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993)] hereby makes the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Acquisition of Land) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The form in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby substituted for Form No. 8 in the Schedule to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Acquisition of Land) Regulations, 1966.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1993.

If confirmed, the order will authorise the local authority to acquire compulsorily the land described in the First Schedule hereto for the purposes of _________________________________________________ .^1

Any objections to the extinguishing of the said public right of way should be made in writing addressed to the Minister for the Environment, Custom House, Dublin, within 6 weeks of the publication of this notice.^2

DIRECTIONS FOR COMPLETING THIS FORM

1. the purpose for which the land is required should be stated as in the Order.

2. References to the extinguishment of a public right of way and to the Second Schedule should be omitted if not applicable and consequential changes should be made in the wording of the notice.

3. In the first Schedule the land should be described as in the order or by reference to boundaries, e.g. "The land to which the order relates is bounded as follows, on the north by............................................................ ............................................................ ................, on the south by............................................................ ......................................... , on the east by............................................................ ..................................................... , and on the west by ............................................................ ...................", or "The area to which the order relates is bounded by a line drawn as follows ............................................................ ...................... ".
