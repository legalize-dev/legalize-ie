# S.I. No. 164/2000 - Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) (Amendment) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 29th day of May, 2000.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations — “Principal Regulations” means the Garda Síochána (Admissions and Appointments) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0164.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Regulation 5 of the Principal Regulations is amended:

- (*a*) in paragraph (1)(*b*) by the substitution for “the surgeon of the force” of “a registered medical practitioner (within the meaning of the [Medical Practitioners Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1978))”.

  and

- (*b*) by the revocation of paragraph (3).

###### 6.

6. Regulation 11 of the Principal Regulations is amended in paragraph (3)(*a*) by the substitution for “the surgeon of the force” of “a registered medical practitioner (within the meaning of the [Medical Practitioners Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1978))”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the certification of trainees by a registered medical practitioner.
