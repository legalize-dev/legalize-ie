# S.I. No. 122/2013 - European Communities (Cooperation between National Authorities Responsible for the Enforcement of Consumer Protection Laws) (Amendment) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th April*, 2013.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 2006/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 October 2004[^1] as amended by Directive 2009/136/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009[^2] , Regulation (EU) No 954/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 September 2011[^3] and Regulation No. 1177/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010[^4] , hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Cooperation between National Authorities Responsible for the Enforcement of Consumer Protection Laws) (Amendment) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2012” means the European Communities (Cooperation between National Authorities Responsible for the Enforcement of Consumer Protection Laws) (Amendment) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 485 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0485.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Cooperation between National Authorities Responsible for the Enforcement of Consumer Protection Laws) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0290.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1) (amended by Regulation 3(*a*) of the Regulations of 2012) by substituting the following definition for the definition of “Council Regulation”:

  > “Council Regulation” means Council Regulation (EC) No. 2006/2004 of the European Parliament of the Council of 27 October 2004[^1] on cooperation between national authorities responsible for the enforcement of consumer protection laws as amended by Directive 2009/136/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009[^2], Regulation (EU) No 954/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 September 2011[^3] and Regulation 1177/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010[^4].”, and

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations amend [S.I. No. 290 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0290.html) on Cooperation between National Authorities responsible for the Enforcement of Consumer laws. The regulations implemented Regulation (EC) 2006/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 October 2004 by designating competent authorities to carry out the functions of information exchange, cooperation and infringement proceedings in respect of specified EU consumer Directives/Regulations.

[S.I. No. 290 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0290.html) is amended in order to designate competent authorities for a new regulation, which was not provided for in the original regulations.

The National Transport Authority is designated for the purpose of Regulation (EU) No 1177/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 concerning the rights of passengers when travelling by sea and inland waterway.
