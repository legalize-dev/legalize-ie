[S.I. No. 1 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0001.html)

DISTRICT COURT (INTOXICATING LIQUOR) RULES 2011

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the District Court (Intoxicating Liquor) Rules 2011, shall come into operation on the 9th day of February 2011.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2011.

###### 2.

2. The Form numbered 72.4 in Schedule C of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be deleted and the Form bearing the like number in Schedule 1 hereof shall be added to the Forms in Schedule C of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) in substitution therefor.
