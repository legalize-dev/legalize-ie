# S.I. No. 190/1975 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 190 of 1975.

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT, 1966 (FIRST SCHEDULE) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1975.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , (No. 6 of 1966), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Order, 1975.

- (2) The [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1972, and this Order may be cited together as the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1975.

###### 2.

2. For the purpose of extending the application of [sections 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0012.html#sec12) , [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) and [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966), the First Schedule to that Act (as amended by the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (First Schedule) (Amendment) Orders, 1968 to 1972) is hereby amended by the insertion in Part III thereof at the end of Class A of the following:

" 13 Teschen disease

14 Swine Influenza

15 Transmissible Gastro-Enteritis

16 Infectious Laryngo-Tracheitis

17 Arizona disease

18 African Horse Sickness

19 Dourine

20 Venezuelan Equine Encephalomyelitis

21 Infectious Equine Anaemia ".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of August, 1975.

**MARK CLINTON,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order extends the definition of "disease" in the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) to include further diseases of pigs, horses and poultry.
