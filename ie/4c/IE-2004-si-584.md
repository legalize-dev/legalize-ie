**Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 2004**

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of its powers under [the National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990) and [the National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 2004.

###### 2.

2. (1) This Regulation shall be deemed to have had effect from the 1st day of January, 2002 to the 20th day of July, 2003.

- (2) Rates of interest on deposits within the meaning of section 14(1) of [the Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) are hereby prescribed as follows:

    - (a) where any such deposit is less than €6,000, the rate of interest shall be 0.25 per cent per annum; and

    - (b) where any such deposit is €6,000 or more, the rate of interest shall be 0.50 per cent per annum.

###### 3.

3. (1) This Regulation shall be deemed to have effect from the 21st day of July, 2003.

- (2) Rates of interest on deposits within the meaning of section 14(1) of [the Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) are hereby prescribed as follows:

    - (a) where any such deposit is less than €6,000, the rate of interest shall be 0.10 per cent per annum; and

    - (b) where any such deposit is €6,000 or more, the rate of interest shall be 0.25 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 446 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0446.html) ) are hereby revoked with effect from 1 January, 2002.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe rates of interest for deposits in the Post Office Savings Bank.
