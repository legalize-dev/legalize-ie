# S.I. No. 363/2021 - Small Public Service Vehicle (Emergency Measure Covid-19) (Expired Licence) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th July,* 2021.

The National Transport Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec7) of the [Taxi Regulation Acts 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) and 2016 (No. 37 of 2013 as amended), hereby makes the following Regulations to make exceptional provision, having regard to the Covid-19 pandemic, and in order to mitigate the adverse economic consequences likely to result from the spread of that disease and to mitigate its impact on essential public transport services:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Small Public Service Vehicle (Emergency Measure Covid-19) (Expired Licence) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Taxi Regulation (Small Public Service Vehicle) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 33 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0033.html) ).

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations, “Covid-19” means a disease caused by infection with the virus SARS-CoV-2 and specified as an infectious disease in accordance with Regulation 6 of, and the Schedule to, the Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) or any variant of the disease so specified as an infectious disease in those Regulations.

###### 4.

4. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 13 March 2021.

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended by substituting at Regulation 17 (2)(a) “twenty four” for “twelve”.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Transport Authority,

16 July 2021.

**FRED BARRY,**

Chairperson, National Transport Authority.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of the Small Public Service Vehicle (Emergency Measure Covid-19) (Expired Licence) Regulations 2021 is to amend Regulation 17 to extend the period for application for a replacement licence by the holder of a small public service vehicle licence that has expired to 24 months.
