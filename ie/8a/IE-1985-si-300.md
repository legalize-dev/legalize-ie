DEFENCE FORCES (PENSIONS) (AMENDMENT)

(NO. 4) SCHEME, 1985.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) , [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0003.html#sec3) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1932), and by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1938), as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973, ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service, make the following Scheme:

1 Short title, collective citation and construction.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 4) Scheme, 1985.

- (2) The collective citation "the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1985" shall include this Scheme.

- (3) The Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1985, shall be construed together as one Scheme.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall come into force on the day after the date on which it is made.

3 Application of [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .

###### 3.

**3.** The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

4 Definitions.

###### 4.

**4.** In this Scheme—

"the Principal Scheme" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) Scheme, 1937 (S. R. & O., No. 249 of 1937)

"the No. 2 Amending Scheme of 1981" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1981 (S.I. No. 362 of 1981).

5 Amendment of Article 42 of Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1947.

###### 5.

**5.** (1) Article 42 of the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1947 (S. R. & O., No. 81 of 1947), is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (7) of the following sub-article:—

- "(7) For the purposes of this Article each of the following and no other persons shall be deemed to be a relative of a deceased soldier, his—

( *a* ) widow,

( *b* ) widower,

( *c* ) son,

( *d* ) daughter,

( *e* ) mother,

( *f* ) father,

( *g* ) brother,

( *h* ) sister,

( *i* ) legal personal representative.".

- (2) Article 10 of the No. 2 Amending Scheme of 1981 is hereby revoked.

- (3) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1982.

6 Amendment of Article 20 of Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1949.

###### 6.

**6.** (1) Article 20 of the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1949 ( [S.I. No. 209 of 1949](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1949/en/si/0209.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (7) of the following sub-article:—

- "(7) For the purposes of this Article each of the following and no other persons shall be deemed to be a relative of a deceased officer, his—

( *a* ) widow,

( *b* ) widower,

( *c* ) son,

( *d* ) daughter,

( *e* ) mother,

( *f* ) father,

( *g* ) brother,

( *h* ) sister,

( *i* ) legal personal representative.".

- (2) Article 11 of the No. 2 Amending Scheme of 1981 is hereby revoked.

- (3) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1982.

7 Amendment of Article 32A of the Principal Scheme.

###### 7.

**7.** (1) Article 32A of the Principal Scheme (inserted by Article 12 of the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0246.html) )), is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (4) of the following sub-article:—

- "(4) For the purposes of this Article each of the following and no other persons shall be deemed to be a relative of a deceased member—

( *a* ) her widower,

( *b* ) her son,

( *c* ) her daughter,

( *d* ) her mother,

( *e* ) her father,

( *f* ) her brother,

( *g* ) her sister,

( *h* ) her legal personal representative.".

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1982.

8 Restriction on grant of pensions and gratuities to soldiers.

###### 8.

**8.** (1) The Principal Scheme is hereby amended by the insertion after Article 28B (inserted by Article 5 of the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 4) Scheme, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0114.html) )) of the following Article—

> "28C. Where a man of the Reserve of Men (First Line) is discharged from the Reserve Defence Force for the purpose of re-enlisting in the Permanent Defence Force, a pension or gratuity shall not be granted to him on such discharge in respect of any period of qualifying service prior to such re-enlistment."

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1985.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of September, 1985.

**PATRICK COONEY.**

The Minister for the Public Service hereby consents to the foregoing Scheme.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Public Service,

this 6th day of September, 1985.

**JOHN BOLAND.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Statutory Instrument provides for amendments to the list of defined relatives for death gratuity purposes and a prohibition on the grant of pensions and gratuities to soldiers discharged from the Reserve Defence Force for the purpose of re-enlisting in the Permanent Defence Force.
