# S.I. No. 315/2001 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Restriction on Movement of Certain Animals) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0005.html#sec5) , [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0027.html#sec27) (a) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (a) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Restriction on Movement of Certain Animals) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order, 2001 and shall come into operation on 2^nd July, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Restriction on Movement of Certain Animals) (No. 2) Order, 2001 is amended, in Article 3 by the insertion after paragraph (3) of the following —

> - “(4) Paragraphs (1) and (2) of this Article and paragraph (3) of Article 4 shall not apply to the movement of an ovine animal -
>
>     - (a) to a slaughterhouse, or
>
>     - (b) from one part of a holding to another part of the same holding.
>
> - (5) With effect from 9^th July, 2001, paragraphs (1) and (2) of this Article and paragraph (3) of Article 4 shall not apply to the movement of a breeding ewe, a ram or a member of the porcine species to a mart.”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order allows for sheep to move to a slaughterhouse or from one part of a holding to another part of the same holding without a licence.
