# S.I. No. 372/1979 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 51, Sixteenth Period) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1979

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 372 of 1979.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA No. 51, SIXTEENTH PERIOD) (MISCELLANEOUS TEXTILES) ORDER, 1979.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 51, Sixteenth period) (Miscellaneous Textiles) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Instrument appoints a nominal Quota of £IR100 as the value of miscellaneous textiles (wearing apparel and other made-up textile articles) originating in the countries listed below, which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1980 to 31st December, 1980. This nominal Quota has been appointed to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister will in fact be prepared to license during the quota period goods up to a limit of not less than 150 tonnes mainly for use for further manufacture.

The countries to which the Instrument applies are as follows:—
