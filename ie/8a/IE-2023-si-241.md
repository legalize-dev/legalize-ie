# S.I. No. 241/2023 - Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th May,* 2023.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )) for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 10 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/392 of 4 March 2021[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0347.html) ) are amended -

- (a) in Regulation 3, by inserting the following definitions:

  > “‘Commission Implementing Regulation’ means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/392 of 4 March 2021[^1] ;
  >
  > ‘real-world data’ shall be construed in accordance with Article 2 (c) of the Commission Implementing Regulation;”, and

- (b) by inserting after Regulation 21 the following:

  > “*Collecting of real-world data and VINS*
  >
  > 21A When carrying out a CVR test on a CVR vehicle to which Article 10 of the Commission Implementing Regulation applies, a CVR tester shall collect real-world data and VINS in accordance with that Article.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 May, 2023.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations give full effect to Article 10 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/392 for the collection of real-world data from new light commercial vehicles that are first registered from 1 January 2021 and that are equipped with on-board fuel and/or energy consumption monitoring devices at the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness Test.
