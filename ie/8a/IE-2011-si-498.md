# S.I. No. 498/2011 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 5) (Administrative Responsibility for Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th October*, 2011.

I, JOAN BURTON, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) , [247](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec247) , [334](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec334) and [341](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec341) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), and by [section 342](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec342) (amended by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec10) and [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec17) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2011)) of that Act, with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Short title, collective citation and construction.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 5) (Administrative Responsibility for Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2011.

- (2) The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2011 and these Regulations shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2011.

*Definition.*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ).

*Commencement.*

###### 3.

3. These Regulations come into operation on 1 October 2011.

*Amendments to Principal Regulations.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in article 225(1) by substituting “the Minister” for “the Minister or the Executive, as the case may be,”;

- (*b*) in article 242(*d*) by substituting “the Department” for “the Department or Executive as appropriate”;

- (*c*) in article 243 by substituting “The Minister” for “The Minister or the Executive, as appropriate”;

- (*d*) in article 244—

    - (i) by substituting “the Minister” for “the Minister or the Executive”; and

    - (ii) in paragraph (*b*) by substituting “the Department” for “the Department or the Executive”;

- (*e*) in article 246(1) by substituting “the Department” for “the Department or the Executive” in each place it occurs in paragraphs (*a*) and (*b*);

- (*f*) in article 247 by substituting “the Minister” for “the Minister or the Executive, as the case may be,”; and

- (*g*) by substituting the following article for article 249:

  > *“Decisions as to the application of Part 9.*
  >
  > 249. All questions which arise in the application of this Part shall be determined by an officer of the Minister.”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Responsibility for the administration of the Supplementary Welfare Allowance scheme is being transferred from the Health Service Executive to the Department of Social Protection with effect from 1st October 2011. These Regulations remove references to the Health Service Executive in relation to the regulatory provisions dealing with overpayments of Supplementary Welfare Allowance (SWA) and for the purposes of treating SWA as paid on account of another social welfare payment.
