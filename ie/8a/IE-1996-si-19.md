# S.I. No. 19/1996 - Garda Síochána (Ranks) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 1 of the Garda Síochána Act, 1972 (No. 2 of 1972), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Ranks) Order, 1996.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby determined that the Garda Síochána shall be divided into the ranks specified in column (1) of the First Schedule to this Order and that each rank shall consist of not more than the number of members of the Garda Síochána specified in column (2) of that Schedule opposite the mention of the rank in column (1).

###### 3.

3. The Statutory Instruments specified in the Second Schedule to this Order are hereby revoked.
