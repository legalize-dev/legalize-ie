EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Port Reception Facilities for Ship-Generated Waste and Cargo Residues) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0117.html) ) in order to give effect to Commission Directive (EU) 2015/2087 which amends Directive 2000/59/EC which improved the availability and use of port reception facilities for ship-generated waste and cargo residues, by replacing the text of Annex II in the earlier Directive with the text in the Annex to the later Directive.
