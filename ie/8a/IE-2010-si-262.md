# S.I. No. 262/2010 - Inland Fisheries Act (Establishment Day) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th June*, 2010.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5(1) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Inland Fisheries Act (Establishment Day) Order 2010.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) is 1 July 2010.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 June 2010.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order appoints the 1st July 2010 as the day on which the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) comes into operation.
