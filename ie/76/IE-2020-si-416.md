# S.I. No. 416/2020 - Forestry (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th October,* 2020.

I, Charlie McConalogue, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec6) , [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec22) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec30) of the [Forestry Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2014) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Forestry (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. The Forestry Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0191.html) ) are amended in Regulation 21(3) by substituting “to the public, for purchase at a fee not exceeding the reasonable cost of doing so,” for “to the public”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 October, 2020.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.
