# S.I. No. 321/1992 - Electoral Act, 1992 (Commencement) Order, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 321 of 1992.

ELECTORAL ACT, 1992 (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 1992.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on by him by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0001.html#sec1) (6) of the [Electoral Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1992) hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electoral Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Commencement) Order, 1992.

###### 2.

2. On the 5th day of November, 1992 the provisions of the [Electoral Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) set out in Column 1 of Part I of the Schedule to this Order shall come into operation to the extent respectively specified in Column 2 thereof.

###### 3.

3. On the 5th day of November, 1992 [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Electoral Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) shall come into operation for the purpose of effecting the repeal of such of the enactment's specified in Part I of the First Schedule to that Act as are set out in Column 1 of Part II of the Schedule to this Order, to the extent respectively specified in Column 2 thereof.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order brings into operation on 5th November, 1992 certain provisions of the [Electoral Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) relating to deposits by candidates, free postage for candidates and obstruction of electors.
