# S.I. No. 51/1993 - Health (Out-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 51 of 1993.

HEALTH (OUT-PATIENT CHARGES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) (5) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970 ) as substituted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Health (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1987) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Health (Out-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The following sub-article is hereby substituted for sub-article (2) of Article 4 of the Health (Out-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0115.html) ):

> - "(2) The charge under sub-article (1) of this Article shall be at the rate of £6 in respect of each visit in the course of which out-patient services are availed of, subject to a maximum of £42 in any period of twelve consecutive months from the 1st day of March, 1993."

###### 3.

3. These regulations shall come into operation on 1st day of March, 1993.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 24th

day of February, 1993.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 24th day of February, 1993.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide that instead of the £10 charge for out-patient services introduced in May 1987 a charge of £6 per visit be introduced from 1st March 1993 subject to a limitation of £42 in any period of twelve consecutive months from that date.
