# S.I. No. 184/1988 - Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 184 of 1988.

SOCIAL WELFARE (SUPPLEMENTARY WELFARE ALLOWANCES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) , [209](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0209.html#sec209) and [219](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0219.html#sec219) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Regulations, 1977 to 1987 may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Regulations, 1977 to 1988.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 25th day of July, 1988.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1987" means the Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 281 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0281.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Article 6 (as amended by the Regulations of 1987) of the Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowances) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 168 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0168.html) ) is hereby amended—

- (*a*) as respects persons to whom article 3 (*b*) of the Regulations of 1987 does not apply, by the substitution in sub-articles (1) (*a*) and (2) of "£3.10" for "£3", and

- (*b*) as respects persons to whom article 3 (*b*) of the Regulations of 1987 applies, by the substitution in sub-articles (1) (*a*) and (2) of "£1.60" for "£3".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare,

this 22nd day of July, 1988.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby sanctions the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

this 22nd day of July, 1988.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations increase from £3 to £3.10 the minimum rent which must be paid by claimants and recipients of rent supplements under the supplementary welfare allowances scheme. They also increase from £1.50 to £1.60 the minimum rent applicable to persons covered by a transitional provision made in 1987.
