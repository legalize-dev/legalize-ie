# S.I. No. 167/1972 - School Attendance Committees (Qualifications of Officers) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 167 of 1972.

SCHOOL ATTENDANCE COMMITTEES (QUALIFICATIONS OF OFFICERS) ORDER, 1972.

I, PÁDRAIG Ó FACHTNA, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/sec0011.html#sec11) (5) and [23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [School Attendance Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1926), hereby, after consultation with the Minister for Local Government, order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the School Attendance Committees (Qualifications of Officers) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. Article 4 (1) (a) (ii) of the School Attendance Committees (Qualifications of Officers) Order, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 329 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0329.html) ), shall not apply in the case of a person to whom the provisions of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956), apply.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of July, 1972.

**PÁDRAIG Ó FACHTNA,**

Minister for Education.
