# S.I. No. 40/1983 - The Solicitors' Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 40 of 1983.

THE SOLICITORS' ACTS 1954 AND 1960 (APPRENTICES' FEES) REGULATIONS, 1983.

The Incorporated Law Society of Ireland in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 5 and 82 of the Solicitors' Act, 1954 and with the concurrence of the President of the High Court hereby make the following Regulations.

###### 1.

1. On and after the date on which these Regulations shall come into operation, the fees specified in the Schedule hereto shall be paid to the Incorporated Law Society of Ireland by the apprentice or applicant in respect of the courses and examinations laid down in the Solicitors' Acts, 1954 and 1960 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 66 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0066.html) ) hereinafter referred to as "the New Regulations".

###### 2.

2. These Regulations may be cited as the Solicitors' Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations, 1983; they shall be read with the Solicitors' Acts, 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 203 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0203.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Solicitors' Acts 1954 and 1960 (Apprentices' Fees) (No. 2) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 204 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0204.html) ) shall be revoked as from the date of the operation of these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1983.

SCHEDULE

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. | On each application to attend the course of lectures and associated studies in the subjects specified in appendix I of the New Regulations known as the Professional Course | £1,580.00 |
| II. | On each application to attend the course of lectures and associated studies in the subjects specified in appendix II of the New Regulations known as the Advanced Course: |  |
|  | ( a ) commencing in Spring, 1983 | £500.00 |
|  | ( b ) thereafter | £565.00 |
| III. | On each application (whether for the first or any subsequent time) to attend the Final Examination—First Part | £35.00 |
| IV. | For each day or part of a day re-attending the Professional Course or the Advanced Course (including re-taking any individual part of the Final Examination—Second Part or the Final Examination—Third Part) | £15.00 |

DATED this 11th day of February, 1983.

Signed on behalf of The Incorporated Law Society of Ireland.

MICHAEL P. HOULIHAN,

President of The Incorporated

Law Society of Ireland.

In pursuance of the provisions of Section 82 of the Solicitors' Act, 1954 as amended by Section 25 (1) of the Solicitors' (Amendment) Act, 1960, I concur in the making of the above Regulations.
