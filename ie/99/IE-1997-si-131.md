# S.I. No. 131/1997 - Veterinary Surgeons (Annual Fees) Order. 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 131 of 1997.

VETERINARY SURGEONS (ANNUAL FEES) ORDER. 1997

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/sec0028.html#sec28) (4) of the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1931) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons (Annual Fees) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. In lieu of the amount appointed by Article 3 of the Veterinary Surgeons (Registration Fees and Annual Fees) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0062.html) ), £130 is hereby appointed as the amount of the fee payable under [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1931), where the person by whom the fee is payable is, on the 1st day of April in the year in respect of which the fee is payable, less than 65 years of age.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

this 25th day of March 1997.

John Bruton

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to fix at one hundred and thirty pounds the annual fee payable to the Veterinary Council of Ireland by veterinary surgeons who are aged less than 65 years. The annual fee payable by veterinary surgeons aged 65 years and over, and the registration fee payable to the Veterinary Council of Ireland are to remain at fifteen pounds and forty pounds respectively.
