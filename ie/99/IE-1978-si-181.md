# S.I. No. 181/1978 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 181 of 1978.

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 1978.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister of State at the Department of the Public Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), and the Public Service (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order. 1978 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0117.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1978.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1976, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1978.

###### 2.

2. The organisations set out in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE.

Church of Ireland College of Education, Rathmines, Dublin

The Defence Forces

Dublin Port and Docks Board

The Garda Síochána

The Irish Dunlop Company Limited

Irish Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children

Mary Immaculate College of Education, Limerick

Nitrigin Éireann Teoranta

Our Lady of Mercy College of Education, Blackrock, County Dublin

Royal Irish Academy

St. Laurence's Hospital

St. Patrick's College of Education, Drumcondra, Dublin

St. Patrick's College, Maynooth

Schools, service in which is pensionable in accordance with a scheme made under the [Teachers' Superannuation Act, 1928](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1928).

GIVEN under my hand, this 4th day of July, 1978.

RAY MACSHARRY,

Minister of State at the Departmentof the Public Service.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and "approved organisations" and between one "approved organisation" and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as "approved organisations".
