# S.I. No. 160/1985 - Gas Regulation Act, 1982 (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 160 of 1985.

GAS REGULATION ACT, 1982 (REMOVAL OF DIFFICULTIES) ORDER, 1985.

The Minister for Energy, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [Section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0016/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Gas Regulation Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1982), and the Industry and Energy (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 385 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0385.html) ), hereby makes the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Gas Regulation Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that [Section 204](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0204.html#sec204) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), applies in a case where a transferor company (within the meaning of that Section) is an undertaker within the meaning of the [Gas Regulation Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1982).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Energy this 4th day of June, 1985.

**SEÁN S. Ó MUIRI,**

A person authorised by the Minister for Energy to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister.
