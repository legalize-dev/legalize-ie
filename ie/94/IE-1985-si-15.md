# S.I. No. 15/1985 - Health (Disqualification of Officers and Servants) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 15 of 1985.

HEALTH (DISQUALIFICATION OF OFFICERS AND SERVANTS) ORDER, 1985.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Rule 8(2) of the Second Schedule of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , (No. 1 of 1970), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Health (Disqualification of Officers and Servants) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. Rule 8 of the Second Schedule of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) shall not apply to any class of officer or servant under health boards, other than the following classes of officer:—

Chief Executive Officer

Programme Manager

Finance Officer

Personnel Officer

Planning and Evaluation Officer

Management Service Officer

Technical Services Officer.

###### 3.

3. The Health (Disqualification of Officers and Servants) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0289.html) ) is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 25th day

of January 1985.

**BARRY DESMOND,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to add Management Services Officer and Technical Services Officer to the list of officers of a health board excluded from being members of the health board.
