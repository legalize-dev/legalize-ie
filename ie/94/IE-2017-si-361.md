# S.I. No. 361/2017 - Labour Affairs and Labour Law (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st August*, 2017.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Labour Affairs and Labour Law (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2017.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 1 September 2017.

###### 2.

2. (1) The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 3 are transferred to the Department of Social Protection.

- (2) References to the Department of Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation contained in any Act or any instrument made under such Act and relating to any administration and business transferred by paragraph (1) shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Department of Social Protection.

###### 3.

3. (1) The functions vested in the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation—

- (*a*) by or under the Acts specified in Part 1 of the Schedule and the provisions of the Acts specified in Part 2 of the Schedule, and

- (*b*) under the Regulations specified in Part 3 of the Schedule,

are transferred to the Minister for Social Protection.

- (2) References to the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation contained in any Act or instrument made under such Act and relating to any functions transferred by this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Social Protection.

###### 4.

4. Where, immediately before the commencement of this Order, any legal proceedings are pending to which the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation is a party and the proceedings have reference to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Social Protection, the name of the Minister for Social Protection shall, in so far as the proceedings relate to any function transferred by this Order, be substituted in the proceedings for that of the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation or added in the proceedings, as may be appropriate, and the proceedings shall not abate by reason of such substitution or addition.

###### 5.

5. Anything commenced before the commencement of this Order by or under the authority of the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation may, in so far as it relates to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Social Protection, be carried on or completed on or after such commencement by the Minister for Social Protection.

###### 6.

6. Every document (including any certificate or licence) granted or made in the performance of a function transferred by this Order, if and in so far as it was operative immediately before the commencement of this Order, has effect on and after such commencement as if it had been granted or made by the Minister for Social Protection.

###### 7.

7. References to the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation contained in the memorandum and articles of association of any company and relating to any function transferred by this Order shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Social Protection.

###### 8.

8. (1) All property, rights and liabilities held, enjoyed or incurred before the commencement of this Order by the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation in connection with the functions transferred by this Order are transferred to the Minister for Social Protection and, accordingly, without any further conveyance, transfer or assignment on such commencement—

- (*a*) that property, real and personal, shall vest in the Minister for Social Protection,

- (*b*) those rights shall be enjoyed by the Minister for Social Protection,

- (*c*) those liabilities shall be the liabilities of the Minister for Social Protection.

- (2) All moneys, stocks, shares and securities transferred to the Minister for Social Protection by this Article which, on the commencement of this Order, are standing in the name of the Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation shall on the request of the Minister for Social Protection be transferred into the name of the Minister for Social Protection.
