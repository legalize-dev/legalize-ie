# S.I. No. 181/1955 - Customs-Free Airport (Extension of Laws) Regulations, 1955

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 181 of 1955.

CUSTOMS-FREE AIRPORT (EXTENSION OF LAWS) REGULATIONS, 1955.

I, WILLIAM NORTON, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0005/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Customs-free Airport Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1947), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Customs-free Airport (Extension of Laws) Regulations, 1955.

###### 2.

2. The [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), except section 14 and subsection (1) of section 15, and the Exchange Control Regulations, 1955 ( [S.I. No. 129 of 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/si/0129.html) ), shall extend to the Customs-free Airport.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of August, 1955.

- (Signed) W. NORTON,

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of these Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 9th day of September, 1955.

- (Signed) GERARD SWEETMAN.
