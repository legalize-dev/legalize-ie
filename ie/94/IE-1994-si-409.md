# S.I. No. 409/1994 - Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Establishment of National Milk Agency) Order 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 409 of 1994.

MILK (REGULATION OF SUPPLY) (ESTABLISHMENT OF NATIONAL MILK AGENCY) ORDER 1994.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) and every and any other power me in this behalf enabling do hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. The National Milk Agency shall stand established on the 30th day of December 1994.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 5th day of December, 1994.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food

and Forestry.
