# S.I. No. 383/2025 - Electoral Act 1997 (Variation of Monetary Amount) Order 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JAMES BROWNE, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(1) (as amended by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0040/print.html#sec44) (b) of the [Seanad Electoral (University Members) (Amendment) Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0040/print.html) (No. 40 of 2024)) of the Electoral Act 1997 (No. 25 of 1997) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 408 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0408.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electoral Act 1997 (Variation of Monetary Amount) Order 2025.

###### 2.

2. The monetary amount specified in paragraph (b) (inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec5) (1) of the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2011)) of subsection (1) of section 21A of the Electoral Act 1997 (No. 25 of 1997) is varied to €250,000, in lieu of €200,000.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 July, 2025.

**JAMES BROWNE,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This Order amends section 21A(1)(b) of the Electoral Act 1997 (No. 25 of 1997) to provide for the reimbursement to a candidate at a presidential election of his/her actual expenses incurred or €250,000 whichever is the less, subject to fulfilling the criteria prescribed in section 21A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Act.
