# S.I. No. 392/2000 - Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997 (Rate of Interest Penalty) (Amendment) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/sec0010.html#sec10) (1) of the [Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1997) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister), Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997 (Rate of Interest Penalty) (Amendment) Order, 2000.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 2nd day of January 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997 (Rate of Interest Penalty) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 502 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0502.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Article 2 of the following Article:

> “2. The rate of interest penalty payable pursuant to [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/sec0004.html#sec4) (2) of the [Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1997), is hereby fixed at 0.0294 per cent per day.”

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order sets out the interest rate, which will be applicable on late payment, as required by [Section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) . It amends the rate set out in the previous Order ( [S.I. No. 62 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0062.html) ) which was effective from 6 April 1999 to 1st January 2001. The rate specified in this Order is 0.0294% per day, which is the equivlaent of 10.74% per annum.
