# S.I. No. 518/2008 - Greyhound Race Track (totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulations, 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 518 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0518.html)

GREYHOUND RACE TRACK (TOTALISATOR) (PERCENTAGE DEDUCTION) REGULATIONS, 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th December*, 2008.

Bord na gCon, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by subsection (2) of [section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0040.html#sec40) of the [Greyhound Industry Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958), and with the consent of the Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, hereby makes the following regulations, that is to say:

- (1) The Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulations, 2004 (No. 143 of 2004) are hereby revoked.

- (2) The percentage deduction on all totalisator pools operated by the Board shall be not less than 20% and not greater than 25% of the moneys staked on each pool.

- (3) These regulations may be cited as the Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulations, 2008.

- (4) These regulations shall come into operation on the 7th day of November, 2008.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of these Regulations is to establish the maximum and minimum amount which the Board may retain out of moneys staked on totalisator pools operated by the Board.
