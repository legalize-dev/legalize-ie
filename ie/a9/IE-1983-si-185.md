# S.I. No. 185/1983 - Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 185 of 1983.

HARBOUR RATES (DUNDALK HARBOUR) ORDER, 1983.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0011.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1983.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The maximum goods rates to be charged by the Dundalk Harbour Commissioners shall, in lieu of the rates prescribed by Schedule "A" (as amended by the Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0141.html) )) to the [Dundalk Harbour and Port Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/prv/0003/index.html) (No. 3 (Private) of 1925), be the rates set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The maximum tonnage rates to be charged by the Dundalk Harbour Commissioners shall, in lieu of the rates prescribed by Section 43 (as amended by the Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1981) of the Dundalk Port and Harbour Act, 1925, be the rates set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1981, is hereby revoked.

## PART II MAXIMUM TONNAGE RATES

For every vessel entering the port of Dundalk on every ton of either the net register tonnage of such vessel or half the gross tonnage of such vessel, whichever is the greater, the sum of 45p per ton.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of June, 1983.

**JIM MITCHELL,**

Minister for Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order authorises Dundalk Harbour Commissioners to charge revised maximum goods and tonnage rates at Dundalk Harbour.
