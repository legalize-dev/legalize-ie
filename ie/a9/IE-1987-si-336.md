# S.I. No. 336/1987 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 336 of 1987.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) (No. 4) REGULATIONS, 1987.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1988.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on a deposit within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1948), is hereby prescribed as 5.25 per cent. per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 3) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0256.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 18th day of December, 1987.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe as 5.25 per cent. per annum the rate of interest payable on deposits in the Post Office Savings Bank as from 1st January, 1988.Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in "Iris Oifigiúil" of 25th December, 1987.
