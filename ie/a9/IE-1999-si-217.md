# S.I. No. 217/1999 - District Court [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1997] Rules, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following Rules of Court:—

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the District Court [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1997] Rules, 1999.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on the 26th day of July 1999, and shall be read together with all other District Court Rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. The following Order shall be added to the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) after Order 99.

> “ORDER 99A
>
> PROCEDURE UNDER HOUSING
>
> (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1997.
>
> Definitions.
>
> 1. In this Order—
>
> “the Act” means the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , as amended.
>
> “house” has the meaning assigned to it by the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) .
>
> “site” has the meaning assigned to it by the Housing (Traveller Accommodation) Act 1998.
>
> Venue.
>
> 2. Proceedings under the Act may be brought, heard and determined before a sitting of the District Court for the Court District where there is situate the house or site in relation to which an application under section 3, 3A, 4 or 9 of the Act is being made.
>
> Proceedings may be heard otherwise than in public.
>
> 3. Proceedings under section 3, 3A, 4 or 9 of the Act may be heard otherwise than in public.
>
> Excluding Orders.

###### 4.

4. (1) An application to the Court under section 3 of the Act for an excluding order shall be preceded by the issue of a notice in the Form 99A.1, Schedule C.

- (2) Such notice shall be served upon the respondent in the manner provided by subsection (1)(*a*) of section 2 of the Act, at least 21 days before the date for hearing of the application.

- (3) A copy of the notice together with a statutory declaration as to service thereof shall be lodged with the Clerk at least four days before the date for hearing the application.

- (4) The order of the Court granting the application shall be in the Form 99A.2, Schedule C.

Site Excluding Orders.

###### 5.

5. (1) An application to the court under section 3A of the Act for a site excluding order shall be preceded by the issue of a notice in the Form 99A.3, Schedule C.

- (2) Such notice shall be served upon the respondent in the manner provided by subsection (1)(*a*) of section 2 of the Act, at least 21 days before the date for hearing of the application.

- (3) A copy of the notice together with a statutory declaration as to service thereof shall be lodged with the Clerk at least four days before the date for hearing the application.

- (4) The order of the Court granting the application shall be in the Form 99A.4, Schedule C.

Interim Excluding Orders/Site Excluding Orders

###### 6.

6. (1) Where an interim excluding order or site excluding order is made under the terms of section 4 of the Act on the occasion of the making of an application for an excluding order or site excluding order, it may be made on the evidence of the applicant viva voce and on oath.

- (2) Where an interim excluding order or interim site excluding order is made under the terms of section 4 of the Act between the making of an application for an excluding order or site excluding order and its determination, it shall be made on the information on oath and in writing of the applicant in the Form 99A.5, Schedule C.

- (3) Where the Court in exceptional cases considers it necessary or expedient in the interests of justice, an interim excluding order or interim site excluding order may be made *ex parte* or notwithstanding the fact that the notice referred to in rule 4/5 of this Order has not been served.

- (4) The order of the Court shall be in the Form 99A.6, Schedule C

Vary Excluding Site Excluding Interim Excluding/Site Excluding Orders.

###### 7.

7. (1) An application to the Court under

- (*a*) subsection (5) of section 3 of the Act to vary an excluding order,

- (*b*) subsection (5) of section 3 of the Act as applied by subsection (2) of section 4 of the Act to vary an interim excluding order

- (*c*) subsection (5) of section 3A of the Act to vary a site excluding order

shall be preceded by the issue of a notice in the Form 99A.7.

- (2) Such notice shall be served upon the respondent in the manner provided by subsection (1)(*a*) of section 2 of the Act, at least 21 days before the date for hearing of the application.

- (3) A copy of the notice together with a statutory declaration as to service thereof shall be lodged with the Clerk at least four days before the date for hearing the application.

- (4) The order of the Court granting the application shall be in the Form 99A.8.

Discharge Excluding Site Excluding or Interim Excluding/Site Excluding Orders.

###### 8.

8. (1) An application to the Court under subsection (1) of section 9 of the Act to discharge an excluding order, site excluding order, interim excluding order or interim site excluding order shall be preceded by the issue of a notice in the Form 99A.9, Schedule C.

- (2) Such notice shall be served upon the respondent in the manner provided by subsection (1)(*a*) of section 2 of the Act, at least 21 days before the date for hearing of the application.

- (3) A copy of the notice together with a statutory declaration as to service thereof shall be lodged with the Clerk at least four days before the date for hearing the application.

- (4) The order of the Court granting the application shall be in the Form 99A.10, Schedule C.

Clerk to supply copies of order

###### 9.

9. (1) Where the Court makes, varies or discharges an excluding order, site excluding order, interim excluding order or interim site excluding order the Clerk shall give or send a certified copy of the order in question as soon as practicable to—

- (*a*) the applicant concerned,

- (*b*) the respondent,

- (*c*) the housing authority and health board in whose functional area the house or site in respect of which the application for the order was made is situate,

by ordinary prepaid post and

- (*d*) the member of the Garda Síochána in charge of the Garda Síochána Station for the area in which the house or site in relation to which the application for the order was made is situate

by prepaid registered post.

Clerk to notify gardai when Interim Excluding/Site Excluding orders ceases to have effect.

###### 10.

10. Where an interim excluding order or interim site excluding order has been made and the application for an excluding order or site excluding order has been determined, the Clerk shall send notice in the Form 99A.11, Schedule C, that the interim excluding order or interim site excluding order has ceased to have effect, by prepaid registered post to the member of the Garda Síochána referred to in Rule 8 hereof.

Effect of appeal from orders

###### 11.

11. (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of Order 25, r.9(4) and Order 101 of these Rules and that an appellant has entered into a recognisance for appeal,

- (*a*) an appeal from an excluding order or site excluding order shall, if the court that made the order or the court to which the appeal is brought so determines (but not otherwise), stay the operation of the order on such terms (if any) as may be imposed by the court making the determination;

- (*b*) an appeal from an interim excluding order or interim site excluding order shall not stay the operation of the order.

- (2)
    - (*a*) An application to the District Court to stay the operation of an excluding order or site excluding order under the terms of section 8 of the Act, may be made following the service and lodgement of a notice of appeal and lodgement of the recognisance of appeal and when made otherwise than upon the occasion of the making of that order shall be preceded by the issue of a notice in the Form 99A.12, Schedule C, which shall be served upon the respondent to the application two days before the hearing of the application.

    - (*b*) Where the Court grants a stay on the operation of an excluding order or site excluding or under this Rule, and the Clerk has supplied the copies of the orders as directed by Rule 8 of this Order, the Clerk shall send notification of the granting of the stay in the Form 99A.13, Schedule C, to the same persons and in the manner directed by Rule 8 of this Order.”

4. The Forms numbered 99.1 to 99.13 hereafter shall be added to the Forms listed in Schedule C to the District Court Rules, 1997. (S.I. 93 of 1997)
