# S.I. No. 494/2019 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th October,* 2019.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) (as amended by the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 382 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0382.html) )) are amended –

- (*a*) in Regulation 2 –

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by substituting for the definition of “levy period” the following:

      > “ ‘levy period’ means the period commencing on 1 October 2019 and ending on 30 September 2020.”,

    - (ii) in paragraph (3), by substituting “30 June 2019” for “30 June 2018”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3(1), by substituting “1 October 2019” for “1 October 2018”,

- (*c*) in Regulation 4(1), by substituting “29 February 2020” for “28 February 2019”, and

- (*d*) in Column (3) of the Schedule, opposite Reference Number 5, by substituting “0.0274 per cent” for “0.0511 per cent”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 September, 2019.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
