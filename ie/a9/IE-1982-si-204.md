# S.I. No. 204/1982 - Post Office Savings Bank Regulations, 1921 (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 204 of 1982.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK REGULATIONS, 1921 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, JOHN P. WILSON, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section II of the Post Office Savings Bank Act, 1861, and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank Regulations, 1921 (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 25th day of June, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 45 of the Post Office Savings Bank Regulations, 1921, is hereby amended by the substitution of "£50" for "£30" (inserted by Regulation 3 (1) of the Post Office Savings Bank Regulations, 1921 (Amendment) Regulations, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0057.html) )).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of June, 1982.

**JOHN P. WILSON.**

I, RAY MACSHARRY, Minister for Finance, hereby consent to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of June, 1982.

**RAY MACSHARRY.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations increase the amount which may be withdrawn on demand from £30 to £50.
