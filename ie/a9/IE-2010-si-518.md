# S.I. No. 518/2010 - Adoption Act 2010 (Section 85) (Fees) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th November*, 2010.

I, BARRY ANDREWS, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 150](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec150) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) and the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 508 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0508.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Section 85) (Fees) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. These regulations come into operation on 2 November 2010.

###### 3.

3. (1)(*a*) The amount of €2 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under paragraph (*a*) of section 85(2) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) for a particular search in the index to the Adopted Children Register.

- (*b*) The amount of €20 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under that paragraph for a general search in that index.

- (*c*) For the purposes of this regulation, a “particular search” means a search over any period not exceeding five years for any particular entry in the register and a “general search” means a search, not exceeding seven consecutive hours over any period exceeding five years.

- (2) The amount of €8 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under paragraph (*b*) of that section for a copy certified by him or her of an entry in the Register.

- (3) The amount of €4 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under that paragraph for a copy (not being a copy so certified) of such an entry.

- (4) The amount of €8 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under that paragraph for a certified extract of such an entry.

- (5) The amount of €2 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under paragraph (*a*) of section 85(3) of the said Act for a search by him or her in the Register or in the index to it.

- (6) The amount of €8 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under paragraph (*b*) of that section for a copy certified by him or her to be a true copy of an entry in the Register.

- (7) The amount of €4 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under that paragraph for a copy (not being a copy so certified) of such an entry.

- (8) The amount of €8 is prescribed as the fee payable to an tArd-Chláraitheoir under that paragraph for a certified extract of such an entry.

GIVEN under my hand,

2 November 2010.

BARRY ANDREWS,

Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations set out the fees prescribed under [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec85) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) .
