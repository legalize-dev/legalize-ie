# S.I. No. 255/1973 - Local Authorities (Travelling Expenses of Members) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 255 of 1973.

LOCAL AUTHORITIES (TRAVELLING EXPENSES OF MEMBERS) ORDER, 1973.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (2) of [section 80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/sec0080.html#sec80) of the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1941) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Travelling Expenses of Members) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. [Section 80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/sec0080.html#sec80) of the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , shall apply to each of the local authorities specified in the Schedule hereto.

###### 3.

3. The Local Authorities (Travelling Expenses of Members) Order, 1960 ( [S.I. No. 201 of 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/si/0201.html) ) is hereby revoked.

SCHEDULE.

Local Authorities to which [section 80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/sec0080.html#sec80) of the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) , applies.

_________

1. A council of a county or other borough, a council of an urban district and commissioners of a town.

2. A committee or joint committee or board or joint board (whether corporate or unincorporated) appointed by or under statute to perform the functions or any of the functions of a council of a county or of any of the bodies mentioned in the immediately preceding paragraph of this Schedule.

3. A committee or joint committee or board or joint board (whether corporate or unincorporated) other than a vocational education committee or a committee of agriculture of or appointed by a council of a county or one or more of the bodies mentioned in paragraph 1 of this Schedule.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government this 30th day of August, 1973.

**J. TULLY,**

Minister for Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order applies to the local authorities specified in the Schedule to the Order [Section 80](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/sec0080.html#sec80) of the [Local Government Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) . Section 80 provides for the payment of travelling expenses to members of local authorities to which the section applies.
