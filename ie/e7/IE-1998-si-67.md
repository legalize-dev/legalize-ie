# S.I. No. 67/1998 - Presidential Establishment Act, 1938 (Increase of Emoluments and Allowances) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 67 of 1998.

PRESIDENTIAL ESTABLISHMENT ACT, 1938 (INCREASE OF EMOLUMENTS AND ALLOWANCES) ORDER, 1998

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 1 (3) (inserted by the [Presidential Establishment (Amendment) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1991)) of the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1938), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Increase of Emoluments and Allowances) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. Paragraph (a) (inserted by the [Presidential Establishment (Amendment) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1991)) of subsection (1) (inserted by the [Presidential Establishment (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1973)) of section I of the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1938), is hereby amended by the substitution for "£120,000" (inserted by the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 1 (3)) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0455.html) )) of£250,000".

GIVEN Under the Official Seal of the Government, this 10th day of March, 1998.

**BERTIE AHERN**

Taoiseach
