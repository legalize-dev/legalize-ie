# S.I. No. 460/1994 - National Milk Agency (Election Day) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 460 of 1994.

NATIONAL MILK AGENCY (ELECTION DAY) ORDER, 1994.

I, IVAN YATES, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph 5 (2) of the Schedule to the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) and any and every other power me in this behalf enabling do hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Milk Agency (Election Day) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The 22nd day of November 1995 is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the National Milk Agency.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of December, 1994.

**IVAN YATES,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food

and Forestry.
