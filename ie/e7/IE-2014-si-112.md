# S.I. No. 112/2014 - European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th March*, 2014.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Animal Health) (Adaptation) Regulations 2014 and come into operation on 6 March 2014.

###### 2.

2. A reference in a provision mentioned in Schedule 1 to “authorised officer” is construed and has effect as if the reference includes a reference to an authorised officer within the meaning of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec2) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. An authorised officer, within the meaning of an instrument mentioned in column (2) of Schedule 2 may, in substitution for the powers mentioned in column (4) opposite that mention, exercise any or all of the powers mentioned in [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec38) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) .

###### 4.

4. Each instrument mentioned in column (2) of Schedule 2 is repealed to the extent specified in column (3) of that Schedule opposite that mention.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of these Regulations is to provide that an authorised officer within the meaning of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) is also an authorised for the purposes of the instruments cited in Schedule 1 and, in place of the powers cite in column (4) of Schedule 2, an authorised officer may exercise the powers conferred by [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec38) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) .
