# S.I. No. 11/2024 - Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board (Amendment) Bye-law 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th January,* 2024.

The Dietitians Registration Board, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec31) of the [Health and Social Care Professionals Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) , with the approval of the Health and Social Care Professionals Council, hereby makes the following bye-law:

###### 1.

1. (1) This bye-law may be cited as the Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board (Amendment) Bye-law 2024.

- (2) The Principal Bye-law and this bye-law may be cited together as the Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board Bye-laws 2023 and 2024.

###### 2.

2. (1) In this bye-law “Principal Bye-Law” means the Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board Bye-law 2023 ( [S.I. No. 357 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0357.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Paragraph 7 of the Principal Bye-Law is amended in subparagraph (2) by substituting “23” for “24”.

###### 4.

4. The Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board Bye-law 2016 ( [S.I. No. 387 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0387.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under the seal of the Dietitians Registration Board,

17 January, 2024.

**GERALDINE MURRAY,**

Chairperson, Dietitians Registration Board.

**ANNEMARIE BENNETT,**

Member, Dietitians Registration Board.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This bye-law corrects a typographical error in paragraph 7(2) of the Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board Bye-law 2023.

This bye-law also revokes the Election of Members for Appointment to the Dietitians Registration Board Bye-law 2016 ( [S.I. No. 387 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0387.html) ).
