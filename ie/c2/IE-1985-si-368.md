# S.I. No. 368/1985 - European Communities (Removal of Restrictions on Immature Spirits) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 368 of 1985.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (REMOVAL OF RESTRICTIONS ON IMMATURE SPIRITS) REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Articles 5 and 30 of the EEC Treaty and having regard to the Reasoned Opinion of the European Communities dated the 17th day of June, 1985, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Removal of Restrictions on Immature Spirits) Regulations, 1985, and shall come into operation on the 1st day of December, 1985.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act of 1947" means the [Immature Spirits (Restriction) Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1947).

###### 3.

3. The following subsection is hereby substituted for subsection (1) of section 2 of the Act of 1947:

> - "(1) Subject to this section, no Irish spirits or foreign spirits shall be delivered for home consumption unless they have been warehoused for at least three years.".

###### 4.

4. The following is hereby substituted for paragraph (*e*) of subsection (3) of section 2 of the Act of 1947:

- "(*e*) foreign liqueurs, or

- (*f*) vodka, or

- (*g*) rum.".

###### 5.

5. The following is hereby inserted in the Schedule to the Act of 1947:

> "

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No. 29 of 1969 | [Immature Spirits (Restriction) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) | The whole Act |
| No. 16 of 1972 | [Immature Spirits (Restriction) Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) | The whole Act |

> "

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of November, 1985.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations, which come into operation on 1 December 1985 provide for the removal of the Compulsory Warehousing Requirements for Vodka and Rum.
