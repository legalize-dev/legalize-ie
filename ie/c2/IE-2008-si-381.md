# S.I. No. 381/2008 - Video Recordings Act 1989 (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 381 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0381.html)

VIDEO RECORDINGS ACT 1989 (FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th September*, 2008.

I, DERMOT AHERN, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 and 31 (as amended by Part 9 of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2008)) of the [Video Recordings Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1989), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Video Recordings Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1st October, 2008.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 3 of the [Video Recordings Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 113 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0113.html) ) is amended:

- (*a*) by the substitution of “subject to paragraphs (1A) to (4),” for “Subject to paragraphs (2) to (4),” in paragraph (1);

- (*b*) by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (1):

  > - “(1A) where a video recording containing the video work in respect of which the application for a supply certificate is being made is to be released for letting on hire or for sale to the ultimate consumer and the video work concerned is a video work to which subparagraph (i) or (ii) of section 31(3)(*b*) (inserted by section 69(*q*) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2008)) refers, €2.50 per minute;”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

25 September 2008

**BRIAN LENIHAN.**

Minister for Finance

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

25 September 2008

**DERMOT AHERN.**

Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the fees to be charged in relation to applications for supply certificates under Section 3 in the case of video works that are likely to appeal only to a limited or specialist audience, or are likely to be distributed for charitable purposes.
