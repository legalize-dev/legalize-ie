# S.I. No. 240/2019 - European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th June,* 2019.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/874 of 22 May 2017[^1] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1270 of 14 July 2017[^2] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1271 of 14 July 2017[^3] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1399 of 28 July 2017[^4] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/74 of 17 January 2018[^5] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/75 of 17 January 2018[^6] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/97 of 22 January 2018[^7] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/98 of 22 January 2018[^8] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/677 of 3 May 2018[^9] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/681 of 4 May 2018[^10] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/682 of 4 May 2018[^11] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1461 of 28 September 2018[^12] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1462 of 28 September 2018[^13] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1481 of 4 October 2018[^14] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1497 of 8 October 2018[^15] as affected by the Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1497[^16] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the Regulations of 2016, the Regulations of 2018 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Union (Food Additives) Regulations 2015 to 2019.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Food Additives) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0330.html) );

“Regulations of 2016” means the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 484 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0484.html) );

“Regulations of 2018” means the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 413 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0413.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 27 of the Principal Regulations is amended by inserting after paragraph (14) the following:

> - “(15) Notwithstanding the provisions of Part 2, a food business operator is not guilty of an offence under these Regulations for failure to comply with Annex II to EC Regulation 1333/2008 as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/97 of 22 January 2018[^7] , in respect of fine bakery products for special nutritional uses containing E 950 Acesulfame K, E 951 Aspartame, E 952 Cyclamic acid and its Na and Ca salts, E 954 Saccharin and its Na, K and Ca salts, E 955 Sucralose, E 959 Neohesperidine DC, E 961 Neotame, E 962 Salt of aspartame-acesulfame or E 969 Advantame, placed on the market prior to 12 February 2018, which may continue to be marketed after that date until stocks are exhausted, provided that, in all such cases, such foods were placed on the market in conformity with the provisions applicable thereto at the date of their placing on the market.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/874 of 22 May 2017, Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1270 of 14 July 2017, Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1271 of 14 July 2017, Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1399 of 28 July 2017, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/74 of 17 January 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/75 of 17 January 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/97 of 22 January 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/98 of 22 January 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/677 of 3 May 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/681 of 4 May 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/682 of 4 May 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1461 of 28 September 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1462 of 28 September 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1481 of 4 October 2018, Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1497 of 8 October 2018 as affected by the Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2018/1497 of 8 October 2018 which amend Regulation (EC) No. 1333/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 231/2012 of 9 March 2012.

These Regulations amend the European Union (Food Additives) Regulations 2015 for that purpose.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Food Additives) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.
