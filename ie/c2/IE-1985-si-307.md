I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) , [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0003.html#sec3) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1932), and by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1938), as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service, make the following Scheme:

1 Short title, collective citation and construction.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 5) Scheme, 1985.

- (2) The collective citation, the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1985, shall include this scheme.

- (3) The Defence Forces (Pensions) scheme, 1937 to 1985, shall be construed together as one Scheme.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall come into force on the day after the date on which it is made.

3 Application of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .

###### 3.

**3.** The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

4 Definition.

###### 4.

**4.** In this Scheme—

"the Principal Scheme" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) Scheme, 1937 (S.R. & O., No. 249 of 1937);

"the Amending Scheme of 1947" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Scheme, 1947 (S.R. & O., No. 81 of 1947);

"the No. 3 Amending Scheme of 1970" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1970 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0298.html) );

"the No. 2 Amending Scheme of 1985" means the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 221 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0221.html) ).

5 Increase of retired pay of officers retiring on or after 2nd day of July, 1985.

###### 5.

**5.** (1) Where retired pay is granted under Article 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 9A, 10 or 10A of the Principal Scheme to an officer who retires or is retired on or after the 2nd day of July, 1985, such pay (as increased by Article 5 of the No. 2 Amending Scheme of 1985) shall be further increased by the appropriate percentage indicated in the Table to this Article opposite the mention of such officer's retiring rank.

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 2nd day of July, 1985.

7 Amendment of Article 26A of the Principal Scheme.

###### 7.

**7.** (1) The Principal Scheme shall be construed and have effect as if the following Article were substitued for Article 26A (inserted by Article 22 of the Amending Scheme of 1947) (as amended by Article 7 of the No. 2 Amending Scheme of 1985);

"26A. (1) Subject to the privisions of this Scheme, there may be granted to any long-service soldier who is discharged on or after the 1st day of July, 1985, and whose qualifying service is 21 years or more, a pension at the rate per week specified in Table 1 to this Article opposite the mention of his retiring rank.

- (2) Where—

( *a* ) a soldier is permitted to extend his service in the Forces beyond 21 years service, and

( *b* ) such soldire's qualifying service is 22 years or more and

( *c* ) such soldier is granted a pension under sub-article (1) of this Article,

Table 1 to this Article shall have effect as if the weekly sum set out in the appropriate column of the said Table opposite the mention of his retiring rank were increased, in respect of each year of his qualifying service in excess of twenty-one years but not in excess of 31 years, by £0.05 plus an additional increase at the rate specified in Table II to this Article.

- (3) In the case of a soldier who is discharged before the 1st day of August, 1978, the additional increase specified in Table II of this Article shall cease to be payable when he reaches the age of 70 years or becomes otherwise entitled to a retirement pension under the Social Welfare Acts.

- (4) Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-article (3) of this Article, the additional increase specified in Table II to this Article shall continue to be payable when a soldier reaches the age of 70 years, if, without his own default, he fails to qualify for a contributory old age pension under the Social Welfare Acts.

- (5) In the case of a soldier who is discharged on or after the 1st day of August, 1978, the additional increase specified in Table II to this Article shall cease to be payable when he reaches the age prescribed for a contributory old age pension or becomes otherwise entitled to a retirement pension under the Social Welfare Acts.

- (6) Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-article (5) of this Article, the additional increase specified in Table II to this Article shall continue to be payable when a soldier reaches the age prescribed for a contributory old age pension, if, without his own default, he fails to qualify for a contributory old age pension under the Social Welfare Acts.

- (7) In this Article and in Table I hereto "retiring rank" has the meaning assigned to it in Article 25 of the Principle Scheme.

9 Increase of gratuity on retirement of certain officers.

###### 9.

**9.** (1) The additional gratuity payable, under Article 6 of the No. 3 Amending Scheme of 1970, as amended to an officer, to whom sub-article (1) (*a*) of the said Article 6 applies, who retired within 122 days before the 1st day of July, 1985, shall, if he was alive on that date, be increased to an amount equal to the amount which would be payable to him if the date of his retirement had been the 2nd day of July, 1985, and his appointment, rank, service in that rank and total service were the same as those on the date of his actual retirement.

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1985.

10 Increase of gratuity on discharge of certain long-service soldiers.

###### 10.

**10.** (1) The gratuity payable, under Article 10 of the No. 3 Amending Scheme of 1970, as amended, or under Article 12 of the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 171 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0171.html) ), to a long service soldier, who was discharged within 122 days before the 1st day of July, 1985, on reaching the age limit prescribed in Defence Force Regulations, shall, if he was alive on that date, be increased to an amount equal to the amount which would be payable to him if the date of his discharge had been the 1st day of July, 1985, and his rank service in that rank and total service were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge.

- (2) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1985.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This statutory instrument provides for increases in the amount of retirement benefits payable to officers of the Permanent Defence Force retiring on or after 2nd July, 1985 and in the pensions and gratuities payable to soldiers discharged or transferred to the Reserve Defence Force on or after 1st July, 1985. It also provides for the increase of gratuities granted to officers who retired and soldiers who were discharged on age grounds within 122 days before 1st July, 1985.
