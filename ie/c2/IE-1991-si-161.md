> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 161 of 1991.

COMPANIES (FORMS) ORDER, 1991.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 160](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0160.html#sec160) , [195](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0195.html#sec195) , [311](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0311.html#sec311) A, [395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Order, 1991.

- (2) The Companies (Forms) Orders, 1964 to 1990, and this Order may be cited together as the Companies (Forms) Orders, 1964 to 1991.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1991.

###### 3.

3. In this Order—

"the Act of 1963" means the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963);

"the Act of 1990" means the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990).

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Part I of the Schedule to this Order, or a form to the like effect, is hereby prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 160 (5A) (inserted by section 183 of the Act of 1990) of the Act of 1963 with such variations as circumstances require.

###### 5.

5. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0045.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Form No. 9 (inserted by Article 3 of the Companies (Forms) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0256.html) )) in the Schedule thereto of Form B10 set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 6.

6. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0256.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Form No. A1 (inserted by Article 4 of the Companies (Forms) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0147.html) )) in Part I of the Schedule thereto of the form set out in Part III of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 7.

7. The form set out in Part IV of the Schedule to this Order, or a form to the like effect, is hereby prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 311A (inserted by section 246 of the Act of 1990) of the Act of 1963 with such variations as circumstances require.

###### 8.

8. The Fifth Schedule to the Act of 1963 is hereby altered by the substitution for the form set out in Part II of the forms set out in Part V of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 9.

9. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1964 is hereby amended by the deletion in the Schedule of Form No. 9A.
