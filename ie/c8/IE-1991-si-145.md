# S.I. No. 145/1991 - Worker Protection (Regular Part-Time Employees) Act, 1991 (Social Welfare Regulations) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 145 of 1991.

WORKER PROTECTION (REGULAR PART-TIME EMPLOYEES) ACT, 1991 (SOCIAL WELFARE REGULATIONS) ORDER, 1991.

I, BERTIE AIIERN, T.D., Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 1 (3) (a) of the [Worker Protection (Regular Part-Time Employees) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1991), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Worker Protection (Regular Part-Time Employees) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (Social Welfare Regulations) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The Social Welfare (Subsidiary Employments) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 73 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0073.html) ), and the Social Welfare (Employment of Inconsiderable Extent) (No. 2) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 72 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0072.html) ), are hereby prescribed for the purposes of paragraphs (*a*) (i), (*f*) (i) and (*g*) of the definition of "excluding provision" in [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0005/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Worker Protection (Regular Part-Time Employees) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) , or any of them, being regulations made by the Minister for Social Welfare and to which either or both subsection 1 of section 4 of the Act of 1967 and section 3 of the Act of 1984 relates.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 17th day of June, 1991.

**BERTIE AHERN, T.D.,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes for the purposes of the "excluding provision" in paragraphs (*a*) (i), (*f*) (i) and (*g*) of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0005/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Worker Protection (Regular Part-Time Employees) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) , particular regulations made by the Minister for Social Welfare relating to subsidiary employment and employment of inconsiderable extent.
