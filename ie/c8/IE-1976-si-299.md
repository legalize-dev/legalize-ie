# S.I. No. 299/1976 - Imposition of Duties (No. 226) (Footwear) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 299 of 1976.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 226) (FOOTWEAR) ORDER, 1976.

The Government, pursuant to a Decision of the Commission of the European Communities dated the 22nd day of December, 1975 (as modified by Decisions of the Commission of the European Communities dated the 25th day of June, 1976, and the 17th day of November, 1976), and addressed to the Government, and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 226) (Footwear) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding the revocation of the Imposition of Duties (No. 225) (Footwear) Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 171 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0171.html) ), effected by Article 4 of this Order, the Imposition of Duties (No. 200) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0220.html) ), shall have effect as if, for the matter contained in the fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth and ninth columns of the Schedule thereto at tariff heading number 64.02A, there were substituted "23.5%", "23.5%", "17.3%", "9%" and "9%", respectively.

###### 3.

3. The duty of customs on goods mentioned in the first column of the Schedule to the said Imposition of Duties (No. 200) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) Order, 1972, at tariff heading number 64.02A and produced or manufactured in and consigned from Member States of the European Economic Community other than the United Kingdom shall be charged at the rate of an amount equal to 18.5 per cent. of the value of the goods in lieu of the rate chargeable thereon immediately before the 1st day of January, 1976.

###### 4.

4. The Imposition of Duties (No. 225) (Footwear) Order, 1976, is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 17th day of

December, 1976.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to maintain the rates of duty, on footwear subject to duty at Tariff Heading 64.02A, which had been introduced on 1st January, 1976.
