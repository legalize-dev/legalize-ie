# S.I. No. 516/2016 - National Minimum Wage Order 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th October*, 2016.

I, PAT BREEN, Minister of State at the Department of Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1) of section 10D (inserted by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec8) of the [National Minimum Wage (Low Pay Commission) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2015)) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) ) and the Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2016 ( [S.I. No. 333 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0333.html) ), having received on 15 July 2016, a recommendation made by the Low Pay Commission under subsection (1) of section 10C (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec7) of the [National Minimum Wage (Low Pay Commission) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) , having considered the report and having had regard to section 10C of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) , hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the National Minimum Wage Order 2016.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 1 January 2017.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that the national minimum hourly rate of pay for the purposes of the National Minimum Wage Acts 2000 and 2015 shall be €9.25.

Given under my hand,

13 October 2016.

PAT BREEN,

Minister of State at the Department of Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order sets the national minimum hourly rate of pay from 1 January 2017 at €9.25.
