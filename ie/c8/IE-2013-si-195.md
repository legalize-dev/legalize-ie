# S.I. No. 195/2013 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot and Mouth Disease Orders) (Revocation) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th June*, 2013.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) , [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0014.html#sec14) , [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0015.html#sec15) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011, ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) ) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot and Mouth Disease Orders) (Revocation) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. The following are revoked—

- (*a*) the Foot and Mouth Disease Order 1956 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0324.html) ),

- (*b*) the Foot and Mouth Disease Order 1956 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1967 ( [S.I. No. 310 of 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/si/0310.html) ),

- (*c*) Swine Vesicular Disease Order 1972 ( [S.I. No. 340 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0340.html) )

- (*d*) African Swine Fever Order 1986 ( [S.I. No. 288 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0288.html) )

- (*e*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease)(Export and Import of Horses) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 105 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0105.html) ),

- (*f*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease)(Import Restrictions)(No. 2)(Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 107 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0107.html) ),

- (*g*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease)(Import Restrictions)(No. 2)(Second Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 143 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0143.html) ),

- (*h*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease)(Import) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 162 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0162.html) ),

- (*i*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease)(Export and Import of Horses) (Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 197 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0197.html) ),

- (*j*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Removal of Certain Import Restrictions) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 239 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0239.html) ),

- (*k*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the United Kingdom) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0277.html) ),

- (*l*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Exhibition, Sale and Transport of Animals) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 318 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0318.html) ),

- (*m*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Artificial Insemination) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0324.html) ),

- (*o*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Restriction on Movement of Certain Animals) (No. 2) (Third Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 382 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0382.html) ),

- (*p*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Exhibition, Sale and Transport of Animals) (Amendment) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 420 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0420.html) ),

- (*q*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Import of Sheep for Fattening and Breeding from Northern Ireland) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 503 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0503.html) )

- (*r*) the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Removal of Certain Restrictions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0031.html) ),

- (*t*) Classical Swine Fever Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 561 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0561.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 May 2013.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations revoke certain orders made under the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) as legislation to transpose Council Directive 2003/85/EC in relation to suspected and confirmed outbreaks of Foot and Mouth has already been enacted.
