# S.I. No. 155/1988 - Copyright (Republic of Indonesia) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 155 of 1988.

COPYRIGHT (REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA) ORDER, 1988.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0010/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Copyright Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1963), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Copyright (Republic of Indonesia) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, "the Act" means the [Copyright Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. Subject to Article 4 of this Order, the Act shall, as respects acts done or omissions made after the commencement of this Order, apply to sound recordings made by a citizen of, or first published (whether before or after the making of this Order) in, the Republic of Indonesia in like manner as if the sound recordings were made by a citizen of or first published within the State.

###### 4.

4. Copyright subsisting by virtue only of this Order in a sound recording shall not include the right to equitable remuneration under section 17(4)(b) of the Act unless that right or a right giving rise to a claim for remuneration subsists in the Republic of Indonesia.

###### 5.

5. This Order shall come into operation on the 30th day of June, 1988.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government this 30th day of June, 1988.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Tanaiste.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this order is to extend the benefits of [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0010/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Copyright Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) to the Republic of Indonesia.
