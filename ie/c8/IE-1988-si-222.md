# S.I. No. 222/1988 - Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974 (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 222 of 1988.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (ROADS AND MOTORWAYS) ACT, 1974 (PRESCRIBED FORMS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Local Government (Roads and motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Prescribed Forms) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Act" means the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1974):

"the 1977 Regulations" means the [Local Government (Roads and Motorways) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Prescribed Forms) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0395.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Form in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby substituted for Form No. 2 in the Schedule to the 1977 Regulations.

###### 4.

4. Form No. 5 in the Second Schedule to these Regulations or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of subsection (1A) of section 5 of the Act.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the 1977 Regulations prescribing the forms to be used by a road authority in connection with the preparation and submission to the Minister of a scheme for the provision of a motorway to include the environmental impact study required under the European Communities (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Motorways) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 221 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0221.html) ).
