# S.I. No. 57/2009 - Public Health (Tobacco) (Retail Sign) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 57 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0057.html)

PUBLIC HEALTH (TOBACCO) (RETAIL SIGN) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th February*, 2009.

I, MARY WALLACE, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 43(4)(*c*) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec14) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) (Amendment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2004)) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002), and the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 4) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 482 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0482.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Public Health (Tobacco) (Retail Sign) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 July 2009.

###### 3.

3. The sign set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 43(4)(*c*) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec14) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) (Amendment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2004)) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002).

###### 4.

4. (1) The sign prescribed in Regulation 3 shall—

- (*a*) contain the text set out in the Schedule,

- (*b*) be printed on white paper or card board, and

- (*c*) conform to the dimensions, type-face colour, font and point size of the sign set out in Schedule.

- (2) The name and the retail register number, when available, of the retailer and the address of the premises concerned shall be inserted where indicated on the sign prescribed in Regulation 3 and set out in the Schedule.

- (3) Attention shall not be drawn to such sign by means of light, sound or any other means.

## SCHEDULE

GIVEN under my hand,

17 February 2009

MARY WALLACE

Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Health (Tobacco) (Retail Sign) Regulations 2009.

With effect from 1 July 2009 section 43(4)(*c*) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec14) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) (Amendment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2004)) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002) requires that a sign as prescribed in these Regulations be displayed at a premises where tobacco products are sold by retail.
