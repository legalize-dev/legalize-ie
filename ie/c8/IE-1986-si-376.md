> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 376 of 1986.

CIRCUIT COURT (FEES) ORDER, 1986.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of December, 1986.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 37 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0037.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for the Schedule thereto of the following Schedule:
