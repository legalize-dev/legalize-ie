# S.I. No. 545/2002 - Gas Act 1976 (Section 10A(1)(E)) (Annual Rate of Consumption) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Dermot Ahern, T.D., Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 4 and 10A(1)(e) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec14) (1) of the [Gas (Interim) (Regulation) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2002)) of the [Gas Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1976), and the Communications, Energy and Geological Survey of Ireland (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0300.html) (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Gas Act 1976 (Section 10A(1)(e) (Annual Rate of Consumption) Regulations 2002.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2003.

###### 2.

2. The rate of 500,000 standard cubic metres per annum is prescribed for the purposes of section 10A(1)(e) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec14) (1) of the [Gas (Interim) (Regulation) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2002)) of the [Gas Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1976) in lieu of the rate of 2,000,000 standard cubic metres per annum specified in that paragraph.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This Order amends section 10A of the [Gas Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) , which was previously amended by [Section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec14) of the [Gas (Interim)(Regulation) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) . The amendment reduces the consumption threshold for third party access from 2,000,000 to 500,000 standard cubic metres per annum.
