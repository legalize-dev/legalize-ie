# S.I. No. 559/2008 - National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000 (Approval) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 559 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0559.html)

NATIONAL BEEF ASSURANCE SCHEME ACT 2000 (APPROVAL) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th December*, 2008.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 1(5) and 8(1) of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000) as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) ), hereby order as follows:

1.This Order may be cited as the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (Approval) Order 2008.

2.The period for participants to apply for the grant of a certificate of approval under section 8 of the Act is extended until 31 December 2011.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 December 2008

**BRENDAN SMITH**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the period for participants to apply for approval under the National Beef Assurance Scheme.
