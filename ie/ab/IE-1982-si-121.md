# S.I. No. 121/1982 - Register of Trout, Coarse Fish and Sea Anglers (Fixed Day) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 121 of 1982.

REGISTER OF TROUT, COARSE FISH AND SEA ANGLERS (FIXED DAY) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, BRENDAN DALY, Minister for Fisheries and Forestry, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0058.html#sec58) (5) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980 ), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0195.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Register of Trout, Coarse Fish and Sea Anglers (Fixed Day) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The 31st day of May is hereby fixed for the purposes of subsection (2) of [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0058.html#sec58) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980 ), and the said 31st day of May is so fixed in relation to any year.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of April, 1982.

**BRENDAN DALY,**

Minister for Fisheries and Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations fix the 31st day of May, in relation to any year, as the day fixed for the purposes of subsection (2) of [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0058.html#sec58) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . In accordance with the provisions of that subsection, the latest date by which a person may, by way of payment of the appropriate annual subscription to a Regional Fisheries Board, renew his registration in the Trout, Coarse Fish and Sea Anglers Register maintained by that Board, shall be the 30th of May each year. Failure to renew his registration by that date shall result in the removal of a person's name from the Register.
