# S.I. No. 306/1994 - European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) (Forms) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 306 of 1994.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (SINGLE-MEMBER PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANIES) (FORMS) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 89/667/EEC of 21 December, 1989 ^(1) hereby make the following Regulations:

^(1) O.J No. L395, 30.12.1989, pp. 40-42.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) (Forms) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the 1994 Single Member Regulations" means the European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 275 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0275.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations, or a form to like effect, is hereby prescribed for the purposes of Regulations 5 of the 1994 Single-Member Regulations with such variations as circumstances require.

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Part 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations, or a form to like effect, is hereby prescribed for the purposes of Regulations 6 of the 1994 Single-Member Regulations with such variations as circumstances require.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Forms Order prescribes forms for use in the return of information to the Companies Registration Office in compliance with Regulations 5 and 6 of the European Communities (Single-Member Private Limited Companies) Regulations, 1994 (S.I. 275 of 1994).
