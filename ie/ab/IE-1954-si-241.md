# S.I. No. 241/1954 - Fines and Penalties (Disposal) Order, 1954

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 241 of 1954.

FINES AND PENALTIES (DISPOSAL) ORDER, 1954.

I, JAMES EVERETT, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0027/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Court Officers Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1926), and with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance (signified by his official seal affixed hereto), hereby order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Fines and Penalties (Disposal) Order, 1954.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. Every fine imposed in respect of an offence under the Health Acts, 1947 to 1954, which is prosecuted by a health authority or by any board or authority authorised in that behalf under section 45, section 46 or [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) , shall, in lieu of being paid into the Exchequer, be paid to that authority or board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of November, 1954.

**JAMES EVERETT,**

Minister for Justice.

I concur.

**GERARD SWEETMAN,**

Minister for Finance.
