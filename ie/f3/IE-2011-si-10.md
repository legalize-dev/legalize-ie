# S.I. No. 10/2011 - European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Movement of Pet Birds) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st January*, 2011.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Decision 2010/734/EU of 30 November 20101 [^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Movement of Pet Birds) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Movement of Pet Birds) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 96 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0096.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “Commission Decision” the following—

> “ ‘Commission Decision’ means Commission Decision 2007/25/EC of 22 December 2006 and Commission Decision 2010/734/EU of 30 November 2010;”.

Given under my Official Seal,

14 January 2011.

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order gives effect to Commission Decision No. 2010/734/EU of 30 November 2010 concerning certain protective measures with regard to pet birds imported from third countries.
