# S.I. No. 84/1998 - Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997 (Section 23) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 84 of 1998.

FISHERIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1997 (SECTION 23) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, MICHAEL WOODS, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0023.html#sec23) (3) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997), as adapted by the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0301.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (Section 23) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 31st day of March, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (3) of [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations prescribe certain organisations for the purpose of enabling them to nominate persons from whom the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources may appoint the 6 ordinary members of the Aquaculture Licences Appeals Board under [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23).
