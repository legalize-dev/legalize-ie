# S.I. No. 396/2010 - Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into operation on 4 October 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th August*, 2010.

I, CONOR LENIHAN, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec20) (1), [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec30) (1), [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec31) (1) and [85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec85) of the [Industrial Designs Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2001) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0185.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 332 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0332.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2010 and come into operation on 4 October 2010.

###### 2.

2. The Industrial Designs Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0280.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 8(1) (as amended by Regulation 2 of the Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 620 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0620.html) )), by substituting “in a state which is a contracting party to the Agreement on the European Economic Area signed at Oporto on 2 May 1992, as amended for the time being,” for “in the European Union”, and

- (*b*) in Schedule 2, by substituting—

    - (i) Form No. 1 in the Schedule to these Regulations for Form No. 1 (substituted by the Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 574 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0574.html) )), and

    - (ii) Form No. 1A in the Schedule to these Regulations for Form No. 1A (substituted by the Industrial Designs (Amendment) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 574 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0574.html) )).

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The Regulations make a slight change to the Industrial Design Regulations 2002 by clarifying that a person, involved in any proceeding before the Controller of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks or a registered proprietor of a design, may have an address for service in the European Economic Area (EEA). The Regulations also amend Forms 1 and 1A in Schedule 2 to those Regulations by improving the layout of Forms 1 and 1A and also amending the address for service requirement to allow applicants to provide an address for service within the EEA.
