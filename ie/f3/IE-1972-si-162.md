# S.I. No. 162/1972 - Imposition of Duties (No. 199) (Excise Duties) (Firearm Certificates) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 162 of 1972.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (NO. 199) (EXCISE DUTIES) (FIREARM CERTIFICATES) ORDER, 1972.

The Government, in exercise of the power conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), hereby order as follows :—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 199) (Excise Duties) (Firearm Certificates) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. [Section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0017/sec0016.html#sec16) of the [Finance Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1966), shall, in respect of firearm certificates granted or renewed on or after the 1st day of August, 1972, have effect as if a rate of £5 were substituted for each of the rates specified at reference numbers 1 to 3 in the Fifth Schedule to that Act.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 27th day of June, 1972.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order increases the excise duty payable in respect of certain firearm certificates.
