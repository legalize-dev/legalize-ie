# S.I. No. 614/2010 - Public Transport Regulation Act 2009 (Dissolution of Commission for Taxi Regulation) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th December*, 2010.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec31) of the [Public Transport Regulation Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2009) and at the request of the National Transport Authority, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Public Transport Regulation Act 2009 (Dissolution of Commission for Taxi Regulation) Order 2010.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed as the day for the dissolution of the Commission for Taxi Regulation is 1 January 2011.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 December 2010.

**NOEL DEMPSEY,**

Minister for Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTICE**

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order appoints 1 January 2011 as the day for the dissolution of the Commission for Taxi Regulation. From that date the Commission for Taxi Regulation is subsumed into the National Transport Authority.
