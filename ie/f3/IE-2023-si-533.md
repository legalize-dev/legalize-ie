# S.I. No. 533/2023 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 9) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th November,* 2023.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) (amended by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec10) of the [Social Welfare Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2020)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , and by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec2) (2)(c), [232](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec232) and [297](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec297) of, and Reference 19 (amended by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec26) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2010)) in Table 2 to Schedule 3 to, the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , with the consent of the Minister of Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

***Citation and construction***

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 9) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2023.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2023 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2023.

***Definitions***

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2023” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 4) (Income Disregard) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 160 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0160.html) ).

***Assessment of Means***

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 are amended-

- (a) in article 8(3), (amended by article 2(1)(a) of the Regulations of 2023) by the insertion of the following sub-article after paragraph (dc):

  > - “(dd) an amount up to a maximum of €7,000.00 per annum from payments made by UCD’s Cothrom na Féine Scholarship Programme,”,

- (b) in article 155, (amended by article 2(1)(b) of the Regulations of 2023) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (dab)

  > - “(dac) any amount to a maximum of €7,000.00 per annum from payments made by UCD’s Cothrom na Féine Scholarship Programme,”,

  and

- (c) in article 174 (amended by article 2(1)(c) of the Regulations of 2023) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (ic):

  > - “(id) any amount to a maximum of €7,000.00 per annum from payments made by UCD’s Cothrom na Féine Scholarship Programme,”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 October, 2023.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 October, 2023.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide that a scholarship payment to a maximum of €7000.00 per annum paid by UCD’s Cothrom na Féine Scholarship Programme to a person will be disregarded in the means test for social assistance payments, increases for qualified adults and the Working Family Payment.
