# S.I. No. 382/1986 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 2, Sixty-Second Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 382 of 1986.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA No. 2, SIXTY-SECOND PERIOD) (CYCLE TYRES) ORDER, 1986.

THE GOVERNMENT, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 6 (as amended by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0008/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Control of Imports (Amendment) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1937)) of the [Control of Imports Act, 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1934), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 2, Sixty-Second Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument renews for the year 1987 an existing Instrument which will expire on 31st December, 1986. It appoints a nominal quota of 200 articles as the quantity of cycle tyres and inner tubes which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1987 to 31st December, 1987. This nominal quota has been appointed to facilitate more satisfactory administration. The Minister for Industry and Commerce is prepared to license, during the quota period, all necessary importations consonant with the restrictions in operation for the different countries concerned.
