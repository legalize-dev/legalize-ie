# S.I. No. 779/2004 - Agriculture and Food (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, on the request of the Minister for Agriculture and Food, and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Agriculture and Food (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. The powers and duties of the Minister for Agriculture and Food conferred on her—

- (*a*) by the enactments specified in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order,

- (*b*) by the instruments specified in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order, and

- (*c*) by or under—

    - (i) the Local Government Acts 1925 to 2001, and

    - (ii) the Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Acts 1926 to 1983,

in so far as they relate to an enactment specified in Part 1 or an instrument specified in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Order, as respects the office, or the holder of the office of, inspector or veterinary inspector appointed by a local authority,

are delegated to Brendan Smith, Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order delegates to Brendan Smith TD, Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture and Food, the powers and duties of the Minister for Agriculture and Food under the Acts, Orders and Regulations specified in the Order.
