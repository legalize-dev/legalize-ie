# S.I. No. 218/1976 - Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1976) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 218 of 1976.

DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1976) ORDER, 1976.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1976) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 30th day of November, 1976, is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Dublin District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of September, 1976.

**MARK CLINTON,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1976 on which ordinary members of the Dublin District Milk Board shall be elected.
