# S.I. No. 311/2009 - Firearms Act 1925 (Prescribed Firearm Certificates) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th August*, 2009.

I, DERMOT AHERN, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3B (as inserted by [section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec30) of the [Criminal Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2009)) and section 27 (as inserted by [section 50](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec50) of the [Criminal Justice Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2006)) of the [Firearms Act 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1925), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the [Firearms Act 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (Prescribed Firearm Certificates) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These regulations come into operation on 1 August 2009.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Act of 1925” means the [Firearms Act 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1925);

“Act of 2000” means the [Firearms (Firearm Certificates for Non-Residents) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2000);

“prescribed fee” means a fee prescribed under Regulation 5;

“prescribed firearm certificate” means a firearm certificate prescribed under Regulation 3.

###### 3.

3. The following firearm certificates are prescribed for the purposes of section 3B of the Act of 1925:

- (*a*) a firearms training certificate;

- (*b*) a restricted firearm certificate;

- (*c*) any other firearm certificate granted under the Act of 1925, other than a firearm certificate granted under the Act of 2000.

###### 4.

4. Where a prescribed firearm certificate is to be issued, granted or renewed, as the case may be, a notice in relation to the payment of the prescribed fee, in the form set out in Schedule 1, shall be sent to the applicant concerned.

###### 5.

5. There shall be charged in respect of the issue, grant or renewal of each prescribed firearms certificate set out in column (1) of Schedule 2 the fee set out in column (2) opposite mention of that firearm certificate.

###### 6.

6. A prescribed fee shall be paid to An Post.

###### 7.

7. The payment of a prescribed fee shall be made within the date specified in the notice referred to in Regulation 4, and may be made—

- (*a*) in any post office,

- (*b*) by post, or

- (*c*) by telephone or internet, in accordance with procedures established by An Post.
