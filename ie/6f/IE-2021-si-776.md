# S.I. No. 776/2021 - Housing (Sale of Local Authority Houses) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th January,* 2022.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec4) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec30) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2014), as adapted by the Housing, Planning and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 408 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0408.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

**Citation and commencement**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Sale of Local Authority Houses) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1^st day of February 2022.

**Amendment of Housing (Sale of Local Authority Houses) Regulations**

###### 2.

2. The Housing (Sale of Local Authority Houses) Regulations 2015 (S.I. 484 of 2015) are amended -

- (*a*) in Regulation 5 –

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by substituting “ten years” for “one year”, and

    - (ii) in paragraph (2), by substituting “both of the tenants” for “one of the tenants”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 6, by substituting “€12,500” for “€15,000”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 December, 2021.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations amend the Housing (Sale of Local Authority Houses) Regulations 2015 (S.I. 484 of 2015) to –

- • provide that the minimum income criteria is reduced to €12,500, and

- • change the requirement for the duration of receipt of social housing support from 1 to 10 years and, in the case of joint applicants, apply the same requirement to the second applicant.
