# S.I. No. 72/1979 - Coroners Act, 1962 (Fees and Expenses) (No. 2) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 72 of 1979.

CORONERS ACT, 1962 (FEES AND EXPENSES) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1962), and after consultation with the Minister for the Environment, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) (No. 2) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The allowances specified in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Regulation 8 of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0011.html) ), shall, in lieu of the allowances specified in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Regulation 8 of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 337 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0337.html) ), be payable as from the 1st day of January, 1978.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 9th day of March, 1979.

**GERARD COLLINS.**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the commencement date for the payment of the increased scale of expenses for lay witnesses attending inquests as set out in Regulation 8 of the [Coroners Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (Fees and Expenses) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0011.html) ).
