**European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2005**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ( [No. 27 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2004/46/EC[^1] of 16 April 2004 amending Directive 95/31/EC[^2] as regards sucralose (E955) and salt of aspartameacesulfame (E 962), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Additives Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2005.

- (2) The collective citation “the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 to 2005” includes these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0437.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended —

- (a) in Regulation 2(1), by substituting for the definition of “the Directives”, the following definition:

**Explanatory Note**

***(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).***

These Regulations give effect to Commission Directive 2004/46/EC of 16 April 2004 amending Directive 95/31/EC as regards E 955 sucralose and E 962 salt of aspartameacesulfame. Directive 2004/46/EC amends Commission Directive 95/31/EC of 5 July 1995 laying down specific criteria of purity concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs (as previously amended by Commission Directive 98/66/EC of 4 September 1998, Commission Directive 2000/51/EC of 26 July 2000 and Commission Directive 2001/52/EC of 3 July 2001).

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0437.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No.192 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0192.html) ).
