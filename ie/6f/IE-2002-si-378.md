# S.I. No. 378/2002 - Local Authority Members (Gratuity) (Amendment) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [Sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec4) and [142](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec142) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following regulations:

Short Title.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Authority Members (Gratuity) (Amendment) Regulations, 2002.

Interpretation.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“the Principal Regulations” means the Local Authority Members (Gratuity) Regulations, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 281 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0281.html) ).

Amendment of Article 2 of the Principal Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Article 2 of the Principal Regulations is hereby amended by the substitution of the following definitions for the definitions of “appropriate local authority” and “qualifying service” contained therein:

‘ “appropriate local authority” means -

- (a) where a person is a member of a local authority other than a town council mentioned in Part 2 of Schedule 6 to the Act, such local authority,

- (b) where a person is a member of a town council mentioned in Part 2 of Schedule 6 to the Act, the county council in whose county the town is situated, and

- (c) where a person is a member of both a county council and a town council, such county council; ’.

‘ “qualifying service” means service given as a member of the local authority, or any other local authority, on or after the 4^th day of May, 2000, subject to the proviso that where a period of qualifying service of a person as a member of a local authority is concurrent with a period of qualifying service by him or her as a member of another local authority, only one such period shall be reckoned, which shall be the period which produces the more favourable result for him or her; ’.

Insertion of new Article in the Principal Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the insertion of the following article after article 10 thereof:

> “11. Without prejudice to any other provision of these Regulations -
>
> - (a) an addition to qualifying service under article 3,
>
> - (b) the grant of a gratuity under article 4 or 7,
>
> - (c) the grant of a preserved gratuity under article 6,
>
>   shall in no case be made where a person is a member of any local authority.”.
