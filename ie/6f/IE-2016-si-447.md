# S.I. No. 447/2016 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th August*, 2016.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5(1) and 42(2)(*c*) (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec10) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 12 August 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 12(8) (inserted by Regulation 2(*c*)(ii) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0242.html) )), by deleting “and instead the applicant, when he or she is issued with a further driving licence, shall submit the licence to the licensing authority”, and

- (*b*) in Regulation 20(8) (inserted by Regulation 2(*e*)(ii) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) Regulations 2015), by deleting “and instead the applicant, when he or she is issued with a further learner permit, shall submit the learner permit to the licensing authority”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 August 2016.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Licensing of Drivers Regulations by removing the requirement to return an old driving licence or learner permit to the licensing authority on receipt of a new one.
