# S.I. No. 264/1981 - Worker Participation (Electricity Supply Board) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, LIAM KAVANAGH, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Electricity Supply Board) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The 15th day of October, 1981, is in relation to the election to be held in 1981 pursuant to the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), as regards the Electricity Supply Board, hereby fixed as the nomination day.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal this 28th day of July, 1981. |
|  | LIAM KAVANAGH, |
|  | Minister for Labour. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations appoint 15th October, 1981, as the day on which the nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in the Electricity Supply Board shall be received by the returning officer.
