# S.I. No. 82/1985 - Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) Scheme 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 82 of 1985.

TELEGRAPH (INLAND WRITTEN TELEGRAM) (AMENDMENT) SCHEME 1985.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) Scheme 1985.

- (2) The Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 to 1983, and this Scheme may be cited together as the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Schemes, 1980 to 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme Shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 196 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0196.html) , are hereby amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule thereto (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 75 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0075.html) )).

###### 5.

5. The Minister for Communications pursuant to [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) (2) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) has concurred in the increase of charge effected by this Scheme.
