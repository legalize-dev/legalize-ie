S.I. No. 134 of 1995.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 3) SCHEME, 1995.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1995.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "the Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 and 1995" includes this Scheme.

- (2) This Scheme shall come into operation on 26 May, 1995.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Part 7 for Part 7 of the First Schedule thereto:

###### 15.

15. A call made from, Eircell GSM equipment outside the State to a telephone in any part of the world, shall be charged at the current tariff applicable to such call which is specified by the appropriate GSM cellular network operator in the state or territory in which the call originates together with an Eircell service charge amounting to 15% of the GSM cellular network operators charge in respect of the call.

###### 16.

16. Eircell GSM subscribers shall be charged in respect of calls received on Eircell GSM equipment outside the State as if the call received were a call made by such subscriber, from Eircell GSM equipment in the State, to a telephone in the state or territory in which the call is actually received.

###### 17.

17. Eircell subscribers shall be charged in respect of calls made from Eircell Analogue equipment or from Eircell GSM equipment in the State to Eircell GSM equipment outside the State, as if such calls were calls described in paragraph 1 or paragraph 2 of Section A of this Part of this Schedule, as appropriate.
