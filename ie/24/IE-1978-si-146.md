# S.I. No. 146/1978 - Value-Added Tax (Reduction of Rate) (No. 4) Order, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 146 of 1978.

VALUE-ADDED TAX (REDUCTION OF RATE) (No. 4) ORDER, 1978.

I, SEOIRSE Ó COLLA, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/sec0011.html#sec11) (8) of the [Value-Added Tax Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1972), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Value-Added Tax (Reduction of Rate) (No. 4) Order, 1978.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The Second Schedule (insterted by the [Finance Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1976)) to the [Value-Added Tax Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , shall be varied by the insertion in paragraph (xx) after subparagraph *(c)* of the following subparagraph:

> - "(*cc*) wax candles and night-lights which are white and cylindrical, excluding candles and night-lights which are decorated, spiralled, tapered or perfumed,".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of May, 1978.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, which will come into effect on 1 July, 1978, makes a marginal adjustment to the VAT zero-ratings (which already apply to electricity, gas etc.) by applying the zero rate to plain white wax candles and night-lights.
