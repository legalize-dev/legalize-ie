# S.I. No. 105/2012 - Road Traffic (Special Permits for Particular Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th April*, 2012.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0011.html#sec11) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Special Permits for Particular Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Regulations of 2009” means the Road Traffic (Specialised Vehicle Permits Regulations 2009 (S.I. 147 of 2009).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2A (inserted by Regulation 11 of the Regulations of 2009) of the Road Traffic (Special Permits for Particular Vehicles) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 283 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0283.html) ) is revoked.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 11 of the Regulations of 2009 is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 April 2012.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This amendment allows the Minister or any other Authority to issue a permit to a vehicle to operate on the designated roads which would otherwise be illegal.
