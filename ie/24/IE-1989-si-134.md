# S.I. No. 134/1989 - Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 134 of 1989.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY OF KILKENNY) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. these Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 19th day of June, 1989.

###### 3.

3. The Second Schedule to the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 271 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0271.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution for subparagraph (*e*) of paragraph 35 of the following subparagraphs:

> - "(*e*) The Abbey Road for a distance of 101 metres from the boundary of the county borough of Waterford".

- (*f*) The Golf Links Road for a distance of 610 metres from its junction with the Waterford-Kilkenny Road (National Road N9)."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 7th day

of June, 1989.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County of Kilkenny) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 271 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0271.html) ) to provide for two 30 mph speed limits on roads adjacent to the County Borough of Waterford.
