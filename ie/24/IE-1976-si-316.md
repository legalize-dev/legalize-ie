# S.I. No. 316/1976 - Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 316 of 1976.

PATENTS, DESIGNS AND TRADE MARKS (INTERNATIONAL ARRANGEMENTS) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1976.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) (5) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927), as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Executive Powers (Consequential Provisions) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1937), [sections 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0008.html#sec8) (2) and [70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0070.html#sec70) (5) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1963), and [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) and [93](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/sec0093.html#sec93) of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1964), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 22 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0022.html) ), as amended by the Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0030.html) ), and the Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0032.html) ), shall have effect as if, for the foreign states specified in the First Schedule thereto, there were substituted the foreign states specified in the Schedule to this Order.
