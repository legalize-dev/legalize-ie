# S.I. No. 265/1989 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979. (Designation of Institutions) Order 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 265 of 1989.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979. (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) ORDER 1989.

I, MARY O'ROURKE, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979) (as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) )), with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance, and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards, hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The institution specified in the Schedule to this Order is hereby designated as being as institution to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), applies.

SCHEDULE

St. Michael's House,

The Association of Parents and Friends of Mentally Handicapped

Children,

Willowfield Park,

Goatstown,

Dublin 14.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 5th day of October, 1989.

**MARY O'ROURKE,**

Minister for Education.

**EXPLANATORY MEMORANDUM**

This order adds

St. Michael's House,

The Association of Parents and Friends of Mentally

Handicapped Children,

Willowfield Park,

Goatstown,

Dublin 14.

to the list of National Council for Educational Awards designated institutions.
