# S.I. No. 347/2005 - European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) (Amendment) Regulations, 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I.No. 347 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0347.html)**

**European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) (Amendment) Regulations, 2005**

**Made by the Minister**

**for Transport**

**European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) (Amendment) Regulations, 2005**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ( [No. 27 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) ), and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive No. 2004/89/EC of 13 September 2004[^1] and Commission Directive No. 2004/110/EC of 9 December 2004[^2] hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (a) These Regulations may be cited as European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

- (b) These Regulations and the European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) Regulations, 2003 ( [S.I. No. 701 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0701.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail) Regulations 2003 to 2005.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the European Communities (Transport of Dangerous Goods by Rail Regulations, 2003 is amended as follows:

- (a) by substituting for the definition of “Directive” the following definition —

  > “ ‘Directive’ means Council Directive 96/49/EC of 24 July 1996[^3] on the approximation of laws of the Member States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail, as amended by Commission Directive 96/87/EC of 13 December 1996[^4] Directive 2000/62/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 October 2000[^5] , Commission Directive 2001/6/EC of 29 January 2001[^6] , Commission Directive 2003/29/EC of 7 April 2003[^7] , Commission Directive No. 2004/89/EC of 13 September 2004[^1] and Commission Directive No. 2004/110/EC of 9 December 2004[^2];”;

- (b) by substituting for the definition of “RID” the following definition —

  > “ ‘RID’ means the regulations concerning the international transport of dangerous goods by rail (RID), set out in Annex I of Appendix B to the Convention concerning international transport by rail (COTIF), as applicable with effect from 1 January 2005 on the understanding that ‘Contracting Party’ and ‘the States or the Railways’ will be replaced by ‘Member State’;”.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

The purpose of these Regulations is to give effect to Commission Directive 2004/89/EC of 13 September 2004 and Commission Directive 2004/110/EC of 9 December 2004 adapting for the fifth and sixth time Council Directive 96/49/EC on the approximation of the laws of the Members States with regard to the transport of dangerous goods by rail.
