# S.I. No. 502/1997 - Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997 (Rate of Interest Penalty) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/sec0010.html#sec10) (1) of the [Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1997) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister), Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:.

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997 (Rate of Interest Penalty) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 2nd day of January, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The rate of interest penalty payable pursuant to [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/sec0004.html#sec4) (2) of the [Prompt Payment of Accounts Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1997) shall be 0.0322 per cent per day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 16th day of December, 1997.

Mary Harney,

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order sets out the interest rate which will be applicable on late payment, as required by Section 10 of the Act. The rate specified in the Order is 0.0322% per day, which is the equivalent of 11.75% per annum. It will be reviewed on a six-monthly basis and will, if deemed necessary, be amended in accordance with Section 10(1) of the Act.
