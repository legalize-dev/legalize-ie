# S.I. No. 174/1972 - Returning Officers' Charges (Bye-Elections To Dáil Éireann) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, SEOIRSE Ó COLLA, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/sec0025.html#sec25) (5) of the [Electoral Act, 1923](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1923), as extended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) (4) of the [Electoral Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963), hereby order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Returning Officers' Charges (Bye-Elections to Dáil Éireann) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The scale of maximum charges for the purposes of [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Electoral Act, 1923](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , as extended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) (4) of the [Electoral Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , shall, in the case of bye-elections, be the scale set out in the Schedule to this Order, but if two or more vacancies in the representation of any constituency in the Dáil are filled at one election, as provided for by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0012.html#sec12) (3) of the [Electoral Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) , the scale of maximum charges applicable to that election shall be the scale for the time being in force in relation to general elections.

###### 4.

4. Nothing in this Order shall be taken as authorising more than one charge in respect of a particular disbursement.

###### 5.

5. The scale prescribed by the Returning Officers' Charges (Bye-Elections to Dáil Éireann) Order, 1970, ( [S.I. No. 27 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0027.html) ), shall not apply to any bye-election held after the making of this Order.

SCHEDULE.

SCALE OF MAXIMUM CHARGES.

## PART I. GENERAL.

1. References in this Scale to returning officers include, where appropriate, references to assistant, deputy and acting returning officers.

2. In this Scale—

" constituency" means any constituency named in the Schedule to the [Electoral (Amendment) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1969) ;

" electors" means, in relation to the ascertainment of the maximum sum payable to a returning officer, the number of persons registered in the register of Dáil electors for the constituency in respect of which he is the returning officer.

3. (1) Parts II and III of this Scale do not apply in the case of a county registrar who is a returning officer.

- (2) No payment shall be made for any of the services referred to in Parts II and III of this Scale to more than one person in respect of the same constituency or portion of a constituency.
