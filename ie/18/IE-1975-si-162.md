# S.I. No. 162/1975 - Urban District of Navan (Alteration of Boundary) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 162 of 1975.

URBAN DISTRICT OF NAVAN (ALTERATION OF BOUNDARY) ORDER, 1975.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by article 26 of the Local Government (Application of Enactments) Order, 1898 (as applied by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Authorities (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/index.html) ) and subsection (3) of section 42 of the Local Government (Ireland) Act, 1898 hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Urban District of Navan (Alteration of Boundary) Order, 1975.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) shall apply to this Order.

###### 3.

3. In this Order, "the Boundary Order" means the order made by the Council of the County of Meath on the 3rd day of September, 1973, under subsection (2) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Authorities (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/index.html) , altering the boundary of the Urban District of Navan.

###### 4.

4. The Boundary Order shall be and is hereby modified in the manner and to the extent necessary to bring it into conformity with the provisions set forth in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 5.

5. The Boundary Order as so modified is hereby confirmed.

SCHEDULE.

COUNTY OF MEATH.

ORDER (AS MODIFIED BY THIS ORDER) OF THE COUNCIL OF THE COUNTY OF MEATH ALTERING THE BOUNDARY OF THE URBAN DISTRICT OF NAVAN.

WHEREAS the Council of the Urban District of Navan have applied under [Section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Authorities (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/index.html) to the Council of the County of Meath (hereinafter referred to as "the County Council") for an order altering the boundary of the Urban District of Navan (hereinafter referred to as the "Urban District") so as to include therein the portion of the County Health District of Meath specified in the Schedule hereunto annexed:

AND WHEREAS the Urban District is an urban district (other than a borough) situate in the County of Meath (hereinafter referred to as "the county"):

AND WHEREAS the County Council being satisfied that a *prima facie* case existed for the alteration of the boundary of the Urban District caused an inquiry to be made in the locality:

AND WHEREAS the County Council have complied with the provisions of the Urban Districts (Alteration of Boundaries) Regulations, 1937:

NOW THEREFORE the County Council in exercise of the powers vested in them by subsection (2) of [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Authorities (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0055/index.html) , hereby order as follows:—

1. (1) This Order shall come into operation on the first day of October, 1975.

- (2) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) shall apply to this Order.

2. On the coming into operation of this Order, the area described in the Schedule hereunto annexed (hereinafter referred to as "the transferred area") shall be detached from the County Health District of Meath and shall be added to the Urban District, and thenceforward the transferred area shall cease to form part of the County Health District of Meath and shall be included in and form part of the Urban District for all purposes and the boundary of the Urban District shall be altered accordingly.

3. Every person holding office on the coming into operation of this Order as urban district councillor for the local electoral area of Navan Urban shall be deemed to have been elected for the local electoral area of Navan Urban as extended by this Order.

4. (1) The provisions of any public General Act adopted by the Council of the Urban District at the coming into operation of this Order and of any statutory instrument applying to the Urban District and then in force shall apply to the Urban District as extended by this Order and references in any such instrument to the area comprised in the Urban District at the coming into operation of this Order shall be construed as references to the Urban District as extended by this Order.

- (2) All bye-laws and regulations of the County Council and of the Council of the Urban District at the coming into operation of this Order shall apply to the Urban District as extended by this Order.

5. (1) The County Council and the Council of the Urban District may by agreement make any adjustment of property, rights and liabilities which may be necessary in consequences of this Order.

- (2) In default of agreement, any such adjustment of property, rights and liabilities shall be made by such person as shall be appointed for the purpose by the Minister for Local Government.

###### 6.

6. In the Schedule to this Order a reference to the existing boundary shall be construed as a reference to the boundary of the Urban District as existing immediately before the coming into operation of this Order.

GIVEN under the Seal of the Council of the County of Meath this

3rd dayof September, 1973.

PATRICK FULLAM, Chairman.

DENIS O'DWYER, Acting Secretary.

## SCHEDULE.

The transferred area shall be all that area comprised between the existing boundary and a line drawn as follows:

Starting at the junction of the existing urban district boundary with the northern boundary of Townparks townland; thence in a westerly direction along the said townland boundary to its intersection by the imaginary northern projection of the western boundary of the hereditament now shown as lot 1A Townparks on the Valuation Map drawn on the 1:10560 Scale Ordnance Survey Meath Sheet 25 edition revised in 1909-10; thence in a southerly direction along the said projection and along the boundary of the hereditament to its junction with the southern boundary of the hereditament shown on the same map as lot 22C Townparks; thence in a south-easterly direction along the last-mentioned boundary to its junction with the existing boundary; thence along the existing boundary initially in a northerly direction and continuing to the starting point.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government

this 22nd day of July, 1975.

**JAMES TULLY,**

Minister for Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to extend the boundary of the urban district of Navan as from 1st October, 1975, by including therein part of the county health district of Meath.
