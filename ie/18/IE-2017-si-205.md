# S.I. No. 205/2017 - Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd May*, 2017.

I, MARY MITCHELL OCONNOR, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 99](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0099.html#sec99) and [114](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0114.html#sec114) of the [Patents Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992) and in relation to the said section 99 with the sanction of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following Rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2017.

- (2) These Rules shall come into operation on 19th May 2017.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules—

“Principal Rules” means the Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) Rules 2001 ( [S.I. No. 482 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0482.html) ).

“Rules of 2012” means the Patents (Amendment) Rules 2012 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0030.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Rules are amended in Schedule 1 (as amended by Rule 3(1)(*a*) of the Rules of 2012)—

- (*a*) by substituting the following text for the text in columns (1), (2) and (3) of reference number 6

###### 4.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Patents Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) has been amended to re-introduce substantive examination of Irish patent applications. These amendments to the Patents, Trade Marks and Designs (Fees) Rules 2001 ( [S.I. No. 482 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0482.html) ) prescribe a fee of €200 for the submission of a report and opinion as to the patentability of an application under [Section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Patents Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) . It also prescribes a fee of €200 for holders of short-term patents to request a search report under [Section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0066.html#sec66) of the [Patents Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) before taking infringement proceedings.
