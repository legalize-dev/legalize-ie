# S.I. No. 228/1978 - Worker Participation (British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 228 of 1978.

WORKER PARTICIPATION (BRITISH AND IRISH STEAM PACKET COMPANY LIMITED) (NOMINATION DAY) REGULATIONS, 1978.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The 20th day of November, 1978, is in relation to the British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited hereby appointed to be the nomination day for the purposes of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Labour this 15th

day of August, 1978.

**B. MAC MAGHNUIS.**

A person authorised by the Minister for Labour

to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations appoint 20th November, 1978, as the day on which nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to the British and Irish Steam Packet Company Limited shall be received by the returning officer.
