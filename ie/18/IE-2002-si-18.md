# S.I. No. 18/2002 - Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) and [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) (as amended by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0018/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Pensions (Amendment) Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1996)) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), and having consulted with the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Pension Schemes (Revaluation) Regulations, 2002.

Revaluation Percentage

###### 2.

2. The revaluation percentage in respect of the 2001 revaluation year shall be 4 per cent.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

[Section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (as amended by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0018/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Pensions (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) ), provides that the Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, after consultation with the Minister for Finance, shall, in respect of each revaluation year, make Regulations determining what amount the preserved benefit is to be increased by in that year.

This Regulation provides for a revaluation percentage of 4% for 2001.
