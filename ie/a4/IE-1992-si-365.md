# S.I. No. 365/1992 - Occupational Benefit Schemes (Equal Treatment) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 365 of 1992.

OCCUPATIONAL BENEFIT SCHEMES (EQUAL TREATMENT) REGULATIONS, 1992.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 and section 71 (3) (as inserted by [section 62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/sec0062.html#sec62) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) ) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Benefit Schemes (Equal Treatment) Regulations, 1992.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1993.

3 Equal Treatment.

###### 3.

**3.** The period prescribed for the purposes of section 71 (3) shall be—

( *a* ) a period of 25 years (commencing on 1 January, 1993) for the purposes of retirement ages; and

( *b* ) a period of 6 years (commencing on 1 January, 1993) in any other case.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 30th day of November, 1992.

CHARLIE McCREEVEY,

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations allow that in applying the principle of equal treatment in occupational benefit schemes from 1 January 1993 under the provisions of the Pensions Act, entitlements which accrued prior to 17 May 1990 under pensions scheme rules may be retained for a period of 25 years where they arise from the application of different retirement ages for men and women and 6 years in any other case.
