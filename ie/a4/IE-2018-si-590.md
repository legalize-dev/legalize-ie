# S.I. No. 590/2018 - Catering Joint Labour Committee and Catering Joint Labour Committee (County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) (Abolition) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th January*, 2019.

WHEREAS, I, PAT BREEN, Minister of State at the Department of Business, Enterprise and Innovation, being in receipt of a copy of a recommendation under subsection (5) of section 41A of the [Industrial Relations Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1946) as amended and being satisfied that subsection (3) of section 41A has been complied with and considering it appropriate to do so:

NOW, I, Pat Breen, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (6) of section 41A of the [Industrial Relations Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1946) as amended and delegated to me under [SI No 569 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0569.html) Business, Enterprise and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2017 hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Catering Joint Labour Committee and Catering Joint Labour Committee (County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) (Abolition) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. The Catering Joint Labour Committee and Catering Joint Labour Committee (County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) are abolished.

###### 3.

3. The Catering Joint Labour Committee Establishment Order, 1977 (S.I. 225 of 1977) and Catering Joint Labour Committee (County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) Establishment Order 1992 (S.I. 351 of 1992) are revoked.

GIVEN under my hand,

18 December 2018.

PAT BREEN TD,

Minister of State at the Department of Business,Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this instrument is to abolish the Catering Joint Labour Committee and Catering Joint Labour Committee (County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) and to revoke the Catering Joint Labour Committee Establishment Order, 1977 (S.I. 225 of 1977) and Catering Joint Labour Committee (County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) Establishment Order 1992 (S.I. 351 of 1992).
