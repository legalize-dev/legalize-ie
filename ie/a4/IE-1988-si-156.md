# S.I. No. 156/1988 - Capital Gains Tax (Multipliers) (1988-89) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 156 of 1988.

CAPITAL GAINS TAX (MULTIPLIERS) (1988-89) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Revenue Commissioners, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (5) of the [Capital Gains Tax (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1978), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Capital Gains Tax (Multipliers) (1988-89) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. In relation to the disposal of an asset made in the year 1988-89, the multiplier for the purposes of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (l) of the [Capital Gains Tax (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1978), shall be the figure mentioned in column (2) of the Table to this Regulation opposite the mention in column (1) of the said Table of the year of assessment in which the deductible expenditure was incurred.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

These Regulations specify the multipliers by reference to which sums (such as the base cost of an asset and enhancement expenditure incurred on it) which are allowable as a deduction from the consideration for the disposal of an asset in the year of assessment 1988-89 are to be increased under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Capital Gains Tax (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , for the purpose of computing the chargeable gain accruing to a person on such a disposal. The multipliers are derived from the consumer price index numbers compiled by the Central Statistics Office expressed on the basis that the consumer price index at mid-November, 1968, is 100.
