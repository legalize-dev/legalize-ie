# S.I. No. 549/2001 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers vested in him by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) (sub-section 5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order (S.I. 308 of 1997), hereby makes the following regulations:-

**Citation**

###### 1.

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2001.

**Rate of Payment**

###### 2.

**2.** Where a person is or becomes entitled to payment under sub-section (3) of [Section 44 of the Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 119 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0119.html) ), any amount so payable in respect of the week commencing 2nd December, 2001 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to 100 per cent of that amount or £20.00 (€25.39) whichever is the greater.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week commencing 2nd December, 2001 the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) by a sum equal to one hundred per cent of that amount or £20.00 (€25.39), whichever is the greater.
