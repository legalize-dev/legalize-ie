# S.I. No. 411/1998 - Totalisator Act, 1929 Totalisator Licence

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 411 of 1998.

TOTALISATOR ACT, 1929 TOTALISATOR LICENCE

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Totalisator Act, 1929](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1929), hereby grant to Bord na gCon a licence to set up, maintain and work one or more totalisators as defined by that Act on every greyhound race track in respect of which a licence under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Greyhound Industry Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958), is for the time being in force during the period from this day up to 30th day of July, 1999.

This licence is granted—

- (a) for all occasions on which the greyhound race tracks are used during the said period from this day up to the 30th day of July, 1999, and

- (b) subject to the conditions set out in the Schedule.

SCHEDULE

Conditions

###### 1.

1. In these conditions—

"the Board", "greyhound race", "greyhound race track" and "greyhound race track licence" have the same meanings respectively as in the [Greyhound Industry Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958).

###### 2.

2. The Board shall not work a totalisator set up under this licence for the staking of money on any event other than a greyhound race held on a greyhound race track in respect of which a greyhound race track licence is for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. (1) The Board shall not allow credit betting on a totalisator set up under this licence.

- (2) The Board shall not allow facilities to any person on any greyhound race track for the acceptance of credit bets which would eventually be staked by means of a totalisator set up under this licence.

- (3) In the foregoing paragraph "facilities" includes payment by way of commission or otherwise.

Given under my Official Seal, this 21 day of October, 1998

**CHARLIE MCCREEVY**

Minister for Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

[Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Totalisator Act 1929](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1929/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) empowers the Minister for Finance to grant a licence to set up, maintain and work totalisators at greyhound race tracks. This Instrument renews Bord na gCon's licence from this day up to 30 July 1999.
