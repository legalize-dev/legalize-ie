# S.I. No. 449/2020 - Health Act 1947 (Section 31A(6A)) (Covid-19) (No. 4) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd October,* 2020.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 31A (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec10) of the [Health (Preservation and Protection and other Emergency Measures in the Public Interest) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2020)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and -

- (a) having regard to the matters specified in subsections (2) and (6A)(b) (inserted by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec13) of the [Criminal Justice (Enforcement Powers) (Covid-19) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2020)) of section 31A, and

- (b) having consulted with the Minister for Justice and Equality, the Minister for Transport, the Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation, the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Tourism, Culture, Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1947 (Section 31A(6A)) (Covid-19) (No. 4) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 22^nd day of October 2020.

###### 2.

2. The following provisions of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A - Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 8) Regulations 2020 (S.I. 448 of 2020), being penal provisions for the purposes of section 31A of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), are prescribed to be relevant provisions for the purposes of the [Criminal Justice (Enforcement Powers) (Covid -19) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2020):

- (a) paragraph (1) of Regulation 12;

- (b) paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of Regulation 13.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 October, 2020.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These regulations provide for the penal provisions relating to premises licensed for the sale or supply of intoxicating liquor for consumption on the premises under the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A - Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (No. 8) Regulations ( [S.I. No. 448 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0448.html) ) to become relevant provisions for the purposes of the [Criminal Justice (Enforcement Powers) (Covid-19) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) .
