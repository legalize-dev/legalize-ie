# S.I. No. 152/1983 - Worker Participation (Córas Iompair Éireann) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, LIAM KAVANAGH, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Worker Participation (Córas Iompair Éireann) (Nomination Day) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of September, 1983, is in relation to the election to be held in 1983 pursuant to the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977) as regards Córas Iompair Éireann, hereby fixed as the nomination day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 31st day of May, 1983.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations appoint 1st September, 1983 as the day on which the nominations of candidates at an election held under the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) in Córas Iompair Éireann shall be received by the returning officer.
