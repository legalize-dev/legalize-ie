# S.I. No. 164/1997 - Dublin Docklands Development Authority Act, 1997, (Council) Regulations, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 164 of 1997.

DUBLIN DOCKLANDS DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY ACT, 1997, (COUNCIL) REGULATIONS, 1997

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/sec0007.html#sec7) , [16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/sec0016.html#sec16) (5) and [16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/sec0016.html#sec16) (15) of the [Dublin Docklands Development Authority Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1997), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Dublin Docklands Development Authority Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , (Council) Regulations, 1997.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:—

"the Act" means the [Dublin Docklands Development Authority Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) ;

"the Council" means the Council established pursuant to section 16 of the Act;

"the Minister" means the Minister for the Environment.

###### 6.

6. GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe organisations for the purposes of selecting candidates for appointment by the Minister as ordinary members of the Council of the Dublin Docklands Development Authority and also provide for related matters.
