# S.I. No. 288/1988 - Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 288 of 1988.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and by section 31 of the said Act, as amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations the expression "Principal Regulations" means the Infectious Diseases Regulations, 1981 (S.I. No. 390 of 1981).

###### 3.

3. The Schedule to the Principal Regulations as amended by the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 268 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0268.html) ) is hereby further amended by the insertion therein before "Influenzal pneumonia" of "Infectious parotitis (mumps)".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this

14th day of November, 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations designate mumps as an infectious disease under the Infectious Diseases Regulations, 1981 (S.I. No. 390 of 1981).
