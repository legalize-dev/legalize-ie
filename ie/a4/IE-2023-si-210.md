# S.I. No. 210/2023 - Road Transport (Operator’s Licence Application) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd May,* 2023.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec2) (6) of the [Road Traffic and Transport Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2006) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), and for the purposes of giving effect to Regulation (EU) 2020/1055 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 July 2020[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Transport (Operator’s Licence Application) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the Road Transport (Operator’s Licence Application) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I No. 398 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0398.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the insertion after the definition of “duly accredited person” of the following definition:

> “ ‘EU Regulation’ means Regulation (EC) No 1071/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009[^2] as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No 613/2012 of 9 July 2012[^3] , Council Regulation (EU) No 517/2013 of 13 May 2013[^4] and Regulation (EU) 2020/1055 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 July 2020[^1] ;”.

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended in Regulation 6 –

- (a) in paragraph (d), by the substitution of “the EU Regulation” for “Regulation (EC) No 1071/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009”, and

- (b) in paragraph (g), by the insertion of “with appropriate equipment and facilities” after “premises”.

###### 5.

5. Schedules 1 and 2 to the Principal Regulations are amended by the insertion under “Transport Manager Details” of the following additional details:

> “NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES
>
> Number of Employees on 31 December of the previous year”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal

27 April, 2023.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend the Road Transport (Operator’s Licence Application) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 398 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0398.html) ) to require a road transport operator to provide to the Minister for Transport the number employees employed by the operator on 31 December of the previous year.
