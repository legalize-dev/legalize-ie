# S.I. No. 661/2007 - Rules of Procedure (Defence forces) (form of Oath of Military Judge) 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 661 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0661.html)

RULES OF PROCEDURE (DEFENCE FORCES) (FORM OF OATH OF MILITARY JUDGE) 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd October*, 2007.

I, WILLIE O’DEA, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 240 (as amended by the [Defence (Amendment) Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 2007)) of the [Defence Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1954), hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) (Form of Oath of Military Judge) 2007.

###### 2.

2. These Rules come into operation on 25 September 2007.

###### 3.

3. The form of oath to be taken or solemn declaration to be made by a military judge before entering upon his or her duties under the [Defence Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1954) shall be as follows:

> “I, , swear by Almighty God (*or* — do solemnly and sincerely promise and declare — *as the case may be*) that I will duly and faithfully and to the best of my knowledge and power execute the office of military judge in accordance with the [Defence Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) and the Rules of Procedure made thereunder without partiality, fear or favour, affection or ill-will towards any person, and that I will uphold the Constitution and the laws.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 September 2007

**WILLIE O’DEA,**

Minister for Defence.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Rules provide for the form of oath to be sworn or the solemn declaration to be made by a military judge before entering upon his or her duties under the Act, as required by section 184L(7) of the [Defence Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) .
