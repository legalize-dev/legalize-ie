# S.I. No. 233/1976 - Conditions of Employment (Cassette Manufacturing Industry) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 233 of 1976.

CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT (CASSETTE MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY) (EXCLUSION) REGULATIONS, 1976.

I, MICHAEL O'LEARY, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936)), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these Regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Cassette Manufacturing Industry) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely, the manufacture of recording cassettes as carried on at the premises of Contek Teoranta, Industrial Estate, Carraroe, in the county of Galway, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purpose of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provisions shall have effect in respect of the work to which these Regulations relate:

( *a* ) no woman shall be employed to do any such work at any time between the hours of 12.00 midnight on any day and the hour of 7.30 a.m. on the following day,

( *b* ) no woman shall be so employed on any day until the expiration of eleven hours from the time at which she ceased to do industrial work on the previous day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of October, 1976.

**MICHAEL O'LEARY,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) provides that female adult workers may not be employed in industrial work between the hours of 10 p.m. on any day and 8 a.m. on the following day. These Regulations permit the employment of female adult workers between 7.30 a.m. and 12.00 midnight on the manufacture of recording cassettes as carried on at the premises of Contek Teoranta, Industrial Estate, Carraroe, Co. Galway.
