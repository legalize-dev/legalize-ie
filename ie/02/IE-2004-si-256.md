# S.I. No. 256/2004 - Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001 (Line a -Tallaght To Abbey Street Light Railway) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, SÉAMUS BRENNAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html#sec43) (7)(*a*) of the [Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (No. 55 of 2001) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), on the application of the Railway Procurement Agency, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001 (Line A — Tallaght to Abbey Street Light Railway) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2004.

###### 2.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order makes amendments to the Schedule of the Light Railway Order ( [S.I. No. 59 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0059.html) ) for Luas Line A (Tallaght to Abbey Street) making amendments to the route of the line as well as changes to the position of several stops and ancillary services.

These changes are:

- • Relocation of the electrical sub-station at Cookstown Way, Tallaght

- • Adjustment to the route of the line at the Red Cow Roundabout

- • Alteration of stop layout at Heuston Station

- • Relocation of Museum Stop at Benburb Street

- • Alteration to the stop layout at Jervis stop, Upper Abbey Street.
