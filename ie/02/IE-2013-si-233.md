# S.I. No. 233/2013 - Non-Use of Motor Vehicles Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th July*, 2013.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 20B of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (as inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec7) of the [Non-Use of Motor Vehicles Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) ) (as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Use of Motor Vehicles Regulations 2013.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 01 July 2013.

###### 2.

2. The prescribed form for the purpose of making a declaration of non-use of a vehicle shall be Form RF150 — Declaration of Non-Use of a Motor Vehicle.

###### 3.

3. In addition to the information specified in subsections 20B(3) (a-d) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (as inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec7) of the [Non-Use of Motor Vehicles Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) ) the following information shall be required on Form RF150:

— the make/model of the vehicle,

— the colour of the vehicle,

— the chassis number of the vehicle,

— the Vehicle Registration Certificate Number of the vehicle,

— the address and telephone number of the registered owner of the vehicle,

— the signature of the registered owner of the vehicle, and

— the date of signature.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government,

28 June 2013.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of the Regulations is to prescribe the form for making a declaration of non-use of a motor vehicle and to prescribe information to be contained therein.
