# S.I. No. 137/1976 - Regulation of Banks (Remuneration and Conditions of Employment) (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1975, (Expiration) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 137 of 1976.

REGULATION OF BANKS (REMUNERATION AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT) (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACT, 1975, (EXPIRATION) ORDER, 1976.

I, MICHAEL O'LEARY, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0027/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Regulation of Banks (Remuneration and Conditions of Employment) (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1975), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Regulation of Banks (Remuneration and Conditions of Employment) (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , (Expiration) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The 29th day of June, 1976, is hereby appointed as the day on which the [Regulation of Banks (Remuneration and Conditions of Employment) (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1975), shall expire.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of June, 1976.

**MICHAEL O'LEARY,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints the 29th June, 1976, as the day on which the [Regulation of Banks (Remuneration and Conditions of Employment) (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) , expires.
