# S.I. No. 584/2006 - Agriculture Appeals Act 2001 (Amendment of Schedule) (No. 2) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 584 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0584.html)**

**Agriculture Appeals Act 2001 (Amendment of Schedule) (No. 2) Regulations 2006.**

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5(2) of the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2001) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (Amendment of Schedule) (No. 2) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule (as amended by the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0065.html) )) to the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2001) is amended by inserting after “Scheme of Investment Aid in Alternative Enterprises (Housing and Handling Facilities) (AES)” the following:

> “Scheme of Investment Aid for Demonstration On-Farm Waste Processing Facilities”,

and the said Schedule, as so amended, is set out in the Table to this Regulation.
