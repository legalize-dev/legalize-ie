# S.I. No. 431/2008 - City of Waterford Local Electoral Areas Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th November*, 2008.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the City of Waterford Local Electoral Areas Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. (1) The City of Waterford shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area described in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of Waterford City Council to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set out in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division shall be construed as a reference to such area as described in the Waterford County Borough (Wards) Regulations 1983 ( [S.I. No. 28 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0028.html) ) and therein referred to as a ward.

###### 4.

4. The Waterford County Borough Local Electoral Areas Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 342 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0342.html) ) is hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of the City of Waterford into local electoral areas and to fix the number of members of Waterford City Council to be elected for each such area.
