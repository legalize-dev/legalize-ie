# S.I. No. 29/1975 - Health Act, 1970 (Adaptation) Regulations, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 29 of 1975.

HEALTH ACT, 1970 (ADAPTATION) REGULATIONS, 1975.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0085.html#sec85) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Adaptation) Regulations, 1975.

###### 2.

2. [Section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0009/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Health Authorities Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1960) shall be adapted—

( *a* ) by the substitution in subsection (1) of the words "the South-Eastern Health Board" for the words "the Waterford Health Authority" and the words "that Board" for the words "that Authority", and

( *b* ) by the substitution in subsection (2) of the words "the Southern Health Board" for the words "the Cork Health Authority" and the words "that Board" for the words "that Authority".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

Health, this 11th day ofFebruary, 1975.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order adapts [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0009/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Health Authorities Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) to bring it into conformity with the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .
