# S.I. No. 104/1987 - Health Contributions (Rate) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 104 of 1987.

HEALTH CONTRIBUTIONS (RATE) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sub-section (2) of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1979) hereby makes the following Regulations as respects which, pursuant to sub-section (5) of the said section, a draft has been approved by a resolution of each House of the Oireachtas.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Contributions (Rate) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The rate specified in sections 5 (1) *(a)*, 6 (1) and 7 of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , is hereby varied with effect from the 6th day of April, 1987, to 1.25%.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 3rd day of April, 1987.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance hereby sanctions the above Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 3rd day of April, 1987.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0009.html#sec9) (2) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , provides that the Minister for Health may by regulation vary the rate specified in sections 5 (1) (*a*), 6 (1) and 7 of the Act. The present rate is 1% and it is now varied by these regulations to 1.25%. The changed rate is operative from the beginning of the contribution year commencing on 6th April, 1987.
