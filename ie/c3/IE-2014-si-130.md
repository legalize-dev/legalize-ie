# S.I. No. 130/2014 - Air Travel Tax (Abolition) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th March*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (2A) (inserted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec2) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2011)) of [section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec55) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2008), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Air Travel Tax (Abolition) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of April 2014 is appointed as the day on or after which subsection (2) of [section 55](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec55) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2008) shall not apply in respect of any departure of a passenger on an aircraft from an airport.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 March 2014.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
