# S.I. No. 566/2015 - Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd December*, 2015.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0029.html#sec29) and by [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0031.html#sec31) (amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Health Act 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule (amended by the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0452.html) )) to those regulations:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the Schedule to the Infectious Diseases Regulations, 1981, (as amended) to include Hepatitis E as an additional infectious disease which requires to be notified.
