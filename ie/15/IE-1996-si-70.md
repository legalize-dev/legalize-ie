# S.I. No. 70/1996 - Comptroller and Auditor General (Section 21) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 70 of 1996.

COMPTROLLER AND AUDITOR GENERAL (SECTION 21) ORDER, 1996.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Comptroller and Auditor General (Section 21) Order, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The First Schedule to the [Comptroller and Auditor General (Amendment) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1993), is hereby amended by the addition thereto of "County Enterprise Boards (that is to say, the bodies specified in the Schedule to the [Industrial Development Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1995))".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 12th day of March, 1996.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.
