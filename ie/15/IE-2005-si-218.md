**NATIONAL CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS ACT 1997 (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER 2005**

I, John O'Donoghue T.D., Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 1(4) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997) and the Arts and Culture (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 302 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0302.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Sport and Recreation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 309 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0309.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Commencement) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The 3rd day of May 2005 is fixed as the day on which the following provisions of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997) come into operation:

- (a) sections 7, 51, 67, 68(1) and (2)(b) and 70;

- (b) sections 48 and 50 (in so far as they are not already in operation);

- (c) Part II (in so far as it is not already in operation); and

- (d) the First Schedule.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 27^th April 2005 |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This order appoints the day of the 3^rd of May 2005 as the day on which the provisions of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1997) which will give effect to the establishment of Bord Ard-Mhúsaem na hÉireann and Bord Leabharlann Náisiúnta na hÉireann, the First Schedule of the Act and certain other provisions come into operation.
