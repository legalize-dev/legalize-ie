# S.I. No. 232/1994 - Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 232 of 1994.

SOCIAL WELFARE (MISCELLANEOUS SOCIAL INSURANCE PROVISIONS) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) , [32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0032.html#sec32) and [43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1994.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation—

- (*a*) in so far as they relate to unemployment benefit, on the 21st day of July, 1994, and

- (*b*) in so far as they relate to disability benefit on the 25th day of July, 1994.

3 Reduced rates of disability benefit and unemployment benefit.

###### 3.

**3.** The Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) (No. 2) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 448 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0448.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution for Schedule A thereto (inserted by article 3 of the Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) (No. 2) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 232 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0232.html) )) of the following Schedule:—

*Article 5*

SCHEDULE

"SCHEDULE A

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amount of reckonable weekly earnings | Weekly Rate | Increase for Adult Dependant (where payable) |
| (1) | (2) | (3) |
| £ | £ | £ |
| 25 to 34.99 | 27.40 | 23.60 |
| 35 to 49.99 | 39.40 | 23.60 |
| 50 to 69.99 | 47.80 | 23.60 |

> ".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 20th day of July, 1994.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 20th day of July, 1994.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Social Welfare Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) provided for increases in the maximum rates of social insurance payments arising from the 1994 Budget, with effect from the end of July, 1994.

These Regulations provide for increases in the reduced rates of Unemployment Benefit and Disability Benefit with effect from the end of July, 1994.
