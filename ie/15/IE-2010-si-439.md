# S.I. No. 439/2010 - European Communities (Approval and Registration of Dealers of Bovine Animals and Swine) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th September*, 2010.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 97/12/EC of 17 March 1997 [^1] amending and updating Council Directive 64/432/EEC of 29 July 1964 [^2] relating to dealers, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Approval and Registration of Dealers of Bovine Animals and Swine) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Approval and Registration of Dealers of Bovine Animals and Swine) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 151 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0151.html) ) are amended by the substitution for—

- (*a*) Regulation 6 of—

  > “6. (1) A person shall not operate as a dealer unless he or she has been approved by the Minister for registration in the dealers’ register under this Regulation.
  >
  > - (2) The Minister shall establish and maintain a register of dealers (“dealers’ register”) which shall contain such information as the Minister considers appropriate.
  >
  > - (3) An application for registration shall be in a form and contain any information that the Minister may require.
  >
  > - (4) The Minister may grant registration in the dealers’ register, attach a condition to registration, vary a condition, suspend or revoke registration or refuse an application.
  >
  > - (5) A person who is registered in the dealers’s register shall make such returns to the Minister as the Minister may require.
  >
  > - (6) Without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (4) the Minister may refuse an application or suspend or revoke registration in accordance with Regulation 8 if he or she is satisfied that—
  >
  >     - (*a*) the applicant or registered person has failed to comply with an act of an institution of the European Union or a Regulation made under the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) in respect of animal health, identification, transport or welfare,
  >
  >     - (*b*) the applicant or registered person has committed an offence relating to animal health, identification, animal transport or animal welfare,
  >
  >     - (*c*) the applicant or registered person has failed to comply with a condition of registration,
  >
  >     - (*d*) in relation to an application for registration, information required has not been furnished or information that is false or misleading in a material respect has been furnished,
  >
  >     - (*e*) the applicant fails to demonstrate to the Minister’s satisfaction that
  >
  >         - (i) he or she understands the limitations relating to dealers, or
  >
  >         - (ii) that he or she can comply with the responsibilities and obligations of being a dealer,
  >
  >     - (*f*) the premises to which the application or registration relates is not a fit premises to be registered,
  >
  >     - (*g*) the applicant or registered person is not a fit person to be registered, or
  >
  >     - (*h*) the registered person has ceased to carry out activity to which the registration relates.
  >
  > - (7) The Minister may approve a dealer for a period of 3 years in accordance with Article 13 of the Council Directive which may be renewed on application by or on behalf of the dealer.”, and

- (*b*) Regulation 7. (11) of—

  > - “(11) The Minister may refuse to grant an application or suspend or revoke a registration in accordance with Regulation 8 if he or she is satisfied that-
  >
  >     - (*a*) the applicant or registered person has failed to comply with an act of an institution of the European Union or a Regulation made under the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) in respect of animal health, identification, animal transport or animal welfare,
  >
  >     - (*b*) the applicant or registered person has committed an offence relating to animal health, identification, transport or welfare,
  >
  >     - (*c*) the applicant or registered person has failed to comply with a condition of registration,
  >
  >     - (*d*) in relation to an application for registration, information required has not been furnished or information that is false or misleading in a material respect has been furnished,
  >
  >     - (*e*) in relation to a person applying for registration or approval, the applicant fails to demonstrate to the Minister’s satisfaction that
  >
  >         - (i) he or she understands the limitations relating to dealers or
  >
  >         - (ii) that he or she can comply with the responsibilities of being a dealer,
  >
  >     - (*f*) the premises to which the application or registration relates is not a fit premises to be registered,
  >
  >     - (*g*) the applicant or registered person is not a fit person to be registered, or
  >
  >     - (*h*) the registered person has ceased to carry out activity to which the registration relates.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 September 2010.

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.
