S.I. No. 119 of 1998.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998.

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963), by section 8 of that Act (as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0021/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1982)), and by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0021/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) , and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) The collective citation "the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulation, 1994 to 1998" shall include these Regulations.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1998.

3 Amendment of 1994 Regulations.

###### 3.

**3.** The Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1994 are hereby amended by the deletion of the Fourth, Fifth, Sixth and Seventh Schedules thereof and the substitution therefor of the Schedules set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

SCHEDULE

"FOURTH SCHEDULE

FEES FOR PLANNING APPLICATIONS

SECTION 1

Interpretation

1. For the purposes of this schedule a dwelling, building or other structure or thing may be provided by—

- (a) the carrying out of works,

- (b) the making of a material change in the use of a structure,

- (c) the retention on land of a structure already constructed, erected or made, or

- (d) the continuance of a use of any structure.

and "provision" shall be construed accordingly.

2. (a) Subject to paragraph (b), at references 7, 8, 13 and 14 of column 1 of Section II of this Schedule "use of land" shall include the continuance of a use of land, and the carrying out of works, or the retention of structures, on, in or under the land which are incidental to the use.

- (b) At reference 13 of column 1 of Section II of this Schedule "use of land" shall not include the carrying out of works for the provision of a club house or related facilities for persons using the golf course or pitch and putt course, or the retention of any such structures.

Section III

Maximum and minimum fees for planning applications

1. The maximum fee payable to a planning authority by an applicant in respect of an outline application shall be£11,250.

2. The maximum fee payable to a planning authority by an applicant in respect of an application to which article 93 applies shall be£3,750.

3. The maximum fee payable to a planning authority by an applicant in respect of an application to which article 91 applies shall be£22,500.

###### 4.

4. The maximum fee payable to a planning authority by an applicant in respect of any planning application other than an application mentioned in paragraph 1, 2 or 3 shall be£15,000.

###### 5.

5. The minimum fee payable to a planning authority by an applicant in respect of a planning application shall be£24 and in any case where the planning authority make a refund in respect of a planning application the refund shall not be such as to reduce the balance of the fee to less than£24.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations revise, with effect from 1 May 1998, the fees payable to planning authorities in respect of planning applications, applications for the extension of the duration of planning permissions, requests for copies of entries in the planning register, the licence fees payable to planning authorities in respect of specified appliances and structures and the fees payable to An Bord Pleanála in respect of appeals, references and related matters.
