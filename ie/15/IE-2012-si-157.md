# S.I. No. 157/2012 - Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) (Amendment) Regulations, 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th May*, 2012.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec4) and [147](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec147) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) (Amendment) Regulations, 2012.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “the Act” means the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. The Local Government (Tenure of Office of Managers) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 573 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0573.html) ) are amended by the insertion of the following Regulations after Regulation 4(*h*)

> - “(i) the position of Limerick City Manager and
>
> - (ii) the position of Limerick County Manager.”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 May 2012.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation thereof.)*

This Regulation makes provision to apply Section 147 (5) of the Act in respect of the positions of Limerick City Manager and Limerick County Manager.
