ELECTRICITY REGULATION ACT 1999 (PUBLIC SERVICE OBLIGATIONS) (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2005

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec39) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999) and the Communications, Energy and Geological Survey of Ireland (Transfer of Departmental and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 300 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0300.html) (and as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )), in consideration of general social, economic and environmental factors and following consultation with the Minister for the Environment Heritage and Local Government, and having, in accordance with section 39(10) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) given a draft of this Order to the Electricity Supply Board being the person upon whom the obligations are to be imposed, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Principal Order” means the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Public Service Obligations) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0217.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Article 2(1) of the Principal Order is amended by substituting for the definition of “AER VI Notification”, the following:

> “ ‘AER VI Notification’ means the correspondence referred to in paragraph 1 of EU Commission C(2002 3 fin dated 15 January 2002 relating to a notification to the EU Commission (Reference No. N553/2001-Ireland pursuant to Articles 16 and 18 of the Treaty and the correspondence referred to in paragraph 1 of EU Commission C(2004) 3284 dated 19 August 2004 relating to a notification to the EU Commission (Reference No. N447/2003-Ireland) pursuant to Articles 16 and 18 of the Treaty.”.

###### 4.

4. Article 6 of the Principal Order is amended by substituting for paragraph (5), the following:

> - “(5) The period of the obligation referred to in paragraph (1B) shall—
>
>     - (*a*) as respects an individual generating station specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1B determine—
>
>         - (i) on 31 December 2019, or
>
>         - (ii) on the date which is 15 years from the date of commercial operation of the generating station concerned,
>
>           whichever first occurs,
>
>     - (*b*) as respects and individual generating station specified in Part 2 of Schedule 1B, determine—
>
>         - (i) on 31 December 2021, or
>
>         - (ii) on the date which is 15 years from the date of commercial operation of the generating station concerned,
>
>           whichever first occurs,
>
>           and
>
>     - (*c*) as respects an individual generating station specified in Part 3 of Schedule 1B determine—
>
>         - (i) on 31 December 2016, or
>
>         - (ii) on the date which is 10 years from the date of commercial operation of the generating station concerned,
>
>           whichever first occurs.”.

###### 5.

5. The Principal Order is amended by substituting for Schedule 1B, the following:

>

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Public Service Obligations) Order 2002, ( [S.I. No. 445 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0445.html) ), provided for the imposition on ESB of public service obligations requiring ESB to purchase, up until 31 December 2019, the output of certain peat and renewable, sustainable or alternative electricity generating stations, in the interests of security of supply and environmental protection respectively and provided for the introduction of a public service obligation (PSO) levy mechanism from 1st January 2003 to compensate the ESB for the additional costs incurred.

This Order amends the aforesaid Order to impose a PSO on the ESB requiring it to purchase the output of additional renewable electricity generating stations up to 31 December 2021 in the interest of environmental protection and to provide compensation to it for any additional costs incurred.
