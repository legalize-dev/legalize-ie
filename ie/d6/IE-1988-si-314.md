# S.I. No. 314/1988 - Finance Act, 1986 (Custom House Docks Area) (Specified Day) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 314 of 1988.

FINANCE ACT, 1986 (CUSTOM HOUSE DOCKS AREA) (SPECIFIED DAY) ORDER, 1988.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (Custom House Docks Area) (Specified Day) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The 25th day of January 1988 is hereby appointed to be the specified day for the purposes of Chapter V of Part I of the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of November, 1988.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints the 25th January, 1988 as the date from which certain incentive tax reliefs for urban renewal provided for in the [Finance Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) are to apply in the Custom House Docks Area. These reliefs comprise capital allowances for commercial buildings, double rent allowance for commercial premises, owner-occupier allowance for residential premises, and allowances for the provision of residential accommodation for renting. The reliefs will be available for a period of 5 years from that date.
