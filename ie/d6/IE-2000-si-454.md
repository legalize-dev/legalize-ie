# S.I. No. 454/2000 - Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) , [76](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0076.html#sec76) , [78](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0078.html#sec78) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0082.html#sec82) of and the [Third Schedule](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1960), as substituted by [section 86](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0086.html#sec86) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) and as further amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/sec0006.html#sec6) and the [Second Schedule](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) to the [Roads Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993) and by [section 222](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec222) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2001.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations —

“the Act of 1960” means the [Local Government (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1960),

“the Act of 1966” means the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966),

“the Act of 1993” means the [Roads Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993),

“the Act of 2000” means the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000),

“the Regulations of 1966” means the Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) Regulations, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 278 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0278.html) ),

“the Regulations of 1993” means the Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 214 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0214.html) ),

“the Regulations of 1998” means the Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 434 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0434.html) ).

###### 4.

4. (1) Forms No. 1, 2, 3, and 4 set out in the Schedule hereto, or forms substantially to the like effect, shall be the prescribed forms to be used for the purposes for which they are respectively expressed to relate where a housing authority propose to acquire land compulsorily for the purposes of the Act of 1966, by means of a compulsory purchase order made under section 76 of the said Act and the Third Schedule thereto, as amended by the Act of 2000.

- (2) Form No. 5 set out in the Schedule hereto, or a form substantially to the like effect, shall be the prescribed form of vesting order to be made by a housing authority in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 81 of the Act of 1966.

###### 5.

5. (1) Forms No. 6, 7, 8 and 9 set out in the Schedule hereto, or forms substantially to the like effect, shall be the prescribed forms to be used for the purposes for which they are respectively expressed to relate where a local authority propose in accordance with the powers conferred upon them by section 10 of Act of 1960, as substituted by section 86 of the Act of 1966 and as further amended by section 6 and the Second Schedule to the Act of 1993 and by section 222 of the Act of 2000, to acquire land compulsorily by means of a compulsory purchase order made under section 76 of the Act of 1966 and the Third Schedule to the said Act, as amended by the Act of 2000.

- (2) Form No. 10 set out in the Schedule hereto, or a form substantially to the like effect, shall be the prescribed form of vesting order to be made by a local authority in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 81 of the Act of 1966, as applied by section 10 of the Act of 1960, as substituted by section 86 of the Act of 1966.

###### 6.

6. Where a compulsory purchase order made by a housing authority or a local authority under section 76 of the Act of 1966, and the Third Schedule to the said Act, relates to land which includes a house within the meaning of section 75 of the Act of 1966, and the house is, in the opinion of the housing authority or the local authority (as the case may be), unfit for human habitation and not capable of being rendered fit for human habitation at reasonable expense, the house shall be described in the compulsory purchase order in the manner set out in sub-paragraph (1) of paragraph 2 of Form No. 1 and of Form No. 6 in the Schedule hereto.

###### 7.

7. Form No. 11 set out in the Schedule hereto, or a form substantially to the like effect, shall be the prescribed form of receipt to be given to a housing authority or a local authority for purchase money or compensation paid by the housing authority or local authority (as the case may be) pursuant to paragraph (a) or (b) of article 2 of the Third Schedule to the Act of 1966.

###### 8.

8. Where a local authority propose to acquire land compulsorily in accordance with the powers conferred on them by section 52 of the Act of 1993, as amended by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0023/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Roads (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1998), Form 12 set out in the Schedule hereto, or a form substantially to the like effect, shall be the prescribed form.

###### 9.

9. The Regulations of 1966, the Regulations of 1993 and the Regulations of 1998 are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation of it.)*

These Regulations prescribe the forms to be used by local authorities in connection with the compulsory purchase of land, by means of a compulsory purchase order under [section 76](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0076.html#sec76) of and the [Third Schedule](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) to the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) . The Regulations replace the Housing Act (Acquisition of Land) Regulations, 1966, 1993 and 1998 in their entirety.
