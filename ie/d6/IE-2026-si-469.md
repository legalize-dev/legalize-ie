# S.I. No. 469/2026 - European Union (Copyright and Related Rights in the Digital Single Market) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th September,* 2026.

I, PETER BURKE, Minister for Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2019/790 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019[^1] on copyright and related rights in the Digital Single Market, hereby make the following regulations:

***Citation***

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Copyright and Related Rights in the Digital Single Market) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

***Amendment of Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000***

###### 2.

2. The [Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000) is amended –

- (a) in section 53A, by the substitution, in subsection (3E), of “rightholder” for “author or performer”,

- (b) in section 53B –

    - (i) in subsection (1), by the substitution of “a rightholder” for “an author”,

    - (ii) in subsection (3), by the substitution of “a rightholder” for “an author”, and

    - (iii) in subsection (5), by the substitution of “rightholder” for “author or performer”,

- (c) in section 57A, by the substitution, in subsection (4), of “rightholder” for “author or performer”,

- (d) in section 82 –

    - (i) in subsection (3), by the substitution of “rightholder” for “author”, and

    - (ii) in subsection (5), by the substitution of “a rightholder” for “an author”,

- (e) in section 225A, by the substitution, in subsection (3E), of “rightholder” for “author or performer”,

- (f) in section 225AA –

    - (i) in subsection (1), by the substitution of “a rightholder” for “an author”,

    - (ii) in subsection (3), by the substitution of “a rightholder” for “an author”, and

    - (iii) in subsection (5), by the substitution of “rightholder” for “author or performer”,

      and

- (g) in section 225C, in subsection (6), by the substitution of “rightholder” for “author or performer”.

***Amendment of European Union (Copyright and Related Rights in the Digital Single Market) Regulations 2021***

###### 3.

3. Regulation 15 of the European Union (Copyright and Related Rights in the Digital Single Market) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 567 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0567.html) ) is amended, in paragraph (3), by the substitution of “a rightholder” for “an author”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 September, 2026.

**PETER BURKE,**

Minister for Enterprise, Tourism and Employment.
