# S.I. No. 4/1941 - Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931, Adaptation Order, 1941

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS. 1941. No. 4.

VETERINARY SURGEONS ACT, 1931, ADAPTATION ORDER, 1941.

WHEREAS it is enacted by sub-section (1) of [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0040/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Constitution (Consequential Provisions) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1937), that for the purpose of ensuring that the laws in force in Saorstát Eireann immediately prior to the date of the coming into operation of the Constitution shall continue to be of full force and effect in accordance with and to the extent provided by Article 50 of the Constitution, the Government may by order make all such general or specific adaptations and modifications of any statute or statutory instrument continued in force by the said Article 50 as shall, in the opinion of the Government, be necessary for the purpose aforesaid :

AND WHEREAS, in the opinion of the Government, the adaptation intended to be made by this Order of the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1931), being an Act continued in force by Article 50 of the Constitution, is necessary for the purpose of ensuring that that Act shall continue to be of full force and effect in accordance with and to the extent provided by the said Article 50 :

NOW, the Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sub-section (1) of [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0040/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Constitution (Consequential Provisions) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1937), and of every and any other power them in this behalf enabling, hereby order as follows :—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) , Adaptation Order, 1941.

###### 2.

2. In this Order the expression " the Act " means the [Veterinary Surgeons Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1931).

###### 3.

3. The register to be prepared and established under sub-section (1) of Section 24 of the Act shall be known as " The Register of Veterinary Surgeons for Ireland," and the Act shall be adapted and modified accordingly.

###### 4.

4. The veterinary register mentioned in sub-section (1) of Section 41 of the Act, shall be printed, published and sold under the title " The Veterinary Register of Ireland," and the Act shall be adapted and modified accordingly.

Given under the Official Seal of the Government this 7th day of January, 1941.

**ÉAMON DE VALÉRA,**

Taoiseach.
