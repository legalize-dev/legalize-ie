# S.I. No. 3/1981 - Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977 (Appointed Day) (Aer Lingus Teoranta/Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM NOLAN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) (i) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977 (Appointed Day) (Aer Lingus Teoranta/Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The 5th day of January, 1981 is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), in relation to Aer Lingus Teoranta and Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 5th day of January, 1981.

**TOM NOLAN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order prescribes the appointed day for determining election years for the purposes of [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to Aer Lingus Teoranta/Aerlínte Éireann Teoranta.
