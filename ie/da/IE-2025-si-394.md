# S.I. No. 394/2025 - European Union (International Labour Organisation Work in Fishing Convention) (Safe Manning) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th August,* 2025.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Article 10 of the Annex to Council Directive (EU) 2017/159 of 19 December 2016[^1] , and to Article 3 of that Directive, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (International Labour Organisation Work in Fishing Convention) (Safe Manning) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 5 of the European Union (International Labour Organisation Work in Fishing Convention) (Safe Manning) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 52 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0052.html) ) is amended by substituting for paragraph (13) the following:

> - “(13) Notwithstanding the revocation of the Fishing Vessels (Certification of Deck Officers and Engineer Officers) Regulations 1988 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0289.html) ), the Merchant Shipping (Safe Manning, Hours of Work and Watchkeeping) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 551 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0551.html) ) and the European Union (International Labour Organisation Work in Fishing Convention) (Safe Manning) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 315 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0315.html) ), any minimum safe manning document issued to a fishing vessel pursuant to any of those Regulations shall continue in force.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 August, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations confirm that, notwithstanding the revocation of the European Union (International Labour Organisation Work in Fishing Convention) (Safe Manning) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 315 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0315.html) ), any safe manning document issued under those regulations continues in force.
