# S.I. No. 253/1975 - Collision Regulations (Ships and Seaplanes on The Water) (Amendment) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 253 of 1975.

COLLISION REGULATIONS (SHIPS AND SEAPLANES ON THE WATER) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1975.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 424 (as adapted by [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0040/sec0058.html#sec58) of the [Air Navigation and Transport Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1936)) and 738 of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1894, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Collision Regulations (Ships and Seaplanes on the Water) (Amendment) Order, 1975.

- (2) The Collision Regulations (Ships and Seaplanes on the Water) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0185.html) ), and this Order may be cited together as the Collision Regulations (Ships and Seaplanes on the Water) Orders, 1965 and 1975.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Collision Regulations (Ships and Seaplanes on the Water) Order, 1965, is hereby amended by the substitution for the Second Schedule thereto of the following:

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order brings up to date the list of countries which have accepted the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, 1960.
