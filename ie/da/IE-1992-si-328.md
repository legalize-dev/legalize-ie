# S.I. No. 328/1992 - Central Bank Act, 1971 (Section 7 (4)) Order, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 328 of 1992.

CENTRAL BANK ACT, 1971 (SECTION 7 (4)) ORDER, 1992.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/sec0007.html#sec7) (4) of the [Central Bank Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1971), as amended by [section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Central Bank Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1989), and after consultation with the Central Bank, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Central Bank Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 7 (4)) Order, 1992.

###### 2.

2. [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/sec0007.html#sec7) (4) of the [Central Bank Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (inserted by [section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Central Bank Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition to paragraph (*a*) (ii) of "ICC Investment Bank Limited".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 10th day of November, 1992.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the addition of ICC Investment Bank Limited, (which is a wholly owned subsidiary of the ICC Bank public limited company) to [Section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Central Bank Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) . The effect of this addition is that ICC Investment Bank Limited does not require a licence from the Central Bank for the purpose of carrying on banking business.
