# S.I. No. 486/1997 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 9) Scheme, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Bord Telecom Éireann plc, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 9) Scheme, 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation *"the Telecommunications Schemes*. 1994 to 1997" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Part 7(i) and Part 8 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall come into operation on I December 1997.

- (3) Part 3 of the First Schedule, Part 6(i) of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule, Section VIII of Part 6 and Section X of Part 6 of the First Schedule shall come into operation on 5th December 1997.

###### 3.

3. The [interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by:

( *a* ) the substitution of the following Part 3 of the First Schedule for Part 3 of the First Schedule thereto:

> "
