# S.I. No. 159/2003 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), for the purpose, *inter alia*, of giving further effect to Article 71 of Directive 2001/82/EC of the European Parliament and the Council of 6 November 2001[^1] , hereby order as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) is amended in Part 1 of the Second Schedule after “Johnes Disease” by inserting “*Mycoplasma gallisepticum*”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This Order provides for control on a certain vaccine for animal health reasons.
