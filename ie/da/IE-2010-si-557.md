# S.I. No. 557/2010 - District Court (Summonses) Rules 2010

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 557 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0557.html) DISTRICT COURT (SUMMONSES) RULES 2010

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the District Court (Summonses) Rules 2010, shall come into operation on the 23rd day of December 2010.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and all other District Court Rules.

- (3) The District Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the District Court Rules 1997 to 2010.

###### 2.

2. The District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) are amended by the substitution for rule 6 of Order 15 of the following rule:

> “*6.**(1)**In the case of every summons issued otherwise than by transmitting it by electronic means to the person who applied for it or a person acting on his or her behalf, there shall be issued with such summons one copy thereof for service upon each person to whom the summons is directed.*
>
> *(2)**Where a summons is issued by transmitting it by electronic means to the person who applied for it or a person acting on his or her behalf, a true copy of such summons shall be served upon each person to whom the summons is directed.*”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules substitute rule 6 of Order 15 of the District Court Rules regarding procedure for the delivery of summonses.
