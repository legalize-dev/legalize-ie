# S.I. No. 398/2023 - Waste Management (Landfill Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th August,* 2023.

I, OSSIAN SMYTH Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications, in exercise of the powers, conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/sec0007.html#sec7) , [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/sec0018.html#sec18) (3), [73](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) of the [Waste Management Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1996) and the Environment, Climate and Communications (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 252 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0252.html) ); hereby make the following regulations:

***Citation and Commencement***

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Management (Landfill Levy)(Amendment) Regulations 2023.

- (2) The Regulations shall come into operation on the 1^st September 2023.

***Interpretation***

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Regulations of 2015” means the Waste Management (Landfill Levy) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 189 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0189.html) ).

***Amendment of the Regulations of 2015***

###### 3.

3. The Regulations of 2015 are amended by substituting –

- (1) For Regulation 4(2) the following:

  > - “(2) The amount of the levy for all wastes shall by €85 for each tonne of waste disposed of at a landfill.”

- (2) For Regulation 8(3) the following:

  > - “(3) For the purpose of defraying expenses incurred in the enforcement and collection of the levy within its functional area, a local authority may deduct and retain up to 2 per cent of any amount paid to the local authority under paragraph (1), but any such deductions shall not exceed a total of €100,000, or such other amount as may from time to time be specified in writing by the Minister, in any period of 12 months.”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 August, 2023.

**OSSIAN SMYTH,**

Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations amend the Waste Management (Landfill Levy) Regulations 2015 by increasing the existing Landfill Levy by €10 per tonne from 1^st September 2023 and also increasing the maximum amount local authorities may retain to help defray the costs associated with collection and enforcement of this levy.
