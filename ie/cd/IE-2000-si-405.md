# S.I. No. 405/2000 - Copyright and Related Rights (Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes For Archival Purposes) (Designated Bodies and Classes) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, TOM KITT, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 105](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec105) (2) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Copyright and Related Rights (Recording of Broadcasts and Cable Programmes for Archival Purposes) (Designated Bodies and Classes) Order, 2000 and shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “the Act” means the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).

###### 3.

3. Any archive under the administration, management or control of a body which is not established or conducted for profit and which maintains an archive of fixations of broadcasts and cable programmes is designated as a body for the purposes of section 105 of the Act.

###### 4.

4. All broadcasts other than encrypted transmissions and all cable programmes are designated as a class for the purposes of section 105 of the Act.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

This Order specifies the bodies and classes of broadcasts and cable programmes that are designated for the purposes of [sections 105](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec105) and [253](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec253) of the [Copyright and Related Rights Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2000).
