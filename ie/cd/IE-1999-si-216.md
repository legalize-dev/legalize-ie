# S.I. No. 216/1999 - District Court (Child Trafficking and Pornography Act 1998) Rules, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17 of the Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following Rules of Court:—

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the District Court (Child Trafficking and Pornography Act 1998) Rules, 1999.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on the 26th day of July 1999 and shall be read together with all other District Court Rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. Order 34 of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) is hereby amended by the addition of the following Rule after Rule 10 thereof:—

## SCHEDULE 1 Order 34

MISCELLANEOUS SEARCH WARRANTS

Warrant—under the Larceny Act, 1916 (6 & 7 Geo. 5 C.H. 50)

1. (1) An application by a member of the Garda Síochána to a Judge of the District Court for the grant of a warrant pursuant to section 42(1) of the Larceny Act, 1916 may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the property is located.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.1, Schedule B, of the member of the Garda Síochána seeking the grant of the warrant.

- (3) A warrant to search for and seize the property, which is granted on foot of such information, shall be in the Form 34.2, Schedule B.

— under the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0017/index.html)

No. 17 of 1987

2. (1) An application for the issue of a search warrant pursuant to [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0017/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) in relation to a place described in that section may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein such place is located.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.3, Schedule B, of the member of the Garda Síochána seeking the issue of the warrant.

- (3) A search warrant issued on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.4, Schedule B.

— under the [Prohibition of Incitement to Hatred Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0019/index.html)

(No. 19 of 1989)

3. (1) An application to a Judge of the District Court for the issue of a warrant pursuant to [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0019/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Prohibition of Incitement to Hatred Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) may, subject to the provisions of section 9(3) of that Act, be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the premises or other place in respect of which the warrant is sought is situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.5 or 34.6, Schedule B, as appropriate, of a member of the Garda Síochána not below the rank of sergeant.

- (3) A warrant issued on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.7, or 34.8 Schedule B, as appropriate.

Warrant — under the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html)

(No. 22 of 1989)

4. (1) An application to a Judge of the District Court for the issue of a 0.34, r.4 warrant pursuant to [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Video Recordings Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) may be made at any sitting of the Court for the Court district wherein the premises or other place in respect of which the warrant is sought is situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.9, Schedule B, of a member of the Garda Síochána not below the rank of sergeant.

- (3) A warrant issued on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.10, Schedule B.

— under the [Firearms and Offensive Weapons Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0012/index.html)

5. (1) An application to a Judge of the District Court for the issue of a search warrant pursuant to [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0012/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Firearms and Offensive Weapons Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the premises in respect of which the warrant is sought are situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.11, Schedule B, of the member of the Garda Síochána seeking the issue of the warrant.

- (3) A search warrant issued on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.12, Schedule B.

— under the [Broadcasting Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0024/index.html)

(No. 24 of 1990)

6. (1) An application for the grant of a search warrant pursuant to [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0024/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Broadcasting Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the place in respect of which the warrant is sought is situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.13, Schedule B, of the member of the Garda Síochána seeking the grant of the warrant.

- (3) A warrant granted to the applicant on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.14, Schedule B.

— under the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html)

(No. 33 of 1990)

7. (1) An application for the issue of a warrant pursuant to [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0020.html#sec20) (1) of the [Companies Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the premises in respect of which the warrant is sought are situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.15, Schedule B, laid by an officer authorised by the Minister for Industry and Commerce or laid under the authority of the said Minister.

- (3) A warrant issued to a member of the Garda Síochána on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.16, Schedule B, and shall continue in force until the end of the period of one month after the date on which it is issued.

— under the [Criminal Damage Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0031/index.html)

(No. 31 of 1991)

8. (1) An application by a member of the Garda Síochána for a search warrant under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0031/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Criminal Damage Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) shall be by information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.17, Schedule B, and may be made to the Judge of the District Court for the court district wherein the premises in respect of which the warrant is sought are situated.

- (2) A search warrant issued under section 13(2) of that Act by the Judge on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.18, Schedule B or, where the property concerned is data or where the warrant is issued on a ground referred to in section 13(1)(c) of the Act, Form 34.19, Schedule B.

— under the [Criminal Law (Sexual Offences) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0020/index.html)

(No. 20 of 1993)

9. (1) Where the issue of a warrant pursuant to [Section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0020/sec0010.html#sec10) (2) of the [Criminal Law (Sexual Offences) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) to search a premises and arrest a person is sought, application therefor may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court district wherein the premises are situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing, in the Form 34.20, Schedule B, of a member of the Garda Síochána not below the rank of sergeant.

- (3) A warrant issued by a Judge to a member of the Garda Síochána on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.21, Schedule B.

— under the [Criminal Law Amendment Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0006/index.html)

(No. 6 of 1935)

10. (1) Where the issue of a search warrant pursuant to [Section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0006/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Criminal Law Amendment Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) in respect of premises suspected to be a brothel is sought, application therefor may be made at any sitting of the Court for the district court area wherein the premises concerned are situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing, in the Form 34.22, Schedule B, of a member of the Garda Síochána not below the rank of sergeant.

- (3) A search warrant issued by a Judge of the District Court on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.23, Schedule B.

Warrant — under the [Child Trafficking and Pornography Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0022/index.html)

(No. 22 of 1998)

11. (1) An application to the District Court for the issue of a warrant pursuant to [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0022/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Child Trafficking and Pornography Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) may be made to the District Court Judge for the District Court District wherein the place in respect of which the warrant is sought is situated.

- (2) Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the Form 34.24, Schedule B, of a member of the Garda Síochána not below the rank of sergeant.

- (3) A warrant issued on foot of such information shall be in the Form 34.25, Schedule B.
