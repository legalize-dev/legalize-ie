# S.I. No. 217/2000 - Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Act, 1926 (Declaration of Offices) (No. 2) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Micheál Martin, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0039/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) (as amended by the [Local Government Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1998)) of the [Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Act. 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1926), with the concurrence of the Local Appointments Commissioners, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Act, 1926 (Declaration of Offices) (No. 2) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The office of chief executive officer of a vocational education committee referred to in [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1930), is hereby declared to be an office to which the [Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1926), applies.
