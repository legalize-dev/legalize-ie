SOCIAL WELFARE (CONSOLIDATED CLAIMS, PAYMENTS AND CONTROL) (AMENDMENT) (NO. 7) (CHANGE IN RATES) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th January*, 2010.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 2 (as amended by sections 8 and 9 of the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2008)), 3 (as amended by section 37 and Schedule 7 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ), 4 (as amended by section 17 and Schedule 3 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ), 41 (as amended by section 15(1)(*a*) of the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ), 49 (as amended by section 4 and Schedule 1 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ), 53 (as amended by Section 16(1) of the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ), 60 (as amended by section 4 and Schedule 1 of the [Social Welfare Law Reform and Pensions Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ), 64 (as amended by section 18(1) of the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) , 125, 178A (amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec23) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) ), and 297 of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No.7) (Increase Change in Rates) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2009 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2009.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These regulations come into operation—

- (*a*) in so far as they relate to jobseeker’s allowance, preretirement allowance and farm assist, on 30 December 2009,

- (*b*) in so far as they relate to jobseeker’s benefit, on 31 December 2009,

- (c) in so far as they relate to illness benefit, maternity benefit, adoptive benefit, health and safety benefit and injury benefit, on 4 January 2010,

- (d) in so far as they relate to disability allowance, on 6 January 2010,

- (e) in so far as they relate to invalidity pension and a payment referred to in paragraph (*a*) of the definition of “relevant payment” in section 178 of the Principal Act, on 7 January 2010, and

- (f) in so far as they relate to widow’s and widowers (contributory) pension and incapacity supplement, on 8 January 2010.

*Definitions.*

###### 3.

3. In these regulations

“the Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) );

*Interpretation.*

###### 4.

4. In these regulations, save where the context otherwise requires, a reference to a Schedule is to a Schedule to these Regulations.

*Maternity and adoptive benefit.*

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended:

- (*a*) in article 32(2) by substituting “€225.80” for “€230.30”, and

- (*b*) in article 33 by substituting “€337.50” for “€350”.

*Reduced rates of benefit.*

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution for Schedule 5 thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 1,

- (*b*) the substitution for Schedule 6 thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 2,

- (*c*) the substitution for Schedule 6A thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 3,

- (d) the substitution for Schedule 11 thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 4.

*Increase for qualified adult.*

###### 7.

7. The Principal Regulations are amended:

- (*a*) by the substitution for Schedule 1 thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 5,

- (*b*) by the substitution for Schedule 7 thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 6, and

- (*c*) by the substitution for Schedule 4 thereto of the Schedule set out in Schedule 7.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Social Welfare and Pensions (No. 2) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0043/print.html) provides for changes in the maximum rates of certain social insurance payments arising from Budget 2010.

These Regulations provide for changes in the reduced rates of Illness Benefit, Jobseeker’s Benefit, Health and Safety Benefit, Widow’s and Widower’s (Contributory) Pension and Deserted Wife’s Benefit, and also provides for changes in the rates of tapered increases in respect of Qualified Adults. All of these changes are effective from the last week in December 2009 to the first week in January 2010.

The Regulations also provide for changes in the minimum and maximum weekly rates of Maternity Benefit and Adoptive Benefit, with effect from 4 January 2010.
