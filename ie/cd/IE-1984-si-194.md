# S.I. No. 194/1984 - Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978 (Fees) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 194 of 1984.

LANDLORD AND TENANT (GROUND RENTS) (No. 2) ACT, 1978 (FEES) ORDER, 1984.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/sec0023.html#sec23) (2) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1978), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Fees) Order, 1984.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The fees to be taken in the Land Registry for the purposes of Part III of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1978), as amended by the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (Amendment) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1984) (other than the fees specified in section 23 (1) of the first mentioned Act) shall be as follows:

- (*a*) for the issue of a vesting certificate on an application under section 20 of the first-mentioned Act — £52;

- (*b*) (i) for an arbitration under section 21 of that Act — — £105;

    - (ii) for the issue of a vesting certificate in that case — £52;

- (*c*) for the inspection of a register kept for the purposes of that Act — 50p;

- (*d*) for a copy of an entry in such register signed by the Registrar of Titles — £2.60.

###### 3.

3. The [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Fees) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 205 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0205.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 19th day of July, 1984.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Finance consent to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 19th day of July, 1984.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the fees to be taken in the Land Registry for the purposes of the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (No. 2) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1978), as amended by the [Landlord and Tenant (Ground Rents) (Amendment) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1984), other than the fees specified in section 23 (1) of the first-mentioned Act.
