# S.I. No. 297/2017 - European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th July*, 2017.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/1190 of 20 July 2015[^1] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1120 of 11 July 2016[^2] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1121 of 11 July 2016[^3] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1143 of 13 July 2016[^4] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1198 of 22 July 2016[^5] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/314 of 4 March 2016[^6] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/237 of 10 February 2017[^7] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 512 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0512.html) ), the Regulations of 2016 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013 to 2017 and shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 440 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0440.html) );

“Regulations of 2016” means the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 317 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0317.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) (as amended by Regulation 3 of the Regulations of 2016) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “EU Regulation” the following:

> “‘EU Regulation’ means Regulation (EC) No. 1223/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 November 2009[^8] , as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 344/2013 of 4 April 201319, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 483/2013 of 24 May 2013[^9] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 658/2013 of 10 July 2013[^10] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1197/2013 of 25 November 2013[^11] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 358/2014 of 9 April 2014[^12] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 866/2014 of 8 August 2014[^13] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1003/2014 of 18 September 2014[^14] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1004/2014 of 18 September 2014[^15] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/1190 of 20 July 20151, Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/1298 of 28 July 2015[^16] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/621 of 21 April 2016[^17] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/622 of 21 April 2016[^18] , Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1120 of 11 July 2016[^2], Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1121 of 11 July 2016[^3], Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1143 of 13 July 2016[^4], Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1198 of 22 July 2016[^5], Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/314 of 4 March 2016[^6] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/237 of 10 February 2017[^7];”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013 to give effect to a number of Commission Regulations. These Regulations have the effect of—

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to permanent hair dyes (Commission Regulation (EU) 2015/1190 of 20 July 2015),

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to carbon black (Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1120 of 11 July 2016),

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Ethyl Lauroyl Arginate HCl (Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1121 of 11 July 2016),

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Titanium Dioxide (Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1143 of 13 July 2016),

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Methylisothiazolinone (Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/1198 of 22 July 2016),

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to Diethylene Glycol Monoethyl Ether (DEGEE) (Commission Regulation (EU) 2016/314 of 4 March 2016),

— accounting for amendments pertaining to the restrictions in relation to permanent hair dyes (Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/237 of 10 February 2017).

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.
