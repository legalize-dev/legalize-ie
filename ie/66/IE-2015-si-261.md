# S.I. No. 261/2015 - European Communities (Marketing of Honey) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd June*, 2015.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2014/63/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Marketing of Honey) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Marketing of Honey) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 367 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0367.html) ) are amended-

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “Council Directive” the following-

  > “‘Council Directive’ means Council Directive 2001/110/EC of 20 December 2001[^2] as amended by Directive 2014/63/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014;”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for Regulation 4 the following-

  > “4.(1) A person shall not place honey on the market unless it complies with Articles 2 and 3 of the Council Directive.
  >
  > - (2) A person may place honey manufactured before 1 August 2004 on the market in accordance with the Regulations revoked by Regulation 10 until stocks are exhausted.
  >
  > - (3) A person may place honey produced or labelled before 24 June 2015 on the market in accordance with Article 3 of Directive 2014/63/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014 until stocks are exhausted.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 June 2015.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations update existing Legislation relating to the marketing of honey.
