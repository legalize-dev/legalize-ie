# S.I. No. 314/1976 - Patents Act, 1964 (Section 93) (Declaration) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 314 of 1976.

PATENTS ACT, 1964 (SECTION 93) (DECLARATION) ORDER, 1976.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 93](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/sec0093.html#sec93) (1) of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1964), and with a view to the fulfilment of an arrangement with the Government of India concerning the protection of inventions, hereby made the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (Section 93) (Declaration) Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that India is a convention country for the purposes of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1964).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 20th day of

December, 1976.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Order declares India to be a Convention Country for the purposes of the [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) .
