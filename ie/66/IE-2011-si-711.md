# S.I. No. 711/2011 - Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd January*, 2012.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 3 January 2012.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2009 (SI 567 of 2009).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 6 of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for paragraphs (1) and (3) the following:

- (1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) the fee to be paid for a test certificate shall be-

    - (*a*) €44.72in the case of a test certificate issued following a test of a vehicle; and

    - (*b*) €22.76in the case of a test certificate issued following a re-test of a vehicle.

- (3) Subject to paragraph (5), in any case where an appointment for a test or re-test has been confirmed to the issuing authority and the vehicle is not presented for the test or re-test at the appointed time and place, the issuing authority may require payment of a fee for the issue of a test certificate being the aggregate of-

    - (*a*) The relevant amount specified in paragraph (1), and

    - (*b*) in respect of an appointment for-

        - (i) a test, €17.89 for each occasion on which the vehicle was not presented for the test at the appointed time and place for the issue of a test certificate, or

        - (ii) a re-test, €9.35 for each occasion on which the vehicle was not presented for a re-test at the appointed time and place (other than in circumstances referred to in paragraph (2)) for the issue of a test certificate.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 December 2011.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations increase the fee for a test certificate issued following a test of a vehicle and following a re-test of a vehicle.

For these purposes, these Regulations amend the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 567 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0567.html) ).
