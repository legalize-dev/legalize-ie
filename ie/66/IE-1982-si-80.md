# S.I. No. 80/1982 - Social Welfare (Maternity Allowance) (Additional Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 80 of 1982.

SOCIAL WELFARE (MATERNITY ALLOWANCE) (ADDITIONAL BENEFIT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981 ) and by Section 75 of that Act (as substituted by the [Social Welfare (Amendment) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1981)), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Maternity Allowance) (Additional Benefit) (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Maternity Allowance) (Additional Benefit) Regulations, 1981 may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Maternity Allowance) (Additional Benefit) Regulations, 1981 to 1982.

3. These Regulations shall come into effect on the 5th day of April, 1982.

3. Sub-article (3) of article 2 of the Social Welfare (Maternity Allowance) (Additional Benefit) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0138.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£70.69" for "£57.18" and the substitution of "6th day of April 1980" for "6th day of April, 1979".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare

this 31st day of March, 1982.

**MICHAEL WOODS**

Minister for Social Welfare.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Regulation provides for the use of a minimum weekly earnings figure for the 1980/81 income tax year of £70.69 thus ensuring a minimum weekly aggregate benefit of £56.55 for women on maternity leave under the provisions of the [Maternity Protection of Employees Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) in the year commencing 5 April 1982.
