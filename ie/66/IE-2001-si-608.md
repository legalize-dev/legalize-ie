# S.I. No. 608/2001 - Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (S.I. No. 21 of 1966), as amended by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (S.I. No. 18 of 1992), and by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) which powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 427 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0427.html) ), I, Robert Molloy, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“the Regulations of 1980” means the Housing Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 296 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0296.html) );

“the Regulations of 1982” means the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0177.html) );

“the Regulations of 1996” means the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 148 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0148.html) );

“the Regulations of 1998” means the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 152 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0152.html) ).

“the Regulations (No. 2) of 1998” means the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 426 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0426.html) )

###### 3.

###### 4.

4. Article 32A of the Regulations of 1980 (as inserted by the Regulations of 1996 and amended by the Regulations of 1998 and the Regulations (No.2) of 1998) is hereby further amended by the substitution for sub-article (1) of the following:

> - “(1) A loan shall not be made by a housing authority for the construction or acquisition of a house unless the application of the income eligibility formula by the authority results in an amount not in excess of:
>
>     - (a) IR£35,000 before 1 January 2002 and €44,440.83 on or after 1 January 2002 in any case where the foundations of the house being constructed were completed or the contract for the acquisition of the house was made, on or after the 1^st day of May, 1995, and before the 13th day of November, 1997; or
>
>     - (b) IR£37,500 before 1 January 2002 and €47,615.18 on or after 1 January 2002 in any case where the foundations of the house being constructed were completed or the contract for the acquisition of the house was made, on or after the 13^th day of November, 1997, and before the 23^rd day of April, 1998; or
>
>     - (c) IR£50,000 before 1 January 2002 and €63,486.90 on or after 1 January 2002 in any case where the foundations of the house being constructed were completed or the contract for the acquisition of the house was made, on or after the 23^rd day of April, 1998, and before the 15^th day of June 2000; or
>
>     - (d) IR£62,500 before 1 January 2002 and €80,000 on or after 1 January 2002 in any case where the foundations of the house being constructed were completed or the contract for the acquisition of the house was made, on or after the 15^th day of June 2000.”
