# S.I. No. 638/2022 - Sea - Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th December,* 2022.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) for the purpose of giving full effect to Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009[^1] (as amended by Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013[^2] , Council Regulation (EU) No 1385/2013 of 17 December 2013[^3] , Regulation (EU) No 508/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^4] and Regulation (EU) No 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015[^5] ) and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011[^6] (other than Title VII) (as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1962 of 28 October 2015[^7] ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ) is amended by substituting the following for the Schedule (as inserted by the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 365 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0365.html) )):

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations provide for the removal of Rathmullan and the Port of Cork as designated ports for landings of pelagic species greater than 10 tonnes.
