# S.I. No. 450/1998 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) (Amendment) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 450 of 1998.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1998

I, Micheál Martin, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) (2) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979) (as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) ), and the Education (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 430 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0430.html) )), with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards, hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) (Amendment) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 252 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0252.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for "College of Industrial Relations, Sandford Road, Dublin." of "National College of Ireland.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of November 1998.

Michael Martin

Minister for Education and Science.

The Minister for Finance concurs in the making of this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 25th day of November, 1998.

A person authorised by the Minister for Finance to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister.
