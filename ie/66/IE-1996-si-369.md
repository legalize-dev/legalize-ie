# S.I. No. 369/1996 - Consumer Credit Act, 1995 (Section 2) (No. 2) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 369 of 1996.

CONSUMER CREDIT ACT, 1995 (SECTION 2) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 2) (No. 2) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby prescribed that the definition of "credit institution" contained in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), shall (by virtue of paragraph (g) of that definition) include —

ACC Bank International,

Montbrison Limited,

Provided that the APR charged by such person in respect of any credit granted to a consumer is less than 23 per cent.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 3rd day of December, 1996.

**RICHARD BRUTON**

Minister for Enterprise and Employment

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Regulation further supplements the definition of "credit institution" in the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
