# S.I. No. 529/2004 - Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001 (Line a - Tallaght To Abbey Street Light Railway) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 529 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0529.html) .**

TRANSPORT (RAILWAY INFRASTRUCTURE) ACT 2001 (LINE A - TALLAGHT TO ABBEY STREET LIGHT RAILWAY) (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) ORDER 2004.

[S.I. No. 529 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0529.html) .

TRANSPORT (RAILWAY INFRASTRUCTURE) ACT 2001 (LINE A - TALLAGHT TO ABBEY STREET LIGHT RAILWAY) (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) ORDER 2004.

I, SEAMUS BRENNAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 43(7)(*a*) of [the Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (No. 55 of 2001) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), on the application of the Railway Procurement Agency, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as [the Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (Line A - Tallaght to Abbey Street Light Railway) (Amendment) (No. 3) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. [The Transport (Dublin Light Rail) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Line A — Tallaght to Abbey Street Light Railway) Order, 1999 is amended in Article 16 by substituting for paragraph (1), as amended by [the Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (Line A — Tallaght to Abbey Street Light Railway) (Amendment) Order 2004, the following:

> - “(1) Construction of authorised works shall be completed within the period of 7 months beginning on 1 March 2004.”.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 2nd day of September, 2004. |
|  | SÉAMUS BRENNAN, Minister for Transport. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order extends the period during which the RPA is authorised to carry out works on Luas Line A (Tallaght to Abbey Street).
