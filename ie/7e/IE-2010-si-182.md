*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th May*, 2010.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY , Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 96/26/EC of 29 April 1996 [^1] as amended by Council Directive No. 2006/103/EC of 20 November 2006 [^2] , hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Road Transport) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Regulations of 2006” means European Communities (Road Transport) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 561 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0561.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) of the European Communities (Road Passenger Transport) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 59 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0059.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) by substituting for the definition of “certificate of competency” the following:

  > “ ‘certificate of competency’ means an international or national certificate of competence granted under Regulation 10 or a corresponding certificate issued by another Member State;”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for the definition of “the Principal Directive” (inserted by Regulation 3(*b*) of the Regulation of 2006) the following:

  > “ ‘Principal Directive’ means Council Directive No. 96/26/EC of 29 April 1996[^1], as amended by Council Directive No. 98/76/EC of 1 October 1998 [^3] , Council Directive No. 2004/66/EC of 26 April 2004 [^4] and Council Directive No. 2006/103/EC of 20 November 2006[^2];”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 2(1) of the European Communities (Merchandise Road Transport) Regulations 1991 ( [S.I. No. 60 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0060.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) by substituting for the definition of “certificate of competency” the following:

  > “ ‘certificate of competency’ means an international or national certificate of competence granted under Regulation 11(3) or (4) or a corresponding certificate issued by another Member State;”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for the definition of “the Principal Directive” (inserted by Regulation 4(*b*) of the Regulation of 2006) the following:

  > “ ‘Principal Directive’ means Council Directive No. 96/26/EC of 29 April 1996 [^1] , as amended by Council Directive No. 98/76/EC of 1 October 1998 [^2] , Council Directive No. 2004/66/EC of 26 April 2004 [^3] and Council Directive No. 2006/103/EC of 20 November 2006 [^4] ;”.

###### 5.

5. Regulations 3(*b*), 4(*b*) and 7 of, and the Schedule to, the Regulations of 2006 are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 April 2010.

**NOEL DEMPSEY,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations transpose and give effect to paragraph A(3) of the Annex to Council Directive 2006/103/EC of 20 November 2006 adapting certain Directives in the field of transport policy, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania, in Ireland.
