# S.I. No. 372/2019 - Extradition Act 1965 (Application Of Part II) (Revocation) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd August,* 2019.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Foreign Affairs and Trade, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8(6) (amended by [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec49) (d) of the [European Arrest Warrant Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2003)) of the [Extradition Act 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1965) (as adapted by the Foreign Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0246.html) )), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Revocation) Order 2019.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 22 July 2019.

###### 2.

2. The following are revoked:

- (a) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) Order 2000 ( [S.I. No. 474 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0474.html) );

- (b) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 173 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0173.html) );

- (c) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 479 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0479.html) );

- (d) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 649 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0649.html) );

- (e) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 587 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0587.html) );

- (f) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 725 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0725.html) );

- (g) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 374 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0374.html) );

- (h) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) Order 2009 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0009.html) );

- (i) the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) (Amendment) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0045.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

18 July, 2019.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Foreign Affairs and Trade.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to revoke the Extradition Act 1965 (Application of Part II) Order 2000 ( [S.I. No. 474 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0474.html) ) and subsequent amending orders made under section 8 of the enabling Act.
