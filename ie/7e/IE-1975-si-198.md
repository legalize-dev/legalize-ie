# S.I. No. 198/1975 - Companies (Stock Exchange) Order, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 198 of 1975.

COMPANIES (STOCK EXCHANGE) ORDER, 1975.

The Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Sections 44, 45, 361 and 362 of, and paragraph 28 of the Sixth Schedule to, the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), hereby orders as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Stock Exchange) Order, 1975.

###### 2.

2. The Stock Exchange is hereby prescribed for the purposes of sections 44, 45, 361 and 362 of, and paragraph 28 of the Sixth Schedule to, the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

###### 3.

3. The Companies (Stock Exchange) Order, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 43 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0043.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Industry and Commerce, this 21st day of August, 1975.

**NIAL MACLIAM.**

A person authorised by the Minister for Industry and Commerce to authenticate the Official Seal of the Minister.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Order prescribes The Stock Exchange for the purposes of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , and revokes The Companies (Stock Exchange) Order, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 43 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0043.html) ).
