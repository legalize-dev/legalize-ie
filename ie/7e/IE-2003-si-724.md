**Citation and construction.**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions (Amendment) (No. 7) (Increase in Rates Regulations, 2003.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations 1994 to 2003 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations 1994 to 2003.

**Commencement.**

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation -

- (a) in so far as they relate to unemployment assistance, pre-retirement allowance and farm assist on 24 December 2003,

- (b) in so far as they relate to unemployment benefit, on 25 December 2003,

- (c) in so far as they relate to disability benefit, maternity benefit, adoptive benefit, health and safety benefit and injury benefit on 29 December 2003,

- (d) in so far as they relate to disability allowance, on 31 December 2003,

- (e) in so far as they relate to retirement pension, invalidity pension, family income supplement and a relevant payment by virtue of [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) (1) (a) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , on 1 January 2004, and

- (f) in so far as they relate to old age (contributory) pension, widow's and widower's (contributory) pension and unemployability supplement on 2 January 2004.

**Definitions.**

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations -

“the Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. no. 417 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0417.html) );

“the Regulations of 2002” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment (No. 3) (Increase in Rates) Regulations, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 631 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0631.html) ).

**Interpretation.**

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations, save where the context otherwise requires, a reference to a Schedule is to a Schedule to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The Social Welfare Act, 2003 provides for increases in the maximum rates of social insurance payments arising from Budget 2004.

These Regulations provide for increases in the reduced rates of Disability Benefit, Unemployment Benefit, Health and Safety Benefit, Old Age (Contributory) Pension, Retirement Pension Widow's and Widower's (Contributory) Pension, Deserted Wife's Benefit, and also provides for increases in the rates of tapered increases in respect of Qualified Adults. All of these increases are effective from the first week in January 2004.

In addition these Regulations also provide for an increase in the minimum family income supplement payment by EUR 7 per week from EUR 13 to EUR 20.

The Regulations also provide for increases in the minimum weekly rate of Maternity Benefit and Adoptive Benefit with effect from January 2004.
