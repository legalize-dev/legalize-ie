S.I. No. 109 of 1981.

TELEGRAPH (INLAND WRITTEN TELEGRAM) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1981.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, hereby make, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981.

- (2) The Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 196 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0196.html) ), and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telegraph (Inland Written Telegram) Regulations, 1980 and 1981.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1981.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.
