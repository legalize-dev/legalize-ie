# S.I. No. 313/1995 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 313 of 1995.

MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 and subsection (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [sections 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) and [72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1995.

2 Rate of Payment.

###### 2.

**2.** Where a person is or becomes entitled to—

( *a* ) payment under subsection (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 140 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0140.html) ); or

( *b* ) payment of a maintenance allowance under [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in accordance with the Disabled Persons Maintenance Allowances Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0141.html) ),

any amount so payable in respect of the week commencing on 4th December, 1995 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20 whichever is the greater.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 30th

day of November, 1995.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 30th

day of November, 1995.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week commencing 4th December, 1995, the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) and [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20, whichever is the greater.
