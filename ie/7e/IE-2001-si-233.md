# S.I. No. 233/2001 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-And-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports From The Netherlands) (Amendment) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Minister and Title of Department) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby order as follows —

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the Netherlands) (Amendment) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the Netherlands) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 210 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0210.html) ) is amended

- (a) in Article 2(1), by the substitution for the definition of “restricted areas” of the following definition —

  > “‘restricted areas’ means the surveillance zone Oene-Kootwijkerbroek, including the vaccination zone Noord Veluwe, in the provinces of Gelderland, Utrecht and Overijssel.”, and

- (b) in Article 8(2), by the insertion after “20 February, 2001” of —

  > “or frozen bovine semen or embryos produced after that date that comply with Article 12 of the Commission Decision”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order amends ‘The Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Restriction on Imports from the Netherlands) Order, 2001’ ( [S.I. No. 210 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0210.html) ), to take account of the new Commission Decision regarding the Netherlands.
