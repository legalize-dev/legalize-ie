# S.I. No. 173/2001 - Road Traffic Act, 1994 (Part III) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) , [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) , [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) and [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994), which powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997, I, Robert Molloy, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act, 1994, (Part III) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations and the Road Traffic Act, 1994 (Part III) Regulations, 1994, ( [S.I. No. 351 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0351.html) ) shall be construed as one and may be cited as the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Part III) Regulations, 1994 to 2001.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1^st day of May, 2001.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic Act, 1994 (Part III) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0351.html) ) are hereby amended-

- (a) by the substitution of the following article for article 4 -

  > “4. The Certificate to be issued by the Medical Bureau of Road Safety under [Section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) shall, in the case of a certificate determining the concentration of alcohol, be in the form set out in Part 1 of the Second Schedule to these Regulations, and in the case of a certificate determining the presence of a drug or drugs be in the form set out in Part 2 of the Second Schedule to these Regulations.”, and

- (b) by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Second Schedule:-

  > “
  >
  > **SECOND SCHEDULE**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**
