# S.I. No. 44/1991 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 44 of 1991.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) REGULATIONS, 1991.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 28th day of January, 1991.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on a deposit within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1948), is hereby prescribed as 6.75 per cent. per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0347.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency

this 6th day of March, 1991.

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**

Chief Executive.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe as 6.75 per cent. per annum the rate of interest payable on deposits in the Post Office Savings Bank as from 28th January, 1991.
