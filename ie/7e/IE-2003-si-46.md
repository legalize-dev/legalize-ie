# S.I. No. 46/2003 - National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (j) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), and with the consent of the Minister for Health and Children, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authorities) Order 2003.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 10^th February 2003.

###### 2.

2. The bodies specified in the Schedule to this Order are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (j) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 93 of 2000).
