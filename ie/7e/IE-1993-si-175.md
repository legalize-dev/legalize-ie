# S.I. No. 175/1993 - Social Welfare (Unemployment Assistance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 175 of 1993.

SOCIAL WELFARE (UNEMPLOYMENT ASSISTANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 3 and section 140 A (1) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0005/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1993)) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Short title.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Unemployment Assistance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

2 ..

###### 2.

**2.** The Social Welfare (Unemployment Assistance) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 238 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0238.html) ) are hereby amended by the insertion after article 9 of the following article—

"Exemption from disqualification.

9A. A person shall not be disqualified for receiving unemployment assistance while attending a course of study within the meaning of section 140 A (2) (inserted by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0005/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) ) of the Principal Act where that person is—

*( a* ) a mature student within the meaning of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0024/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Local Authorities (Higher Education Grants) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1968) (as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0019/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Local Authorities (Higher Education Grants) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1992)), or

*( b* ) participating in an activity as defined in article 2 of these Regulations.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 25th day of June, 1993.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

Section 140A of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) disqualifies a person engaged in a course of study from receiving unemployment assistance other than in such circumstances and conditions as may be prescribed.

These Regulations exempt from this disqualification a person who is—

*( a* ) a mature student as defined in [Section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0024/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Local Authorities (Higher Education Grants) Act 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , or

*( b* ) participating in one of the educational initiatives for the long-term unemployed such as the Vocational Training Opportunities Scheme and the Third Level Allowance scheme, or

*( c* ) participating in a course of education approved by a company (known as an Area Partnership) established under the Programme for Economic and Social Progress.
