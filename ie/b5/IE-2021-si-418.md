# S.I. No. 418/2021 - European Union (Payment Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th August,* 2021.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) No. 2015/2366 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2015[^1] and Regulation (EU) No. 2021/1230 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 July 2021[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Payment Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 24 August 2021.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 86 of the European Union (Payment Services) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 6 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0006.html) ) is amended in paragraph (6) by the substitution of the following subparagraph for subparagraph (b):

> - “(b) those payment services to which Regulation (EU) No. 2021/1230 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 July 2021[^3] applies.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

10 August, 2021.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
