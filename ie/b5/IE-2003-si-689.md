# S.I. No. 689/2003 - Local Elections (Forms) Regulations, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government in exercise of the power conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec3) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 1999) hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Elections (Forms) Regulations 2003.

###### 2.

2. Each form in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form for the purpose for which such form is expressed to be applicable.

###### 3.

3. The Local Elections (Forms) Regulations, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 136 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0136.html) ) are hereby revoked.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These Regulations prescribe the forms to be used by candidates, national agents designated persons and third parties for furnishing statements of donation and election expenses at local elections in accordance with [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec13) of the [Local Elections (Disclosure of Donations and Expenditure) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) . They repeal [S.I. No. 136 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0136.html) which prescribed the forms used following the 1999 local elections.
