# S.I. No. 136/2019 - National Treasury Management Agency (State Authority) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil”* 5*th of April,* 2019.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (j) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000), and with the consent of the Minister for Health, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (State Authority) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. Children’s Health Ireland is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (j) of the definition of “State authority” in [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html#sec7) (1) of the [National Treasury Management Agency (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0039/print.html) (No. 39 of 2000).

The Minister for Health consents to the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 March 2019

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Finance.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 March 2019

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
