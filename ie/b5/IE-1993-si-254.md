S.I. No. 254 of 1993.

ADOPTION REGULATIONS, 1993.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0014/sec0009.html#sec9) (3) of the [Adoption Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1991), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of September, 1993.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Act" means the [Adoption Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) ;

"place" has the meaning assigned to it in the [Adoption Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. Where a document or documents of the kind described in section 9 (1) of the Act is or are not provided for or made available under the law of the place where the adoption was effected, a document, duly authenticated, which purports to be—

( *a* ) a certified copy of an entry made, under and in accordance with the law of the place where the adoption was effected, in a public register relating to the recording of adoptions and showing that the adoption has been effected in that place, or

( *b* ) a certificate or statement that the adoption has been effected, signed or purporting to be signed by a person authorised by the law of the place where the adoption was effected to sign such a certificate or statement, or a certified copy of such certificate or statement,

shall, in substitution for a document or documents of the kind described in the said section 9 (1) and without further proof, unless the contrary is shown, be admissible as evidence that an adoption outside the State has been effected.

###### 5.

5. For the purposes of Article (4) of these Regulations a document shall be regarded as duly authenticated if it purports—

( *a* ) to bear the seal of the court or other authority or the person or persons by which or by whom it was issued or executed, or

( *b* ) to be certified—

- (i) by a person in his capacity as a judge or officer of that court or in his capacity as that authority or as a member or officer of that authority, or

- (ii) by the person or persons by whom it was issued or executed.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 31st day of August, 1993.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the circumstances in which certain documents other than those specified in [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0014/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Adoption Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) shall be acceptable as evidence of an adoption effected outside the State.
