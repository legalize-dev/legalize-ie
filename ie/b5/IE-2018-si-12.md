S.I. No. 12 of 2018

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (FAMILY LAW: MEDIATION) 2018

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Family Law: Mediation) 2018, shall come into operation on the 22nd day of January 2018.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018.

- (3) The Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018 as amended by these Rules may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018.

###### 2.

2. Order 59 of the Circuit Court Rules is amended:

- (i) by the substitution for sub-rule (2) of rule 7 of the following sub-rule:

  > - “(2) Where required, the appropriate statutory declaration made pursuant to section 5 of the Judicial Separation Act or section 6 of the Divorce Act, which shall be in the Form 37D of the Schedule of Forms, shall be filed together with the Family Law Civil Bill.”;

- (ii) by the substitution for paragraph (i) of sub-rule (1) of rule 8 of the following paragraph:

  > - “(i) a copy of the appropriate statutory declaration made pursuant to section 5 of the Judicial Separation Act or section 6 of the Divorce Act, where required;”, and

- (iii) by the substitution for sub-rules (2) and (3) of rule 30 of the following sub-rules respectively:

  > - “(2) Where required, the appropriate statutory declaration made pursuant to section 6 of the Judicial Separation Act or section 7 of the Divorce Act, which shall be in the Form 37D of the Schedule of Forms, shall be filed together with the Appearance.
  >
  > - (3) The respondent shall serve a copy of the Appearance and appropriate statutory declaration on the applicant’s solicitor or, where the applicant is acting in person, on the applicant.”.

###### 3.

3. The Form 37D in the Schedule shall be substituted for the form bearing the like number in the Schedule to the Circuit Court Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules amend Order 59 and substitute Form 37D to facilitate the operation of the [Mediation Act, 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) .
