# S.I. No. 18/1994 - Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979 (Section 3) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 18 of 1994.

MILK (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1979 (SECTION 3) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979) (adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 11 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0011.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into force on the 1st day of February, 1994.

###### 3.

3. The rate of fee specified in Section 3, subsection (1) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979) is hereby altered by the substitution of "0.3580 new pence equivalent to 0.0787 new pence per litre" for "0.3153 new pence (inserted by the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 425 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0425.html) )).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of January, 1994.

**JOE WALSH.**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and

Forestry.
