# S.I. No. 198/1967 - Summer Time Order, 1967

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 198 of 1967.

SUMMER TIME ORDER, 1967.

I, BRIAN JOSEPH LENIHAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Summer Time Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1925), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Summer Time Order, 1967.

###### 2.

2. The period of summer time appointed by the Summer Time Order, 1926, shall not be the period of summer time for the purposes of the [Summer Time Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , in the year 1968.

###### 3.

3. The period of summer time for the purposes of the [Summer Time Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) , in the year 1968 shall be the period beginning at two o'clock, West-European time, in the morning of the 18th day of February, 1968, and ending at two o'clock, West-European time, in the morning of the 27th day of October, 1968.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of August, 1967.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Justice.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order fixes the period of Summer Time for the year 1968.
