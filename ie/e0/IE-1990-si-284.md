# S.I. No. 284/1990 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 284 of 1990.

MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS 1990.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 and subsection (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), and by [sections 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) and [72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations 1990.

###### 2.

2. Where a person is or becomes entitled to—

( *a* ) payment under sub-section (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 190 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0190.html) ): or

( *b* ) payment of a maintenance allowance under [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in accordance with the Disabled Persons (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0193.html) );

any amount so payable in respect of the week ending on the 7th day of December, 1990 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount.

GIVEN under Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 29th day of November, 1990.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 29th day of November 1990.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week ending 7th December, 1990, the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowances payable under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) and [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount.
