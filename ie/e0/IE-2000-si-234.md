# S.I. No. 234/2000 - Garda Síochána (Ranks) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0002/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Garda Síochána Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1972), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Ranks) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby determined that the Garda Síochána shall be divided into the ranks specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order and that each rank shall consist of not more than the number of members of the Garda Síochána specified in column (2) of that Schedule opposite the mention of the rank in column (1).

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order prescribes the ranks of the Garda Síochána and the maximum number of members of each rank.
