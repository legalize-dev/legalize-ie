# S.I. No. 118/1985 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 118 of 1985.

PRISONS ACT, 1970 (SECTION 7) ORDER, 1985.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (l) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the section" means [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970).

###### 3.

3. (1) The section shall come into operation on the 26th day of April, 1985.

- (2) The section shall be in operation for the period of six months commencing on the 26th day of April, 1985, and shall cease to be in operation upon the expiration of that period.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of April, 1985.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order brings [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) into operation for a period of six months.
