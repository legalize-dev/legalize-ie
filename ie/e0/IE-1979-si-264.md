# S.I. No. 264/1979 - Money Order Amendment (No. 23) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 264 of 1979.

MONEY ORDER AMENDMENT (No. 23) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, TOM FITZPATRICK, Minister of State at the Department of Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23 of the Post Office Act, 1908, and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Post Office (Amendment) Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1969), and the Posts and Telegraphs (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 3 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0003.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Money Order Amendment (No. 23) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1979.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 18 (1) of the Money Order Regulations, 1903, is hereby amended by the substitution of the following tables for Table A (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Money Order Amendment (No. 22) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 51 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0051.html) ) and Table B (inserted by Regulation 4 of the Money Order Amendment (No. 21) Regulations, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 317 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0317.html) )) which set out the poundage payable in respect of money orders:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| " A | Poundage |
| In respect of inland money orders: |  |
| for an amount not exceeding £50.00 | 42p |
| for an amount exceeding £50 but not exceeding £100.00 | 60p |
| "B | Poundage |
| In respect of foreign money orders: |  |
| for an amount not exceeding £25.00 | 50p |
| for an amount exceeding £25.00 but not exceeding £50.00 | 100p |

GIVEN under my Hand, this 27th day of July, 1979.

TOM FITZPATRICK,

Minister for Posts and

Telegraphs.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents

to the provisions of the foregoing Warrant.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 27th

day of July, 1979.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This warrant applies to the Money Order service and comes into operation on the 1st August, 1979. It authorises increases in charges on inland and foreign orders.
