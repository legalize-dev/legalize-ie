# S.I. No. 530/2019 - Supreme Court, Court of Appeal and High Court (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st November*, 2019.

I, CHARLES FLANAGAN, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 65 (as amended by [section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec66) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2011)) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court, Court of Appeal and High Court (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2019.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 25 October 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Supreme Court, Court of Appeal and High Court (Fees) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 492 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0492.html) ) is amended in Schedule 1 -

- (*a*) by the insertion in the Arrangement of Parts after “Part 6 - Fees payable in the Taxing-Masters’ Office” of “Part 6A - Fees payable in the Office of the Legal Costs Adjudicators”,

- (*b*) by the substitution of the following for Part 6:

  > ### “Part 6
  >
  > Fees payable in the Taxing-Masters’ Office
  >
  > |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- |
  > |  | Item (1) | Fee (2) |
  > | 1 | On the taxation of a bill of costs - for every full €100.00 in the amount allowed (including witnesses’ expenses) | €8.00 (Credit is to be given for the fee paid in respect of the notice to tax concerned) |
  > | 2 | On the issue of a certificate of taxation | €68.00 |
  > | 3 | On carrying in objections to the allowance or disallowance of any item or items in a bill of costs | €68.00 |
  > | 4 | On a report of a taxing master as to the grounds of his or her decision to allow or disallow any item or items in a bill of costs | €24.00 |
  > | 5 | On a report of a taxing master as to security for costs in a matrimonial case | €24.00 |
  >
  > ”,

  and

- (*c*) by the insertion of the following after Part 6:

  > ### “Part 6A
  >
  > Fees payable in the Office of the Legal Costs Adjudicators
  >
  > |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- |
  > |  | Item (1) | Fee (2) |
  > | 1 | On a notice of application for adjudication of costs | €275.00 |
  > | 2 | On the adjudication of a bill of costs - for every full €100.00 in the total amount confirmed by the Legal Costs Adjudicator (including witnesses’ expenses) | €8.00 (Credit is to be given for the fee paid in respect of - (a) the item at reference number 1 above, or (b) in a matter to which section 165(3) of the [Legal Services Regulation Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0065/print.html) (No. 65 of 2015) applies, the notice to tax concerned.) |
  > | 3 | On the issue of a certificate of determination | €68.00 |
  > | 4 | On a notice of application for consideration of a decision and the making of a determination under [section 160](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0065/print.html#sec160) of the [Legal Services Regulation Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0065/print.html) . | €68.00 |
  >
  > ”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Order amends the Supreme Court, Court of Appeal and High Court (Fees) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 492 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0492.html) ) in order to provide for fees payable in the Office of the Legal Costs Adjudicators which was formerly known as the Taxing-Masters’ Office.
