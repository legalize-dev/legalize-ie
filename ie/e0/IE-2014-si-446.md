# S.I. No. 446/2014 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th October*, 2014.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) (as amended by the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 376 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0376.html) )) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2—

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by substituting for the definition of “levy period” the following:

      > “ ‘levy period’, in respect of credit institutions referred to at reference numbers 1 to 3 of the Schedule, means the period commencing on 1 October 2014 and ending on 31 December 2014, and, otherwise, means the period commencing on 1 October 2014 and ending on 30 September 2015.”,

    - (ii) in paragraph (2), by substituting “30 June 2014” for “28 June 2013” in each place where it occurs, and

    - (iii) in paragraph (3), by substituting “30 June 2014” for “30 June 2013”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3(1), by substituting “1 October 2014” for “1 October 2013”,

- (*c*) in Regulation 4(1), by substituting “28 February 2015” for “28 February 2014”, and

- (*d*) in the Schedule—

    - (i) at reference number 1, by substituting—

        - (I) “30June 2014” for “28 June 2013” in each place where it occurs in column (2), and

        - (II) “€266.25”for “€1,065” in column (3),

    - (ii) at reference number 2, by substituting—

        - (I) “30June 2014” for “28 June 2013” in each place where it occurs in column (2), and

        - (II) “€88.75”for “€355” in column (3),

          and

    - (iii) at reference number 3, by substituting—

        - (I) “30June 2014” for “28 June 2013” in each place where it occurs in column (2), and

        - (II) “€23.75”for “€95” in column (3).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 October 2014.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
