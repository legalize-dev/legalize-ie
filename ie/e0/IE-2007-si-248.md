HOUSES OF THE OIREACHTAS (MEMBERS) PENSIONS (AMENDMENT) SCHEME 2007.

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st June*, 2007.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 6A (inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0012/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1960), and amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0032/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices (Amendment) Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1983), [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0021/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1994), [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998) and [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec27) of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2001)) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1938), hereby make the following scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Houses of the Oireachtas (Members) Pensions (Amendment) Scheme 2007.

- (2) This Scheme comes into operation on 24 May 2007.

###### 2.

No. 212 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0212.html) ) (as amended by the Houses of the Oireachtas (Members) Pensions (Amendment)) (No. 2) Scheme 1998 ( [S.I.       >     - (I) where the former member had been in receipt of an additional allowance in respect of one position only, the additional allowance currently payable to the holder of such position, or
      >
      >     - (II) where the former member held more than one position in respect of which he had been in receipt of an additional allowance, the weighted average additional allowance calculated as follows—
      >
      >       (AA1 x T1 ) .... (AAn x Tn )
      >
      >       T 1 .... T n
      >
      >       where—
      >
      >       AA 1 .... AA n represents the additional allowances in descending order of monetary value currently payable in respect of each position which was held by the former member and in respect of which an additional allowance was payable, provided that in calculating (AA 1 x T 1 ) .... (AA n x T n ), the sum of the periods (T 1 , T 2 ... T n ) in respect of which each additional allowance was held shall not exceed 3,650 days,
      >
      >       T 1 .... T n represent the period in days during which each of those positions was held subject to the sum of T 1 .... T n not exceeding 3,650 days, and
      >

      > (7,300 x 2)”,

    - (i) by substituting the following for subclause (1):

      > - “(1) A pension payable under subclause (1) of Clause 5 of the old Scheme to a former member, who had at any time been in receipt of an additional allowance, shall be increased in accordance with the following formula—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This scheme amends the basis on which certain additional allowances payable to members of the Oireachtas (for example in respect of responsibility as Chairperson of an Oireachtas Committee) can be reckoned in the calculation of benefits under the Houses of the Oireachtas (Members) Pensions Scheme.
