# S.I. No. 863/2004 - Child Care Act, 1991 (Children's Residential Centres) (Superannuation) Order, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/sec0066.html#sec66) of the [Child Care Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1991) (as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Child Care Act of 1991 (Children's Residential Centres) (Superannuation) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 20th day of December, 2004.

###### 3.

3. [Section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/sec0066.html#sec66) of the [Child Care Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) shall apply with effect from the 20th day of December, 2004, to the children's residential centres specified in the Schedule which are children's residential centres approved (or deemed to be approved) for the purposes of [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/sec0036.html#sec36) of the [Child Care Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) .

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this Order is to apply the statutory superannuation scheme provided for in [Section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/sec0066.html#sec66) of the [Child Care Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) to the staffs of those children's residential centres indicated in the schedule.
