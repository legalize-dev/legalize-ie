# S.I. No. 366/1995 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) (Temple Bar) Order, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 366 of 1995.

URBAN RENEWAL ACT, 1986 (DESIGNATED AREAS) (TEMPLE BAR) ORDER, 1995.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986), which powers are delegated to me by the Environment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 3 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0003.html) ), I, LIZ McMANUS, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment, hereby Order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) (Temple Bar) Order, 1995.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, "the Temple Bar Area" has the meaning assigned to it by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0019/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Temple Bar Area Renewal and Development Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1991).

###### 3.

3. The Temple Bar Area is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

Dated this 21st day of December, 1995.

LIZ McMANUS,

Minister of State at the Department of the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

21st day of December, 1995.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the designation under the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) of the Temple Bar Area in Dublin.
