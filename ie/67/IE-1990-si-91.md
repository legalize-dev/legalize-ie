S.I. No. 91 of 1990.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS SCHEME, 1990.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), hereby makes the following Scheme:

PART I PRELIMINARY AND GENERAL

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications Scheme, 1990.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on the second day of April, 1990.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme

###### 5.

5. (1) In this Scheme:

"the Acts" means the Telegraph Acts, 1863 to date, the Postal and Telecommunications Services Acts, 1983 and 1984 and any secondary legislation or other measures thereunder;

"agreement" means an agreement (whether contained in one or more documents or in information in any form) made, or deemed to have been made, between Bord Telecom Éireann and a person, relating to the provision of a telecommunications service;

"alarm clock call service" means the service whereby a telephone call is made at the request of a caller or telephone subscriber, from a telephone exchange at a specified time and includes all ancillary services;

"automatic telex call" means a telex call obtained without the intervention of a telex exchange operator;

"call" means the initiation or establishment of the means of transmission of a single communication or message through a telecommunications service:

"caller" means a person using a telecommunications service for the purpose of transmitting or receiving a telecommunications message;

"certified" means certified by an employee of Bord Telecom Éireann;

"coinbox" means a receptacle for coins (or a device for recording other means of payment) connected to a telephone to which is connected a telephone exchange line;

"coinbox exchange line" means a telephone exchange line with which is connected a coinbox;

"Eircell" is the name assigned by Bord Telecom Éireann to the cellular radio telephone system operated by it whereby cellular radio telephone service is provided through the public switched telephone exchange system (the "fixed telephone network") and a wireless telegraphy link provided by means of a cellular radio system or network and, where the context so admits or requires, applies to fixtures and accessories pertaining to the said cellular radio telephone system or network and the terms "Eircell" and "Eircell Services" shall be construed accordingly;

"Eircell equipment" means a cellular radio telephone (whether mobile or portable) within the Eircell service to which service is provided in accordance with the agreement for Eircell service and includes other equipment and apparatus pertaining to the same;

"Eircell subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes or has made an agreement for the purpose of providing such person with Eircell service and also means a person to whom such service has been or is being provided;

"Eirmail" is the name assigned by Bord Telecom Éireann to the Eirmail Computer Messaging Service and Eirmail Service operated by it and, where the context so requires or admits, applies to fixtures and accessories pertaining to the said service;

"Eirmail computer" means switching and computer equipment and apparatus which forms part of the Eirmail Computer Messaging Service operated by or on behalf of Bord Telecom Éireann whether or not in conjunction with others for the purposes of storing and conveying messages or information in the form of data by means of telecommunications and, where the context so admits or requires, includes the building, vehicle or other external covering which houses such equipment and apparatus;

"Eirmail service" refers to the Eirmail computer Messaging Service and includes, where the context requires or admits, any facilities, fixtures and accessories comprised in it;

"Eirmail subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes or has made an agreement for the purposes of providing such person with Eirmail service and also means a person to whom such; service has been or is being provided;

"Eirpac" is the name assigned by Bord Telecom Éireann to the Irish National Packet Switched Data Network operated by it and, where the context so admits or requires, applies to facilities, fixtures and accessories pertaining the said Network.

"Eirpac service" refers to the service provided on the Irish National Packet Switched Data Network and includes, where the context admits or requires, facilities, fixtures and accessories comprised in it;

"Eirpac subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes or has made an agreement for the purpose of providing such person with Eirpac service and also means a person to whom such service has been or is being provided;

"Eirpage service" means the one-way national wide-area radio paging service operated by Eirpage Limited and interconnected to the fixed telecommunications networks of Bord Telecom Éireann;

"Eirpage equipment" means paging units (and other associated equipment) to which Eirpage service is supplied;

"exchange" in relation to any telecommunications service means switching equipment and apparatus which forms part of the Bord Telecom Éireann system for provision of that service and, where the context so admits or requires, includes the building or part of the building or other external covering housing such apparatus;

"external extension" means an extension line other than an internal extension;

"fee" means any charge other than a charge specified in this Scheme made to a person by Bord Telecom Éireann in respect of a service rendered to the said person;

"fixed time call" means a telephone call to be effected at or about a time specified in advance by a telephone subscriber or caller;

"fixed time charge" means a charge in respect of a fixed time call or in respect of an attempt to effect a fixed time call;

"fixtures and accessories", means fixtures and accessories owned and provided by Bord Telecom Éireann in connection with a telecommunications service supplied by it;

> "Freefone 1800 call" means a telephone call which is made by a telephone subscriber or caller, in which the connection of the call is effected by automatic switching, which call is connected to the telephone exchange line of a telephone subscriber identifiable by a Freefone 1800 number allocated by Bord Telecom Éireann and which call is charged to and payable by the telephone subscriber identified by such Freefone 1800 number and the service whereby such calls may be received shall be known as the "Freefone 1800 service".
>
> "1850 call" means a telephone call which is made by a telephone subscriber or caller, in which the connection of the call is effected by automatic switching, which call connected to the telephone exchange line of a telephone subscriber identifiable by a 1850 number allocated by Bord Telecom Éireann and which is in part charged to and payable by the telephone subscriber identified by such 1850 number and the service whereby such calls may be received shall be known as the "1850 service";

"group centre" has the meaning assigned to it by Article 52 of this Scheme;

"Internal extension" means an extension line wholly within or intended to be wholly within a subscriber's premises and by means of which two points within a subscriber's premises are connected or intended to be connected for the purpose of conveying telecommunications messages;

"local call" means any telephone call referred to in Article 53 of this Scheme;

"packet switching exchange" means an exchange forming part of Eirpac and which is operated by Bord Telecom Éireann for the purpose of conveying data by means of telecommunications through the medium of that network;

"Pactex" means a facility whereby a message may be transmitted through the telex service and thereafter through the Eirpac service to an Eirpac subscriber or to an electronic mailbox rented by an Eirpac subscriber and whereby a message may be transmitted through the Eirpac service and thereafter through the telex service to a telex subscriber;

"personal call"s means a call which is specified by the subscriber or caller to be a call for establishment of communication through a telecommunications service between the subscriber or caller and a designated person or, at the request (made when the application for the call is made) of the subscriber or caller, between the subscriber or caller and a designated person or a substitute for that person;

"personal charge" means a charge in respect of a personal call or in respect of an attempt to effect a personal call;

"place" includes a vessel, aircraft and vehicle;

"premises" includes a vessel, aircraft and vehicle;

"private branch exchange" means switching apparatus installed or intended to be installed in a subscriber's premises and connected or intended to be connected both to telephone exchange lines and to extension lines, and designed to provide inter-communication between the extension lines and communication between the extension lines and the telephone exchange lines;

"private telecommunications circuit" means a telecommunications line, fixtures and accessories which connect one subscriber's premises with another subscriber's premises or another premises of the same subscriber (or in the case of a private telecommunications circuit between a point in the State and a point outside the State, a premises of any person) and which is rented from Bord Telecom Éireann by the person or persons whose premises is or are so connected for his or their use for the purpose of transmitting and receiving telecommunications messages between such premises on his or their business or private affairs (and includes all other fixtures and accessories associated therewith);

"private telecommunications circuit subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes or has made an agreement for the purpose of enabling such person to communicate by means of a private telecommunications circuit and also means a person to whom such circuit has been or is being provided;

"public telephone" means a coinbox exchange line (other than a coinbox exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber) to which members of the public have access, whether continuously or for such daily periods as may be determined, prescribed or permitted from time to time by Bord Telecom Éireann or its agents, as the case may be;

"reverse charge call" means a telephone call between telephones in the State or between a telephone outside the State and a telephone in the State where the person making the call requests the telephone exchange operator that the charge or charges payable in respect of the call (save any personal charge) shall be charged to and payable by the person who is the telephone subscriber in respect of the called telephone and the telephone exchange operator obtains from the person answering on the called telephone a consent to the request before the call is established;

"subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes, has made or is deemed to have made an agreement for the provision to such person of a telecommunications service and also means a person to whom such service has been or is being provided;

"subscriber dialled trunk call" means a trunk call which is made by a telephone subscriber or a caller in which the connection of the call is effected by automatic switching;

"subscriber's premises" means a building, or part of a building, vehicle, vessel or other structure or place, owned or occupied or controlled by a subscriber, and includes any premises in which a telecommunications line or fixtures and accessories used by or provided to a subscriber are installed;

"subscriber equipment" means equipment or apparatus owned by a person other than Bord Telecom Éireann, and used or intended to be used for the enjoyment of or in conjunction with a telecommunications service;

"subscription" means the sum or sums due in accordance with the terms of an agreement in respect of rental of telecommunications services, fixtures and accessories;

"Telecard" means a card issued to a Telecard subscriber by Bord Telecom Éireann bearing a serial number allotted to such subscriber and entitling the Telecard bearer to make a Telecard call;

"Telecard bearer" means a person who, whether or not he has the authority of the Telecard subscriber, is for the time being in possession of a Telecard and includes the Telecard subscriber or a person who has knowledge of the Telecard serial number whether or not so authorised;

"Telecard call" means any telephone call (other than a call of a type not for the time being permitted by Bord Telecom Éireann) whether authorised by the Telecard subscriber or not, made by a Telecard bearer, where the Telecard bearer cites the Telecard serial number and requests the telephone exchange operator to charge such call to the account of the said subscriber;

"Telecard subscriber" means a telephone subscriber to whom a Telecard has been issued pursuant to Article 67 of this Scheme;

"telecommunications line" includes a telegraphic line, a telegraph (as such terms are defined in the Telegraph Acts, 1863 to date) and includes all lines, circuits, apparatus and installations used for telecommunications services and, for the avoidance of doubt, includes Eircell service and equipment;

"telecommunications message" means a message or communication transmitted by a telecommunications service;

"telecommunications service" means any service or facility provided by Bord Telecom Éireann as a means of or relating to telecommunications and includes, where the context admits or requires, a service or facility comprised in or ancillary to any such service or facility;

"telephone" means a transmitting or receiving instrument used or intended to be used for the purpose of transmitting or receiving telephonic messages and includes Eircell equipment;

"telephone call" means a call made to or from a telephone;

"telephone exchange" means an exchange forming part of the Bord Telecom Éireann system for provision of telephone service;

"telephone exchange line" means a telecommunications line provided for the purpose of connecting a telephone exchange with a public telephone or a telephone subscriber's telephone, together with all fixtures and accessories associated therewith whether or not in or on the telephone subscriber's premises but excluding Eircell equipment;

"telephone group" has the meaning assigned to it by Article 52 of this Scheme;

"telephone information service" means a service (whether provided by Bord Telecom Éireann or by others or by Bord Telecom Éireann in conjunction with others) whereby information is provided to callers;

"telephone service" means a service whereby telephonic messages may be transmitted through the public switched telephone exchange system of Bord Telecom Éireann and includes, where the context admits or requires, any service or facility provided by Bord Telecom Éireann and comprised in such service;

"telephone subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes, has made, or is deemed to have made an agreement for the purpose of enabling such person to communicate with other persons by means of the telephone service and also means a person to whom such service has been or is being provided;

"telephone subscriber's premises" means the subscriber's premises of a telephone subscriber;

"telephonic message" means a message or communication transmitted by telephone and includes the transmission of data;

"teleprinter" means a transmitting or receiving instrument used or intended to be used for the purpose of transmitting or receiving telex messages by means of electricity or otherwise;

"telex call" means a call made through the telex service;

"telex booth" means a telex installation (other than a telex installation rented by a telex subscriber) to which members of the public have access whether continuously or for such daily periods as may be determined, prescribed or permitted from time to time by Bord Telecom Éireann or its agents as the case may be;

"telex exchange" means an exchange forming part of the Bord Telecom Éireann system for provision of telex service;

"telex installation" means that part of a telex line and also the fixtures and accessories associated therewith on a telex subscriber's premises and includes, where the context admits or requires, Eirpage equipment;

"telex line" means a telecommunications line provided for the purpose of connecting a telex subscriber's premises and a telex exchange together with any fixtures and accessories associated therewith whether or not in or on the telex subscriber's premises;

"telex message" means a message or communication transmitted by telex;

"telex service" means the service whereby telex messages may be sent directly between the sender and the recipient through the public telex network of Bord Telecom Éireann;

"telex subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes, has made, or is deemed to have made an agreement for the purpose of enabling such person to communicate with other persons by means of the telex service and also means a person to whom such service has been or is being provided;

"telex subscriber's premises" means the subscriber's premises of a telex subscriber;

"trunk call" means any telephone call which is not a local call and includes all telephone calls made to Eircell equipment;

"type-approved apparatus" means apparatus or equipment which is of a type approved by a competent authority for the time being duly authorised in that behalf for connection to or use in conjunction with a telecommunications line of Bord Telecom Éireann;

"urgent call" means a trunk call for which precedence is requested by the telephone subscriber or caller and to which Bord Telecom Éireann assigns such degree of precedence over other trunk calls which it considers appropriate;

"videoconference service" means a point to point non-switched service providing communication between persons in different locations combining audio communications and moving pictures of persons using the service and may include data transmission, still picture display and facsimile; and

"videoconference subscriber" means a person with whom Bord Telecom Éireann makes or has made an agreement for the purpose of enabling such person to use the videoconference service of Bord Telecom Éireann and also means a person to whom such service has been or is being provided.

- (2) References in this Scheme to the use of a telecommunications service by a subscriber shall be construed as references to use by means of facilities provided to such subscriber whether the telecommunications service is used by the subscriber or by another person (whether or not such person is acting with the authority of the subscriber).

Chapter 4 — Use of telecommunications services.

###### 25.

( *c* ) for the transmission of any message or other matter, which the sender knows to be false, for the purpose of causing annoyance, inconvenience or needless anxiety to any person;

( *d* ) in any manner which, in the opinion of Bord Telecom Éireann, may adversely affect the use of any telecommunications service by other subscribers or which may adversely affect any telecommunications service as a whole; or

( *e* ) otherwise than in accordance with instructions given by Bord Telecom Éireann from time to time under Article 47 (2) (*c*) of this Scheme or otherwise.

###### 52.

( *a* ) what telephone groups, if any, are to be regarded as adjacent to it; and

( *b* ) the point (in this Scheme referred to as the "group centre") which is to be used for measuring distances for the purpose of charging for telephone calls.

- (2) Bord Telecom Éireann shall supply to a telephone subscriber, on demand, a list of the telephone exchanges which are for the time being either in the same telephone group as the telephone exchange with which the subscriber's telephone exchange line is connected or in the telephone groups which are regarded by Bord Telecom Éireann as being adjacent to that telephone group.

###### 53.

53. (1) In respect of every telephone call made from a telephone exchange line to:

( *a* ) a telephone exchange line in the same telephone group; or,

( *b* ) a telephone exchange line in another telephone group which is regarded by Bord Telecom Éireann as being adjacent to the telephone group of the telephone subscriber making the call; or,

( *c* ) Eirpage equipment

there shall be charged to the telephone subscriber and paid by him whichever of the charges specified in paragraphs 1 and 3 of Part 2 of the First Schedule to this Scheme is appropriate to such telephone call.

- (2) ( *a* ) In respect of every telephone call made from a coinbox exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber to:

    - (i) a telephone exchange line in the same telephone group; or,

    - (ii) a telephone exchange line in another telephone group which is regarded by Bord Telecom Éireann as being adjacent to the telephone group of the coinbox exchange line from which the telephone call is made; or,

    - (iii) Eirpage equipment

there shall be charged to the telephone subscriber and paid by him whichever of the charges specified in paragraphs 2 (*a*) and 4 (*a*) of Part 2 of the First Schedule to this Scheme is appropriate to such telephone call.

( *b* ) In respect of every telephone call made from a public telephone to:

- (i) a telephone exchange line in the same telephone group; or,

- (ii) a telephone exchange line in another telephone group which is regarded by Bord Telecom Éireann as being adjacent to the telephone group of the public telephone from which the telephone call is made; or,

- (iii) Eirpage equipment

there shall be charged to the caller and placed in the coinbox or credited to Bord Telecom Éireann in such manner as it may from time to time prescribe whichever of the charges specified in paragraphs 2 (*b*) or 4 (*b*) of Part 2 of the First Schedule to this Scheme is appropriate to such telephone call.

###### 54.

54. (1) In respect of every subscriber dialled trunk call made from a telephone exchange line there shall be charged to the telephone subscriber and paid by him a charge calculated in accordance with Part 3 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (2) In respect of every subscriber dialled trunk call made from a public telephone there shall be charged or placed in the coinbox or otherwise credited to Bord Telecom Éireann in such manner as it may from time to time prescribe (as appropriate) a sum calculated in accordance with Part 3 of the First Schedule to the Scheme.

- (3) In respect of every subscriber dialled trunk call made from a coinbox exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber, there shall be charged to the telephone subscriber and paid by him a charge calculated in accordance with Part 3 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (4) In respect of any subscriber dialled call (including a subscriber dialled trunk call) made from a telephone exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber or made from a public telephone to Eircell equipment and to which all paragraphs (1), (2) or (3) of this Article and Part 2 and Part 3 of the First Schedule to this Scheme would apply if it had been to a telephone exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber or public telephone, the charges applicable to the said call shall nevertheless be governed by paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of this Article and Part 3 of the First Schedule to this Scheme as if the call were to a telephone exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber or to a public telephone save that in the case of calls made between points in the State and made from points in the State to points in Northern Ireland the period of time appropriate shall in all cases irrespective of distance be that specified in respect of distances exceeding 80 kilometres.

###### 55.

55. (1) In respect of every trunk call (excluding subscriber dialled trunk calls) made by a telephone subscriber who rents an exchange line without a coinbox there shall be charged to the subscriber and paid by him the charge stated in Part 4 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (2) In respect of every trunk call (excluding subscriber dialled trunk calls) made from a coinbox exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber there shall be charged to the telephone subscriber and paid by him the charge stated in Part 5 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (3) In respect of every trunk call (excluding subscriber dialled trunk calls) which is made by a caller from a public telephone or coinbox exchange line rented by a subscriber there shall be placed or credited in the coinbox the appropriate charge stated in Part 5 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (4) In respect of every call made from a telephone exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber or a public telephone to Eircell equipment and to which call paragraphs (1), (2) or (3) of this Article and Part 4 and Part 5 of the First Schedule to this Scheme would have applied if it had been to a telephone exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber or a public telephone, the charges applicable to the said call shall nevertheless be governed by paragraphs (1), (2) and (3) of this Article as if the call were made to a telephone exchange line rented by a telephone subscriber or to a public telephone save that in the case of calls from telephone exchange lines rented by telephone subscribers in the State or public telephones in the State to Eircell equipment in the State and from telephone exchange lines rented by telephone subscribers in the State or public telephones in the State to Eircell equipment (or its equivalent in Northern Ireland) the period of time appropriate to the call shall in all cases irrespective of distance be that specified in respect of distances exceeding 80 kilometres.
