# S.I. No. 79/1995 - Social Welfare (Optional Contributions) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 79 of 1995.

SOCIAL WELFARE (OPTIONAL CONTRIBUTIONS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993) and 24C of that Act (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0032/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Social Welfare (No. 2) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1993)), hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation and construction.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Optional Contributions) (Amendment) Regulations, 1995.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Optional Contributions) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 53 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0053.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Optional Contributions) Regulations, 1994 and 1995.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 6th day of April, 1995.

3 Determination of optional contributions in certain circumstances.

###### 3.

**3.** Article 9 of the Social Welfare (Optional Contributions) Regulations, 1994 is hereby amended by the substitution of "shall be the sum specified in section 18 (1) (*h*) of the Principal Act." for "shall be £250.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 3rd day of April, 1995.

**PRIONSIAS DE ROSSA,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

Where a person applies to become an optional contributor (under the scheme of social insurance for share fishermen) in the contribution year in which they first became insured as a self-employed contributor, the optional contribution payable is £250. These Regulations reduce the optional contribution payable in this situation to the minimum social insurance contribution payable by a self-employed contributor, i.e. £230 from 6 April, 1995.
