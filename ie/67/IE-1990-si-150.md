S.I. No. 150 of 1990.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (LIFE ASSURANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1990.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purposes of giving effect to Council Directive No. 79/267/EEC^(1), hereby make the following Regulations:

^(1)O.J. No. L63, 13.3. 1979, pp. 1-18.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Life Assurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1990 and shall come into operation on the 18th day of June, 1990.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations the "Regulations of 1986" means the European Communities (Life Assurance Accounts, Statements and Valuations) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0437.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Life Assurance) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 57 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0057.html) ), the Regulations of 1986, the European Communities (Life Assurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 143 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0143.html) ), and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Life Assurance) Regulations, 1984 to 1990.

###### 4.

4. The Regulations of 1986 are hereby amended by the substitution—

( *a* ) of the following subparagraph for subparagraph (*a*) of paragraph (1) of Article 61—

> "( *a* ) an Irish head office undertaking and a Community branch undertaking shall establish and maintain assets in the State equal to not less than 60 per cent of its combined linked and non-linked liabilities subject to the provision that assets equal to not less than 80% of the undertaking's non-linked liabilities shall be so established and maintained;", and

( *b* ) of the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations for Form 4 contained in Schedule 2.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Life Assurance Accounts, Statements and Valuations) Regulations, 1986 by reducing the 80% localisation requirement (in respect of Irish head office undertakings and Community branch undertakings) to 60% overall, subject to the 80% requirement remaining in respect of non-linked liabilities. Amendment is also made to the format of Form 4 contained in the 1986 Regulations. The effect of the amendment is to relieve life companies of the requirement to detail single premium information on this Form.
