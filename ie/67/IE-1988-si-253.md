# S.I. No. 253/1988 - Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1988) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 253 of 1988.

DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1988) ORDER, 1988.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936) (as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0097.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1988) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 28th day of November, 1988 is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Dublin District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of September 1988.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1988 on which ordinary members of the Dublin District Milk Board shall be elected.
