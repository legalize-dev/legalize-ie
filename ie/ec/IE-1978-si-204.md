# S.I. No. 204/1978 - Exchange Control (No. 3) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 204 of 1978.

EXCHANGE CONTROL (No. 3) REGULATIONS, 1978.

I, SEOIRSE Ó COLLA, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), as continued by the [Exchange Control (Continuance) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1974), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Exchange Control (No. 3) Regulations, 1978.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 25th day of July, 1978.

- (3) The collective citation "the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959 to 1978" shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. The Irish Intercontinental Bank Limited, 91 Merrion Square, Dublin 2, is hereby appointed to be an authorised dealer.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 18th day of July, 1978.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Exchange Control Regulations, 1959, by appointing Irish Intercontinental Bank Limited, 91 Merrion Square, Dublin 2, to be an authorised dealer for the purpose of the Exchange Control Acts.
