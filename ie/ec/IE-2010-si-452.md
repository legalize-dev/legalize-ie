# S.I. No. 452/2010 - Road Traffic (Weight Laden of 5 Axle Articulated Vehicles) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th September*, 2010.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No.305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) and for the purposes of giving further effect to Directive 96/53/EC of 25 July 1996[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Weight Laden of 5 Axle Articulated Vehicles) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 14(2) of the Road Traffic (Construction and Use of Vehicles) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0005.html) ) is amended by substituting “30 June 2011” for “30 September 2010”.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Weight Laden of 5 Axle Articulated Vehicles) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 576 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0576.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 September 2010.

**NOEL DEMPSEY,**

Minister for Transport.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide that, until 30 June 2011, the weight laden of a combination of a 2 axle tractor unit and a 3 axle semi-trailer may exceed 40 tonnes but shall not exceed 42 tonnes.
