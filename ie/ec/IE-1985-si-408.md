# S.I. No. 408/1985 - Maximum Prices (Intoxicating Liquor) (Revocation) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 408 of 1985.

MAXIMUM PRICES (INTOXICATING LIQUOR) (REVOCATION) ORDER, 1985.

The Minister for Industry, Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 22A and 22C (inserted by the [Prices (Amendment) Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1965)) of the [Prices Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1958), as amended by the [Prices (Amendment) Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1972), the Prices Stabilisation Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 96 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0096.html) ), and the Trade, Commerce and Tourism (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 384 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0384.html) ), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Maximum Prices (Intoxicating Liquor) (Revocation) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 10th day of December, 1985.

###### 3.

3. The Maximum Prices (Intoxicating Liquor) (No. 2) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 345 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0345.html) ) is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Industry, Trade,

Commerce and Tourism, this 9th day of December, 1985.

**SEAN DORGAN,**

A person authorised by the Minister for Industry,

Trade, Commerce and Tourism to authenticate the

Official Seal of the Minister.
