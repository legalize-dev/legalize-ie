# S.I. No. 109/1986 - Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 109 of 1986.

HEALTH SERVICES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and by [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Health Services (No. 2) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 139 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0139.html) ) (as amended by article 2 of the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 145 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0145.html) )) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£14,500)" for £13,500" in sub-article (2) (*a*).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1986.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this

16th day of May, 1986.

**BARRY DESMOND,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Health Services (No. 2) Regulations, 1983 by raising the income ceiling from £13,000 to £14,500 which limits the extent to which services provided under [Section 52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0052.html#sec52) (1), [56](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0056.html#sec56) (2), [62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0062.html#sec62) and [63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) are available to certain persons.
