# S.I. No. 218/1986 - Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 218 of 1986.

SOCIAL WELFARE (RENT ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Housing (Private Rented Dwellings) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1982) and subsections (2) and (3) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1982 to 1985, may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1982 to 1986.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 17th day of July, 1986.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"the Principal Regulations" means the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0220.html) );

"the Regulations of 1985" means the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 236 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0236.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations (as amended by the Regulations of 1985) are hereby further amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution in article 5(2) of "£93" for "£90", "£53" for "£51" and of "£4" for "£3.80" in each case in which the latter sum appears;

( *b* ) the substitution in article 5(3)(a) of "£93" for "£90" and "£103" for "£100" in each case in which the said sum appears;

( *c* ) the substitution in article 5(3)(b) of "£53" for "£51" and of "£58" for "£56" in each case in which the said sum appears;

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare

this 16th day of June, 1986.

**GEMMA HUSSEY,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance

this 16th day of June, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the conditions for receipt of a rent allowance in relation to the means test and calculation of the amount of an allowance, to take account of general increases in social welfare payments from July 1986.
