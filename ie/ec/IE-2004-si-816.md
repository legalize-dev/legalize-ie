# S.I. No. 816/2004 - Environmental Protection Agency (Advisory Committee) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0006.html#sec6) , [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0027.html#sec27) (5) and [27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0027.html#sec27) (16) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Protection Agency (Advisory Committee) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:—

“the Act” means the [Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) ;

“Advisory Committee” means the Advisory Committee provided for in section 27 of the Act.

###### 3.

3. The prescribed organisations for the purposes of section 27(5)(*a*) of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) The Agricultural Science Association,

- (*b*) The County and City Managers Association,

- (*c*) The Environmental Health Officers Association,

- (*d*) The Institute of Chemistry of Ireland,

- (*e*) The Institution of Engineers of Ireland,

- (*f*) The Institution of Water and Environmental Management — Republic of Ireland Branch,

- (*g*) The Irish Planning Institute,

- (*h*) The Royal Town Planning Institute, Irish Branch — Southern Section, and

- (*i*) The Directors of Public Health of the Health Boards.

###### 4.

4. The prescribed organisations for the purposes of section 27(5)(*b*) of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) Fáilte Ireland,

- (*b*) Heritage Council,

- (*c*) Environmental (Ecological) NGOs Core Funding Ltd.

###### 5.

5. The prescribed organisations for the purposes of section 27(5)(*c*) of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) Forfás,

- (*b*) The Irish Business and Employers Confederation,

- (*c*) The Irish Congress of Trade Unions,

- (*d*) The Irish Creamery Milk Suppliers Association,

- (*e*) The Irish Farmers Association, and

- (*f*) The Chambers of Commerce of Ireland.

###### 6.

6. The prescribed organisations for the purposes of section 27(5)(*d*) of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) Aontacht Cumann Riartha Aitreabhthóirí (A.C.R.A.),

- (*b*) The Association of Municipal Authorities in Ireland,

- (*c*) The General Council of County Councils,

- (*d*) The Irish Countrywomen's Association,

- (*e*) Muintir na Tíre,

- (*f*) The National Association of Tenants Organisations,

- (*g*) The National Women's Council of Ireland,

- (*h*) The National Youth Council of Ireland,

- (*i*) The National Federation of Group Water Schemes Society Limited,

- (*j*) Planet, and

- (*k*) The Conference of Religious in Ireland.

###### 7.

7. The prescribed organisations for the purposes of section 27(5)(*e*) of the Act shall be—

- (*a*) The Central Fisheries Board,

- (*b*) The Industry Research and Development Group,

- (*c*) The Marine Institute,

- (*d*) The National Council for Curriculum and Assessment,

- (*e*) The Royal Dublin Society,

- (*f*) The Royal Irish Academy, and

- (*g*) Teagasc.

###### 8.

8. Where a request is made to prescribed organisations pursuant to section 27(7) of the Act the organisations shall, before the expiration of 4 weeks commencing on the day on which the request is made, inform the Minister of the names of the candidates selected and of the reasons why, in the opinion of the organisations, they are suitable for such appointment and shall send to the Minister a *curriculum vitae* in relation to each candidate together with a written consent of each candidate to the selection of that candidate in accordance with the request.

###### 9.

9. The Environmental Protection Agency (Advisory Committee) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 48 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0048.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the various organisations for the purposes of selecting candidates for consideration for appointment by the Minister as members of the Advisory Committee of the Environmental Protection Agency and deal with related matters.
