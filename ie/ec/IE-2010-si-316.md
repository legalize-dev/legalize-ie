# S.I. No. 316/2010 - Inland Fisheries Act 2010 (Form of Instrument of Appointment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd July*, 2010.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) and section 31(2) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries Act 2010 (Form of Instrument of Appointment) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 July 2010.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed as the form of an instrument of appointment for the purposes of [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec31) (2) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010).

###### 3.

3. The Fisheries Act 1980 (Section 26) Regulations 1983 ( [S.I. No. 71 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0071.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations prescribes the format of warrants of appointment of authorised persons under [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec31) (2) of the [Inland Fisheries Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) for the purposes of Part XVIII of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
