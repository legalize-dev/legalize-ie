# S.I. No. 368/2009 - Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 368 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0368.html)

DISABLED DRIVERS AND DISABLED PASSENGERS (TAX CONCESSIONS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th September*, 2009.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 92 (as amended by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1992)) of the [Finance Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1989), after consultation with the Minister for Health and Children (as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) )) and the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (as adapted by the Environment and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0233.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Disabled Drivers and DisabledPassengers (Tax Concessions) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 6 (as amended by the Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 566 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0566.html) )) of the Disabled Drivers and Disabled Passengers (Tax Concessions) Regulations 1994 ( [S.I. No. 353 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0353.html) ) is amended in paragraph (1)—

- (*a*) by inserting after subparagraph (*b*) the following:

  > - “(*ba*) Where the chairperson of the Board is temporarily unable to perform his or her duties, whether because of absence, ill-health or for any other reason, the Minister for Finance may appoint one of the medical practitioners appointed under subparagraph (*a*) to be an acting chairperson to assume the duties of the chairperson for a specified period not exceeding the remainder of the period of the chairperson’s appointment.”,

  and

- (*b*) by substituting the following subparagraph for subparagraph (*d*):

  > - “(*d*) At a meeting of the Board—
  >
  > - (i) the chairperson shall, if present, be the chairperson of the meeting,
  >
  > - (ii) where an acting chairperson has been appointed under subparagraph (*ba*), the acting chairperson shall, if present, be the chairperson of the meeting, or
  >
  > - (iii) if neither the chairperson nor the acting chairperson appointed under subparagraph (*ba*) is present, the members who are present shall choose one of their number to be chairperson of the meeting.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 September 2009

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of the new Regulations is to amend the existing legislation relating to the appointment of a Chairperson of the Disabled Drivers Medical Board of Appeal, in order to provide specifically for the appointment of an acting Chairperson to assume the duties of the Chairperson for a specified period not exceeding the remainder of the period of the Chairperson's appointment, where the Chairperson is unable to perform his or her duties.
