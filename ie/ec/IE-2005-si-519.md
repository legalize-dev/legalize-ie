# S.I. No. 519/2005 - Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000 (Section 5) (Specified Period) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No. 519 OF 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0519.html)

ORDER

entitled

Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000 (Section 5) (Specified Period) Order 2002

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 5(5)(b) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000) and after consultation with the Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (Section 5)(Specified Period) Order 2002.

###### 2.

2. The period of 5 years from the establishment day is hereby specified for the purposes of section 5(5)(b) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000).
