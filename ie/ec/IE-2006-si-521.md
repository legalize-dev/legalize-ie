# S.I. No. 521/2006 - Finance Act 2006 (Section 122(2)) (Designation of Company) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 521 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0521.html)**

**ORDER**

entitled

Finance Act 2006 (Section 122(2)) (Designation of Company) Order 2006.

Made by the Minister for Finance

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 122(2) of the [Finance Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Finance Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (Section 122(2) (Designation of Company) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The company designated to be the Trust for the purposes of section 1003A (inserted by section 122(1) of the [Finance Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No.6 of 2006)) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No 39 of 1997) is the company incorporated under the Companies Acts on 30 June 2006 as The Irish Heritage Trust Limited.
