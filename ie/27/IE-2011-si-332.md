# S.I. No. 332/2011 - Secondary, Community and Comprehensive School Teachers Pension (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th July*, 2011.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) (as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Teachers’ Superannuation (Amendment) Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1990)) of the [Teachers’ Superannuation Act 1928](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1928/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1928) (as adapted by the Education and Science (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0184.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Secondary, Community and Comprehensive School Teachers Pension (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme 2011.

###### 2.

2. The Secondary, Community and Comprehensive School Teachers Pension Scheme 2009 ( [S.I. No. 435 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0435.html) ) is amended by substituting for paragraph (3) of Article 4 the following:

> - “(3) a person is eligible for membership in this Part if he or she was appointed before 1 August 1996 as a teacher in a secondary school at the age of not less than 21 years and was—
>
>     - (*a*) a registered secondary teacher serving in a full time post in a secondary school without an interruption in service and who was receiving remuneration paid by the Minister,
>
>     - (*b*) serving in a comprehensive school or a community school in a permanent teaching post without an interruption in service, or
>
>     - (*c*) serving in a comprehensive school or a community school in the post of chaplain without an interruption in service where his or her appointment occurred on or after 1 August 1990.”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing statutory instrument.
