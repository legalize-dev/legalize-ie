# S.I. No. 387/2001 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1992 (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3(4), (as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998), and by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2001)), and 5A (inserted by section 20 of the 1998 Act aforesaid) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1992), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1992 (Allowances) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. (a) Regulations 4 and 6 are to be taken to have come into operation on 6 June 1997.

- (b) Regulation 5 is to be taken to have come into operation on 1 January 2001.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations the “Principal Regulations” means the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1992 (Allowances) (No. 2) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0397.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by inserting the following after Regulation 6:

> “6A The rate of the allowance payable to the holder of the office of Attorney General while not a member of the Oireachtas, is with effect from 26 June 1997, the sum of £9,770 per year.”.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 7 of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting -

- (a) “£7,000” for “£5,120” in paragraph (1)(a)

- (b) “£7,000” for “£5,000” in paragraph (1)(b), and

- (c) “£3,500” for “£2,500” in paragraph (2).

###### 6.

6. Regulation 8 of the Principal Regulations is amended in paragraph (1)(b) by substituting “5, 6 and 6A” for “5 and 6”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide for the payment of an office holders expense allowance to an Attorney General who is not a member of the Oireachtas with effect from 26th June 1997 and for an increase in the constituency office allowances payable to members of Dáil Éireann.
