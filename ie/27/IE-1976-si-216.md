# S.I. No. 216/1976 - Adoption Rules, 1976

S.I. No. 216 of 1976.

ADOPTION RULES, 1976.

An Bord Uchtála with the consent of the Minister for Justice and in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Adoption Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1952), hereby makes the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Adoption Rules, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The adoption Rules, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0019.html) ), are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. In these rules—

"the Acts" means the Adoption Acts, 1952 to 1976;

"the Board" means An Bord Uchtála;

"the Registrar" means the Registrar of the Board.

###### 4.

4. Each of the forms set out in the Schedule shall be used in the case and manner indicated by the form.

###### 5.

5. Notwithstanding the revocation of the Adoption Rules, 1965, the forms prescribed in those Rules may continue to be used to the extent permitted by the Board.

###### 6.

6. The Board may require an applicant for an adoption order to submit medical evidence of the state of his health.

###### 7.

7. The Board may require the person or body that placed a child for adoption to submit medical evidence of the state of the child's health.

###### 8.

8. The Board may require any statement in a prescribed form to be verified by oral or other evidence.

###### 9.

9. The Registrar shall give to every applicant for an adoption order at least ten days' notice in writing or such shorter notice as may be accepted by the applicant of the date, hour and place of hearing of the application.

###### 10.

10. The Registrar shall give to every person whose consent to the making of an adoption order is required (other than such person as has indicated that he does not wish to be informed of the date on which the Board would hear him or his counsel or solicitor on the application for the adoption order) at least ten days' notice in writing of the date, hour and place at which the Board will hear him or his counsel or solicitor on the application for the order.
