# S.I. No. 350/1978 - Exchange Control (Scheduled Territories) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 350 of 1978.

EXCHANGE CONTROL (SCHEDULED TERRITORIES) REGULATIONS, 1978.

I, SEOIRSE Ó COLLA, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Exchange Control (Continuance and Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1978), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Exchange Control (Scheduled Territories) Regulations, 1978.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 18th day of December, 1978.

###### 2.

2. The definition of "the scheduled territories" in [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1954), is hereby amended by the deletion of paragraph (*b*).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of December, 1978.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the definition of the "scheduled territories" in [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Exchange Control Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) by the deletion of paragraph (*b*) from the definition. This has the effect of excluding Northern Ireland, Great Britain, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man from the "scheduled territories".
