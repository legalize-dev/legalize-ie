# S.I. No. 389/1986 - Conditions of Employment (Medical Health Care Products) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 389 of 1986.

CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT (MEDICAL HEALTH CARE PRODUCTS) (EXCLUSION) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, RUAIRI QUINN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functionss) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these Regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Medical Health Care Products) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely the manufacture of medical health care products, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purpose of [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provision shall have effect in respect of the work to which these Regulations relate, that is to say, no shift shall be longer than 8½ hours in duration.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of December, 1986.

**RUAIRI QUINN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) prohibits the employment of adult female workers between the hours of 10 p.m. on any day and 8 a.m. the following day. The effect of these Regulations is to permit the employment of adult females on shift work between the hours of 8 a.m. on any day and 8 a.m. on the following day on the manufacture of medical health care products.
