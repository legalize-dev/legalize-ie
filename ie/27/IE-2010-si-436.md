# S.I. No. 436/2010 - Companies (Forms) (No. 2) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th September*, 2010.

I, BATT O’KEEFFE, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) (3) and [226](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0226.html#sec226) (amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec3) (*f*) of the [Companies (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2009)) of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0185.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Companies (Forms) (No. 2) Regulations 2010.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of [section 226](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0226.html#sec226) (amended by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0045/print.html#sec3) (*f*) of the [Companies (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) (No. 45 of 2009)) of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990).

###### 3.

3. The Companies (Forms) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0101.html) ) are revoked.

SCHEDULE

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 September 2010.

**BATT O’ KEEFFE,**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Innovation.
