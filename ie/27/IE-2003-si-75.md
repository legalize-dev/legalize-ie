# S.I. No. 75/2003 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) (Amendment) (Credited Contributions) Regulations, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 4 (as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec12) of the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2002)) and 27 (as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1996)) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

**Citation and construction.**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) (Amendment) (Credited Contributions) Regulations, 2003.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) Regulations, 1996 to 2002 shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) Regulations, 1996 to 2003.

**Credited contributions.**

###### 2.

- (a) the substitution for sub-article (1) of article 63B (inserted by article 3 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) (Amendment) (Credited Contributions) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 76 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0076.html) )) of the following sub-article:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

***(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)***

These Regulations provide that an employment contribution will be credited to an insured person for each week during which he or she avails of carer's leave, under the terms of the [Carer's Leave Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) , maternity leave under the terms of the [Maternity Protection Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) or adoptive leave under the terms of the [Adoptive Leave Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , where he or she is not entitled to receive either Carer's Benefit Carer's Allowance, Maternity Benefit or Adoptive Benefit.

The Regulations also provide that employment contributions will be credited to insured persons who avail of additional unpaid adoptive leave under the terms of the [Adoptive Leave Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) . This is similar to the provisions which apply to additional unpaid maternity leave.
