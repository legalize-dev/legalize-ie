# S.I. No. 345/1984 - The Local Government Services (Establishment) (Orders) (Amendment) Order, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 345 of 1984.

THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT SERVICES (ESTABLISHMENT) (ORDERS) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1984.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Local Government Services (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1971) and section 4 of that Act as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government Services (Establishment) (Orders) (Amendment) Order, 1984.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 of the Local Government Staff Negotiations Board (Establishment) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0217.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (14) of the following sub-article:—

> - "(14) The [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956) and Schemes and Regulations made under the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) shall apply to the Board as if it were a local authority subject to the modification that the provisions of section 13(2) of the said Act of 1956, as amended by article 5(1) of the Local Government (Superannuation) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0032.html) ), shall apply in respect of the office of Chief Executive Officer of the Board as if that office were an office of city or county manager.".

###### 3.

3. Article 7 of the Local Government Computer Services Board (Establishment) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 212 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0212.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (15) of the following sub-article:—

> - "(15) The [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956) and Schemes and Regulations made under the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) shall apply to the Board as if it were a local authority.".

###### 4.

4. Article 13 of the Fire Prevention Council (Establishment) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 206 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0206.html) ) as amended by article 3 of the Fire Prevention Council (Establishment) (Amendment) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0246.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (3) of the following sub-article:—

> - "(3) The [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956) and Schemes and Regulations made under the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) shall apply to the Council as if it were a local authority.".

###### 5.

5. The Irish Water Safety Association (Establishment) Order, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 244 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0244.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution for article 16 of the following article:—

> "16. The [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956) and Schemes and Regulations made under the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) shall apply to the Association as if it were a local authority.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

18th day of December, 1984.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for the Public Service hereby consents to the Local Government Services (Establishment) (Orders) (Amendment) Order, 1984.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Public Service this

18th day of December, 1984.

**JOHN BOLAND,**

Minister for the Public Service.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the Local Government Services (Establishment) (Orders) (Amendment) Order, 1984 as respects its application to the Fire Prevention Council and the Irish Water Safety Association.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 18th day

of December, 1984.

Alan M. Dukes,

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order applies the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1956) and Schemes and Regulations made under the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) to the Local Government Staff Negotiations Board, the Local Government Computer services Board, the Fire Prevention Council and the Irish Water Safety Association as if they were local authorities.
