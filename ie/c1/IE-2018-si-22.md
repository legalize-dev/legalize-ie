# S.I. No. 22/2018 - Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th January*, 2018.

Regulation of Utilities

The Commission for Regulation of Utilities, having received and considered a request made to it by Ervia and, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Gas (Amendment) Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , hereby orders as follows:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2018.

*Interpretation.*

###### 2.

2. In this Order, “Principal Order” means Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 196 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0196.html) ) as amended by Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 47 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0047.html) ) and the Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2) (Distribution) (Amendment) Order 2015 ( [S.I. No 59 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0059.html) ).

*Commencement*

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into operation on 26 January 2018.

*Amendment of Principal Order*

###### 4.

4. (1) The Principal Order is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule to that Order:

SEALED with the common seal of the Commission for Regulation of Utilities on the

26 January 2018.

PAUL MCGOWAN,

Chairperson.

JOHN MELVIN,

Member of Staff of Commission.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order amends the Schedule of the Principal Order so as to bring the entirety of Kerry county and Longford county within Ervia’s remit in respect of the applicable regulations.
