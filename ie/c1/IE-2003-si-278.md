# S.I. No. 278/2003 - Abattoirs Act 1988 (Veterinary Examination) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/sec0039.html#sec39) and [63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/sec0063.html#sec63) of the [Abattoirs Act 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1988) hereby make the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Abattoirs Act 1988 (Veterinary Examination) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Abattoirs Act 1988 (Veterinary Examination) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 153 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0153.html) ) and the Abattoirs Act 1988 (Veterinary Examination) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0182.html) are revoked.
