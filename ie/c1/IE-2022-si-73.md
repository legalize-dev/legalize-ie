# S.I. No. 73/2022 - Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd February,* 2022.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage in exercise of powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec3) , [20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec20) and [32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec32) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2009), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on the 14^th March 2022.

*Substitution*

###### 3.

3. The Schedule to the Social Housing Assessment (Amendment) Regulations 2021 is substituted by the following Schedule:

(*Regulation 4*)

## SCHEDULE APPLICATION FORM FOR SOCIAL HOUSING SUPPORT

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 February, 2022.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations amend the Social Housing Assessment Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0116.html) ) to—

- • Provide for a revised Schedule containing an updated social housing support application form.

The Regulations come into force on the 14th March 2022.
