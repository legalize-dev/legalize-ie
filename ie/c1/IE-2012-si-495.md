# S.I. No. 495/2012 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Notification and Control of Animal Diseases) (Amendment) Order 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th December*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0012.html#sec12) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Notification and Control of Animal Diseases) (Amendment) Order 2012 and comes into operation on 1 January 2013.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Notification and Control of Animal Diseases) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0101.html) ) is amended in the Schedule, (inserted by the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Notification and Control of Animal Diseases) (Amendment) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 475 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0475.html) ) by the insertion after “Bovine Leucosis” of “Bovine Viral Diarrhoea”.

Given under my Official Seal

23 November 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order makes Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (BVD) a notifiable disease.
