# S.I. No. 390/2014 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th September*, 2014.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998);

“Order of 2012” means the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First Schedule) Order 2012 ( [S.I. No. 346 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0346.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Part II (as amended by Article 3 of the Order of 2012) of the First Schedule to the Act is amended—

- (*a*) by deleting:

  > “European Communities (Marketing of Fruit Juices and Certain Similar Products) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 240 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0240.html) ).”, and
  >
  > “European Communities (Control on Imports of Animal Products for Personal Consumption) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 267 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0267.html) ).”, and

- (*b*) by inserting after “European Communities (Equine) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 357 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0357.html) )” the following:

  > “European Communities (Import of Personal Consignments of Products of Animal Origin) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 374 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0374.html) ).”
