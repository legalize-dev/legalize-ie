# S.I. No. 688/2011 - Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th December*, 2011.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) and [21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Urban Areas) Regulations, 2011.

- (2) These Regulations and the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2010 may be cited together as the Derelict Sites Regulations, 1990 to 2011.

*Prescription of Urban Areas.*

###### 2.

2. The area in the administrative county of Laois indicated in Column 2 of the Schedule to these Regulations opposite the mention of the relevant administrative county in Column 1 of the said Schedule is hereby prescribed to be an urban area for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .

SCHEDULE

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Column 1Administrative County** | **Column 2 Urban Areas** |
| **Laois County Council** | The townland of Mountrath (Maryborough West By) in the electoral division of Mountrath |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government,

21 December 2011.

**PHIL HOGAN T.D.,**

Minister for the Environment Community and LocalGovernment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations prescribe an area in county Laois, as indicated, to be an urban area for the purposes of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) .
