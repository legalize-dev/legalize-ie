# S.I. No. 375/2000 - Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) (No. 2) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary O'Rourke, T.D., Minister for Public Enterprise, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/sec0013.html#sec13) (6) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998), with the consent of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with Aer Rianta cuideachta phoiblí theoranta, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) (No. 2) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The amount specified in [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/sec0013.html#sec13) (5) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) is varied by the substitution for “£350 million” (inserted by the Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Aggregate Borrowings Limit Variation) Order, 2000 ( [S.I. No. 287 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0287.html) )) of “£450 million”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of this Order.

**Explanatory Note**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).**

This Order provides for an increase, from £350 million to £450 million, of the aggregate amount of moneys borrowed at any one time by Aer Rianta cuideachta phoiblí theoranta as specified in [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/sec0013.html#sec13) (5) of the [Air Navigation and Transport (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1998).
