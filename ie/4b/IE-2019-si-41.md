# S.I. No. 41/2019 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (No. 1) (Earnings Disregard) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th February,* 2019.

I, REGINA DOHERTY, Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4 (adapted by the Social and Family Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0366.html) )), and by Rule 1(2)(b)(iv) of Part 4 of Schedule 3 to the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005) hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction*

1 (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (No.1) (Earnings Disregard) Regulations 2019.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 to 2018 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 to 2019.

*Commencement*

2 These Regulations shall have effect from 31 December 2018.

*Earnings disregard — supplementary welfare allowance.*

3 The Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the following article for article 33:

> ***“Earnings disregard — supplementary welfare allowance.***
>
> 33 In assessing the means of a person for the purposes of Rule 1(2)(b)(iv) of Part 4 of Schedule 3 to the Principal Act, the amount to be disregarded in respect of earnings received by that person from employment shall be the first €120.00 of such weekly earnings.”

GIVEN under the Official Seal,

12 February 2019

**REGINA DOHERTY,**

Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec20) of the [Social Welfare, Pensions and Civil Registration Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) removed the requirement that the earnings disregard applicable to an assessment of means for supplementary welfare allowance purposes needed to relate to earnings from employment of a rehabilitative nature.

These Regulations amend article 33 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007, which specify the level of the weekly earnings disregard to be applied, to remove the requirement that the disregard can only be applied to earnings from employment of a rehabilitative nature. The disregard currently amounts to €120 per week.
