# S.I. No. 422/1997 - Abattoirs Act, 1988 (Amendment) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 422 of 1997.

ABATTOIRS ACT, 1988 (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1997

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/sec0002.html#sec2) (3) of the [Abattoirs Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1988), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Abattoirs Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (Amendment) Order 1997.

###### 2.

2. The definition of "animal" in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Abattoirs Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1988) is hereby amended to include the "ratite" or running birds, ostriches, emus and rheas, so as to extend the application of this Act and its provisions to such animals.

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into effect on the 27th day of October 1997.

Given under my Official Seal this 8th day of October, 1997.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to extend the application of the [Abattoirs Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) and its provisions to include the ratite or running birds, ostriches, emus and rheas.
