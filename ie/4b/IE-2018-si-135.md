# S.I. No. 135/2018 - Animal Health and Welfare (Application of Act to disease) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th May*, 2018.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec7) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), hereby make the following order:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Animal Health and Welfare (Application of Act to disease) Order 2018.

*Stated Diseases*

###### 2.

2. All provisions of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013) shall apply to the following diseases to the extent they would if they were included in Schedule 2 of that Act-

- (*a*) Bovine genital campylobacteriosis,

- (*b*) Heartwater, and

- (*c*) Leishmaniasis.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 April 2018.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order applies the Animal Health and Welfare Act to the additional 3 diseases mentioned in the Order.
