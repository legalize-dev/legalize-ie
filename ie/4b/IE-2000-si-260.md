# S.I. No. 260/2000 - Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997 (Designation of Urban Renewal Areas) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 340 (as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0003/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Finance Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) , (No. 3 of 1998) and [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec44) of the [Finance Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 1999)) of the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), and after consultation with the Minister for the Environment and Local Government, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997 (Designation of Urban Renewal Areas) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “the Act” means the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997).

###### 3.

3. Each of the areas described in the provisions of the orders set out in Schedule 1 to this Order shall be a designated area for the purposes of Chapter 3 of Part 10 of the Act.

###### 4.

4. Each of the streets or parts of the streets described in the provisions of the orders set out in Schedule 2 to this Order shall be a designated street for the purposes of Chapter 3 of Part 10 of the Act.

###### 5.

5. Each of the designated areas described in the provisions of the orders set out in Schedule 3 to this Order shall be an enterprise area for the purposes of Chapter 3 of Part 10 of the Act.

###### 6.

6. The definition of “qualifying period” in section 339 of the Act shall, as respects the designated areas, designated streets and enterprise areas described in the provisions of the orders set out in Schedules 1, 2 and 3 to this Order, be construed as a reference to the period in paragraph (a) of that definition as set out in section 339(1) of the Act.

###### 7.

7. The following orders are hereby revoked:

- (a) the Finance Act, 1994 (Designation of Urban Renewal Areas) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 179 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0179.html) ), and

- (b) the Finance Act, 1994 (Designation of Urban Renewal Areas) Order, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 55 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0055.html) ).

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This Order extends the end dates for the application of certain incentives under the 1994 Urban Renewal Scheme to those dates curently specified in the [Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (as amended by the Finance Acts in subsequent years).

The incentives apply to certain buildings in Enterprise Areas, Designated Areas and Designated Streets.
