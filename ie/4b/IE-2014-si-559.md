# S.I. No. 559/2014 - Road Traffic (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th December*, 2014.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 103 (as amended by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec14) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ) and the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )) and after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality in relation to declaring offences (other than the offence mentioned in Regulation 4(*b*)(v) of the following regulations) to be fixed charge offences, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2014.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 8 December 2014.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Act of 2006” means Road Traffic Act 2006 (No. 23 of 2006);

“Principal Act” means Road Traffic Act 1961 (No. 24 of 1961);

“Regulations of 2006” means Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The offences mentioned in Regulation 4 are declared to be fixed charge offences.

###### 4.

4. The amount of a fixed charge is—

- (*a*) €80, in respect of an offence under—

    - (i) section 42 (inserted by section 10 of the Act of 2006) of the Principal Act, for a contravention of Regulation 17(6)(*b*)(iv) of the Regulations of 2006, and

    - (ii) section 55 (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec4) of the [Road Traffic (No. 2) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2011) of the Principal Act,

      and

- (*b*) €60, in respect of an offence under—

    - (i) section 11(5) of the Principle Act, for a contravention of sub-article (2) or (3) of Article 3 of the Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 224 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0224.html) ),

    - (ii) section 18(2) of the Principal Act,

    - (iii) section 42(6) (inserted by section 10 of the Act of 2006) of the Principal Act, for a contravention of Regulation 17(6)(*b*)(iii) (inserted by Regulation 2(*b*) of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Learner Drivers) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 719 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0719.html) )) of the Regulations of 2006,

    - (iv) [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0035.html#sec35) (5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994), for a contravention of Regulation 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 or 15 of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 332 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0332.html) ),

    - (v) Regulation 4(6) of the European Communities (Installation and Use of Speed Limitation Devices in Motor Vehicles) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 831 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0831.html) ), and

    - (vi) [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec4) (2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2014).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 December 2014.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This not is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations, operative from 8 December 2014, declare offences involving the driving or use of mechanically propelled vehicles to be fixed charge offences for the purposes of [section 103](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0103.html#sec103) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , as amended, and prescribe the amount of the fixed charge for each offence.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Description of Offence | Fixed Charge |
| Learner permit holder driving unaccompanied by qualified person | €80 |
| Parking a vehicle in a dangerous position | €80 |
| Using a vehicle in a public place without an authorisation plate | €60 |
| Using a vehicle in a public place that has been modified or altered such that the authorisation plate is inaccurate. | €60 |
| Using a vehicle without a test certificate | €60 |
| Failure to display an L-plate or tabard | €60 |
| Contravention of the rules for use of mini-roundabouts | €60 |
| Proceeding beyond no entry to vehicle sign | €60 |
| Proceeding beyond maximum vehicle length sign where vehicle length exceeds the maximum displayed | €60 |
| Proceeding beyond maximum width sign where vehicle width exceeds the maximum displayed | €60 |
| Proceeding beyond maximum design gross vehicle weight sign where vehicle design gross vehicle weight exceeds the maximum displayed | €60 |
| Proceeding beyond maximum vehicle axle loading weight sign where vehicle axle loading weight exceeds the maximum displayed | €60 |
| Proceeding beyond a traffic lane control sign other than in accordance with such sign or without yielding | €60 |
| Using vehicle not equipped with a speed limitation device or using a vehicle equipped with a speed limitation device not complying with requirements specified in Regulation 4. | €60 |
| Failure to display an N-plate or tabard | €60 |
