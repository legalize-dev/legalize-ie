# S.I. No. 130/1985 - Local Government (Planning and Development) (Exempted Development) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 130 of 1985.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (EXEMPTED DEVELOPMENT) REGULATIONS, 1985.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0004.html#sec4) and [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (*a*) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Exempted Development) Regulations, 1985.

( *b*) These Regulations and the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1984 may be cited together as the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1985.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 9th day of May 1985.

###### 3.

3. The Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0065.html) ) are hereby amended by the insertion in article 10 (4) of "and the excluded areas as defined in [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1985)" after "the Act of 1963".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

8th day of May, 1985.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations. 1977 to continue in effect certain exempted development provisions in those Regulations in the excluded areas as defined in [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
