# S.I. No. 121/1999 - Electoral Act, 1997 (Section 33) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Electoral Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0004/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Electoral (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1998), hereby makes the following order, a draft of which has been approved by a resolution passed by each House of the Oireachtas:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Electoral Act, 1997 (section 33) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The aggregate of election expenses which may be incurred by or on behalf of a candidate in a European Parliament election constituency in connection with his or her candidature at the European election shall not exceed £150,000.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order provides for the setting of a limit of £150,000 for the aggregate election expenses for a candidate in any European Parliament constituency.
