# S.I. No. 107/1971 - Health Boards (Functions of Chief Executive Officers) Order, 1971

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 107 of 1971.

HEALTH BOARDS (FUNCTIONS OF CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICERS) ORDER, 1971.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0006.html#sec6) (3) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Health Boards (Functions of Chief Executive Officers) Order, 1971.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st April, 1971.

###### 3.

3. Each reference to a health authority, specified in the third column of the Schedule in the enactments and statutory instruments mentioned in the second column, shall be construed as a reference to the chief executive officer of a health board.

**ERSKINE H. CHILDERS,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order vests certain functions on chief executive officers of health boards which before the 1st April, 1971 were vested in the managers of health authorities.
