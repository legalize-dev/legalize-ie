# S.I. No. 99/1981 - Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations 1981

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 99 of 1981.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (MAINTENANCE) REGULATIONS 1981.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and sub-section (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0091.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

" the Act" means the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html)

"outgoings" means the rent, ground rent, rates and purchase annuity payable in respect of premises and mortgage repayments relating to the purchase of premises;

"receiving in-patient services" means receiving in-patient services made available under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. of 1970 );

"person" means a person to whom section 44 of the Act applies by virtue of sub-section (1) of that section, and

"the payment" means the payment to be made under sub-section (3) of section 44 of the Act to or in respect of a person or his dependants.

###### 4.

4. The payment shall, with effect from the 1st day of April, 1981 be a weekly payment not exceeding:-

( *a* ) the sum specified in Part One of the Schedule hereto which is appropriate to the person, and

( *b* ) such of the increases specified in Part Two of the said Schedule as are appropriate.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of these Regulations is to amend the existing Regulations governing the payment of maintenance allowances to persons being treated for certain infectious, diseases—the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0091.html) ) — and to provide as from 1st April, 1981, for increases in the maximum rates of allowance payable.

The Infectious Diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are acute anterior poliomyelitis, diphtheria, dysentery, lassa fever, paratyphoid A, paratyphoid B, rabies, salmonella infection, scarlet fever, streptococcal sore throat, tuberculosis, and typhus fever.
