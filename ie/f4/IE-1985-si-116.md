> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 116 of 1985.

AQUACULTURE (ACHILL SOUND) ORDER, 1985.

I, PADDY O'TOOLE, Minister for Fisheries and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0054.html#sec54) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980), as amended by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), and by the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0195.html) )), having complied with the requirements of subsection 3 (*a*) of the said section 54 and having considered a report made to me under subsection (4) of the said section 54 and being of the opinion that, notwithstanding the existence of any public right to fish in the waters to which the following order relates, it is in the public interest that the order be made, hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Communications, order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aquaculture (Achill Sound) Order, 1985.

- (2) The area described in the Schedule hereto is hereby designated to be an area within which it shall be lawful to engage in aquaculture pursuant to and in accordance with a licence granted under [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0054.html#sec54) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980).

SCHEDULE.

All that area of Achill Sound and adjoining waters which is bordered by the high-water mark and which lies east of a line drawn due north from the most northerly point of the high-water mark at Ridge Point in the townland of Tonatanvally, barony of Burrishoole, County of Mayo, and terminating at the high-water mark of the townland of Roy, barony of Erris, County of Mayo, and which lies north of a line drawn due east from the most easterly point of the high-water mark at Darby's Point in the townland of Cloghmore, barony of Burrishoole, County of Mayo, and terminating at the high-water mark of the townland of Gubnahardia, barony of Burrishoole, County of Mayo.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of March, 1985.

**PADDY O'TOOLE,**

Minister for Fisheries and Forestry.

The Minister for Communications hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Communications,

this 2nd day of April, 1985.

**D. O'MAHONY,**

A person authorised under [Section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0015.html#sec15) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , to authenticate the Seal of the said Minister.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order designates a specified area of Achill Sound, Co. Mayo within which it shall be lawful to engage in aquaculture pursuant to a licence issued by the Minister for Fisheries and Forestry under [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/sec0054.html#sec54) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .
