# S.I. No. 293/1998 - Intellectual Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1998 (Patents Office Location Designation) Order, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 293 of 1998.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1998 (PATENTS OFFICE LOCATION DESIGNATION) ORDER, 1998

I, Tom Kitt, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 97(3A)(inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) (c) of the [Intellectual Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1998)) of the [Patents Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alternation of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 330 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0330.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Intellectual Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Patents Office Location Designation) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Patents Office shall be located at Government Buildings, Kilkenny, in the county of Kilkenny from the 31st day of August, 1998.

GIVEN under my hand, this 18th day of August, 1998.

TOM KITT

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order designates Kilkenny as the location of the Patents Office from 31st August, 1998.
