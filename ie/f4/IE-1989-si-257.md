# S.I. No. 257/1989 - Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 257 of 1989.

TELEPHONE (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) SCHEME, 1989.

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Section 90, [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Telephone (Amendment) (No. 2) Scheme, 1989.

- (2) The Telephone Schemes 1980 to 1989 and this Scheme may be cited together as the Telephone Schemes 1980 to 1989.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on 1 October, 1989.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. In this Scheme "the Scheme of 1988" means the Telephone (Amendment) Scheme 1988 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0256.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Telephone Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0195.html) ), are hereby amended by:

( *a* ) the substitution of the following Schedule for the Second Schedule thereto (inserted by paragraph 6 (*d*) of the Scheme of 1988):

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Scheme is to provide for a decrease of about 16% in telephone charges to Great Britain, the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands.
