# S.I. No. 346/1996 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 346 of 1996.

MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 1996.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and section 44 (sub-section 5) of of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following regulations:-

1 Citation

###### 1.

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1996.

2 Rate of Payment

###### 2.

**2.** Where a person is or becomes entitled to payment under sub-section (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 166 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0166.html) ), any amount so payable in respect of the week commencing on the 2nd December, 1996 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20.00 whichever is the greater.

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health

this 27th day November, 1996.

Michael Noonan

____________________

**MINISTER FOR HEALTH**

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance

this 27th day November, 1996.

Ruairi Quinn

_____________________

**MINISTER FOR FINANCE**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week commencing the 2nd December, 1996 the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20.00, whichever is the greater.
