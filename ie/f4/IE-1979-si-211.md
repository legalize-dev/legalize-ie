# S.I. No. 211/1979 - Intoxicating Liquor Act, 1962 (Section 9) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 211 of 1979.

INTOXICATING LIQUOR ACT, 1962 (SECTION 9) ORDER, 1979.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0021/sec0009.html#sec9) (2) of the [Intoxicating Liquor Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1962), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Intoxicating Liquor Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (Section 9) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of September, 1979.

###### 3.

3. The sum of £2.00 is hereby fixed for the purposes of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0021/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Intoxicating Liquor Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 14th day of June, 1979.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order provides that for the purposes of the Licensing Acts and the Registration of Clubs Acts the minimum guideline price for a substantial meal will be increased to £2 with effect from 1st September, 1979, in substitution for the 25p provided in the [Intoxicating Liquor Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) .
