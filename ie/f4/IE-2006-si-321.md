# S.I. No. 321/2006 - Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992 (Established Activities) (Amendment) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Dick Roche, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0007.html#sec7) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0082.html#sec82) (4) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), as amended by [sections 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec9) and [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Protection of the Environment Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2003) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992 (Established Activities) (Amendment) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. This Order comes into operation on 30 June 2006.

###### 3.

3. The Environmental Protection Agency Act 1992 (Established Activities) Order 2006 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0279.html) ) is amended by substituting for the Schedule the following Schedule:
