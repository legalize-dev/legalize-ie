# S.I. No. 227/2023 - Oireachtas (Allowances) (Members and Holders of Parliamentary and Certain Ministerial offices) (Amendment) Order 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th May,* 2023.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 3A (inserted by [section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec40) of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2001) (“Act of 2001”) and amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec2) of the [Ministers and Secretaries and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2020)), 4 and 8 (as amended by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) of the Act of 2001) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Members and Holders of Parliamentary and Certain Ministerial Offices) (Amendment) Order 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Oireachtas (Allowances) (Members and Holders of Parliamentary and Certain Ministerial Offices) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 613 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0613.html) ) is amended –

- (a) by the substitution of the following Article for Article 5:

  > “5. There is payable to –
  >
  > - (a) Hildegarde Naughton, Minister of State at –
  >
  >     - (i) the Department of the Taoiseach, and
  >
  >     - (ii) the Department of Health,
  >
  > - (b) Jack Chambers, Minister of State at –
  >
  >     - (i) the Department of Transport, and
  >
  >     - (ii) the Department of the Environment, Climate and Communications,
  >
  >       and
  >
  > - (c) Pippa Hackett, Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine,
  >
  >   for any period beginning on or after 17 December 2022, as the holders of those offices, respectively, who regularly attend meetings of the Government, an annual allowance at the rate of €12,173.”,

- (b) by the substitution of the following Article for Article 6:

  > “6. (1) There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of an Oireachtas Committee specified in Schedule 3 an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually –
  >
  > - (a) at the rate of €9,500 in respect of any period beginning on or after 12 May 2020,
  >
  > - (b) at the rate of €9,595 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2021,
  >
  > - (c) at the rate of €9,691 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 February 2022,
  >
  > - (d) at the rate of €9,982 in respect of any period beginning on or after 2 February 2022,
  >
  > - (e) at the rate of €10,082 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2022, and
  >
  > - (f) at the rate of €10,284 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 March 2023,
  >
  >   during which the member held that position.
  >
  > - (2) There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of the Oireachtas Committee known as the Committee on Members’ Interests of Seanad Éireann an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually –
  >
  >     - (a) at the rate of €3,100 in respect of any period beginning on or after 12 May 2020,
  >
  >     - (b) at the rate of €3,131 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2021,
  >
  >     - (c) at the rate of €3,162 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 February 2022,
  >
  >     - (d) at the rate of €3,257 in respect of any period beginning on or after 2 February 2022,
  >
  >     - (e) at the rate of €3,290 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2022, and
  >
  >     - (f) at the rate of €3,356 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 March 2023,
  >
  >       during which the member held that position.
  >
  > - (3) There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of the Oireachtas Committee known as the Joint Committee on Autism an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually –
  >
  >     - (a) at the rate of €9,982 in respect of any period beginning on or after 2 February 2022,
  >
  >     - (b) at the rate of €10,082 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2022, and
  >
  >     - (c) at the rate of €10,284 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 March 2023,
  >
  >       during which the member held that position.
  >
  > - (4) There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who held the position of chairperson of the Oireachtas Committee known as the Joint Committee on Gender Equality an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually –
  >
  >     - (a) at the rate of €9,595 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2021,
  >
  >     - (b) at the rate of €9,691 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 February 2022,
  >
  >     - (c) at the rate of €9,982 in respect of any period beginning on or after 2 February 2022, and
  >
  >     - (d) at the rate of €10,082 in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 October 2022,
  >
  >       during which the member held that position.
  >
  > - (5) There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who held the position of chairperson of the Oireachtas Committee known as the Joint Committee on International Surrogacy an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually at the rate of €9,982 in respect of any period beginning on or after 2 February 2022, during which the member held that position.”,

  and
