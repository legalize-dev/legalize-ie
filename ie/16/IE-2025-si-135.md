# S.I. No. 135/2025 - Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd April,* 2025.

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on the said Society by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec11) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Health, hereby makes the following rules: -

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2025.

- (2) The Principal Rules, the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2015 ( [S.I. No. 238 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0238.html) ), the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2016 ( [S.I. No. 311 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0311.html) ), the Rules of 2017, the Rules of 2020 and these Rules may be cited together as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 to 2025.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules—

“Principal Rules” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) );

“Rules of 2017” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2017 ( [S.I. No. 99 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0099.html) );

“Rules of 2020” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2020 ( [S.I. No. 213 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0213.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Rule 3(1) (as amended by Rule 3(a) of the Rules of 2020) of the Principal Rules is amended—

- (a) by substituting for the definition of “Act” the following:

  > “‘Act’ means the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007), as amended from time to time;”, and

- (b) by substituting for the definition of “Registration Rules” the following:

  > “‘Registration Rules’ means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Registration) Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 494 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0494.html) ), as amended from time to time;”.

###### 4.

4. The Schedule (as amended by Rule 4 of the Rules of 2017) to the Principal Rules is amended by substituting for paragraph 38 the following:

> *“Fees in respect of applications for recognition of third country applications*
>
> 38. Fees payable for application under s. 16(2)(b) of the Act, by reference to the application stages prescribed under Regulation 19(5) of the Registration Rules:
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> |  | Stage 1 | €500.00 |
> |  | Stage 2 |  |
> |  | *Initial Assessment:* | €1,000.00 |
> |  | *Appeal (if required):* | €300.00 |
> |  | Stage 3: | N/A |
> |  | Stage 4: | €3,000.00 |
> |  | Stage 5: | N/A”. |

I, JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL, Minister for Health, consent to the making of the foregoing Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Rules amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) ) to change the fees payable in connection with an application for recognition of a qualification as a pharmacist obtained in a third country, by reference to application stages prescribed under Regulation 19(5) of the Registration Rules.

These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2025.
