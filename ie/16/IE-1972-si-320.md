# S.I. No. 320/1972 - European Communities (Rules of Court) Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 320 of 1972.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (RULES OF COURT) REGULATIONS, 1972.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), hereby make the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Rules of Court) Regulations, 1972.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1973.

###### 3.

3. The powers conferred by [sections 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0036.html#sec36) , [66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0066.html#sec66) and [91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1924), in respect of rules of court shall include corresponding powers in respect of rules of court for enabling [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), to have full effect.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of December, 1972.

**DESMOND O'MALLEY,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations, which come into operation on 1st January, 1973, enable rules of court to be made for the purpose of giving full effect to [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) .
