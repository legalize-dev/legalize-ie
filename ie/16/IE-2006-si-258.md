# S.I. No. 258/2006 - European Communities (Safeguard Decisions) (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 258 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0258.html)

European Communities (Safeguard Decisions) (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2006

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), hereby make the following regulations: -

**Citation**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Safeguard Decisions) (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. The following are revoked: -

- (a) European Communities (Newcastle Disease) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from Australia) Regulations 2002. ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0351.html) ),

- (b) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from Chile) Regulations 2002. ( [S.I. No. 420 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0420.html) .).

- (c) European Communities (Newcastle Disease) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from Australia) (Amendment) Regulations 2002. ( [S.I. No. 560 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0560.html) ),

- (d) European Communities (Foot and Mouth Disease) (Control on Imports of Meat from Paraguay) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No 94 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0094.html) ).

- (e) European Communities (Newcastle Disease) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from the United States of America) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No 95 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0095.html) ),

- (f) Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Animal Products from Belgium) (No. 2) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 155 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0155.html) ),

- (g) Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Animal Products from Germany) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 221 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0221.html) ),

- (h) European Communities (Newcastle Disease)(Control on Imports of Avian Products from the U.S.A.)(Amendment) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 229 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0229.html) ),

- (i) European Communities (Newcastle Disease) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from Australia) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No 300 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0300.html) ),

- (j) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from the United States of America) Regulations 2004. ( [S.I. No 266 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0266.html) ),

- (k) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from Canada) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No 268 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0268.html) ),

- (l) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from South Africa) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No 509 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0509.html) ),

- (m) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products and Live Birds from certain Asian countries (Amendment) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No 536 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0536.html) ),

- (n) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products from South Africa) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No 538 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0538.html) ),

- (o) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products and Live Birds from certain Asian countries (Amendment) Regulations 2005 (S.I. 913 of 2004),

- (p) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports of Avian Products and Live Birds from Certain Asian Countries (Amendment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No 362 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0362.html) ),

- (q) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports from Russia) Regulations 2005. ( [S.I. No. 669 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0669.html) ),

- (r) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports from Russia) (Amendment) Regulations 2005. ( [S.I. No 690 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0690.html) ), and

- (s) European Communities (Avian Influenza) (Control on Imports from Russia)(Amendment) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I No. 81 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0081.html) ),

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order removes certain restrictions imposed as result of EU Commission Safeguard Decisions, which have lapsed.
