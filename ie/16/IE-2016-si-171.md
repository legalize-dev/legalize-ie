S.I. No. 171 of 2016

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (ACTIONS FOR POSSESSION, SALE AND WELL-CHARGING RELIEF) 2016

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Actions for Possession, Sale and Well-Charging Relief) 2016, shall come into operation on the 19th day of May 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2016.

- (3) The Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2015 as amended by these Rules may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. The Circuit Court Rules are amended by the substitution therein for the title of Order 5B of the following title:

> “Order 5B
>
> PROCEDURE IN CERTAIN ACTIONS FOR POSSESSION OR SALE OF LAND AND ACTIONS FOR WELL-CHARGING RELIEF”.

###### 3.

3. The Forms 2R and 54 in the Schedule shall be substituted for the forms bearing the like numbers respectively set out in the Schedule to the Circuit Court Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules amend the Circuit Court Rules by revising the forms of Civil Bill (Form 2R) and the verifying affidavit (Form 54) in the Schedule to the Circuit Court Rules. The Title to Order 5B has also been revised.
