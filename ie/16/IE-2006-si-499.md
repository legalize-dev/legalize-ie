# S.I. No. 499/2006 - Mental Health (Criminal Law) Review Board (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS

[S.I. No. 499 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0499.html)

**Mental Health (Criminal Law) Review Board (Establishment Day) Order 2006**

I, Michael McDowell, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0011/print.html#sec10) of the [Criminal Law (Insanity) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0011/print.html) (No. 11 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Mental Health (Criminal Law) Review Board (Establishment Day) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The 27th day of September, 2006 is appointed to be the establishment day.
