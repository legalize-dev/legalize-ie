# S.I. No. 197/1961 - Electricity (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1961 (Termination) Order, 1961

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 197 of 1961.

ELECTRICITY (TEMPORARY PROVISIONS) ACT, 1961 (TERMINATION) ORDER, 1961.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by subsection (2) of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0042/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Electricity (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) , hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electricity (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) (Termination) Order, 1961.

###### 2.

2. The [Electricity (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) , shall expire on the 13th day of September, 1961.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 12th day of September, 1961.

**SEÁN F. LEMASS,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order declares that the [Electricity (Temporary Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0042/index.html) , shall expire on the 13th September, 1961.
