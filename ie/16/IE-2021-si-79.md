# S.I. No. 79/2021 - Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) (Amendment) Rules 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th February,* 2021.

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on the said Society by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec11) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Health, hereby makes the following rules:-

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) (Amendment) Rules 2021.

- (2) The Principal Rules, Rule 14 of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Continuing Professional Development) Rules 2015 ( [S.I. No. 553 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0553.html) ), the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) (Amendment) Rules 2017 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0097.html) ), the Rules of 2020 and these Rules may be cited together as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) Rules 2014 to 2021.

###### 2.

2. In these Rules—

“Principal Rules” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 377 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0377.html) );

“Rules of 2020” means the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) (Amendment) Rules 2020 ( [S.I. No. 148 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0148.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Rule 14 (as amended by Rule 4 of the Rules of 2020) of the Principal Rules is amended by substituting for paragraph (1A) the following:

> - “(1A) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) and Rule 6(b), in the exceptional circumstances arising from the Covid-19 emergency, the in-service practical training requirement set out in paragraph (1) may be reduced, at the discretion of the recognised institution, to a requirement to complete 10 months of in-service practical training consisting of—
>
>     - (*a*) at least four months of the training referred to in paragraph (1)(a), and
>
>     - (*b*) not less than six months of the training referred to in paragraph (1)(b).”.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, consent to the making of the foregoing Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Rules continue in force the special provision allowing recognised institutions, in the exceptional circumstances arising from the Covid-19 emergency, to reduce the requirement that fifth year pharmacy students complete eight consecutive months of in-service practical training under the direct supervision of a tutor pharmacist, in a registered retail pharmacy business in the State or in the pharmacy department of a hospital in the State, to a requirement that they complete six months of such training.

These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) (Integrated Course) (Amendment) Rules 2021.
