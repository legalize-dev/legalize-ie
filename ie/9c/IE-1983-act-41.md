# APPROPRIATION ACT, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE TO THE PROPER SUPPLY SERVICES AND PURPOSES SUMS GRANTED BY THE CENTRAL FUND (PERMANENT PROVISIONS) ACT, 1965. [27*th December*, 1983].

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Appropriation of sums voted for supply services, viz., £5,682,294,575.

**1.**—(1) The sums granted by the Central Fund (Permanent Provisions) Act, 1965, out of the Central Fund towards making good the supply granted, amounting in the aggregate to the sum of five thousand, six hundred and eighty-two million, two hundred and ninety-four thousand, five hundred and seventy-five pounds for the service of the year ending on the 31st day of December, 1983, are appropriated for the services and purposes expressed in the *Schedule* annexed hereto.

- (2) In addition to the grants out of the Central Fund referred to in the foregoing subsection of this section, there may be applied out of any money directed under section 2 of the Public Accounts and Charges Act, 1891, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid of the grants for the services and purposes specified in the *Schedule* annexed hereto, the sums respectively set forth in column (4) of the said *Schedule* amounting, as appears thereby, in the aggregate to the sum of six hundred and thirty-four million, one hundred and twenty-four thousand, five hundred and eighty pounds.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 1983.
