# S.I. No. 132/2020 - Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th April,* 2020.

The Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (National Car Test) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 415 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0415.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended -

- (*a*) in Regulation 2, in the definition of “biennial” -

    - (i) by the substitution of “ ‘biennial’, in relation to a date, means the date” for “ ‘biennial’ means a date”, and

    - (ii) by the substitution of “that date” for “a test due date”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3, in paragraph (3) -

    - (i) by the substitution of “Subject to paragraph (3A), where,” for “Where,”, and

    - (ii) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (3):

      > - “(3A) Where Regulation 3A applies -
      >
      >     - (*a*) each reference in paragraph (3)(a) to “the tenth anniversary of first registration of the vehicle” shall be read as a reference to “the date that is 4 months after the tenth anniversary of first registration of the vehicle”,
      >
      >     - (*b*) each reference in paragraphs (3)(b)(i) and (ii) to “the tenth anniversary of first registration of the vehicle” shall be read as a reference to “the date that is 4 months after the tenth anniversary of first registration of the vehicle”, and
      >
      >     - (*c*) the reference in paragraph (3)(b)(iii) to “the thirtieth anniversary of first registration of the vehicle” shall be read as a reference to “the date that is 4 months after the thirtieth anniversary of first registration of the vehicle”,
      >
      >         - (*c*) by the insertion of the following Regulations after Regulation 3:
      >
      >           “3A. Notwithstanding Regulation 3 and subject to Regulation 3B, the test due date for a vehicle to which Regulation 3 applies is the date that is 4 months after each date that is determined in accordance with Regulation 3 to be a test due date for the vehicle.
      >
      >           3B. Regulation 3A applies in respect of a vehicle referred to in that Regulation where -
      >
      >     - (*a*) there was in force on 28 March 2020 a test certificate in respect of the vehicle, or
      >
      >     - (*b*) the first test due date for the vehicle, determined in accordance with Regulation 3, is a date during the period beginning on 28 March 2020 and ending on 31 July 2020.”,
      >
      >         - (*d*) in Regulation 11, by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (10):
      >
      > - “(11) Where a test certificate was, on 28 March 2020, in force in respect of a vehicle to which Regulation 3A applies, the expiry date of the certificate and of the test disc that forms part of that certificate shall be the date that is 4 months after the expiry date referred to in that certificate and that disc.”,
      >
      >   and
      >
      >     - (*e*) in Regulation 13(1), by the deletion of the words “to which it relates”.
      >
      >       GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport,
      >
      >       20 April, 2020.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (National Car Test) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 415 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0415.html) ) to provide that a 4 month extension is given to test due dates for vehicles with a test certificate in force on 28 March 2020, or to vehicles with a first test due falling during the period beginning on 28 March 2020 and ending on 31 July 2020.
