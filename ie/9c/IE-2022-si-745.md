# S.I. No. 745/2022 - Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013 (Application to Specified Diseases) Order 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th March,* 2023.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec7) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013(Application to specified diseases) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. The provisions of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) apply to each disease specified in the Schedule.

###### 3.

3. The [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (Application to specified diseases) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 516 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0516.html) ) is revoked.

## **Schedule**

1. *Batrachchytrium salamandrivorans* (BSa1).

2. Bovine besnoitiosis

3. Bovine genital campylobacteriosis.

4. Covid 19 (Coronavirus)

5. Foot pad dermatitis in poultry

6. Heartwater.

7. Leishmaniasis.

8. Tuberculosis in animals other than ruminants.

9. A disease that may be transmitted to an animal from a human or to a human from an animal, other than a zoonotic disease referred to in Schedule 2 to the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) , or otherwise referred to in this Schedule.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 August, 2022.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.
