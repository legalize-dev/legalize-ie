# S.I. No. 130/2008 - Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 130 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0130.html)

SAFETY, HEALTH AND WELFARE AT WORK (CONSTRUCTION) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th May*, 2008.

I, BILLY KELLEHER, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec58) of the [Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2005) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 561 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0561.html) ), and after consultation with the Health and Safety Authority, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. The Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 504 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0504.html) ) are amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (13) of Regulation 1:

  > “ (13) Regulations 19(1)(*b*), 25(1)(*b*), 29(1)(*g*), 74(*e*) and 97(*b*) and (*c*) shall—
  >
  > - (*a*) in respect of the tasks specified in clauses (*e*) and (*n*) of paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 4, apply from 6 May 2008, and
  >
  > - (*b*) in respect of the tasks specified in clauses (*c*), (*r*), (*s*) and (*t*) of that paragraph, apply from 6 July 2009.”, and

- (*b*) the substitution of the following clause for clause (*n*) of paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 4:

  > - “(*n*) Mini-digger operation — where the employee has not been trained in the task specified in clause (*o*);”.

GIVEN under my Hand,

2 May 2008

BILLY KELLEHER

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (Construction) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 504 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0504.html) ) as regards the operational date for Regulations 19(1)(*b*), 25(1)(*b*), 29(1)(*g*), 74(*e*) and 97(*b*) and (*c*), which is changed from 6 May 2008 to 6 July 2009, in respect of the possession of a Construction Skills Registration Card under the Construction Skills Certification Scheme, as it applies to the following tasks-

(i)Mobile tower scaffold (where the employee has not been trained in basic or advanced scaffolding),

(ii)Signing, lighting and guarding on roads,

(iii)Locating under-ground services, and

(iv)Shotfiring,

which are listed at paragraphs 1(1)(*c*), (*r*), (*s*) and (*t*) of Schedule 4 to the 2006 Regulations.

These Regulations also rectify a typographical error in paragraph 1(1)(*n*) of Schedule 4 to the 2006 Regulations.
