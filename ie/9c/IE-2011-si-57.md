# S.I. No. 57/2011 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Registration of Poultry Premises) (Amendment) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th February*, 2011.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Registration of Poultry Premises) (Amendment) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. The [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Registration of Poultry Premises) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 42 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0042.html) ) is amended—

- (*a*) in Article 2 by substituting for the definition of “premises”—

  > “ ‘premises’ includes land (with or without buildings), vehicle, ship or other vessel, aircraft, railway wagon or other means of transport or any container used in transport;”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for Article 4(1)—

  > “4.(1) The owner or person in charge of poultry shall maintain, on a daily basis, a record of—
  >
  > - (*a*) all mortalities that occur in the flock or on the premises,
  >
  > - (*b*) the daily production of eggs in the flock,
  >
  > - (*c*) the intake of food and water by the flock, and
  >
  > - (*d*) purchases, sales and supplies of eggs used for breeding purposes or poultry and copies of associated health certificates and commercial documents.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 February 2011

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order amends the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Registration of Poultry Premises) Order 2008 in relation to bio-security measures and the definition of premises.
