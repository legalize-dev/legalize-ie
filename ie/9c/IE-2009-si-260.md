# S.I. No. 260/2009 - Electoral (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 260 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0260.html)

ELECTORAL (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th July*, 2009.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 3, 46 and 111 of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1992), hereby makes the following Regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Electoral (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Electoral Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 156 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0156.html) ) are hereby amended by:—

- (*a*) the substitution of the following article for article 4:

  > “4. The prescribed photographic identification for the purposes of section 46(6)(*b*) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) shall be:
  >
  > - (i) a passport;
  >
  > - (ii) a driving licence;
  >
  > - (iii) an employee identity card containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (iv) a student identity card issued by an educational institution and containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (v) a bank card with a photograph;
  >
  > - (vi) a travel document containing name and photograph.”, and

- (*b*) the substitution of the following article for article 8:

  > “8. The specified documents for the purposes of section 111(2) of the [Electoral Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) shall be:
  >
  > - (i) a passport;
  >
  > - (ii) a driving licence;
  >
  > - (iii) an employee identity card containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (iv) a student identity card issued by an educational institution and containing a photograph;
  >
  > - (v) a travel document containing name and photograph;
  >
  > - (vi) a Bank or Savings or Credit Union book containing address in constituency or electoral area;
  >
  > - (vii) a cheque book;
  >
  > - (viii) a cheque card;
  >
  > - (ix) a credit card;
  >
  > - (x) a birth certificate;
  >
  > - (xi) a marriage certificate.
  >
  >   Items (vii) to (xi) shall be accompanied by a further document which establishes the address of the holder in the constituency or electoral area.”.

###### 3.

3. The Electoral (Amendment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 75 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0075.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 July 2009

**JOHN GORMLEY.**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*

These regulations amend the list of specified identification documents which a voter may be required to produce at a polling station when voting and the list of documents one of which a person, when assenting to the nomination of a non-party candidate, must present to the person witnessing the required statutory declaration.
