# S.I. No. 785/2007 - Infectious Diseases Maintenance Allowance (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 785 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0785.html)

INFECTIOUS DISEASES MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCE (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th December*, 2007.

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers vested in her by Section 5 and Section 44(5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order (S.I. 308 of 1997), hereby makes the following regulations—

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases Maintenance Allowance (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2007.

*Rate* *of* *Payment*

###### 2.

2. Where a person is or becomes entitled to payment under sub-section (3) of [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 2007 ( [S.I. No. 618 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0618.html) ), any amount so payable, in respect of the week commencing 3rd December, 2007 to or in respect of the person or his/her dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to 100 per cent of that amount or €30.00 whichever is the greater.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

30 November 2007

**BRIAN COWEN.**

Tánaiste and Minister for Finance.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health and Children,

30 November 2007

**MARY HARNEY.**

Minister for Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week commencing 3rd December, 2007, the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) by a sum equal to one hundred per cent of that amount or €30.00, whichever is the greater.
