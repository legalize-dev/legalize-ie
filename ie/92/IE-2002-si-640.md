# S.I. No. 640/2002 - National Cultural Institutions Act 1997 (Amendment To Second Schedule) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, T.D., Minister for Arts, Sport and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/sec0045.html#sec45) (1) of the [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1997), hereby order as follows:

1.This Order may be cited as the National Cultural Institutions Act 1997 (Amendment to Second Schedule) Order 2002.

2. The [National Cultural Institutions Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) is amended by substituting the following for the Second Schedule:

> “SECOND SCHEDULE
>
