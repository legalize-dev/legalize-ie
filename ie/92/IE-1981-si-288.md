# S.I. No. 288/1981 - Industry (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Industry (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1981.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 20th day of August 1981.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “functions” includes powers and duties.

###### 3.

3. (1) The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 4 of this Order are hereby transferred to the Department of Energy.

- (2) References to the Department of Industry, Commerce and Tourism contained in any Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any administration and business transferred by paragraph (1) of this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Department of Energy.

###### 4.

4. (1) (*a*) The functions vested in the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism by or under the statutes and regulations mentioned in the Schedule to this Order are hereby transferred to the Minister for Energy.

- (*b*) Subparagraph (*a*) of this paragraph does not apply in relation to the functions specified in [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0020/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Industrial Research and Standards Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1961), or transferred by the Shannon Free Airport Development Company Limited (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 225 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0225.html) ).

- (2) Any functions in relation to industry vested in the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0001.html#sec1) (vii) of the [Minister and Secretaries Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1924), and not transferred by paragraph 1 of this Article, are hereby transferred to the Minister for Energy.

- (3) References to the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism contained in any Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any functions transferred by this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Energy.

###### 5.

5. Where, immediately before the commencement of this Order, any legal proceedings are pending to which the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism is a party and the proceedings have reference to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Energy, the name of the Minister for Energy shall be substituted in the proceedings for that of the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism and the proceedings shall not abate by reason of such substitution.

###### 6.

6. Anything commenced before the commencement of this Order by or under authority of the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism may, so far as it relates to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for Energy, be carried on or completed on or after such commencement by the Minister for Energy.

###### 7.

7. Every document (including any certificate or licence) granted or made in the exercise of a function transferred by this Order shall, if and in so far as it was operative immediately before the commencement of this Order, have effect on and after such commencement as if it had been granted or made by the Minister for Energy.

###### 8.

8. References to the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism contained in the memorandum and articles of association of any company and relating to any function transferred by this Order shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for Energy.

###### 9.

9. (1) All property, rights and liabilities held, enjoyed or incurred before the commencement of this Order by the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism in connection with the functions transferred by this Order shall be and are

hereby transferred to the Minister for Energy and, accordingly, without any further conveyance, transfer or assignment—

- (*a*) the said property, real and personal, shall, on such commencement, vest in the Minister for Energy,

- (*b*) the said rights shall, as on and from such commencement, be enjoyed by the Minister for Energy,

- (*c*) the said liabilities shall, as on and from such commencement, be the liabilities of the Minister for Energy.

- (2) All moneys, stocks, shares and securities transferred to the Minister for Energy by this Article which, on such commencement, are standing in the name of the Minister for Industry, Commerce and Tourism shall upon the request of the Minister for Energy be transferred into his name.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order transfers to the Minister for Energy and to the Department of Energy, respectively, with effect from the operative date of this Order, Ministerial functions and Departmental administration in relation to industry.
