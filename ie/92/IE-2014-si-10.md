# S.I. No. 10/2014 - Agriculture Appeals Act 2001 (Amendment of Schedule) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st January*, 2014.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec5) (2) of the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2001) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Agriculture Appeals Act 2001 (Amendment of Schedule) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (as amended by the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (Amendment of Schedule) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 106 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0106.html) )) is amended by substituting the following—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations have the purpose of updating the Schedule of Schemes which may be referred to the Agriculture Appeals Office for appeal.
