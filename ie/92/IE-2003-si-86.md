# S.I. No. 86/2003 - European Communities (Licensing and Supervision of Credit Institutions) (Amendment) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 89/646/EEC[^1] of 15 December 1989, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Licensing and Supervision of Credit Institutions) (Amendment) Regulations 2003.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 14(13) of the European Communities (Licensing and Supervision of Credit Institutions) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0395.html) ) is amended in subparagraph (a by substituting “section 38” for “section 28”.

|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, |
|  | 5 March, 2003. |
|  |  |
|  | Charlie McCreevy, T.D. |
|  | MINISTER FOR FINANCE |

**Explanatory Note**

**(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)**

The amendment at Regulation 2 is required due to an error made in the original Regulations. The correct reference in Regulation 14(13)(a) of the 1992 Regulations should have been “section 38”, as [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Central Bank Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) is the section which confers powers on the Central Bank of Ireland to issue directions to credit institutions.
