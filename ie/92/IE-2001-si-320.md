# S.I. No. 320/2001 - Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) and [78](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0078.html#sec78) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1960), as substituted by [section 86](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0086.html#sec86) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) and as further amended by [section 214](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec214) of the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“the Regulations of 2000” means the Housing Act, 1966 (Acquisition of Land) Regulations, 2000 ( [No. 454 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0454.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Form No. 4 in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby substituted for Form No. 4 in the Schedule to the Regulations of 2000.

###### 4.

4. Form No. 9 set out in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby substituted for Form No. 9 in the Schedule to the Regulations of 2000.
