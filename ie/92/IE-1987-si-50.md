# S.I. No. 50/1987 - Sea Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation) (Amendment) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 50 of 1987.

SEA FISHERIES (CONSERVATION AND RATIONAL EXPLOITATION) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1987.

I, PADDY O`TOOLE, Minister for Tourism, Fisheries and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 223A (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1978), and amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1983)), of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Fisheries and Forestry (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 40 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0040.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Sea Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation) (Amendment) Order, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The Sea Fisheries (Conservation and Rational Exploitation) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0002.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Article 2 of the following Article:

> "2. In this Order "the Council Regulation" means Council Regulation (E.E.C.) No. 3094/86 of 7 October, 1986^1, as amended by Council Regulation (E.E.C.) No. 4026/86 of 18 December, 1986^2, (*aa*) and by any regulation of the Council made after the making of this Order which amends the said Council Regulation (E.E.C.) No. 3094/86."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of February, 1987.

**PADDY O`TOOLE,**

Minister for Tourism, Fisheries and

Forestry.

^1O.J. L288/1 of 11.10.1986.

^2O.J. L376/1 of 31.12.1986.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to prescribe and adopt Council Regulation (EEC) 4026/86 of 18 December, 1986 which, in so far as Ireland is concerned, permits the continued use of nets with a minimum mesh size of 80mm south of the parallel of 56N.
