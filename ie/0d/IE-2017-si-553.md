# S.I. No. 553/2017 - Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th December*, 2017.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 59 (amended by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec1) of the [Health (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2010)) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), being of the opinion that a further variation of the amounts referred to in subsections (1A) and (1B)(a) of the said section 59 is desirable, having formed such opinion in accordance with subsection (4)(b) of the said section 59, and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2018.

###### 3.

3. The amount referred to in [Section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0059.html#sec59) (1A) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No.1 of 1970) is varied to €2.00.

###### 4.

4. The amount referred to in [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0059.html#sec59) (1B)(a) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) is varied to €20.00.

###### 5.

5. The Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0437.html) ) are revoked.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 November 2017.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 December 2017.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide for a reduction in the prescription charge from €2.50 to €2.00 per item, and for a reduction in the monthly cap from €25.00 to €20.00, per person or family.

These Regulations revoke Health Services (Prescription Charges) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0437.html) ).
