# S.I. No. 293/1983 - Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933 (Fees) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 293 of 1983.

PERPETUAL FUNDS (REGISTRATION) ACT, 1933 (FEES) ORDER, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1933), and the Industry, Commerce and Tourism (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0290.html) ) hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 17th day of October, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The fees to be paid to the Registrar of Friendly Societies pursuant to [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/sec0015.html#sec15) (2) of the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1933) shall be the fees specified in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 5.

5. The [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Fees) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 85 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0085.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of September, 1983.

**FRANK CLUSKEY,**

Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

**ALAN DUKES,**

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance,

this 30th day of September, 1983.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted under the [Perpetual Funds (Registration) Act, 1933](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1933/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
