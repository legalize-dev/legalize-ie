# S.I. No. 380/1988 - Valuation (Revisions and New Valuations) (Fees) Regulation, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 380 of 1988.

VALUATION (REVISIONS AND NEW VALUATIONS) (FEES) REGULATION, 1988.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988) hereby make the following Regulation:

###### 1.

1. This Regulation may be cited as the Valuatior (Revisions and New Valuation) (Fees) Regulation, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The fee to be charged in respect of an application by an owner or occupier of any property or by a rating authority under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , shall be £20.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 30th day of December, 1988.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Regulation sets a fee of £20 for any application made to have the rateable valuation of a property revised or fixed for the first time.
