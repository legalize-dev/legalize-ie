# S.I. No. 93/1996 - District Court [Local Government (Delimitation of Water Supply Disconnection Powers) Act, 1995] Rules, 1995

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 93 of 1996.

DISTRICT COURT [LOCAL GOVERNMENT (DELIMITATION OF WATER SUPPLY DISCONNECTION POWERS) ACT, 1995] RULES, 1995.

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and section 34 of the Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act. 1961, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, makes the following Rules of Court:

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the District Court [ [Local Government (Delimitation of Water Supply Disconnection Powers) Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) ] Rules, 1995.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on the 19th day of April, 1996.

###### 3.

3. In these Rules—

"the Act of 1962" means the [Local Government (Sanitary Services) Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) ;

"authorised person" means a person who is appointed in writing by a Sanitary Authority to be an authorised person for the purposes of the [Local Government (Delimitation of Water Supply Disconnection Powers) Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) .

"consumer" means the occupier of premises for which a supply of water for domestic purposes is provided or, where the premises are either unoccupied or are not owned by a local authority and comprise more than one dwelling, the owner of the premises;

"the Court" means the District Court;

"section 65A of the Act of 1878" means the section inserted by section 7 of the Act of 1962 into the Public Health (Ireland) Act, 1878 and amended by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) (No. 2) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) ;

"water discontinuance order" means an order under section 3 of the Local Goverment (Delimitation of Water Supply Disconnection Powers) Act, 1995.

###### 4.

4. An application by the sanitary authority for an order under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0018/sec0003.html#sec3) (2) of the [Local Government (Delimitation of Water Supply Disconnection Powers) Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) may be made at any sitting of the Court for the court area wherein the supply of water for domestic purposes is provided to the consumer. Notice of such application shall be in the Form 1 in the schedule hereto and shall be served on the respondent by prepaid registered post at least twenty one days prior to the date fixed for hearing the application.

When service has been effected, the original notice together with a statutory declaration as to service thereof, shall be lodged with the District Court Clerk at least seven days prior to the said date for hearing.

###### 5.

5. The order of the Court granting the application shall be in the Form 2 in Schedule hereto.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

These Rules prescribe the procedure to be followed and the forms to be used by a Sanitary Authority in making applications to the Court for an Order to discontinue a supply of water for domestic purposes under [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0018/sec0003.html#sec3) (2) of the [Local Government (Delimitation of Water Supply Disconnection Powers) Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) .
