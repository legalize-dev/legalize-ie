# S.I. No. 737/2005 - Companies (Fees) (No. 4) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 737 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0737.html)**

**Companies (Fees) (No. 4) Order 2005**

I, Michael Ahern, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 395 (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) and 396 of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 808 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0808.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) (No. 4) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 1 December 2005.

###### 3.

3. In this Order “Eighth Schedule” means the Eighth Schedule (substituted by the Companies (Fees) (No.3) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 517 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0517.html) )) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

###### 4.

4. Part I of the Eighth Schedule is altered by adding the following to the Table of Fees therein:

> “
>
> |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> |  | For registration of a notification to the registrar pursuant to section 57(4)(a) of the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) that the company has authorised an electronic filing agent | €15 | Nil |
> |  | For registration of a notification to the registrar pursuant to section 58(2) of the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) that the company has revoked the authorisation of its electronic filing agent | €15 | Nil |
>
> .”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order lays down the fee to be paid in respect of the filing of Form B77 (notification to the registrar of companies of the appointment/revocation of appointment of an electronic filing agent by a company), and makes separate provision for the fee payable in respect of a paper notification and the fee payable in respect of an electronic notification.
