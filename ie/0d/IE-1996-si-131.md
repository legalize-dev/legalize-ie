# S.I. No. 131/1996 - Consumer Credit Act, 1995 (Section 86) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 131 of 1996.

CONSUMER CREDIT ACT, 1995 (SECTION 86) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 86](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0086.html#sec86) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 86) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. [Section 84](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0084.html#sec84) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), is hereby amended by the substitution in—

( *a* ) subsection (1) (*a*) (ii) of "hirer" for "buyer",

( *b* ) subsection (1) (*b*) (ii) of "owner" for "seller", and

( *c* ) subsection (5) (*a*) of "owner" for "seller".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of May, 1996.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations correct an error in terminology in [section 84](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0084.html#sec84) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
