# S.I. No. 29/1982 - Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979 (Section 3) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 29 of 1982.

MILK (MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 1979 (SECTION 3) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 3) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1982.

###### 3.

3. The rate of fee specified in [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1979), is hereby altered by the substitution of "three-twentieths" for "one-tenth".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 25th day of February, 1982.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations increase with effect from 1 March 1982, the rate of fee payable under [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Milk (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
