# S.I. No. 551/2007 - Public Service Management (Recruitment and Appointments) Act 2004 (Extension of Application to Health Information and Quality Authority) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 551 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0551.html)

PUBLIC SERVICE MANAGEMENT (RECRUITMENT AND APPOINTMENTS) ACT 2004 (EXTENSION OF APPLICATION TO HEALTH INFORMATION AND QUALITY AUTHORITY) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st July*, 2007.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1) of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec6) of the [Public Service Management (Recruitment and Appointments) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2004) and, for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of that subsection, after consulting with the Commission for Public Service Appointments and the Minister for Health and Children, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Public Service Management (Recruitment and Appointments) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (Extension of Application to Health Information and Quality Authority) Order 2007.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on the 15th day of August 2007.

###### 2.

2. To the extent that the recruitment and appointment provisions of the [Public Service Management (Recruitment and Appointments) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2004) do not apply in respect of the Health Information and Quality Authority, that Act applies in respect of positions within that Authority.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 July 2007

**BRIAN COWEN.**

Minister for Finance.
