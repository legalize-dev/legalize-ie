# S.I. No. 187/1977 - Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925), hereby, with the approval of the Government, make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Notwithstanding the provisions of the Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) Regulations, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 64 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0064.html) ), as amended by the Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0118.html) )—

( *a* ) the members of the Representative Bodies that are provided for by Regulation 3 of those Regulations, as so amended, and that hold office on the commencement of these Regulations shall continue to hold office until the 31st day of December, 1977, or until the election of new members of each of those Representative Bodies has been completed, whichever is the earlier, and

( *b* ) the elections in the year 1977 under those Regulations, as amended, of members of each of those Representative Bodies shall be held as soon as may be practicable after the commencement of these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Garda Síochána (Representative Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 94 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0094.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of June, 1977.

**PATRICK COONEY,**

Minister for Justice.

The Government hereby approve of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 24th day of June, 1977.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide that the members of the Representative Bodies shall continue to hold office up to the 31st December, 1977 (instead of 30th June, 1977) or until the election of new Bodies whichever is the earlier and that elections in the year 1977 shall be held as soon as may be practicable.
