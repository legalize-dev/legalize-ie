# S.I. No. 207/1986 - Canals Act, 1986 (Vesting Day) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 207 of 1986.

CANALS ACT, 1986 (VESTING DAY) ORDER, 1986.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0003/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Canals Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1986), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Canals Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (Vesting Day) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The first day of July, 1986, is hereby appointed to be the vesting day for the purposes of the [Canals Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 5th day of June, 1986.

**JIM MITCHELL,**

Minister for Communications.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints 1 July 1986 as the vesting day, under the [Canals Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) , for the transfer of the Royal Canal and the Grand Canal (including the Barrow Navigation) and associated property, etc., from Córas Iompair Éireann to the Commissioners of Public Works in Ireland.
