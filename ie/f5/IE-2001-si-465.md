# S.I. No. 465/2001 - Mackerel (Prohibition on Fishing) (Revocation) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Frank Fahey, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 223A (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1978), and amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1983)) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) )) (as adapted by the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0301.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Mackerel (Prohibition on Fishing) (Revocation) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 19th day of October, 2001.

###### 3.

3. The Mackerel (Prohibition on Fishing) Order, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 159 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0159.html) ) is hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

The effect of this Order is to permit the fishing for, transhipment, landing or having on board mackerel by Irish sea-fishing boats, duly licensed, of length greater or equal to 65 feet in the polyvalent segment in ICES sub-areas and divisions IIa,IVa, Vb, VI, VII, VIIIa,b,d,e and XII and XIV with effect from 19th day of October, 2001.
