# S.I. No. 392/2001 - Garda Síochána (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, T.D., Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Police Forces Amalgamation Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1925) (as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) )), and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Promotion) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 9th August, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The Garda Síochána (Promotion) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 39 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0039.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution of the following Regulation for Regulation 6:

> “6. (1) The selection of members for promotion to the rank of assistant commissioner shall be made in accordance with this Regulation.
>
> - (2) The Commissioner shall, not later than 15 days before the holding of a competition under this Regulation, provide each member (who, upon the date on which it is proposed that the competition will commence, will have served not less than 2 years at the rank of Chief Superintendent) with a description in writing of the role of assistant commissioner and a statement in writing of the competencies required for the carrying out of the duties of assistant commissioner.
>
> - (3) A member to whom paragraph (2) relates may apply for promotion to the rank of assistant commissioner; and a member who so applies is in this Regulation referred to as an ‘applicant’.
>
> - (4) A competition for promotion to the rank of assistant commissioner shall consist of an interview or such number of interviews, as the interview board concerned considers appropriate, of each applicant.
>
> - (5) An interview board referred to in paragraph (4) shall comprise -
>
>     - (a) the Commissioner,
>
>     - (b) a deputy commissioner,
>
>     - (c) a person nominated by the body commonly known as the Top Level Appointments Committee, and
>
>     - (d) a person nominated by the body commonly known as the Civil Service and Local Appointments Commissioners.
>
> - (6) An application for a promotion to which this Regulation applies shall be in such form as the Commissioner may determine.
>
> - (7) Each applicant shall be provided with copies of all reports, assessments, evaluations, notes and other records relating to him or her that -
>
>     - (a) have been created, or
>
>     - (b) it is intended will be referred to,
>
>       for the purposes of a competition under this Regulation.”

The Government hereby approve the making of these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These Regulations provide for changes in the selection procedures for promotion to Assistant Commissioner.
