# S.I. No. 152/1985 - Imposition of Duties (No. 278) (Stamp Duty on Letters of Renunciation) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 152 of 1985.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (NO. 278) (STAMP DUTY ON LETTERS OF RENUNCIATION) ORDER, 1985.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 278) (Stamp Duty on Letters of Renunciation) Order, 1985.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 11 day of June 1985, and shall not have effect in relation to any instrument executed before such coming into operation.

###### 3.

3. (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0015/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Finance Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1964), any instrument which releases or renounces or has the effect of releasing or renouncing a right under a letter of allotment, or under any other document having the effect of a letter of allotment, to any share in an unquoted company shall be chargeable to stamp duty as if it were a release or renunciation of property consisting of stocks or marketable securities by reference to the Heading "RELEASE or RENUNCIATION of any property, or of any right or interest in any property" in the First Schedule (inserted by the [Finance Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1970)) to the Stamp Act, 1891, and that Schedule shall be construed accordingly.

- (2) In this Article—

"share" includes stock;

"unquoted company" means a company none of whose shares, stocks or debentures are listed in the official list of a recognised stock exchange or dealt in on an unlisted securities market recognised by such a stock exchange.

###### 4.

4. This Order shall be construed together with the Stamp Act, 1891, and the enactment amending or extending that Act.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 28 day of May, 1985.

**GARRET FITZGERALD,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the charging to stamp duty of a renunciation of a right under a letter of allotment, to stocks or shares which are not quoted on the Stock Exchange. Such renunciations are charged at the rate applicable to the transfer of securities.
