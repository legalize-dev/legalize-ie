# S.I. No. 507/2001 - Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991 (Section 4) (Hague Convention) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Brian Cowen, T.D., Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred upon me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1991), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991 (Section 4) (Hague Convention) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared -

- (a) that the states specified in the First Schedule to this Order are Contracting States for the purposes of Part II of the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) ;

- (b) that declarations (the texts of which in the English language are set out in Part I of the Second Schedule hereto) have been made to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands pursuant to Articles 39 and 40 of the Convention; and

- (c) that reservations (the texts of which in the English language are set out in Part II of the Second Schedule hereto) have been made to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands pursuant to Articles 24 and 26 of the Convention.

###### 3.

3. The Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991 (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No 123 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0123.html) ), the Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991 (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) (Amendment) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No 476 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0476.html) ), and the Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991 (Contracting States) (Hague Convention) (Section 4(1)(b)) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No 478 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0478.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. This Order shall come into operation on the 12^th day of November 2001.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) enables the Minister for Foreign Affairs to declare which states are Contracting States to the Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction, done at The Hague on the 25^th day of October 1980 and that a declaration or reservation has been made to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands. This Order specifies which States are Contracting States and sets out the texts of the declarations and reservations which have been received by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
