# S.I. No. 599/2007 - European Communities (Transnational Information and Consultation of Employees Act 1996) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 599 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0599.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (TRANSNATIONAL INFORMATION AND CONSULTATION OF EMPLOYEES ACT 1996) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th September*, 2007.

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive 2006/109/EC of 20 November 2006 [^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Transnational Information and Consultation of Employees Act 1996) (Amendment) Regulations 2007.

###### 2.

2. Section 10(4) of the [Transnational Information and Consultation of Employees Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) is amended by substituting the following for paragraph (*a*):

> - “(*a*) subject to paragraphs (*b*) and (*c*), the Body shall have not less than 3 members nor more than such number of members as equals the number of Member States for the time being, elected or appointed in accordance with this Act;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 August 2007

**MICHEÁL MARTIN**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

The purpose of these Regulations is to transpose into Irish law Council Directive 2006/109/EC of 20 November 2006 adapting Directive 94/45/EC on the establishment of a European Works Council or a procedure in Community-scale undertakings or Community-scale groups of undertakings for the purposes of informing and consulting employees, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania to the European Community.
