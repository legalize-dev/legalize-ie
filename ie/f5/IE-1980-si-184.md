# S.I. No. 184/1980 - Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act 1977 (Appointed Day) (Córas Iompair Éireann) Order, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) ( I ) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Appointed Day) (Córas Iompair Éireann) Order, 1980.

###### 2.

2. The 18th day of June, 1980, is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1977), in relation to Córas Iompair Éireann.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 18th day of June, 1980.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order prescribes the appointed day for determining election years for the purposes of [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Worker Participation (State Enterprises) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , in relation to Córas Iompair Éireann.
