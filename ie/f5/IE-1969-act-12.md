# ELECTRICITY (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) (REPEAL) ACT, 1969

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO REPEAL THE ELECTRICITY (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, 1966. [27*th May*, 1969.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Repeal.

**1.**—The Electricity (Special Provisions) Act, 1966, is hereby repealed.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Electricity (Special Provisions) (Repeal) Act, 1969.
