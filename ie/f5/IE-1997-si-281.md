S.I. No. 281 of 1997.

NATIONAL ARCHIVES ACT, 1986 (PRESCRIPTION OF CLASSES OF RECORDS) ORDER, 1997

I, JOHN BRUTON, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/sec0008.html#sec8) (II) of the [National Archives Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1986), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Archives Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Prescription of Classes of Records) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The classes of records referred to—

( *a* ) in the First Schedule to this Order found in all Departments of State, and

( *b* ) in column (2) of the Second Schedule to this Order found in a particular Department of State referred to in column (1) of that Schedule opposite the mention of a class of records,

are hereby prescribed as classes of records in relation to which a certificate granted under subsection (2) or (4) of [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [National Archives Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1986), may relate.

###### 3.

3. The [National Archives Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Prescription of Classes of Records) Order, 1991, is hereby revoked.

FIRST SCHEDULE

Classes of records found in all Departments of State (including the courts and scheduled bodies).

1. Personnel case files and related records concerning individual civil servants and public servants (including members of the Defence Forces and the Garda Síochána and employees of local authorities, state sponsored bodies and other public service organisations) recording matters such as their appointment, employment, performance, attendance, leave, salary or wages, promotion or re-grading, and retirement, resignation, dismissal or death.

2. Pension case files and related records concerning pensions payable to individual civil servants and public servants (including members of the Defence Forces and the Garda Síochána and employees of local authorities, state sponsored bodies and other public service organisations) and their spouses and children.

3. Accounting records concerning payments of salaries, wages, allowances, expenses, pensions etc. to individual civil servants and public servants (including members of the Defence Forces and the Garda Síochána and employees of local authorities, state sponsored bodies and other public service organisations) and their spouses or children.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of orders made under [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/sec0008.html#sec8) (11) of the [National Archives Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) , is to allow certificates made under sections 8(2) or 8(4) of the Act to refer to entire classes of records without giving details of individual records. Certificates made under section 8(2) or 8(4) of the Act are required if Departmental records which are more than 30 years old are to be retained by Departments and/or are to be withheld from public inspection. Where a class of records is not prescribed by an order made under section 8(11), details of each record which is to be retained or withheld must be given in the relevant certificate made under section 8(2) or 8(4).

This order is the second order made under section 8(11). It revokes the first order which was made in 1991.

The following class which was prescribed by the 1991 order is not prescribed by this order:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Department of the Taoiseach | Rúnaí Aire files |

In the case of the Central Statistics Office, all records of that office were prescribed as a class of records by the 1991 order. Only those records which contain information obtained in any way under the Statistics Acts, 1926, 1946 and 1993 which can be related to an identifiable person or undertaking are prescribed as a class of records by this order.

All other classes of records prescribed in the 1991 order are also prescribed by this order. No additional classes have been prescribed, by this order.
