# S.I. No. 271/1981 - Imposition of Duties (No. 252) (Stamp Duty on Bills of Exchange and Promissory Notes) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 252) (Stamp Duty on Bills of Exchange and Promissory Notes) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 16th day of August, 1981.

###### 3.

3. [Section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/sec0041.html#sec41) of the [Finance Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1970), is hereby amended by the substitution of “5p” for “3p” in subsections (1) and (3) (inserted in each case by the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1981)).

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the charging of stamp duty at fivepence on bills of exchange and promissory notes drawn in the State including cheques.

This Order comes into operation on 16 August, 1981.
