# S.I. No. 11/1988 - Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 11 of 1988.

MISUSE OF DRUGS (LICENCE FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) and [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

###### 2.

2. Sub-article (1) of article 4 of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0164.html) ), as amended by the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 172 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0172.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-paragraph (*a*) of "£150" for "£105", in sub-paragraph (*b*) of "£100" for "£70", in sub-paragraph (*c*) of "£100" for "£70", in sub-paragraph (*d*) of "£50" for "£35", in sub-paragraph (*e*) of "£25" for "£18", and in sub-paragraph (*f*) of "£50" for "£35".

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1988.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

Health this 27th day of January, 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

Finance this 27th day of January, 1988

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for increases in fees charged for licences granted under the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977).
