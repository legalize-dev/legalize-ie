# S.I. No. 80/2026 - European Union (Markets in Financial Instruments) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th March,* 2026.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and Council of 15 May 2014[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

## **PART 1** **Preliminary and General**

**Citation and commencement**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Markets in Financial Instruments) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 31 August 2026.

**Definition**

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Markets in Financial Instruments) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 375 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0375.html) ).

## **PART 2** **Amendment of Principal Regulations**

**Amendment of Regulation 4 of Principal Regulations**

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended, in paragraph (1) of Regulation 4, by the substitution of the following subparagraph for subparagraph (r):

> - “(r) CSDs, except as provided for in Article 73 of Regulation (EU) No 909/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^2] ;”.

**Amendment of Regulation 36 of Principal Regulations**

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended, in Regulation 36, by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (4):

> - “(4) Unless the Bank otherwise directs, an investment firm shall comply with the obligation under this Regulation to make public a client limit order, where that order is large in scale compared with normal market size, as determined under Article 4 of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014[^3] .”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 March, 2026.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Finance.
