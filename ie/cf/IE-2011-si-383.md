EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Sea Pollution (Prevention of Air Pollution from Ships) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 313 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0313.html) ) on the prevention of air pollution from ships.

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide for the insertion of the North American Emission Control Area in Regulations 12 and 13 of S.I. 313 of 2011, which deal with emissions of Nitrogen Oxides and Sulphur Oxides respectively.
