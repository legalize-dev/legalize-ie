# S.I. No. 841/2005 - Railway Safety Act 2005 (Part 2) (Establishment Day) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 841 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0841.html)**

**ORDER**

entitled

**Railway Safety Act 2005 (Part 2) (Establishment Day) Order 2005**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec7) of the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2005), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (Part 2) (Establishment Day Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of Part 2 of the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2005) is 1 January 2006.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  |  |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 20 December 2005. |
|  | Martin Cullen Minister for Transport. |

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This order appoints 1 January 2006 as the day on which the Railway Safety Commission is established.
