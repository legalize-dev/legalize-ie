# S.I. No. 318/2012 - National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000 (Approval) Order 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th August*, 2012.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec1) (5) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec8) (1) of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000) as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000 (Approval) Order 2012 and is deemed to have come into operation on 31 December 2011.

###### 2.

2. The period for participants to apply for the grant of a certificate of approval under section 8 of the Act is extended until 1 January 2014.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 July 2012.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the period for participants to apply for approval under the National Beef Assurance Scheme.
