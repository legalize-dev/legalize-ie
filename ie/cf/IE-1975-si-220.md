# S.I. No. 220/1975 - Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1975

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 220 of 1975.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (MAINTENANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1975.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and subsection (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1975.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1975.

###### 3.

3. The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1975, ( [S.I. No. 40 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0040.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution for the Schedule thereto of the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of these Regulations is to amend the existing Regulations governing the payment of maintenance allowances to persons being treated for certain infectious diseases—the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 40 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0040.html) )—to provide as from 1st October, 1975, for increases in the maximum rates of allowance payable.

The infectious diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are tuberculosis, acute anterior poliomyelitis, typhoid, diphtheria, paratyphoid A, paratyphoid B, typhus fever, dysentery, salmonella infection, scarlet fever and streptococcal sore throat.
