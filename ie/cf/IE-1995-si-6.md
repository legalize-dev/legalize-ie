S.I. No. 6 of 1995.

FRIENDLY SOCIETIES (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 96 of the Friendly Societies Act, 1896, as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Registry of Friendly Societies Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1936), and the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0019.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1995.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) The fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Friendly Societies Acts, 1896 to 1977, shall be the fees that are specified in Part I of the Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) No fee shall be payable for the services specified in Part II of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Friendly Societies (Fees) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 290 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0290.html) ), are hereby revoked.

## PART II SERVICES FOR WHICH NO FEE IS PAYABLE

1. Acknowledgement of registry of, or acknowledgement of registry of any amendment of rules of, all class of Societies, other than Specially Authorised Societies, under the Friendly Societies Acts, 1896 to 1977.

2. Cancellation or suspension of registry of a society.

3. Any document or copy document supplied to a public department.

4. Any document or copy document in respect of which a fee is otherwise chargeable under the Friendly Societies Acts, 1896 to 1977, or under any other Act.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Friendly Societies Acts, 1896 to 1977.
