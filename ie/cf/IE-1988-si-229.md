# S.I. No. 229/1988 - Valuation Act, 1988 (Section 10) Regulation, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 229 of 1988.

VALUATION ACT, 1988 (SECTION 10) REGULATION, 1988.

I, RAY MACSHARRY, T.D., Minister for Finance, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), hereby make the following Regulation:

###### 1.

1. This Regulation may be cited as the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (Section 10) Regulation, 1988.

###### 2.

2. Different dates may be prescribed under subsection (8) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , for the coming into operation of different provisions of that section and the said subsection (8) shall stand modified accordingly.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 5th day of July, 1988.

**RAY MACSHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Regulation allows the Minister for Finance to prescribe different dates on which different provisions of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , will come into operation.
