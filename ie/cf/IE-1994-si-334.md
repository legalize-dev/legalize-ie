# S.I. No. 334/1994 - National Council For Educational Awards Act, 1979 (Designation of Institutions) (No. 3) Order, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 334 of 1994.

NATIONAL COUNCIL FOR EDUCATIONAL AWARDS ACT, 1979 (DESIGNATION OF INSTITUTIONS)(NO. 3) ORDER, 1994.

I, NIAMH BHREATHNACH, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979) (as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) )), with the concurrence of the Minister for Finance and after consultation with the National Council for Educational Awards, hereby make the following order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Designation of Institutions) (No. 3) Order, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The institutions specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as being institutions to which the [National Council for Educational Awards Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1979), applies.

SCHEDULE

Burren College of Art

St. Nicholas Montessori College Ireland

The American College, Dublin

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of October, 1994.

**NIAMH BHREATHNACH,**

Minister for Education.

The Minister for Finance hereby concurs in the making of the foregoing order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 27th day of October, 1994.

JAMES McCAFFREY,

Assistant Secretary, Department

of Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order includes the Burren College of Art, St. Nicholas Montessori College Ireland and the American College, Dublin as institutions designated under the National Council for Educational Awards.
