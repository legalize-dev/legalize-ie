# S.I. No. 311/2016 - Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th June*, 2016.

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on the said Society by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec11) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Health, hereby makes the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2016.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed as one with the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) ) and the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2015 ( [S.I. No. 238 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0238.html) ) and may be cited together with those Rules as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 to 2016.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall come into operation on 24 June 2016.

###### 3.

3. Paragraph 35 of the Schedule to the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 is amended by substituting “€3,000.00” for “€2,750.00” in Column 3.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, consent to the making of the foregoing Rules.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

15 June 2016.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Health.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland,

15 June 2016.

**ANN FRANKISH,**

President.

**DAMHNAIT GAUGHAN,**

Registrar.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Rules amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2014 ( [S.I. No. 378 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0378.html) ) to change the fee payable under Rule 16(3) of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Education and Training) Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 493 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0493.html) ) in connection with an application to undertake the in-service practical training programme.

These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2016.
