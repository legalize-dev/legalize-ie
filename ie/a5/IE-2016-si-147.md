# S.I. No. 147/2016 - Companies Act 2014 (Section 839) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st April*, 2016.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec12) and [839](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec839) (1)(a) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Section 839) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Act of 2014” means the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014).

###### 3.

3. The offences under [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec6) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec7) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002) are prescribed for the purposes of section 839(1)(a) of the Act of 2014.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

23 March 2016.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these regulations is to prescribe certain enactments for the purposes of automatic disqualification on conviction of certain indictable offences pursuant to [section 839](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec839) (1)(a) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) .
