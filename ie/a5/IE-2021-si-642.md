# S.I. No. 642/2021 - Wireless Telegraphy Act 1926 (Section 3) Satellite Earth Stations for Satellite Personal Communication Services (Revocation) Order 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November,* 2021.

The Commission for Communications Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0003.html#sec3) (6)(*a*) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/sec0011.html#sec11) (*c*) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972)), transferred to the Commission for Communications Regulation by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec4) (2) of the [Communications Regulation (Amendment) Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2007), hereby makes the following Order:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy Act 1926 (Section 3) Satellite Earth Stations for Satellite Personal Communication Services (Revocation) Order 2021

*Interpretation*

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2005) applies to this Order.

*Revocation*

###### 3.

3. The following statutory instrument is hereby revoked:

- (1) Statutory Instrument Number 173 of 2000, the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (Section 3) (Exemption of Mobile Satellite Earth Stations For Satellite Personal Communication Systems Operating in Bands Below 1 GHz (S-Pcs<<1 GHz)) Order, 2000.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Commission for Communications Regulation,

25 November, 2021.

**ROBERT MOURIK,**

Commissioner, For and on Behalf of the Commission for Communications Regulation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of this order is to revoke Statutory Instrument Number 173 of 2000, the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (Section 3) (Exemption of Mobile Satellite Earth Stations for Satellite Personal Communication Systems Operating in Bands Below 1 GHz (S-Pcs<<1 GHz)) Order, 2000
