# S.I. No. 335/1977 - Wildlife Act, 1976 (Section 44) (Recognised Bodies) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 335 of 1977.

WILDLIFE ACT, 1976 (SECTION 44) (RECOGNISED BODIES) REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1976), as adapted by the Lands (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 29 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0029.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (Section 44) (Recognised Bodies) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 11th day of November, 1977.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations "game" has the meaning assigned to it by [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/sec0044.html#sec44) (8) of the [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1976).

###### 4.

4. Each of the bodies of persons specified in the Schedule to these Regulations (being bodies of persons each of which has for or amongst its objects the conservation of game) is hereby declared to be a recognised body for the purposes of [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Wildlife Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) .
