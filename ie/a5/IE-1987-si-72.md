# S.I. No. 72/1987 - Lottery Prizes Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 72 of 1987.

LOTTERY PRIZES REGULATIONS, 1987.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0028/sec0033.html#sec33) (2) of the [National Lottery Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1986), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Lottery Prizes Regulations, 1987.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 23rd day of March, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The [Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1956), is hereby amended—

- (*a*) by the substitution in subsections 2 (*b*) and 4 of section 27 of "£3,000" for "three hundred pounds",

  and

- (*b*) by the substitution in section 28 (2) (*c*) of "£10,000" for "five hundred pounds".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 9th day of March, 1987.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations increase, with effect from 23 March, 1987, the limits on the total value of prizes for lotteries held under [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/sec0027.html#sec27) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) . The limit on the total value of prizes for the lotteries held under section 27 of 1956 Act is increased form £300 to £3,000. The limit on the total value of prizes for the lotteries held under section 28 of 1956 Act is increased from £500 to £10,000.
