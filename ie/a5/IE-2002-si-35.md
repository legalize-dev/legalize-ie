# S.I. No. 35/2002 - The Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act, 1999 (Health Boards Executive) (Establishment) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Micheál Martin, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec21) of the [Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 1999), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (Health Boards Executive) (Establishment) Order, 2002.

###### 2.

2. The 14th day of February 2002, is hereby the appointed day for the purposes of [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec21) of the [Health (Eastern Regional Health Authority) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) .
