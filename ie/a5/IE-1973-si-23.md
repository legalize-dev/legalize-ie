# S.I. No. 23/1973 - Vocational Education Act, 1930 (Miscellaneous Orders and Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 23 of 1973.

VOCATIONAL EDUCATION ACT, 1930 (MISCELLANEOUS ORDERS AND REGULATIONS) (REVOCATION) ORDER, 1973.

I, PÁDRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/sec0060.html#sec60) and [125](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/sec0125.html#sec125) of the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1930), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Miscellaneous Orders and Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1973.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The following are hereby revoked—

( *a* ) the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Part V) (Principal Teachers' Returns) Regulations, 1938 (S.R. & O., No. 28 of 1938),

( *b* ) the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Part V) (Registers and Records) Regulations, 1938 (S.R. & O., No. 29 of 1938),

( *c* ) the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Part V) (Statutory Forms) Regulations, 1938 (S.R. & O., No. 33 of 1938),

( *d* ) the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Application of Part V to Cork City) Order, 1938 (S.R. & O., No. 12 of 1938),

( *e* ) the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Application of Part V to Limerick City) Order, 1942 (S.R. & O., No. 363 of 1942).

( *f* ) the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Application of Part V to Waterford City) Order, 1946 (S.R. & O., No. 360 of 1946).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of January, 1973.

**PÁDRAIG FAULKNER.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order revokes the Regulations applying Part V of the [Vocational Education Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) to Cork City, Limerick City and Waterford City.
