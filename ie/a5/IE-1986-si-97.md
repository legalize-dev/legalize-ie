# S.I. No. 97/1986 - Prisons Act, 1970 (Section 7) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 97 of 1986.

PRISONS ACT, 1970 (SECTION 7) ORDER, 1986.

I, ALAN M. DUKES, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) (1) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1970), and after consultation with the Visiting Committee to Saint Patrick's Institution, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0118.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for Article 3(2) of the following:

> - "(2) The section shall be in operation for the period of two years commencing on the 26th day of April, 1985, and shall cease to be in operation upon the expiration of that period.".

###### 3.

3. The [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Section 7) (No. 2) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 348 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0348.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of April, 1986.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order continues in operation [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Prisons Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) for a further period of twelve months.
