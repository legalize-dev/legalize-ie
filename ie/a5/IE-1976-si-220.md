# S.I. No. 220/1976 - County Management (Sligo and Leitrim) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 220 of 1976.

COUNTY MANAGEMENT (SLIGO AND LEITRIM) ORDER, 1976.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [City and County Management (Amendment) Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , with the consent of the Minister for Health and the Minister for Social Welfare, hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the County Management (Sligo and Leitrim) Order, 1976.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the first day of December, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The application in relation to the Counties of Sligo and Leitrim of subsection (2) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , is hereby terminated.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government

this 24th day of September, 1976.

**JAMES TULLY,**

Minister for Local Government.

The Minister for Health hereby consents to the above Order.

Dated this 24th day of September, 1976.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Social Welfare hereby consents to the above Order.

Dated this 24th day of September, 1976.

**BRENDAN CORISH,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order terminates, as from 1st December, 1976 the application of subsection (2) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , to the counties of Sligo and Leitrim. Under that subsection, the same person was to be county manager for each of these counties. The effect of this Order is to require each of those counties to have its own individual county manager.
