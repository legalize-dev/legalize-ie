# S.I. No. 157/2013 - Student Support (Amendment of Second Schedule) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th May*, 2013.

I, RUAIRI QUINN, Minister for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) of the [Student Support Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2011), having consulted with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, hereby make the following Regulations:—

*Citation and commencement*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Student Support (Amendment of Second Schedule) Regulations 2013.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 14 May 2013.

###### 2.

2. [Schedule 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) of the [Student Support Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2011) is amended by inserting the following after “13. A person with whom an awarding authority has an agreement under section 13.”:

> “14.Central Applications Office.
>
> 15. Oifig an Ard-Chláraitheora”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 May 2013.

**RUAIRI QUINN,**

Minister for Education and Skills.
