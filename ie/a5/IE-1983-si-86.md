# S.I. No. 86/1983 - Health Contributions (Yearly Reckonable Income) (Variation) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 86 of 1983.

HEALTH CONTRIBUTIONS (YEARLY RECKONABLE INCOME) (VARIATION) REGULATIONS, 1983.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sub-section (3) of [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1979) hereby makes the following Regulations as respects which, pursuant to sub-section (5) of the said section, a draft has been approved by a resolution of each House of the Oireachtas.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Contributions (Yearly Reckonable Income) (Variation) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The sum specified in [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) (2), [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) and [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) is hereby varied with effect from the 6th day of April, 1983 to the sum of £11,000.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 29th

day of March, 1983.

**BARRY DESMOND,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance hereby sanctions the above Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 29th

day of March, 1983.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/sec0009.html#sec9) (3) of the [Health Contributions Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , provides that the Minister for Health may by regulation vary the ceiling amount specified in sections 5 (2), 6 (2) and 8 of the Act. The present income ceiling is £9,500 and it is now varied by these regulations to £11,000. The changed income ceiling is operative from the beginning of the contribution year commencing on 6th April, 1983.
