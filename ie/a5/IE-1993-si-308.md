# S.I. No. 308/1993 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 308 of 1993.

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) (NO. 4) REGULATIONS, 1993.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of its powers under the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990, hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 4) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of November, 1993.

###### 3.

3. Rates of interest on deposits within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , are hereby prescribed as follows:

( *a* ) where any such deposit is less than £3,000, the rate of interest shall be 2.00 per cent per annum; and

( *b* ) where any such deposit is £3,000 or more, the rate of interest shall be 2.75 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) (No. 3) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 155 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0155.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency this 27th day of October, 1993.

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**

Chief Executive.
