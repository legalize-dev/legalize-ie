# S.I. No. 218/2004 - Housing (Transfer of Functions) (No. 2) Order 1993 (Amendment) Order 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0023.html#sec23) (3) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1992) hereby makes the following order:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Housing (Transfer of Functions) (No. 2) Order 1993 (Amendment) Order 2004.

###### 2.

2. The following article is hereby substituted for article 3 of the Housing (Transfer of Functions) (No. 2) Order 1993 ( [S.I. No. 284 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0284.html) ).

> “3. The functions of the council of the county of Wexford as respects [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0006.html#sec6) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) , are, in the case of the borough of Wexford, hereby transferred to the Mayor, Aldermen and Burgesses of Wexford.”

|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, this 19th day of May 2004. |
|  | MARTIN CULLEN, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order transfers back to Wexford County Council certain functions performed by Wexford Borough Council, as respects their functional area, in relation to the granting of shared ownership leases.
