# S.I. No. 37/2001 - Members of the Oireachtas and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Allowances and Salaries) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by -

- (a) section 3A (inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0022/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1973) (“the Act of 1973”), as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0032/sec0006.html#sec6) , [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0032/sec0008.html#sec8) and [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0032/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1983) (the “Act of 1983”)), of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1938), and

- (b) section 8A (inserted by section 11 of the Act of 1973, as amended by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0029/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1977), and sections 6, 8 and 9 of the Act of 1983)), of the [Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1938),

hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Members of the Oireachtas and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Allowances and Salaries) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. In this Order -

“Act of 1938” means the [Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1938);

“Act of 1973” means the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1973);

“Act of 1977” means the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices (Amendment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1977);

“Act of 1996” the means [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1996).

###### 3.

3. (1) The annual sum by way of salary payable monthly under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1938), to each member of Dáil Éireann, with effect from 25 September 2000, shall be £41,143.

- (2) The annual sum by way of salary payable monthly under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1938), to each member of Dáil Éireann, with effect from 1 March 2001, shall be £45,473.

###### 4.

4. (1) The annual sum by way of salary payable monthly under subsection (2) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , to each member of Seanad Éireann, with effect from 25 September 2000, shall be £26,034.

- (2) The annual sum by way of salary payable monthly under subsection (2) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , to each member of Seanad Éireann, with effect from 1 March 2001, shall be £28,773.

###### 5.

5. (1) The annual sum by way of salary payable under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) or subsection (2) (inserted by the Act of 1977) of section 3 of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 25 September 2000, a sum not exceeding -

- (a) in the case of the Taoiseach - £79,146,

- (b) in the case of the Tánaiste - £62,295,

- (c) in the case of a member of the Government, other than the Taoiseach or the Tánaiste - £54,855, and

- (d) in the case of a Minister of State appointed under [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977) - £24,640.

- (2) The annual sum by way of salary payable under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) or subsection (2) (inserted by the Act of 1977) of section 3 of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 1 March 2001, a sum not exceeding -

    - (a) in the case of the Taoiseach - £87,475,

    - (b) in the case of the Tánaiste - £68,850,

    - (c) in the case of a member of the Government, other than the Taoiseach or the Tánaiste - £60,628, and

    - (d) in the case of a Minister of State appointed under [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977) - £27,232.

###### 6.

6. (1) The annual sum by way of salary payable under section 4 (inserted by the Act of 1973) of the Act of 1938 to the Attorney General shall be, with effect from 25 September 2000, a sum not exceeding -

- (a) in case the person who holds the office is not a member of either House of the Oireachtas - £95,998, and

- (b) in case a member of either House of the Oireachtas holds the office - £54,855.

- (2) The annual sum by way of salary payable under section 4 (inserted by the Act of 1973) of the Act of 1938 to the Attorney General shall be, with effect from 1 March 2001, a sum not exceeding -

    - (a) in case the person who holds the office is not a member of either House of the Oireachtas - £106,101, and

    - (b) in case a member of either House of the Oireachtas holds the office - £60,628.

###### 7.

7. (1) The annual sum by way of salary payable under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of section 5 of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 25 September 2000, a sum not exceeding -

- (a) in the case of the Chairman of Dáil Éireann - £54,855, and

- (b) in the case of the Deputy Chairman of Dáil Éireann - £24,640.

- (2) The annual sum by way of salary payable under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of section 5 of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 1 March 2001, a sum not exceeding -

    - (a) in the case of the Chairman of Dáil Éireann - £60,628, and

    - (b) in the case of the Deputy Chairman of Dáil Éireann - £27,232.

###### 8.

8. (1) The annual sum by way of salary payable under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of section 6 of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 25 September 2000, a sum not exceeding -

- (a) in the case of the Chairman of Seanad Éireann - £22,518, and

- (b) in the case of the Deputy Chairman of Seanad Éireann - £12,227.

- (2) The annual sum by way of salary payable under subsection (1) (inserted by the Act of 1973) of section 6 of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 1 March 2001, a sum not exceeding -

    - (a) in the case of the Chairman of Seanad Éireann - £24,889, and

    - (b) in the case of the Deputy Chairman of Seanad Éireann - £13,515.

###### 9.

9. The annual sum by way of allowance for expenses payable under section 10 (inserted by section 5 of the Act of 1996) of the Act of 1938 shall be, with effect from 25 September 2000, a sum not exceeding -

- (a) where payable under subsection (1)(a) of the said section 10 - £35,530,

- (b) where payable under subsection (1)(b) of the said section 10 - £28,424,

- (c) where payable under subsection (1)(c) of the said section 10 - £14,210,

- (d) where payable under subsection (1)(d) of the said section 10 - £7,231, and

- (e) where payable under subsection (4) of the said section 10 - £14,210.

**Explanatory Note**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order applies increases with effect from 25 September 2000 and 1 March 2001 to the salaries of members of the House of the Oireachtas, including Office Holders, arising from the recommendations in *Report No. 38 of the Review Body on Higher Remuneration in the Public Sector*, together with an increase in the sums payable to leaders of qualified parties and Independents in Dáil Éireann with effect from 25 September 2000. The increase with effect from 1 March 2001 takes account of a general pay increase on 1 October 2000 under the *Programme for Prosperity and Fairness*.
