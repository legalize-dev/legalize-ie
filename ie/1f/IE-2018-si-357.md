# S.I. No. 357/2018 - Control of Exports (Dual Use Items) (Amendment) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st September*, 2018.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec3) and [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec4) of the [Control of Export Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2008) and for the purposes of giving further effect to Council Regulation (EC) No 428/2009 of 5 May 2009[^1] and of giving effect to:

- (a) Regulation (EU) No 1232/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 November 2011[^2]

- (b) Regulation (EU) No 388/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 April 2012[^3]

- (c) Regulation (EU) No 599/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014[^4]

- (d) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1382/2014 of 22 October 2014[^5]

- (e) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/2420 of 12 October 2015[^6]

- (f) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2016/1969 of 12 September 2016[^7]

- (g) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2017/2268 of 26 September 2017[^8]

hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Exports (Dual Use Items) (Amendment) Order 2018

###### 2.

2. Article 2 of the Control of Exports (Dual Use Items) Order 2009 ( [S.I. No. 443 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0443.html) ) is amended, in paragraph (1), by the substitution of the following definition of “Council Regulation”:

> “ ‘Council Regulation’ means Council Regulation (EC) No 428/2009 of 5 May 2009, as amended by:
>
> - (a) Regulation (EU) No 1232/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 November 2011
>
> - (b) Regulation (EU) No 388/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 April 2012
>
> - (b) Regulation (EU) No 599/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014
>
>     - (d) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1382/2014 of 22 October 2014
>
> - (e) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2015/2420 of 12 October 2015
>
> - (f) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2016/1969 of 12 September 2016
>
> - (g) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2017/2268 of 26 September 2017

###### 3.

3. The Control of Exports (Dual Use Items) (Amendment) Order 2013 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0242.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

14 September 2018.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation.
