# S.I. No. 520/1998 - Freedom of Information Act, 1997 (Section 25(6)) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 520 of 1998.

FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT, 1997 (SECTION 25(6)) REGULATIONS, 1998

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (Section 25(6)) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Minister for Finance and the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment are hereby prescribed for the purposes of [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0025.html#sec25) (6) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of December, 1998.

CHARLIE McCREEVY. T.D.,

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The FOI Act provides for the use of ministerial certificates in certain limited circumstances and for periodic review of such use by the Taoiseach and prescribed Ministers. These Regulations prescribe the Minister for Finance and the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment for this purpose.
