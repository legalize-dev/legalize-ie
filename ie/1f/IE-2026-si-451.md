###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Guardian ad litem and next friend) 2026, shall come into operation on the 16^th day of September 2026.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2026.

- (3) The Circuit Court Rules as amended by these Rules may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2026..

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall apply in proceedings commenced from the date on which these Rules come into operation.

###### 3.

3. The Circuit Court Rules are amended:

- (i) by the substitution for rules 5 and 6 of Order 6 of the following rules respectively:

  > “5. (1) A child may sue by his or her next friend, and in such case there shall be lodged with the Civil Bill a written consent in the Form No. 58A in the Schedule of Forms authorising the commencement and prosecution of the suit on behalf of the child and the use of the name of such person as next friend, signed by such person.
  >
  > - (2) Where a child sues by a next friend, any solicitor applying to issue the Civil Bill shall make an affidavit in the Form No. 58B in the Schedule of Forms, which shall be filed together with the consent of the person proposed to act as next friend, provided that in any case in which a statutory declaration of the solicitor under [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec14) (2) of the [Mediation Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) is required, this requirement may be satisfied by a single statutory declaration for the purposes of the [Mediation Act 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) and this rule.
  >
  >   6. (1) A child may defend by his or her guardian *ad litem*, who may be appointed without an order of the Court or County Registrar.
  >
  > - (2) Where a guardian *ad litem* proposes to enter an appearance on behalf of a child, the appearance shall be filed in the Office together with the consent of the guardian *ad litem* in the Form No. 58D in the Schedule of Forms. Where the appearance is entered by a solicitor, the appearance shall be filed in the Office together with an affidavit in the Form No. 58C in the Schedule of Forms, to which is annexed the consent of the guardian *ad litem* in the Form No. 58D in the Schedule of Forms.”, and

- (ii) by the deletion from rule 1 of Order 19 of paragraph (i) (“(i) for the appointment of a guardian ad litem of a child;”).

###### 4.

4. The Forms 58A, 58B, 58C and 58D in the Schedule shall be inserted in the appropriate sequence in the Schedule of Forms annexed to the Circuit Court Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These rules substitute rules 5 and 6 of Order 6 to allow a child plaintiff or defendant to litigate by their next friend or guardian on the swearing of an affidavit of suitability by a solicitor and the signing of a consent by the proposed next friend or guardian, thus removing the need for an order appointing a guardian *ad litem* for a child defendant. Accordingly, the power of a County Registrar to make an order under Order 19, rule 1(i) is removed.
