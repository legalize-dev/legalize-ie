> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 56 of 1976.

JURY SUMMONS REGULATIONS, 1976.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Jury Summons Regulations, 1976.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 5th day of March, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule hereto is hereby prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1) of [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976).
