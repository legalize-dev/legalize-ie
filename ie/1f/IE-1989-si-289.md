S.I. No. 289 of 1989.

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (No. 1), 1989.

We, the Circuit Court Rules Committee, constituted pursuant to the provisions of [Section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) and [Section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0020/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , by virtue of the powers conferred upon us by [Section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0066.html#sec66) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) and [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (as applied by [Section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ), and Section 27 of the Courts (Supplemental Provision) Act, 1961, and of all other powers enabling us in this behalf, do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, make the annexed Rules of Court.

Dated this 31st day of October, 1989.

THOMAS F. ROE

MICHAEL MORIARTY

JOHN MacMENAMIN

KEVIN T. FEENEY

MICHAEL T. NEARY (Secretary)

I concur in making the annexed Rules of Court.

Dated this 8th day of November, 1989.

RAY BURKE,

Minister for Justice.

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (No. 1), 1989.

###### 1.

1. These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (No. 1), 1989, shall come into operation on the 15th day of November, 1989.

###### 2.

2. The Order referred to in these Rules shall be added to and construed together with those Orders contained in the Circuit Court Rules, 1950, as amended.

ORDER 72

[JUDICIAL SEPARATION AND FAMILY LAW REFORM ACT, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , SECTIONS 5 AND 6.

1. In this Order, "the Judicial Separation Act" means the [Judicial Separation and Family Law Reform Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1989).

2. The Certificate required by Section 5 of the Judicial Separation Act shall be in accordance with Form No. 1 in the Schedule attached hereto.

###### 3.

3. The Certificate required by Section 6 of the Judicial Separation Act shall be in accordance with Form No. 2 in the Schedule attached hereto.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

These Rules, which come into operation on 15th November, 1989, specify the forms to be used for the purpose of the certificates required by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0006/sec0005.html#sec5) and [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0006/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Judicial Separation and Family Law Reform Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) .
