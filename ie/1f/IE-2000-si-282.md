# S.I. No. 282/2000 - Aquaculture (Licence Fees) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, FRANK FAHY, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/sec0064.html#sec64) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1997) (as adapted by the Marine (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 301 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0301.html) )), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Aquaculture (Licence Fees) Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 October, 2000.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 5 of the Aquaculture (Licence Application and Licence Fees) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 270 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0270.html) ), and Regulation 5 of the Aquaculture (Licence Application and Licence Fees) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0324.html) ), are hereby amended by the insertion of the following proviso:

> “Provided that where the fee payable exceeds £1,000, the licensee may pay to the Minister—
>
> - (*a*) one-half of the fee on the granting of the licence and one-half of the fee not later than 6 months after the granting of the licence, and
>
> - (*b*) while the licence is in force, one-half of the fee on each anniversary of the granting of the licence and one-half of the fee not later than 6 months after each anniversary.”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations permit holders of aquaculture licences to pay their fees in two equal instalments (at six-monthly intervals), where the fee exceeds £1,000 on the granting or on the anniversary of the granting of the licences.
