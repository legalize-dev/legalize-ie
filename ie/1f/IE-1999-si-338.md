# S.I. No. 338/1999 - Gas (Amendment) Act, 1987 (Section 2(7)) (Amendment) Order, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE JACOB, Minister of State at the Department of Public Enterprise in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/sec0002.html#sec2) (7) of the [Gas (Amendment) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1987), the Tourism and Trade (Transfer of Department Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 422 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0422.html) ) (as adapted by the Tourism, Transport and Communications (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 17 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0017.html) )) and the Transport, Energy and Communications (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 395 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0395.html) ) (and as further adapted by the Transport, Energy and Communications (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 299 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0299.html) ) and the Public Enterprise (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 16 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0016.html) ) hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Gas (Amendment) Act, 1987 (Section 2(7)) (Amendment) Order, 1999.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 2nd day of November 1999.

###### 3.

3. The Gas (Amendment) Act, 1987 (Section 2) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 283 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0283.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule to that Order (inserted by the Gas (Amendment) Act 1987 (Section 2(7)) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 119 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0119.html) ).

> “SCHEDULE”

*Article 4(1)*

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Area of application | Functions Application. |
| The geographical counties of Carlow, Cavan, Clare, Cork, Dublin, Kildare, Kilkenny, Laois, Limerick, Louth, Meath, Monaghan, Tipperary, Waterford and Wicklow. | All the functions contained in this Order. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my hand, this 2nd day of November, 1999. |
|  | JOE JACOB T.D. |
|  | Minister of State at the Department of Public Enterprise. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order further amends the Schedule to the Gas (Amendment) Act, 1987 (Section 2) Order, 1987 by the substitution of a new Schedule containing additional areas where the functions conferred by the 1987 Order on Bord Gáis Éireann may be exercised.
