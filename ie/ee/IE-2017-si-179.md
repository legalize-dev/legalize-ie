# S.I. No. 179/2017 - National Vehicle and Driver File (Access) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd May*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/sec0060.html#sec60) (3) of the [Finance Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1993), section 22(4)(*b*)(ii) (inserted by [section 57](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec57) (1) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the Roads and Road Vehicles (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 815 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0815.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Vehicle and Driver File (Access) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the National Vehicle and Driver File (Access) (No. 2) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 287 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0287.html) ) is amended—

- (*a*) by the substitution of the following for reference number 6:

  > “
  >
  > |  |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  > |  | 6 | Competition and Consumer Protection Commission | Assisting with investigations into commercial practices connected with mechanically propelled vehicles under the [Consumer Protection Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2007) |
  >
  > ”,

  and

- (*b*) by the substitution of the following for reference number 9:

  > “
  >
  > |  |  |  |  |
  > | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  > |  | 9 | A local authority within the meaning of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) | Identifying the registered owners of mechanically propelled vehicles connected to alleged offences under the [Local Authorities (Traffic Wardens) Act 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1975), the [Waste Management Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1996), the [Litter Pollution Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1997) and the [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1987) |

**SHANE ROSS,**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the National Vehicle and Driver File (Access) (No. 2) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 287 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0287.html) ) to prescribe the persons or categories of person who may have access to the National Vehicle and Driver File records established under [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/sec0060.html#sec60) of the [Finance Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) and the purpose for such access.
