# S.I. No. 25/2019 - Local Government Act 2019 (Transfer Day) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th February,* 2019.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec7) of the [Local Government Act 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2019), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act 2019 (Transfer Day) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. The 31^st day of May 2019 is appointed as the transfer day for the purposes of the [Local Government Act 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 January 2019

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides that, for the purposes of the [Local Government Act 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) , the transfer day on which the boundary of Cork City Council and Cork County Council will be altered is 31 May 2019.
