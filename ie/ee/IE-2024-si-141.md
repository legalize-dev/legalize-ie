# S.I. No. 141/2024 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Calculation of Means) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th April,* 2024.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) (amended by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec10) of the [Social Welfare Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2020)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , and by reference 19 in Table 2 to Schedule 3 to, the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , with the consent of the Minister of Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

**Citation and construction**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Calculation of Means) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 to 2024 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 to 2024.

**Assessment of means**

###### 2.

2. The Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ) are amended in article 36 (amended by article 2 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 534 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0534.html) )) **–**

- (a) by the deletion of “or” after paragraph (i),

- (b) by the substitution of “2022,” for “2022.” in paragraph (j), and

- (c) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (j):

  > - “(k) any payment made under the Mother and Baby Institutions Payment Scheme, established under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec5) of the [Mother and Baby Institutions Payment Scheme Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2023),
  >
  >   or
  >
  > - (l) any payment made under the scheme operated by the State Claims Agency, known as the ‘Ex gratia scheme – Implementation of the ECtHR (European Court of Human Rights) Judgement in O’Keeffe v Ireland’.”.

The Minister of Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform consents to the foregoing Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations provide that any payments made to:

- • Survivors and former residents of Mother and Baby and County Home Institutions, that come within the scope of the [Mother and Baby Institutions Payment Scheme Act 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) ,

and

- • People who experienced sexual abuse as a pupil in a recognised day school prior to the issuing of the Guidelines for Procedures for Dealing with Allegations or Suspicions of Child Abuse and who had issued legal proceedings against the State in this regard,

will be disregarded in the assessment of means for the purposes of Supplementary Welfare Allowance.
