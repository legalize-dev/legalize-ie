# S.I. No. 369/1991 - Social Welfare (Modifications of Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 369 of 1991.

SOCIAL WELFARE (MODIFICATIONS OF INSURANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1991.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Short title and collective citation.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Modifications of Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1991.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Modifications of Insurance) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 94 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0094.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Modifications of Insurance) Regulations, 1991.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation—

- (*a*) on the 28th day of November, 1991 as respects retirement pension, and

- (*b*) on the 29th day of November, 1991 as respects old age (contributory) pension.

3 Amendment of 1991 Regulations.

###### 3.

**3.** The Social Welfare (Modifications of Insurance) Regulations, 1991 are hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the insertion in paragraph (*b*) of sub-article (2) of article 5 of "subject to articles 7 and 10 of the Social Welfare (Old Age (Contributory) Pension and Retirement Pension) Regulations, 1991," before "employment contributions",

- (*b*) the insertion in paragraph (*b*) of sub-article (2) of article 6 of "subject to articles 7 and 10 of the Social Welfare (Old Age (Contributory) Pension and Retirement Pension) Regulations, 1991," before "employment contributions", and

- (*c*) the insertion in paragraph (*b*) of sub-article (2) of article 7 of "subject to articles 7 and 10 of the Social Welfare (Old Age (Contributory) Pension and Retirement Pension) Regulations, 1991," before "employment contributions".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare

this 28th day of November, 1991.

**BRENDAN DALY,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this

28th day of November, 1991.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Regulation amends the Social Welfare (Modifications of Insurance) Regulations to allow contributions at the modified rate to be taken into account for the purposes of calculating the pro-rata mixed insurance pensions.
