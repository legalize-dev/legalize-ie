# S.I. No. 156/1974 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 28, Forty-Fourth Period) (Miscellaneous Brushes) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 156 of 1974.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 28, FORTY-FOURTH PERIOD) (MISCELLANEOUS BRUSHES) ORDER, 1974.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 28, Forty-fourth Period) (Miscellaneous Brushes) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints the quantity of Miscellaneous Brushes of a value of £1·50 and over per dozen, which may be imported during the period 1st July, 1974 to 30th June, 1975, at 660,000 articles.
