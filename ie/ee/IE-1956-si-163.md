# S.I. No. 163/1956 - Factories Act, 1955 (Building Operations, Engineering Works, Docks, Etc.) (Modification) Regulations, 1956

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 163 of 1956.

FACTORIES ACT, 1955 (BUILDING OPERATIONS, ENGINEERING WORKS, DOCKS, ETC.) (MODIFICATION) REGULATIONS, 1956.

I, WILLIAM NORTON, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by paragraph (*l*) of subsection (1) of section 86, subsection (2) of section 88 and subsection (2) of [section 89](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/sec0089.html#sec89) of the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1955), hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1.—(1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Building Operations, Engineering Works, Docks, etc.) (Modification) Regulations, 1956.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1956.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations " the Act " means the [Factories Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1955).

###### 3.

3. Each provision of the Act mentioned in the first column of the Schedule to these Regulations shall, in its application by virtue of section 88 or section 89 of the act to building operations and works of engineering construction to which the said sections 88 and 89, respectively, apply, have effect with the modification indicated in the second column of the said Schedule opposite the mention of such provision.

###### 4.

4. Subsection (1) of section 122 of the act shall, in its application by virtue of section 86 of the act to docks, wharves, quays and warehouses to which the said section 86 applies, be construed as requiring every person who is by virtue of the said section 86 deemed to be the occupier of a factory to keep, in relation to the persons employed by him, and to those provisions of the Act for the observance of which he is responsible, a general register in the prescribed form in which shall be entered, or to which shall be attached, such of the particulars and reports mentioned in subsection (1) of the said section 122 as may be applicable.
