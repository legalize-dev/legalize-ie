# S.I. No. 266/1987 - Dublin District Milk Board (Minimum Prices of Milk) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 266 of 1987.

DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD (MINIMUM PRICES OF MILK) ORDER, 1987.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) (Amendment) Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1941), and the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0097.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1987, and shall come into operation on the 19th day of October, 1987.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

"registered producers" means persons registered in the register of producers kept by the Dublin District Milk Board;

"registered retailers" means persons registered in the register of retailers kept by the Dublin District Milk Board;

"registered wholesalers" means persons registered in the register of wholesalers kept by the Dublin District Milk Board.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Dublin District Milk Board (Minimum Prices for Milk) Order, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 210 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0210.html) ), is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. The minimum prices to be paid to registered producers for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and registered retailers shall be at the rates shown in the Schedule to this Order.

SCHEDULE

*Minimum Prices*

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Price per gallon (whether supplied at the registered premises of the producer, or at a collection place agreed on with the purchaser, or otherwise supplied). | 79.81p | 83.51p | 101.81p |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 16th day of October, 1987.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture &

Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the minimum prices to be paid to registered producers, as from the 19th October, 1987 for milk sold by them to registered wholesalers and registered retailers in the Dublin District.
