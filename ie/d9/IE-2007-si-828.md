# S.I. No. 828/2007 - Post office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 828 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0828.html)

POST OFFICE SAVINGS BANK (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st January*, 2008.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of its powers under the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2007.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of August 2007.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on a deposit within the meaning of section 14(1) of the [Finance Act 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) is hereby prescribed as 1.0 per cent per annum.

###### 4.

4. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 584 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0584.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency,

16 October 2007

**MICHAEL J. SOMERS,**

Chief Executive.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe a new rate of interest for deposits in the Post Office Savings Bank.
