> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

SOCIAL WELFARE (CONSOLIDATED SUPPLEMENTARY WELFARE ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) (RENT SUPPLEMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd June*, 2009.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 4, 198 (as amended by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec7) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2009)) and 242 (as amended by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec29) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2007)), of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (Rent Supplement) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These Regulations shall be construed as one with the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ) and shall be included in the collective citation “the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 to 2009”.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 June 2009.

*Definitions.*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations the “Principal Regulations” mean the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ).

*Amount of supplement.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended in article 12 (as amended by the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 603 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0603.html) )) by substituting “€24” for “€18” in each place where it occurs.

*Time and manner of payment.*

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended in sub-article 25(1)(*a*)—

- (*a*) in sub-paragraph (iii) by deleting the final word “or”, and

- (*b*) after sub-paragraph (iii) by inserting the following—

  > - “(iv) in cash payable at a post office, or”.

*Maximum amount of rent in respect of which a supplement is payable.*

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended in paragraph (i) of article 9(2) (as amended by the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (Maximum Rent Limits) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 334 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0334.html) )) by substituting “1 June 2009 and ending on 31 May 2010” for “26 January 2007 and ending on 31 December 2009”.

*Entitlement to rent supplement.*

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations provide for an increase in the minimum weekly contribution payable by a recipient of Rent Supplement. The minimum contribution is being increased from €18 to €24.

These regulations also provide for a reduction of the levels of maximum rent until 31 May 2010, as set out in Schedule 1 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ), in respect of which rent supplement is paid.

These measures come into operation on 1 June 2009.
