# EXPIRING LAWS ACT, 1924

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO MAKE PERMANENT CERTAIN EXPIRING ENACTMENTS AND TO CONTINUE FOR A LIMITED PERIOD CERTAIN OTHER EXPIRING ENACTMENTS. [20*th December*, 1924.]

WHEREAS the Acts mentioned in the First and Second Schedules to this Act, in so far as they are in force in Saorstát Eireann, and are temporary in their duration, are limited to expire on the 31st day of December, 1924:

AND WHEREAS it is expedient that the Acts mentioned in the First and Second Schedules to this Act, and the enactments amending or affecting the same should be respectively made permanent and continued as in this Act is mentioned:

BE IT THEREFORE ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS OF SAORSTÁT EIREANN AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Acts in First Schedule to become permanent.

**1.**—(1) The Acts mentioned in the First Schedule to this Act shall to the extent specified in the third column of that schedule, become permanent Acts, and any provision in any Act in force at the date of the passing of this Act which limits the period for which any of those Acts or any parts thereof are to remain in operation shall cease to have effect.

- (2) Any unrepealed enactments amending or affecting the enactments made permanent by this section shall, in so far as they are temporary in their duration, become permanent in like manner, whether they are mentioned in the First Schedule to this Act or not.

###### 2 Acts in Second Schedule to be continued.

**2.**—(1) The Acts mentioned in the Second Schedule to this Act shall, to the extent specified in the third column of that schedule be continued until the 31st day of December, 1925, and shall then expire unless further continued.

- (2) Any unrepealed enactments amending or affecting the enactments continued by this section shall, in so far as they are temporary in their duration, be continued in like manner, whether they are mentioned in the Second Schedule to this Act or not.

###### 3 Short title.

**3.**—This Act may be cited as the Expiring Laws Act, 1924.
