# S.I. No. 568/2017 - European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th December*, 2017.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Directives No. 2015/2115/EU of 23 November 2015[^1] , No. 2015/2116/EU of 23 November 2015[^2] and No. 2015/2117/EU of 23 November 2015[^3] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 176 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0176.html) ) are amended by substituting for Regulation 2 (*e*) the following:

> “Commission Directive 2014/84/EU of 30 June 2014[^4] ”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

12 December 2017.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Business, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend an incorrect reference in [S.I. No. 176 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0176.html) to a European Commission Directive.
