# S.I. No. 344/1992 - Returning Officers' Charges (12Th, 13Th and 14Th Amendments of the Constitution and General Election To Dáil Éireann) Regulations, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 12 (5) of the Referendum Act, 1942 (No. 8 of 1942), (as extended by [section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0064.html#sec64) (4) of the [Electoral Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963)), and [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/sec0025.html#sec25) (5) of the [Electoral Act, 1923](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1923), (as extended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) (4) of the [Electoral Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963) and by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0035/sec0026.html#sec26) (2) of the [Electoral (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1986)), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Returning Officers' Charges (12th, 13th and 14th Amendments of the constitution and General Election to Dáil Éireann) Regulations, 1992.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"Dáil Election" means a General Election within the meaning of [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Electoral Act 1923](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1923), as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Electoral Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963);

"the Referendums" means, the referendums on the proposals for the amendment of the Constitution contained in the Twelfth, Thirteenth and Fourteenth Amendments of the Constitution Bills, 1992;

"Dáil returning officer" means, a person who, pursuant to [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0011.html#sec11) (1) of the [Electoral Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963), as amended by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0012/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1986), is the returning officer for a Dáil Constituency;

"local returning officer" means, a person who, pursuant to section 6 of the Referendum Act 1942 (No. 8 of 1942) is the referendum returning officer for the constituency.

"accounts" means the accounts of a Dáil or local returning officer in respect of services and expenses which have been properly rendered or incurred by such officers for the purposes of or in connection with a referendum or general election;

"electors" means—

( *a* ) in relation to the ascertainment of the maximum sum payable to a local returning officer, the number of persons registered in the register of Presidential electors for the constituency in which such officer is the local returning officer;

( *b* ) in relation to the ascertainment of the maximum sum payable to a Dáil returning officer, the number of persons registered in the register of Dáil electors for the constituency in which such officer is the Dáil returning officer;

"constituency" means any constituency named in the Schedule to the [Electoral (Amendment) Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1990).

###### 3.

3. The scale of maximum charges for the purposes of—

( *a* ) [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Electoral Act 1923](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1923/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1923) as extended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0023.html#sec23) (4) of the [Electoral Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963) and by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0035/sec0026.html#sec26) (2) of the [Electoral Amendment (No. 2) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1986), and

( *b* ) section 12 of the Referendum Act, 1942 (No. 8 of 1942), as extended by [section 64](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/sec0064.html#sec64) (4) of the [Electoral Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1963) and by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0035/sec0026.html#sec26) (2) of the [Electoral (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1986),

shall, as respects the Referendums and Dáil Election, be the scale set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The accounts shall be rendered to the Minister for Finance by being sent to the Accountant, Department of Finance, Accounts Branch, Setanta Centre, Nassau Street, Dublin 2, within a period of 26 weeks beginning on the 25th November 1992.

The accounts shall be accompanied by a declaration made and subscribed by the returning officer rendering them and every such declaration shall be in the following form:

"I hereby declare that, to the best of my knowledge and belief, the accounts which are rendered by me in respect of the services and expenses rendered or incurred by me and accompany this declaration are correct.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Signed, | ". |

###### 5.

5. In the case of disbursements which have already been settled, vouchers and receipts shall be submitted with the accounts and, in the case of charges which have not been settled, the relevant bills or other documents shall be submitted with the accounts,

###### 6.

6. The accounts shall classify and show the disbursements and other charges claimed, together with their totals, in the same manner as the charges allowable under these Regulations are classified in the scale of maximum charges set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 7.

7. In rendering the accounts due regard shall be had to any directions issued by the Minister for Finance to local and Dáil returning officers with reference to the particulars and information to be furnished in support of claims for payments out of the Central Fund.

###### 8.

8. Nothing in these Regulations shall be taken as authorising more than one charge in respect of a particular disbursement.

###### 9.

9. The scale of maximum charges to be paid to local returning officers prescribed by the Referendum (Local Returning Officers' Charges) Regulations 1992 (S.I. 145 of 1992) shall not apply to any referendum held after June 1992.

###### 10.

10. The scale of maximum charges to be paid to Dáil returning officers prescribed by the Returning Officers' and Local Returning Officers' Charges Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 132 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0132.html) ) shall not apply to any Dáil Election held after 1989.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order sets out the scale of maximum charges for local returning officers in respect of a Referendum.
