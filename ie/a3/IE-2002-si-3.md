# S.I. No. 3/2002 - Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Eligible Customer) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary O'Rourke, Minister for Public Enterprise, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec27) (3) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. (a) This Order may be cited as the Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Eligible Customer) Order 2002.

- (b) This Order shall come into operation on 19 February 2002.

###### 2.

2. In [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec27) (2) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , the reference to the figure of “4 Giga Watt hours” is varied to a reference to the figure of “1 Giga Watt hour”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order varies the consumption threshold for eligible electricity customers established under [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec27) (2) of the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) . By varying the eligibility threshold from “4 Giga Watt hours” to “1 Giga Watt hour”, it provides for an increased level of electricity market opening to approximately 40% with effect from 19 February 2002.
