# S.I. No. 284/1973 - Land Act, 1965 (Congested Areas) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 284 of 1973.

LAND ACT, 1965 (CONGESTED AREAS) ORDER, 1973.

I, TOM FITZPATRICK, Minister for Lands, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 4 (1) (b) [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965), hereby order as follows:—

- (1) This Order may be cited as the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (Congested Areas) Order, 1973.

- (2) The area comprising the counties of Longford, Cavan and Monaghan is hereby declared to be a congested area for the purposes of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965).

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 17th day of October, 1973.

**TOM FITZPATRICK,**

Minister for Lands.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of the Order is to declare counties Longford, Cavan and Monaghan to be a congested area for the purposes of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965).
