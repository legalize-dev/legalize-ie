# S.I. No. 703/2003 - Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John Browne, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 68(1)(b) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1976)) 159(5)(a) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1994)), 160(5)(a) (as so inserted) and 279(2) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) and the Fisheries(Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions Order 1977 ( [S.I. ###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Fisheries (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences)(Alteration of Duties) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall have effect as respects licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after 1 January 2004.

###### 3.

3. The amount of the ordinary licence duty in respect of an ordinary fishing licence authorising the use of a fishing engine of a kind specified in column (2) of the Schedule to this Order at any reference number is altered to the amount specified in column (3) of the said schedule at that reference number.

###### 4.

4. The licence duty in respect of an oyster fishing licence is altered to €85.

###### 5.

5. Section 159(1)(b)(ii) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1994) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) is amended by substituting “€110,” for “sixty pounds,”.

###### 6.

6. Section 160(1)(b) (inserted by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) ) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) is amended by substituting “€110,” for “twenty-five pounds,”.

###### 7.

7. The Salmon, Eel and Oyster (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences) (Alteration of Duties) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 343 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0343.html) ) and the Salmon, Eel and Molluscan Shellfish Dealers' Licences (Alteration of Duties) Order 1994 ( [S.I. No. 463 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0463.html) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order prescribes the licence fees to be payable in respect of salmon, eel and oyster commercial fishing licences and salmon, eel and molluscan shellfish dealers' licences issued or renewed for a period commencing on or after 1 January 2004.

The new fees take account of inflation since their last increase and are in lieu of the licence fees set out in the Salmon, Eel and Oyster (Miscellaneous Commercial Licences (Alteration of Duties) Order 1987 ( [S.I. No. 343 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0343.html) ) and Salmon, Eel and Molluscan Shellfish Dealers' Licences (Alteration of Duties) Order 1994 ( [S.I. No. 463 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0463.html) ).
