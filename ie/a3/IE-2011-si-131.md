# S.I. No. 131/2011 - European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th March*, 2011.

I, JAMES REILLY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Regulation (EU) No. 187/2011 of 25 February 2011[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 April 2011.

- (3) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2010 and 2011.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0391.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “EC Regulation 669/2009” the following—

‘“EC Regulation 669/2009” means Commission Regulation (EC) No. 669/2009 of 24 July 2009^5 implementing Regulation (EC) No. 882/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the increased level of official controls on imports of certain feed and food of non-animal origin and amending Decision 2006/504/EC, as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 187/2011 of 25 February 2011^6;’,

and by substituting for footnote 6 thereto the following:

> “^6 OJ No. L. 53, 26.02.2011, p.45.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 March 2011.

**JAMES REILLY,**

Minister for Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Regulation (EU) No. 187/2011 of 25 February 2011 amending Annex 1 to Regulation (EC) No. 669/2009 implementing Regulation (EC) No. 882/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the increased level of official controls on imports of certain feed and food of non-animal origin.

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0391.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Official Controls on the Import of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.
