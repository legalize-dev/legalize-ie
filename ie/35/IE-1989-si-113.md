# S.I. No. 113/1989 - Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 113 of 1989.

HEALTH SERVICES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 of the health Act. 1947 (No. 28 of 1947) and by [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970. hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Health Services (No. 2) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 139 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0139.html) ) (as amended by article 2 of the Health Services (Amendment) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 106 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0106.html) )) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£16.000" for "£15,500 in sub-article (2) (*a*).

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1989.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 17th day of May,

1989.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Health Services (No. 2) Regulations. 1983 by raising the income ceiling from £15,000 to £ 16,000 which limits the extent to which services provided under Section 52 (1), 56 (2). 62 and 63 of the Health Act. 1970 are available to certain persons.
