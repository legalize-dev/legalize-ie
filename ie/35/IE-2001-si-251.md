# S.I. No. 251/2001 - Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) Order, 2001.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 16th July, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. There shall be charged in the offices of the Supreme Court and the High Court and in the district probate registries to which Schedule 1 to this Order relates, in respect of each item set out in column (1) of that Schedule, the fee set out in column (2) of that Schedule opposite the mention of the item in the said column (1).

###### 4.

4. The payment of the fee chargeable in respect of an item mentioned in column (1) of Schedule 1 to this Order shall be recorded by means of a stamp impressed on the document or court fee card, as may be appropriate, mentioned in column (3) of the said Schedule opposite the mention of the item in the said column (1).

###### 5.

5. (1) In every financial year there shall be payable, by way of court fees, the percentages specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to this Order on the annual income arising in that year from the property of every person of unsound mind under the jurisdiction vested in the High Court by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1961), and the provisions of the said Part 1 shall be in substitution for the provisions of section 109 of the Lunacy Regulation (Ireland) Act, 1871.

- (2) In every financial year there shall be payable, by way of court fees, the percentages specified in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to this Order on the annual income arising in that year from the property of every minor under the jurisdiction vested in the High Court by the said section 9.

###### 6.

6. No fee shall be payable under this Order in the Central Office or in the Office of the Registrar of the Supreme Court on any application, order, judgment or report or on the filing of any notice of motion, affidavit or other document necessary in connection with -

- (a) proceedings under Article 40.4 of the Constitution,

- (b) proceedings under the Extradition Acts, 1965 to 1994,

- (c) bail proceedings, or

- (d) an application, in proceedings for a criminal offence, for judicial review under Order 84 rule 18 of the Rules of the Superior Courts.

###### 7.

7. (1) No fee shall be payable under this Order in any family law proceedings, in any matrimonial cause or matter, or in proceedings brought by a health board under the Children Acts, 1908 to 1997.

- (2) No fee shall be payable under this Order in contravention of paragraph 5 of Article 3 of the Luxembourg Convention (within the meaning of the [Child Abduction and Enforcement of Custody Orders Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1991)).

- (3) No fee shall be payable under this Order in respect of proceedings to which [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec5) (5) of the [Immigration Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 1999), applies.

###### 8.

8. No fee shall be payable under this Order by, or on behalf of, any party represented in any civil proceedings by the Chief State Solicitor or a local State Solicitor acting in that capacity.

###### 9.

9. Fees payable in the Office of the Official Assignee in Bankruptcy shall be those chargeable on the date of the granting of the petition in arrangement matters or on the date of the adjudication in bankruptcy matters, as the case may be.

###### 10.

10. (1) In the case of the sale of property the subject of any mortgage, charge or lien the surplus (if any) remaining in the mortgage account after payment of the amount of the mortgage, charge or lien and after payment of the costs, fees and expenses shall be included in the assets for the purpose of calculating the appropriate fee for item 1 of Part 7 of Schedule 1 to this Order.

- (2) Where, in proceedings in bankruptcy the net assets of the bankrupt are less than £120 the petitioning creditor shall lodge with the Official Assignee in Bankruptcy -

    - (a) a sum, which when added to the net amount realised, will equal £120, and

    - (b) a sum sufficient to cover the expenses of the Official Assignee in Bankruptcy.

###### 11.

11. The Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) Order, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 341 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0341.html) ), is hereby revoked.

## **Schedule 2** **Percentages payable in the Office of Wards of Court**

## **Part 1** *Lunacy Percentages*

1. In this Part “clear annual income” does not include the following payments:

- (a) unemployment assistance,

- (b) old age (non-contributory) pension,

- (c) widow's (non-contributory) pension,

- (d) widower's (non-contributory) pension,

- (e) deserted wife's allowance,

- (f) prisoner's wife's allowance,

- (g) one-parent family payment,

- (h) blind pension.

2. Subject to paragraph 3, a percentage on the clear annual income of each ward of court found to be of unsound mind and whose clear annual income is not less than £720 shall be payable according to the following rates -

- (a) where the clear annual income is not less than £720 but is less than £1,800, 2½% of the clear annual income;

- (b) where the clear annual income is not less than £1,800 but is less than £3,600, 3% of the clear annual income;

- (c) where the clear annual income is not less than £3,600, 4% of the clear annual income subject to a maximum payment of £600.

3. Any fractional part of £1.00 in the amount of a percentage shall be disregarded and shall not be levied or paid.

## **Part 2** *Minor Percentages*

1. A percentage on the clear annual income of each minor taken into the wardship of the High Court shall be payable at the same rates as apply to the incomes of wards of court who are of unsound mind, save that the percentage shall be charged only on that part of the clear annual income that exceeds £720.

2. Where a minor has an interest in property, the Registrar of Wards of Court may apportion as remuneration for any services rendered by the minor in connection therewith such part of the profits accruing to him from the property as appears to the Registrar to be reasonable and no percentage shall be charged on the amount thus apportioned.

## **Part 3** *Calculation, Collection and Disposal*

The rules heretofore in operation under the Lunacy Regulation (Ireland) Act, 1871, with respect to the method of calculation, collection and disposal of the percentage charged on the incomes of persons of unsound mind shall continue to apply to the percentage charged on such incomes under this Order and shall apply likewise or in an analogous manner to the percentage charged on the incomes of minors under this Order. Where, in any such rule, reference is made to the death of a person of unsound mind, the reference shall, in its application to a minor include (where the context so admits) a reference to his “coming of age”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

This Order revises, as from 16th July, 2001 the fees chargeable in the Office of the Registrar of the Supreme Court, the Central Office, the Examiner's Office, the Office of the Official Assignee in Bankruptcy, the Taxing Masters' Office, the Accountant's Office, the Office of Wards of Court, the Probate Office and District Probate Registries.

In addition, the Order provides for the exemption from fees of certain proceedings, including family law proceedings.

The Order revokes the Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) Order, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 341 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0341.html) ).
