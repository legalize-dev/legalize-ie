# S.I. No. 125/1973 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 35, Forty-Ninth Period) (Sparking Plugs) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 125 of 1973.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA No. 35, FORTY-NINTH PERIOD) (SPARKING PLUGS) ORDER, 1973.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 35, Forty-ninth Period) (Sparking Plugs) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

SCHEDULE.

| Control of Imports (Quota No. 35) Order, 1937 (S. R. & O., No. 217 of 1937), as amended. | The period beginning on the 1st day of July, 1973, and ending on the 30th day of June, 1974. | Nine hundred thousand (900,000) articles) | Certain sparking plugs and certain component parts of sparking plugs. |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 18th day of May, 1973.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints the quantity of sparking plugs which amy imported during the period 1st July, 1973 to 30th June, 1974 at 900,000 articles.
