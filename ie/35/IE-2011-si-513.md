# S.I. No. 513/2011 - Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th October*, 2011.

I, PAT RABBITTE, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec39) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999) (as adapted by the Communications, Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 706 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0706.html) )), following consultation with the Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, and having, in accordance with section 39(10) of that Act, given a draft of the following order to the persons upon which the obligation concerned is to be imposed, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Public Service Obligations) (Amendment) Order 2011.

###### 2.
