# S.I. No. 116/1987 - Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 116 of 1987.

HEALTH (IN-PATIENT CHARGES) REGULATIONS, 1987

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987,

###### 2.

2. In these regulations—

" the Act" means the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) ;

"the Minister" means the Minister for Health.

###### 3.

3. These regulations shall come into operation on the 18th day of May, 1987.

###### 4.

4. (1) A charge shall be made for in-patient services provided under section 52 of the Act, for persons other than—

- (*a*) persons with full eligibility

- (*b*) women receiving services in respect of motherhood,

- (*c*) children up to the age of six weeks,

- (*d*) children suffering from diseases prescribed under section 52 (2) of the Act,

- (*e*) children in respect of defects noticed at a health examination held pursuant to the service provided under section 66 of the Act,

- (*f*) persons receiving services for the diagnosis or treatment of infectious diseases prescribed under Part IV of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) ,

- (*g*) persons who are subject to a charge under the Health (Charges for In-patient Services) Regulations, 1976 (S.I, No. 180 of 1976),

- (*h*) persons who are deemed, pursuant to section 45 (7) of the Act, to be persons with full eligibility in relation to an in-patient service.

- (2) The charge referred to in article 4 (1) shall be at the rate of £10 in respect of each day or part of a day during which in-patient services are availed of subject to a maximum payment of £100 in any period of twelve consecutive months.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 14th day of April, 1987.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 14th day of April, 1987,

**RAY MAC SHARRY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for a £10 daily charge for in-patient services in certain circumstances and on certain categories of persons subject to a maximum payment of £100 in any period of twelve consecutive months. The categories of persons exempted from charges are set out in Article 4 (1).
