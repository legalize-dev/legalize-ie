HARBOUR RATES (TRALEE AND FENIT PIER AND HARBOUR) ORDER 2006

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Tralee and Fenit Pier Harbour) Order 2006.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 14 November 2006.

###### 2.

2. In this Order -

“Commissioners” means the Tralee and Fenit Pier and Harbour Commissioners; “harbour” means the Tralee and Fenit Pier and Harbour.

###### 3.

3. (1) The rates specified in Schedule 1 are fixed as the maximum rates.

- (2) The maximum rate chargeable in respect of a quantity that is less than a metric tonne is to be calculated on a pro rata basis rounded down to the nearest first decimal place.

- (3) In this Article “maximum rate” means, in relation to goods, the maximum rate to be charged by the Commissioners in respect of the goods.

###### 4.

4. (1) The rates specified in Schedule 2 are fixed as the maximum rates.

- (2) The maximum rate chargeable in respect of a quantity that is less than a gross ton is to be calculated on a pro rata basis rounded down to the nearest first decimal place.

- (3) In this Article “maximum rate” means, in relation to a vessel, the maximum rate to be charged by the Commissioners in respect of the gross tonnage of the vessel.

###### 5.

5. The Harbour Rates (Tralee and Fenit Pier and Harbour) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 215 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0215.html) ) is revoked.
