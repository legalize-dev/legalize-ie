# S.I. No. 37/1996 - Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 37 of 1996.

DESIGNS AND TRADE MARKS (INTERNATIONAL ARRANGEMENTS)(AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1996

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) (5) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927) (as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Executive Powers (Consequential Provisions) Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) ) (No. 20 of 1937), and [section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0070.html#sec70) (5) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1963), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Designs and Trade Marks (International Arrangements) (Amendment) Order, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. It is hereby declared that the provisions of [section 152](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/sec0152.html#sec152) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1927) (as amended by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0013/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Industrial and Commercial Property (Protection) (Amendment) Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1957) and [section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Trade Marks Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) ) (No. 9 of 1963), apply in the case of Foreign States specified in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Patents, Designs and Trade Marks, (International Arrangements) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 223 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0223.html) ), is hereby revoked.
