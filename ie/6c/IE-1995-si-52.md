# S.I. No. 52/1995 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 52 of 1995.

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1995.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1993 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations)Regulations, 1964 to 1995.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE

An Foras Áiseanna Saothair

The Environmental Research Unit

The Housing Finance Agency

The Pensions Board

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of February, 1995.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and "approved organisations", and between one "approved organisation" and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as "approved organisations".
