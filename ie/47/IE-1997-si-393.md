# S.I. No. 393/1997 - Electoral Act, 1997 Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 393 of 1997.

ELECTORAL ACT, 1997CONSTITUENCY COMMISSION (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1997

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [Electoral Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. A Constituency Commission is hereby established to undertake the function assigned to it by Part II of the [Electoral Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

and Local Government this 30" day of September, 1997.

**NOEL DEMPSEY**

________________________________________

Minister for the Environment and Local Government.

Explanatory Note

This order establishes a constituency commission on report on Dáil and European constituencies.
