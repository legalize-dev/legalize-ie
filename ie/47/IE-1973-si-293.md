# S.I. No. 293/1973 - Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1973 (Appointed Day) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 293 of 1973.

MINISTERS AND SECRETARIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1973 (APPOINTED DAY) ORDER, 1973.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1973), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (Appointed Day) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of November, 1973, is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1973).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 30th day of October, 1973.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints the 1st day of November, 1973, as the day on which the Department of the Public Service shall stand established.
