# S.I. No. 6/1982 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 6 of 1982.

DIPLOMATIC AND CONSULAR FEES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, JAMES DOOGE, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931), and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939), as adapted by the External Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0158.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 to 1981, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 to 1982.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 20th day of January, 1982.

###### 4.

4. [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 263 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0263.html) ) (as amended by the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 260 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0260.html) )), are hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution for subparagraph (*b*) of Regulation 9 (1) of the following subparagraph:

> "( *b* ) in the case of the fees mentioned at reference numbers 10 to 13 and 15 in the Schedule-by any officer of the Minister appointed by the Minister to be a person to whom the Act of 1939 applies,".

( *b* ) the insertion in the First Schedule thereto after reference number 45 of the following:

**JOHN BRUTON.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to alter as indicated in the Regulations fees chargeable for certain services rendered under the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931) and the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939).
