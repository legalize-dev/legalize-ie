# S.I. No. 258/1993 - County and City Management (Galway) Order, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 258 of 1993.

COUNTY AND CITY MANAGEMENT (GALWAY) ORDER, 1993.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0007.html#sec7) (7) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1985) and [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) (2) of the [City and County Management (Amendment) Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) as modified by the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (County Borough of Galway) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 426 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0426.html) ), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the County and City Management (Galway) Order, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The operation of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0007.html#sec7) (5) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) is hereby terminated.

###### 3.

3. This Order shall come into operation on the 15th day of November, 1993.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 9th day of September One Thousand Nine Hundred and Ninety Three.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order terminates, as from the 15th November, 1993, the operation of subsection 5 of [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) to the county and county borough of Galway. Under the subsection the same person was to be county manager for the county and city manager for the county borough. The effect of this Order is to require the county and the county borough each to have its own individual manager.
