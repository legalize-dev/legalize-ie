# S.I. No. 346/1974 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 50, Eighteenth Period) (Superphosphates) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 346 of 1974.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 50, EIGHTEENTH PERIOD) (SUPERPHOSPHATES) ORDER, 1974.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 50, Eighteenth Period) (Superphosphates) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints the quantity of superphosphates which may be imported during the period 1st January, 1975 to 30th June, 1975, from countries other than the United Kingdom at 8,760 tons.
