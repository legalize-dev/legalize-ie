# S.I. No. 47/2011 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th February*, 2011.

I, BRIAN COWEN, Taoiseach and Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Provision of Services) Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1993), as amended by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) of the [Passports Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2008), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 11 April 2011.

###### 2.

2. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0117.html) ) (as amended by the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 365 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0365.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations for Part 1 of the Second Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2001, as amended, to provide for the re-introduction of passport fees for over 65s and the increase of new administration fees in respect of applications not submitted through a diplomatic mission or consular post or through a special arrangement for the express processing of passport applications entered into between the Minister and providers of postal services.
