# S.I. No. 683/2010 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Occupational Injuries) (Amendment) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th January*, 2011.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) , [74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec74) and [75](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec75) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Occupational Injuries) (Amendment) Regulations 2010.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation—

- (*a*) in so far as they relate to injury benefit and disablement gratuity on 3 January 2011, and

- (*b*) in so far as they relate to disablement pension on 7 January 2011.

*Definition.*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations, the “Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Occupational Injuries) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 102 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0102.html) ).

*Increased rates of benefits and pensions under the occupational injuries scheme.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by—

- (*a*) substituting the Schedule set out in Schedule A to these Regulation for Schedule 1, and

- (*b*) substituting the Schedule set out in Schedule B to these Regulation for Schedule 3, subject in respect of the Scale set out in the said Schedule B, to the following provisions—

    - (i) the rates of pension specified in column (3) of the Scale shall have effect on and from 7 January 2011 whether the period taken into account by the assessment began on, before, or after that date, and,

    - (ii) the amounts of gratuity specified in column (2) of the Scale shall be payable only where the period taken into account by the assessment of the degree of disablement began on or after 3 January 2011.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Social Welfare Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) provides for changes in the maximum rates of benefits payable under the Occupational Injuries Benefits Scheme arising from Budget 2001.

These regulations provide for changes in the reduced rates of Occupational Injuries Benefits—

- • in respect of Disablement Gratuities where the degree of disablement is assessed at 19% or less, with effect from 3 January 2011,

- • in respect of Disablement Pensions payable in lieu of such Gratuities, with effect from 7 January 2011, and

- • in respect of Injury Benefit payable to persons under the age of sixteen, with effect from 3 January 2011.
