# S.I. No. 462/2006 - Road Safety Authority Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No 462 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0462.html)**

**Road Safety Authority Act 2006 (Establishment Day) Order 2006**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Safety Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Road Safety Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (Establishment Day Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The day appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Road Safety Authority Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2006) is 1^st September 2006.
