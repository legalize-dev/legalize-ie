EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This not eis not part of the Statutory Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations revise the list of schemes exempt from the preservation requirements set down in Part III if the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

The Act provides for fthe preservation of pension benefits for members of occupational pension schemes who leave the relevant employment before normal pensionable age and who have completed a minimum service of at least two years. This preserved benefit is a proportion of the benefits to which the member would have been entitled if he or she had remained in the relevant employment until normal pensionable age.

This list amends the schedule to the Occupational Pension Schemes (Preservation of Benefits) Regulations 2002 (S.I. 279 of 2002) which specifies those occupational pension schemes exempt from the preservation requirements of the Pensions Act.
