# S.I. No. 627/2023 - Personal Injuries Resolution Board Act 2022 (Change of Name of Board) Order 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th December,* 2023.

I, DARA CALLEARY, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0042/print.html#sec2) of the [Personal Injuries Resolution Board Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0042/print.html) (No. 42 of 2022) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 15 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0015.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Personal Injuries Resolution Board Act 2022 (Change of Name of Board) Order 2023.

###### 2.

2. The Personal Injuries Assessment Board shall, on and from the 14^th day of December 2023, be known, in the Irish language, as An Bord um Réiteach Díobhálacha Pearsanta and, in the English language, as the Personal Injuries Resolution Board.

GIVEN under my hand,

12 December 2023

DARA CALLEARY,

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment
