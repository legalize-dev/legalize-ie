# S.I. No. 436/2021 - Non-Life Insurance (Provision of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th August,* 2021.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Central Bank of Ireland (the Bank) by sections 43D and 43F of the [Insurance Act 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No.3 of 1989), the Bank hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Non-Life Insurance (Provision of Information) (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 September 2021.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulation” means the Non-Life Insurance (Provision of Information) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 74 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0074.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 6 of the Principal Regulations is amended by deleting paragraph (4).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 6(5) of the Principle Regulation is amended by deleting, after the words “the information referred to in Regulations 6(1)(a) to (j)”, the words “and (4)”.

Signed for and on behalf of the CENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND

23 August 2021

DERVILLE ROWLAND,

Director General (Financial Conduct)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Non-Life Insurance (Provision of Information) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 74 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0074.html) ).
