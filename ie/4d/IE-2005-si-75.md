[S.I. No. 75 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0075.html)

ELECTORAL (AMENDMENT) ACT 2001 — PART 3 ORDER 2002 (REVOCATION) ORDER 2005

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(4) of the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) 15 of 2004), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electoral (Amendment Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) — Part 3 Order 2002 (Revocation) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. [The Electoral (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) — Part 3 Order 2002, insofar as it relates to the Dáil constituency of Meath is hereby revoked.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government this 17th day of February 2005. |
|  | DICK ROCHE, Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*.

This Order revokes, insofar as it relates to the Dáil constituency of Meath, the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) — Part 3 Order 2002 which requires use of electronic voting and counting in the constituency.
