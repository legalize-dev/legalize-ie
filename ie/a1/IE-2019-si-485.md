# S.I. No. 485/2019 - The Roads (Schemes) (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil”* 1*st of October,* 2019.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0279.html) and sections [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0279.html) and [51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0279.html) (as amended by the European Union (Roads Act 1993) (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No 279 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0279.html) )) of the [Roads Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1993), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Roads (Schemes) (Forms) (Amendment) Regulations 2019.

###### 2.

2. The Roads (Schemes) (Forms) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0049.html) ) are amended -

- (*a*) by the substitution of “environmental impact assessment report” for “environmental impact statement” in each place that it occurs,

- (*b*) in Schedule 4, by the deletion of “(as amended by section 9(1)(e) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) )”, and “(as amended by section 51(3)(a) of the [Planning and Development (Strategic Infrastructure) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) )”,

- (*c*) by the deletion of “(as amended by section 9(1)(e)(i) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) )” in each place that it occurs,

- (*d*) by the deletion of “(as amended by section 9(1)(d) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) )” in each place that it occurs,

- (*e*) by the deletion of “(as amended by section 51(3)(c) of the [Planning and Development (Strategic Infrastructure) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) )” in each place that it occurs,

- (*f*) in Schedule 5, by the deletion of “(as amended by section 51(3)(e) of the [Planning and Development (Strategic Infrastructure) Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) )”, and

- (*g*) in Schedule 6, by the deletion of “(as amended by [section 215](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec215) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) )”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

17 September, 2019.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations set out the amendments to the Roads (Schemes) (Forms) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0049.html) ) as a result of the European Union (Roads Act 1993) (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0279.html) ), which transpose Directive 2014/52/EU on the assessment of the effects of certain public and private projects on the environment.
