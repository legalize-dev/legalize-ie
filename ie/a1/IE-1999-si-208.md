# S.I. No. 208/1999 - Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992 (Urban Waste Water Treatment) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0006.html#sec6) and [59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0059.html#sec59) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), which said powers were delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 428 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0428.html) ), and for the purpose of giving effect to provisions of the Council Directive of 21st May, 1991 (No. 91/271/EEC)[^1] as amended by the Commission Directive of 27th February 1998 (No. 98/15/EC)[^2] I, DAN WALLACE, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992 (Urban Waste Water Treatment) (Amendment) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992 (Urban Waste Water Treatment) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 419 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0419.html) ) are hereby amended by the deletion of Part II of the Second Schedule thereof and the substitution therefor of the following Part:

> ### “Part II
>
> Requirements for discharges from urban waste water treatment plants to sensitive areas. One or both parameters may be applied depending on the local situation. The values for concentration or for the percentage of reduction shall apply.

>
> EXPLANATORY NOTE.
>
> *(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*
>
> These Regulations amend the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Urban Waste Water Treatment) Regulations, 1994 by clarifying certain technical aspects in relation to methodologies for monitoring for compliance with requirements of the Regulations regarding the discharges from waste water treatment plants.
>
> These Regulations give effect to EU Commission Directive 98/15/EC of 27 February 1998 (including Corrigendum of 2 June 1999, O.J. No. L 139/34).

The conditions concerning temperature could be replaced by a limitation on the time of operation to take account of regional climatic conditions.”
