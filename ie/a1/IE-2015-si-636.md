# S.I. No. 636/2015 - Land Commission (Dissolution) Act Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th January*, 2016.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0025/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Land Commission (Dissolution) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1992) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Land Commission (Dissolution) Act Regulations 2015.

- (2) These Regulations come into force on 1 January 2016.

###### 2.

2. All financial accounts held by the Minister in respect of the Commission are closed.

###### 3.

3. All credits in accounts closed under Regulation 2 shall transfer to the Minister and shall be accounted for by the Minister in the Departmental accounts required under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1866/en/act/pub/0039/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Exchequer and Audit Departments Act 1866](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1866/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (1866, (29 & 30 Vic) c. 39).

###### 4.

4. All liabilities to accounts closed under Regulation 2 shall transfer to the Minister and shall be accounted for by the Minister in the Departmental accounts required under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1866/en/act/pub/0039/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Exchequer and Audit Departments Act 1866](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1866/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal

17 December 2015.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the transfer of accounts held in the name of the former Irish Land Commission to be transferred into the accounts of the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.
