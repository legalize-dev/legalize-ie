**S.I. No. 229 2005**

**National Monuments Act, 1930 (Section 14B) Regulations, 2005.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Section 14B(11) of the [National Monuments Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) 2 of 1930), (as inserted by Section 5 of the National Monuments (Amendment Act, 2004 [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) 22 of 2004)), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [National Monuments Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (Section 14B) Regulations, 2004.

###### 2.

2. For the purposes of these Regulations, a reference to section 14B or to a provision of that section shall be read as a reference to section 14B of the [National Monuments Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) as inserted by section 5 of the National Monuments (Amendment) Act, 2004.

###### 3.

3. For the purposes of paragraph (c) of section 14B(7) the prescribed bodies or persons are listed in Schedule A to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The prescribed forms for the purposes of section 14B(7) are set out in Schedule B to these Regulations as follows:

- (a) Form No. 1 or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of paragraph (b) of section 14B(7).

- (b) Form No. 2 or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of paragraph (c) of section 14B(7).

- (c) Form No. 3 or a form substantially to the like effect shall be the prescribed form for the purposes of paragraph (d) of section 14B(7).

## **SCHEDULE A**

The prescribed bodies or persons for the purposes of Article 3 of these Regulations shall be:

- (a) The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (where it appears to the road authority that the proposed change to the approved road development might have significant effects in relation to the architectural or archaeological heritage or to nature conservation).

- (b) Road Authorities adjacent to the area in which the proposed road development is located,

- (c) The Heritage Council,

- (d) An Taisce — The National Trust for Ireland and

- (e) Fáilte Ireland

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

These Regulations prescribe the forms of notice to be used by a road authority and the bodies or persons to be notified by it pursuant to section 14B(7) of the [National Monuments Act 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (as amended).
