# S.I. No. 26/2021 - Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd February,* 2021.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0031.html#sec31) (amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Health Act 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2021.

###### 2.

2. The Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) are amended –

- (*a*) in Regulation 2 (amended by Regulation 4(1) of the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 559 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0559.html) )) by the insertion of the following definition:

  > “ ‘registered pharmacist’ means a person registered in a register set up under [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec13) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007)”, and

- (*b*) in paragraph (c) of Regulation 13(1) (amended by Regulation 4(5) of the Infectious Diseases (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 559 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0559.html) )) by the insertion of “or any registered pharmacist” after “a functional area of the Health Service Executive”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 January, 2021.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend paragraph (c) of Regulation 13(1) (as amended) of the Infectious Diseases Regulations 1981 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0390.html) ) to provide for the supply from the Health Service Executive to registered pharmacists of such agents as per that regulation.
