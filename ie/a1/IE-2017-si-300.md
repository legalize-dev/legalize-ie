# S.I. No. 300/2017 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Section 44(3)) (Prescribed Notice) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th July*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) and section 44(3) (inserted by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec27) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Section 44(3)) (Prescribed Notice) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The form of notice set out in the Schedule is the form of notice prescribed for the purposes of section 44(3) (inserted by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec27) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010).
