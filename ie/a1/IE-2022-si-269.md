# S.I. No. 269/2022 - The Solicitors Acts 1954 to 2015 (Apprentices' Fees) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd June,* 2022.

The Law Society of Ireland in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0005.html#sec5) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Solicitors Act 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) and with the concurrence of the President of the High Court hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as The Solicitors Acts 1954 to 2015 (Apprentices’ Fees) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July 2022 and The Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 2008 (Apprentices’ Fees) Regulations, 2008 ( [S.I. No. 577 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0577.html) ) shall from that date stand revoked.

###### 3.

3. (*a*) The words and phrases in these Regulations and in the Schedule hereto shall have the meanings respectively assigned by the Solicitors Acts 1954 to 2015 and, except insofar as may be inconsistent there-with, the provisions of the Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 2001 (S.I. No. 546 of 2001) and the provisions of the Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 2011 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment) Regulations, 2019 ( [S.I. No. 503 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0503.html) ).

- (*b*) The Interpretation Act 2005 shall apply for the purposes of the inter-pretation of these Regulations as it applies for the purposes of the interpretation of an Act of the Oireachtas, except insofar as it may be inconsistent with the provisions of the Solicitors Acts 1954 to 2015 or The Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 546 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0546.html) ) or The Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 2011 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 2019 ( [S.I. No. 503 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0503.html) ) or these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. As and from the date on which these Regulations shall come into operation, the fees specified in the Schedule hereto shall be paid to the Society in respect of courses and examinations provided for in The Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 546 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0546.html) ) and The Solicitors Acts 1954 to 2011 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment) regulations, 2019 ( [S.I. No. 503 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0503.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The principal purpose of the Instrument is to provide for increases in the fees payable to the Law Society of Ireland in respect of courses and examinations provided for in The Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 1994 (Apprenticeship and Education) Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 546 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0546.html) ) and The Solicitors Acts, 1954 to 2011 (Apprenticeship and Education) (Amendment) Regulations, 2019 ( [S.I. No. 503 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0503.html) ) operative from the 1st day of July 2022.
