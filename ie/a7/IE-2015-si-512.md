# S.I. No. 512/2015 - European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 20*th November*, 2015.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 1223/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 November 2009[^1] , as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 344/2013 of 4 April 2013[^2] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 483/2013 of 24 May 2013[^3] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 658/2013 of 10 July 2013[^4] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1197/2013 of 25 November 2013[^5] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 358/2014 of 9 April 2014[^6] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 866/2014 of 8 August 2014[^7] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1003/2014 of 18 September 2014[^8] , Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1004/2014 of 18 September 2014[^9] and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 2015/1190 of 20 July 2015[^10] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013 and 2015 and shall be construed together as one.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 440 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0440.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “EU Regulation” the following:

> “‘EU Regulation’ means Regulation (EC) No. 1223/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 November 2009[^1], as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 344/2013 of 4 April 2013[^2], Commission Regulation (EU) No. 483/2013 of 24 May 2013[^3], Commission Regulation (EU) No. 658/2013 of 10 July 2013[^4], Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1197/2013 of 25 November 2013[^5], Commission Regulation (EU) No. 358/2014 of 9 April 2014[^6], Commission Regulation (EU) No. 866/2014 of 8 August 2014[^7], Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1004/2014 of 18 September 2014[^9] and point (1) of the Annex to Commission Regulation (EU) No. 2015/1190 of 20 July 2015[^10];”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 18 of the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) in paragraph (1)(*d*), by inserting “of the EU Regulation” after “Article 5(2)”,

- (*b*) in paragraph (3), by deleting subparagraph (*d*), and

- (*c*) by inserting after paragraph (4) the following paragraph:

  > - “(4A) A responsible person, distributor, manufacturer or other person who places on the market, a cosmetic product which contains a preservative other than those listed in Annex V to the EU Regulation, as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1003/2014 of 18 September 2014[^8], or a preservative listed in that Annex, as so amended, but not used in accordance with the corresponding conditions laid down in that Annex, as so amended, including a substance listed in that Annex, as so amended, but not intended to be used as a preservative, is guilty of an offence.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

16 November 2015.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations give full effect to Regulation (EC) No. 1223/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 November 2009, as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No. 344/2013 of 4 April 2013, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 483/2013 of 24 May 2013, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 658/2013 of 10 July 2013, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1197/2013 of 25 November 2013, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 358/2014 of 9 April 2014, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 866/2014 of 8 August 2014, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1003/2014 of 18 September 2014, Commission Regulation (EU) No. 1004/2014 of 18 September 2014 and Commission Regulation (EU) No. 2015/1190 of 20 July 2015.

These Regulations amend the European Union (Cosmetic Products) Regulations 2013.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cosmetic Products) (Amendment) Regulations 2015.
