# S.I. No. 379/1996 - Competition (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Richard Bruton, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/sec0007.html#sec7) (4) and [23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/sec0023.html#sec23) of the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1991), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Competition (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1996.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the first day of January 1997.

###### 2.

2. Every agreement, decision or concerted practice to be notified to the Authority under [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1991), shall be accompanied by a fee of £250.00.

###### 3.

3. The Competition (Notification Fee) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No 250 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0250.html) ) are hereby revoked.

Given under my Official Seal, this 9th day of December, 1996

Richard Bruton

Minister for

Enterprise & Employment

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These regulations revoke [SI No 250 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0250.html) "Competition (Notification Fee) Regulations 1991" and prescribe a revised appropriate fee to be sent with any agreement, decision and concerted practice which is notified under the [Competition Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0024/index.html)
