# S.I. No. 25/2001 - European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive No. 91/494/EEC[^1] of 26 June, 1991 as amended by Council Directive No. 93/121/EEC[^2] of 22 December, 1993 and as last amended by Council Directive No. 1999/89/EC[^3] of 15 November, 1999 hereby make the following Regulations:-

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 12th of February, 2001.

- (3) The European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1997 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1997 and 2001.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1997 are hereby amended as follows:

- (a) by the substitution in Regulation 3(1) for the definition of “the Council Directive” of the following:

  > “the Council Directive” means Council Directive No. 91/494/EEC[^1] of 26 June, 1991, as amended by Council Directive No. 93/121/EEC[^2] of 22 December 1993 and as last amended by Council Directive No. 1999/89/EC[^3] of 15 November, 1999”.

- (b) by the deletion of Regulation 4 (1)(b), and

- (c) by the deletion of Regulation 4(1)(g).

4. Regulation 10 of the European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 3 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0003.html) ) as amended by the European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0125.html) ) is hereby deleted.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 3 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0003.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution for paragraph (1) (f) of Regulation 11 [ as inserted by Regulation 12 of the European Communities (Fresh Poultrymeat) Regulations, 1997 ] as follows:

> - “(f) it is accompanied by a health certificate relating to the poultrymeat concerned in accordance with Article 12 of Council Directive 91/494/EEC, as amended by Council Directive No. 93/121/EEC of 22 December 1993 and as last amended by Council Directive No. 1999/89/EC of 15 November, 1999, supplemented, if necessary, by a declaration drawn up in accordance with Article 21 of Annex B to the Council Directive.”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations implement Council Directive No. 1999/89 of 15 December, 1999 which amends Council Directive No. 91/494/EEC of 26 June 1991 as amended by Council Directive No. 93/121/EEC of 22 December 1993 on animal health conditions governing intra-Community trade in and imports from third countries of fresh poultrymeat.
