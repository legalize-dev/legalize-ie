# S.I. No. 158/1972 - Trade Union Act, 1971 (Notice of Intention To Apply For Negotiation Licence) Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 158 of 1972.

TRADE UNION ACT, 1971 (NOTICE OF INTENTION TO APPLY FOR NEGOTIATION LICENCE) REGULATIONS, 1972.

I, JOSEPH BRENNAN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Trade Union Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1941) (as extended by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0033/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Trade Union Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1971)) and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the [Trade Union Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (Notice of Intention to apply for Negotiation Licence) Regulations, 1972.

###### 2.

2. Every notice under Section 2 (1) (*a*) (ii) of the [Trade Union Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1971), of intention to make an application under [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Trade Union Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1941), for a negotiation licence under Part II of that Act, shall be in the form contained in the Schedule to these regulations.

SCHEDULE.

TRADE UNION ACTS, 1871 TO 1971.

Notice is hereby given, pursuant to Section 2 (1) (*a*) (ii) of the [Trade Union Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , by the Trade Union referred to below, of its intention to make an application under [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Trade Union Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , for a negotiation licence under that Act.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of June, 1972.

**JOSEPH BRENNAN,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order sets out the form of the press notice which a trade union must publish of its intention to apply for a negotiation licence under the [Trade Union Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) .
