# S.I. No. 559/2012 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Fees) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st January*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 42 (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec10) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Fees) Regulations 2012.

###### 2.

2. The fees to be paid, with effect from 1 January 2013, to a licensing authority on the application to it—

- (*a*) for an Irish driving licence,

- (*b*) a learner permit, or

- (*c*) the exchange of a foreign driving licence for an Irish driving licence,

in the circumstances referred to in column (1) of Part 1 of the Schedule are as set out opposite in column (2) of that Part.

###### 3.

3. The fees to be paid, with effect from 12 January 2013, to a licensing authority on the application to it in respect of any of the matters referred to in column (1) of Part 2 of the Schedule are the fees set out opposite in column (2) of that Part.

###### 4.

4. Regulations 11 and 18 of the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) are revoked with respect to applications in respect of a driving licence or learner permit referred to in Regulation 2, on 1 January 2013.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 December 2012.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for increases in driver licence fees. Those fees outlined in Part 1 of the Schedule to the Regulations will apply from 1 January 2013 and the fees outlined in Part 2 of the Schedule will apply from 12 January 2013.
