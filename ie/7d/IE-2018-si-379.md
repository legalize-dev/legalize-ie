S.I. No. 379 of 2018

CIRCUIT COURT RULES (FINES (PAYMENT AND RECOVERY)) 2018

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules, which may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules (Fines (Payment and Recovery)) 2018, shall come into operation on the 17th day of October 2018.

- (2) These Rules shall be construed together with the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018.

- (3) The Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018 as amended by these Rules may be cited as the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018.

###### 2.

2. The Circuit Court Rules are amended:

- (i) by the insertion in the “Interpretation of Terms” provisions immediately following paragraph 2 of the following paragraph:

  > “2A. “Combined court office manager” means a person appointed under [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0036/print.html#sec19) of the [Courts and Court Officers Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) to be the manager of a combined court office established under section 14 of that Act.”;

- (ii) by the insertion in paragraph 5 (“County Registrar”) of the “Interpretation of Terms” provisions, of the following sub-paragraph immediately after sub-paragraph (*b*)(ii):

  > - “(iiA) Order 41A, rule 1;”, and

- (iii) by the insertion immediately following Order 41 of the following Order:

  > “
  >
  > *Order 41A (Fines (Payment and Recovery))*
  >
  > 1. (1) The County Registrar or (as the case may be) the combined court office manager may at any time after the due date for payment of any fine:
  >
  > - (*a*) imposed by the Court on appeal from the District Court, or
  >
  > - (*b*) imposed by the Court otherwise than on appeal,
  >
  >   issue a notice by ordinary prepaid post for the purposes of section 7(4) of the [Fines (Payment and Recovery) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) requiring a fined person who has not paid the fine (or, as the case may be, a relevant instalment) by the due date for payment in accordance with that Act to appear before the Court at a date and time specified in the notice.
  >
  > - (2) The statement in writing of the financial circumstances of the fined person shall be in the Form 55 in the Schedule of Forms.”

###### 3.

3. The Form numbered 55 in Schedule 1 shall be added to the Schedule of Forms appended to the Circuit Court Rules 2001 to 2018, immediately following Form 54.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules insert Order 41A into the Circuit Court Rules to facilitate the operation of the provisions of the [Fines (Payment and Recovery) Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) .
