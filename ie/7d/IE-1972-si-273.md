S.I. No. 273 of 1972.

RROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY BOROUGH OF LIMERICK AND COUNTY OF LIMERICK) REGULATIONS, 1972.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) , [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) and [46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), amended as regards the said section 46 by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968), hereby makes the following Regulations—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Limerick and County of Limerick) Regulations, 1972.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Regulations shall come into operation on the 20th day of November, 1972.

3 Interpretation.

###### 3.

**3.** In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires—

"the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"road" means a public road;

"special speed limit" and "built-up area speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road;

"perimeter road" means a road on and along the roadway of which there runs the boundary of a county or other borough, an urban district or a town.

4 Built-up area speed limit.

###### 4.

**4.** (1) Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

5 Special speed limits.

###### 5.

**5.** (1) Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) Fifty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the road specified in the Fourth Schedule to these Regulations.

6 Exceptions.

###### 6.

**6.** There shall be excepted from the special speed limits prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following vehicles when being used in the performance of statutory powers or duties:—

- (*a*) ambulances,

- (*b*) fire brigade vehicles, and

- (*c*) vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochána.

7 Revocation.

###### 7.

**7.** The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Limerick and County of Limerick) Regulations, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 80 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0080.html) ) and the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Limerick and County of Limerick) (Amendment) Regulations, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 28 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0028.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Limerick and County of Limerick) Regulations, 1966 and 1969, and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in these areas.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochána, while being used in the performance of statutory powers or duties, are excepted from the speed limits imposed by these Regulatitons.
