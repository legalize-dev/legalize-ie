# S.I. No. 271/1997 - Youth Work Act, 1997 (Section 22) Regulations, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 271 of 1997.

YOUTH WORK ACT, 1997 (SECTION 22) REGULATIONS, 1997

I, Niamh Bhreathnach, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 22(2)(*a*) of the [Youth Work Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1997), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Youth Work Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Section 22) Regulations, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The body known as the National Youth Council of Ireland is hereby prescribed to be the organisation representing voluntary youth work organisations for the purposes of [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0030/sec0011.html#sec11) (4) of the [Youth Work Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1997).

Given under my Official Seal, this 20th day of June, 1997.

Niamh Bhreathnach,

Minister for Education.
