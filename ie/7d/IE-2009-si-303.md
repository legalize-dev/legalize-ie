# S.I. No. 303/2009 - Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2005 (Commencement) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 303 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0303.html)

INVESTMENT FUNDS, COMPANIES AND MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS ACT 2005 (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th August*, 2009.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 2(1) of the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2005), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (Commencement) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of September 2009 is appointed as the day on which sections 59 and 60 of the [Investment Funds, Companies and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2005) shall come into operation.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 July 2009

**MARY COUGHLAN**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order fixes 1 September 2009 as the date on which sections 59 and 60 of the Act comes into operation. These provisions allow for the reservation of a company name for a specified period of time prior to incorporation.
