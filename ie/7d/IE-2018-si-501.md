# S.I. No. 501/2018 - Planning and Development (Fees) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November*, 2018.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec33) and [262](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec262) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 358 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0358.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Development (Fees) Regulations 2018.

- (2) These Regulations shall be included in the collective citation the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 to 2018.

###### 2.

2. Article 168 of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ) is amended, in sub-article (2), by the insertion of the following paragraph:

> - “(aa) a member of a local authority acting in his or her capacity as such member,”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal

16 November 2018.

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend Article 168 of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ) to provide that the fee for making a submission or observation to a planning authority in respect of a planning application shall not be payable by a member of a local authority acting in his or her capacity as such member.
