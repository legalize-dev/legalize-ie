# S.I. No. 348/2022 - European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th July,* 2022.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 June 2009[^1] as amended by Commission Directive 2020/2088/EU of 11 December 2020[^2] and Commission Directive 2020/2089/EU of 11 December 2020[^3] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These regulations may be cited as the European Union (Safety of Toys) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 8^th day of July 2022.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 1 to the European Union (Safety of Toys) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0014.html) ) is amended by the insertion of the following paragraphs after paragraph 14:

> “15. Commission Directive 2019/1020/EU of 20 June 2019[^4] ,
>
> 16. Commission Directive 2020/2088/EU of 11 December 2020[^2] ,
>
> 17. Commission Directive 2020/2089/EU of 11 December 2020.[^3] ”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

**LEO VARADKAR,**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

These Regulations give effect to two Commission Directives (Directive 2020/2088/EU of 11 December 2020 and Directive 2020/2088/EU of 11 December 2020). Directive 2020/2088/EU of 11 December 2020 amends the allergenic fragrances listed in the table in the third paragraph of point 11 of Part III of Annex II to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on the safety of toys, to include the allergenic fragrances listed in Table 1 3-1 of the Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety (SCCS) opinion of 26 and 27 June 2012 and amend entry 4 of the table in respect of citronellol. Commission Directive 2020/2089/EU of 11 December 2020 amends the table on the first paragraph of point 11 of Part III of Annex II to Directive 2009/48/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on the safety of toys, to include the prohibition of the use of atranol, chloroatranol and methyl heptine carbonate in toys.
