# S.I. No. 369/2008 - European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 369 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0369.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (ADDITIVES, COLOURS AND SWEETENERS IN FOODSTUFFS) (AMENDMENT) (No. 3) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th September*, 2008.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Directive 2008/60/EC [^1] of 17 June 2008 laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs, hereby make the following regulations—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2008.

- (2) The collective citation “the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 to 2008” includes these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0437.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended–

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1), in the definition of “the Directives”, by substituting—

  > “Commission Directive 2008/60/EC7A of 17 June 2008”

for

> “Commission Directive 95/31/EC6 of 5 July 1995 (as amended by Commission Directive 98/66/EC7 of 4 September 1998, Commission Directive 2000/51/EC7A of 26 July 2000, Commission Directive 2001/52/EC7B of 3 July 2001, Commission Directive 2004/46/EC7C of 16 April 2004 and Commission Directive 2006/128/EC7D of 8 December 2006)”

and by the deletion of all previous footnotes to that part of the definition of “the Directives”,

and by the addition of the following footnote—

> “7A OJ L 158, 18.6.2008, p. 17”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 2(1), by substituting for the definition of “Directive 95/31/EC” the following definition:

  > “‘Directive 2008/60/EC’ means Commission Directive 2008/60/EC7A of 17 June 2008 laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs”, and

- (*c*) and by substituting for Regulation 16 the following Regulation:

  > “16. (1) The manufacturer of a product containing a sweetener shall comply with the purity criteria for the relevant sweetener as set out in Annex I to Directive 2008/60/EC.
  >
  > - (2) A person shall not place on the market any product which contains a sweetener which, he or she knows or in the circumstances ought to know, does not comply with purity criteria for the sweetener as set out in Annex I to Directive 2008/60/EC.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 September 2008

**MARY HARNEY**

Minister for Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations give effect to Commission Directive 2008/60/EC1 of 17 June 2008 laying down specific purity criteria concerning sweeteners for use in foodstuffs.

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) Regulations 2000 ( [S.I. No. 437 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0437.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Additives, Colours and Sweeteners in Foodstuffs) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2008.
