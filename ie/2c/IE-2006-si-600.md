# S.I. No. 600/2006 - Commission for Public Service Appointments (Additional Function) (No. 2) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec13) (3) of the [Public Service Management (Recruitment and Appointment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2004) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Commission for Public Service Appointments (Additional Function) (No. 2) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The following additional function is conferred on the Commission for Public Service Appointments:

Codes of practice published by the Commission relating to recruitment and selection processes may make provision, in respect of any such process, that it may be confined to persons with a disability within the meaning of the [Disability Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2005) and to which Part 5 of that Act relates, and may so provide either generally or in respect of any class or classes of such persons.
