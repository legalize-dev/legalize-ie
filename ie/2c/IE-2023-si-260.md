# S.I. No. 260/2023 - National  Minimum  Wage  Act 2000 (Amendment of Schedule 1) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th May,* 2023.

I, NEALE RICHMOND, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec3) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec19) (3) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0014.html) ), and having consulted with such representatives of employers and employees in the State as I consider appropriate, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Minimum Wage Act 2000 (Amendment of Schedule 1) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 1 to the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000) is amended –

- (a) in Part 1, by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph 5:

  > “5. The amount of any service charge distributed to the employee through the payroll (excluding any charge distributed by the employer to which section 4D(1) of the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) applies).”,

  and

- (b) in Part 2, by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph 5:

  > “5. Any amount of —
  >
  > - (a) a tip or gratuity (within the meaning of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) ) paid into a central fund managed by the employer and distributed to the employee through the payroll, or
  >
  > - (b) a charge distributed by the employer (to which section 4D(1) of that Act applies).”.

GIVEN under my Hand,

24 May, 2023.

NEALE RICHMOND,

Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*).

The purpose of these Regulations is to re-classify service charges as a non-reckonable component in the calculation of the average hourly rate of pay. This re-classification will only apply to the sectors in the economy to whom the [Payment of Wages (Amendment) (Tips and Gratuities) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) applies. This will have the effect of ensuring that service charges are not used by employers in those sectors when calculating an employee’s hourly rate of pay for the purposes of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) .
