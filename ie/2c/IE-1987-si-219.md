# S.I. No. 219/1987 - Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 219 of 1987.

HARBOUR RATES (DUNDALK HARBOUR) ORDER, 1987.

I, BRENDAN DALY, Minister for the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 91 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0091.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of the said [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1987.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 7th day of September, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The maximum goods rates to be charged by the Dundalk Harbour Commissioners shall, in lieu of the rates prescribed by Schedule "A" (as amended by the Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 4 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0004.html) )) to the [Dundalk Harbour and Port Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/prv/0003/index.html) (No. 3 (Private) of 1925), be the rates set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The maximum tonnage rates to be charged by the Dundalk Harbour Commissioners shall, in lieu of the rates prescribed by section 43 (as amended by the Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1986) of the [Dundalk Harbour and Port Act, 1925](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1925/en/act/prv/0003/index.html) , be the rates set out in Part II of the Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. The Harbour Rates (Dundalk Harbour) Order, 1986, is hereby revoked.

## PART II MAXIMUM TONNAGE RATES

1. For every vessel entering the harbour of Dundalk on every ton of either the net register tonnage of such vessel or half the gross tonnage of such vessel, whichever is the greater, the sum of 56p per ton.

2. For every vessel remaining in the harbour of Dundalk after the expiration of a consecutive period of six weeks on every ton of either the net register tonnage of such vessel or half the gross tonnage of such vessel, whichever is the greater, the sum of 20p per ton, per week or part thereof.

**BRENDAN DALY,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order authorises Dundalk Harbour Commissioners to charge revised maximum goods and tonnage rates at Dundalk Harbour.
