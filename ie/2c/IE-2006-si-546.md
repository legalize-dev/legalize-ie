# S.I. No. 546/2006 - Credit Union Act 1997 (Alteration of Financial Limits) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 546 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0546.html)**

**REGULATIONS**

entitled

**Credit Union Act 1997 (Alteration of Financial Limits) Regulations 2006**

I, BRIAN COWEN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 182(1)(i) of the [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1997) (as amended by Item 121 of Part 24 of Schedule 1 to the [Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0012/print.html) (No. 12 of 2003)) and after consulting the Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland and the Advisory Committee referred to in [section 180](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/sec0180.html#sec180) of the [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (as amended by Item 119 of Part 24 of Schedule 1 to the said Act of 2003) and the Irish League of Credit Unions, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (Alteration of Financial Limits) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. The [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1997) is amended -

- (a) in section 21(3) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec6) of the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001)) by substituting “€23,000” for “€13,000” in both places where it occurs, and

- (b) in section 23(2) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec6) of the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001)) by substituting “€15,000” for “€6,400”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not a legal part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations increase the limits provided for in Section 21(3) and Section 23(2) of the [Credit Union Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) .
