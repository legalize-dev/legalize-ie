# S.I. No. 677/2024 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) (Amendment) (No. 3) (Jobseeker’s Pay-Related Benefit) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th December,* 2024.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) (amended by reference 1 of the [Schedule to the Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 2024)), [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec14) , [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec30) and [33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec33) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), with the consent of the Minister for Finance given after consultation with the Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) (Amendment) (No. 3) (Jobseeker’s Pay-Related Benefit) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability Regulations) 1996 to 2024 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) Regulations 1996 to 2024.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 31 March 2025.

*Jobseekers Pay-Related Benefit*

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 312 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0312.html) ) are amended –

- (a) in sub-article (2) of article 49 by the insertion of “, jobseeker’s pay-related benefit,” after jobseeker’s benefit (self-employed),”

- (b) in sub-article (5) of article 58 by the insertion of “, or jobseeker’s pay-related benefit,” after “jobseeker’s benefit (self-employed),”

- (c) in sub-article (3) of article 63 is amended by the insertion of “jobseeker’s pay-related benefit,” after “jobseeker’s benefit,”

  and

- (d) in sub-article (2)(b) of article 86 by the insertion of “or jobseeker’s pay-related benefit” after “jobseeker’s benefit”.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) amended the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) to provide for a new social insurance scheme entitled Jobseeker’s Pay-Related Benefit which provides that the amount of benefit a person may get when they become unemployed will be directly linked to their previous earnings. The scheme will commence from 31^st March 2025.

These Regulations, which come into effect from 31^st March 2025, amend the Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 312 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0312.html) ) consequential to the introduction of Jobseeker’s Pay-Related Benefit.
