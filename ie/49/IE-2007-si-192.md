# S.I. No. 192/2007 - Electoral (Amendment) Act 2001 Part 3 Order 2002 (Revocation) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 192 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0192.html)

ELECTORAL (AMENDMENT) ACT 2001 — PART 3 ORDER 2002 (REVOCATION) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th May*, 2007.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(4) of the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2004), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) — Part 3 Order 2002 (Revocation) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) — Part 3 Order 2002 is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 April 2007

**DICK ROCHE**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This order revokes the [Electoral (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) — Part 3 Order 2002 which requires use of electronic voting and counting at Dáil elections in certain constituencies.
