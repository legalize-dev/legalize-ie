S.I. No. 369 of 1998.

COUNTY OF LOUTH LOCAL ELECTORAL AREAS ORDER, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the County of Louth Local Electoral Areas Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. (1) The County of Louth shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area set forth in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of the council of the County of Louth to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set forth in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 3.

3. Every reference in the Schedule to this Order to an electoral division, borough or urban district shall be construed as referring to such electoral division, borough or urban district as existing at the date of this Order.

###### 4.

4. (1) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along any road, street, place or bridge shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road, street, place or bridge.

- (2) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which any road, street, place or bridge, or imaginary projection intersects or joins a boundary shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road, street, place or bridge, or in the case of an imaginary projection along the projection, would intersect or join such boundary.

###### 5.

5. The Louth County Council Order 1942, the Louth County Council (Amendment) Order, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0032.html) ) and Article 8 of the County Electoral Areas (Amendment) Order, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 109 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0109.html) ) are hereby revoked.

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of County Louth into county electoral areas and to fix the number of members to be elected for each electoral area.
