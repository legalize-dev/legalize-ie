# S.I. No. 260/2013 - Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 260 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0260.html)

GARDA SÍOCHÁNA (RETIREMENT) REGULATIONS 2013

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th July*, 2013.

I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 122](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec122) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2005) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), after consulting with the Commissioner of the Garda Síochána and with the approval of the Government, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. Notwithstanding anything contained in—

- (*a*) the Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1934 ( [S.R. & O. No. 146 of 1934](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1934/en/si/0146.html) ),

- (*b*) the Garda Síochána (Retirement) (No. 2) Regulations 1951 ( [S.I. No. 335 of 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/si/0335.html) ),

- (*c*) the Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1990 ( [S.I. No. 318 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0318.html) ),

- (*d*) the Garda Síochána (Retirement) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 16 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0016.html) ),

as continued in force by [section 128](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec128) of the [Garda Síochána Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) , the member of the Garda Síochána who, immediately before the making of these regulations, holds the rank of Commissioner of the Garda Síochána shall retire on 7 August 2015.

The Government hereby approves of the making of the foregoing regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government

9 July 2013.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 July 2013.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
