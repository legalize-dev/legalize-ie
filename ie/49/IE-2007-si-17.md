**S.I. No. 17 of 2007**

**SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) ORDER 2007**

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 48 (1) of the Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001 (No. 33 of 2001), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to this Order are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of Part 10 of the Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001 (No. 33 of 2001).

###### 3.

3. This Order is deemed to have come into effect on 16 July 2001.

## **SCHEDULE**

Local authorities and other bodies to which schemes and regulations made under the Local Government (Superannuation) Act 1980 (No. 8 of 1980) apply.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order designates Local authorities and other bodies to which schemes and regulations made under the Local Government (Superannuation) Act 1980 (No. 8 of 1980) apply as an approved organisations for the purposes of Part 10 of the Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001.
