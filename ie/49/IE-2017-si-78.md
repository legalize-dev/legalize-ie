# S.I. No. 78/2017 - Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th March*, 2017.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving full effect to Council Regulation (EC) No 811/2004 of 21 April 2004[^1] (as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009[^2] ), Council Regulation (EC) No 1342/2008 of 18 December 2008[^3] (as amended by Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009, Council Regulation (EU) No 1243/2012 of 19 December 2012[^4] and Regulation (EU) 2016/2094 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 November 2016[^5] ), Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009 (as amended by Regulation (EU) No 1379/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013[^6] , Council Regulation (EU) No 1385/2013 of 17 December 2013[^7] , Regulation (EU) No 508/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^8] and Regulation (EU) No 2015/812 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2015[^9] ) and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011[^10] (other than Title VII) (as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1962 of 28 October 2015[^11] ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Community Control System) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 54 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0054.html) ) is amended by-

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations provide for the designation of Renard Pier for landings of Hake greater than 2 tonnes in accordance with Article 9 of Council Regulation (EC) No 811/2004 of 21 April 2004 establishing measures for the recovery of the Northern Hake Stock.
