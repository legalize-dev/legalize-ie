# S.I. No. 119/1975 - Pensions (Increase) (No. 2) Regulations, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 119 of 1975.

PENSIONS (INCREASE) (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1975.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for the Public Service, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1964), and the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pensions (Increase) (No. 2) Regulations, 1975.

###### 2.

2. For the purposes of these Regulations and subsequent Regulations under [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1964), the Schedule to that Act shall have effect, with effect from the 1st day of July, 1974, as if the following paragraph were added to Part 1 of that Schedule—

> "( *x* ) A pension granted under [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963)."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 9th day of June, 1975.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for the Public Service.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the addition to the Schedule to the [Pensions (Increase) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , of the pensions mentioned in Paragraph 2 of these Regulations.
