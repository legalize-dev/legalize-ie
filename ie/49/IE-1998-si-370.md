S.I. No. 370 of 1998.

BOROUGH OF DROGHEDA LOCAL ELECTORAL AREAS ORDER, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0003.html#sec3) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Local Government Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1994), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Borough of Drogheda Local Electoral Areas Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Borough" means the Borough of Drogheda as altered for the purposes of local elections by the Local Government (Boundaries) (Town Elections) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 114 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0114.html) ) and references to the borough boundary shall be construed accordingly.

###### 3.

3. (1) The Borough of Drogheda shall be divided into the local electoral areas which are named in the first column of the Schedule to this Order.

- (2) Each such local electoral area shall consist of the area set forth in the second column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of such local electoral area.

- (3) The number of members of the council of the Borough of Drogheda to be elected for each such local electoral area shall be the number set forth in the third column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

- (4) The number of the said members to be elected for each such local electoral area who are to be aldermen shall be the number set forth in the fourth column of the Schedule to this Order opposite the name of that local electoral area.

###### 4.

4. (1) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to a line drawn along any bridge, river, road or street shall be construed as a reference to an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such bridge, river, road or street.

- (2) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which any bridge, river, road or street intersects any other bridge, river, road or street shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of one would be intersected by an imaginary line drawn along the centre of the other.

- (3) A reference in the Schedule to this Order to the point at which a road or river intersects the borough boundary shall be construed as a reference to the point at which an imaginary line drawn along the centre of such road or river would intersect the said boundary.

###### 5.

5. The Borough of Drogheda Local Electoral Areas Order, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 110 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0110.html) ) is hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to amend the division of the Borough of Drogheda into local electoral areas and to fix the number of members to be elected for each electoral area.
