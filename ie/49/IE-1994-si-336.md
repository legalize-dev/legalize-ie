# S.I. No. 336/1994 - Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 336 of 1994.

MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5 and sub-section (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [sections 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) and [72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** The Regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 1994.

2 Rate of Payment.

###### 2.

**2.** Where a person is or becomes entitled to—

- (*a*) payment under sub-section (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 236 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0236.html) ); or

- (*b*) payment of a maintenance allowance under [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in accordance with the Disabled Persons Maintenance Allowances Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 237 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0237.html) ),

any amount so payable in respect of the week commencing on the 5th December, 1994 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20.00 whichever is the greater.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health, this 11th day of November, 1994.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 11th day of November, 1994.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week ending the 5th December, 1994, the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) and [section 69](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0069.html#sec69) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , by a sum equal to seventy per cent of that amount or £20.00, whichever is the greater.
