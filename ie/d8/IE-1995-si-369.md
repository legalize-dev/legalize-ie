# S.I. No. 369/1995 - Local Government Act, 1991 (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 369 of 1995.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT ACT, 1991 (REGIONAL AUTHORITIES) (ESTABLISHMENT ORDER) 1993, (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ORDER, 1995.

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) and [43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0043.html#sec43) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No.11 of 1991), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1995.

###### 2.

2. This Order, the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 208 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0208.html) ) and the principal order shall be construed together as one.

###### 3.

3. In this Order, the "principal order" means the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 394 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0394.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Article 16 of the principal order is hereby amended by the addition after paragraph (*e*) of the following paragraph:—

> - "(*f*) in the case of each of the Border, Mid West and West regional authorities, the need for co-ordination with the Western Development Partnership Board Limited".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, this

20th day of December, 1995.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Order.

DATED this 20th day of December, 1995.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order amends the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 394 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0394.html) ) in relation to certain co-ordination matters.
