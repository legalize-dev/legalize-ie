# S.I. No. 172/1986 - Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 172 of 1986.

MISUSE OF DRUGS (LICENCE FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 14 and 38 of the Misuse of Drugs Act,1977 (No. 12 of 1977), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendments) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. Sub-article (1) of article 4 of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0164.html) ), as amended by the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 29 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0029.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-paragraph (a) of "£105" for "£60", in sub-paragraph (b) of "£70" for "£40", in sub-paragraph (c) of "£70" for "£40", in sub-paragraph (d) of "£35" for "£20", in sub-paragraph (e) of "£18" or "£10", and in sub-paragraph (f) of "£35" for "£20".

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 1986.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this

28th day of May, 1986.

**RUAIRI QUINN,**

Minister for Labour.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this

28th day of May, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for increases in the fees payable to the Minister for Health in respect of certain licences granted under the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) .
