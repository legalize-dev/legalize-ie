# S.I. No. 210/2020 - Guardianship of Children (Statutory Declaration) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th June,* 2020.

The Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 2(4)(e) (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0040/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Children Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1997)) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )) and section 6B(4)(c) (inserted by [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec49) of the [Children and Family Relationships Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2015)) of the [Guardianship of Infants Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Guardianship of Children (Statutory Declaration) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Act of 1964” means the [Guardianship of Infants Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964).

###### 3.

3. The form of statutory declaration set out in Schedule 1 is hereby prescribed for the purpose of section 2(4)(e) (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0040/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Children Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1997)) of the Act of 1964.

###### 4.

4. The form of statutory declaration set out in Schedule 2 is hereby prescribed for the purpose of section 6B(4)(c) (inserted by [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec49) of the [Children and Family Relationships Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2015)) of the Act of 1964.

###### 5.

5. The Guardianship of Children (Statutory Declaration) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0005.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations set out in Schedule 1 the form of statutory declaration prescribed for the purpose of section 2(4)(e) (amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0040/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Children Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1997)) of the [Guardianship of Infants Act 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964) to be made by the mother and father of a non-marital child who wish the father to become a guardian of the child jointly with the mother.

These Regulations also set out in Schedule 2 the form of statutory declaration prescribed for the purpose of section 6B(4)(c) (inserted by [section 49](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0009/print.html#sec49) of the [Children and Family Relationships Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0009/print.html) (No. 9 of 2015)) of the Act of 1964 to be made by the mother and the other parent of a child (where the other parent is, along with the mother, a parent of the child under Section 5 of the Act of 2015) and both agree to the other parent becoming a guardian of the child jointly with the mother.

The Guardianship of Children (Statutory Declaration) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 5 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0005.html) ) are revoked by these Regulations.
