# S.I. No. 34/1973 - Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972 (Period For The Purpose of Section 4) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 34 of 1973.

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY ACT, 1972 (PERIOD FOR THE PURPOSE OF SECTION 4) ORDER, 1973.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (Period for the Purpose of Section 4) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The period of ninety days is hereby fixed for the purpose of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1972).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 8th day of February, 1973.

**GERARD COLLINS,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order fixes a period of 90 days (from the date of registration as a dealer) within which dealers must provide the Minister for Posts and Telegraphs with information about certain television set transactions which took place on or before 31 March, 1973 and for which payments are due on or after that date.
