# S.I. No. 436/2016 - Criminal Justice Act 1994 (Section 44) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th August*, 2016.

I, FRANCES FITZGERALD, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0015/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Criminal Justice Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1994) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations with respect to which, pursuant to that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Justice Act 1994 (Section 44) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 3rd day of August, 2016.

###### 3.

3. The sum of €1,000 is hereby prescribed as the prescribed sum for the purposes of [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0015/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Criminal Justice Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. The Criminal Justice Act, 1994 (Section 44) Regulations, 1996 (S.I. 167 of 1996) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this

1 August 2016.

**FRANCES FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.
