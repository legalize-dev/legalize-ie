# S.I. No. 348/1995 - Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Application For Registration) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 348 of 1995.

MILK (REGULATION OF SUPPLY) (APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, IVAN YATES, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Application for Registration) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, "the Act" means the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. (1) An application under subsection (1) (*b*) of section 7 of the Act for registration in a register of producers, by a producer who is a party to a registered contract, shall be in the Form M.A.1 of the Schedule hereto.

- (2) An application under subsection (1) (*c*) (i) of section 7 of the Act for registration in a register of processors, by a processor who is a party to a registered contract, shall be in the Form M.A.2 of the Schedule hereto.

- (3) An application under subsection (1) (*c*) (ii) of section 7 of the Act for registration in a register of processors by a processor who processes milk produced on the processor's own milk production holding shall be in the Form M.A.3. of the Schedule hereto.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of December, 1995.

**IVAN YATES,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry.
