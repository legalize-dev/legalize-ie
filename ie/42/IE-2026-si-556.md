The Veterinary Council of Ireland, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec33) and [section 133](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec133) of the [Veterinary Practice Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2005) (as amended), hereby makes the following regulations:-

## **Part 1** *Preliminary*

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the Veterinary Council of Ireland (Fees) Regulations 2026.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These regulations shall come into force on 16^th November 2026.

*Interpretation*

###### 3.

3. (1) In these regulations—

*“Act”* means the [Veterinary Practice Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2005), as amended;

*“Council”* means the Veterinary Council of Ireland established under section 11 of the Act; and

*“Registrar”* means the chief executive officer of the Council appointed under section 23 of the Act.

- (2) Any other word or expression that is used in these regulations and is also used in the Act shall have in these regulations the same meaning as it has in the Act unless the contrary intention appears.

## ***Part 2*** *Fees*

*Setting of fees*

###### 4.

4. The fees payable to the Council for various purposes referred to in the Act shall be the amounts indicated in Column 3 of the Schedule opposite the description of the fee in Column 2 of the said Schedule.

*Discretions available to the Council in respect of fees*

###### 5.

5. (1) The Council may, in circumstances where it considers it appropriate to do so, waive, remit or refund, either in whole or in part, any fee that would otherwise be payable to it under these regulations.

- (2) Where a fee is payable in respect of any matter prescribed by these regulations, the Council, Registrar or any other member of the staff of the Council may refuse to do any act, or permit any act to be done, or to receive any document in respect of which that fee is payable unless the fee is paid.

*Revocation*

###### 6.

6. The Veterinary Council of Ireland (Fees) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 279 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0279.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These regulations set out the fees payable to the Council in relation to the performance of certain functions under the Act. The fees shall be the amounts indicated in Column 3 of the Schedule opposite the description of the fee in Column 2 of the said Schedule.

These regulations may be cited as the Veterinary Council of Ireland (Fees) Regulations 2026.

These regulations come into operation on 16^th November 2026.
