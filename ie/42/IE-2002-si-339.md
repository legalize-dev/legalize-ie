# S.I. No. 339/2002 - Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) (No. 2) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 2 (No. 2) Regulations 2002.

###### 2.

2. (1) Vodafone Ireland p.l.c. is, subject to paragraph (2) of this Regulation prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (g) of the definition of “credit institution” in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995).

- (2) Vodafone Ireland p.l.c. is not to be regarded as prescribed for the foregoing purposes in any case where the APR charged by it in respect of any credit granted to a consumer equals or exceeds 23 per cent.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations further supplement the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) to include Vodafone Ireland p.l.c.
