# S.I. No. 156/2013 - Control of Dogs (Amendment) Regulations, 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th May*, 2013.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0005.html#sec5) , [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0019.html#sec19) , [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0028.html#sec28) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Control of Dogs Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1986) (as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation.*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Dogs (Amendment) Regulations, 2013.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of June, 2013.

*Interpretation.*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations:— (1) “Principal Regulations” means the Control of Dogs Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 442 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0442.html) ) as amended by the Control of Dogs Regulations, 2011 ( [S.I. No. 700 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0700.html) ). (2) “Act” means the [Control of Dogs Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1986).

*Exemptions from certain requirements.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by inserting after Article 7(1)(*d*) the following:

> - “(*e*) kept by the Canine Unit of the Irish Prison Service,”.

*Exemption from holding a licence.*

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended by inserting after Article 8(*b*) the following:

> “or
>
> - (*c*) a member of the Canine Unit of the Irish Prison Service,”.

*Prescribed amount for certain offences.*

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended by substituting for Article 10 the following:

> “10.The prescribed amount for the purpose of Section 28(1) of the Act shall be €100.”.

*Prescribed form.*

###### 7.

7. The Second Schedule to the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution of the form set out in the Schedule for Form No. 3.
