# S.I. No. 268/1975 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1975

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 268 of 1975.

DIPLOMATIC AND CONSULAR FEES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1975.

I, GARRET FITZGERALD, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931), and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1975.

- (2) The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 ( [S.I. No. 263 of 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/si/0263.html) ), the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 334 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0334.html) ) and these Regulations may be cited together as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 to 1975.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the first day of January, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. Article 8 (3) of the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956, is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 20th day of November, 1975.

**GARRET FITZGERALD.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 20th day of November, 1975.

**RICHIE RYAN.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations are made under the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931) and the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939) and they remove the requirement for the use of Consular Service stamps in connection with fees for services rendered under the foregoing Acts.
