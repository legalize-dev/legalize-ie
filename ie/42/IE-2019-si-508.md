# S.I. No. 508/2019 - Adoption Act 2010 (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 18*th October,* 2019.

I, REGINA DOHERTY, Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 89](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec89) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010), following consultation with the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs (pursuant to article 3 and the Schedule of the Office of the Minister for Children and Youth Affairs (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 218 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0218.html) )), and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform (pursuant to article 3 and Schedule 1 of the Finance (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0418.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2019.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation from the date of signature.

*Interpretation*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations –

“Abridged Certificate” means, as respects an entry in the Adopted Children Register, a certificate of the items set out in the Schedule to these Regulations endorsed in the manner set out in the said Schedule.

“uncertified copy” (of an Abridged Certificate) means a copy of the items set out in the Schedule to these Regulations that is not endorsed in the manner set out in the said Schedule.

*Prescribed fees*

###### 4.

4. (1) An tArd-Chláraitheoir shall issue an Abridged Certificate to any applicant on payment of a fee of €20.

- (2) An tArd-Chláraitheoir shall issue an uncertified copy of an Abridged Certificate to any applicant on payment of a fee of €4.

*Form of application*

###### 5.

5. (1) In order to allow an tArd-Chláraitheoir to issue an Abridged Certificate, or an uncertified copy, the application shall contain as many as possible of the following particulars:

- (*a*) the forename,

- (*b*) the date and country of birth,

- (*c*) the name, address and occupation of the adopter or adopters,

- (*d*) the date of the adoption order.

- (2) The fact that any of the information specified in Regulation 5(1) of these Regulations is not furnished shall not be a valid reason for an tArd-Chláraitheoir to refuse to issue an Abridged Certificate, or an uncertified copy, if he or she is satisfied that the appropriate entry can be identified.

*Revocation*

###### 6.

6. The [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 611 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0611.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The effect of this Order is:

- • to allow for the issue of an Abridged Certificate, containing specified particulars, from a register of adoptions maintained by an tArd-Chláraitheoir,

- • to extend the list of particulars to include details of “parent(s)” as well as details of “mother” and “father”,

- • to increase the fee to be charged for the issue of such Abridged Certificates to €20, in line with fees for other similar certificates, and

- • to make provision for an uncertified copy, for a fee of €4, in line with fees for other similar uncertified copies.
