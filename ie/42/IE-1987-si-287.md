# S.I. No. 287/1987 - Local Government (Planning and Development) (Exempted Development) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 287 of 1987.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT) (EXEMPTED DEVELOPMENT) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0004.html#sec4) and [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963), hereby makes the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (Planning and Development) (Exempted Development) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1985 shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 to 1987.

###### 3.

3. The Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0065.html) ) are hereby amended by the deletion of Class 21 in columns 1 and 2 of Part I of the Third Schedule and the substitution therefor of the following matter—

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 6th day of November, 1987.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Local Government (Planning and Development) Regulations, 1977 by restating the categories of exempted development for the purposes of the Local Government (Planning and Development) Acts, 1963 to 1983 having regard to the assumption by Bord Gáis Éireann of functions corresponding to those available to the Dublin Gas Company under the Alliance and Dublin Gas Acts 1866 - 1909
