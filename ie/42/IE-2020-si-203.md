# S.I. No. 203/2020 - European Union (Seed Potatoes) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th June,* 2020.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2020/177 of 11 February 2020[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Seed Potatoes) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Seed Potatoes) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 641 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0641.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “Directive” (as amended by European Union (Seed Potatoes) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 199 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0199.html) )) the following:

> “‘Directive’ means Council Directive 2002/56/EC of 13 June 2002[^2] (as amended by Council Directive 2003/61/EC of 18 June 2003[^3] , Commission Implementing Directive 2013/63/EU of 17 December 2013[^4] , Commission Implementing Decision 2014/367/EU of 16 June 2014[^5] and Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2016/317 of 3 March 2016[^6] ), and Commission Decision 2004/3/EC of 19 December 2003[^7] (as amended by 2014/105/EU: Commission Implementing Decision of 24 February 2014[^8] ), Commission Decision 2004/842/EC of 1 December 20004[^9] (as amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/320 of 3 March 2016[^10] ); Commission Implementing Directive 2014/20/EU of 6 February 2011[^11] , Commission Implementing Directive 2014/21/EU of 6 February 2014[^12] , Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/547 of 21 March 2017[^13] and Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2020/177 of the 11 February 2020;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 May, 2020.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations give effect to the provisions of Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2020/177 by amending the Annexes I and II to Directive 2002/56/EC as they relate to inspections for Union regulated non-quarantine pests (RNQPs) and measures intended to prevent their presence on seed potatoes for planting.
