# S.I. No. 244/1988 - Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Act, 1988 (Determination of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 244 of 1988.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (MULTI-STOREY BUILDINGS) ACT, 1988 (DETERMINATION OF MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1988.

I, CHARLES J. HAUGHEY, Taoiseach, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order and determine as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government (Multi-storey Buildings) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (Determination of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1988.

###### 2.

2. Whereas a doubt has arisen as to the Minister in whom the functions conferred by [sections 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/sec0011.html#sec11) (1) and [15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/sec0015.html#sec15) (2) of the [Local Government (Multi-Storey Buildings) Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1988), are vested, it is hereby determined that the said functions are vested in the Minister for the Environment.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of September, 1988.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to designate the Minister for the Environment as the appropriate Minister for the purposes of the Act.
