# S.I. No. 46/1988 - Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 46 of 1988.

PLANT VARIETIES (PROPRIETARY RIGHTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1988.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0012.html#sec12) and [26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988.

- (2) The Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Regulations, 1981 to 1986 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Regulations, 1981 to 1988.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Act" means the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980).

###### 3.

3. (1) The species specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby specified for the purpose of section 4 of the Act.

- (2) The maximum period to be specified in a grant of plant breeders' rights as being that in which the rights thereby granted are to be exercisable in respect of a plant variety which is of the species specified in the said Schedule shall be a period of 20 years.

- (3) For the purpose of paragraph (*d*) (ii) of Article 2 of the First Schedule to the Act, the period of four years is hereby prescribed in relation to every plant variety which is of a species specified in the said Schedule.

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of section 12 of the Act all plant varieties shall be regarded as being within the same class if they are each a plant variety of any species of the genus potentilla.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations extend the list of plant species for which plant breeder's rights may be granted to include the species Potentilla fruticosa L.
