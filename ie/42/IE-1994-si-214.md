# S.I. No. 214/1994 - Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) and [243](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0243.html#sec243) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations, 1994.

2 Interpretation.

###### 2.

**2.** In these Regulations "the Principal Act" means the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) .

3 Application of Principal Act.

###### 3.

**3.** Paragraph (i) of Rule 1 (4) of Part II of the Third Schedule to the Principal Act shall apply, without modification, to the calculation of weekly means for the purposes of ascertaining a person's entitlement to supplementary welfare allowance pursuant to section 117 of the said Act.

4 Calculation of means.

###### 4.

**4.** In calculating the weekly means of a person for supplementary welfare allowance pursuant to section 177 of the Principal Act, there shall be included in the list of exclusions set out in Rule 1 (1) of Part III of the Third Schedule of the said Act the following paragraph:

> - "(k) any income arising from a grant or allowance in pursuance of a scheme for promoting the welfare of the blind prepared under section 2 of the Blind Persons Act, 1920.".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 12th day of July, 1994.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide that the Blind Welfare Allowance will not be taken into account in calculating means for the purposes of ascertaining a person's entitlement to Supplementary Welfare Allowance.
