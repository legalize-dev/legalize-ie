# S.I. No. 90/1996 - An Bord Bia Act, 1994 (Levy on Slaughtered Or Exported Livestock) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 90 of 1996.

AN BORD BIA ACT, 1994 (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1996

I, Ivan Yates, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/sec0037.html#sec37) (3) of [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1994), hereby make the following Order, as respects which, pursuant to section 37(4) of the said Act, a draft has been approved by a resolution of each House of the Oireachtas:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The rate of levy prescribed in paragraph (*c*) of [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/sec0037.html#sec37) (2) of [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (as varied by the [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 197 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0197.html) ) is hereby varied by the substitution in that paragraph of "20p" for "1p".

###### 3.

3. The [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Levy on Slaughtered or Exported Livestock) Order, 1995, is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of April, 1996

**IVAN YATES**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry
