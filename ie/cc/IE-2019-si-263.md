I, DAMIEN ENGLISH, Minister of State at the Department for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0003.html#sec3) and [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Building Control Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990) as amended and the Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 131 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0131.html) ) hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Building Regulations (Part F Amendment) Regulations 2019.

- (2) The Building Regulations 1997 - 2017 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Building Regulations 1997 to 2019 and shall be construed together as one.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. Subject to Regulation 5, these Regulations shall come into operation on 1 November 2019.

*Interpretation generally*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations: –

“Building Regulations 1997 - 2019” means the Building Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 497 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0497.html) ) as amended by the Building Regulations (Amendment) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 556 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0556.html) );

“Directive” means Directive 2010/31/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 May 2010 on the energy performance of buildings (recast);

“Substantial work has been completed” means that the structure of the external walls of the dwelling has been erected;

*Application*

###### 4.

4. These Regulations shall apply to works, to a building in which a material change of use takes place or to a building which undergoes a major renovation, where the relevant works, change of use or major renovation commence or take place, as the case may be, on or after 1^st November 2019 except where -

- (a) a planning application is made on or before 31^st October 2019 for planning permission or approval pursuant to the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) and where substantial work has been completed by 31^st October 2020; or

- (b) a notice pursuant to the provisions of Part 8 of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 600 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0600.html) ) has been published on or before 31^st October 2019 and where substantial work has been completed by 31^st October 2020.

*Amendments to the Building Regulations 1997 - 2019*

###### 5.

5. The Building Regulations 1997 - 2019 are amended by: -

- (a) substituting for Part F of the Second Schedule, the following -

  > ### “Part F
  >
  > Ventilation
  >
  > *Means of Ventilation.*
  >
  > F1 Adequate and effective means of ventilation shall be provided for people in buildings. This shall be achieved by
  >
  > a) limiting the moisture content of the air within the building so that it does not contribute to condensation and mould growth, and
  >
  > b) limiting the concentration of harmful pollutants in the air within the building.
  >
  > *Condensation in roofs.*
  >
  > F2 Adequate provision shall be made to prevent excessive condensation in a roof or in a roof void above an insulated ceiling.”

GIVEN under my hand,

17 June 2019

DAMIEN ENGLISH,,

Minister of State at the Department of Housing, Planning and Local Government

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend Part F (Ventilation) of the Second Schedule to the Building Regulations 1997 – 2018 to set effective standards of ventilation and support the measures introduced in Part L Regulations 2017 European Union (Energy Performance of Buildings) Regulations 2019– [S.I. No. 538 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0538.html) and [S.I. No. 183 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0183.html) .

The regulations apply in respect of dwellings which are commenced on or after 1^st November 2019.

Transitional arrangements apply in relation to buildings for which planning permission or approval is applied for on or before 31^st October 2019, and where substantial work has been completed by 31^st October 2020.
