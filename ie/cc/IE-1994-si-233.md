SOCIAL WELFARE (AMENDMENT OF MISCELLANEOUS SOCIAL INSURANCE PROVISIONS) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) , [53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0053.html#sec53) , [54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0054.html#sec54) , [57](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0057.html#sec57) , [84](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0084.html#sec84) , [89](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0089.html#sec89) and [101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0101.html#sec101) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

PART I General

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Amendment of Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

"the Regulations of 1988" means the Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 263 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0263.html) );

"the Regulations of 1993" means the Social Welfare (Amendment of Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 231 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0231.html) ).

PART II Increased Rates of Benefits and Pensions under the Occupational Injuries Scheme

###### 3.

3. This Part shall come into operation on the 25th day of July, 1994.

###### 4.

4. The Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Regulations, 1967 ( [S.I. No. 77 of 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/si/0077.html) ) are hereby amended by—

- (*a*) the substitution for the Second Schedule thereto (inserted by article 4 of the Regulations of 1993) of the Schedule set out in Schedule A to this Part, subject in respect of the Scale set out in the said Second Schedule (as amended by these Regulations), to the following provisions:—

    - (i) the rates of pension specified in column (3) of the Scale shall have effect on and from the 25th day of July, 1994 whether the period taken into account by the assessment began on, before or after that date, and,

    - (ii) the amounts of gratuity specified in column (2) of the Scale shall be payable only where the period taken into account by the assessment of the degree of disablement began on or after the 25th day of July, 1994,

- (*b*) the substitution for the Fourth Schedule thereto (inserted by article 4 of the Regulations of 1993) of the Schedule set out in Schedule B to this Part.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Social Welfare Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) provided for increases in the maximum rates of social insurance payments arising from the 1994 Budget, with effect from the end of July, 1994. These Regulations provide for increases in the reduced rates of various social insurance payments from the end of July, 1994.

*Part II.*

Part II of the Regulations provides for increases in the rates of benefit payable under the Occupational Injuries Benefits scheme—

—in respect of disablement gratuities appropriate to degrees of disablement assessed at 19 per cent or under,

—in respect of Disablement Pension payable in lieu of such gratuities,

—in respect of Injury Benefit payable to persons under the age of sixteen, and

—in respect of certain Constant Attendance Allowances.

*Part III*.

Part III of the Regulations provides for increases in the reduced rates of Old Age (Contributory) Pension, Widow's (Contributory) Pension and Retirement Pension where the contribution conditions for receipt of the maximum rates are not satisfied.

*Part IV.*

Part IV of the Regulations provides for increases in the reduced rates of Old Age (Contributory) Pension and Widow's (Contributory) Pension payable to certain people who came back into insurance on the abolition of the earnings limit for liability for social insurance contributions in April, 1974, but who did not have sufficient contributions paid to entitle them to maximum rate pensions.
