# S.I. No. 484/2019 - Referendum Commission (Establishment) (No. 2) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st September,* 2019.

I, Eoghan Murphy, Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsections (1) and (8) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Referendum Commission (Establishment) (No.2) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. A Referendum Commission is established to perform the functions conferred on it by the Referendum Act 1998, as amended by the Referendum Act 2001, in relation to the proposal for an amendment of the Constitution contained in the Thirty-ninth Amendment of the Constitution (Presidential Elections) Bill 2019.

###### 3.

3. The 26th day of September 2019 is appointed to be the day for the purposes of section 2(5) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the chairperson of the Referendum Commission.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government

26 September 2019.

**EOGHAN MURPHY**

Minister For Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order establishes a Referendum Commission for the purposes of the Referendum Acts 1998 and 2001 in connection with the Thirty-ninth Amendment of the Constitution (Presidential Elections) Bill 2019 and appoints 26 September, 2019 as the day on which the position of chairperson of the Commission takes effect.
