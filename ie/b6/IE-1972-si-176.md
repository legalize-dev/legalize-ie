# S.I. No. 176/1972 - Industrial Development Act, 1969 (Designated Areas) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 176 of 1972.

INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT ACT, 1969 (DESIGNATED AREAS) ORDER, 1972.

I, PATRICK J. LALOR, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0032/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Industrial Development Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1969), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Order :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Industrial Development Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (Designated Areas) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 28th day of July, 1972.

###### 3.

3. The areas in the counties of Meath and Waterford for the time being determined to be Gaeltacht areas by order under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0021/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1956), are hereby declared to be designated areas.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of July, 1972.

**PATRICK J. LALOR.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 7th day of July, 1972.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to declare to be designated areas for the purposes of the [Industrial Development Act, 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) , the areas in the counties of Meath and Waterford which are classified as Gaeltacht areas, for as long as they shall be so classified.
