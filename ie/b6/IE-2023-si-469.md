# S.I. No. 469/2023 - European Union (Cross - Border Conversions, Mergers and Divisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th September,* 2023.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2017/1132 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017[^1] and Directive (EU) 2019/2121 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 November 2019[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cross-Border Conversions, Mergers and Divisions) (Amendment) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Cross-Border Conversions, Mergers and Divisions) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 233 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0233.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended, in Part 1, by the insertion of the following Regulation after Regulation 3:

> “**Penalties**
>
> 3A. A person guilty of an offence under these Regulations shall be liable –
>
> - (a) on summary conviction, to a class A fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or both, or
>
> - (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding €50,000 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or both.”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 4 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the insertion of the following definition:

> “ ‘converting company’, in relation to a cross-border conversion, means a company that is carrying out, or is proposing to carry out, the cross-border conversion;”.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 9 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution of “an offence” for “a category 2 offence” in each place where it occurs.

###### 6.

6. Regulation 11 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution of “an offence” for “a category 2 offence” in each place where it occurs.

###### 7.

7. Regulation 21 of the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph (4), by the substitution of “an offence” for “a category 2 offence”.

###### 8.

8. Regulation 44 of the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph (4), by the substitution of “an offence” for “a category 2 offence”.

###### 9.

9. Regulation 66 of the Principal Regulations is amended, in paragraph (4), by the substitution of “an offence” for “a category 2 offence”.

###### 10.

10. Regulation 91 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution of “an offence” for “a category 2 offence” in each place where it occurs.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 September, 2023.

**SIMON COVENEY,**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the European Union (Cross-Border Conversions, Mergers and Divisions) Regulations 2023, ( [S.I. No. 233 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0233.html) ).

Regulation 3A “Penalties” is introduced and Regulations 9, 11, 21, 44, 66 and 91 are amended to align accordingly.

Regulation 4 is amended to include a definition of ‘converting company’.
