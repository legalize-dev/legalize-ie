# S.I. No. 359/1999 - Litter Pollution Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [Sections 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/sec0028.html#sec28) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Litter Pollution Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1997), which said powers were delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 428 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0428.html) ), I, DAN WALLACE, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Litter Pollution Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 3rd January, 2000.

###### 3.

3. The prescribed amount for the purpose of [Section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Litter Pollution Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) shall be £50.

###### 4.

4. Forms A and B set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed forms for the purposes of offences to which [Section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Litter Pollution Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) applies.

###### 5.

5. The Litter Pollution Regulations, 1997 ( [No. 214 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0214.html) ) are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations increase the “on-the-spot” fine for litter offences, relate to procedural matters of prescribing the “on-the-spot” fine notice for such offences and revoke the Litter Pollution Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 214 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0214.html) ).
