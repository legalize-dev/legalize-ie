# S.I. No. 277/1996 - European Communities (Consumer Credit Act, 1995) (Amendment) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 277 of 1996.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (CONSUMER CREDIT ACT, 1995) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1996

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purposes of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 87/102/EEC of 22 December 1986^1, as amended by Council Directive No. 90/88/EEC of 22 February 1990^2, hereby make the following regulations:

^1O.J. No. L42, 12.2.87, p48

^2O.J. No. L61, 10.3.90, p14

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities ( [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ) (Amendment) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), is hereby amended—

- (a) by the substitution in the definition of "credit" contained in section 2(1) for ", or any other form of financial accommodation" of "or other similar financial accommodation", and

- (b) by the substitution in section 3(1) for "Subject to subsections (2) and (3)," of "Subject to this Act,".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of September, 1996.

**RICHARD BRUTON**

Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The amendment to the definition of "credit" as contained in section 2(1) and to [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) bring the Act into line with the aims of Council Directive 87/102/EEC.
