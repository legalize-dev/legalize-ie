# S.I. No. 248/1973 - Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1973) Order 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 248 of 1973.

DUBLIN DISTRICT MILK BOARD (ELECTION DAY, 1973) ORDER 1973.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/sec0011.html#sec11) (2) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply and Price) Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0043/index.html) (No. 43 of 1936), as adapted by the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dublin District Milk Board (Election Day, 1973) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The 30th day of October, 1973, is hereby appointed to be an election day in respect of the Dublin District Milk Board.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of August,

1973.

**MARK CLINTON,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints a day in 1973 on which ordinary members of the Dublin District Milk Board shall be elected.
