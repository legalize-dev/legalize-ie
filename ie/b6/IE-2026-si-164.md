# S.I. No. 164/2026 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Learner Permit Form) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 21*st April,* 2026.

I, SEAN CANNEY, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0042.html#sec42) (2)(b) (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec10) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), and the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 144 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0144.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Learner Permit Form) Regulations 2026.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 27 April 2026.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) are amended—

- (a) in Regulation 17, by substituting for paragraph (1) the following:

  > “17. (1) (a) A learner permit shall be in scheduled form D.202.
  >
  > - (b) In learner permit form D.202—
  >
  >     - (i) ‘Date of issue’ indicates the date on which the learner permit concerned comes into force,
  >
  >     - (ii) ‘Date of expiry’ indicates the date on which the learner permit concerned expires,
  >
  >     - (iii) ‘Valid from’ indicates the date on which the permit holder first held a learner permit in the category concerned, and
  >
  >     - (iv) ‘Valid to’ indicates the date on which the learner permit expires in respect of the category concerned.”,

  and

- (b) in Schedule 1, by substituting for form D.202 (inserted by ( [S.I. No. 684 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0684.html) )) set out in Part 1 the following:

  > ### “Part 1
  >
  > Form D.202
  >
  > Side 1
  >
  > Side 2
  >
  > ”.

GIVEN under my hand,

15 April 2026.

SEAN CANNEY,

Minister of State at the Department of Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) by replacing Form D.202 (learner permit) in Part 1 of Schedule 1 with an update to the Irish language on the form.
