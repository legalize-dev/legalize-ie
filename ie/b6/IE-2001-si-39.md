# S.I. No. 39/2001 - Greyhound Race Track (Racing) (Amendment) Regulations 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

Bord na gCon in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Greyhound Industry Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958) and also by sub-section (3) of Section 5 of the said Act, hereby makes the following regulations, that is to say:-

- (1) These regulations may be cited as the Greyhound Race Track (Racing) (Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) The Greyhound Race Track (Racing) Regulations, 1993 ( [No. 158 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0158.html) ) are amended as follows:-

    - (a) By the deletion of sub-article 23(1)(g) of the Greyhound Race Track (Racing) Regulations, 1993 and the substitution therefor of a sub-article in the following terms.

(1)(g) *“the racing manager shall not allow to compete in a race any unraced greyhound whose last qualifying trial over the distance for which it is entered was run more than forty-two days prior to the race and shall not permit to compete in a race any raced greyhound whose last previous performance was recorded more than forty-two days prior to the race. This provision shall not apply to a greyhound listed as a runner or reserve for a particular race at a particular track where such race could not have been run for any reason provided the said greyhound races at that track within 14 days of the postponed or abandoned race.”*

- (b) By the deletion of sub-article 25 (4) and the substitution therefor of sub-articles in the following terms:

25 (4) *“Performances on the flat will re-qualify a raced hurdle greyhound for hurdle racing.”*

25 (5) *“Performances on the flat may be published in the race card for hurdle races and vice-versa.”*

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

The effect of these regulations is to amend the Greyhound Race Track (Racing) Regulations, 1993, regarding the qualifications of **(a)** unraced greyhounds for racing **(b)** raced hurdle greyhounds for hurdle racing and **(c)** publication of hurdle/flat form in certain instances.
