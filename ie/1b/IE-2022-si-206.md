# S.I. No. 206/2022 - Education Act 1998 (Composition of National Council for Curriculum and Assessment) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th April,* 2022.

I, NORMA FOLEY, Minister for Education, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 40 of the Education Act 1998 (No. 51 of 1998) (as adapted by the Education and Skills (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 (S.I. No. 450 of 2020)), following consultation with the persons specified in that section, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Education Act 1998 (Composition of National Council for Curriculum and Assessment) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. The National Council for Curriculum and Assessment shall be composed of the persons specified in the Schedule to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Education Act 1998 (Composition of National Council for Curriculum and Assessment) Order 2009 (S.I. No. 131 of 2009) is revoked.

## SCHEDULE

1. One Chairperson.

2. One Vice-Chairperson nominated by the Joint Managerial Body.

3. One Vice-Chairperson nominated by the Irish National Teachers’ Organisation.

4. One nominee of the Minister for Education.

5. One Representative of the Joint Managerial Body.

6. One Representative of Education and Training Boards Ireland.

7. One Representative of the Catholic Primary Schools Management Association.

8. One Representative of the Church of Ireland Board of Education.

9. One Representative of the National Association of Boards of Management in Special Education.

10. Two Representatives of the Association of Secondary Teachers in Ireland.

11. Two Representatives of the Teachers’ Union of Ireland.

12. Two Representatives of the Irish National Teachers’ Organisation.

13. One Representative of the Irish Federation of University Teachers.

14. One Representative of the Irish Business and Employers Confederation.

15. One Representative of the Irish Congress of Trade Unions.

16. One Representative of the Association of Community and Comprehensive Schools.

17. One Representative of the National Parents Council (Primary).

18. One Representative of the National Parents Council (Post-Primary).

19. One Nominee of the Minister for Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth.

20. One Representative of Foras na Gaeilge.

21. One Representative of the State Examinations Commission.

22. One Representative of the Department of Education.

23. The President of the Irish Second-Level Students’ Union.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 April, 2022.

**NORMA FOLEY,**

Minister for Education.
