# S.I. No. 506/2002 - Personal Retirement Savings Accounts (Fees) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) , [96](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) and [117](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations: -

**Citation.**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Personal Retirement Savings Accounts (Fees) Regulations, 2002.

**Definitions.**

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “the Act” means the [Pensions Act, 1990 (No. 25 of 1990)](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

**Fees.**

3.(1) The fees payable under section 96(1)(f) of the Act, at the time of application to the Board by the applicant, shall be:

- (a) in the case of an applicant in respect of whom no PRSA product has yet been approved by the Board, a fee of €20,000 to include approval of one PRSA product, and

- (b) in the case of an applicant in respect of whom a PRSA product has been approved by the Board, a fee of €5,000 to include approval of one PRSA product.

- (2) The amount of the annual fee payable by a PRSA provider to the Board under section 117(5) shall be:

    - (a) €2000 multiplied by the number of PRSA products of that PRSA provider for the time being approved by the Board, and

    - (b) an amount equal to 0.05% of the total PRSA assets held under the terms of PRSA contracts of that PRSA provider.

- (3) The fee specified in sub-article (2) shall be remitted to the Board on or before 31 January in each year.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

[Section 96](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) provides for the payment of a fee by an applicant for approval of a PRSA product. [Section 117](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (5) of the [Pensions Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) provides for the payment of an annual fee by a PRSA provider. These regulations contain the detailed provisions in relation to the amount of these fees and the time of payment.
