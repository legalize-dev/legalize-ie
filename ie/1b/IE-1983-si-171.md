# S.I. No. 171/1983 - Insurance (Provision of Services) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 171 of 1983.

INSURANCE (PROVISION OF SERVICES) ORDER, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0007/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Insurance Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1953) (inserted by the [Insurance Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1983) ), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Insurance (Provision of Services) Order, 1983, and shall come into operation on the 23rd day of June, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The following services are hereby specified for the purpose of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0007/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Insurance Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , (No. 7 of 1953) (inserted by the [Insurance Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1983)):—

( *a* ) agricultural development and processing services;

( *b* ) construction-related services;

( *c* ) medical services;

( *d* ) training services covering all activities;

( *e* ) technical and general consulting services, including commercial laboratory services and research and development services;

( *f* ) international financial services;

( *g* ) computer software and data processing services;

( *h* ) public administration services;

( *i* ) media recording and publishing services.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of June, 1983.

**F. CLUSKEY,**

Minister for Trade, Commerce

and Tourism.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order specifies, in Paragraph 2, those service activities in respect of which the Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism may provide export credit insurance.
