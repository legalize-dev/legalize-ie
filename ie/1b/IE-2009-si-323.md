# S.I. No. 323/2009 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control On Animal and Poultry Vaccines) (Amendment) Order 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th August*, 2009.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )) for the purpose, inter alia, of giving further effect to Article 71 of Directive 2001/82/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 November 2001 [^1] on the Community code relating to veterinary medicinal products, hereby order as follows—

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines)(Amendment) Order 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) is amended in the First Schedule by the deletion of “Salmonellosis (caused by or involving salmonella enteriditis or salmonella typhimurium) in poultry”

###### 3.

3. The Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) is amended, in Part 1 of the Second Schedule, by the insertion before “swine fever”of “Salmonellosis (caused by or involving salmonella enteriditis or salmonella typhimurium) in poultry”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 July 2009.

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order provides for the removal of a certain disease from the First Schedule of the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ) and the insertion of a certain disease in Part 1 of the Second Schedule of the Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Control on Animal and Poultry Vaccines) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 528 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0528.html) ).
