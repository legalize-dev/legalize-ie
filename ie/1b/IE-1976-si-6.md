# S.I. No. 6/1976 - Harbour Rates (Harbour of Dublin, Skerries and Balbriggan) Order, 1976

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 6 of 1976.

HARBOUR RATES (HARBOUR OF DUBLIN, SKERRIES AND BALBRIGGAN) ORDER, 1976.

I, PETER BARRY, Minister for Transport and Power, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 104](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/sec0104.html#sec104) of the [Harbours Act, 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1946), and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), and after due compliance with section 106 of that Act, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Harbour Rates (Harbours of Dublin, Skerries and Balbriggan) Order, 1976.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 19th day of January, 1976.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the Harbours" means Dublin Harbour, Skerries Harbour and Balbriggan Harbour.

###### 3.

3. Section 40 of the Dublin Port and Docks Act, 1902, shall have effect as if for the matter contained in the Fifth Schedule to that Act, there were substituted the matter contained in the First Schedule to this Order.

###### 4.

4. (1) The rates set out in Part II of the Second Schedule to this Order are hereby fixed as the maximum tonnage rates chargeable by the Dublin Port and Docks Board in respect of the Harbours.

- (2) The provisions set out in Part I of the Second Schedule to this Order shall apply in relation to the rates chargeable under this Article.

###### 5.

5. The Harbour Rates (Port of Dublin) Order, 1946 (S. R. & O., No. 39 of 1946), the Harbour Rates (Harbours of Dublin, Skerries and Balbriggan) Order, 1958 ( [S.I. No. 188 of 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/si/0188.html) ), the Harbour Rates (Harbours of Dublin, Skerries and Balbriggan) Order, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0187.html) ), and the Harbour Rates (Harbours of Dublin, Skerries and Balbriggan) Order, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 269 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0269.html) ), are hereby revoked.

SECOND SCHEDULE.

## PART I. PROVISIONS APPLYING IN RELATION TO TONNAGE RATES

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1. | A vessel shall be deemed to be trading with a particular place if it takes on board cargo or passengers at any of the harbours to be landed at that place or if it lands cargo or passengers at any of the harbours taken on board at that place. |
| 2. | The tonnage of a vessel upon which tonnage rates shall be based and chargeable shall be the net register tonnage or half the gross tonnage, whichever is the greater. |
| 3. | Tonnage rates shall not be chargeable in respect of both the arrival and departure of a vessel but if a vessel on which tonnage rates have been charged in respect of its arrival takes on board at any of the harbours cargo or passengers for a port or place the tonage rates for which are higher than those charged on such arrival, the difference between the two rates shall be chargeable. |
| 4. | A vessel which does not land cargo or passengers but which takes on board cargo or passengers at any of the harbours shall be deemed to be trading only with the port of destination of such cargo or passengers. |
