# S.I. No. 357/1996 - European Commmunities (Undertakings For Collective Investment in Transferable Securities) (Amendment) Regulations 1996

S.I. No. 357 of 1996.

EUROPEAN COMMMUNITIES (UNDERTAKINGS FOR COLLECTIVE INVESTMENT IN TRANSFERABLE SECURITIES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 1996.

I, Richard Bruton, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 85/611/EEC of 20 December, 1985^1 as amended by Council Directive No. 88/220/EEC of 22 March, 1988^2 hereby make the following Regulations:

^1O.J. No. L375/3 of 31/12/85

^2O.J. No. L100/31 of 19/4/88

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities) (Amendment) Regulations, 1996.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January 1997.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Undertakings for Collective Investment in Transferable Securities) Regulations 1989 ( [S.I. No. 78 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0078.html) ) to provide that the requirements of [Sections 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0058.html#sec58) , [119](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0119.html#sec119) and [125](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0125.html#sec125) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) will not apply to investment companies with fixed capital or investment companies with variable capital which are engaged in the collective investment business. In addition the Regulations also provide that [Sections 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0070.html#sec70) and [72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) will not apply to Companies with fixed capital engaged in the collective investment business.
