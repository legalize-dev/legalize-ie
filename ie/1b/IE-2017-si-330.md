# S.I. No. 330/2017 - Water Services Act 2014 (Extension of Suspension of Domestic Water Charges) (Amendment) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th July*, 2017.

I, EOGHAN MURPHY, Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government, in the exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (1A)(*c*) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0044/print.html#sec3) of the [Water Services Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0044/print.html) (as inserted by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec2) of the [Water Services (Amendment) Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Water Services Act 2014 (Extension of Suspension of Domestic Water Charges) (Amendment) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. Article 2 of the Water Services Act 2014 (Extension of Suspension of Domestic Water Charges) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 118 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0118.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of 31 December 2017 for 31 July 2017.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 July 2017.

**EOGHAN MURPHY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the extension of the suspension of domestic water charges to 31 December 2017, to enable the Government to complete its consideration of the recommendations of the Joint Oireachtas Committee on the Future Funding of Domestic Water Services and to bring forward legislation to give effect to the recommendations of the Committee.
