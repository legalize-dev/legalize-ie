S.I. No. 363 of 1989.

INLAND POST AMENDMENT (No. 44) SCHEME, 1989

AN POST, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Communications, hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Inland Post Amendment (No. 44) Scheme, 1989.

- (2) This Scheme shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1990.

###### 2.

2. In this Scheme

"the Principal Warrant" means the Inland Post Warrant, 1939 ( [S.I. No. 202 of 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/si/0202.html) ).

"Scheme No. 37" means the Inland Post Amendment (No. 37) Scheme, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0092.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 37 of the Principal Warrant is hereby amended by the substitution for that Regulation of the following:

> "Subject to the provisions of this Warrant, any postal packet (other than a parcel for delivery within the State) may be registered".

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5 of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 5) Warrant, 1953 (inserted by Regulation 12 of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 19) Warrant, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 280 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0280.html) ), and as amended by Regulation 11 of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 29) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 137 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0137.html) )), is hereby amended by the substitution for "twenty-five pounds" of "forty pounds".

###### 5.

5. Regulation 3 (2) (ii) of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 30) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 396 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0396.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for "twenty pounds" or "£20" of "twenty-five pounds".

###### 6.

6. Regulation 8 of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 31) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 94 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0094.html) ), is hereby deleted.

###### 7.

7. ( *a* ) Regulation 6 (*b*) of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 33) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0444.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for "5p" of "6p".

( *b* ) Regulation 8 (*a*) of Inland Post Amendment (No. 33) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0444.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for "twenty-five pounds" or "£25" of "forty pounds".

###### 8.

8. Regulation 7 (*a*) of the Inland Post Amendment (No. 34) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 445 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0445.html) ), is hereby amended by substitution for "twenty five pounds" of "forty pounds".

14. The First Schedule to the Inland Post Amendment (No. 31) Scheme, 1985 ( [S.I. EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Scheme applies to the Inland Post Service and comes into operation on the 1st day of January, 1990. It authorises increases in the letter, postcard, printed packet, newspaper, sample packet and parcel post rates of postage and in the fees charged for the express delivery, registration, customs clearance, cash-on-delivery, Business Reply, Freepost, Parcel Sure-Service, Parcel Forward and other miscellaneous services.
