# S.I. No. 326/1999 - Road Traffic Act, 1994 (Section 17) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) and [17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994), which powers were delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 427 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0427.html) ), I, ROBERT MOLLOY, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby make the following Regulations.

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act, 1994, (Section 17) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations the “Act of 1994’ means the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 22nd day of October, 1999.

###### 4.

4. The Statements to be produced, pursuant to section 17 of the Act of 1994, shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. For the purpose of completing the statements referred to in article 4, the member of the Garda Síochána who required the arrested person to provide two specimens of breath shall:—

- (*a*) prior to the provision of the specimens, input the following information into the apparatus referred to in section 13(1) of the Act of 1994—

    - (i) the name and address of the person providing the specimens,

    - (ii) the section of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) which it is alleged that the person contravened,

    - (iii) his or her name and number, and

- (*b*) following the automatic production of the statements by the apparatus, sign the said statements.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the form of the statements to be produced by an apparatus for determining the concentration of alcohol in the breath pursuant to [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) and prescribe the manner in which the statements are duly completed by a member of the Garda Síochána.
