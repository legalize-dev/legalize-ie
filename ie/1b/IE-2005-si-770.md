# S.I. No. 770/2005 - Registration of Adoptions and Marriages (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 770 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0770.html)**

**Registration of Adoptions and Marriages (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2005**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Vital Statistics and Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1952) and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Adoption Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1952), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Adoptions and Marriages (Abridged Certificate) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 5^th December 2005.

###### 3.

3. An tArd-Chláraitheoir, a Superintendent Registrar or a Registrar may issue, as respects an entry in a register of marriages or the Adopted Children Register in which corrections have been made, a certificate of the matters at such entry, as so corrected, endorsed in the manner as set out in Part I, or Part II, as appropriate, of the Schedule to these Regulations on payment of a fee of €8.00, together with the appropriate search fee payable under the Civil Registration (Births and Deaths (Fees) Regulations 2005 or the appropriate search fee payable under the Civil Registration (Marriages) (Fees) Regulations 2005.

###### 4.

4. The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages Regulations 1987 ( [S.I. No. 234 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0234.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

**(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

These regulations permit the issue, in respect of an entry in a register of marriages or the Adopted Children Register in any of which corrections have been made, of a certificate incorporating the particulars as corrected, without the inclusion of the correction or marginal note appearing in the entry.
