# S.I. No. 354/1977 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 2, Fifty-Third Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 354 of 1977.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 2, FIFTY-THIRD PERIOD) (CYCLE TYRES) ORDER, 1977.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 2, Fifty-Third Period) (Cycle Tyres) Order, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

SCHEDULE.

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quota Order for which quota period and quota are appointed. | Quota Period. | Quota. | General description of subject matter of quota. |
| (1) | (2) | (3) | (4) |
| Control of Imports (Quota No. 2) Order, 1934 (S. R. & O., No. 231 of 1934), as amended. | The period beginning on the 1st day of January, 1978, and ending on the 31st day of December, 1978. | Two hundred articles. | Certain pneumatic tyres for bicycles or tricycles. |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government this 18th day of November, 1977.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This instrument appoints 200 articles as the quantity of cycle tyres and inner tubes which may be imported from certain countries during the period 1st January, 1978 to 31st December, 1978.
