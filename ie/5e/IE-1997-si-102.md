# S.I. No. 102/1997 - Irish Aviation Authority (Airworthiness of Aircraft) (Amendment) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 102 of 1997.

IRISH AVIATION AUTHORITY (AIRWORTHINESS OF AIRCRAFT) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1997

The Irish Aviation Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) (5) of the [Irish Aviation Authority Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1993), hereby makes the following order—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Irish Aviation Authority (Airworthiness of Aircraft) (Amendment) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. In the Irish Aviation Authority (Airworthiness of Aircraft) Order, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 324 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0324.html) )—

- (1) references to Joint Aviation Regulations shall be construed as Joint Aviation Requirements and the reference to "regulations" shall be construed as a reference to "requirements".

- (2) the reference in Article 12 (3) (*a*) to the Air Navigation (Nationality and Registration of Aircraft) Order, 1996 shall be construed as a reference to the Irish Aviation Authority (Nationality and Registration of Aircraft) Order, 1996 ( [S.I. No. 322 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0322.html) ).

GIVEN under the common seal of the Irish Aviation Authority, this 3rd day of March, 1997.

**J. LACEY,**

Director.

**ROSEMARY QUINN,**

Director.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

Joint Aviation Requirements as adopted by the Joint Aviation Authorities achieve the status of EU Regulations when adopted under EU Regulation 3922/91. It is appropriate to reflect in the Order that certain Joint Aviation Requirements not yet adopted under that Regulation are applicable in the State under the terms of the Arrangements referred to in section 14 of the Act.
