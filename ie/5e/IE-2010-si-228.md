# S.I. No. 228/2010 - Mussel Seed (Closing of Fisheries) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th May*, 2010.

I, BRENDAN SMITH, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec15) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea-Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 705 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0705.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Mussel Seed (Closing of Fisheries) Regulations 2010 and come into operation on 25 May 2010.

###### 2.

2. The Mussel Seed (Opening of Fisheries) Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 174 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0174.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 May 2010.

**BRENDAN SMITH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations are to provide for the closing of the mussel seed fishery from 25 May 2010.
