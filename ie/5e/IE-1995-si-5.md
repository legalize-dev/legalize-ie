S.I. No. 5 of 1995.

TRADE UNION (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 13 of the Trade Union Act, 1871, as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Registry of Friendly Societies Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1936), [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1975), and the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 19 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0019.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trade Union (Fees) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of February, 1995.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) The fees specified in Part 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations shall be payable in respect of matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1990, other than matters to be transacted under the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1975).

- (2) The fees specified in part II of the Schedule shall be payable in respect of matters to be transacted under the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) .

- (3) No fee shall be payable in respect of the services specified in Part III of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. The Trade Union (Fees) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 292 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0292.html) ), are hereby revoked.

## PART III *Services for which no fee is payable*

1. Cancellation or withdrawal of certificate of registry of trade union.

2. Any document or copy document supplied to a public department.

3. Any document in respect of which a fee is already chargeable under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1990, or under any other Act.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for this inspection of documents under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1990.
