# S.I. No. 309/1993 - Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 309 of 1993.

COURTS-MARTIAL (LEGAL AID) REGULATIONS, 1993.

I, DAVID ANDREWS, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Courts-Martial Appeals Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1993.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1991" means the Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 210 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0210.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Courts-Martial (Legal Aid) Regulations, 1986, ( [S.I. No. 425 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0425.html) ), are hereby amended with effect from the 1st day of January, 1992—

( *a* ) by the substitution in Regulation 12 (2) (a) of "£111.68" for "£108.44" (inserted by the Regulations of 1991) and of "£49.68" for "£48.24" (inserted by the Regulations of 1991);

( *b* ) by the substitution in Regulation 14 of "£28.95" for "£28.11" (inserted by the Regulations of 1991).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 4th day of August, 1993.

**DAVID ANDREWS,**

Minister for Defence.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 4th day of August, 1993.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.
