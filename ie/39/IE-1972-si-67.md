# S.I. No. 67/1972 - Garda Síochána (Ranks) Order, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the power conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0002/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Garda Síochána Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1972), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Garda Síochána (Ranks) Order, 1972.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby determined that the Garda Síochána shall be divided into the ranks specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order and that each rank shall consist of not more than the number of members of the Garda Síochána specified in column (2) of the said Schedule opposite the mention of the rank in the said column (1).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 7th day of March, 1972.

**SEÁN Ó LOINSIGH,**

*Taoiseach.*

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prescribes the ranks of the Garda Sáíáochááána and the maximum number of members of each rank.
