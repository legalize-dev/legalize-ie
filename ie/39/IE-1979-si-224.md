# S.I. No. 224/1979 - Protection of Young Persons (Employment) (Section 4) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 224 of 1979.

PROTECTION OF YOUNG PERSONS (EMPLOYMENT) (SECTION 4) ORDER, 1979.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0004.html#sec4) (5) of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Protection of Young Persons (Employment) (Section 4) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 4th day of July, 1979.

###### 3.

3. [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1977), shall continue in force for the period of 12 months commencing on the commencement of this Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of June, 1979.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides that [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0004.html#sec4) (3) of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , which governs the hours of work of children under 15, but over 14 years of age, shall remain in force for a period not exceeding twelve months from the date of commencement of the Order.
