# S.I. No. 440/2017 - National Minimum Wage Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th October*, 2017.

I, REGINA DOHERTY, Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection (as adapted by the Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 366 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0366.html) )), in exercise of the powers conferred on me, (as adapted by the Labour Affairs and Labour Law (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 361 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0361.html) ));

- by subsection (1)(a) of section 10D (inserted by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec8) of the [National Minimum Wage (Low Pay Commission) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) (No. 22 of 2015)) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000), and

- by section 3(1) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) ,

having received on 13 July 2017, a recommendation made by the Low Pay Commission under subsection (1) of section 10C (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec7) of the [National Minimum Wage (Low Pay Commission) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) , and having received a recommendation by the Low Pay Commission under subsection 4 of section 10C (inserted by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html#sec7) of the [National Minimum Wage (Low Pay Commission) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0022/print.html) ) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) and

having considered the reports, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the National Minimum Wage Order 2017.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 1 January 2018.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby declared that the national minimum hourly rate of pay for the purposes of the National Minimum Wage Acts 2000 and 2015 shall be €9.55.

###### 3.

3. The national minimum hourly rate of pay may include the following allowances:

- (i) for board only, €0.85 per hour worked

- (ii) for lodgings only, €22.56 per week, or €3.24 per day.

Given under my Official Seal,

11 October 2017.

**REGINA DOHERTY,**

Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order sets the national minimum hourly rate of pay from 1 January 2018 at €9.55, and the board and lodging rates as follows;

Lodgings: €22.56 per week, or €3.24 per day.

Board: €0.85 per hour worked.
