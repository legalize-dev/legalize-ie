# S.I. No. 313/2001 - Irish Pound Notes and Coins (Cessation of Legal Tender Status) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/sec0009.html#sec9) (1) of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Irish Pound Notes and Coins (Cessation of Legal Tender Status) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The 9th day of February 2002, is hereby specified as the earlier operative date for the purposes of Chapter III of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1998).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 11th day of July 2001. |
|  | Charlie McCreevy |
|  | _________________________ Minister for Finance |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order provides for the appointment of 9 February 2002 as the “earlier operative date” for the purposes of Chapter III of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .

The Order was approved in draft by resolution of each House of the Oireachtas.
