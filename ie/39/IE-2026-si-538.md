# S.I. No. 538/2026 - European Union (Breakfast Directives) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd October,* 2026.

I, MARTIN HEYDON, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2024/1438 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

Citation

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Breakfast Directives) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

Amendment of S.I. 367/2003

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Marketing of Honey) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 367 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0367.html) ) are amended by inserting after Regulation 4(1) the following paragraph:

> - “(1A) Not withstanding paragraph (1) and for the avoidance of doubt, the derogation at Article 2(4)(a) second subparagraph of the Council Directive does not apply in the State in respect of indicating the percentage share only of the four largest countries of origin.”.

Amendment of S.I. 410/2013

###### 3.

3. The European Union (Marketing of fruit juices and certain similar products) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 410 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0410.html) ) are amended in Regulation 3 by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (1):

> - “(1A) Not withstanding paragraph (1) and for the avoidance of doubt, the derogation in Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Directive does not apply in the state in respect of stipulating an official language to be used for the designations listed in Part II of Annex III to that Directive.”.

Amendment of S.L. 298/2003

###### 4.

4. (1) The European Communities (Dehydrated Preserved Milk) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0298.html) ) are amended in Regulation 3 by inserting the following paragraph as paragraph (2) and consequently, re-numbering Regulation 3 as paragraph (1) of that Regulation:

- (2) For the avoidance of doubt, the derogation in subpoint (d) of point (3) of annex I of the Directive does not apply in the state in respect of restricting or prohibiting the lactose reduction modifications.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 October, 2026.

**MARTIN HEYDON,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be legal interpretation.)

These Regulations give further effect to Directive (EU) 2024/1438 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024. They confirm that Ireland does not avail of certain derogations contained in that Directive.
