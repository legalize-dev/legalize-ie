# S.I. No. 292/2003 - Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [Electoral Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1997), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Constituency Commission (Establishment) Order, 2003.

###### 2.

2. A Constituency Commission is hereby established to undertake the functions assigned to it by Part II of the [Electoral Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

**Explanatory Note**

This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.

This order establishes a Constituency Commission to report on Dáil and European constituencies.
