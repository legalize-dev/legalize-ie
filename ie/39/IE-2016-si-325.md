# S.I. No. 325/2016 - Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th June*, 2016.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Central Bank of Ireland (the “Bank”) by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No 24 of 1995), the Bank hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Consumer Credit Act 1995 (Section 2) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) is amended by the deletion from the list of such persons prescribed pursuant to paragraph (*e*) of the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) of the following:

> “Woodchester Finance Limited”

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2 of the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 2)(No. 2) Regulations 1996 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/si/0369.html) ) is amended by deleting “Woodchester Finance Limited”.

Signed for and on behalf of the CENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND,

17 June 2016.

CYRIL ROUX,

Deputy Governor (Financial Regulation).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to revoke the prescription of a certain entity prescribed pursuant to paragraph (*e*) of the definition of “credit institution” in the [Consumer Credit Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
