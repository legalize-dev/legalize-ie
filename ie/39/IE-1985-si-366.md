# S.I. No. 366/1985 - Dublin Meter Parking Places (No. 2) Rules 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 366 of 1985.

DUBLIN METER PARKING PLACES (No. 2) RULES 1985.

Rules for the administration of meter parking places in the County Borough of Dublin, made by the Right Honourable the lord Mayor, Aldermen, and Burgesses of Dublin, the appropriate local authority, in exercise of powers conferred by Section 90 (3) (b) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (no. 24 of 1961)—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Rules may be cited as the Dublin Meter parking Places (no. 2) Rules 1985.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** These Rules shall come into force on 18th November, 1985.

3 ..

###### 3.

**3.** In these Rules—

"the Principal Rules" means the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1985 ( [S.I. No. 27 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0027.html) ).

4 ..

###### 4.

**4.** Rule 7 of the Principal Rules is hereby amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (c)

- "(*c*) The parking fee payable under paragraph (a) of this rule shall in the residual area be:—

- (i) One ten pence coin for a continuous period not exceeding thirty minutes, or

- (ii) two ten pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour, or

- (iii) three ten pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding one hour and thirty minutes, or

- (iv) four ten pence coins for a continuous period not exceeding two hours".

PRESENT when the corporate seal of the Corporation was affixed hereto, the Right Honourable the Lord Mayor also being present.

MICHAEL O'CONNELL,

City Hall Dublin,

Corporation Official.

F. J. FEELY,

City Manager and Town Clerk

7th November 1985.

The Minister for the Environment hereby approves the foregoing Dublin meter Parking Places (no. 2) Rules, 1985.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this

14th day of November 1985.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Rules amend Rule 7 of the Dublin Meter Parking Places Rules 1985 (S.I. 27 of 1985) to provide for an increase in the rate of parking fees payable in the "residual area" from the 10p per hour to 20p per hour.
