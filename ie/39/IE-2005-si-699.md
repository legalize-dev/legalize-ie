**International Carriage Of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 2005**

**SI 699 of 2005**

**International Carriage Of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 2005**

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 15 of the INTERNATIONAL CARRIAGE OF PERISHABLE FOODSTUFFS ACT, 1987 (No. 20 of 1987), (as adapted by the International Carriage Of Perishable Foodstuffs (Consolidation) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 188 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0188.html) )), and the [International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1993 ( [S.I. No. 397 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0397.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These regulations may be cited as the International Carriage Of Perishable Foodstuffs (Amendment of Fees) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. In these regulations:

“Principal Regulations” means The International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs (Consolidation) Regulations, 1993 (No. 188 of 1993).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended by the substitution of the Schedule to these regulations for the Schedule to the Principal Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Fees set out in Parts I (Tests) & Part II (Type approval) of the schedule to these Regulations are in Pounds Sterling. (GB£Stg)

###### 5.

5. The Fees set out in Part III (Examinations & Certification) of the schedule to these Regulations are in EURO (EU€).

###### 6.

6. The Certifying Authority shall be entitled to be paid their reasonable expenses in addition to the fees set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations update the fees contained in previous regulations with respect to testing, type approval, examination and certification activities associated with the International Transport of Perishable Foodstuffs (ATP).
