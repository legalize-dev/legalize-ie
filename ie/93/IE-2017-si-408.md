# S.I. No. 408/2017 - Notification and Control of Diseases affecting Terrestrial Animals (No. 2) Regulations 2016 (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd September*, 2017.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec36) of the [Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2013), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Notification and Control of Diseases affecting Terrestrial Animals (No. 2) Regulations 2016 (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Notification and Control of Diseases affecting Terrestrial Animals (No. 2) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 130 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0130.html) ) are amended, in Part C of the Schedule, by the insertion—

- (*a*) before “Crimean Congo haemorrhagic fever” of “Bovine genital campylobacteriosis”,

- (*b*) before “Infectious bovine rhinotracheitis/ infectious pustular vulvovaginitis” of “Heartwater”, and

- (*c*) before “Monkey pox virus” of “Leishmaniasis”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 August 2017.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations make bovine genital campylobacteriosis, heartwater and leishmaniasis notifiable diseases.
