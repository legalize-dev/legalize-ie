S.I. No. 310 of 1974.

ROAD TRAFFIC (SPEED LIMITS) (COUNTY BOROUGH OF CORK AND COUNTY OF CORK) REGULATIONS, 1974.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) , [45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) and [46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) amended as regards the said section 46 by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Cork and County of Cork) Regulations, 1974.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 4th day of November, 1974.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations save where the context otherwise requires

"the Act" means the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) ;

"road" means a public road;

"perimeter road" means a road on and along the roadway of which there runs the boundary of a county or other borough, an urban district or a town;

"special speed limit" and "built-up area speed limit" have the meanings respectively assigned to them in Part IV of the Act;

"distance" means distance as measured along the centre line of a road.

###### 4.

4. (1) Each of the roads described in the First Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared not to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

- (2) Each of the roads described in the Second Schedule to these Regulations is hereby declared to be a public road in a built-up area for the purposes of section 45 of the Act.

###### 5.

5. (1) Forty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Third Schedule to these Regulations.

- (2) Fifty miles per hour shall be the speed limit for mechanically propelled vehicles on the roads specified in the Fourth Schedule.

###### 6.

6. There shall be excepted from the special speed limit prescribed in article 5 of these Regulations and from the built-up area speed limit the following:—

( *a* ) ambulances,

( *b* ) fire brigade vehicles, and

( *c* ) vehicles used by members of the Garda Síochána in the performance of their duties as such members.

###### 7.

7. The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Cork and County of Cork) Regulations, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 282 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0282.html) ) and The Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Cork and the County of Cork) (Amendment) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 263 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0263.html) ) are hereby revoked.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations replace the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Cork and County of Cork) Regulations, 1968 ( [S.I. No. 282 of 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/si/0282.html) ) and the Road Traffic (Speed Limits) (County Borough of Cork and County of Cork) (Amendments) Regulations, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 263 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0263.html) ) and alter the speed limits applicable to certain roads in these areas.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area.

The Regulations exclude the public roads mentioned in the First Schedule from the scope of the built-up area speed limit of 30 m.p.h. prescribed by [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) thus enabling special speed limits, e.g., 40 m.p.h. or 50 m.p.h., to be applied to these roads. Public roads to which special speed limits apply are specified in the Third, Fourth and Fifth Schedules.

The Regulations declare that the roads mentioned in the Second Schedule, which would otherwise be outside the scope of the built-up area speed limit of 30 m.p.h., shall be subject to that limit.

The Regulations prescribe a speed limit of 40 m.p.h. in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Third Schedule, a speed limit of 50 m.p.h. in respect of the public roads mentioned in the Fourth Schedule.

Ambulances, fire brigade vehicles and Garda Síochána vehicles while being used in the course of duty are excepted from all speed limits by the Regulations.
