# S.I. No. 57/2013 - National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Irish Bank Resolution Corporation Act, 2013) Delegation of Functions Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th February*, 2013.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Irish Bank Resolution Corporation Act, 2013) Delegation of Functions Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. The following functions of the Minister for Finance are delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency:

- (*a*) the function of the Minister under [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/sec0054.html#sec54) (7F) of the [Finance Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (inserted by the [Irish Bank Resolution Corporation Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2013)), and

- (*b*) the function of the Minister under [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec17) (1) of the [Irish Bank Resolution Corporation Act, 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2013).

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

7 February 2013.

**ENDA KENNY,**

Taoiseach.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order provides that certain functions of the Minister for Finance under the [Finance Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) and under the Irish Bank Resolution Corporation Act, 2013 are delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency.
