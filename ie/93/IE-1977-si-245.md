# S.I. No. 245/1977 - Conditions of Employment (Acrylic Yarn Spinning Industry) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 245 of 1977.

CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT (ACRYLIC YARN SPINNING INDUSTRY) (EXCLUSION) REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0029.html#sec29) and [52](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0052.html#sec52) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936), and the Labour (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1966 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/si/0164.html) ), and after consultation with representatives of employers interested in the form of industrial work specified in these Regulations and with representatives of workers so interested, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Conditions of Employment (Acrylic Yarn Spinning Industries) (Exclusion) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The following form of industrial work, namely, acrylic yarn spinning as carried on at the premises of Asahi Spinning (Ireland) Limited, at Killala, in the county of Mayo, is hereby declared to be excluded industrial work for the purposes of [sections 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0046.html#sec46) and [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1936).

###### 4.

4. The following provisions shall have effect in respect of the work to which these Regulations relate :

( *a* ) no woman shall be employed to do any such work at any time between the hours of 12 midnight on any day and 8.00 a.m. on the following day;

( *b* ) no young person shall be employed to do any such work at any time between the hours of 10 p.m. on any day and 7.00 a.m. on the following day;

( *c* ) no woman or young person shall be so employed on any day until the expiration of eleven hours from the time at which she ceased to do industrial work on the previous day.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of July, 1977.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The [Conditions of Employment Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) provides that female adult workers may not be employed on industrial work between the hours of 10 p.m. on any day and 8 a.m. on the following day and young persons between the hours of 8 p.m. on any day and 8 a.m. on the following day.

The effect of these regulations is to permit the employment of female adult workers between 8 a.m. and 12 midnight and young persons between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m. on acrylic yarn spinning at the premises of Asahi Spinning (Ireland) Limited, Killala, Co. Mayo.
