# S.I. No. 64/2000 - Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999 (Bonding) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec3) and [43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec43) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999 (Bonding) Order, 2000, and shall come into operation on the 18th day of April, 2000.

###### 2.

2. In this Order -

“Act” means [the Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999);

“bond” means a bond effected by a person for the purposes of section 43(3) of the Act;

“insurance company” means a company authorised under the European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) Framework Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 359 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0359.html) ) to carry on business of the type referred to in Class 15 of Annex 1 to those Regulations;

“Minister” means the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment;

“nominated person” means the person nominated by the registrar of companies or the Revenue Commissioners, or jointly by the registrar of companies and the Revenue Commissioners, pursuant to section 43(3) of the Act.

###### 3.

3. Arrangements in relation to a bond shall only be entered into by a person of a class specified in Schedule 2 to this Order (in this Order referred to as the “Surety”).

###### 4.

4. The form of bond set out in Schedule 1 to this Order is hereby prescribed as the form of bond for the purposes of section 43 of the Act.

###### 5.

5. The period of 2 years is hereby prescribed as the minimum period to be specified in the bond as being the period for which it shall be valid, which period shall commence not earlier than -

- (a) the occurrence of the event which gave rise to the requirement to effect a bond, or

- (b) if an existing bond is in force immediately before the bond is effected, the day following the expiry of that existing bond.

## **SCHEDULE 2** **Classes of persons authorised to enter into a bond pursuant to [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec43) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html)**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The purpose of this Order is to prescribe the form of bond for the purposes of [section 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec43) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) .

## Footnotes

[^note-one]: Surety must be a member of a class specified in Schedule 2 to the Companies (Amendment)(No. 2) Act, 1999 (Bonding) Order 2000.

[^note-two]: Period must not be less than 2 years for the bond and may not commence earlier than the occurrence of the appropriate event which gave rise to the requirement for the bond.

[^note-three]: The Bond must be executed under the seal of the Surety.
