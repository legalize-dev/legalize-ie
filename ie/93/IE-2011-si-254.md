# S.I. No. 254/2011 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd June*, 2011.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 2011.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 2010 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 2011.

###### 2.

2. The organisation specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby designated as an approved organisation for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE

National Tourism Development Authority

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 May 2011.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and “approved organisations”, and between one “approved organisation” and another. These regulations designate the organisation set out in the schedule as an “approved organisation”.
