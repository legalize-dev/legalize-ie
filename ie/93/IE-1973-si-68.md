# S.I. No. 68/1973 - Imposition of Duties (No. 202) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) (Amendment) Order, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 68 of 1973.

IMPOSITION OF DUTIES (No. 202) (CUSTOMS AND EXCISE DUTIES AND FORM OF TARIFF) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1973.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 202) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) (Amendment) Order, 1973.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1973.

###### 3.

3. The Imposition of Duties (No. 200) (Customs and Excise Duties and Form of Tariff) Order, 1972 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/si/0220.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for the list of developing countries and territories to which the scheme of tariff preferences applies specified in the Annex to the Schedule thereto of the following:

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to extend the scheme of tariff preferences for developing countries to Western Samoa, Bhutan, Cuba, Fiji, Nauru, Oman, Sikkim and Tonga.
