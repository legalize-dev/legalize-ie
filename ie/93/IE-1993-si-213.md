# S.I. No. 213/1993 - Environmental Protection Agency (Establishment) Order, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 213 of 1993.

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1993.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Environmental Protection Agency (Establishment) Order, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The 26th day of July, 1993 is hereby appointed as the day on which the Environmental Protection Agency shall stand established.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, this 22nd day of July, 1993.

**MICHAEL SMITH, T.D.,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order appoints the 26th day of July, 1993, to be the establishment day for the Environment Protection Agency.
