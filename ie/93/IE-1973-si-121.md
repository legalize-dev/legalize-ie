# S.I. No. 121/1973 - Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 121 of 1973.

CENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND (FORM OF STATEMENT OF ACCOUNTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, RICHIE RYAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0032/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Currency Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1927), as adapted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942), and after consultation with the Board of Directors of the Central Bank of Ireland, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations, 1973.

- (2) The Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1961 to 1970, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1961 to 1973.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1961 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/si/0187.html) ), are hereby amended by the substitution for the matter at reference D (inserted by the Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) (Amendment) Regulations, 1970 ( [S.I. No. 148 of 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/si/0148.html) ) in Part II of the Schedule (inserted by the Central Bank of Ireland (Form of Statement of Accounts) Regulations, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 120 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0120.html) )) of the following:

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations revise a number of headings in the annual statement of accounts of the Central Bank of Ireland so as to bring them up to date. They also insert new headings to give further information regarding the activities of the Bank.
