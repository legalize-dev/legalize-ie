# S.I. No. 708/2007 - Local Government (An Chomhairle Leabharlanna) (Amendment) Regulations, 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 708 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0708.html)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (AN CHOMHAIRLE LEABHARLANNA) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th October*, 2007.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 79](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec79) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) hereby makes the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government (An Chomhairle Leabharlanna) (Amendment) Regulations, 2007.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations, the Principal Regulations and the Regulations of 2003 shall be construed as one and cited together as the Local Government (An Chomhairle Leabharlanna) Regulations, 1997 to 2007.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on 3rd September 2007.

###### 4.

4. In these Regulations, — the “Principal Regulations” means the Local Government (An Chomhairle Leabharlanna) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 499 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0499.html) ) — the “Regulations of 2003” means the Local Government (An Chomhairle Leabharlanna) (Amendment) Regulations, 2003 ( [S.I. No. 28 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0028.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Article 5(3) of the Principal Regulations, as amended by Article 5 of the Regulations of 2003, is hereby amended by substituting the following for subarticle 5(3)(*e*):

> “5(3)(*e*) one, on the nomination of the Board of the National Library of Ireland.”

GIVEN under the Official Seal,

24 August 2007

**JOHN GORMLEY.**

Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Local Government (An Chomhairle Leabharlanna) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 499 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0499.html) ) by incorporating the new legislative nomenclature of the Board of the National Library of Ireland.
