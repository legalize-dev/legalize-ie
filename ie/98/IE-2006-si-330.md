# S.I. No. 330/2006 - Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 330 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0330.html)**

**Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2006.**

I, John Browne, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959) [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec2) 5 (inserted by section 3(b) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2000)) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999) and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec2) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )) and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions Order 2006 ( [S.I. No. 82 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0082.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations-

“Act of 1999” means the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999);

“Act of 2000” means the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2000);

“Regulations of 2002” means the Fisheries Acts 1959 to 2001 (Payment in Lieu of Prosecution) Regulations 2002 ( [S.I. No. 414 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0414.html) ).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that section 25 (inserted by section 3(b) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 applies to offences under section 24(6) (inserted by section 3(a)(i) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 in respect of a contravention of Regulation 3, 10(3), 10(4), 10(5) 10(6), 10(7), 11(2), 13, 14, 15 or 19 of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 208 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0208.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations is prescribed for the purposes of section 25(2) (inserted by section 3(b) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 and section 2(1) of the Act 2000.

###### 5.

5. The amount standing specified in section 25 (2) (b) (inserted by section 3 (b) of the Act of 2000) of the Act of 1999 and section 2 (1) (ii) of the Act of 2000 (varied by Regulation 8 of the Regulations of 2002) is varied, in lieu of that amount, to €70.

###### 6.

6. The following are revoked:

- (a) Regulation 8 of the Regulation of 2002, and

- (b) the Inland Fisheries (Payment in Lieu of Prosecution) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 348 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0348.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These regulations provide for a system of on-the-spot fines in the area of inland fisheries to be administered by the regional fisheries boards, in order to cater for new fisheries legislation. The form of notice of an on-the-spot fine is set out in the Schedule to the Regulations.
