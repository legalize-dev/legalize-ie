# S.I. No. 393/2017 - Sea Pollution (Control of Pollution by Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th September*, 2017.

I, SHANE ROSS, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 10, 12, 14, 15, 17 and 18 of the [Sea Pollution Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1991) and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), and for the purposes of giving effect to the MARPOL Convention, to Annex II, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Sea Pollution (Control of Pollution by Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 September 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Sea Pollution (Control of Pollution by Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0217.html) ) are amended by the substitution of the material in the Schedule for Schedule 1 to those Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations substitute an amended Schedule 1 to the Sea Pollution (Control of Pollution by Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0217.html) ) which give effect to Annex II of the International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships, 1973, as modified by the Protocol of 1978 relating thereto (MARPOL 73/78).

These Regulations effectively implement amendments, contained in Resolution MEPC.270(69) adopted on 22 April 2016 by the Marine Environment Protection Committee of the International Maritime Organization, regarding the revised GESAMP Hazard Evaluation Procedure in Appendix I to Annex II of MARPOL 73/78.
