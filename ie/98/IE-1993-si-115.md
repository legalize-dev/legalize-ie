# S.I. No. 115/1993 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 115 of 1993.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1993.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1993.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1993.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall—

( *a* ) as regards milk, be 1.3 penny per gallon,

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, be £7.30 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0177.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of April, 1993.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and

Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which came into effect on the 1st of May, 1993 prescribe new rates of disease levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
