# S.I. No. 149/2000 - Commission To Inquire Into Child Abuse Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHAEL WOODS, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html#sec2) of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) (No. 7 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

- (1) This Order may be cited as the Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2000.

- (2) The 23rd day of May, 2000, is hereby appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Commission to Inquire into Child Abuse Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0007/print.html) .

|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of May, 2000. |
|  | MICHAEL WOODS, T.D. |
