# S.I. No. 166/2026 - Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 24*th April,* 2026.

I, TIMMY DOOLEY, Minister of State at the Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1962) and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Function) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Environment, Climate and Communications (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 235 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0235.html) )) and the Climate, Energy and the Environment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2025 ( [S.I. No. 384 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0384.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2026.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Order of 2021” means the Control of Fishing for Salmon Order 2021 ( [S.I. No. 102 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0102.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Order of 2021 is amended –

- (a) by the substitution of the wording specified in Schedule 1 to this Order for Schedule 1 to the Order of 2021,

  and

- (b) by the substitution of the wording specified in Schedule 2 to this Order for Schedule 2 to the Order of 2021.
