# S.I. No. 575/2001 - Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Licensing of Industrial Plant) (Fees Amendment) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0010.html#sec10) and [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Air Pollution Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1987), which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 428 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0428.html) ), I Dan Wallace, Minister of State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby make the following Regulations:-

**Citation**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Licensing of Industrial Plant) (Fees Amendment) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations and the Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Licensing of Industrial Plant) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 266 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0266.html) ) shall be construed together as one and may be collectively cited as the Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Licensing of Industrial Plant) Regulations, 1988 — 2001.

**Entry into Force**

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2002.

**Euro Amount**

###### 3.

3. The Air Pollution Act, 1987 (Licensing of Industrial Plant) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 266 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0266.html) ) are hereby amended by:-

- (a) the substitution of “€126” for “£100” in article 9,

- (b) the substitution of “€60” for “£50” in articles 15(2), 21 and 26 (3),

- (c) the substitution of “€10” for “£10” in article 22 (1), and

- (d) the substitution of “€6” for “£5” in article 37 (2).
