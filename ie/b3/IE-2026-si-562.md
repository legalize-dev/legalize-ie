# S.I. No. 562/2026 - European Union (Capital Requirements) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th November,* 2026.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2013/36/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013[^1] , as amended by Directive (EU) 2024/2994 of the European Parliament and Council of 27 November 2024[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

## **PART 1** **Preliminary and General**

**Citation**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Capital Requirements) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2026.

**Definition**

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Capital Requirements) Regulations 2014 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0158.html) ).

## **PART 2** **Amendment of Principal Regulations**

**Amendment of Regulation 61 of Principal Regulations**

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended, in Regulation 61, by the substitution of “long term, as well as concentration risk arising from exposures towards central counterparties, taking into account the conditions set out in Article 7a of Regulation (EU) No 648/2012[^3] ;” for “long term;”.

**Amendment of Regulation 64 of Principal Regulations**

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended, in Regulation 64, by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (4D):

> - “(4E) The management body shall develop specific plans and quantifiable targets in accordance with the requirements laid down in Article 7a of Regulation (EU) No 648/2012[^4] to monitor and address the concentration risk arising from exposures towards central counterparties offering services of substantial systemic importance for the Union or one or more of its Member States.”.

**Amendment of Regulation 69 of Principal Regulations**

###### 5.

5. The Principal Regulations are amended, in Regulation 69, by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (2) –

> - “(3) The Bank shall assess and monitor –
>
>     - (a) developments in the practices of institutions concerning the management of their concentration risk arising from their exposures towards central counterparties, including the plans developed in accordance with Regulation 64(4E), and
>
>     - (b) progress made in institutions’ adaptation of their business models to the requirements laid down in Article 7a of Regulation (EU) No 648/2012[^5] .”.

**Amendment of Regulation 92 of Principal Regulations**

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended, in Regulation 92(2) –

- (a) in subparagraph (n), by the substitution of “services;” for “services.”, and

- (b) by the insertion of the following subparagraph after subparagraph (n):

  > - “(o) to require institutions, where the Bank considers that there is excessive concentration risk arising from exposure towards a central counterparty, to reduce exposures towards the central counterparty or realign exposures across their clearing accounts in accordance with Article 7a of Regulation (EU) No 648/2012.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 October, 2026.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Finance.
