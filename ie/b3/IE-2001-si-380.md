# S.I. No. 380/2001 - Vocational Education (Amendment) Act, 2001 (Commencement Order), 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHAEL WOODS Minister for Education & Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec1) (3) of [the Vocational Education (Amendment) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2001), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Vocational Education (Amendment) Act, 2001 (Commencement) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The 20^th day of July, 2001, is appointed as the day on which —

- (a) the repeal of section 23 of the Principal Act, effected by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec4) of the [Vocational Education (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2001), save in so far as the said section 23 relates to a person appointed under that section, who immediately before such day held —

    - (i) office as chief executive officer of a vocational education committee,

    - (ii) office as an officer of such a committee (other than chief executive officer), or

    - (iii) any other position under that section, and

- (b) sections 11, 12, 15 and 20, of the second-mentioned Act,

shall come into operation.
