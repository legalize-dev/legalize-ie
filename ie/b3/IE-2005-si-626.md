# S.I. No. 626/2005 - National Salmon Commission (Prescribed Bodies and Organisations) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 626 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0626.html)**

**National Salmon Commission (Prescribed Bodies and Organisations) Order 2005**

I, Pat the Cope Gallagher, Minister of State at the Department of Communications, Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 5(1) (as amended by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html#sec4) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999)) and section 55A (inserted by section 22(1) of that Act) of the of the [Fisheries Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980 and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Marine and Natural Resources (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0307.html) )), and the Marine (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 703 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0703.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Salmon Commission (Prescribed Bodies and Organisations) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The bodies and organisations specified in the Schedule to this Order are prescribed for the purposes of section 55A (2) (b) (inserted by section 22(1) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999)) of the [Fisheries Act 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980).

###### 3.

3. Article 3 of and the Schedule to the National Salmon Commission (Establishment Order 2000 ( [S.I. No. 80 of 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/si/0080.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This regulation prescribes those Bodies and Organisations from which not more than 16 members of the National Salmon Commission can be appointed.
