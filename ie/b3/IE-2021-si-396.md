# S.I. No. 396/2021 - Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2021

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th July,* 2021.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec12) (1), [1302](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec1302) (2) and [1304](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec1304) (1) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (No. 38 of 2014) (as adapted by the Business, Enterprise and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 519 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0519.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Companies Act 2014 (Forms) Regulations 2021.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 August 2021.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2015” means the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) (Forms) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 147 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0147.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The form (Form F12) set out in Part 1 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 57 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

###### 4.

4. The form (Form F13) set out in Part 2 of the Schedule is substituted for the form set out in Part 63 of the Schedule of the Regulations of 2015.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe amended forms for the purposes of sections 1302(2) and 1304(1) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) .
