# S.I. No. 62/2003 - Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Building Control Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order, 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0002.html) ) is hereby amended by the insertion of “Gerard Grogan”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the appointment of a member to the Building Regulations Advisory Body (BRAB).
