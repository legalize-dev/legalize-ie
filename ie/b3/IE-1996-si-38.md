# S.I. No. 38/1996 - Patents (International Arrangements) Order, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 38 of 1996.

PATENTS (INTERNATIONAL ARRANGEMENTS) ORDER, 1996

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 3(4) and [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0025.html#sec25) (5) of the [Patents Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992 ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Patents (International Arrangements) Order, 1996.

###### 2.

2. In this Order —

"Country of the World Trade Organisation" means a country which has ratified or has acceded to the Marrakesh Agreement Establishing the World Trade Organisation Done at Marrakesh on the 15th day of April, 1994.

###### 3.

3. It is hereby declared that subsections (1) to (4) of [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Patents Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1992 ), shall apply in the case of the countries of the World Trade Organisation.

###### 4.

4. The [Patents Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (Section 93)(Declaration) Order, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 314 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0314.html) ), is hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 30th

day of January, 1996.

**JOHN BRUTON**

Taoiseach
