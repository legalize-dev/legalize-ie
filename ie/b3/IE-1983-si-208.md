# S.I. No. 208/1983 - Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 208 of 1983.

CIRCUIT COURT (FEES) ORDER, 1983.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1983.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 4.

4. In this Order "the Principal Order" means the Circuit Court (Fees) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 44 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0044.html) ).

###### 5.

5. The Principal Order is hereby amended by the deletion of article 7.

###### 6.

6. The Schedule to the Principal Order is hereby amended by the Substitution of the following for Item 1 of Part II:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "1. On the issue of any ordinary civil bill, matrimonial civil bill or summons (including Interpleader, Ejectment, Replevin, civil bills in relation to the grant or revocation of Probate or Letters of Administration and Criminal Injury Applications), irrespective of the amount involved | £10.00 | The original civil bill or summons. |

> ".

###### 7.

7. The Schedule to the Principal Order is hereby amended by the insertion after Part X of the following Part:

## PART XI.

Proceedings under the following:

[Illegitimate Children (Affiliation Orders) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1930)

[Married Womens Status Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1957)

[Guardianship of Infants Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964)

[Family Law (Maintenance of Spouses and Children) Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1976)

[Family Home Protection Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1976)

[Family Law (Protection of Spouses and Children) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1981)

[Family Law Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1981)

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Item | Item | Fee | Document to be stamped |
| 1. | On the issue or entry of every originating application | £10.00 | The application. |
| 2. | On the filing of an answer | £2.00 | The form of answer. |
| 3. | On every Order | £5.00 | The Order. |

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 19th day of July, 1983

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Justice.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 19th day of July, 1983.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order, which comes into operation on 1st August, 1983, provides that fees will be payable in relation to proceedings under the [Guardianship of Infants Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1964) and prescribes the fees payable in relation to proceedings under this and other specified family law legislation.
