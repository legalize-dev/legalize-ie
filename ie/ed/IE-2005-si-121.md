DISTRICT COURT (SMALL CLAIMS (AMENDMENT) RULES 2005.

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Small Claims) (Amendment) Rules 2005.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 18th day of March 2005 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. Order 53A of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution in the definition of “Small claim” in rule 1 thereof for sub-paragraph (3) thereof of the following:

> - “(3) in relation to a tenancy, by the tenant (not being a body corporate) against the landlord in respect of the non-return of any sum paid by the tenant as rent deposit or any such sum known as “key money”, unless such claim is a dispute that may be referred to the Private Residential Tenancies Board under Part 6 of the [Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) ”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Rules amend Order 53A of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) and provide for the exclusion of claims, which are now required to go before the Residential Tenancies Board, from the small claims procedure.
