# S.I. No. 404/2004 - Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, SÉAMUS BRENNAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 and 35 (as amended by [section 62](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html#sec62) of the [Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (No. 55 of 2001)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2003 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2004.

###### 2.

2. Article 29(1) of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) ) is amended by substituting “junction between one or more roads and a light railway” for “junction”.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Amendment Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 98 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0098.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Article 9—

    - (i) by substituting for sub-article (2) the following:

      > - “(2) Subject to sub-article (3), a person shall not enter a tram-only street or part thereof in the city of Dublin specified in the Schedule with a vehicle other than a light rail vehicle.”, and

    - (ii) by inserting after sub-article (3) the following:

      > - “(4) Where traffic sign number RUS 011 is accompanied by an information plate placed below the sign indicating the period of operation of a tram-only street, sub-article (2) applies during the period indicated only.
      >
      > - (5) In this Article, “access” means entry or exit by a vehicle (other than a light rail vehicle) to or from a road or premises adjacent to or accessible only from a tram-only street without stopping or parking the vehicle on the street.”, and

- (*b*) by substituting for the Schedule the following:

  > “SCHEDULE
  >
  > Tram-only streets
  >
  > 1. Steevens Lane.
  >
  > 2. Sean Heuston Bridge.
  >
  > 3. Arran Quay Terrace between its junction with Burges Lane and its junction with Smithfield.

###### 4.

4. Chancery Street between its junction with Church Street and its junction with Greek Street.

###### 5.

5. Abbey Street Upper between its junction with Liffey Street Lower and its junction with Jervis Street.

###### 6.

6. Abbey Street Upper between its junction with Jervis Street and its junction with Wolfe Tone Street.

###### 7.

7. Peter Place for a distance of 32 metres south of its junction with Adelaide Road.

###### 8.

8. Benburb Street from its junction with Temple Street West to its junction with Liffey Street West.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 to 2003 in relation to the application of certain traffic and parking regulations in connection with the on-street running of light rail vehicles and designate specified streets (or part thereof) in the city of Dublin as tram-only streets.
