# S.I. No. 227/1991 - European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 227 of 1991.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (RETIREMENT OF FARMERS) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive No. 72/160/EEC of 17 April 1972^(1), hereby make the following Regulations:

^(1)O.J. No. L96/9,23.4.72

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1991.

###### 3.

3. The European Communities (Retirement of Farmers) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0116.html) ) are hereby amended:

( *a* ) by the substitution of "£3,252" for "£600" and "£2,160" for "£400" in Article 7 of the Scheme set out in the Schedule to the said Regulations, and

( *b* ) by the substitution of "£2,160" for "£400" in Article 8 of the said Scheme.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 12th day ofJuly, 1991.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.
