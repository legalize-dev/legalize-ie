# S.I. No. 324/2024 - Protection of Employment Act 1977 (Notification of Proposed Collective Redundancies) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd July,* 2024.

I, PETER BURKE, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/sec0012.html#sec12) (2) of the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1977) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Protection of Employment Act 1977 (Notification of Proposed Collective Redundancies) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July 2024.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Act of 1977” means the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1977).

###### 3.

3. The following particulars shall be specified by an employer or a responsible person in a notification under section 12 of the Act of 1977:

- (a) (i) the name and address of the employer and, where applicable, of the responsible person;

    - (ii) contact details of the employer or responsible person;

    - (iii) whether the employer is a sole trader, a partnership or a body corporate;

    - (iv) where the employer is a body corporate, its registration number on the register (within the meaning of [section 887](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html#sec887) (2) of the [Companies Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0038/print.html) ) kept by the Registrar of Companies;

- (b) the address or addresses of the establishment where the collective redundancies are proposed;

- (c) the total number of persons normally employed, within the meaning of section 8 of the Act of 1977, at that establishment;

- (d) the number and descriptions or categories of employees whom it is proposed to make redundant;

- (e) the period during which the collective redundancies are proposed to be effected, stating the dates on which the first and the final dismissals are expected to take effect;

- (f) the reasons for the proposed collective redundancies;

- (g) the names and contact details of the employee representatives;

- (h) the date on which consultations with the employee representatives commenced or is expected to commence and, if commenced, the progress achieved in those consultations to the date of the notification.

###### 4.

4. The [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Notification of Proposed Collective Redundancies) Regulations 1977 ( [S.I. No. 140 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0140.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 July, 2024.

**PETER BURKE,**

Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

**EXPLANTORY NOTE**

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal Interpretation*)

These Regulations prescribe the particulars to be specified by an employer or responsible person in a notification under [Section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
