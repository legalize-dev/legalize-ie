# S.I. No. 429/1997 - Petroleum and Offshore Exploration (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 429 of 1997.

PETROLEUM AND OFFSHORE EXPLORATION (TRANSFER OF DEPARTMENTAL ADMINISTRATION AND MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1997

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0036/index.html) (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Petroleum and Offshore Exploration (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1997

###### 2.

2. In this Order "functions" includes powers and duties.

###### 3.

3. (1) The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 4 of this Order are hereby transferred to the Department of the Marine and Natural Resources.

- (2) References to the Department of Public Enterprise contained in any Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any administration and business transferred by paragraph (1) of this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Department of the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 4.

4. (1) The functions vested in the Minister for Public Enterprise by or under

( *a* ) the [Petroleum and Other Minerals Development Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1960),

( *b* ) the [Continental Shelf Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1968),

( *c* ) the [Safety, Health and Welfare (Offshore Installations) Act, 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1987), and

( *d* ) sections 10,12 and 13 of the [Energy (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0035/index.html) (No. 35 of 1995), insofar as those sections relate to energy infrastructures which are used or have been used for the purposes of the production (including the exploration of land, seabed or subsoil and the exploration of the natural resources thereof with a view to such production) of petroleum and any storage or transmission connected therewith,

including any statutory instrument made thereunder, are hereby transferred to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

- (2) References to the Minister for Public Enterprise contained in any Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any functions transferred by this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 5.

5. Where, immediately before the commencement of this Order, any legal proceedings are pending to which the Minister for Public Enterprise is a party and the proceedings have reference to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, the name of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources shall, insofar as the proceedings relate to any function transferred by this Order, be substituted in the proceedings for that of the Minister for Public Enterprise or added in the proceedings, as may be appropriate, and the proceedings shall not abate by reason of such substitution.

###### 6.

6. Anything commenced before the commencement of this Order by or under the authority of the Minister for Public Enterprise may, insofar as it relates to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources be carried on or completed on or after such commencement by the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 7.

7. Every document (including any certificate or licence) granted or made in the exercise of a function transferred by this Order or in relation to any such function shall, if and insofar as it was operative immediately before the commencement of this Order, have effect on and after such commencement as if it had been granted or made by the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 8.

8. References to the Minister for Public Enterprise contained in the memorandum and articles of association of any company and relating to any function transferred by this Order shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 9.

9. (1) All property, rights and liabilities held, enjoyed or incurred before the commencement of this Order by the Minister for Public Enterprise in connection with the functions transferred by this Order shall be and are hereby transferred to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources and, accordingly, without any further conveyance, transfer or assignment -

( *a* ) the said property, real and personal, shall, on such commencement, vest in the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources,

( *b* ) the said rights shall, as on and from such commencement, be enjoyed by the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, and

( *c* ) the said liabilities shall, as on and from such commencement, be the liabilities of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

- (2) All money s, stocks, shares and securities transferred to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources by this Article which, on such commencement, are standing in the name of the Minister for Public Enterprise shall on the request of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources be transferred into the name of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 30th day of September, 1997.

**BERTIE AHERN T.D.,**

**TAOISEACH**
