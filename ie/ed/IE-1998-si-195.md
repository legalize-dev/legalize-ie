# S.I. No. 195/1998 - Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997 (Section 5 (9)(A)) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 195 of 1998.

HEPATITIS C COMPENSATION TRIBUNAL ACT, 1997 (SECTION 5 (9)(A)) REGULATIONS, 1998.

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 5(9)(a) and [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No 34 of 1997) (as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (Section 5 (9)(a)) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. It is hereby prescribed that a claimant shall, as well as the period referred to in section 5(9)(a) of the [Hepatitis C Compensation Tribunal Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) , have a period of not later than one month from the date of commencement of rules of court in relation to such appeals, during which the claimant may decide in writing either to accept or reject the award of the Tribunal or appeal the award under subsection (15) of that section.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health and Children, this 9th day of June, 1998.

**BRIAN COWAN,**

Minister for Health and Children
