# S.I. No. 50/1965 - Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund For Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1965

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 50 of 1965.

GREAT NORTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY (IRELAND) PENSION FUND FOR WAGES STAFF (AMENDMENT) SCHEME (CONFIRMATION) ORDER, 1965.

WHEREAS by subsection (4) of [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1958), the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff was continued in being:

AND WHEREAS Córas Iompair Éireann has, in pursuanc eof subsection (10) of section 15 of the ( [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), submitted to the Minister for Transport and Power a scheme (in these Recitals referred to as the amending scheme) amending the provision of the said Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff:

AND WHEREAS the Minister for Transport and Power has thought it proper to modify the amending scheme in certain respects:

AND WHEREAS the Minister for Transport and Power, having considered objections and representations in relation to the amending scheme, proposes to confirm the amending scheme as so modified:

NOW, I, ERSKINE H. CHILDERS, Minister for Transport and Power, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (10) of [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1958) and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), hereby, after consultation with the Minister for Finance, order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1965.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "the amending scheme" means the scheme (amending the provisions of the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff) submitted by Córas Iompair Éireann under subsection (10) of [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) , and modified by the Minister for Transport and Power.

###### 3.

3. The amending scheme (a copy of which is set out in the Schedule to this Order) is hereby confirmed and shall come into operation on the 22nd day of March, 1965.

SCHEDULE.

GREAT NORTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY (IRELAND) PENSION FUND FOR WAGES STAFF (AMENDMENT) SCHEME.

1. *Definitions:*

In this amending Scheme the following words and expressions shall have the meanings hereby assigned to them unless there is something in the subject matter or context repugnant to such construction:—

"The existing Scheme" means the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff as amended by the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund (Amendment) Scheme for Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 53 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0053.html) ).

"This Scheme" means the existing Scheme as hereby amended.

"Operative date" means the date prescribed by the Order confirming this amending Scheme as the date upon which this amending Scheme is to come into operation.

"Existing member" means a contributing member of the existing Scheme at the operative date.

"Existing pensioner" means a person alive at the operative date who on that date is receiving or is entitled to receive a full or reduced pension under the existing Scheme.

"Total payments" means the minimum total of payments of pension or the maximum total of payments of pension respectively provided for by Articles 7(3) and 9(4) of the Schedule to [S.I. No. 53 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0053.html) >, or the total payments appearing, in column 5 of Table A in Article 4(1) herein, as the case may be.

2. *Continuance of Existing Scheme:*

- (1) The benefits provided by the existing Scheme shall continue to be paid or be payable under and in accordance with the terms thereof and without alteration to every existing pensioner whose right to pension accrued prior to the 1st April, 1963, and the personal representative of any deceased pensioner if the deceased's right to pension accrued prior to the 1st day of April, 1963.

- (2)
    - (*a*) Nothing in this amending Scheme shall affect the rights of any of the persons mentioned in the preceding sub-article, nor shall this amending Scheme (save in so far as it makes retrospective provision for payment of increased contributions and pensions) affect any subsisting right or liability accrued to or incurred by any person under the existing Scheme prior to the operative date.

    - (*b*) Deferred and special pensions now payable or at any time to become become payable shall not be affected by this amending Scheme.

3. *Amendment of Existing Scheme:*

- (1) Subject to the provisions of Article 2 of this amending Scheme, the existing Scheme shall be amended so as to conform to the provisions hereinafter contained, and every provision of the existing Scheme which is inconsistent with the provisions hereinafter contained shall cease to have effect.

- (2) The increased contributions and benefits provided for by this amending Scheme are, where applicable, in substitution for the corresponding contributions and benefits provided for by the existing Scheme.

###### 4.

###### 5.

5. *Reduced Pensions:*

Reduced pensions payable to pensioners whose right thereto accrued on or after the 1st April, 1963, shall be calculated as follows:

- (*a*) during the period until the pensioner reaches the age of 70—such part of the appropriate alternative rate in column 3 of Table A as is proportionate to the ratio which the number of years service bears to the number of years of possible service had such former member remained in the Board's employment until the normal age of retirement, and

- (*b*) thereafter—a like part of the appropriate alternative rate in column 4 of Table A.

###### 8.

8. *Benefits and Contribution of Existing Pensioners:*

- (1) On and from the operative date every existing pensioner whose right to pension accrued on or after the 1st day of April, 1963 and the personal representative of any deceased pensioner if the deceased's right to pension accrued on or after the 1st day of April, 1963 shall be—

    - (*a*) liable to contribute to this Scheme at the appropriate rate herein provided as from the 1st April, 1963, to the date upon which the pension under the existing Scheme first became payable;

    - (*b*) entitled as from the date upon which the pension under the existing Scheme first became payable, to payment of a like pension under this Scheme.

- (2) The amount thus due by each existing pensioner or by the personal representative of any deceased pensioner in respect of arrears of contribution (credit being given for contributions made under the existing Scheme since the 1st day of April, 1963) shall be deducted from the amount likewise due to him in respect of arrears of pension (credit being taken for pension paid under the existing Scheme since the said date) and the balance, if any, shall be forthwith paid to him out of the Fund.

- (3) If the said amount due by any such person as aforesaid exceeds the said amount payable to him, the excess shall be discharged by deducting the difference between the present and former rate of contribution from the pension payable to him or his personal representative until such excess shall have been fully paid off.

###### 9.

9. *Recovery of Contributions:*

If any contribution is not so deducted as aforesaid, it may be deducted from any money payable by the Fund to the existing member or pensioner or to the personal representative of a deceased member or pensioner or it shall be paid into the Fund by the existing member or pensioner or by the personal representative of a deceased member or pensioner liable to pay the same in accordance with any directions of the Trustees in that behalf.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of March, 1965.

**ERSKINE H. CHILDERS,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of the Order is to confirm a scheme to amend the existing Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff so as to provide for increased pensions for members of the scheme with effect from 1st April, 1963.
