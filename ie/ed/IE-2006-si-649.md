# S.I. No. 649/2006 - Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 649 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0649.html)**

**Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by sections 53 and 72 of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (as amended by the [Health (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) (Amendment) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. The Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 276 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0276.html) ) are amended by substituting for Regulation 2 the following:

> “2(1) Subject to paragraph (2) herein, these regulations shall apply to all persons to whom in patient services are provided and whether or not such persons have full or limited eligibility.
>
> - (2) These regulations shall not apply to any person receiving in patient services pursuant to s 52 of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the diagnosis and treatment of diseases prescribed under Part IV of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) .”

###### 3.

3. These regulations shall come into operation on 15^th day of December, 2006.

**Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006**

Explanatory Memorandum

These regulations amend the Health (Charges for In-Patient Services) Regulations 2005 to exclude people receiving services for the diagnosis and treatment of diseases prescribed under Part IV of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) from charges under the regulations.
