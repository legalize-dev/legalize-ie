DISTRICT COURT (COMBINED COURT OFFICES) RULES 2009

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Combined Court Offices) Rules 2009.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 11th day of January 2010 and shall be construed together with the District Court Rules 1997 to 2009.

###### 3.

3. The District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) are amended by the substitution in the “Interpretation of Terms” provisions for the definition of “Clerk” of the following:

> *“ “Clerk”, save where the context otherwise requires, means a District Court Clerk attached to the District Court in accordance with [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0027/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Court Officers Act 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) and assigned to any one or more court areas in accordance with section 48 of that Act or a person temporarily appointed to perform the duties and fulfil the functions of such Clerk, and where a District Court Clerk conducts business in a combined courts office established under [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0036/print.html#sec14) of the [Courts and Court Officers Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) , any reference in these Rules to the office of that Clerk or to the District Court office to which that business relates is or includes a reference to that combined courts office, and any form in the Schedule of Forms may be modified accordingly.”*

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This does not form part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These rules amend the District Court Rules to facilitate the transaction of business in a combined court office established under [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0036/print.html#sec14) of the [Courts and Court Officers Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0036/print.html) .
