PHARMACEUTICAL: SOCIETY OF IRELAND (FEES) (AMENDMENT) RULES 2010

The Council of the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland, in exercise of thefunctions conferred on the said Society by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec11) of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007) and paragraph 20(1) of the Schedule 1 to that Act, hereby makes the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These Rules may be cited as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 2010.

###### 2.

2. These Rules shall be construed as one with the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 496 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0496.html) ) and may be cited together with those Rules as the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2008 and 2010.

###### 3.

3. The Schedule to the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2008 is hereby amended by—

- (1) the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph 7:—

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (7A). | Fee payable under Rule 4(3)(b) of the Retail Pharmacy Businesses Rules, being the fee payable, in addition to, and without prejudice to, any fee due under paragraph 7, in connection with an application for the temporary registration of a retail pharmacy business, for a maximum period of one year, in the Register of Retail Pharmacy Businesses, in circumstances where an additional temporary registration is necessitated by the temporary relocation of a registered retail pharmacy business due to the renovation and/or refurbishment of its registered premises: | €1000.00 |

- (2) the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph 9:—

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9A. | Late fee payable under Rule 4(5) of the Retail Pharmacy Business Rules, being a fee additional to that payable under paragraph 7A in connection with an application for the temporary registration of a retail pharmacy business in the Register of Retail Pharmacy Businesses: | €500.00 |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The Rules amend the Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (Fees) Rules 2008 ( [S.I. No. 496 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0496.html) ) by inserting provisions setting fees in connection with the temporary registration of retail pharmacy businesses due to the renovation and/or refurbishment of the premises of a registered retail pharmacy business.
