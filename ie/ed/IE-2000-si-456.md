# S.I. No. 456/2000 - Water Supplies (Application For Provisional Order) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0001/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Water Supplies Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1942), hereby makes the following Regulations:-

Citation, commencement and purpose.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the “Water Supplies (Application for Provisional Order) Regulations, 2000”.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January, 2001.

- (3) The purposes for which these Regulations are made include the purpose of giving effect to section 8 of the Act of 1942 as amended by the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000).

Interpretation.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations:—

“the Act of 1942” means the [Water Supplies Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1942).

Revocation.

###### 3.

3. The Water Supplies (Application for Provisional Order) Regulations, 1945 ( [S.R. and O. No. 265 of 1945](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1945/en/si/0265.html) ) is hereby revoked.

Certificate to accompany an application by a sanitary authority.

###### 4.

4. The certificate referred to in paragraph (*d*) of sub-section (2) of section 8 of the Act of 1942 which is to accompany an application by a sanitary authority under the said section 8 for a provisional order, shall be in the form set forth in the schedule to these regulations.

Additional documents to accompany application.

###### 5.

5. In addition to the documents mentioned in paragraphs (*a*), (*b*), (*c*) and (*d*) of sub-section (2) of section 8 of the Act of 1942, the following documents shall accompany every application by a sanitary authority for a provisional order declaring that a proposal may come into force, that is to say:-

- (*a*) one copy of the notice of such proposal given in pursuance of paragraph (*d*) of sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Act of 1942, and

- (*b*) one copy of the newspaper containing the notice of such proposal published in pursuance of paragraph (*e*) of sub-section (1) of section 4 of the Act of 1942.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations revoke the Water Supplies (Application for Provisional Order) Regulations, 1945 ( [S.I. No. 265 of 1945](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1945/en/si/0265.html) ) and provide replacement Regulations to implement [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0001/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Water Supplies Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) as amended by the [Planning and Development Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) . The new Regulation enables applications for a provisional order for the taking of a supply to be made to An Bord Pleanála.
