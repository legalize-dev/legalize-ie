**[S.I. No. 179 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0179.html)**

**Companies (Fees) Order 2005**

I, Michael Ahern, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 395 (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 28 of 2001)) and 396 of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) 33 of 1963), (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No.2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 808 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0808.html) ) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Eighth Schedule” means the Eighth Schedule (substituted by the Companies (Fees) Order 2001 ( [S.I No. 477 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0477.html) ), and amended by the Companies (Fees) (No.2) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 569 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0569.html) ), the Companies (Fees) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 557 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0557.html) ) and the Companies (Fees) Order 2003 ( [S.I. No. 187 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0187.html) ) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) 33 of 1963).

###### 3.

3. Part I of the Eighth Schedule is altered by substituting the following for paragraphs (a) to (j):

> **Explanatory Note**
>
> *(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*
>
> This Order amends the Companies (Fees) Order 2001 (S.I. 477 of 2001), as amended
>
> The Order provides for disapplication of filing fees where certain specified documents are filed electronically with the Registrar of Companies.
