# S.I. No. 148/2001 - Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, on the request of the Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development and in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) (No. 2) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1977), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. All the powers and duties of the Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development conferred on the Minister -

- (*a*) by or under the Acts specified in the First Schedule to this Order,

- (*b*) under the regulations and orders specified in the Second Schedule to this Order, and

- (*c*) by or under the Local Government Acts, 1925 to 2000 and the Local Authorities (Officers and Employees) Acts, 1926 to 1983, in relation to the office or to the holder of the office of veterinary inspector under a local authority,

are hereby delegated to Noel Davern, Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture, Food and Rural Development.
