# S.I. No. 245/1983 - Licensing of Sea-Fishing Boats (Exemption) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 245 of 1983.

LICENSING OF SEA-FISHING BOATS (EXEMPTION) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, PADDY O'TOOLE, Minister for Fisheries and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) (*b*) of section 222 B (inserted by the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1983)) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) ) (No. 14 of 1959), and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 195 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0195.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Licensing of Sea-fishing Boats (Exemption) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "length", in relation to a sea-fishing boat, means the distance from the forepart of the stem of the boat to—

( *a* ) in case the boat has a sternpost, the aft side of the head of the sternpost,

( *b* ) in any other case, the forepart of the rudder stock.

###### 3.

3. A sea-fishing boat which measures 65 feet or less in length shall be exempt from the provisions of subsection (2) of section 222 B (inserted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1983)) of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1959).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of August, 1983.

**PADDY O'TOOLE,**

Minister for Fisheries and Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to exempt Irish sea-fishing boats of 65 feet and under in the defined length from the provisions of subsection (2) of section 222 B of the [Fisheries (Consolidation) Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) which requires that sea-fishing boats shall not fish except under licence by the Minister for Fisheries and Forestry.
