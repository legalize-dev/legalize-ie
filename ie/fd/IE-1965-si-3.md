# S.I. No. 3/1965 - County Management (Kilkenny and Waterford) Order, 1965

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 3 of 1965.

COUNTY MANAGEMENT (KILKENNY AND WATERFORD) ORDER, 1965.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [City and County Management (Amendment) Act, 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , with the consent of the Minister for Health and the Minister for Social Welfare, hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1.—(1) This Order may be cited as the County Management (Kilkenny and Waterford) Order, 1965.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the first day of April, 1965.

###### 2.

2. The application in relation to the Counties of Kilkenny and Waterford of subsection (2) of [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , (No. 12 of 1940) is hereby terminated.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for

Local Government this Fourteenth day of January, 1965.

**NEIL T. BLANEY,**

Minister for Local Government.

The Minister for Health hereby consents to the above Order.

Dated this 14th day of January, 1965.

**SEÁN MAC ENTEE,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Social Welfare hereby consents to the above Order. Dated this 14th day of January, 1965.

**CAOIMHGHÍN Ó BEOLÁIN,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order terminates, as from 1st April, 1965, the application of subsection (2) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [County Management Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , to the Counties of Kilkenny and Waterford. Under that subsection, the same person was to be county manager for each of those counties. The effect of this Order is to require each of those counties to have its own individual county manager.
