# S.I. No. 187/2018 - Central Bank Reform Act 2010 (Sections 20 and 22 - Credit Unions) (Amendment) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th June*, 2018.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Central Bank of Ireland (“the Bank”) by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec20) (1) and [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec22) (2) of the [Central Bank Reform Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) the Bank hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Central Bank Reform Act 2010 (Sections 20 and 22 — Credit Unions) (Amendment) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the Central Bank Reform Act 2010 (Sections 20 and 22 - Credit Unions) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 171 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0171.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 4 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the insertion of “and Schedule 3” after the phrase “Schedule 2”.

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended by the insertion therefor of the Schedule of these Regulations after Schedule 2 of SI 171 of 2013 (as amended).

###### 5.

5. These Regulations come into operation on 1 July 2018.

Signed for and on behalf of the CENTRAL BANK OF IRELAND,

31 May 2018.

ED SIBLEY,

Deputy Governor (Prudential Regulation).

## **Schedule**

## Schedule 3 Pre-Approval Controlled Functions

In relation to

Credit Unions with minimum total asset size of €100 million according to the credit union’s latest audited balance sheet.

A person who holds or performs the duties of any of the following positions or offices in the credit union:

- (*a*) Risk Management Officer (CUPCF-3),

- (*b*) Head of Internal Audit (CUPCF-4),

- (*c*) Head of Finance (CUPCF-5).
