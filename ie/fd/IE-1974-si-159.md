# S.I. No. 159/1974 - Control of Imports (Quota No. 36, Fifty-Second Period) (Laminated Springs) Order, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 159 of 1974.

CONTROL OF IMPORTS (QUOTA NO. 36, FIFTY-SECOND PERIOD) (LAMINATED SPRINGS) ORDER, 1974.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by the Control of Imports Acts, 1934 to 1964, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Imports (Quota No. 36, Fifty-second Period) (Laminated Springs) Order, 1974.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The period specified in the second column of the Schedule to this Order is hereby appointed to be the next quota period under the quota order specified in the first column of the said Schedule and the quantity of goods to which such quota order applies specified in the third column of the said Schedule is hereby appointed to be the quota in respect of the said next quota period.

SCHEDULE.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Control of imports (Quota No. 36) Order, 1937 (S. R. & O., No. 314 of 1937), as amended. | The period beginning on the 1st day of July, 1974, and ending on the 30th day of June, 1975. | A quantity equal in value to two hundred thousand pounds (£200,000). | Certain laminated springs and certain component parts of laminated springs. |

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 24th day of

May, 1974.

**LIAM MAC COSGAIR,**

Taoiseach.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Instrument appoints a quantity of Laminated Springs equal in value to £200,000 as the quantity that may be imported during the period 1st July, 1974 to 30th June, 1975.
