# S.I. No. 235/1998 - Oireachtas (Allowances To Members) (Travelling Facilities and Overnight Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 235 of 1998.

OIREACHTAS (ALLOWANCES TO MEMBERS) (TRAVELLING FACILITIES AND OVERNIGHT ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998.

I, CHARLIE McCREEVY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 1 (inserted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Oireachtas (Miscellaneous Provisions) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1996), as amended by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998) (" the Act of 1998 ")) and 3 (as amended by section 17 of the Act of 1998) of the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) Act, 1962 (No. 32 of 1962), [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) (as amended by section 18 of the Act of 1998) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Amendment) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1964), and the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 81 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0081.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Travelling Facilities and Overnight Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of May, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) (Travelling Facilities and Overnight Allowance) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0101.html) ), are hereby amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 4, by the substitution—

    - (i) in paragraph (*a*), for "paragraph (*b*) (ii) (I)" of "paragraph (*b*) (ii) ",

    - (ii) in subparagraph (i) of paragraph (*b*), for "paragraph (*b*) (ii) (I)" of "paragraph (*b*) (ii) ", and

    - (iii) in paragraph (*b*), for subparagraph (ii) of the following subparagraph:

      > - "(ii) a mileage allowance at the rates payable to civil servants for cars coming within the highest engine capacity category in respect of each relevant period.",

- (*b*) by the insertion of the following Regulation after Regulation 10:

  > "10A. The form in which a member may make a claim for payment of an allowance under these Regulations shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.",

- (*c*) in Regulation 11 (*b*), by the insertion after "1994" of "( [S.I. No. 343 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0343.html) ) ", and

- (*d*) by the insertion after Regulation 11 of the following Schedule:
