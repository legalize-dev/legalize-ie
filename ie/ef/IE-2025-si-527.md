# S.I. No. 527/2025 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th November,* 2025.

I, SEAN CANNEY, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) (1), [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0035.html#sec35) (1) and [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0042.html#sec42) (inserted by the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), and the Transport (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2025 ( [S.I. ###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 November 2026.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) are amended—

- (a) in Regulation 17—

    - (i) by substituting for paragraph (2) the following:

      > - “(2) A learner permit shall be granted—
      >
      >     - (a) for a period of 2 years beginning on the day on which the permit is granted, or
      >
      >     - (b) for a period of two years together with the unexpired period of a learner permit previously granted to the applicant.”,

      and

    - (ii) by substituting for paragraph (4) the following:

      > - “(4) A one year learner permit shall be granted -
      >
      >     - (a) for a period of one year beginning on the day on which the permit is granted, or
      >
      >     - (b) for a period of two years together with the unexpired period of a learner permit previously granted to the applicant.”,

- (b) by substituting for Regulation 19 the following:

  > “**Application for learner permit**.
  >
  > 19. (1) (a) A person shall not apply for a learner permit to drive vehicles of a category where that person has held a learner permit in respect of vehicles in that category for a period of 4 years or more unless, within the period of 2 years ending on the date of application, that person underwent a driving test in relation to vehicles of that category and produces evidence to that effect to the licensing authority.
  >
  > - (b) A person to whom subparagraph (a) relates and who has not produced evidence to the effect that he or she underwent a driving test, may subsequently apply for a learner permit in respect of that category (other than category A, A1, A2, AM, B or W) where such application is accompanied by evidence that a driving test has been arranged for that person in respect of a vehicle in that category. Notwithstanding Regulation 17(2), a learner permit granted in accordance with this subparagraph has effect for a period of one year.
  >
  > - (c) A person to whom subparagraph (a) relates and who has held a learner permit to drive vehicles of category A, A1, A2, AM, B or W for a period of 3, 5 or 6 years at the expiry date of the then current learner permit held by him or her, any subsequent learner permit granted shall, notwithstanding Regulation 17(4), be issued for a period of one year only.
  >
  > - (d) Where a person has held a learner permit for driving vehicles of category A, A1, A2, AM, B or W for a period of 7 years or more at the expiry date of the then current learner permit held by the person, the application shall be regarded as an application for a first learner permit.
  >
  > - (2) A person shall not apply for a first learner permit in respect of vehicles in—
  >
  >     - (a) category C1, C, D1 or D, unless the person holds a driving licence in respect of vehicles in category B,
  >
  >     - (b) category BE, CE, C1E, DE or D1E, unless the person holds a driving licence in respect of the drawing vehicle appropriate to the category which is the subject of the application,
  >
  >     - (c) any category in respect of which the person holds a recognised driving licence, or
  >
  >     - (d) category A, unless the person holds for not less than 2 years a driving licence in respect of category A2 or has attained the age of 24 years.
  >
  > - (3) An application for a learner permit in respect of a category of vehicles is regarded as an application for a first learner permit in respect of that category, unless the applicant held a learner permit in that category at some point in time within the period of 2 years preceding the date of application that was not revoked under Regulation 21A(2).”,

- (c) in Regulation 20(5)(d), by substituting “or a learner permit within the period of 2 years” for “or a provisional licence within the period of 5 years”,

  and

- (d) by inserting after Regulation 27 the following:

  > “**Certain limitation on learner permit holder applying for certificate of competency**.
  >
  > 27A. A person who is granted a learner permit for a vehicle in category A, A1, AM, W, B by a licensing authority and has not previously held, within the period of 2 years prior to the granting, such a permit in that category, is not entitled to make an application for a certificate of competency to an issuing authority within the period of 6 months from the day the permit comes into force.”,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) to —

- • amend the standard period for which a learner permit is granted from 2 years only to a period of 2 years or 2 years together with the unexpired period of a learner permit previously granted to the applicant.

- • limit the number of times an applicant can renew their learner permit in categories AM, A, A1, A2, B or W without having provided evidence to the licencing authority that they underwent a driving test in relation to vehicles of that category.

- • limit the number of years an applicant can hold a learner permit in those categories to a maximum of 7 years, where any subsequent application in those categories shall be regarded as an application for a first learner permit.

- • reduce the time in which an expired learner permit may be renewed, from 5 years to 2 years.

- • remove the illness concession, where a learner who did not sit a test because of ill health may still renew their permit.

- • provide that a person who is granted a learner permit for a vehicle in category A, A1, AM, W, or B by a licensing authority and has not previously held, within the period of 2 years prior to the granting, such a permit in that category, is not entitled to make an application to an issuing authority for a certificate of competency within the period of 6 months from the day the permit comes into force.
