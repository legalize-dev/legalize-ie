# S.I. No. 67/1986 - Redundancy Payments (Variation of Employer's Redundancy Contribution) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 67 of 1986.

REDUNDANCY PAYMENTS (VARIATION OF EMPLOYER'S REDUNDANCY CONTRIBUTION) REGULATIONS, 1986

I, RUAIRI QUINN, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the power conferred on me by subsection (3) of section 28 (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Redundancy Payments Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1979)) of the [Redundancy Payments Act, 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1967), with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Redundancy Payments (Variation of Employer's Redundancy Contribution) Regulations, 1986.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 6th day of April, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The employer's redundancy contribution specified in subsection (1) of section 28 (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Redundancy Payments Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1979) and amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0021/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Protection of Employees (Employers' Insolvency) Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1984)) of the [Redundancy Payments Act, 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1967), shall, in lieu of the rate specified in the Redundancy Payments (Variation of Employer's Redundancy Contribution) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 75 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0075.html) ), be calculated at a rate of 0.6 per cent of the amount of the reckonable earnings referred to in the said subsection (1).

Given under my Official Seal, this 24th day of March, 1986.

**RUAIRI QUINN,**

Minister for Labour.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this

24th day of March, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to change the rate of contribution payable by employers under the Redundancy Payments Acts 1967 to 1984 from 0.5% to 0.6% of the reckonable earnings of each worker covered by these Acts with effect from 6th April, 1986.
