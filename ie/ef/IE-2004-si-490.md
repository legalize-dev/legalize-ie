# S.I. No. 490/2004 - European Communities (Amendment of S.I. No. 68 of 2003) Regulations, 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 490 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0490.html) .**

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (AMENDMENT OF [S.I. NO. 68 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0068.html) ) REGULATIONS, 2004.

[S.I. No. 490 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0490.html) .

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (AMENDMENT OF [S.I. NO. 68 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0068.html) ) REGULATIONS, 2004.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to certain provisions of Directive No. 2000/31/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2000[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and commencement.*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Amendment of [S.I. No. 68 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0068.html) ) Regulations 2004.

*Amendment of Regulation 21*

###### 2.

2. Regulation 21 of the European Communities (Directive 2000/31/EC) Regulations 2003 is amended by substituting for paragraph (2) the following:

> - “(2) The Director may appoint one or more persons to be an authorised officer or authorised officers for the purpose of this Regulation.”

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of July, 2004. |
|  | MARY HARNEY, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to clarify who the Director of Consumer Affairs may authorise for the purposes of Regulation 21.
