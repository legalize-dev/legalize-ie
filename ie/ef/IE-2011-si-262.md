S.I. No. 262 of 2011

PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2011

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th June*, 2011.

The Minister for Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec11) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec12) , [13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec13) , [20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec20) , [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec24) , 37D, 181C, 182E and [262](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec262) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) , as amended, hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and commencement*

1. (*a*) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning and Development (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

- (*b*) These Regulations and the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 to 2010 shall be construed as one and may be collectively cited as the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 to 2011.

- (*c*) These Regulations shall come into operation on 3 June 2011.

*Interpretation*

2. In these Regulations “the Regulations” means the Planning and Development Regulations 2001, as amended.

*Substitution of Article 14 of the Regulations*

4. Article 14 of the Regulations is substituted by the following article:

> “14.The prescribed authorities for the purposes of section 20 of the Act shall be—
>
> - (*a*) the Minister,
>
> - (*b*) the Minister for Arts, Heritage and the Gaeltacht,
>
> - (*c*) any local authority, including town councils, in the area to which the local area plan, proposed local area plan or proposed amended plan, as appropriate, relates, and any relevant city and county development board, and

> - (*d*) any planning authority whose area is contiguous to the area to which the local area plan, proposed local area plan or proposed amended plan, as appropriate, relates.”.
