# S.I. No. 117/2000 - National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency (Delegation of Functions) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The functions of the Minister for Finance under subsections (2), (3) and (4) of [section 157](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec157) of the [Finance Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2000), are hereby delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

As provided for in [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) and [Section 157](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec157) of the [Finance Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) , this Order delegates to the National Treasury Management Agency the statutory functions of the Minister for Finance relating to deposit accounts for Exchequer moneys.
