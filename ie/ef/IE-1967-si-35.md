# S.I. No. 35/1967 - Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Act, 1966 (Appointed Day) Order, 1967

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 35 of 1967.

SOCIAL WELFARE (OCCUPATIONAL INJURIES) ACT, 1966 (APPOINTED DAY) ORDER, 1967.

I, JOSEPH BRENNAN, Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0016/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1966), hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (Appointed Day) Order, 1967.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of May, 1967, shall be the appointed day for the purposes of the [Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) .

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 17th day of February, 1967.

**JOSEPH BRENNAN,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order brings into operation on 1st May, 1967 the provisions of the [Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , which extends the present Social Insurance Scheme to provide for occupational injuries and diseases.
