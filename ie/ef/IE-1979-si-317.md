S.I. No. 317 of 1979.

TELEX (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, PADRAIG FAULKNER, Minister for Posts and Telegraphs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 2 of the Telegraph Act, 1885, and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Telex (Amendment) Regulations, 1979.

- (2) The Telex Regulations, 1955 to 1979, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Telex Regulations, 1955 to 1979.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 1979.

###### 3.

3. (1) In these Regulations "the 1955 Regulations" means the Telex Regulations, 1955 ( [S.I. No. 229 of 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/si/0229.html) ).

- (2) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) The 1955 Regulations are hereby amended by:—

( *a* ) the substitution in Regulation 5 (1A) of "18p" for "15p" (inserted by Regulation 4 (1) (*a*) of the Telex (Amendment) Regulations, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 73 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0073.html) ),

( *b* ) the substitution in Regulation 29 of "55.5p" for "46.5p" (inserted by the said Telex (Amendment) Regulations, 1977.

*and*

( *c* ) the substitution of the following Schedule for the Schedule (inserted by the said Telex (Amendment) Regulations, 1977:

4. (11) Regulations 5 (1A) and 29 of the 1955 Regulations, as amended by paragraph (1) of this Regulation, are set out in the Table to this Regulation.

TABLE

###### 5.

5. (1A)—There shall be charged and paid in respect of every call made by a caller from a call office, in addition to the sum chargeable under paragraph (1) of this Regulation, a call office fee of 18p.

29. If any subscription or any fee, charge, expense or damages payable by the subscriber under the agreement or under the other provisions of these Regulations is in arrear for fourteen days, excluding Sundays and public holidays, after the relative account has been rendered, or the subscriber has failed to offer such security as may be required by the Minister in accordance with Regulation 17, the Minister may, without notice, and without prejudice to the terms of the agreement or to the provisions of these Regulations, suspend service on the installation or disconnect the installation from the telex exchange and may make in respect of any restoration of service after suspension or disconnection under the terms of this Regulation a charge of 55.5p to be payable on demand.
