# S.I. No. 246/1997 - Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 246 of 1997.

TELECOMMUNICATIONS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 3) SCHEME, 1997

BORD TELECOM ÉIREANN plc, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Telecommunications (Amendment) (No. 3) Scheme, 1997.

###### 2.

2. (1) The collective citation "the Telecommunications Schemes, 1994 to 1997" includes this Scheme.

- (2) Article 5(*f*) Part I, Article 5(*f*) Part 2 and Article 5(*h*) Part 6 shall be deemed to have come into operation on I December 1996.

- (3) Article 5(*c*) and Article 5(*h*) Part 9 shall be deemed to have come into operation on 27 January 1997.

- (4) Articles 5(*a*) and 5(*b*) and Article 5(*h*) Parts 7 and 8 shall be deemed to have come into operation on 24 March 1997.

- (5) Articles 5(*e*) and 5(*i*) shall be deemed to have come into operation on 9 April 1997.

- (6) Articles 5(*f*) Part 3 and 5(*h*) Part 3 shall come into operation on 2 May 1997.

- (7) Articles 5(*d*), 5 (*g*) and 5(*j*) shall come into operation on 15 May 1997.

###### 3.

3. References in this Scheme to "NCRTN" are references to "National Cellular Radio Telecommunications Networks".

###### 4.

4. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Scheme.

###### 5.

5. The Telecommunications Scheme, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 177 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0177.html) ) is hereby amended by:

( *a* ) the addition of the following Article 19(C) after Article 19 (B) thereof:

"19(c)(1) Bord Telecom Eireann plc may provide a facility (in this Scheme referred to as "Business Selections Discount" facility) to telephone subscribers generally or to such groups of telephone subscribers as it shall determine whereby a telephone subscriber, connected to the fixed telephone network, through a telephone exchange, other than a metered telephone exchange may, on payment of a monthly subscription charge, be allowed discounted rates on "Eligible Business Selections Calls". The discounted rates are set out in Part 7(i) of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme. Bord Telecom Eireann plc may at its absolute discretion withdraw the Business Selections Discount Facility from all or any subscribers.

- (2) For the purposes of this Scheme "Eligible Business Selections Calls" shall mean subscriber direct dialled calls made on "Inter City Routes" and between exchanges in the State and from exchanges in the State to Northern Ireland and from exchanges in the State to exchanges outside the State (excluding Chargecard calls, Eirpac calls, calls to other Packet Switching Networks, unrestricted 64 Kb calls made by use of the Integrated Services Digital Network (ISDN) facility, calls made to Premium Rate Telephone Services, Directory Enquiry calls, calls to Call Management Services, calls made by use of the National Virtual Private Network facility, calls made by use of the International Virtual Private Network facility, calls made to Inmarsat and Skyphone mobile telephone system and Freefone 1800, 1850 and 1890 calls received by the subscriber); and

Calls made on "Inter City Routes" shall mean calls made between Exchanges on the routes specified at Part 7(ii) of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme.

- (3) It shall be a condition of the application of the Business Selections Discount facility that:

    - (i) the subscriber pays the monthly subscription charge set out in Part 7(i) of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule hereto;

    - (ii) Eligible Business Selections Calls shall be made only from a single premises occupied by the subscriber;

    - (iii) accounts to which the Business Selections Discount facility apply must be discharged in full within 14 days of the date of issue of the bill or by means of a direct debit mandate approved by Bord Telecom Éireann plc; and

    - (iv) Eligible Business Selections Calls shall not be eligible for any further or other discounts (including any discounts under Articles 18, 19, 19(A), 19(B), 19(D) and 19(E) of this Scheme)."

( *b* ) the addition of the following Article 19(D) after Article 19(0 thereof:

"19 (D) (1) Bord Telecom Eireann plc. may provide a facility (in this Scheme referred to as "Multi-Site Volume Discount" facility) to telephone subscribers generally or to such groups of telephone subscribers as it shall determine whereby a telephone subscriber connected to the fixed telephone network through a telephone exchange other than a metered telephone exchange may, on payment of a Network Fee and a Site Fee, receive a discount on Eligible Subscriber Dialled Calls made from Nominated Telecommunications Lines situated at two or more Sites occupied by the subscriber. The Multi-Site Volume Discounted rates are set out in Part 8 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme. Bord Telecom Eireann plc. may, at its absolute discretion withdraw the "Multi-Site Volume discount" facility from all or any subscribers.

- (2) For the purposes of this Scheme "Eligible Subscriber Dialled Calls" means

    - (i) subscriber dialled calls (excluding Chargecard calls, Eirpac calls, calls made to Premium Rate Telephone Services, calls made by use of the International Virtual Private Network facility, Freefone 1800, 1850 and 1890 calls received by the subscriber, unrestricted 64 Kb calls made by use of the Integrated Services Digital Network facility (ISDN), calls to Call Management Services and calls made to Inmarsat and Skyphone mobile telephony systems) made between exchanges in the State (including Northern Ireland) and from exchanges in the State to exchanges outside the State;

    - (ii) "Network Fee" means the fee payable by the subscriber to avail of the Multi-Site Discount facility as set out in Part 8 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule hereto.

    - (iii) "Nominated Telecommunications Line(s)" means the telecommunications line(s) nominated by the subscriber and situated at a Site; and

    - (iv) "Site" means a single premises occupied by the subscriber or a wholly owned subsidiary of the subscriber.

    - (v) "Site Fee" means the fee payable by the subscriber in respect of each Site where Nominated Telecommunications Lines are situated as set out in Part 8 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule hereto.

- (3) It shall be a condition of the application of the Multi-Site Discount facility that:

    - (i) the subscriber pays the Network Fee and the Site Fee set out in Parts 8 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule hereto;

    - (ii) Eligible Subscriber Dialled Calls shall be made only from Site(s) of which there shall be at least two (as distinct from a single Site);

    - (iii) charges for Eligible Subscriber Dialled Calls shall be brought to account by way of a single bill in respect of all Nominated Telecommunications Lines;

    - (iv) accounts to which the Multi-Site Discount facility applies shall be discharged in full within 14 days of the date of issue of the bill, or by means of a direct debit mandate approved by Bord Telecom Eireann plc.;

    - (v) the average monthly call usage of all the Nominated Telecommunications Lines for Multi Site Volume Discount must exceed £20 per month: and

    - (vi) Eligible Subscriber Dialled calls shall not be eligible for any further or other discounts (including any other discounts under Articles 18, 19, 19(A), 19(B), 19(0 and 19(E) of this Scheme).

( *c* ) the addition of the following Article 19(E) after Article 19(D) thereof:

"19(E) (1) Bord Telecom Eireann plc may provide a facility (in this Scheme referred to as "Residential International Bonus" facility) to telephone subscribers generally or to such groups of telephone subscribers as it shall determine, whereby a telephone subscriber connected to the fixed telephone network through a telephone exchange other than a metered telephone exchange may, on the payment of a subscription receive a discount on Eligible Calls on a single telephone account. The Residential International Bonus shall be calculated as set out in Part 9 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule to this Scheme. Bord Telecom Eireann plc. may, at its absolute discretion withdraw the "Residential International Bonus" facility from all or any subscribers.

2. For the purposes of this Scheme "Eligible Calls" are subscriber dialled calls made to telephone exchanges outside the State (excluding Northern Ireland) made from a telephone exchange line rented by a subscriber (excluding Chargecard calls, Eirpac calls, calls to packet switched networks, unrestricted 64 Kb calls made by use of the Integrated Services Digital Network (ISDN) facility, calls made to Premium Rate Telephone Services, Directory Enquiry calls, calls to Call Management Services, calls made by use of the International Virtual Private Network Facility, calls made to Inmarsat and Skyphone Mobile Telephony Systems, Operator Assisted calls and Freefone 1800, 1850 and 1890 calls received by the subscriber).

- (3) It shall be a condition of the application of the "Residential International Bonus" facility that:

    - (i) the subscriber pays the subscription charge set out in Part 9 of Section VII of Part 6 of the First Schedule hereto;

    - (ii) an account to which the Residential International Bonus facility applies shall be discharged in full within 14 days of the date of issue of the bill or by means of a direct debit mandate approved by Bord Telecom Eireann plc.; and

    - (iii) Eligible Calls shall not be eligible for any further or other discounts (including any discount under Articles 18, 19(A), 19(B), 19(0 and 19(D) of this Scheme)."
