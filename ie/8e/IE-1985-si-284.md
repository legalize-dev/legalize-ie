# S.I. No. 284/1985 - Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980 (Convention Countries) Order, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 284 of 1985.

PLANT VARIETIES (PROPRIETARY RIGHTS) ACT, 1980 (CONVENTION COUNTRIES) ORDER, 1985.

WHEREAS it is enacted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1980), that for the purpose of enabling any international convention or agreement for the protection of plant varieties and plant breeders' rights to which the state is a party to be carried into effect, the Government may by order declare one or more foreign countries, which foreign country or each of which foreign countries, shall be one whose government, or any of whose departments of state is a party to the convention or agreement, to be a convention country for the purposes of that Act, and that for so long as the order remains in force any foreign country which is one specified in the declaration contained therein shall be a convention country for the purposes of that Act:

AND WHEREAS each of the foreign countries mentioned in the Schedule to this Order is one whose government or a department of state of which is party to the International Convention for the protection of New Varieties of Plants:

NOW, the Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , (No. 24 of 1980). hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Convention Countries) Order, 1985.

- (2) The [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Convention Countries) Order, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 190 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0190.html) ) and this Order may be cited together as the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Convention Countries) Orders 1981 and 1985.

###### 2.

2. Each of the foreign countries mentioned in the Schedule to this order shall be a convention country for the purposes of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .

SCHEDULE.

Hungarian People's Republic.

Japan.

New Zealand.

United States of America.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government, this 9th day of

May, 1985.

**DICK SPRING,**

Tánaiste.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to designate as Convention countries for the purposes of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , those countries which have become party to the International Convention for the protection of New Varieties of Plants (known as the UPOV Convention) since the first Order under the Act was made in 1981.
