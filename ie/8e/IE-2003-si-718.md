# S.I. No. 718/2003 - National Development Finance Agency Act 2002 (Amendment of Schedule) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec22) (1) of the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002), after consultation with the Minister for Transport, hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to section 22(2) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and no resolution disapproving of the draft has been passed by either such House within the next subsequent 21 days on which each House sat after the draft was laid before it:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Development Finance Agency Act 2002 (Amendment of Schedule) Order 2003.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002) is amended by adding the following to it:

> “Bus Átha Cliath - Dublin Bus.
>
> Bus Éireann — Irish Bus.
>
> Córas Iompar Éireann.
>
> Iarnród Éireann-Irish Rail.
>
> The Railway Procurement Agency.”.
