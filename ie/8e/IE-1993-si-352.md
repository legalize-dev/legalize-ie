# S.I. No. 352/1993 - Greyhound Race Track (Racing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 352 of 1993.

GREYHOUND RACE TRACK (RACING) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

Bord na gCon in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Sections 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0013.html#sec13) , [25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0025.html#sec25) and [48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Greyhound Industry Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958) and also by sub-section 3 of section 5 of the said Act, hereby makes the following regulation:

That is to say:

These regulations may be cited as the

- (1) Greyhound Race Track (Racing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

- (2) Greyhound Race Track (Racing) Regulations 1993, [S.I. No. 158 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0158.html) are hereby amended by the insertion of the following:

3 (8) The Control Committee established pursuant to these regulations shall have the power to consider any matter which arose prior to their establishment and to consider de novo any matter which was brought to the attention of any Control Committee established pursuant to the Greyhound Race Track (Racing) Regulations, 1967 and which had been considered by the said previous Control Committee or Committees in any way (other than by final order).

These regulations shall come into operation on 3rd day of December, 1993.

GIVEN under the Seal of Bord na gCon this 1st day of December, 1993.

**KEVIN G. FROST,**

Chairman of the Board.

**SEAN M. COLLINS,**

Chief Executive of the Board, an officer authorised by the

board to authenticate the seal of the board.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these regulations is to amend the Greyhound Race Track (Racing) Regulations, 1993 regarding the establishment and duties of the Control Committee.
