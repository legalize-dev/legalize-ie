# S.I. No. 148/1991 - Derelict Sites (Case Stated For High Court) (Fee) Regulations, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 148 of 1991.

DERELICT SITES (CASE STATED FOR HIGH COURT) (FEE) REGULATIONS, 1991.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (3) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), as applied by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0022.html#sec22) (10) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Derelict Sites (Case Stated for High Court) (Fee) Regulations, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The fee payable by a party requiring the Valuation Tribunal to state and sign a case, in relation to a determination of that tribunal under subsection (5) of [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1990), for the opinion of the High Court under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), as applied by subsection (10) of the said section 22, shall be £300.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of June, 1991.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , any party to an appeal to the Valuation Tribunal can, if dissatisfied in relation to points of law, request that a Case Stated be put before the High Court. [Section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/sec0022.html#sec22) (10) of the [Derelict Sites Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) , provides that the provisions of section 5 of the 1988 Act shall also apply in like manner to determinations of the Valuation Tribunal in Derelict Sites appeals cases. This Regulation provides that the fee payable in connection with a request for a Case Stated in Derelict Sites appeals cases is £300.
