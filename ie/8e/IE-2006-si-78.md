# S.I. No. 78/2006 - Electricity Regulation Act 1999 (Market Arrangements for Electricity) (Revocation) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 78 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0078.html)**

**ELECTRICITY REGULATION ACT 1999 (MARKET ARRANGEMENTS FOR ELECTRICITY) (REVOCATION) REGULATIONS 2006**

The Commission for Energy Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 9(1)(d) of the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 1999) (hereinafter referred to as “the 1999 Act”), having taken into account the matters raised in the public consultation process carried out by the Commission under Section 9(1)(b) of the 1999 Act, hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and commencement.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Market Arrangements for Electricity) (Revocation) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 18 February 2006.

Revocation.

###### 2.

2. The [Electricity Regulation Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (Market Arrangements for Electricity) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 304 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0304.html) ) are revoked.
