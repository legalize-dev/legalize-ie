# S.I. No. 389/2025 - Air Pollution Act 1987 (Solid Fuels, Moisture Content) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, DARRAGH O BRIEN , Minister for Climate, Energy and the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by 53 of the [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1987) and the Climate Action and Environment (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2016 ( [S. I. No. 393 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0393.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications, Climate Action and Environment) (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S. I. No. 373 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0373.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

Citation and commencement

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Pollution Act 1987 (Solid Fuels, Moisture Content) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1st August 2025.

###### 2.

2. The [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Solid Fuels) Regulations 2022, Regulation 5(5)(b) is amended -

- (1) By substituting for Regulation 5(5)(b) the following:

    - (b) with effect from 1 September 2026, less than 20%.

- (2) By Substituting in Schedule 1 the words ‘Form of words in respect of wood sold in volumes of more than two cubic metres from 1 September 2025.’ with ‘Form of words in respect of wood sold in volumes of more than two cubic metres from 1 September 2026’

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

1 August, 2025.

**DARRAGH O’BRIEN,**

Minister for Climate, Energy and the Environment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend the [Air Pollution Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Solid Fuels) Regulations 2022 to provide that the moisture content of 100% biomass fuels, including wood products and wood logs will be reduced from 25% to 20% from the 1st of September 2026.
