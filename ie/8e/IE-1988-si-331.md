# S.I. No. 331/1988 - Valuation (Case Stated For High Court) (Fee) Regulation, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 331 of 1988.

VALUATION (CASE STATED FOR HIGH COURT) (FEE) REGULATION, 1988.

I, ALBERT REYNOLDS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (3) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1988), hereby make the following Regulation:

###### 1.

1. This Regulation may be cited as the Valuation (Case Stated for High Court) (Fee) Regulation, 1988.

###### 2.

2. The fee payable by a party requiring the Valuation Tribunal to state and sign a case for the opinion of the High Court under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , shall be £300.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 8th day of December, 1988.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

Under the [Valuation Act, 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , any party to an appeal to the Valuation Tribunal can, if dissatisfied in relation to points of law, request that a Case Stated be put before the High Court. This Regulation provides that the fee payable in connection with such a request is £300.
