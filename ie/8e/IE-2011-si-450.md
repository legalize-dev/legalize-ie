# S.I. No. 450/2011 - Companies (Forms) (No. 2) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th September*, 2011.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 195](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0195.html#sec195) (6) and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/sec0003.html#sec3) (1) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982), [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/sec0005.html#sec5) (5) and [59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/sec0059.html#sec59) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983) and [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec3) , [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec42) (2) and [44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html#sec44) (2) of the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) (No. 2) Order 2011.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 1 September 2011.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Act of 1999” means the [Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 1999).

###### 3.

3. (1) The form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of subsection (6) of section 195 (inserted by [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1990)) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

- (2) The form set out in Part 2 of the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of—

    - (*a*) subsection (1) of [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1982),

    - (*b*) subsection (5) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983), and

    - (*c*) subsection (2) of section 42 of the Act of 1999.

- (3) The form set out in Part 3 of the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of subsection (2) of section 44 of the Act of 1999.

###### 4.

4. The following are revoked:

- (*a*) Articles 4 and 5 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, the Companies (Forms) (No. 2) Order 2004 ( [S.I. No. 829 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0829.html) );

- (*b*) Article 10 of, and Schedule 7 to, the Companies (Forms) Order 2008 ( [S.I. No. 350 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0350.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The purpose of this Order is to prescribe forms to be used for the purposes of certain provisions of the Companies Acts 1963 to 2009. The requirement that an Irish-registered company must have an Irish resident director was replaced by a requirement to have an European Economic Area resident director pursuant to the [Companies (Amendment) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) .
