# S.I. No. 309/2017 - Educational Research Centre (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th July*, 2017.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/sec0054.html#sec54) of the [Education Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1998) and having complied with subsection (7) of the said [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/sec0054.html#sec54) , hereby make the following order with respect to which, pursuant to subsection (8) of the said [section 54](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/sec0054.html#sec54) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Educational Research Centre (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Educational Research Centre (Establishment) Order 2015 ( [S.I. No. 392 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0392.html) ) is amended as follows:

- (*a*) in Article 46, by the substitution of—

(i)“the 1st day of October 2015” for “the establishment day specified in Article 4”, and

- (ii) “2016” for “2014”;

- (*b*) in Article 48, by the substitution of “Article 45” for “Article 43”;

- (*c*) in Article 49, by the substitution of “Article 45” for “Article 43”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 July 2017.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Education and Skills.
