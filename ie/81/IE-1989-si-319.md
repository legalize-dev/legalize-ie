# S.I. No. 319/1989 - Fire Services Act, 1981 (Prescribed Premises) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 319 of 1989.

FIRE SERVICES ACT, 1981 (PRESCRIBED PREMISES) REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/sec0003.html#sec3) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Fire Services Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1981), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the [Fire Services Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (Prescribed Premises) Regulations, 1989.

2 Application of Section 18, [Fire Services Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) .

###### 2.

**2.** The use of premises or any part thereof as a factory within the meaning of the Safety in Industry Acts, 1955 and 1980, is hereby prescribed as a use to which [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Fire Services Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1981), applies.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 5th day of December, 1989.

**PÁDRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Regulation provides that the provisions of the [Fire Services Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) shall apply to premises used as a factory within the meaning of the Safety in Industry Acts, 1955 and 1980.
