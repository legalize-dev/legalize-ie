# S.I. No. 351/1986 - Industrial and Provident Societies (Forms) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 351 of 1986.

INDUSTRIAL AND PROVIDENT SOCIETIES (FORMS) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Industry and Commerce in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 74 of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act, 1893, as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Registry of Friendly Societies Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1936), and the Industry, Trade, Commerce and Tourism (Alteration of name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 41 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0041.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Industrial and Provident Societies (Forms) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of November, 1986.

###### 3.

3. ( *a* ) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), shall apply to these Regulations.

( *b* ) In these Regulations "the Regulations of 1894" means the Regulations dated January 1, 1894, and made by the Treasury under the Industrial and Provident Societies Act,

###### 4.

4. The Regulations of 1894 are hereby amended by the insertion after Regulation (8) of the following Regulation:

> - "(8a) Notwithstanding Regulation (8), notice before cancelling of registry shall be in Form Ga where that form is appropriate.".

###### 5.

5. The forms annexed to the Regulations of 1894 are hereby amended:—

( *a* ) by the substitution in paragraph 5 of Form A (substituted by Regulation dated March 28, 1914, made by the Treasury under the Industrial and Provident Societies Act, 1893) and in paragraph 5 of Form D (as so substituted) for "not exceeding £200 sterling," of "not exceeding £ ," ,

( *b* ) by the insertion after Form G of the form set out in Part I of the Schedule to these Regulations, and

( *c* ) by the substitution for Form AC of the form set out in Part II of the Schedule to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend certain provisions in respect of registry and procedure under the Industrial and Provident Societies Act, 1893 and the forms to be used for such registry.
