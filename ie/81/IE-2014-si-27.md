# S.I. No. 27/2014 - Hotels Joint Labour Committee (for the areas known, until 1st January, 1994, as the County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) (Abolition) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st January*, 2014.

WHEREAS I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, being in receipt of a copy of a recommendation under subsection (5) of section 41A (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html#sec11) of the [Industrial Relations (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2012)) of the [Industrial Relations Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1946) and being satisfied that subsection (3) of section 41A has been complied with and considering it appropriate to do so:

NOW, I, RICHARD BRUTON, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (6) of section 41A (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html#sec11) of the [Industrial Relations (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2012)) of the [Industrial Relations Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1946) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) )) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Hotels Joint Labour Committee (for the areas known, until 1st January, 1994, as the County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) (Abolition) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. (1) The Hotels Joint Labour Committee (for the areas known, until 1st January, 1994, as the County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) is abolished.

- (2) The Hotels Joint Labour Committee (For the Areas Known, until 1st January, 1994, as the County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) Establishment Order 1997 (S.I. 174 of 1997) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 January 2014.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this instrument is to abolish the Hotels Joint Labour Committee (for the areas known, until 1st January, 1994, as the County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) and to revoke the Hotels Joint Labour Committee (For the Areas Known, until 1st January, 1994, as the County Borough of Dublin and the Borough of Dun Laoghaire) Establishment Order 1997 (S.I. 174 of 1997.)
