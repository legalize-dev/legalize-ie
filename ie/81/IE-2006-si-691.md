# S.I. No. 691/2006 - Local Government Act, 1991 (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 691 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0691.html)**

**Local Government Act, 1991 (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 2006**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 3 and 43 of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No.11 of 1991), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 2006.

###### 2.

2. This Order, the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 208 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0208.html) ), the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 369 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0369.html) ), the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No.1 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0001.html) ), and the principal order shall be construed together as one.

###### 3.

3. In this Order,

“the principal order” means the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment) Order, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 394 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0394.html) ), “the Order of 1995” means the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Regional Authorities) (Establishment Order) 1993, (Amendment) Order, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 208 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0208.html) ),

###### 4.

4. Article 2 of the principal order is hereby amended by the insertion of:

> ““the Act of 2001” means the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001)””.

###### 5.

5. This Order shall come into operation on the 1^st January 2007.

###### 6.

6. Article 32 of the principal order is hereby amended by:

- (i) the substitution of the following for sub-article (1)

  > - “(1) A regional authority shall keep proper accounts and records of its receipts and expenditure and shall prepare, as soon as may be after the end of the financial year, a statement of the accounts of the authority for that year based on accounting policies and in such form as the Minister shall direct.”

- (ii) the substitution of the following for paragraph (a) of sub-article (3)

  > - “(a) The statement of accounts of a regional authority shall be audited by a local government auditor appointed in accordance with section 116 (4) (b) of the 2001 Act. The auditor shall give an opinion on such statement of accounts and may also issue a separate report thereon in accordance with section 120 of the 2001 Act.”

###### 7.

7. Article 34A of the principal order as inserted by the Order of 1995 is hereby amended by the substitution of “£7,000” by “€12,000” in sub-article (2).

###### 8.

8. Article 37 of the principal order is hereby amended by the insertion of the following after sub-article (10)

> - “(11) The Minister may give directions in accordance with article 35 amending the list of specified public authorities included in the Third Schedule to this order.”

**Explanatory Memorandum**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).
