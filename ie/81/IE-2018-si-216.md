# S.I. No. 216/2018 - Oireachtas (Allowances) (Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees and Holders of Certain Parliamentary Offices) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th June*, 2018.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 3 (as amended by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec39) of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2001) (“Act of 2001”) and [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec8) (5) of the [Houses of the Oireachtas Commission Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2003)), 4 and 8 (as amended by section 43 of the Act of 2001) of the [Oireachtas (Allowances to Members) and Ministerial, Parliamentary, Judicial and Court Offices (Amendment) Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Oireachtas (Allowances) (Chairpersons of Oireachtas Committees and Holders of Certain Parliamentary Offices) Order 2018.

- (2) Article 5 is deemed to have come into operation on 10 March 2016.

###### 2.

2. There is payable to a member of Seanad Éireann who holds or held a position in Seanad Éireann, specified in column (2) of Schedule 1 at any reference number, an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually at the rate specified in column (3) A of that Schedule opposite that reference number in respect of any period beginning on or after 12 December 2017, and at the rate specified in column (3) B of that Schedule opposite that reference number in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 January 2018, during which the member held that position.

###### 3.

3. There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of the Oireachtas Committee specified in Part 1 of Schedule 2, an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually at the rate specified in column (2) A of that Part in respect of any period beginning on or after 13 December 2017, and at the rate specified in column (2) B of that Part in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 January 2018, during which the member held that position.

###### 4.

4. There is payable to a member of the Oireachtas who holds or held the position of chairperson of the Oireachtas Committee specified in Part 2 of Schedule 2, an allowance, in respect of the member’s duties as holder of that position, annually at the rate specified in column (2) A of that Part in respect of any period beginning on or after 23 February 2017, at the rate specified in column (2) B of that Part in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 April 2017, and at the rate specified in column (2) C of that Part in respect of any period beginning on or after 1 January 2018, during which the member held that position.

###### 5.

5. The Oireachtas (Allowances) (Members and Holders of Parliamentary and Certain Ministerial Offices) Order 2017 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0014.html) ) is amended in Schedule 1 by deleting the matter at reference number 1.
