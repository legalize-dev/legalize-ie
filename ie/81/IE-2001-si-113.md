# S.I. No. 113/2001 - National Pensions Reserve Fund Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, T.D., Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0033/print.html#sec3) of the [National Pensions Reserve Fund Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Pensions Reserve Fund Act, 2000 (Establishment Day) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. The 2nd day of April, 2001, is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Pensions Reserve Fund Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2000).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | Given under my Official Seal 29 March 2001. |
|  | Charlie McCreevy |
|  | Minister for Finance. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not a part of the Instrument, and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

This Order appoints 2 April 2001 as the establishment day for the [National Pensions Reserve Fund Act, 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) . On that day shall stand established the National Pensions Reserve Fund and the National Pensions Reserve Fund Commission.
