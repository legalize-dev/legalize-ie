# S.I. No. 576/2006 - Railway Safety Act 2005 (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 576 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0576.html)**

**Railway Safety Act 2005 (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2006**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 133](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec133) of the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2005), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed as the form of the notice to be served on a person in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under section —

a) 22A(2) or 59(1) of the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1950),

b) 25(1) of the [Transport (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1971), or

c) 118 or 132 of the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2005).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

The purpose of these regulations is to prescribe the form of notices and documents to be used where an Authorised Officer or a member of the Garda Síochána alleges that one of several specific offences under the [Transport Act 1950](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1950/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) , the [Transport (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) and the [Railway Safety Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) has been committed and the amounts which a person who is liable to be prosecuted may pay as an alternative to the institution of a prosecution for the offence(s).
