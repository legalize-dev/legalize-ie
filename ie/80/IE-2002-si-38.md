# S.I. No. 38/2002 - Companies (Forms) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Noel Treacy, Minister for State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 127](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0127.html#sec127) (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)), [395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 1998 (S.I. 265 of 1998), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Order 2002.

- (2) The Companies (Forms) Orders 1964 to 2001 and this Order may be cited together as the Companies (Forms) Orders 1964 to 2002.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 1^st March 2002

###### 3.

3. (1) In this Order-

“Act of 1963” means the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963)

“Act of 2001” means the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001).

- (2) In this Order a reference to a Schedule is a reference to the Schedule to this Order unless it is indicated that reference to some other Order is intended.

###### 4.

4. The Fifth Schedule to the Act of 1963 is altered by substituting Form B1 set out in Part 1 of the Schedule for the form set out in Part II (as amended by the Companies (Forms) Order 1991 ( [S.I. No 161 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0161.html) )) of the said Fifth Schedule.

###### 5.

5. The Form B73 set out in Part 2 of the Schedule is hereby prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 127(9) (as substituted by section 60 of the Act of 2001) of the Act of 1963.

###### 6.

6. The Form B73(a) set out in Part 3 of the Schedule is hereby prescribed as the form to be used for the purposes of section 127(11) (as substituted by section 60 of the Act of 2001) of the Act of 1963.

**Explanatory Note**

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

The purpose of this Order is to amend the form outlined in Part II of the Fifth Schedule to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , as amended by the Companies (Forms) Order 1991 ( [No. 161 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0161.html) ) by substituting it with the new Form B1. This form updates the form to be completed when furnishing an annual return with the Registrar of Companies. The annual return form requires updating in preparation for the introduction of the new annual return date on 1 March 2002.

The new Form B73 and Form B73(a) have been prescribed for the purpose of [section 127](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0127.html#sec127) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) as amended by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec60) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) . Form B73 relates to subsection 9 of section 127 and can be used by all companies only once in every five years, to extend the annual return date. Form B73a relates to subsection 11 of section 127 and may be used, only once, by companies incorporated prior to the 1 March 2002 in order to extend the first annual return date post 1 March 2002. Form B73 cannot be used immediately following the use of Form B73a.
