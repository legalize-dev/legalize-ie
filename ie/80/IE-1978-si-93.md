# S.I. No. 93/1978 - Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1978

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 93 of 1978.

SOCIAL WELFARE (PRISONER'S WIFE'S ALLOWANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1978.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1952) and [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0014/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Social Welfare (No. 2) Act, 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1974), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 220 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0220.html) ) may be cited collectively as the Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 to 1978.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1978.

###### 4.

4. Article 6 of the Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 is hereby revoked.

###### 5.

5. Article 8 of the Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-article (1) for the words "and 63." of the words ", 63 and 80 (2)." and by the insertion after sub-article (2) of the following sub-article—

> - "(3) In the application of section 80 (2) of the said Act any reference to 'widow's (non-contributory) pension under the Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Acts, 1935 to 1952' shall be construed as including an allowance."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 29th day of March, 1978.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend the Social Welfare (Prisoner's Wife's Allowance) Regulations, 1974 by providing that a person applying for a prisoner's wife's allowance does not have to satisfy a residence test and that a person who, immediately before attaining pension age, is in receipt of a prisoner's wife's allowance shall not be required to satisfy the residence conditions for old age (non-contributory) pensions purposes imposed by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0018/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Old Age Pensions Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) .
