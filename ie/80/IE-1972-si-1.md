# S.I. No. 1/1972 - Dublin Parking (Pedestrianisation) Temporary Rules, 1971

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 1 of 1972.

DUBLIN PARKING (PEDESTRIANISATION) TEMPORARY RULES, 1971.

I, MICHAEL J. WYMES, Commissioner of the Garda Síochána in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 90](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0090.html#sec90) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1968)), hereby make the following temporary rules in respect of the area comprising the county borough of Dublin:

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Dublin Parking (Pedestrianisation) Temporary Rules, 1971.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 10th day of January, 1972.

###### 3.

3. The Dublin Parking (No. 2.) Temporary Rules, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0251.html) ), are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. (1) No person shall park a vehicle in a street or portion of a street to which this rule applies for any period during the period from 11 a.m. to 6.30 p.m. on any weekday.

- (2) This rule shall not apply to—

    - (*a*) any vehicle which is being used in connection with—

        - (i) the removal of any obstruction to traffic,

        - (ii) the maintenance, improvement or reconstruction of any street or part of a street,

        - (iii) the provision, alteration or repair of any sewer or of any main, pipe or apparatus for the supply of gas, water or electricity or any telegraphic or telephonic line, or

    - (*b*) a fire engine or ambulance or any vehicle in the service of the Garda Síochána while being used in pursuance of statutory powers or duties.

- (3) This rule applies to each street named or portion of a street described at a reference number in the Schedule to these rules.

SCHEDULE.

*REF. NO.*

- (1) Henry Street.

- (2) The portion of Henry Place from Henry Street to a point 15 yards north of the junction of Henry Place and Henry Street.

- (3) The portion of Moore Street from Henry Street to a point 20 yards south of Henry Place.

- (4) The portion of Cole's Lane from Henry Street to Sampson's Lane.

GIVEN under my hand, this 22nd day of December, 1971.

MICHAEL J. WYMES,

Commissioner of the Garda Síochána.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

1. These rules revoke the Dublin Parking (No. 2) Temporary Rules, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 251 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0251.html) ).

2. Rule 4 (1) prohibits a person from parking a vehicle within the pedestrianised streets or portion of streets set out in the schedule to the rules between 11 a.m. and 6.30 p.m. on weekdays.

3. Rule 4 (2) outlines exceptions to the prohibition on parking.

4. These rules apply in the area comprising the County Borough of Dublin.
