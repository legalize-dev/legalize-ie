# S.I. No. 40/1970 - Land Act, 1965 (Additional Category of Qualified Person) Regulations, 1970

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 40 of 1970.

LAND ACT, 1965 (ADDITIONAL CATEGORY OF QUALIFIED PERSON) REGULATIONS, 1970.

I, SEÁN FLANAGAN, Minister for Lands, in exercise of the power conferred on me by section 45 (1) (*x*) of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965) hereby make the following Regulations:—

1 Title.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (Additional Category of Qualified Person) Regulations, 1970.

2 Additional category of qualified person.

###### 2.

**2.** For the purposes of the definition of a qualified person in [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965), the following category is hereby declared to be an additional category, namely, a person acquiring an interest in land which is situate in a town specified in Part II of the First Schedule to the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1963).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of March, 1970.

**SEÁN FLANAGAN,**

Minister for Lands.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations exclude from the provisions of Section 45, [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , the "non-municipal" towns specified in Part II of the First Schedule to the [Local Government (Planning and Development) Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , the boundaries of these towns having been fixed by the respective planning authorities.
