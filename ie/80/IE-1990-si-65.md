# S.I. No. 65/1990 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in County Health District of Roscommon) Order, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 65 of 1990.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN COUNTY HEALTH DISTRICT OF ROSCOMMON) ORDER, 1990.

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for Health by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), which said powers are delegated to me by the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1989, I, NOEL TREACY, Minister of State at the Department of Health, hereby order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in County Health District of Roscommon) Order, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of June, 1990 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the County Health District of Roscommon unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

Dated this 16th day of March, 1990.

NOEL TREACY,

Minister of State at the

Department of Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of June, 1990 the sale of milk in the County Health District of Roscommon unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
