# S.I. No. 522/1998 - Freedom of Information Act, 1997 (Section 47(3)) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0003.html#sec3) and [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0047.html#sec47) (3) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Freedom of Information Act, 1997 (Section 47(3))(Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 3 of the Freedom of Information Act, 1997 (Section 47(3)) Regulations, 1998, ( [S.I. No. 139 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0139.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for paragraphs (a), (b), and (c) of the following paragraphs:

- "(a) the amount of 3 pence per sheet in relation to a photocopy,

- (b) the amount of 40 pence in relation to a 3½" computer diskette,

- (c) the amount of £8 in relation to a CD-ROM (that is to say, a compact disk read-only memory), and

- (d) the amount of £5 in relation to a Radiograph."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of December, 1998.

CHARLIE McCREEVY, T.D.,

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend existing regulations ( [S.I. No. 139 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0139.html) ) on application of charges under the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997) by providing an additional category of record for which a charge can be imposed. The Regulations provide for a charge of up to £5 for a Radiograph (X-ray) in addition to the previous maximum charges of £16.50 per hour for search and retrieval of records to which access is granted, 3 pence per sheet for a photocopy, 40 pence for a 3½" computer diskette, and £8 for a CD-ROM.
