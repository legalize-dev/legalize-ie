# S.I. No. 684/2020 - Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 11) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th January,* 2021.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) and [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0042.html#sec42) (2)(b) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec2) of the [Road Traffic Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2006) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), and for the purposes of giving further effect to Annex I of Directive No. 2006/126/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006,[^1] as amended by Commission Directive No. 2011/94/EU of 28 November 2011,[^2] Council Directive No. 2013/22/EU of 13 May 2013,[^3] and Commission Directive (EU) 2015/653 of 24 April 2015,[^4] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) (Amendment) (No. 11) Regulations 2020.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 23 December 2020.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 1 to the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 537 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0537.html) ) is amended -

- (*a*) by the substitution of form D.202 set out in Part 1 of the Schedule for form D.202 in Schedule 1, and

- (*b*) by the substitution of form D.402 set out in Part 2 of the Schedule for form D.402 in Schedule 1.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Licensing of Drivers) Regulations 2006 (S.I. 537 of 2006) by replacing Form D.202 (learner permit) and Form D.402 (driving licence) in Schedule 1 with an updated version of the forms in order to identify Item 4d as the driver number.
