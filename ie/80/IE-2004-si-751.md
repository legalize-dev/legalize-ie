# S.I. No. 751/2004 - Housing (Rent Books) Regulations 1993 (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS.

**[S.I. No. 751 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0751.html) .**

HOUSING (RENT BOOKS) REGULATIONS 1993 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2004.

[S.I. No. 751 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0751.html) .

HOUSING (RENT BOOKS) REGULATIONS 1993 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2004.

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966), as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1992), and by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) , hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Rent Books) Regulations 1993 (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

###### 2.

2. The Housing (Rent Books) Regulations 1993 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0146.html) ) are hereby amended by the deletion of paragraphs 2 and 3 from the Schedule thereto and the substitution therefor of the following paragraphs:

> “2. The tenant of a house is entitled to enjoy peaceful and exclusive occupation of the house.

###### 3.

3. Notice of termination of a tenancy must be in writing and must be made in accordance with the provisions of [the Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2004).”

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the Schedule to the Housing (Rent Books) Regulations 1993 for compatibility with [the Residential Tenancies Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) .
