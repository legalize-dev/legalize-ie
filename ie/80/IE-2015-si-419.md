# S.I. No. 419/2015 - Workplace Relations Act 2015 (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd October*, 2015.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) and [36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec36) of the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2015), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Workplace Relations Act 2015 (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2015.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2015” means the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2015).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015.

###### 4.

4. (1) The amount of €2,000 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No.7 of 1977).

- (2) The amount of €1,500 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under subsection (4) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No.25 of 1991).

- (3) The amount of €1,500 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec23) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the form of Fixed Payment Notices in lieu of prosecution, if appropriate, and the amount of the fixed payment in each case in relation to alleged offences under [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , subsection (4) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) or [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec23) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) .
