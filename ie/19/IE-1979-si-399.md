DEFENCE FORCES (PENSIONS) (AMENDMENT), (No. 5) SCHEME, 1979.

I, ROBERT MOLLOY, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on my by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) , [3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0003.html#sec3) and [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1932), and by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1938), as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), and the Public Service (Delegation of Ministerial Functions Order, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0117.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister of State at the Department of the Public Service, make the following Scheme:

1 Short title, collective citation and construction.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) This Scheme may be cited as the Defence Forces (Pensions) (Amendment) (No. 5) Scheme, 1979.

- (2) The collective citation, the Defence Forces (Pensions) Schemes, 1937 to 1979, shall include this Scheme.

- (3) The Defence Forces (Pensons) Schemes, 1937 to 1979, shall be construed together as one Scheme.

2 Commencement.

###### 2.

**2.** This Scheme shall come into force on the day after the date on which it is made.

3 Application of [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) .

###### 3.

**3.** The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this scheme.

4 ..

###### 4.

**4.** (1) Where an officer—

( *a* ) retires from the Permanent Defence Force, without a grant of retired pay, and is re-appointed to the Permanent Defence Force, or

( *b* ) retires from the Permanent Defence Force, without a grant of retired pay, is appointed to the Reserve of Officers (First Line) and is called out—

- (i) on permanent service, or

- (ii) in aid of the civil power

different periods of full-time service may be aggregated for purposes of pensionable service subject to such recovery of any gratuities paid in respect of the previous full-time service together with compound interest at such rates as may be determined by the Minister from time to time with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service.

- (2) Different periods of pensionable service may be aggregated under sub-article (1) of this Article for the purposes of a gratuity only in respect of an officer retiring or dying in service on or after the coming into operation of this Article.

- (3) This Article shall not apply as regards any period of service in the Defence Forces which is reckonable for superannuation purposes in any other organisation or service.

- (4) This Article shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1979.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 10th day of December, 1979.

**ROBERT MOLLOY,**

The Minister of State at the Department of the Public Service hereby consents to the foregoing Scheme.

GIVEN under my hand, this 10th day of December, 1979.

**RAY MACSHARRY.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Statutory Instrument provides for aggregation of different periods of full-time service in the Defence Forces for certain officers.
