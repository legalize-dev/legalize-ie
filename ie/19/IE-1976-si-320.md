S.I. No. 320 of 1976.

MARITIME JURISDICTION (EXCLUSIVE FISHERY LIMITS) ORDER, 1976.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1959), as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction (Amendment) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1964), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Maritime Jurisdiction (Exclusive Fishery Limits) Order, 1976, and shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1977.

###### 2.

2. In this Order a reference to [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction Act 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1959), is a reference to that section as amended by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction (Amendment) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1964).

###### 3.

3. Subject to Article 4 of this Order, the exclusive fishery limits of the State shall include, in addition to the sea areas specified in [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , all sea areas between those areas and the line every point of which is at a distance of 200 nautical miles from the nearest point of the baseline.

###### 4.

4. Where because of the proximity of the fishery limits of another state the exclusive fishery limits specified in Article 3 of this Order cannot be applied, the exclusive fishery limits of the State shall include, in addition to the sea areas specified in [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) , all sea areas between those areas and the equitable equidistant line between the State and the other state, being the appropriate line set out in the Schedule to this Order.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is, from 1 January 1977, to extend the exclusive fishery limits of the State to 200 nautical miles from the nearest point of the baseline or, where because of the proximity of another State the Order cannot be applied, to the equitable equidistant line between the State and the other State. This Order is made under [Section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) as amended by [Section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Maritime Jurisdiction (Amendment) Act, 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) .
