# S.I. No. 453/2006 - Credit Union Act 1997 (Alteration of Financial Limits) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 453 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0453.html)**

**ORDER**

entitled

**Credit Union Act 1997 (Alteration of Financial Limits) Order 2006**

Made by the Minister for Finance

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 27(5) of the [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1997), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (Alteration of Financial Limits Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. [Section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Credit Union Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1997) is amended -

- (a) in subsection (2) by substituting “100 per cent.” for “75 per cent.”, and

- (b) in subsection (4) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec6) of the [Euro Changeover (Amounts) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2001)) by substituting -

    - (i) “€100,000” for “€26,000” in paragraph (a), and

    - (ii) “€200,000” for “€64,000” in paragraph (b).
