# S.I. No. 403/2004 - Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, SÉAMUS BRENNAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 95 (as amended by [section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0037.html#sec37) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2004.

- (2) The Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2003 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2004.

###### 2.

2. Article 6 of the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 181 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0181.html) ) is amended

- (*a*) in sub-article (4)—

    - (i) in paragraph (*c*) (inserted by the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 273 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0273.html) )) by substituting “cyclists; or” for “cyclists.”, and

    - (ii) by substituting for paragraph (*d*) (inserted by Article 8 of the Road Traffic (Signs) (Amendment) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 97 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0097.html) )) the following:

      > - “(*d*) the message “Except Trams — Ach amháin Tramanna”, to indicate that the prohibitions by those signs do not apply in the case of light rail vehicles; or”, and

- (*b*) by inserting after sub-article (4) the following:

  > - “(5) In sub-article (4)(*e*), “access” means entry or exit by a vehicle (other than a light rail vehicle) to or from a road or premises adjacent to or accessible only from a tram-only street without stopping or parking the vehicle on the street.”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Signs) Regulations 1997 to 2003 in relation to the application of certain regulatory traffic signs in connection with the on-street running of light rail vehicles.
