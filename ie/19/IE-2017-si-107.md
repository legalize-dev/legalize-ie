# S.I. No. 107/2017 - Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st March*, 2017.

I, MARCELLA CORCORAN KENNEDY, Minister of State at the Department of Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )) and the Health (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 4) Order 2016 ( [S.I. No. 512 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0512.html) ), after consultation with the Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine and the Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2017.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“Act” means the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998);

“Order of 2014” means the Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998 (Amendment of First and Second Schedules) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 390 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0390.html) ).
