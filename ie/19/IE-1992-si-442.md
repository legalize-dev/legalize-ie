# S.I. No. 442/1992 - Aliens (Amendment) (No. 3) Order, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 442 of 1992.

ALIENS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 3) ORDER, 1992.

I, PADRAIG FLYNN, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Aliens Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1935), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Aliens (Amendment) (No. 3) Order, 1992.

- (2) The Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1992, and this Order may be cited together as the Aliens Orders, 1946 to 1992, and shall be construed together as one Order.

- (3) The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

- (4) This Order shall come into operation on the 31st day of December, 1992.

###### 2.

2. The Principal Order is hereby amended by:

- (1) the deletion of "Czechoslovakia" in the Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 [ [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) ]); and

- (2) the insertion of the "Czech Republic" after "Cyprus" and the insertion of the "Slovak Republic" after "Singapore" in the Sixth Schedule (inserted by the Aliens (Amendment) Order, 1975 [ [S.I. No. 128 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0128.html) ].

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 30th day of December, 1992.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for Justice.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides that nationals of the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic will not require visas to enter or transit the State.
