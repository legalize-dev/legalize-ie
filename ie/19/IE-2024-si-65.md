# S.I. No. 65/2024 - European Union (Motor Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st March,* 2024.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purposes of giving further effect to Directive (EU) 2021/2118 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2021[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Motor Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 5 of the European Union (Motor Insurance) Regulations of 2023 ( [S.I. No. 643 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0643.html) ) is amended by deleting paragraph (3).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

27 February, 2024.

**EAMON RYAN,**

Minister for Transport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend Regulation 5 of the European Union (Motor Insurance) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 643 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0643.html) ) by deleting paragraph (3), which amended the European Communities (Non-Life Insurance) Regulations 1976 ( [S.I. No. 115 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0115.html) ). These Regulations were previously revoked by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec20) (1) of the [Finance (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2015).
