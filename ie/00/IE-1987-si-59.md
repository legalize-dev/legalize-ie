S.I. No. 59 of 1987.

CONTROL OF DOGS (No. 2) REGULATIONS, 1987

The Minister for the Environment in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0025.html#sec25) and [31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Control of Dogs Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1986), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Control of Dogs (No. 2) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule hereto shall be the prescribed form of notice for the purpose of [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/sec0025.html#sec25) (2) of the [Control of Dogs Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) .

Where it appears to the District Court that a nuisance has been created as a result of excessive barking, the Court may

- (*a*) order you to abate the nuisance by exercising due control over the dog;

- (*b*) make an order limiting for such period as may be specified in the order the number of dogs to be kept by you on your premises;

- (*c*) direct that the dog be delivered to a dog warden to be dealt with by him in accordance with the provisions of the [Control of Dogs Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) , as if the dog were an unwanted dog.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the form of notice to be served on the occupier of the premises in which a dog is kept where a person intends to make a complaint to the District Court in relation to a nuisance alleged to have been caused by the excessive barking of that dog.
