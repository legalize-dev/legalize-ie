# S.I. No. 248/2000 - Health (Dental Services For Children) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0011/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Health (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1994), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ), make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Dental Services for Children) Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on the first day of August, 2000.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations the “Act of 1994” means the [Health (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1994).

###### 3.

3. Fifteen years of age is specified for the purposes of section 1 (1) of the Act of 1994.

###### 4.

4. The following dental health services are specified for the purposes of section 1 (1) of the Act of 1994:

- (*a*) a dental health screening service,

- (*b*) a preventive dental treatment service, and

- (*c*) a primary care dental treatment service in respect of defects noted during a screening examination carried out under paragraph (*a*) of this Regulation.

###### 5.

5. The Health (Dental Services for Children) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 21 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0021.html) ), are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

(*This note is not part of the Regulations and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of these Regulations is to extend eligibility for the health boards' dental services to children up to their sixteenth birthday.
