# S.I. No. 290/1984 - European Assembly Elections Free Postage (Amendment) Scheme, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 290 of 1984.

EUROPEAN ASSEMBLY ELECTIONS FREE POSTAGE (AMENDMENT) SCHEME, 1984.

An Post, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by Rule 18 (1) of the First Schedule to the [European Assembly Elections Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0030/index.html) (No. 30 of 1977), and by [Section 74](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0074.html#sec74) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983), having consulted the Minister for the Environment and with the consent of the Minister for Communications, hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Scheme may be cited as the European Assembly Elections Free Postage (Amendment) Scheme, 1984.

- (2) The European Assembly Elections Free Postage Regulations 1979 and this Scheme may be cited as the European Assembly Elections Free Postage Schemes 1979 to 1984.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall be construed as one with the European Assembly Elections Free postage Regulations, 1979.

###### 3.

3. The European Assembly Elections Free Postage Regulations 1979 are hereby amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (a) of Regulation 8:

> - "(*a*) communications shall either be enclosed in closed or unclosed covers or be folded or be in such other form (whether or not in covers) consistent with these Regulations as shall, in the opinion of An Post, not be likely to impede sorting or to entrap other postal packets."

GIVEN under the Common Seal of An Post this 5th day of October, 1984.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Scheme is to broaden the criteria of acceptability of the form of communication which may be sent, free of postage, by candidates at a European Assembly Election to every person registered as an elector.
