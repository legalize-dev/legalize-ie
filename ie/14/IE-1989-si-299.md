# S.I. No. 299/1989 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 299 of 1989.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1989.

I, MICHAEL O `KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercises of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1989.

- (2) The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall

( *a* ) as regards milk be 1.2 penny per gallon,

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live be £6.90 per animal.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 109 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0109.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 15th day of November, 1989.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which come into effect on 1st December 1989 prescribe new rates of disease levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
