ROAD TRAFFIC (IMMOBILISATION OF VEHICLES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th October*, 2009.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 101B (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0034/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Dublin Transport Authority (Dissolution) Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1987) (as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec12) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2007)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961), the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )) after consultation with the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1st day of January 2010.

###### 3.

3. The Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 247 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0247.html) ) are amended by substituting for the Schedule to those Regulations the following:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations prescribe the notice to be used for the purposes of section 101B of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , as amended, in bi-lingual format and extend the application of the enforcement notice issued under section 101B to illegal parking offences under section 36A of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , inserted by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html#sec12) of the [Roads Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) .
