# S.I. No. 551/2001 - Local Government Act, 2001 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec7) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act, 2001 (Commencement) (No. 2) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. On the 1^st day of January, 2002, [section 142](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec142) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) , shall come into operation.

###### 3.

3. On the 1^st day of January, 2002, [section 51](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/sec0051.html#sec51) of the [Local Government Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (No. 11 of 1991) shall be repealed.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order commences section 142 which enables the Minister to make regulations relating to the payment of remuneration and allowances to local authority members.
