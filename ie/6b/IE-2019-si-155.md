# S.I. No. 155/2019 - Maritime Jurisdiction (Bay Closing Lines) Order 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th April,* 2019.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec85) (2) of the [Sea Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Maritime Jurisdiction (Bay Closing Lines) Order 2019.

###### 2.

2. Straight lines connecting the following points, defined by coordinates of latitude and longitude on the World Geodetic System 1984 datum (WGS 1984), are prescribed as bay closing lines:

- (*a*) in relation to Dundalk Bay, the points specified at reference numbers 1 and 2 of the Schedule;

- (*b*) in relation to Dublin Bay, the points specified at reference numbers 3 and 4 of the Schedule;

- (*c*) in relation to Wexford Harbour, the points specified at reference numbers 5 and 6 of the Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this Order is to prescribe bay closing lines in Dundalk Bay, Dublin Bay and Wexford Harbour.
