# S.I. No. 109/2022 - Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th March,* 2022.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for the Environment, Climate and Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1962) and the Fisheries (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 1977 ( [S.I. No. 30 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0030.html) ) (as adapted by the Communications, Climate Action and Environment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 373 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0373.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Control of Fishing for Salmon (Amendment) Order 2022.

###### 2.

2. In this Order “Order of 2021” means the Control of Fishing for Salmon Order 2021 (S.I. No 102 2021).

###### 3.

3. The Order of 2021 is amended –

- (*a*) by the substitution of Schedule 1 to this Order for Schedule 1 to the Order of 2021, and

- (*b*) by the substitution of Schedule 2 to this Order for Schedule 2 to the Order of 2021.
