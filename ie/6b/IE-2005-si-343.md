# S.I. No. 343/2005 - National Archives Act 1986 (Prescription of Classes of Records) (Amendment) Order 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 343 OF 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0343.html)**

**National Archives Act 1986 (Prescription of Classes of Records)(Amendment) Order 2005**

I, Bertie Ahern, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8(11) of the [National Archives Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) [(No.](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) 11 of 1986), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Archives Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Prescription of Classes of Records) (Amendment) Order 2005.

###### 2.

2. The Second Schedule to the [National Archives Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0011/index.html) (Prescription of Classes of Records) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 281 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0281.html) ) is amended by adding after mention of the matter contained in columns (1) and (2) the following:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “ |  |
| The Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions | All records concerning criminal investigations or the prosecution of criminal offences. ”. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 30 June 2005. |
