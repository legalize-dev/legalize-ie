# S.I. No. 303/1977 - Protection of Young Persons (Employment) (Exclusion of Close Relatives) Regulations, 1977

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 303 of 1977.

PROTECTION OF YOUNG PERSONS (EMPLOYMENT) (EXCLUSION OF CLOSE RELATIVES) REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, GENE FITZGERALD, Minister for Labour, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0017.html#sec17) (1) of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1977), and having complied with the provisions of subsections (2) and (3) of that Section, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Protection of Young Persons (Employment) (Exclusion of Close Relatives) Regulations, 1977.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 30th day of September, 1977.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations "close relative" means a person who is—

( *a* ) employed by his spouse, father, mother, grandfather, grandmother, step-father, step-mother, brother, sister, half-brother or half-sister, and

( *b* ) employed in a private dwelling house or on a farm, in or on which both the employee and the employer reside.

###### 3.

3. Subject to the modification contained in Regulation 4 of these Regulations, the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1977), shall apply to close relatives.

###### 4.

4. None of the provisions contained in [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0004.html#sec4) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0005.html#sec5) , [6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0006.html#sec6) , [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0007.html#sec7) , [8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0008.html#sec8) , [9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0009.html#sec9) , [10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0010.html#sec10) or [11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) , shall apply to the employment of close relatives.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 26th day of September, 1977.

**GENE FITZGERALD,**

Minister for Labour.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations modify certain provisions of the [Protection of Young Persons (Employment) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) in regard to their application to close relatives.
