# S.I. No. 554/2018 - Health Act 1970 (Fifth Schedule) Regulations 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 28*th December*, 2018.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) of section 55 (amended by [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec13) of the [Health (Amendment) Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2013)) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970), having regard to the matters referred to in paragraph (*c*) of that subsection and having consulted with the Health Service Executive, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1970 (Fifth Schedule) Regulations 2018.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2019.

###### 3.

3. The following hospital is prescribed for the purposes of the Fifth Schedule (inserted by [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec16) of the [Health (Amendment) Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2013)) to the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970):

Children’s Health Ireland, Dublin

Given under my Official Seal,

17 December 2018.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations prescribe Children’s Health Ireland, Dublin, as a hospital for the purposes of the Fifth Schedule to the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .

These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1970 (Fifth Schedule) Regulations 2018 and come into operation on 1 January 2019.
