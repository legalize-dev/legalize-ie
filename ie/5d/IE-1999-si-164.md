# S.I. No. 164/1999 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Increase in Rates) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 2 (as amended by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec19) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 1999)), 3 (as amended by [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec17) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 4, 32 (as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec24) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 41B (inserted by the European Communities (Social Welfare) Regulations, 1995 ( [S.I. No. 25 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0025.html) ) and amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec24) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 43 (as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec24) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 84 (as amended by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec21) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) ), 89 (as amended by [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1997)), 102 (as amended by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/sec0027.html#sec27) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1996)), 111 and 245A (as amended by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) ) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

Citation and construction.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Increase in Rates) Regulations, 1999.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations, 1994 to 1999 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations, 1994 to 1999.

Commencement.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation—

- (*a*) in so far as it relates to unemployment assistance on the 26th day of May, 1999,

- (*b*) in so far as it relates to unemployment benefit, on the 27th day of May, 1999,

- (*c*) in so far as it relates to disability benefit, maternity benefit, adoptive benefit, health and safety benefit and injury benefit on the 31st day of May, 1999.

- (*d*) in so far as it relates to disability allowance, on the 2nd day of June, 1999,

- (*e*) in so far as it relates to retirement pension, pre-retirement allowance and a relevant payment by virtue of [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) (1) (*a*) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , on the 3rd day of June, 1999, and

- (*f*) in so far as it relates to old age (contributory) pension, widow's and widower's (contributory) pension and unemployability supplement on the 4th day of June, 1999.

Definitions.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

“the Principal Regulations” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) Regulations, 1994 ( [S.I. No. 417 of 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/si/0417.html) );

“the Regulations of 1997” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Payments Provisions) (Amendment) (No. 11) (Increase for Qualified Adult) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 492 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0492.html) );

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Social Welfare Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) provided for increases in the maximum rates of social insurance payments arising from the 1999 Budget, with effect from June 1999.

These Regulations provide for increases in the reduced rates of Disability Benefit, Unemployment Benefit, Health and Safety Benefit, Old Age (Contributory) Pension, Retirement Pension, Widow's and Widower's (Contributory) Pension, Deserted Wife's Benefit, and also provides for increases in the rates of tapered increases in respect of Qualified Adults. All of these increases are effective from June 1999.

The Regulations also provide for increases in the minimum weekly rate of Maternity Benefit and Adoptive Benefit with effect from June 1999.
