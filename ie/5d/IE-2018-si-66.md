# S.I. No. 66/2018 - Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2018

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th March*, 2018.

The Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsections (1) and (8) of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1998), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This order may be cited as the Referendum Commission (Establishment) Order 2018.

###### 2.

2. A Referendum Commission is established to perform the functions conferred on it by the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , as amended by the [Referendum Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0053/print.html) , in relation to the proposal for an amendment of the Constitution contained in the Thirty-sixth Amendment of the Constitution Bill 2018.

###### 3.

3. The 9th day of March 2018 is appointed to be the day for the purposes of [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/sec0002.html#sec2) (5) of the [Referendum Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) in respect of the chairperson of the Referendum Commission.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government,

9 March 2018.

**JOHN MCCARTHY,**

A Person Authorised Under [Section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/sec0015.html#sec15) of the [Ministers and Secretaries Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) to Authenticate the Seal of the Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This order establishes a Referendum Commission for the purposes of the Referendum Acts 1998 and 2001 in connection with the Thirty-sixth Amendment of the Constitution Bill 2018 and appoints 9 March, 2018 as the day on which the position of chairperson of the Commission takes effect.
