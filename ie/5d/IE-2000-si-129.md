# S.I. No. 129/2000 - Regional Technical Colleges (Amendment) Act, 1999 (Section 5(5)) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Michael Woods, Minister for Education and Science, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0020/print.html#sec5) (5) of the [Regional Technical Colleges (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 1999), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Regional Technical Colleges (Amendment) Act, 1999 (Section 5(5)) Order, 2000.

- (2) This order shall come into operation on 12^th May 2000.

###### 2.

2. Paragraphs (c), (d) and (e) of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0029/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Regional Technical Colleges (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1994) shall apply to the Institute of Technology, Blanchardstown.
