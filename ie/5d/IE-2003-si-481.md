# S.I. No. 481/2003 - Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Micheál Martin, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec47) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition Regulations 2003.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 26 January 2004.

###### 2.

2. The smoking of tobacco products in a place or premises (other than a dwelling specified in the Schedule to these Regulations is prohibited.

###### 3.

3. The Tobacco (Health Promotion and Protection) Regulations 1995 ( [S.I. No. 359 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0359.html) ) are revoked.

## SCHEDULE

1. A place of work.

2. An aircraft, train, ship or other vessel, a public service vehicle or other vehicle used for the carriage of members of the public for reward, in so far as it is a place of work.

3. A place or premises to which paragraph (b), (c), (d) or (e) of [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec47) (1) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002) applies, in so far as it is a place of work.

4. A licensed premises, in so far as it is a place of work.

5. A registered club, in so far as it is a place of work.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations may be cited as the Tobacco Smoking (Prohibition) Regulations 2003.

With effect from 26 January 2004 the Regulations prohibit the smoking of tobacco products in a place or premises (other than a dwelling) as specified in the Schedule to the Regulations. The places or premises so specified are:-

1. A place of work.

2. An aircraft, train, ship or other vessel, a public service vehicle or other vehicle used for the carriage of members of the public for reward, in so far as it is a place of work.

3. A place or premises to which paragraph (b), (c), (d) or (e) of [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html#sec47) (1) of the [Public Health (Tobacco) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0006/print.html) (No. 6 of 2002) applies, in so far as it is a place of work.

4. A licensed premises, in so far as it is a place of work.

5. A registered club, in so far as it is a place of work.

The Regulations also revoke the Tobacco (Health Promotion and Protection Regulations 1995 ( [S.I. No. 359 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0359.html) ).
