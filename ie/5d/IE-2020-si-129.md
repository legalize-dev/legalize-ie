# S.I. No. 129/2020 - Planning and Development Act 2000 (Subsection (3) of Section 251A) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 14*th April,* 2020.

WHEREAS the Minister for Housing, Planning and Local Government has, after consultation with the Minister for Health and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, made a request under subsection (3) of section 251A (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec9) of the [Emergency Measures in the Public Interest (Covid-19) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2020)) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000);

NOW the Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by subsection (3) of section 251A of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) (as adapted by the Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order ( [S.I. No. 358 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0358.html) )) and having had regard to the matters specified in subsection (5) of that section, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Planning and Development Act 2000 (Subsection (3) of Section 251A) Order 2020.

###### 2.

2. The date on which the period to be disregarded under subsection (1) of section 251A (inserted by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0002/print.html#sec9) of the [Emergency Measures in the Public Interest (Covid-19) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2020)) of the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) (No. 30 of 2000) shall end is 20 April 2020.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

29 March, 2020.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Taoiseach.
