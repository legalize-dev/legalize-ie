S.I. No. 349 of 1977.

POUNDS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1977.

I, GERARD COLLINS, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Pounds (Provision and Maintenance) Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1935), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1977.

###### 2.

2. The Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1975, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1977.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Pounds Regulations, 1938 (S. R. & O., No. 149 of 1938), shall have effect, in relation to chattels and animals impounded on or after the 1st day of December, 1977, as if the following Table were substituted for the Table (inserted by the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 289 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0289.html) )) in the Second Schedule.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to increase, with effect as on and from 1st December, 1977, the poundkeepers' fees prescribed in the Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1975.
