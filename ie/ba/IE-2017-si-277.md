# S.I. No. 277/2017 - Immigration Act 2004 (Registration Certificate Fee) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th July*, 2017.

I, CHARLES FLANAGAN, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 19 (as amended by [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec34) (*c*) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2011)) and [20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec20) of the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2004) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minster) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Immigration Act 2004 (Registration Certificate Fee) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Regulations of 2012” means the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (Registration Certificate Fee) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 444 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0444.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2 of the Regulations of 2012 is amended—

- (*a*) by the deletion of the definition of “Act of 1996”, and

- (*b*) by the insertion of the following definition:

  > “ ‘Act of 2015’ means the International Protection Act (No. 66 of 2015);”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 4 of the Regulations of 2012 is amended—

- (*a*) by the substitution of the following for paragraph (*b*):

  > - “(*b*) persons in respect of whom a declaration under section 47(1) of the Act of 2015 is, for the time being, in force;”,

- (*b*) by the substitution of the following for paragraph (*c*):

  > - “(*c*) persons to whom a permission has been given under section 56 of the Act of 2015 to enter and reside in the State, which permission is, for the time being, in force;”,

- (*c*) by the substitution of the following for paragraph (*d*):

  > - “(*d*) programme refugees within the meaning of section 59 of the Act of 2015;”,

- (*d*) in paragraph (*f*), by the substitution of “with those Arrangements;” for “with those Arrangements.”,

- (*e*) by the insertion after paragraph (*f*) of the following:

  > - “(*g*) persons in respect of whom a declaration under section 47(4) of the Act of 2015 is, for the time being, in force;
  >
  > - (*h*) persons to whom a permission has been given under section 49(4)(*a*) of the Act of 2015, which permission is, for the time being, in force;
  >
  > - (*i*) persons to whom a permission has been given under section 60(6) of the Act of 2015, which permission is, for the time being, in force;
  >
  > - (*j*) persons to whom a permission has been given under section 50(4) of the Act of 2015, which permission is, for the time being, in force.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 June 2017.

**CHARLES FLANAGAN,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations which amend the [Immigration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (Registration Certificate Fee) Regulations 2012 provide that certain categories of persons granted a permission or a declaration under the [International Protection Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0066/print.html) are exempt from paying the fee for obtaining a registration certificate.
