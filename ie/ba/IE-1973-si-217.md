# S.I. No. 217/1973 - Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935 (Part II) (No. 4) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 217 of 1973.

PIGS AND BACON ACT, 1935 (PART II) (No. 4) REGULATIONS, 1973.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [sections 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0008.html#sec8) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935) (as amended by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/sec0006.html#sec6) (*a*) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1954)) and the Agriculture (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 146 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0146.html) ), hereby make the following regulations, with respect to which pursuant to section 6 *(b)* of the said [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 4) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1969.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), the prescribed sum shall be 2.0833 new pence.

###### 5.

5. The [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 3) Regulations, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 37 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0037.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 23rd day of July, 1973.

**MARK CLINTON,**

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations adjust as from 1st January, 1969, the fees payable by fresh-meat exporters for veterinary examination of animals in accordance with the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .
