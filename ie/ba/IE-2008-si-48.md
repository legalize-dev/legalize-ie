# S.I. No. 48/2008 - Taxi Regulation Act 2003 (Grant of Hackney and Limousine Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 48 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0048.html)

TAXI REGULATION ACT 2003 (GRANT OF HACKNEY AND LIMOUSINE LICENCE FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 4*th March*, 2008.

The Commission for Taxi Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 34 and 39 of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2003), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Grant of Hackney and Limousine Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 2008.

###### 2.

2. The Fourth Schedule to the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 1995 ( [S.I. No. 136 of 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/si/0136.html) ) as amended by Regulation 7 of the Road Traffic (Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 534 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0534.html) ) is amended by substituting the following for paragraph 2.:

> “2. A fee of €1,000 shall be payable in respect of—
>
> - (*a*) the grant of a hackney licence,
>
> - (*b*) the grant of a limousine licence.”.

GIVEN under the seal of the Commission for Taxi Regulation,

1 February 2008

**KATHLEEN DOYLE.**

Commissioner for Taxi Regulation

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations increase the fee for the grant of a hackney or limousine licence to €1,000 from 1 February 2008.
