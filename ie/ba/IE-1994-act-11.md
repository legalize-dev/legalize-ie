# HEALTH (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO ENABLE DENTAL HEALTH SERVICES TO BE MADE AVAILABLE WITHOUT CHARGE TO CERTAIN CHILDREN WHO HAVE ATTENDED NATIONAL OR CERTAIN OTHER SCHOOLS. [16*th May*, 1994]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Free dental health services for certain children.

**1.**—(1) A health board shall make available without charge, for children who have attended a national school or a school standing specified in an order under section 66 (3) of the Health Act, 1970, and are of or under such age as may be specified by the Minister by regulations, such dental health services as may be so specified.

- (2) The Minister may make regulations for the purposes of this section.

###### 2 Short title, collective citation and construction.

**2.**—(1) This Act may be cited as the Health (Amendment) Act, 1994.

- (2) The Health Acts, 1947 to 1991, and this Act may be cited together as the Health Acts, 1947 to 1994.

- (3) The Health Acts, 1947 to 1991, and this Act shall be construed together as one.
