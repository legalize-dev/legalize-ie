# S.I. No. 156/1991 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) Order, 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 156 of 1991.

URBAN RENEWAL ACT, 1986 (DESIGNATED AREAS) ORDER, 1991.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) Order, 1991.

###### 2.

2. The area described in Part II of the Schedule to this Order is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

SCHEDULE

## PART I

In this Schedule—

"thoroughfare" includes any bridge, green, lane, river and street;

a reference to a line drawn along any thoroughfare is a reference to a line drawn along the centre of that thoroughfare;

a reference to a projection of any thoroughfare is a reference to a projection of a line drawn along the centre of that thoroughfare;

a reference to the point where any thoroughfare or projection of any thoroughfare intersects or joins any other thoroughfare is a reference to the point where a line drawn along the centre of one thoroughfare, or in the case of a projection of a thoroughfare, along the projection, would be intersected or joined by a line drawn along the centre of the other thoroughfare.

## PART II *Description of Temple Bar Designated Area*

That part of the county borough of Dublin bounded by a line commencing at the point (hereafter in this description referred to as the first-mentioned point) where the River Liffey is intersected by O'Connell Bridge, then continuing, initially in a southerly direction, along O'Connell Bridge, Westmoreland Street, College Green and Dame Street to the point where it joins Crane Lane, then continuing along Crane Lane to the point where it joins Essex Street East, then continuing in an easterly direction along Essex Street East, Temple Bar and Fleet Street to the point where it joins Price's Lane, then continuing along Price's Lane and the northerly projection thereof to the point where it intersects the River Liffey, then continuing in an easterly direction along the River Liffey to the first-mentioned point.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 25th day of June, 1991.

**PADRAIG FLYNN,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 25th day of June, 1991.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order provides for the designation under the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) of an area around Temple Bar in Dublin. The Order contains a formal description of the designated area.
