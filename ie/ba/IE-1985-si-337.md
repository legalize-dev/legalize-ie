# S.I. No. 337/1985 - Social Welfare (Family Income Supplement) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 337 of 1985.

SOCIAL WELFARE (FAMILY INCOME SUPPLEMENT) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1985.

The Minister for Social Welfare in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) and [115](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0115.html#sec115) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981) and section 232F of that Act (as inserted by the [Social Welfare Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) , (No. 5 of 1981)), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Family Income Supplement) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Family Income Supplement) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 278 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0278.html) ) may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Family Income Supplement) Regulations, 1984 and 1985.

###### 2.

2. The Social Welfare (Family Income Supplement) Regulations 1984 ( [S.I. No. 278 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0278.html) ) are hereby amended by:—

( *a* ) the substitution in article 19 of "£1,000" for "£500", and by

( *b* ) the substitution in section 114 (4) (i) and (ii) and 115 (1) (*d*), (i) and (ii) and 115 (3) (*a*) and (*b*) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) as set out and modified in the Schedule to the Regulations of "£1,000" for "£500" and "£3,000" for "£2,000".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 25th

day of September, 1985.

**BARRY DESMOND,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 25th day of

September, 1985.

**ALAN DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations amend provisions as to fines to correspond with amendments to similar provisions relating to other social welfare payments effected by the [Social Welfare Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1985).
