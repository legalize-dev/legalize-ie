# S.I. No. 441/1998 - Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 441 of 1998.

ROAD TRAFFIC (TRAFFIC AND PARKING) (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1998

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) and [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Road Traffic Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations, 1998.

- (2) The collective citation "the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations, 1997 and 1998" shall include these Regulations.

###### 2.

2. Sub-articles (2), (3), and (4) of article 32 of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution of the words "a large public service vehicle" for the words "an omnibus".

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 23rd day of November, 1998.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment and Local Government this 19th day of November, 1998

Noel Dempsey

Minister for the Environment and Local Government

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations, 1997 to provide for the use of bus lanes by all large public service vehicles.
