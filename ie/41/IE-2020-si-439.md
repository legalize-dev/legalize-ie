# S.I. No. 439/2020 - District Court (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th October,* 2020.

I, HELEN MCENTEE, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0065.html#sec65) (as amended by [section 66](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec66) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2011)) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the District Court (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2020.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 23^rd October 2020.

###### 2.

2. The District Court (Fees) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 22 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0022.html) ) is amended by the insertion of the following Articles after Article 4–

> “4A. Notwithstanding Article 2, no fee shall be payable under this Order in the year 2020 in respect of the items specified in column (1) of Part 4 of the Schedule at the following reference numbers:
>
> - (*a*) 4 (On an application for a certificate of transfer of a licence);
>
> - (*b*) 8 (On an application for a General Exemption Order);
>
> - (*c*) 16a (On an application for a restaurant certificate or a limited restaurant certificate);
>
> - (*d*) 18 (On an application for a certificate of registration of a club);
>
> - (*e*) 22 (On an application for any other public dancing licence);
>
> - (*f*) 23 (On an application for a music and singing licence).
>
>   4B. Notwithstanding Article 2, no fee shall be payable under this Order in the year 2020 in respect of the item specified, at reference number 5 (On the issue of a certificate of renewal of a licence), in column (1) of Part 4 of the Schedule where the licence in respect of which the certificate of renewal is issued is a spirit retailer’s on-licence.”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal,

9 October, 2020.

**MICHAEL MCGRATH,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 October, 2020.

**HELEN MCENTEE,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Order amends the District Court (Fees) Order 2014 ( [S.I. No. 22 of 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/si/0022.html) ) in order to provide that no fee shall be payable in the year 2020 in respect of certain items.
