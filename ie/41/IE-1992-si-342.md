# S.I. No. 342/1992 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) Order, 1992

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 342 of 1992.

URBAN RENEWAL ACT, 1986 (DESIGNATED AREAS) ORDER, 1992.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) (2) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) Order, 1992.

###### 2.

2. The area described in Part II of the Schedule to this Order is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

SCHEDULE

## Part I

In this Schedule—

a reference to a line drawn along any road or roadway is a reference to a line drawn along the centre of that road or roadway;

a reference to the point where any road or roadway intersects any other road or roadway is a reference to the point where a line drawn along the centre of one road or roadway would be intersected by a line drawn along the centre of the other road or roadway;

## Part II Description of Designated Area.

That part of the administrative county of Dublin bounded by a line commencing at the point (hereafter in this description referred to as the first-mentioned point) on the Blessington Road which is 250 metres west along Blessington Road from the point where that road intersects Belgard Road, then continuing in a northerly direction along the boundary of the area declared to be a designated area by the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) (No. 2) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 287 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0287.html) ) for a distance of 183 metres at which point it intersects the unnamed roadway joining Belgard Road, then continuing in a westerly direction along that roadway for a distance of 159 metres, then continuing in a southerly direction parallel to Belgard Road to the point where it intersects Blessington Road, then continuing in an easterly direction along Blessington Road to the first-mentioned point.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment this 18th day of November, 1992.

**MICHAEL SMITH,**

Minister for the Environment.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 18th day of November, 1992.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This order provides for the designation of an area in Tallaght, Co. Dublin under the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) . The order contains a formal description of the designated area.
