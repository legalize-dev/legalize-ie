# S.I. No. 71/2003 - Stores For Explosives (Amendment) Order 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 83 of the Explosives Act, 1875, as adapted, and on the recommendation of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, order as follows:—

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Stores for Explosives (Amendment Order 2003.

- (2) This Order and the Stores for Explosives Order, 1955, ( [S.I. No. 42 of 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/si/0042.html) may be cited together as the Stores for Explosives Order, 1955 and 2003.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) applies to this order.

###### 3.

3. The Stores for Explosives Order, 1955 is amended by the substitution of the following

Article for Article 6:

> “6. (a) Subject to paragraph (b), a store shall not be situated below ground in any quarry, tunnel or other underground place in which work is carried on or in which any persons are employed or in any place which opens into or out of or is in communication with any such quarry, tunnel or underground place.
>
> - (b) Paragraph (a) does not apply in relation to a mine.”

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

This Order provides for an amendment to The Stores for Explosives Order, 1955, ( [S.I. No. 42 of 1955](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1955/en/si/0042.html) ) and provides for the underground storage of explosives in a mine.
