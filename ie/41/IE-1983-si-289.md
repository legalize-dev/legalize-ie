S.I. No. 289 of 1983.

COMPANIES (FORMS) ORDER, 1983.

I, FRANK CLUSKEY, Minister for Trade, Commerce and Tourism, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), and [section 59](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/sec0059.html#sec59) of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Companies (Forms) Order, 1983.

- (2) The Companies (Forms) Order, 1964 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 1964](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1964/en/si/0045.html) ), the Companies (Forms) Order, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 256 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0256.html) ), and this Order may be cited together as the Companies (Forms) Orders, 1964 to 1983.

###### 2.

2. (1) The Forms set out in Part I of the Schedule hereto, or forms to the like effect, are hereby prescribed as the forms to be used in connection with [section 115](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0115.html#sec115) of the [Companies Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963), with such variations as circumstances require.

- (2) The Forms set out in Part II of the Schedule hereto, or forms to the like effect, are hereby prescribed as the forms to be used in connection with the provisions of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1983), with such variations as circumstances require.

###### 3.

3. The Companies (Forms) Order, 1964, shall be construed and have effect as if Form Nos. 44 and 44A were deleted from the Schedule thereto.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to prescribe the forms to be used in connection with the provisions of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) and to prescribe new forms to replace forms No. 44 and 44A which are deleted from the Schedule to the Companies (Forms) Order, 1964.
