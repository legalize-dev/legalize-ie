# S.I. No. 174/2000 - Lottery Prizes Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0028/sec0033.html#sec33) (2) of the [National Lottery Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1986), as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Lottery Prizes Regulations, 2000.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 2000.

###### 2.

2. [The Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1956), is hereby amended by the substitution in paragraph (*c*) of section 28(2) of “£15,000” for “£10,000” in each place where it occurs in that paragraph.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of June, 2000. |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE, |
|  | Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform. |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations increase, with effect from 1st day of August, 2000, the limit on the total value of prizes for lotteries held under [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Gaming and Lotteries Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) from £10,000 to £15,000.
