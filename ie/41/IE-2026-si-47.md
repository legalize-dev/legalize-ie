# S.I. No. 47/2026 - European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 17*th February,* 2026.

I, JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026[^1] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

- (2) The Principal Regulations, the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 244 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0244.html) ), the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 638 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0638.html) ), the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 328 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0328.html) ), the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 51 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0051.html) ), the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 549 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0549.html) ), the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 ( [S.I. No. 38 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0038.html) ), the Regulations of 2025 and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2020 to 2026.

- (3) These Regulations come into operation on 18 February 2026.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“Principal Regulations” means the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 575 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0575.html) );

“Regulations of 2025” means the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2025 ( [S.I. No. 409 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0409.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 2(1) (as amended by Regulation 3 of the Regulations of 2025) of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of—

- (a) “Annex I to EU Regulation 2019/1793” the following:

  > “‘Annex I to EU Regulation 2019/1793’ means Annex I to EU Regulation 2019/1793 as inserted by Article 1 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026[^1] ;”,

- (b) “Annex II to EU Regulation 2019/1793” the following:

  > “‘Annex II to EU Regulation 2019/1793’ means Annex II to EU Regulation 2019/1793 as inserted by Article 1 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026[^1] ;”, and

- (c) “EU Regulation 2019/1793” the following:

  > “‘EU Regulation 2019/1793’ means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 of 22 October 2019[^2] as amended by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/625 of 6 May 2020[^3] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/608 of 14 April 2021[^4] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1900 of 27 October 2021[^5] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/913 of 30 May 2022[^6] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/286 of 16 January 2024[^7] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3153 of 18 December 2024[^8] , Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1441 of 18 July 2025[^9] and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026[^1] ;”.

###### 4.

4. The European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 50 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0050.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

11 February, 2026.

**JENNIFER CARROLL MACNEILL,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations give further effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/194 of 28 January 2026 amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 on the temporary increase of official controls and emergency measures governing the entry into the Union of certain goods from certain third countries implementing Regulations (EU) 2017/625 and (EC) No. 178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council.

These Regulations amend the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 575 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0575.html) ) in the manner specified in these Regulations.

These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Official Controls in relation to Food Legislation) (Imports of Food of Non-Animal Origin) (Amendment) Regulations 2026 and come into operation on 18 February 2026.
