# S.I. No. 433/2017 - Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th October*, 2017.

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html#sec15) of the [Central Bank and Credit Institutions (Resolution) Act 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/act/pub/0027/print.html) (No. 27 of 2011), having consulted with the Central Bank of Ireland and the Credit Union Advisory Committee, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 381 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0381.html) ) (as amended by the Credit Institutions Resolution Fund Levy (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 499 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0499.html) )) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2—

    - (i) in paragraph (1), by substituting for the definition of “levy period” the following:

      > “ ‘levy period’ means the period commencing on 1 October 2017 and ending on 30 September 2018.”,

      and

    - (ii) in paragraph (3), by substituting “30 June 2017” for “30 June 2016”,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3(1) by substituting “1 October 2017” for “1 October 2016”, and

- (*c*) in Regulation 4(1), by substituting “28 February 2018” for “28 February 2017”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 September 2017.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Finance.
