# S.I. No. 70/1986 - Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 70 of 1986.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANISATION (SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS) (DUBLIN) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1985), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. Article 3 of the Local Government Reorganisation (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 128 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0128.html) ), as amended by the Local Government (Supplementary Provisions) (Dublin) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 418 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0418.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution in paragraph (*f*) of "31st day of December, 1986" for "31st day of March, 1986".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 24th day of March, 1986.

**JOHN BOLAND,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for the continuation in effect until 31st day of December 1986 of certain provisions of the School Attendance Acts, 1926 to 1967 in the added areas and in the excluded areas as defined in [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Local Government (Reorganisation) Act, 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .
