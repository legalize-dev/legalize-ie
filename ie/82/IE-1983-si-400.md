# S.I. No. 400/1983 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 400 of 1983.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1983.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1983.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1984.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall be—

( *a* ) as regards milk, 0.3 new pence per gallon,

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, £1.90 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0101.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 21st day of December, 1983.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations which come into effect from 1 January, 1984 provide for the payment of a levy on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
