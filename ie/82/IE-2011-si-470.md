# S.I. No. 470/2011 - Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001 (Additional Functions) (Public Transport by Road) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 23*rd September*, 2011.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html#sec11) (2) of the [Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) (No. 55 of 2001) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) ) and with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001 (Additional Functions) (Public Transport by Road) Order 2011.

###### 2.

2. In this Order, “public transport infrastructure” has the same meaning as it has in [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0015/print.html#sec2) of the [Dublin Transport Authority Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0015/print.html) (No. 15 of 2008).

###### 3.

3. The Railway Procurement Agency shall have the following additional functions:

- (*a*) to enter into agreements with the National Transport Authority in relation to the development of one or more bus rapid transit systems;

- (*b*) to secure the provision of, or to provide, public transport infrastructure as required in the performance of an agreement referred to in paragraph (*a*);

- (*c*) to enter into agreements with other persons in order to secure the provision of public transport infrastructure as required in the performance of an agreement referred to in paragraph (*a*) whether by means of a concession, joint venture, public private partnership or any other means.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 September 2011.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

20 September 2011.

**LEO VARADKAR,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the allocation of additional functions to the Railway Procurement Agency, under [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html#sec11) (2) of the [Transport (Railway Infrastructure) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0055/print.html) , in relation to the development of bus rapid transit systems.
