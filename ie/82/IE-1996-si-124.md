# S.I. No. 124/1996 - European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) (Amendment) Regulations, 1996

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 124 of 1996.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (INDICATION OF PRICES OF FOODSTUFFS AND NON-FOOD PRODUCTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to European Parliament and Council Directive No. 95/58/EC of 29 November 1995*, hereby make the following Regulations:

* O.J. No. L299, 12.12.1995, p.11.

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) (Amendment) Regulations, 1996.

- (2) These Regulations and the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 329 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0329.html) ), and the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0307.html) ), may be cited together as the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991 to 1996.

- (3) These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 7th day of June, 1995.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991, are hereby amended—

( *a* ) in paragraph (1) of Regulation 2—

- (i) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of "the Foodstuffs Directive":

  > "'the Foodstuffs Directive' means Council Directive No. 79/581/EEC of 19th June, 1979^1, as amended by Council Directive No. 88/315/EEC of 7th June, 1988^2, and European Parliament and Council Directive No. 95/58/EC of 29th November, 1995^4;",

^1 O.J No. L158, 26.6.1979, p.19.

^2 O.J No. L142, 9.6.1988, p.23.

^4 O.J No. L299, 12.12.1995, p.11.

and

- (ii) by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of "the Non-Food Products Directive":

  > "'the Non-Food Products Directive' means Council Directive No. 88/314/EEC of 7th June 1988, as amended by European Parliament and Council Directive No. 95/58/EC of 29th November, 1995^4;",

^3 O.J No. L142, 9.6.1988, p.19.

and

( *b* ) in paragraphs (2) and (3) of Regulation 3 by the substitution of "the 6th day of June, 1997" for "the 6th day of June, 1995".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 13th day of May, 1996.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Enterprise and Employment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations have been made to give effect to European Parliament and Council Directive No. 95/58/EC, which amends Council Directive No. 79/581/EEC as amended by Council Directives No. 88/314/EEC and 88/315/EEC.

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991.

It is the intention of the amending Regulations to retrospectively extend, by a further two years, an existing deadline of 6th June, 1995. It had originally been intended that the requirement to indicate the unit-price for all prepackaged goods would come into effect on that date. This requirement will now come into effect on 6th June, 1997.
