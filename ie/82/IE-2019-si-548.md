# S.I. No. 548/2019 - Road Traffic Act 2010 (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th November,* 2019.

I, Shane Ross, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec34) (as amended by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) and 41 of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0141.html) )), and after consultation with the Minister for Justice and Equality, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic Act 2010 (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2019.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 12 November, 2019.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Part 3) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 244 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0244.html) ).

###### 3.

3. Regulation 4 of the Principal Regulations is amended by the substitution of “Schedules 1 to 6” for “Schedules 1 to 5”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5 of the Principal Regulations is amended -

- (*a*) in paragraph (d), by the deletion of “and”,

- (*b*) in paragraph (e), by the substitution of “Schedule 5, and” for “Schedule 5”, and

- (*c*) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (e):

  > - “(f) €120 in respect of an offence mentioned in Schedule 6.”.

###### 5.

5. The Table in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Principal Regulations is amended -

- (*a*) in the column entitled “Article” by the substitution of “10 (other than paragraph (1)(a)) (as amended by Regulation 4 of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 495 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0495.html) ))” for “10 (as amended by Regulation 16(d) of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 332 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0332.html) ))”, and

- (*b*) in the column entitled “Description of Contravention” by the substitution of “Overtaking in prohibited circumstances (other than overtaking of a pedal cyclist)” for “Overtaking when unsafe or not permitted”.

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are amended by the insertion of the following Schedule after Schedule 5:

> ### “**Schedule 6**
>
> *Regulation 5(f)*
>
> **€120 fixed charge**
>
> An offence under section 35(5) of the Act of 1994 consisting of a contravention of Regulation 10(1)(a) of the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 being overtaking of a pedal cyclist in the circumstances prohibited.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 November, 2019.

**SHANE ROSS,**

Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for an increase in the fixed charge for the dangerous overtaking of cyclists.
