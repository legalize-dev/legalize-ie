# S.I. No. 213/1974 - Wool Marketing (Wool Exporter's Licence) (Fee) Regulations, 1974

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 213 of 1974.

WOOL MARKETING (WOOL EXPORTER'S LICENCE) (FEE) REGULATIONS, 1974.

I, MARK CLINTON, Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0026/sec0008.html#sec8) (1) of the [Wool Marketing Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1968), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Wool Marketing (Wool Exporter's Licence) (Fee) Regulations, 1974.

###### 2.

2. £20 is hereby fixed as the fee to be paid pursuant to [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0026/sec0008.html#sec8) (1) of the [Wool Marketing Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1968), in respect of an application for a licence under section 7 of that Act.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 4th day of July, 1974.

- (Signed) MARK CLINTON,

Minister for Agriculture and Fisheries.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 4th day of

July, 1974.

- (Signed) RICHIE RYAN,

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations prescribe a fee of £20 to accompany an application for a licence to carry on the business of exporting wool.
