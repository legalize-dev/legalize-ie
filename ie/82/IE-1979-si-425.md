# S.I. No. 425/1979 - Údarás na Gaeltachta (Appointed Day) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, MÁIRE NIC EOCHAGÁIN UÍ CHUINN, Minister for the Gaeltacht, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0005/sec0002.html#sec2) (1) of the [Údarás na Gaeltachta Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1979), hereby make the following Order:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Údarás na Gaeltachta (Appointed Day) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January, 1980, is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of Part II of the [Údarás na Gaeltachta Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1979).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 27th day of December, 1979.

**MÁIRE NIC EOCHAGÁIN UÍ CHUINN,**

Minister for the Gaeltacht.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order fixes 1 January, 1980, as the appointed day for the purposes of Part II of the [Údarás na Gaeltachta Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) .

This is an official translation of the Order.
