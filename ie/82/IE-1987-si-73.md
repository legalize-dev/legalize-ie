# S.I. No. 73/1987 - Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act 1930 (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 73 of 1987.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE (FRESH MEAT) ACT 1930 (EXPORTER'S LICENCES) (FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1986.

I. AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 51 of, and the Schedule (inserted by the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1978)) to, the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1930), and the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations, with respect to which, pursuant to section 13(9) (inserted by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1986.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38. of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Exporter's Licences) (Fees) Regulations, 1985 ( [S.I. No. 246 of 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/si/0246.html) ), are hereby amended by the insertion after Regulation 3 of the following Regulation:—

> "3A Notwithstanding the provisions of Regulation 3 of these Regulations, for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of Rule 1 of the Schedule (inserted by the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) ) of the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , the sum of £0.50 is hereby prescribed, for the period beginning on the 1st day of July, 1986, and ending on the 30th day of June, 1987, as the appropriate sum for a pork exporter's licence."

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 6th day of March, 1987.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations reduce as and from 1 July 1986 fees payable by fresh meat exporters for veterinary examination of pigs in accordance with the [Agricultural Produce (Fresh Meat) Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) .
