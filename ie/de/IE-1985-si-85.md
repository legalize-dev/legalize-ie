# S.I. No. 85/1985 - Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 85 of 1985.

WIRELESS TELEGRAPHY (EXPERIMENTER'S LICENCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1985.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Communications, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [Wireless Telegraphy Act, 1926](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1926/en/act/pub/0045/index.html) (No. 45 of 1926), and [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0040/index.html) (No. 40 of 1983), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985.

- (2) The Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) Regulations, 1937 to 1983, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) Regulations, 1937 to 1985.

- (3) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of April, 1985.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. Regulation 11 of the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) Regulations, 1937 (S.R. & O., No. 330 of 1937) as amended by Regulation 3 of the Wireless Telegraphy (Experimenter's Licence) (Amendment) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 87 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0087.html) ), is hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution in paragraph (1) of "seven pounds and fifty new pence" and "six pounds" for "six pounds and fifty new pence", and "five pounds", respectively, and,

( *b* ) the substitution in paragraph (2) of "three pounds" for "two pounds and fifty new pence",

and the said paragraphs (1) and (2), as so amended, are set out in paragraphs 1 and 2, respectively, of the Table to this Regulation.

TABLE.

1. A fee of seven pounds and fifty new pence shall be payable on the issue of an experimenter's licence and a fee of six pounds shall be payable on every renewal thereof.

2. Where an applicant for an experimenter's licence submits himself at the request of the Minister to a test of his operating speed held by or on behalf of the Minister, a fee of three pounds shall be paid by such an applicant at or before the commencement of such test.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 29th day of March, 1985.

**JIM MITCHELL.**

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, this 29th

day of March, 1985.

**ALAN M. DUKES.**

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for increases in the licence fees and in the examination fee for the experimenter's licence.
