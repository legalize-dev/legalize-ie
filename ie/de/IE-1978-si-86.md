S.I. No. 86 of 1978.

TRADE UNION (FEES) REGULATIONS, 1978.

I, DESMOND O'MALLEY, Minister for Industry, Commerce and Energy, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 13 of the Trade Union Act, 1871, as adapted by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Registry of Friendly Societies Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1936), and [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1975), and the Industry and Commerce (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0306.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance and with the consent of the Minister for Labour, insofar as these Regulations relate to fees chargeable under the said [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) , make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trade Union (Fees) Regulations, 1978.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 14th day of April, 1978.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. (1) The fees specified in Part I of the Schedule of these Regulations shall be payable in respect of matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1975, other than matters to be transacted under the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1975).

- (2) The fees specified in Part II of the Schedule shall be payable in respect of matters to be transacted under the [Trade Union Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) .

- (3) No fee shall be payable in respect of the services specified in Part III of the schedule to these Regulations.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 24 of the Regulations dated November 1, 1876, made by the Secretary of State, as amended by Regulations dated April 29, 1890, made by the Secretary of State, is hereby revoked.

1. Cancellation or withdrawal of certificate of registry of trade union.

3. Any document supplied to a public department.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of these Regulations is to increase the fees to be paid for matters to be transacted or for the inspection of documents under the Trade Union Acts, 1871 to 1975.
