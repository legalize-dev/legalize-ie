> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*“Iris Oifigi úil ”of* 26 *th July,*2024.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec5) (2) of the [Agriculture Appeals Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2001) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations have the purpose of updating Schedule 1 of the Act setting out the list of Schemes which may be referred to the Agriculture Appeals Office for appeal.
