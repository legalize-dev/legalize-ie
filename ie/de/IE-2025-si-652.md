# S.I. No. 652/2025 - Road Traffic (Small Public Service Vehicle) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 2*nd January,* 2026.

I, DARRAGH O’BRIEN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 34(3) (inserted by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) and [section 41](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec41) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

**Citation**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Small Public Service Vehicle) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2025.

**Definition**

###### 2.

2. In this Regulation “Act” means Taxi Regulation Act 2013 (No. 37 of 2013).

**Declaration of fixed charge offences – small public service vehicles**

###### 3.

3. An offence under—

- (a) section 20(4)(b) of the Act, consisting of a contravention of a provision of—

    - (i) the Taxi Regulation (Small Public Service Vehicle) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 33 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0033.html) ), or

    - (ii) the Small Public Service Vehicle (Cashless Payment Facility) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0294.html) ),

referred to in Schedule 1, and

- (b) Part 3 of the Act, consisting of a contravention of a provision of the Act referred to in Schedule 2,

is declared to be a fixed charge offence for the purpose of paragraph (e) of subsection (2) of section 34 (inserted by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec23) of the [Road Traffic Act 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2016)) of the [Road Traffic Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2010).

**Prescribed fixed charge amounts**

###### 4.

4. The amount prescribed for a fixed charge offence referred to in—

- (a) column (1) of Schedule 1, is the amount indicated in column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention of the fixed charge offence, and

- (b) column (1) of Schedule 2, is the amount indicated in column (3) of that Schedule opposite the mention of the fixed charge offence.

**Revocation**

###### 5.

5. The Road Traffic Acts 1961 to 2014 (Small Public Service Vehicle) (Fixed Charge Offences) Regulations 2014 (S. I. No. 507 of 2014) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations declare the contraventions of offences under Section 20(4)(b) and Part 3 of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) ;the Taxi Regulation (Small Public Service Vehicle) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 33 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0033.html) ) and the Small Public Service Vehicle (Cashless Payment Facility) Regulations 2022 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/si/0294.html) ) relating to the operation of small public service vehicles for which fixed charge notices may be issued by An Garda Síochána and prescribe the amounts in respect of such contraventions.
