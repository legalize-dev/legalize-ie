# S.I. No. 589/2001 - Local Government Act, 2001 (County and City Development Boards) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 129](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec129) (1) of the [Local Government Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2001) hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government Act, 2001 (County and City Development Boards) Order, 2001.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 1 January, 2002.

###### 3.

3. There shall be established in each county or city a body titled in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

This Order gives statutory effect to the establishment of County and City Development Boards which are listed in the Schedule to the Order.
