# S.I. No. 679/2024 - Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th December,* 2024.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Regulation (EU) 2024/257 of 10 January 2024[^1] (as amended by Council Regulation (EU) 2024/1015 of 26 March 2024[^2] and Council Regulation (EU) 2024/1856 of 28 June 2024[^3] ), and effect to Council Regulation (EU) 2024/2678 of 10 October 2024[^4] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) Regulation 2024 ( [S.I. No. 457 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0457.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by the insertion after “28 June 2024” in the definition of “Council Regulation” of the following: “and Council Regulation (EU) 2024/2678 of 10 October 2024”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

4 December, 2024.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This instrument amends the Sea-Fisheries (Quotas) Regulation 2024 ( [S.I. No. 457 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0457.html) ) to give effect to Council Regulation (EU) 2024/2678 of 10 October 2024 amending Regulation (EU) 2024/257 fixing for 2024, 2025 and 2026 the fishing opportunities for certain fish stocks, applicable in Union waters and, for Union fishing vessels, in certain non-Union waters, and amending Regulation (EU) 2023/194 fixing for 2023 and 2024 such fishing opportunities.
