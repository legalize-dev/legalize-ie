# S.I. No. 329/1988 - Adoption (No. 2) Rules, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 329 of 1988.

ADOPTION (No. 2) RULES, 1988.

An Bord Uchtála, with the consent of the Minister for Health and in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Adoption Act, 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1952), hereby makes the following rules:—

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the Adoption (No. 2) Rules, 1988.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall be included in the expression "the Adoption Rules, 1976 to 1988".

###### 3.

3. In the Schedule to the Adoption Rules, 1976 ( [S.I. No. 216 of 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/si/0216.html) ) and in the Schedule to the Adoption Rules, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 134 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0134.html) ), in any place where the address of An Bord Uchtala appears as "65 Merrion Square, Dublin 2", the following shall be substituted as the address of An Bord Uchtala—

> "Hawkins House, Dublin 2".

###### 4.

4. These rules shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1989.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Board this 1st day of December, 1988.

**AGNES B. CASSIDY,**

Chairman.

The Minister for Health hereby consents to the foregoing rules.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 8th day of December 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Adoption Board will shortly be moving its offices to Hawkins House, Dublin 2. Various forms used by the Board in the adoption process refer to its address as 65 Merrion Square. The purpose of these rules is to change the reference in the forms to Hawkins House with effect from 1 January 1989.
