# S.I. No. 367/2002 - Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment) Regulations, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and [Section 53](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0053.html#sec53) of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following regulations:-

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment Regulations, 2002.

###### 2.

2. Article 4 of the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 116 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0116.html) ) (as amended by article 2 of the Health (In-Patient Charges) (Amendment Regulations, 2001 ( [S.I. No. 582 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0582.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution for sub-article (2) of the following:

> - “(2) The charge referred to in sub-article 4 (1) shall be at the rate of €36.00 in respect of each day, subject to a maximum payment of €360.00 in any period of twelve consecutive months, during which the person is maintained as an in-patient provided that in calculating the charge, where such person is so maintained for a single period of more than one day, no account shall be taken of the final day.”.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 2002.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).

These Regulations amend the Health (In-Patient Charges) Regulations, 1987 and 2001 by raising the daily charge for in-patient services from €33.00 to €36.00 and by raising the maximum amount payable in any period of twelve consecutive months from €330 to €360. The usual exemptions, which include medical card holders and hardship provision shall continue to apply. The revised charge comes into effect on 1 August, 2002.
