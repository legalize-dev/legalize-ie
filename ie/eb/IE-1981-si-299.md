# S.I. No. 299/1981 - Imposition of Duties (No. 254) (Excise Duty on Tobacco Products) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Imposition of Duties Act, 1957](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1957/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1957), [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Finance Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1962), [section 46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0006/sec0046.html#sec46) of the [Finance Act, 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1975), and [section 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0034/sec0029.html#sec29) of the [Value-Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0034/index.html) (No. 34 of 1978) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Imposition of Duties (No. 254) (Excise Duty on Tobacco Products) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The duty of excise on tobacco products imposed by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0032/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Finance (Excise Duty on Tobacco Products) Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0032/index.html) (No. 32 of 1977), shall, in so far as it is chargeable on cigarettes (within the meaning of the Imposition of Duties (No. 243) (Excise Duty on Tobacco Products) Order, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 296 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0296.html) )), be charged, levied and paid, as on and from the 1st day of September, 1981, at the rate of £17.90 per thousand together with an amount equal to 18.75 per cent. of the price at which the cigarettes are sold by retail in lieu of any other rate.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order alters the composition of the rate of excise duty on cigarettes. The new rate is designed to ensure that there is no increase in the excise duty burden on cigarettes as a consequence of the increase in the rate of Value-Added Tax from 10 per cent on 1 September, 1981.

The excise duty on tobacco products, other than cigarettes, is not affected by this Order.
