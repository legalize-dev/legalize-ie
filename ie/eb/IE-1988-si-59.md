# S.I. No. 59/1988 - Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order, 1988

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 59 of 1988.

BEAUMONT HOSPITAL BOARD (ESTABLISHMENT) ORDER, 1977 (AMENDMENT) (No. 2) ORDER, 1988.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) and [4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Health (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1961) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) No. 2) Order 1988.

###### 2.

2. Article 5 of the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 255 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0255.html) ) (as substituted by the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) Order, 1988 [ [S.I. No. 13 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0013.html) ]) is hereby amended by the substitution of "fifteen" for "twelve".

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 6th day of April, 1988.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

The effect of this Order is to increase the membership of the Beaumont Hospital Board from twelve to fifteen.
