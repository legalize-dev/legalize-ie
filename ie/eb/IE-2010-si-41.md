# S.I. No. 41/2010 - National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2009) Delegation of Functions Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 41 of 2010.

NATIONAL TREASURY MANAGEMENT AGENCY ACT 1990 (SOCIAL WELFARE AND PENSIONS ACT 2009) DELEGATION OF FUNCTIONS ORDER 2010.

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th February*, 2010.

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990 (Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2009) Delegation of Functions Order 2010.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on 17 February 2010.

###### 2.

2. The functions of the Minister for Finance under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec22) (5) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) , being functions of the Minister for Finance specified at paragraph (*u*) of the [First Schedule](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) to the [National Treasury Management Agency Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (inserted by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec22) (11) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) ), are delegated to the National Treasury Management Agency.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Government,

9 February 2010.

**BRIAN COWEN,**

Taoiseach.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order delegates to the National Treasury Management Agency certain functions of the Minister for Finance under [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html#sec22) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) in connection with the making of a Pensions Insolvency Payment Scheme.
