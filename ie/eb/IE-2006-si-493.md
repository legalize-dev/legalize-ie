**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 493 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0493.html)**

**Taxi Regulation Act 2003 (Small Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006**

The Commission for Taxi Regulation, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sections 34 and 39 of the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2003), and with regard to any amendment or revocation of any regulation made under [section 82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0082.html#sec82) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) with the consent of the Minister for Transport, hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Taxi Regulation Act 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (Small Public Service Vehicles) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2006.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 25 September 2006.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “dispatch operator” means a person engaged in the activity of taking a booking of a small public service vehicle that is owned or driven by another person (other than an employee of the first person) and arranging with that small public service vehicle driver to provide the service booked.

###### 3.

3. (1) References to “licensing authority” in the Road Traffic (Small Public Service Vehicles) Regulations 1963 to 2002 shall be deemed to mean the Commission for Taxi Regulation.

- (2) References to “taximeter area” in the Road Traffic (Small Public Service Vehicles) Regulations 1963 to 2002 shall be deemed to mean the national taximeter area, as declared by the Commission for Taxi Regulation under section 41 of the Taxi Regulation Act 2003 with effect from 25 September 2006, and references to Dublin Cork, Galway, Donegal or other taximeter areas shall be read accordingly.

**Explanatory Memorandum**

**(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)**

These Regulations amend existing Regulations to provide for matters relating to the regulation of small public service vehicles, including the following:

▪ What information is required to be included on printed taxi receipts,

▪ What driver identification is required to be displayed inside a small public service vehicle,

▪ What information is allowed to be displayed on the roof signs of taxis generally and what information is required to be displayed on roof signs of wheelchair accessible taxis, and where on the sign this information is to be displayed.
