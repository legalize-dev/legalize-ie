# S.I. No. 223/2022 - Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th May,* 2022.

I, CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec3) and [14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec14) of the [Sea-Fisheries and Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2006) and the Sea Fisheries, Foreshore and Dumping at Sea (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2007 ( [S.I. No. 707 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0707.html) ) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), for the purpose of giving further effect to Commission Regulation (EEC) No. 3440/84 of 6 December 1984[^1] (as amended by Regulation (EEC) No 955/87 of 1 April 1987[^2] , Commission Regulation (EEC) No 2122/89 of 14 July 1989[^3] and Commission Regulation No 146/2007 of 15 February 2007[^4] ), and Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019[^5] , and effect to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2324 of 23 August 2021[^6] hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0520.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by the insertion after “20 June 2019” in the definition of “Council Regulation” of the following: “as amended by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2324 of 23 August 2021”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 March, 2022.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

This instrument amends the Sea-Fisheries (Technical Measures) Regulations 2019 ( [S.I. No. 520 of 2019](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2019/en/si/0520.html) ) to give effect to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2324 of 23 August 2021 as regards technical measures for demersal and pelagic fisheries in the Celtic Sea, the Irish Sea and West of Scotland.
