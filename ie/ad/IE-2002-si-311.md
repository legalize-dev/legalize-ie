# S.I. No. 311/2002 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, CHARLIE McCREEVY, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations 2002.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 2000 and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations 1964 to 2002.

###### 2.

2. The organisations specified in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisations for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

## **SCHEDULE**

Food Safety Authority

Office of Director of Telecommunications Regulation

Environmental Protection Agency

Dublin Docklands Development Authority

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and “approved organisations”, and between one “approved organisation” and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as “approved organisations”.
