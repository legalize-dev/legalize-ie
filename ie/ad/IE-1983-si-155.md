# S.I. No. 155/1983 - Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1983

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 155 of 1983.

INFECTIOUS DISEASES (MAINTENANCE) REGULATIONS, 1983

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers vested in him by section 5 and sub-section (5) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) , (No. 28 of 1947), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1983.

###### 2.

2. The Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 76 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0076.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations—

"outgoings" means the rent, ground rent, rates and purchase annuity payable in respect of premises and mortgage repayments relating to the purchase of premises;

"receiving in-patient services" means receiving in-patient service made available under the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970); and

"person" means a person to whom [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) applies by virtue of sub-section (1) of that section.

###### 4.

4. The payment to be made under sub-section (3) of [section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) to or in respect of a person or his dependants shall, with effect from the 27th day of June, 1983, be a weekly payment not exceeding:—

( *a* ) the sum specified in Part One of the Schedule hereto which is appropriate to the person, and

( *b* ) such of the increases specified in Part Two of the said Schedule as are appropriate.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of these Regulations is to amend the existing Regulations governing the payment of maintenance allowances to persons being treated for certain infectious diseases — the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 76 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0076.html) ) — and to provide as from 27th June, 1983, for increases in the maximum rates of allowance payable.

The infectious diseases for which maintenance allowances are payable are acute anterior poliomyelitis, diphtheria, dysentery, lassa fever, paratyphoid A, paratyphoid B, rabies, salmonella infection, scarlet fever, streptococcal sore throat, tuberculosis, and typhus fever.
