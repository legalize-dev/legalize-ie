# S.I. No. 214/2017 - European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 26*th May*, 2017.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2016/317 of 3 March 2016[^1] and further effect to 2004/842/EC: Commission Decision of 1 December 2004[^2] (as amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/320 of 3 March 2016[^3] ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Cereal Seed) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0217.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “Directive” (as amended by the European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 375 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0375.html) )) the following:

> “‘Directive’ means Council Directive 66/402/EEC of 14 June 1966[^4] (as amended by Council Directive 69/60/EEC of 18 February 1969[^5] , Council Directive 71/162/EEC of 30 March 1971[^6] , Council Directive 72/274/EEC of 20 July 1972[^7] , Council Directive 72/418/EEC of 6 December 1972[^8] , Council Directive 73/438/EEC of 11 December 1973[^9] , Council Directive 75/444/EEC of 26 June 1975[^10] , Council Directive 78/55/EEC of 19 December 1977[^11] , Council Directive 78/692/EEC of 25 July 1978[^12] , Commission Directive 79/641/EEC of 27 June 1979[^13] , Council Directive 79/692/EEC of 24 July 1979[^14] , Council Directive 86/155/EEC of 22 April 1986[^15] , Commission Directive 86/320/EEC of 20 June 1986[^16] , Commission Directive 87/120/EEC of 14 January 1987[^17] , Council Directive 88/380/EEC of 13 June 1988[^18] , Council Directive 90/654/EEC of 4 December 1990[^19] , Council Directive 96/72/EC of 18 November 1996[^20] , Council Directive 98/95/EC of 14 December 1998[^21] , Council Directive 98/96/EC of 14 December 1998[^22] , Commission Directive 1999/54/EC of 26 May 1999[^23] , Council Directive 2001/64/EC of 31 August 2001[^24] , Council Directive 2003/61/EC of 18 June 2003[^25] , Council Directive 2004/117/EC of 22 December 2004[^26] , Commission Directive 2009/74/EC of 26 June 2009[^27] , Commission Implementing Directive 2012/1/EU of 6 January 2012[^28] , Commission Implementing Directive 2012/37/EU of 22 November 2012[^29] , Commission Implementing Decision 2014/150/EU of 18 March 2014[^30] , Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2015/1955 of 29 October 2015[^31] , Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2016/317 of 3 March 2016 and 2004/842/EC: Commission Decision of 1 December 2004 (as amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/320 of 3 March 2016);”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 March 2017.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations give effect to the provisions of Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2016/317 of 3 March 2016 which amends Council Directive 66/402 as regards the official label of seed packages

These Regulations also give effect to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2016/320 of 3 March 2016 amending Decision 2004/842/EC concerning implementing rules whereby Member States may grant an authorization to place on the market seed belonging to varieties for which an application for entry in the national catalogues of varieties of agricultural plant species or vegetable species has been submitted.
