# S.I. No. 617/2002 - National Development Finance Agency Act 2002 (Establishment Day) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html#sec2) (2) of the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Development Finance Agency Act 2002 (Establishment Day) Order 2002.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of January 2003 is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002).

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 20 December 2002. |
|  |  |
|  | CHARLIE McCREEVY Minister for Finance |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order prescribes that the 1st day of January 2003 is the establishment day of the National Development Finance Agency for the purposes of the [National Development Finance Agency Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0029/print.html) (No. 29 of 2002).
