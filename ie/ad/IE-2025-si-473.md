# S.I. No. 473/2025 - Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th October,* 2025.

I, DARRAGH O’BREIN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0003.html#sec3) and [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0035.html#sec35) of the [Road Traffic Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1994) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 (S.I. No. [35](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0007/sec0035.html#sec35) 1 of 2020)), hereby make the following regulations:

**Citation**

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

**Amendment of [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html)**

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Traffic and Parking) Regulations 1997 ( [S.I. No. 182 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0182.html) ) are amended—

- (a) in article 4—

    - (i) in sub-article (1)—

        - (I) by substituting for the definition of “bus-only street” the following:

          > ““bus-only street” means a street or a portion of a street at each entrance to which is provided traffic sign number RUS 011 (no straight ahead) accompanied by an information plate to indicate that vehicles, other than large public service vehicles, pedal cycles, e-mopeds, electric scooters and vehicles entering for access only, are prohibited from entering that street;”;

        - (II) by substituting for the definition of “car club” (inserted by S. I. No. 325 of 2014) the following:

          > “‘car share’ means a car share scheme or car club, licensed or authorised by a road authority under bye-laws made under [section 199](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec199) of the [Local Government Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) , for the purpose of the sharing of mechanically propelled vehicles by members of the scheme or club renting or using the vehicles for short periods, such as by the hour or day;”;

        - (III) by substituting for the definition of “car club vehicle parking bay” (inserted by S. I. No. 325 of 2014) the following:

          > “‘car share vehicle parking bay’ means a space intended for the parking of a mechanically propelled vehicle in which is displayed a car club vehicle permit or a car share vehicle permit”;

        - (IV) by substituting for the definition of “car club vehicle permit” (inserted by Regulation 2(a) of the Regulations of 2014) the following:

          > “‘car club vehicle permit’” means a car club vehicle permit issued by a road authority for the purposes of Article 44A”;

        - (V) by inserting after the definition of “car club vehicle permit” the following:

          > “‘car share vehicle permit’ means a car share scheme permit issued by a road authority for the purposes of Article 44A;
          >
          > ‘car share electrically powered vehicle recharging and parking bay’ means a space intended for the recharging and parking of an electrically powered vehicle in which is displayed a car share electrically powered vehicle permit;
          >
          > ‘car share electrically powered vehicle permit’ means a car share electrically powered vehicle permit issued by a road authority for the purposes of Article 44C.”;

        - (VI) by substituting for the definition of “L1e-A e-moped” the following:

          > “‘ e-moped’ means a pedal-assisted mechanically propelled vehicle that is a bicycle or tricycle capable of propulsion solely by the physical exertions of a person or persons seated on it, and equipped with an auxiliary electric motor—
          >
          > - (a) which has a maximum continuous rated power greater than 0.25 kilowatts but less than or equal to 1 kilowatt, and
          >
          > - (b) the output of which cuts off when the physical exertions stop and before, or when, the bicycle or tricycle reaches the speed of 25 kilometres per hour”;

        - (VII) by substituting for the definition of “median strip” the following:

          > “‘median strip’ means a boundary between the two carriageways of a road other than a traffic sign to which Regulation 58 of the Regulations of 2025 refers”;

        - (VIII) by substituting for the definition of “the Regulations of 2024” the following:

          > “‘Regulations of 2025’ means Road Traffic (Traffic Signs) Regulations 2025 ( [S.I. No. 433 of 2025](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2025/en/si/0433.html) );”;

        - (IX) by substituting for the definition of “parking bay” the following:

          > “‘parking bay’ means the area contained between lines of the type referred to in Regulation 66 of the Regulations of 2025”;

    - (ii) by substituting for sub-article (3) the following:

      > - “(3)
      >     - (a) Notwithstanding paragraph (b), a reference in these Regulations to a traffic sign prescribed in the Regulations of 2025 and any number referred to in association with a traffic sign shall be the number allocated to such sign in such Regulations.
      >
      >     - (b) Descriptions given to traffic signs in the Regulations of 2025 may be referred to in these Regulations for the purpose of identification.”;

    - (iii) by substituting for sub-article (5) (a) the following:

      > - “(a) Subject to paragraph (b), a reference in these Regulations to a vehicle unless otherwise specified, means a mechanically propelled vehicle (other than a mechanically propelled wheelchair), a pedal cycle, an e-moped or an electric scooter.”;

- (b) in article 8—

    - (i) by substituting for sub-article (3) the following:

      > - “(3) A driver of a vehicle approaching a road junction or a cycle track crossing shall yield the right of way to another vehicle which has commenced to turn or cross at the junction or cycle track crossing in accordance with these Regulations and to a pedestrian who has commenced to cross at the junction in accordance with these Regulations.”,

    - (ii) in sub-article (6) (inserted by [S.I. No. 222 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0222.html) ), by substituting for sub-article (6B) the following:

      > - “**(6B)** A driver of a pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter approaching a zebra pedestrian crossing where traffic sign number RPC 001B (zebra pedestrian crossing of a cycle track) is provided shall yield the right of way to any pedestrian who is waiting to cross at the zebra pedestrian crossing or has commenced crossing the cycle track at the crossing.
      >
      > - **(6C)** A driver of a vehicle approaching a parallel zebra crossing where traffic sign number RPC 005 (parallel zebra crossing) or traffic sign RPC 005A (parallel zebra crossing without belisha beacons) is provided shall yield right of way to any pedestrian or person driving a pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter who is waiting to cross at the crossing, or any pedestrian or person driving a pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter who has commenced crossing the road at the crossing.
      >
      > - **(6D)** A driver of a vehicle approaching a combined zebra crossing where traffic sign number RPC 006 (combined zebra crossing) or traffic sign RPC 006A (combined zebra crossing without belisha beacons) is provided shall yield right of way to any pedestrian or person driving a pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter who is waiting to cross at the crossing, or any pedestrian or person driving a pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter who has commenced crossing the road at the crossing.”

- (c) in article 10—

    - (i) by substituting for sub-article (1) (inserted by S. I. No. 222 of 2024) the following:

      > “10 (1) A driver shall not overtake or attempt to overtake if to do so would endanger or cause inconvenience to—
      >
      > - (a) a pedal cyclist or an e-moped or electric scooter driver, or
      >
      > - (b) another person.”,

    - (ii) by substituting for sub-article (3) the following:

      > - “(3) A driver shall not overtake, or attempt to overtake, on a stretch of roadway on which traffic sign number RUS 014 (no overtaking) and a driver (other than a pedal cyclist or an e-moped or electric scooter driver) shall not overtake, or attempt to overtake, on a stretch of roadway on which traffic sign number RUS 068 (cycle street) has been provided.”,

    - (iii) by substituting for sub-article (5) (inserted by S. I. No. 222 of 2024) the following:

      > - “(5)
      >     - (a) A driver (other than a pedal cyclist or an e-moped or electric scooter driver) may only overtake on the left—
      >
      >         - (i) where the driver of the vehicle about to be overtaken has signalled an intention to turn to the right and the driver of the overtaking vehicle intends, after overtaking, to go straight ahead or turn to the left,
      >
      >         - (ii) where the driver of the overtaking vehicle intends, after overtaking, to turn to the left at the next road junction and has signalled this intention, or
      >
      >         - (iii) in slow-moving traffic, when vehicles in the traffic lane on the driver’s right are moving more slowly than the overtaking vehicle.
      >
      >     - (b) A pedal cyclist or an e-moped or electric scooter driver may overtake on the left where vehicles to the pedal cyclist’s, the e-moped driver’s or the electric scooter driver’s right are stationary or are moving more slowly than the overtaking pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter, except where the vehicle to be overtaken—
      >
      >         - (i) has signalled an intention to turn to the left and there is a reasonable expectation that the vehicle in which the driver has signalled an intention to turn to the left will execute a movement to the left before the pedal cycle, e-moped or electric scooter overtakes the vehicle,
      >
      >         - (ii) is stationary for the purposes of—
      >
      >             - (I) permitting a passenger or passengers to alight or board the vehicle, or
      >
      >             - (II) loading or unloading the vehicle.”,

  >
  >     - (c) The restriction to mechanically propelled vehicles in paragraph (a) does not apply in the case of a cycle track with traffic sign number RRM 036 (cycle track symbol) in conjunction with traffic sign number RRM 038 (elephant’s footprint markings) or traffic sign number RRM 023 (broken white line).

  >
