# S.I. No. 347/1995 - Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Levy Returns) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 347 of 1995.

MILK (REGULATION OF SUPPLY) (LEVY RETURNS) REGULATIONS, 1995.

I, IVAN YATES, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 20](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/sec0020.html#sec20) of the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1994) hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Milk (Regulation of Supply) (Levy Returns) Regulations, 1995.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, "the Act" means the [Milk (Regulation of Supply) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. A return under subsection (2) of section 8 of the Act shall—

( *a* ) be made in the form M.A.4 of the Schedule hereto, and

( *b* ) be made not later than the 28th day of the month following the end of the accounting period.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 15th day of December, 1995.

**IVAN YATES.**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry.
