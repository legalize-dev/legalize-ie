# S.I. No. 314/1997 - Exploration and Mining (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 314 of 1997.

EXPLORATION AND MINING (TRANSFER OF DEPARTMENTAL ADMINISTRATION AND MINISTERIAL FUNCTIONS) ORDER, 1997

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 6 (1) of the Ministers and Secretaries (Amendment) Act. 1939 (No. 36 of 1939), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Exploration and Mining (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order. 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 15th day of July, 1997.

###### 2.

2. In this Order "functions" includes powers and duties.

###### 3.

3. (1) The administration and business in connection with the exercise, performance or execution of any functions transferred by Article 4 of this Order are hereby transferred to the Department of the Marine and Natural Resources.

- (2) References to the Department of Public Enterprise contained in any Act or any instrument made thereunder and relating to any administration and business transferred by paragraph (1) of this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Department of the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 4.

4. (1) There are hereby transferred to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources the functions vested in the Minister for Public Enterprise by or under the Minerals Company Acts. 1941 to 1950. and the Minerals Development Acts, 1940 to 1995.

- (2) References to the Minister for Public Enterprise contained in any Act or instrument made thereunder and relating to any functions transferred by this Article shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 5.

5. Where, immediately before the commencement of this Order, any legal proceedings are pending to which the Minister for Public Enterprise is a party and the proceedings have reference to Functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, the name of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources shall, in so far as the proceedings relate to any function transferred by this Order, be substituted in the proceedings for that of the Minister for Public Enterprise or added in the proceedings, as may be appropriate, and the proceedings shall not abate by reason of such substitution.

###### 6.

6. Anything commenced before the commencement of this Order by or under the authority of the Minister for Public Enterprise may, in so far as it relates to functions transferred by this Order to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources be carried on or completed on or after such commencement by the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 7.

7. Every document (including any certificate or licence) granted or made in the exercise of a function transferred by this Order shall, if and in so far as it was operative immediately before the commencement of this Order, have effect on and after such commencement as if it had been granted or made by the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 8.

8. References to the Minister for Public Enterprise contained in the memorandum and articles of association of any company and relating to any function transferred by this Order shall, on and after the commencement of this Order, be construed as references to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

###### 9.

9. (1) All property, rights and liabilities held. enjoyed or incurred before the commencement of this Order by the Minister for Public Enterprise in connection with the functions transferred by this Order shall be and are hereby transferred to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources and. accordingly, without any further conveyance, transfer or assignment

( *a* ) the said property, real and personal, shall, on such commencement, vest in the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources,

( *b* ) the said rights shall, as on and from such commencement, be enjoyed by the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, and

( *c* ) the said liabilities shall, as on and from such commencement, be the liabilities of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

- (2) All moneys stocks shares and securities transferred to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources by this Article which on such commencement are standing in the name of the Minister for Public Enterprise shall on the request of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources be transferred into the name of the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

Given under the official seal of the Government this 15th day of July. 1997

Bertie Ahern T.D.

Taoiseach

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order transfers to the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources with effect from the date specified Ministerial functions and Departmental administration in relation to Exploration and Mining.
