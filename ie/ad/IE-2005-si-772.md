# S.I. No. 772/2005 - European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I No 772 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0772.html)**

**European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 2005**

**I, Martin Cullen** , Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving further effect to Article 7(1) of Council Directive No. 91/439/EEC of 29 July 1991[^1] , as amended by Council Directive No. 2000/56 of 14 September 2000[^2] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) (Amendment) Regulations 2005.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 12 December 2005.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Driving Theoretical Tests) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No 52 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0052.html) ) are amended in Regulation 5(4), by substituting “€35.60” for “€34”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations revise the fee payable in respect of an application for a driver theory test certificate.
