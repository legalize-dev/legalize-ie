# S.I. No. 29/2002 - Lottery Prizes Regulations 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, John O'Donoghue, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0028/sec0033.html#sec33) (2) of the [National Lottery Act 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1986) as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Lottery Prizes Regulations 2002.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 March 2002.

###### 2.

2. The [Gaming and Lotteries Act 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1956), is amended by substituting in paragraph (c) of section 28 (2) “€20,000” for “£15,000” in each place where it occurs in that paragraph.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | Given under my Official Seal, 31 January 2002. |
|  | **JOHN O'DONOGHUE** MINISTER FOR JUSTICE, EQUALITY AND LAW REFORM |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation thereof).*

These Regulations increase, with effect from 1 March 2002, the limit on the total value of prizes for lotteries held under [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Gaming and Lotteries Act 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) . The limit on the total value of prizes for lotteries held under section 28 of the 1956 Act is increased from £15,000 (€19,046.07) to €20,000.
