# S.I. No. 603/2024 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 13) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 8*th November,* 2024.

I, HEATHER HUMPHREYS, Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec4) (amended by reference 1 of the [Schedule to the Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 2024)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , and by sections 2(2)(c), [232](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) and [297](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) of, and Reference 19 (amended by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec26) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) (No. 37 of 2010)) in Table 2 of Schedule 3 to, the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) , with the consent of the Minister of Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform, hereby make the following Regulations:

**Citation and construction**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 13) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2024 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2024.

**Commencement**

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 9 August 2024.

**Definitions**

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations, “Regulations of 2024” means the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 10) (Assessment of Means) Regulations 2024 ( [S.I. No. 433 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0433.html) ).

**Assessment of Means**

###### 4.

4. The Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 are amended —

- (a) in article 8(3), (amended by article 4(a) of the Regulations of 2024) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (bb):

  > - “(bba) any ex gratia payments made under the Stardust Redress Scheme (2024),”,

- (b) in article 155, (amended by article 4(b) of the Regulations of 2024) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (bb):

  > - “(bba) any ex gratia payments made under the Stardust Redress Scheme (2024),”,

  and

- (c) in article 174, (amended by article 4(c) of the Regulations of 2024) by the insertion of the following paragraph after paragraph (fc):

  > - “(fca) any ex gratia payments made under the Stardust Redress Scheme (2024),”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform consents to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 October, 2024.

**PASCHAL DONOHOE,**

Minister for Public Expenditure, National Development Plan Delivery and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 October, 2024.

**HEATHER HUMPHREYS,**

Minister for Social Protection.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide that any *ex gratia* payments made under the Stardust Redress Scheme (2024) by the Department of Justice will be disregarded in the means test for social assistance payments, increases for qualified adults and the Working Family Payment.
