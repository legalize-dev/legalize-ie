# S.I. No. 470/2016 - European Union (Identification of Equidae) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 9*th September*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) for the purpose of giving full effect to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/262 of 17 February 2015[^1] , and giving effect to Council Directive No. 90/427/EEC of 26 June 1990[^2] , and Commission Decision No. 92/353/EEC of 11 June 1992[^3] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Identification of Equidae) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Identification of Equidae) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 62 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0062.html) ) (as amended by the European Union (Identification of Equidae) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0141.html) )) are amended by substituting for Regulation 1(1) the following—

> “1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Identification of Equidae) Regulations 2016.”.

###### 3.

3. The European Union (Identification of Equidae) (Amendment) Regulations 2016 ( [S.I. No. 141 of 2016](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2016/en/si/0141.html) ) are revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

31 August 2016.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

**EXPLANATORY NOTICE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the citation to Statutory Instrument No. 62 of 2016 and revoke Statutory Instrument No. 141 of 2016.
