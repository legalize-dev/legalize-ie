# S.I. No. 177/1990 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 177 of 1990.

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS, 1990.

I, MICHAEL O'KENNEDY, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) (2) and [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0019.html#sec19) (1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1990.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), shall—

( *a* ) as regards milk, be 1.4 penny per gallon,

( *b* ) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, be £7.90 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 299 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0299.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 17 day of July, 1990.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Agriculture and Food.
