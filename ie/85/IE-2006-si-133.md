> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**S. I. No.133 of 2006**

**District Court (Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1997) Rules, 2006**

**[SI No.133 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0133.html)**

**District Court (Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1997) Rules 2006**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) and [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec24) of the [Interpretation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1997) Rules 2006.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 19^th day of April 2006 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. Order 99A of the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be amended:

- (i) by the substitution for rule 6 of the following:

  > “*6.(1) An application for an interim excluding order or interim site excluding order made under the terms of section 4 of the Act on the occasion of the making of an application for an excluding order or site excluding order or between the making of an application for an excluding order or site excluding order and its determination shall be grounded on the information on oath and in writing of the applicant in the Form 99A.5, Schedule C or on an affidavit sworn by the applicant incorporating the matter in that Form*.
  >
  > - *(2) The order of the Court shall be in the Form 99A.6, Schedule C*.
  >
  > - *(3) An application for confirmation under paragraph (d) of subsection (3) of section 4 of the Act of an interim excluding order or interim site excluding order made ex parte under the terms of subsection (3) of section 4 of the Act shall be preceded by the issue and service on the respondent by the applicant at least 2 days before the date fixed for such application of a notice in the Form 99A.14, Schedule C. The applicant shall also serve on the respondent the documents referred to in subparagraph (ii) of paragraph (c) of subsection (3) of section 4 of the Act*.
  >
  > - *(4) A copy of the notice of application together with a statutory declaration as to service shall be lodged with the Clerk before or at the sitting of the Court on the date for hearing the application*.
  >
  > - *(5) Where, on such application, the interim excluding order or interim site excluding order is confirmed, such confirmation shall be indorsed upon the order and signed by the Judge*.”

- (ii) by the insertion in sub-rule (1) of rule 7, immediately following paragraph (c), of the following:

  > - “*(d) subsection (5) of section 3 of the Act as applied by subsection (2) of section 4 and subsection (9) of section 3A of the Act to vary an interim excluding order*”.

- (iii) by the substitution for paragraph (b) of sub-rule (2) of rule 11 of the following:

  > - “*(b) Where the Court grants a stay on the operation of an excluding order or site excluding order under this rule, the order granting such stay shall be in the Form 99A.13, Schedule C. Where the Clerk has supplied the copies of the orders as directed by rule 9 of this Order, the Clerk shall also send a copy of the order granting the stay, to the persons referred to, and in the manner directed, by rule 9 of the Order*.”

###### 4.

4. The Forms numbered 99A.1 to 99A.14 inclusive in the Schedule hereto shall be added to the Forms in Schedule C to the District Court Rules 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ), in substitution for the Forms numbered 99A.1 to 99A.13 inclusive in the said Schedule C.
