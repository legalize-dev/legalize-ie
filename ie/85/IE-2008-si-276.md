PENSIONS ACT 1990 (REGISTRATION AND RENEWAL OF REGISTRATION OF ADMINISTRATORS) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd July*, 2008.

The Minister for Social and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on her by section 5(1) of the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and Commencement*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (Registration and Renewal of Registration of Administrators) Regulations 2008.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1st November 2008.

*Definitions*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act” means the [Pensions Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1990).

*Registration of Registered Administrator*

###### 3.

3. The form set out in Schedule A—

- (*a*) is prescribed for the purposes of section 64D of the Act,

- (*b*) may be adapted for filing and use electronically, and

- (*c*) shall only be used by a single applicant.

*Renewal of Registration of Registered Administrator*

###### 4.

4. The form set out in Schedule B—

- (*a*) is prescribed for the purposes of section 64E of the Act,

- (*b*) may be adapted for filing and use electronically, and

- (*c*) shall only be used by a single registered administrator.

**NOTES ON COMPLETION OF FORM OF APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION**

**GENERAL**

1. All sections of the form must be competed in full. “None” or “Not Applicable” should be entered where appropriate, but blank spaces or “to be advised” or similar responses may result in the application being rejected as incomplete.

2. Where there is insufficient space on the form a continuation sheet may be used as necessary, if adequately attached to the main form and bearing the name of the Applicant and date of application.

3. If the applicant is a body corporate, the form must be completed and signed by a duly authorised director, manager or officer of the applicant.

4. The application form is also available in electronic format from the website www.pensionsboard.ie.

###### 5.

5. This form should be submitted to: The Pensions Board, Registration Unit, Verschoyle House, 28/30 Lower Mount Street, Dublin 2 or electronically, subject to such requirements as the Board may stipulate.

**Note one:** The core administration functions that currently may be undertaken by a Registered Administrator are set out in section 64G(1)(*a*), (*b*) and (*c*) of the Act and are:

- (*a*) prepare on behalf of the trustees an annual report in the prescribed form and deliver it to the trustees not less than one month prior to the date by which the trustees are required by regulations under the Act to make the annual report available;

- (*b*) prepare on behalf of the trustees annual benefit statements for members and deliver them to the trustees not less than one month prior to the date by which the trustees are required by regulations under the Act to make such statements available;

- (*c*) keep accurate and sufficient records of members and of their entitlements to enable the registered administrator to discharge the duties specified in paragraphs (*a*) and (*b*).

**Note two:** Delete the inapplicable phrase as appropriate. Where the applicant is already in the business of providing “core administration functions” services to schemes or trust RACs prior to 1 November 2008 and intends to continue providing such services after 1 November 2008 then delete “intends to provide”. For new entrants to the registered administrator business post 1 November 2008 delete the word “provides” as the provision of such services is prohibited unless registration has been obtained.

**Note three:** The trustees of every scheme or trust RAC are required by section 55(1) of the Act and regulations to select a period for disclosure purposes. Insert in this column the end date of that period for each scheme and trust RAC.

**Note four:** This would for example require you, before taking on new business during the registration year, to be satisfied that you have appropriate systems and procedures in place and are competent to take on that new business.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations prescribe the content of—

- •the application form which must be completed and submitted to the Pensions Board by persons applying to be registered with the Board as a registered administrator; and

- •the renewal form which must be completed and submitted to the Pensions Board by a registered administrator on an annual basis.
