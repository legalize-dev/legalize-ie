# S.I. No. 512/2010 - Adoption Act 2010 (Establishment Day) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 5*th November*, 2010.

I, BARRY ANDREWS, Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html#sec6) of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010) and the Health and Children (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 508 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0508.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Adoption Act 2010 (Establishment Day) Order 2010.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of November 2010 is appointed as the establishment day for the purposes of the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) (No. 21 of 2010).

GIVEN under my hand,

1 November 2010.

BARRY ANDREWS,

Minister of State at the Department of Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal instrument)*

The effect of this Order is to provide that the [Adoption Act 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0021/print.html) establishment day shall be the 1st day of November 2010.
