# S.I. No. 289/2016 - Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th June*, 2016.

The National Treasury Management Agency, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1990) and the [National Treasury Management Agency Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Delegation of and Declaration as to Functions) Order, 1990 ( [S.I. No. 277 of 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/si/0277.html) ), hereby makes the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 5 June 2016.

###### 3.

3. The Post Office Savings Bank (Interest on Deposits) Regulations 2012 ( [S.I. No. 533 of 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/si/0533.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 4.

4. The rate of interest on a deposit within the meaning of [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) (1) of the [Finance Act, 1948](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1948/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) is hereby prescribed as 0.15 per cent per annum.

GIVEN under the Seal of the National Treasury Management Agency,

2 June 2016.

**ANDREW O’FLANAGAN,**

Authorised Officer.

**FRANK O’CONNOR,**

Authorised Officer.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe a new rate of interest for deposits in the Post Office Savings Bank.
