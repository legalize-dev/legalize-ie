# S.I. No. 238/1986 - Urban Renewal Act, 1986 (Designated Areas) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 238 of 1986.

URBAN RENEWAL ACT, 1986 (DESIGNATED AREAS) ORDER, 1986.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (No. 19 of 1986), hereby orders as follows—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (Designated Areas) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. Each of the areas described in Parts III, IV, V, VI and VII of the Fourth Schedule to the [Finance Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1986) is hereby declared to be a designated area for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment

this 2nd day of July, 1986.

**JOHN BOLAND,**

Minister for the Environment

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to this Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this

2nd day of July, 1986.

**JOHN BRUTON,**

Minister for Finance

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order declares the areas which, in addition to the Custom House Docks Area, are to be designated areas for the purposes of the [Urban Renewal Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) .
