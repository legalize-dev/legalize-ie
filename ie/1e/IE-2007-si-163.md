# S.I. No. 163/2007 - Diseases of Animals Act 1966 (Foot-and- Mouth Disease) (Regulation of Sheep Shearers) (Revocation) Order 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 163 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0163.html)

DISEASES OF ANIMALS ACT 1966 (FOOT-AND- MOUTH DISEASE) (REGULATION OF SHEEP SHEARERS) (REVOCATION) ORDER 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 27*th April*, 2007.

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 3 and 15 (as amended by the Disease of Animals (Amendment) Order 2001 (No. 3 of 2001)) of the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966)) as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 (S.I. No. of 2002), hereby make the following order-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Foot-and- Mouth Disease) (Regulation of Sheep Shearers) (Revocation) Order 2007.

###### 2.

2. The [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Foot-and- Mouth Disease) (Regulation of Sheep Shearers) Order 2001( [S.I. No. 248 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0248.html) ) is revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 April 2007

**MARY COUGHLAN**

Minister for Agriculture and Food

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

This Order revokes the [Diseases of Animals Act 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (Foot-and- Mouth Disease) (Regulation of Sheep Shearers) Order 2001
