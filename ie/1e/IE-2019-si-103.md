# S.I. No. 103/2019 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 5) (Jobseeker’s Transitional Payment) Regulations 2019

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd March,* 2019.

I, REGINA DOHERTY, Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html#sec4) (as adapted by the Social Protection (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2017 (No. 366 of 2017)) of, and Rule 1(5) of Part 2 of Schedule 3 to, the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby make the following Regulations:

***Citation and construction***

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 5) (Jobseeker’s Transitional Payment) Regulations 2019.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2019 shall be construed together as one and may be cited together as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 to 2019.

***Commencement***

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 28 March 2019.

***Calculation of means***

###### 3.

3. Article 145A(3)(d) (amended by article 3 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) (Amendment) (No. 3) (Jobseeker’s Transitional Payment) Regulations 2018 ( [S.I. No. 103 of 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/si/0103.html) )) of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Claims, Payments and Control) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 142 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0142.html) ) is amended by the substitution of “€150” for “€130”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

13 March 2019.

**REGINA DOHERTY,**

Minister for Employment Affairs and Social Protection.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide for an increase of €20, from €130 to €150, in the weekly earnings disregard in the case of recipients of the Jobseeker’s Transitional Payment in line with the increase provided for the One-Parent Family Payment in [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0037/print.html#sec22) of the [Social Welfare, Pensions and Civil Registration Act 2018](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2018/en/act/pub/0037/print.html) .

The increase in the weekly earnings disregard in the case of recipients of the Jobseeker’s Transitional Payment is effective from 28 March 2019.
