# S.I. No. 430/2010 - Companies (Fees) Order 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th September*, 2010.

I, BILLY KELLEHER, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Innovation, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 395 (amended by subsection (1) of [section 63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec63) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001)) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 185 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0185.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 3) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 334 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0334.html) ), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order 2010.

###### 2.

2. The Eighth Schedule to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) is altered by the insertion of the text set out in the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This is not a part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this Order is to prescribe the fees to be paid to the Companies Registration Office (CRO) by applications under sections 256F and 256G of the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) . These sections were inserted into the [Companies Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) Act by section 3(*j*) of the [Companies (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0045/print.html) .

An applicant under these sections would be seeking to migrate the registered office of a certain type of collective investment fund entity to or from Ireland from or to a prescribed jurisdiction without firstly having to wind up the entity in its original jurisdiction.
