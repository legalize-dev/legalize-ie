Made by the Minister for Finance.

S.I. 110 of 2006

**SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) ORDER, 2006**

I, Brian Cowen, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Section 48 (1) of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2001), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Order 2006.

###### 2.

2. The organisation specified in the Schedule to this Order is hereby designated as an approved organisation for the purpose of Part 10 of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) (No. 33 of 2001).

###### 3.

3. This Order is deemed to have come into effect on 16 July 2001.

## **SCHEDULE** The Commissioners of Irish Lights

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order designates The Commissioners of Irish Lights as an approved organisation for the purposes of Part 10 of the [Ministerial, Parliamentary and Judicial Offices and Oireachtas Members (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0033/print.html) .
