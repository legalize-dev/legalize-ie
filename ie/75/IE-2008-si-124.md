# S.I. No. 124/2008 - European Communities (Dehydrated Preserved Milk) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 124 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0124.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (DEHYDRATED PRESERVED MILK) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th May*, 2008.

I, **Mary Coughlan**, Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 2001/114/EC of 20 December 2001 [^1] , and Council Directive 2007/61/EC of 26 September 2007 [^2] , hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as European Communities (Dehydrated Preserved Milk) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Dehydrated Preserved Milk) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0298.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1), by substituting for the definition of “Directive”—

> “Directive” means Council Directive 2001/114/EC of 20 December 2001, and Council Directive 2007/61/EC of 26 September 2007.”

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 April 2008

**MARY COUGHLAN.**

Minister for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Dehydrated Preserved Milk) Regulations 2003. They give legal effect to Directive 2007/61/EC providing for the standardisation of protein content of partly or wholly dehydrated milk, and should be read in conjunction with the Directive.
