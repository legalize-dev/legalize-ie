# S.I. No. 383/2014 - Statistics (Income of Private Hospitals) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th August*, 2014.

I, ENDA KENNY, Taoiseach, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/sec0025.html#sec25) (1) of the [Statistics Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1993), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Statistics (Income of Private Hospitals) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. In this Order—

“health care” means services for or in connection with the prevention, diagnosis or treatment of human illness;

“medical provider” includes—

- (*a*) a registered medical practitioner within the meaning of the [Medical Practitioners Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2007),

- (*b*) a registered dentist within the meaning of the [Dentists Act 1985](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1985/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1985), and

- (*c*) a registered pharmacist within the meaning of the [Pharmacy Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0020/print.html) (No. 20 of 2007);

“private hospital” means a hospital (including an approved centre within the meaning of [section 63](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0025/print.html#sec63) of the [Mental Health Act](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0025/print.html) (No. 25 of 2001)) which—

- (*a*) provides health care, and

- (*b*) is not a publicly-funded hospital,

and includes a private hospital where arrangements have been or may be made for the provision of services for a public patient by agreement with the HSE;

“publicly-funded hospital” means a hospital which provides services to a person pursuant to his or her entitlement under Chapter II of Part IV of the [Health Act 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970).

###### 3.

3. This Order applies to a private hospital in the State.

###### 4.

4. A private hospital, to which this Order applies, shall provide the Central Statistics Office with the information in relation to its economic activities that is specified in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

Article 4

1. Detailed income and expenditure statement in accordance with a private hospital’s audited financial statements for the years 2012 and 2013.

2. The following particulars in accordance with a private hospital’s audited financial statements for the years 2012 and 2013—

- (*a*) income from public sources,

- (*b*) income from private health insurers,

- (*c*) income from patient contribution (classified by care type),

- (*d*) income from charitable sources,

- (*e*) income from private sources,

- (*f*) income from bequests,

- (*g*) income from medical providers for use of a private hospital’s facilities,

- (*h*) surplus of income over costs and expenditure, and

- (*i*) other sources of income.

GIVEN under my hand,

11 August 2014.

ENDA KENNY,

Taoiseach.
