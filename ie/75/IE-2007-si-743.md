# S.I. No. 743/2007 - Civil Registration (Fees and Allowances) (Revocation) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 743 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0743.html)

CIVIL REGISTRATION (FEES AND ALLOWANCES) (REVOCATION) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th November*, 2007.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 9 (as amended by the [Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2002)) of the [Vital Statistics and Births, Deaths and Marriages Registration Act 1952](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1952/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1952) and sections 3, 67 and 67A (inserted by the [Civil Registration (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2005)) of the [Civil Registration Act 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2004) and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Registration (Fees and Allowances) (Revocation) Regulations 2007.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 5 November 2007.

###### 2.

2. The Civil Registration (Fees and Allowances) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 768 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0768.html) ) are revoked.

I consent to the foregoing Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 November 2007

**BRIAN COWEN**

Minister for Finance.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 November 2007

**MARY HARNEY**

Minister for Health and Children.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to revoke the fees and allowances payable under the Civil Registration (Fees and Allowances) Regulations 2005 with effect from 5th November 2007
