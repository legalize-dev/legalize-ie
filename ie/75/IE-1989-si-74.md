# S.I. No. 74/1989 - Social Welfare (Modification of Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 74 of 1989.

SOCIAL WELFARE (MODIFICATION OF INSURANCE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1989

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) and [7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No.1 of 1981 ), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as Social Welfare (Modification of Insurance) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989.

###### 2.

2. Article 7 of the Social Welfare (Modification of Insurance) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 87 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0087.html) ) (as amended by the Social Welfare (Miscellaneous Provisions) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 65 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0065.html) )) is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-article (3) of "in sub-articles (1) (*a*) and (1) (*l*)" for "in sub-article (1) (*a*)".

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 6th day of April, 1989.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare this 5th day

of April, 1989.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the making of the foregoing regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 5th day

of April, 1989.

**ALBERT REYNOLDS,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide that cover for Occupational Injuries Insurance for members of the Garda Sióchana will be on a similar basis to that which applies in the case of civil servants at present.
