**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. No. 598 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0598.html)**

**MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 2006**

**MAINTENANCE ALLOWANCES (INCREASED PAYMENT) REGULATIONS, 2006**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers vested in her by Section 5 and Section 44 (sub-section 5) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order (S.I. 308 of 1997), hereby makes the following regulations:-

**Citation**

###### 1.

**1.** These regulations may be cited as the Maintenance Allowances (Increased Payment) Regulations, 2006.

**Rate of Payment**

###### 2.

**2.** Where a person is or becomes entitled to payment under sub-section (3) of [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) in accordance with the Infectious Diseases (Maintenance Allowances) Regulations, 2005 ( [S.I. No 825 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0825.html) ), any amount so payable in respect of the week commencing

4th December, 2006 to or in respect of himself or his dependants shall be increased by a sum equal to 100 per cent of that amount or €30.00 whichever is the greater.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.

The effect of these Regulations is to increase, in respect of the week commencing 4th December, 2006, the maximum weekly rate of maintenance allowance payable under [Section 44](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0044.html#sec44) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) by a sum equal to one hundred per cent of that amount or €30.00, whichever is the greater.
