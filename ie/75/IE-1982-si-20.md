S.I. No. 20 of 1982.

POUNDS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, JIM MITCHELL, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/sec0008.html#sec8) of the [Pounds (Provision and Maintenance) Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1935), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. The Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1980, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1982.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. The Pounds Regulations, 1938 ( [S. R. & O. No. 149 of 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/si/0149.html) ), shall have effect, in relation to chattels and animals impounded on or after the 15th day of February, 1982, as if the following Table were substituted for the Table (inserted by the Pounds (Amendment) Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 215 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0215.html) )) in the Second Schedule:

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to increase with effect as on and from the 15th February, 1982 the poundkeepers' fees prescribed in the Pounds Regulations, 1938 to 1980.
