# S.I. No. 833/2005 - Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and use of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 (Revocation) Regulations 2005

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS**

**[S.I. NO. 833 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0833.html)**

**ROAD TRAFFIC (CONSTRUCTION, EQUIPMENT AND USE OF VEHICLES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 1993 (REVOCATION) REGULATIONS 2005**

I, Martin Cullen, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) and the National Roads and Road Traffic (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0298.html) ) (as adapted by the Public Enterprise (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2002 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0305.html) )) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 (Revocation) Regulations 2005.

###### 2.

2. The Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) (Amendment) Regulations 1993 ( [S.I. No. 299 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0299.html) ) are revoked.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 19 DECEMBER 2005. |
|  | MARTIN CULLEN MINISTER FOR TRANSPORT |

**Explanatory Note**

(This Note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)

These Regulations revoke the Road Traffic (Construction, Equipment and Use of Vehicles) (Amendment Regulations 1993 ( [S.I. No. 299 of 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/si/0299.html) ).
