# S.I. No. 243/2026 - Competition Act 2002 (Section 27) Order 2026

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th June,* 2026.

I, PETER BURKE, Minister for Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 27](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec27) (1) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002), having regard to, and only to, such economic data as I consider to be relevant to the purpose, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Competition Act 2002 (Section 27) Order 2026.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on 1 July 2026.

###### 3.

3. [Section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec18) (1)(a) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2002) is amended -

- (a) in subparagraph (i), by the substitution of “€100,000,000” for “€60,000,000”, and

- (b) in subparagraph (ii), by the substitution of “€15,000,000” for “€10,000,000”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 June, 2026.

**PETER BURKE,**

Minister for Enterprise, Tourism and Employment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*.)

This Ministerial Order amends [section 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec18) (1) of the [Competition Act 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (as amended) by altering the monetary amounts standing relating to the obligation to notify certain mergers and acquisitions.
