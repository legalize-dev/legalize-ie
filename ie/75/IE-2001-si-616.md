# S.I. No. 616/2001 - Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) (No. 1) (Euro) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Social, Community and Family Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/sec0023.html#sec23) (as amended by [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0004/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Social Welfare Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1989)) of the [Housing (Private Rented Dwellings) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1982) and subsections (2) and (3) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:

**Citation and construction.**

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) (Amendment) (No.1) (Euro) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations and the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1998 to 2001 shall be construed together as one and may be cited as the Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1998 to 2001.

**Commencement.**

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 3 January 2002.

**Amendments consequential on the introduction of the euro.**

###### 3.

3. The Social Welfare (Rent Allowance) Regulations, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 188 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0188.html) ) are amended by the substitution in article 8(5) for “£13.20” of “EUR 16.80”.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

Regulations currently provide for the disregard of the qualified child rate of £13.20 in the assessment of means in relation to the Rent Allowance scheme for people affected by the decontrol of rents.

These Regulations provide for the conversion of this punt amount to a convenient Euro amount in line with the conversion to the new Euro currency from the beginning of January 2002.
