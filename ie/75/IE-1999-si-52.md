# S.I. No. 52/1999 - Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980, (Section 13(4)) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN O'DONOGHUE, Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (4) (inserted by [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0002/sec0001.html#sec1) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1989) of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment), Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1980) as adapted by the Justice (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 298 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0298.html) ), hereby make the following regulations with respect to which, pursuant to that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980, (Section 13(4)) Regulations, 1999.

###### 2.

2. The period of 15 years from the passing of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1989), is hereby specified for the purposes of subsection (4) (inserted by section 1 of the said Act) of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1980).

|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 24th day of February, 1999. |
|  | JOHN O'DONOGHUE, |
|  | Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform |

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations extend the period of operation of the provisions of [section 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/sec0013.html#sec13) (3) of the [Landlord and Tenant (Amendment) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , by five years.
