# S.I. No. 187/2015 - Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 (Living City Initiative) (Special Regeneration Area) (Waterford) Order 2015

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th May*, 2015.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 372AAA of the [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1997), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 (Living City Initiative) (Special Regeneration Area) (Waterford) Order 2015.

###### 2.

2. The area in Waterford indicated by red lines on the map attached to this order, is specified as a special regeneration area for the purposes of Chapter 13 of Part 10 of the Act.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

5 May 2015.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Finance.
