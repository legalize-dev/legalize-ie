**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*Part I*—Provides for increases in the existing scale of reduced rates of disability benefit and unemployment benefit payable where the contribution condition for receipt of the maximum rate of benefit is not satisfied. The new rates are payable from 23rd July, 1990 for disability benefit and from 19th July, 1990 for unemployment benefit.

*Part II*—Provides for increased benefit rates, under the occupational injuries scheme:

—in the amounts of disablement gratuities appropriate to degrees of disablement assessed at 19% or under,

—in respect of disablement pension in lieu of such gratuities,

—in respect of injury benefit payable to persons under the age of sixteen,

—in respect of certain constant attendance allowances.

The new rates are payable from 23rd July, 1990.

*Part III*— Provides for increases in the existing scales of reduced rates of old age (contributory) pensions, widows' (contributory) pensions, retirement pensions and deserted wife's benefit where the contribution conditions for receipt of the maximum rates are not satisfied. These increases take effect from 27th July, 1990 in the case of old age contributory and widows' contributory pensions and from 26th July, 1990 in the case of retirement pensions and deserted wife's benefit.

*Part IV*— Provides for an increase from 27th July, 1990 in the reduced rates of old age (contributory) pension payable to certain persons who came back into full rate insurance on the abolition of the earnings limit for liability for social insurance contributions in April, 1974 but who did not have a sufficient record of contributions to entitle them to a pension. Increases are also payable from 27th July, 1990 to widows who qualify for contributory pension on this basis and similarly to deserted wives from 26th July, 1990.
