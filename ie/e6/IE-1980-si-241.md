# S.I. No. 241/1980 - Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1980

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 241 of 1980.

MEDICAL PREPARATIONS (AMENDMENT OF FEES) REGULATIONS, 1980.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) and [65](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0065.html#sec65) of the [Health Act, 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947), as amended by [section 39](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/sec0039.html#sec39) of the [Health Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1953), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Medical Preparations (Amendment of Fees) Regulations, 1980.

###### 2.

2. Article 8 of the Medical Preparations (Licensing of Manufacture) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 225 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0225.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£300" for "£200".

###### 3.

3. Article 8 of the Medical Preparations (Wholesale Licences) Regulations, 1974 ( [S.I. No. 333 of 1974](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1974/en/si/0333.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of "£100" for "£50".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 23rd day of July, 1980.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to articles 2 and 3 of these Regulations.

Dated this 23rd day of July, 1980.

**MICHAEL O'KENNEDY,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for increases in the fees charged for licences granted in respect of the manufacture and sale by wholesale of medical preparations.
