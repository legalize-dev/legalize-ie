# S.I. No. 26/2014 - Hairdressing Joint Labour Committee Establishment (Amendment) Order 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 31*st January*, 2014.

WHEREAS I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation, being in receipt of a copy of a recommendation under subsection (5) of section 41A (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html#sec11) of the [Industrial Relations (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2012)) of the [Industrial Relations Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1946) and being satisfied that subsection (3) of section 41A has been complied with and considering it appropriate to do so:

NOW, I, RICHARD BRUTON, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (6) of section 41A (inserted by [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html#sec11) of the [Industrial Relations (Amendment) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0032/print.html) (No. 32 of 2012)) of the [Industrial Relations Act 1946](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1946/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1946) (as adapted by the Enterprise, Trade and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 245 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0245.html) )) hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Hairdressing Joint Labour Committee Establishment (Amendment) Order 2014.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the Hairdressing Joint Labour Committee Establishment Order 2009 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0045.html) ) is amended by—

- (*a*) substituting the following paragraph for paragraph 1:

  > “1.Hairdressers employed in any undertaking where hairdressers are employed in Dublin, Dún Laoghaire, Bray or Cork.”, and

- (*b*) in paragraph 2:

    - (i) deleting the definition of “hairdressing undertaking”;

    - (ii) inserting the following definition:

      > “‘hairdresser’ means a worker who performs hairdressing and includes an apprentice or learner hairdresser.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

28 January 2014.

**RICHARD BRUTON,**

Minister for Jobs, Enterprise and Innovation.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of this instrument is to amend the Hairdressing Joint Labour Committee Establishment Order 2009 ( [S.I. No. 45 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0045.html) .)
