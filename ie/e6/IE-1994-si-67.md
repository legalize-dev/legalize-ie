# S.I. No. 67/1994 - Land Act, 1965 (Additional Category of Qualified Person) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 67 of 1994.

LAND ACT, 1965 (ADDITIONAL CATEGORY OF QUALIFIED PERSON) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Forestry, in exercise of the power conferred on my by section 45 (1) (x) of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965), and the Lands (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 28 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0028.html) ), hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (Additional Category of Qualified Person) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. For the purpose of the definition of a "qualified person" in [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1965), the following category is hereby declared to be an additional category, namely, a person acquiring an interest in land which is situate within the counties of Dublin South, Fingal and Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown as specified in the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1993).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 28th day of March, 1994.

**JOE WALSH,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and

Forestry.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations exclude from the provisions of [section 45](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/sec0045.html#sec45) of the [Land Act, 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) , the "Counties" of Dublin South, Fingal and Dún Laoghaire-Rathdown as specified in the [Local Government (Dublin) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) , the boundaries of these having been fixed by the said Act.
