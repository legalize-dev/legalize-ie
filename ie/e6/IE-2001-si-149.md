# S.I. No. 149/2001 - Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Section 29A(4)) (No. 2) Order, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/sec0003.html#sec3) and 29A(4) (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec3) of the [Diseases of Animals (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2001)) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) (No. 6 of 1966) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), and consider it reasonably necessary to avoid the outbreak of disease within the State, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals Act, 1966 (Section 29A(4))(No. 2) Order, 2001 and shall come into operation on 14 April, 2001.

###### 2.

2. Subsection (3) of section 29A (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0003/print.html#sec3) of the [Diseases of Animals (Amendment) Act, 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0003/print.html) (No. 3 of 2001) of the [Diseases of Animals Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0006/index.html) , shall have effect for the period of two months from 14 April, 2001 within the whole of the State.

**Explanatory Note**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of this Order is to prohibit for a period of 2 months from the 14^th April, 2001 the movement of animals within 30 days of purchase save under permit.
