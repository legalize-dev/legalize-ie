# S.I. No. 344/2013 - Education Act 1998 (Section 42) (Designation of Organisations) Order 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 13*th September*, 2013.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Education and Skills, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/sec0042.html#sec42) of the [Education Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1998) (as adapted by the Education and Science (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2010 ( [S.I. No. 184 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0184.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Education Act 1998 (Section 42) (Designation of Organisations) Order 2013.

###### 2.

2. Each of the following organisations is designated as an organisation for the purposes of [section 42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/sec0042.html#sec42) of the [Education Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0051/index.html) (No. 51 of 1998):

- (*a*) An Chomhairle Ealaíon; and

- (*b*) The organisation known as Educate Together/Ag Foghlaim Le Chéile.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

3 September 2013.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Education and Skills.
