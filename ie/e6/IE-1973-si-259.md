# S.I. No. 259/1973 - Housing Act, 1970 (Supplementary Grants) Regulations, 1973

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 259 of 1973.

HOUSING ACT, 1970 (SUPPLEMENTARY GRANTS) REGULATIONS, 1973.

The Minister for Local Government in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and section 26 of the said Act as amended by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0018/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Housing Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1970) with the consent of the Minister for Finance hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Housing Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (Supplementary Grants) Regulations, 1973.

###### 2.

2. The appropriate limit for the purposes of paragraph (*b*) of [section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0026.html#sec26) (1) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) shall be—

one thousand five hundred pounds per annum in respect of a house the erection of which was commenced within the period beginning on 1st July, 1972, and ending on 24th May, 1973,

one thousand seven hundred pounds per annum in respect of a house for which an application for a supplementary grant was received on or after 25th May, 1973.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Local Government this 3rd day of September, 1973.

**JAMES TULLY,**

Minister for Local Government.

The Minister for Finance hereby consents to the foregoing Regulations.

Dated this 3rd day of September, 1973.

**RICHIE RYAN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations lay down the qualifyng income limits of eligibility for supplementary new house grants payable by housing authorities under [Section 26](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0026.html#sec26) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) as amended by [Section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0018/sec0010.html#sec10) of the [Housing Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) .

The Regulations provide that for the purposes of determination by a housing authority of the income of an applicant for a new house supplementary grant, the qualifying income limit for eligibility for such a grant shall not exceed £1,500 in respect of houses the erection of which commenced between 1st July, 1972 and 24th May, 1973 and shall not exceed £1,700 in respect of applications for supplementary grants received on or after 25th May, 1973.
