# S.I. No. 673/2006 - Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 673 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0673.html)

BOVINE DISEASES (LEVIES) REGULATIONS 2006

I, MARY COUGHLAN, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 2(2) and 19(1) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979), (as adapted by the Agriculture, Food and Rural Development (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 306 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0306.html) )), hereby make the following Regulations with respect to which, pursuant to section 2(5) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 2006.

These Regulations come into operation on the 1st day of January 2007.

###### 2.

2. The prescribed amount of levy payable under [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Bovine Diseases (Levies) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1979) shall be-

- (*a*) as regards milk, 0.06cent per litre and

- (*b*) as regards animals slaughtered or exported live, €1.27 per animal.

###### 3.

3. The Bovine Diseases (Levies) Regulations, 2004 ( [S.I. No 838 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0838.html) ) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations, which come into effect on 1st January 2007 prescribe new rates of disease levies on milk for processing and on bovine animals slaughtered or exported live.
