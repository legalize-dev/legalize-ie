# S.I. No. 257/1986 - Companies (Amendment) Act, 1986 (Commencement) Order, 1986

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 257 of 1986.

COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1986 (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 1986.

I, MICHAEL NOONAN, Minister for Industry and Commerce, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsections (4) and (5) of section 25 of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1986), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , (Commencement) Order, 1986.

###### 2.

2. Subject to paragraph 3 of this Order, the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1986) ( other than section 7 (1) (*a*) (ii)) shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1986.

###### 3.

3. Each provision of the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (other than section 7 (1) (*a*) (ii) and subsections (7) and (8) of section 8), shall apply as respects the matters specified in section 25 (5) (*a*) of that Act for each financial year of a company beginning after the 31st day of December, 1986.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 18th day of July 1986.

**MICHAEL NOONAN,**

Minister for Industry and Commerce.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of this Order is to bring into effect the [Companies (Amendment) Act, 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , which provides for new requirements in relation to the preparation, publication and auditing of the accounts of public and private limited companies.
