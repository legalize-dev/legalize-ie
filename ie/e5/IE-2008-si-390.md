# S.I. No. 390/2008 - Merchant Shipping Fees (Amendment) Order 2008

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 390 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0390.html)

MERCHANT SHIPPING FEES (AMENDMENT) ORDER 2008

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd October*, 2008.

I, NOEL DEMPSEY, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0002/sec0031.html#sec31) of the [Merchant Shipping Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1992), and the Maritime Transport, Safety and Security (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order 2005 ( [S.I. No. 842 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0842.html) ), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Merchant Shipping Fees (Amendment) Order 2008.

###### 2.

2. Part 1 of the First Schedule to the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 475 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0475.html) ) is amended in paragraph 1.8 by inserting, “where the renewal survey relates to a certificate for a period of more than 30 days,” after “provided that”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

26 September 2008

**NOEL DEMPSEY**

Minister for Transport.

The Minister for Finance consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 September 2008

**BRIAN LENIHAN**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order amends the Merchant Shipping (Fees) Order 1998 ( [S.I. No. 475 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0475.html) ) relating to the minimum fee for the grant of a passenger certificate for up to 30 days duration.
