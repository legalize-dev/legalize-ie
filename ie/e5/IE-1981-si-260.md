# S.I. No. 260/1981 - Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOHN KELLY, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931), and [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939), as adapted by the External Affairs (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1971 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/si/0158.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations, 1981.

###### 2.

2. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 to 1980, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations, 1956 to 1981.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1981.

###### 4.

4. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 5.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to alter as indicated in the Regulations fees chargeable for services rendered under the [Commissioners for Oaths (Diplomatic and Consular) Act, 1931](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1931/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1931) and the [Diplomatic and Consular Fees Act, 1939](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1939/en/act/pub/0031/index.html) (No. 31 of 1939).
