# S.I. No. 382/1982 - Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935 (Part II) (No. 9) Regulations, 1982

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 382 of 1982.

PIGS AND BACON ACT, 1935 (PART II) (NO. 9) REGULATIONS, 1982.

I, AUSTIN DEASY, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 8](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0008.html#sec8) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (inserted by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/sec0005.html#sec5) (1) of the [Agricultural Produce (Meat) (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1978)) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), and the Agriculture and Fisheries (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1977 ( [S.I. No. 31 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0031.html) ), hereby make the following regulations, with respect to which, pursuant to subsection (7) of the said section 28, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 9) Regulations, 1982.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 1983.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to these Regulations.

###### 4.

4. For the purposes of [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/sec0028.html#sec28) (1) of the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1935), the prescribed sum shall be 72p.

###### 5.

5. The [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Part II) (No. 8) Regulations, 1982 ( [S.I. No. 153 of 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/si/0153.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of December, 1982.

**AUSTIN DEASY,**

Minister for Agriculture.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations increase as from 1 January, 1983 the fees payable by bacon curers for veterinary examination of pigs in accordance with the [Pigs and Bacon Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) .
