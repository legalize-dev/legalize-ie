# S.I. No. 80/2000 - National Salmon Commission (Establishment) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Frank Fahey, Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 55A (inserted by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html#sec22) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) (No. 35 of 1999)) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1980), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the National Salmon Commission (Establishment) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. There shall stand established a committee to be known as the National Salmon Commission.

###### 3.

3. The bodies and organisations specified in the Schedule to this Order are prescribed for the purposes of section 55A(2)(b) (inserted by [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html#sec22) of the [Fisheries (Amendment) Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0035/print.html) ) of the [Fisheries Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) .

Explanatory Note

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the establishment of the National Salmon Commission and prescribes those bodies and organisations who will have representation on the Commission.
