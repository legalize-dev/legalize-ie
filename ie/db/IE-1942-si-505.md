# S.I. No. 505/1942 - Central Bank Act, 1942 (Appointed Day) Order, 1942

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS. 1942. No. 505.

CENTRAL BANK ACT, 1942 (APPOINTED DAY) ORDER, 1942.

WHEREAS it is enacted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942), that the Minister for Finance shall by order appoint a day to be the appointed day for the purposes of the said Act :

NOW, I, SEÁN T. Ó CEALLAIGH, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942), and of every and any other power me in this behalf enabling, hereby order as follows :—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Appointed Day) Order, 1942.

###### 2.

2. The 1st day of February, 1943, is hereby appointed to be the appointed day for the purposes of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1942).

Dated this 2nd day of December, 1942.

- (Signed) SEÁN T. Ó CEALLAIGH,

Minister for Finance.
