# S.I. No. 321/2009 - Ethics In Public office (Designated Positions In Public Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 321 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0321.html)

ETHICS IN PUBLIC OFFICE (DESIGNATED POSITIONS IN PUBLIC BODIES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th August*, 2009.

I, BRIAN LENIHAN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0022/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Ethics in Public Office Act 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1995) hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

###### 2.

2. The Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 ( [S.I. No. 606 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0606.html) ) are amended by substituting the following for paragraph 2:

> “2. The Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 698 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0698.html) ) are amended by substituting for the matter in the Schedule the matter in the Schedule to these Regulations.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

24 July 2009.

**BRIAN LENIHAN,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations correct a grammatical error in [S.I. No. 606 of 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/si/0606.html) (Ethics in Public Office (Designated Positions in Public Bodies) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008.
