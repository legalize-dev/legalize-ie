S.I. No. 188 of 1989.

SOCIAL WELFARE (AMENDMENT OF MISCELLANEOUS SOCIAL INSURANCE PROVISIONS) REGULATIONS, 1989.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0003.html#sec3) , [19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0019.html#sec19) , [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0030.html#sec30) , [42](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0042.html#sec42) , [43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0043.html#sec43) , [46](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0046.html#sec46) , [79](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0079.html#sec79) , [84](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0084.html#sec84) , [93](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0093.html#sec93) and [101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/sec0101.html#sec101) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1981 ) hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Amendment of Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1989.

PART I ALTERATION IN REDUCED RATES OF DISABILITY BENEFIT AND UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFIT

###### 2.

2. This Part shall come into operation on the 24th day of July, 1989 for Disability Benefit and on the 20th day of July, 1989 for Unemployment Benefit.

###### 3.

3. The following Schedule shall be substituted for the First Schedule to the Social Welfare (Disability, Unemployment and Marriage Benefit) Regulations, 1953 ( [S.I. No. 7 of 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/si/0007.html) ).

PART II INCREASED RATES OF BENEFITS AND PENSIONS UNDER THE OCCUPATIONAL INJURIES SCHEME

###### 4.

4. This Part shall come into operation on the 24th day of July, 1989 and shall be construed as one with the Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Regulations, 1967 to 1983, and the Social Welfare (Amendment of Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1984 to 1988.

###### 5.

5. In this Part:—

"the Principal Regulations" means the Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) Regulations, 1967 ( [S.I. No. 77 of 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/si/0077.html) );

"the Regulations of 1988" means the Social Welfare (Amendment of Miscellaneous Social Insurance Provisions) Regulations, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 179 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0179.html) ).

###### 6.

6. The Principal Regulations are hereby amended—

- (1) by the substitution for the Second Schedule thereto (inserted by the Regulations of 1988) of the Schedule set out in Schedule A to this Part, subject in respect of the Scale set out in the said Second Schedule, as amended by these Regulations, to the provisions as follows—

( *a* ) the rates of pension specified in column (3) of the Scale shall have effect on and from the 24th day of July, 1989 whether the period taken into account by the assessment began on, before or after that date, and,

( *b* ) the amounts of gratuity specified in column (2) of the Scale shall be payable only where the period taken into account by the assessment of the degree of disablement began on, or after the 24th day of July, 1989.

- (2) by the substitution for the Fourth Schedule thereto (added to the said Regulations by the Social Welfare (Occupational Injuries) (Amendment) Regulations, 1981 ( [S.I. No. 340 of 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/si/0340.html) ) and as inserted by the Regulations of 1988) of the Schedule set out in Schedule B to this Part.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*Part I*—Provides for increases in the existing scale of reduced rates of disability benefit and unemployment benefit payable where the contribution condition for receipt of the maximum rate of benefit is not satisfied. The new rates are payable from 24th July, 1989 for disability benefit and from 20th July, 1989 for unemployment benefit.

*Part II*—Provides for increased benefit rates, under the occupational injuries scheme,

— in the amounts of disablement gratuities appropriate to degrees of disablement assessed at 19% or under,

— in respect of disablement pension in lieu of such gratuities,

— in respect of injury benefit payable to persons under the age of sixteen,

— in respect of certain constant attendance allowance.

The new rates are payable from 24th July, 1989.

*Part III*—Provides for increases in the existing scales of reduced rates of old age contributory pensions, widows' contributory pensions, retirement pensions and deserted wife's benefit where the contribution conditions for receipt of the maximum rates are not satisfied. These increases take effect from 28th July, 1989 in the case of old age contributory and widows' contributory pensions and from 27th July, 1989 in the case of retirement pensions and deserted wife's benefit.

*Part IV*—Provides for an increase from 28th July, 1989 in the reduced rates of old age (contributory) pension payable to certain persons who came back into full rate insurance on the abolition of the earnings limit for liability for social insurance contributions in April, 1974 but who did not have a sufficient record of contributions to entitle them to a pension. Increases are also payable from 28th July, 1989 to widows who qualify for contributory pension on this basis and similarly to deserted wives from 27th July, 1989.
