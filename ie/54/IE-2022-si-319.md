# S.I. No. 319/2022 - Forestry (Amendment) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 1*st July,* 2022.

I, Charlie McConalogue, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec6) , [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec22) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html#sec30) of the [Forestry Act 2014](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2014/en/act/pub/0031/print.html) (No. 31 of 2014) and for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2009/147/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 November 2009[^1] and Council Directive 92/43/EEC of 21 May 1992[^2] in so far as those Directives relate to forestry related development, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Forestry (Amendment) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. The Forestry Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0191.html) ) are amended In Regulation 8 (3), by-

- (a) deleting subparagraph (d), and

- (b) substituting for subparagraph (f) the following:

  > - “(f) 20 metres of -
  >
  >     - (i) a monument or place recorded under [section 12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/sec0012.html#sec12) of the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1994),
  >
  >     - (ii) a historic monument or archaeological area entered in the Register of Historic Monuments under [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0017/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1987), or
  >
  >     - (iii) a national monument in the ownership or guardianship of the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage under the National Monuments Acts 1930 to 2014.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

29 June, 2022.

**CHARLIE MCCONALOGUE,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.
