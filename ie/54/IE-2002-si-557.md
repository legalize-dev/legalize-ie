# S.I. No. 557/2002 - Companies (Fees) Order 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Mary Harney, Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 395](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0395.html#sec395) (as amended by the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) (No. 28 of 2001) and [396](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0396.html#sec396) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Companies (Fees) Order 2002.

###### 2.

2. This order comes into operation on 5^th December 2002.

###### 3.

3. In this Order the “Eighth Schedule” means the Eighth Schedule (substituted by the Companies (Fees) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 477 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0477.html) ) and amended by the Companies (Fees) (No. 2) Order 2001 ( [S.I. No. 569 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0569.html) )) to the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1963).

###### 4.

4. Part I of the Eighth Schedule is altered:

- (a) by deleting -

  > “
  >
  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | For registration of an annual return that is received by the registrar within 77 days after the date to which it is made up | €30 |
  >
  > ”,

  and

- (b) by substituting -

  > “
  >
  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | For registration of an annual return made up to a date prior to 1 March 2002 that is not received by the registrar within 77 days after the date to which it is made up (“the 77 day period”) | €130 plus €3.00 per day for each day on which the failure to file the return continues after the 77 day period, subject to a maximum fee of €1,230 for each return |
  > | For registration of an annual return made up to a date after 28 February 2002 that is received by the registrar within the period specified in [section 127](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0127.html#sec127) (2) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (substituted by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec60) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ) | €30 |
  > | For registration of an annual return made up to a date after 28 February 2002 that is not received by the registrar within the period specified in section 127(2) of [the Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (substituted by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec60) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ) (“the statutory filing period”) | €130 plus €3.00 per day for each day on which the failure to file the return continues after the statutory filing period, subject to a maximum fee of €1,230 for each return |
  >
  > for -
  >
  > “

  >

  > **EXPLANATORY NOTE**
  >
  > *(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*
  >
  > This Order amends the Companies (Fees) Order, 2001 ( [SI No 477 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0477.html) ) as amended
  >
  > The Order provides for -
  >
  > - • Application of the late filing penalty having regard to the annual return filing deadline imposed on companies pursuant to [section 127](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/sec0127.html#sec127) of the [Companies Act 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (substituted by [section 60](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html#sec60) of the [Company Law Enforcement Act 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/act/pub/0028/print.html) ).
