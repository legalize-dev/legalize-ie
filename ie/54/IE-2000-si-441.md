# S.I. No. 441/2000 - Building Regulations (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for the Environment and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Sections 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0003.html#sec3) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Building Control Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990), hereby makes the following Regulations—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Building Regulations (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations, 2000.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of January 2001.

###### 3.

3. The Building Regulations, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 497 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0497.html) ) are hereby amended by the substitution of the following for Class 9, Column 1, Article 8 of the Third Schedule (Exempted Buildings):

> “A building constructed for and used by the Electricity Supply Board as a generating, transmission or distribution station, or constructed for and used by a transmission system operator within the meaning of Council Directive 96/92/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19th December 1996[^1] concerning common rules for the internal market in electricity, as a transmission station, or constructed by the holder of an authorisation under [section 16](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec16) of the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) , and used by the holder of a licence under section 14(1)(a) of the [Electricity Regulation Act, 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) as a generating station”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations amend the Building Regulations, 1997, so as to extend the Class 9 building exemption from the Electricity Supply Board to other licensed Electricity Supply Utilities. The Regulations will come into operation on the 1st January 2001.
