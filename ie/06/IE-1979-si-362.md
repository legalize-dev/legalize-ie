# S.I. No. 362/1979 - Trustee Savings Banks (Interest on Deposits in Special Account) Regulations, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 362 of 1979.

TRUSTEE SAVINGS BANKS (INTEREST ON DEPOSITS IN SPECIAL ACCOUNT) REGULATIONS, 1979.

I, SEOIRSE Ó COLLA, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/sec0009.html#sec9) (2) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0037/index.html) (No. 37 of 1968), hereby make the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Trustee Savings Banks (Interest on Deposits in Special Account) Regulations, 1979.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 21st day of November, 1979.

###### 3.

3. The rate of interest on moneys paid by the trustees of every trustee savings bank into the special account referred to in [section 31](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/sec0031.html#sec31) (3) of the [Finance Act, 1940](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1940/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1940), is hereby prescribed as 9.15 per cent.

###### 4.

4. The Trustee Savings Banks (Interest on Deposits in Special Account) Regulations, 1978 ( [S.I. No. 322 of 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/si/0322.html) ), are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under my Official Seal this 9th day of November, 1979.

**SEOIRSE Ó COLLA,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These regulations prescribe the rate of interest payable on moneys paid by the trustees of every trust savings bank into the special account in the name of the Minister for Finance as 9.15 per cent per annum as from 21 November, 1979.
