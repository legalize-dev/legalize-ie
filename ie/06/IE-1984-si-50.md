# S.I. No. 50/1984 - Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1984

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 50 of 1984.

HOUSING REGULATIONS, 1980 (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1984.

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Housing Act, 1966](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1966/en/act/pub/0021/index.html) (No. 21 of 1966) and by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) and [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/sec0018.html#sec18) of the [Housing (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1979), hereby makes the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Housing Regulations, 1980 (Amendment) Regulations, 1984.

###### 2.

2. Articles 10 and 78 of the Housing Regulations, 1980 ( [S.I. No. 296 of 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/si/0296.html) ) are hereby revoked.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of March, 1984.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 22nd day of February, 1984.

**LIAM KAVANAGH,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The Regulations terminate the application of the Certificate of Reasonable Value system to housing grants and house purchase loans from lending agencies. The system will continue to have effect in relation to applications for tax relief under [sections 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0023.html#sec23) and [24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/sec0024.html#sec24) of the [Finance Act, 1981](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1981/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) and [sections 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/sec0029.html#sec29) and [30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/sec0030.html#sec30) of the [Finance Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) .
