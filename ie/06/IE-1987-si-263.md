# S.I. No. 263/1987 - Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations, 1987

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 263 of 1987.

MISUSE OF DRUGS (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1987.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0004.html#sec4) , [5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0005.html#sec5) and [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Regulations, 1987.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 2 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0032.html) ) is hereby amended by the insertion, after the word "Bezitramide", of the word "Buprenorphine".

###### 3.

3. Schedule 4 to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations, 1979 is hereby amended by the deletion in sub-paragraph (*b*) of paragraph 3 of the word "dihydrocodeine", and by the insertion after paragraph 3 of the following paragraph:—

> "3A. Any preparation of dihydrocodeine (not being a preparation designed for administration by injection) containing, per dosage unit, not more than 10 milligrammes of dihydrocodeine calculated as base and which in the case of an undivided preparation has a concentration of not more than 1.5 per cent of dihyrocodeine calculated as base."

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 16th day of October, 1987.

**RORY O'HANLON,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations amend the Schedule to the Misuse of Drugs Regulations, 1979 for the purpose of

- (1) applying Schedule 2 type controls to the substance buprenorphine,

- (2) reducing the concentration levels of dihydrocodeine in preparations above which Schedule 2 type controls apply and below which Schedule 4 type controls apply.
