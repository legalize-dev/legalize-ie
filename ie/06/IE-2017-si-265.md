# S.I. No. 265/2017 - European Communities (Marine Strategy Framework) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th June*, 2017.

I, SIMON COVENEY, Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Directive 2008/56/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 June 2008[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Marine Strategy Framework)(Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Marine Strategy Framework) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 249 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0249.html) ) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 5(6) by substituting “A plan of action referred to in paragraph (5)” for “Subject to paragraph (7), a plan of action referred to in paragraph (5)”,

- (*b*) by substituting the following for Regulation 5(7):

  > - “(7) Where a plan of action is devised in accordance with paragraphs (5) and (6), the Minister shall, in accordance with Article 5(3) of Directive 2008/56/EC, inform the Commission of the revised timetable, as provided for in that Article and invite the Commission to consider the supportive action referred to in that Article.”,

  and

- (*c*) in Regulation 17(2)(*a*) by substituting “Regulation 9” for “Regulation 10”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 June 2017.

**SIMON CCOVENEY,**

Minister for Housing, Planning, Community and Local Government.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations provide further for the transposition into Irish Law of the Marine Strategy Framework Directive, giving further effect to Directive 2008/56/EC of 17 June 2008, and amends Statutory Instrument No. 249 of 2011.
