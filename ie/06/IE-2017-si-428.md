# S.I. No. 428/2017 - Health Act 2007 (Care and Welfare of Residents in Designated Centres for Older People) (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th October*, 2017.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 101](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0023/print.html#sec101) of the [Health Act 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) (No. 23 of 2007) (as adapted by the Health and Children (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0219.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 2007 (Care and Welfare of Residents in Designated Centres for Older People) (Amendment) Regulations 2017 and shall come into operation on the 2nd day of October 2017.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 2 of the Health Act 2007 (Care and Welfare of Residents in Designated Centres for Older People) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 415 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0415.html) ) is amended by the substitution of the following definition for the definition of “designated centre”:

> “ ‘designated centre’ means a designated centre for older people to which paragraph (*a*)(iii) or paragraph (*c*) of the definition of ‘designated centre’ (amended by section 97 of, and Item I of Part 20 of Schedule 2 to, the [Child and Family Agency Act 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/act/pub/0040/print.html) (No. 40 of 2013)) in section 2 of the Act applies;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 October 2017.

**SIMON HARRIS,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This is a technical amendment to the Health Act 2007 (Care and Welfare of Residents in Designated Centres for Older People) Regulations 2013.
