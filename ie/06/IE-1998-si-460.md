S.I. No. 460 of 1998.

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY ACT, 1992 (ESTABLISHED ACTIVITIES) ORDER, 1998

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Minister for the Environment and Local Government by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0007.html#sec7) and [82](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/sec0082.html#sec82) (2) of the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1992), which said powers are delegated to me by the Environment and Local Government (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 428 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0428.html) ), I, Dan Wallace, Minister for State at the Department of the Environment and Local Government, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (Established Activities) Order, 1998.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 8th day of December, 1998.

###### 3.

3. In this Order—

any reference to a Schedule or article which is not otherwise identified is a reference to the Schedule or an article of this Order;

"the Act" means the [Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) .

###### 4.

4. Subject to articles 5 and 6, the date specified in column (3) of the Schedule opposite a class of established activity identified in column (2)

- (a) shall be the specified date for such activity for the purposes of section 82(2) of the Act,

- (b) shall be the date on, or after which, such activity shall not be carried on unless a licence or revised licence under Part IV of the Act is in force, and

- (c) shall apply to such activity and to any other established activity which is associated with such activity and on, at or adjacent to the site.

###### 5.

5. Notwithstanding article 4, and without prejudice to section 92 of the Act, the date specified in relation to a class of established activity shall be the date on which an application for a licence is made under Part IV of the Act in relation to any activity on, at, or adjacent to the site of the established activity and associated with the established activity, where such date of application is earlier than the date specified.

###### 6.

6. Notwithstanding article 4 and without prejudice to section 92 of the Act, the date specified in relation to a class of established activity shall be—

- (a) (i) the date (being a date on or after the commencement of this Order) on which an application for a licence or revised licence or permit or revised permit under any of the enactments to which section 99(1) of the Act relates is made to a local authority, sanitary authority or the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources, or

    - (ii) the date (being a date on or after the commencement of this Order) on which a review of a licence or revised licence or permit or revised permit under any of the enactments to which section 99(1) of the Act relates is commenced by the local authority, sanitary authority or the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources

where such date is earlier than the relevant date specified, or

- (b) the 8th day of December, 1998, where, on that date, there is an application for a licence or revised licence, or permit or revised permit, or a review of a licence or revised licence or permit or revised permit, under any of the enactments to which section 99(1) of the Act relates, and the application or review has yet to be determined by the local authority, sanitary authority or the Minister for the Marine and Natural Resources.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

This Order, which should be read in conjunction with section 82(3) of the 1992 Act, specifies dates on or after which the established activities concerned are required to have applied to the Environmental Protection Agency for an integrated pollution control licence.
