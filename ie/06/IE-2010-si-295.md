# S.I. No. 295/2010 - Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (Rent Supplement) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 22*nd June*, 2010.

The Minister for Social Protection, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 4 and 198 (as amended by [section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0043/print.html#sec14) of the [Social Welfare and Pensions (No. 2) Act 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/act/pub/0043/print.html) (No. 43 of 2009)) of the [Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0026/print.html) (No. 26 of 2005), hereby makes the following Regulations:

*Citation and construction.*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (Rent Supplement) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations shall be construed as one with the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ) and shall be included in the collective citation “the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 to 2010”.

*Commencement.*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 16 June 2010.

*Definitions.*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations the “Principal Regulations” mean the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ).

*Maximum amount of rent in respect of which a supplement is payable.*

###### 4.

4. The Principal Regulations are amended in paragraph (i) of article 9(2) (as amended by the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) (Amendment) (Rent Supplement) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 202 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0202.html) )) by substituting the following for sub-paragraph (iii):

> - “(iii) the rent payable by the claimant is just and proper having regard to the nature, character and location of the residence,
>
>   and for this purpose, the appropriate maximum amount of rent in respect of which a supplement is payable for the period commencing on 16 June 2010 and ending on 31 December 2011, shall be—
>
>     - (I) the monthly amount set out in Schedule 1 to the Regulations in respect of such class or classes of persons referred to in row (1) of the said Schedule, and
>
>     - (II) the amount as determined by the Minister in respect of any other class or classes of persons,
>
>       having regard to the family circumstances and the location of the residence of such persons, and”

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations provide for a reduction of the levels of maximum rent until 31st December 2011, as set out in Schedule 1 of the Social Welfare (Consolidated Supplementary Welfare Allowance) Regulations 2007 ( [S.I. No. 412 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0412.html) ), in respect of which rent supplement is paid.

These measures come into operation on 16 June 2010.
