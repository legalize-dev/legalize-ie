# S.I. No. 922/2005 - Dormant Accounts (Amendment) Act 2005 (Establishment Day) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, ÉAMON Ó CUÍV, Minister for Community, Rural and Gaeltacht Affairs, in exercise of the power conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0008/print.html#sec2) of the [Dormant Accounts (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2005), hereby order as follows:

Citation.

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Dormant Accounts (Amendment) Act 2005 (Establishment Day) Order 2006.

Establishment day.

###### 2.

2. The 4th day of January, 2006 is appointed to be the establishment day for the purposes of the [Dormant Accounts (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) (No. 8 of 2005).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The [Dormant Accounts (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) provides for changes in relation to decision-making on disbursements from the Dormant Accounts Fund and for the establishment of a reconstituted Board (the Dormant Accounts Board).

This Order provides that the Dormant Accounts Board is established on 4th day of January, 2006 as per the provisions of [Section 30](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) of the [Dormant Accounts (Amendment) Act 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/act/pub/0008/print.html) .

The functions of the Dormant Accounts Board are to prepare a Disbursement Plan for the disbursement of moneys from the Dormant Accounts Fund and to review and assess the impact and additionality of spending from the Dormant Accounts Fund.
