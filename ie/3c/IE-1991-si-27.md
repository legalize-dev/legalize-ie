# S.I. No. 27/1991 - Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces), 1991

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 27 of 1991.

RULES OF PROCEDURE (DEFENCE FORCES), 1991.

I, BRENDAN DALY, Minister for Defence in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 240](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/sec0240.html#sec240) of the [Defence Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) (No. 18 of 1954), hereby make the following rules:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Rules may be cited as Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces), 1991.

- (2) The Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) 1954 to 1987, and these Rules may be cited together as the Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces), 1954 to 1991.

###### 2.

2. The Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces), 1954 ( [S.I. No. 243 of 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/si/0243.html) ), are hereby amended—

- (*a*) by the substitution in Rule 60 (1) (inserted by the Rules of Procedure (No. 3) (Defence Forces), 1983 ( [S.I. No. 202 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0202.html) )), for "death" of "imprisonment for life", and

- (*b*) by the substitution in Rule 61 (1) (inserted by the Rules of Procedure (No. 3) (Defence Force), 1983)), for "death" of "imprisonment for life".

###### 3.

3. Rule 3 (1) of the Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces), 1983 ( [S.I. No. 22 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0022.html) ), is hereby amended by the substitution for "a capital charge or a charge of murder" of "a charge of an offence for which a person would be required on conviction to be sentenced to imprisonment for life".

###### 4.

4. The Rules of Procedure (No. 2) (Defence Forces), 1983 ( [S.I. No. 72 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0072.html) ), are hereby amended—

- (*a*) by the substitution in Rule 4 (1) for "a capital charge or a charge of murder" of "a charge of an offence for which a person would be required on conviction to be sentenced to imprisonment for life",

- (*b*) by the substitution in Rule 8 (1) for "a capital charge or a charge of murder" of "a charge for an offence for which a person would be required on conviction to be sentenced to imprisonment for life", and

- (*c*) by the substitution in Rule 10 for "a capital charge of murder" of "a charge of an offence for which a person would be required on conviction to be sentenced to imprisonment for life".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 7th day of February, 1991.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Rules amend the Rules of Procedure (Defence Forces) 1954 to 1987 consequent on the amendment of the [Defence Act, 1954](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1954/en/act/pub/0018/index.html) by the [Criminal Justice Act, 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) .
