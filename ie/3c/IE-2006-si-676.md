# S.I. No. 676/2006 - National Beef Assurance Scheme Act, 2000 (Approval) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 676 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0676.html)

NATIONAL BEEF ASSURANCE SCHEME ACT, 2000 (APPROVAL) ORDER 2006

I, Mary Coughlan, Minister for Agriculture and Food, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 1(5) and 8(1) of the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (No. 2 of 2000), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [National Beef Assurance Scheme Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0002/print.html) (Approval) Order, 2006.

###### 2.

2. The period for participants to apply for the grant of a certificate of approval under section 8 of the Act is extended until 31 December 2008.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|  | GIVEN under my Official Seal, 20 December 2006 |
|  | Mary Coughlan |
|  | Minister for Agriculture and Food |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

The effect of this Order is to extend the period for participants to apply for approval under the National Beef Assurance Scheme.
