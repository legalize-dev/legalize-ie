# S.I. No. 29/1985 - Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 29 of 1985.

MISUSE OF DRUGS (LICENCE FEES) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1985.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0014.html#sec14) and [38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/sec0038.html#sec38) of the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1977), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) (Amendment) Regulations, 1985.

###### 2.

2. Sub-article (1) of article 4 of the Misuse of Drugs (Licence Fees) Regulations, 1979 ( [S.I. No. 164 of 1979](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1979/en/si/0164.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution in sub-paragraph (*a*) of "£60" for "£30", in sub-paragraph (*b*) of "£40" for "£20", in sub-paragraph (*c*) of "£40 for "£20", in sub-paragraph (*d*) of "£20" for "£10", in sub-paragraph (*e*) of "£10" for "£5", and in sub-paragraph (*f*) of "£20" for "£10".

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of March, 1985.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 11th day

of February, 1985.

**BARRY DESMOND,**

Minister for Health.

The Minister for Finance consents to these Regulations.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance this 11th day

of February, 1985.

**ALAN M. DUKES,**

Minister for Finance.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for increases in the fees payable to the Minister for Health in respect of certain licences granted under the [Misuse of Drugs Act, 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) .
