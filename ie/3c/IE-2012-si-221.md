# S.I. No. 221/2012 - Waste Management (Landfill Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 29*th June*, 2012.

I, PHIL HOGAN, Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/sec0007.html#sec7) , [18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/sec0018.html#sec18) (3) and [73](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) of the [Waste Management Act 1996](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1996/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1996), hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Waste Management (Landfill Levy) (Amendment) Regulations 2012.

*Commencement*

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 July 2012.

*Interpretation*

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations, save where the context otherwise requires—

“Regulations of 2011” means the Waste Management (Landfill Levy) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 434 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0434.html) ).

*Amendment of Regulation 3 of Regulations of 2011*

###### 4.

4. Regulation 3(3) of the Regulations of 2011 is amended by deleting—

> ““end-of-life vehicle” has the meaning assigned to it by article 4(3) of the Waste Management (End-of-Life Vehicles) Regulations 2006 ( [S.I. No. 282 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0282.html) );”

*Amendment of Regulation 4 of Regulations of 2011*

###### 5.

5. (1) Regulation 4(2)(*a*) of the Regulations of 2011 is amended by the substitution of “€65” for “€50”.

- (2) Regulation 4(2)(*b*) of the Regulations of 2011 is amended by the substitution of “€65” for “€50”.

*Amendment of Regulation 6 of Regulations of 2011*

###### 6.

6. Regulation 6(1) of the Regulations of 2011 is amended by deleting subparagraph (*h*).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

25 June 2012.

**PHIL HOGAN,**

Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations increase the landfill levy for waste disposed of at authorised and unauthorised landfill facilities from €50 per tonne to €65 per tonne with effect from 1 July 2012.

The Regulations also remove the exemption from the levy for non-metallic residues arising from the shredding of end-of-life vehicles, white goods and other metal waste.
