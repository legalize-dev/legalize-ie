# S.I. No. 375/2016 - European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2016

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 19*th July*, 2016.

I, MICHAEL CREED, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2015/1955 of 29 October 2015[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Cereal Seed) (Amendment) Regulations 2016.

###### 2.

2. The European Union (Cereal Seed) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0217.html) ) are amended in Regulation 2(1) by substituting the definition of “Directive” for the following-

> “‘Directive’ means Council Directive 66/402/EEC of 14 June 1966[^2] as amended by Council Directive 69/60/EEC of 18 February 1969[^3] , Council Directive 71/162/EEC of 30 March 1971[^4] , Council Directive 72/274/EEC of 20 July 1972[^5] , Council Directive 72/418/EEC of 6 December 1972[^6] , Council Directive 73/438/EEC of 11 December 1973[^7] , Council Directive 75/444/EEC of 26 June 1975[^8] , Council Directive 78/55/EEC of 19 December 1977[^9] , Council Directive 78/692/EEC of 25 July 1978[^10] , Commission Directive 79/641/EEC of 27 June 1979[^11] , Council Directive 79/692/EEC of 24 July 1979[^12] , Council Directive 86/155/EEC of 22 April 1986[^13] , Commission Directive 86/320/EEC of 20 June 1986[^14] , Commission Directive 87/120/EEC of 14 January 1987[^15] , Council Directive 88/380/EEC of 13 June 1988[^16] , Council Directive 90/654/EEC of 4 December 1990[^17] , Council Directive 96/72/EC of 18 November 1996[^18] , Council Directive 98/95/EC of 14 December 1998[^19] , Council Directive 98/96/EC of 14 December 1998[^20] , Commission Directive 1999/54/EC of 26 May 1999[^21] , Council Directive 2001/64/EC of 31 August 2001[^22] , Council Directive 2003/61/EC of 18 June 2003[^23] , Council Directive 2004/117/EC of 22 December 2004[^24] , Commission Directive 2009/74/EC of 26 June 2009[^25] , Commission Implementing Directive 2012/1/EU of 6 January 2012[^26] , Commission Implementing Directive 2012/37/EU of 22 November 2012[^27] , Commission Implementing Decision 2014/150/EU of 18 March 2014[^28] and Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2015/1955 of 29 October 2015;”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

30 June 2016.

**MICHAEL CREED,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Regulations give effect to the provisions of Commission Implementing Directive (EU) 2015/1955 of 29 October 2015 which amends Annexes I and II to Council Directive 66/402/EEC on the marketing of cereal seed.
