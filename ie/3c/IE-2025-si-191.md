# S.I. No. 191/2025 - Noxious Weed Order 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 16*th May,* 2025.

WHEREAS I am satisfied that the plants of the species and varieties mentioned in Article 2 of the following Order are noxious weeds;

NOW, I, Martin Heydon, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 2](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0038/sec0002.html#sec2) of the [Noxious Weeds Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1936) (as adapted by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 455 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0455.html) )), order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Noxious Weeds Order 2025 and comes into operation on 14 May 2025.

###### 2.

2. Blackgrass (*Alopecurus myosuroides* Huds.), Broad-Leaved Dock (*Rumex obtusifolius* L.), Common Ragwort (*Jacobaea vulgaris* Gaertn.), Creeping Thistle (*Cirsium arvense* (L.) Scop.), Curled Dock (*Rumex crispus* L.), Spear Thistle (*Cirsium vulgare* (Savi) Ten.), Spring Wild Oat (*Avena fatua* L*.*) and Winter Wild Oat (*Avena sterilis* L.) are declared to be noxious weeds.

###### 3.

3. The area specified for this Order is the whole of the State.

###### 4.

4. The following are revoked:

- (a) the Noxious Weeds (Thistle, Ragwort and Dock) Order 1937 ( [S.I. No.103 of 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/si/0103.html) ),

- (b) the Noxious Weeds (Common Barberry) Order 1958. [S.I. No. 120 of 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/si/0120.html) ),

- (c) the Noxious Weeds (Male Wild Hop Plant) Order 1965 ( [S.I. No.189 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0189.html) ), and

- (d) the Noxious Weeds (Wild Oat) Order 1973 ( [S.I. No. 194 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0194.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

8 May, 2025.

**MARTIN HEYDON,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The Order declares that the plants mentioned in Article 2 are noxious weeds.
