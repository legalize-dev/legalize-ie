# S.I. No. 242/2007 - European Communities (Infant formulae and Follow-on formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

[S.I. No. 242 of 2007](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2007/en/si/0242.html)

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (INFANT FORMULAE AND FOLLOW-ON FORMULAE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2007

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th May*, 2007.

I, MARY HARNEY, Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving effect to specific provisions relating to infant formulae and follow-on formulae of Commission Directive 2006/82/EC [^1] of 23 October 2006 adapting Directive 91/321/EEC [^2] on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive 1999/21/EC [^3] on dietary foods for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-On Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2007.

- (2) The Principal Regulations and these Regulations may be cited together as the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-On Formulae) Regulations 2004 and 2007.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-On Formulae) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0242.html) ).

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 2(1), by inserting after the definition of “Authority”, the following definition:

  > “‘Commission Directive 2006/82/EC’ means Commission Directive 2006/82/EC 9A of 23 October 2006 adapting Directive 91/321/EEC 5 on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive 1999/21/EC 9B on dietary foods for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania;”,

and by the addition of the following footnotes thereto:

9A OJ L 362, 20.12.2006, p. 94

9B OJ L 91, 7.4.1999, p. 29,

- (*b*) in Regulation 3, by inserting the following paragraph after paragraph (6):

  > - “(7) The provisions of this Part are without prejudice to Article 7 of the Directive as amended by Commission Directive 2006/82/EC.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

22 May 2007

**MARY HARNEY**

Minister for Health and Children

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation).*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-On Formulae) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 242 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0242.html) ). These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Infant Formulae and Follow-On Formulae) (Amendment) Regulations 2007.

These Regulations give partial effect to Commission Directive 2006/82/EC of 23 October 2006 adapting Directive 91/321/EEC on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive 1999/21/EC on dietary foods for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania.
