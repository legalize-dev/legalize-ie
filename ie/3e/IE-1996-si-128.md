# S.I. No. 128/1996 - Consumer Credit Act, 1995 (Section 36) Regulations, 1996

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 128 of 1996.

CONSUMER CREDIT ACT, 1995 (SECTION 36) REGULATIONS, 1996.

I, RICHARD BRUTON, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/sec0036.html#sec36) of the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 36) Regulations, 1996.

###### 2.

2. The forms of notice set out in Parts I and II in the Schedule to these Regulations shall be in lieu of the form of notice set out in Part 1 of the Third Schedule to the [Consumer Credit Act, 1995](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1995/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1995).
