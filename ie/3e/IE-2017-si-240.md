# S.I. No. 240/2017 - Communications (Mobile Telephone Roaming) Regulations 2013 (Amendment) Regulations 2017

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th June*, 2017.

I, DENIS NAUGHTEN, Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving full effect to Article 7(5) of Regulation (EU) 2015/2120[^1] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2015, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Communications (Mobile Telephone Roaming) Regulations 2013 (Amendment) Regulations 2017.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations come into operation on 15 June 2017.

###### 3.

3. In these Regulations “Principal Regulations” means the Communications (Mobile Telephone Roaming) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 228 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0228.html) ).

###### 4.

4. Regulation 2 of the Principal Regulations is amended by substituting for the definition of “Mobile Phone Roaming Regulation” the following:

> “ ‘Mobile Phone Roaming Regulation’ means Regulation (EU) 531/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2012[^2] as amended by Regulation (EU) 2015/2120 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2015[^3] ”.

###### 5.

5. Regulation 7 of the Principal Regulations is amended—

- (*a*) by substituting for clause (i) of paragraph (1)(a) the following:

  > - “(i) its obligations under Article 3, 4, 5, 6a, 6e, 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15 or 16, or”,

  and

- (*b*) by substituting for clause (i) of paragraph (4)(a) the following:

  > - “(i) its obligations under Article 3, 4, 5, 6a, 6e, 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15 or 16, or”.

GIVEN under my official Seal,

1 June 2017.

**DENIS NAUGHTEN,**

Minister for Communications, Climate Action and Environment.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to give effect to article 7(5) of Regulation (EU) No 2015/2120 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2015, which amends Regulation (EU) No 531/2012 on roaming on public telephone networks within the European Union.
