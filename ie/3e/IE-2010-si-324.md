# S.I. No. 324/2010 - Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2010

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th July*, 2010.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Communications, Energy and Natural Resources, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 70 and 80 of the [Inland Fisheries Act, 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/act/pub/0010/print.html) (No. 10 of 2010) hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation and commencement*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Inland Fisheries (Fixed Charge Notice) Regulations 2010.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 July 2010.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 2010” means Inland Fisheries Act 2010 (No. 10 of 2010).

###### 3.

3. It is declared that section 70 of the Act of 2010 applies in respect of a contravention of Regulation 3, 4, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22(2), 22(3), 22(4), 22(5), 22(6), 22(7) or 22(8) of the Wild Salmon and Sea Trout Tagging Scheme Regulations 2010 ( [S.I. No. 323 of 2010](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2010/en/si/0323.html) ).

###### 4.

4. The Bye-laws mentioned in Schedule 1 are prescribed for the purposes of section 80(1)(*b*) and (*c*) of the Act of 2010.

###### 5.

5. The form set out in Schedule 2 is prescribed for the purposes of sections 70(2) and 80(1) of the Act of 2010.

###### 6.

6. The prescribed amount of a fixed charge is—

- (*a*) in respect of a fixed charge notice under section 70 — €130, and

- (*b*) in respect of a fixed charge notice under section 80 — €90.

###### 7.

7. The Inland Fisheries (Fixed Payment) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 560 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0560.html) ) are revoked.

## SCHEDULE 1

## Part 1

Tagging of Nets and Numbering of Boats for Salmon or Trout Fishing Bye-law No. 583, 1976.

Prohibition on the Use of Live Fish as Bait in Fresh Water Bye-law No. 592, 1977.

Control of Fishing for Coarse Fish in Fresh Water Bye-law No. 595, 1977.

Conservation of Pike Bye-law No. 809, 2006.

Conservation and Prohibition on the Sale of Coarse Fish Bye-law No. 806, 2006.

## Part 2

Bye-laws prohibiting the taking, killing or having in possession a fish taken by rod and line and hooked other than in the mouth.

Bye-laws prohibiting or restricting trolling and or the use of more than a specified number of rods by a person at any one time.

Bye-laws prohibiting the taking of salmon and sea trout by means of any net or rod and line in specified waters.

Bye-laws prohibiting angling with rod and line in specified waters.

Bye-laws prohibiting the taking of salmon and sea trout by means of rod and line, prohibiting the use of any fish hooks other than single barbless hooks and a prohibition on the use of worms as bait in angling in specified fishery districts.

Bye-laws conserving the taking of salmon or trout with draft nets.

Bye-laws prohibiting the use of drift nets, snap nets or other fishing engines.

Bye-laws prohibiting the use of specified bait.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These regulations provide for a system of on-the-spot fines in the area of inland fisheries to be administered by Inland Fisheries Ireland, in order to cater for new fisheries legislation. The form of notice of an on-the-spot fine is set out in the Schedule to the Regulations.
