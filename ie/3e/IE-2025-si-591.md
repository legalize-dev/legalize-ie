# S.I. No. 591/2025 - European Communities (Marketing of Honey) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th December,* 2025.

I, MARTIN HEYDON, Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purpose of giving effect to Directive 2024/1438 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Marketing of Honey) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Marketing of Honey) Regulations 2003 ( [S.I. No. 367 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0367.html) ) are amended-

- (a) in Regulation 2(1) by substituting for the definition of “Council Directive” (as amended by the European Communities (Marketing of Honey) (Amendment) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 261 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0261.html) )) the following-

  > “‘Council Directive’ means Council Directive 2001/110/EC of 20 December 2001[^2] as amended by Directive 2014/63/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014[^3] and Directive 2024/1438/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024;”,

- (b) in Regulation 3, by substituting for paragraph (2) the following–

  > - “(2) A person shall not place on the market a product to which these Regulations apply unless that product meets the requirements set out in Annex II to the Council Directive.”, and

- (c) in Regulation 4, by inserting after paragraph (3) (inserted by the European Communities (Marketing of Honey) (Amendment) Regulations 2015) the following-

  > - “(4) A person may place honey produced or labelled before 14 June 2026 on the market in accordance with Article 6 of Directive 2024/1438/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024 until stocks are exhausted.”.

###### 3.

3. These Regulations shall be deemed to be food legislation for the purposes of the [Food Safety Authority of Ireland Act 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1998).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 December, 2025.

**MARTIN HEYDON,**

Minister for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations amend [S.I. No. 367 of 2003](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2003/en/si/0367.html) (Marketing of Honey Regulations) and give effect to Directive 2024/1438.
