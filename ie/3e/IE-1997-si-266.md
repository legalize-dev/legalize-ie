# S.I. No. 266/1997 - Local Government (Equalisation) Fund (County Clare) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 266 of 1997.

LOCAL GOVERNMENT (EQUALISATION) FUND (COUNTY CLARE) ORDER, 1997

The Minister for the Environment, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/sec0005.html#sec5) (2) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) (No. 29 of 1997), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Local Government (Equalisation) Fund (County Clare) Order, 1997.

###### 2.

2. The percentage specified for the purposes of section 5 (2) (a) of the [Local Government (Financial Provisions) Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0029/index.html) in relation to the Council of the County of Clare shall be 45 per cent.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment,

this 19th day of June, 1997.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for the Environment.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order specifies that Clare County Council shall pay 45 per cent of the car tax collected by it into the Local Government (Equalisation) Fund.
