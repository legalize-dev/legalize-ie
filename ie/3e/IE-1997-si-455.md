# S.I. No. 455/1997 - Presidential Establishment Act, 1938 (Section 1 (3)) Order, 1997

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 455 of 1997.

PRESIDENTIAL ESTABLISHMENT ACT, 1938 (SECTION 1 (3)) ORDER, 1997

The Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 1 (3) (inserted by the [Presidential Establishment (Amendment) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1991)) of the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1938), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (Section 1(3)) Order, 1997.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 11th day of November, 1997.

###### 2.

2. Paragraph (*a*) (inserted by the [Presidential Establishment (Amendment) Act, 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (No. 10 of 1991)) of [section 1](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/sec0001.html#sec1) (1) of the [Presidential Establishment Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1938), is hereby amended by the substitution of "£120,000" for "£100,000".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the

Government, this 4th day of November, 1997.

**BERTIE AHERN**

Taoiseach

Explanatory Note

This Order provides for an increase in the annual allowance paid to the President from £100,000 per annum to £120,000 per annum, with effect from 11 November 1997.
