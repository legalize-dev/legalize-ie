# S.I. No. 242/1976 - Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) (No. 2) Order, 1975 (Revocation) Order, 1976

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 242 of 1976.

AGRICULTURAL WAGES (MINIMUM RATES) (No. 2) ORDER, 1975 (REVOCATION) ORDER, 1976.

The Agricultural Wages Board, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [Section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0053/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Agricultural Wages Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0053/index.html) (No. 53 of 1936) and after due compliance with subsection (7) of the said Section 17, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) (No. 2) Order, 1975 (Revocation) Order, 1976.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 22nd day of November, 1976.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. The Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) (No. 2) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 257 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0257.html) ) is hereby revoked.

Sealed with the Common Seal of the Agricultural Wages Board this

15th day of October, 1976.

The Seal of the Board was affixed in the presence of—

SEÁN LEE,

Chairman of the Board.

EXPLANATORY NOTE.

The effect of this Order is to revoke the Agricultural Wages (Minimum Rates) (No. 2) Order, 1975 ( [S.I. No. 257 of 1975](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1975/en/si/0257.html) ).
