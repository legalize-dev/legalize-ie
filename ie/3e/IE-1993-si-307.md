# S.I. No. 307/1993 - European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 307 of 1993.

EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES (INDICATION OF PRICES OF FOODSTUFFS AND NON-FOOD PRODUCTS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1993.

I, RUAIRÍ QUINN, Minister for Enterprise and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act, 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive No. 79/581/EEC of 19 June, 1979^(a), as amended by Council Directive No. 88/315/EEC of 7 June, 1988^(b), and to Council Directive No. 88/314/EEC of 7 June, 1988^(c), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) (Amendment) Regulations, 1993.

- (2) These Regulations and the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 329 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0329.html) ), may be cited together as the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991 and 1993.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991, are hereby amended by the insertion of the following Regulation after Regulation 10:

> "10A. Any written or printed advertisement or catalogue which mentions the selling price of foodstuffs or non-food products shall indicate the unit price.".

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 22nd day of October, 1993.

**RUAIRÍ QUINN,**

Minister for Enterprise

and Employment.

^(a) O.J. No. L158, 26.6.1979, p. 19.

^(b) O.J. No. L142, 9.6.1988, p. 23.

^(c) O.J. No. L142, 9.6.1988, p. 19.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations have been made to give further effect to Council Directive No. 79/581/EEC as amended by Council Directive No. 88/315/EEC on consumer protection in the indication of the prices of foodstuffs, and Council Directive No. 88/314/EEC on consumer protection in the indication of the prices of non-food products.

The Regulations, which come into effect on 22 October, 1993, amend the European Communities (Indication of Prices of Foodstuffs and Non-Food Products) Regulations, 1991, and require the indication of the unit price of foodstuffs and non-food products, as defined by those Regulations, in written or printed advertisements and catalogues.
