# S.I. No. 466/2012 - Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulations, 2012

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 30*th November*, 2012.

Bord na gCon, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by subsection (2) of [section 40](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/sec0040.html#sec40) of the [Greyhound Industry Act 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1958), and with the consent of the Minister of State for Agriculture Food and the Marine, hereby makes the following regulations, that is to say:

- (1) The Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulations, 2008 (No. 518 of 2008) are hereby revoked.

- (2) The percentage deduction on all totalisator pools operated by the Board shall be not less than 10% and not greater than 30% of the monies staked on each pool.

- (3) These regulations may be cited as the Greyhound Race Track (Totalisator) (Percentage Deduction) Regulations, 2012.

- (4) These regulations shall come into operation on 14th day of November 2012.

GIVEN under the Seal of Bord na gCon this

13 November 2012.

**SIGNED: PHILIP MEANEY,**

Chairman of the Board.

**SIGNED: ADRIAN NEILAN,**

Chief Executive Officer of the Board.

An Officer authorised by the Board to authenticate the Seal of the Board

I hereby consent to the above Regulations.

6 November 2012.

**SIGNED: SHANE MCENTEE,**

Minister of State for Agriculture, Food and the Marine.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal Interpretation)*

The effect of these Regulations is to establish the maximum and minimum amount which the Board may retain out of monies staked on totalisator pools operated by the Board.
