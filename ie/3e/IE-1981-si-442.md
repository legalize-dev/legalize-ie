# S.I. No. 442/1981 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in County Health District of Kildare) Order, 1981

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on her by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 ( [S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/si/0058.html) ), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in County Health District of Kildare) Order, 1981.

###### 2.

2. [The Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1st day of March, 1982 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the County Health District of Kildare unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1st day of March, 1982 the sale of milk in the County Health District of Kildare unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
