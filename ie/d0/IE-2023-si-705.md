# S.I. No. 705/2023 - Workplace Relations Act 2015 (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2023

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 12*th January,* 2024.

I, NEALE RICHMOND, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) and [36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec36) of the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2015) (as adapted by the Business, Enterprise and Innovation (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 519 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0519.html) )) and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order 2023 ( [S.I. No. 14 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0014.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Workplace Relations Act 2015 (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2023.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations -

“Act of 2015” means the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2015);

“Act of 1991” means the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (No. 25 of 1991).

###### 3.

3. The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015.

###### 4.

4. (1) The amount of €2,000 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) (No. 7 of 1977).

- (2) The amount of €1,500 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under any of the following:

    - (a) subsection (4) of section 4 of the Act of 1991;

    - (b) [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec23) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) (No. 5 of 2000);

    - (c) section 6B of the [Terms of Employment (Information) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1994).

- (3) The amount of €750 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under any of the following:

    - (a) subsection (8) of section 4B of the Act of 1991;

    - (b) subsection (2) of section 4D of the Act of 1991.

- (4) The amount of €500 is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of section 36 of the Act of 2015 in relation to an offence alleged to have been committed under any of the following:

    - (a) subsection (3) of section 4E of the Act of 1991;

    - (b) subsection (3) of section 4F of the Act of 1991.

###### 5.

5. The following regulations are revoked:

- (a) the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 419 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0419.html) );

- (b) the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0032.html) ).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

[Section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec36) (1) of the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) provides that, where an inspector has reasonable grounds for believing that a person has committed a relevant offence, the inspector may give to the person a Fixed Payment Notice in the prescribed form for each of these offences.

These Regulations prescribe the form of Fixed Payment Notices in lieu of prosecution, if appropriate, and the amount of the fixed payment in each case in relation to alleged offences under [Section 36](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec36) (1) of the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) and prescribe the amount of the fixed payment notice in each case in relation to alleged offences under subsection (4) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) , subsection (8) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) B, subsection (2) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) D, subsection (3) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) E and subsection (3) of [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/sec0004.html#sec4) F of the [Payment of Wages Act 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/act/pub/0025/index.html) (as inserted by the [Payment of Wages (Amendment) (Tips and Gratuities) Act 2022](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2022/en/act/pub/0023/print.html) ), section 6B of the [Terms of Employment (Information) Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) , [section 11](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/sec0011.html#sec11) of the [Protection of Employment Act 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/act/pub/0007/index.html) , and [section 23](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html#sec23) of the [National Minimum Wage Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0005/print.html) .

The [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2015 ( [S.I. No. 419 of 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/si/0419.html) and the [Workplace Relations Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (Fixed Payment Notice) Regulations 2017 ( [S.I. No. 32 of 2017](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2017/en/si/0032.html) ) are revoked.
