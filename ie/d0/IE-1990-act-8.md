# HORSE BREEDING ACT, 1990

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO REPEAL THE HORSE BREEDING ACT, 1934. [15*th May*, 1990]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:

###### 1 Repeal of Horse Breeding Act, 1934.

**1.**—The Horse Breeding Act, 1934, is hereby repealed.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Horse Breeding Act, 1990.
