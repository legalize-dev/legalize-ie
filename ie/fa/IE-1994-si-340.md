# S.I. No. 340/1994 - National Monuments (Prescription of Form) (No. 2) Regulations, 1994

S.I. No. 340 of 1994.

NATIONAL MONUMENTS (PRESCRIPTION OF FORM) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS, 1994.

I, MICHAEL D. HIGGINS, T.D., Minister for Arts, Culture and the Gaeltacht, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 23 (7) (as inserted by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [National Monuments (Amendment) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0017/index.html) (No. 17 of 1994)) of the [National Monuments Act, 1930](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1930/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1930), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the National Monuments (Prescription of Form) (No. 2) Regulations, 1994.

###### 2.

2. The form to be furnished under subsection (4) of the said section 23 to a person making a report of the finding of an archaeological object shall be in the form set out in the Schedule hereto.
