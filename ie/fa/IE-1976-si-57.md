S.I. No. 57 of 1976.

JURY DISTRICTS ORDER, 1976.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Justice, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by subsection (2) of [section 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/sec0005.html#sec5) of the [Juries Act, 1976](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1976/en/act/pub/0004/index.html) (No. 4 of 1976), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Jury Districts Order, 1976.

###### 2.

2. This Order shall come into operation on the 5th day of March, 1976.

###### 3.

3. The Jury District of the County of Cork (including the County Borough of Cork) is hereby limited to the parts of the said county shown in Part I of the Schedule hereto.

###### 4.

4. The Counties of Donegal, Galway, Kerry, Kildare, Leitrim, Louth, Mayo, Monaghan, Roscommon, Offaly and Wicklow are hereby divided respectively into the several Jury Districts shown in Part II of the Schedule hereto.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order (i) creates a jury district of County Cork (including the County Borough of Cork) limited to parts of the said county and (ii) divides each of the Counties of Donegal, Galway, Kerry, Kildare, Leitrim, Louth, Mayo, Monaghan, Offaly, Roscommon and Wicklow into two jury districts.
