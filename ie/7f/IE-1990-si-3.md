S.I. No. 3 of 1990.

THE HEALTH (CORPORATE BODIES) (ESTABLISHMENT) (ORDERS) (AMENDMENT) ORDER, 1990.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the power conferred on him by [Section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Health (Corporate Bodies) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1961) and Section 4 of that Act as amended by [Section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0008/index.html) (No. 8 of 1980) hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Health (Corporate Bodies) (Establishment) (Orders) (Amendment) Order, 1990.

###### 2.

2. The Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment Order) 1977, ( [S.I. No. 255 of 1977](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1977/en/si/0255.html) ), as amended by the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) Order, 1988 ( [S.I. No. 13 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0013.html) ), the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 1988, ( [S.I. No. 59 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0059.html) ) and the Beaumont Hospital Board (Establishment) Order, 1977 (Amendment) Order, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 234 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0234.html) ) is hereby amended by addition after Article 24 (A) of the following Article:—

"24 (B) As respects a person whose name is entered in the register of pensionable officers or in Part 1 of the Superannuation Revision Scheme Register and to whose pensionable local service there is not added a period in accordance with paragraph (*a*) (i) of sub-article (5) of article 10 of the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) (Consolidation) Scheme 1987 ( [S.I. No. 316 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0316.html) ) or paragraph (*a)* (i) of sub-article (5) of article 10 of the Local Government (Superannuation Revision) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0391.html) ) and who—

- (i) was required as a condition of his appointment to be a registered medical or dental practitioner, a registered pharmacist, or a member of such other profession as may be specified by the Minister for Health for the purposes of the subparagraph,

- (ii) has not less than ten years of pensionable local service, and

- (iii) is not retiring from office as a non-consultant hospital doctor or as a junior hospital dentist,

the Board may add to his pensionable local service the period which could be added in accordance with article 10 of the said Schemes if the circumstances set out in paragraph (*a*) (i) of sub-article (5) of article 10 of the said Consolidation Schemes applied in relation to the person.".

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The effect of this Order is to provide for the same additions to service under the Local Government (Superannuation Revision) (Consolidation) Scheme, 1986 and the [Local Government (Superannuation) Act, 1956](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1956/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) in the case of certain professionals in health corporate bodies as is provided for in the case of their counterparts employed in health boards.
