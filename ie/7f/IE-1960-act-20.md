# RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (CONFIRMATION OF ORDER) ACT, 1960

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

AN ACT TO CONFIRM THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES (CARPETS) ORDER, 1960, MADE BY THE MINISTER FOR INDUSTRY AND COMMERCE UNDER SECTION 9 OF THE RESTRICTIVE TRADE PRACTICES ACT, 1953. [19*th July,* 1960.]

BE IT ENACTED BY THE OIREACHTAS AS FOLLOWS:—

###### 1 Confirmation of Order.

**1.**—The Restrictive Trade Practices (Carpets) Order, 1960, made by the Minister for Industry and Commerce under section 9 of the Restrictive Trade Practices Act, 1953, is hereby confirmed.

###### 2 Short title.

**2.**—This Act may be cited as the Restrictive Trade Practices (Confirmation of Order) Act, 1960.
