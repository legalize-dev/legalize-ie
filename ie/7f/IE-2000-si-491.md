# S.I. No. 491/2000 - Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Section 19(2)) Order, 2000

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, JOE WALSH, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/sec0019.html#sec19) (2) of the [Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0041/index.html) (No. 41 of 1998) (as adapted by the Agriculture and Food (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1999 ( [S.I. No. 307 of 1999](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1999/en/si/0307.html) )), hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Plant Varieties (Proprietary Rights) (Amendment) Act, 1998 (Section 19(2)) Order, 2000.

###### 2.

2. The farm saved seed exemption applies to the variety of any plant genus or species specified in the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The effect of this Order is to designate the variety of any plant genus or species to which the farm saved seed exemption applies.
