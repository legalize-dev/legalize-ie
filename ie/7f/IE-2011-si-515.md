# S.I. No. 515/2011 - Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2011

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th October*, 2011.

I, ALAN SHATTER, Minister for Justice and Equality, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 65 (as amended by [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0014/print.html#sec9) of the [Civil Law (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0014/print.html) (No. 14 of 2008)) of the [Courts of Justice Act 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) (No. 48 of 1936) (as adapted by the Justice and Law Reform (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 138 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0138.html) )), with the consent of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform, hereby order as follows:

###### 1.

1. (1) This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) (Amendment) Order 2011.

- (2) This Order comes into operation on 10 October 2011.

###### 2.

2. Schedule 1 to the Supreme Court and High Court (Fees) Order 2011 (No. 406 of 2011) is amended—

- (a) in item 1(a) in column (1) of Part 1, by the substitution of “a copy of an order” for “a copy of an order, per page”, and

- (b) in item 11 in column (1) of Part 6, by the deletion of “, per page”.

The Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform consents to the making of the foregoing Order.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform,

6 October 2011.

**BRENDAN HOWLIN,**

Minister for Public Expenditure and Reform.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

6 October 2011.

**ALAN SHATTER,**

Minister for Justice and Equality.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This does not form part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

This Order amends the Supreme and High Court (Fees) Order, 2011 ( [S.I. No. 406 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0406.html) ), which came into effect on 22 August, 2011.
