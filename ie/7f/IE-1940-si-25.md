# S.I. No. 25/1940 - Agricultural Produce (Cereals) (Scheduled Feeding Stuff) Order, 1939

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS. 1940. No. 25.

AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE (CEREALS) (SCHEDULED FEEDING STUFF) ORDER, 1939.

WHEREAS it is enacted by sub-section (3) of [Section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0016/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1938), that the Minister for Agriculture may, whenever and so often as he thinks fit, by order under the said sub-section declare that a particular article shall be a scheduled feeding stuff for the purposes of Part III of the said Act :

NOW, THEREFORE, I, SÉAMAS O RIAIN, Minister for Agriculture, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sub-section (3) of [Section 19](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0016/sec0019.html#sec19) of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1938), and of every and any other power me in this behalf enabling, hereby order as follows, that is to say :—

###### 1.

1.—(1) This Order may be cited as the Agricultural Produce (Cereals) (Scheduled Feeding Stuff) Order, 1939.

- (2) This Order shall come into operation on the 27th day of December, 1939.

###### 2.

2. Flax seed in grain, otherwise linseed in grain shall be a scheduled feeding stuff for the purposes of Part III of the [Agricultural Produce (Cereals) Act, 1938](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1938/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) (No. 16 of 1938).

Given under my Official Seal this 21st day of December, 1939.

- (Signed) SÉAMAS O RIAIN,

Minister for Agriculture.
