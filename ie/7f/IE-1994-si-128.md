# S.I. No. 128/1994 - Social Welfare (Unemployment Benefit) Regulations, 1994

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 128 of 1994.

SOCIAL WELFARE (UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFIT) REGULATIONS, 1994.

The Minister for Social Welfare, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [sections 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0004.html#sec4) and [47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1993), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

1 Short title.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Social Welfare (Unemployment Benefit) Regulations, 1994.

2 Disqualification for Unemployment Benefit.

###### 2.

**2.** The amount prescribed for the purpose of subsection 4 (*e*) of [section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) shall be £15,000.

3 Revocation.

###### 3.

**3.** The Social Welfare (Unemployment Benefit) Regulations, 1992 ( [S.I. No. 189 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0189.html) ) are hereby revoked.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 4th day of May, 1994.

**MICHAEL WOODS,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 47](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/sec0047.html#sec47) of the [Social Welfare (Consolidation) Act, 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) provides that a person under age 55 who receives money in respect of redundancy in excess of a prescribed amount may be disqualified for receiving unemployment benefit for a period of up to nine weeks. These regulations increase from £12,000 to £15,000 the amount prescribed for the purposes of this disqualification.
