# S.I. No. 64/1979 - Milk and Dairies Act, 1935 (Sale of Milk in County Health District of Dublin) Order, 1979

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 64 of 1979.

MILK AND DAIRIES ACT, 1935 (SALE OF MILK IN COUNTY HEALTH DISTRICT OF DUBLIN) ORDER, 1979.

The Minister for Health in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (1) of [section 33](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/sec0033.html#sec33) of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1935), as adapted by article 4 of the Health (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1947 (S.R. and O. 1947 No. 58), hereby orders as follows:—

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sale of Milk in County Health District of Dublin) Order, 1979.

###### 2.

2. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937) applies to this Order.

###### 3.

3. On and after the 1 July, 1979 the sale of milk is hereby prohibited in the County Health District of Dublin unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 2nd day of March One Thousand Nine Hundred and Seventy-nine.

**CHARLES J. HAUGHEY,**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Order prohibits, with effect from 1 July, 1979 the sale of milk in the County Health District of Dublin unless such milk is sold under a special designation within the meaning of Part IV of the [Milk and Dairies Act, 1935](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1935/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) .
