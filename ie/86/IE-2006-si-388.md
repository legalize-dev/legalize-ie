# S.I. No. 388/2006 - Central Bank Act 1942 (Sections 33J And 33K) Regulations 2006

> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 388 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0388.html)**

**REGULATIONS**

entitled

**CENTRAL BANK ACT 1942 (SECTIONS 33J and 33K) REGULATIONS 2006**

**[S.I. No. 388 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0388.html)**

**Central Bank Act 1942 (Section 33J and 33K) Regulations 2006**

I, Patrick Neary, Chief Executive of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by Sections 33J and 33K of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (as inserted by the Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act, 2003) with the agreement of the members of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority, and with the approval of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K Regulations 2006.

###### 2.

2. Part B of the Schedule to the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0447.html) ) as amended by the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2005 (S.I. 273 of 2005) is hereby amended by the substitution therefor of the Schedule to these Regulations.

###### 3.

3. The [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Section 33J and 33K) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0447.html) ) are hereby amended by the deletion of the definition “Authority”, and by the insertion following the definition of “designated enactment” of

> ““Financial Regulator” means the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority”.

###### 4.

4. Regulation 5(b) of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2004 shall take effect and be applied for the levy period 1 January 2006 to 31 December 2006 as if the due date was the earlier of the date set out in a levy notice and 16 weeks from the making of the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K Regulations 2006.

These Regulations, made by the Chief Executive of the Irish Financial Services Regulatory Authority in accordance with Sections 33J and 33 K of the [Central Bank Act, 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (as amended) amend the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 447 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0447.html) ) and the [Central Bank Act 1942](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1942/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (Sections 33J and 33K) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 273 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0273.html) ) which set out the requirements of regulated financial service providers to pay a levy contribution to the Financial Regualtor. The 2004 Regulations also provide for the collection and recovery of the levies as well as providing for certain obligations as to self-assessment and record keeping by regulated financial service providers. These Regulations will be published from time to time by the Financial Regulator on its website and in other ways that the Chief Executive thinks appropriate.
