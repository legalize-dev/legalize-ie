# S.I. No. 207/2002 - District Court (Company Law Enforcement) Rules, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , and by section 21 (1) of the [Economic and Monetary Union Act, 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Company Law Enforcement) Rules 2002.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 13th day of June, 2002 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. Order 34, rule 7 of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) is hereby amended:

- (i) by the substitution for paragraph (2) of rule 7 of the following:

  > *“Such application shall be by the information on oath and in writing in the form 34.15, Schedule B, laid by an officer of the Director of Corporate Enforcement designated for the purposes of section 20 by the said Director or laid under the authority of the said Director.”;*

- (ii) by the substitution in paragraph (3) of rule 7 of the words *“to a designated officer of the said Director”* for the words *“to a member of the Garda Siochana”*

###### 4.

4. The Forms numbered 34.15 and 34.16 in Schedule 1 hereof shall be substituted for the Forms numbered 34.15 and 34.16 in Schedule B of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).
