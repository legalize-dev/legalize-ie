# S.I. No. 533/2001 - An Bord Bia (Levy) Regulations, 2001

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Joe Walsh, Minister for Agriculture, Food and Rural Development, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 38](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/sec0038.html#sec38) (11) of the [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1994), make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the An Bord Bia (Levy) Regulations, 2001.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 January 2002.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1994), is amended:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

The purpose of these Regulations is to provide that the stamps, by which the export levy under [Section 37](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/sec0037.html#sec37) of the [An Bord Bia Act 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) is payable are in convenient Euro amounts.
