# S.I. No. 206/2003 - Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Freedom of Information Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997), hereby make the following regulations with respect to which, pursuant to subsection (3) of that section, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Miscellaneous Revocations) Regulations 2003.

###### 2.

2. The following Regulations are revoked:

- (a) the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Sections 6(4), 6(5) and 6(6) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 516 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0516.html) ),

- (b) the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Section 6(9)) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 517 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0517.html) ), and

- (c) the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Section 28(1)) (Amendment Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 521 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0521.html) ).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)

These Regulations revoke the following Regulations

i. the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Sections 6(4), 6(5) and 6(6)) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 516 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0516.html) ),

ii. the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Section 6(9)) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 517 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0517.html) )

iii. the Freedom of Information Act 1997 (Section 28(1)) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 ( [S.I. No. 521 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0521.html) ).

This revocation arises because the provisions of these three Regulations have been incorporated into [Sections 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0006.html#sec6) and [28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0028.html#sec28) of the [Freedom of Information Act 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) thereby rendering the Regulations superfluous and requiring their revocation.
