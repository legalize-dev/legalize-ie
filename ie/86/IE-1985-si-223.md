**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations make a number of further amendments to the Housing Regulations, 1980. The amounts of loans a housing authority may advance for the building, acquisition or improvement of a house and the income limits applicable to such loans are being increased. Some minor changes are made in the conditions governing eligibility for such loans. The regulations also provide that 40% of the proceeds of houses sold by a housing authority may continue to be credited to the current revenue account of the authority.
