S.I. No. 524 of 1998.

FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT, 1997 (THIRD SCHEDULE) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1998

I, Charlie McCreevy, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997), hereby make the following regulations with respect to which, pursuant to section 3(3) of that Act, a draft has been laid before each House of the Oireachtas and a resolution approving of the draft has been passed by each such House:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (Third Schedule) (Amendment) Regulations, 1998.

###### 2.

2. The Third Schedule to the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997) is hereby amended -

- (*a*) in Part I thereof, by the substitution -

    - (i) in the first column of "No.22 of 1994" for "No.21 of 1996", and

    - (ii) in the second column of "An Bord Bia Act, 1996" for " [An Bord Bia Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) ", and

- (*b*) in Part II thereof -

CHARLIE McCREEVY, T.D.,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations provide for the correction of a typographical error with regard to the number and year of an enactment listed in the Third Schedule to the [Freedom of Information Act, 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/act/pub/0013/index.html) (No. 13 of 1997). They also provide for addition of a reference to the Access to Information on the Environment Regulations, 1998, and of a reference to the Health Services Regulations, 1971, to that Schedule. This means that the Freedom of Information Act will override restrictions on access to information under those Regulations.
