# S.I. No. 218/1989 - Money Order Amendment (No. 31) Scheme 1989

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 218 of 1989.

MONEY ORDER AMENDMENT (No. 31) SCHEME 1989.

AN POST, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by [Section 70](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/sec0070.html#sec70) of the [Postal and Telecommunications Services Act, 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1983) and with the concurrence of the Minister for Communications, hereby makes the following Scheme:

###### 1.

1. This Scheme may be cited as the Money Order Amendment (No. 31) Scheme, 1989.

###### 2.

2. This Scheme shall come into operation on the 1st day of August, 1989.

###### 3.

3. The [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) (No. 38 of 1937), applies to this Scheme.

###### 4.

4. In this Scheme:—

"the Principal Regulations" means the Money Order Regulations, 1903.

"the Regulations of 1960" means the Money Order Amendment (No. 16) Regulations, 1960 ( [S.I. No. 49 of 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/si/0049.html) ).

"the Regulations of 1986" means the Money Order Amendment (No. 29) Regulations, 1986 ( [S.I. No. 95 of 1986](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1986/en/si/0095.html) .

"the Regulations of 1989" means Money Order Amendment (No. 30) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 33 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0033.html) ).

###### 5.

5. Regulation 3(3) of the Principal Regulations as amended by Regulation 5 of the Regulations of 1960 is hereby replaced by the following:

"3 ( *a* ) No inland money order shall be issued for an amount exceeding £500.00.

( *b* ) No foreign money order shall be issued for an amount exceeding £250.00, or such lesser sum as An Post may from time to time direct".

###### 6.

6. **EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

This Scheme applies to the Money Order service and comes into operation on the 1st of August 1989. It authorises an increase in the amount for which a money order can be written and specifies and increases the charges to be levied.
