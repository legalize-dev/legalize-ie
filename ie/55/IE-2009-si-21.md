[S.I. No. 21 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0021.html)

DIPLOMATIC AND CONSULAR FEES (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS 2009

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 3*rd February*, 2009.

I, MICHEÁL MARTIN, Minister for Foreign Affairs, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Provision of Services) Act 1993](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1993/en/act/pub/0033/index.html) (No. 33 of 1993), as amended by [section 28](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html#sec28) of the [Passports Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) (No. 4 of 2008), and with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2009.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on 1 February 2009.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations—

“emergency passport” has the meaning assigned to it by the [Passports Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) ;

“emergency travel certificate” has the meaning assigned to it by the [Passports Act 2008](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2008/en/act/pub/0004/print.html) .

###### 3.

3. The Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2001 ( [S.I. No. 117 of 2001](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2001/en/si/0117.html) ) (as amended by the Diplomatic and Consular Fees (Amendment) Regulations 2005 ( [S.I. No. 391 of 2005](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2005/en/si/0391.html) )) are amended—

- (*a*) in Regulation 9, by substituting for paragraph (*b*) the following:

  > - “(*b*) where the service for which the fee is chargeable is rendered for, or on behalf of, a person who is in straitened circumstances or the levying of the fee would for other reasons constitute a serious hardship or the waiver is otherwise desirable on compassionate grounds;”

- (*b*) in Part II of the Second Schedule, by substituting the following for the matter at Reference Number 5—

  > “
  >

  > | --- | --- | --- |
  > | 5 | For processing an application for a working holiday authorisation—(a) in the case of a citizen of the United States of America(b) in the case of a citizen of any other state | €250€60 |

- (*c*) by the substitution of the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Regulations for Part 1 of the Second Schedule.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

The purpose of these Regulations is to amend the Diplomatic and Consular Fees Regulations 2001, as amended, to provide for changes in the fee structure and increases in the fees for passports, travel documents and working holiday authorisations.
