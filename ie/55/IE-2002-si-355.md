# S.I. No. 355/2002 - The Southern Health Board (District Mental Hospitals) Order, 2002

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The Minister for Health and Children, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by Sections 162, 165, 166 and all other enabling sections in that behalf of the [Mental Treatment Act 1945](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1945/en/act/pub/0019/index.html) (as amended and as adapted by the Health (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 308 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0308.html) ), hereby orders as follows:

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Southern Health Board (District Mental Hospitals Order, 2002.

###### 2.

2. The Acute Psychiatric Unit, Tralee General Hospital is hereby designated as a district mental hospital to serve the administrative county of Kerry.

###### 3.

3. The following hospitals are hereby designated as district mental hospitals to serve the administrative county of Cork:

St. Stephen's Hospital, Glanmire

Bantry General Hospital

###### 4.

4. The following acute psychiatric units are hereby designated as district mental hospitals to serve the county borough and administrative county of Cork:

Acute Psychiatric Unit, Mercy Hospital

Acute Psychiatric Unit, Cork University Hospital
