S.I. No. 236 of 1984.

ARMY PENSIONS (INCREASE) (No. 4) REGULATIONS, 1984.

I, PATRICK COONEY, Minister for Defence, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0012/sec0007.html#sec7) of the [Army Pensions Act, 1968](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1968/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1968), as adapted by the Public Service (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1973 ( [S.I. No. 294 of 1973](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1973/en/si/0294.html) ), hereby, with the consent of the Minister for the Public Service, make the following Regulations:

PART IPRELIMINARY

1 Short title, collective citation.

###### 1.

**1.** (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Army Pensions (Increase) (No. 4) Regulations, 1984.

- (2) The collective citation, the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 to 1984, shall include these Regulations.

2 Definitions and construction.

###### 2.

**2.** (1) In these Regulations—

"the Act of 1927" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1927](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1927/en/act/pub/0012/index.html) (No. 12 of 1927);

"the Act of 1932" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1932](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1932/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1932);

"the Act of 1937" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1937);

"the Act of 1941" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1941](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1941/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1941);

"the Act of 1943" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1943](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1943/en/act/pub/0014/index.html) (No. 14 of 1943);

"the Act of 1953" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1953](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1953/en/act/pub/0023/index.html) (No. 23 of 1953);

> "the Act of 1959" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/act/pub/0015/index.html) (No. 15 of 1959)"

"the No. 2 Act of 1960" means the [Army Pensions (No. 2) Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) (No. 39 of 1960);

"the Act of 1962" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/act/pub/0022/index.html) (No. 22 of 1962);

"the Act of 1971" means the [Army Pensions Act, 1971](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1971/en/act/pub/0026/index.html) (No. 26 of 1971);

"the Acts" means the Army Pensions Acts, 1923 to 1973;

"the Increase Regulations of 1969" means the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 ( [S.I. No. 191 of 1969](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1969/en/si/0191.html) );

"the No. 3 Increase Regulations of 1984" means the Army Pensions (Increase) (No. 3) Regulations, 1984 ( [S.I. No. 157 of 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/si/0157.html) ).

- (2) These Regulations shall be construed as one with the Acts and the Army Pensions (Increase) Regulations, 1969 to 1984.

- (3) References in these Regulations to any enactment shall be construed as references to that enactment as amended by any subsequent enactment or regulations.

4 Amendment of paragraph 4 of Increase Regulations of 1969.

###### 4.

**4.** A wound or disability pension to which paragraph 4 of the Increase Regulations of 1969 applies, and which is payable to an officer discharged from the forces before the 2nd day of February, 1984, shall—

( *a* ) in case such officer was discharged from the forces before the 2nd day of September, 1983, be increased to the amount which would be payable to such an officer under the Acts if he had been discharged from the forces on the 2nd day of September, 1983, and his rank and service in that rank on that date were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge increased by 3.25 per cent;

( *b* ) in case such officer was discharged from the forces on or after the 2nd day of September, 1983, and before the 2nd day of October, 1983, be the actual amount payable to him under the Acts increased by 3.25 per cent;

( *c* ) in case such officer was discharged from the forces on or after the 2nd day of October, 1983, and before the 2nd day of February, 1984, be increased to the amount which would be payable to such an officer under the Acts if he had been discharged from the forces on the 2nd day of February, 1984, and his rank and service in that rank were the same as those on the date of his actual discharge.
