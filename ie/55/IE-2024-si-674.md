# S.I. No. 674/2024 - Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2024

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th December,* 2024.

I, JAMES LAWLESS, Minister of State at the Department of Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html#sec4) of the [Road Safety Authority (Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness) Act 2012](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2012/en/act/pub/0016/print.html) (No. 16 of 2012) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 351 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0351.html) )), and the Transport (Delegation Of Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order 2024 ( [S.I. No. 354 of 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/si/0354.html) ), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 January 2025.

###### 2.

2. The Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2023 ( [S.I. No. 513 of 2023](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2023/en/si/0513.html) ) are revoked.

###### 3.

3. The Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0347.html) ) are amended by substituting for Schedules 1, 2 and 3 the following:

> ### **“Schedule 1**
>

EXPLANATORY NOTE

These Regulations substitute Schedules 1, 2 and 3 to the Commercial Vehicle Roadworthiness (Vehicle Testing) (No. 2) Regulations 2013 ( [S.I. No. 347 of 2013](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2013/en/si/0347.html) ) – CVR vehicles and test fees.
