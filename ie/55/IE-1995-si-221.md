# S.I. No. 221/1995 - National Social Service Board (Membership) Regulations, 1995

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 221 of 1995.

NATIONAL SOCIAL SERVICE BOARD (MEMBERSHIP) REGULATIONS, 1995.

The Minister for Social Welfare in exercise of the powers conferred on him by subsection (7) of [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/sec0006.html#sec6) of the [National Social Service Board Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) (No. 2 of 1984), hereby makes the following Regulations:

1 Citation.

###### 1.

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the National Social Service Board (Membership) Regulations, 1995.

2 Number of members.

###### 2.

**2.** The maximum number of members of the Board, in addition to the chairman and vice-chairman, specified in [section 6](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/sec0006.html#sec6) (1) of the [National Social Service Board Act, 1984](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1984/en/act/pub/0002/index.html) is hereby varied by the substitution of "fifteen" for "ten".

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Social Welfare, this 11th day of August, 1995.

**PROINSIAS DE ROSSA,**

Minister for Social Welfare.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for an increase in the number of members of the National Social Service Board, in addition to the chairman and vice-chairman, from ten to fifteen.
