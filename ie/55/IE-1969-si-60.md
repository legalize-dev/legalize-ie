# S.I. No. 60/1969 - Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund For Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1969

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 60 of 1969.

GREAT NORTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY (IRELAND) PENSION FUND FOR WAGES STAFF (AMENDMENT) SCHEME (CONFIRMATION) ORDER, 1969.

WHEREAS by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) (4) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1958), the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff was continued in being :

AND WHEREAS Coras Iompair Eireann has, in pursuance of [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) (10) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), submitted to the Minister for Transport and Power a scheme (in these Recitals referred to as the amending scheme) amending the said Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff :

AND WHEREAS the Minister for Transport and Power proposes to confirm the amending scheme :

NOW I, ERSKINE H. CHILDERS, Minister for Transport and Power, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) (10) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) (No. 20 of 1958), and the Transport, Fuel and Power (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) Order, 1959 ( [S.I. No. 125 of 1959](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1959/en/si/0125.html) ), hereby, after consultation with the Minister for Finance, order as follows :

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1969.

###### 2.

2. In this Order " the amending scheme " means the scheme (amending the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff) submitted by Cúoras Iompair Eireann under [section 15](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/sec0015.html#sec15) (10) of the [Great Northern Railway Act, 1958](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1958/en/act/pub/0020/index.html) .

###### 3.

3. The amending scheme (a copy of which is set out in the Schedule to this Order) is hereby confirmed.

SCHEDULE

GREAT NORTHERN RAILWAY COMPANY (IRELAND) PENSION FUND FOR WAGES STAFF (AMENDMENT) SCHEME

1. Definitions :

In this amending Scheme "the existing Scheme" means the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff as amended by (a) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund (Amendment) Scheme for Wages Staff (Confirmation) Order, 1962 ( [S.I. No. 53 of 1962](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1962/en/si/0053.html) ) ; (b) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1965 ( [S.I. No. 50 of 1965](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1965/en/si/0050.html) ) and (c) the Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff (Amendment) Scheme (Confirmation) Order, 1967 ( [S.I. No. 9 of 1967](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1967/en/si/0009.html) ).

The term "operative date" means the date of the Order of the Minister for Transport and Power confirming this amending Scheme.

The terms "total payments" and "full pension" shall have the meanings assigned to them by the existing Scheme.

2. Continuance of the existing Scheme :

- (1) The benefits provided by the existing Scheme shall continue to be paid or be payable under and in accordance with the terms thereof and without alteration to every pensioner whose right to pension accrued prior to the operative date and to the personal representative of any deceased pensioner if the deceased's right to pension accrued prior to the operative date.

- (2) Nothing in this amending Scheme shall affect the rights of any of the persons mentioned in the previous sub-article, nor shall this amending Scheme affect any subsisting right or liability accrued to or incurred by any person under the existing Scheme prior to the operative date.

- (3) Deferred and special pensions, now payable or at any time to become payable shall not be affected by this amending Scheme.

3. Amendment of the existing Scheme :

- (1) Subject to the provisions of Article 2 of this amending Scheme, the existing. Scheme shall be amended so as to conform to the provisions hereinafter contained, and every provision of the existing Scheme which is inconsistent with the provisions hereinafter contained shall cease to have effect.

- (2) The increased contributions and benefits provided for by this amending Scheme are, where applicable, in substitution for the corresponding contributions and benefits provided for by the existing Scheme.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

The purpose of the Order is to confirm a scheme to amend the existing Great Northern Railway Company (Ireland) Pension Fund for Wages Staff so as to provide for increased pensions for certain members of the scheme.
