# S.I. No. 471/2013 - European Communities (Road Vehicles: Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2013

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 6*th December*, 2013.

I, LEO VARADKAR, Minister for Transport, Tourism and Sport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving effect to Council Directive 2013/15/EU of 13 May 2013[^1] ,in so far as it applies to the entry into service of motor vehicles and their trailers, and systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Road Vehicles: Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2013.

###### 2.

2. The European Communities (Road Vehicles: Type-Approval) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No.158 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0158.html) ) are amended by substituting for Schedule 1 (inserted by Regulation 2(*b*) of the European Communities (Road Vehicles: Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 ( [S.I. No. 422 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0422.html) )) the following:

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations amend the European Communities (Road Vehicles: Type-Approval) Regulations 2009 ( [S.I. No. 158 of 2009](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2009/en/si/0158.html) ), which gave effect to Directive 2007/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 September 2007 (the Framework Directive) and to each of the separate Directives that individually regulate many components, systems and separate technical units of relevant vehicles, in so far as those Directives relate to the type-approval of certain motor vehicles and of certain components, systems and separate technical units. These Regulations replace the list of separate Directives set out in Schedule 1 to the Regulations of 2009 in order to take account of Council Directive 2013/15/EU on the accession of the Republic of Croatia to the European Union.

The Regulations may be cited as the European Communities (Road Vehicles: Type-Approval) (Amendment) Regulations 2013.
