# S.I. No. 409/2003 - District Court (Terms of Employment Information) Rules, 2003

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

The District Court Rules Committee, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by [section 91](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/sec0091.html#sec91) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1924](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1924/en/act/pub/0010/index.html) , [section 72](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/sec0072.html#sec72) of the [Courts of Justice Act, 1936](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1936/en/act/pub/0048/index.html) , [section 17](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/sec0017.html#sec17) of the [Interpretation Act, 1937](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1937/en/act/pub/0038/index.html) [as applied by [section 48](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0048.html#sec48) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) ] and [section 34](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/sec0034.html#sec34) of the [Courts (Supplemental Provisions) Act, 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0039/index.html) , do hereby, with the concurrence of the Minister for Justice, Equality and Law Reform, make the following rules of court:-

###### 1.

1. These rules may be cited as the District Court (Terms of Employment Information) Rules 2003.

###### 2.

2. These rules shall come into operation on the 9th day of October, 2003 and shall be read together with all other District Court rules for the time being in force.

###### 3.

3. Order 99B of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) shall be amended by the insertion immediately following Order 99B, rule 4 of the following:

*5. “An application to the Court for an order pursuant to [Section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0005/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Terms of Employment (Information) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) may be made to any sitting of the Court for the court district in which the respondent employer carries on any trade profession, business or occupation.*

*6. Such application shall be by notice of application in the Form 99B.3, Schedule C and shall be served on the respondent by prepaid registered post not later than seven days before the date of the sitting for which the application is returnable. An order made pursuant to section 9 of the said Act of 1994 on such application shall be in the Form 99B.4, Schedule C”.*

###### 4.

4. The Forms numbered 99B.3 and 99B.4 in Schedule 1 hereof shall be added to the Forms in Schedule C of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ).

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

These Rules amend Order 99B of the District Court Rules, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 93 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0093.html) ) to allow applications to be made pursuant to [section 9](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0005/sec0009.html#sec9) of the [Terms of Employment (Information) Act, 1994](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1994/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) to any sitting of the Court for the court district in which the respondent employer carries on any trade, profession, business or occupation. Such application shall be by notice of application in the Form 99B.3, Schedule C and shall be served on the respondent by prepaid registered post not later than seven days before the date of the sitting for which the application is returnable. An order made pursuant to section 9 of the said Act of 1994 on such application shall be in the Form 99B.4, Schedule C.
