# S.I. No. 503/2006 - Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order 2006

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

**[S.I. No. 503 of 2006](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2006/en/si/0503.html)**

**Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order 2006**

The Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [Section 14](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/sec0014.html#sec14) of the [Building Control Act 1990](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1990/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1990), hereby orders as follows:-

###### 1.

1. This Order may be cited as the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 (Amendment) Order, 2006.

###### 2.

2. The Schedule to the Building Regulations Advisory Body Order, 2002 ( [S.I. No. 2 of 2002](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2002/en/si/0002.html) ) is hereby amended by the substitution of:-

- (1) “Rose Kenny” for “Ann McGuinness”.

- (2) “Gerry Farrell” for “Paul Kelly”

- (3) “Erik Koornneef” for “Niamh Doherty”

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| L.S. | Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government this 28^th day of September 2006 |
|  | ***Dick Roche T.D*.** Minister for the Environment, Heritage and Local Government. |

**EXPLANATORY NOTE**

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation)*

This Order provides for the appointment of three members to the Building Regulations Advisory Body (BRAB) to fill three casual vacancies on the BRAB.
