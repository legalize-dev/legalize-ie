# S.I. No. 27/2022 - Health Act 1947 (Regulations Relating to Certain Restrictions Under Section 31A) (COVID-19) (REVOCATION) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 25*th January,* 2022.

I, STEPHEN DONNELLY, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/sec0005.html#sec5) , 31A (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec10) of the [Health (Preservation and Protection and other Emergency Measures in the Public Interest) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No.1 of 2020)), 31AB and 31AD (inserted by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/act/pub/0024/print.html#sec3) of the [Health (Amendment) (No.2) Act 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/act/pub/0024/print.html) (No. 24 of 2021)) and 31C (inserted by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0019/print.html#sec4) of the [Health (Amendment) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0019/print.html) (No. 19 of 2020)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) -

- (*a*) having regard to the immediate, exceptional and manifest risk posed to human life and public health by the spread of Covid-19 and to the matters specified in subsections (2), (6A)(b), (6C)(b) and (6D)(b) of section 31A of that Act,

  and

- (*b*) having consulted with the Minister for Foreign Affairs, the Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, the Minister for Transport, the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment, the Minister for Finance, the Minister for Justice and the Minister for Tourism, Culture, Arts, Gaeltacht, Sport and Media,

hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1947 (Regulations relating to Certain Restrictions under Section 31A)(Covid-19)(Revocation) Regulations 2022.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 22^nd day of January 2022 at 6.00 a.m.

###### 3.

3. The following Regulations are revoked:

- (*a*) Regulation 4, in so far as it applies to the provisions specified at entries 6, 7, 8, and 9 of Column 1 of Schedule 1 to, and Regulation 5 of, the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Fixed Payment Notice and Dwelling Event Provisions) (Covid-19)(No.2) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 219 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0219.html) );

- (*b*) the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A-Temporary Restrictions)(Covid-19)(No.2) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 217 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0217.html) );

- (*c*) the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Sections 31AB and 31AD) (Covid-19) (Operation of certain indoor premises) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 385 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0385.html) );

- (*d*) the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A (6A))(Covid-19)(No.3) Regulations 2021 ( [S.I. No. 738 of 2021](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2021/en/si/0738.html) ).

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

21 January, 2022.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These regulations revoke the majority of the Covid related public health restrictions including SIs 385 of 2021, 217 of 2021, 738 of 2021 and partially revokes SI 219 of 2021 with effect from 6am on Saturday the 22nd of January 2022.
