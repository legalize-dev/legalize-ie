S.I. No. 175 of 1992.

TRAVEL AGENTS (LICENSING) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 1992.

I, MÁIRE GEOGHEGAN-QUINN, Minister for Tourism, Transport and Communications in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 7](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0007.html#sec7) , [12](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0012.html#sec12) and [25](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/sec0025.html#sec25) of the [Transport (Tour Operators and Travel Agents) Act, 1982](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1982/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1982), and the Communications (Transfer of Departmental Administration and Ministerial Functions) (No. 2) Order, 1987 ( [S.I. No. 92 of 1987](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1987/en/si/0092.html) ), as adapted by the Tourism and Transport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1991 ( [S.I. No. 25 of 1991](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1991/en/si/0025.html) ), and, insofar as the Regulations relate to the prescription of fees and charges, with the consent of the Minister for Finance, hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1992.

- (2) The Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 to 1989, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 to 1992.

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall come into operation on the 1st day of July, 1992.

###### 3.

3. The Travel Agents (Licensing) Regulations, 1983 ( [S.I. No. 101 of 1983](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1983/en/si/0101.html) ), as amended by the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1989 ( [S.I. No. 159 of 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/si/0159.html) ), are hereby amended by—

( *a* ) the substitution for Regulation 7 of the following Regulation:

"7. ( *a* ) an application for a licence shall be accompanied by the applicant's latest accounts duly audited and certified, covering a date not earlier than twelve months before the date of application.

( *b* ) notwithstanding paragraph (*a*) of this Regulation the Minister may require a licensed Travel Agent to furnish to the Minister the licensee's audited financial statements by a specified date following the end of the licensee's twelve month accounting period or the end of such other accounting period as shall be agreed to by the Minister.";

( *b* ) the substitution in Regulation 9 (*a*) (inserted by the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988), ( [S.I. No. 132 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0132.html) )) for "£175" of "£201";

( *c* ) the substitution in Regulation 9 (*b*) (inserted by the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988, ( [S.I. No. 132 of 1988](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1988/en/si/0132.html) )) for "£17.50" of "£20";

( *d* ) the substitution for paragraphs "(*d*), (*e*), (*f*) and (*g*)" of Regulation 9 (inserted by the Travel Agents (Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations, 1988) of the following paragraphs:

"( *d* ) In the case of an applicant for a travel agent's licence who has not held a tour operator's or travel agents licence within the two years immediately prior to application, an additional fee of £201 shall be payable;

( *e* ) Where an application for a travel agent's licence is received less than two months but more than one month prior to the date on which the licence is required to commence, an additional fee of £500 shall be payable;

( f ) Where an application for a travel agent's licence is received less than one month prior to the date on which the licence is required to commence, an additional fee of £1,000 shall be payable;

( *g* ) Where an applicant for a travel agent's licence has failed to submit audited financial statements as required by the Minister under Regulation 7 (*b*), an additional fee of £500 shall be payable."; and

( *e* ) the insertion of Regulation 9 (*h*):

> "( *h* ) The Minister may, at her discretion, waive the additional fees payable under paragraphs (*e*) and (*f*) where she is satisfied that the delay in submission of an application was due solely to circumstances outside the control of the applicant, his agents or servants."; and

( f ) the substitution of the following Schedule for the first Schedule:

SECTION 3 — *Shareholding in Other Companies*

Give full details of any shareholding of more than 10 per cent by the applicant or any director, shareholder or partner in any other company, including shareholdings held in the same name of a spouse, nominee or agent.

SECTION 4 — *Borrowings, Credit Arrangements, etc.*

Specify:

- (i) (a) overdraft facilities available (if any) and name of bank or other financial institution involved,

- (b) security given for overdraft(s), (if any),

- (c) other borrowings and securities/collateral given in relation thereto,

    - (ii) details of mortgages, liens, charges or other encumbrances, etc.,

    - (iii) (a) amount and nature of directors' loans invested in the Company,

- (b) name of director(s) to whom the Company is liable for loan capital.

SECTION 5 — *Assets*

Specify:

- (i) details of premises and whether owned, leased, rented or mortgaged,

- (ii) a list of all other assets of the applicant.

SECTION 6 — *Past Activities*

Specify:

- (i) details of companies, enterprises or business ventures with which the applicant or any director, shadow director, shareholder or partner was previously associated in a proprietorial role or as a director or shareholder,

- (ii) details of any company, enterprise or business venture involved in bankruptcy or winding-up proceedings with which the applicant or any director, shadow director, shareholder or partner was involved,

- (iii) details of any prosecutions against the applicant or any director, shadow director, shareholder or partner for fraud, embezzlement, larceny or other offences involving dishonesty, or under the [Consumer Information Act, 1978](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1978/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) , or the [Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act, 1980](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1980/en/act/pub/0016/index.html) , and the outcome of such prosecution.

SECTION 7 — *State whether the Applicant:—*

- (i) is a member of any trade association (e.g. I.T.A.A., A.B.T.A., etc.),

- (ii) is an agent for airlines which are members of the International Air Transport Association,

- (iii) also carries on business as a tour operator within the State, or

- (iv) operates as a tour operator or travel agent in any other Member State of the European Community, and if so, specify the Member State involved and indicate whether the applicant holds a licence, certificate, approval or authorisation from the competent national authorities of such Member State.

SECTION 8 — *Level of Operation and Turnover*

Specify:

- (i) total turnover last year (see Note A),

- (ii) turnover licensable last year (see Note B),

- (iii) nature of non licensable activity last year,

- (iv) projected total turnover for the period of the licence now sought,

- (v) projected licensable turnover for the period of the licence now sought,

- (vi) nature of proposed non licensable activity, if any.

*Notes:* (A) Where turnover is not shown in the audited accounts a separate audited certificate of turnover should be submitted.

- (B) The term "last year" as used in (i), (ii) and (iii) above means the period covered by the audited accounts required to be submitted by the applicant.

SECTION 9 — *Nature of Business*

Where the applicants proposes to operate tour or holiday packages where he contracts directly for accommodation or other services he should provide full details including:—

- (i) whether such packages are based on:—

- (a) the scheduled services on a transport carrier, or

- (b) the purchase of charter seats from a tour operator,

    - (ii) destinations involved,

    - (iii) name of transport carrier or tour operator involved,

    - (iv) number of passengers involved and average inclusive price,

    - (v) copy of brochure or publicity material and booking conditions.

SECTION 10 — *Premises and Staffing*

Specify:

- (i) length of time in business.

- (ii) number of branch offices, outlets or premises with addresses and number of staff employed in each,

- (iii) how, or to what extent, premises are publicly identified and specify normal hours of opening,

- (iv) whether premises are used solely for the sale of travel and, if not, specify what other activity is carried on,

- (v) in the case of managerial staff, names and brief career history and an indication of experience, competence or qualifications (e.g. financial, economic, business administration, accountancy or approved recognised third level managerial qualifications),

- (vi) for other staff (clerks, sales staff, etc.) names and length of time employed by applicant and indicate the extent to which they have experience of, or familiarity with, normal practice in the travel industry including:—

- (a) tour operator booking procedures,

- (b) airline booking procedures,

- (c) surface carrier booking procedures,

- (d) consumer legislation,

- (e) basic insurance terms.

SECTION 11 — *Other Information*

Specify:

- (i) name and address of applicant's legal representative,

- (ii) name and address of applicant's banker(s),

- (iii) name and address of applicant's auditor/accountant,

- (iv) address of applicant's principal place of business.

SECTION 12 — *Bonding*

Specify the type of bond which you propose to lodge with the Minister, if he approves of this application.

SECTION 13 — *Emergency Contact*

State name, address and telephone number of persons to be contacted outside normal office hours or in an emergency.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations provide for—

( *a* ) an increase in fees payable in respect of applications for Travel Agents' licences and for the amendment of such licences,

( *b* ) the introduction of additional fees for licensees who have failed to submit audited financial statements by a date specified by the Minister, and

( *c* ) a revised Application Form for a Travel Agent's licence.
