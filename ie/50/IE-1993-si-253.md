# S.I. No. 253/1993 - Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1993

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 253 of 1993.

SUPERANNUATION (DESIGNATION OF APPROVED ORGANISATIONS) REGULATIONS, 1993.

I, BERTIE AHERN, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) (1) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963), hereby make the following Regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1993.

- (2) The Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisatins) Regulations, 1964 to 1989, and these Regulations may be cited together as the Superannuation (Designation of Approved Organisations) Regulations, 1964 to 1993.

###### 2.

2. The organisations set out in the Schedule to these Regulations are hereby designated as approved organisatins for the purposes of [Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1963).

SCHEDULE

Royal Irish Academy of Music

National Safety Council

Combat Poverty Agency

The National Development Corporation Ltd.

Irish National Council on Alcohol

GIVEN under my Official Seal, this 1st day of September, 1993.

**BERTIE AHERN,**

Minister for Finance.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

[Section 4](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/sec0004.html#sec4) of the [Superannuation and Pensions Act, 1963](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1963/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) , provides for the transfer of pensionable service in the case of staff transfers between the Civil Service and "approved organisations", and between one "approved organisation" and another. These regulations designate the organisations set out in the schedule as "approved organisations".
