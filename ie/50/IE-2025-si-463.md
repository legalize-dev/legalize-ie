# S.I. No. 463/2025 - European Union (Environmental Assessment of Certain Plans and Programmes) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 7*th October,* 2025.

I, JAMES BROWNE, Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [section 3](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/sec0003.html#sec3) of the [European Communities Act 1972](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1972/en/act/pub/0027/index.html) (No. 27 of 1972), for the purposes of giving further effect to Directive 2001/42/EC of the European Parliament and Council of 27 June 2001[^1] , hereby make the following regulations:

*Citation*

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Environmental Assessment of Certain Plans and Programmes) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

- (2) These Regulations (other than Regulation 4(b)) come into operation on the 2nd day of October 2025.

- (3) Regulation 4(b) comes into operation on the commencement of sections 25(8) and 25(9) and Chapters 5 and 6 of the [Planning and Development Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2024).

*Definition*

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations, “Principal Regulations” means European Communities (Environmental Assessment of Certain Plans and Programmes) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 435 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0435.html) ).

*Amendment of Principal Regulations*

###### 3.

3. The Principal Regulations are amended—

- (a) in Article 3, by inserting after paragraph (2) the following:

  > - “(3) Articles 9 to 17 shall not apply to a plan or programme made under Part 3, 21 or 22 of the [Planning and Development Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) (No. 34 of 2024).”,

  and

- (b) by inserting after Article 17 the following:

  > “*Joint or coordinated assessments*
  >
  > 18. Where in relation to a plan or programme, there is an obligation to carry out an assessment under these Regulations and Article 6 of the Habitats Directive, a competent authority may for the purpose of avoiding duplication of effort—
  >
  > - (a) carry out both assessments jointly to the extent that the carrying out of the assessments jointly, or
  >
  > - (b) provide for a coordinated procedure in relation to both assessments to fulfil the requirements of these Regulations or that Article to the extent that the coordinated procedure,
  >
  >   would not hinder compliance with any of the requirements of these Regulations or Article 6 of the Habitats Directive in relation to either such assessment.”.

*Revocation*

###### 4.

4. The following are revoked:

- (a) Article 7 of the Principal Regulations and

- (b) Articles 4 to 6 and 8 of the Principal Regulations.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

2 October, 2025.

**JAMES BROWNE,**

Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

These Regulations provide for consequential amendments to the European Communities (Environmental Assessment of Certain Plans and Programmes) Regulations 2004 ( [S.I. No. 435 of 2004](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2004/en/si/0435.html) ) following the enactment of the [Planning and Development Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) .

The amendments provide that these Regulations do not apply to the following plans made under Part 3, Part 21 and Part 22 of the [Planning and Development Act 2024](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2024/en/act/pub/0034/print.html) :

- • National Planning Framework;

- • National Planning Statement;

- • Regional Spatial and Economic Strategy;

- • Development Plan;

- • Urban Area Plan;

- • Priority Area Plan;

- • Coordinated Area Plan;

- • Ministerial Direction;

- • Ministerial Order;

- • Planning Scheme for Strategic Development Zone; and

- • Planning Scheme for Urban Development Zone.

The Regulations also revoke Articles 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 which provided regulation making powers for the Minister in the [Planning and Development Act 2000](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2000/en/act/pub/0030/print.html) regarding strategic environmental assessment.
