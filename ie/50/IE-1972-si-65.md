# S.I. No. 65/1972 - Health Act, 1970 (Adaptation) Regulations, 1972

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

S.I. No. 65 of 1972.

HEALTH ACT, 1970 (ADAPTATION) REGULATIONS, 1972.

The Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 85](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/sec0085.html#sec85) of the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (No. 1 of 1970) hereby makes the following regulations :

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the [Health Act, 1970](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1970/en/act/pub/0001/index.html) (Adaptation) Regulations, 1972.

###### 2.

2. The [Meath Hospital Act, 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) (No. 5 of 1951) as amended by [section 24](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0009/sec0024.html#sec24) (12) of the [Health Authorities Act, 1960](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1960/en/act/pub/0009/index.html) (No. 9 of 1960) shall be adapted by the substitution of "Eastern Health Board" for "Dublin Health Authority" in subsections (1) and (4) of section 4A.

GIVEN under the Official Seal of the Minister for Health this 29th day of February, 1972.

**ERSKINE H. CHILDERS.**

Minister for Health.

**EXPLANATORY NOTE.**

These Regulations adapt the [Meath Hospital Act, 1951](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1951/en/act/pub/0005/index.html) , to enable those members of the hospital board of the Meath Hospital who were formerly elected by the Dublin Health Authority to be elected henceforth by the Eastern Health Board.
