# S.I. No. 54/2022 - Road Traffic (Prescribed Charge – Removal of Clamp) Regulations 2022

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 15*th February,* 2022.

I, EAMON RYAN, Minister for Transport, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/sec0005.html#sec5) and 101B(7) (inserted by [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec32) of the [Vehicle Clamping Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) (No. 13 of 2015)) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961) (as adapted by the Transport, Tourism and Sport (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( S. I. No. 351 of 2020) and the Justice and Equality ) (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2020 ( [S.I. No. 452 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0452.html) )) after consultation with the Minister for Justice and the National Transport Authority, hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Traffic (Prescribed Charge – Removal of Clamp) Regulations 2022.

- (2) These Regulations come into operation on 1 March 2022.

###### 2.

2. In these Regulations “Act of 1961” means the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) (No. 24 of 1961).

###### 3.

3. The amount of €125 is prescribed as the charge for the removal of an immobilisation device for the purpose of section 101B(7)(a) of the Act of 1961.

###### 4.

4. The form of notice set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purpose of section 101B(7)(b) of the Act of 1961.

###### 5.

5. Payment of the prescribed charge shall be made to a clamping officer as specified in the prescribed notice by payment card or cash.

###### 6.

6. The Road Traffic (Immobilisation of Vehicles) Regulations 2017 (S. I. No. 420 of 2017) are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation*)

These Regulations are made under sections 5 and 101B(7)(inserted by [section 32](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html#sec32) of the [Vehicle Clamping Act 2015](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2015/en/act/pub/0013/print.html) ) of the [Road Traffic Act 1961](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1961/en/act/pub/0024/index.html) and their purpose is to prescribe the amount of the charge to be paid for the removal of an immobilisation device, the form of the notice affixed to a clamped vehicle and the manner in which and the person to whom such charge shall be paid when a vehicle is clamped on a public road. The Regulations also revoke the previous Regulations.
