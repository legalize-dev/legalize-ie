# S.I. No. 297/2020 - Health Act 1947 (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid-19) (Relevant Counties) (Amendment) Regulations 2020

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 11*th August,* 2020.

I, Stephen Donnelly, Minister for Health, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by sections 5 and 31A (inserted by [section 10](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html#sec10) of the [Health (Preservation and Protection and other Emergency Measures in the Public Interest) Act 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/act/pub/0001/print.html) (No. 1 of 2020)) of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1947) and -

- (*a*) having regard to the immediate, exceptional and manifest risk posed to human life and public health by the spread of Covid-19 and to the matters specified in subsection (2) of section 31A, and

- (*b*) having consulted with the Minister for Justice and Equality,

hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Health Act 1947 (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid**-**19) (Relevant Counties) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

###### 2.

2. Regulation 5 of the [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid**-**19) (Relevant Counties) Regulations 2020 ( [S.I. No. 295 of 2020](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2020/en/si/0295.html) ) is amended by the substitution of the following paragraph for paragraph (2):

> - “(2) A person shall not organise, or cause to be organised, a funeral to be held in a relevant county other than where the number of people, excluding a minister of religion or priest (or any equivalent thereof in any religion), attending the funeral does not exceed 25.”.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

9 August, 2020.

**STEPHEN DONNELLY,**

Minister for Health.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(*This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.*)

The [Health Act 1947](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1947/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (Section 31A – Temporary Restrictions) (Covid - 19) (Relevant Counties) Regulations 2020 provide for certain temporary restrictions in County Kildare, County Laois and County Offaly because of Covid-19. These Regulations amend those Regulations to clarify that up to 25 people may attend a funeral.
