# S.I. No. 437/1999 - Insurance Act, 1989 (Reinsurance) (Form of Notification) Regulations, 1999

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

I, NOEL TREACY, Minister of State at the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by [sections 5](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/sec0005.html#sec5) and [22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/sec0022.html#sec22) of the [Insurance Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1989) (as adapted by the Enterprise and Employment (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order, 1997 ( [S.I. No. 305 of 1997](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1997/en/si/0305.html) ), and the Enterprise, Trade and Employment (Delegation of Ministerial Functions) Order, 1998 ( [S.I. No. 265 of 1998](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1998/en/si/0265.html) )), hereby make the following regulations:

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Insurance Act, 1989 (Reinsurance) (Form of Notification) Regulations, 1999.

- (2) These Regulations shall come into operation on the 30th day of December, 1999.

- (3) In these Regulations “general manager” includes chief executive and chief operating officer.

###### 2.

2. The form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby prescribed as the form of notification to be made by a person to whom [section 22](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/sec0022.html#sec22) (1) (*b*) of the [Insurance Act, 1989](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1989/en/act/pub/0003/index.html) (No. 3 of 1989), refers.
