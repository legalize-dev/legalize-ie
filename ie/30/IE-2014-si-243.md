# S.I. No. 243/2014 - Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2014

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

*Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in*

*“Iris Oifigiúil” of* 10*th June*, 2014.

The Minister for the Environment, Community and Local Government, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) (No. 28 of 1992) (as adapted by the Environment, Heritage and Local Government (Alteration of Name of Department and Title of Minister) Order 2011 ( [S.I. No. 193 of 2011](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/2011/en/si/0193.html) )), hereby makes the following Regulations:—

###### 1.

1. (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) Regulations 2014.

- (2) These Regulations and the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 1992 to 2013 shall be construed as one and may be cited together as the Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) Regulations 1992 to 2014.

- (3) In these Regulations, “trade licence” means a licence issued under [section 21](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/sec0021.html#sec21) of the [Finance (No. 2) Act 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/act/pub/0028/index.html) .

###### 2.

2. These Regulations shall apply to trade licences issued on or after 01 June 2014.

###### 3.

3. The Road Vehicles (Registration and Licensing) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1992 ( [S.I. No. 409 of 1992](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/1992/en/si/0409.html) ) are hereby amended:—

EXPLANATORY NOTE

*(This note is not part of the Instrument and does not purport to be a legal interpretation.)*

These Regulations provide for the new licensing authority titles of Limerick City and County Council, Tipperary County Council and Waterford City and County Council for the purposes of issuing trade plates to motor dealers.
